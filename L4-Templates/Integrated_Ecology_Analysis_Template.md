# INTEGRATED ECOLOGY ANALYSIS TEMPLATE
## Church in Conversation — V7
### Doc_07 — Per-World Integration Document

**File naming convention:** [world-code]_Integrated_Ecology_Analysis.md  
**Produced at:** Construction Step 7 (alongside Doc_08 Forces Document)  
**Required inputs:** Doc_01, Doc_02, Doc_03, Doc_04, Doc_05, Doc_06 all complete  
**Required before:** Doc_08 Forces-and-Gravities Synthesis, World Profile, Representative emergence  
**Voice:** Analytical throughout — this is a construction record, not a runtime document  
**Target length:** 3,000–6,000 words depending on world complexity

This document integrates the construction work of Steps 1–6 into a single coherent
formation logic. It is not a summary of previous documents. It is the synthesis that
reveals what those documents, taken together, show about how this world formed people —
the coherence that no single document could reveal alone.

The critical distinction:

Doc_04/05 described the ecology. Doc_07 integrates what the ecology reveals when all
lenses are applied simultaneously.

**Wrong:** "This community understood kinship through shared suffering. The Eucharist
was central. Authority was episcopal."

**Right:** "When the kinship structure, the martyrdom memory, and the Eucharistic gravity
are held together, they reveal a formation logic organized around the body as the site of
both death and life — a logic in which physical suffering is not peripheral to formation
but its primary crucible."

The lenses reveal what the individual ecology documents cannot show: the coherence of
the world, the way its parts hang together into a distinctive way of being Christian.
That coherence is what the Representative ultimately inhabits.
