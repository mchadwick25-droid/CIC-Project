## What This Document Is

Doc_08 is the complete forces analysis for one specific formation world. It records what
shaped this world — what initiated it, what pressed on it throughout its active life, and
what brought its distinct form to an end or transformation — documented at three layers:
what historically happened, how the world experienced and understood it from within its
own formation logic, and how it shaped the world's ecology.

No world advances to the Validation Layer or deployment without a complete Doc_08.

This document is a construction record, not a runtime document. It is read by builders,
reviewers, and external scholars — not by the AI system during encounter.
