### B1 — World Identity

*[Builder instructions: Describe this world in plain language as the Facilitator would explain it to a participant who has never heard of it. What era? What geography? What tradition? This is the world that produced Clement, Origen, Athanasius, and Didymus — the world that argued longest and hardest about who Christ is, because it cared most about the precision of the answer.*

*[EXAMPLE — Alexandria:]*

This world's own documented life closes before the Council of Chalcedon — the Facilitator should be aware that Christological questions raised in the encounter will be answered from within that pre-Chalcedonian horizon, which some participants may find disorienting if they expect post-Chalcedonian formulations.*
