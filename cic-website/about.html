About

What everything is for

Mission

Church in Conversation exists to help people experience Jesus in new ways through engagement with the living traditions and practices of His Church throughout history and today.

For two thousand years, Christians have sought to understand Jesus, interpret His Word, worship faithfully, cultivate life in Christ, and embody faith within their world. These efforts have taken shape in countless traditions, movements, communities, and expressions of the Church. Each bears witness to something. Together, Christ's many movements across history form one vast testimony to the ongoing work of Jesus among His people.

Church in Conversation exists to help people enter that testimony — through meaningful encounter with faithful witnesses from across the life of the Church. The goal is deeper encounter with the One these witnesses ultimately point to.

We are committed to representing each Christian movement in full — its testimony, beautiful and hard alike.

We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. What it means for you is yours to own and share.

Where we are today

That's the full scope of what this project is for. What's actually built and live right now is its first chapter — Church in Conversation: The First Centuries — traditions from the Church's own first four hundred years. Later centuries are planned, each its own careful, review-gated build, not a shortcut on top of the first.

The Five Convictions

What we believe, and what governs every decision we make

These convictions gave rise to this project. The technology exists to serve them.

  1. We Encounter More of Christ Through the Witnesses of His Church

    The richness and faithfulness of Christ are revealed through the many historical and contemporary movements of His Church. Together, they form a vast testimony to His ongoing work among His people.

  2. Understanding Grows Most Deeply Through Encounter

    Rather than presenting history, theology, and tradition as information alone, we create opportunities for meaningful encounter. Encounter transforms information into understanding, and understanding into possibility.

  3. Every Witness Bears Testimony Faithfully

    A witness speaks for a particular movement within Christ's Church — allowed to speak openly in its own voice, pointing beyond itself to the One it ultimately serves, plainly and on its own terms.

  4. Authentic Encounter Requires Trustworthy Transparency

    Participants get access to sources, evidence, tensions, disagreements, uncertainties, and limitations — the truth, plainly, held to a higher standard than simplification, sharing the beauty and hard things alike.

  5. Technology Should Serve Encounter, Never Replace It

    Technology exists to create access, preserve fidelity, and support transparency — in service of human judgment, community, and spiritual formation. It expands what's possible; the encounter itself is the point.

How It Works

The build process is the foundation of the product

Each Christian tradition is built by going through a disciplined ten-step process. We gather and weigh its own letters, sermons, records, and validated sources. We research its own vocabulary, and the internal and external forces that shaped its beliefs and convictions. Every claim is checked against those sources directly and labeled for how well it's attested. External academic review is the standard we're building toward for every tradition — not yet complete, since it isn't funded yet. Only then, last, is a representative voice created out of that finished, self-contained record. It speaks only from the tradition's own letters, sermons, and primary sources. Where the historical record is thin, we let the silence stand.

Safety & Disclosure

What governs this experience

This is an AI system. We are committed to representing each Christian movement in full — its testimony, beautiful and hard alike.

Every conversation is watched in real time for a softened truth, an invented detail, or knowledge the voice shouldn't have — and corrected before it reaches you. If a conversation touches real distress, a separate, clearly-labeled voice steps in to direct you toward real human support.

Every conversation is recorded. We review conversations — in real time, to catch and correct the kind of problem named above, and afterward, to see how well each voice is representing its tradition and to make it better. Conversations are not sold or shared outside this work.

About Us

Who's behind this

It's grown into a disciplined, scholarship-first construction process, continually learning from real participation and aspiring to an academic review process to ensure the historical record is being accurately represented.
