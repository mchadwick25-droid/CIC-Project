About

What everything is for

Mission

Church in Conversation exists to help people experience Jesus in new ways through engagement with the living traditions and practices of His Church throughout history and today.

For two thousand years, Christians have sought to follow Jesus together. They have gathered to worship, engaged the Scriptures, built lives reflecting their understanding of Christ, and lived out their faith in every kind of circumstance. This has taken shape in countless traditions, movements, and communities. Each one bears witness to something real. Together, they form one vast testimony to the ongoing work of Jesus among His people.

Church in Conversation exists to help people engage these Christian traditions. That means conversations with representative voices, and access to their letters, stories, and legacy. The goal is deeper encounter with the One these traditions followed.

We are committed to representing each Christian movement as completely as possible — its testimony, lived reality, beauty and humanity.

Where we are today

That's the full scope of what this project is for. What's actually built and live right now is its first chapter — Church in Conversation: The First Centuries — traditions from the Church's own first four hundred years. Later centuries are planned, each its own careful, review-gated build, not a shortcut on top of the first.

The Five Convictions

What we believe, and what governs every decision we make

These convictions gave rise to this project. The technology exists to serve them.

  1. We Encounter More of Christ Through the Witnesses of His Church

    The richness and faithfulness of Christ are revealed through the many historical and contemporary movements of His Church. Together, they form a vast testimony to His ongoing work among His people.

  2. Formation Grows More Deeply Through Encounter

    Rather than presenting history, theology, and tradition as information alone, we strive to create opportunities for meaningful encounter that we hope engages the soul.

  3. The Representative Voice Is a Faithful Witness

    A representative voice speaks for a single Christian tradition, built only from that movement's own record.

  4. Authentic Encounter Requires Trustworthy Transparency

    Participants get access to the evidence, sources and documents. We are transparent about the whole story the record gives.

  5. Technology Should Serve Encounter, Never Replace It

    Technology exists to create access, preserve fidelity, and support transparency — in service of human judgment, community, and spiritual formation. It expands what's possible; the encounter itself is the point.

How It Works

The build process is the foundation of the product

We build each Christian tradition through a disciplined eight-step process. We gather and weigh its own letters, sermons, records, and validated sources. We research its own vocabulary, and the internal and external forces that shaped its beliefs and convictions. We check every claim directly against those sources, and label it for how well it's attested. External academic review is the standard we're building toward for every tradition — not yet complete, since it isn't funded yet. Only then do we create a representative voice from that finished, self-contained record. It speaks only from the tradition's own letters, sermons, and primary sources. Where the historical record is thin, we let the silence stand.

Safety & Disclosure

What governs this experience

We use artificial intelligence to generate each conversation. The record behind it — the Atlas and its sources — is real historical research.

We monitor every conversation to protect against a softened truth, an invented detail, or knowledge the voice shouldn't have. We correct it before it reaches you. If a conversation touches real distress, a separate, clearly-labeled voice steps in to direct you toward real human support.

Who's behind this
