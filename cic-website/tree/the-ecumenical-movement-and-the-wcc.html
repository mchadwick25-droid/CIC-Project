About This Movement

Synthesized voice — not a recording.

The World Missionary Conference at Edinburgh in 1910 left three currents running, which came together over the following decades. The International Missionary Council was founded in October 1921; Life and Work, concerned with the churches' common action in society, took shape in 1925; Faith and Order, concerned with the doctrinal questions that divided them, in 1927. The last two merged into the World Council of Churches, constituted at Amsterdam on 22 August 1948 with 147 member churches, and the International Missionary Council joined them at New Delhi in 1961. It is a council of churches, not a church: it has no authority over its members and no confession of its own, and each member body keeps its own. Orthodox churches are members. The Roman Catholic Church is not, and works with it in other ways.
