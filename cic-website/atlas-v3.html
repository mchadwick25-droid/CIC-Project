Reading the marks
House — built and open: you can sit down with this tradition now.  Plans — chosen for construction; not yet built.  Question — real history under review, deferred, or not (yet) in the plan.  Closed door — outside the Nicene base this project builds from; the grounds are always stated in the entry, and exclusion is never a judgment of unimportance.
Time flows downward in order — vertical distance is not to scale. Each movement is a box at its birth; its tail falls through the events of its lifetime (a fading tail runs past this slice's edge). Color names the high-level tradition family; a movement's status is stated in words on hover and in its entry. Threads are drawn only where the reviewed record carries a relationship — confidence always in words: documented · widely accepted · contested (contested also dashed). Hover or tap to trace a lineage; click for the full entry — the same template for every movement, built or not.
