Welcome to the whole landscape

Two thousand years of Christians have known Jesus from inside very different traditions. Several of those traditions are open for conversation today — they glow at full color.

Everything else is real history, honestly labeled: chosen-but-not-built, deferred with reasons, not yet assessed, or excluded on stated grounds you can read in full. Nothing here is hover-dead. Absence is never a judgment of unimportance.

The World Atlas — Research Table

Every candidate Christian tradition across two thousand years of Christian history — built, chosen, deferred, excluded-with-reasons, and not-yet-surveyed — with the plain explanation a participant would read for each. Eras 1–2 reproduce the Phase One Step 0 record (Phase One deliberately spans both, creed to council); Eras 3–10 carry signals, not verdicts.
