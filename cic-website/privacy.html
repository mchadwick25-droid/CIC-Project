Privacy

What we do with a conversation

What's stored

Your conversation is saved

This is a pilot. This page says what we store, how long we keep it, and how to ask us to delete it.

When you talk with a Representative or at a Table, we save the conversation. That includes your messages and every reply. We use it to check accuracy, catch problems, and see how real conversations go.

We do not ask for your name or email. We do not collect them unless you send them to us yourself, for example by writing to us.

Each conversation gets a session code. We store only a scrambled version of that code (a hash), not the code itself. The code lets you come back to your conversation later.

Your messages are stored in the same database as that scrambled code and your visitor ID (see below). A conversation can be linked to both.

We also keep a usage log. It records the cost and size of each model call, the time, and which tradition was used. It does not hold the text of your messages.

Contributions

Giving goes through Stripe

If you give to the project, you pay on Stripe's own pages. Stripe holds your name, email and payment details. Our conversation system never receives them, and nothing links a contribution to a conversation.

The visitor cookie

One cookie, for a daily limit

When you start a conversation or send a message, our server may set a cookie named cic_visitor. It holds a random ID and a signature that proves we issued it. It holds no name, email, or device details.

The cookie does one job. It lets us set a daily limit: 5 new conversations and 150 messages per visitor each day (UTC). The limit stops one person from running up costs for the pilot. We also count unique visitors from it.

The cookie does two jobs. It lets us set a daily limit: 5 new conversations and 150 messages per visitor each day (UTC). The limit stops one person from running up costs for the pilot. It also keeps track of your free allowance, described in the next section. We also count unique visitors from it.

The cookie is only sent to this site. It does not follow you to other sites, and we do not use it for advertising. You do not need an account.

It lasts up to 400 days. You can delete it in your browser settings at any time. If you do, you get a new ID.

Go Deeper

Tokens and the free allowance

Go Deeper lets you carry on a conversation with tokens. You pay for them on Stripe's own pages. Stripe holds your name, email and payment details. Our conversation system never receives them.

A code holds your tokens. We store only a scrambled version of the code (a hash), how many tokens it holds, the Stripe payment ID, the day it was made, and the week it was last used. We do not store your name or email with it. We do not record which conversation used which code.

We keep these records apart from your conversations and from Stripe, and no ID is shared between them. Someone who could see all of them, and your Stripe account, might still be able to guess which conversation used a code, for example from how many tokens it spent and when.

Your free allowance is kept for each visitor. We store a scrambled value made from your visitor ID, the day you first used it, and how much you have used. We delete it 30 days after that first day. A backup can keep it for up to 14 more days. If you delete your cookie, we can no longer match you to it.

Retention

Kept until you ask us to delete it

We keep conversations until you ask us to delete them. We have not set an automatic deletion date yet. We will update this page when we do.

If a conversation sits untouched for 7 days, we mark it as idle. That is a label only. Nothing is deleted, and you can still pick the conversation back up with your code.

Every day we back up our databases to private object storage. The backups include saved conversations. We keep the last 14 days of backups, and older ones are deleted automatically. So when we delete a conversation, it can stay in a backup for up to 14 more days.

Who reads it

The project team, only

Only the project team reads conversations. We do not sell conversation data. We will not publish or quote a conversation without asking you first.

Deletion

Ask us, and we'll remove it

There is no delete button yet. To have a conversation deleted, email info@churchinconversation.com. Tell us roughly when it happened and which tradition you spoke with. If you have your session code, send it. We will confirm when it is done. Please allow a few days.

Questions

Ask us anything

We are a small team. If anything here is unclear, write to info@churchinconversation.com.
