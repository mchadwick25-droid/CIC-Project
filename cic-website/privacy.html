Privacy

What we do with a conversation

What's stored

Your conversation is saved and cataloged

If you talk with a Representative at the Table, the text of that conversation — your messages and the Representative's — is saved. This is a pilot: we're still learning what's working and what isn't, and the transcript is how we find out. We don't collect your name, email, or any other identifying information as part of a conversation unless you choose to give it to us directly (for example, by emailing us).

Who reads it

The project team, only

Conversations are reviewed by the Church in Conversation project team for the purpose of improving the project — checking accuracy, catching problems, and understanding what a real conversation with a Representative is actually like. We do not sell conversation data, and we do not publish or quote from a specific conversation publicly without asking you first.

Retention

Kept for as long as this pilot needs it

Because this is an early-stage pilot, we don't yet have a fixed retention schedule the way a mature product would. Transcripts are kept for as long as they're useful to the pilot's review process, and this page will be updated with a concrete retention period once that's settled. If you'd like your conversation deleted sooner, see below — we'll act on that request directly.

Deletion

Ask us, and we'll remove it

Email info@churchinconversation.com and ask us to delete a conversation — tell us roughly when it happened and which tradition you talked with, since we don't collect anything else that would let us find it for you. Deletion is handled by hand by the project team at this stage — there's no self-serve button — so please allow a few days. We'll confirm once it's done.

Questions

Ask us anything

This page describes a pilot-stage project built and operated by a small team, not a large company with a dedicated privacy office. If anything here is unclear, or you want to know more about how a specific conversation was handled, just ask: info@churchinconversation.com.
