How it works

Every flag is checked against the sources

Under each reply in a conversation, you can mark it as not clear or not accurate. A person then checks that sentence against the sources behind it.

Each one ends in one of five ways:

  • Corrected. We found an error and fixed it.
  • Checked, it stands. We checked, and the sources support the reply.
  • Open lead, still being researched. We cannot settle it yet, and we are still looking.
  • A conviction of the tradition, not an error. The reply states what that tradition believed. You may disagree with it, but it is not a mistake.
  • No action. Nothing needed to change.

Closed items are listed below. Each entry gives the world, the date, what was questioned, what we checked and the outcome. No one is named or quoted.
