How it works

Pay, and carry on

A new conversation uses 50 tokens. Each reply uses 20, or 25 after the third. A Table uses more, by the number of voices. The count sits above the conversation, and it comes down as you go.

Without an account, your tokens are saved in this browser. To use them on another device, open Get more conversations in the room and choose Show my code. With an account, your home holds them on every device.

Free to start

550 free tokens every 30 days

Every conversation starts free. 50 tokens launch a new conversation, 20 to 25 go on each question. When the free tokens run out, tokens you buy carry on in the same room.

Already have a code?

A code from a sponsor or the pilot goes in the room: open Get more conversations and choose I have a code.

Kept apart

What you say stays separate from what you paid

Stripe knows who paid. What you say in a conversation is never stored with your payment. Without an account, it is never stored with your code either.
