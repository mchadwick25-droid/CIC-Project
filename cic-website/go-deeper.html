Go deeper

Keep a conversation going

Every conversation here starts free. A free conversation lasts three rounds, and each day has a free allowance. When the free part ends, tokens let you carry on in the same conversation.

How it works

Pay, get a code, use it

You pay on Stripe's own pages. We never see your card details.

Stripe then sends you back here, and the next page shows your code.

In a conversation, open Go deeper beside the message box and enter your code. The conversation carries on from where it stopped.

What tokens pay for

Each new conversation and each round

A new conversation uses 50 tokens. Each round, one question from you and one answer, uses 20 tokens, or 25 after the third round. A Table uses more, by the number of voices. We show how many tokens you have left beside the message box.

Three packs

Choose how many tokens

  • $7: 1,100 tokens, about ten conversations of three rounds
  • $15: 2,750 tokens, about twenty-five
  • $30: 6,600 tokens, about sixty

Stripe shows the amount before you pay.

Keep it safe

A code works for whoever holds it

A code is not tied to you or to your device. Anyone who has it can use it, so keep it as you would keep cash. If you lose it, we cannot find it again.

Kept apart

What you say stays separate from what you paid

Stripe knows who paid. What you say in a conversation is never stored with your code or your payment.
