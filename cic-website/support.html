Help Us Open the Door a Little Wider

Faithways Studio, Inc., a Colorado public benefit corporation that exists to create safe spaces to explore faith and the story of Jesus, builds and operates Church in Conversation. Our aspiration is to make this accessible to everyone, historically rigorous, and fully self-sustaining over time.

What It Actually Costs

Every conversation has a real cost, turn by turn. Multi-voice Table conversations increase that cost, since each additional voice generates its own response. That's not the whole picture, either. Keeping this running also means hosting, storage, and the ongoing work of maintaining it. And as we grow — adding more Christian traditions and reaching more people — those costs grow with us. Keeping the door open to as many people as possible is where we need your help.

What We're Doing About It

We keep working to bring the cost down while improving quality. We're making the technology more efficient, and building features like the Atlas that carry no extra runtime cost. Our Accessibility Fund covers the rest — participants who couldn't otherwise afford it, translation into other languages, and building out more worlds and features.

Be Part of It

Every gift goes to the Accessibility Fund — helping more people, in more languages, explore more Christian traditions.

Church in Conversation is part of Faithways Studio, Inc., a Colorado public benefit corporation — not a nonprofit. Contributions are not tax-deductible.
