Help Us Open the Door a Little Wider

Church in Conversation is built by Faithways Studio, Inc., a Colorado Public Benefit Corporation that exists to create safe spaces to explore faith and the story of Jesus. Our aspiration is to make this accessible to everyone, historically rigorous, and fully self-sustaining over time.

What It Actually Costs

A conversation with one of our Representative voices costs us about $2 an hour in computing. That figure comes from metering our earlier setup — we've since rebuilt the engine and haven't reconciled it against a current bill, so treat it as a close estimate rather than an audited one. Multi-person conversations aren't running yet; when they are, each added voice will add real cost on top of that.

What We're Doing About It

Bringing the cost down, while continuing to improve the quality. We're making the technology remember what it's already generated instead of paying to relearn it every few minutes, fixing places where it was doing more work than it needed to, and finding faster ways to answer the questions almost everyone asks first.

Creating less expensive features. The Atlas maps over 175 historical Christian movements across ten eras, from 70 CE to today. We're also building guided tours, where a Representative hosts a participant through a real moment from their world. Neither one carries the runtime cost above.

Building an Accessibility Fund. Access matters as much as rigor does. This fund covers the cost for participants who couldn't otherwise afford it, translation into other languages, and building out more worlds and features — all the ways this reaches more people.

Funding academic review. We want independent historians and theologians conducting external scholarly review of each Representative against the historical record, on top of the sourcing safeguards already built in. That review isn't funded yet — a gift toward it funds that rigor directly.

Why It's Worth It

Most people only really know one or two Christian traditions. Many were trained to defend that one more than to explore the ones beyond it.

The Old Testament reveals Jesus through the whole of Israel's story — its worship, its psalms, its exile, its need for a rescuer. The story of the Church reveals him the same way, through two thousand years of traditions who each hold some part of what following him looks like.

Church in Conversation lets a participant research and meet any of those traditions in conversation, sourced directly from what it said and believed, with no pressure to convert to any of them.
