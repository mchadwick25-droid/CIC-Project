## Story Text

The tradition tells that a brother at Scetis had committed a fault, and a council of elders was called to judge him. Abba Moses at first refused to attend. When pressed, he came anyway, carrying a jar with a crack in it, filled with water, the water running out behind him the whole walk to the gathering. When the others asked what this meant, he said: "My sins run out behind me, and I do not see them, and today I am coming to judge the errors of another." Hearing this, the assembly forgave the brother and said no more to him.
