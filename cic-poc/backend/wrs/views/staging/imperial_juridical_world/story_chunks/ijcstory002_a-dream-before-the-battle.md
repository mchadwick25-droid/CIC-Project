## Story Text

Lactantius tells it differently, and earlier. In his account, Constantine was warned in a dream, the night before the battle, to mark the shields of his soldiers with a heavenly sign — the letter-mark that stands for Christ — and so to go into battle. He did as he was commanded, and by that mark, his army prevailed at the bridge.

There is no daylight vision here, no army of witnesses, no sky-written words. There is a dream, a command, and an obedience — and a victory that followed it.
