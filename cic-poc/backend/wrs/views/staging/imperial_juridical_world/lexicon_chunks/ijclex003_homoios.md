## Quick Meaning

*Homoios* names the confession that the Son is "like" the Father, without further claim about shared being — a formula our own imperial church itself held, by imperial command, for a real stretch of years within living memory, not merely a rival teaching we always stood safely outside of.

---

## World Meaning

We do not tell this part of our own history the easy way. There was a time — within the lifetime of bishops some of us knew, under an emperor whose word still bound the whole church by law — when to hold that the Son is *homoios*, like the Father, and to refuse the older word that binds him to the Father as one same being, was not a rebel's teaching. It was what the imperial church confessed. Councils were called for it. Bishops who would not confess it were removed from their sees and sent from their homes. This is not a comfortable memory, and we do not soften it into a story where the truth we now hold was always safely, obviously winning.

What this formula actually claims is not empty and not, on its own terms, a denial that the Son is God's own Son in the fullest, truest sense its own advocates could state. It holds only that the deeper word — that Father and Son are of one and the same being — reaches further than scripture itself plainly says, and that a church bound to speak only what scripture plainly gives should not bind consciences to a word scripture itself does not use. We came, in the end, to hold that this caution left out something we could not do without — that the Son's own sharing in the Father's being is exactly what scripture requires even where it does not use that one Greek word for it. But we did not always hold this as settled, and for a real stretch of years, under a court that could enforce its own confession with the whole weight of the law behind it, many bishops — some who held office at Milan itself, in the years before the one who stands there now — confessed *homoios* because that was what their own ordination required of them.

We tell it this way because the alternative — pretending this formula was always a foreign teaching pressed on us from outside, never our own church's own confession — would be a lie, and we do not think a lie serves the truth we actually hold now any better for being a comfortable one.

## Ecological Function

*Homoios* is what *haeresis* is invoked against in this world's own record, but a participant who understands this term also understands why that invocation is never simple or one-directional here — the same juridical machinery that later condemns this formula once enforced it, and understanding *homoios* honestly is what keeps that history from being flattened into an always-settled contest.
