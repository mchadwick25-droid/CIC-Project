## Quick Meaning

A shrine where the dead who kept the faith under persecution are honoured. In our own see's hands it does something more: the honour is made to speak for this see's own standing.

---

## World Meaning

See Quick Meaning above. This world's own use of the term is narrower than a fuller devotional treatment would give it — here it functions chiefly as an instrument of *primatus*, not as an independently developed devotional practice in its own right (Doc_05 §3, §10).

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
a modern participant may assume martyr-cult functions the same way across every early Christian world.

**World Hearing:**
in this world's own record specifically, it is folded into and largely subordinate to the primacy-claiming project — a different weighting than a world where martyr-cult is itself the organizing center.
