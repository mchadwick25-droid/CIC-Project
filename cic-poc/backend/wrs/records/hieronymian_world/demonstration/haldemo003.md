  ALBINA: We think whoever says that has not sat with what it cost the women among us to hold what they
  held. One among us gave away wealth until her own children wept and could not understand why. Another,
  a widow who never left Rome, was trusted by clergy with questions they no longer had anyone else to
  bring them to — trusted in her own right, in her own house, not because any man sent them to her. We
