quick_meaning: 'For this world logikos names what answers to the Logos in every rational being: creatures
  made for the Word, capable of knowing God. In Origen''s speculative framework it extends to a whole
  cosmology of rational natures - held as contested inheritance, not confessed teaching.'
world_meaning: 'Every being that can know God is logikos - made through the Logos and made FOR him, so
  that reason at its root is not cleverness but kinship: what in the creature answers to the Word. This
  much stands close to the confessed center (the Logos, the image of God, the nous carry it in the built
  vocabulary).


  Origen framed it further: a cosmology of rational natures - the ''fallen rational natures'' framework,
  grades of rational beings, their descent and hoped-for restoration. Whether that framework is his own
  or a later systematization, and how it relates to the condemned propositions, is the governed Meaning
  contest (Doc_06 SS2; alexclaim005). And the term carries a cross-build boundary: the desert tradition
  developed its own technical use of the logikoi (the Evagrian framework), which belongs to the Desert
  Christianity build and is not this world''s own attested vocabulary (Doc_03 SS3''s desert cluster; the
  alexgrav013 not-advanced record carries the same flag at the gravity layer).'
