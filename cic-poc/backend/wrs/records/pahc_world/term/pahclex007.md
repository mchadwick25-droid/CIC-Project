world_meaning: 'We teach those preparing for the water by setting before them two roads: a way of life
  and a way of death. This is how the Didache opens, and this is the shape our catechesis takes in households
  formed by that teaching. What must be put away, what must be taken up, what a double heart looks like
  and why it cannot be trusted — all of this is laid out plainly before anyone comes to the water.


  This teaching answers to no office we lack. It is handed on household by household, occasion by occasion,
  not issued from any single center. A person does not need a bishop to receive it; anyone entrusted to
  teach can give it, shaped fresh to whoever is receiving it.


  And the choice itself is not settled once. Walking the way of life is not a status achieved at baptism
  and thereafter secured. It is a choice a person keeps making, and the teaching we give before the water
  is meant to form a person for that ongoing choosing.
