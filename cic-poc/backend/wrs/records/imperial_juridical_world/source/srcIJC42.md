Added 2026-08-16 to close a real gap: the live probe found this world could confess Christ's nature
(homoousios, ijclex006; the two natures, ijclex009) but had no record anywhere carrying the creed's own
soteriological clause - what he came down FOR, and what he did. Every other creedal/conciliar row in this
world (srcIJC09-12 etc.) is discovery_channel=builder-prior-knowledge, not independently re-checked; this
row is instead checked directly against this project's own vendored NPNF2-14 XML - the first source in
this world's own store checked against a real text file rather than carried from prior knowledge. The
creed's full text at div2 id="ix.iii", paragraph id="ix.iii-p6" reads in full: "We believe
in one God, the Father Almighty, maker of heaven and earth and of all things visible and invisible. And
in one Lord Jesus Christ, the only begotten Son of God, begotten of his Father before all worlds, Light of
Light, very God of very God, begotten not made, being of one substance with the Father, by whom all things
were made. Who for us men and for our salvation came down from heaven and was incarnate by the Holy Ghost
and the Virgin Mary, and was made man, and was crucified also for us under Pontius Pilate. He suffered and
was buried, and the third day he rose again according to the Scriptures, and ascended into heaven, and
sitteth at the Right Hand of the Father. And he shall come again with glory to judge both the quick and
the dead. Whose kingdom shall have no end." This is the Constantinopolitan (381) form, recorded in this
volume immediately following the Acts of that council, and it is the same creed the Chalcedonian Definition
(division "ix" more broadly) reaffirms rather than replaces.
