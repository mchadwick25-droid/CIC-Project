## Finding 2 — [SUBSTANTIAL] The DEV Battery's flagship "synthesis-creep" finding rests on a citation error, and the error runs the wrong direction

Phase 5B §4 states: *"Doc_07's own synthesis, reproduced verbatim at World Profile Section 10, names exactly three surface expressions of the underlying pattern: raza's binding, Iḥidaya's oneness, and the harmonized Gospel's narrative unity — **not the qyama vow's own 'keeping' as a fourth**."* This claim is the entire evidentiary basis for flagging Turn 3 as "synthesis-creep."

This is factually wrong. World Profile §10 (Doc_07 Section 5, the actual verbatim Integrative Observation) does **not** contain a "raza/Iḥidaya/Gospel" triad at all. Its actual structure is: Iḥidaya named as the overarching telos a practitioner is formed toward, followed by three parallel sentences — "The raza does not merely illustrate..."; **"The qyama vow does not merely regulate behavior — it is the lifelong shape undividedness takes..."**; "The harmonized Gospel does not merely summarize..." — closed by "**beneath all three**, this world never gave its practitioners one settled authority..." The "three" in Section 10 are explicitly **raza, the qyama vow, and the harmonized Gospel** — the vow is one of the three, not excluded from them.

The "raza's binding, Iḥidaya's oneness, and the Diatessaron's narrative unity" triad Phase 5B actually means to invoke belongs to a *different* passage — World Profile §4B / Phase Seven §7.1 (Doc_07 Section 2B) — which is a separate synthesis about symbolic ontology, not the Integrative Observation.

Phase 5B has conflated two distinct three-part groupings from two different sections of Doc_07 and, in doing so, asserted the opposite of what its own cited source says. Once corrected, Turn 3's move — treating the vow's "keeping" as "much the same kind of thing" as the raza's "binding" — is not an unlicensed fourth element; it is close to what Section 10 *itself* already pairs. This does not fully vindicate Turn 3 (a narrower, more defensible concern remains: Turn 3 imports the raza's specific ontological mechanism — pre-existing connection revealed, not invented — onto the vow, which Section 10 does not itself claim), but the document's actual stated argument for its own headline "genuine new finding" is unsound as written. This is not a cosmetic mis-citation; it undercuts the credibility of the one result Phase 5B repeatedly foregrounds as this round's most significant contribution (carried into Section 4.2's AMBIGUOUS score, the Summary Table, and the Overall Verdict's closing recommendation).
