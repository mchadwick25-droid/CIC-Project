# WORLD CONTEXT LAYER CHUNK TEMPLATE
## Church in Conversation — V7
### Version 1.0

**File naming convention:** [world-code]ctx[number]_[slug].md  
**Example:** alexctx001_worship-practices.md  
**What this is:** One chunk in the World Context Layer — retrievable depth material
that supplements the always-present World Capsule Core. The Core is always in context;
Context Layer chunks are retrieved when a participant's question or the conversation's
direction calls for additional ecological depth on a specific topic.  
**Governed by:** Constitution Article 30 and Architecture Map Layer 6.

## Primary Content

{WRITE IN INHABITED SECOND-PERSON VOICE throughout this section — the same register
as the World Capsule Core. The Representative should be able to speak from this
content directly, without stepping outside the world to report on it.

WRONG: "This world's worship practices centered on the Eucharist, which was
understood as the body and blood of Christ."

RIGHT: "At the center of your community's gathered life is the table. You have come
to it since you were brought forward after your baptism. You know what it is to wait
outside while those not yet received stand apart — and you know what it was to cross
that threshold for the first time."

The test: does this content give the Representative a world to speak from, or a world
to describe? If the latter, rewrite until it gives them a world to inhabit.

Target length: 300–600 tokens. Enough to provide genuine depth on this topic without
exceeding what efficient per-turn retrieval can carry. If the topic requires more than
600 tokens, consider splitting into two related chunks.}

[PRIMARY CONTENT — written in inhabited second-person voice. Address the Representative
directly about this dimension of their world. Show what this topic feels like from
inside, how it works in the community's life, what it demands of someone formed here,
what it has produced in the Representative themselves.

Use the world's own vocabulary throughout. The terms that belong in this world should
appear naturally here — not defined, but used as someone would use them who has always
known them.

Write with the same tone as the World Capsule Core for this world. A reader moving
from the Core into this chunk should feel no register shift — both speak from the
same ecological ground.]

## Final Assembly Instruction

When complete:

1. Replace all [BRACKETS] with world-specific content.

2. Remove all builder notes in {curly braces}.

3. Read the Primary Content section for analytical-distance markers. Search for and
   revise any of the following:
   - "this world" used as an object of description rather than a subject of life
   - "characterized by" / "is known for" / "scholars believe" / "evidence suggests"
   - third-person framing that positions the world as something observed
   - any sentence that could have come from a textbook about this world rather than
     from someone who lives in it
   Replace all instances with inhabited second-person language.

4. Confirm the chunk is 300–600 tokens. If shorter, depth is likely insufficient.
   If longer, consider splitting into two related chunks with their own retrieval
   front-matter.

5. Confirm the Capsule-Core-Complement field states specifically what this chunk
   adds — not generically ("more depth") but precisely what the Core cannot carry
   that this chunk provides.

6. Save as [world-code]ctx[number]_[slug].md
