## 1. Required Integration Lenses

### 1A — Emotional / Affective Ecology
What it felt like to inhabit this world is part of its formation logic, not a secondary question. The affective register shifts across the horizon:
- **Early / formative (Clement, to c. 215):** a confident, expansive, intellectually energized synthesis — the sense that all genuine truth already belongs to Christ, that learning is a homecoming rather than a danger.
- **Mid (Origen, c. 215–254):** intense, inward, formation-urgent, and pressed by persecution — the interior life taken with ultimate seriousness under the weight of possible torture and death (Origen's own imprisonment under Decius).
- **Late / post-Nicene (Athanasius and Didymus, c. 296–400):** embattled, doctrinally urgent, communally resilient — a formation life lived under repeated exile and contested from within the church rather than only from outside it.

*[Inhabited — literate-attested]* At Nicaea the air changed: what had been open was now, in one place, closed. The settlement was relief — the boundary drawn, the Word confessed of one substance — and it was also a new weight, because now what one had loved to hold loosely had to be held within a line.

**The emotional texture of the four tensions** (each a felt reality, not only a structural one): the **Teacher–Bishop** tension as the ache of two real authorities; the **Learning–Community** tension as the ecology's most persistent structural ache — the knowledge that the depth the school offers the few is not the mode through which most of the community is being formed; the **Speculative–Doctrinal** tension as the ache around Origen (some of what he perceived can no longer be held quite as he held it); the **Martyrdom–Contemplative** tension as the pull between the sudden ultimate witness and the long ascent.

[Construction note: the *tone* is **Widely Accepted for the school tradition** (readable directly in Clement, Origen, Athanasius); the affective *reconstruction* built from it is **Dominant Modern Reconstruction** (felt interior is inherently more inferential than structure); the late-horizon configuration is **less directly evidenced** (thinner) than the early/mid. **The interior emotional life of the non-literate majority is not narrated** — a named absence (OG-4).]

### 1B — Intellectual / Philosophical Ecology
This is, in the school tradition, **among the most philosophically engaged** early Christian formation worlds of its first four centuries — its defining intellectual character (the comparative claim is a broadly-held scholarly characterization, not a strictly-quantified ranking). Three moves: the **inheritance** (Philo's synthesis, Middle Platonism, the Stoic Logos as a pre-shaped intellectual environment the ecology entered rather than built); the **transformation** (what the ecology does to that inheritance — chiefly at the *telos*: it keeps the Platonic ascent of the soul but reorients it toward a Logos who first *descended*); and the **limit** (philosophy is preparation and vehicle, never destination — the Gnostic boundary marks where the ecology refuses to let knowledge become an esoteric possession). [Construction note: **Widely Accepted for the school tradition, early/mid horizon** (Clement's *Stromateis*, Origen's corpus — extensive direct evidence; Author-Gravity Origen-concentrated → **DMR** for ecology-wide reach); the late-horizon philosophical configuration is **thinner** (Didymus the best-documented late witness, and his surviving evidence is less).]

### 1C — Authority Structures
The **Teacher–Bishop tension** is the most architecturally significant feature of the authority ecology. Pre-Nicene: two authorities — the teacher's, grounded in demonstrated wisdom, and the bishop's, grounded in appointed office — genuinely coexisting. At Nicaea the bishop becomes the enforcer of conciliar orthodoxy (authority now "apostolic succession *plus* conciliar definition"), and the tension turns asymmetric toward the office-pole. What the tension *produces* is mutual accountability — and its late-horizon shift is directly tied to the **attenuation of Learning-Formation Integration** (§4A). [Construction note: the tension rests on **structural coexistence** (Widely Accepted); the Origen–Demetrius episode, its paradigmatic illustration, is **Eusebius-mediated, HIGH Author-Gravity risk** — its value is taken as *structural* (what it expresses about two kinds of authority), not as secure factual particulars (Doc_02 §3.6; Doc_04 T1).]

### 1D — Boundary Structures
The world knew itself partly by what it refused. Three boundaries: the **Gnostic boundary** (early/mid — truth is for the whole community, not an esoteric elite; the *Flesh/Sarx* vs *Body/Soma* distinction is boundary-maintenance vocabulary); the **doctrinal boundary** (late — the Arian controversy; Athanasius's argument that the *homoousios* is a *formation* stake, not only a doctrinal one; the Rule of Faith clarifying toward the Nicene Creed as custodial); and the **internal boundary — Speculative vs Doctrinal**, which runs *inside* the tradition, its primary site being the Origen inheritance. On that internal boundary this world holds Origen as **inheritance and unease at once** — the teacher whose perception it treasures and whose boldest speculations it can no longer hold quite as he held them. *(A world-level feature; how any future Representative would carry it is a Step-10 matter, out of scope here.)* [Construction note: the Gnostic and Arian refusals **Widely Accepted**; the internal Origen-unease **Widely Accepted** as a feature of the horizon; the 553 condemnation must **not** be read back into the earlier local dispute (Doc_01 §9).]

### 1E — Formation Logic *(the lens on how formation actually works)*
Formation runs on the **Participation↔Perception dynamic**: the soul participates and thereby perceives more, and perceives and thereby participates more deeply — a **cumulative spiral** (catechesis → illumination → gnosis → wisdom → participation, toward the horizon of theosis), never stages left behind, requiring genuineness on both sides. It operates differently across three populations:
- **The school-tradition participant:** formation through guided depth-reading and the teacher relationship.
- **The whole-community participant (the non-literate majority):** formation *through communal, bodily channels* — the assembly's proclamation, the Eucharist, the Paschal fast. This mode requires no literacy and no teacher-access; it is why the Eucharist, the Paschal cycle, and the proclaimed word are ecologically *essential*, not supplementary. **[Stratum discipline: the majority's formation is marked here by its channels and by the desire and attention visible in the practice — not by any narrated account of their interior, which the evidence does not support (OG-4).]**
- **The martyr:** formation revealed (not produced) in a single ultimate moment — a mode that, uniquely, crosses every stratum.

What formation produces: in the individual, wisdom, love, and virtue; in the community, the capacity to *hold* the tensions without collapsing them.

## 2. Additional Lenses (Where Evidence Supports)

### 2A — Memory Structures
How the ecology carries its formation inheritance across time, through three instruments: **martyrological memory** (commemorations, hagiography, the Festal-Letter/Paschal cycle — the *continuous* pole of the Martyrdom–Contemplative tension, keeping the martyr present between persecutions); the **interpretive inheritance** (what is transmitted is interpretive *capacity*, not fixed content — which makes it powerful and **specifically fragile**: a succession can pass on texts and method while losing the formed perception that made them live — the evidence "cannot fully answer" whether the capacity was in fact maintained across the horizon, and the structural vulnerability is real); and **creedal / Rule-of-Faith memory** (constraining rather than generative — necessary but not sufficient). [Construction note: martyr memory **DMR** (Stream 8; Tier-3 hagiography); the interpretive-succession vulnerability is **self-flagged as evidentially open**; the Pantaenus→Clement→Origen succession is Eusebius-mediated (HIGH risk).]

### 2B — Interpretive Ecology
Interpretation is the primary mode through which school-tradition formation operates. The **levels of Scripture** are formation logic, not a learnable technique — the literal/moral/spiritual senses map onto formation stages, so the depth one perceives tracks the formation one has undergone; the **christological *skopos*** is the formation center (the whole read toward Christ); the **allegorical discipline** is a formation *practice* — accompaniment that requires formation in the one who reads, not a decoding method transferable to anyone. [Construction note: **Widely Accepted for the school tradition**; the systematized levels-account is Origen-concentrated → **DMR** ecology-wide (Doc_04 C1).]

### 2C — The World's Own Theological-Reasoning Patterns
*(Re-framed from the source's "Representative Theological Patterns": these are patterns of **the world's own** theological reasoning — how this world thinks — carried at the world level, with no Representative voice constructed or presupposed.)* Five patterns characterize Alexandrian theological reasoning: the **participation grammar** (reality described in participatory, not representational or forensic, terms — a soul *shares in* rather than *stands before*); the **gradation instinct** (formation is always graduated; nothing is one-size); the **holding-together move** (both/and without collapsing a genuine tension); the **Logos-center** (the Logos is the *grammar* of the reasoning, not one topic within it); and **incompleteness-holding** (a refusal to rush a genuine tension or an unfinished formation to premature resolution — Hope, *alexlex045*, is the term for this posture). [Construction note: these patterns are abstracted from the school-tradition corpus (Clement, Origen, Athanasius) — **Widely Accepted for the school tradition**, **DMR** as ecology-wide.]

## 4. Integrative Observation and Cross-Lens Synthesis

### 4A — Cross-Gravity Interactions
Four interactions the lenses revealed:
1. **Scripture ↔ Transformation — mutual constitution.** The two Primary gravities are not parallel but *mutually constituting*: Scripture is the medium through which transformation operates; transformation opens the capacity through which Scripture's depth is received. The Participation↔Perception dynamic *is* this relationship (mechanism, not metaphor). What it produces that neither alone would: the cumulative spiral — which is why formation here cannot be reduced to instruction.
2. **Divine Pedagogy + Logos-Centered Unity — the theological frame.** The two "framing" Supporting gravities interact mainly *with the Primaries*: Divine Pedagogy answers *why* Scripture forms (God is teaching); Logos-Centered Unity answers *why all the practices belong to one formation* (one divine center). Together they keep the Primaries from being two parallel activities.
3. **Teacher–Bishop ↔ Learning–Community — two distinct tensions sharing a single cause.** These remain two distinct Tensional gravities (Doc_04 T1, T2), but they are tightly linked: the Learning–Community tension is the *social* face of what the Teacher–Bishop tension is on the *authority* face (school-depth vs community-breadth), and the *same* post-Nicene shift makes both asymmetric at once. Reading one inflection rightly is reading the other — without collapsing them into one tension.
4. **Learning-Formation attenuation — the fourth effect.** The late-horizon attenuation of the Learning-Formation Integration gravity is not independent: as episcopal authority achieves dominance on a ground (apostolic succession) that does *not* require demonstrated wisdom, the institutional argument for the inseparability of learning and formation weakens.

### 4B — The Two Hubs (the deepest integrating insight)
The synthesis converges on **two hubs**:
- **The Participation↔Perception dynamic** is the *mechanism-hub* — the single relationship through which the Primary gravities actually do their forming.
- **The post-Nicene authority shift** is the *single structural cause* producing **four visible effects at once**: the Teacher–Bishop asymmetry and the Learning–Community asymmetry (interaction 3), the Learning-Formation attenuation (interaction 4), and the boundary shift from external to internal threat (1D) — with the late-horizon affective register (1A) as the felt accompaniment of the same shift. Reading any one of these inflections rightly is reading all of them.

### 4C — Tensions the Reconstruction Must Preserve
*(In the source these were framed as "tensions that must travel into deployment" and attributed to the Representative. Here they are held at the **world level** — features of the reconstructed ecology that must not be smoothed away by any downstream use; how a Representative would carry them is a Step-10 matter, out of this thread's scope.)* **Six** genuinely-distinct features the world held and the reconstruction preserves — the **four Tensional gravities** (each a genuine two-pole tension, Doc_04 T1–T4) plus **two world-level features** that are a *posture* and a *vulnerability* rather than two-pole tensions:
1. **Teacher–Bishop** (T1 — two real authorities). 2. **Learning–Community / the stratum non-integration** (T2 — school-depth and communal-breadth formation both required, not fully integrable; the world-level form of OG-4). 3. **Speculative vs Doctrinal-Boundary** (T3 — freedom of inquiry vs the drawn line, whose *paradigmatic site is the Origen inheritance*: his perception treasured, his speculation now under question; §1D). 4. **Martyrdom vs contemplative ascent** (T4 — two pictures of a formed life, uncollapsed). Plus two features that are not two-pole gravities but must equally not be smoothed away: 5. **The incompleteness of formation** (a *posture* — Hope, *alexlex045*: the refusal to declare the soul finished). 6. **The transmission fragility** (a *vulnerability* — perceptual capacity that cannot be passed as content). A reconstruction that *resolves* any of these has failed the world; so has one that merely *lists* them without showing what each costs. *(This corrects the source, which listed seven by counting the Origen inheritance separately from the speculative/doctrinal boundary it is the site of — they are one tension, T3.)*
