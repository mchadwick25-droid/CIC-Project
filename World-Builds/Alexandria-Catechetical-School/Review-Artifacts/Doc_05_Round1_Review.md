## SUBSTANTIAL FINDINGS

### S1 — [SUBSTANTIAL] §5 (Ministry/Formation lens): T4 Martyrdom-vs-Contemplative-Ascent is resolved, not held
**Location:** §5, "Martyrdom as formation" bullet and its inhabited passage.
**What's wrong.** Doc_04 confirmed **T4 (Martyrdom-as-Formation vs Contemplative-Ascent-as-Formation)** as a *Tensional* gravity with genuine practice/population separation and a **competing** interaction with C2 ("T4 ↔ C2 (C→X): martyrdom and contemplative-ascent **compete** as formation modes"). Doc_04 §7 handoff instructed Doc_05 to "write the four Tensional gravities as *held* tensions (Article 15/16), **not resolved**." §5 does the opposite for T4: it presents martyrdom as *"the sharpest and most broadly accessible form of what formation aims at … what the community's own formation looks like when it is tested to the end."* That frames martyrdom as the **apex of a single unified formation**, dissolving the two-mode tension into continuity. The competing/parallel relationship and the practice/population separation Doc_04 established disappear from the lens where T4 actually lives.
**Evidence.** Doc_04 §3.6 T4 and §6 ("compete as formation modes"); Doc_04 §7 handoff ("held tensions … not resolved"); Framework Complexity-Preservation Principle and Constitution Arts. 15/16 (preserve tensions unresolved where evidence supports). §9 of Doc_05 *does* still list "Martyrdom–Contemplative in the Formation lens" as a tension point — so the document contradicts itself: the inventory says "held," the home-lens prose resolves it.
**Concrete fix.** In §5, hold T4 as an unresolved tension: name both poles as distinct formation pathways (the sudden ultimate witness reaching all strata vs. the gradual contemplative ascent available to the school), note that the tradition carried both without collapsing one into the other, and preserve Doc_04's "compete/reshape" relationship rather than presenting martyrdom as the culmination of the contemplative telos. Keep the (accurate) point that martyrdom is the one mode crossing strata, but as a feature of the *tension*, not its resolution.

### S2 — [SUBSTANTIAL] §0.2 / §0.4 make "every-lens" discipline claims that §6.3 (Temporal) does not meet
**Location:** §0.2 ("**every lens** below … marks the interior formation experience of the non-literate majority as Inferential-Thin and does not narrate it"), §0.4 ("**Each lens** below carries an explicit **Forces** move"); failure instance at §6.3 Temporal Ecology.
**What's wrong.** These are governing self-claims a reviewer and downstream builders rely on, and they are false as written. §6.3 Temporal carries **neither** an explicit Forces move (only a buried half-clause, "persecution periods as intense formation *events*," inside the opening sentence — not the set-off, substantive forces move every other lens gets) **nor** the majority-interior non-narration guard that §1, §2, §3, §5, and §6.2 all carry. This matters because temporal/festal practice (daily prayer-rhythm, the fast, Pascha, the festal cycle) is precisely a formation channel that reached the **whole** community, and §6.3's inhabited passage makes a whole-community interior-formation claim — *"the year is shaped to walk the community through death and resurrection until it is **written into them**"* — with none of the stratum guard the exactly-parallel §3 Worship passage carries ("The interior experience of non-literate participants in these practices is Inferential-Thin and is not narrated"). The discipline is applied unevenly, and the one lens where it lapses is the one whose practices most reach the majority. (§4, §6.1, §6.5 also omit the guard, but defensibly — they carry no whole-community interior claim; §6.3 does.)
**Evidence.** §0.2 and §0.4 self-claims vs. §6.3 text; contrast with the guarded parallel at §3 (Worship) and §6.2 (Spatial). OG-4 / Doc_04 §5 / Doc_01 §7 require the majority interior be a named absence in every lens that reaches it; Forces Framework Step 5 and Doc_05 §0.4 require an explicit per-lens forces move.
**Concrete fix.** Either (a) bring §6.3 into line with the other lenses — add a set-off Forces move (Roman administrative/festal calendar and persecution-period pressure on the temporal rhythm) and a construction-note stratum guard marking the non-literate majority's interior temporal formation as Inferential-Thin / not narrated, and reword "written into them" so it renders the *attested* theology of liturgical time rather than the community's felt interior; or (b) if the intent is that not all lenses implicate the majority interior, correct §0.2/§0.4 to say so precisely instead of claiming "every lens." Option (a) is preferred, since Temporal genuinely does reach the majority.

## COSMETIC FINDINGS

### C1 — [COSMETIC] §2 construction note mis-cites the stream for catechumenal incorporation
**Location:** §2 (Community) construction note: "the assembly-as-formation and the catechumenal incorporation are **Widely Accepted (Stream 3** …)."
**What's wrong.** In Doc_02, **Stream 3** is *Worship and Prayer* (assembly-as-formation) but the **catechumenate** is **Stream 2** (*Catechetical Formation* — "the catechumenate as a staged path"). Citing Stream 3 for both is a minor cross-reference error.
**Fix.** Cite Stream 3 for assembly-as-formation and **Stream 2** for catechumenal incorporation.

### C2 — [COSMETIC] §5 "Care as ministry" bullet is the one formation channel with no confidence marker or source
**Location:** §5, "Care as ministry" bullet; §5 construction note.
**What's wrong.** The §5 construction note assigns confidence/sources to the transformation-telos, the Scripture/learning channels, martyrdom, and Learning-Formation Integration — but not to "Care as ministry — the community's mercy to widows, orphans, and the poor," which is asserted as a formation channel without attestation. Article 17 asks every reconstruction to carry a visible confidence level. (Charitable care of widows/orphans/poor is historically safe — broadly Documented in early Christianity, and consecrated widows appear in Doc_02 Stream 11 — so this is calibration, not a factual error.)
**Fix.** Add a brief confidence tag and source anchor for the care/charity channel (e.g., the *Paedagogus*' pastoral instruction; general Documented early-Christian practice) in the §5 construction note.

### C3 — [COSMETIC] §10 Tier-4 note gives blanket rather than per-element traceability
**Location:** §10 header note: "composite typical-practice narration, **traceable to Doc_02 streams**."
**What's wrong.** The Framework Tier-4 rule requires that "the construction notes **identify the specific attested sources each element draws from**," and that a builder "provide a specific source for every practice, detail, or claim." §10's note is a blanket gesture. The traceability does exist — distributed across the §1–§6 lens notes the synthesis re-narrates — but §10's own note should point to those per element rather than asserting traceability wholesale.
**Fix.** Add a one-line element-to-lens/stream map in the §10 note (Scripture-depth → §5/Stream 1; learning=holiness → C5/Streams 1,2,5; worship inhabited → §3/Stream 3; God-actively-teaching → C3/Streams 2,4), or an explicit "each element sourced in the corresponding lens note above."

### C4 — [COSMETIC] §7 omits T4's post-Nicene recession from the Nicaea configuration list
**Location:** §7 (Temporal Configuration), the five listed changes.
**What's wrong.** Doc_04 §6 recorded that at Nicaea (persecution ending in Egypt) **T4 recedes to episodic/memory form**. §7 lists five configuration changes and names only T1/T2/T3 as becoming asymmetric (correctly), but omits T4's recession. It is covered obliquely by §6.5 (Memory), so this is minor.
**Fix.** Add a short clause noting T4's post-Nicene recession toward memory-form, cross-referenced to §6.5.

### C5 — [COSMETIC] §3 worship-as-whole-community confidence sits slightly firmer than Doc_02
**Location:** §3 construction note: "worship as the whole community's primary formation gathering is **Widely Accepted** structurally."
**What's wrong.** Doc_02 Stream 3 rated "how worship was lived **across the community**" at **Dominant Modern Reconstruction** (with literary near-silence about ordinary worship). The "structurally" qualifier mostly carries the distinction (structural fact that all gathered vs. how it was lived), but the calibration reads slightly firmer than the upstream source.
**Fix.** Either keep "structurally" and add "(the *lived* experience across the community being DMR / not narrated, per Doc_02 Stream 3)," or downgrade the structural claim to DMR to match Doc_02 exactly.
