## 0. What This Document Is (and Is Not)

Step 3 produces a **candidate vocabulary list** — the world's own terms, with preliminary definitions, **initial tier estimates**, tags, and Author-Gravity risk flags — generated *before* gravity discovery so that Doc_04 tests gravities in the world's own language rather than importing modern analytical vocabulary (Framework Step 3). It is **not** the full lexicon (that is Doc_06/Step 6, after ecological reconstruction), and it does **not** produce deployment chunks.

Three current-cycle disciplines govern this list:

1. **No permanent numbers yet.** `alexlex` chunk numbers are assigned at Doc_06, not here. The Alexandria-v7 archive's `alexlex001–045` numbering is a *downstream, post-gravity-discovery* artifact and is deliberately not reproduced as if it originated at Step 3. Candidates are listed by name only.
2. **Tiers are preliminary estimates, pending Doc_04.** Every tier below is an initial estimate. Final tier (which term earns full three-level Tier-1 treatment) depends on this build's own gravity discovery (Doc_04) and is set at Doc_06. Several estimates below will shift; that is expected (the Alexandria-v7 track saw exactly this drift, and its final tiers differ from its own Step-3 estimates).
3. **Strand attribution: N/A (strand-singular).** Doc_01 found the world strand-singular for internal construction, so no strand attribution is carried on candidates. The learned-vs-desert gravity question and the desert *world-attribution* question remain open (Doc_01 §3.3, §6) and surface here as the **cross-build (CB)** flag on desert-concentrated terms.

**Tag legend** (per the lexicon-index standard): **AS** = signature vocabulary (distinctively this world's); **SC** = shared vocabulary (held with the wider tradition but functioning distinctively here); **DR** = high distortion risk (modern hearing likely to mislead); **TC** = technical concept; **RT** = likely runtime term (likely to surface in live encounter); **PV** = plural voices (the world held more than one account); **CT** = contested tradition (live scholarly contest — Article 26; every CT candidate names its contest in §4). **CB** (not a lexicon tag but a construction flag) = cross-build/desert provisional constraint (evidence concentrated in desert-attributed sources; world-attribution held open per Doc_01 §3.3).

**Author-Gravity discipline (carried from Doc_02).** Most of this world's signature vocabulary is attested primarily through **Origen** (SYSTEMIC risk) and secondarily **Clement**; the incarnational/Nicene cluster through **Athanasius** (post-325); the ascetic-technical cluster through **Evagrius** (which is also the CB cluster); and the institutional/office cluster through **Eusebius** (HIGH-risk for institutional claims). The "AG-risk (dominant)" column names the dominant voice where one term rests disproportionately on a single source — a flag for Doc_04, not a defect.

## 4. Contested-Tradition (CT) Candidates and Their Contests (Article 26)

Six candidates carry **[CT]** (a seventh — Restoration — carries a *deferred* CT). Article 26 requires that where the CT tag is applied, the contest be specified. At candidate stage these are **preliminary contest statements**, to be finalized (with contest-type) at Doc_06; every CT candidate below is tagged [CT] here (correcting the Alexandria-v7 source, which assessed six as contested but tagged only one).

1. **Apokatastasis** — *contest: meaning + present-tradition relationship.* Whether Origen's universalist hope was his settled position or an exploratory conjecture is contested among specialists; propositions associated with it were condemned in the sixth-century anti-Origenist controversy (the anathemas of 543/553 — whose precise conciliar status, i.e. Justinian's 543 synod versus the official acts of the Second Council of Constantinople in 553, is itself debated in the scholarship). Sole systematic source: Origen — maximal Author-Gravity risk.
2. **Restoration** *(CT deferred)* — the broadly-shared restorative conviction is not itself contested, but its **separability** from Origen's condemned *apokatastasis* is the open question. Doc_04 decides whether Restoration stands as its own candidate (with [CT]) or folds into Salvation.
3. **Nous** — *contest: meaning.* Whether Origen's account of the nous is the same position condemned in 553 (later systematization/distortion vs. genuine continuity) is contested. (The Evagrian-attribution question is a *cross-build* matter, not a second CT prong.)
