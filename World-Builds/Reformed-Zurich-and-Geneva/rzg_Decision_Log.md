### 2026-09-15 — Doc_01 (World Identification, Boundaries, and Orientation)

- **Document:** `Doc_01_World_Identification_Boundaries_Orientation.md`
- **Review rounds:** 1 so far, independent cold adversarial review (model=Opus, no drafting context):
  - `Review-Artifacts/Doc01_Round1_Review.md` — **SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED (6 high, 13 medium, 13 low findings).** The two central findings: (1) the Strand Determination (§4 in Revision 1) marshaled evidence — two independent origins, opposite worship conclusions, distinct authority structures, a negotiated bridge between "already-distinct parties" — and read it as strand evidence, without ever running the Construction Framework's own World Separation Criteria test, which is the test that decides whether Zurich and Geneva are one world or two; the reviewer noted this is the same failure shape Donatism's own Doc_01 Round 1 recorded (a Step 1 deliverable not clearing its own governing Article's actual test). (2) The Dort scoping decision (§6 in Revision 1) claimed the census had "already made" a portfolio decision to exclude Dort from this world's story; the reviewer found the census's own VI.9 and VI.26 entries state the opposite — the scope boundary is explicitly left open ("a real Step 0 call," "Tier 3 signal; awaiting the gate") — and that Revision 1's own citation of VI.26 dropped the sentence that says so. Four further high findings: Doc_01 was drafted before Step 0 cleared review, against Step 0's own explicit instruction, without §8 naming this; a required Part I element (World Separation Criteria) was missing entirely; the project's five-level confidence vocabulary appeared nowhere despite several flat-asserted claims that needed it; and Step0 §4 item 1 (state plainly that Zwingli and Calvin never met and that coherence rests on Bullinger's mediation) was the only one of Step 0's six binding items neither discharged nor carried forward. Thirteen medium findings included: two mis-citations of the Constitution (a false "quoted in full" claim on Article 22; a pointer to Article 21 "quoted... at §5's own heading" that actually quotes Article 22); an anachronism placing Calvin's own position at the 1529 Marburg Colloquy, before Geneva was reformed and while Calvin was a twenty-year-old law student; Beza's *Tabula praedestinationis* mis-dated to "after Calvin's death" (it is 1555, nine years before Calvin's 1564 death) and the Remonstrance overstated as written directly against Beza rather than against Arminius's actual Leiden opponents; an unhedged Grossmünster-organ date; the first Anabaptist baptism misattributed to Grebel and Manz jointly when the standard account has Grebel baptizing Blaurock, who then baptized the others including Manz; a "no documented direct contact" claim about the Jesuits borrowed uncritically from a different world's (Lutheran Wittenberg's) Step 0 finding; two corpus-map misreadings (the Zwingli *Selected Works* row overstated as itemized when only the Sixty-Seven Articles are separately rowed; the Zwingli/Heidelberg-Catechism corpus-sharing claim was simply wrong — no Zwingli file is shared with anything); an overstated Article 4 floor-clearance claim (only commitments (1)–(3) were text-verified, not all five); a wrong citation for the Living Tradition Status confirmation mechanism (Article 29 alone, rather than Formation World Blueprint V7.3 §17); episcopal vocabulary ("see") applied to five non-episcopal Reformed figures/institutions; two undisclosed unvendored-source dependencies (the Genevan Psalter, the Ecclesiastical Ordinances) underwriting the Strand Determination's own evidence; and Marie Dentière's 1539 *Epistre* wrongly cited as an example of publishing under her own name, when it was published anonymously and its printer prosecuted. Thirteen low findings (a compressed Bern-alliance date; "Second Battle of Kappel" vs. the standard "Battle of Kappel"; spelling inconsistency on Zwingli's forename; an unhedged Ursinus/Olevianus joint-authorship claim; missing path citations; unmarked verbatim borrowing from sibling documents; a mis-located Step 0 citation; an overstated Trent "direct doctrinal rival" attribution belonging to a different document; an uncommented TULIP-vocabulary anachronism applied to the 1559 Institutes; the `rzg` code not yet registered anywhere outside this document; no `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` for this world; and an imprecise "daily" characterization of the Prophezei, which met most weekdays).
  - The reviewer also independently verified a long list of quotations and historical dates as character-exact/correct — recorded in the review artifact's own "Verified clean" section — including nearly every date in the §2 catalyst chain, the Anabaptist-origin correction's own substance (right in kind, wrong in the specific administrant), and the Diodati/Tronchin Genevan-delegation-to-Dort claim, which Revision 1 had itself correctly hedged as unverified — the reviewer's finding on that item was that it was the *only* hedge in a document containing several claims that needed one, not that the claim itself was wrong.
- **Revision decision:** Substantial. Both central judgments required genuine re-argument, not wording fixes: Revision 2 adds a dedicated World Separation Criteria section (new §4) that runs the Framework's own six-question test explicitly and reaches a disclosed, reasoned single-world finding before Strand Determination (now §5) is applied to the same evidence from the other side: reaches the same two-strand conclusion, now properly premised. The Dort scoping question (now §7) is corrected to state what the census actually says, and is **escalated to the project lead rather than self-disposed** — three named options offered with a tentative recommendation, marked PENDING. All 32 Round 1 findings are addressed in Revision 2; see the document's own §9 Disposition and `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` items 1–10 for the disclosed exceptions and open items this revision carries forward rather than silently resolving.
- **Escalation check:** One category applies and is not self-disposed — the Dort scoping question (portfolio-level/cross-world decision, per `cic-build-cycle`'s own definition and `CLAUDE.md`'s default-action table). No other category applies: the Strand Determination is ordinary Step-1 evidence work under Article 21; the Step 0 sequencing question is treated as a disclosed, already-authorized exception rather than an unresolved tension, with the authorization's own record being this build thread's 2026-09-15 launch instruction.
- **Disposition:** Not yet disposed. Revision 2 is submitted for Round 2 review. The document does not claim readiness for "Approved to proceed" while the Dort escalation remains open — that item stays PENDING regardless of Round 2's outcome on everything else, the same pattern Living Tradition Status already uses.
