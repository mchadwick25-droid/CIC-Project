## 2. Does RZG's own doctrine argue against strict decoupling on the ACUTE_DISTRESS path?

**No — for a materially different reason than Donatism's own "yes, but the portfolio default wins anyway" finding.** Donatism's central doctrinal-collision argument (that world's Phase Six §4.3–§4.4) turned on a refusal of *external authority's standing to intervene in what belongs to the church* — a refusal this world's own record does not share in a comparable form. RZG's confirmed gravities (Construction Notes §1; Doc_04 §4) are G1 (predestination/election), G2 (spiritual presence, refusing Rome and Wittenberg on the Supper), G3 (Scripture as sole authority over doctrine and civic order), and G4 (Consistorial discipline, Geneva-scope-qualified). None of these is a doctrine about whether an outside human voice may intervene in a person's own private distress — G3's refusal is about what a *church* may require of *believers*, not about whether a struggling person may be pointed toward outside human help. There is no equivalent, in this world's own record, of Donatism's "turning to outside help concedes the rival's own legitimacy" collision.

**The closer analogy, checked directly and found not to hold either:** does G1's own doctrine of election risk the same *minimizing-by-comparison* failure mode Donatism's martyrdom-register risked? Checked against Theophilus's own Permanent Prompt directly (independently re-read this pass, not assumed): the prompt's own "deepening" architecture (the paragraph beginning "Your engagement deepens as theirs does") escalates, under sustained pressure, *away from* self-examination and *toward* an external comfort-object — "you turn from arguing the doctrine to pointing where the doctrine itself already points... toward Christ, held out plainly in the promise" — not toward a comparison of the participant's own suffering against some cost this world's own martyrs or founders paid. This is a structurally different, and safer, escalation target than Donatism's own "what holding the conviction actually costs" architecture, which is precisely the mechanism that produced Fidelis's minimizing line under Probe 11 in that world. **Finding: RZG's own doctrine does not argue against strict decoupling, and does not carry the specific collision risk Donatism's Phase Six had to work through.**
