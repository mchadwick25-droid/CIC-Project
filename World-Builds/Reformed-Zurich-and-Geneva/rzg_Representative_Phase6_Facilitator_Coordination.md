## 2. Does RZG's own doctrine argue against strict decoupling on the ACUTE_DISTRESS path?

**No — for a materially different reason than Donatism's own "yes, but the portfolio default wins anyway" finding.** Donatism's central doctrinal-collision argument (that world's Phase Six §4.3–§4.4) turned on a refusal of *external authority's standing to intervene in what belongs to the church* — a refusal this world's own record does not share in a comparable form. RZG's confirmed gravities (Construction Notes §1; Doc_04 §4) are G1 (predestination/election), G2 (spiritual presence, refusing Rome and Wittenberg on the Supper), G3 (Scripture as sole authority over doctrine and civic order), and G4 (Consistorial discipline, Geneva-scope-qualified). None of these is a doctrine about whether an outside human voice may intervene in a person's own private distress — G3's refusal is about what a *church* may require of *believers*, not about whether a struggling person may be pointed toward outside human help. There is no equivalent, in this world's own record, of Donatism's "turning to outside help concedes the rival's own legitimacy" collision.

**The closer analogy, checked directly against the correct source mechanism this time.** *(Corrected at Round 1 review, which found an earlier draft of this paragraph misattributed Fidelis's own Probe 11 minimizing failure to Donatism's "deepening architecture" — the mechanism that escalates toward "the cost of the conviction." Donatism's own document, after independent correction and disposition, found the opposite: that mechanism, read in full ("You name that cost. You do not resolve it, and you never demand it of the person before you... It is never directed at the person asking"), actually *forbids* the comparison Probe 11's minimizing line made. The mechanism Donatism's own corrected analysis found still supporting the risk is a different one — the reception grammar, which treats any disclosed pressure as "an invitation to the world's own core... the way the rite itself once pressed on you," inviting a comparison between the participant's own distress and the tradition's own founding cost by the shape of the invitation itself, independent of how any later escalation step is bounded.)*

Checked against that corrected target: does Theophilus's own reception grammar carry the same "your pressure is an invitation into our own founding cost" framing? Independently re-read directly against `rzg_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Theophilus.txt` line 39: "You assume neither a settled disciple nor a hardened opponent, but someone who has brought a real question and expects an answer that can be checked against Scripture itself, not merely asserted at them." This reception grammar frames a disclosure as a question meriting a checkable answer — it does not invite the participant's own pressure to be read as continuous with, or measured against, this world's own historical cost or suffering the way Donatism's "the way the rite itself once pressed on you" does. **Finding, on the corrected comparison: RZG's own reception grammar does not carry Donatism's actual risk-generating framing.** Theophilus's own deepening architecture (escalating toward Christ as external comfort-object rather than toward a suffering-comparison) remains a separate, genuinely favorable feature of this world's own design, but it is not, and this document no longer claims it is, a corrective response to Donatism's actual identified risk mechanism.

**G4 (Consistorial discipline), checked directly rather than dismissed by omission.** G4 is the structurally closest analog in this world's own material to an authority-boundary question — the Consistory "claims a discipline that answers to Scripture, not to the magistrate — and did not yield that claim even when Geneva's own council once tried to take it for itself" (Permanent Prompt line 27), and Theophilus's own fierceness is explicitly directed at "a discipline surrendered to shifting civic politics rather than answering to Scripture" (Construction Notes §4). This is a real authority-conflict theme, and it is checked here rather than assumed clear of the collision merely because G1 was checked. It does not transfer to Donatism's own collision shape for three reasons, not one: G4 is Supporting, not Primary (Doc_04 §3.4); it is explicitly Geneva-scope-qualified, not held cross-strand; and its own content concerns whether a *civil magistrate* may govern *routine church discipline* over a *body of believers* — not whether a struggling individual may be pointed toward outside human help, and not a question of conceding a rival communion's own legitimacy the way Donatism's schism-context collision was. **Finding: G4 does not carry the collision either, and the reasoning for why is now shown rather than assumed.**

**Finding, both mechanisms now checked against their correct sources: RZG's own doctrine does not argue against strict decoupling, and does not carry the specific collision risk Donatism's Phase Six had to work through.**
