## Section 2 — Preliminary Forces Identification

This section restates, without redrawing, the preliminary forces identification already recorded at Doc_01 §6, which governed Doc_02's own source-ecology scope. It is not the complete forces analysis — that is Section 3 below.

**What generated this world:** two independent local triggers, not one shared cause — Zwingli's own Erasmian-humanist biblical scholarship, applied to Zurich's own civic reform context and catalyzed by the 1522 Sausage Affair; and, wholly separately, Geneva's 1526 alliance with Bern and 1536 political break from Savoy and the Prince-Bishop, which created the civic opening Calvin's own arrival and 1541 recall filled. The two triggers are connected only retrospectively, by the Consensus Tigurinus (Doc_01 §6).

**What larger world it was embedded in:** the same late-medieval Latin-Christian civic and clerical order Wittenberg's own Reformation contested, read through the Swiss Confederacy's own decentralized, republican city-state structure; and, more distantly, the Augustinian tradition on predestination and grace — a direct theological line across eleven centuries with no institutional continuity, influence rather than identity (Doc_01 §6, quoting the census's own transmission-edge language in full).

**What it was responding to:** the same late-medieval sacramental and clerical system Wittenberg responded to, but pressed further on the legitimacy of images and instrumental worship in a scripture-governed church, and, at Geneva specifically, genuine civic disorder and moral laxity the Consistory's own weekly discipline was built to address (Doc_01 §6).

**What it was refusing:** image veneration and the Mass's own sacrificial character, refused alongside Wittenberg; and, once both strands' own positions had matured, any claim that Christ's body is locally or corporeally present in the bread and wine — Zurich's own refusal specifically at Marburg (1529), since Calvin was not yet a public figure and Geneva was not yet reformed. This world's own Geneva strand also, over time, refused unchecked civil-magistrate control over church discipline specifically (Doc_01 §6).

**How this preliminary identification governed Step 2 scope:** it directed Doc_02 to treat the Author Gravity concentration (Calvin's corpus roughly four times Zwingli's) as this world's own central evidentiary problem (Doc_01 §8 item 1); to reconstruct both strands' own record without collapsing them into one undifferentiated ecology (Doc_01 §4/§5's own confirmed two-strand finding); and to hold the Dort scoping boundary the project lead confirmed at Doc_01 §7 (international Reformed dimension in, Dutch domestic controversy out) as a settled constraint on what this world's own transmission may claim. All three directions bore out directly in what Doc_02 through Doc_07 actually found.

### CELL 1A — INITIATING / EXTERNAL

**Governing question:** What external conditions made this particular form of Christianity possible, necessary, or urgent at its origin?

---

#### Force 1A-1: The late-medieval Latin sacramental and clerical order

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** The late-medieval Western church's own sacramental system — the Mass understood as a repeated propitiatory sacrifice, image veneration, and a clerical hierarchy claiming sole mediating authority — was the inherited ecclesial order both this world and Lutheran Wittenberg contested, read here through the Swiss Confederacy's own decentralized, republican city-state structure rather than a princely territorial settlement. **Documented** (Doc_01 §6; Doc_05 §6.3's own civic-political contrast with Wittenberg).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** What was believed and practiced before was not a small error to be corrected but a system whose central claims had to be tested against Scripture and found wanting on their own terms — a repeated sacrifice where Christ himself declared his own offering finished, once, and images bowed to where nothing in Scripture licensed it.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This force is the deep external root of G2 (Spiritual Presence/Rejection of Corporeal Mediation) — the specific system its own doctrine of the Supper is built in refusal of — and, at one remove, of G3 (Scripture as Sole Authority), the method that licensed testing the inherited order at all rather than accepting it on institutional authority.

---

#### Force 1A-2: The Augustinian inheritance on predestination and grace

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Calvin's own Institutes draws explicitly and heavily on Augustine's anti-Pelagian writings for total depravity, unconditional election, and irresistible grace — Calvin named Augustine his primary authority next to Scripture itself, "a direct theological line across eleven centuries with no institutional continuity — influence, not identity" (Doc_01 §6, quoting the census's own transmission-edge language in full; note that "total depravity, unconditional election, and irresistible grace" are themselves categories drawn from the 1619 Canons of Dort, applied here to Calvin's 1559 Institutes — an anachronism inherited from the census's own wording and disclosed rather than silently adopted, per Doc_01 §6). **Documented** as this world's own inherited theological antecedent, not itself part of the late-medieval system Force 1A-1 names.

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** What is taught here of God's own free election is not a new doctrine invented to answer a new crisis; it is the same conviction the church has always held wherever it read Paul rightly and refused to let human merit creep back in — Augustine saw it first against Pelagius, and this world sees it again, now, against a church that has let it grow faint.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This force grounds G1 (Sovereignty of God/Predestination and Election) directly, as an inherited theological line rather than a local invention — the specific external antecedent Doc_04 §3.1 places in Cell 1A, distinct from and prior to Zurich's and Geneva's own two local origins (Force 1B-1, Force 1B-2 below). Zwingli's own 1527 election exposition (Doc_04 §3.1) independently corroborates this same Cell 1A/2B grounding in Zurich's own voice, without displacing it into Force 1B-1's own cell (Section 5 below) — corrected at Doc08 Round 2 review, which found this corroboration named at Section 5's own G1 entry but not, until now, at Force 1A-2's own entry as Section 5 itself claimed.

### CELL 1B — INITIATING / INTERNAL

**Governing question:** What internal commitments, convictions, or inherited traditions gave this world its earliest distinctive shape from within?

---

#### Force 1B-1: Zwingli's 1519 preaching decision and the 1522 Sausage Affair (Zurich's own origin)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Ulrich Zwingli began continuous, book-by-book scriptural exposition at Zurich's Grossmünster from 1519, rather than following the fixed lectionary; the 1522 Sausage Affair (a public Lenten-fast violation Zwingli defended on scriptural grounds) catalyzed open civic reform, leading to the First Zurich Disputation (1523), where the Sixty-Seven Articles were argued and ratified by the city council. **Documented** (Doc_01 §2; Doc_02 §1's own contemporary institutional record of the Disputation Acts).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — the first sentence carried from Doc_05 §1, the second from Doc_05 §5's own parallel Zurich passage]* Scripture read continuously, book by book, not cut into a fixed year's own lectionary — that was the discipline Zwingli set for himself at the Grossmünster from his first January, and it is the discipline that made every later argument possible: whatever the text does not say, the church may not require. What that reading yielded was refused as much as affirmed, argued in public, before the whole city, rather than asserted from a chair.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the deep root of G3's own Zurich-strand enactment (public Disputation) and, through it, of G1 and G2 as Zwingli's own 1523 Art. XVIII and 1527 election exposition first state them in Zurich's own voice. It is also the founding root of T1's own Zurich pole (council-led authority, sustained unbroken across the whole window) and, indirectly, of the 1525 Anabaptist schism (Force 2B-1 below), which breaks from this same circle rather than arising independently.

---

#### Force 1B-2: Geneva's 1526 Bern alliance, 1536 break from Savoy, and Calvin's arrival (Geneva's own origin)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Geneva's 1526 alliance with Bern and Fribourg and its 1536 political break from Savoy and the Prince-Bishop created the civic opening; Calvin arrived the same year, was exiled to Strasbourg (1538–1541), and was recalled to consolidate the 1541 Ecclesiastical Ordinances. **Documented** (Doc_01 §2, §6), five years after Zwingli's death and with no contact between the two triggers.

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — carried from Doc_05 §1]* A church is not rightly ordered by doctrine alone. Someone must watch how the doctrine is lived — not the magistrate, whose sword is for the body, but pastors and elders together, whose office is to unite in pronouncing censure where conscience and conduct diverge from what has been professed.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the deep root of G3's own Geneva-strand enactment (fixed catechesis) and, through Calvin's own systematic elaboration, of G1's own fullest worked form and G2's own mature spiritual-presence doctrine. It is also the direct precondition for Force 1B-3 below (the 1541 Ordinances) and, through it, of G4 and T1's own Geneva pole.

---

#### Force 1B-3: The 1541 Ecclesiastical Ordinances and the founding of the Consistory

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Calvin's 1541 recall to Geneva produced the Ecclesiastical Ordinances, creating a Consistory of pastors and lay elders with disciplinary censure authority, structurally distinct from a body answerable to the civil council alone. **Documented** as this world's own general doctrine, Confidence A, directly in Calvin's own words (Institutes IV.3.8: "seniors selected from the people to unite with the bishops in pronouncing censures and exercising discipline," lines 2831–2832, independently re-verified this pass); Geneva's own specific 1541 institutional text itself remains unvendored, Confidence E (Registry row 14; Doc_04 §3.4).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — the opening sentence carried from Doc_05 §4; the fuller passage, including its own middle sentence on the council's later challenge, belongs to Force 2B-4 below, where it is quoted in full]* The magistrate governs bodies and property; he does not sit in judgment on whether a soul may come to the Lord's own table.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the specific institutional founding act behind G4 (Consistorial Church Discipline) and the founding moment of T1's own Geneva pole — an independence claim built into the institution from its own origin, not asserted later, which is why this body had to be its own thing from the start rather than an arm of the council that had ratified the Sixty-Seven Articles at Zurich; the claim is what the Perrinist crisis (Force 2B-4 below) tests, decades on, rather than invents.

### CELL 2A — ONGOING / EXTERNAL

**Governing question:** What external forces required this community's continuous response and adaptation throughout its active life?

---

#### Force 2A-1: The Marburg Colloquy (1529)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** At Marburg (1–4 October 1529), Zwingli and Oecolampadius stood against Luther and Melanchthon over whether Christ's body is locally or corporeally present in the Supper; Calvin, then about twenty and a law student, was not present, and Geneva was not yet reformed. **Documented** (Doc_01 §6; Doc_05 §3).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — Zurich, pre-1549, carried from Doc_05 §3]* The bread is bread, and the body is in heaven; what happens at this table is remembrance and thanksgiving, not another sacrifice repeated, and any preacher who tells you Christ's own flesh is chewed by the mouth has not read what Scripture actually says of him.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the specific rupture that separates G2's own refusal of Wittenberg from its own earlier, shared refusal of Rome — the Boundary Ecology's own second refusal (Doc_07 §2H) — and the direct trigger, twenty years on, for Force 2B-2's own Consensus Tigurinus: "the break at Marburg is what made the 1549 Consensus necessary twenty years later" (Doc_05 §3, quoted in full).

---

#### Force 2A-2: The Wars of Kappel (1529, 1531) and Zwingli's death

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** The First War of Kappel (1529) was resolved without battle; the Second War of Kappel (1531) killed Zwingli himself, fought between Reformed and Catholic Swiss cantons over the Confederacy's own decentralized cantonal structure making reform a city-by-city political decision. **Documented** (Doc_01 §2; Doc_05 §6.3).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — Zurich, after Kappel, carried from Doc_05 §6.3]* Our own pastor was killed in that same fighting — reform here was never a private conviction sheltered from the world's own violence; it was a city's own public stand, paid for in the same coin as any other war the Confederacy has fought.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This force removes Zurich's own founding voice from the ongoing life of the movement it began, which is the direct precondition for Bullinger's own forty-four-year pastorate and, through it, for the Second Helvetic Confession (Force 2B-5 below) — a fixed text written specifically because a founding generation's own oral and disputational method needed to outlive its founder (Doc_05 §6.4).

---

#### Force 2A-3: Geneva's refugee inflow and Bernese dependence

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Geneva became a print and refugee center over this window, sheltering French Huguenots and, under Mary I, English Marian exiles, while remaining dependent on Bernese support in ways that both stressed and internationalized the city. **Documented** (Doc_01 §2; Doc_05 §6.3).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** A city that takes in the persecuted of every other reformed church cannot govern its own daily life as though nothing outside its own walls mattered — every new household arriving under threat is one more soul this city's own discipline must answer for, not merely one more mouth to feed.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the direct external pressure Doc_01 §6 names as what G4's own weekly Consistory discipline was specifically built to address — "genuine civic disorder and moral laxity" arising in real part from sustained refugee inflow, not from Geneva's own settled population alone.

---

#### Force 2A-4: The Counter-Reformation as sustained external pressure

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Catholic pressure was sustained across the whole window: the Jesuits, founded 1540, established at Fribourg from 1580, directly facing Reformed Bern and Geneva; the 1590s Catholic reconquest of the Chablais was fought on Geneva's own doorstep. **Documented**, not independently assessed beyond Doc_01 §7's own characterization (Doc_07 §2H). The 1553 execution of Michael Servetus at Geneva is a separate, sharper episode of Counter-Reformation-adjacent pressure and controversy but is explicitly scoped to a separate registered world (VI.14) per this world's own Step 0 §2 A1 and is not independently treated here (Doc_01 §2; Doc_05 §6.2).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** Rome did not simply lose this ground once and withdraw; it kept pressing at the edge of what this world had won, and every generation had to hold what the last one had claimed, not merely inherit it settled.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This force sustains the Rome-facing pole of Boundary Ecology's own triple refusal (Doc_07 §2H) across the entire window without ever converting into the kind of world-ending event this project's own Donatism precedent shows (the Vandal capture of Carthage) — a contrast this document names explicitly at Section 4 below.

---

#### Force 2A-5: The Synod of Dort's international Reformed dimension (1618–19)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** The Synod of Dort seated an international Reformed delegation, including, per standard historiography and not yet independently verified against a primary vendored source, two delegates from Geneva itself and a joint delegation from the Swiss Reformed cantons of Zurich, Bern, Basel, and Schaffhausen — meaning, if this holds, both this world's own confirmed strands were represented in person. **Confirmed by the project lead, 2026-09-15:** Dort's international Reformed dimension is treated as part of this world's own transmission, citable at this confidence level; the specifically Dutch domestic controversy (the Remonstrance, Oldenbarnevelt, the exiled Remonstrant Brotherhood) remains a separate world's own future story (VI.26), not this world's own content (Doc_01 §7).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** What was decided at Dort was not a new question; it was the same conviction about God's own free election, argued now before delegates from every Reformed church that shared it, pressed to its sharpest and most systematic statement yet.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This force sharpens rather than closes G1 (Doc_01 §7's own confirmed transmission scope) via Beza's own doctrinal throughline (Force 2B-5 below, at one remove — Arminius's own immediate teacher was Beza, but his own immediate opponents at Leiden were Gomarus and Junius, not Beza directly, the same qualification Doc_01 §7 itself states). It is this world's own clearest instance of an external force operating at the very edge of the construction window (1618–19, thirty-two years before the 1650 close) without becoming an ending force — it transmits forward rather than closing anything.

#### Force 2B-1: The Anabaptist schism (1525)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** The first Swiss Brethren baptisms (21 January 1525), at Felix Manz's own house in Zurich — Conrad Grebel baptizing Georg Blaurock, who then baptized the others present, including Manz — split this world's own founding community from within, only two years after the First Zurich Disputation had settled its founding doctrine. Grebel and Manz were both formerly of Zwingli's own reform circle. **Documented** (Doc_01 §7; Doc_05 §2).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — carried from Doc_05 §2]* Some among us read the same Scripture and heard the same doctrine of a church gathered by profession, not born into by baptism as an infant — and where we stopped, they went further, and would have unmade the very unity of city and church that reform was built to strengthen, not divide. We do not deny they read Scripture; we deny they read it whole.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This force tests G3's own scriptural method against a rival reading from inside, the sharpest of the Boundary Ecology's own triple refusal precisely because it answers a challenge from within rather than outside (Doc_07 §2H). It is a direct continuation of Force 1B-1 above, not an unrelated contemporary movement — this world's own founding force and its own first internal fracture share a single root.

---

#### Force 2B-2: The Consensus Tigurinus (1549)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** In 1549, Zurich's own ministers and Geneva's own Calvin put their names to a single jointly-negotiated formula on the Supper neither city had used alone before. **Documented**, verified verbatim: "Wherefore, though we distinguish, as we ought, between the signs and the things signified, yet we do not disjoin the reality from the signs" (9th Head of Agreement, lines 768–770, independently re-verified this pass).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — carried from Doc_05 §3]* We do not divide the sign from the thing it signifies, as though bread were merely bread and nothing more were given; but neither do we say the body itself descends into it. Christ's own promise, received in faith, is truly given here — not carnally, but by the Spirit's own power, to those who come believing.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the direct textual bridge across the strand divide Force 1B-1/1B-2 opened — the one document in this world's entire corpus literally co-authored across the boundary — and the second pole of T2 (Doc_04 §3.5), resolving that tension in text without settling, in this world's own words, whether the resolution is genuine synthesis or diplomatic elasticity (the live [CT] contest on Sign and the Thing Signified, carried forward, not adjudicated here).

#### Force 2B-3: The Bolsec controversy (1551)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Jerome Bolsec publicly challenged Calvin's own predestination doctrine at Geneva in 1551, a live pastoral and disciplinary crisis over the doctrine's own content. **Documented** (Doc_01 §2; Doc_04 §3.1).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** To question this doctrine publicly, before the whole congregation, is not an abstract theological dispute; it is to ask whether the comfort this church offers its own people — that salvation rests on God's own free choice, not on anything foreseen in the one chosen — is true or merely asserted, and this city cannot let that question stand unanswered.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the direct trigger that tests G1 as a live, contested pastoral question rather than settled doctrine alone — Doc_04 §3.1's own Explanatory test names this controversy as explaining "the specific shape" of G1's own formation-facing register, not merely its content.

#### Force 2B-4: The Perrinist crisis (resolved 1555)

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — carried from Doc_05 §4]* When the council's own men tried to claim that authority for themselves, the Consistory did not yield it — not because the pastors sought power for its own sake, but because a discipline answerable to the city's own shifting politics is no discipline at all.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the direct test of Force 1B-3's own founding independence claim and the specific episode constituting T1's own Geneva pole in its sharpest, most concentrated ongoing-internal form (Doc_04 §3.5) — a genuine, named political party inside Geneva itself, not an external threat.

#### Force 2B-5: The Second Helvetic Confession (1566) and the Heidelberg Catechism (1563)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** Bullinger's Second Helvetic Confession (1566), written decades after Zwingli's death, and the Heidelberg Catechism (1563, a third, non-Zurich/non-Geneva Reformed voice from the German wing), together fix this movement's shared confessional core in transmissible written form. **Documented** (Doc_01 §2; Doc_05 §6.4, §7).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** *[Inhabited — carried from Doc_05 §6.4]* A confession is not written to say something new; it is written so that what is already believed can be tested, taught, and handed to the next generation without drift — which is why Zurich's own mature statement and Geneva's own catechism, forty years and a language apart, can still be read as one faith stated twice.

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** This is the specific mechanism behind Doc_07 §3's own confirmed Memory Structures lens — textual codification by a named successor once a founding generation's own oral and disputational method needed a fixed text to outlive it, matching the same succession-proofing role Beza's own *Tabula praedestinationis* (1555) performs for G1's own systematized form, at Force 2A-5's own remove toward Dort.

## Section 6 — Transmission as a Force Dimension — Synthesis

### Who Transmitted This World's Characteristic Material

A materially different condition from this project's own Donatism precedent, where transmission ran substantially through a hostile party's own refutation literature. Here, this world's own tradition transmitted itself, through its own continuing institutional life: Zwingli's *Selected Works* and Calvin's Institutes and Geneva Catechism survive because the Reformed tradition's own later institutions had continuing reason to keep them in print and teach from them, not because an opposing party needed them for refutation. The Second Helvetic Confession and Heidelberg Catechism, and the Consensus Tigurinus, transmitted the same way — confessional texts a living, continuing tradition kept current rather than a defeated party's words preserved only inside a victor's own polemic.

### Under What Conditions Transmission Occurred

Doctrinal and confessional texts transmitted under strong institutional incentive: a continuing Reformed tradition, across four descendant lines, needed its own founding doctrine in fixed, teachable form (the same Memory Structures mechanism Doc_07 §3 already names). Institutional daily-practice material transmitted under no comparable incentive: the 1541 Ecclesiastical Ordinances, the Genevan Psalter, and Geneva's own consistory registers were the specific working documents of a single city's own ordinary administration, not texts a wider, continuing tradition needed reprinted for its own doctrinal identity — and Geneva's own consistory registers, the specific archive the census itself selected this world for, remain confirmed copyrighted in the one identified modern edition (Doc_02 §5/§9), a present-day rights condition compounding, not replacing, the original genre-selection effect.

### What Selection Effects Shaped What Survives

What survives at volume is doctrine and confession, for both strands, and Calvin's own doctrine and confession at roughly four times Zwingli's own volume specifically. What survives only thinly, or not at all, is the daily, individual, non-elite experience of either city's own religious life — no ordinary citizen's own account of a Sunday service or a consistory summons is vendored for either Zurich or Geneva (Doc_05 §0 item 4, restated throughout). What is lost, structurally rather than accidentally, is not one strand's voice relative to the other's — Doc_04 already found the confirmed Primary gravities are not artifacts of the volume asymmetry alone — but the entire institutional-daily-practice genre, for both cities equally.

### What the Transmission Pattern Reveals About Forces

A tradition that transmits its own record, rather than having it transmitted by a hostile party, still selects — but it selects for what serves its own continuing doctrinal and institutional identity, not for what serves a victor's own refutation. What such a tradition keeps in print across centuries is argument and confession, because argument and confession are what a still-living communion needs to teach its own next generation; what it lets fall out of print, or never digitizes, or leaves under a single modern publisher's copyright, is the specific administrative record of one city's own ordinary practice, because no continuing institutional interest outside that one city ever needed it reprinted. Read together with Donatism's own hostile-transmission pattern (Doc_08, that world's own Section 6), the two forces documents in this project's portfolio now show two structurally distinct transmission conditions producing two structurally distinct evidentiary gaps: a hostile party's own refutation-driven selection (Donatism), and a self-transmitting tradition's own doctrine-over-practice selection (this world) — worth naming explicitly as a finding this portfolio's own comparative method makes visible, not merely an incidental difference between two unrelated worlds.

### Connection to Author Gravity Findings (Doc_02)

This is the explicit link Doc_02 §2/§6 and Doc_04 §0 already establish and this document confirms rather than restates from scratch: Calvin's own corpus dominates this world's own Author Gravity concentration because a continuing Reformed institutional tradition had every reason to keep his own systematic theology in print and teach from it, at a volume Zwingli's own thinner two-volume corpus and Bullinger's own single confession never matched — not because Zwingli's own tradition was suppressed or hostilely mediated, but because it was, in this specific transmission sense, less prolifically continued in print. G4's own most vivid, formation-defining content (the actual weekly operation of the Consistory) is, by contrast, this document's own least well-transmitted content of any confirmed gravity — precisely because it belongs to the institutional-daily-practice genre this section names as structurally, not accidentally, thin.
