## Quick Meaning

The Consistory is Geneva's own body of pastors and lay elders, holding disciplinary authority over the whole population's ordinary conduct — a distinctly Genevan institution this world's own record can describe in general doctrine but not yet in its own day-to-day operation.

---

## World Meaning

Doctrine rightly taught still leaves open whether a professed believer's own daily conduct answers to what has been professed. At Geneva, someone had to ask that question directly — not the magistrate, whose office is for the body and its property, but a body of pastors and lay elders together, seniors selected from the people to unite with the bishops in pronouncing censures and exercising discipline. This body, established under the 1541 Ecclesiastical Ordinances, is what made Geneva's own church government presbyterian rather than purely clerical: laypeople, not only ordained ministers, hold real disciplinary standing in it.

This was not a settled arrangement handed down without contest. The council itself tried, at points, to claim the authority to judge who might come to the Lord's own table — and the Consistory did not yield that judgment, not because the pastors sought power for themselves, but because a discipline answerable to the city's own shifting politics is no discipline at all. That fight, the Perrinist crisis, was substantially resolved in the Consistory's own favor only by 1555, a full generation after Calvin's own 1541 recall — general historical background carried forward from Doc_01 §2 and Doc_04's own T1 discussion, not itself part of the vendored primary-text record this entry's own Key Sources supply below.

## Ecological Function

Excommunication — formal exclusion from the sacraments — is this body's own most severe enacted tool, tying this institution directly to the Supper's own doctrine of who may rightly receive it. Its own claimed authority rests on Sola Scriptura's own conviction, not bare civil law: discipline here is argued as scriptural warrant applied to conduct, the same method that grounds this world's other central doctrines. And its own struggle for independence from the council (T1) is this world's own permanent, unresolved authority-structure tension with Zurich's council-led alternative — a divergence that did not converge within this world's own construction window.

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A harsh moral-surveillance body, imagined in vivid institutional detail — a court that summoned households, inspected private life, and enforced conformity street by street, since this is the popular image "Geneva's Consistory" has come to carry.

**World Hearing:**
The *general doctrine* — that lay elders, not only clergy, hold real disciplinary standing, and that the church's own discipline must answer to pastors and elders rather than the civil magistrate — is stated plainly and directly in Calvin's own words. What this world's own vendored record cannot yet supply is the Consistory's own actual, day-to-day operation: its real case-by-case practice, its own composition in detail, what a summons before it actually involved. A vivid, specific picture of that daily operation is not something this document's own sources can currently support, and none is offered here beyond the general doctrine itself.
