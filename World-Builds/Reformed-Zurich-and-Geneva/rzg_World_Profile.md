## Section 1 — World Identity

**World name (as the world would name itself):** No single self-name distinct from this world's own doctrinal vocabulary is attested in this world's own vendored record. "Reformation" is this world's own later, retrospective name for the whole ecology, tested and correctly not advanced as a gravity in its own right (Doc_04 §2, D-B) precisely because participants already feel they understand it (Doc_03 §0). Zurich and Geneva did not share a single institutional name for themselves together — the strand division is real at the level of self-naming, not only institutional structure.

**World code:** `rzg`

**Temporal scope:** 1519–1650. The window opens with Zwingli's own 1519 preaching decision at Zurich and the 1522 Sausage Affair, and closes at the census's own administrative continues-cap for a still-living tradition (Doc_01 §6) — not a historical rupture. No internal or external force closes this world within the window (Doc_08 Forces 3A-1, 3B-1, both confirmed as honest "no ending force attested" findings); its gravities continue unbroken into four named descendant traditions. This document does not fix a specific vantage point within the window for a future Representative — the single-versus-multi-figure Representative question, and any specific temporal anchoring within 1519–1650 that decision implies, remains an explicit standing escalation reserved for Representative Emergence (Doc_07 §3; Section 9 below), not decided or anticipated here.

**Geographic scope:** Zurich (German-speaking, council-governed) and Geneva (French-speaking, substantially populated by religious refugees), both within or adjacent to the Swiss Confederacy's own decentralized, republican city-state structure (Doc_01 §1, §6; Doc_05 §6.1, §6.3). The two cities are geographically separated, politically distinct (Zurich a full Confederacy member; Geneva allied with Bern and Fribourg from 1526, independent of Savoy from 1536), and never institutionally merged — bridged only doctrinally, by the Consensus Tigurinus (1549), mediated through Bullinger's Zurich specifically since Zwingli and Calvin themselves never met.

**Community character:** This is a genuinely two-stranded world, not a single community with two campuses (Doc_01 §4/§5, confirmed by the project lead). Each strand understood itself as engaged in the same project — testing all inherited practice against Scripture and refusing what Scripture does not warrant — while enacting that shared conviction through two different, strand-specific institutional mechanisms: public Disputation before the city council at Zurich, and fixed catechesis backed by consistorial discipline at Geneva. Its self-understanding organized around one shared conviction, worked out independently in each strand's own idiom: *Scripture alone is sufficient to test and reform both doctrine and civic order, and what it does not warrant, the church may not require.* This world understood itself as continuous with, not a departure from, the true gospel obscured by the late-medieval church's own accretions — never a doctrinal innovation in its own self-understanding, however much its own opponents characterized it as one.

## Section 3 — Formation Logic

Drawn directly from Doc_07 §2I (Formation Logic, the current section under the M4 lens spine — see the Method Note above).

### The Fundamental Aim of Formation

A fully formed member of this world holds that Scripture alone tests and warrants both doctrine and civic order, and that what it does not warrant, the church may not require. That same formed member holds God's own sovereign election as the ground of assurance rather than a source of dread, and refuses — as one settled conviction, not two separate refusals — both a repeated sacrifice at the altar and a body confined to bread. Where that member is Genevan, formation adds one further step the shared method does not itself require: a standing body of pastors and elders answerable for whether daily conduct answers to what has been professed.

### The Formation Mechanism

Formation here runs through one shared method, enacted two different ways. It does not happen through a single uniform practice repeated identically in both cities — it happens through public Disputation, argued before the whole city and its own council, at Zurich; and through fixed catechesis, built up book by book, backed by consistorial oversight, at Geneva (Doc_04 §3.3, §5; Doc_07 §2I). A person is formed less by encountering one doctrine taught one way than by living inside whichever of these two civic mechanisms their own city actually built to enact the shared underlying conviction.

### The Formation Arc

There is no single dated threshold-crossing comparable to a rite of initiation — belonging here runs through citizenship itself, since the ordinary case is that to be a citizen of Reformed Zurich or Geneva was already to belong to its church (Doc_05 §2). The arc instead runs through continuous, repeated instruction: the Prophezei's public exposition at Zurich, fixed catechesis at Geneva. Maturity in this world looks like holding the doctrine at the register its own confessional texts increasingly turn it toward — not merely correct belief, argued and defended, but settled comfort, taught and handed to the next generation without drift (Doc_05 §6.4).

### Cross-Strand Formation Logic

Applicable, and central rather than peripheral (unlike Donatism's own strand-singular precedent, where this subsection does not apply). The same underlying formation logic — Scripture tested and enacted — produces two different formation mechanisms in each strand's own civic context, which Article 21's own logic treats as evidence *strengthening*, not fragmenting, this world's own claim to a single irreducible core (Doc_04 §3.3, §5; Doc_07 §2I).

### Formation Logic and the Confirmed Gravities

G3 is the generative logic, not one gravity among three Primaries — it is the tool both Zwingli's and Calvin's own hands use to derive G1 and to ground G2's own rejection of corporeal reading. G1 (sovereignty) is the doctrine this method derives; G2 (Spiritual Presence) is the doctrine the same method grounds against a rival reading. G4 is Geneva's own further institutional step, not a shared channel. T1 and T2 are not exceptions to this formation logic but what the ecology actually lived rather than resolved — T1 the institutional-level expression of G3's own strand-specific enactment difference; T2 what G2's own mature, post-1549 form directly resolves in text without settling, in this world's own words, how much it actually changed the earlier reading.

**Usability test applied:** this section can be read directly into the World Capsule Core's "How This World Forms People" section without returning to Doc_07 — the fundamental aim, mechanism, and arc are each stated at the specificity Doc_07 §2I itself reached, not summarized down from it.

## Section 4 — Ecological Summary

Covers all nine of Doc_07's current M4 lenses (see Method Note above). The four dimensions the template names as required — Emotional Texture, Philosophical Ecology, Authority Structures, Boundary Structures — are each present below, correctly mapped to their current Doc_07 lens.

### 4A — Ritual/Practical *(Doc_07 §2A)*

Ritual life is defined as much by subtraction as by practice, and the subtraction differs by strand before 1549: Zurich suppressed instrumental music and congregational singing; Geneva built congregational singing into its own devotional center through the Genevan Psalter. What both strands shared, and eventually practiced from a single jointly-agreed text, is the Supper itself. The other genuinely enacted practice this world's record documents in contemporary detail is disputational and catechetical, not liturgical: the Zurich Disputations and Geneva's fixed catechesis — G3's own two strand-specific enactments, the actual instrument of formation, not a doctrine about it.

### 4B — Experiential/Emotional *(Doc_07 §2B)*

Genuinely thin, and the thinness is itself a finding, not a coverage failure. What survives is doctrinal *language of* assurance and comfort — the Second Helvetic Confession's own pastoral register, "Let... Christ be the mirror in which we behold [our] predestination" — not first-person report of feeling it. Whether an ordinary believer actually experienced comfort, or instead anxious self-examination, is Inferential-Thin and not narrated. The one place this world's record comes closest to a documented emotional register beyond doctrinal language is negative and institutional: the weight of a Consistory summons, unrecoverable behind the still-unvendored consistory registers.

### 4C — Doctrinal/Philosophical *(Doc_07 §2D)*

The richest lens this world's record offers. Three Primary gravities anchor it directly, all generated by one shared scriptural-authority method (G3) — this world's own single deepest structural fact, stated once here rather than three times from three separate angles. This is also the lens where the Author-Gravity asymmetry is sharpest and most specifically located: G1's core conviction is cross-strand Documented, but its fullest systematized form is substantially Calvin/Beza-concentrated — a divergence named, not smoothed over.

### 4D — Ethical/Legal *(Doc_07 §2E)*

Required under M4, and substantively present, distinct from the doctrinal lens: this is where doctrine becomes enforceable conduct. Geneva's Consistory is this world's own most concretely legal-ecclesial institution, fought over directly with the civil magistrate in the Perrinist crisis. Zurich shows no comparable distinct ecclesial-legal body — discipline there runs through the city council directly. This lens is exactly where G4's own strand-scope qualification bites hardest: Geneva's disciplinary law is a real, distinct legal-ecclesial fact; Zurich's is civil law exercised on scripturally-disputed grounds, not a separate legal order.

### 4E — Social/Institutional *(Doc_07 §2F)*

Inseparable from this world's own two-strand architecture. Zurich: population coextensive with citizenship itself. Geneva: population governed jointly by the Company of Pastors and a Consistory answerable to something other than the council alone, substantially populated by religious refugees. The Consensus Tigurinus is this lens's own central artifact — two named institutions putting their names to one page without their own two cities becoming institutionally one. This world's own social boundary against re-entry runs by exclusion (the Anabaptist schism) on one axis at the same moment it builds cross-strand doctrinal coherence (the Consensus) on another.

### 4F — Material *(Doc_07 §2G)*

A culture defined chiefly by subtraction, not production: image veneration refused; instrumental music silenced at Zurich (Dominant Modern Reconstruction for the specific date); the communion table, not an altar, following directly from G2's own refusal of the Mass's sacrificial character. No vendored source directly describes physical worship spaces, furnishings, or dress — everything beyond the doctrinal refusal itself is this document's own inference from that doctrine's logical entailments, Inferential-Thin, disclosed as such.

### 4G — Narrative/Mythic *(Doc_07 §2C)*

Also genuinely thin, and directly connected to this world's own principled refusal of the late-medieval devotional genre narrative/hagiography belongs to — a world that refuses image veneration and cultic memory of a holy figure's life on doctrinal principle should not be expected to produce the genre those refusals target, and its absence here is evidence the refusal worked, not a gap. The closest sustained narrative-adjacent source is the Acts of the Zurich Disputations — a contemporary institutional record, not commemorative narrative. Doc_09's own research pass, reading this world's vendored corpus directly for narrative content rather than relying on prior summaries, found real primary narrative material previously unmined: Hegenwald's own eyewitness account of the First Disputation, Calvin's own account of traveling to frame the Consensus, and Myconius's own near-eyewitness account of Zwingli's death at Kappel — three genuinely Tier 1 stories, still a small roster by this project's own portfolio standard, consistent with (not contradicting) this lens's own general thinness finding.

### 4H — Boundary Structures *(Doc_07 §2H, CiC addition)*

A triple refusal, each refusal distinct in kind: against Rome (what this world was responding to — the Mass's sacrificial character); against Wittenberg (what pressed from outside, within the wider Protestant movement — corporeal presence, the specific break at Marburg); against the Anabaptists (what fractured from within — the sharpest of the three, since it answers a challenge from inside Zwingli's own circle rather than outside it). The Counter-Reformation supplies this lens's own sustained external pressure across the whole window without ever converting into a world-ending event, unlike this project's own Donatism precedent.

### 4I — Formation Logic *(Doc_07 §2I, CiC addition)*

Covered fully at Section 3 above, per the template's own instruction that Formation Logic receives its own dedicated section rather than a compressed Ecological Summary entry.

### Cross-Lens Coherence

This world's central coherence, per Doc_07 §5: doctrinal/confessional richness (4C) and genuine cross-strand integration coexist with institutional/authority-structure divergence (4D, 4E) that never converges within the window — a world can be doctrinally unified and institutionally divergent at once, and this world's own record shows exactly that combination. The genre asymmetry, not the Author-Gravity asymmetry, is this world's own binding limit on three lenses at once (4B, 4F, 4G) — a single underlying cause (doctrine and confession richly vendored; daily practice, material culture, and first-person report almost entirely absent, for both strands alike) surfacing at three different scales, the same pattern-repeating-at-every-scale structure Donatism's own World Profile names for its own, differently-caused transmission asymmetry. The sharpest external comparison point remains Lutheran Wittenberg, sharpening on the civic-political axis (4D, 4E) at least as much as the doctrinal one (4C, 4H).
