**This document therefore applies:** Smart's seven dimensions (ritual/practical, experiential/emotional, narrative/mythic, doctrinal/philosophical, ethical/legal, social/institutional, material) as the fixed required spine, plus CiC's own two standing additions (Boundary Structures, Formation Logic, applied last as synthesis) — nine required lenses in total, labeled 2A–2I below — plus three optional further lenses (Memory Structures, Interpretive Ecology, Representative Theological Patterns), applied only where this world's own evidence specifically supports them, with reasoning disclosed either way (§3).

**The confirmed gravity spine, carried through every lens below (Doc_04 §4).** **G1** Sovereignty of God/Predestination and Election (Primary); **G2** Spiritual Presence and the Rejection of Corporeal/Sacrificial Mediation (Primary); **G3** Scripture as Sole and Sufficient Authority, Enacted Through Disputation and Catechesis (Primary, the strongest cross-strand candidate in this world's build); **G4** Consistorial Church Discipline (Supporting, Geneva-scope-qualified — cross-strand attested only in its general doctrine, not its formation-defining institutional specifics); **T1** Council-Led Civic Authority (Zurich) vs. Consistorial Independence (Geneva), permanent within-window per Doc_01 §4's own explicit finding; **T2** Zwingli's Own "Remembrance" Reading of the Supper vs. the Negotiated Spiritual-Presence Consensus (1549).

These are two distinct axes (Doc_05 §8) and this document does not merge them.

## 2. The Required Lenses

### 2A. Ritual/Practical

This world's ritual life is defined as much by subtraction as by practice, and the subtraction itself differs by strand before 1549. At Zurich, Zwingli's own governing discipline — continuous, book-by-book scriptural exposition rather than the fixed lectionary, the practice this world's own beginning point rests on (Doc_01 §2; Doc_05 §1) — extended to worship itself: instrumental music and congregational singing were suppressed as without scriptural warrant, and by most accounts the Grossmünster's own organ fell silent in the mid-1520s (Dominant Modern Reconstruction, not vendor-verified — Doc_01 §5; Doc_05 §3). Geneva's worship ran the opposite direction on this one point: congregational singing became devotionally central through the metrical Genevan Psalter (Marot and Beza, complete 1562, itself unvendored, Registry row 15). What both strands shared, and eventually practiced from a single jointly-agreed text, is the Supper itself — refusing a repeated sacrifice and a corporeal presence, holding instead that Christ is truly, spiritually given to those who receive believing (Doc_05 §3, the Consensus Tigurinus's own 9th Head of Agreement, verified verbatim: "though we distinguish, as we ought, between the signs and the things signified, yet we do not disjoin the reality from the signs," lines 768–770).

The other ritual-practical register this world's record documents in first-person, contemporary detail is not liturgical but disputational and catechetical: the First and Second Zurich Disputations (1523), a genuinely enacted public practice with a contemporary institutional record (Doc_02 §1), and Geneva's fixed catechesis (the Geneva Catechism; the Heidelberg Catechism, a third, non-Zurich/non-Geneva Reformed voice, Doc_05 §6.4). These are G3's own two strand-specific enactments of one shared conviction (Doc_04 §3.3) — not a doctrine about formation, but the actual instrument of it.

**Gravities most visible:** G2 (the Supper); G3 (Disputation, catechesis); T2 (the pre-/post-1549 Supper-practice divide).

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** naming worship-practice and disputation/catechesis together, under one lens, makes visible that this world's two strands diverge sharply on one ritual axis (music) while converging, eventually, on the doctrinally load-bearing one (the Supper) — a pattern the separate Worship and Ministry/Formation lenses in Doc_05 each partially showed but did not state as a single ritual-practical finding.

### 2B. Experiential/Emotional

This lens is genuinely thin, and the thinness is itself a finding (M4's own governing instruction: "a thin dimension is a result, not a coverage failure"). What this corpus offers directly is doctrinal *language of* assurance and comfort, not first-person report of feeling it: the Second Helvetic Confession's own pastoral register — "Let... [our] Christ be the mirror in which we behold our predestination" (Bullinger, 1566, lines 683–684, "onr" corrected to "our," an OCR artifact, disclosed rather than silently cleaned) and "We reject those who seek out of Christ whether they are chosen" (line 667) — is written to produce assurance in a hearer, and its own choice of image (a mirror, not an argument) is itself evidence of an intended emotional register. But whether an ordinary Zurich or Geneva believer actually experienced that comfort, or instead experienced predestination doctrine as a source of anxious self-examination (a documented risk within the wider Reformed tradition, not independently assessed against this world's own vendored corpus this pass), is Inferential-Thin and not narrated here (Doc_05 §0 item 4, §5, §10).

The one place this world's record comes closest to a documented emotional register beyond doctrinal language is negative and institutional rather than devotional: the weight of a Consistory summons at Geneva. Doc_05 §4/§10 already named this precisely as a genre-asymmetry absence — the consistory registers that would show what an actual disciplinary session felt like to the person summoned remain unvendored (Confidence E) — and this document does not attempt to fill that absence with invented interiority.

**Gravities most visible:** G1 (assurance/comfort as this doctrine's own pastoral register); G4 (the Consistory, where an emotional register is plausible but unrecoverable).

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** naming this lens explicitly, rather than letting assurance-language appear only inside the Ministry and Formation lens's inhabited passages, makes the gap between *doctrinal comfort as written* and *comfort as actually felt* a distinct, named finding rather than an implicit background fact.

[Construction note: both Second Helvetic Confession quotations independently re-verified this pass (lines 667, 683–684). The mirror image's own rhetorical function as evidence of intended emotional register is this document's own reading, not an inherited finding.]

### 2C. Narrative/Mythic

Also genuinely thin, confirming Doc_05 §11's own forward note to Doc_09: "this world's own formation narrative material is thin by design, not by gap... no martyrdom account or saint's life exists in this corpus and none should be manufactured." This is not incidental — it is a direct consequence of this world's own G2/Boundary-Ecology refusal of image veneration and, more broadly, of a devotional register built around saints' lives and cultic memory, the specific late-medieval practice this world's confessional core positions itself against (Doc_01 §6; Doc_05 §6.2). A world that refuses the genre on principle should not be expected to produce it, and its absence here is evidence of the refusal working, not a gap this document should try to close.

The closest thing to sustained first-person narrative this corpus holds is the Acts of the Zurich Disputations — a contemporary institutional record of an actual public event, not commemorative narrative composed after the fact to shape memory (Doc_02 §1; Doc_05 §11). Zwingli's own death in battle at Kappel (1531) is a real, documented historical event with genuine formative weight for Zurich specifically (Doc_01 §2, §6; Doc_05 §6.3's own inhabited line: "our own pastor was killed in that same fighting"), but this world's own record does not develop it into hagiographic or martyrological narrative — consistent with, rather than an exception to, this lens's own general thinness.

**Gravities most visible:** G2/Boundary Ecology (the refusal that produces this thinness); G3 (the Disputation Acts, the one genuinely narrative-adjacent source).

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** connecting the narrative thinness directly to the boundary refusal (rather than treating it as a separate acquisition gap) shows this absence is *itself* a formation-shaping fact about this world, not merely a limit on what this document can say about it.

### 2E. Ethical/Legal

Required under M4 (previously the one Smart dimension with no CiC lens) and substantively present in this world's own record, distinct from the doctrinal lens above: this is where doctrine becomes enforceable conduct. Geneva's Consistory — "seniors selected from the people to unite with the bishops in pronouncing censures and exercising discipline" (Calvin, Institutes IV.3.8, lines 2831–2832, verified this pass) and, restated later in the same work, "the consistory of elders, which was in the Church what a council is in a city" (lines 10503–10504, verified this pass) — is this world's own most concretely legal-ecclesial institution: a body with the standing power of censure and excommunication (Doc_03 Cluster 4), fought over directly with the civil magistrate in the Perrinist crisis and "substantially resolved in [Calvin's] favor" only by 1555 (Doc_01 §2; Doc_05 §4, T1). Excommunication's own most severe form is formal exclusion from the sacraments — a direct, practice-level link from this ethical/legal lens straight into G2's own doctrinal domain (Doc_04 §6, the G2↔G4 Interaction Matrix pair).

Zurich's own record shows no comparable distinct ecclesial-legal body: discipline there runs through the city council directly, on the outcome of public disputation, not through a lay-inclusive court independent of civil authority (Doc_04 §3.4, §5). This is G4's own confirmed strand-scope qualification, restated here specifically because the ethical/legal lens is exactly where that qualification bites hardest — Geneva's disciplinary law is a real, distinct legal-ecclesial fact; Zurich's is civil law exercised on scripturally-disputed grounds, not a separate legal order.

**Gravities most visible:** G4 (Consistorial discipline, Supporting, Geneva-scope-qualified); T1 (the council/Consistory jurisdictional fight); G3 (the scriptural warrant G4 claims for itself).

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** isolating "ethical/legal" from "organizational/authority" as its own question (rather than one lens covering both structure and enforcement) sharpens exactly where this world's genre asymmetry bites — the *general legal doctrine* is Confidence A, directly vendored; the *specific case law* (what actually got someone excommunicated, and on what showing) is Confidence E, unvendored, the same split Doc_04 §3.4 already found but restated here at the level Doc_09's own Story Inventory will need it.

### 2F. Social/Institutional

This world's social structure is inseparable from its own two-strand architecture (Doc_05 §6.1, this world's own most structurally central lens). Zurich: German-speaking, council-governed, its population coextensive with citizenship itself — to be a citizen was, in the ordinary case, already to belong to the church (Doc_05 §2). Geneva: French-speaking, substantially populated by religious refugees, its population governed jointly by the Company of Pastors and a Consistory of pastors and lay elders answerable to something other than the council alone (Doc_05 §1, §4). The Consensus Tigurinus (1549) is this lens's own central social-institutional artifact — the one document in this world's entire corpus literally co-authored across the strand boundary, two named institutions ("the Ministers of the Church of Zurich"; "John Calvin, Minister of the Church of Geneva") putting their names to one page without their own two cities becoming institutionally one (Doc_05 §6.1).

This world's own social boundary against re-entry runs differently from a world defined by a threshold rite: there was no rebaptism here, and this world's confessional core actively refuses the Anabaptist claim that scripturally consistent reform requires re-baptizing believing adults — a refusal answering a challenge that arose, at Zurich specifically, from inside Zwingli's own reform circle (Grebel and Manz, both formerly of that circle, 21 January 1525, Doc_01 §7; Doc_05 §2).

**Gravities most visible:** G3 (the two strand-specific institutional enactments); G4 (Geneva's own institutional elaboration); T1 (the institutional separation itself, which *is* the social/institutional finding at its sharpest).

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** stating the Anabaptist schism as a social/institutional fact (a citizenry that excludes a specific competing membership-model) alongside the Strand lens's own doctrinal bridge (the Consensus) shows this world building its own social coherence by exclusion on one axis (the Anabaptists) at the same moment it builds cross-strand doctrinal coherence on another (the Consensus) — two social-institutional movements Doc_05's own separate lenses did not put side by side.

Image veneration is refused from this world's own founding statement onward (Art. XVIII's own wider polemic; Doc_05 §6.2). Instrumental music and, by most accounts, the organ itself fell silent at Zurich (Dominant Modern Reconstruction, §2A above). The communion table, not an altar, is this world's own liturgical furniture, following directly from G2's refusal of the Mass's sacrificial character — a table does not host a sacrifice; an altar does. ### 2H. Boundary Structures

Per the Forces Framework's own explicit instruction for this lens specifically — "what the world was responding to, what pressed from outside, and what fractured from within are the three movements of the boundary ecology analysis" — this lens is written through forces directly, not merely alongside them. This world knew itself through a **triple refusal**, a richer structure than a world defined against one rival (Doc_05 §6.2):

- **Against Rome** — *what it was responding to*: the Mass's own sacrificial character and image veneration, refused from Zurich's own founding statement onward.
- **Against Wittenberg** — *what pressed from outside, within the wider Protestant movement itself*: once both strands' positions had matured, any claim that Christ's body is locally or corporeally present in the bread and wine — the specific break at Marburg (1–4 October 1529), Zwingli and Oecolampadius against Luther and Melanchthon, Calvin then about twenty and not yet a public figure (Doc_01 §6; Doc_05 §3).
- **Against the Anabaptists** — *what fractured from within*: infant baptism's own legitimacy and the magistrate's own standing to enforce religious uniformity, refused against a movement that arose, at Zurich specifically, from inside Zwingli's own circle — the sharpest of the three refusals, because it answers a challenge from inside rather than outside (Doc_05 §6.2; Doc_01 §7).

The Counter-Reformation supplies this lens's own sustained external pressure across the whole window — the Jesuit presence at Fribourg from 1580, directly facing Reformed Bern and Geneva, and the 1590s Catholic reconquest of the Chablais fought on Geneva's own doorstep (Doc_01 §7, not independently assessed this pass) — while the Anabaptist fracture remains this world's own only attested internal schism within the window (Doc_01 §6, Cell 3A/3B: no fracture *closes* this world, but the Anabaptist schism is a real internal fracture that does not close it).

**Gravities most visible:** G2 (Rome, Wittenberg); G3 and G4 (the Anabaptist refusal's own scriptural and magistrate-enforcement grounds).

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** structuring the triple refusal explicitly around the Forces Framework's own three movements (responding to / pressed from outside / fractured from within) shows the three refusals are not symmetric in kind — one is a response to an inherited order, one is resistance to a contemporary external rival, and one is a wound from within — a distinction Doc_05 §6.2 names but does not organize around these three specific categories.

[Construction note: all three refusals and their own dating are **Documented** (Doc_01 §6, §7; Doc_05 §6.2). The Jesuit/Fribourg and Chablais material is carried from Doc_01 §7 as "not independently assessed this pass," the same qualification stated there.]

### 2I. Formation Logic

Applied last, as synthesis, per M4's own instruction. Formation in this world runs on one shared method producing two strand-specific enactments, plus one Geneva-specific further step (Doc_05 §5, the central lens this document now closes the loop on). **G3** — Scripture as sole and sufficient authority — is the actual generative logic: it is the tool both Zwingli's 1527 argument and Calvin's Institutes use to derive **G1** (the sovereignty doctrine), and the same scriptural-literalist method both strands use to argue against a corporeal reading of "This is my body," grounding **G2** (Doc_04 §6, the G1↔G3 and G2↔G3 Interaction Matrix pairs, both **R**, reinforcing). This is why G3 is this document's own single deepest structural fact (§2D above): it is not one gravity among three Primaries but the mechanism generating the other two.

That one method is *enacted* differently by strand — public Disputation before the city council at Zurich; fixed catechesis at Geneva — which Article 21's own logic treats as evidence *strengthening*, not weakening, G3's own claim to centrality: the same underlying conviction producing different institutional practice in each strand's own civic context is exactly the pattern Article 21 identifies as diagnostic of a world's own irreducible core (Doc_04 §3.3, §5). Geneva alone then takes a further formation step this shared method does not itself require: a standing disciplinary body (G4) to ask directly whether a professed believer's own daily conduct answers to what has been professed (Doc_05 §5). This is not a shared Zurich/Geneva formation channel — it is Geneva's own institutional elaboration of the same underlying conviction, correctly classified Supporting and strand-scope-qualified rather than folded into the shared spine.

**T1** and **T2** are not exceptions to this formation logic; they are what the ecology actually lived rather than resolved. T1 (council-led versus consistorial authority) is the institutional-level expression of G3's own strand-specific enactment difference, described from two angles rather than found as two separate facts (Doc_04 §6, G3↔T1, **R**). T2 (Zwingli's remembrance reading versus the negotiated Consensus) is what G2's own mature, post-1549 form directly resolves in text — though whether it resolves it in substance or merely in diplomatic language remains the live [CT] contest this document does not adjudicate (Doc_04 §3.2, §3.5).

**Gravities most visible:** all six (G1–G4, T1, T2) — this is the synthesis lens by design.

**What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** stating explicitly that G3 is not a third Primary alongside G1 and G2 but the *generative logic underneath* both of them, with G4 as a strand-specific further step rather than a fourth peer channel, gives this world's formation architecture a single explicit shape (one method, two enactments, one further step, two held tensions) that Doc_05's own lens-by-lens presentation implies but never states as a single formation-logic finding in its own right.

## 3. Additional Lenses

Applied only where this world's own evidence specifically supports them, per M4's own instruction, with reasoning disclosed either way.

**Memory Structures — applied, on direct evidence.** This world's own confessional corpus is not only doctrine; it is doctrine written specifically to survive its own founding generation. Doc_05 §6.4 states this directly: "a confession is not written to say something new; it is written so that what is already believed can be tested, taught, and handed to the next generation without drift" — and the Second Helvetic Confession (1566) is dated evidence of exactly this mechanism, composed by Bullinger decades after Zwingli's death, once "a founding generation's oral and disputational method needed a fixed text to outlive it" (Doc_05 §6.4). Beza's own *Tabula praedestinationis* (1555) performs the same memory-fixing function for G1's own systematized form, published nine years before Calvin's death specifically so the doctrine would not depend on Calvin's own continued presence to be taught correctly (Doc_01 §7). This world's mechanism of memory is textual codification by a named successor, not commemorative narrative or relic — consistent with, and a further instance of, the Narrative/Mythic lens's own thinness (§2C above): this world remembers by confession, not by story.

**Interpretive Ecology — applied, on direct evidence.** Disputation and catechesis are not merely two delivery mechanisms for one fixed content; they are two genuinely different *processes* of interpretation producing the same doctrinal outcome. Zurich's own method is adversarial and public: doctrine is tested and confirmed at open disputation before the whole city, with the outcome ratified by civic vote (Doc_05 §4, §5 — "there is no second court to appeal to, and none is wanted; the council that ratified the Sixty-Seven Articles is the same council that governs what follows from them"). Geneva's own method is sequential and instructional: doctrine "built book by book, article by article, until the whole shape of what Scripture actually teaches... stands built up rather than merely defended" (Doc_05 §5). Both interpretive ecologies answer to the same scriptural authority (G3) and reach convergent conclusions (G1, G2), but the *manner* of reaching them — contested in public assembly versus built up in fixed sequence — is itself a distinct ecological fact about how this world's two communities related to their own shared texts, not reducible to the Ritual/Practical lens's own account of *what* they did.

**Representative Theological Patterns — applied partially, with the Representative decision itself explicitly reserved.** This world's own record shows two genuinely distinct patterns of theological reasoning-in-person, not merely two doctrines: Zwingli's own combative, publicly-argued, occasionally polemical style (the 1527 *Refutation*, the Sixty-Seven Articles' own disputational form) against Calvin's own systematic, expository, book-length style (the Institutes). This is an evidenced ecological pattern this document can and does name — it is not, and is not intended to be, a decision about which pattern, or what combination, a future Representative should embody. Representative identity, title, and voice remain a standing escalation category under `cic-build-cycle`, already flagged as a live, genuine choice for this world specifically (a single Representative, likely Calvin given the Author-Gravity concentration, versus a multi-figure Representative reflecting the confirmed two-strand structure) — this document names the ecological evidence bearing on that future choice without making it, consistent with Doc_10's own governing rule that "the Representative emerges last, built only from the completed and frozen world's ecology."

[Construction note: the Second Helvetic Confession and Beza's *Tabula* dating are **Documented** (Doc_01 §7; Doc_05 §6.4). The Disputation/catechesis process-distinction is this document's own reading of Doc_05 §4/§5's own already-established material, not independently re-researched. The two reasoning-style characterization draws on Doc_02's own genre description of each figure's corpus (disputational tracts and polemics for Zwingli; systematic theology for Calvin) and is offered as evidenced pattern, not as any part of the Representative decision itself.]

## 5. Cross-Lens Synthesis

Three findings hold across every lens above, and none of them was visible from any single lens alone.

**First: this world's coherence is real but runs on one axis, not two.** The doctrinal/confessional axis (§2D, and Doc_05's own Interpretive/Confessional Ecology) shows genuine cross-strand integration — G3 generating G1 and G2 independently in each strand's own hands, converging without merging (§2I). The institutional/practice axis does not show the same integration: T1's own authority-structure divergence is permanent within the window, and G4's own most vivid, formation-defining content is Geneva-only. A world can be doctrinally unified and institutionally divergent at once, and this world's own record shows exactly that combination — not a contradiction, but this world's own particular shape.

**Second: the genre asymmetry, not the Author-Gravity asymmetry, is this world's own binding limit on several required lenses at once.** Experiential/Emotional (§2B), Narrative/Mythic (§2C), and Material (§2G) are all thin for the same underlying reason — this corpus holds confession, catechism, systematic theology, and contemporary disputation record in depth, and almost nothing of institutional daily practice, material culture, or first-person interior report, for either strand alike (Doc_05 §8). This is a different limit from the Author-Gravity volume imbalance (Calvin four times Zwingli), which affects *whose voice* is fuller within the doctrinal lens (§2D) but does not explain why the experiential, narrative, and material lenses are thin for both strands together. Holding these as two separate findings, per Doc_05 §8's own instruction, prevents either from being used to explain a gap it does not actually cause.

**Third: the sharpest external comparison point remains Lutheran Wittenberg, and it sharpens on the civic-political axis at least as much as the doctrinal one.** Doc_01 §1 and Doc_05 §6.3 already name the republican city-council structure, contested case by case across the Swiss Confederacy, as "the single sharpest structural contrast with the sibling Lutheran Wittenberg world" — Wittenberg's own reform proceeding through a princely territorial church rather than city-by-city civic disputation. This document's own Ethical/Legal (§2E) and Social/Institutional (§2F) lenses corroborate that contrast independently: this world's own disciplinary and legal structure (the Consistory, the council) is civic and republican in a way a princely-territorial Lutheran polity's own mechanism is not, meaning the Wittenberg contrast is not confined to the Marburg Eucharistic break (§2H) but runs through this world's entire institutional architecture. Full reciprocal comparison remains open pending Lutheran Wittenberg's own Doc_01, which does not yet exist in this repository (Doc_01 §7 item 5, unresolved as of this document).

## 6. Integrative Observation

*[Inhabited — cross-strand, holding both voices at once, per Article 23]* We were never one city, and we never pretended to be — a pastor at Zurich reading Scripture straight through before an assembled council, and a theologian at Geneva building doctrine book upon book before a Consistory of pastors and elders, are not the same practice wearing two names. But set side by side, what we refused and what we built rhyme too closely to be coincidence: no repeated sacrifice, no body confined to bread, no salvation resting on anything foreseen in the one saved, and no discipline exercised without scriptural warrant behind it — reached independently, in two languages, by two men who never met, and confirmed only later, when Zurich's own ministers and Geneva's own theologian put their names to a single page neither could have written alone. What that page could not do, and did not try to do, was make our two councils one, or our two ways of testing what we believe the same way. We remain what we always were: one faith, arrived at twice.

## 7. Gaps and Limits

- **Genre asymmetry (structural, affecting three required lenses at once).** Experiential/Emotional (§2B), Narrative/Mythic (§2C), and Material (§2G) are each thin because this corpus documents doctrine and confession far more richly than daily practice, interior experience, or material culture, for both strands alike (§5, second finding).
