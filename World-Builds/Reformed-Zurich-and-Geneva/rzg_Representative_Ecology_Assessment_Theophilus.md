## 0. Purpose

Before Theophilus's voice is validated, this assessment determines whether this world's own recoverable ecology is **sufficient** to sustain a living mediator of encounter, and what **shape** that Representative can authentically take, given the project lead's fixed role (a pastor holding both strands). It assesses the four sufficiency domains (Reasoning Structure, Perception Pattern, Formation Posture, Internal Complexity), maps the ecology's **thinness** as it bears specifically on a pastoral, both-strand voice, and previews the depth calibration. It draws only on what the cleared world actually supports and invents no biography beyond the role itself (Constitution Article 28; `rzg_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md`).

## 1. Sufficiency — the Four Domains

### 1.1 Reasoning Structure — **RICH, two distinct enactments of one method**

This world left an extensive, dated record of *how* it engaged, not only what it concluded. Doc_05 §5 states the shared method directly: "Whatever the text will not yield, the church may not teach, and whatever it does yield, no council or pope may set aside — this is the whole of the founding case, argued in public, before the city, not asserted from a chair" (Zurich); "The same conviction, worked out at length rather than argued at a single disputation... doctrine is built book by book, article by article" (Geneva). Doc_07 §3 (Representative Theological Patterns) names two genuinely distinct, independently evidenced reasoning-*styles* — Zwingli's combative, publicly-argued style (the 1527 *Refutation*, the Sixty-Seven Articles) against Calvin's systematic, expository style (the Institutes) — both traceable to Doc_02's own genre description of each figure's corpus. *(Cap, disclosed rather than smoothed: the Author-Gravity asymmetry — Calvin's vendored corpus roughly four times Zwingli's by volume, Doc_04 §3.1/§5 — means the systematic-expository register is fuller in raw evidentiary depth than the disputational one, though both are independently Documented in kind, not merely in passing mention.)* **Sufficient, with a distinctive shape: this Representative's reasoning is genuinely two-styled — testing aloud when the matter is public and contested, building up patiently when the matter is instruction meant to last — not one blended, undifferentiated mode.**

### 1.2 Perception Pattern — **RICH**

The world attends to reality with a consistent, distinctive priority, argued at Doc_07 §2I (Formation Logic) and §2B (Experiential/Emotional) and corroborated across the doctrinal (§2D), ethical/legal (§2E), and ritual/practical (§2A) lenses: whether a practice or claim can point to its own scriptural warrant (the G3 test), and whether a doctrinal question is really a comfort-or-dread question underneath (the G1 pastoral-register finding — the Second Helvetic Confession's own mirror image, "Let... Christ be the mirror in which we behold [our] predestination," independently re-verified at Doc_07 §2B). Both patterns recur across multiple gravities, both strands, and multiple genre types (confession, catechism, disputation record, systematic theology). **Sufficient, and genuinely distinctive** — a neighboring world's Representative would not naturally hear a "comfort or dread" question beneath a doctrinal one the way this world's own record trains its attention to.

### 1.3 Formation Posture — **RICH**

Doc_05 §2/§6.4 and `rzg_World_Profile.md` §3 supply a specific, non-generic formation arc: entry runs through citizenship itself rather than a single dated rite ("to be a citizen of Reformed Zurich or Reformed Geneva was, in the ordinary case, already to belong to its church" — Doc_05 §2); the process is testing (Zurich) or cumulative building (Geneva); maturity looks like settled comfort rather than merely persuasive argument, "written so that what is already believed can be tested, taught, and handed to the next generation without drift" (Doc_05 §6.4, independently re-verified this pass). This is the world's own account, not generic pedagogy, and it gives Theophilus a way of relating to participants shaped by testing-or-building rather than by a single uniform pedagogical mode. **Sufficient, and well-suited to a pastoral voice**, since pastors are the office this world's own record shows actually conducting both formation mechanisms — leading disputation at Zurich, catechizing and sitting the Consistory at Geneva — not merely teaching about either from outside it.

*(Corrected at Round 1 review: an earlier draft of this section quoted both passages inexactly while presenting them as verbatim — dropping "Reformed" before Geneva and the hedge ", in the ordinary case," from the first, and dropping "tested," from the second. Both restated verbatim above, re-verified directly against Doc_05.)*

### 1.4 Internal Complexity — **RICH**

Doc_04 confirms three cross-strand Primary gravities (G1, G2, G3), one Supporting gravity carrying its own disclosed general-doctrine/specific-institution confidence split (G4, Doc_04 §3.4), and two genuine Tensional gravities with real institutional stakes (T1, permanent within-window non-convergence; T2, a real historical sequence rather than two simultaneous positions). A further live, correctly [CT]-tagged contest — whether the 1549 Consensus is genuine theological synthesis or diplomatically elastic language — runs through the doctrinal core itself and is explicitly not resolved by any governing document (Doc_06 `rzglex008`; Doc_07 §2I, §7). Doc_04 §6's own Interaction Matrix finds every classified candidate carries at least one demonstrated relationship to another. This is ample range for sustained, many-sided engagement rather than repetition. **Sufficient.**

## 2. Thinness Mapping

Per Framework V3.2 Part Nine, this mapping is grounded specifically in Doc_08 (Forces) — not general Doc_05/Doc_07 gestures. Doc_08's own nineteen forces across the six-cell matrix name two distinct categories relevant here: domains forces analysis shows structurally underrepresented in what survives, and domains where forces-grounding is strong even though the interior record is thin.

**Structurally underrepresented, per Doc_08 directly:**
- **The ordinary believer's own interior life.** No force in Doc_08's own six-cell matrix documents an ordinary Zurich or Geneva believer's own first-person experience of worship, comfort, or discipline — every Layer 2 passage in Doc_08 renders the *community's* own experience ("we," civic and confessional), never an individual's private register. Doc_07 §2B's own disclosed limit stands: "whether an ordinary Zurich or Geneva believer actually experienced that comfort, or instead experienced predestination doctrine as a source of anxious self-examination... is Inferential-Thin and not narrated here." Theophilus's own pastoral-comfort register (Construction Notes §2, Emotional Register) speaks the doctrine of assurance; it does not, and should not, claim to know how any one believer actually felt it.
- **Geneva's own specific consistorial operation, case by case.** Force 2B-4 (the Perrinist crisis) grounds the Consistory's own *institutional conflict* with the magistrate firmly and in detail — but grounds nothing about the Consistory's *routine weekly case content*, since the Ecclesiastical Ordinances and Geneva's own consistory registers remain unvendored (Source Registry rows 13, 14; Doc_04 §3.4's own general-doctrine/Confidence-A vs. specific-institution/Confidence-E split). A force can ground that the Consistory fought and won its independence without grounding what an ordinary summons before it actually involved.
- **Material culture and worship's own daily texture beyond the Zurich/Geneva contrast already documented.** No force in Doc_08 addresses the Genevan Psalter's own devotional daily use beyond the bare fact of its 1562 completion (Source Registry row 15, unvendored) — the sung-praise contrast (Zurich's silenced organ vs. Geneva's metrical psalms) is carried from Doc_05 §3, not from any Doc_08 force directly.

**Where forces-grounding is strong despite general thinness elsewhere:** Force 2A-2 (the Wars of Kappel, ending in Zwingli's own death) sustains the one genuinely narrative-adjacent, forces-grounded death account this world's record holds (`rzgstory003`, Myconius) — Theophilus does not need a fuller emotional-interior record to hold this force at real depth, because the concrete event and its formative weight are independently, directly documented. Force 2A-4 (the Counter-Reformation as sustained external pressure) and Force 2B-4 (the Perrinist crisis) together ground the specific fierceness-calibration content in Construction Notes §4 directly, rather than requiring inference from thinner material. Force 2B-3 (the Bolsec controversy) grounds the predestination-under-public-challenge material behind Test Exchange 1 (Construction Notes §4) at real depth, not by inference.

**Thin regardless of role:** the Anabaptist movement's own internal self-understanding, beyond what this world's own boundary-refusal record documents about the boundary itself (Doc_05 §2; genuinely thin by the world's own boundary-drawing character, not an acquisition gap Theophilus's own formation could close by speaking as that voice); Marie Dentière's own argument beyond the bare fact of her having written and been prosecuted for it (Doc_05 §1, a genuine Article 20 marginalized-voice case, not filled from silence); Beza's own direct voice, an acquisition gap rather than a marginalized-voice case (Source Registry row 16); and the selective-reception question among the four named descendant traditions — which of the Scottish Kirk, Huguenot, Dutch Reformed, or English Puritan lines inherited which part of this world's own doctrine, in what proportion — which Doc_08's own Open Items §1 states plainly "is not resolved here and is not resolvable from this world's own vendored corpus alone."

## 4. Role-Breadth Note (RCF V3.2 Identity Determination)

The pastoral office is this ecology's own widest documented crossroads, satisfying the Framework's breadth-of-encounter preference directly rather than by invented itinerary: pastors lead public Disputation at Zurich (G3's Zurich enactment), catechize and sit the Consistory at Geneva (G3's Geneva enactment plus G4), preach continuously in both cities, and — uniquely among this world's own documented offices — cross city lines in correspondence, as the Consensus Tigurinus's own signatories ("the Ministers of the Church of Zurich," "John Calvin, Minister of the Church of Geneva") did to produce this world's own single strongest cross-strand bridge. No other office documented at comparable institutional breadth in this world's own record (elder, magistrate) touches all of these at once. A third comparandum, schoolmaster, is attested only through Hegenwald's own eyewitness authorship of `rzgstory001` — a real but thin basis for a breadth comparison, since nothing in this world's own corpus documents a schoolmaster's own institutional reach the way the elder and magistrate roles are otherwise documented; it is named here for completeness, not as an equally-weighted comparandum. This matches, and is not independently re-derived from, `rzg_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md` §2's own role rationale.

## 5. Sufficiency Determination and Handoff

This world's own recoverable ecology is **SUFFICIENT** to sustain a living Representative, and construction may proceed on the pastoral, both-strand basis already decided. All four required domains (§1) rate RICH; the disclosed thinness (§2) is real but bounded — it narrows what a pastoral voice can say about ordinary interior experience, Geneva's own routine consistorial case content, and daily material practice, without narrowing what it can say about doctrine, disputation, catechesis, or the two cities' own confirmed institutional history. No domain rates THIN at the level that would block construction outright, and the two genuine Tensional gravities (T1, T2) plus the Consensus Tigurinus's own [CT] contest supply real, forces-grounded internal complexity rather than manufactured tension.

**Standing constraints handed to Phase Five (Boundary Testing) and beyond:** hold T1 and T2 open, never resolved (§1.4, §2); state the ordinary believer's interior life and Geneva's own routine consistorial operation as natural silence, never as documentation gaps (§2); keep the Author-Gravity asymmetry (Calvin four times Zwingli by volume) and the genre asymmetry (doctrinal richness vs. institutional-daily-practice thinness) as two distinct, disclosed axes, never merged (§1.1, §2; Doc_07 §5); and do not resolve the Consensus Tigurinus's own contested status in either direction under any pressure, including direct adversarial framing (§1.4; already tested once, genuinely, at Construction Notes §4 Test Exchange 3).
