8. **The Anabaptist-origin correction.** The Source Readiness Dossier §5 and this world's own Step0 §3 B3 both state "no figure overlap" with the Anabaptist movements (VI.3). Doc_01 §7 corrects this: the first Swiss Brethren baptisms (21 January 1525), at Felix Manz's house, were performed by Conrad Grebel, baptizing Georg Blaurock, who then baptized the others present, including Manz. Grebel and Manz were both formerly of Zwingli's own Zurich circle; Blaurock, a former priest from Chur who joined Zurich's radical circles in 1524–25, is not established on the standard accounts as having belonged to that circle specifically, and Doc_01 does not extend the claim to him — this entry's own prior wording ("both formerly of Zwingli's own Zurich circle," naming only Grebel and Blaurock as joint administrants) repeated an error Doc_01 Revision 2 itself had already corrected; fixed here to match Doc_01 Revision 3. Doc_02 must carry the corrected sequence forward and flag it for cross-reference to VI.3's own build thread if and when active.
