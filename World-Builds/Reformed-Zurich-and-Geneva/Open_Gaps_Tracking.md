3. **The Consensus Tigurinus (1549) — no confirmed PD English translation acquired.** The single document most directly evidencing the Zurich/Geneva strand bridge (Doc_01 §5). Beveridge's Calvin Translation Society *Tracts and Treatises* (Edinburgh, 1844–51) almost certainly contains it; only a 1958 Eerdmans reprint (access-restricted) was located on archive.org as of the Source Readiness Dossier. A dedicated HathiTrust/Google Books follow-up search is owed before Doc_02 cites it directly.

4. **The 1541 Ecclesiastical Ordinances and the Genevan Psalter (complete 1562) are not vendored.** Both are load-bearing for Doc_01 §5's Strand Determination (Authority-structure and Practice limbs respectively) and are currently characterized from the census's own descriptive notes and general historical knowledge, not from vendored text. Acquisition priority for Doc_02/G1.

5. **Geneva's own named archive (the consistory registers) is not vendored.** The census's own selection rationale for this world (`"why"` field) rests substantially on "Geneva's consistory registers... one of the best ordinary-life archives in Christian history." The only identified English edition (Kingdon, Lambert & Watt, trans. McDonald, Eerdmans 2000, vol. 1 only, 1542–44) is confirmed copyrighted per the Source Readiness Dossier and not vendored. Whether any legitimately usable substitute or excerpt exists is a live acquisition question for Doc_02/G1.

6. **Missing voices named by the census, not yet vendored: Theodore Beza and Marie Dentière.** Census `"voices"` field names both as this world's own figures under Article 20's Affirmative Duty regarding the marginalized within the community — Dentière in particular, as a woman reformer whose 1539 *Epistre très utile* was published anonymously and whose printer was prosecuted by Geneva's own authorities. Doc_02 must name this absence explicitly and assess acquirability.

7. **Zwingli's *Selected Works* (Jackson 1901) — internal table-of-contents completeness, still open.** Only the Sixty-Seven Articles are individually corpus-mapped; the volume's remaining contents (letter to Erasmus, the Constance petition, the Acts of the Zurich Disputations, "other shorter writings") are kept as one collective corpus-map row rather than itemized. Doc_01 Revision 1 incorrectly described this as substantially resolved; corrected in Revision 2.

8. **The Anabaptist-origin correction.** The Source Readiness Dossier §5 and this world's own Step0 §3 B3 both state "no figure overlap" with the Anabaptist movements (VI.3). Doc_01 §7 corrects this: the first Swiss Brethren baptisms (21 January 1525) were performed by Conrad Grebel and Georg Blaurock, both formerly of Zwingli's own Zurich circle, at Felix Manz's house. Doc_02 must carry this correction forward and flag it for cross-reference to VI.3's own build thread if and when active.
