## 0. What This Document Is (and Is Not)

Step 3 produces a **candidate vocabulary list** — this world's own terms, with preliminary definitions, initial tier estimates, tags, and Author-Gravity risk flags — generated *before* gravity discovery, so that Doc_04 tests gravities in this world's own language rather than importing modern analytical vocabulary. It is **not** the full lexicon (that is Doc_06, after ecological reconstruction), and it does **not** produce deployment chunks.

Governing disciplines for this list:

1. **No permanent numbers yet.** `rzglex` chunk numbers are assigned at Doc_06, not here. Candidates are listed by name only.
2. **Tiers are preliminary estimates, pending Doc_04.** Final tier depends on this build's own gravity discovery and is set at Doc_06.
3. **Strand attribution: live, not N/A.** Unlike a strand-singular world, Doc_01 §5 found this world genuinely two-stranded (Zurich: Zwingli, succeeded by Bullinger; Geneva: Calvin, succeeded by Beza), confirmed rather than provisional (Doc_01 §4/§9). Every candidate below carries a **Strand** column — Zurich / Geneva / Shared — and this is itself load-bearing evidence for Doc_04's own cross-strand convergence test (Article 21: "where strands exist, convergence across them is a test of a gravity's centrality").

**Tag legend** (per the lexicon-index standard): **AS** = signature vocabulary (distinctively this world's own); **SC** = shared vocabulary (held with the wider Christian tradition, or with this world's own rival, but functioning distinctively here); **DR** = high distortion risk; **TC** = technical concept; **RT** = likely runtime term; **PV** = plural voices (the world's own record holds more than one account); **CT** = contested tradition (live scholarly contest — Article 26; every CT candidate names its contest in §4 below).

**Candidate-discovery method, per Framework V7.4 Step 3's own instruction:** candidates are drawn from `Source_Registry.md`'s own **Native**-boundary entries (rows 1–10, the ten vendored primary-text rows) and from Doc_01's and Doc_02's own findings built on them — not from Doc_02's raw ecology discussion directly. Registry rows 13–17 (Native but not yet acquired: the Ecclesiastical Ordinances, the Genevan Psalter, the consistory registers, Beza's and Dentière's own works) contribute no candidates yet — several terms below (Consistory, Elder) are named from general knowledge and Doc_01's own findings rather than from a vendored text using the term itself, and are flagged AG-risk/unverified accordingly rather than treated as text-grounded.

**Author-Gravity discipline (carried from Doc_02 §2).** This world's evidentiary record is heavily concentrated in Calvin's own corpus (three Institutes volumes plus the Geneva Catechism, against Zwingli's two volumes and Bullinger's single confession). The "AG-risk" column below names Calvin wherever a candidate's specific content rests on his corpus alone, consistent with Doc_02's own finding that this asymmetry must be managed directly rather than averaged away.

## 2. Author-Gravity Concentration (screen for Doc_04)

The single largest concentration is **Calvin**, dominant across the entire Sovereignty-of-God cluster (Predestination, Election, Providence) and across the Spiritual Presence and Excommunication entries. This is the vocabulary-level expression of the asymmetry Doc_01 §8 item 1 and Doc_02 §2/§6 both name directly: if Doc_04 tests candidate gravities using vocabulary whose specific developed content is Calvin-concentrated, gravity discovery risks confirming Geneva's own theological framing as the whole movement's own emphasis, understating Zurich's own real but less systematized contribution (Predestination and Providence both exist in Zwingli's own thought, per Doc_02 §2, just not developed to the same degree in what is vendored). **Doc_04 must keep this screen active** when testing any gravity resting on the Sovereignty-of-God cluster, and should treat convergence across both strands (per Article 21) as the more decisive test of a gravity's centrality than Calvin's own corpus volume alone.

A second, narrower concentration risk sits in Cluster 4: **Consistory** and **Elder** are named from Doc_01's own findings and general knowledge, not from a vendored primary text — the Ecclesiastical Ordinances themselves remain unacquired (Registry row 13). Doc_04 should not treat these two candidates' own specific institutional detail as text-grounded until Doc_02/G1's own acquisition work closes that gap.

## 3. Shared-Root Vocabulary — Adjacent-World Boundary Awareness

Two adjacent-world relationships bear directly on this document's own vocabulary, per Doc_01 §7:

- **Lutheran Wittenberg (VI.1):** The Lord's Supper / Spiritual Presence cluster (Cluster 3 above) is the specific vocabulary this world's own confession defines itself against at Marburg (1529) — Doc_02 for both worlds should expect real, direct contrast on this exact cluster once a Lutheran Wittenberg Doc_02 exists (none does yet, per Doc_01 §7/§8 item 5). - **Latin Pastoral-Congregational Christianity (World #8, Augustine, not yet built):** the Predestination/Election cluster (Cluster 1 above) inherits directly from Augustine's own anti-Pelagian theology (Doc_01 §6, the census's own transmission edge, quoted there in full). Per the Interpretive Lexicon Development Framework's own Cross-World Consistency section, this shared vocabulary should be developed independently within each world's own entry, not cross-referenced as though it carries identical weight in both — this document names the relationship rather than resolving anything about World #8's own future vocabulary.

## 4. Contested-Tradition (CT) Candidate and Its Contest (Article 26)

One candidate carries **[CT]**.

1. **Sign and the Thing Signified** — *contest: whether the Consensus Tigurinus represents a genuine theological synthesis or a diplomatically ambiguous formula.* Scholarly opinion is divided on whether the Consensus Tigurinus's own careful language actually reconciles Zwingli's earlier memorial view and Calvin's own spiritual-presence doctrine into one coherent position, or whether it is a deliberately elastic form of words that let both parties sign without either fully conceding — the same tension Doc_01 §5 names when it observes that Beza's own later, harder predestinarian and Eucharistic positions were "developed at the Genevan Academy," not settled once for all by the 1549 document. This document does not adjudicate the contest; Doc_06 should carry both readings forward, tagged, rather than resolve to one.
