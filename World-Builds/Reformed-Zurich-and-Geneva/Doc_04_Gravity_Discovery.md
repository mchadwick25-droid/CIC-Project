## 0. Method and the Governing Discipline

**Purpose.** Gravity Discovery identifies the forces this world's own ecology actually *organizes around* — a gravity organizes; a topic merely recurs (Framework Part III). Candidates are generated only from elements recurring across multiple Doc_02 evidence sections and Doc_03's own candidate roster (never from general impression), run through six tests (Repetition, Dependency, Formation, Explanatory, Persistence, Interaction), cross-checked against the five-level confidence vocabulary (Constitution Article 17), tested for genuine cross-strand status (Article 21 — this world has two *confirmed*, not merely possible, strands, per Doc_01 §5), connected to the forces already identified in this build (Forces Framework Step 4, Doc_01 §6's own preliminary six-cell sketch), and classified Primary / Supporting / Tensional.

**This world tests real cross-strand gravities, not a declared substitute.** Doc_01 §4/§5 confirmed **one world, two strands** (Zurich: Zwingli, succeeded by Bullinger; Geneva: Calvin, succeeded by Beza) — a settled premise, not provisional. Unlike a strand-singular world (Donatism, Alexandria), this document runs Article 21's own actual cross-strand test on every confirmed candidate: does it hold in both strands' own evidence, or is it strand-bound? §5 below is that test, not a substitute for it.

**The governing discipline for this world (stated up front because it bears on every classification below): the Author Gravity concentration named as this world's central evidentiary problem in Doc_01 §8 item 1, Doc_02 §2/§6, and Doc_03 §2.** Calvin's own vendored corpus is roughly four times Zwingli's own vendored word count, with no vendored institutional or lay counterweight (Doc_02 §2). For every candidate below, this document asks not only whether the candidate is real, but whether its *specific doctrinal articulation* is genuinely attested in **both** strands' own voice, or whether an apparent cross-strand gravity is actually a Calvin/Geneva doctrine read back onto a thinner Zurich record. Where a candidate's core reality is cross-strand attested but its *specific systematized form* is Calvin/Beza-concentrated, that divergence is named explicitly (the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check below), not smoothed over — the same discipline this world's own Doc_01/Doc_02/Doc_03 review history has already enforced twice against fabricated or overstated cross-references.

## 2. Candidates Considered and Not Advanced

- **D-A Antistes → not a gravity.** Fails Repetition (attested as a title, once, distinguishing Bullinger's own office from Zwingli's earlier *Leutpriester*, Doc_01 §1 — not a recurring practice-cluster in its own right); fails Dependency (no other candidate below depends on the *title* itself — G3's disputation practice and G4's disciplinary practice both operate regardless of which title the presiding pastor holds); fails Formation (nothing in this world's own record suggests the title itself, as opposed to the office-holder's own actions, shaped participants). It remains a genuine, correctly-flagged lexicon-level fact (Doc_03 Cluster 4), but a title is not an organizing force, and this document does not manufacture one from it.
- **D-B "Reformation" (the term itself) → not a gravity.** Doc_03 §0 and Cluster 6 already flag this as the single highest-distortion-risk term in this world's own lexicon precisely because participants already feel they understand it. Tested against the Framework's own governing line — "a topic recurs, a gravity organizes" — this candidate fails cleanly: it is this world's own retrospective name for the whole ecology (G1 through G4 collectively), not a distinct force any of them, or the ecology as a whole, organizes *around*. Running gravity tests on a name for the phenomenon under study would test the label against itself, not the ecology against the label. Carried forward to Doc_06 as a Tier 3, DR-tagged lexicon entry (per Doc_03), not as a gravity candidate.

### 3.1 G1 — Sovereignty of God / Predestination and Election → **PRIMARY**

- **Repetition — PASS (strong), with a correction to this world's own prior characterization.** Doc_01 §1 (Core Identity: "a theology in which predestination carries load-bearing doctrinal weight"); Doc_02 §1, §8; Doc_03 Cluster 1 (Predestination, Election, Providence). Doc_01 §5 and Doc_02 §2 characterize Zwingli's own treatment as present only "in seed form," thinner and less systematic than Calvin's. **This document's own closer check against the actual vendored text sharpens, without contradicting, that finding:** the Sixty-Seven Articles (1523) themselves contain no predestination or election language at all — confirmed by direct search, zero occurrences in the cited range (`zwingli_selected-works_jackson1901.txt`, lines 4485–4700) — so "seed form" is accurate for Zwingli's own *founding* document specifically. But Zwingli's own later *Refutation of the Tricks of the Baptists* (1527, within the same volume's own row-7 "collective bucket," previously unitemized per Doc_01 §8 item 6) contains a sustained, independent treatment: a dedicated section headed "On Election" (lines 9591–9762) that works through Romans 8–9 in Zwingli's own words to argue election precedes and grounds calling, faith, and justification — "God's election, predestination or marking out, calling, [justification]... you will ever say right" (line 9678). This is a real, if later, first-person Zurich-strand exposition, not merely "seed form" carried by inference from Calvin's own doctrine. Doc_05/Doc_06 should carry this sharpened finding forward rather than repeat Doc_01/Doc_02's own "seed form" characterization unqualified.
- **Dependency — PASS (strong).** G3's own scriptural method is the tool both Zwingli's 1527 argument and Calvin's Institutes use to derive this doctrine; the Second Helvetic Confession's own Chapter X and the Heidelberg Catechism's own (Contested-confidence, per Doc_02 §8) treatment both depend on this conviction already being settled Reformed teaching by the time they are written.
- **Formation — PASS (strong).** Doc_01 §1 names this as load-bearing doctrine, not peripheral belief; the Second Helvetic Confession's own Chapter X gives it a distinctly pastoral, formation-facing register — "Let... ### 3.5 The Two Tensional Gravities

A Tensional Gravity requires two genuinely distinct poles with real institutional or textual separation. Two qualify on this world's own record.

- **T1 — Council-Led Civic Authority (Zurich) vs. Consistorial Independence from Civil Control (Geneva).** Poles: (a) Zurich's own reform, enacted and sustained throughout the entire 1519–1650 window by the city council acting on the outcome of public clerical disputation — no distinct ecclesiastical court independent of the council is attested in this world's own vendored record for Zurich; (b) Geneva's 1541 Ecclesiastical Ordinances creating the Consistory as a body Calvin fought to keep independent of civil-council control, a fight "substantially resolved in Calvin's favor" only by 1555 (Doc_01 §2, the Perrinist crisis). **Institutional separation is real and, per Doc_01 §4's own explicit finding, permanent within this world's own window:** "this did **not** converge within the window on Zurich's own side: Zurich's council-led governance persisted throughout 1519–1650, while Geneva's Consistory is a genuinely distinct disciplinary institution... The wider Reformed world's later convergence... is a fact about this world's own descendants, not about Zurich and Geneva as they actually stood within this window" (Doc_01 §4, quoted in full). **Tests:** Repetition PASS (attested at both cities across the whole window); Persistence PASS, and unusually strong for a Tensional gravity — most tensions resolve or are superseded; this one is named by Doc_01 itself as *not* resolving within this world's own construction window; Interaction PASS (directly qualifies G4, §3.4 above, and is the specific mechanism-level expression of G3's own strand-specific enactment difference, §3.3). **Confidence:** each pole is independently **Documented** on Doc_01's own already-confirmed terms (§4, §5); the characterization of the divergence as "permanent within-window non-convergence" is Doc_01's own explicit finding, not this document's own synthesis, a stronger evidentiary footing than a Tensional gravity's characterization typically rests on. **Forces-connection.** Cell 1B (Zurich's own council-based reform mechanism from the outset; Geneva's distinct 1541 Ordinances). Cell 2B (the Perrinist crisis, 1555, the direct internal-ongoing episode testing Geneva's own pole). This tension is not resolved by any Ending/Transforming force within the window (Doc_01 §6 Cell 3A/3B) — it persists to the window's own administrative close.
- **T2 — Zwingli's Own "Remembrance" Reading of the Supper vs. the Negotiated Spiritual-Presence Consensus.** Poles: (a) Zwingli's own founding statement, Sixty-Seven Articles Art. XVIII (1523): the Mass "is not a sacrifice, but is a remembrance of the sacrifice and assurance of the salvation which Christ has given us" — Zwingli's own word, directly quoted, not a hostile or later characterization of his position; (b) the Consensus Tigurinus's own negotiated formula (1549), jointly agreed by Bullinger's Zurich and Calvin's Geneva, that "though we distinguish, as we ought, between the signs and the things signified, yet we do not disjoin the reality from the signs" (9th Head of Agreement, verified verbatim, Source Registry row 5) — language that affirms more than a bare remembrance while still refusing corporeal presence. **Institutional/textual separation is real:** these are two distinct, dated, directly-quoted texts (1523 and 1549), not an abstract inconsistency read into one figure's thought. **Tests:** Repetition PASS (both texts are load-bearing primary sources in this world's own corpus, Registry rows 6 and 5); Dependency PASS (G2's own mature, post-1549 form is specifically what resolves this tension, and cannot be understood without both poles on the record); Interaction PASS (directly reshapes G2, §3.2 above). **Confidence:** both poles are independently **Documented** — each is a direct, verbatim quotation from a vendored primary text, not a paraphrase or hostile report. **The degree to which the Consensus's own formula "softens" or "modifies" Zwingli's own original emphasis, as opposed to simply restating it in fuller language, is this document's own interpretive judgment, not an inherited finding — the same correction Doc_03 Round 1 review already required of Doc_03 itself** (Doc_03's Memorial/Commemoration entry originally, and wrongly, attributed this exact characterization to Doc_02 §8, which contains no such finding; corrected there to state plainly that this is the builder's own open judgment call). This document adopts the same discipline: the *existence* of two distinct formulations, textually verified, is Documented; *whether* the later formula meaningfully modifies or merely elaborates the earlier one is not independently adjudicated here and is carried forward, tagged, to Doc_06 (matching Doc_03 §4's own CT-candidate handling for the closely related Sign and the Thing Signified contest). **Forces-connection.** Cell 1A/1B (Zwingli's own founding 1523 refusal, part of Zurich's own earliest reforming moment). Cell 2B (the Consensus Tigurinus, 1549, the direct sustained-internal force that produces the negotiated resolution). Spans both cells, the same shape T1 above and G5's own T1 in this project's Donatism precedent both take — part of what marks this as a genuine Tensional gravity rather than a single-episode inconsistency.

## 5. Cross-Strand Gravity Testing (Article 21)

Doc_01 §4/§5 confirmed this world **genuinely two-stranded**, so this section runs Article 21's own actual test — "where strands exist, convergence across them is a test of a gravity's centrality" — on every confirmed candidate, rather than declaring a substitute discipline (contrast this project's own strand-singular precedent, Donatism's Cross-Voice Test, which does not apply here because a real second strand, not merely a transmission asymmetry, exists).

**Result, applied to each confirmed gravity:**
- **G1 (Sovereignty of God/Predestination)** passes cross-strand with the qualification already stated at §3.1: the *core conviction* is independently attested in both strands' own voice (Zwingli's 1527 exposition; the Second Helvetic Confession's own Chapter X), not merely asserted of Zwingli by inference from Calvin's corpus. The *specific systematized form* remains Calvin/Beza-concentrated. This is a genuine convergence, not a manufactured one — the Second Helvetic Confession's own more pastoral, Christocentric register (§3.1) shows Zurich's own strand did not simply adopt Geneva's doctrine wholesale but restated the same core conviction in its own idiom, which is itself evidence of real cross-strand centrality rather than one-directional influence.
- **G2 (Spiritual Presence/Rejection of Corporeal Mediation)** passes cross-strand definitionally and evidentially: the Consensus Tigurinus is the one document in this world's entire corpus literally co-authored across the strand boundary (Doc_02 §2), and both strands' own founding/mature texts independently reject the Mass's sacrificial character before 1549. The qualification is temporal, not cross-strand in the ordinary sense — pre-1549, the two strands' own specific positive formulations differ (T2); post-1549, they converge on a jointly-negotiated text.
- **G3 (Scripture as Sole Authority)** passes cross-strand most cleanly of the three Primaries: the same underlying conviction is independently enacted through two different, strand-specific institutional mechanisms (Disputation at Zurich; catechesis-plus-Consistory at Geneva). Per Article 21's own logic, this is a *stronger*, not weaker, convergence signal than if both strands had adopted an identical practice — it shows the conviction itself, not merely one strand's specific method, is this world's own irreducible core.
- **G4 (Consistorial Church Discipline)** **fails** a full cross-strand standard for its own most distinctive, formation-defining content. The *general doctrine* (lay elders exercising disciplinary censure alongside pastors) is cross-strand attested in the sense that nothing in Zurich's own record contradicts it, but no comparable Zurich-side *institution* is independently evidenced — Zurich's own disciplinary practice, to the extent this corpus shows one, runs through the council directly (G3's own domain). This is precisely the strand-bound gravity Article 21's own test exists to catch and correctly downgrade, and it is the direct evidentiary basis for this document's own Supporting, strand-scope-qualified classification (§3.4).
- **T1 and T2** are not tested for cross-strand convergence in the same sense as G1–G4, because the institutional/textual separation *is* the strand divergence itself in both cases — T1's own two poles are, respectively, Zurich's own authority structure and Geneva's own authority structure; T2's own two poles are Zwingli's own earlier Zurich-strand text and the later, jointly-negotiated bridging text. Testing them for "convergence" would test the tension against itself. Both are correctly classified Tensional rather than cross-strand Primary/Supporting precisely because they name a real, persistent divergence rather than a shared core.

**A note on what this test does and does not show.** This cross-strand test, run directly rather than substituted for, corroborates Doc_01 §5's own finding that this world's shared confessional core (predestination, sola scriptura, rejection of the Mass, and, after 1549, a formally agreed spiritual reading of the Supper) is real and substantial — three of this document's four confirmed candidates (G1, G2, G3) pass it cleanly or with a disclosed, non-disqualifying qualification, and the one candidate that fails it (G4) fails for a specific, evidentially-grounded reason (a genuine institutional asymmetry, not an oversight) rather than an even split across the board. This finding should not be read as resolving Doc_01's own disclosed Author Gravity concentration (Calvin's corpus remains roughly four times Zwingli's own vendored volume) — only as showing that the *specific gravities* classified Primary here are not artifacts of that volume asymmetry alone, since each has independent, direct textual grounding in Zurich's own strand as well as Geneva's.
