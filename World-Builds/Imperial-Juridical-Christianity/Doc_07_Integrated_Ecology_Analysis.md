## 2. Authority Ecology (integrated)

Doc_04 confirmed three Primary/Supporting-central gravities organizing this axis: Juridical Primacy-Claiming (Primary), Church-State Alliance and Its Limits (Primary), and Orthodoxy-Enforcement Through Imperial Power (Primary, cross-strand to Strands A/B only). Doc_05 §4 showed this world's three *strands* (not a one-to-one mapping onto the three gravities above — Candidate 1 spans Strands A and B, Candidate 2 spans all three) operating through three distinct organizational logics (apostolic-succession, imperial-proximity, sacramental-leverage). Doc_06's lexicon supplies the vocabulary layer connecting the gravities: *primatus* is Strand A's own name for the first gravity; *presbeia* is Strand B's rival name for the same underlying drive; *communio* is the actual enforcement *mechanism* — a participant who understands *communio* understands why *primatus* and *presbeia* are not merely competing opinions but competing claims on an enforceable, consequential status.

**Integration point.** Doc_06's Related-Terms graph is the direct evidence for how the gravity-defining vocabulary actually connects. *primatus* (Candidate 1's own vocabulary) and *homoios* (Candidate 3's own vocabulary) are **not directly linked** in that graph, despite Doc_04 finding the two gravities they respectively vocabularize mutually reinforcing at the classification level. Multiple indirect paths connect them — through *homoousios* (general Nicene confessional vocabulary, two steps, the shortest route, itself belonging to a third gravity, Candidate 5, not Candidate 1 or 3, and itself directly linked to both *primatus* and *homoios* in their own Related-Terms lists), and separately through *communio* and *haeresis* (enforcement-mechanism vocabulary, three steps) — and this document does not privilege one of these routes over the other as *the* mechanism connecting the two gravities. The verifiable, narrower finding is: this world's own lexicon shows no single-hop bridge between its two most institutionally central Primary-gravity terms, and every path between them enters *homoios* specifically through one of its own only two neighbors — general confessional vocabulary (*homoousios*) or institutional-enforcement vocabulary (*haeresis*) — never a direct edge to *homoios* itself, even though a longer route can still pass through other primacy-claiming vocabulary along the way (*presbeia*, for instance, sits on one valid three-hop path). That narrower, no-direct-edge finding, confirmed by direct enumeration of the current graph, is what this section asserts — and no more than that.

## 3. Boundary Ecology (integrated, with mandatory explicit forces analysis)

Per the Forces Framework's own instruction, boundary ecology is written as three movements: what this world was responding to, what pressed from outside, what fractured from within.

**What this world was responding to:** the prior era's persecution (Doc_01 §5) and the practical problem of empire-wide doctrinal coherence once Christianity became state-patronized — a problem this world's own actors did not inherit a ready solution for, since no prior formation world in this project's portfolio had faced state-backed doctrinal enforcement as a live, two-directional possibility (Doc_02 §7's own finding that the mechanism could serve either Nicene or Homoian ends).

**What pressed from outside:** Cell 2A's own recurring content (Doc_01 §5, Doc_04 Candidate 3) — imperial religious policy oscillation, concretely: Constantius II's and Valens's Homoian establishment, then Theodosius's Nicene re-establishment, then the Western Empire's own accelerating collapse visible within this world's window (Alaric, 410). Doc_06's *haeresis* entry shows this pressure operating at the vocabulary level too — the same term serves as the boundary-marker regardless of which confession currently holds imperial favor, meaning the boundary itself, not merely its content, is externally contingent throughout this world's own window.

**What fractured from within:** two distinct fractures, at two different points in this world's own window, not one collapsed into "the closing moment." The earlier fracture, bounded to Ambrose's own career (374–397, Doc_04 Candidate 4), is Doc_04's own corrected finding (Doc_04 §5) that Candidate 3 (Orthodoxy-Enforcement) holds cross-strand only to Strands A and B, with Strand C's own relationship to it being a *reshaping* one (demonstrating a non-state-channeled alternative) — evidence of an internal fracture in how this world's own actors understood the boundary-enforcement mechanism's own necessity, decades before the world's own close. The second, later, better-attested fracture is Strand A/B's own collision at Chalcedon (451; Canon 28, Leo's rejection) — genuinely at the closing moment — where Doc_06's *Nea Rhōmē* and *presbeia* entries, read together, show this fracture was not a late surprise but the culmination of a boundary-question (whose rank rests on what ground) this world's own vocabulary had been carrying unresolved since at least 381.

**Integration point:** the same three-strand structure Doc_01 found for *authority* (§2 above) reappears here for *boundary*, but with a different alignment — Strand C, marginal to the authority contest at world level (Doc_04, Supporting only), is central to the boundary-fracture picture (it is the strand that demonstrates the boundary-enforcement mechanism's own contingency). A lens-by-lens reading would miss this; only comparing Authority Ecology's own strand-weighting against Boundary Ecology's own strand-weighting surfaces it.

## 4. Formation Logic (integrated)

Doc_05 §6 already established this world's own formation logic in full — standing-acquisition, not belief-formation, transferable from civil administrative competence (Ambrose's own case, carefully bounded per Doc_05's own Round 1 correction against over-generalizing from one atypical figure), and already named the culminating act as producing a document that binds (Julius's letter, Damasus's decretals, Leo's Tome). This integration does not claim that finding as new — it did not need restating. The genuinely incremental piece Doc_06 adds is narrower still: the *Tomus* entry's own single-instance risk flag (Doc_03, Doc_06) applies specifically to the *Tome* as its own document genre, not to "a binding document" generally — Doc_05 §6 already named three distinct binding-document instances across this world's record (Julius's letter, Damasus's decretals, Leo's Tome), and this caution does not extend to all three. What is genuinely single-instance is narrower: this world's own record shows the *Tome* genre specifically clearly enough to name it, but only one fully-realized example (Leo's own) of a bishop's formation culminating in one. This integration's own contribution is naming that narrower thinness as a caution carried forward, not treating Leo's Tome as more representative of the Tome genre than the single-instance evidence supports — a caution, not a new mechanism, and not a claim that this world's own binding-document formation logic generally rests on one instance.

## 5. Memory-and-Interpretive Ecology (integrated)

Doc_05 §7 and §8 describe memory-construction (Damasus's epigraphy, Eusebius's historiography) and interpretive habit (Petrine-text reading, conciliar-precedent reading) separately, each in its own terms; reading them as operating by the *same* underlying logic — both are acts of shaping what counts as already-established, in the past, so that a present claim can rest on it rather than argue from nothing — is this integration's own synthesis, not a restatement of a unification Doc_05 itself already made. Doc_06's *martyrium* entry, deliberately scoped narrower than a fuller devotional treatment (per its own Do-Not-Retrieve-When line, which cross-references the Step 0 Conclusion and Doc_02 §6 without naming a specific other world), confirms this at the lexicon level: this world's own vocabulary for its own dead does not develop independent devotional richness the way a different world's own lexicon might — it develops specifically in service of the memory-construction project this section already identifies above. Interpretive habit and memory-construction are, in this world, not two adjacent practices but one practice serving one end, visible only once the lexicon's own comparative thinness (relative to what a fuller-devotion world's lexicon would show) is read alongside Doc_05's own ecological finding.

## 7. Integrative Observation

Read across the five lenses, a real pattern recurs: this world's three strands differ not only in their claimed *ground* of authority (Doc_01 §4 — apostolic succession, imperial proximity, sacramental leverage) but in whether that ground generates a *citable, precedent-binding document genre*. Strand A's decretals and Tomes, and Strand B's conciliar canons, are each designed to be invoked by later claimants as binding precedent — Nicaea's own canons are cited at every subsequent council in this world's window. Strand C does produce real, transmitted documents — Ambrose's letters (Epistula 51, the Callinicum correspondence) and the *Sermo contra Auxentium*, the latter's own central formula elevated to Tier 1 vocabulary in Doc_06 — but those documents are occasional, addressed to one immediate crisis, and this world's own record shows no later actor citing them as binding precedent the way conciliar canons and Roman decretals are cited (Doc_01 §4's own finding that "no later actor in this window asserts primacy on Ambrosian sacramental-leverage grounds specifically" is direct, independent support for this).

This asymmetry is not offered here as an independent or deeper cause of Strand C's own narrower legacy, competing with what Doc_01 §6 already found (its claim was "not a see's claim to rank at all," and Doc_04 Candidate 4 found it temporally bounded to Ambrose's own career, 374–397) — it is that same finding's own downstream consequence at the level of documentary genre: a claim never grounded in an inheritable office or lineage correspondingly never generates the citable-precedent instrument that office-and-lineage claims produce and depend on. Doc_05 §10 makes no independent finding on this point and is not cited as a second, corroborating source — the citation is to Doc_01 §6 alone. The integration is genuine and narrower than first claimed: asking the same question (does this strand's documentary output function as citable precedent for later claimants?) of all three strands at once surfaces a real, if modest, connective thread between Doc_01's own strand-grounding finding and Doc_06's own lexicon-tier choices, not a wholly new causal story standing apart from what those documents already established.

## 8. Cross-Lens Synthesis

Authority (§2), Boundary (§3), Formation (§4), Memory-and-Interpretation (§5), and Worship-and-Affective Ecology (§6) are not five independent findings about this world; §3–§6 connect through the structure §7 names — a world organized around the production and contest of *citable, precedent-binding documentation* as one of the actual mechanisms of authority, with Strand C standing out specifically for producing real documents that never functioned as citable precedent for later claimants, not for producing no documents at all. §4's Tomus-based single-instance caution connects to this same documentary theme independently, which is a modest confirmation that §7's observation is tracking something real in the ecology. §2 stands somewhat apart from this particular thread — its own verified finding (no direct lexicon-level link between Candidate 1's and Candidate 3's own vocabulary) is a real integration point about this world's own gravity-to-vocabulary relationship generally, not itself part of the documentation-genre pattern §7 names, and this document does not force a connection between them that the evidence does not support. This synthesis does not reopen or contradict any confirmed gravity, strand finding, or lexicon tier classification from Doc_01–06. This document's own revision history — three passes at §2 before a verified, appropriately modest claim survived independent checking — is left visible here rather than smoothed over, since it is itself the clearest illustration in this build so far of why every specific claim about a source document has to be checked against that document directly, not recalled or asserted with confidence.
