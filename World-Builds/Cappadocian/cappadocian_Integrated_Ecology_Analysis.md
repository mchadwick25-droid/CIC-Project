## Section 2 — Integration Lenses

### 2A — Emotional Ecology [REQUIRED]

**What this lens reveals:** that this world's affective life is structured by a single discipline applied at two poles — the same reverence that governs what may be *said* of God governs what may be *felt* before death and loss: exactness without presumption, fullness without despair.

**Dominant emotional register:** doxological awe with tender grief. The Tier 1 vocabulary carries the pair: akatalēpsia as a felt state, not just a doctrine — "every word true, no word sufficient" is an emotional posture before it is an epistemology (Doc_06 §2; Doc_05 §7); and penthos/paraklēsis as the era's densest genre-cluster — funeral orations, deathbed dialogue, consolation letters, the infant-death treatise (Doc_02 §3; Doc_06 §28). Between the poles: festal joy (the panēgyris register, Doc_06 §13), the ache of hēsychia (longing as a settled state — Doc_06 §10), and friendship's extravagant warmth and documented wounds (Doc_05 §7).

**Emotionally dangerous states:** presumption before God above all — the confident definer is this world's emblematic spiritual disaster (Eunomius as cautionary figure: cleverness without trembling — Doc_06 §16); asebeia in its affective form, the unbowed tongue. Then the social passions the world's own homilies anatomize by name: phthonos (envy — "the diabolical sin," its own homily, Doc_06 §35), philotimia (ambition — the clergy's disease, the synod-weariness on the record, Doc_06 §36), orgē (anger), and pleonexia felt as the deadness that can watch famine at the door (Doc_06 §24). And despair in grief — refused as firmly as tears are permitted (Doc_05 §7).

**Emotional arc of formation:** entry — the warmth of the household's faith (psalms at the lamp, the Forty's story on their night — Doc_05 §§1–2, with the visibility caveat standing); middle — the affections schooled by repetition (psalmody until the psalms interpret the singer; feasts training gladness; fasts training hunger's honesty — Doc_05 §3); the formed state — awe that deepens as knowledge grows (the more truly you speak, the more the ground goes — Doc_05 §7's inhabited rendering of the akatalēpsia literature), desire that grows rather than concludes (theōsis' horizon — Doc_06 §9), tears fully human at the graveside inside a hope argued and held (the deathbed dialogue's whole method — Doc_02 §3).

**Gravities most visible in the emotional texture:** Primary 1 (the awe-pole is its affect), Tensional 8 (hēsychia's ache), Tensional 9 (the anxiety of how much may be said — reserve as a *felt* burden), Primary 2 (the famine homilies' moral heat; compassion as commanded feeling).

**What Emotional Ecology reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** the world's two most distinctive emotional products — trembling precision and grieving hope — are the *same trained capacity*: holding fullness and limit together without letting either cancel the other. A person formed here can say "true, and not enough" about a word for God and about a deathbed hope in the same breath. That is one skill, not two.

### 2B — Philosophical Ecology [REQUIRED]

**What this lens reveals:** the ontology beneath the affects — a two-term structure (Uncreated/created, with nothing between) whose consequences organize everything the emotional lens found.

**Fundamental ontological assumptions:** reality divides once and absolutely: the Uncreated (Father, Son, Holy Spirit — one ousia) and everything made (Doc_06 §1). There is no ladder of intermediate divinities — which is exactly what the era's fight was about: an almost-divine Son or a creaturely Spirit would populate the forbidden middle (Doc_04 §3.1 Candidate 1). The creature-Creator interval (diastēma — Doc_06 §33, Nyssen-weighted and flagged) cannot be crossed from below; it can be *inhabited from above* — God's workings (energeiai) reach us, and participation (theōsis) is real without ever abolishing the gap (Doc_06 §§9, 15). Creation is good, time-bound, and moving; evil has no substance of its own (shared patristic inheritance, carried here without the Latin world's anti-Manichaean scar tissue).

**Understanding of the human person:** the eikōn — a made image of the Unmade (Doc_06 §8): social by constitution (koinōnikon zōon — Doc_06 §6's anthropology), royal in dignity down to the leper and the slave (the image-argument's outward edge — Doc_06 §§7–8), wounded by disordered passion (askēsis' diagnosis — Doc_06 §§21, 27), and *unbounded in calling* — made for likeness to the Limitless, therefore never finished (Doc_06 §§9, 25). The person's fundamental problem is disorder and presumption; the fundamental possibility is growth without end.

**Temporal assumptions:** history runs from creation toward a consummation the martyrs already touch (anastasis — Doc_06 §29); the present is an era of contest in which the truth's public fortunes swing with reigns (Doc_04 Primary 3) while its substance does not move — a world that had watched formulas rise and fall at court learned to distinguish the age's weather from the age's direction (Doc_05 §§4, 8).

**How philosophical ecology grounds the emotional ecology:** the two-term ontology *is* the two-pole affect. Awe-with-precision is what it feels like to stand as a creature before the Uncreated with true words that cannot cross the interval; grief-with-hope is what it feels like to love creatures — who really die — inside a participation that really holds. The dangerous states are all attempts to abolish the interval: Eunomian definition (crossing it upward by logic), despair (denying the participation), pleonexia (the creature hoarding as if it were owner, not steward — Doc_06 §38's oikonomos re-description).

**Gravities most visible in the philosophical structure:** Primary 1 wholly; Primary 2 through the image-anthropology (the poor man's dignity is ontology, not sentiment); theōsis as the system's telos.

**What Philosophical Ecology reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** the world's social ethics and its Trinitarian dogma are one metaphysics applied in two directions — the same refusal of intermediate rank that keeps the Spirit fully God keeps the beggar fully image. A world that had allowed graded quasi-divinity above would have found it easier to allow graded quasi-humanity below. (This synthesis judgment is the build's own; flagged for external review as such.)

### 2C — Authority Structures [REQUIRED]

**What this lens reveals:** authority in this world is *inherited, weighed, and suffered-for* — a fabric of receptions (Scripture, creed, custom, founders, family elders) in which the decisive modern-sounding question "who decides?" was answered less by office than by endurance and recognized holiness.

**Primary sources of authority:** Scripture, supreme and pervasive (the corpora argue from it constantly — Doc_02 §1); the baptismal formula as the community's charter (Doc_06 §18 — an authority *everyone* had received, which is why the era argues from it); the creed as "the faith of the 318 fathers" (Doc_06 §30); the **unwritten customs** — argued explicitly as carriers of the faith (paradosis agraphos, Doc_06 §4: this world is unusual in having *theorized* custom's authority); the founders' lineage (the Thaumaturgan inheritance claimed as warrant — Doc_02 §§1.6, 3); recognized holiness (the ascetic teacher's weight — Eustathius' early authority over the movement, Documented and later ruptured; Macrina as "the Teacher" within her community's memory as Gregory presents it — Doc_02 §1.4); the episcopate with its two-level structure (metropolitan and chōrepiskopos — Doc_06 §31); councils near and far; and the household's elders (the grandmother's teaching cited by a metropolitan as his warrant of continuity — Doc_02 §1.1 at work level, with the family-visibility caveat).

**Transmission of authority:** through formation-lineages more than through institutional succession alone — household to household, teacher to disciple, and (the era's emerging pattern) the ascetic community as the episcopate's seminary (Doc_05 §4; Sterk-line synthesis, Dominant Modern Reconstruction). Ordination often *against the will* — the documented dragging of contemplatives to the chair (Doc_06 §10) — which the world read not as dysfunction but as a sign: authority sought is suspect (philotimia — Doc_06 §36); authority fled and accepted is trustworthy.

**Sites of contestation (carried, not resolved):** the court's claim to settle doctrine by formula and exile (two reigns of it — Doc_05 §§4, 8) against the church's claim to have received what no court may edit; the metropolitan structure's politics (the 372 division; Anthimus; Sasima — authority redrawn by provincial boundary, Doc_02 §2); the ascetic movement's charismatic authority against episcopal order (the Gangra line — Doc_01 §3); the distant great sees (letters West largely disappointed — Doc_05 §4); and within the winning side, the authority of *reserve* against the authority of *candor* (the oikonomia argument — Doc_06 §17).

**Authority and formation:** one is formed under received weights (formula, psalm, custom, elder) into someone who can weigh — and, distinctively here, into someone who may be *seized* by the community for office precisely because they did not want it. The formation product is the trustworthy conscript.

**Gravities most illuminated:** Primary 3 (the court-church contest is an authority-contest), Tensional 8 (flight as authority's paradoxical credential), Supporting 6 (the household lineage), Primary 1 (the formula as the one authority every register shares).

**What Authority Structures reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** the world's authority-fabric has no functioning center — the court is adversarial for most of the period, the great sees are far and disappointing, the councils are weather — and yet the world does not fragment, because its real authority-carriers (formula, custom, psalm, household, recognized holiness) are *distributed and portable*. This is why exile fails against it: you can banish a bishop, but not a doxology.

### 2D — Boundary Structures [REQUIRED — forces-integrated]

**What this lens reveals:** nearly every boundary this world maintains is a *near* boundary — drawn against kin, colleagues, and structures that share its Scriptures, its region, or its own ascetic conviction — and the forces analysis explains why: the pressures on this world arrived through the church's own institutions (court-backed formulas, rival bishops, a mentor turned opponent), not from an external persecutor.

**Membership logic:** baptism into the Threefold Name (the charter — Doc_06 §18), with the graded penitential system for failure (the canonical letters — Doc_02 §2) and the enrolled estates (kanonikai — Doc_06 §32) as recognized intensities of membership; the brotherhoods as a further intensity that must remain *inside* the church's fabric (the Gangra settlement — Doc_01 §3, Doc_06 §20).

**Relation to what is outside:** the **Homoian imperial church** — the defining boundary: same Scriptures, near formulas, the court behind it; the boundary is a preposition and a word, and it is enforced against this world by exile and endured (Doc_05 §9; forces 2A). The **Eunomians** — rebaptism marks the far edge (their re-baptizing declares this world's baptism void, and the canonical framework answers with graded reception rules — Doc_05 §9: boundary technology of unusual precision, Documented). The **radical ascetics** — the boundary defends household, marriage, and feast against renunciation's overreach (Gangra — Doc_01 §3): a boundary against the world's own left wing. The **old religion** — temples, charms, and a hostile emperor's schoolroom claim (Doc_05 §§1, 6, 9): a boundary fought over the calendar and the classics more than across battle lines. The **frontier and neighbor churches** — porous and pastoral (Armenian ordinations; Gothic relic-diplomacy — Doc_05 §9). **Jews** — textual engagement; thin as social evidence in this corpus and stated as thin (Doc_05 §9).

**Forces most visible in the boundary structures:** imperial religious policy (Cells 1A/2A of the forces work) made the doctrinal boundary a legal one — but in the *opposite direction* from the Latin world: there the church's boundary acquired the magistrate's sword; here the magistrate's sword was mostly on the other side, and the boundary was held by endurance (Doc_05 §8's named asymmetry). The ascetic ferment (2B) made the household itself a boundary zone (whose marriage, whose slaves, whose fast). The Eunomian confidence (2A/2B) made epistemology a boundary: what you claim to *know* of God locates you.

**Boundary contestation:** how much formula-agreement suffices for communion (the era's ecumenical letters wrestle receptions and rehabilitations — Documented arc); whether the reserved could be trusted (allies resenting Basil's economy — Doc_06 §17); whether the fierce renouncers were saints or wreckers (Gangra's line contested by those it censured — adversarial record, flagged).

**Boundary logic and formation:** members are formed to hold boundaries *exactly and without hate* (Doc_05 §9's inhabited close, grounded in the canonical graded-reception practice — a system that assumes returnees and provides for them). The formation product is precision without cruelty: the same person who will not yield a preposition is trained to receive back, by measured steps, almost anyone.

**What Boundary Structures reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** this world's boundaries and its epistemology are the same structure. The graded receptions (heretic/schismatic/parasynagogue), the graded penances, and the graded speech-registers (theologia/oikonomia) are one habit of mind: distinctions made finely so that mercy can be administered precisely. The world draws lines the way it uses words — as instruments of exactness in the service of communion, not weapons.

### 2E — Formation Logic [REQUIRED — synthesis lens, applied last]

**What this lens reveals (the coherence named):** this world forms people by **binding every register of life — word, household, wealth, learning, grief — into the right glorification of the God no mind can compass, so that precision of confession and mercy toward the image-bearer become one trained reverence (eusebeia).**

**The fundamental aim of formation:** the eusebēs — the reverent one — whose marks the world's own vocabulary supplies: the Name held exactly (Doc_06 §§1–3), the tongue schooled to reach without seizing (§§2, 15), the psalms by heart (§19), the door open and the surplus already the poor's (§§7, 24, 38), tears permitted and despair refused (§28), stillness longed for and the summons obeyed (§10), and the whole aimed at likeness without terminus (§9). The intensified form is the brother or sister of the common life; the ideal's public form is the trustworthy conscript (2C); but the baseline is the reverent householder — the world's formation documents address farmers and hoarders, not only monks (the famine homilies' audience — Doc_02 §1.1).

**The formation mechanism:** *reception before analysis, repetition before articulation.* The formula is said over you before you can weigh it (baptisma — §18); the psalms are sung until they interpret the singer (§19); the customs are kept because they were handed down, and their defense comes later, if ever (§4); the feasts train gladness and the fasts train honesty by the calendar's own repetition (§§13, 21); doctrine arrives, when it arrives, as the *explanation of what you already do* — the era's own argumentative order (from the font and the doxology to the dogma — §3). Analysis is honored (this is a world of formidable argument) but always downstream of worship and always disciplined by the interval (2B).

**The formation arc:** the household's lamp (psalms, stories of the ones who stood — with the visibility caveat standing) → hearer → baptism into the Name, commonly after adult delay the preachers scold (§18) → the long fidelity: psalmody, feasts, alms, discipline-if-needed (the canons' graded road back) → for some, the brotherhood's intensification (obedience, common goods, the guest-door) → for a conscripted few, office fled and accepted → and for all, an end that is not an end: the deathbed inside the resurrection's grammar, and the horizon of endless approach (§§28–29, 9, 25 with its [PV] flag).

**How the lenses integrate as a formation system:** the affects (2A) are the creature's right feelings before the ontology (2B); the authorities (2C) are the distributed carriers — formula, custom, psalm, elder — that install the ontology below the level of argument; the boundaries (2D) are the same exactness turned outward, graded so that mercy stays administrable; and the whole system's one enemy, met in every lens, is *presumption* — the tongue that defines God, the ambition that seeks the chair, the granary that defines ownership, the grief that defines the future. Formation here is the systematic training-out of presumption without the training-out of confidence: the formed person is bold (parrhēsia — §26) precisely because they have stopped grasping.

**Formation logic and confirmed gravities:** Primary 1 supplies the system's charter and its epistemic spine (the formula; the interval); Primary 2 supplies its social body (household → koinōnia → guest-door); Primary 3 is the weather it was all built to survive (authority distributed and portable because the center was hostile — 2C's finding); the Supporting gravities are its carriers (household lineage, festival calendar, converted paideia, patron-bishop); the Tensional gravities are its live interior arguments (stillness/summons; precision/reserve; renunciation/order; Athens/fishermen). The logic explains the gravities' centrality and the gravities explain the logic's shape, mutually, as the Framework requires.

**What no individual document showed:** that this world's formation system is best understood as **doxology institutionalized under hostile weather** — a whole way of life (household, brotherhood, poorhouse, festival, canon) built so that the right glorification of the incomprehensible God could survive when the court, the councils, and half the region's pulpits said otherwise. Its instruments are all *portable* (formula, psalm, custom, alms) because for two generations nothing fixed could be trusted to stay in friendly hands.

### 2F — Memory Structures [OPTIONAL — applied; evidence warrants]

**Why applied:** the martyr cult (Supporting Gravity 5) and the founding-lineage claim (the Thaumaturgan inheritance) make memory a distinct formation vehicle here, and the memory is unusually *fresh and familial* (Doc_04 §3.3).
**What was remembered and how:** the last persecutions' martyrs (the Forty above all — homilies at the shrines, relics in the estate chapel, the family's own confessor-generation named — Doc_02 §3, Doc_06 §12); the founding mission (the Thaumaturgan legend — Tier 3, its formation function real: regional custom warranted by apostolic-age memory — Doc_02 §3); and the world's own recent giants, memorialized almost immediately by their own family and friends (Or. 43; the Vita — the world writing its own memory as it ended, Doc_02 §§1.2–1.4).
**Formation function:** courage by rehearsal (the standing retold each feast); continuity-confidence (we received this from named hands); and — distinctive to this world — *family memory as church memory*: the grandmother who knew the persecution, the relics in the household shrine. The line between "our family's dead" and "the church's martyrs" is deliberately thin.
**Gravities illuminated:** Supporting 5 and 6 directly; Primary 1 through the "faith of the 318 fathers" as memorial formula (§30).
**Insight:** this world's memory is *lineal* — organized as inheritance down named generations, not as civic archive; which is also its transmission pattern (Doc_08), and the reason its record is a household's memory (Doc_04 §3.1 Candidate 5's caveat).

### 2G — Interpretive Ecology [OPTIONAL — applied briefly; distinctive enough to warrant]

**Why applied:** how this world reads is distinct enough from its Alexandrian inheritance to need naming (the blur-risk discipline).
**Hermeneutical assumptions:** Scripture is one, is about Christ, and is read *doxologically* — the exegetical fights are about which readings the church's worship and baptismal faith can bear (the Son's names; the Spirit-texts — Doc_05 §6); the literal is honored (the Hexaemeron's pointed restraint — Documented stance); the figural serves formation where formation needs it (the ascent-readings — Nyssen-weighted, flagged); and the reading of God's *names* is theorized (epinoia — §15): interpretation's limits are the interval's limits.
**Formation effect:** hearers learn that words — Scripture's and the church's — are trustworthy instruments that do not exhaust their object: the same lesson at the lectern as at the font.
**Gravities illuminated:** Primary 1; Supporting 4 (the schooled instrument in the pulpit's service).
**Insight:** exegesis here is doxology's servant, not a school's discipline — the decisive functional difference from Alexandria's reading-culture (Doc_04 §7), stated as this build's synthesis and flagged for the external reviewer with Alexandria expertise.

**What this lens reveals:** this world possessed real legal-canonical technology — graded, precise, genuinely administered. **The world's own legal instruments:** Basil's canonical letters to Amphilochius became canon law in the strict sense (Doc_02 §1.1's own wording) — penances graded by offense for murder, abortion, bigamy and remarriage, magic-use, grave-robbery, and apostasy under pressure (Doc_02 §2, Documented; the single best window this world offers on ordinary village sin). **Where one preacher's ethical claim and the world's legal practice visibly diverge — named at full strength, neither smoothed nor left standing as the world's own self-image:** Nyssen's homily on Ecclesiastes makes the era's sharpest surviving protest against slaveholding as such — "who can buy the image of God?" is Doc_02's own flagged paraphrase of an argument that is nonetheless real (Doc_02 §1.3, §6.3). This is one man's preached protest, not "the world's own ethics": Doc_09 #16 states the constraint this lens follows — "no documented in-world reception," to be told "never as the world's self-image." The world's actual legal instruments do not enact the protest, and do not resolve toward it: Basil's canons 40 and 42 regulate enslaved persons' marriages contracted without an owner's consent — a legal question about whose consent is required, not whether the institution itself may stand (Doc_02 §6.3); the Asketikon counsels amelioration for slaves who flee to the brotherhoods — instruction and, ordinarily, return to the owner, stated in this build without laundering (Doc_02 §6.3, Doc_05 §5); Gangra's canon 3 censures encouraging slaves to leave their masters, siding with the institution against the radical ascetic line (Doc_02 §6.3). Doc_02 §6.3 names two further traces this lens's first draft omitted, and they belong in the same paragraph rather than a separate one, since they complicate the finding without overturning it: Gregory of Nazianzus' own will manumits slaves he owned (Doc_02 §1.2, §6.3), and the Vita claims a freed maidservant among Macrina's community (Doc_02 §3, §6.3) — private acts of manumission by individuals, not a legal or institutional position that the practice itself should end. The honest statement of the finding is therefore narrower than "never adopted": no documented in-world reception of Nyssen's protest exists; private manumission by individuals coexists with it; and the world's canon law regulates the institution's terms without ever legislating against it. Absence of documented reception is not documented rejection — both must be held at the confidence the evidence actually supports, not upgraded toward the stronger claim.

**A second divergence, named at Doc_02 §6.1 and belonging here as much as there:** Basil's own canon 21 (within Ep. 199) grades adultery's penance unequally by sex — the wife punished, the husband not — and Basil himself states plainly in the canon that he cannot account for the double standard. This is the world's own legal instrument recording, on the record, that its author could not supply the custom's own rationale when asked. Whether that inability reflects felt discomfort, as opposed to simple silence about a custom he did not examine, is this lens's own reading of the gap — flagged here as an inference this lens draws, not a claim Doc_02 §6.1 itself makes about Basil's interior state.

**Ethical ground the sources state, at the confidence they actually support:** the poor bear the image of God, and the surplus belongs to them — "the bread you keep belongs to the hungry" is Doc_05 §5's own preached logic, carried there at work-level, not independently verified as a direct quotation, and carried at that same level here; Doc_05 §5's own *[Inhabited]* rendering goes further ("the man with two coats has stolen one of them"), which is this build's own inhabited extension of the preached logic, not the frame's own claim, and is named as such rather than folded in silently. Pleonexia (greed/grasping-for-more) is the vice the famine homilies anatomize (Doc_03 Cluster 3; Doc_06 §24) — a moral, not itself a legal, category in the sources, though this lens's own reading is that the surplus-belongs-to-the-poor logic functions as a legal-adjacent claim on property rather than mere exhortation to charity; that reading is this lens's synthesis, flagged as such. Julian's schoolroom edict (Doc_01 §4; Doc_02 §2) is met with the world's own argument that the classics belong to everyone, not to the emperor who claims the old gods — the argument itself is at Doc_05 §6 (work-level) and in the invectives Doc_02 §1.2 names (not §1.6, which covers Julian's own measures, not the world's reply to them); the fuller repossession argument (paideia as bee-flowers, taken and disciplined rather than surrendered) is Doc_06 §11 and Doc_03 Cluster 4 — 2B (Philosophical Ecology) contains no paideia content.

**Gravities most illuminated:** Primary 2 (the ascetic-philanthropic gravity) through the wealth-as-legal-claim synthesis and through the slavery divergence, which is this gravity's least comfortable evidence and belongs inside it, not filed separately; Primary 3 (the contested church under contested empire) through the imperial-law instruments (the communion law, Julian's Rescript) that make doctrine a legal fact. Tensional 9 (precision against reserve, per Doc_04 §4 and Doc_08 §5 — a documented argument about how much of the confession may be spoken aloud) is **not** extended to this lens's legal material — canon 21's self-scrutiny is a genuine finding of this lens, but it is not an instance of Tensional 9 as this world's build already defines it.

**What Ethical/Legal Ecology reveals that no other lens named directly:** Authority Structures (2C) found this world's authority distributed and portable; Boundary Structures (2D) found its boundaries graded for mercy's sake. This lens finds the same grading habit applied to persons rather than to doctrine or communion — and finds that habit stopping short exactly where a modern reader most wants it to continue. The world that could grade the wife's adultery against the husband's and say plainly "I cannot account for this" did not extend the same self-scrutiny to the ownership of persons, with one preached exception the surviving law never reflects. Article 20's duty is to carry this forward as this world's own record shows it: real ethical reach, a real legal system, and a real, named limit neither this world nor this document may quietly close.

**The single most important thing the forces-as-integration-lens reveals:** the world's formation system and its historical situation are one fact: **doxology became institution because it had to survive as a minority's possession.** The distinctive Cappadocian fusion — exact confession + portable practice + communal ascetic body + philanthropic institution — is what right worship builds when it cannot rely on the empire, the great sees, or the next reign.

## Section 4 — Cross-Lens Synthesis

**The central coherence:** This is the world where the unknowable God is confessed with exactness and served in the poor — where the same reverence disciplines the tongue at the doxology, the granary in the famine, and the heart at the graveside, and where that whole reverence had to live as a portable, household-carried, community-embodied possession through two reigns of hostile weather.

**What this world's distinctiveness consists of** (against neighbors in-period): against **Alexandria** — formation's axis is doxological-communal, not school-exegetical; the confession lives in a preposition sung and a formula baptized-in, not in a curriculum; the teacher-bishop tension is internal to vocations (the conscripted contemplative) rather than between institutions; and the signature vocabulary barely overlaps (Doc_04 §7). Against the **Desert** — askēsis is ordered toward the guest-door and the city (whose feet will you wash alone?); stillness is a longing that yields to summons, not an achieved geography. Against the **early Latin West** — the controversies are Trinitarian-pneumatological, not ecclesiological-soteriological; the church's relation to power runs pressed-not-pressing for nearly the whole span; and the martyr-memory is fresh and familial rather than transposed into a century-old civic cult.

**The most significant cross-lens insight:** every lens found the same structure — *fine distinction in the service of communion*: ousia/hypostasis (2B), graded penance and graded reception (2D), graded speech-registers (2C's reserve argument), graded belonging (household/congregation/brotherhood — 2E) — this world makes distinctions the way other worlds build walls or bridges: precisely, and almost always so that someone can be rightly included. Its exactness is hospitable. That conjunction — which modern instinct treats as a trade-off — is the world's signature.

**What the cross-lens synthesis gives the Representative:** a voice that can be precise without coldness and warm without vagueness — that treats a hard distinction as an act of care; that can hold "true, and not enough" about its own best words; that grieves openly inside hope; and that reflexively turns questions of God toward worship and questions of goods toward the door.

## Section 5 — Integrative Observation

**Integrative Observation:** In this world, reverence is one skill exercised at every scale: eusebeia is the trained refusal to grasp — the tongue does not grasp at God's essence (it says what was given and bows), the hand does not grasp at the surplus (it opens toward the image-bearer at the door), the heart does not grasp at office (it flees, and serves when seized), and grief does not grasp at the beloved (it weeps, and lets the resurrection keep them). The world's enemies in every register are the graspers — the definer, the hoarder, the ambitious, the despairing — and its formation is the long schooling of a creature to live open-handed before the God it cannot compass and the neighbor it cannot own: precision without possession, all the way down. Lose this and every practice in the world falls apart into unrelated pieces — dogmatics here, charity there, monasticism elsewhere; hold it and the doxology, the granary, the guest-door, and the deathbed are one motion.
