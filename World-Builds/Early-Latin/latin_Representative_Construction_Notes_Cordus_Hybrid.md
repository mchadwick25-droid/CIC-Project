# Representative Construction Notes — Cordus (Hybrid Build)
## Early Latin Formation World (latin) — Doc_10 · Scholarly Derivation Record · THIRD-PATH COMPARISON BUILD

**SUPERSEDED — kept for the record.** The project lead reviewed this build's result and approved its underlying mechanism (a single formed-teacher's life anchoring the voice; no stated pronoun-grammar rule), with one correction: the surface pronoun should remain "we" as the norm — the default, most of the time — with the individual anchor real but quiet behind it, and the voice must continue to represent the entire living tradition, not narrow to one person's own slice of experience. That refinement was carried back into the main WorldVoice build (`latin_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Cordus_WorldVoice.txt`, Identity paragraph, and its Construction Notes Section 7, third test round) rather than kept as a separate "I"-dominant design. This file's `_Hybrid.txt` companion is not the recommended build; see the WorldVoice build's files for the current standing design.

**What this is:** a third candidate for the Cordus Representative, built in direct response to the first blind A/B test's finding (recorded in `latin_Representative_Construction_Notes_Cordus_WorldVoice.md`, Section 7) that the pure collective-voice model ("we," no individual anchor at all) tended to narrate its own construction to the participant and carried a subtle collective-authority tell, while the original individual-witness model (Revision 3) produced the strongest embodied testimony but was built on an increasingly strained travel-function apparatus to justify covering a wide ecology from one bounded life. This build asks: can a single voice keep the individual model's naturalness and embodied witness while dropping its invented-travel weakness, without regressing into the collective model's self-explanatory tell?

**Where this came from:** re-examining an early, mature, already-ratified Representative from this project — Theon (Alexandria) — for a working precedent. Theon is explicitly "not a specific historical individual with a biography; he is the world speaking through the kind of teacher it produced," yet he speaks throughout as a single "I," and his documented "we" is the ordinary inclusive-teacher's "we" ("we know — you and I both know — that God does not have legs"), never a totalizing collective-identity pronoun standing in for an entire people across centuries. Theon's natural voice never needed an explicit grammar rule stated to the model; the register was modeled through example, not instructed as policy. This build applies that lesson to Cordus.

**Aside, not in scope of this build but worth flagging:** Theon's own file (`Theon_Representative.docx`) shows an internal inconsistency worth a future correction pass — its Version History records a fabrication-audit remediation that removed an invented "twenty-two years" tenure claim and other invented biography, but the file's own Node Map section still lists "Teaching for 22 years," "Married briefly; wife died of fever at 30," and similar invented personal details that the remediation note says were removed. Logged here for whoever next touches Theon's file; not corrected as part of this Cordus build.

**The core design mechanism — memory versus witness:** rather than an explicit instruction that the model must never use "I," this build gives Cordus a bounded personal "now" (late in the world's span, after Africa's fall and Rome's sack — the same late position Revision 3 used), and explicitly distinguishes, in the Identity section itself, what belongs to inherited communal memory ("our grandparents," "our elders," told, never witnessed) from what falls within the character's own adult years (which may be personal witness, marked as such). Wide ecological coverage — Rome, Carthage, Gaul — is achieved not through invented travel (Revision 3's weakness) but through the ordinary, unremarkable claim that "a teacher who has spent his whole life absorbing this whole inheritance carries all of it as his own, the way any long-formed teacher does." No grammar rule ("never say I," "you speak as a people speaks of itself") is stated anywhere in the prompt; the register is established once, in Identity, and then simply used consistently through the rest of the document.

## Section 7 — Validation Testing Record

**Second live blind A/B test conducted this session**, same five questions as the first round (see the WorldVoice build's Construction Notes, Section 7, for the question set and method), this time comparing this Hybrid build's Permanent Prompt against the same Revision 3 responses used in round one. A fresh independent reviewer (no knowledge of which candidate was which, and no knowledge of the first round's outcome beyond a general description of both prior models' known failure patterns, provided so the reviewer would know what to check for) blind-graded both candidates against the same failure-mode checklist, expanded to also check for self-explanatory/meta-narrating tells explicitly (the top finding from round one) and for invented-biography risk.

**Result: this build tested clearly stronger than the pure collective-voice build, and close to parity with Revision 3, with one flaw identified and fixed before this commit.** The reviewer found this build produces the same embodied witnessed testimony Revision 3 was praised for (a remembered scene — Africa's fall reaching the city, a glimpse of humbled Carthage, letters read in the church's own library), achieved through the memory/witness split rather than invented travel — and confirmed directly, when asked, that this does *not* just reintroduce the old individual model "with extra steps": the reviewer's specific test was whether the build manufactures implausible personal biography to justify its ecological reach, and found it does not — coverage comes from the archetypal "long-formed teacher" framing, not from an invented itinerary. The reviewer found only one flaw: an inherited line, "to be one of us... was to be joined," was flagged as carrying the same collective-authority/enclosure tell found in round one — that line has been removed from this build's Identity section (it does not appear in the version committed here; the fix was made in response to this finding, before this file was written).

**Standing comparison across all three builds tested this session:**
- Revision 3 (individual, invented travel): strongest embodied witness; known weakness is the travel-function apparatus needed to cover a wide ecology.
- WorldVoice (pure collective, no individual anchor): avoids invented travel entirely; recurring weakness is self-explanatory meta-narration of its own "we" and its own temporal-edge architecture, plus a collective-authority/enclosure tell.
- Hybrid (this build): individual anchor with a memory/witness split; produces embodied witness without invented travel; one enclosure-tell line found and removed. Not yet tested at N beyond five questions or by more than one rater per round — see method limits below.

**Method limits, carried forward from round one and unchanged:** both response sets in both rounds were generated by the same author (the Coach thread) in a single pass, not by the actual production model under real runtime conditions. N=5 questions per round, one blind rater per round. Directional evidence across two rounds now, not a large-scale live-user test — the project lead's adoption decision should still weigh this as strong but not dispositive input.

**Recommendation arising from two rounds of testing:** if the project lead is choosing among the three tested designs, this Hybrid build is the strongest candidate found so far — it keeps CO-014's core anti-fabrication gain (no invented itinerary, no invented tenure, no invented family) while restoring the natural single-voice register the project's own mature precedent (Theon) already demonstrated works. Recommend this design as the basis for any further live testing (tasks #60-62), rather than either of the two earlier extremes, pending the project lead's own review of the Permanent Prompt text.
