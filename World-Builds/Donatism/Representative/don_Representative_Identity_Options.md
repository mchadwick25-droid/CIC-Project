## 0. The governing constraint

Every sibling world that has reached this step has deliberately avoided naming its Representative after a real, documented figure already present in that world's own sources: Alexandria named its Representative Theon, not Origen or a documented catechist; Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary explicitly considered naming the Representative "Jerome" (Option A of its own preliminary-decision document), then the project lead proposed and adopted a fifth option instead — a period-typical but non-documented figure, "Albina," precisely so the Representative would not collapse into one person's own biography.

Donatism's own Source Registry names its Donatist voices with unusual precision for a hostile-mediated record: **Petilian** of Constantina (row 12, this world's fullest surviving primary voice in the vendored corpus), **Donatus** (the movement's founding namesake, consecrated *c.* 313), **Majorinus**, **Parmenian**, **Primian**, **Maximian** the deacon, **Cresconius**, **Lucilla** (row 30), **Tyconius**, **Marculus** the martyred priest (row 19), **Macrobius** (author of the letter behind the *Passio Isaac et Maximiani*, row 20), and **Honoratus**, bishop of Sicilibba, named in row 50's own sermon. None of these names is eligible for the Representative. Every option below pairs a role-label genuinely typical of this world with a constructed name — checked against the Source Registry and not found there — carrying no invented biography beyond the role itself.

## 1. What this world's own record actually lets a voice draw on

Doc_04 confirms four Primary gravities (**G1** Ministerial Purity/Traditor-Free Sacramental Validity; **G2** Rebaptism as Boundary-Marking; **G3** Martyr-Cult Identity/"Church of the Martyrs"; **G5** Refusal of Imperial/State Religious Legitimacy), two Supporting (**G4** Parallel Institutional Hierarchy; **D-A** Circumcellion/*Agonistici*, scope-qualified to the Numidian regional sub-ecology only — "not confirmed ecology-wide"), and two Tensional (**T1** Principled Refusal vs. Pragmatic Recourse to Imperial Power; **T2** Purity-Rigor vs. Institutional Reception, the Maximianist precedent). Doc_01 §4 finds this world **strand-singular** across its whole 311/312–439 window — one continuous pattern, no second strand — and Doc_01 §2 names Carthage, Numidia, and Cirta (Petilian's own see) as its geographic centers, with participation running "from bishops and clergy of the parallel hierarchy down to a genuinely broad lay base," since Donatism was, for substantial periods and regions, the numerically dominant church, not an elite minority current.

Doc_04 §5's Cross-Voice Test and Doc_05 §8's Proportionality Assessment both name the same structural fact from different angles: nearly all textual evidence passes through the hostile, eventually-victorious Caecilianist/Catholic party's own literature, and the divergence "is not elite-versus-majority; it is hostile-corpus volume versus a genuinely, numerically dominant population's own voicelessness." World Profile Section 8 (Honest Limits) states plainly that "the ordinary Numidian believer's own interior life" and "the emotional experience of doubt, fear, or quiet defection" are both genuine gaps in the record, not construction shortcuts — and prescribes how the Representative should hold that absence rather than fill it.

## 2. Three grounded options

### Option 1 — A Donatist bishop of the parallel hierarchy. Constructed name: **Restitutus**

*("Restitutus" — "the one restored" — a common, genuinely period-attested Latin cognomen, resonant with the movement's own restoration-of-purity logic without naming a specific documented office-holder.)*

**What it voices directly.** This role sits closest to where this world's own evidentiary record actually concentrates, and can voice all four Primary gravities from lived clerical experience: **G1** (a bishop is the office whose ordination-line purity the whole doctrine turns on — Petilian, row 12, is this world's own model for what a bishop's voice sounds like, "not independently checkable outside Augustine's own quotation" but licensed as this world's fullest surviving voice); **G2** (bishops administer rebaptism and are the addressees of the Council of Carthage 419's own canons on receiving Donatist clergy); **G3** (the *Passio Donati* sermon, row 50, is proposed by Monceaux as preached by "Carthage's Donatist bishop, possibly Donatus the Great, and an eyewitness," and its own narrated martyrs are themselves clergy — the bishop of Sicilibba wounded, the bishop of Advocata killed); **G5** (the documented turns to imperial machinery T1 names — the 313 petition to Constantine, the 361 petition to Julian — are episcopal-conciliar acts). It also directly reaches **G4** (the hierarchy is the office itself) and both Tensional gravities **T1**/**T2** (the Maximianist reception-without-reordination precedent is a conciliar, episcopal-level decision, per Doc_01 §4's own strand-determination discussion).

**Evidentiary richness.** The strongest base of the three options: row 12 (Petilian, the fullest surviving Donatist voice), row 50 (the sermon, plausibly episcopal per Monceaux), rows 19–20 (the two Passiones, whose named martyrs hold clerical office), and the institutional record Doc_04 §3.4 calls "Documented to an unusually high degree even by this world's own hostile-source standards" (bishop counts, rival consecrations, the 411 Conference's own 284-against-286 tally).

**What it struggles with.** This is also the most hostile-mediated of the three role-options in its *argumentative texture*, not its bare existence: Doc_04 flags G1's and G5's own specific argumentative shape as substantially Augustine-mediated even where the underlying doctrine is cross-voice attested, and this document does not smooth that divergence away. A bishop-voiced Representative also cannot, on this world's own record, speak with confidence to the ordinary believer's interior life (World Profile §8) — it would need construction-notes discipline to keep that gap honestly disclosed rather than papered over with plausible-sounding clerical omniscience.

**Trade-off against the other two.** Richest and best-attested option, but furthest from the "genuinely broad lay base" Doc_01 §2 says this world actually had — voicing this world chiefly through its clergy risks under-representing the numerically dominant membership Doc_05 §8 finds structurally voiceless, not marginal.

### Option 2 — A Numidian lay believer of a Donatist congregation. Constructed name: **Deodata**

*("Deodata" — "given by God," feminine — a period-plausible Latin Christian name following the same naming pattern as the real, common "Adeodatus"/"Deogratias," not a documented figure in this world's own record.)*

**What it voices directly.** Directly reaches **G2** (Doc_04 §3.2 itself calls rebaptism "the most concretely enacted, individually experienced marker of belonging this world's record documents" — the strongest fit of any gravity to an ordinary participant's own lived first-person experience) and **G3** (attending, and being formed by, the annual commemorative preaching the sermon itself describes — row 50's own *admonitio* names an "annual commemoration"; the Passiones report "numerous faithful," not only clergy, dying alongside the named bishops). Reaches **G5** at the level of lived consequence (living under recurring legal jeopardy as a rebaptized, non-compliant believer) rather than at the level of the specific documented episodes (petitions, conciliar acts), which belong to Option 1. This option directly answers Doc_01 §2's own finding that this world's participation "spans... down to a genuinely broad lay base," and gives the Representative's voice a defensible basis for gender diversity — row 30 documents Lucilla's and a second, unnamed woman's real historical role in this world's own founding and Maximianist-era disputes, though neither name is itself usable here.

**Evidentiary richness — genuinely thinner, and this must be disclosed plainly.** No row in the Source Registry preserves an ordinary layperson's own first-person voice the way row 12 preserves Petilian's or row 50 preserves a preacher's. World Profile Section 8 states directly that "the ordinary Numidian believer's own interior life" is "known to us mostly through what our opponents chose to argue against," and that the Representative "does not speak as one who knows their own interior life from inside." This option's own construction would draw on real, attested *practices* (rebaptism, attendance at martyr commemoration, living under imperial legal jeopardy) rather than a documented individual biography — which is exactly the kind of evidentiary base this project's own discipline treats as sufficient for a role-label, but it is a genuinely thinner base than Option 1's, and any construction-notes file built on it would need to name that thinness on every page it bears on, not once at the top.

**What it struggles with.** G1's specific doctrinal reasoning (the *traditor*-to-invalid-ordination-to-invalid-sacrament chain) and G4's institutional operation are both, on this world's own record, worked out at the clerical/conciliar level — a layperson can hold and live the conclusion with full conviction but has no documented basis in this world's sources for reconstructing the argument's own internal steps the way a bishop-voiced Representative could.

**Trade-off against the other two.** Best answers this world's own "numerically dominant, not elite minority" finding and gives the richest lived texture for G2 and G3 specifically, but trades away G1's and G4's own argumentative depth, and requires the most careful, continuous honesty about what a layperson's voice in this world's own record cannot support.

### Option 3 — A Numidian *agonistici*/Circumcellion-adjacent voice. Constructed name: **Marcianus**

*("Marcianus" — a common Latin cognomen across Roman North Africa, unattached to any specific figure in this world's own Source Registry.)*

**What it voices directly.** The most regionally and texturally distinctive option: reaches **G4** at its Numidian-regional strength (Doc_01 §2 names Numidia as "the region of Donatism's greatest institutional strength... and the origin region of... the Circumcellion/*agonistici* phenomenon") and is the direct object of **G5**'s own imperial legislative record — *Codex Theodosianus* 16.5.52, independently confirmed this build against the Mommsen & Meyer critical edition, assesses Circumcellions a distinct silver-pound fine "unlike every other listed rank." It ties, narrowly, to **T2** (Doc_01 §4 reports Circumcellion involvement in the Maximianist suppression).

**Evidentiary richness — the thinnest of the three, and disclosed plainly per this document's own instructions.** Doc_04 §3.5 rates D-A's own character component, at most, **"Dominant Modern Reconstruction"** — contested even among modern scholars, not settled fact — and finds this "the sharpest divergence" in the entire Gravity Discovery document, "across three tiers": bare existence is Documented (independent imperial legislative attestation); the *agonistici* self-designation term itself "reaches this record only through Augustine's own report of it"; and character, scale, and typical conduct are hostile-mediated and contested. Doc_04 §5's own Cross-Voice Test names D-A as "the one candidate that most clearly fails a strict cross-voice standard for anything beyond bare existence and self-designation: no Donatist-voiced or non-mediated text in this world's vendored corpus corroborates the group's character, scale, or typical conduct." D-A is also classified **Supporting for the Numidian sub-ecology specifically, not confirmed ecology-wide** — a Representative built on this role would be voicing a regional and evidentially contested slice of the world, not the ecology as a whole.

**What it struggles with.** Everything beyond bare existence and imperial-legal targeting. This option cannot, on this world's own record, be built with the same confidence any of the other two can bring to G1, G2, or G3 — its own defining "character" comes to us entirely through the party that fined and suppressed it. A Representative built here would need construction-notes handling closer to a disclosed reconstruction than a directly-attested voice, at every point beyond the fact that the fine existed and the group was named.

**Trade-off against the other two.** The most distinctive and regionally specific option, and the one most directly answering this world's own Numidian strength — but the thinnest evidentiary base by a wide margin, on a gravity this document's own governing document (Doc_04) explicitly declined to classify ecology-wide for exactly this reason.

## 3. Recommendation (for the project lead's own decision)

**Recommended: Option 1 — a Donatist bishop, constructed name Restitutus** — with Option 2 (Deodata) named as the genuine, considered alternative, not a runner-up dismissed in passing.

The reasoning follows the same discipline Alexandria's own Representative Identity Options document used for its own recommendation: voice the world from where its own evidence concentrates, and hold what that voice cannot reach as honest disclosure rather than invented texture. Donatism's own record concentrates hardest at the episcopal/clerical level — Petilian's own quoted words, the sermon's probable episcopal preacher, the martyred bishops of the Passiones, the conciliar record of the Maximianist affair — and a bishop-voiced Representative can draw on all four Primary gravities and both Tensional gravities directly, from where this world's own sources are strongest, while carrying World Profile Section 8's own honest-limits language for the ordinary believer's interior life as a stated, not smoothed-over, boundary of the role.

Option 2 remains a real and defensible choice: it answers Doc_01 §2's own "genuinely broad lay base" finding more directly than Option 1 does, and Doc_04 itself identifies G2 as the gravity best suited to exactly this kind of lived, individually-experienced first-person voice. If the project lead weighs representing this world's numerically dominant, structurally voiceless majority more heavily than this document does, Option 2 is the stronger fit, not a compromise choice.

Option 3 is offered for completeness and because a Numidian regional voice is a genuine possibility this world's own record raises — but this document does not recommend it as the primary Representative identity, given Doc_04's own explicit, repeated finding that D-A's character is contested even among reviewers and fails this world's own declared Cross-Voice Test for anything beyond bare existence.

This document does not decide among these options. Per `cic-build-cycle`'s own escalation categories, that decision belongs to the project lead alone, and Step 10 proper — the Part-Three Ecology Assessment, Formation Calibration, Voice Construction — begins only once name and role are fixed.
