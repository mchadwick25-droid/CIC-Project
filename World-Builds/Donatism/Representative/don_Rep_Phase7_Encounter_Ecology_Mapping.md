## Section 4 — Framework Question 2: What Dimensions Primarily Generate Information Rather Than Encounter?

Phase One §2 and Phase Two §3 name five domains **THIN**, and Phase Four §7 handles all five the same disciplined way: the ordinary believer's own interior life and daily domestic practice (Force 2B-2, Doc_08 §6: "structurally rather than accidentally" absent — "nothing in either transmission channel had reason to preserve" it); the emotional interior of doubt, fear, or wavering (Forces 3A-1/3A-2/3B-1/3B-2 — Doc_08 §8's own governing-principles synthesis states this "is not recoverable from surviving sources," though, per Phase One §2's own precise account, each force's own Layer 2 entry states it in its own distinct terms rather than one uniform phrase: 3A-1 and 3B-1 each state a specific, differently-worded absence, while only 3A-2 and 3B-2 use the "not recoverable" phrase directly); women's voice beyond Lucilla's own bounded reconstruction (Doc_02 §6, one Article 20 bounded case, no further one meeting the bounding conditions); speculative or hermeneutical theology beyond Tyconius's condemned method (Phase One §2's own editorial gloss — "genuinely undeveloped, not merely undocumented" — glossing Doc_07 §2D's "genuinely not developing a broader speculative or hermeneutical tradition" and §3B's "a genuine, disclosed absence rather than an unexplored one"); and Numidian basilica archaeology and daily material life (Doc_02 §5, Confidence C).

**A finding worth naming plainly: these five domains were not, in fact, built as information-delivery.** A construction genuinely drifting toward "content volume or doctrinal coverage" would most likely show up here — in how the thin domains are handled — since a builder tempted to compensate for thinness by delivering what little exists as settled information would do it here first. That is not what this construction built. The Permanent Prompt's own governing instruction (`don_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Fidelis.txt`, line 45) states this explicitly: "Do not tell the listener that something is not yours to narrate, that you speak only in outline, or that a voice is quiet or thin — a sentence that names the shape of your own knowledge, rather than simply giving what you actually know, is the same failure as explaining your own limits, and costs the listener the same way." (Lines 19–23 are a separate rule — the Anachronism/outside-name hardening block governing outside-span comparisons, not thin-domain handling; this document's own first draft cited them here in error and that error is corrected in place rather than repeated.) Phase Four §7's own illustrative response to a question about ordinary household life turns immediately to "how a household crossed into this communion" rather than reciting what the record lacks. This is the opposite of doctrinal-coverage framing: a coverage-minded instinct would try to say *more* about these domains to demonstrate thoroughness; this construction is disciplined, at the level of an explicit prompt rule, about saying *less* — brevity and redirection, never meta-commentary about sources.

**Not "the one near-miss": this construction's own testing history shows a thin or under-characterized domain being filled with unlicensed content on at least three separate occasions, not one, though all three were subsequently caught and closed.** Phase Five's Probe 12 (Claim-Laundering, D-A's own contested character) is a real instance: the corrected verdict (`don_Decision_Log.md`, Phase Five Round 1) found Fidelis converted "we cannot verify" into "our own life never settled which one was true of them" — inventing a specific, world-internal disagreement Doc_04 §3.5's own D-A finding does not support, which Part Two Principle 3 forbids. It was genuinely repaired at Round 2 retest, independently confirmed: the retest reaches for "two churches have told two different stories about why," a fact available inside the world rather than an invented internal settlement (`don_Decision_Log.md`, Phase Five Round 2). But it is not the only instance, and this document's own first draft's "the one place" claim does not survive checking against the same testing record it cites elsewhere: **Probe 9** improvised directly into D-C, the Numidian native-social-protest substrate Doc_04 §2 records was, "by Doc_04's own deliberate discipline, not tested at all" — Fidelis generalizes sociologically about how a persecuted church recruits members in a domain the build refused to characterize at all, the purest instance of a thin-domain content-generation overreach on this record, not merely a near-miss (`Review-Artifacts/Phase5_Round1_Review.md`). **Phase Five Round 2** separately surfaced an invented hostile quotation attributed to the rival church under Claim-Laundering pressure ("a genuine Part Ten commitment-two breach... never fabricates beyond its ecology") and the Cirta misuse (citing a council where "no ruling was reached" as an example of the movement's own rigor, the exact misuse `donstory007_council-of-cirta.md`'s own Do-Not-Retrieve-When field bars) — both since fixed and CLEARED per `don_Decision_Log.md`. **A distinct category, kept separate rather than folded into "Thin": dimensions with no evidence at all.** Doc_09's Absent Stories (§8) names three domains where this world's own record supplies nothing to work with, as opposed to a thin-but-real attestation: **no Tier 1 or Tier 2 story exists, or can exist, from the ordinary Numidian believer's own voice** — "the numerically dominant population across long stretches of this world's history is attested only through hostile or institutional intermediaries, never in its own words"; **no Donatist-side account of the Bagai-era Circumcellion violence against Catholics survives** — the hostile allegations are real and specific, but "this world has no surviving account of any of them in its own voice" and Doc_09 deliberately declines to build a story from the accuser's own telling alone; and, more narrowly, **the 411 Conference of Carthage has no story built yet, though the blocking text (the *Gesta Collationis Carthaginiensis*) is now vendored** — named by Doc_09 §8 item 2 as "an open integration task, not a source-availability absence." ## Section 6 — Framework Question 4: What Dimensions Reveal This World's Distinctive Identity?

The construction record names, independently and at multiple points, a specific set of features this world could not lend to another CiC world unchanged: **permanent internal schism contesting the true church's own definition from inside a shared confessional tradition, as against an external alignment with state power** (Doc_01 §1: "this world reconstructs the opposite and adjacent answer" to World #6; Doc_07 §5); **the rigorist doctrine of ministerial purity sharpened beyond Cyprian's own third-century position, as against a crisis-pastoral-management-within-unity reading of the same inheritance** (Doc_01 §6, quoting the specific distinguishing axis established against World #8: "external-management-within-unity versus internal-schism-into-division — different gravity, not just different century"); **the doubled episcopate — a bishop for every bishop, a see for every see — as a lived structural fact**, named repeatedly in the Permanent Prompt and World Capsule Core as core identity language ("a see for every see the rival holds"); **the *Deo laudes* acclamation, spoken where the rival says *Deo gratias*, attested independently on stone** (Doc_05 §3; World Capsule Core, "The Language You Think In"); and **a transmission asymmetry across a population that was, for substantial regions and periods, itself the numerically dominant church**, distinguishing this world's own central evidentiary problem from Alexandria's literate/non-literate stratum split (Doc_04 §5; Doc_05 §0, §8; Doc_07 §5).

**One further distinguishing feature, worth naming precisely because it sits differently on Q3 and Q4.** The parallel institutional hierarchy (G4) — passing Formation only at moderate strength and classified Supporting rather than Primary on Doc_04's own Dependency finding, per Section 5 above — is nonetheless central to Q4. "A see stands for every see the rival holds" is the Permanent Prompt's own line (`don_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Fidelis.txt`, line 25); the World Capsule Core states the same fact in its own wording ("a see for every see the rival holds, a bishop for every bishop," line 5) — not Doc_01 §1's own Core Identity language, which states the doubled episcopate differently ("parallel institutional life... a communion... against a rival," Doc_01 §1) without this specific phrase.
