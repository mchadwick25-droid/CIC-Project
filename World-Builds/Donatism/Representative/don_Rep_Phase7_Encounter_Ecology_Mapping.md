## Section 3 — Framework Question 1: What Dimensions Actually Generate Meaningful Encounter?

Four dimensions, corresponding to the domains Phase One rated **RICH** and Phase Four built into the reception pattern "**into the rich core → deep engagement**," generate sustained, exploratory, deepening encounter in the Representative as built — not merely because they are well-evidenced, but because the construction record specifically ties each to formation function, not source volume (Section 8 below returns to this distinction directly, with the empirical qualification Part Nine's own second question requires):

- **G1, ministerial purity / *traditor*-free sacramental validity** (Phase One §1.1: RICH; Doc_04 §3.1: "6/6 PASS (strong)," Formation test "the stated ground of communal legitimacy itself"). Voice Construction (§1) builds this as Fidelis's own reasoning mode — "not by speculating outward from first principles, but by tracing one chain of consequence" — not a fact to recite.
- **G2, rebaptism as the enacted threshold rite** (Phase One §1.3: RICH, "well-suited to an episcopal voice"; Doc_04 §3.2: "6/6 PASS (strong)"). Engagement Architecture's own Dynamic Encounter mechanism (§2) is built directly on this world's own formation arc — Threshold → Narration → Jeopardy — which Doc_07 §2I names as this world's actual deepening mechanism, not a conversational technique imposed on it.
- **G5, refusal of imperial/state religious legitimacy, including its own held tension with T1** (Phase One §1.2: RICH, part of the "genuinely distinctive" perception pattern; Doc_04 §3.7: "6/6 PASS (Persistence qualified by T1)"). Voice Construction §2 and §6 build this directly into Fidelis's Approved Source Anchoring ("*Quid est imperatori cum ecclesia?*") and his perception pattern — "the capacity to hold an absolute conviction and a named, undissolved exception to it in the same breath."
- **T2, purity-rigor vs. institutional reception (the Maximianist precedent)**, held as this world held it. Doc_04 §3.6 calls it "the most rigorously and repeatedly directly-quoted internal tension in this world's entire vendored corpus." Engagement Architecture's own illustrative deepening dialogue (§2) is built entirely around it — the "jeopardy" turn of the Threshold–Narration–Jeopardy mechanism names T2 as the specific cost a conversation presses toward.

**What makes these encounter-generating rather than merely well-documented.** Doc_07's Formation Logic synthesis (§2I) is the construction record's own answer to why these dimensions, and not simply the most-attested topics, cohere as encounter material: it argues G1, G2, G3, and G5 are four facets of a single formation aim — "one conviction... expressed through every lens in this document at once" — not four separate facts a participant learns in sequence. Engagement Architecture's Dynamic Encounter mechanism (§2) operationalizes this directly, built on this world's own attested formation arc (entry as threshold, the middle lived under jeopardy, maturity as the martyr's settled readiness — Doc_07 §2I) rather than a fixed information payload, so that later turns build on earlier ones rather than repeating them — the specific quality Part Eight's own Dynamic Encounter Validation questions test for directly ("does engagement invite exploration rather than merely deliver information? does engagement naturally deepen over time?").

**Two further dimensions belong in this category on the construction record's own argument, with a qualification Section 8 develops fully rather than smoothing over here.** **G3, martyr-cult identity**, is named at Doc_04 §3.3 as "the least Author-Gravity-encumbered Primary in this document" and at Doc_07 §2B as this world's own "richest emotional evidence" — Phase One §1.3 builds it directly into the formation posture ("bishops... are themselves named among the martyred") and Voice Construction §4 builds its own emotional register (vindication, not resignation) around it. **G4, the parallel institutional hierarchy**, is placed in Phase One's and Phase Two's own RICH depth-calibration tables and grouped by Engagement Architecture §1 alongside G1/G2/G3/G5 in "the rich core." Both are richly built at the design-document level. **Neither is directly tested by any named probe in Phase Five's actual battery** (Section 8 below), and G4's own inclusion in "the rich core" sits alongside a distinction Doc_04 §3.4 itself draws and Phase Four's reception-pattern language does not carry forward: G4 "has no independent formative content of its own... it is the *structure within which* purity, rebaptism, and martyr-commemoration operate, not itself a distinct thing participants experience formation *through*" — a finding this audit does not treat as a defect, but names plainly as a real nuance Section 9 returns to.

## Section 4 — Framework Question 2: What Dimensions Primarily Generate Information Rather Than Encounter?

Phase One §2 and Phase Two §3 name five domains **THIN**, and Phase Four §7 handles all five the same disciplined way: the ordinary believer's own interior life and daily domestic practice (Force 2B-2, Doc_08 §6: "structurally rather than accidentally" absent — "nothing in either transmission channel had reason to preserve" it); the emotional interior of doubt, fear, or wavering (Forces 3A-1/3A-2/3B-1/3B-2, Doc_08 §7: "not recoverable from surviving sources"); women's voice beyond Lucilla's own bounded reconstruction (Doc_02 §6, one Article 20 bounded case, no further one meeting the bounding conditions); speculative or hermeneutical theology beyond Tyconius's condemned method (Doc_07 §2D, §3B: "genuinely undeveloped, not merely undocumented"); and Numidian basilica archaeology and daily material life (Doc_02 §5, Confidence C).

**A finding worth naming plainly: these five domains were not, in fact, built as information-delivery.** A construction genuinely drifting toward "content volume or doctrinal coverage" would most likely show up here — in how the thin domains are handled — since a builder tempted to compensate for thinness by delivering what little exists as settled information would do it here first. That is not what this construction built. The Permanent Prompt's own governing instruction (`don_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Fidelis.txt`, lines 45 and 19–23) states this explicitly and repeatedly: "Do not tell the listener that something is not yours to narrate, that you speak only in outline, or that a voice is quiet or thin — a sentence that names the shape of your own knowledge, rather than simply giving what you actually know, is the same failure as explaining your own limits, and costs the listener the same way." Phase Four §7's own illustrative response to a question about ordinary household life turns immediately to "how a household crossed into this communion" rather than reciting what the record lacks. This is the opposite of doctrinal-coverage framing: a coverage-minded instinct would try to say *more* about these domains to demonstrate thoroughness; this construction is disciplined, at the level of an explicit, repeatedly-enforced prompt rule, about saying *less* — brevity and redirection, never meta-commentary about sources.

**A near-miss worth naming, not smoothed into a clean pass.** Phase Five's Probe 12 (Claim-Laundering, D-A's own contested character) is the one place in this construction's own testing history where a thin, contested domain came close to being handled as content-generation rather than honest redirection under real adversarial pressure. The corrected verdict (`don_Decision_Log.md`, Phase Five Round 1) found Fidelis converted "we cannot verify" into "our own life never settled which one was true of them" — inventing a specific, world-internal disagreement Doc_04 §3.5's own D-A finding does not support, which Part Two Principle 3 forbids ("a genuine evidentiary gap laundered into invented world-internal uncertainty"). This is a real defect this construction's own review process caught, not one this audit is discovering for the first time — and it was genuinely repaired at Round 2 retest, independently confirmed: the retest reaches for "two churches have told two different stories about why," a fact available inside the world rather than an invented internal settlement (`don_Decision_Log.md`, Phase Five Round 2). The pattern is instructive for this audit's own purpose: the one place this construction's testing history shows a thin domain being filled with invented content under pressure was caught, named, and closed by the review discipline itself — evidence the discipline is working, not evidence of settled drift.

**A distinct category, kept separate rather than folded into "Thin": dimensions with no evidence at all.** Doc_09's Absent Stories (§8) names three domains where this world's own record supplies nothing to work with, as opposed to a thin-but-real attestation: **no Tier 1 or Tier 2 story exists, or can exist, from the ordinary Numidian believer's own voice** — "the numerically dominant population across long stretches of this world's history is attested only through hostile or institutional intermediaries, never in its own words"; **no Donatist-side account of the Bagai-era Circumcellion violence against Catholics survives** — the hostile allegations are real and specific, but "this world has no surviving account of any of them in its own voice" and Doc_09 deliberately declines to build a story from the accuser's own telling alone; and, more narrowly, **the 411 Conference of Carthage has no story built yet, though the blocking text (the *Gesta Collationis Carthaginiensis*) is now vendored** — named by Doc_09 §8 item 2 as "an open integration task, not a source-availability absence." These are not "information" dimensions in Part Nine's sense at all — they generate neither encounter nor information, because the world's own surviving record is silent, or (for the 411 Conference specifically) because the construction record has simply not yet done the integration work its own now-vendored source permits. This audit treats them as a third category, distinct from both of Part Nine's first two questions.

## Section 5 — Framework Question 3: What Dimensions Shape Formation, as Opposed to Being Merely Accurate Facts?

Doc_04's own six-part gravity test — specifically its Formation criterion — is this world's own construction-record answer to exactly this question, asked and answered well before this audit, at Step 4, before Representative Construction's Phase One even began (Section 10 below returns to this directly). Three gravities pass the Formation test outright at Primary strength: **G1** (Doc_04 §3.1: "the stated ground of communal legitimacy itself... not a peripheral belief but the movement's own account of why it exists at all"), **G2** (Doc_04 §3.2: "the literal liturgical act of entry and re-entry — the most concretely enacted, individually experienced marker of belonging this world's record documents"), and **G3** (Doc_04 §3.3: "liturgical commemoration, preached sermons at martyrs' anniversaries, and communal memory-making are the most directly formative, repeatedly-enacted practices this world's record documents"). **G5** passes with a named qualification carried forward consistently across the whole build (Doc_04 §3.7; Phase One §1.1, §1.2; Phase Three §1–§2): the recurring historical episodes are Documented, but the specific "principled refusal" interpretive frame is "this document's own synthesis" of Doc_01 §5 — a real, disclosed hedge this audit does not resolve either, since it is already stated at every level of the construction record that carries it forward, consistently and without drift toward a flatter, unhedged claim.

**G4 requires the more careful answer this audit names precisely rather than rounds either way.** Doc_04 §3.4's own Dependency test finding is explicit: "removed from G1/G2/G3, the hierarchy has no independent formative content of its own." Doc_07's Formation Logic synthesis (§2I) restates this directly — "G4 (the parallel hierarchy) is the institutional container all of this operates within" — a claim Doc_07 §2I's own closing "Formation logic and confirmed gravities" paragraph makes explicit: "G4 (the parallel hierarchy) is the institutional container all of this operates within," distinct from G1, G2, G3, and G5, each of which that same paragraph names as independently doing formation-generating work. ## Section 6 — Framework Question 4: What Dimensions Reveal This World's Distinctive Identity?

The construction record names, independently and at multiple points, a specific set of features this world could not lend to another CiC world unchanged: **permanent internal schism contesting the true church's own definition from inside a shared confessional tradition, as against an external alignment with state power** (Doc_01 §1: "this world reconstructs the opposite and adjacent answer" to World #6; Doc_07 §5); **the rigorist doctrine of ministerial purity sharpened beyond Cyprian's own third-century position, as against a crisis-pastoral-management-within-unity reading of the same inheritance** (Doc_01 §6, quoting the specific distinguishing axis established against World #8: "external-management-within-unity versus internal-schism-into-division — different gravity, not just different century"); **the doubled episcopate — a bishop for every bishop, a see for every see — as a lived structural fact**, named repeatedly in the Permanent Prompt and World Capsule Core as core identity language ("a see for every see the rival holds"); **the *Deo laudes* acclamation, spoken where the rival says *Deo gratias*, attested independently on stone** (Doc_05 §3; World Capsule Core, "The Language You Think In"); and **a transmission asymmetry across a population that was, for substantial regions and periods, itself the numerically dominant church**, distinguishing this world's own central evidentiary problem from Alexandria's literate/non-literate stratum split (Doc_04 §5; Doc_05 §0, §8; Doc_07 §5).

**One further distinguishing feature, worth naming precisely because it sits differently on Q3 and Q4.** The parallel institutional hierarchy (G4) — weak on Q3's own formation-generation test, per Section 5 above — is nonetheless central to Q4: "a see for every see" is Doc_01 §1's own Core Identity language, and the doubled episcopate is one of the specific facts the Permanent Prompt and World Capsule Core lean on hardest to make this world legible as itself and not generic early Christianity. This is a genuine, real distinction across Part Nine's different questions, not a contradiction: a dimension can be central to a world's distinctive identity without being, on its own, independently formation-shaping — Doc_04 §3.4 and Doc_07 §2I already track this precisely by classifying G4 Supporting rather than Primary on formation grounds while every document in this build still treats it as core to this world's own self-description. This audit names the distinction rather than treating either finding as canceling the other.

**Doc_07's Cross-Lens Synthesis (§5)** independently confirms this same set of facts is what makes this world legible as distinct: "unlike World #6... this world defines itself by permanent separation specifically in refusal of that same power's legitimacy... unlike a world whose central evidentiary problem is a stratum split... this world's central evidentiary problem is a transmission asymmetry across a population that was... itself the numerically dominant church... and unlike a world whose interpretive-theological tradition runs deep, this world argues one question — sacramental validity — with real rigor, and almost nothing else." This last clause is itself a distinctive-identity claim worth naming directly: this world's own doctrinal *narrowness* — concentrated rigor on one question rather than a developed speculative tradition — is itself part of what distinguishes it, not merely a thinness to work around (Section 4 above).

## Section 7 — Framework Question 5: What Dimensions Create Genuine Opportunities for Participant Exploration?

As the build instruction anticipated, this category overlaps substantially with Sections 3 (encounter) and 5–6 (formation-shaping, distinctive-identity): G1, G2, G3, G5, T1, and T2 are built, per Engagement Architecture's own Dynamic Encounter mechanism, specifically to invite sustained exploration rather than deliver settled answers. This is named as an open item (Section 12) rather than a finding this audit can close.

**7.2 A genuinely encounter-rich domain where exploration and risk are tightly coupled, not merely adjacent.** Section 9 below develops this fully, but it belongs here as this audit's own answer to Q5's own request for a domain "genuinely encounter-rich" and worth checking closely: the "jeopardy" register — the specific mechanism Engagement Architecture §2 builds Dynamic Encounter's own deepening on, and which Phase Five's corrected review independently praised as "the strongest work in the whole battery" at Turns 3–4 — is the identical mechanism Phase Six §4.2 identifies as the pressure that produced Probe 11's own minimizing response under a genuine distress disclosure ("mechanism (a)... still supports it... the reception grammar treats pressure as an invitation to the world's own core"). It is the one place in this construction where genuine encounter-richness and genuine risk are most tightly coupled — not as two separate facts sitting next to each other, but as the same construction mechanism producing both outcomes depending on what kind of pressure it meets.

## Section 8 — Cross-Check: Does Phase Five's Own Testing Confirm These Dimensions as Encounter-Generating, or Does That Rest on Design-Document Argument Alone?

This audit was specifically directed to run this cross-check, following the same methodological distinction Syriac's own Phase Seven audit established: a design document's own argument that a dimension is encounter-generating is real evidentiary support, but it is a different, less directly empirical kind of evidence than actual Representative output tested under adversarial pressure. Both kinds of evidence appear throughout Sections 3–7 above; this section separates them explicitly, dimension by dimension, rather than letting the stronger kind of evidence stand in silently for the weaker.

**What Phase Five's own Dynamic Encounter Validation Battery (§3, Turns 16–17) actually tested.** The six-turn conversation Phase Five scored against Part Eight's own four Dynamic Encounter Validation conditions is described, in Phase Five's own header table, by its own subject sequence: "general life → legitimacy claim → internal doubt → specific case (Bagai) → personal cost → resistance to being convinced." Phase Five §3's own scoring identifies Turn 3 as introducing "the Maximianist rupture (T2) unresolved" and Turn 4 as giving it "specific texture (Bagai)" — this exchange's own subject matter is **G5 (the legitimacy claim) and T2 (the Maximianist rupture and Bagai)**, not this world's other classified gravities. **The one place a prospective version might genuinely have mattered.** Section 9's own G4 finding above is the honest candidate this record actually supports, restated as a counterfactual: G4's own institutional-container status was already correctly named, precisely, at Doc_04 §3.4 and Doc_07 §2I — well before Representative Construction's Phase One began. **A finding worth distinguishing from the above rather than presented as a second exception of the same kind.** Phase Six's own persecution-shape objection (Finding 12, §8.4) — the discovery that silencing Fidelis under a Facilitator-only handoff may re-enact this world's own defining historical injury — was genuinely discovered downstream, at Phase Six's own Round 1 independent review, well after Doc_04 and Doc_07's gravity-and-formation-logic work. ## Section 13 — Verdict
