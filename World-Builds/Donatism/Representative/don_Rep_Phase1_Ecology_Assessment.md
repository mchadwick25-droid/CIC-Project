## 0. Purpose

Before Fidelis's voice is built, this assessment determines whether Donatism's own recoverable ecology is **sufficient** to sustain a living mediator of encounter, and — if so — what **shape** that Representative can authentically take, given that the role fixed by the project lead is a bishop of the Donatist communion. It assesses the four sufficiency domains (Reasoning Structure, Perception Pattern, Formation Posture, Internal Complexity), maps the ecology's **thinness** as it bears specifically on a bishop-voice, and previews the depth calibration (grounded in Doc_04 gravities and Doc_08 forces). It draws only on what the cleared world actually supports and invents no biography beyond the role itself (Framework Part Four §63; `don_Representative_Identity_Options.md` §0).

## 1. Sufficiency — the Four Domains

### 1.1 Reasoning Structure — **RICH, concentrated rather than broad**
This world left an extensive record of *how* it engaged, not only what it concluded — but the mode is legal-liturgical-enacted, not speculative-argumentative. Doc_07 §2I (Formation Logic): "formation here does not happen primarily through speculative teaching or a developed interpretive tradition... it happens through enacted threshold-crossing (rebaptism), through repeated commemorative narration..., and through lived legal jeopardy." Doc_07 §2E (Ethical/Legal) confirms this is "one of the most directly evidenced lenses in this document" — every Primary gravity has "a direct legal-procedural trace." Doc_01 §3 and Doc_07 §2D both find the doctrinal-philosophical record "comparatively thin and largely reactive" beyond G1/G2 — a real, disclosed concentration, not smoothed over. *(Cap from Doc_04 §3.1: G1's specific argumentative texture is substantially Augustine-mediated even where the core doctrine is cross-voice attested. G5's own divergence is different in kind, not the same mediation: Doc_04 §3.7 attributes it to Donatus's own retort reaching this document "through Optatus's own hostile narrative frame," and names the "principled refusal" interpretive frame itself as "this document's own synthesis" of Doc_01 §5 — a build-thread reading, not primarily a hostile author's own mediation.)* **Sufficient, with a distinctive shape: a bishop's own reasoning here is conciliar, legal, and enacted — arguing one question with real rigor, not broadly philosophical.**

### 1.2 Perception Pattern — **RICH**
The world attends to reality with a consistent, distinctive priority, argued substantively at Doc_07 §2H (Boundary Structures) and §2I (Formation Logic) and stated at its sharpest in the Integrative Observation (Doc_07 §6; `don_World_Profile.md` §10) — with the underlying gravities it names (G1, G5, T1, T2) surfacing in other forms across the remaining lenses (§2A–§2G), not the pattern itself independently re-argued in each: the capacity to hold an absolute conviction and a named, undenied exception to it in the same breath, without experiencing contradiction — the purity doctrine stated at full strength alongside the Maximianist reception it did not, in practice, survive intact (T2), and the refusal of imperial legitimacy stated as principle alongside three named pragmatic exceptions (T1). Doc_07 §2H names a second, equally recurring pattern: "the boundary and the center are the same line, viewed from two directions" — this world does not experience boundary-drawing as a separate activity from its central doctrine. Both patterns recur across multiple gravities, multiple named voices (Petilian, Donatus's retort, the Bagai decree, Emeritus of Caesarea's own courtroom interventions), and both phases of the window. **Sufficient, and genuinely distinctive.**

### 1.3 Formation Posture — **RICH**
Doc_07 §2I supplies a specific, non-generic formation arc: entry is rebaptism itself, "a specific, dated, individually experienced threshold"; the middle is lived "inside a parallel institutional life under continuous legal jeopardy," punctuated by acute persecution that intensifies rather than interrupts commemorative practice; maturity looks like the martyr — not necessarily literal death, but the settled readiness the martyr narratives hold up as this world's own formation ideal. This is the world's own account, not generic pedagogy, and it gives Fidelis a way of relating to participants shaped by threshold-crossing and lived jeopardy rather than by incremental instruction. **Sufficient, and well-suited to an episcopal voice**, since bishops administer the threshold (rebaptism), preside the commemoration (the sermon, on Monceaux's own hypothesis per Doc_02 §4, preached by "the Donatist bishop of Carthage in 320 — possibly Donatus the Great himself"), and are themselves named among the martyred (the bishop of Sicilibba wounded, the bishop of Advocata killed, per the sermon's own content).

### 1.4 Internal Complexity — **RICH**
Doc_04 confirms two genuine Tensional gravities with real institutional separation (T1, T2), a Supporting gravity carrying its own sharp, disclosed confidence divergence (D-A), a contested naming battle running in both directions (Doc_05 §6.1; Doc_07 §2H), and a condemned internal dissenter whose work nonetheless shaped the eventual rival's own hermeneutics (Tyconius, D-B). Doc_04 §6's own Interaction Matrix finds every classified candidate carries at least one demonstrated relationship — no isolated row. This is ample range for sustained, many-sided engagement rather than repetition. **Sufficient.**

## 2. Thinness Mapping

Per Framework V3.2 Part Nine, this mapping is grounded specifically in Doc_08 (Forces) — not general Doc_05/Doc_07 gestures. Doc_08's own thirteen forces name two distinct categories relevant here: domains forces analysis shows structurally underrepresented in what survives, and domains where forces-grounding is strong even though the interior record is thin.

**Structurally underrepresented, per Doc_08 directly:**
- **The ordinary believer's own interior life.** Force 2B-2's own Layer 2 (Transmission, Cell 2B) states plainly: "no surviving Donatist chronicle of Arles, no surviving Donatist administrative account of the Macarian repression exists." Doc_08 §6 ("What Selection Effects Shaped What Survives") names what is lost this way as "structurally rather than accidentally... the ordinary Donatist believer's own voice... not because these things were never produced... but because nothing in either transmission channel had reason to preserve them." This is a thinness Fidelis's own bishop-voice does not close — a bishop's record is exactly the record that survives; the layperson's is exactly what does not.
- **Emotional interior of doubt, fear, or wavering.** Doc_08 §8's own governing-principles synthesis states that this world's own self-understanding "is not recoverable from surviving sources" at Forces 3A-1, 3A-2, 3B-1, and 3B-2 — though each force's own Layer 2 entry states this in its own terms, not one uniform phrase: 3A-1 ("this world's own record does not preserve a direct account of how its own participants received this specific verdict"), 3A-2 (using the "not recoverable from surviving sources" phrase directly), 3B-1 ("does not preserve a direct account of what this attrition felt like from inside"), 3B-2 (also using the phrase directly). Across all four: the community's own reception of the 411 verdict, of the Vandal conquest, of the later institutional attrition, and of its own weakening transmission position are each named absences, not filled inferences. The surviving emotional register (Doc_07 §2B) is vindication and defiant joy — the register of those who held firm — because that is what the transmission mechanism preserved.
- **Women's voice beyond Lucilla's own bounded reconstruction.** Doc_02 §6 permits one Article 20 bounded reconstruction — Lucilla, "a named woman of means with the standing to make a rival consecration happen" — and states no further case meets the bounding conditions. A bishop-voice has no natural route into this domain at all.

**A genuine, recent correction, disclosed rather than overstated.** Doc_08's own "Where Forces Analysis Surfaced Gaps" subsection (drafted before this session's source-integration work) characterizes Force 3A-1's own Layer 2 entry as stating that the community's own reception of the 411 Conference verdict is "not recoverable from what this construction currently holds" — noting explicitly, at the time, that "the *Gesta Collationis Carthaginiensis* itself... remains unvendored." Doc_02's subsequent revision (2026-09-07) vendors that same *Gesta* — a court transcript recording Emeritus of Caesarea's own procedural interventions across the Conference (at least ten confirmed acts, several closed under explicit protest, "*salva appellatione recognovi*"). This narrows Force 3A-1's own gap for the courtroom exchange itself — a bishop arguing procedure, pressing that "the truth is concealed by a great device" — but does **not** close it for the verdict's own reception afterward, which remains unattested. A real strengthening of Fidelis's natural register at the Conference specifically, not a resolution of the wider transmission gap.

**Where forces-grounding is strong despite general thinness:** Force 2A-1 (oscillating imperial policy) sustains G5 and directly produces T1's own three dated instances (313, 361, the 390s) — Fidelis does not need the interior emotional record to hold this tension at real depth, because the concrete pattern of pragmatic exception is itself well-documented. Force 2B-4 (the Maximianist fracture) "carries the longest and most detailed Layer 1 in Doc_08" and grounds T2 directly at the conciliar level Fidelis naturally inhabits.

**Thin regardless of role:** speculative/hermeneutical theology beyond Tyconius (Doc_07 §2D, §3B — genuinely undeveloped, not merely undocumented, though Fidelis can speak to Tyconius's own condemnation as a conciliar-disciplinary act); Numidian basilica archaeology and daily domestic practice (Doc_02 §5, Confidence C, not force-grounded at all — an archaeological gap, distinct in kind from the transmission-caused gaps above).

## 4. Role-Breadth Note (L3C V3.2 role preference)

The Framework prefers, among a world's *authentic* roles, one of wide pastoral reach — a crossroads position — where the ecology supports it equally well among the options considered. `don_Representative_Identity_Options.md` §2 already weighed this directly against the two alternatives (a lay believer, a Circumcellion-adjacent voice) and the project lead decided for the bishop. This section confirms, rather than re-argues, that the decided role genuinely satisfies the preference on its own evidentiary terms.

A Donatist bishop's documented range of contact is real and wide, not asserted: clergy and laity within his own see (administering rebaptism, the rite every member crosses); other bishops at councils that met repeatedly across the window (Cebarsussi 393, Bagai 394, the 411 Conference seating 284 Donatist bishops); imperial officials directly (Anulinus's 313 *relatio*, the 361 petition to Julian, and now the *Gesta*'s own record of Emeritus arguing procedure before Marcellinus, the presiding imperial tribune, and named Catholic bishops in open court). This is a genuine crossroads by the Framework's own definition — grounded in Doc_07 §2E and §2F, not invented. **The breadth is directional, named plainly rather than smoothed:** it runs toward clergy, councils, and imperial officials, not toward the ordinary believer's own domestic or interior world, which Doc_01 §2 confirms was this world's genuinely broad lay base. Fidelis is a crossroads for this world's *institutional* life, not for its numerically dominant membership's own daily experience — exactly the trade-off `don_Representative_Identity_Options.md` §2 already named against Deodata. No family, see, age, or itinerary is added to establish this breadth; the role-label alone carries it, per Framework Part Four §63.

## 5. Sufficiency Determination and Handoff

**Determination: the recoverable ecology is SUFFICIENT to sustain a living Representative, and construction should proceed.** This finding rests on real weight, not assumption: eight prior review rounds across Doc_01 through Doc_08, plus the World Profile, each independently cleared; four Primary gravities and two genuine Tensional gravities with real institutional separation; a distinctive, consistently-recurring perception pattern (the held, undissolved gap between absolute doctrine and named exception) attested across multiple lenses and voices; and a formation posture with a specific, non-generic arc. All four sufficiency domains are RICH, though Reasoning Structure is honestly concentrated rather than broad — a real feature of this world, not a construction shortfall, since the Framework's own test asks about the richness of whatever mode of engagement the world actually left, and this world's mode is legal-liturgical-enacted.

The thinness is real, structurally explained by Doc_08's own forces (the transmission mechanism, Forces 2B-2/3B-2, not mere silence), and concentrated exactly where a bishop-voice would naturally be quiet: the ordinary believer's interior life, the emotional record of doubt or wavering, and women's voice beyond Lucilla's own bounded case. This should convert into natural reticence at construction, not apologetic disclaimer.

**Handoff to Phase Two (Formation Calibration, Part Four):** carry the identity (Fidelis, a bishop of the Donatist communion, role-label only, no invented biography — decided) and the temporal horizon (*c.* 311/312–439, this world's own full construction window). Set the depth calibration from §3 above, with each level tied to specific Doc_04 gravities and Doc_08 forces at the fuller Phase Two pass. **Standing constraints carried into all subsequent phases:** the honest thinness on the ordinary believer and on women's interior voice (World Profile §8); T1 and T2 held as this world held them, not resolved by hindsight; the *Gesta*'s newly-integrated courtroom register as a genuine, bounded strengthening of Fidelis's own natural voice at the 411 Conference specifically, not a general resolution of the transmission problem; no see, family, or anecdote added to the identity; and — from Part Five — the accessibility standard (Flesch-Kincaid ~8–10, Reading Ease ≥60; sentence length and clause-chaining adjusted, never this world's own vocabulary or imagery).
