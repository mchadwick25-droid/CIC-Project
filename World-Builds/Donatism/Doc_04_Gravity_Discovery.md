## 0. Method and the Governing Discipline

**Purpose.** Gravity Discovery identifies the forces this world's own ecology actually *organizes around* — a gravity organizes; a topic merely recurs (Framework Part III). Candidates are generated only from elements recurring across multiple Doc_02 evidence sections (never from general impression), run through six tests (Repetition, Dependency, Formation, Explanatory, Persistence, Interaction), cross-checked against the five-level confidence vocabulary (Constitution Article 17), tested for cross-strand status (Article 21), connected to the forces already identified in this build (Forces Framework Step 4), and classified Primary / Supporting / Tensional.

**The governing discipline for this world (stated up front because it bears on every classification below): the Author Gravity concentration named as this world's central evidentiary problem in Doc_01 §7 item 1 and Doc_02 §1.** Nearly all textual evidence for this world passes through Optatus and, above all, Augustine — the hostile, eventually-victorious party's own literature. For every candidate below, this document asks not only whether the candidate is real, but whether its *specific evidentiary content* is independently corroborated beyond Optatus's and Augustine's own argumentative framing, or rests on their hostile characterization alone. Where a candidate's core reality is well-attested but its *specific argumentative shape* is Augustine-mediated, that divergence is stated explicitly (the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check below), not smoothed over.

**Strand status.** Doc_01 §4 found this world **strand-singular** — neither Tyconius's own condemned dissent nor the Maximianist schism rises to strand status. Standard Article 21 cross-strand testing therefore does not apply directly. Per this project's own established practice for a strand-singular world (`Alexandria-Catechetical-School/Doc_04_Gravity_Discovery.md` §5), a strand-singular world declares a substitute discipline serving the same purpose Article 21 testing serves — catching a gravity that holds in only one part of the record and is mistaken for a gravity of the whole. §5 below declares that substitute for this world.

## 2. Candidates Considered and Not Advanced

- **D-B Tyconius's universalist hermeneutics/ecclesiology → not a gravity.** Fails Repetition (attested in exactly one figure's own surviving work, corroborated only by Monceaux's secondary treatment of that same work — no second Donatist voice shares this position); fails Persistence (his own party's council condemned him, c. 380, and no following continued his approach — Doc_01 §4's own strand-singular finding turns on exactly this absence of a surviving pattern); fails Dependency (no other dimension of this world's ecology — rebaptism practice, martyr-cult, the parallel hierarchy, imperial relations — depends on Tyconius's own hermeneutics for its own operation). He remains a genuine, individually significant data point (Doc_01 §4's strand determination, Doc_03's [PV]-tagged lexicon entry), but an individual dissenting voice is not an organizing force of the ecology, and this document does not manufacture one from him.
- **D-C Numidian native-social-protest substrate → not tested as an independent gravity.** Doc_02 §5 itself treats this at Confidence C, explicitly contested between Frend's older reading and Shaw's/Brown's standard correctives, "pending independent verification of the underlying distributional data against a primary source." A candidate this evidentially unsettled should not be run through gravity testing as though it were settled; testing it now would manufacture false precision about a question Doc_02 itself left open. If a future pass resolves the underlying dispute, this candidate should be regenerated then, from the resolved evidence — not tested provisionally here.

### 3.6 The Two Tensional Gravities

A Tensional Gravity requires two genuinely distinct poles with real institutional or practice-cluster separation (not merely an abstract tension within one person's own thought). Two qualify on this world's own record.

- **T1 — Principled Refusal vs. Pragmatic Recourse to Imperial Power.** Poles: (a) the movement's own ideological stance that the state has no standing to adjudicate who the true church is, voiced directly in Donatus's own reported retort, "*Quid est imperatori cum ecclesia?*" ("What has the emperor to do with the church?" — verified this session against `optatus_against-the-donatists.txt`, Book III); (b) the movement's own repeated, documented turns to that same imperial machinery for its own advantage at three specific, named points across the window (the 313 petition to Constantine via Anulinus's *relatio*; the 361 petition to Julian for restoration of confiscated basilicas; the 390s invocation of existing imperial and proconsular anti-heretical legislation against its own Maximianist dissidents — all three named explicitly in Doc_01 §5). **Institutional separation is real**: these are three distinct, documented acts at three distinct moments, not an abstract inconsistency. **Tests:** Repetition PASS (three separate instances); Persistence PASS (spans the whole window, 313 to the 390s); Interaction PASS (directly qualifies G5, §3.7 below). **Confidence:** each of the three instances is independently **Documented** on its own historical terms (Anulinus's *relatio* is part of Optatus's own vendored Appendix of Documents; the Maximianist-era legal invocation is corroborated in Augustine's own primary text). The *characterization* of the tension as "principled refusal against pragmatic exception," rather than simple incoherence, is this document's own synthesis of Doc_01 §5's language ("the pattern remains refusal-under-pressure on balance... not an absolute refusal at every point or against every target") — a defensible reading, not itself independently attested as the Donatists' own self-description of the tension. **Forces-connection.** Spans Cells 1B through 2B rather than sitting in a single cell: the 313 Anulinus *relatio* falls at the same founding-dispute moment as Cell 1B's own rigorist rupture — the movement's earliest documented turn to state adjudication, arising alongside the rupture itself, not decades after it; the 361 Julian petition and the 390s Maximianist-era invocation of existing imperial and proconsular legislation both belong to the sustained "ongoing" period Cells 2A (external, oscillating imperial policy) and 2B (internal, the Maximianist fracture) already name. That this tension's own forces-connection spans two periods of Doc_01's own six-cell sketch, rather than sitting in one, is itself part of what makes it a genuine Tensional gravity rather than a single-episode inconsistency.

## 5. Cross-Strand Testing (Article 21) — Declared Substitute: the Cross-Voice Test

Doc_01 §4 found this world **strand-singular**, so standard cross-strand testing does not apply. Following this project's own established practice for a strand-singular world, this document declares a substitute discipline and argues it serves the same purpose Article 21 testing serves — catching a gravity that holds in only one part of the record and is mistaken for a gravity of the whole.

**Declared substitute: the Cross-Voice Test.** This world's defining evidentiary fact is not a stratum asymmetry (as in Alexandria's literate/non-literate divide) but a **transmission asymmetry**: nearly all textual evidence passes through the hostile, eventually-victorious Caecilianist/Catholic party's own literature (Optatus, Augustine), a concentration Doc_01 §7 item 1 and Doc_02 §1 both name as this world's central evidentiary problem. The analogue of "a gravity visible in only one strand" here is **a gravity visible only within the hostile-mediated argumentative record — generated by what Optatus and Augustine chose to attack, ridicule, or refute — and not found, even thinly, in the rare surviving Donatist-own-voice or non-mediated material.** The test asks of each confirmed gravity: is this attested only in how the hostile record argues against the Donatists, or also in something closer to their own voice or an unmediated trace (Petilian's quoted words, the Passiones and the sermon, the *Deo laudes* epigraphy, Tyconius)?

**Result, applied to each confirmed gravity:**
- **G1 (Purity)** passes with a qualification already stated in §3.1: its *existence* is cross-voice attested (Petilian's own quoted words independently confirm the doctrine is genuine Donatist teaching, not a hostile invention), but its *specific argumentative texture* remains substantially hostile-mediated. This is the same divergence the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check already named — the Cross-Voice Test and the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check converge on the same finding for this gravity, which is itself a form of corroboration.
- **G2 (Rebaptism)** passes: the practice is not merely characterized by its hostile opponents but argued *for* in Petilian's own quoted words (Registry row 12) — the same non-hostile-argument voice §3.1 already credits for G1. This is corroboration from within the hostile text's own quotation of a Donatist voice, not from a source entirely outside the Optatus/Augustine corpus (unlike G3, §5's next bullet); the same qualification named for G1 applies here in the same degree.
- **G3 (Martyr-cult)** passes most cleanly of all four Primaries: it is the one gravity in this document substantially attested *in* the Donatist-voiced or non-mediated record itself (three distinct texts plus epigraphy), not merely corroborated by a fragment of it.
- **G4 (Parallel hierarchy)** passes on the bare institutional fact, which even hostile sources do not dispute reporting; individual characterizations of specific bishops remain hostile-mediated and are treated accordingly throughout §3.4.
- **G5 (Refusal of imperial legitimacy)** passes with the same qualification as G1 and G2: Donatus's own quoted retort is a direct utterance attributed to a Donatist voice, not merely a hostile characterization of Donatist motives, though it reaches this document through Optatus's own hostile narrative frame rather than an unmediated Donatist-authored text (§3.7).
- **D-A (Circumcellion/*agonistici*)** is the one candidate that most clearly **fails** a strict cross-voice standard for anything beyond bare existence and self-designation: no Donatist-voiced or non-mediated text in this world's vendored corpus corroborates the group's character, scale, or typical conduct. This is precisely the "artificially confirmed" risk the Cross-Voice Test exists to catch, and it is the direct evidentiary basis for this document's own decision (§3.5) not to classify this candidate as an ecology-wide gravity.
- **T1 and T2** both pass: each names a specific, dated episode with its own documentary trace (Anulinus's *relatio*, part of Optatus's own Appendix of Documents; the Bagai and Cebarsussi sentences, quoted directly in Augustine's primary text) rather than resting on a purely interpretive hostile characterization.

This world's own transmission asymmetry is therefore not a blanket disqualifier requiring every gravity to be held open (unlike Alexandria's stratum-bias finding, which left ecology-wide primacy itself unconfirmed) — it is a **discriminating test** that this document's own candidate set substantially passes, with D-A as the one clear exception, precisely because this world happens to hold an unusually rich (if still comparatively small) set of non-hostile-mediated primary texts for its central gravities. This finding should not be read as resolving Doc_01's and Doc_02's own central evidentiary-problem disclosure — the record remains overwhelmingly hostile-mediated in volume — only as showing that the specific gravities classified Primary here do not rest on that hostile volume alone.

**A note on what this substitute does and does not test.** The Cross-Voice Test, as declared above, asks a *source-mediation* question — is a gravity's evidence hostile-argued or self-voiced? — closely related to, and in G1's case explicitly convergent with, the Author Gravity discipline already governing this whole document. Article 21 cross-strand testing proper asks a different, *population-mediation* question: might a different population or sub-group within the ecology organize around different gravities than the ones the available evidence foregrounds? This document does not claim the Cross-Voice Test covers that second question. The one place a genuine population/regional variation surfaces in this world's own record — D-A's own Numidian regional concentration (§3.5) — is handled directly on its own terms (a scope-qualified classification, not ecology-wide), not folded into or assumed covered by the Cross-Voice Test's own source-mediation finding. No other candidate in this document's own evidence shows a comparable population- or region-specific variation; if a future construction stage surfaces one, it should be tested on these same terms, not assumed already covered here.
