**G1. Tyconius, *Liber Regularum* — F.C. Burkitt (ed.), *The Book of Rules of Tyconius, Newly Edited from the MSS.* (Texts and Studies III/I, Cambridge University Press, 1894).** Public domain (1894). Latin critical text with an English scholarly introduction; no English translation of the Rules themselves. Three Internet Archive items were found hosting this edition: `archive.org/details/bookofrulesoftyc00ticouoft`, `archive.org/details/rulesoftyconius00tico`, and `archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.167130` (a 244-page Digital Library of India scan of the same printing). Registry rows 15, 41. **Vendored file:** `cic/texts/tyconius_liber-regularum_burkitt1894.txt` — Mark downloaded the first of the three items (`bookofrulesoftyc00ticouoft`, FULL TEXT/OCR format) and supplied it 2026-09-01 as a DOCX attachment; the file is confirmed as the genuine Burkitt 1894 edition (preface, table of contents, and introduction all present) and public domain on two independent grounds stated in its own header (archive.org's copyright-evidence record for this item, and the 1894 publication date alone). The delivered format is plain OCR text rather than PDF, so the Destination corrected to `.txt` from this entry's own original `.pdf` placeholder, as anticipated below.

**G2. Optatus, critical Latin text — Karl Ziwsa (ed.), *S. Optati Milevitani libri VII*, CSEL 26 (Prague/Vienna/Leipzig: F. Tempsky / G. Freytag, 1893).** Public domain. This is the critical edition underlying the already-vendored 1917 Vassall-Phillips translation (row 1). Registry row 38. **Vendored file:** `cic/texts/optatus_libri-vii-critical_ziwsa1893.txt` — Mark downloaded a Google-Books-scan archive.org item and supplied it 2026-09-01 as a DOCX attachment; the file is confirmed as the genuine CSEL 26 edition (title page and the *Gesta Purgationis Felicis* appendix document both present) and public domain on two independent grounds stated in its own header (the Google Books scan's own public-domain finding for this item, and the 1893 publication date alone). The second-edition/Book-count questions row 1's Verification Note leaves open have not themselves been settled — the apparatus criticus has not yet been read for that purpose.

**G3. Codex Theodosianus, Book 16 — Theodor Mommsen and Paul M. Meyer (eds.), *Theodosiani Libri XVI cum Constitutionibus Sirmondianis* (Berlin: Weidmann, 1905).** Public domain (1905). The standard critical Latin edition, in two volumes (the first in two parts). **Status: discharged, 2026-09-07.** Five acquisition attempts (2026-09-01, Mark's manual-download channel) all located `archive.org/details/theodosianilibri02code`, confirmed to hold only the Prolegomena — Mommsen's manuscript-tradition and textual-critical apparatus, Vol. I Pars Prior — not Book 16's own text. A sixth download, from a site calling itself "sourcelibrary.org," was rejected outright on source-integrity grounds (an unlicensed CC BY-SA "translation" carrying a systematic invisible Unicode payload — see `don_Decision_Log.md`; that site should not be used for any future acquisition). **The correct volume — Voluminis I Pars Posterior: Textus cum Apparatu, item `theodosianilibr01sirmgoog` — was located and vendored 2026-09-07**, this session, once direct outbound network access was confirmed working (the prior sessions' egress block, not a bad search, was the root blocker for the five failed attempts). Vendored as `cic/texts/theodosianus-16_mommsen-meyer1905.txt`; Registry row 16 (upgraded to Confidence A) and new row 51. Law 16.5.52 located and read in full, confirmed to contain "circumcelliones argenti pondo decem" verbatim — the standing gap this Manifest and Doc_02 §9 both flagged is now closed for the edition itself. Clyde Pharr's 1952 English translation is the only full English translation reported to exist, but it is not public domain (see §2 below) — this project has already reached the same determination for the identical work in a different world's build (`world-build-docs/ijc/SOURCE-REQUEST-MANIFEST.md` §3: "Theodosian Code in English — Pharr (1952) treated as in copyright, fails closed"), so this is a confirmed, not merely inferred, closure.

**Substitute source acquired at the time, before G3 was later discharged in full:** William K. Boyd, *The Ecclesiastical Edicts of the Theodosian Code* (Columbia University, 1905) — a scholarly narrative history of the Book 16 Title 5 Donatist-targeted legislation, found by web search and vendored as a stopgap while the critical edition itself remained unlocated. **Vendored file:** `cic/texts/boyd_ecclesiastical-edicts-theodosian-code_1905.txt`. Registry row 49. It corroborates the legislative sequence and the 405 edict (C. Th. xvi.5.38–39) with several laws quoted verbatim in Latin, but does not itself quote 16.5.52. **Corrected 2026-09-08, Round 4 Opus review, from this paragraph's own stale "remain unheld in any vendored text" claim:** per G3 immediately above, the Mommsen-Meyer critical edition was subsequently vendored (2026-09-07) and does hold 16.5.52 — "circumcelliones argenti pondo decem," confirmed verbatim. The *agonistici* term itself is still not held in that law's own text (the law names the group by the outsider term "circumcelliones," not by their own self-designation) — that narrower, still-accurate point is the one this paragraph should have kept.

**G4. Passio Marculi and Passio Isaac et Maximiani — as printed in J.-P. Migne (ed.), *Patrologia Latina*, vol. 8 (Paris, compiled 1844).** Public domain. Registry rows 19, 20. **Vendored file:** `cic/texts/monumenta-vetera-donatistarum_migne-pl8.txt` — Mark downloaded the archive.org Google-Books-scan item (`patrologiae_cursus_completus_lat_vol_008`) and supplied it 2026-09-01 as a DOCX attachment; both texts are confirmed present and correctly headed ("PASSIO MARCULI SACERDOTIS DONATISTAE," dated by its own heading to "ANNO DOMINI 348"; "PASSIO MAXIMIANI ET ISAAC DONATISTARUM," with an appended epistle of the martyr Macrobius). Only the relevant excerpt was vendored, not the full ~4.3M-character volume — see the file's own header for the exact boundary. **Incidental find in the same excerpt:** a genuine Donatist-authored sermon (manuscript title *De passione sanctorum Donati et Advocati*), edited by Mabillon and dated by his own analysis to *circa* 340 — a rare surviving Donatist primary voice, not itself part of this request. Registry row 50. Latin only — the modern standard English translation is Tilley's *Donatist Martyr Stories* (§2 below), in copyright; Monceaux (G6 below) is a second, independent public-domain route if this text proves unsatisfactory.

**G5. This is the critical Latin edition of *Contra Cresconium* and *Contra epistulam Parmeniani* (Registry rows 17, 18). Doc_01 §7 item 6's binding to track these two works for acquisition is discharged by their being named here and in the Registry (Doc_02 §10). Registry row 39. **Vendored file:** `cic/texts/augustini_scripta-contra-donatistas-pars-i-iii_petschenig1908-1910.txt` — Mark downloaded an archive.org scan and supplied it 2026-09-01 as a DOCX attachment. **G6. Paul Monceaux, *Histoire littéraire de l'Afrique chrétienne depuis les origines jusqu'à l'invasion arabe*, vols. IV–VI (Paris: Ernest Leroux, 1912–1922).** Public domain (pre-1929). Registry rows 40, 52, 53. **Status: fully discharged, 2026-09-07.** `cic/texts/monceaux_histoire-litteraire-afrique-chretienne-tome5_1920.txt` — Mark downloaded Tome V (*Saint Optat et les premiers écrivains donatistes*, 1920) from archive.org and supplied it 2026-09-01 as a DOCX attachment. Both confirmed by title page and chapter-heading survey only this session (Registry rows 52, 53); their body-text content has not yet been read and is named as a candidate for a future Doc_02/Doc_04 revision pass, not integrated here.
