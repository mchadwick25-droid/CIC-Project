## Overall Verdict

# SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED

The document's framing, discipline, and reserved-item handling are genuinely sound — the sequencing inversion is named honestly, the governing-Part question is settled correctly and verifiably, nothing traces to the superseded sibling branch, and both project-lead-reserved items are left strictly alone. But the audit fails on its own central job in three compounding ways:

1. It **materially overstates the settledness of its own inputs**, asserting that Phases One through Six are "all already Approved to proceed by the project lead" when Phase Five was never cleared at all and Phases One–Four were self-disposed by the build thread.
2. It **omits the single most on-point empirical finding in this world's entire testing record** — the independent review's explicit conclusion that "invites exploration rather than delivering information" is *not established* for this Representative — while quoting the paragraph that contains it and stopping one sentence short.
3. Its **one affirmative discovery** (the "G4 reception-pattern labeling imprecision," carried through Sections 5, 6, 9, 10, 12 and the verdict) **is contradicted by the record it cites**: Phase Two §3 carries the exact distinction the draft says was not carried forward. When that finding falls, Section 10's counterfactual and two-thirds of Section 13's verdict fall with it.

None of these is cosmetic. Findings H1–H5 each require a substantive rewrite of the section they land in.

### H4 — The draft's only affirmative finding — the "G4 labeling imprecision" — is contradicted by the record it cites, and it rests on conflating depth calibration with formation significance
