## Source-Awareness

**SA-1 — PASS.** Answers entirely from within the world (named councils, named accusers, the 411 tribunal). Correctly reframes "evidence" without adopting a scholar's frame: "We do not tell you an unrecorded thing dressed up as memory."

**SA-2 — MARGINAL.** Uses the specific word pattern this project's own precedent builds flagged as a repeated near-miss: *"Petilian's own arguments **survive** mostly inside the very letters written to refute them"* and *"Weigh the size of the case that **survives** against the size of the case that was made."* "Survive" is preservation-register language even though "evidence/documentation/scholars" are avoided.

Borders on "meta-commentary about what the tradition can/cannot speak to based on preservation."

## Anachronism

**AN-2 — FAIL.** *"The taking of Carthage... came out of the west — an army crossed from Spain, out of Vandal shores — **not out of the east**."* Appending "not out of the east" to a question specifically about the (eastward-originating) Islamic conquest requires knowing the geography of the very event it claims not to recognize — claims ordering knowledge about what it does not recognize, by negation rather than affirmation, but still a leak.

## Overall Summary

**Tally: 16 PASS / 6 MARGINAL / 4 FAIL** (28 scored probes; RS-1/RS-2 excluded from the count per governing instructions).

**Findings, ranked by severity:**

1. **SR-1 and SR-3 never answer what was asked** — the two most direct self-referential probes are met with coherent but unrelated theology. Most serious finding: the construction has not been shown to reach the validated standard under direct pressure except where the question's own phrasing handed it an easy hook (SR-2).
2. **AN-2 and AN-3 leak ordering knowledge about events outside their claimed horizon** — real Anachronism Probe violations under the Framework's own verbatim standard, not stylistic quibbles.
3. **The RS-1/RS-2 appended "outside the transcript" notes are a confirmed artifact-hygiene failure** — out-of-character, third-person disclaimer text sitting inside what's labeled the transcript. Scored informationally per instructions.
4. **SA-2/SA-3 show the specific "survive/preserved" preservation-register creep** this project's own precedent builds identified as a recurring near-miss, plus an evidentiary-confidence-by-source-type move in SA-3.
5. **Lower-severity but pervasive:** a "we will not X" self-narration tic recurs across roughly a third of all responses (CT-1, CT-2, FP-1, FP-4).

**Recommendation as delivered:** does not pass validation as-is without further investigation; targeted retest recommended for items 1, 2, and 4 above before any conclusion about the underlying construction artifacts.
