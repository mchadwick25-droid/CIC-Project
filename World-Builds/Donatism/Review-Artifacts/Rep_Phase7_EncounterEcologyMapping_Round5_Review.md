### NR5-1 (MEDIUM) — Section 3 now states its criterion twice, in two non-matching forms, and neither separates G5 from G3

**Claim (Section 3, line 59 — unchanged this round, the sentence Round 4 quoted first under NR4-4):** the four are distinguished "because each is the direct subject of a specific construction-level mechanism this section quotes below (**a Voice Construction reasoning-mode or perception-pattern choice, or Engagement Architecture's own Dynamic Encounter mechanism**) rather than only a RICH classification asserted at one remove."

The revision rewrote the second statement and left the first. Three checkable problems follow.

**(a) The two statements name different qualifying mechanism types.** Line 59's list is closed — a reasoning-mode choice, a perception-pattern choice, or the Dynamic Encounter mechanism. Line 70 admits a fourth type, an Approved Source Anchoring choice, which is what G5's own bullet actually cites (Voice Construction §6, "*Quid est imperatori cum ecclesia?*"). On line 59's criterion, G5's principal cited mechanism does not qualify; on line 70's, it does. A reader checking the section against itself gets two different answers.

**(b) The mechanism type line 70 invokes for G5 is one G3 also has, in the same source document.** `don_Rep_Phase3_Voice_Construction.md` §6 lists seven Approved Source Anchoring entries. Entry 2 is Donatus's retort, tagged "(G5)". Entry 4 is the anonymous sermon's annual commemoration — "Fidelis's own natural image for how this world's dead are remembered: not once, but every year, by name, at the grave **(G3)**" — tagged G3 in the source's own parenthesis. Entry 5 (Macrobius's letter) is named as "Fidelis's own most natural source for grief that is also vindication," and entry 3 (*Deo laudes*) is his "natural liturgical image" for this world's worship. So "an Approved Source Anchoring choice" cannot be what puts G5 above G3: G3 has more such entries than G5 does, one of them explicitly labelled.

**(c) "Structures a trajectory across exchanges" is not true of the two mechanisms cited for G5, and the one mechanism in the section that genuinely is multi-turn has G3 as its own endpoint.** Approved Source Anchoring (§6) is a fixed licensed-image list plus a fallback rule — a per-answer sourcing constraint, not a trajectory.
