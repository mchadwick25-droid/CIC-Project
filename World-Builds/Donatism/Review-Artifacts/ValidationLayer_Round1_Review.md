A second, unrelated "paraphrase labeled verbatim" issue does exist in this world's build history, but it is at **Doc_02**, not Doc_05, and it is not about Latin: `Doc02_Round1_Review.md` L4 found that §1's "corpus map, verbatim" label was attached to a paraphrase of an English statistic (Cyprian cited "306 times... in 92,000 words"), not a verbatim quotation — "in a document holding itself to a fabrication-check standard, 'verbatim' should mark only verbatim text." **Why this matters:** This is not a stylistic quibble — it is exactly the class of error Section 2 exists to test for, appearing inside Section 2 itself, in a document whose own closing line claims "each independently re-verified before being applied rather than taken on a reviewer's own word." A reader relying on this document to know where a specific class of error occurred in this world's construction would be pointed at the wrong document (Doc_05, whose own review record explicitly certifies excellent quotation discipline) and would not learn that the actual event happened at Doc_09 pre-review, self-caught rather than review-caught — a materially different fact about how the error was found.

**Fix:** Correct the attribution. The accurate sentence is closer to: "a false-quotation-marks risk in Doc_09's own Isaac material (an improvised English rendering of raw Latin OCR), self-caught and converted to disclosed indirect speech before Round 1 review was ever dispatched; and, earlier, at Doc_02 §1, a paraphrase of an English statistic mislabeled 'verbatim,' caught and fixed at Round 1 review." Doc_05 should not appear in this list at all unless a genuine, independently-checkable Doc_05 quotation-marks finding is located that this review did not find.
