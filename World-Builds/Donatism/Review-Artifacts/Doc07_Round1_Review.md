## HIGH SEVERITY

### H1. The Integrative Observation (§6) and the Cross-Lens Synthesis's own flagship insight (§5) substantially restate Boundary Structures' (§2H) own closing claim, rather than each adding a distinct layer of integration — against the L4 template's own explicit instruction

**The template's own test (quoted directly):** Section 5's instruction states the Integrative Observation "is not a summary of the synthesis," and gives an explicit test: "if a Representative lost this insight but retained everything else in this document, what would be missing from their inhabitation of this world? If the answer is 'nothing important,' the observation is not yet specific enough." Section 4 separately requires the Cross-Lens Synthesis to state "the pattern that no single lens could reveal."

**What I found, reading the three passages side by side:**

- **§2H's own closing paragraph** ("What this reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show"): "the boundary-work in this world is not a separate activity from its central doctrine — it *is* the doctrine, applied outward. The same purity logic that determines who may validly baptize inside this communion is the logic that determines who is, and is not, 'the church' at all; the boundary and the center are the same line, viewed from two directions."
- **§5's own "most significant cross-lens insight"**: "the same logic that makes this world's own boundary (§2H) is the logic that makes its own formation (§2I) — purity, once adopted as the ground of sacramental validity, does not stay a private doctrinal commitment. It necessarily becomes a rite (rebaptism), a memory (martyr-cult), a parallel institution (G4), and a posture toward the state (G5), because a doctrine about who may validly minister cannot be held privately in a communion that also has to decide, publicly and repeatedly, whose baptism counts, whose ordination counts, and whose church this actually is."
- **§6's Integrative Observation**: "purity is not a private virtue but a public, checkable, sacramental fact... this single fact, once taken seriously, forces open every other question a church must answer: who belongs, who governs, what is remembered, and what is owed to the power outside. This world did not arrive at rebaptism, at martyr-cult, at a parallel hierarchy, and at a qualified refusal of the emperor as four separate conclusions. It arrived at one conclusion..."

These are the same claim — *purity, once adopted, cannot stay private; it necessarily becomes public across every domain* — stated a third time with different surface vocabulary (rite/memory/institution/posture in §5; who-belongs/who-governs/what-is-remembered/what-is-owed in §6) but no new analytical content beyond what §2H already named explicitly ("the boundary and the center are the same line"). Applying the template's own test directly: if a Representative retained §2H, §2I, and §5 but lost §6 specifically, would anything specific and irreplaceable be missing from their inhabitation? On the evidence of these three passages, the honest answer is no — §6 is a third framing of a conclusion the document has already stated twice by the time the reader reaches it, not a distinct, irreplaceable, deepest-level insight.

**Why this matters:** This is not a matter of the underlying claim being wrong — it is well-grounded (G1's purity doctrine genuinely does generate G2, G3, G4, and G5 as the document argues throughout, and the "boundary = doctrine applied outward" reading is a real, earned finding). The problem is architectural: the template asks for four *progressively deeper* payoffs (Formation Logic's own synthesis at §2I; a genuinely new cross-lens pattern at §5; a single named, irreplaceable insight at §6) and Doc_07 delivers the same payoff three times instead. This is exactly the failure mode task item 3 and item 5 both name — the "wrong" pattern is restating findings together rather than revealing something none of them showed alone, and here the restatement is not even confined to describing separate lens findings side by side (which would already fail the test) but is a near-paraphrase of one lens's own already-integrated conclusion, presented twice more as though each were a fresh discovery.

**What is NOT the problem:** §5's *first* paragraph ("The central coherence") and *second* paragraph ("What this world's distinctiveness consists of, against neighboring worlds") are genuine, non-redundant contributions — the distinctiveness paragraph in particular draws a real, checkable three-way contrast against World #6 and Alexandria that appears nowhere else in this document. The redundancy is specific to §5's "most significant cross-lens insight" bullet and §6 in their relationship to §2H, not to §5 as a whole.

**Fix:** Either replace §6 with an insight genuinely distinct from what §2H and §5 already state — something that would specifically be missing, not merely restated, if lost (a candidate direction: something about what it felt like, or what a formed member's own posture toward failure/doubt was, given how much of this document's honest gaps-work already circles that exact absence) — or, if the "purity becomes public" claim really is judged this world's single deepest insight, cut the redundant restatement at §5's "most significant cross-lens insight" bullet and replace it with a different cross-lens pattern (a candidate direction already latent in the document: the transmission-asymmetry pattern that Doc_07 itself notes recurs identically across §2B, §2C, §2G, and §5's own distinctiveness paragraph — evidence surviving in inverse proportion to how directly it can be checked without literary mediation — which is a genuine cross-lens pattern this document has not yet named as such in one place).
