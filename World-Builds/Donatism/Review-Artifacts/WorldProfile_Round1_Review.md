## HIGH SEVERITY

### H1. The Method Note's justification for the Section 4 scope expansion misstates its own cited precedent, and the underlying decision is the kind `cic-build-cycle` requires be escalated, not self-disposed

**Claim checked:** Method Note, paragraph 4: "Editing the template itself is outside this build thread's own authority (`cic-build-cycle`: template editing belongs to a coach thread); this finding is disclosed here, and should be flagged to a coach thread **the same way Doc_07's own analogous template-staleness finding** (`Integrated_Ecology_Analysis_Template.md`) **was disclosed rather than corrected unilaterally**."

**What I found:** Doc_07's own actual precedent, read directly at Doc_07 §1 and independently confirmed in `don_Decision_Log.md`, is not "disclosed rather than corrected unilaterally" — it is an explicit escalation to the project lead, resolved by direct instruction, before drafting began. Doc_07 §1: "This is a genuine cross-document inconsistency this build thread could not resolve on its own authority — editing the L4 template belongs to a coach thread, not a build thread (`cic-build-cycle`) — **so it was put to the project lead directly rather than guessed through silently. The project lead's direction: build this document on Smart's seven dimensions as the fixed lens spine, per M4**." `don_Decision_Log.md` records the same event in stronger terms: "**A live cross-document methodology question, surfaced and escalated before drafting**... this build thread could not resolve the inconsistency on its own authority, so it was put to Mark directly via `AskUserQuestion` rather than guessed through silently. **Mark's direction: build on Smart's seven dimensions per M4.** This is recorded here as the escalation, and its resolution... not a new governance decision made by this build thread, which only applies the already-adopted, project-lead-directed structure." Doc_07's own subsequent disposition ("no escalation category applies") is clean specifically *because* the live methodology fork had already been escalated and resolved by the person with authority to resolve it — not because disclosure alone was judged sufficient.

The World Profile draft faces a comparable fork and does not escalate it. The template's own stale Section 4 (`L4-Templates/World_Profile_Template.md` §4, and its own completion checklist: "Section 4 Ecological Summary covers all four required dimensions: Emotional, Philosophical, Authority, Boundary") names four required dimensions. The draft decides, on its own build-thread judgment, to cover nine instead, reasoning that "omitting content Doc_07 actually established, on the strength of an outdated dimension count, would leave the canonical world description thinner than the world's own build record supports" (Method Note, final paragraph). That reasoning is framed entirely as an ecology-grounded, Donatism-specific judgment call. But the underlying fact driving it — that an M4-built Doc_07 always yields nine lenses where the World Profile template expects four — is not specific to Donatism's own ecology at all; it will recur identically for every other M4-era world's Doc_07 in this portfolio, since M4 is a portfolio-wide Change Order, not a Donatism-specific finding. `cic-build-cycle`'s own escalation categories require exactly this distinction be made explicit: "Portfolio-level or cross-world strategic decisions — anything decided for a reason external to this specific world's own ecology. Label it explicitly as portfolio-level in whatever document records it, distinct from an ecology-grounded finding." The draft does the opposite — it frames a decision with plain portfolio-wide implications (should every M4-era World Profile's Ecological Summary cover nine dimensions instead of four?) entirely as a local, ecology-grounded call, and defers only the L4 template's own text-editing to "a coach thread," not the substantive question of what this document type should require going forward.

This is not a hypothetical concern about document length: the finished draft runs to 8,079 words (`wc -w`), against the template's own explicitly stated "Target length: 2,000–4,000 words depending on world complexity" — roughly double the stated ceiling, concrete evidence that this was a scope decision with real weight, not a minor citation correction. Doc_07's own §1 also independently establishes that no sibling world's Doc_07 has yet applied M4 literally ("Alexandria's own Doc_07 predates M4... Imperial-Juridical-Christianity's own Doc_07... uses a third, hybrid lens naming matching neither the old template nor M4's own seven-dimension spine"), confirming this is a genuinely novel fork for the portfolio, not a well-trodden one a build thread can safely resolve by analogy.

**Why this matters:** `cic-build-cycle`'s own stage-4 instruction is unambiguous: "Check it against the four escalation categories below. If any apply, stop and escalate directly to the project lead — do not self-dispose, regardless of how clean the review came back." A document that clears review with no substantial revision called for, but that rests on a scope decision meeting the portfolio-level or methodology-decision bar, is not eligible for build-thread self-disposition ("Approved to proceed") no matter how accurate its content otherwise is. The draft's own invocation of Doc_07's precedent to justify *not* escalating is not merely imprecise — it cites, as its own supporting authority, a document whose actual conduct was the opposite of what is being claimed for it. This is a genuine methodology-decision candidate under `cic-build-cycle`'s own fourth escalation category (governance/methodology decisions), and possibly the second (portfolio-level, given the M4 dimension-count question's cross-world reach), that the draft resolved by build-thread judgment where its own cited precedent resolved an analogous fork by direct project-lead consultation.

**Fix:** Before this document is dispositioned, put the actual question to the project lead: should an M4-era World Profile's Ecological Summary cover all of Doc_07's actually-built lenses, or only the four the template's own (stale) text names, with the remaining five left for the L4 template's own eventual coach-thread revision? Correct the Method Note's mischaracterization of Doc_07's own precedent regardless of the outcome — it should not continue to cite "disclosed rather than corrected unilaterally" as equivalent to what Doc_07 actually did. The Doc_07-section-number remapping itself (§2E→§2I, §2D→§2H, §5→§6, §6→§7) is a different matter and does not require this same escalation — it is a citation-locus correction pointing at already-approved content that already exists at those numbers, not a decision about what content this document type should contain; see "What Verified Clean" below.

## MEDIUM SEVERITY

### M1. Section 5's Forces Summary omits two forces Doc_08 itself describes as centrally significant to this world's own Primary gravities

**Claim checked:** Section 5 (Forces Summary) includes six named forces plus one Transmission entry, covering (by my count) 9 of Doc_08's 13 identified forces: 1A-1, 1B-2, 2A-2, 2B-4, 3A-1+3A-2 (merged into one entry), 3B-1, and Transmission (covering 2B-2 and 3B-2).

**What I found:** Two forces are entirely absent from Section 5 that Doc_08 itself flags as unusually significant: **Force 2A-1 (Oscillating imperial religious policy)** — connected, per Doc_08's own "By Connected Gravity" inverted table, to four gravities (G2, G5, D-A, T1), more than any other single force in the matrix except 2B-4 — and **Force 2B-1 (Sustained purity doctrine and rebaptism practice)**, which Doc_08's own Layer 3 text calls, in its own words, "**this world's own central, continuously operating internal force** — it sustains G1 and G2 across the entire window." Also absent: Force 1B-1 (the Cyprianic rigorist inheritance, connected to G1 and G2) and Force 2B-3 (martyr-cult and confessor memory sustaining identity, connected to G3). The template's own instruction is not satisfied by "at least one force per cell" alone: "Section 5 is complete when every force with significant Layer 3 formation impact has an entry here," and Doc_08's own language for 2B-1 in particular ("this world's own central, continuously operating internal force") is about as strong a self-description of significance as any force in the document carries.

**Why this matters:** A builder or Representative-emergence process reading Section 5 alone would not learn that the specific enacted practice sustaining G1 and G2 across the entire construction window is itself a named, Documented force, or that oscillating imperial policy is the single most gravity-connected external force in the matrix. This is a completeness gap against the template's own stated completion bar, not merely a stylistic choice.

**Fix:** Add entries for Force 2A-1 and Force 2B-1 at minimum; consider 1B-1 and 2B-3 as well, or state explicitly why they were judged not to meet the "significant Layer 3 impact" bar if they were deliberately excluded.

### M2. Force 1A-1's "Gravity connection" field inherits, and silently resolves rather than discloses, an internal inconsistency in Doc_08 itself

But the draft picked the more expansive ("G1, G3") reading without noting that Doc_08's own tables say otherwise, which is exactly the kind of "two already-cleared documents disagree, even if one of them is internally split against itself" situation `cic-build-cycle`'s cross-document-consistency discipline asks be checked and disclosed, not silently resolved one way.

**Why this matters:** A future builder checking Doc_08's own Force Index against the World Profile would find a discrepancy with no explanation on either side.

**Fix:** Either cite the Layer 3 text explicitly as the basis for including G1 (rather than the table), or flag the Doc_08 internal inconsistency itself as a small, disclosed correction item for a future coach-thread pass (per `cic-build-cycle`'s own allowance for small, purely technical corrections to a world-build file).

### M3. Section 7's grounding for T1 cites a Doc_07 lens (§2H) whose actual content is about T2, not T1 — and T2's own grounding is missing the citation that actually belongs there

**Claim checked:** Section 7, Tension 1 (Principled Refusal vs. Pragmatic Recourse): "**Grounding:** Doc_01 §5; Doc_04 §3.6; Doc_07 §2H, §6..." Tension 2 (Purity-Rigor vs. Institutional Reception): "**Grounding:** ...Doc_07 §6..." (no §2H).

**What I found:** Doc_07 §2H (Boundary Structures) discusses G1, G5, and — by name — T2: "The boundary is not static: it was contested even within this world's own membership, most sharply in the Maximianist affair, where the boundary the purity doctrine draws against outsiders did not hold, in practice, against the movement's own returning dissidents (Doc_04 §3.6, **T2**)." The lens that actually discusses T1 by name is §2E (Ethical/Legal): "T1 and T2 (both are, at root, disputes over what legal/ethical standard actually governs reception and legitimacy)." So the citation to §2H under T1's own grounding appears to be misattributed, and it is T2 — which explicitly and substantively is §2H's own subject — whose grounding is missing that citation.

**Why this matters:** This is the same "cited to the wrong nearby section" pattern Doc07_Round1_Review already found and fixed five times in this world's own build (a misattributed quotation, a wrong "tribune" citation, a wrong document for a "catholic"-pun claim, a missing second source, an omitted thinness finding) — a recurring, checkable failure mode in this world's own construction, not a one-off.

**Fix:** Move the §2H citation from T1's grounding to T2's; consider adding §2E to T1's grounding instead, since that is where T1 is actually named.
