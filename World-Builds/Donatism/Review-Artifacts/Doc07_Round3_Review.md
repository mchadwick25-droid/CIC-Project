That claim is accurate for all three texts (Doc_04's own G3 Repetition test independently counts all three as "distinct Donatist-authored/voiced texts"), regardless of which are grammatically first-person. "First-person voice" is elaboration on top of that operative claim, not the claim itself, and it does not appear in §5's own version of the synthesis (§5 says only "not filtered through Optatus or Augustine," with no first-person claim). This is a precision nit in supporting language, not a defect in the finding the fix was written to repair, and it is offered as an optional future tightening rather than a required change — the same treatment Round 2 gave L-R2-2.
