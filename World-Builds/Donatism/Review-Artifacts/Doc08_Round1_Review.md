## Verdict: SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED

The document's core interpretive work is sound. Line-by-line comparison of every one of the eight gravity-by-gravity force connections in Section 5 against Doc_04 §3.1–§3.7's own "Forces-connection" bullets confirms six of the eight (G1, G3, G4, G5, D-A, T2) are accurate, careful restatements, not merely plausible-sounding paraphrases. T1's connection — the specific case this review was asked to check hardest, since Doc_04 §3.6 states it spans Cells 1B through 2B in a carefully-reasoned way — is reproduced correctly in both Section 5 and the Force Index (1B-2, 2A-1, 2B-4, in that order, matching Doc_04's own sequencing of the three instances). D-A's connection is not overstated: Doc_04 §3.5's explicit finding that "no clean forces-connection is assertable beyond the hostile record" is carried forward almost verbatim rather than smoothed into something tidier. The Cross-Cell Connections in Section 4 are genuinely distinct relationships, and the Force Index's own Cross-Cell Connections column reproduces all eight of them, with correct direction, without a single mismatch. Both required transmission entries (2B-2, 3B-2) are present as their own dedicated forces, satisfying the Transmission Specificity Principle with named mechanisms rather than "the tradition" generically.

Against that, three High-severity findings and a cluster of Medium findings mean the document's own self-certification in Sections 8 and 9 is not fully earned, in ways that closely track the exact failure modes IJC's own Doc_08 review rounds found in this project before. Most concretely: **the document repeatedly claims "twelve identified forces" (Section 7 twice, Section 8's Proportionality Principle, and the closing line) when Section 3 actually documents thirteen** — a plain miscount in the document's own headline arithmetic, the very thing Section 9's own completion checklist exists to certify. **Open Items and Process Findings item 2 makes a comparative claim about its own data that its own data contradicts** — asserting D-A has the narrowest force-connection count of any classified gravity, when T2 (with exactly one connected force against D-A's two) is narrower still. And **the From-Within Principle's own "CONFIRMED" status, which explicitly claims no Layer 2 entry contains a cell-reference or similar marker, is falsified by Force 3A-1's own Layer 2**, which names a gravity by its analytical ID ("the same conviction G5 states throughout the window") — precisely the "strand labels/force-ID references leaking into the world's-own-experience voice" failure mode IJC's Round 1 review caught and required fixed. A further pattern of Medium-severity propagation gaps between Section 5, the Force Index's main table, and its own inverted "By Connected Gravity" table — plus a misattached superlative claim and a Confidence Assessment section that silently omits one of the thirteen forces — round out a document whose individual arguments are well-reasoned but whose cross-references and self-certifications have not been checked against each other with the rigor the genre requires.

**Counts:** 3 High-severity, 5 Medium-severity, 3 Low-severity finding.

## HIGH SEVERITY

### H1. The document claims "twelve identified forces" four separate times; Section 3 actually documents thirteen

**Claim checked:** Section 7 ("None of this document's twelve identified forces rests primarily on Dominant Modern Reconstruction" / "None of the twelve identified forces is itself contested"); Section 8, Proportionality Principle ("All twelve identified forces received full three-layer treatment; none was abbreviated to Layer 1 only. ... every one of these twelve forces has a genuine, traceable formation impact"); the closing line ("Six cells, twelve identified forces, all three-layer...").

**What I found:** Counting every `#### Force` heading in Section 3 directly: 1A-1 (1); 1B-1, 1B-2 (2); 2A-1, 2A-2 (2); 2B-1, 2B-2, 2B-3, 2B-4 (4); 3A-1, 3A-2 (2); 3B-1, 3B-2 (2). Total = **13**, confirmed independently by `grep -c "^#### Force"` and by counting the Force Index table's own rows, which also runs to 13 (1A-1 through 3B-2). Cell 2B alone carries four forces, not three — a legitimate structure under the template ("Add additional 2B forces as the ecology supports"), but the document's own summary arithmetic never accounts for the fourth. Every place the document states a total count of forces says "twelve"; none says "thirteen."

**Why this matters:** This is not a cosmetic slip. The miscount sits inside the Proportionality Principle's own certification ("all twelve... none was abbreviated") and the document's own closing summary line — exactly the self-monitoring text a reader relies on to trust the rest of the certification without re-deriving it independently. A wrong headline count in a document whose entire structural claim is "six cells, N forces, all three-layer, all confirmed gravities connected" undermines confidence in the surrounding certifications until it is checked, which is what this review had to do.

**Fix:** Change every instance of "twelve" to "thirteen" (Section 7 ×2, Section 8, closing line), and re-verify that no other summary count in the document (e.g., any residual count of forces per cell) was derived from the wrong total.

### H2. Open Items and Process Findings item 2 makes a false comparative claim: D-A is not the narrowest-connected classified gravity — T2 is

**Claim checked:** "D-A's own force connections (2A-1, 2B-4) remain narrower than every other classified gravity's own connection count, consistent with — not contradicting — Doc_04 §3.5's own finding that this candidate carries the sharpest confidence divergence in the entire gravity-discovery document."

**What I found:** Counting connected forces per gravity directly from Section 5's own "Gravity-by-Gravity Force Connections" (cross-checked against the Force Index's own inverted "By Connected Gravity" table, where the two agree):

| Gravity | Connected forces (Section 5 / inverted table) | Count |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | 1B-1, 1B-2, 2B-1, 2B-4 | 4 |
| G3 | 1A-1, 2A-2, 2B-3, 3B-2 | 4 |
| G4 | 1B-2, 2B-4, 3A-1, 3A-2 | 4 |
| G2 | 1B-1, 2B-1, 2A-1 | 3 |
| G5 | 2A-1, 3A-1, 3A-2 | 3 |
| T1 | 1B-2, 2A-1, 2B-4 | 3 |
| **D-A** | **2A-1, 2B-4** | **2** |
| **T2** | **2B-4** | **1** |

T2 connects to exactly one force (2B-4). D-A connects to two. T2 is therefore the narrowest-connected classified gravity in the document, not D-A — the claim in Open Items item 2 is the reverse of what the document's own Section 5 and Force Index actually show.

**Why this matters:** This is the same failure mode IJC's own Round 1 review found in its Proportionality note — a specific, falsifiable comparative claim about the document's own data that does not survive checking the data. It appears in a section whose entire purpose is honest self-monitoring of exactly this kind of thing, which makes the error more consequential than an equivalent slip in ordinary prose.

**Fix:** Either correct the claim to state T2 is the narrowest-connected classified gravity (and reconsider whether that fact, rather than D-A's, is the one worth flagging alongside Doc_04 §3.5's confidence-divergence finding — T2's single connection is a function of it being a tightly-scoped, single-episode Tensional gravity, not an evidentiary-divergence signal the way D-A's scope-qualification is), or drop the comparative claim entirely if it isn't load-bearing for anything else in the document.

### M3. A misattached superlative: "the most direct forces-connection of any gravity in this document" is presented as Doc_04's finding about Force 3A-2 specifically, when Doc_04 attributes it to Cell 3A as a whole

**Claim checked:** Doc_08 §5, "Where Forces and Gravities Most Tightly Cohere": "G5 and Force 3A-2 (the Vandal capture) are the second-strongest: Doc_04 §3.7 already states this is 'the most direct forces-connection of any gravity in this document,' and nothing in this forces analysis disturbs that finding." The same attribution recurs at Force 3A-2's own Layer 3: "This is, on Doc_01's own terms, the single most direct forces-connection of any gravity in this document."

**What I found:** Doc_04 §3.7's actual text reads: "Cell 3A — **the most direct forces-connection of any gravity in this document**: the 411 Conference's verdict, the penal legislation that followed, **and** the Vandal capture of Carthage (439) **together** remove the specific power... this gravity is defined in refusal of." The superlative's grammatical subject in Doc_04 is the joint effect of both 3A forces "together," not Force 3A-2 (the Vandal capture) in isolation — Doc_08 itself splits what Doc_04 treated as one combined Cell-3A finding into two separate forces (3A-1, 3A-2) and then assigns the full weight of the superlative to only one of them, correspondingly undercrediting Force 3A-1 ("presses this refusal to its sharpest test" — a distinctly lesser role than "the most direct connection"). Doc_01 §2's own quoted language (which Doc_04 itself cites) does attach specifically to 439/the Vandal capture, so the narrower attribution is defensible as an interpretation — but Doc_08 presents it as a direct, unqualified restatement of Doc_04's own finding ("Doc_04 §3.7 already states this"), when Doc_04's own finding was about the two forces jointly.

**Why this matters:** This is the same "misattached quotations between adjacent forces" pattern IJC's own Doc_08 reviews found repeatedly — content from one force's own combined finding presented as if squarely attached to just one of its two component parts, without disclosing that the attribution required a choice Doc_04 itself did not make explicitly.

**Fix:** Either restore the joint framing ("Force 3A-1 and Force 3A-2 together are the most direct forces-connection..."), matching Doc_04's own "together" language, or — if the narrower, Doc_01-anchored reading is preferred — say so explicitly ("this document reads Doc_04's joint Cell-3A finding as attaching most specifically to Force 3A-2, on Doc_01 §2's own narrower language") rather than citing Doc_04 §3.7 as though it already said this about 3A-2 alone.

## LOW SEVERITY

### L1. The Proportionality Principle's "fullest"/"most restrained" comparative claims are asserted with more confidence than a plain read of the entries supports

**What I found:** Section 8 claims Force 2A-2 (Macarian repression) and Force 2B-4 (Maximianist fracture) "receive the fullest Layer 1 documentary grounding," and Force 3B-1's Layer 2/3 are "correspondingly the most restrained in this document." On direct comparison, Force 2B-4's Layer 1 is genuinely the longest and most detailed in the document — that half of the claim holds. But Force 2A-2's own Layer 1 (two sentences, roughly 45 words) is shorter and less detailed than Force 2A-1's (five distinct dated events across four decades, roughly 75 words) or Force 1B-2's — "fullest" is defensible only if "documentary grounding" means multiple-independent-corroborating-source count (2A-2 is corroborated by two distinct Donatist-voiced texts) rather than Layer 1 length or event-density, a distinction the document doesn't make explicit. Separately, Force 3B-1's Layer 2 (~45 words) is not shorter than Force 3B-2's own Layer 2 (~38 words, the shortest "not recoverable" entry in the document) — "correspondingly the most restrained" doesn't hold on a word-count comparison, though it may hold on a different, unstated criterion (e.g., Inferential/Thin vs. a flat "not recoverable" statement).

**Why this matters:** Not a black-and-white error like H1/H2, but the same genre of claim IJC's Round 1 review found unsupported by the document's own data ("Force 1B-1... in fact connects to more gravities... than two of the four 'fullest-treatment' forces"). The underlying idea (depth proportional to attestation strength) is sound; the specific comparative claims naming which forces exemplify it are not clearly demonstrated by the text as written.

**Fix:** Either state explicitly which criterion "fullest"/"most restrained" is being measured by (corroborating-source count, not length), or soften the claim to avoid a specific ranking the text doesn't clearly support.
