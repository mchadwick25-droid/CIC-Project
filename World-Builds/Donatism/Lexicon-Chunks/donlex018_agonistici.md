## Quick Meaning

We call ourselves *agonistici* — "contestants," those who strive for the truth — a name our opponents twisted into "Circumcellion" and used to paint us as itinerant fanatics; what that name actually names, and how far the violent portrait is deserved, is a real and unsettled question this world's own record cannot fully answer on its own.

---

## World Meaning

In the countryside of Numidia, especially, there are those among us who call themselves *agonistici* — contestants, strivers, the ones who fight for the truth rather than merely hold it. Our opponents do not use that name. They call us Circumcellions — those who linger around the martyrs' shrines — and in their telling, that lingering is inseparable from violence: attacks on our rivals' own clergy, forced rebaptisms, disruption wherever our cause needed defending by force rather than argument. We do not pretend this reputation came from nowhere; the empire itself has taken legal notice of us, by name, more than once. But we do not accept our opponents' own account of what we are, or how typical that violence actually was among us, as the last word on the matter either.

What is certain is this: we exist, as a recognized body, concentrated in Numidia's countryside specifically — not invented by hostile pens out of nothing. What is far less certain is how much of the character our opponents give us is true to the whole of us, and how much is the picture a hostile hand naturally paints of country people it already despises for being poor, for speaking their own tongue, and for taking their own faith to lengths the cities found alarming. We hold both of these at once: our own existence and our own self-chosen name are not in serious doubt; what we were actually like, on any given day, in any given place, is a harder question than either our opponents or, honestly, we ourselves can now fully settle from what survives.

This is not the whole of Numidia, and it is not the whole of this church. In that region specifically, our strength runs deep and wide — for many there, to belong to this world was simply to belong to the ordinary church of one's own village. What is contested is narrower and sharper: whether the specific, vivid, often violent character our opponents attribute to us describes this whole regional strength, or only ever described a smaller, more provocative element within it that the hostile record chose to make stand for the rest.

## Ecological Function

This group's own existence is the direct object of specific imperial legislation, tying it to this world's own refusal of the state's authority to adjudicate legitimacy. Its reported involvement in the Maximianist suppression connects it to this world's sharpest internal crisis. But its own confirmed standing in this ecology is regional, not world-wide — a Numidian phenomenon, not a claim about this world's membership as a whole — and any account of this world's own ordinary believer that quietly borrows this group's character to stand for the whole movement has overreached what the evidence supports.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
"Circumcellion" as this world's own accepted self-description — a violent fringe faction whose reputation can be taken at face value as simply what this part of the movement was like, since the hostile sources are detailed and vivid.

**World Hearing:**
*Agonistici* is our own name, not our opponents'; the violent, itinerant character attached to it reaches this world's own record almost entirely through the very people who had every reason to make us look as dangerous as possible. Our bare existence, in Numidia specifically, is beyond real doubt. What we were actually like beyond that bare fact is a live, unresolved question — one this world's own honest record cannot close by itself, and one a fair account should name as open rather than settle by repeating the hostile portrait as though it were simply what happened.

## CT Contest Type

**Meaning + historical scope.** Whether this group's hostile literary characterization — itinerant, socially marginal, prone to violence — substantially reflects its actual character and scale, or is substantially a polemical construction of its Catholic and imperial opponents, is live scholarly contest. Frend's older reading treats more of the hostile portrait as genuinely evidentiary, tied to a native-social-protest thesis about rural Numidia's own Punic- and Libyan-speaking substrate; Shaw's *Sacred Violence* is the standard corrective, reading the same portrait as considerably overstated against the group's own genuinely independent core (its bare existence, attested in imperial legislation). This world's own construction does not adjudicate the contest and does not treat it as settled; it is confirmed **Supporting only for the Numidian regional sub-ecology**, not as an ecology-wide gravity, precisely because the character question this contest turns on remains open (Doc_04 §3.5).
