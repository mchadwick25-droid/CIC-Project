## 0. What This Document Is (and Is Not)

Step 3 produces a **candidate vocabulary list** — this world's own terms, with preliminary definitions, initial tier estimates, tags, and Author-Gravity risk flags — generated *before* gravity discovery, so that Doc_04 tests gravities in this world's own language rather than importing modern analytical vocabulary (Framework V7.4 Step 3). It is **not** the full lexicon (that is Doc_06, after ecological reconstruction), and it does **not** produce deployment chunks.

Three current-cycle disciplines govern this list:

1. **No permanent numbers yet.** `donlex` chunk numbers are assigned at Doc_06, not here. Candidates are listed by name only.
2. **Tiers are preliminary estimates, pending Doc_04.** Every tier below is an initial estimate. Final tier depends on this build's own gravity discovery (Doc_04) and is set at Doc_06.
3. **Strand attribution: N/A (strand-singular).** Doc_01 §4 found this world strand-singular for internal construction — neither Tyconius's own dissent nor the Maximianist schism rises to strand status — so no strand attribution is carried on candidates below. The Circumcellion/*agonistici* self-vs.-outsider naming question and Tyconius's own atypicality surface instead as the **[PV]** (Plural Voices) tag on individual candidates, not as strand divisions.

**Tag legend** (per the lexicon-index standard): **AS** = signature vocabulary (distinctively this world's own); **SC** = shared vocabulary (held with the wider Christian tradition, or with this world's own rival, but functioning distinctively here); **DR** = high distortion risk; **TC** = technical concept; **RT** = likely runtime term; **PV** = plural voices (the world's own record holds more than one account); **CT** = contested tradition (live scholarly contest — Article 26; every CT candidate names its contest in §4 below).

**Candidate-discovery method, per Framework V7.4 Step 3's own instruction:** candidates are drawn from `Source_Registry.md`'s own **Native**-boundary entries and from Doc_01's and Doc_02's own findings built on them — not from Doc_02's raw ecology discussion directly, since Doc_02 also names material Step 2's own Boundary Check treats as comparandum or excluded (the Novatian row, Registry row 29, is Excluded and contributes no candidate here). Doc_02 §9 item 4 names three terms this world's own record actually uses — *traditor*, *agonistici*, *Deo laudes* — as priority candidates over generic scholarly labels, and flags "Circumcellion" itself for an explicit lexicon-status decision rather than default inclusion; §1 below settles that decision.

**Author-Gravity discipline (carried from Doc_02 §1–§2).** This world's evidentiary record is unusually concentrated: nearly all vendored material for the rebaptism/purity cluster and the Circumcellion/*agonistici* cluster passes through **Optatus** and, above all, **Augustine** — the hostile, eventually-victorious party's own literature. Independent, non-hostile-mediated attestation is narrower still than a first look suggests, and this document distinguishes two things Doc_02 §6 itself bundles together under one "independently attested" heading: the *Deo laudes* acclamation is genuinely non-mediated (epigraphic — stone, no hostile literary framing at any point, Registry row 27/48); the Circumcellion group's bare **existence** is independently attested (imperial legislation, CTh 16.5.52, vendored 2026-09-07 and now directly confirmed to name the group by its own text — "circumcelliones argenti pondo decem" — Doc_02 §1); but the specific word *agonistici* — the group's own reported **self-designation** — reaches this record only through Augustine's own report of it, and is Augustine-mediated in that literal transmission sense even though Doc_02 §6 judges him a reliable *reporter* of this specific fact despite being a hostile *interpreter* of the group's character. The "AG-risk" column below names the dominant hostile voice where a candidate's specific content rests on it, consistent with Doc_02's own Transmission History findings, and marks this existence/self-designation distinction explicitly on the Agonistici entry itself (Cluster 5).

## 2. Author-Gravity Concentration (screen for Doc_04)

The single largest concentration is **Augustine**, dominant across the rebaptism/purity cluster (Reception without Reordination, and the specific application of Rebaptism to the Maximianist case) and, together with **Optatus**, across the Circumcellion/*agonistici* cluster's own *character* claims and its own *self-designation* term (though not its bare existence, which is independently attested via imperial legislation, vendored 2026-09-07 — Doc_02 §1, §6). This is the vocabulary-level expression of the Author Gravity concentration Doc_01 §7 item 1 and Doc_02 §1 both name as this world's central evidentiary problem: if Doc_04 tests candidate gravities using vocabulary whose specific content is Augustine-concentrated, gravity discovery risks confirming Augustine's own argumentative framing as the ecology's own emphasis. **Doc_04 must keep this screen active** when testing any gravity resting on Reception without Reordination or on the Circumcellion/*agonistici* cluster's character claims — including the *agonistici* self-designation term itself, which reaches this record only through Augustine's own report of it and should not be treated as evidentially on par with a genuinely non-mediated source. The *Deo laudes* acclamation (epigraphic — stone, no hostile literary mediation at any point) is this world's one clearly non-Augustine-mediated anchor; the Circumcellion group's bare existence is a second, narrower anchor (independent legislative attestation, the law's own text vendored 2026-09-07), distinct from anything about the group's character or its own reported name for itself.

Secondary concentration: **Optatus**, dominant for the "Donatist"/naming-cluster's own documentary record (Cluster 2), since the only vendored quotation of the Donatist party's own petition language reaches this document through his own hostile narrative frame.

## 3. Shared-Root Vocabulary — World #6/#8 Boundary Awareness

Doc_01 §6 carries forward two already-settled boundaries: World #6 (Imperial and Juridical Christianity) is a productive contrast, not an overlap risk. World #8 (Latin Pastoral-Congregational Christianity, Cyprian and Augustine, **not yet built**) is the genuine adjacency — this world inherits Cyprian's own rebaptism and purity theology directly (Doc_01 §6), and several candidates above (**Rebaptism, Purity, Church/Ecclesia, Schism, Martyr/Martyrdom**) sit on vocabulary World #8 will also need when it is eventually built. Per the Interpretive Lexicon Development Framework Part VII (Cross-World Consistency), a term shared across two worlds' lexicons should be developed independently within each world's own entry, not cross-referenced as though it carries equivalent weight or meaning in both — apparent similarity is a finding for that later world's own Differentiation Testing, not an assumption built in here. This document does not resolve anything about World #8's own future vocabulary; it names the five terms above so a future builder opening World #8's own Doc_03 does not treat this document's entries as settling that world's own case.

## 4. Contested-Tradition (CT) Candidate and Its Contest (Article 26)

One candidate carries **[CT]**.

1. **Agonistici / Circumcellion** — *contest: meaning and historical scope.* Whether the group's hostile literary characterization (itinerant, socially marginal, prone to violence) substantially reflects its actual character and scale, or is substantially a polemical construction of its Catholic and imperial opponents, is live scholarly contest — Frend's older native-social-protest reading treats more of the hostile portrait as evidentiary; Shaw's *Sacred Violence* (Registry row 24) is the standard corrective, reading the same portrait as considerably overstated against the group's own genuinely independent core: its bare existence, attested in imperial legislation (CTh 16.5.52, vendored 2026-09-07). The *agonistici* self-designation itself, and any specific claim about the group's typical conduct or rural/itinerant character, reach this record only through Augustine's and Optatus's own report and are not part of that independent core, even where Doc_02 §6 judges Augustine's report of the self-designation reliable as reportage. Boyd (1905, Registry row 49) restates the hostile characterization uncritically and is explicitly not licensed for it (Doc_02 §3). This document does not adjudicate the contest; Doc_06 should carry both readings forward, tagged, rather than resolve to one.
