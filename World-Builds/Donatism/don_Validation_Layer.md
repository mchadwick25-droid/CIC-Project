This document therefore validates what exists — the construction record, Doc_01 through Doc_09 (Story Inventory) and the World Profile — and names plainly, category by category, which validation work genuinely cannot be performed yet, rather than either skipping those categories silently or performing a hollow substitute against a Representative that does not exist. It means this document does **not** conclude the world is Freeze-eligible (Section 9 below states this explicitly).

## Section 3 — Ecological Integrity Testing

- **Balance Testing.** This world's own ecology is deliberately *not* balanced across all lenses, and the imbalance is disclosed and evidence-tracked, not an unexamined skew: Doc_07 §7 states plainly that Doctrinal/Philosophical is "thin by its own account everywhere outside the purity/rebaptism cluster," while Ethical/Legal "is unusually well-evidenced... one of this document's strongest sections." **PASS** on the Framework's own terms (balance proportional to what the evidence actually supports, not artificially equalized across lenses that were never equally documented).
- **Reduction Testing.** This world does not reduce to a single explanatory factor — Doc_04 confirmed four Primary gravities (G1, G2, G3, G5), two Supporting (G4, D-A), and two Tensional (T1, T2), with genuine, disclosed tension between the Tensional pair and the Primaries they qualify (T1 tests G5's own consistency; T2 tests G1's). **PASS.**
- **Complexity Testing.** Tensions and ambiguities are preserved, not resolved by hindsight: Doc_07's own Integrative Observation (copied verbatim into the World Profile, Section 10) states directly that this world "did not resolve that gap by softening the doctrine, and did not treat the gap as proof the doctrine was hollow" — the T1/T2 tensions are held open in every document that touches them (Doc_04, Doc_07, the World Profile Section 7, and Doc_09's own donstory007 and donstory008), never quietly settled toward whichever reading is more comfortable. **PASS.**

- **Worship Integration Testing.** Worship is reconstructed as integrated into, not separate from, this world's own central doctrine: Doc_07 §2A (Ritual/Practical) states this world's "ritual life does not merely express its doctrine; it is the doctrine's own most concrete evidence" — rebaptism, the annual martyr commemoration, and the *Deo laudes* acclamation are each tied directly back to G1's purity logic and G3's martyr-cult identity, not treated as a separate devotional layer alongside the doctrine. **PASS.**
