## Section 2 — Integration Lenses

### 2A — Emotional Ecology [REQUIRED]

**What this lens reveals for this world:** Doc_05 catalogued emotional registers; holding them together against the Tier 1 vocabulary reveals that this world's affective system is *organized by direction* — every documented emotion is a mode of leaning: thanksgiving leans on what was given, watchfulness leans toward what is coming, dipsychia is the failure to lean one way.

**Dominant emotional register:** *Eucharistia as disposition* — thanksgiving practiced until it becomes temperament. The evidence is structural, not anecdotal: the world's central weekly act is named for an emotion-act (Doc_06 entry 5); its meal-prayers thank God even "for the enjoyment of food and drink" while the same community rehearses arrest-procedure (Did. 9–10 beside Pliny's record, Doc_02); Ignatius writes doxologies from a prison convoy. Mature practitioners of this world are people who have learned to give thanks *from inside exposure* — gratitude with its back to the wall. Second register, inseparable: *longing* — Maranatha at the table (Doc_06 entry 28), the sojourner's homeward lean (paroikia, Tier 1 entry 4). The pairing is the world's emotional signature: **grateful longing** — full hands, unarrived home.

**Emotionally dangerous states:** the world's own diagnostics, from the Tier 1/2 vocabulary: *dipsychia* (Hermas — the hedged heart, the incense-pinch of the soul; Doc_06 entry 18) — dangerous because the whole ecology runs on undivided allegiance (kyrios, to onoma); *cooled love and quarrel* (1 Clem.'s horror at schism; Did. 14.2's reconciliation-rule — the assembly's dissolution from within is feared more than the magistrate, Doc_05 §7); *presumption and despair* around post-baptismal failure (the twin errors Hermas' one-metanoia rule fights on both fronts, Doc_05 §2); and *self-chosen intensity* (Quintus' volunteering, refused — Doc_04's declined candidate; the world distrusts manufactured zeal).

**Emotional arc of formation:** entry feels like *rupture and adoption* — grief for severed household ties absorbed by new kin (Doc_05 §1 [Inhabited] passage's sourced texture); the middle years feel like *practiced vigilance* — the daily small refusals, fasts, and prayers building an allegiance that no longer startles (Did. 8's rhythm); maturity feels like *settled, tested gladness* — the register of Polycarp's "eighty-six years I have served him, and he has done me no wrong": gratitude and fearlessness fused, the world's emotional finish-line (Doc_02 §2.7).

**Gravities most visible in the emotional texture:** G5 (the Table names the dominant register), G6 (the Expectation names the longing), G3 (the Name names the vigilance), T9 (Purity/Mercy names the shame-and-restoration cycle).

**What Emotional Ecology reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** the emotions are not a list but a *single economy of directed attention* — thanksgiving backward, longing forward, vigilance outward, mercy inward — and dipsychia is the world's name for that economy breaking. This is why Hermas' odd coinage deserved its lexicon place: the world had to invent a word for its own characteristic failure.

### 2B — Philosophical Ecology [REQUIRED — grounded in 2A]

**What this lens reveals for this world:** the affective economy of 2A rests on an ontology of *gift and allegiance*: reality is something given by one Maker, and the fundamental act of a creature is to answer the Giver — with thanks (the right answer) or with service to "dead gods" (the wrong one, Did. 6.3).

**Fundamental ontological assumptions:** one God, Maker of all, whose creation is good and giveable ("you created all things for your Name's sake, and gave food and drink to men for enjoyment, that they might give you thanks" — Did. 10, the world's ontology in a table-prayer); the created order as *orderly gift* (1 Clem. 20's liturgical cosmos — seasons, stars, and seas keeping peace under command); the gods of the city as *dead* — not rival metaphysics but necro-ontology: idols are corpses pretending to be causes (Did. 6.3; the apologists' whole argument). Against Marcion at mid-span the world stakes everything on the identity of Maker and Father — the one interpretive-metaphysical decision with everything downstream (Doc_05 §8).

**Understanding of the human person:** a creature made for undivided allegiance, currently walking one of two ways (hai duo hodoi — the anthropology is a road-map, Doc_06 entry 12); capable of crossing over (baptisma as real transfer, not symbol of a private state); dangerously divisible (dipsychia); restorable within limits (one metanoia); destined for resurrection — the body is not a shell but the thing God raises (anastasis; Ignatius' insistence on the Kyrios' real flesh is anthropology as much as christology: what he did not truly have, we are not truly promised).

**Temporal assumptions:** time is a span between arrival and arrival — the Kyrios has come and will come (parousia), and the assemblies live in the between as sojourners (paroikia): engaged non-belonging is a *temporal* posture before it is a social one. The tower is still building; the pause is mercy (Hermas Vis. 3 — delay itself moralized as time-to-repent). History is going somewhere, and the somewhere has a table at it (Did. 9.4's gathering-prayer; basileia entries).

**How philosophical ecology grounds the emotional ecology:** if reality is gift, thanksgiving is realism; if the gods are dead, refusal is sanity; if time is a between, longing is accuracy; if allegiance is the creature's fundamental act, dipsychia is the fundamental disease. 2A's affective economy is 2B's ontology *felt*.

**Gravities most visible:** G3 (dead gods refused), G5 (gift answered), G6 (the between), G4 (a deposit matters only in a world where truth is handed, not discovered fresh each generation).

**What Philosophical Ecology reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** the world's boundary-conduct (idol-food casuistry, oath-refusal) and its meal are the *same metaphysical act* performed negatively and positively: refusing dead givers, thanking the living One. Ethics and eucharist are one ontology in two directions.

### 2C — Authority Structures [REQUIRED]

**What this lens reveals for this world:** authority in this world is *plural in ground and singular in test* — five competing grounds (appointment, conduct, Spirit, seniority, deposit-fidelity — Doc_05 §4), one universal audit: does the life carry the Way?

**Primary sources of authority:** the deposit itself outranks every officeholder (the Didache tests the teacher against the teaching, 11.1–2 — the message judges the messenger); within that, the grounds compete without a settled hierarchy: 1 Clement argues appointment-in-succession; the Didache honors conduct-tested prophets as "your high priests"; Hermas — a lay freedman — rebukes leaders on visionary mandate; Papias walks to seniority ("the living voice"); Ignatius urges the one-bishop pattern *because it is not yet the rule* (Doc_02 §2.3's against-the-grain finding).

**Transmission of authority:** one generation deep, in every direction — appointment reasoning reaches back exactly one link (apostles → appointed → "other approved men," 1 Clem. 42/44); teaching authority is apprenticed in the assembly, not schooled (the schools are the successor world's invention — Doc_01 boundary); prophetic authority cannot be transmitted at all, only recognized and tested per instance (Mand. 11).

**Sites of contestation (carried at full strength):** Corinth's deposition (the congregation seated them; may it unseat them? — 1 Clement says no; Corinth evidently thought yes; *we never hear Corinth's reasons*, Doc_02 §2.2); prophet versus office (T7 — Did. 15's "do not despise" is spoken to people who despised); the visionary layman versus the presbyters (Hermas' entire premise); at span's end, the New Prophecy detonating T7 trans-locally, and Victor's Rome discovering how far a bishop's writ runs (Doc_01 end-boundary evidence).

**Authority and formation:** whoever forms you, the *assembly* authorizes them — catechists teach a community-owned Way; prophets speak to (and are audited by) the gathering; even the proestos' thanksgiving is sealed by everyone's Amen (Justin 67 — the people's word is the liturgy's ratification). Formation-authority is congregational in a sense later worlds lose.

**Gravities most illuminated:** G4 (the deposit as super-authority), T7 (the whole lens is its anatomy), G1 (the assembly as authorizer).

**What Authority Structures reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** the world's *test-culture* — everything (prophets, teachers, travelers, even emotions) is subject to communal audit by conduct — is the mirror-image of its lack of settled structure. Where offices cannot yet guarantee, character must. The audit is the institution.

### 2D — Boundary Structures [REQUIRED — forces-integrated]

**What this lens reveals for this world:** the boundary is a *membrane, not a wall* — engineered to keep allegiance in and the gods out while letting everything else (trades, marriages, streets, letters, strangers) pass through daily. Its shape is exactly the shape of the forces pressing on it.

**Membership logic:** in by instruction-then-water (the audited crossing: Two Ways, communal fasting, baptism into the Name — Doc_05 §1); sustained by gathering and table; out by conduct that denies the Name or the Way (exclusion from the meal is the operative sanction — Did. 14.2's logic); back — once — by metanoia (T9's procedure). The lethal edge of membership is externally administered: the same confession that constitutes ("Christianus sum") convicts (Pliny's test — the state runs a sincerity-audit of its own, in reverse).

**Relation to what is outside:** triple-fronted, and differently textured on each front (a Doc_05/Doc_02 synthesis): toward the *city* — engaged non-belonging (paroikia: obey, work, pray for rulers, refuse the gods; 1 Clem. 60–61's prayer for the very authorities that endanger); toward the *synagogue* — the unhealed wound (T8: shared book, divided people, polemic that presupposes ongoing entanglement — Did. 8's "hypocrites," Ignatius' anti-sabbath polemic as against-the-grain evidence of continuing attraction); toward the *counterfeit teachers* — the audit (test, refuse, name: christemporos, Doc_06 entry 6's frame).

**Forces most visible in the boundary structures:** the accusation regime (Force-family 2A in Doc_08) explains the membrane's oddest feature — *low profile with high cohesion*: no buildings, no processions, pre-dawn hours, yet fierce interior definition; a wall would have been visible and fatal, a membrane survives. The cultic saturation of the city (Doc_08 1A) explains the casuistry's granularity (meat, oaths, festivals — the boundary runs through the shopping list). The mid-span counterfeit-flood (Doc_08 2B) explains why the membrane grew an *inward-facing* audit as elaborate as its outward one.

**Boundary contestation:** how much of the city is bearable? — the Didache's own "bear what you can" (6.2–3) states a negotiable perimeter around a non-negotiable core (idol-food), and the world's internal range (rigorist to accommodating) is documented at every point where evidence exists (Hermas' merchants; the apologists' civic loyalty; Revelation's milieu refusing far more). Whose calendar? (Quartodeciman dispute — the boundary's *temporal* front, T8). The Israel-front's whole placement is contested within the world (Barnabas' wall versus Justin's argued engagement versus the Didache's proximity — Doc_02 §2.9's pole-mapping).

**Boundary logic and formation:** members are formed *as the membrane* — each believer at work, at market, at the family table is the boundary in person, deciding case by case what passes. Hence conduct-catechesis (the Two Ways is boundary-training), hence the vigilance-register (2A), hence the audit-culture (2C). This world does not have border guards; it *is* its border.

**What Boundary Structures reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show:** internal coherence and external situatedness are one system — the world's most distinctive institutions (conduct-audit, membrane-boundary, low-profile assembly) are precise negatives of the forces (accusation regime, cultic saturation, counterfeit-flood). The ecology is a die cast by its pressures — and yet not merely reactive: what fills the membrane (thanksgiving, kinship, hope) is generated from inside (2A/2B).

### 2E — Formation Logic [REQUIRED — synthesis lens, applied last]

**What this lens reveals for this world:** the underlying logic: **this world forms people by gathering them, because for this world, gathering is how anything survives** — memory, kinship, allegiance, hope, the very ekklesia. The formation system is a weekly-rehearsed act of re-collection: scattered people, scattered fragments of the deposit, scattered emotions, gathered epi to auto and made one.

**The fundamental aim of formation:** a person who can *keep the Name with a whole heart among the dead gods until the Lord comes* — in the world's own terms: one who has left the way of death, is grafted among the adelphoi, gives thanks at the table, guards what was handed over, and waits without hedging. The finished portrait is documented in its extreme case (Polycarp's serene "eighty-six years") and its ordinary case (the tested member of Did. 11–13 and Mand. 11: known by conduct, trusted with guests, fund, and truth).

**The formation mechanism:** *incorporation, then rehearsal*. Incorporation: instruction in the Way → communal fasting → the water → the kiss → the table (Doc_05 §1's arc — formation begins as adoption, not curriculum). Rehearsal: the week itself is the pedagogy — thrice-daily prayer, two fasts, the Lord's Day gathering where the whole vocabulary is heard, sung, eaten, and answered with Amen; the boundary practiced daily at market and hearth; the audit practiced on every traveler and prophet. Transformation is understood as allegiance re-habituated: not illumination-by-study (Alexandria's future logic) nor grace-through-sacramental-order (the Latin future), but *belonging practiced until it holds under trial*. The trial — market-scale daily, tribunal-scale rarely — is the mechanism's proof-load, not its engine.

**The formation arc:** hearer → learner of the Way (weeks-to-months; no formalized catechumenate yet — Doc_02 honesty) → baptized kin → tested member (years: conduct observed at face-scale, roles recognized — host, deacon, widow, elder, prophet as gift and audit allow) → the mature: those whose fidelity has been weathered — the very old (Pothinus, Polycarp), the proven (Grapte with her charge), the confessors who came back from the tribunal alive. Stalling is named: the dipsychos, the round stone not yet squared, has a word and a mercy (one metanoia) waiting.

**How the lenses integrate as a formation system:** 2B's ontology (reality as gift demanding undivided answer) sets the aim; 2A's affective economy (grateful longing, vigilant mercy) is the aim's felt form; 2C's audit-culture is the quality-control a structure-poor world runs on; 2D's membrane is the formation-field (every member formed as the boundary in person); and the gathering is where all four are rehearsed at once. The organizing principle that makes this a *system*: **survival-by-assembly** — everything this world must keep, it keeps by gathering, because it has nothing else to keep things with: no buildings, no schools, no legal standing, no professional class, no state, no scale. One thing is always in the room: everyone.

**Formation logic and confirmed gravities:** G1 is the mechanism's site; G2 its product and premise; G3 its proof-load; G4 its content; G5 its central rehearsal; G6 its direction; T7/T8/T9 the three seams the rehearsal never quite closes — who speaks, whose book, who returns. The gravities and the formation logic are mutually explanatory in exactly the template's sense: the logic explains why these gravities organize; the gravities explain what the logic is for.

### 2F — Memory Structures [OPTIONAL — applied]

**Why applied:** this world's generative crisis *is* a memory crisis (Doc_01 §4; G4), and its distinctive memory-vehicles are documented.

**What this world remembered and how:** the Kyrios — in meal-prayer, hymn ("to Christ as to a god" before dawn), and the memoirs read aloud; the founders — as the handing generation (1 Clem.'s Peter and Paul "of our own generation," Papias' chains of asking); its own recent dead — the fallen-asleep, and at Smyrna the first documented martyr-anniversary (bones, grave, "birthday" — Doc_06 entry 7); its Aramaic beginnings — one word wide (Maranatha, a fossil kept on purpose). Mechanisms: assembly reading (a hearing world's archive), fixed prayer-forms, letters copied and re-read, the yearly Pascha (Quartodeciman or Sunday — the dispute proves the observance), and graves.

**The formation function of communal memory:** memory is *anti-scattering* — the same function as the gathering itself. A member carries a remembered world wider than their room: names across the sea (letters), a lineage behind their teacher (paradosis), a martyr behind their courage. What the formed carry that the unformed cannot: the sense that their small assembly is one gathering of a single scattered loaf (Did. 9.4).

**Gravities most illuminated:** G4, G6, G2.

**What Memory Structures reveals:** the world's memory is *forward-loaded* — it remembers in order to await (the meal remembers and asks the Lord to come in the same breath). Memory and expectation are one practice; the past is kept because it is the promise's receipt.

## Section 3 — Forces as Integration Lens

**Forces documentation status at time of writing:** Doc_08 IN PROGRESS (drafted in parallel; Step 1 preliminary identification and Step 2 forces-lens complete; six-cell matrix drafted). This section draws on that work.

**Forces most visible in the emotional texture:** the accusation regime and cultic saturation (2A/1A cells) explain vigilance-as-virtue; the memory crisis (1B) explains why *fidelity* outranks brilliance in the world's affective hierarchy; the delay (2B-internal) explains the moralization of waiting (grateful longing rather than fever). Knowing the forces, the emotional economy reads as *formed courage under sustainable pressure* — not trauma-response (the pressure was intermittent — Doc_02 [CT] carried) but weather-hardening.

**Forces most visible in the boundary structures:** treated at 2D (the membrane as force-negative); added here: the *hetaeriae ban* (a single administrative act visible in Pliny) shows the membrane's plasticity — the meal itself reorganized under pressure (evening supper suspended; the rite persisting pre-dawn). The world's practices bent without breaking; that plasticity is itself a formation fact (what must be kept is allegiance, not schedule).

**Forces most visible in the formation logic:** survival-by-assembly is force-legible end to end: no buildings (couldn't own), no schools (no scale, no safety), no professional class (no funds, no standing) — therefore formation had to run on the one resource the forces couldn't confiscate: the gathered people. The formation logic is the forces answered.

**Where forces and ecology are most tightly coherent:** the triple identity of the gathering — formative act (ecology), incriminating act (external force), and defiant act (the world's own experience: "when you are together, the powers of Satan are destroyed" — Ign. Eph. 13.1). One event, three descriptions, fully mutually explanatory. This is the build's highest-integration point.

**Where forces analysis complicates the ecology analysis:** (1) the aid economy reads internally as pure kinship-devotion; the forces lens adds that it was also the world's resilience infrastructure under pressure (prisoner-support, ransom, network-funds) — devotion and survival-function are the same practice, and reducing it to either falsifies it. (2) The audit-culture (2C) reads internally as deposit-fidelity; the forces lens shows it also as *scarcity of trust under infiltration risk* — a community that can be denounced learns to test strangers. The ecology documents under-state this; the construction record should not.

**The single most important thing the forces-as-integration-lens reveals:** this world's situation made its central religious act and its central vulnerability *the same act*. Everything distinctive in the ecology — membrane-boundary, audit-culture, kinship-economics, low-profile worship, even the emotional economy of grateful vigilance — follows from that identity. A world whose treasure and whose exposure coincide in one weekly hour will look exactly like this one.

## Section 4 — Cross-Lens Synthesis

**The central coherence:** *Everything this world must keep, it keeps by gathering — the assembly epi to auto is simultaneously its treasury, its school, its family, its altar, and its exposure, and formation is learning to be one of the gathered.* (Two sentences, world-vocabulary, and inapplicable to any neighboring world: Alexandria keeps by teaching, the Latin world by sacramental order under public roofs, the Desert by withdrawal — none *is* its gathering the way this world is.)

**What this world's distinctiveness consists of:** against its own-period neighbors and successors: no other registered world's formation is single-sited (Alexandria adds the school; Early Latin the basilica-and-catechumenate machinery; the Desert the cell). A practitioner formed here would find in every successor world a strange new thing: religious life that survives the week without the whole assembly — and would miss what no successor world provides in this form: the face-scale audit-kinship in which every member is known, tested, fed, and personally constitutes the boundary. What this world lacks is equally constitutive: no monastic elite, no learned class, no shrine, no settled hierarchy to defer to — flat necessity, not egalitarian theory (the record refuses the romantic reading: offices exist, constraint exists, slavery persists — Doc_05 honesty carried).

**The most significant cross-lens insight:** the world's three tensional gravities are the three places where *gathering itself* is contested — who may speak in the gathering (T7), whose book and calendar the gathering keeps (T8), who may return to the gathering (T9). The tensions are not flaws in the system; they are the system's price. A world that stakes everything on the assembly will fight precisely and only there. (Not visible in any single document; emerges only when the gravity register, the authority lens, and the boundary lens are held together.)

**What the cross-lens synthesis gives the Representative:** a voice that can *locate any question at the table* — asked about belief, it can speak of what is prayed and eaten; asked about belonging, of who is at the meal and who is missing from it; asked about suffering, of what the gathering costs and holds; asked about authority, of who is tested and how; asked about hope, of the prayer at the meal's end. A Representative built only from Doc_05/Doc_06 would know the facts of these domains; the synthesis gives the single room they all happen in.

## Section 5 — Integrative Observation

**Integrative Observation:**

**The scattered loaf.** This world knew itself in one image it prayed weekly over broken bread: *as this fragment was scattered upon the mountains and was gathered together and became one, so let your ekklesia be gathered from the ends of the earth into your kingdom.* Scattered is what the world was — households apart, cities apart, kin cut from kin by conversion, eyewitnesses gone, the Temple gone, members deniable and dispersible by any afternoon's accusation. Gathered is what it did about it: epi to auto, one room at a time, week upon week, until scattered people were one loaf — and gathered is what it awaited, the ends of the earth brought in. Formation in this world is the practice of being gathered: taken from the way of death into the kindred, re-collected weekly at the table, held together against quarrel and counterfeit and fear, kept for the final gathering. Lose this and every practice in the record falls apart into fragments — which is exactly what the world believed about itself without the gathering. (Image and vocabulary: Did. 9.4 with G1/G2/G6; the observation is the build's synthesis and carries Inferential/Thin confidence as synthesis, resting on Documented elements.)

*Test applied: a Representative retaining every fact but losing this insight would describe practices serially and miss that they are one act performed in many keys — the inhabitation would lose its center.*
