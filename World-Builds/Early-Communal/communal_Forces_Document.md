## Section 2 — Preliminary Forces Identification (as recorded at Step 1, before source work)

**What generated this world:** the removal, within one generation, of everything that had located the movement — eyewitnesses dead, Temple destroyed, synagogue closing, legal innocuousness gone — falling on communities already equipped with a Kyrios-confession, a meal, a baptism, scriptures, and a network habit.
**What larger world it was embedded in:** the high empire at maximum stability — roads, cities, koine (enabling); cultic saturation and the emperor's cult (exposing); associational culture and diaspora synagogues (the templates neighbors parsed it by); the household (its given container).
**What it was responding to at its origin:** the memory crisis; the accusation regime; rival teachers within; the delay; the unresolved question of Israel.
**What it was refusing:** the gods in every register; the full-Torah requirement *and* Marcion's severed Bible; docetic denial of the flesh; volunteered martyrdom; the culture's verdict of superstitio.
**How this governed Step 2 scope:** outside witnesses elevated to first-class evidence; manuals and letters treated as ecological core; the Eusebian filter and proto-orthodox survivorship expected and named; silences (Torah-observant, rural, enslaved, women, lapsed, "heretics" in their own words) treated as transmission effects; material emptiness read as data. (Doc_02 executed this scope; see its §1 and §8.)

## Section 3 — The Six-Cell Matrix

### CELL 1A — INITIATING / EXTERNAL

#### Force 1A-1: The destruction of the Temple and the reordering of Judaism (66–70/135)

**Layer 1 — Historical Event:** the Jewish revolt and Rome's destruction of Jerusalem and the Temple (70 CE) — Documented; the Bar Kokhba aftermath (135) completing Jerusalem's loss — Documented; the consequent long reorganization of Jewish life around text, synagogue, and emerging rabbinic authority, within which the Jesus-assemblies' position became progressively less tenable — Widely Accepted in outline, Contested in mechanism and pace (birkat ha-minim's dating and force — carried as tension; "parting of the ways" vs. "ways that never parted" — carried, Doc_02 §4).

**Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** the assemblies read the event through their own scriptures and their Kyrios' words: judgment had come as spoken; the true temple was not made with hands; the inheritance had passed to the people gathered in his Name. Grief and vindication are both audible, unevenly, in the range from the Didache's quiet proximity to Jewish practice to Barnabas' harsh forfeiture-claim. *Reported as the world's own self-understanding — not assessed for historical accuracy; confidence calibration applies to the historical-event layer only.*

**Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** cut loose from the movement's geographic and cultic anchor, formation re-anchored in the portable and gatherable: assembly, meal, deposit (G1, G4, G5). Simultaneously opened the wound that never closes in-span — Israel's scriptures held by a people leaving Israel's practice (T8) — which shaped calendar (Quartodeciman front), polemical vocabulary ("the hypocrites," Did. 8), catechesis (Two Ways' Jewish moral inheritance kept), and the refused Marcionite exit.

#### Force 1A-2: The Neronian precedent — the Name made actionable (64)

**Layer 1:** Nero's scapegoating of Rome's Christians for the fire (64) — Documented (Tacitus, Ann. 15.44); its legal legacy: by Pliny's day (c. 112) profession of the name alone sufficed for execution, without proof of crimes — Documented for Bithynia; generalized as the span's legal pattern at Widely Accepted (with the Moss-line caution that enforcement was local, sporadic, accusation-driven — carried).

**Layer 2:** the world learned to speak of itself as bearing *to onoma* — suffering "for the Name," gathering in the Name, baptized into it. What Rome made a charge, the assemblies made a treasure; the tribunal's question ("Are you a Christian?") became the confession's form ("Christianus sum"). *Reported-Experience Status as above.*

**Layer 3:** produced G3's lethal edge and its vocabulary (onoma, homologia, martys); fused worship and risk (the gathering as the incriminating act — the build's highest-integration point, Doc_07 §3); shaped the emotional economy toward tested vigilance; and set the boundary's shape as membrane rather than wall (Doc_07 2D).

#### Force 1A-3: The imperial peace — roads, cities, koine (enabling condition)

**Layer 1:** the second-century empire's stability, urban network, safe roads and sea-lanes, and a common Greek — Documented as background; the assemblies' demonstrable use of it (letter-networks, traveling teachers, Ignatius' escorted route, Marcion's shipping fortune) — Documented.

**Layer 2:** the world did not thank Rome for its roads in any surviving line; it used them and prayed for the rulers who kept them while refusing their gods (1 Clem. 60–61) — the sojourner's double posture (paroikia) applied to infrastructure. *Reported-Experience Status.*

**Layer 3:** made the trans-local network possible and with it the world's unity-in-scatteredness (G1's cosmic register; G2's cross-sea kinship; G4's letter-borne deposit). The same peace carried the counterfeiters (2B-1) — the roads ran both ways.

### CELL 1B — INITIATING / INTERNAL

#### Force 1B-1: The inherited equipment — confession, meal, washing, scriptures, network habit

**Layer 1:** the post-70 assemblies received from the founding generation a Kyrios-confession with devotional practice already attached (hymnody "as to a god" — Pliny; Hurtado's early-devotion case, Widely Accepted), a meal and a baptism already central, Israel's scriptures in Greek, and the habit of trans-local address — Documented as inheritance by their earliest texts' unargued assumption of all five.

**Layer 2:** the world experienced these not as options but as *the things handed over* — what one received, kept, and handed on (paradosis). Its prayers kept even the founders' Aramaic (Maranatha) — fidelity audible at the level of syllables.

**Layer 3:** grounded every gravity: the confession (kyrios) gave G3 its positive core; the meal became G5; the washing the boundary's gate; the scriptures G4's body and T8's battlefield; the network habit G2's reach. The world's distinctive work was not inventing but *keeping-under-new-conditions* — its creativity (Gospels, offices, tests) is all preservation-engineering.

#### Force 1B-2: The death of the eyewitnesses — the memory crisis as conviction

**Layer 1:** by c. 70–100 the founding generation was dying or dead; the written Gospels, the succession-reasoning of 1 Clem. 42/44, and Papias' living-voice anxiety are this crisis' documentary precipitate — Documented texts; the crisis-frame as their unifying cause is Dominant Modern Reconstruction (Doc_04's C4 reservation, restated here rather than smoothed).

**Layer 2:** the world spoke of it as the passing of "our generation's" pillars (1 Clem. 5 on Peter and Paul) and answered with relay-language: what was received must be delivered whole; the one who asks the elders is nearer the spring. *Reported-Experience Status.*

**Layer 3:** produced G4 entire — the test-culture, the office-rationale, the codex-borne memoirs, the embryonic rule of practice; weighted the world's virtue-hierarchy toward fidelity over originality; and pre-loaded the ending forces (the guarding apparatus, once grown, becomes the successor world's skeleton — see 3B-1).

### CELL 2A — ONGOING / EXTERNAL

#### Force 2A-1: The accusation regime (the span's legal weather)

**Layer 1:** Trajan's rescript (c. 112): Christians not to be hunted; anonymous accusations inadmissible; the accused who sacrifice go free; the obstinate die — Documented. Enforcement therefore local, occasional, neighbor-driven; datable flashpoints (Smyrna c. 155; Rome c. 165; Lyons 177; Scilli 180) against long quiet stretches; mass lapse documented (Pliny's twenty-year deserters) — Documented episodes; "sporadic, not systematic" as the span-wide characterization: Widely Accepted (Moss-line vs. martyr-memory tension carried at full strength — the *felt* intensity is Contested).

**Layer 2:** the world called this living "under the Name" — every member deniable by an afternoon's accusation, every gathering a quiet risk, the tribunal rare but total. Its own literature schooled members for the hour ("do not praise those who hand themselves over" — and do not break) while its manuals barely mention it — the weather, not the climate of every day. *Reported-Experience Status.*

**Layer 3:** sustained G3 across the span; kept the boundary a membrane (visibility itself dangerous); gave the audit-culture its edge (infiltration consciousness); made lapse-and-return (T9) a permanent pastoral fact (Pliny's deserters; Hermas' whole occasion); and generated the martyr-memory practice at its flashpoints (Smyrna's annual "birthday" — the calendar's first expansion).

#### Force 2A-2: Cultic saturation — the gods at every table

**Layer 1:** civic festivals, guild banquets, household shrines, oaths by the emperor's genius, meat markets supplied from altars — the ordinary religious infrastructure of every city, Documented abundantly from the non-Christian side; the assemblies' resulting daily casuistry Documented from theirs (Did. 6.3; the apologists; Rev. 2's milieu).

**Layer 2:** "the worship of dead gods" — the world's necro-ontology (Doc_07 2B): the city's divine landlords are corpses, and every social occasion is an invitation to serve them. The burden is negotiable at the edges ("bear what you can"), never at the core.

**Layer 3:** made formation boundary-training (the Two Ways as departure-manual); put the boundary through the shopping list (eidolothyta); generated the slander-front (refusers read as haters of humankind); and gave thanksgiving its polemical depth — eucharistia as allegiance to the living Giver against the dead ones (Doc_07 2B's one-ontology-two-directions finding).

#### Force 2A-3: The culture's verdict — slander and, later, serious attack

**Layer 1:** popular charges (atheism, incest, cannibalism — the kinship-and-meal language misheard) answered by every apologist — Documented; elite contempt (Tacitus' odium humani generis; Suetonius' superstitio) — Documented; by c. 175 the first serious intellectual assault (Celsus) — Documented via Origen.

**Layer 2:** the world answered as the slandered kin-group it was: "among us nothing evil is done" (Blandina's plain sentence); the apologists' longer form of the same. Being hated wrongly was read through their Kyrios' words and worn as confirmation — but the record also shows the wound (the apologists' very existence is the wound documented).

**Layer 3:** produced apologetic literature and with it the world's first outward-facing self-descriptions (Diognetus' sojourner-portrait — the paroikia vocabulary sharpened under attack); tightened the meal's privacy (feeding the slanders it answered — a feedback loop the world never escaped in-span); and at the doorway pulled Christian talent toward the public-intellectual arena (3A-2).

### CELL 2B — ONGOING / INTERNAL [Transmission dimension included]

#### Force 2B-1: The counterfeit flood — rival teaching inside the assemblies' own rooms

**Layer 1:** endemic false-prophet and false-teacher traffic (Did. 11–13's tests; Mand. 11; 1 John's milieu) — Documented; docetic teaching in Asia (Ignatius' polemic) — Documented; the mid-span organized alternatives: Marcion (expelled at Rome c. 144; his counter-church trans-local and durable — Justin: spread "to every race") and the Valentinian teachers (Rome and the East, c. 140s onward) — Documented via opponents; the opponents-only survival of all of it — the standing filter (Doc_02 §7.5).

**Layer 2:** the world named it counterfeit kin: the christemporos trafficking in Christ; wolves among sheep; those who "abstain from the eucharist because they do not confess." The grief-register is real: these were people at the same tables. *Reported-Experience Status.*

**Layer 3:** hardened G4 from habit into apparatus (tests → rule-of-practice → the doorway's rule of faith); forced the scripture-decision (the two-Testament Bible against Marcion — the span's most consequential single bequest); intensified the audit-culture and its costs (suspicion as ambient virtue); and fed 3B-1 (the consolidation that ends the world).

#### Force 2B-2: Transmission (named force) — who carried the world's material, and how

**Layer 1:** in-span carriers: the assemblies themselves (corporate reading and re-copying; letters requested and forwarded — Polycarp collecting Ignatius; Smyrna's letter asked "to send on"); the traveler-network; no scriptoria, no institutions — transmission ran on the same kinship-hospitality web as everything else — Documented pattern. Selection already operating in-span: what assemblies re-read and re-copied was what served assembly life (manuals, letters, memoirs, martyr-accounts).

**Layer 2:** the world understood its copying and forwarding as the relay itself — delivering what was received, to those "in every place." It did not distinguish preserving texts from preserving the kindred; a letter was kin-speech, kept as family keeps letters. *Reported-Experience Status (transmission-as-experienced is thinly attested — noted per template).*

**Layer 3:** determined the surviving corpus' *genre-shape* (occasional, practical, communal — the ordinary year undocumented, Doc_02 §8); and its *side* (the proto-orthodox stream carried forward what its heirs valued; the alternatives' texts died with their networks or survive as quotations in refutations). Author Gravity connection (Doc_02): Ignatius survives because Polycarp collected and the later church valued; the Didache barely survived at all (one medieval manuscript) — a warning that the in-span corpus is the tip of a lost literature; Papias survives only as Eusebius' scraps.

#### Force 2B-3: Growth and the settling of order

**Layer 1:** numerical growth across the span (multi-provincial by 112 per Pliny; "in every city" rhetoric by the apologists; magnitudes unrecoverable — Stark's models Contested, flagged); with growth, the documented drift from plural-and-varied leadership toward the threefold pattern, complete enough by the 190s for trans-local episcopal action — Documented endpoints; the middle Dominant Modern Reconstruction (Lampe for Rome).

**Layer 2:** experienced as the tension the manuals speak aloud: honor the prophets, *and* do not despise the appointed; obey the elders, *and* test everything by conduct. The world felt order as both safety (against wolves) and loss (Spirit's freedom audited) — T7 lived, not theorized.

**Layer 3:** sustained T7 as the span's live internal argument; professionalized the deposit's guardianship (G4); and prepared 3B-1. The New Prophecy's eruption at span's end (c. 165–175, Phrygia) is this force meeting 2B-1's anxiety: prophecy itself now audited as possible counterfeit — the Spirit's pole losing the argument as the world closes.

#### Force 2B-4: The delay — time itself as internal pressure

**Layer 1:** the expected arrival deferred across generations; the texts renegotiating expectation in real time (Did. 16's urgency; Hermas' pause "for the sake of your repentance"; 2 Peter's milieu answering scoffers) — Documented texts; the "cooling" developmental narrative Contested (Doc_04 C6 — classification not built on it).

**Layer 2:** the world moralized the delay: the Lord is not slow but patient; the tower pauses so stones can be re-cut. Waiting became a practice with a name at the table — Maranatha, asked weekly, unanswered, un-despaired.

**Layer 3:** shaped G6's mature form (grateful longing rather than fever — Doc_07 2A); underwrote the sojourner-posture (paroikia as long-haul identity, not tent-folding); and quietly funded the institutional drift (a community expecting centuries builds differently than one expecting weeks — inference, flagged as such).

### CELL 3A — ENDING/TRANSFORMING / EXTERNAL

#### Force 3A-1: The empire begins to see the church whole

**Layer 1:** from the 170s–200s: larger and better-organized local persecutions (Lyons 177's public fury; the Severan-era actions just past span); serious intellectual engagement (Celsus c. 175; Galen's notices) treating Christians as a named phenomenon requiring analysis rather than a rumor requiring police — Documented items; the aggregate characterization ("the visibility threshold crossed") is this build's synthesis at Dominant Modern Reconstruction.

**Layer 2:** at the span's very edge the world begins answering as a *public*: apologies addressed to emperors, bishops corresponding across provinces about a common calendar. To whatever degree the change was felt from inside, the sources render it as boldness more than loss — "we are but of yesterday, and we have filled everything of yours" is the next generation's boast (Tertullian, at the doorway). *The community's own interpretation of its transformation is only partly recoverable — noted per template.*

**Layer 3:** made the membrane-strategy (low profile, face-scale, unmarked space) progressively untenable; rewarded exactly the developments (settled spokesmen, coordinated networks, literary professionals) that dissolved the world's distinct form; and handed the successor worlds their opening condition: a church the empire could *find*.

#### Force 3A-2: The public-intellectual arena opens

**Layer 1:** the later second century's philosophical marketplace (Second Sophistic culture; schools as the era's prestige institutions) drawing Christian talent into its forms: Justin's free-lance school in Rome (mid-span, the embryo), then Irenaeus' book-length systematics, the Alexandrian catechetical school (c. 180s, registered to the Alexandria world), Tertullian's literary Latin — Documented figures; the pull-as-force framing Widely Accepted.

**Layer 2:** experienced in-span as opportunity and suspicion at once: Justin keeps his philosopher's cloak and calls the faith "the true philosophy"; the same decades' communities test teachers as potential wolves. The world did not live to feel the school's full victory.

**Layer 3:** detached teaching from the assembly — the precise boundary with the successor ecology (Doc_01): formation's single site becomes two sites, then several. What had been survival-by-assembly becomes, in the successor worlds, assembly-plus-institutions; the change is the world's end as a distinct form.

### CELL 3B — ENDING/TRANSFORMING / INTERNAL [Transmission dimension included]

#### Force 3B-1: The world's own solutions consolidate — and outgrow it

**Layer 1:** by c. 190–230: the threefold office general; a functional two-Testament collection with a near-stable fourfold Gospel; the rule of faith citable; the catechumenate formalizing; martyr-commemoration becoming calendar — every item the maturation of an in-span defense mechanism (offices ← memory crisis; canon ← Marcion; rule ← counterfeit flood; catechumenate ← boundary-training; cult ← flashpoint memory) — Documented endpoints; the "solutions outgrow the world" framing is this build's synthesis (Dominant Modern Reconstruction).

**Layer 2:** the world's last in-span voices speak of these as *the same fidelity* — Irenaeus defends the fourfold Gospel and the successions as simply what was handed down. From inside, nothing ended; the deposit was being kept. That the keeping had built a new world is visible only from outside the span. *The community's own interpretation of its ending is largely not recoverable as such — it did not experience an ending.*

**Layer 3:** transformed rather than destroyed: every gravity persists into the successors *re-housed* — the assembly inside basilica-and-parish structures, the kindred inside institutional charity, the Name inside a legalizing church's martyr-cult, the deposit inside canon-creed-and-episcopate, the table inside developed eucharistic orders, the expectation inside doctrine. What was lost was the *coincidence of everything in one gathered room* — the world's defining condition (Doc_07 §4).

#### Force 3B-2: Transmission in ending (named force) — the successors' selection

**Layer 1:** third-and-fourth-century copying, collecting, and history-writing determined what of this world survived: the proto-orthodox stream's texts institutionalized (some canonized; Hermas read then dropped from canons while still copied; the Didache absorbed into later manuals and nearly lost); the alternatives' literature destroyed, abandoned, or preserved only in refutation; Eusebius' excerpting fixing the narrative frame — Documented pattern (Doc_02 §3.8).

**Layer 2:** not applicable in-span — this force operates after the world's close. *The world's own experience of this selection does not exist; noted plainly.*

**Layer 3:** created the reconstruction-terrain this build works: an episcopally-flavored, orthodoxy-vetted, crisis-weighted corpus (Doc_02 §1's four findings). Every Author Gravity thinness documented in Doc_02 — the Torah-observant, the prophets' own voices, the ordinary year, the losers entire — traces to this force. The build's elevated Contested/Inferential-Thin proportion is this force's fingerprint, reported as a true finding.

#### Force 3B-3: The Spirit/Order tension resolves toward order — the New Prophecy crisis

**Layer 1:** the New Prophecy (Montanus, Priscilla, Maximilla; Phrygia, c. 165–175 — onset date Contested 156/172) claiming the Paraclete's living voice; assemblies and then synods rejecting it; prophecy as an ordinary honored office thereafter marginal in the great church — Documented via (hostile) sources in Eusebius; the marginalization-narrative Widely Accepted with named dissent about how dead prophecy actually was.

**Layer 2:** the anti-Montanist writer preserved by Eusebius still must argue from the *world's* premise — he lists true prophets (Ammia) to prove the gift's reality while refusing these claimants: the world's own experience is of discernment, not cessation. *Reported-Experience Status; hostile-filter noted.*

**Layer 3:** closed the span's live T7 argument in favor of the audit's institutional pole; cost the successor worlds the resident-prophet office and re-routed Spirit-language into office and text; retroactively shaped transmission (prophetic literature not carried). One of the clearest cases in the record of a tensional gravity fracturing under force.

## Section 4 — Cross-Cell Connections

**Connection 1:** Force 1A-2 (Neronian precedent) → Force 2A-1 (accusation regime): the 64 precedent, juridically routinized by Trajan's rescript, becomes the span's standing legal weather — initiating shock institutionalized as ongoing pressure. Evidence: Tacitus' retrospect plus Pliny's procedural assumption that the name suffices.

**Connection 2:** Force 1B-2 (memory crisis) → Force 2B-2 (transmission): the conviction that the deposit must be relayed created the copying-forwarding-testing apparatus; the crisis built the network that later determined what survived. Evidence: succession-reasoning (1 Clem. 42/44) and living-voice anxiety (Papias) maturing into the letter-collecting practice (Polycarp on Ignatius).

**Connection 3:** Force 2B-1 (counterfeit flood) → Force 3B-1 (consolidation): each organized rival provokes the corresponding permanent structure — Marcion the canon-decision, docetism the credal insistences, the prophet-frauds the audit that hardens into office. Evidence: the point-for-point match between mid-span polemic and doorway-era institutions (Doc_04 §4 C4).

**Connection 4:** Force 2A-1 (accusation regime) → Force 2B-3 (order-settling): communities that can be decapitated by accusation develop durable, replaceable, recognized leadership; the regime selects for structure. Evidence: inferential-synthetic (flagged Inferential/Thin) — grounded in the documented coincidence of flashpoint decades and consolidation decades, but a causal claim beyond direct attestation.

**Connection 5:** Force 1A-3 (imperial peace) → Forces 2B-1 and 2B-2 jointly: the same roads carry the deposit and its counterfeits; network-connectivity is neutral infrastructure amplifying both fidelity and contagion. Evidence: Marcion's and Ignatius' identical travel-medium.

**Connection 6:** Force 2B-4 (delay) → Force 2B-3 (order-settling): expectation stretched funds patience for institution-building; the tower's pause and the offices' rise are the same decades. Evidence: correlational, in-span texts joining the themes (Hermas holds both); flagged Inferential/Thin as causation.

### What the Cross-Cell Pattern Reveals

The pattern is *self-transformation under pressure*: nearly every ending force is an ongoing force's solution matured (2B-1→3B-1; 2B-3→3B-3; 1B-2→2B-2→3B-2). This world was not conquered, dispersed, or suppressed; it *succeeded* its way out of its own form. The forces analysis thereby explains what gravity analysis alone could not: why the world's end looks like health rather than collapse, and why its successors could plausibly claim unbroken continuity while inhabiting a different ecology — both things are true, and the six-cell matrix shows how.

## Section 6 — Transmission as a Force Dimension — Synthesis

**Who transmitted:** in-span — the assemblies themselves as corporate copyists and forwarders; named nodes: Polycarp's Smyrna (the Ignatian collection), the Roman and Corinthian assemblies (1 Clement kept at both ends; Dionysius attests its continued reading c. 170), the Smyrna–Philomelium line (Mart. Pol. with its own forwarding instruction), the apologists' addressees (public copies). Post-span — the proto-orthodox episcopal-and-school stream; then Eusebius' excerpting; then canon-formation's inclusions and exclusions (Hermas' fate; the Didache's near-loss to one 11th-century manuscript, Bryennios 1873).

**Under what conditions:** no institutions, no scriptoria in-span — kin-network copying under the same legal weather as everything else; post-span, institutional interests selecting for order, orthodoxy, and usable glory.

**What selection effects shaped what survives:** crisis over ordinariness; winners over losers; office over Spirit; men over women (the record's women survive chiefly as objects, examples, or greeted names); Greek over Aramaic-Syriac substrata; the assembly's practical genres over whatever private devotion existed. Which Author Gravity gaps trace here: every one listed in Doc_02 §7 — this is the explicit Doc_02↔Doc_08 link the template requires.

**What the transmission pattern reveals about forces:** the transmitters preserved against their fears — manuals against disorder, refutations against counterfeit, martyr-acts against forgetting the cost, successions against rootlessness. The preservation-map is a fear-map, and the fears are Cells 2B-1, 2B-3, and 2A-1 exactly.

**Connection to Author Gravity (Doc_02):** Ignatius' dominance is a transmission artifact (one collector, later institutional affinity) as much as a merit artifact — his bishop-program suited the preservers; the Didache's marginality is the inverse case; Eusebius' filter governs the narrative layer entire. The build's discount rule ("via Eusebius" = one confidence level down unless corroborated) operationalizes this synthesis.
