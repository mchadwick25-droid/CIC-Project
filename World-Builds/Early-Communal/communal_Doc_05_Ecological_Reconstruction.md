## 1. Human Ecology — the person this world receives and the person it makes

**Who arrives.** The convert of this span arrives as an adult (infant practice unevidenced in-span either way — stated, not assumed), out of the ordinary religious saturation of a Greco-Roman city or, less visibly, out of the synagogue's orbit (God-fearers and diaspora Jews — Widely Accepted as a continuing recruitment stream, proportions unrecoverable). Celsus' sneer gives the social texture the apologists dress up: wool-workers, cobblers, fullers, women, the young — workshop and household recruitment, person to person (Documented as hostile observation; Widely Accepted as broadly accurate). Pliny adds every age, every rank, both sexes, city and countryside. What the convert leaves is not a private opinion but a cultic web: household gods at the hearth, guild gods at the trade dinner, civic gods at the festival, the emperor's genius at every oath. Leaving costs relationships, sometimes livelihood, occasionally life. The world knows this about itself: its catechesis is built as a departure-manual (the Two Ways), its kinship absorbs the dislocated, its baptism is a burned bridge.

**The formation arc.** Entry runs: hearing (in a shop, a household, a member's word) → instruction in the Way (the Two Ways material: life versus death rendered as concrete conduct — Did. 1–6; Documented as catechetical pattern, Widely Accepted as widespread) → pre-baptismal fasting, the community fasting alongside (Did. 7.4; Justin 1 Apol. 61 — two independent attestations, Documented) → the washing in the threefold Name, running water preferred and pragmatism permitted (Did. 7) — Justin calls the whole event *photismos*, illumination → immediate incorporation: led to where the brothers and sisters are gathered, the kiss, the prayers "as one who now belongs," and the table (Justin 65). Maturity in this world is not scholarly attainment (the schools belong to the successor worlds) but tested fidelity: the one whose conduct carries the Way (the Didache tests even prophets by conduct — "if he does not do what he teaches, he is a false prophet," Did. 11.10), whose speech is without doubleness (Hermas' war on *dipsychia*), who can be trusted with the fund, the guest, and the Name before a magistrate.

**[Inhabited — Tier 4; elements sourced below]** You were not born into this. Someone spoke to you — at the fuller's bench, across a courtyard, in the room where your mistress prays now instead of pouring the libation — and what they said had a shape: two ways, one of life, one of death, and you already walking one of them. **Daily and weekly texture.** Thrice-daily Lord's Prayer (Did. 8 — Documented for that community; extension elsewhere Inferential); Wednesday and Friday fasts set against "the hypocrites'" Monday and Thursday (Did. 8 — same caveat); the week aimed at the Lord's Day. Work continues — this world has no leisure class to speak of and no cloister; its holiness is negotiated inside trades, kitchens, marriages, and markets (Documented by the boundary-casuistry itself: idol-food, oaths, festivals). The pre-dawn hour of Pliny's report is the working world's only free hour (inference, flagged as such).

**Slaves and masters; women and men.** Both enslaved believers (as members and ministers — Pliny's ministrae) and believing slaveholders (Ign. Pol. 4.3) sit in one assembly; the world calls them kin and does not abolish the structure — it relativizes it at the table and leaves it standing in the house (Documented; the tension carried, not resolved, per Article 23 — the world itself did not resolve it). Women host, patronize, prophesy (Ammia — via Eusebius), serve as ministrae, teach within bounded spheres (Grapte, to the widows and orphans), are enrolled as widows ("altar of God" — Polycarp 4.3), and are, in the record we have, increasingly instructed toward constraint (the Pastorals' trajectory — Contested in scope and pace; both the documented agency and the documented constraint are carried).

## 2. Community Ecology — how the kindred holds together

**The assembly as household-network.** The single congregation of a city is, for most of this span and most places, a fiction of later retrospect: Rome is a constellation of cells in apartments and shops, loosely federated, exchanging letters and funds (Lampe — Dominant Modern Reconstruction); other cities plausibly similar at smaller scale (Inferential). The unit that matters is the gathering small enough to know its members' conduct — the Didache's tests, Hermas' congregational reading of character, and the discipline practiced by exclusion-and-return all presuppose face-scale community (Widely Accepted).

**The aid economy.** The collection is free-will, deposited with the proestos, spent on orphans, widows, the sick, prisoners, and traveling strangers (Justin 1 Apol. 67 — Documented, Rome c. 150); firstfruits go to prophets "for they are your high priests," else to the poor (Did. 13); fasting-to-feed appears as ordinary piety (Aristides; Hermas Sim. 5); almsgiving carries quasi-atoning weight (2 Clem. 16.4 — Documented as preached). Lucian confirms from outside that the network mobilizes across provinces for one prisoner. This is the kinship gravity operating as economics: not community of goods (Doc_01 label-test) but kin-obligation extended to the unrelated.

**Hospitality and the letter-web.** Assemblies receive travelers on recommendation, test them (two days' stay honored; three begins to prove an apostle false; asking for money in the Spirit settles the question — Did. 11–12), lodge them, and send them on; 1 Clement travels with named envoys; Ignatius' route is lined with delegations; Dionysius of Corinth answers letters from half the Aegean (via Eusebius). The network is the world's circulatory system and its immune system at once (Documented pattern).

**Conflict and repair.** The world's documented conflicts are internal before they are external: Corinth deposes its presbyters and Rome writes; Valens falls and Polycarp prescribes grief, not expulsion ("as suffering and straying members"); the Didache orders reconciliation before the sacrifice ("let none who has a quarrel with his fellow join you until they be reconciled, that your sacrifice be not defiled," Did. 14.2); Hermas negotiates one post-baptismal repentance against both rigorists and the lax (all Documented). Restoration is real and bounded — the Purity/Mercy tension (Doc_04 T9) as lived procedure.

**[Inhabited — Tier 4; elements sourced below]** When the letter came from the assembly across the sea, it was read to all of you at the gathering, standing between the prophets' words and the meal, and you heard it the way you hear family news — names you did not know, and yet your own. *Source-note: letters read in assembly (1 Clement's addressed corporate reading; Justin 67's reading-then-exhortation order as assembly template); traveler-testing and provisioning (Did. 11–12); recommendation practice (network pattern, Doc_02; 1 Clem. envoys). The emotional register ("family news") is the interpretive element, grounded in the adelphoi-address and Lucian's outside testimony.*

## 3. Worship Ecology — the Lord's Day at the table

Under Bradshaw-discipline, this section separates its one thick description from its generalizations. **The thick description (Rome, c. 150, Justin 1 Apol. 65–67 — Documented):** on the day named of the sun, all in city and country gather in one place; the memoirs of the apostles and the writings of the prophets are read as long as time permits; the proestos exhorts; all stand for prayers; bread and a cup of water-mixed wine are brought; the proestos gives thanks "to the best of his ability" (free prayer over a fixed shape — a datum both for form and freedom); the people assent with Amen; the deacons distribute and carry portions to the absent; the collection follows; the day is kept because it is creation's first day and the resurrection's. **The second anchor (Bithynia, c. 112, Pliny — Documented, hostile):** the fixed-day pre-dawn gathering, the alternating hymn to Christ as to a god, the binding oath (sacramentum in Pliny's Latin — very possibly his rendering of baptismal or covenantal commitment; Contested in referent), the separate, later, "ordinary and harmless" meal, suspended when associations were banned. **The third anchor (Syria-orbit, c. 100: Didache 9–10, 14 — Documented text; Contested in liturgical interpretation):** thanksgivings over cup then bread, the scattered-bread gathering-prayer, meal-fullness ("after you are filled"), confession before the Lord's-Day breaking of bread, Maranatha at the meal's end, and prophets free to give thanks "as much as they wish."

What may responsibly be generalized (Widely Accepted): weekly Lord's-Day assembly; reading and exhortation as a hearing-community's formation; prayer standing, communally; a meal-act called eucharistia at the center; the absent remembered bodily (portions carried, funds distributed). What may not: uniform wording, uniform meal-shape, uniform officiant profile, the agape/eucharist relation (Contested), or any single "early liturgy."

**Worship's formative work:** the assembly is where the world's whole vocabulary is learned by use — a member hears the graphai and the memoirs aloud (most cannot read them), prays the kingdom near, tastes the kinship, and rehearses weekly the allegiance that the market and magistrate will test. Worship and boundary are one system: the same act that feeds is the act that incriminates (Pliny's suspects are identified precisely as gatherers). Hence gathering itself has confession's weight — Ignatius' "gather more frequently" is spoken into that weather.

## 4. Organizational Ecology — offices in mid-settlement

The span opens with plural, local, and varied leadership (episkopoi-and-diakonoi appointed alongside honored prophets and teachers — Did. 15; presbyters in Corinth deposed by the congregation that seated them — 1 Clem.; Rome plural into the mid-second century — Lampe, DMR) and closes at the doorway of the settled threefold office acting trans-locally (Victor, c. 190s). Ignatius' seven letters urge the single-bishop pattern with a fervor that proves its incompleteness (against-the-grain, Doc_02 §2.3). Authority's grounds are themselves plural and negotiated: appointment-in-succession (1 Clem. 42/44 — one generation deep, not yet the later chains), demonstrated conduct (the Didache's only universal criterion), Spirit-endowment (prophets; Hermas a lay visionary with a mandate to rebuke leaders), seniority and proximity-to-witnesses (Papias' living voice; Polycarp's eighty-six years), and the deposit itself (whoever teaches "so as to increase righteousness and knowledge of the Lord" — Did. 11.2). No one ground has yet defeated the others: that is this world's organizational signature (Widely Accepted as synthesis).

## 5. Ministry Ecology — the works the world does

Orphans and widows (the fund's first named objects); the sick visited; prisoners fed and accompanied (Lucian's dawn vigil of widows; the officials who sleep in the prison); strangers lodged; the dead buried and the fallen-asleep remembered (commemoration documented at Smyrna for a martyr — the general practice Inferential in-span); captives' ransom named early (1 Clem. 55's self-sale hyperbole; Aristides) — Documented as a pattern across in-world and outside witnesses. Ministry is not a program beside the assembly; it is the kinship-gravity's public face, and outsiders saw it before they understood anything else (Lucian; later Galen's puzzled respect).

## 6. Meaning-Transmission Ecology

A hearing world: reading is performance, memory is storage, and the living voice is preferred even where books exist (Papias — Documented as attitude). Meaning moves by: assembly reading and exhortation; the Two Ways taught mouth to mouth; fixed prayers and forms carried in the body (thrice-daily prayer; meal-thanksgivings; the Aramaic Maranatha surviving untranslated inside Greek worship — a fossil of transmission itself); letters read aloud and re-copied; travelers carrying news, texts, and tested teaching; and stories — of the founders, of recent confessors (Smyrna writes Polycarp's death to Philomelium "that you may also send the letter on"), of judgment and the tower. The Gospels themselves are this world's memory-crisis answered in codex form; by span's end the memoirs are assembly-reading beside the prophets (Justin), and a fourfold pattern is consolidating at the doorway (Irenaeus) — the world transmits its way into a canon it never lives to see closed (Documented milestones; pace Contested).

## 7. Emotional and Affective Ecology

Named from the world's own vocabulary (full development at Doc_07 2A): **joy-in-thanksgiving** (eucharistia as disposition before it is rite; Ignatius' strange exultation; the meal-prayers' gladness "you have given food and drink to men for enjoyment, that they might give you thanks" — Did. 10); **watchfulness** (Did. 16 "be watchful for your life"; Hermas' visions as alarm-bells; the boundary's daily vigilance); **fear rightly placed** (before God, not the magistrate — "eighty-six years..." as its serene extreme); **grief with hope** (the fallen-asleep; the deserters; Valens); **shame and its healing** (Hermas' whole affective world — exposure, blush, restoration); **double-mindedness as the diagnosed disease** (dipsychia — anxiety, hedging, the divided heart of people living between two cities' claims); and **longing** (Maranatha — desire as liturgy). What the world fears most from within is not death but dissolution: the unraveling of the little assembly by quarrel, counterfeit teaching, or shame-driven drift (1 Clem.'s horror at schism; the Didache's reconciliation-rule; Hermas' tower-stones cast aside). Confidence: each register anchored to named texts (Documented as expressed emotion); the synthesis is this build's construction (Inferential at the pattern level, per the Framework's participatory-reconstruction rule).

## 8. Interpretive Ecology

The scriptures are Israel's, in Greek, read as the book about the Kyrios: promise-and-fulfillment and typology are the world's reflexes (Justin's proof-from-prophecy; Barnabas' aggressive allegory as one pole; Melito's typological Pascha), practiced aloud, in assembly, toward conduct and confession rather than system (Documented range). The memoirs join the prophets as reading; "it is written" begins to stretch over new texts (2 Clem. citing gospel material as scripture — early instance; pace Contested). Interpretation's stakes are communal and bodily: what the text means decides whose Pascha you keep, what you may eat, whether the Maker is the Father of the Kyrios (contra Marcion — the world's one interpretive decision with everything at stake, made in this span and inherited by all successors: the two-Testament Bible). The world reads for allegiance, not curiosity.

## 9. Representative Theological Patterns

Not a system; recurring load-bearing convictions (each Documented as recurring; systematization refused as anachronism): one God the Maker, whose creation is good and whose scriptures are kept (against Marcion); the Kyrios Jesus — truly flesh, truly suffered, truly raised (Ignatius' anti-docetic insistence; the meal unintelligible otherwise) — to whom the hymn is sung "as to a god" (Pliny) and whose arrival is asked for; the Spirit present in gift and prophecy, tested by conduct; the ekklesia as God's scattered-and-gathered people, older than the mountains in Hermas' vision, still a tower building; baptism the seal, the meal the thanksgiving, the resurrection the horizon (athanasia — the medicine-of-immortality register at its Ignatian pole, contempt-of-death noted even by Galen at its plainest); judgment real, the Two Ways serious. Pre-creedal: the rule-of-faith exists as practiced pattern, not fixed text — fixity belongs to the doorway (Widely Accepted).

## 10. Power, Influence, and Historical Dynamics

The world holds no public power at any point in the span — its entire relation to power is negative space: it cannot build, march, litigate as itself, or (mostly) publish; it can gather, feed, refuse, suffer, and multiply. History's impact on it is the force-set of Doc_08; its impact on history, within the span, is almost invisible from outside — a governor's nuisance, a satirist's target, a physician's footnote — and yet by 200 it has seeded the institutions (episcopate, canon-in-formation, catechumenate, martyr-memory) that every successor world will live in. The asymmetry is the finding: **a world with no power and immense consequence** (Widely Accepted as historical judgment).
