## 2. The world-boundary decision (the single most important thing to review)

**Decision: "Early Communal" = the assemblies of the brothers and sisters, c. 70 – c. 200 CE — the household-assembly ecology of the Greek-speaking Mediterranean between the passing of the founding eyewitnesses (and the Temple's fall) and the emergence of the schools, the settled threefold office, and the first purpose-marked Christian spaces.**

Reasoning, in brief (full argument in communal_Doc_01):
- **The label was tested and found defective in two directions.** "Early" unqualified would claim the apostolic era (c. 30–70) — refused on three ranked grounds: constitutional (a world claiming to *be* the apostolic church is the maximal Living-Tradition hazard, since every communion owns that origin), evidential (maximally contested terrain, unbuildable under the confidence discipline), and ecological (a generation with living eyewitnesses and a standing Temple is a different formation world). The apostolic events enter as inherited memory (initiating internal force), exactly as Early Latin carried Cyprian without annexing him. **Honesty note (Critic 1, Finding 5): the start-boundary is therefore partly a governance artifact, not purely a historical one — stated plainly rather than disguised.**
- "Early" unbounded at the far end would swallow the third century (Cyprianic institutional Africa, Decian crisis, Alexandria's school — the last already registered to another world). **End boundary c. 200, soft edge**, argued from four converging markers (order settled enough for Victor's trans-local ultimatum c. 190s; teaching detaching into schools; material invisibility beginning to fail; the legal regime's character shifting) — graded Dominant Modern Reconstruction, the build's softest edge, with alternatives (c. 190; c. 235) named for external review.
- **"Communal" was tested for its two mis-hearings** — property-communism (refused: mutual-aid economy, not community of goods) and implied-contrast (later worlds were also communal). Its defensible sense: **in this world the gathered community is the entire formation apparatus** — no schools, buildings, professional class, or public standing beside it. That claim survived gravity testing (Doc_04) and became the world's central coherence (Doc_07: "everything this world must keep, it keeps by gathering").
- **Strand determination: strand-singular at the resolution the evidence permits** — with the Torah-observant ("Jewish-Christian") strand-candidate *declined* on evidence discipline (their own voices do not survive; everything is polemical or retrospective) and carried instead as the world's major internal plurality under the Article 20 duty. This is the build's most reviewable single finding; the functional difference (strand would require affirmative representative coverage; plurality requires naming the absence) is argued at Critic 1 Finding 6.
- **Differentiation** against all four registered worlds is tabulated in Doc_01 and argued for the hard case (Alexandria overlap 150–200; why Justin's Rome is in and Clement's school is out) at World Profile §6.

## 4. The world's honest evidentiary character (a finding, not a defect)

As predicted in the assignment and confirmed in the build: this world carries **the highest proportion of Contested and Inferential/Thin material of any registered world.** Its two best worship descriptions come from a hostile governor and an apologist writing for outsiders; its narrative history arrives through Eusebius' fourth-century filter (a standing one-level confidence discount was applied to uncorroborated Eusebian material); its material record is near-total silence (read as data: the world met in unmarked space); its dissenters and its suppressed voices survive only in enemies' quotations. The center of the ecology (assembly, meal, kinship-economics, boundary, deposit, entry-process) is nonetheless secured at Documented-to-Widely-Accepted by convergence of independent streams. The build says which is which throughout, and the named scholarly tensions (Ignatian date, 1 Clement's date, parting-of-the-ways, persecution intensity, women's-leadership arc, canon pace, Adams-vs-house-church, and more) are carried at full strength, never resolved.

## 5. The Representative decision (second item needing Mark's review)

**Chloe, a host of the assembly** — CO-014 world-voice model ("we/our/among us" as unremarked default; a single formed voice — a woman who kept one door for the gathering across decades — standing quietly behind as identity fact, never announced; no stated grammar rule anywhere, per the Cordus v2.1 test findings, inherited as design constraints). Role chosen on the Blueprint's crossroads criterion (everything in this world happens in somebody's room; the host sees all of it). The female voice is argued ecologically in the Construction Notes (documented range: hosts, ministrae, Grapte, Ammia; Article 20 audibility duty) with the projection-risk named and the constraint carried in the voice itself ("our sisters' words were seldom written down"). Name chosen for period-plausibility, non-conflation (explicitly not the Corinthian Chloe of 1 Cor 1:11), and modern connectability. Alternatives weighed and recorded (male presbyter-teacher; traveling prophet; documented individuals; an enslaved member's voice — the last weighed seriously and declined as unfundable without systematic invention). **Critic 2 Finding 3: this choice requires explicit project-lead sign-off, not ratification by silence. The prompt is also entirely untested (zero live rounds, unlike Cordus) — a hard gate before any participant contact.**

## 6. What the Critic passes found (unsoftened highlights)

- **Systemic (both checkpoints, highest weight):** every citation is unverified model memory, and several quoted strings are remembered renderings presented in quotation marks — verification is a hard gate before any external use.
- **Shape risk:** the Didache is doing Augustine's job (the build's most texture-bearing details lean on one manual of unknown typicality) — mitigated in Doc_09 and the chunks, standing in the Capsule/Prompt.
- **Frame risk:** the "fourfold removal" genesis-frame is a builder's epigram partly resting on a contested model (parting-of-the-ways) that the build elsewhere suspends — visible, not deleted.
- **Romanticization pressure:** this is the world moderns most want to idealize; the build carries its ugliness (slavery unresolved inside the kinship, supersessionist voices, quarrels, mass lapse) and the Facilitation Brief's first caution targets restorationist projection — but a romanticization probe is a required live test.
- **Silent modernization caught:** "brothers and sisters" for adelphoi is a defensible but modern translational choice embedded as world-speech — named at Critic 2 Finding 4.
- Compliance audits, minor register slips, and the full lists are in the two checkpoint files.
