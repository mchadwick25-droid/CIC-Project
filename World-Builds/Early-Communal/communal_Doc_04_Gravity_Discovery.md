**Test detail worth preserving (selected):**

- **C1 Formation test:** everything this world does to form a person happens in the gathering or points to it — catechesis leads to baptism which leads to the table; discipline is exclusion *from the gathering*; restoration is return *to it*; prophecy is tested *in it*; the collection is deposited *at it*; the writings are read *aloud in it* (a hearing world, Doc_02 §6). No other registered world's formation is this radically single-sited.
- **C1/C5 Interaction:** Pliny is the decisive witness that assembly and meal were separable under pressure (the food-meeting suspended after the hetaeriae ban, the pre-dawn gathering apparently persisting) — this is why C5 sits inside C1's field rather than beside it.
- **C2 Explanatory test:** kinship explains the aid economy, the hospitality network, the address-forms, the adoption of orphans and enrollments of widows, the scandal of masters and slaves at one table, and the outside world's simultaneous mockery (Lucian) and suspicion (incest slanders answered by the apologists — the kinship language itself generated the accusation).
- **C3 Formation test:** the Two Ways is boundary-catechesis (life versus death, the gods versus the God); baptism enacts the crossing; the daily reality is not the tribunal but the market, the guild dinner, the household shrine — a formation in continuous small refusals, with the tribunal as its rare, defining extremity. The Name (to onoma) is the world's own compression of this whole condition.
- **C4 Dependency test:** offices are argued *from* transmission (1 Clem. 42/44); Gospels exist because memory required a form that outlives witnesses; prophet-testing exists because the deposit could be counterfeited; even the meal-prayers ("as this bread was scattered...") transmit fixed forms. C4 passes dependency strongly.
- **C7 as tension, not gravity-proper:** it does not organize the ecology; it runs *through* C1 and C4 as their unresolved seam — exactly the Tensional profile.
- **C8:** the world's deepest wound; organizes scripture-reading (whose graphai?), calendar (Quartodeciman Pascha), polemic, and identity — but as an unhealed question pulling against the primaries (a gentile-majority people claiming Israel's book while refusing Israel's practice and losing Israel's people). Tensional at full strength.

## 4. Forces Test (Forces Framework Step 4 — how each gravity behaves under the Step 1–2 forces)

- **C1 assembly:** produced by the fourfold removal (Doc_01 §4) — with Temple, synagogue, and eyewitnesses gone, the gathering *is* where the Lord is; intensified by the legal weather (assembly is the incriminating act — Pliny — so gathering itself becomes confession); strained by growth and fractionation (Rome's many cells).
- **C2 kinship:** produced by conversion's social cost (leaving household gods severed household ties; the assembly re-housed the dislocated); intensified by outside pressure (the network as survival infrastructure); strained from within by the very structures it relativized (masters/slaves, husbands/wives — the household codes' anxieties).
- **C3 boundary-holiness:** the direct interior face of the external force-pair (cultic city + accusation regime); holds across the span because the force held; its extremity (martyrdom) intensifies at datable local flashpoints (Smyrna 155, Rome 165, Lyons 177) while its ordinary form (refusals in market and guild) is constant.
- **C4 deposit:** produced by the memory crisis; intensified mid-span by the organized alternatives (Marcion, Valentinians) — the force arrives and the gravity visibly hardens (from Didache's conduct-tests to Irenaeus' doorway-era rule-of-faith).
- **C5 meal:** stressed directly by the hetaeriae ban (Pliny — the world's clearest force-on-practice datum); its shape possibly reorganized under that pressure (evening meal → pre-dawn rite; Dominant Modern Reconstruction, flagged).
- **C6 hope:** generated by the founding events and the delay; pressed by time itself; the span's own texts renegotiate it (Hermas' tower-pause "for your sake").
- **C7:** the institutionalization pressure (growth, false teachers, founder-death) drives order; the Spirit's documented continuance (prophets honored in the same manuals that test them) resists absorption; the New Prophecy at span's end is the tension detonating.
- **C8:** driven by the Temple's fall, the synagogue separation, gentile influx, and Marcion's forcing of the question from the other side.
- **C9:** driven by baptismal-purity theology colliding with lived lapse (Pliny's deserters; Hermas' own household).

Every confirmed gravity traces to at least one force; the full three-layer treatment is Doc_08's.

## 5. Confirmed Gravity Register

**Primary:**
1. **The Assembly (epi to auto)** — the gathering as the entire formation apparatus. Cross-regional: all four regions; cross-source-type: all six types. Confidence: Documented elements / Widely Accepted pattern.
2. **The Kindred (adelphoi)** — fictive kinship as household of God, enacted in aid, hospitality, and address. All regions; all types. Documented / Widely Accepted.
3. **The Name under the Watching City (to onoma)** — bounded holiness as everyday refusal with a lethal edge. All regions; all types. Documented / Widely Accepted.
4. **The Deposit (paradosis)** — guarding what was handed over, against forgetting and counterfeit. All regions; five of six types (outside witnesses register it only obliquely — Pliny's "they bound themselves by oath" glimpses fixed forms). Documented elements; unifying frame Dominant Modern Reconstruction — **Primary with stated reservation, flagged for external review.**

**Supporting:**
5. **The Table (eucharistia/agape)** — the meal as the assembly's central act: thanksgiving, incorporation, and the kingdom prefigured. Documented; theology-register author-gravity flagged.
6. **The Expectation (parousia/Maranatha)** — hope leaning toward the Lord's arrival, prayed weekly at the table. Documented texts; developmental claims Contested.

**Tensional:**
7. **Spirit and Order** — prophets and offices in one unresolved room.
8. **The Israel Question** — Israel's scriptures held by a people leaving Israel's practice; the wound that does not close in this span.
9. **Purity and Mercy** — the holy people and its failing members; one repentance, contested restorations.

**Interaction summary (the ecology's shape in one paragraph):** the Assembly is the site; the Kindred is what the site makes; the Name is the price of belonging to it; the Deposit is what it convenes around; the Table is its central act; the Expectation is its direction. The three tensions run through the middle: who speaks in the assembly (Spirit/Order), whose book and calendar it keeps (Israel), and who may return to the table after failing the Name or the Way (Purity/Mercy). No candidate sits inert; the Interaction test is satisfied structurally, not decoratively.
