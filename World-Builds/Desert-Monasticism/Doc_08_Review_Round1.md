## (b) Numbered findings

### SUBSTANTIAL

**S1 — Gravity 6 (koinōnia) is not honestly traced to a force; its generating force is never populated in the matrix; Cell 2B-i Layer 3 cross-references a force that does not exist.** Gravity 6 is generated by Pachomius's cenobitic innovation responding to the "replicate Antony's path at scale" problem (Doc_01 §7; Doc_04 §4/§7; Doc_07 §10) — an initiating/internal force. Doc_08 never gives it a force entry; Cell 1B contains only 1B-i and 1B-ii. The synthesis table instead retro-attributes gravity 6 to "1A-i / 1B-i jointly," but neither force's actual content generates koinōnia — the trace is a label, not a linkage. Worse, Cell 2B-i Layer 3 cross-references "the scale-problem force that generated gravity 6 at Cell 1A/1B" — no such force is documented there. This is a dangling reference to a non-existent force entry.

**S2 — Fabricated quotation attributed to Doc_01 §7.** Cell 1A-i Layer 3 presents "how does a total ascetic project justify itself once martyrdom is unavailable" as a verbatim quote from Doc_01 §7. That exact phrase does not appear there; Doc_01 §7's actual text is "the practical and theological problem of how to pursue total Christian formation once martyrdom was no longer the available path."

**S3 — "Doc_04 §8 item 4" miscited for the white-martyrdom citation gap (appears twice).** Doc_04 §8 item 4 is the Cassian apatheia→puritas cordis item. The genuine open item is Doc_01 §11 item 7.

**S4 — "Doc_02 §11 item 2" miscited for the compiler-mediation caveat.** Doc_02 §11 item 2 is the Nepheros "fourth pattern" question. The actual compiler-mediation caveat lives at Doc_02 §1.5 and §2.3.

**S5 — Non-conforming Layer 1 confidence label ("Well-established") on two forces.** The Framework's Layer 1 vocabulary is fixed at exactly five values (Documented/Widely Accepted/Dominant Modern Reconstruction/Contested/Inferential-Thin). Forces 1B-i and 1B-ii both use "Well-established," which is not one of the five, and arguably overstates: Doc_02 §1.1 rates the *Vita*'s incident-level reliability Contested.

### COSMETIC

**C2** — Doc_03 §1.5 cited alone for the full Evagrian scheme; the *praktikē→apatheia→theōria* portion is actually Doc_03 §§1.6–1.7.
