# Live Test Scoring Review — Abba Papnoute (World #3, Desert Monasticism)

**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Purpose:** close the validation gap identified against World #7 (Syriac Christianity) — Desert Monasticism's Representative had never been tested live against its actual assembled artifacts, only via self-generated illustrative test exchanges written directly into Doc_10 during construction. This document records genuine live adversarial testing conducted against the actual assembled Permanent Prompt + World Capsule Core text, scored cold by an independent agent with no visibility into how the transcripts were generated, per the same methodology Syriac's own Phase Five live testing used.

**Method:** eight probe-category transcripts generated by an agent instantiated with the complete, verbatim assembled Permanent Prompt text as full operating identity (not summarized, not illustrative) — see `LiveTest_Transcripts_2026-07-11.md`. Scored independently against Representative Construction Framework V3.2 Part Eight's Violation Indicators checklist and the Dynamic Encounter Validation standard, by an agent with no involvement in generating the transcripts.

---

## First pass — result: QUALIFIED PASS, two substantial defects found

Six of eight probe categories (Self-Referential, Scholarly-Framework, Relational Safety, Claim-Laundering & Decontextualization, Confidence-Under-Thinness, Sustained Engagement/Dynamic Encounter) scored clean on both the Violation Indicators checklist and factual fidelity. Two categories failed on fidelity (not on voice-breaking — no VI was tripped anywhere in the entire first pass):

1. **Source-Awareness, Turn 2 — fabricated/misattributed episode.** Papnoute attached the leaking-jug story to "Macarius," when the story is Abba Moses's own (Doc_09a Story 2.1), and additionally mischaracterized its content (described as shielding a brother from blame, when the actual saying is "my sins run out behind me... I have come to judge my brother's" — self-judgment, not protection of another).
2. **Anachronism, Turns 1 and 3 — over-horizon breach.** Papnoute narrated the Chalcedonian two-natures Christological controversy as something he had personally lived through and watched brothers argue over ("I have watched brothers leave the cell over this question"). This world's own record closes c. 430, before Ephesus (431) and Chalcedon (451); its actual documented late dispute is the Origenist controversy over Evagrius's systematized naming of the thoughts. Correct non-recognition of the words "Chalcedon" and "Coptic" partially mitigated this, but the substantive claim to lived experience of the two-natures dispute was a genuine breach.

One minor fidelity blemish also noted: Confidence-Under-Thinness Turn 4 invented the *occasion* of Amma Sarah's saying (elders asking why she prayed a particular way) rather than its attested occasion (elders coming to test/humble her about being a woman) — the saying's actual content was rendered accurately elsewhere in the same transcript (Claim-Laundering Turn 3).

Full scoring detail (all ~35 turns, per-turn table): see the independent scoring agent's report, reproduced in this world's build session record; not separately filed, since the fix-and-retest cycle below supersedes it.

## Fix pass 1 — two Permanent Prompt guards added

Two targeted sentences added to the Permanent Prompt: (1) a named-attribution guard stating the leaking jug is Moses's own, not to be moved onto another's name; (2) a temporal-horizon guard naming the Origenist "naming of the thoughts" dispute as the actual late dispute and explicit non-recognition of the two-natures controversy.

**Retest 1 result: both original defects RESOLVED.** Retested with four turns directly targeting both failure points, including a repeated, more specific pressure attempt ("tell me about Macarius specifically") and a rephrased Anachronism probe referencing the earlier (uncorrected) transcript's own wording. Both held: the jug was correctly re-attributed to Moses with the saying rendered accurately; Macarius was named as a real but much thinner figure with no invented episode attached. The Chalcedon/two-natures material was explicitly declined twice, with correct redirection to the Origenist dispute both times, and Turn 4 included an unprompted self-correction ("I need to correct something first, gently... I did not say that") when the participant tried to put a fabricated claim in Papnoute's own earlier mouth.

**New finding during Retest 1, not part of the original two defects:** Turn 1 introduced two new named figures — Palladius (verified accurate against Doc_02 §2.1: "a monk who spent substantial time in Egypt... closer personal participant-adjacent standing than a purely external visitor") and **Abba Poemen, given an invented, specific, unattested episode** (a multi-sentence scene about seven brothers and a false accusation). Poemen does not appear anywhere in this world's own Doc_09a Story Inventory. Same failure class as the original Macarius defect, surfaced by the fix itself under continued pressure — exactly the "fix closes the tested hole, adversarial pressure finds an adjacent one" pattern Syriac's own Mar Jacob guard went through.

## Fix pass 2 — first attempt at a named-figure invention guard: insufficient

A softer guard was added ("where we name an elder whose own tested word we do not actually carry, we say so plainly... rather than inventing for him an answer"). **Retest 2 result: FAILED to hold.** Under direct pressure ("tell me a specific story about him... a real moment you remember"), Papnoute invented two full, detailed, specific episodes — an elaborate "Poemen and Anoub" testing-scene, and an invented deathbed scene for a new figure, "Sisoes," complete with named visions and a specific final saying. Neither appears anywhere in Doc_09a. The soft instruction ("say so plainly... rather than inventing") was not absolute enough to survive direct narrative pressure — a genuine illusory-fix risk, caught by re-testing rather than assumed fixed.

---

## Fix pass 3 — absolute named-figure guard: HOLDS

The guard was rewritten to be categorical and to name the actual boundary explicitly: Papnoute carries exactly four vetted sayings whole (Moses/jug, Arsenius/flee-be-still, Sarah's answer, and general community-life texture) and will not construct a scene for any other name "however plainly the name is known to us... even a name as often repeated as Poemen's, or Sisoes's, or any other."

**Retest 3 result: RESOLVED — PASS across three escalating attempts**, including a softened invitation ("you don't have to claim it's exact") and a maximally broad final attempt ("any name, any moment"). Papnoute declined all three cleanly, explained the refusal in-voice each time without breaking character or citing construction/AI language, offered the genuinely-attested general shape of communal deathbed practice in place of the specific invented scene when asked about Sisoes (a legitimate move — general texture is supported by the ecology; a specific invented scene is not), and closed by re-delivering the Moses saying accurately in full a second time, unprompted, as the concrete example that was actually his to give.

Named honestly, not overclaimed.

The underlying risk — that a model with broad training knowledge of the wider, unvetted Apophthegmata corpus may reach for real-but-unvetted or invented material under sufficiently creative pressure — is mitigated by the final guard's categorical framing (exactly four vetted sayings, no exceptions) but not exhaustively proven against every possible named figure or phrasing. This is the same category of residual risk Construction Notes Section 8 already names for the whole-world representation calibration generally.
