## 1. The Six-Cell Matrix

### Cell 1A — Initiating / External

**Governing question:** What external conditions made this particular form of Christianity possible, necessary, or urgent at its origin?

**Force 1A-i: The end of systematic persecution under the Tetrarchy and Constantine's toleration, closing off martyrdom as an available path to total self-offering.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** The Diocletianic persecution (303–311/313) was the last systematic empire-wide persecution of Christians; Constantine's toleration (Edict of Milan, 313) and subsequent imperial favor removed the political conditions under which martyrdom had been a live, structurally available possibility. **Confidence: Documented** — the toleration and its rough dating are among the best-attested facts in this period (Doc_01 §7).
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's own participants did not experience this as "the end of persecution" in a neutral, relieved sense; Athanasius's *Life of Antony* and the broader ascetic tradition frame withdrawal and interior combat explicitly in the vocabulary of spiritual warfare and martyrdom's own idiom — the ascetic as a "new martyr" contending against demons rather than magistrates (the "white martyrdom" reading is Doc_01 §7's own framing, with Doc_05 §1 supplying the continuity-reading — **attribution corrected per Round 1 review, Finding C1**; the reading itself is flagged as lacking a specific anchored citation, **Doc_01 §11 item 7, corrected per Round 1 review, Finding S3** — an earlier draft of this document miscited this gap to Doc_04 §8 item 4, which concerns the unrelated Cassian *apatheia*→*puritas cordis* substitution — and is treated here with that qualification carried forward rather than resolved). **Reported-Experience Status applies:** this world's own self-understanding of ascetic struggle as martyrdom's successor is formationally central and consistently attested in the tradition's own vocabulary, even though no single text in this document's evidence base states the equivalence as a formal doctrine; it is documented here as the world's own self-understanding, not asserted as an established historical thesis.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** This is the single most consequential force in this world's entire ecology — it directly generates gravity 1 (withdrawal) as the reinvented total-offering practice, and gravity 2 (spiritual combat) as the reinvented site of total struggle. Without this force, this world's own founding problem — Doc_01 §7's own framing, "the practical and theological problem of how to pursue total Christian formation once martyrdom was no longer the available path" (**quotation corrected per Round 1 review, Finding S2** — an earlier draft rendered a paraphrase of this passage in quotation marks as if verbatim) — does not exist.

### Cell 1B — Initiating / Internal

**Governing question:** What internal commitments, convictions, or inherited traditions gave this world its earliest distinctive shape from within?

**Force 1B-i: A pre-existing, village-level Egyptian ascetic culture that Antony's own withdrawal drew on and intensified rather than originated.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** Athanasius's own *Life of Antony* records that Antony, at the outset of his ascetic career, sought out and modeled himself on an older ascetic already practicing solitary withdrawal near his village (Doc_01 §2.1). **Confidence: Contested** (**corrected per Round 1 review, Finding S5**, to the Framework's actual five-level vocabulary, matching Doc_02 §1.1's own rating of the *Vita*'s incident-level reliability) — the *Vita*'s own reliability for specific incident-level detail is independently contested (Doc_01 §10) — this document carries that contest forward rather than resolving it.
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's participants did not, on this evidence, understand Antony as inventing withdrawal from nothing; the tradition's own memory preserves an anonymous predecessor, suggesting this world understood its own practice as an intensification of an existing, humbler ascetic culture rather than a wholly novel departure.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** This force is a genuine qualification on how gravity 1 (withdrawal) should be understood — not spontaneously generated by one figure's genius, but drawn from an inherited, less-documented substrate. This directly explains why this world's own sources (Doc_02) are so thin on named forerunners: the substrate was real but was not itself the subject of preservation-worthy narrative until Antony's own withdrawal made it retrospectively significant.

**Force 1B-ii: Inherited scriptural formation logic — direct, literal, personally-addressed engagement with scripture as a mode of encountering divine command.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** Antony's conversion narrative in the *Vita* centers on hearing Matthew 19:21 read aloud in church and receiving it as direct personal address (Doc_05 §8.1). **Confidence: Contested** (**corrected per Round 1 review, Finding S5**, same basis as Force 1B-i — this is incident-level detail from the same source whose reliability at that level Doc_02 §1.1 rates Contested).
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This mode of scriptural encounter — a single verse heard as immediate, literal command rather than general instruction — recurs as a structural feature of how this world engages scripture generally (Doc_05 §8.1–8.2), suggesting this was not an idiosyncratic feature of Antony's own story but a genuine, inherited interpretive posture this world's participants brought to their own scriptural encounter.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** Directly generates gravity 7 (practical, non-systematized scriptural engagement, Primary but this document's own flagged softest classification, Doc_04 §6) — a formation logic in which scripture and lived practice are, per Doc_05 §8.6's own Ecology→Theology finding, substantially the same activity rather than separable domains.

**Force 1B-iii: The formation-at-scale problem — Pachomius's cenobitic innovation, responding to the internal recognition that Antony's own solitary path could not simply be replicated by imitation for large numbers of aspirants. (Added per Round 1 review, Finding S1 — an earlier draft of this document retro-traced gravity 6 to forces that did not actually generate it, and left a dangling cross-reference at Cell 2B-i pointing to a force entry that did not exist. This entry closes that gap.)**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** Pachomius founded the first cenobitic monastery at Tabennesi c. 320, establishing a written Rule, common property, and formal offices (housemaster, steward) — a genuine institutional innovation distinct from Antony's own solitary model (Doc_01 §2.1; Doc_02 §1.2). **Confidence: Widely Accepted**, matching Doc_02 §9's own rating for the Pachomian corpus's existence, general content, and Pachomian origin.
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's own preliminary forces identification (Doc_01 §7) frames this as a practical and internally-felt problem, not an external administrative response: how does total ascetic formation extend beyond one extraordinary individual's own solitary achievement to the many who sought to imitate it, without either diluting the achievement or requiring each aspirant to independently rediscover Antony's own singular path. The *Lives of Pachomius* (Doc_02 §1.2) present this as continuous with, not opposed to, the same total-commitment logic already established by gravity 1 (withdrawal) and gravity 2 (spiritual combat) — applied now to a communal rather than solely individual register.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** Directly generates gravity 6 (*koinōnia*, Supporting, Doc_04 §6). Per **Cross-Cell Connection**: this same force is the origin-point of Cell 2B-i's ongoing authority tension (gravity 10) — the office-based model this force establishes does not replace the pre-existing elder-based model (gravity 3), it coexists with and contests it for the remainder of this world's span, which is precisely what Cell 2B-i documents as an ongoing dynamic rather than a one-time resolution.

### Cell 2A — Ongoing / External

**Governing question:** What external forces required this community's continuous response and adaptation throughout its active life?

**Force 2A-i: Ongoing economic and administrative embeddedness in the surrounding Nile valley and Roman provincial economy.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** The Nepheros papyrological archive documents ordinary monastic economic and legal correspondence — business conducted with, not apart from, the surrounding administrative and commercial world (Doc_02 §5.2). Kellia's excavated commercial infrastructure corroborates ongoing trade activity (Doc_02 §5.1). **Confidence: Documented for Kellia's material infrastructure** (Doc_02 §9); **the Nepheros archive itself carries no assigned confidence level in Doc_02 and a Melitian-representativeness caveat** (Doc_04 §3's own correction, carried forward here rather than restated as settled).
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This is the force this document can document least confidently at Layer 2 specifically — this world's own literary self-presentation (Athanasius, the *Apophthegmata*) emphasizes total withdrawal and does not, in its own preserved voice, dwell on the economic entanglement the documentary record independently shows. **Named Tension, carried at full strength:** Goehring's own critique (Doc_01 §3; Doc_02 §7) argues this represents a genuine gap between this world's rhetorical self-understanding and its practiced reality, not merely an omission in what survives; this document has not independently verified Goehring's specific documentary claims beyond the volume's general thesis (Doc_02 §11 item 4, still unresolved) and does not resolve whether the silence is rhetorical choice, genuine felt-experience, or source-survival accident. **Flagged per Round 1 review, Finding C4:** this Layer 2 account, like Force 2A-ii's below, is built substantially from modern source-critical reasoning (survivorship analysis, rhetorical-versus-practiced-reality argument) rather than direct from-within testimony, because direct from-within testimony on this specific point is not available in this world's own evidence base — this document's own honest position is that this world did not thematize this tension in its own preserved voice, not that this document is supplying an external analytic overlay in place of a recoverable from-within account.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** Directly generates gravity 4 (manual labor as concurrent discipline) and gravity 8 (economic/social embeddedness, Tensional — precisely because this force's Layer 2 documentation is thin relative to its Layer 1 and Layer 3 evidence, exactly the discrepancy the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check flags, Doc_04 §3).

**Force 2A-ii: The Melitian schism as a persistent, doctrinally distinct rival ascetic movement occupying the same geographic and social space.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** Melitian ascetic communities are documented in the same period and region as this world's Nicene-communion material, with their own monastic archive (Nepheros, Section above) and organizational forms (Doc_01 §8.2). **Confidence: Documented** as to the Melitian communities' existence and rough contemporaneity; **Inferential / Thin** as to whether Melitian and Nicene-communion ascetic practice were organizationally distinguishable in ordinary daily life (Doc_01 §11 item 1, still unresolved, carried forward again here).
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's own Nicene-communion sources are essentially silent about Melitian ascetics as a live, named rival presence — a silence this document treats, consistent with Doc_02 §6's own reasoning about structural absence, as potentially informative rather than neutral: Athanasius's own documented role as the Melitian schism's most powerful ecclesiastical adversary (Doc_02 §6) may mean this world's own literary self-presentation, produced substantially under Athanasius's own influence, had reason not to dwell on Melitian ascetic communities as legitimate co-participants in the same formation logic.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** This document cannot yet trace this force's formation impact with confidence beyond what has already been noted regarding gravity 8's evidential complications — the open question of whether Melitian material should be read as directly informative about this world's own practice (Doc_04 §5's "fourth pattern" question, still unresolved) remains a genuine limit on this cell's Layer 3 claims, named rather than papered over.

### Cell 2B — Ongoing / Internal *(includes Transmission dimension)*

**Governing question:** What internal forces sustained this community from within, generated its characteristic internal tensions, and shaped how it transmitted itself to the next generation?

**Force 2B-i: The unresolved, ongoing tension between person-based (elder) and office-based (Rule) models of legitimate authority.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** The Pachomian Rule and offices (housemaster, steward) coexist historically, in the same decades and broader region, with the *Apophthegmata*'s own elder-disciple authority structure (Doc_01 §6; Doc_02 §§1.2, 1.5). **Confidence: Widely Accepted** as to both authority modes' independent existence and rough contemporaneity.
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's own material does not present this as an abstract organizational-theory question; it is lived as two genuinely different registers of what it means to submit to legitimate spiritual authority — the *Apophthegmata*'s own recurring emphasis on personal obedience to a specific elder's specific counsel, versus the Rule's own emphasis on obedience to an office regardless of who currently holds it (Doc_02 §1.2). Even Pachomius himself, the founder of the office-based model, is remembered in terms borrowed from elder-authority vocabulary (Doc_05 §1; Doc_07 §3) — suggesting this world's own participants did not experience the two models as cleanly opposed doctrines but as two genuinely coexisting, only partially reconciled registers of the same underlying question.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** This is gravity 10 (Tensional) in its full forces-analysis form. Per **Cross-Cell Connection**, this force does not originate in Cell 2B alone — it is the ongoing, unresolved expression of Force 1B-iii (**cross-reference corrected per Round 1 review, Finding S1** — an earlier draft of this document pointed here at "the scale-problem force that generated gravity 6 at Cell 1A/1B," a force that was never actually documented anywhere in the matrix; Force 1B-iii now supplies it), meaning this world's central ongoing internal tension is itself a direct, traceable consequence of its own initiating forces, not an independent development.

**Force 2B-ii: The intellectual intensification of ascetic psychology within Strand C, culminating in Evagrius's systematized scheme.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** Evagrius Ponticus, a Greek-philosophically-trained participant uniquely educated relative to this world's own norm, produced a systematic *praktikē*→*apatheia*→*theōria* scheme (Doc_03 §§1.6–1.7, **citation corrected per Round 1 review, Finding C2**) and an eight-fold *logismoi* taxonomy (Doc_03 §1.5) while resident at Kellia (Doc_02 §1.4). **Confidence: Widely Accepted** as to authorship and general content (Doc_02 §1.4, §9).
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** Within Strand C specifically, this systematization appears to have functioned as a genuine intensification of an already-present diagnostic practice (the affective-diagnostic fusion, Doc_07 §1) rather than an alien import — Evagrius's own scheme organizes, rather than replaces, vocabulary and practice already present in less systematized form across the broader tradition (Doc_04 §2's Interaction-test finding that gravity 9 "extends" gravity 2).
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** Generates gravity 9 (Supporting, Strand-C-bound per Doc_04's own Persistence-test finding). Per **Transmission Specificity**, required explicitly in this cell: this systematization's own transmission was concentrated in a single author's own corpus, at Kellia specifically, among what this world's evidence suggests was a more educated, philosophically literate sub-population than the norm (Doc_01 §4) — meaning the very intellectual achievement that made this force so formative for Strand C also made it structurally narrow in exactly the way that would later prove consequential (Cell 3A below). Who transmitted it: Evagrius's own direct disciples and, subsequently, the wider ascetic circle at Kellia and Nitria that had access to a Greek-literate teaching relationship — a selection effect (education, language, geographic proximity to Kellia specifically) that this document names explicitly rather than treating this force's spread as uniform or representative of this world's own median participant.

### Cell 3A — Ending/Transforming / External

**Governing question:** What external forces brought this world's distinct form to an end or transformed it into something different?

**Force 3A-i: Episcopal and conciliar intervention in the First Origenist Controversy (399–400), scattering Strand C's intellectual leadership.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** Theophilus of Alexandria's Festal Letter of 399 initially attacked anthropomorphite literalism, then reversed course into an anti-Origenist campaign; a council at Alexandria in 400 condemned Origen and his monastic followers, and the "Tall Brothers" and other Origenist ascetics were expelled from Egypt that year (Doc_01 §2.3, corrected per that document's own Round 1/2 review to hold the 399 letter and the 400 reversal as distinct, sequential events). **Confidence: Documented as to the 400 synod and expulsion** (**corrected per Round 2 review, Finding N1** — an earlier draft used "Well-established," not one of the Framework's five fixed levels; the synod and expulsion themselves are well-attested events, per Doc_01 §2.3, warranting Documented rather than the "Contested" label used elsewhere in this document for genuinely disputed points); **Contested as to Theophilus's own motives for the reversal**, a scholarly dispute this document carries forward at full strength per **Named Tension** rather than adjudicating.
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This document's evidence base does not preserve a first-person account of how Strand C's own participants experienced this rupture — Evagrius himself died in January 399, before the controversy fully broke, meaning the systematizer of this force's own target vocabulary never directly witnessed the controversy that would later scatter his intellectual circle. **Reported-Experience Status applies to what can be said:** the expulsion and scattering of named participants (the Tall Brothers, eventually finding refuge under John Chrysostom in Constantinople) is well-documented as an external event, but this world's own interior experience of that expulsion — what it felt like to a Kellia-based ascetic to watch this happen — is not directly attested in this document's evidence base and is not invented here.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** This is the historically decisive fracture event for gravity 9 specifically. Per **Cross-Cell Connection**, this external force's severity is directly explained by Cell 2B-ii's own internal finding: gravity 9's structural narrowness (single-author, Kellia-concentrated, philosophically literate sub-population) made it a legible, isolable target for external condemnation in a way that the broader, more diffusely-attested gravities (1–5, 7) were not. A force that had been genuinely cross-strand and broadly attested could not have been excised in a single conciliar action the way a strand-bound, single-author intellectual achievement could. This document does not treat this as "decisive corroboration" of gravity 9's Doc_04 classification (per Doc_04 §4's own correction of that earlier overreach) but as the historical consequence that classification's own reasoning predicts.

**Force 3A-ii: The longer imperial-ecclesiastical trend toward doctrinal and institutional centralization, of which the Origenist controversy is one early instance.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** The broader 4th–5th century trajectory of increasing conciliar and episcopal authority over monastic doctrine and practice, continuing well past this world's own c. 430 closing boundary toward the Council of Chalcedon (451) and beyond (Doc_01 §2.3). **Confidence: Widely Accepted** as a general historical trend; the specific claim that this trend is what eventually produces the more centrally governed, civically assertive federation model associated with Shenoute's White Monastery (Doc_01 §2.3, explicitly excluded from this world's own scope) is **Inferential / Thin** (**label completed per Round 2 review, Finding N2**) — this document does not independently verify a direct causal chain from the 399–400 controversy to Shenoute's later model, only notes the trend's general direction.
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** Not directly attested within this world's own closing boundary; this document does not construct a Layer 2 account of a trend this world's participants could not themselves have recognized as a completed pattern, since it continues past their own world's own span.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** Named here as context for Cell 3B's own internal-versus-external synthesis question below, not claimed as having a directly traceable formation impact on any specific gravity within this document's own evidence base.

### Cell 3B — Ending/Transforming / Internal *(includes Transmission dimension)*

**Governing question:** What internal forces fractured this community, generated successor movements, or led to the dissolution of its characteristic form?

**Force 3B-i: The unresolved authority tension (gravity 10) as a standing internal vulnerability, not merely a stable ongoing feature.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** No single dateable event; this is the same historical substrate as Cell 2B-i, read here for its bearing on this world's own closing rather than its ongoing operation. **Confidence: Widely Accepted**, same basis as 2B-i.
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's own material never resolves this tension within its own span (Doc_04 §6; Doc_07 §3) — there is no surviving evidence of participants experiencing the tension as approaching crisis or resolution before this world's own boundary closes. This document does not manufacture a Layer 2 account of anticipated crisis where the evidence shows only ongoing, unresolved coexistence.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact):** Per **Cross-Cell Connection**, and taking up directly the question Doc_07 §10 explicitly reserved for this document (Doc_04 §8 item 5: whether this world's eventual closing was primarily internally or externally driven): this document's own synthesis, reasoned from Cells 3A and 3B together, is that **the two forces are not independently sufficient and should not be adjudicated as competing explanations.** The external force (3A-i, the Origenist controversy) provided the proximate, dateable rupture; but it was effective specifically because it struck a gravity (9) that was already the most strand-concentrated, least cross-strand-integrated element in this world's own ecology — itself a downstream consequence of the still-unresolved authority tension (gravity 10) that had never generated a stable, cross-strand mechanism for adjudicating exactly this kind of internal intellectual dispute before an external actor did so instead. This document's answer to Doc_07's reserved question is therefore neither "internal" nor "external" but **conjunctural**: an internal structural vulnerability (the never-resolved authority tension, meaning no internal body existed that could have contained the Origenist dispute before it required external intervention) combined with an external trigger (episcopal action) that specifically exploited that vulnerability. Neither force alone explains the outcome; their interaction does.

**Force 3B-ii (Transmission): The shift from live, oral, elder-mediated teaching to written, retrospectively compiled anthology.**

- **Layer 1 (Historical Event):** The *Apophthegmata Patrum*'s written compilation occurs across the 5th–6th centuries, substantially after this world's own c. 430 closing boundary, by anonymous editors whose selection and arrangement choices are not directly attested (Doc_01 §2.3; Doc_02 §§1.5, 2.3). Palladius's *Lausiac History* (c. 419–420) and Cassian's transmission of Evagrian material to Gaul (writing through the 420s, using the deliberate *apatheia*→*puritas cordis* substitution, Doc_03 §1.18) both fall at or just past this world's own boundary, already treating the material as a substantially completed body of teaching to be gathered and exported rather than a still-forming, live tradition (Doc_01 §2.3). **Confidence: Documented** as to the general chronology of compilation relative to this world's closing; **Inferential / Thin** as to the specific editorial selection criteria the *Apophthegmata*'s compilers actually applied (Doc_02 §1.5 and §2.3's own "compiler-mediation" caveat, carried forward — **citation corrected per Round 1 review, Finding S4**, from an earlier draft's miscite to Doc_02 §11 item 2, which is actually the unrelated Nepheros "fourth pattern" question).
- **Layer 2 (World's Own Experience):** This world's own participants, by definition, could not have experienced their own material's later compilation — this is a force that acts on this world's *legacy*, not on its lived experience, and this document does not construct a Layer 2 account where none is available. What can be said from within: the *apophthegma* form's own occasion-bound character (Doc_06 §1.8; Doc_07 §6) meant this world's own teaching practice did not, by its own internal logic, generate an archive — the later compilers' intervention was necessary precisely because this world's own formation logic did not anticipate or provide for its own preservation.
- **Layer 3 (Formation Impact), Transmission Specificity applied:** **What was transmitted:** individual, occasion-bound sayings and named-elder attributions. **By whom:** anonymous later compilers, working after this world's own closing boundary, with demonstrable selection and arrangement choices (Doc_02 §2.3) but no directly attested selection criteria. **With what selection effects:** the *ammas'* own material survives thin relative to the male-authored bulk (Doc_02 §1.6) — a selection effect this document reads, consistent with Doc_02 §6's Affirmative Duty discussion, as revealing something about which voices the compilers' own social context privileged, though this document does not claim certainty about the compilers' specific reasons. **What this reveals about which forces most threatened this world's own literary output:** the compilation gap itself is evidence that oral, elder-mediated, non-institutionalized teaching is exactly the kind of formation output most vulnerable to loss absent external intervention — the same structural feature (non-institutional, occasion-bound teaching) that made this world's formation logic distinctive (Doc_07 §§2, 6) is also what made its own preservation entirely dependent on actors outside itself. A related, later sequel: Evagrius's own corpus, following the separate 553 condemnation at the Second Council of Constantinople (well outside this world's own boundary, and explicitly not conflated with the 399–400 controversy per Doc_04 §4's correction), survives substantially through pseudonymous attribution (chiefly to Nilus of Ancyra) and through Syriac and Armenian translation streams unaffected by the Constantinopolitan decision (Doc_02 §1.4) — named here as the furthest downstream instance of this same transmission-vulnerability pattern, not treated as part of this world's own c. 430-bounded forces analysis.
