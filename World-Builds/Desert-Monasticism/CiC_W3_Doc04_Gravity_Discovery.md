## 3. Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check (per Framework Part III)

Per the Framework: *"A candidate gravity should not be classified Primary unless the evidence supporting it independently reaches Documented or Widely Accepted confidence... A candidate that passes the gravity tests impressively but rests primarily on Inferential/Thin evidence should be classified Supporting or Tensional regardless of how strongly it appears to organize the ecology, with the discrepancy between apparent organizing strength and evidential support noted explicitly rather than resolved by upgrading the classification."*

- **Candidates 1, 3, 4, 5** all rest on confidence levels Doc_02 independently assigns as Documented or Widely Accepted across multiple, independent evidence types (Doc_02 §9 confidence map) — no discrepancy between gravity-test strength and evidential confidence.
- **Candidate 2 (general spiritual combat)** rests on Widely Accepted confidence (Doc_02 §1.1, §1.5) — clear.
- **The Rubenson/Antony-literacy tension applies to candidates 1 and 3 specifically, addressed here per Doc_01/Doc_02's explicit routing to this Cross-Check:** if Rubenson's reading of the *Letters of Antony* is substantially correct, Antony was not the unlettered rustic Athanasius portrays (Doc_01 §10), which would qualify — without undermining — candidates 1 (withdrawal) and 3 (elder authority): both gravities are independently attested across the whole *Apophthegmata* tradition and the Pachomian corpus, not solely through Antony's own characterization, so their confidence basis does not depend on resolving the Rubenson/Athanasius question. This document treats the tension as a live qualification on how *any single figure's* formation is narrated, not as a threat to either gravity's cross-strand attestation.
- **Candidate 6 (koinōnia)** rests on Widely Accepted confidence for the Pachomian corpus generally (Doc_02 §1.2) — the confidence level itself is not the limiting factor here; the limiting factor is Persistence and Cross-Strand testing (Section 5 below, **citation corrected per Round 1 review, Finding 8**).
- **Candidate 9 (Evagrian systematization).** Doc_02 §1.4 rates Evagrius's ascetic-practical corpus **Widely Accepted** for authorship and general content — a confidence level that meets, not fails, the Primary floor. The Cross-Check as the Framework actually defines it does **not** bar candidate 9 from Primary. What genuinely disqualifies it is the Persistence and Formation tests (Section 2 above): it **fails cross-strand persistence** entirely, with no comparable systematization in Strand A or B. Single-author concentration (an Author Gravity/representativeness concern, correctly flagged at generation in Section 1) is a *distinct* axis from evidential confidence, and this document does not conflate the two: candidate 9 is held at Supporting on Persistence grounds, exactly as candidate 6 is, and its confidence level (Widely Accepted) would not by itself have barred Primary status.
- **Candidate 7 (practical scriptural engagement)** rests on Widely Accepted confidence for the *Apophthegmata*'s general content (Doc_02 §1.5), tempered by that same source's own compiler-mediation caveat — sufficient for Primary classification given strong performance across the other five tests, but this document flags the compiler-mediation caveat as a standing qualification rather than treating it as settled.
- **Candidate 8 (economic embeddedness).** Doc_02 §9's Documented tier lists only Kellia; the Nepheros archive carries no assigned confidence level in Doc_02 and is subject to a heavy Melitian-representativeness caveat (Doc_02 §5.2, and the still-open Doc_01 §11 item 1 / Doc_02 §11 item 5 question of whether Melitian and Nicene-communion practice were organizationally indistinguishable). Candidate 8's point-evidence is accordingly limited — Kellia alone is Documented; Nepheros is unrated and caveated — while the larger interpretive claim that this represents a general world-wide pattern rather than a Lower-Egypt-concentrated one remains at Contested/Inferential confidence (Doc_02 §11 item 4; Doc_01 §11 item 6, Goehring's specific claims not independently verified). This evidential picture *reinforces*, rather than undermines, this document's classification of candidate 8 as Tensional rather than Primary or Supporting — a gap between real but partial point-evidence and thin generalizing confidence is exactly the Cross-Check's concern.
- **Candidate 10 (authority tension)** rests on Widely Accepted confidence for both of its constituent bodies of evidence (the Pachomian corpus, Doc_02 §1.2; the *Apophthegmata*, §1.5) — again, not a confidence problem. Its Tensional classification (Section 6) follows from its own nature as an opposition between two other gravities rather than a broadly organizing force in its own right, which is precisely the Framework's definition of a Tensional gravity, not from any evidential weakness.

## 4. Forces test (per Forces Framework Step 4, against Doc_01's preliminary forces)

Doc_01 §7 names four preliminary forces: what generated this world (martyrdom's unavailability after persecution's end), what larger world it was embedded in (Christianizing imperial Egypt), what it was responding to (the problem of total formation without martyrdom, and of replicating Antony's path at scale), and what it was refusing (ordinary settled life as sufficient, without full ecclesial separation).

- **Candidates 1–5 (withdrawal, combat, elder authority, labor, diakrisis) all intensify under the generating force** (martyrdom's unavailability) rather than merely coexisting with it — each is a direct, load-bearing response to that specific historical pressure, which is itself strong corroboration of their Primary status beyond the six tests alone.
- **Candidate 6 (koinōnia) and candidate 10 (authority tension) both emerge specifically from the "replicating Antony's path at scale" sub-problem** named in Doc_01 §7 — Pachomius's cenobitic innovation solves a problem (how does formation scale beyond one extraordinary individual) that candidates 1–5 alone do not solve; candidate 10 is the friction this solution generates against the pre-existing elder-model, not a separately generated force.
- **Candidate 9 (Evagrian systematization).** Doc_02 §1.4 attributes Evagrius's pseudonymous transmission specifically to the **553** condemnation at the Second Council of Constantinople, not to the 399–400 controversy — two events roughly 150 years apart. The 399–400 controversy, which occurred within this world's own c. 430 closing window, scattered the Origenist circle at Kellia and drove the Tall Brothers' expulsion (Doc_01 §2.3); it is a real, world-internal event, but it is not among Doc_01 §7's four Step-1 preliminary forces, and Doc_02 §8 explicitly reserves this whole pattern for **Doc_08's Forces Document** rather than for resolution here. Candidate 9's Supporting classification rests on the Persistence-test failure established in Sections 2–3 above, on evidence internal to this world's own c. 320s–c. 430 span; the 399–400 controversy and its 553 sequel are flagged here only as material Doc_08 should develop, not as grounds for this document's own classification.
- **Candidate 8 (economic embeddedness) holds steady rather than intensifying or fracturing** under the named forces — it is less a response to the martyrdom-unavailability force than a structural precondition (the world had to eat) that persisted underneath the more visible responses, which is consistent with its Tensional rather than Primary classification: it does not organize the ecology's own self-understanding the way candidates 1–5 do, even though it is materially real throughout.
- **Candidate 7 (practical scriptural engagement), added per Round 3 review, Finding C2:** neither clearly intensifies nor fractures under any of Doc_01 §7's four named forces — its Primary classification rests on the six-test and Cross-Check results (Sections 2–3), not on a strong forces-test relationship, consistent with it already being flagged (Section 6, Finding NEW-3) as the softest of the six Primary gravities.

## 7. Strand Determination reconsidered in light of gravity testing

Doc_01 §6 flagged its own two/three-strand framing as "an interpretive choice made under real uncertainty," reserved for Doc_04's cross-strand testing to assess. Having now run that testing: the three-strand finding holds up well. All six Primary gravities are genuinely cross-strand, which supports treating this world as one coherent ecology rather than three unrelated formation patterns coexisting by coincidence. At the same time, exactly the features that most sharply differentiate the three strands in Doc_01 (Pachomius's Rule, Evagrius's systematic psychology) are also the two Supporting gravities that fail cross-strand persistence here, and the tension between the first of those and the elder-authority Primary gravity is itself named as a distinct Tensional gravity (10) — a structurally satisfying result: the strands are real and substantively distinct precisely where Doc_01 said they were, and genuinely unified precisely where the shared vocabulary in Doc_03 suggested they would be.
