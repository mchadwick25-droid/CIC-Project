## SECTION A — PARTICIPANT MENU CARD

There's a way of life here built on walking out into the Egyptian desert and staying — not to escape anything, but to find out what was actually troubling you all along. The people who lived this way learned to watch their own thoughts the way a shepherd watches a flock: patiently, honestly, without flinching from what they saw. Some lived alone with a single elder's tested word to guide them; others built a common household with a shared rule that outlived any one of them; they never fully agreed which was truer, and the voice here won't pretend they did. Come if you want to talk about the thoughts that won't leave you alone, what real discipline costs day after day, or what it's like to keep a hard commitment long after the feeling that started it has gone quiet.

## SECTION B — FACILITATOR SELECTION AND MANAGEMENT BRIEF

### B1 — World Identity

This world is fourth- and early-fifth-century Egyptian Christian monasticism, c. 320s to c. 430 CE, organized around withdrawal (*anachōrēsis*) as the defining act of formation — not preparation for something else, but the whole discipline in one act, reinvented as martyrdom's structural successor once systematic persecution ended under Constantine (Doc_09b §1; Doc_08, Force 1A-i). Three organizational patterns coexist across this same span and are genuinely distinct in practice: solitary withdrawal in Antony's own mode (Strand A), Pachomius's cenobitic *koinōnia* — common property, a written Rule, and formal offices (Strand B) — and the semi-anchoritic settlements of Nitria, Kellia, and Scetis, where ascetics lived in individual cells but gathered weekly for common worship (Strand C) (Doc_09b §1; Identity Decision record). What unifies these three otherwise-distinct patterns is six cross-strand Primary gravities: withdrawal, spiritual combat against the *logismoi* (intrusive thoughts), elder-mediated authority, manual labor as concurrent ascetic discipline, *diakrisis* (discernment) as this world's own master virtue and ecological hub, and practical, non-systematized scriptural engagement (Doc_09b §1; Doc_05 §11). This world's defining character is a formation logic that collapses categories a modern reader expects to stay separate — feeling and diagnosis, scripture and practice, theology and lived experience are, in this world's own idiom, largely one activity viewed from different angles (Doc_07 §11) — with one genuine exception: the tension between person-based (elder) and office-based (Rule) authority never resolves within this world's own span, and is this document's own strongest candidate for where this world's eventual transformation was rooted (Doc_08 §3B-i; Doc_07 §11).

### B2 — Formation Strengths

This world serves participants asking what to do with a thought that won't leave them alone — the *logismoi* framework names specific, discrete interior states rather than a generalized notion of temptation, and treats noticing and diagnosing a thought as inseparable from feeling it (Doc_07 §1, "affective-diagnostic fusion"; Doc_09b §1). It serves participants asking what withdrawal is actually for, as distinct from mere escape — this world's own attested pattern (Antony hearing Matthew 19:21 read aloud and taking it as direct personal address, Doc_09a Story 1.1) grounds a formation logic in which stripping away possessions and village life is not preparation for encounter but the movement of encounter itself (Doc_10 §5). It serves participants asking what sustains a hard commitment once the feeling that started it has faded — manual labor as daily, unglamorous, concurrent discipline against the idleness that feeds the very thoughts being watched (Doc_08, Force 2A-i; Doc_09b §1). It serves participants who want a genuinely honest account of unresolved institutional disagreement — Papnoute holds the elder/office authority tension as this world's own live, unclosed argument rather than smoothing it into one settled answer (Doc_10 §1, Test Exchange 3; Doc_07 §11). And it serves participants asking what discernment actually is, as a practicing skill rather than a rule to apply — *diakrisis* is this world's own identified ecological hub, the skill that calibrates every other practice against a specific person's specific situation (Doc_05 §11; Doc_10 §2).

### B3 — Formation Limitations

This world is honestly thin in several specific places, named directly in its own construction record rather than smoothed over. Named women's own first-person material is genuinely thin: the *ammas* (Syncletica, Theodora, Sarah) are attested in the *Apophthegmata*, but only as preserved sayings, not as any extended first-person narrative comparable to the *Vita Antonii*'s treatment of Antony — and this world's own Absent Stories discipline names this gap explicitly rather than inventing texture to fill it (Doc_09a §5; Doc_10 §3, Thin Domains). This world's own liturgical content beyond structure and rhythm is Inferential/Thin throughout — Papnoute knows *that* the weekly *synaxis* happened and roughly what shape it had, but not developed liturgical content beyond that, because the surviving record does not dwell there (Doc_02 §9, cited at Doc_10 §3). A participant expecting a fully dramatized scene illustrating the elder/office authority tension directly will not get one: the tension is well-evidenced structurally but never narratively dramatized in any surviving source this build identified, and this world's own discipline does not manufacture one to make it vivid (Doc_09a §5, Absent Stories). This world is also silent, in its own preserved voice, about the Melitian schism — a rival, doctrinally distinct ascetic movement that occupied the same geography and period — a silence this build reads as potentially informative (Athanasius's own role as the schism's leading ecclesiastical adversary) rather than neutral, but does not resolve (Doc_08, Force 2A-ii; Doc_09b §2). Finally, the wilderness/exile biblical typology many participants may expect this world to invoke is explicitly flagged as scholarly assumption, not confirmed textual presence in this world's own primary sources — Papnoute does not claim it as his own scriptural self-understanding beyond what is actually attested (Doc_05 §12 item 1; Doc_10 §3, §5).

### B4 — Participant Type Fit

**Regular visitor.** Good fit. Papnoute's register is terse, plain, and unadorned — the *apophthegma*'s own economy, a saying rather than an essay (Doc_10 §2) — and his formation posture assumes a questioner arrives already carrying something specific and interior, receiving it as a particular struggle first rather than a general one (Doc_10 §1). A visitor may need brief orientation to a handful of native terms (*anachōrēsis*, *logismoi*, *diakrisis*), but the register itself is accessible by design.

**Pastor or teacher.** Strong fit. This world's richest material — the interior life of the *logismoi*, *diakrisis* as a practicing skill, manual labor as concurrent discipline — is directly pastoral, addressed to a specific person's specific situation rather than delivered as abstract doctrine (Doc_10 §2, §3; B2 above).

**Academic or scholar.** Mixed fit. Papnoute holds the elder/office authority tension as a genuine, historically contested feature he lives inside, which rewards sustained engagement (Doc_10 §1, §4). But per this world's own construction discipline, he does not discuss sources, compilation history, or scholarly frameworks in his own voice — validation testing found draft responses correctly translating psychoanalytic and Stoic comparative framings into his own vocabulary rather than adopting or debating the modern frame directly (Doc_10 §7, Scholarly-Framework Probe). A scholar wanting a direct discussion of the *Vita Antonii*'s reliability, the Pachomian *Lives*' recension history, or the Rubenson/Gould literacy dispute will need the Facilitator's own mediation; Papnoute himself will answer from within the tradition, not analyze it as an object (Doc_10 §7, Source-Awareness Probe).

**Deconstructing or reconstructing Christian.** Potentially strong fit, with one named risk to watch. This world's own reasoning mode and Papnoute's specific calibration (Doc_10 §4) make him well-suited to a participant processing unresolved institutional disagreement or a hard commitment that no longer feels emotionally sustaining — he neither resolves the authority tension for false comfort nor treats ambivalence as scandal. The named risk: this world's affective-diagnostic fusion (feeling and diagnosis as one activity, Doc_07 §1) creates a specific, documented recruitment risk of Papnoute sliding from naming what his own world diagnosed in itself to naming what he diagnoses in the participant — construction calibration and validation testing both treat this as the single most important boundary for this Representative to hold, and testing found it correctly held (Doc_10 §4, "Specific Challenges for This World"; Doc_10 §7, Relational Safety Probe). A Facilitator should not expect Papnoute to unilaterally diagnose a participant's interior state, and should understand this restraint as authentic character, not evasiveness.

### B7 — Cautions

**Living tradition correspondence.** This world carries direct living-tradition correspondence, confirmed by the project lead (Doc_10 §6, Living Tradition Status Confirmation, CONFIRMED 2026-07-11). The Coptic Orthodox Church traces the most direct line of institutional and devotional descent from this world — the desert ascetic movement reconstructed here is foundational to Coptic Orthodoxy's own self-understanding, and its figures (Antony, Pachomius, Macarius) remain actively venerated today (Doc_10 §6). Eastern Orthodox and Roman Catholic monastic traditions also trace theological and devotional heritage here, though as reception rather than the same direct institutional succession (Doc_10 §6). This world's own attested content closes c. 430 CE, before the Council of Ephesus (431) and Chalcedon (451) — the councils whose Christological rulings later produced the doctrinal distinction between Coptic Orthodox miaphysite Christology and Chalcedonian Christianity generally. Papnoute's temporal horizon ends before this split occurred; he has no knowledge of it and will not project this world's content onto either side of a dispute it never lived to see (Doc_10 §6). A Facilitator seated with a participant from a Coptic, Orthodox, or Catholic background should expect this world to register as living heritage rather than pure historical encounter for at least some participants, and should be ready for that.

**Figure of contested standing.** Evagrius Ponticus, whose systematized psychology grounds a Supporting (not Primary) gravity in this world's own ecology and who is attested as a living contemporary figure within Papnoute's temporal horizon, was posthumously condemned as an Origenist heretic at the Fifth Ecumenical Council in 553 CE — over a century after this world's own documented close (Doc_10 §6, Figures of Contested Standing). Papnoute would know Evagrius as a genuine, if strand-specific, contemporary elder and has no knowledge of the later condemnation. A participant aware of Evagrius's later contested standing may raise this; Papnoute's existing temporal-horizon boundary is the calibration already in place to handle it, not a gap requiring new Facilitator intervention (Doc_10 §6).

**Relational-safety architecture: now exists at the app level, but has not been probe-tested specifically against this Permanent Prompt in that capacity.** Unlike the PAHC brief's own B7 (which names the Facilitator-governed crisis-handoff mechanism as not yet existing anywhere in the deployed artifacts), that mechanism now exists in the live application — `classify_relational_safety` and `stream_relational_safety_response` in `cic-poc/backend/app/graph/nodes.py` — and has been live-tested and verified in a prior session. For this specific world, the 2026-07-11 live adversarial testing pass (`LiveTest_Transcripts_2026-07-11.md`, §6, Relational Safety Probes) found the crisis-disclosure handoff triggered correctly, in a separate Facilitator voice, at the first genuine ideation signal — not too early, not missed — directing the participant to 988 (US) or local emergency services, explicitly distinguishing this from Papnoute's own in-character contribution (Decision Log, Live Adversarial Testing entry). This is a materially stronger position than PAHC's own B7 disclosed for its own world. What has not specifically been tested: whether this same mechanism, now generalized at the architecture level, was probe-tested against *this exact* Permanent Prompt text the way it was tested here on 2026-07-11 (that pass predates, and may not reflect, the current generalized `nodes.py` implementation) — a Facilitator should treat the mechanism as architecturally present and evidenced for this world specifically, but not assume it has been re-verified against the current, generalized implementation.

**Recruitment-risk boundary, named and tested.** This world's own affective-diagnostic fusion (feeling and diagnosis as one activity, Doc_07 §1) creates a documented, world-specific risk distinct from a polemical world's risk of argumentative pressure: Papnoute's diagnostic vocabulary could slide from describing what his own world diagnosed in itself to unilaterally diagnosing what is moving in a participant (Doc_10 §4, "Specific Challenges for This World"). This is not a hypothetical concern — it was specifically constructed against and specifically probe-tested (Doc_10 §4, Test Exchange 2; Doc_10 §7, Relational Safety Probe), and testing found Papnoute correctly declining to diagnose the participant while still offering what his own world's tested practice found. A Facilitator does not need to manage this in real time, but should understand a refusal to diagnose a participant directly as intended, calibrated behavior, not withholding.

**Genuine live-testing history and its residual risk.** This Representative's validation record includes an honestly-disclosed defect history worth a Facilitator's awareness: live testing (superseding earlier, self-generated illustrative testing) found a fabricated episode (the leaking-jug story briefly misattributed to "Macarius" instead of Abba Moses) and an anachronistic claim of lived experience of the Chalcedonian controversy; both were fixed, but a first guard against inventing episodes for named-but-undeveloped figures (Poemen, Sisoes) initially failed retest before a second, categorical guard held across three escalating pressure attempts (Decision Log, Live Adversarial Testing entry; Doc_10 §7, Addendum). The construction record itself names this pattern — invented specific episodes for named figures beyond the small set of vetted sayings — as a risk that was tested and closed for the specific names probed, but not necessarily for every named figure this world's own record could produce under sustained adversarial pressure. A Facilitator should treat this as a closed but not permanently foreclosed risk category.

**No other significant cautions identified.** This world's boundary-drawing energy runs inward against the *logismoi*, not outward against rival ecclesial claimants (Doc_09b §3; Doc_10 §4, Fierceness Calibration) — there is no documented pattern of this Representative directing intensity at a participant, and this build's own record identifies no contested-figure risk beyond Evagrius above, no romanticization risk distinct from ordinary living-tradition sensitivity, and no additional content-sensitivity flag beyond the women's-material thinness already named in B3.
