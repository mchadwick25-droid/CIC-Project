## 1. Affective/Emotional Life (extending Doc_05 §3)

This world's own emotional vocabulary is unusually explicit and cataloged, relative to most formation worlds: the *logismoi* (Doc_03 §1.5) name specific, discrete interior states rather than a generalized notion of temptation. Doc_05 §3 already established the dominant hope (*apatheia*/purity of heart) and dominant fear (self-deception, vainglory counterfeiting its own opposite). Integrated more fully here: this world's affective life is structured as a *diagnostic* practice, not merely an experienced one — the *logismoi* taxonomy (in its Evagrian, Strand C-specific form, Doc_04 gravity 9) functions simultaneously as felt experience and as an analytical category the participant is expected to apply to their own interior state in real time. This is a genuinely distinctive integration: emotional life and intellectual analysis are not separate domains here, they are the same activity viewed from two angles. A participant's feeling of anger, in this world's own logic, is not merely endured or expressed — it is immediately also an object of diagnostic attention (which *logismos* is this, what does it want, how should it be met). This affective-diagnostic fusion is most fully realized in Strand C (where Evagrius supplies the systematic vocabulary) but the underlying practice — feeling a thing and simultaneously assessing it — is attested more broadly across the *Apophthegmata* (Doc_02 §1.5), consistent with Doc_04's own general-form-vs-systematized-form distinction for gravity 2/9.

## 2. Intellectual/Conceptual Structures

This world produced, in Evagrius, one of the most systematic psychological-theological schemes of the entire late antique Christian world (*praktikē* → *apatheia* → *theōria*, Doc_03 §§1.6–1.7) — and this document treats that fact itself as ecologically significant, not merely biographically interesting. The scheme's very existence, within a world otherwise dominated by occasional, non-systematic teaching (the *apophthegma* form, Doc_06 §1.8), is a genuine anomaly requiring explanation, not just description. This document's own working explanation, consistent with Doc_04's Persistence-test finding that gravity 9 is strand-bound: systematic conceptual structure emerged specifically where a Greek-philosophically-trained participant (Evagrius, uniquely educated relative to this world's own norm, Doc_01 §4) encountered this world's raw ascetic material and organized it using intellectual tools this world did not otherwise generate internally. The system is native to this world's *content* (it describes real ascetic experience attested elsewhere) but not to this world's *characteristic mode of producing knowledge* (oral, occasional, elder-mediated). This is precisely why the system proved vulnerable when later external doctrinal scrutiny arrived (the 399–400 controversy, reserved for full Doc_08 treatment per Doc_04 §4's corrected reasoning) — it was, in a specific and demonstrable sense, this world's least characteristic intellectual product even as it became one of its most historically influential.

## 3. Authority Structures (extending Doc_05 §§4, 6)

Doc_04's gravity 10 (person-based vs. office-based authority) is this world's own central, unresolved authority tension, and this lens exists to draw out its full implications rather than restate its discovery. Three observations beyond what Doc_04/Doc_05 already established:

First, the tension is not symmetrical in its own evidentiary visibility: elder-based authority (gravity 3) is attested primarily through its *products* (the *apophthegmata* themselves), while office-based authority (gravity 6) is attested through its *instruments* (the Rule, the offices it names). This means the two authority modes are not just organizationally different but are known to us through structurally different kinds of evidence — a methodological, not merely historical, asymmetry this document flags for the Validation Layer (Doc_09 in this world's own sequence is the Story Inventory, per Doc_02 §2.3).

Second, the tension maps only loosely onto the three-strand geography (Doc_01 §6): Strand B is office-dominant and Strand A/C are elder-dominant, but Pachomius himself — the very founder of the office-based model — is remembered (Doc_05 §1) in terms that borrow directly from elder-authority vocabulary (a father whose personal example, not merely his Rule, forms his community). The office-based model did not simply replace the elder-based one within Strand B; it was built using the elder-based model's own legitimating vocabulary, even as it also formalized authority in ways that model resists.

Third, this authority tension is this document's strongest candidate for what a Representative emerging from this world (Doc_10) will need to navigate explicitly — a Representative drawn primarily from Strand A or C material will speak with elder-authority's own assumptions about how spiritual guidance works, and should not be expected to represent Strand B's institutional logic as if it were the same thing wearing different clothes.

## 4. Boundary Structures (lighter weight, per Doc_05 §4's own thinner evidence base)

This world's boundary-drawing energy runs predominantly inward (against the *logismoi*) rather than outward (against rival ecclesial claimants) — a genuine structural feature, not merely an absence of boundary material (Doc_01 §7; Doc_05 §4). Where an outward boundary does appear, it is thin and unresolved: the Melitian question (Doc_01 §8.2, §11 item 1; Doc_02 §5.2, §11 item 5; Doc_04 §5) remains this document's most significant open boundary item, carried forward again here rather than resolved, since no new evidence has emerged since Doc_04 to close it. This document adds one observation: the very thinness of this world's outward boundary-drawing is itself consistent with its own affective-diagnostic orientation (Section 1 above) — a formation logic oriented toward interior vigilance has structurally less occasion to produce polemical outward-boundary literature than a formation logic oriented toward doctrinal or institutional self-definition (contrast World #4, named "Donatism" in this build's own ground truth and characterized here as martyr-cult-anchored on this document's own reading, not an established cross-world finding; or World #6, "Imperial and Juridical Christianity," Doc_01 §4). This is offered as a plausible ecological connection, not a settled finding, and — per Doc_05 §12 item 4's own standard that cross-world comparisons in this build are claims pending confirmation, not established parallels — the World #4/#6 characterizations themselves carry the same flag (**added per Round 1 review, Finding 5**).

## 5. Formation Logic (synthesizing Doc_05 §1 and the gravity structure)

Restating Doc_05's own formation-logic reconstruction in integrated form: this world forms participants through progressive withdrawal, diagnostic engagement with interior thought, manual labor as concurrent discipline, and elder-mediated (or, in Strand B, office-mediated) correction — with *diakrisis* (Doc_06 §1.5) functioning as the governing skill that calibrates how intensely any individual participant should pursue the others. What this integration lens adds beyond Doc_05: formation here has no terminal state described with confidence. *Apatheia* and *theōria* (Doc_06 §§2.2–2.3) function as named goals within Evagrius's own systematic scheme, but this document's evidence base does not establish that most participants, across the broader, non-systematized tradition, understood formation as progressing toward a defined endpoint rather than as an open-ended, lifelong discipline without a claimed completion. This is a genuine gap this document flags rather than papering over with the Evagrian scheme's own more definite structure — most of this world's own evidence (the *Apophthegmata*'s repeated, non-cumulative sayings, Doc_02 §1.5) is more consistent with formation-as-ongoing-practice than formation-as-progression-toward-mastery.

## 6. Memory Structures

This world's relationship to its own memory is unusually mediated: virtually everything known about its interior life survives only because later compilers (the *Apophthegmata*'s anonymous editors, Doc_02 §2.3) chose to preserve it, in a compiled form produced after this world's own c. 430 closing boundary (Doc_01 §2.3). This document treats this compilation gap as itself formative evidence: a world whose primary teaching vehicle is the occasion-bound saying (Doc_06 §1.8) does not, by its own internal logic, generate an archive — it generates scattered, disposable-seeming utterances that only survive because someone outside the immediate teaching relationship decided they were worth writing down. This world's memory, in other words, was not designed by its own participants to be a memory at all; its transmission to us depends entirely on a layer of editorial intervention this world's own formation logic did not itself produce or anticipate. This should inform the Validation Layer directly: confidence in any specific saying's original context should remain calibrated to this structural fact, not merely to the saying's individual attestation.

## 7. Interpretive Ecology (extending Doc_05 §8)

Doc_05 §8 already established this world's practical-literal dominant interpretive mode, its documented contrast with World #2's systematic allegory, and Evagrius's own hybrid *antirrhēsis* technique (Doc_06 §3.1). This lens adds one integration point: this world's interpretive mode and its formation logic (Section 5 above) are the same practice under two descriptions. A verse invoked to answer a specific disciple's specific struggle (the *apophthegma*'s own scriptural deployment, Doc_05 §8.1) is not scriptural interpretation *applied to* formation; it is formation happening *through* scriptural interpretation, with no meaningful seam between the two activities in this world's own practice. This is consistent with, and further corroborates, Doc_05 §8.6's own Ecology→Theology finding that theology here emerges from lived practice rather than existing as a separable doctrinal layer.

## 9. Material Culture (extending Doc_02 §§5.1–5.2)

This world's material record is unusually rich relative to most formation worlds' typical evidence base: the Kellia excavations (over 1,500 structures, Doc_02 §5.1) and the Nepheros papyrological archive (Doc_02 §5.2) together mean this document's claims about daily economic and architectural life rest on genuinely independent, non-literary evidence streams — a methodological strength this document has leaned on repeatedly (gravity 4's cross-check status, Doc_04 §3; candidate 8's evidential mix, Doc_04 §3, corrected). **Integrated observation:** the excavated cell architecture (individual units with attached oratories, clustered around shared communal-service buildings, Doc_02 §5.1) is a physical instantiation of the solitude-versus-communal-gathering duality internal to Strand C's own daily practice — not gravity 10's elder-versus-office authority tension. Kellia is Strand C throughout, and Strand C's authority remains entirely elder-mediated (Doc_01 §6: "loose, non-codified spiritual authority of resident elders rather than a written rule or formal hierarchical office") — the cell (solitary practice) and the *synaxis* infrastructure (periodic communal worship, Doc_06 §2.6) built into the same settlement are both instances of the elder-authority pole, not evidence that the built environment "holds both poles" of gravity 10. What the architecture does show is that Strand C's own formation logic (Section 5 above) required material provision for both intensities of ascetic life — deep solitude and periodic communal accountability — without either displacing the other. This is a genuine integration finding about Strand C's internal formation logic, not a resolution-in-miniature of this world's cross-strand authority tension, which Section 11 correctly identifies as the one place this document's broader integration thesis does not hold.

## 11. Cross-Lens synthesis (Integrative Observation)

Reading Sections 1–9 together: this world's most distinctive integrative feature is the collapse of categories a modern reader would expect to remain separate — feeling and diagnosis (Section 1), scripture and formation (Section 7), theology and lived practice (Section 7, extending Doc_05 §8.6), and, within Strand C specifically, solitude and communal accountability held together materially (Section 9 — its actual contribution is about Strand C's internal formation logic, not gravity 10). This is not merely a stylistic observation; it is this document's strongest candidate for what makes this world's own formation logic genuinely distinct from its documented neighbors (World #9's text-centered, patronage-sustained model; World #2's systematic exegetical model, Doc_01 §8.1, §4) — not the presence or absence of any single practice, but the degree to which practices that elsewhere function as separable domains function here as a single, undifferentiated activity viewed from different angles. The one place this collapse does *not* hold, and holds least well, is exactly where Doc_04 already found this world's own single point of genuine, unresolved internal division: the authority tension (gravity 10, Section 3). Person-based and office-based authority are not two descriptions of one activity; they are two different activities competing for the same ecological space. This document's own synthesis is therefore that this world is characterized by an unusual degree of integration everywhere except in its own authority structure — and that this one genuine seam is precisely where its eventual closing pressure (Section 10) is most plausibly rooted.
