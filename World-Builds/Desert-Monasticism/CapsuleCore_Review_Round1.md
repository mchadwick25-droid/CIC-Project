## REVIEW: World Capsule Core — Abba Papnoute (World #3, Desert Monasticism)

### SUBSTANTIAL FINDINGS

**S1 — The "Note on file-code" contains a false claim and the file is consequently misnamed.**
- **Location:** Line 4 (the `**Note on file-code:**` block) and the filename `dm_World_Capsule_Core.md`.
- **Defect:** The note asserts "no world file-code was assigned earlier in this world's build (unlike World #7's 'syr,' assigned at that world's own Doc_06 step)." This is factually wrong. Doc_06 line 4 explicitly assigns **`World-code: desert`** — at the Doc_06 step, in exact parallel to World #7's "syr." The deployment lexicon chunks already use `desertlex001…` etc. The correct filename per the template's convention (`[world-code]_World_Capsule_Core.md`) is therefore `desert_World_Capsule_Core.md`, not `dm_…`. This is precisely the invented/self-contradictory claim class this project's review is meant to catch: the note invents a gap ("no code assigned") that the record contradicts. Compounding it: a meta-note addressed to "a coach thread" about file-naming is builder scaffolding sitting inside a file that is "always present in context for every conversation" — an analytical-register intrusion into a Core that should be pure inhabited voice. The whole note should be removed and the file renamed to `desert_`.
- **Severity: SUBSTANTIAL.**

**S2 — Temporal horizon opens at a different point than the Permanent Prompt and Doc_10 (internal inconsistency, dimension F).**
- **Location:** Section 8 (The Span You Speak From), line 88: "You speak from a community that has lived from the years an old man first went out alone to test himself against the desert, through the founding of common households…"
- **Defect:** The Capsule opens the lived span with the *solitary anchoritic withdrawal* (Antony, pre-320s), placing it as the first phase *before* the founding of the common households. But the companion Permanent Prompt (line 5) anchors the horizon start explicitly at the *later* point: "Your temporal horizon runs from **the founding of the first common houses** of prayer and labor…" — and Doc_10's Temporal Horizon fixes the floor at "the 320s CE (Pachomius's founding of the koinōnia at Tabennesi)," with Antony's earlier withdrawal held only as "authorizing background." Doc_01 §2.1 deliberately retains the 320s floor as the "documented starting point," treating Antony's c.270s–280s withdrawal as pre-floor generative background, not as the span's opening. The Capsule's Section 8 thus contradicts both the Permanent Prompt's stated horizon start and Doc_01/Doc_10's deliberate floor decision. (A charitable reading — the world "remembers" Antony's generative withdrawal — is available, but the sentence frames it as the years the community "has lived from," i.e., the span's start, which is exactly what PP and Doc_10 place at the common houses.)
- **Severity: SUBSTANTIAL** (load-bearing calibration; dimension F requires the two artifacts share the same temporal horizon).

**S3 — "an old man first went out alone" mischaracterizes this world's own founding withdrawal.**
- **Location:** Section 8, line 88 (same sentence as S2).
- **Defect:** The originating solitary withdrawal in this world's own founding narrative is Antony's, and Doc_09a Story 1.1 (and Doc_01 §2.1) establish he was "not yet twenty years old and recently orphaned" — a *young* man renouncing his inheritance. Describing the span's originating act as "an old man first went out alone" is inaccurate to that founding fact. Even read archetypally (the desert *gerōn*), it misrepresents the actual character of the first going-out (a youth's renunciation). At minimum this should be revised to avoid the age mischaracterization.
- **Severity: SUBSTANTIAL** (factual mischaracterization contradicting Doc_09a Story 1.1; borderline given possible archetypal intent, but should not stand as written).

**S4 — Amma Sarah's one attested saying is paraphrased into something close to its opposite.**
- **Location:** Section 8, line 90: "one who answered visiting elders that a woman's nature was no different from a man's where the interior life was concerned."
- **Defect:** Sarah's actual attested saying (Doc_09a Story 2.3, web-verified in that document's Round 1 check) is: "According to nature I am a woman, but not according to my thoughts. It is I who am a man and you who are women." Sarah explicitly *affirms* a difference in nature ("according to nature I am a woman") while claiming manliness in thought — a pointed reversal, not an egalitarian "no difference" claim. The Capsule's paraphrase, "a woman's nature was no different from a man's," distorts the content and softens a provocative saying into a modern equal-natures sentiment the source does not state. This lands in a domain (named *ammas'* thin material) the build has repeatedly flagged as requiring exactness, and Doc_10's own Confidence-Under-Thinness probe specifically corrected overclaiming here.
- **Severity: SUBSTANTIAL** (misrepresents attested content in a sensitivity-flagged domain; borderline-cosmetic since it is a gist-paraphrase in a memory list, but the distortion is real).
