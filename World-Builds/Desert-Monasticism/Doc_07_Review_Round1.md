## (b) Findings

### Finding 1 — SUBSTANTIAL — Section 9, reinforced into Section 11

Section 9 claims the excavated Kellia cell architecture "is a physical instantiation of exactly the person-based/office-adjacent tension named in Section 3" (gravity 10) and that "this world's built environment was designed to hold both poles of it simultaneously." But gravity 10 (Doc_04 §2, §5, §6) is specifically the tension between elder-based (candidate 3) and office/Rule-based (candidate 6) authority, spanning the A/C–B strand boundary. Kellia is Strand C, which Doc_01 §6 states operates "under the loose, non-codified spiritual authority of resident elders rather than a written rule or formal hierarchical office" — Kellia sits entirely on the elder pole. Its architecture displays a solitude-versus-communal-gathering duality (cell versus synaxis), which is an axis of daily practice, not the elder-versus-office authority-constitution duality gravity 10 names. The synaxis is communal worship under elder authority, not office-based authority — the architecture does not "hold both poles" of gravity 10.

This is not an isolated hedge: Section 11 folds this claim into its list of category-collapses supporting the thesis that integration is this world's most distinctive feature — yet Section 11's own conclusion is that authority is precisely the *one place the collapse does not hold* ("Person-based and office-based authority are not two descriptions of one activity; they are two different activities"). The Section 9 claim and the Section 11 claim are in direct tension with each other.

**Recommended remedy:** reframe Section 9 to the tension it actually evidences (a within-Strand-C solitude/communion duality) and reconcile with Section 11's exception claim.

### Finding 2 — COSMETIC (should fix) — lens-selection rationale omits Formation Logic

The intro's rationale justifies eight lenses by name but the document applies nine — Section 5 (Formation Logic) is real, substantive content the rationale never names or justifies. The named eight are each genuinely and evidentially grounded; the rationale's quality is real, its scope is incomplete.

### Finding 3 — COSMETIC — "Doc_09's Validation work" routing is a function mismatch, with partial project precedent

Per Doc_02 §2.3, Doc_09 in this world is the Story Inventory, not a Validation Layer document — the reference layer treats Validation as a separate downstream stage. Doc_07 four times routes interpretive-claim scrutiny to "Doc_09's Validation work." There is partial precedent for loose coupling (the Syriac build's Doc_01 references "the Doc_09 Validation Layer cross-document audit"), so this may be an established loose usage rather than a clean error — recommend "the Validation Layer" (unqualified) for precision.

### Finding 4 — COSMETIC — Section 3's Pachomius claim cites the wrong section

"Pachomius himself... is remembered (Doc_02 §1.2) in terms that borrow directly from elder-authority vocabulary" — Doc_02 §1.2 describes the Pachomian corpus's transmission, not this interpretive claim. The actual anchor is Doc_05 §1 ("Pachomius himself functioned as a father-elder even within the Rule's formal structure").

### Finding 5 — COSMETIC — Section 4's cross-world characterizations are unflagged

"World #4's martyr-cult-anchored identity" and "World #6's juridical self-definition" are Doc_07's own characterizations (World #4 is named only "Donatism" in the ground-truth docs), not established findings — consistent with Doc_05 §12 item 4's own standard that cross-world comparisons in this build are claims pending confirmation, these should carry the same flag.

### Finding 6 — COSMETIC — Section 8's internal cross-reference is imprecise

"*diakrisis*-as-governing-skill (Section 1, 5 above)" — this is established in Section 5 (and Doc_05 §11), not Section 1, which covers different (affective-diagnostic) material.
