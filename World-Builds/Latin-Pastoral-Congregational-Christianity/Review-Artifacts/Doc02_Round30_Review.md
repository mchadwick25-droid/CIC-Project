## VERDICT: NO SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED — CLEARED REVIEW (0 HIGH, 0 MEDIUM, 0 LOW, 1 COSMETIC)

**All three claims under test survive independent re-derivation from the primary texts, byte for byte.**

1. **Letter 185 §25 genuinely reads "ten pounds of gold," not silver, and the passage is genuinely in `div2 id="v.vi"` ("The Correction of the Donatists"), Chapter 7, the section the Registry cites.** Confirmed by independent `grep` and direct reading: "...if they would take the law which Theodosius, of pious memory, enacted generally against heretics of all kinds, to the effect that any heretical bishop or clergyman, being found in any place, should be fined ten pounds of gold..." — matches the Registry's row 44 quotation exactly.

2. **The sentence Round 28's fix pass wrongly attributed to Letter 185 is genuinely in *Answer to the Letters of Petilian*, Book II, a different `div2`.** Confirmed: "...the law should be put in force against you which imposes a fine of ten pounds of gold, which none of you have ever paid to this very day..." occurs exactly where Round 29 said it does — `div2 id="v.v"`, Book II (`div3 id="v.v.iv"`) — and nowhere inside `div2 id="v.vi"`. Round 29's correction is right.

3. ***CTh* XVI.5.21 (392 Iun. 15) genuinely reads `denis libris auri viritim` ("ten pounds of gold each"), and XVI.5.52 is genuinely a different provision** — a graduated fine schedule by social rank, with *circumcelliones* alone assessed in silver (`argenti pondo decem`) rather than gold, at a different date (412, per the file's own heading). The identification and the distinction both hold.

4. **Bonus check, outside the stated scope:** the `ACTORES VII` roster and Augustine's own entry (`Augustinus Uipporegiensis`) are genuinely present in `pl11-zeno-optatus-collatio-carthaginiensis_migne.txt`, in the delegate-roster section the Registry describes.

**COSMETIC — a one-line locator drift, root-caused rather than left unexplained.** The Registry's cited line numbers for the *CTh* and *Gesta* sources are each exactly one line lower than their current position on `origin/main` (e.g., the XVI.5.21 heading is cited at line 86852; it is now at 86853). This is not a content error and not a re-derivation failure: diffing the local, pre-reconciliation checkout of `theodosianus-16_mommsen-meyer1905.txt` against `origin/main`'s current blob shows exactly one line inserted near the file's own header block — `Language: lat` — which shifts every subsequent line number down by one. This is almost certainly a partial, in-flight touch from the still-pending fleet-wide language-header fix (Task tracked separately: "fix 8 language headers and merge source-library-integration to main"), not a defect introduced by Rounds 28–29 or by this round. **Not fixed here, deliberately:** hand-patching these locators now would go stale again the moment that fleet-wide header fix actually lands and touches this file (and, presumably, seven others) again — the same "fix on a fix" this document set's own root-cause entry (2026-09-10, later) already named and rejected as a pattern. The content the locators point to is independently confirmed correct by direct search; only the exact line number is one off, and only in the two files that fix pass has apparently already partially touched.
