### H2 — Candidate 5's new Supporting and Tensional tests each run against one limb of a two-limb Framework definition, and the omitted limb is in each case the one Candidate 5 meets

**Sites:** lines 104 (Against Supporting), 105 (Against Tensional).

This is the fix for the review's own most consequential finding, so the bar is that the definitions are *applied*, not merely quoted. The quotations themselves are accurate — I re-checked all three against the fresh extraction. The application is incomplete in the same direction twice.

Relatedly: **"narrow dependency" is not a Framework phrase.** It appears nowhere in Part III. Presented in quotation marks inside a sentence otherwise quoting the Framework, it reads as a Framework quotation and is not one — a small defect, but at the one site in this document where quotation hygiene is load-bearing.

**(b) Tensional.** CF V7.4 Part III, in full:

> *"Tensional Gravities function as persistent counter-forces, alternatives, or unresolved pressures within the ecology. **They may not organize as broadly as primary gravities but they prevent the ecology from being reducible to its primary forces.**"*

Doc_04 line 105 engages the first sentence, and within it argues one word — *persistent* — against Candidate 8's two evidentiary sites as the benchmark. The second sentence is never addressed. The argument also imports a requirement the Framework does not state. Line 105 rejects Tensional because there is "no evidence either was a standing, live pressure either bishop had to keep responding to **within his own phase**." The Framework says "unresolved pressures within the ecology" — the ecology is both phases. And line 99 still describes Candidate 5 as *"a live, unresolved theoretical residue of the century-gap itself,"* which is Round 1's H4(a) observation surviving verbatim in the fixed document: the classification section and the forces notation two paragraphs above it describe the same candidate in incompatible terms, and only one of the two is reconciled to the other.

Why HIGH: this is the document's own most consequential judgment, and the classification section exists to close H4. It is closer to closing it than the draft was — Primary is properly ruled out, and the fourth label is now grounded on Template §4 rather than on a precedent that did not support it. But a definition tested against one of its two limbs has not been tested, and both omissions run in the same direction.

**Fix:** quote each definition in full and state, for each limb, whether it is reached. For Supporting, address "they pass multiple gravity tests but may not demonstrate the same breadth of dependency or persistence" directly against Candidate 5's four narrow passes. For Tensional, address "they prevent the ecology from being reducible to its primary forces" directly, and reconcile the conclusion with line 99's "live, unresolved theoretical residue" and Open Item 2's "real, substantial... live theological difference" — or revise those two sites. Replace "narrow dependency" with the Framework's own words. Round 1's own standing note applies unchanged: if the fuller test produces Tensional, §5's substantive non-reopening finding still holds on Doc_01 §8 item 10's evidence-scoped trigger; only the sentence carrying it would need rebuilding.
