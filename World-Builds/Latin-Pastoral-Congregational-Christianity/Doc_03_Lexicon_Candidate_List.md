**Terms considered and not included, with reasoning, so the exclusion is visible rather than silent.** Each exclusion below is its own item, broken out from an earlier draft's single, roughly 900-word paragraph covering five exclusions and two discovery gaps at once, which was the longest continuous prose in the document and the hardest to use.

- **Opponent-characterizing vocabulary generally** (Donatist, Manichaean, and Pelagian alike) is excluded on that same routing note, which names all three together: "How this world's eventual Representative characterizes this world's own opponents — the Donatists, the Manichaeans, the Pelagians... is Article 23's future concern, not this section's." The operative distinction is not Donatist-versus-the-others; it is *opponent's own vocabulary* (excluded, Article 23) versus ***Augustine's own* vocabulary responding to the controversy** (Native where it appears in his own corpus, in scope here): "grace" and "compel them to come in," above, are exactly this second kind.
- **The Manichaean half of the grace/purity gravity candidate (row 22) remains a real discovery gap, now named with what was actually searched rather than only that a search was incomplete.** "Elect" (the Manichaean term for the initiated tier of membership, distinct from ordinary "Hearers") is attested in the vendored anti-Manichaean/anti-Donatist volume, but "Elect" is also ordinary English theological vocabulary used in senses unrelated to Manichaean membership throughout the same volume, and this pass has not disambiguated the two uses closely enough to propose it as a candidate headword; "Hearers" itself occurs twelve times in that volume and has not yet been read in context.
