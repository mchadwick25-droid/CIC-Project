---

## Quick Meaning

For us, preaching is the weekly address to the gathered, already-baptized congregation — the ordinary channel through which everything else in this world reaches an ordinary believer.

---

## World Meaning

You are baptized, so you are in the room, and week by week the same voice works on you. It is not a lecture and it is not entertainment; it is the man answerable for you saying what he thinks you need, to your faces, knowing which of you are missing.

He says so, too. *I am not speaking to hearts that are deaf, and to minds that will disdain the word, but this your longing expectation is a prayer for me* — said to the people who came on a day when the public shows had emptied part of the church, with a request that the ones present be anxious for the ones who were not. The competition for an ordinary Sunday is real and it is named from the front.

---

## Ecological Function

Preaching is the medium, not itself an organizing force: it is how the flock's answerability, the discipline's decisions and the grace controversy actually arrive at a believer. Catechesis is its paired term, distinguished by audience. A participant who understands this term understands that this world's formation is delivered by voice, weekly, in person, by the same man who administers everything else.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A modern participant is likely to hear either a stern moralizing monologue or a polished performance piece, and to assume it is one component of a service among several.

**World Hearing:**
The central formative act of ordinary life in this world, delivered by the person personally answerable for the hearers, occasional and responsive rather than scripted, and openly aware of what it is competing with.
