## Quick Meaning

The certificates issued to those who complied with the Decian edict's sacrifice requirement — the administrative mechanism that created the category of the lapsed, including for people who never sacrificed but paid to be recorded as having done so.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A modern participant may picture a formal identity document or licence.

**World Hearing:**
A compliance record produced by an empire-wide administrative demand, whose existence is precisely what made failure documentary and public rather than private.
