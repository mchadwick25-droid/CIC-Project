- **Formation:** Passes strongly. Doc_01 §1's own Core Identity states this world's formation logic is "pastoral and sacramental before it is juridical" — the whole catechetical and penitential apparatus exists because a bishop is personally answerable for a bounded flock.
- **Explanatory:** Passes strongly. Explains why Cyprian's crisis correspondence exists at all, why Augustine's sermon corpus is so large, and why both bishops' own accounts of coming *to* office (Doc_03's "suffrage"/"the people" entries) are independently attested and treated as formation-significant.
- **Persistence:** Passes. Visible in both Carthage and Hippo, across both phases, in both bishops' own words.
- **Interaction:** Passes — see §6. Reinforces Candidates 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8; no independently demonstrated relationship with Candidate 7.
- **Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check:** Evidence reaches Documented — both bishops' own accounts of holding and exercising this office are directly quoted and independently re-verified across Doc_01's nine review rounds (the 256 preface; Letters XXXI and CCXIII; Pontius's and Possidius's own narratives). - **Forces-connection notation:** Stable across Doc_01 §6's own six-cell sketch — holds under the Initiating cell (Cyprian's own rise from convert to bishop of an already-organized church) and persists into the Ending/Transforming cell (Augustine's own vast corpus, "produced under this world's pastoral pressures"). ### Candidate 5 — Conciliar Authority Theory (Egalitarian vs. Hierarchical)

- **Repetition:** Passes narrowly and locally. Cyprian's own egalitarian formula recurs once, at length, in the 256 preface (Row 4) — this document does not find a second, independent Cyprian-authored locus restating the same authority *theory* elsewhere in his own corpus (the Novatianist/Felicissimus correspondence addresses *who* is readmitted, not the *structure* of inter-episcopal authority). - **Dependency:** Passes narrowly. - **Formation:** Does not clearly pass. - **Explanatory:** Passes narrowly. Explains the specific shape of the Cyprian/Stephen rebaptism dispute and of Augustine's own extended argument against Cyprian's ruling, but does not explain multiple, independent aspects of the wider ecology the way Candidates 1–3 and 6 do.

- **Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check:** Evidence for the *existence* of both formulas, in their own words, reaches Documented (both directly quoted and re-verified across Doc_01's nine rounds). The claim that this constitutes a world-organizing *gravity*, rather than a real but narrow, twice-instantiated theoretical disagreement, does not reach comparable support — precisely the Cross-Check's own governing concern, that a candidate's textual attestation and its organizing breadth are distinct properties. Flagged rather than resolved: the formulas are Documented; their status as a gravity is not supported at the same level, and this document does not upgrade the classification to match the vividness of the two quoted formulas.
- **Forces-connection notation:** Sits inside Doc_01 §6's own "Ongoing/Internal" cell placement note — Augustine's own engagement with Cyprian's conciliar acts, prompted by the Donatists' own citation of them, an internal force triggered by an external prompt, per Doc_01's own "placement judgment rather than a clean case" disclosure. Does not hold, shift, intensify, or fracture under a named external force the way Candidates 2, 3, and 6 do; better described as a live, unresolved theoretical residue of the century-gap itself than as this world's own organizing response to an identifiable force.

**Provisional classification: this document's own independent finding is that this candidate does not reach gravity status** — see §5 below for what this does, and explicitly does not, mean for Doc_01's own strand-singular determination.

- **Persistence:** Passes as a persistent, unresolved counter-pressure specifically within the Cyprian-phase crisis window, rather than as a broadly world-organizing force in its own right — the Tensional classification's own defining character, not a failure.
- **Interaction:** Passes — see §6. Competes with Candidate 2; reinforces Candidate 1.
- **Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check:** Documented for both poles' existence (Epistles XX–XXI's own first-person confessor correspondence; *De Lapsis*'s own regulating argument). No divergence — the tension itself, not a confidence gap, is the finding.

**Provisional classification: Tensional.**
