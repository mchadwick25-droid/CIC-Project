### 2026-09-01 — Doc_01, "orthogonality to state power" framing: project-lead instruction on method

**Context.** Doc_01's own Round 5 independent adversarial review found that the document's World #6 (Imperial and Juridical Christianity) boundary discharge had come to depend on choosing among three competing interpretive readings of a clause in the portfolio-level Step 0 Conclusion's World #8 entry — "confirmed distinct from world #6 (non-overlapping authority structure, **orthogonality to state power**, temporal overlap rather than sequence)." Round 5 held that choosing among the three readings was a decision about a portfolio-level determination that this build thread could not make unilaterally, and recommended escalating the question to the project lead: which reading governs, given that Augustine (one of this world's two anchor figures) is independently documented soliciting Roman state coercion against the Donatists, and that the neighboring, already-built and cleared World #6 (Imperial and Juridical Christianity)'s own completed Doc_04 found the church-state relationship a co-equal, cross-strand Primary gravity for that world.

**The question put to the project lead, in substance:** which of three readings of "orthogonality to state power" should govern this world's own boundary section — (1) a narrow claim that the office is literally never touched by state power (already known to be false and not defended); (2) a narrower claim about the *ground* of episcopal office, i.e. that the office's own legitimacy is not constituted by a relationship to state power, even where the office instrumentally solicits it; or (3) a broader "organizing axis" reading, under which this world's formation logic would need to be shown not to be centered on its relationship to state power at all.

**The project lead's response (Mark, 2026-09-01, in this build session's own conversation, quoted verbatim):**

> "our principle is always truth, transparency in good and grounded in the sources (we dont fabricate or midigate) i try very hard not to influence the sources"

And, when asked to confirm this reframing before treating it as settled:

> "is this a part of the world source and ecology, we are truthful about how the sources present the christian tradistion, but we are not tied to a single influencer/scholor from the time, it is a living ecology that doesn't alway have consistancy. we just say that, augustine did this in the begining then changed his approach and did that"

**What this instruction actually settles, stated narrowly rather than inferred broadly.** The project lead did not select one of the three offered readings. The instruction is about *method*: this project's own governing practice, for this document and generally, is to report what the primary sources document — including where a figure's or a world's own relationship to a question changed over time, or was never consistent — rather than to resolve that record into a single theory manufactured to fit a portfolio-level screening phrase. This is a standing instruction about how sourced claims are made in this build, consistent with Constitution Article 15's own principle that formation worlds are "historically developing ecclesial realities shaped, to the degree the evidence attests, through instability, adaptation, and incomplete continuity." It is not, on its own terms, a ruling that no escalation category applies to Doc_01's disposition — that is a separate question under CO-022, to be assessed on the document's own merits against the actual escalation-category text, not inferred from this instruction alone.

**Consequence for Doc_01, in two steps rather than one, matching Doc_01 §9's own segregation of them.** First, immediately on this instruction (the "Post-Round-5, pre-Round-6" revision): §7's World #6 boundary discharge was rewritten to report Cyprian's and Augustine's own differing relationships to state power rather than adjudicate which of three prior readings of "orthogonality to state power" is correct, and §9's escalation-category assessment was revised on the premise that this reframing removes the triggers the three-readings framing had created. Second, at Round 6, independent adversarial review (`Review-Artifacts/Doc01_Round6_Review.md`) found that first revision's own account of the arc itself factually wrong — compressed into "present, but late," when Letter 185 §25 shows Augustine already argued for a real, narrower solicitation of state power — though, in the event, not granted (Letter 185 §26) — early in his own episcopate (a council NPNF's own editorial note dates 401), before the later, broader position Letter XCIII §17 records him arguing for after a change of mind (a.d. 408) — and §7 was revised again on that finding. This entry's own wording of the arc is kept in step with Doc_01 §7's own current formulation, per CO-022's *Cross-document fact consistency* rule; both steps are checked independently, not assumed, per this project's own review discipline. See `Review-Artifacts/Doc01_Round6_Review.md`, `Doc01_Round7_Review.md` and `Doc01_Round8_Review.md` for the independent adversarial checks this reasoning received and the further revisions each required.

**Disposition status of this specific item:** see Doc_01 §9 for the current, independently-reviewed escalation-category assessment. This entry records the project-lead instruction that occasioned the revision; it does not itself constitute Doc_01's disposition.

### 2026-09-01 — Cross-document correction: Cyprian's "your suffrage and God's judgment" is Epistle XXXIX, not Epistle XL

**Context.** Doc_01's own Round 9 revision (§5, the Ecological-orientation bullet) cited Cyprian's own letter to his people on the Felicissimus schism — "your suffrage and God's judgment," against a rival faction's "ancient venom" toward his episcopate — as "Ep. XL." Doc_01 §9's own revision log (the Round 9 entry) repeated the same citation twice in describing the fix. Round 9's independent adversarial review (`Doc01_Round9_Review.md`) certified the surrounding passage without re-verifying this specific locus against the vendored text, and Doc_01 reached Approved to proceed with the misattribution live in both its narrative text and its own revision history.

**How it was found.** During Doc_02's own Step 2 work (Source Ecology / Source Registry), the citation was carried into `Source_Registry.md` row 1 with a fresh claim that it had been "directly verified against `anf05` in Doc_01's own Round 9 review." Doc_02's own Round 1 independent adversarial review re-checked the claim at source rather than accepting the inherited certification, and found both phrases occur inside `div3 id="iv.iv.xxxix"` (Epistle XXXIX, "To the People, Concerning Five Schismatic Presbyters of the Faction of Felicissimus") — not Epistle XL, "To Cornelius, on His Refusal to Receive Novatian's Ordination," a different letter on an unrelated subject that contains neither phrase. Independently re-verified against `cic/texts/anf05_hippolytus-cyprian-caius-novatian.xml` before this entry was written, per this project's own standing discipline of never accepting a review finding without re-deriving it.

**Consequence.** Doc_01 §5 (live text) and §9 (the Round 9 log, two instances) were corrected in place, with a dated inline note at each site pointing to this entry, per CO-022's *Naming and term propagation* rule. `Doc01_Round9_Review.md` itself is left unchanged, as the historical record of what that round actually certified — the error, not this correction, is what that file documents having happened. `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` and `Source_Registry.md` are corrected in the same pass this entry records. The underlying substantive finding — that Cyprian's own words ground his office in "suffrage and God's judgment" rather than the state's favor — is unaffected; only the citation was wrong.

**Escalation check.** This is a citation-accuracy correction to an already-Approved-to-proceed document, not a reopening of any finding, conclusion, or disposition Doc_01 reached — the strand-singular finding, the World #6 boundary discharge, and the escalation-category assessment all stand as before. No CO-022 escalation category applies to a correction of this kind (it changes no claim's substance, only a citation), consistent with CO-022's own *Coach verification* exception for "small, purely technical corrections... provided the correction changes no substantive claim, finding, or decision." Logged here per the same exception's own disclosure requirement, even though this correction was made by the build thread itself (still inside its own world-build folder) rather than by a coach thread.

### 2026-09-01 — Round 1/Round 2 disagreement: `donatism.yaml` does contain an Optatus entry; an unre-derived review finding reached a live document

**Context.** Doc_02's own Round 1 independent adversarial review (`Review-Artifacts/Doc02_Round1_Review.md`, finding H2) found, correctly, that an earlier draft of Doc_02 §1 had the Optatus placement backwards (claiming he was homed on the sibling Donatism build rather than this one). In the course of that finding, Round 1 also asserted, as a supporting claim, that the sibling build's own corpus map (`donatism.yaml`) "does not contain him at all." The Round 1 fix pass adopted that supporting claim into Doc_02 §1's own revised text without independently re-deriving it — a lapse in this project's own standing discipline of never accepting a review finding without re-checking it at source.

**How it was found.** Doc_02's own Round 2 independent adversarial review (`Review-Artifacts/Doc02_Round2_Review.md`, finding H1) independently re-parsed `donatism.yaml` and found the supporting claim false: the file carries its own Optatus entry (`author: optatus`, `role: context`, `confidence: assigned`, note "Assigned twice with different roles, deliberately"), unchanged since commit `4c4a6541` (2026-08-27) — well before Round 1 ran — and the sibling Donatism build's own Doc_02 §1 and Registry row 1 cite that very entry directly. Independently re-verified a third time by this build thread before writing this entry and the corresponding fixes: `git log` on the file, a direct parse of its own YAML, and a direct read of the sibling branch's own Doc_02 and Registry all confirm the entry's existence and content.

**Consequence.** The census has therefore already answered the question Doc_01 §8 item 2 posed to this step (re-home, hold, or double-place Optatus): it is already double-placed, deliberately, on its own recorded reasoning — `tradition` on this world's own map, `context` on the sibling Donatism build's. `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` §1 and §10, and `Source_Registry.md` row 27, are corrected in the same pass this entry records, each with an inline note. The correction changes a fact about what a companion world's generated corpus-map file contains; it does not change this document's own position that Optatus is not drawn on for any specific claim in this world's own construction, and nothing in Doc_01's or Doc_02's own substantive findings depends on which way the correction runs.

**A second, unrelated correction from the same review round, recorded here rather than in a separate entry since both were caught together.** Round 2 also found that `Source_Acquisition_Manifest.md`'s G3 candidate (Possidius's *Vita Augustini*) named the wrong Internet Archive identifier: `archive.org/details/PossidiusAug` is a 2008 audio recording of Weiskotten's translation, not the scanned bilingual text edition, which is a separate item, `archive.org/details/sanctiaugustiniv00possrich`. Independently re-confirmed by live web search before this entry was written. The work, editor, press, year, and public-domain status G3 states were all already correct; only the identifier was wrong. Corrected at the Manifest and at Registry row 45.

**Escalation check.** Neither correction changes a claim's substance, a sourcing conclusion this world's own construction rests on, or any disposition — Doc_02, the Registry, and the Manifest remain undisposed drafts at the time of this entry, mid-review-cycle, so there is no cleared document being reopened. CO-022's fourth escalation category (unresolved tensions) has a specific limb for two reviews disagreeing with each other; this is exactly that case, and it is closed, not left open, by returning to the primary artifact (the corpus-map file itself, its `git log`, and the sibling branch's own record) rather than by preferring the more recent review on authority alone — consistent with Doc_01 §9's own established handling of the same limb. No escalation category applies. Logged here because CO-022's own rule requires a review disagreement to be recorded rather than silently resolved in the prose it corrects, and because this is the second time in this world's build that an unre-derived review claim has reached a live document (the first being the Ep. XL/Epistle XXXIX citation, logged above) — a pattern worth naming plainly for whoever next relies on a review finding in this build without re-deriving it themselves.
