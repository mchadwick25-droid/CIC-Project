## A. The new Smyrnaeans 8 love-feast correction (Section 4)

**Quote accuracy — VERIFIED ACCURATE.** Independently fetched the actual Roberts-Donaldson/ANF translation of *Smyrnaeans* (newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm) and the Lightfoot & Harmer translation (earlychristianwritings.com). Both confirm chapter 8 contains, in the same chapter as the bishop-authorized-Eucharist clause: "It is not lawful without the bishop either to baptize or to celebrate a love-feast" (R-D) / "It is not lawful apart from the bishop either to baptize or to hold a love-feast" (Lightfoot, §8:2). The Greek behind this is indeed ἀγάπην ποιεῖν, consistent with the document's citation. The correction's core factual claim is accurate and its calibration (this establishes the label has *some* primary-source anchor; it does *not* resolve whether Ignatius's love-feast and Pliny's "ordinary meal" are the same practice) is properly hedged and does not overstate. **This part of the fix is sound.**

**A real problem, however, sits immediately adjacent to the fix and was not caught by it.** Both the pre-existing sentence the correction attaches to, and the new correction paragraph itself, misattribute the phrase "one eucharist" to *Smyrnaeans* 8:

> "Ignatius's own letters supply eucharistic language (*Philadelphians* 4, *Smyrnaeans* 8's "one eucharist" and bishop-presided requirement)..."
> "**Correction...**: *Smyrnaeans* 8, already cited immediately above for its "one eucharist" clause, contains a second, distinct clause..."

I independently checked both *Philadelphians* 4 and *Smyrnaeans* 8 in two independent translations (Roberts-Donaldson and Lightfoot & Harmer). The "one eucharist" language ("Take heed, then, to have but one Eucharist. For there is one flesh of our Lord Jesus Christ, and one cup...one altar; as there is one bishop") is *Philadelphians* 4, not *Smyrnaeans* 8. *Smyrnaeans* 8 does contain real eucharistic content — "Let that be deemed a proper [valid] Eucharist, which is administered either by the bishop, or by one to whom he has entrusted it" — but this is a *validity-under-the-bishop* claim, not the numerically-emphatic "one eucharist" language, which belongs to a different letter entirely.

This is a genuine, independently-verifiable citation error, not a stylistic quibble: the new correction paragraph's own rhetorical framing ("already cited immediately above for its 'one eucharist' clause") builds directly on the wrong attribution, and it has now been copied into the Source Registry's P03 row twice (see Section E below). It predates this revision (the underlying sentence was already there before the fix) and survived all five previously-claimed review rounds, but the round-6 fix pass — which specifically re-engaged with the *Smyrnaeans* 8 text line by line — was the moment this should have been caught and was not.

**Verdict on Task A: SUBSTANTIAL.** The love-feast citation itself is accurate and adequately scoped; the "one eucharist"/*Smyrnaeans* 8 attribution it sits beside, and now doubles down on, is wrong and should be corrected to *Philadelphians* 4 (or the *Smyrnaeans* 8 reference reworded to describe what that chapter actually says: a bishop-validity requirement, not "one eucharist" language).
