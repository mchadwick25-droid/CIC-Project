## Fix 1 — Doc_08 Cell 3A "Definitional note"

**Verdict: VERIFIED CORRECT**

The current note (Doc_08, immediately following the "CELL 3A" heading) opens with an explicit correction disclosure: *"corrected 2026-07-08 after an independent cold review of this note found its original geographic form factually inconsistent with this document's own Force 2A-2 content, and re-argued on a corrected, non-geographic ground."*

(a) **Geographic claim withdrawn — confirmed.** The note now states directly: *"A geographic reading of 'External' — outside this world's own three core regions — therefore cannot be what makes Force 2A-2 External, since 2A-2's own rivals operated substantially inside two of those three regions. That reading was this document's original error, caught on independent review, and is withdrawn here rather than left standing."* No remaining sentence in the note asserts that "External" means "outside this world's own three core regions." The old, broken form is gone, not merely softened.

(b) **New ground stated and checked for consistency.** The replacement rule: *"'External' names a force generated by a community, movement, or institution other than this world's own six-primary-voice evidentiary network — the Didache, 1 Clement, Ignatius, Polycarp, Hermas, and Justin, and the specific ekklesiai those six voices document (Doc_02 §1) — regardless of where that outside community happens to be physically located."*

  - Cross-checked against Doc_08 Cell 2A, Force 2A-2's own Layer 1 text: *"All three were geographically overlapping with this world's own three core regions in the same decades this world's own texts were circulating"* — Marcion (Rome), Valentinian Christianity (Rome and Asia Minor), Montanism (Asia Minor to Rome). This matches the note's own restatement exactly and no longer contradicts it, since the note no longer needs the movements to be outside the three regions — only outside the six-voice network, which they demonstrably are (Marcion, Valentinus, and Montanus are not any of the six named voices).
  - Cross-checked against Doc_01 §8.3: independently confirms the same geography (Marcion in Rome; Valentinus "present in this world's own Roman core"; Valentinian Christianity in "two of this world's own three core regions at once"; Montanism spreading "from Asia Minor to Rome"). Consistent with both Doc_08's Force 2A-2 Layer 1 and the corrected note.
  - Cross-checked the six-primary-voices list against Doc_02 §1 ("Six primary voices anchor this world's evidentiary base"): Didache (1.1), 1 Clement (1.2), Ignatius (1.3), Polycarp (1.4), Hermas (1.5), Justin Martyr (1.6). This is an exact match to the list Doc_08's note now uses — not a fabricated or redefined list.

(c) **Load-bearing factual claim for 3A-1/3A-2 checked.** The note claims Irenaeus, Tertullian, and Alexandria's teaching institution "are none of them among this world's own six primary voices." This is trivially and verifiably true against the Doc_02 §1 list above — none of Irenaeus, Tertullian, or the Alexandrian catechetical tradition appears among the Didache/1 Clement/Ignatius/Polycarp/Hermas/Justin set. Doc_08's own Force 3A-1 Layer 1 independently corroborates that Irenaeus (*Adversus Haereses*, Gaul) and Tertullian (*Apologeticus*, Carthage) write from outside the three core regions, and Force 3A-2 Layer 1 dates the Alexandrian tradition to c. 190–254 CE as an independently emerging, distinct formation logic — neither passage is contradicted by, or in tension with, the new non-geographic rule.

No other cell in the Six-Cell Matrix (1A, 1B, 2A, 2B, 3B) was found to depend on the withdrawn geographic reading of "External"; Cell 1A's two External forces (Roman imperial infrastructure; the Neronian persecution) are consistent with the new rule as well as the old one, so the correction introduces no new cross-cell inconsistency.

**Residual observation (not a defect):** Force 3A-1's own Layer 1 still states Irenaeus and Tertullian "write from regions genuinely outside this world's own three core regions." This is left in as true, corroborating background, not as the operative definition — the note itself explicitly demotes geography to non-definitional. No action needed.
