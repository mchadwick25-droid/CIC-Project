## Overall Verdict: QUALIFIED PASS

Across roughly 60+ individually checked citations and direct quotations, the document is overwhelmingly accurate: I did not find a single fabricated quotation, and every verbatim quotation I checked against its cited source (several dozen, spanning Doc_01 through Doc_09, the World Profile, the Identity Decision, and the Validation & Testing document) matched the source text exactly, including compressed quotations using ellipses. The document's structure genuinely matches what Framework V3.2 Part Three requires (the four domains plus Thinness Mapping), and its Section 6 (Forces-and-Situatedness) responds to a real, independently-verified Framework requirement (Part Nine: "Doc_08 is a required input to Phase One Ecology Assessment... An Ecology Assessment conducted without Doc_08 is an Ecology Assessment conducted without the world's situatedness"). The two claims flagged as this document's own "significant new findings" — the Grapte/Strand-B vs. household-salutation/Strand-A tension, and the characterization of Doc_04's G06 finding — both check out as accurate and honestly, not overstated.

That said, the review surfaced real defects, not merely cosmetic ones: one genuine misattribution (a quotation given in quotation marks and attributed to "Doc_08's own framing" that does not actually appear anywhere in Doc_08 — it is Framework V3.2's own text, not Doc_08's), one substantive unsupported/overstated comparative claim about the household-leader role's access to G02 that is in tension with the document's own Section 1 analysis and with the Identity Decision's own text, and an undisclosed tension between this document's own proceeding and Framework V3.2's literal precondition that Representative Construction begins only once the world is "complete and frozen" — which, per this world's own Validation & Testing document, it explicitly is not. None of these rise to a level that would change Section 7's bottom-line conclusions, but they are real, and a genuinely adversarial pass should not wave them through as cosmetic.

## Itemized Findings

### Finding 1 — Misattributed quotation: "An Ecology Assessment conducted without Doc_08..." is not Doc_08's own text

**Severity: MODERATE (real defect)**

**What I checked:** Section 6 opens: *"Doc_08's own framing states the stakes for this Ecology Assessment directly and should be taken at face value: 'An Ecology Assessment conducted without Doc_08 is an Ecology Assessment conducted without the world's situatedness.' This section applies that requirement."*

I read `CiC_W1_Doc08_Forces_Document.md` in full (475 lines, all sections through the Document Log and sign-off) and this sentence does not appear anywhere in it.

**What I found:** The sentence is verbatim — but it is Framework V3.2's own text (Part Nine, "V7 Build Sequence Alignment"), not Doc_08's:

> "Doc_08 is a required input to Phase One Ecology Assessment. The Representative emerges not only from the formation ecology but from the forces that shaped it... An Ecology Assessment conducted without Doc_08 is an Ecology Assessment conducted without the world's situatedness."

This is the governing Framework document describing Doc_08's required role in the *Representative Construction* process — not a sentence Doc_08 says about itself. The quotation itself is accurate and not fabricated, and the underlying point (this Ecology Assessment needed Doc_08's forces analysis and used it) is sound. But the attribution is wrong, and it matters: the document's own front matter is careful to attribute other verbatim Framework language explicitly to "the governing framework" (e.g., the opening paragraph's "whether the recoverable ecology of a given world is sufficient to sustain a living mediator of encounter" is correctly sourced). Section 6 breaks that same discipline by dressing a Framework requirement up as if it were Doc_08 speaking about its own importance. This is exactly the kind of citation slippage this project's own review practice exists to catch.

### Finding 2 — Overstated/unsupported claim: household-leader's access to G02 "more so than to almost any other role... considered"

**Severity: MODERATE (real defect — internal tension, not merely unsupported)**

**What I checked:** Section 6: *"The correspondence-network response is the force most directly, concretely available to a household-leader Representative — more so than to almost any other role this world's identity decision considered."*

**What I found:** This comparative claim is not supported by a citation, and it is in real tension with two things the document itself and its own sibling document establish:

1. `CiC_W1_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md`'s own "Role options considered" list states of Option 1 (Presbyter-overseer, explicitly "modeled on Polycarp's own attested self-designation pattern"): *"Strong candidate: aligns with both this world's Primary gravities (G02 correspondence network, G07 eucharist)."* So the option most directly modeled on the one person in this world's entire evidentiary base who is *named performing a G02 transmission act* (Polycarp forwarding Ignatius's letters) was already on record, in the very document this Ecology Assessment says it does not re-litigate, as aligning with G02 — undercutting the claim that the household-leader role has uniquely or unusually strong G02 access "more so than almost any other role... considered."

2. The document's own Section 1 explicitly calibrates Amma's relationship to G02 as that of "a *participant and carrier*... rather than as their author," specifically contrasting her with the letter-writers themselves — "bishops, presbyters, and one apologist." Polycarp, the single concretely-named transmission example the document leans on repeatedly (Section 5.6, Section 6, citing Doc_08 §6), *is* one of those bishops/presbyters, not a household host. The document does not acknowledge that its own headline evidentiary anchor for "a household leader who receives, reads, copies, and forwards letters... genuinely available to Amma without requiring office-holder status" (Section 5.6) is itself an office-holder's own attested act, not a household host's — the opposite of what the sentence claims it demonstrates.

By contrast, the document is scrupulously careful to flag an analogous vulnerability elsewhere (the Grapte/Strand-A polity mismatch, Section 4) as something Phase Two must treat as "analogous... not a direct positional transplant." It does not extend that same discipline to its own G02/household-host claim, where the identical structural problem exists (the concrete textual anchor is an office-holder's act, being used to license a non-office-holder inference). This is a real, if narrow, inconsistency in how rigorously the document applies its own stated standard of self-scrutiny.

### Finding 3 — Undisclosed tension with Framework's "complete and frozen" precondition for Representative Construction

**Severity: LOW-MODERATE (real, but likely a known/accepted project-wide practice, not a novel oversight)**

**What I checked:** Framework V3.2, Part Nine ("V7 Build Sequence Alignment"): *"Representative construction begins only after Steps 1 through 9 are complete and the following construction documents exist: ... Doc_09 — Story Inventory, World Profile, and Validation (the world complete and frozen)."*

I then checked `CiC_W1_Validation_Testing.md` directly. It states, repeatedly and explicitly: *"this document does not claim the world is freeze-ready"*; *"this world is **not freeze-ready**, and is not expected to be at this stage of Step 9"*; and its own Freeze Criteria table lists External Scholarly Review (Article 31, an explicit "freeze-eligibility gate") as **"Not yet conducted — outside this session's scope; requires an outside human reviewer,"** with Representative-voice-dependent testing also marked not yet assessable.

**What I found:** The Ecology Assessment's own front matter states its evidentiary basis as: *"Doc_01 through Doc_09, the World Profile, and Validation & Testing — all APPROVED TO PROCEED, all project-lead signed off... and the Validation & Testing pass dated 2026-07-08."* It never mentions that the Framework's own literal precondition for beginning Representative Construction is that the world be "complete and **frozen**" — a status distinct from "Approved to Proceed" throughout every document in this build's own terminology — and never discloses that External Scholarly Review, an explicit freeze-eligibility gate, has not happened.

This is mitigated by the fact that "APPROVED TO PROCEED is sufficient to build on; Frozen is a separate, later status" is an extremely well-established, consistently-stated convention across nearly every document in this build's own sign-off entries, so it is plausible this is a known and accepted project-wide practice the project lead has already implicitly authorized (proceeding via "approved to proceed" chains rather than waiting for a world-level freeze). But this document does not say so. Given this project's own explicit standing discipline — quoted by this very document in its own Section 7 ("disclose the dependency, rather than let a downstream document discover it by accident") — this is a real, if modest, gap: the one Framework precondition most directly relevant to *whether this document should exist yet at all* is not addressed anywhere in it.

### Finding 4 — Minor unsupported superlative in the Sufficiency Determination

**Severity: LOW (cosmetic)**

Section 7: *"This is not a marginal or borderline world for Representative construction; it is, on its own construction record, one of the more thoroughly tested and most honestly self-disclosing world-builds in this project's history to date."*

No comparative data (e.g., review-round counts for other world-builds) is presented anywhere in this document to support "one of the more thoroughly tested... in this project's history to date." The underlying point (this world's construction record shows extensive, real review activity) is true and independently verifiable from the source documents' own Document Logs, but the comparative superlative itself is asserted, not evidenced — a minor lapse in a document that is otherwise unusually careful to ground every claim.

### Finding 5 — Citation section imprecision: "live, contemporary, geographically overlapping rivals" attributed to Doc_01 §8.3

**Severity: LOW (cosmetic; inherited, not a new error)**

The phrase "live, contemporary, geographically overlapping rivals," quoted three times in the document (Sections 1, 4, 6) and attributed each time to "Doc_01 §8.3," is verbatim — but it appears in Doc_01 **§7**, not §8.3: *"Marcionite, Valentinian/Gnostic, and Montanist movements were live, contemporary, geographically overlapping rivals during this same window, not later or external threats (see Section 8.3)."* §8.3 develops the same point in more detail but with different wording ("live, undefeated, contemporary neighbors"). This is not a new error introduced by this document — Doc_04's own G05 entry already cites this identical phrase to "Doc_01 Section 8.3's disclosure," so this document is following an established (if slightly imprecise) citation convention already present in the prior build, not introducing a fresh mistake.
