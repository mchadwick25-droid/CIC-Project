## Findings

### 1. Core Section 2 ("What Organizes Everything") omits G03 (State Pressure/Legal Precarity) entirely — MODERATE

**What it is:** The Core Template's Section 2 instruction reads: "Draw from Doc_02 Historical Gravity document. Render primary, supporting, and tensional gravities as forces the Representative lives inside." Doc_04 classifies six gravities that reached classification (G01 Supporting, G02 Primary, G03 Supporting, G04 Supporting, G05 Tensional, G07 Primary — G06 did not reach gravity status). The Core's Section 2 renders G02, G07 (paras 1–2), G01 (para 3), G04 (para 4), and G05 (para 5) — five of six. G03 (Supporting, cross-strand, one of only three Supporting gravities this world's construction confirmed) does not appear anywhere in Section 2. It appears only in Section 5 ("What This World Is Responding To," the Forces section), as "a particular danger presses on you... a name can be given, an accusation made."

**Where:** `CiC_W1_World_Capsule_Core.md`, Section 2 (lines 11–21) vs. Section 5 (lines 43–51).

**Assessment:** There is a defensible rationale for this choice — Doc_04 itself says of G03, "this candidate *is* one of the primary forces named in Doc_01 §7 and Doc_02 §9; its own 'test' is better read as a question of character... than of how it responds to some other force" (Doc_04, G03). That gives the builder real cover for treating G03 as belonging more naturally to the Forces section than the Gravities section. But the Core template's own Section 2 instruction is explicit that it should render *all three tiers* (primary, supporting, tensional) as living forces, and G03 — a confirmed, cross-strand Supporting gravity — is the only classified gravity absent from that section. This is a template-instruction gap, not a factual error; nothing said about G03 anywhere in the Core is wrong, it simply never appears as a *gravity* the Representative lives inside, only as an external force.

### 4. Prompt Section 6 (Witness-Not-Recruitment): "who ask for silver, or who stay too long in a household not their own" is unsupported anywhere in the reviewed construction record — MODERATE-SIGNIFICANT

**What it is:** In the fierceness-calibration passage of the mandatory Witness-Not-Recruitment section: "It is turned toward those who claim a new voice has silenced every old one, or who ask for silver, or who stay too long in a household not their own." The first clause (a new voice silencing every old one) is well-grounded (Doc_01 §8.3's Montanism disclosure). But "ask for silver" and "stay too long in a household not their own" read as a direct invocation of the Didache's own instructions for testing itinerant apostles/prophets (Didache 11–13: a true apostle stays no more than one or two days and does not ask for money). This is real, accurate content from the actual historical Didache — but it does not appear anywhere in the reviewed construction chain. It is not developed in Doc_03's or Doc_06's *prophetes* entry, not mentioned in Doc_01, Doc_04, Doc_07, or Doc_08, and not present in Doc_09 (checked directly — no hits for "silver," "money," "itinerant," or related terms). Voice Construction §3 explicitly flags the *prophetes* connection to Amma as "this document's own inference, not directly attested," and recommends she "use this term more sparingly and more consciously" — it never mentions testing criteria for false teachers at all. Every other document in this build states explicitly and repeatedly that "no content is drawn from general period knowledge; every claim traces to a specific document and section" (Doc_07 front matter; nearly identical language recurs in Doc_08, the Ecology Assessment, and others). This specific detail appears to violate that discipline: it is plausible, vivid, and historically accurate to the genre, but untraceable to anything this project's own construction record actually established.

**Where:** `CiC_W1_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Amma.txt`, line 33 (Witness-Not-Recruitment / fierceness calibration).

**Severity note:** This sits inside a mandatory section, which raises its severity above a normal content quibble, though it does not contradict anything and does not read as an "old-model" biography violation — it is a scope-of-sourcing problem, not a voice-register or grammar problem.

**Where:** Throughout `CiC_W1_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Amma.txt`; the Section 1 instance is the most notable given the template's own explicit modeling instruction for that section.
