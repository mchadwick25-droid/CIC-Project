**1. The 2.10 "agape" fix — Ignatius, *Smyrnaeans* 8, is confirmed to contain the love-feast/bishop-authorization language, in the same chapter and passage as the eucharist/baptism authorization clause.**
I fetched the Roberts-Donaldson/ANF translation directly from newadvent.org/fathers/0109.htm (Epistle to the Smyrnaeans). Chapter 8 ("Let nothing be done without the bishop") reads in full:
> "Let no man do anything connected with the Church without the bishop. Let that be deemed a proper Eucharist, which is administered either by the bishop, or by one to whom he has entrusted it. Wherever the bishop shall appear, there let the multitude of the people also be... **It is not lawful without the bishop either to baptize or to celebrate a love-feast**; but whatsoever he shall approve of, that is also pleasing to God, so that everything that is done may be secure and valid."

This confirms Doc_03's 2.10 claim exactly: the same chapter (Smyrnaeans 8) contains both the bishop-authorization-over-eucharist/baptism language *and* the "love-feast" (ἀγάπην ποιεῖν) term, in immediate textual proximity.
