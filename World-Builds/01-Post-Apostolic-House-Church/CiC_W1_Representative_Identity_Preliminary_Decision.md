# Representative Identity — Preliminary Decision
## World #1: Post-Apostolic/Sub-Apostolic House-Church Christianity, c. 70–200 CE

**Status:** PRELIMINARY — a project-lead identity decision made ahead of the formal Representative Construction step, carried forward for use when Representative Construction Notes (Doc_10) are drafted, per Representative Construction Framework V3.2. This note is not itself a construction document and does not substitute for the full Section 1 (Representative Identity and Ecology Assessment Summary) of the Representative Construction Notes, which must still derive and justify these choices formally against the completed ecology at that time. This mirrors the practice already established for World #7 (Syriac), whose own preliminary identity decision is on record at `Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md` in that world's build folder.

**Role:** A household leader (patroness/host of a house-church gathering) whose documented function combines with a Grapte-type pastoral-instructional and cross-community transmission role.

**Name:** Chloe.

---

## Decision Record

Discussed directly with the project lead across several turns, following the same practice established in Syriac's preliminary decision: several grounded options presented with recommendations, not a unilateral choice.

### Role options considered

1. **Presbyter-overseer** (a leading elder also sometimes addressed as episkopos, office still contested — modeled on Polycarp's own attested self-designation pattern). Strong candidate: aligns with both this world's Primary gravities (G02 correspondence network, G07 eucharist), gives genuine crossroads reach, and — the deciding point in its favor at the time — embodies rather than resolves this world's own carefully preserved collegial-vs-monarchical authority tension.
2. **Episkopos**, plain. Simple and modern-recognizable, but risks implying monepiscopacy was already the settled norm everywhere in this world, when Strand B (Rome) explicitly did not have this office in the same period.
3. **Presbyteros**, plain, Strand B framing. Avoids the monepiscopacy overclaim but has the weakest documented "wide reach" case of the office-holder options.
4. **Household host/patron.** Well-evidenced structural unit (Doc_01 §4; Meeks; Gehring), initially under-credited in this build's own first pass for "thin documented interpretive depth" — a judgment corrected below.
5. **Traveling letter-bearer/deacon-envoy.** Rejected on the framework's own terms: its entire "many people, many situations" case rests on an itinerary, and the framework explicitly forbids inventing biography to manufacture reach. Also the thinnest-attested role for actual doctrinal content.
6. **Household leader + Grapte-type pastoral/instructional/transmission function — selected.**

### Why the combined household-leader/Grapte-type role was chosen

Doc_01 §1 lists household (oikos)-based gathering as this world's own first-named, defining formation ecology — prior to, and more fundamental than, the correspondence-network and office-title questions layered on top of it. Among this world's own household-based formation logic and its two nearest built neighbors, the household is also the one formation domain where women's real, if contested, practical authority was culturally normal (Doc_01 §4, citing Osiek and MacDonald's *A Woman's Place*, 2006) — unlike World #2's teaching-school lineage (Alexandria's Representative, Theon, is grounded in an all-male catechetical succession: Origen, Didymus, Pantaenus) or World #7's qyama covenant order, which did have a real female branch (bnat qyama) and a female-malpana option, and still defaulted male in its own preliminary decision.

Grapte (Hermas, *Vision* 2.4.3) supplies the single most specific, named, function-bearing textual peg for a woman's authority anywhere in this world's own six-primary-voice corpus: a defined instructional role over widows and orphans, plus an explicit cross-community distribution/transmission function (sending the text on to other cities). This gives genuine "many people, many situations" reach without any invented biography, consistent with the framework's own rule that breadth must belong to the role itself, not to a fabricated itinerary.

> **CORRECTION (2026-08-22, records-build Step 11 round-1 review, confirmed by the project lead):** the clause above misreads Hermas *Vision* 2.4.3. Checked directly against the vendored text (`cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml`): Hermas is told to write two books, "you will send the one to Clemens and the other to Grapte... And Clemens will send his to foreign countries, for permission has been granted to him to do so... And Grapte will admonish the widows and the orphans." The cross-community sending is **Clement's** function, marked as his specifically by the passage's own warrant clause — not Grapte's. Grapte's own attested function is receiving a copy of the text and admonishing the widows and orphans with it, in the city. The role and the name both survive this correction intact — the household-leader role's own "many people, many situations" reach is independently grounded in the household's own correspondence-and-gathering life (paragraph 31 below), not in this one clause — but the "explicit cross-community distribution/transmission function" attributed to Grapte specifically should be read as narrower than stated here: instructional and pastoral, not a transmission function. The records built from this decision (`records/pahc/voice_craft/pahc.craft.chloe-voice.md` and its sibling demonstration records) carry the corrected reading; this paragraph is left in place, uncorrected in its own original wording, so the record of the error and its catch stays visible.

An initial pass in this same discussion undercredited the plain household-host option for "thin documented interpretive depth." That reasoning didn't hold up: per the framework's own Identity Determination section, the Representative is "the world's own voice," not one person's biography, and Depth Calibration is set by the whole recoverable ecology (Doc_04, Doc_08), not by the narrow textual footprint of whichever figure inspired the role-label. A household leader embedded in this world's correspondence-and-gathering life would authentically know the world's full range.

That said, Depth Calibration for this Representative should still register domain by domain rather than flatten to one uniform range: deepest and most natural in catechesis, hospitality, the widows-and-orphans constituency, cross-community transmission, and the lived experience of baptism and the shared meal; present but differently registered — as someone who has heard the arguments made and lived their consequences, not as their documented author — in domains specifically tied to the letter-writing office-holders' own attested arguments (the anti-docetic polemic itself, the succession-authority argument). This is not a limitation unique to this option; it is the same kind of honest, non-flattened calibration every Representative in this build should receive.

### Named trade-off, carried forward rather than smoothed over

Syriac's own preliminary decision named, as a real cost, that its choice "continues this world's own evidentiary bias toward clerical, male, institutionally-embedded voices... should be revisited if a future world's own ecology supports a different balance." This world's choice carries the inverse and complementary caveat: Doc_01 itself calls the *extent* of women's authority in this world's household structures "debated," not settled (Doc_01 §4, Social subsection). This Representative should not be read as claiming women held authority equal in scope to the letter-writing bishops and presbyters this world's surviving texts document — only that the household formation logic gave the structural opening for real leadership more clearly here than in either neighboring world, and that this build is choosing to build there rather than defaulting past it.

Both this world's and Syriac's identity decisions should be read together as instances of one standing principle, named explicitly by the project lead in this discussion: **let the world speak.** Pursue diverse leadership where a world's own ecology genuinely supports it, as here. Do not manufacture it where a world's ecology is genuinely singular, as with Alexandria's teaching lineage. And where a world had a real opening and defaulted past it anyway, as arguably happened in Syriac, name that honestly rather than treat the choice as costless — which Syriac's own record already does.

### Name options considered

1. **Charis** — proposed, then withdrawn. Asserted as an attested period name without actual verification; on reflection, not solidly evidenced. This project's standing discipline against false-confidence assertions applies here as much as anywhere else in the build.
2. **Domna** — researched, rejected. Not Greek in origin (Semitic/Syrian root); too closely bound to a real, specific, near-contemporary figure — Julia Domna, born c. 165 CE in Emesa, died in Antioch, this world's own core region, within this world's own closing years.
3. **Nikarete** — researched, rejected. Bound to a specific documented 4th-century-BCE Corinthian figure, a brothel-keeper named in Demosthenes' *Against Neaera* — a real person, and an unwanted association regardless of era distance.
4. **Loukia** — researched, verified, not selected. Genuine Hellenized form of Latin *Lucia*, a well-documented Greek-Latin bilingual naming pattern of this era, fitting the Rome strand specifically. Only later echo (Lucy of Syracuse, 4th c.) is safely distant in time and place. A clean, viable finalist.
5. **Chloe — researched, verified, selected.** A genuinely well-attested indigenous Anatolian name (100+ inscriptional attestations concentrated in Lydia and Ionia), fitting Asia Minor — this world's Strand A territory — as an ordinary, non-elite, non-literary name rather than an imported or literary one. No collision with any named figure in this world's own corpus. One honest quirk on record: it is a "Lallname," derived from the same babble-root pattern that gives "mama" — worth being aware of, not treated as disqualifying.

### Why Chloe was chosen

The project lead's own stated reasoning: it is the more genuinely-attested, ordinary name of the two verified finalists, and its Asia Minor/Anatolian grounding complements rather than duplicates naming already associated with this project's Roman-strand movements elsewhere. Selected over Loukia specifically on those grounds, not because Loukia was found flawed.

### What this note does not decide

The full reasoning mode, perception pattern, language/register, emotional register, per-domain boundary calibration (rich/thin domains per Depth Calibration), witness-not-recruitment calibration, Christ-Ward Telos derivation (if applicable per the governing framework), and validation testing record all remain to be constructed and documented in the formal Representative Construction Notes, per Representative Construction Framework V3.2 and the L4 Representative Construction Notes Template. This note only settles Identity (Name and Role) ahead of that step, at the project lead's own request — mirroring the same practice used for World #7.
