## A. Writing-From-Inside Principle compliance — FAILS on independent re-check

Doc_05's own text (Section 0, line 15) asserts: "sourcing, classification apparatus, and cross-references to Doc_01–04 are confined to clearly marked **Grounding notes** set apart from the inhabited prose, not folded into it." The Document Log (Section 11) further claims that round 6 and round 7 each ran "a fresh word-by-word sweep of all inhabited paragraphs in Sections 1–6 for all three previously-identified leak categories" (gravity-ID/citation leaks, anachronistic-comparison leaks, oversold-certainty leaks) and "found none."

On an independent, fresh read of the actual inhabited body paragraphs (excluding Grounding notes), I found **four clear violations** that a genuinely fresh sweep should have caught. None of them fall into the three leak categories the document's own review history says it tested for — which is itself informative: the categories narrowed to specific historically-caught patterns and never re-tested for the more basic failure mode of the document's *own methodological vocabulary* leaking into supposedly inhabited prose.

1. **Line 115 (Section 4.1, opening sentence):** "Worship did the heaviest **ecological** lifting of any single practice in this world, without being the only thing that mattered." — "Ecological" is the project's own analytical term (as in "Ecological Reconstruction," "Human Ecology," the whole apparatus this document is built to produce). No person inside this world thought of their worship in terms of "ecological lifting." This is a document-vocabulary leak sitting in the very first sentence of a lens's inhabited prose. **SUBSTANTIAL.**

2. **Line 133 (Section 5.2, closing sentence):** "...and this world's own **formation logic** held that felt precariousness rather than the calm of an already-settled institution." — "Formation Logic" is the literal title of this exact subsection (line 131: "### 5.2 Formation Logic") and one of the eleven Framework-defined reconstruction dimensions (confirmed against `CiC_L3B_...V7.4_DRAFT.docx`, Step 5 Activities list). Attributing "formation logic" to the world itself, in its own inhabited voice, imports the Framework's own dimension-label as if it were something ancient practitioners possessed and could be described using that term. **SUBSTANTIAL.**

3. **Line 67 (Section 1.2, closing sentence):** "That fear...occupied a Roman member's emotional life in a way **no letter from Ignatius's own communities ever speaks of**." — This is a meta-textual claim about what the surviving correspondence does and doesn't attest — precisely the analytical-distance move the document's own Revision 4 fix log (line 240) claims to have *already corrected once*, by rewording an earlier leak ("this evidence does not show") into exactly this phrase. The reword swapped "evidence" for "letter," but the underlying move — a claim about the absence of content in a defined corpus of surviving texts — is unchanged. This is the same leak class round 5 caught recurring in Section 6.3 ("this world's own surviving record") after round 4 thought it had fixed it. It recurred again here, undetected through rounds 5, 6, and 7. **SUBSTANTIAL.**

4. **Line 81 (Section 2.1, second sentence):** "...the **near-total absence of any institutional self-documentation** from these communities is itself something this world's own conditions help explain..." — This is a direct historiographical claim about the state of the surviving documentary record (the same claim the Grounding note at line 82 correctly confines to itself: "the institutional-silence-as-forces-effect claim draws directly on Doc_02 Section 9's 'what silences reveal' finding"). A member of this world could not have observed or thought in terms of "near-total absence of institutional self-documentation" — that is a claim only visible from outside the world, looking at what has and hasn't survived into the modern record. This is the identical substantive content of the Grounding note, stated a second time in the supposedly-inhabited paragraph itself. **SUBSTANTIAL.**

**Assessment:** Four independent, verifiable instances directly contradict the document's central, repeatedly-tested claim of full WFI compliance, and contradict the specific FINALIZATION claim (Section 11) that "Writing-From-Inside Principle [was] maintained throughout inhabited prose." This is not a matter of degree or interpretation — "ecological" and "formation logic" are literally lifted from the document's own section headers into the inhabited voice. This finding alone is sufficient to override the document's self-certified "no substantial issues" status.

*Minor/cosmetic note:* Section 1's own header (line 49) reads "## 1. Human Ecology — the person this world's evidence can actually show us" — itself a claim about evidentiary limits, sitting in a section title rather than body prose. Headers are arguably a different register than "inhabited prose" proper, so I log this as cosmetic rather than substantial, but it is in the same family as the finding above.

## C. Primary-source citations — verified accurate against real translations (this is the strongest part of the document)

I independently fetched primary texts from newadvent.org (Roberts-Donaldson/ANF) and earlychristianwritings.com (Lightfoot & Harmer, 1891) rather than trusting Doc_05's transcriptions.

- **Ignatius, *Ephesians* 4** (Doc_05 line 127): "your justly renowned presbytery... is fitted as exactly to the bishop as the strings are to the harp" — **verified verbatim** against ANF: "For your justly renowned presbytery, worthy of God, is fitted as exactly to the bishop as the strings are to the harp." Accurate, with the ellipsis correctly marking the elided "worthy of God."
- **Ignatius, *Philadelphians* 4** ("one eucharist" instruction) — **verified**: chapter is literally titled "Have but one Eucharist, etc." in both translations checked, and Doc_03 (line 80) independently documents this exact correction (from a prior *Smyrnaeans* 8 misattribution), later verified twice more by Doc_03's own round-4 review using two separate scholarly translation traditions.
- **Ignatius, *Smyrnaeans* 13:1** (Doc_05 line 220): "I salute the households of my brethren with their wives and children, and the virgins who are called widows" — **verified verbatim, word for word**, against the Lightfoot & Harmer translation of *Smyrnaeans* 13:1.
- **Ignatius, *Smyrnaeans* 13:2 "Tavia/Gavia" variant** (Doc_05 line 220) — **verified genuine**: Lightfoot's translation reads "I salute the household of **Gavia**," while the ANF/Roberts-Donaldson translation reads "I salute the house of **Tavias**." Doc_05's claim that this is "a genuine textual variant across translations" with "Lightfoot's edition read[ing] 'Gavia,' other editions render[ing] it 'Tavia'" is **exactly correct** — a genuinely well-verified, non-trivial textual-critical point.
- **Letter to Polycarp 8** (Doc_05 line 220): "I salute all by name, and especially the wife of Epitropus with her whole household and her children" — **verified near-verbatim** against Lightfoot ("I salute all by name, and especially the wife of Epitropus with her whole household and her children's") — trivial difference only in a stray possessive.
- **Ignatius, *Romans* 4** "food for wild beasts" (cited via Doc_04/Doc_01, drawn on by Doc_05 line 25) — **verified**: "Allow me to become food for the wild beasts" (ANF). Accurate compressed quotation.
- **Martyrdom of Polycarp 18**, *dies natalis*/"birthday" language (Doc_05 line 121) — **verified**: Lightfoot 18:3, "to celebrate the **birth-day** of his martyrdom for the commemoration of those that have already fought in the contest." Accurate characterization.
- **Didache 9–10**, cup-before-bread thanksgiving sequence — **verified**: Didache 9 opens "First, concerning the cup...And concerning the broken bread," confirming the cup-before-bread order Doc_04/Doc_05 both rely on.
- **Didache 9:4 "scattered and gathered" grain image** — I confirmed this image is real in the primary text ("Even as this broken bread was scattered over the hills, and was gathered together and became one") but confirmed independently, by grepping Doc_02 in full, that **Doc_02 never discusses this specific verse or image anywhere** (no hits for "scattered," "grain," "hills," or "Didache 9" in `doc02_text.md`). Doc_05's own account of why this image was removed from inhabited prose (line 121: "not developed anywhere in Doc_02 and remained untraceable") is therefore an accurate, honest description of a real Registry-traceability gap, not a claim that the image itself is inauthentic.

**No misattributed, fabricated, or inaccurately characterized primary-source citation was found anywhere in Doc_05.** This is a genuine strength of the document — its historical-source handling is careful and independently verifiable.

## Overall verdict: COSMETIC FIXES ONLY — with one caveat

The substance of Doc_05's historical reconstruction, its citation chain back through Doc_01–04, and its primary-source engagement are all sound on independent adversarial re-verification. However, the document's central self-certified claim — that Sections 1–6's inhabited prose contains zero analytical-distance language after seven review rounds — is **false**. Four genuine, independently-verifiable Writing-From-Inside violations remain in the current "FINALIZED" text, in four different subsections, and at least one of them (Section 1.2) is a demonstrated recurrence of a leak class the document's own history says was already fixed once.

These are wording-level fixes (each can be corrected by rewording the specific sentence, exactly as prior rounds did for similar leaks) — they do not change any classification, confidence rating, sourcing conclusion, or scope boundary anywhere in the document. On the substance/scope axis used elsewhere in this review, they would be COSMETIC. But because Task A explicitly asked whether the document's own governing-principle compliance claim is actually true, and it is not, I am flagging this as requiring an actual fix-and-recheck pass rather than being wavable as a stylistic nicety: a Constitution-level principle (Article 23) is currently violated in the FINALIZED text in four places, which the document itself asserts is not the case.

**Recommended disposition: one more light revision pass — reword the four identified sentences (lines 67, 81, 115, 133) to remove the analytical-distance/methodological-vocabulary leaks — followed by finalization. No reclassification, no sourcing change, no re-derivation of the Interaction Matrix, Proportionality Assessment, or Forces Integration table is needed; all of that content is independently verified sound.**
