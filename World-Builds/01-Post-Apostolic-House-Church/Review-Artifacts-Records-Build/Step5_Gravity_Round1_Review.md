## SUBSTANTIAL findings

### S1. The G01↔G03 relation is missing from both records, so the index prints "no demonstrated relationship" for a cell Doc_04 affirms.

Doc_04's final matrix (Section 4, after seven rounds) states: *"Nine relationships now carry '(inferential)': **G01↔G03**, G01↔G04, G01↔G05, G01↔G07, G02↔G03, G02↔G04, G03↔G04, G04↔G05, G05↔G07."* Section 6 item 5 repeats the identical list.

`pahc.gravity.authority-consolidation` declares four relations — translocal-network, boundary-drawing, liturgical-practice, martyrdom-meaning. `pahc.gravity.state-pressure` declares two — translocal-network, martyrdom-meaning. Neither declares the other. Because the generator fills unmatched cells with a literal default (`generate_gravity_index.py` line 91: `rel_map[row_id].get(col_id, "no demonstrated relationship")`), the index now asserts, in the two symmetric cells, that authority-consolidation and state-pressure have **no demonstrated relationship** — a positive claim Doc_04 does not make and specifically corrected *toward* the opposite in round 3 (G01↔G03 was one of the three cells marked "(inferential)" in that round's fixes).

This is not a borderline reading. Three independent places contradict the records:
- Doc_04, G01 Forces test: *"Intensifies under state pressure — Ignatius explicitly deploys his own impending martyrdom as an argument for his authority program."*
- Doc_04, G03 Dependency test: *"G01's urgency partly depends on it."*
- Doc_08 line 316, under Force 2B-1's own linkage list: *"2A-1 (State Legal Precarity): intensifies under this pressure — Ignatius explicitly deploys his own impending martyrdom as an argument for his authority program (Doc_04, G01)."*

And most damningly, **the authority-consolidation record's own trailing body says it**: *"intensifies under state pressure (Force 2A-1) - Ignatius deploys his own impending martyrdom as an argument for his authority program."* The record states the relationship in prose and then omits it from the structured field the index reads.

The index's matrix is therefore 9 relationship-pairs where Doc_04 has 10. The mechanical gates cannot catch this: reciprocity passes (neither side declares it), the isolated-candidate check passes (both have other relations), so the error is invisible to every automated check in the battery. **Fix:** add `associated-with → pahc.gravity.state-pressure` to authority-consolidation and `associated-with → pahc.gravity.authority-consolidation` to state-pressure; regenerate.

### S3. Two paraphrases presented as direct quotations, in records claiming `verification_state: verified-direct` and `citation_specificity: A`.

**(a) The harp image.** `pahc.gravity.authority-consolidation`, manifestations: `"one bishop, flanked by presbyters and deacons, obeyed as the shape of unity ('fitted together... as a harp's strings are tuned', Ignatius, Ephesians 4)"`. The vendored anf01 text of Ephesians ch. IV reads: *"For your justly renowned presbytery, worthy of God, is fitted as exactly to the bishop as the strings are to the harp."* Neither "fitted together" nor "tuned" appears; the ellipsis implies elided text that is not there. This is a **regression from an approved sibling**: `pahc.term.presbyterion` quotes the same passage verbatim in its locus (*"your justly renowned presbytery... fitted as exactly to the bishop as the strings are to the harp"*), states in its own trailing note *"The harp wording is verified verbatim against vendored anf01 (v.ii.iv-p1, shorter recension),"* and — correctly — drops the quotation marks when it paraphrases in `plain_meaning` ("tuned to him the way strings are tuned to a harp"). The gravity record does the opposite of the record that already solved this.

**(b) 'one of the presbyters'.** Same record: `"Polycarp addressed as bishop by Ignatius while naming himself only 'one of the presbyters' in his own letter"`. The vendored salutation reads *"Polycarp, and the presbyters with him, to the Church of God sojourning at Philippi"*, with the ANF's own footnote offering the variant *"Polycarp, and those who with him are presbyters."* The quoted phrase is a prior-build gloss (it recurs in `CiC_W1_World_Profile.md`, Doc_06, and Doc_09) that matches neither reading. Again the approved new-regime records get this right: `pahc.source.polycarp-philippians`, `pahc.figure.polycarp`, and `pahc.term.presbyteros` all quote *"Polycarp, and the presbyters with him"*, and the Step 3 review verified that string verbatim. The substance (Polycarp counting himself among presbyters) is Doc_04-faithful; only the quotation marks fail — and the underlying reading is the *footnoted variant*, which is worth stating rather than presenting as the text.

**This is the third consecutive step to carry this exact defect class**: Step 2 Finding 3 (Doc_01's "more precious than the finest jewels" gloss against anf01's "most exquisite jewels"), Step 4 S5 (the condensed *pertinacia* quotation) and C1/C2. The martyrdom record in this very batch gets it right — it uses *"more precious than the most exquisite jewels"*, honoring the step-2 fix. The rule is known and was applied unevenly. **Fix:** quote the vendored wording, or drop the quotation marks and let the manifestation read as description (which is what the other twenty-odd manifestations in this set already do).

### S4. Doc_04's inferential/plain and Reinforcing/Reshaping distinctions vanish in the compression, with no disclosure anywhere.

The schema's `RELATION_TYPES` (schemas.py lines 15–24) offers eight types; none encodes strength-of-grounding. Compressing Doc_04's richer vocabulary to `associated-with` is therefore forced, and is the right call. What is not forced is doing it silently.

Doc_04 spent **rounds 4, 5, 6 and 7 — four of its seven review rounds — establishing exactly one distinction**: that nine cells are "(inferential)" because their grounding is a single shared passage doing multiple jobs, and that precisely one cell (G01↔G02) is *not*, because it rests on genuinely independent multi-voice grounding on both sides. Round 7's own cosmetic fix was a further tightening of that one cell's citation. Section 4's closing paragraph and Section 6 item 5 both state the 9/5/1 split as a headline result.

In the new artifacts:
- The index's matrix prints `associated-with` in all ten relationship cells with no marker, no legend, and no note that a distinction was compressed away. A reader who has not read Doc_04 cannot recover that G01↔G02 is the one structurally different cell, or that nine of the ten rest on single-source grounding.
- Of six records, only `pahc.gravity.liturgical-practice` preserves it in prose ("*two of five candidates in demonstrated relationship (authority, boundary-drawing, **both inferential**)*"). The other five say nothing. In particular, neither of the two records constituting the special cell — authority-consolidation and translocal-network — mentions that G01↔G02 is the set's only non-inferential relationship.
- The sole "Reshaping" in Doc_04's matrix (G02↔G03) survives in `state-pressure`'s prose ("*the network (reshaping)*") but not in `translocal-network`'s, so the same pair reads with two different characters depending on which record you open.

**On the specific question the compression most risked getting wrong — it is clean.** All five of Doc_04's "No demonstrated relationship" cells (G02↔G05, G02↔G07, G03↔G05, G03↔G07, G04↔G07) are correctly *absent* from the records' `relations[]`, not one converted to `associated-with`. Verified pair-by-pair against every record's relations block. Directionality is also preserved where Doc_04 states it (martyrdom's "runs mostly INTO… not the reverse"; boundary's "runs mostly one direction"). The loss is entirely in strength-of-grounding.

**Fix:** one sentence under the index's matrix stating that `associated-with` is the schema's only available relation type here and that Doc_04's Reinforcing/Reshaping and inferential/plain distinctions are recorded in Doc_04 §4, not in these cells; plus a clause in authority-consolidation and translocal-network noting that their mutual relation is the set's one non-inferential cell.
