## SUBSTANTIAL findings

**S1. two-ways: formation_confidence "Contested" is calibration drift from the approved chunk, and inconsistent with its own siblings.**
`pahclex007`'s approved Confidence is "**Documented** within this community's own record … but single-source, and not established as this world's network-wide catechetical norm." Doc_06's tag table gives Two Ways **no CT tag** — nothing about it is a live contest. The record (`pahc.term.two-ways.md`) carries `formation_confidence: Contested` with `divergence_note: null`. The two structurally identical cases — prophetes and presbyterion, both "Documented but single-source" in their chunks — were handled correctly in this same batch (Documented + a divergence_note stating the single-source limit). Two-ways should match that pattern. Compounding: because the record lists Barnabas as a second source row, the index's mechanical single-source column reads "no (2 sources)" — so the term Doc_03 flags as "Author Gravity risk: Yes, maximally" now appears on the watch list only as "Contested" (wrong label) and not as single-source (masked). The Barnabas source row's own SCOPE GUARD licenses only the schema-circulated claim; the sequence remains Didache-only. Fix: formation_confidence → Documented, divergence_note stating single-source-for-the-sequence (Barnabas = circulation witness only), and regenerate the index.

**S2. eucharistia: formation_confidence "Widely Accepted" flattens the approved chunk's Documented/Contested split to a value appearing in neither half.**
`pahclex004`'s approved Confidence is split: "**Documented** for the baseline practice — thanksgiving over bread and cup is independently attested across every strand … **Contested** for Justin's fuller account specifically." The record carries `Widely Accepted`, `divergence_note: null`. Mitigation noted: Doc_04/Doc_06 state **G07**'s (gravity-level) confidence as Widely Accepted — but that is the gravity's calibration, not the term's, and Doc_06's own round-1 cold review specifically corrected a conflation of exactly these two levels. Either restore the chunk's split (Documented + divergence_note carrying the Contested-representativeness half, which the record's own evidential sense already states correctly) or, if Widely Accepted is a deliberate record-level midpoint, say so in divergence_note. Silent-null is the one option the schema's design (divergence_note exists for exactly this) argues against.

**S3. ministrae: formation_confidence "Contested" matches no approved calibration for this term.**
`pahclex009`'s approved split is "**Documented** that the term and the report itself exist … **Inferential-Thin** for what it tells us about these women's actual role." The approved sibling `pahc.figure.ministrae` carries **Documented** + a divergence_note stating the same split. Doc_06's tag table gives ministrae **no CT tag** (its flags are AS, DR). "Contested" implies a live dispute between positions; the record's own evidential sense says the opposite ("this world's own voices never say" — that is thinness, not contest). The trailing build note discloses the flattening ("carried here as Contested at the record level with the split stated") but the disclosure lives outside the schema's field for it: `divergence_note: null`. Fix: either Documented + divergence_note (mirroring the approved figure record) or Inferential-Thin for the role-content with the Documented-existence half in the note — not Contested.

**S4. ministrae: register "emic" is inconsistent with the record's own content and with its Tier 3 siblings.**
The record's `world_word` is "ministrae (**Pliny's word, not the community's own**)" — the identical parenthetical hetaeria and pertinacia carry, and both of those are `register: etic`. The schema's register enum ("the record's STANCE") includes `emic-unavailable`, which appears designed for exactly this record's situation — the record's whole point is that the women's own word for their service was never kept ("What they themselves called that service, no one kept"). The approved `pahc.figure.ministrae` is emic, but that record's subject is the in-world *women*; this record's subject is the outside *label*. Either change to etic/emic-unavailable, or state the rationale for emic explicitly (the *referent* — recognized service inside the community — is emic even though the label is not). As it stands the three Pliny-derived terms classify the same situation two different ways with no stated reason.

**S5. pertinacia: a paraphrase presented as a direct quotation, in a verified-direct record.**
The informational sense quotes: "'for I did not doubt that, **whatever it was they confessed**, pertinacious and inflexible obstinacy ought to be punished.'" The vendored npnf201 note (McGiffert's note to HE III.33) reads: "For I did not doubt that **whatever that might be which they confessed, at any rate** pertinacious and inflexible obstinacy ought to be punished." The record's shorter locus quote ("pertinacious and inflexible obstinacy ought to be punished") is an exact vendored substring; the longer sense-field quote is a condensed rewrite inside quotation marks. In a record claiming `verification_state: verified-direct` under this build's own verbatim discipline, this fails the wording gate. Fix: quote the vendored wording exactly, or drop the quotation marks around the lead-in.

**S6. ministrae: strong missing canon tag F6-P.**
The record's translational sense engages canon question **F6-P-06** ("could a woman carry real authority among you, and what did it cost her?") nearly verbatim — "the modern question — 'could a woman carry real authority among you?' — meets this world's hardest evidence exactly here" — yet `canon_cells` lists only F5-I and F6-E. A record that quotes a canon question in its own text is the definition of a strong candidate for that cell. If F6-P-06 is being deliberately reserved for a later honest_limit/contested_claim record (a defensible plan, given the coverage rule), that reservation should be stated in the trailing note the way prophetes's note states its F6-P-05 deferral; silently untagged, it reads as an omission.
