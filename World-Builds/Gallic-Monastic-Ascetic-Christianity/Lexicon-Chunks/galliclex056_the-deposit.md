## Quick Meaning

At Lérins the deposit is what the Apostle told Timothy to keep, as Vincent reads it: "That which has been intrusted to thee, not that which thou hast thyself devised: a matter not of wit, but of learning; not of private adoption, but of public tradition; a matter brought to thee, not put forth by thee, wherein thou art bound to be not an author but a keeper" — "Thou hast received gold; give gold in turn."

---

## World Meaning

Vincent takes one verse and turns it over for three chapters. "O Timothy, keep the deposit, avoiding profane novelties of words." "What is 'The deposit'? That which has been intrusted to thee, not that which thou hast thyself devised: a matter not of wit, but of learning; not of private adoption, but of public tradition; a matter brought to thee, not put forth by thee, wherein thou art bound to be not an author but a keeper, not a teacher but a disciple, not a leader but a follower." Then the charge: "Preserve the talent of Catholic Faith inviolate, unimpaired. That which has been intrusted to thee, let it continue in thy possession, let it be handed on by thee. Thou hast received gold; give gold in turn. Do not substitute one thing for another. Do not for gold impudently substitute lead or brass. Give real gold, not counterfeit." And the address widens from Timothy to every teacher: "O Timothy! O Priest! O Expositor! O Doctor! if the divine gift hath qualified thee by wit, by skill, by learning, be thou a Bazaleel of the spiritual tabernacle, engrave the precious gems of divine doctrine, fit them in accurately, adorn them skilfully, add splendor, grace, beauty. Let that which formerly was believed, though imperfectly apprehended, as expounded by thee be clearly understood" — but nothing is to be changed. The deposit is the faith as a thing held in trust, whose keeper's whole excellence is fidelity of transmission.

The word belongs to Lérins. Cassian says the same of the monastery's customs — "the Institutes which are not mine but the fathers'" — and Martin of a cult; but the word "deposit" and its gold are Vincent's, and a Gallic reader who learns it learns the faith as a talent to be returned, not a possession to be improved.

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
"The deposit of faith" as a later dogmatic-theology term — a defined body of doctrine administered by a magisterium; or "deposit" as a bank balance.

**World Hearing:**
One verse read as a charge to a young bishop, and through him to every teacher — gold received, gold returned, nothing substituted — the faith as a trust whose keeper is forbidden to be its author, spoken by a monk writing against his own forgetfulness.
