## Quick Meaning

For this world the Pelagians are the named heresy its own voices define themselves *against*: Vincent's "ephemeral, moribund set of frogs, fleas, and flies" who "attributed so much antecedent strength to Free-will, as to deny the necessity of God's grace," and Cassian's "profane notion of some who attribute everything to free will" — the error on the far side from the one the world was later accused of.

---

## World Meaning

Vincent uses them as the type of the novelty his rule exists to refuse. Against the apostle's anathema on any other gospel, "an ephemeral, moribund set of frogs, fleas, and flies, such as the Pelagians, call out in opposition, and that to Catholics, 'Take our word, follow our lead, accept our exposition, condemn what you used to hold.'" When he lists the innovators, the charge is exact: who before Pelagius "attributed so much antecedent strength to Free-will, as to deny the necessity of God's grace to aid it towards good in every single act?" Cassian's Chaeremon, at the very moment he defends the reality of the will, refuses the same thing in his own words: it is not "the profane notion of some who attribute everything to free will and lay down that the grace of God is dispensed in accordance with the desert of each man; but we plainly assert our unconditional opinion that the grace of God" abounds beyond merit. For both, the Pelagian is the man who made grace unnecessary; and the boundary the south draws is drawn on that side by name, while on the other side — against a God who wills only some to be saved — it is drawn without naming anyone.

The men who reported the Massilians to Africa said the same: these brethren, holding that wills are anticipated by grace, are "abundantly" distinguished "from the error of the Pelagians." And the north has one entry in the file, a stranger's: Gennadius writes that Sulpitius "in his old age ... was led astray by the Pelagians, and recognizing the guilt of much speaking, kept silent until his death" — a charge the Life's own text neither confirms nor knows.

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
That these monks *were* Pelagians, or "semi-Pelagians" as a settled verdict; Pelagianism as a general term for "works" or optimism about human nature.

**World Hearing:**
The world's own named foil — those who "deny the necessity of God's grace" and dispense it "in accordance with the desert of each man" — refused by Vincent as novelty and by Cassian in the same breath as his defence of the will; a boundary drawn by name on one side only.
