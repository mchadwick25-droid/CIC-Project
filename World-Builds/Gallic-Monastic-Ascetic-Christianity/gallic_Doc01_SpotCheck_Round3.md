## New issues introduced by this fix round

1. **§8.2 — two bad cross-references inside the S6 fix sentence (same class as N1).** "Augustine's replies are real and vendored **(§9)**" — §9 is "Disclosure obligations carried from G0" and says nothing about vendoring; the vendoring record is §10/G1. And "(so far as this world's own vendored library shows, **§10, Open Item 5**)" — Open Item 5 is the Conference XIII/Augustine relative chronology, not the question of whether the monks addressed a text to Augustine; the relevant item is §11 item 2. Both pointers are new in this round, in the sentence that closes out S6.
2. **§2.4 — "(quoted in full at §4 below)" is inaccurate.** §4 quotes only two fragments of the Commonitory-introduction sentence ("afterwards successively bishops of Arles", "afterwards bishop of Riez") and omits the very clause §2.4 attributes to it ("opposed to St. Augustine's later teaching, and holding what was afterwards called Semipelagian doctrine"). The clause itself is verbatim-correct against the file (line ~11791); only the pointer over-promises. This pointer was *created* by the N1 repair.
3. **§2.3 — broken quotation frame (cosmetic).** "…Castor of Apta Julia in Gallia Narbonensis, **whose own province Cassian describes as a place** 'you are anxious that the institutions of the East … should be established in your province, which is at present without monasteries.'" The frame does not govern the quoted clause (which is second-person address to Castor, not a description of a place). The quotation is accurate; only the sentence around it is ungrammatical.

**Lesser notes, no action required:** §1's "see §2.3 for the fuller passage" now resolves to real Preface material, but not to the fuller context of the Pontus/Mesopotamia clause it is attached to. §2.3 calls the Prolegomena dedication passage "its own editorial introduction to the *Institutes*"; it is in the volume's Prolegomena and concerns the *Conferences*' dedications. §2.4's "(ed. August Engelbrecht, 1861–1925; … 1891)" puts the editor's life dates where publication dates are read. Several corrections are labelled "Round 2 finding S6 / S3 / C11" when those are Round 1 finding numbers carried forward at PARTIAL/NOT FIXED status. §12 says "both remaining minor findings (N9, N11, N12)" — three, not both.
