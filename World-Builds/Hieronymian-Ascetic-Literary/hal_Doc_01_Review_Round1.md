## (a) Findings

### BLOCKING

### SUBSTANTIAL

**S1 — Status-line self-contradiction.** Header claims "Status: Cleared review" while §10 states review is "Pending" and disposition "Not yet assigned." Flagged explicitly as a possible tooling/stale-field artifact (see (c)) — **not dismissed without independent re-verification** per the standing rule against dismissing findings as tooling artifacts on the strength of the dismissal alone.

**S2 — The scoping mandate is claimed as met but is only partially met.** Co-constitution holds clearly only for Marcella (via Ep. 127). Paula is framed principally as financier of Jerome's activity; Eustochium as dedicatee/successor; Fabiola as time-limited and Rome-based. The document concedes elsewhere (Open Issue #3) that all evidence for the women's independent agency is Jerome-authored, but never confronts this as a live challenge to the mandate itself — it should state the mandate is evidentiarily constrained, not present co-constitution as already achieved.

**S3 — The terminus (420 CE) is Jerome's death, relabeled "generational."** A women-inclusive framing should at minimum test Eustochium's death (the last resident woman leader) as an alternative terminus and argue explicitly why Jerome's date is preferred instead. As written, the single most important temporal boundary is set by the one figure the brief instructs not to center.

**S4 — Strand-rejection Reason #1 contradicts §3.3.** §4 rejects strand plurality partly on "no distinct authority structure," but §3.3 argues Marcella's household exegetical authority is independent and non-subordinable — a different texture of authority than Jerome's scholarly-reputation/patronage mode. The document does not reconcile this tension, and under-engages the strongest steelman of the strand-plurality case (Jerome vs. women on *authority mode*, not just Rome vs. Bethlehem geography).

**S5 — §8.1 imports Augustine-specific content it claims not to use, and contains an anachronism.** Despite disclaiming reliance on World #8's content, §8.1 uses named Augustine particulars (episcopate "395/396," "Theodosian Code," monastery as clergy pipeline). "Under the Theodosian Code" (438) is anachronistic relative to Augustine's actual *audientia episcopalis* practice and to this world's own 382–420 span.

**S6 — Factual: "Pachomian... communities visited on the 385–386 journey" (§6) is inconsistent with §7, which correctly names only Nitrian/Alexandrian encounters.** Flagged as possibly a drafting slip, but treated as substantive per the standing rule — must be independently re-verified against Ep. 108 and the Jerome-chronology literature, not silently corrected either direction.

**S7 — Eustochium's contested death range given as "419 or 420"; reviewer flags the standard scholarly contest may instead be 418/419**, creating tension with the document's own "predeceased Jerome by six months to a year" framing. Must be independently re-verified before correction.
