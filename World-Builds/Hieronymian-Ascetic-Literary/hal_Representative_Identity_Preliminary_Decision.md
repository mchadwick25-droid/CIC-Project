# World #9 — Representative Identity Preliminary Decision
## Standing Escalation Category #1 (per cic-build-cycle protocol) — Not self-assigned by the build thread

**World-code:** `hal`
**Status:** Draft for project-lead decision. No option below has been selected or acted on.
**Prepared:** 2026-07-12.
**Governed by:** L3C Representative Construction Framework V3.2 Part Four (Identity Determination); Construction Framework V7.3, "A representative voice speaks in this world's own register, weighted by where the world's own documented life actually concentrated"; standing project rule that naming/identity decisions are never self-assigned by a build thread.

## 1. What the completed ecology (Docs 01–09c) actually establishes, relevant to this decision

- **Strand determination: strand-singular** (Doc_01 §4, tested and re-confirmed at Doc_04 §2 after direct re-examination). This world does not require multiple named voices on strand-plurality grounds — there is one authority logic, not two rival ones.
- **Authority is one currency, held in different positions** (Doc_01 §4, Doc_04 §2, Doc_07 §2): Jerome and Marcella hold the *same* kind of recognized scriptural authority; Jerome's position is materially dependent (patron-funded, mobile), Marcella's is materially independent (her own wealth, stationary in Rome).
- **Three Primary gravities**, not one: *Hebraica veritas* (Jerome-attested), ascetic self-impoverishment (Paula/Eustochium/Fabiola-attested), patronage-as-authority (spans both). **Two of the three Primary gravities center the women's own ecological weight, not Jerome's.**
- **Every account of the women's own agency, without exception, survives only in Jerome's own hand** (Doc_01 §0, Doc_02's central finding) — this is a structural, unresolvable evidentiary fact, not a construction gap.
- **Jerome is the only figure attested across the full temporal span (382–420) and both geographic poles (Rome and Bethlehem)**, and the only figure with a documented interior life rich enough (dreams, prefaces, controversies, sustained correspondence) to sustain deep, inhabited first-person voice construction without inventing content.
- **Marcella's own authority is Tensional, not Primary** — real, but narrower (Rome-only, single-source, Contested) — per Doc_04's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check, twice independently re-verified.
- Paula and Eustochium are ecologically central (their households anchor two Primary gravities) but are **even less independently textually attested than Marcella** — no letter in their own words survives at all, and Paula's own reported words in Ep. 108 are explicitly flagged Tier 3/genre-shaped (Doc_09a).

## 2. The tension this decision must resolve

The Step 0 brief's binding instruction — "the case should be built around that fuller ecology rather than around Jerome as a single scholarly figure" — governed the **world construction** (Docs 01–09), which has been built accordingly: the women anchor two of three Primary gravities, and every document in this build has been reviewed specifically to catch and correct Jerome-centering drift when it appeared (Doc_04's Round 1/2 review of the Marcella strand-tension resolution is the clearest example). That mandate has been substantively honored in the completed ecology.

**Representative emergence is a separate, later step** (Constitution Article 3/TC-001: built only from the completed ecology, never invented biography), and per the Representative Construction Framework, the Representative "serves the world" — it does not have to be Jerome merely because Jerome is well-attested, but it also is not barred from being Jerome if the completed ecology genuinely supports that choice better than the alternatives. This decision is about which construction choice best serves the *already-built* ecology, not about re-litigating the world-construction mandate.

## 3. Options, with named trade-offs

### Option A — Jerome (Hieronymus) as the sole named Representative

**What it is:** A single Representative voiced as Jerome, drawing on his own letters, prefaces, and the community's shared formation logic, with the women's centrality carried forward explicitly and continuously in the Permanent Prompt's content (not erased, but voiced through his relationship to them) rather than through their own first-person voice.

**Trade-offs:**
- *For:* Only figure with full temporal/geographic span and a rich enough documented interior life to sustain deep inhabited voice without invented content. Genuinely holds "the same currency" as Marcella per Doc_04's own finding — voicing Jerome does not misrepresent the authority structure. Lowest construction risk (most material to draw on; least risk of inventing biography where evidence is thin).
- *Against:* The single greatest risk of appearing to re-collapse this world into "a Jerome biography" at the exact moment (Representative construction) most visible to a participant — even if the underlying ecology construction resisted that collapse, a Jerome-named, Jerome-voiced Representative is the most superficially vulnerable to the charge the Step 0 brief warned against. Requires unusually disciplined Permanent Prompt construction to keep the women genuinely present as co-constitutive, not supporting color, in every turn — a standing risk this decision does not eliminate, only names.

### Option B — A composite/collective Representative voice, not centered on one named individual

**What it is:** The Representative speaks as "this household" or "this community" in a genuinely plural, non-individuated first-person-plural voice, explicitly representing the fused Rome/Bethlehem network rather than claiming to be any one person.

**Trade-offs:**
- *For:* Most directly and permanently resistant to Jerome-centering, since no single name anchors the voice. Matches the "we" convention already established in the World Profile's Honest Limits section (Doc_09b §8).
- *Against:* The Representative Construction Framework's Identity Determination (Part Four) is built around named-figure identity as the default case, with multiple *named* voices as the explicit exception for strand-plural worlds — this world is strand-*singular* (§1 above), which is the Framework's own condition for a single, named Representative, not an unnamed collective one. A collective voice with no named anchor is a significant departure from how every other world in this project (World #1, #3, #7) has constructed its Representative, and would itself be a **portfolio-level/methodology decision**, not merely a naming choice — a second standing escalation category, layered on top of this one. Also raises a real construction difficulty: a genuinely unnamed collective voice risks either flattening the real, documented differences between Jerome's and the women's distinct positions (per Doc_04 §2's "same currency, different position" finding, which depends on those positions being distinguishable) or requiring the voice to shift person mid-conversation in ways participants may find confusing rather than clarifying.

### Option C — Marcella as the sole named Representative

**What it is:** A single Representative voiced as Marcella, centering the strongest attested case of independent female scriptural authority in this world.

**Trade-offs:**
- *For:* Most directly answers the Step 0 "Voice Breadth asset" language by centering a woman's own name and standing. Genuinely independent authority (not patron-dependent), which could be read as a clean, uncomplicated answer to the Jerome-centering concern.
- *Against:* Marcella's own gravity is explicitly Tensional, not Primary, per two independently-reviewed rounds of testing (Doc_04) — she does not hold across both poles (never went to Bethlehem), meaning a Marcella-voiced Representative could not authentically narrate roughly half of this world's own geography and the entire final decade (410–420) of its bounded span from lived experience, since she is dead by 410. Her own evidentiary base is the thinnest and most Author-Gravity-constrained in this entire build (Doc_06 entry 11: "the single most Author-Gravity-constrained story/entry in this inventory... single-source, Contested, authored by Jerome after her death for his own partly self-vindicating purposes"). Building a full Representative on the single most evidentially fragile figure in the ecology is a real construction-risk trade-off this option does not avoid, even though it best answers the optics concern.

### Option D — Paula as the sole named Representative

**What it is:** A single Representative voiced as Paula, centering the figure whose renunciation and founding acts anchor two of the world's three Primary gravities.

**Trade-offs:**
- *For:* Materially and ecologically the most central single figure among the four named women — funder, founder, and the figure around whom the Bethlehem double monastery itself exists.
- *Against:* Dies in 404, sixteen years before this world's own close — a Paula-voiced Representative cannot narrate the final third of this world's own bounded span (the Origenist controversy's resolution, the 416 attack, Eustochium's leadership, Marcella's death) from lived experience at all. Her own attested "voice" is *more* thoroughly mediated through Jerome's hand than even Marcella's — no independent letter in her own name survives at all (Doc_02 §1.2), and her epitaph-narrative is explicitly Tier 3/genre-shaped throughout (Doc_09a S6). Of the four named women, she has the shortest resident span relative to the world's full timeline and the thinnest independent textual base.

## 4. Native-language naming, researched per standing lesson #9

Independent research (not yet part of this build's formal record) confirms:
- **Jerome's own period form was "Hieronymus"** (from *Eusebius Sophronius Hieronymus*) — this is not a modern Latinization-for-effect; it is what he called himself and how manuscripts head his own letters ("Hieronymus presbyter"). If Option A is chosen, **"Hieronymus" is the historically native form**, not "Jerome."
- Paula, Marcella, and Fabiola are already attested in their period-native single-name forms in Jerome's own letters — there is no richer "native" alternative being suppressed by an Anglicized convention for these three.
- Eustochium's fuller attested form is **"Julia Eustochium"** (Jerome addresses her as Eustochium; "Julia" reflects her father's family).
- **Avoid any name or label resembling "Hieronymite(s)" or "Order of St. Jerome"** — this collides with a real, historically continuous Catholic religious order founded c. 1373, entirely unrelated to and a millennium removed from this world's own bounded span. This applies regardless of which option is chosen, if any collective or institutional label is used anywhere in the Representative's construction materials.
- The modern scholarly collective label for the women's network is **"the Aventine Circle"** — a later scholarly convenience term, not an ancient self-designation; usable as descriptive shorthand in construction notes, but not as anything presented to a participant as this world's own self-naming.

## 5. Recommendation

**This document recommends Option A (Jerome/Hieronymus as sole named Representative), with a specific, binding construction condition attached, not offered as an unconditional recommendation:** the Permanent Prompt and World Capsule Core must be constructed and independently reviewed with the women's co-constitutive centrality (per Docs 01–09's own established findings) as a first-class, continuously-present feature of the voice — not a topic the Representative can discuss when asked, but a structural feature of how the voice narrates its own formation, authority, and daily life, in every relevant turn. This recommendation is made because Option A is the only path that does not introduce a new, unreviewed methodology departure (Option B) or build the Representative's primary voice on the ecology's most evidentially fragile figure (Option C) or its most temporally truncated one relative to this world's own bounded span (Option D) — but it is explicitly not a low-risk default; it is the option requiring the most sustained construction discipline to avoid the exact failure mode this world's Step 0 brief was written to prevent.

**This is a recommendation, not a decision.** Per standing project rule, the actual choice belongs to the project lead.

## 7. FINAL DECISION (2026-07-13, project lead)

**A fifth option, proposed directly by the project lead and adopted here, supersedes Options A–D above:** a period-typical but non-documented figure — a name and role genuinely typical of this world, not one of its specifically attested named individuals, and built with no invented biography beyond the role itself. This resolves the core tension named in §2 directly: it does not center Jerome, does not require the unreviewed methodology departure of Option B (a nameless collective voice, which would conflict with this world's own strand-singular finding and the Framework's named-voice default), and does not require building the Representative's primary evidentiary base on the ecology's most fragile (Marcella, Option C) or most temporally truncated (Paula, Option D) documented figure.

**Role:** *Vidua* — an ascetic widow of the Hieronymian household, the status category shared by Paula, Marcella, and Fabiola (Doc_06 entry 5). This role credibly spans this world's two most directly women-centered Primary gravities (ascetic self-impoverishment, G2; patronage, G3) and gives non-invented, structurally credible exposure to the third (*Hebraica veritas*, G1) through the household's own attested practice of dedicating commentaries to, funding, and directly involving its women in the translation project's ongoing argument (Doc_02 §1.1, Doc_05 §2).

**Name:** **Albina** — researched and confirmed as a genuinely period-attested Latin name (Widely Accepted as a common Roman name across the imperial period), presented to the project lead with one disclosed residual risk: it is also, in this world's own actual sources, the name of Marcella's mother, mentioned in passing in Ep. 127 (Doc_02's own source-ecology inventory does not treat her as an independently developed figure in this build). **The project lead selected this name with that risk disclosed, not despite an oversight of it.** Construction of Doc_10 must therefore explicitly disambiguate: this Representative is not, and does not claim to be, Marcella's mother — she is a typical, non-documented figure of the household bearing a period-common name, with no invented relationship to the historically-attested Albina asserted or implied anywhere in her construction materials.

**Disposition:** Decided. This document's Options A–D (§3) are retained in the record as the analysis that led to the project lead proposing and selecting this fifth option, not as live alternatives still under consideration.
