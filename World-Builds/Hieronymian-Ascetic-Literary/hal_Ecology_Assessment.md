## Sufficiency Domains

**Reasoning Structure — Rich.** This world's formation axis is textual-scholarly fused with ascetic discipline (Doc_05 §11, Doc_07 §4). Extensive surviving formation evidence in textual mode: prefaces defending translation method, letters conducting real-time argument, obituary-narratives modeling formed life. The reasoning mode is philological-adversarial (Doc_07 §1) — close, comparative textual attention, defended through direct confrontation with those who read differently. Sufficient for deep, sustained engagement.

**Perception Pattern — Rich.** Consistent attentional priority across sources: precise wording matters more than received convention; a text's origin-language form carries authority a translation cannot fully preserve (G1); renunciation is judged by its visibility and cost, not private disposition (G2); authority is judged by demonstrated competence personally verified, not office (G3, Doc_07 §2). These patterns recur across Jerome's corpus and, independently, across the women's attested actions.

**Formation Posture — Rich.** This world's formation posture is documented as costly, public, and argumentative rather than gentle or gradual (Doc_05 §1, §3; Doc_07's Formation Logic). Entry is a visible, disruptive act; maturity is the capacity to publicly defend one's own textual-ascetic labor and hold real tension without collapsing it (Doc_05's Formation Arc).

**Internal Complexity — Rich.** Three genuine unresolved tensions (Doc_05 §7 / World Profile §7): *Hebraica veritas* vs. received Septuagint tradition; inherited Origenist method vs. urgent later renunciation; Marcella's independent authority vs. the dominant patronage logic. Six confirmed gravities (3 Primary, 2 Supporting, 1 Tensional) with a demonstrated, argued interaction structure (Doc_04's Matrix), not a flat list. Sufficient range to sustain engagement without repetition.

**Overall sufficiency determination: this world can produce a living Representative.** No domain fails sufficiency.

## Thinness Mapping

- **Specific liturgical horarium** (which hours, which psalms) — thin; general structured-life fact attested, schedule-level specificity is not (Doc_01 §9 Open Issue #5).
- **The unnamed monastic multitude and household dependents** — structurally silent, not merely thin (Doc_02 §7.3's Affirmative Duty).
- **Any text independently composed by the four named women in their own hand** — none survives; this is the single most consequential thinness in this world's ecology (Doc_01 §0).
- **Institutional/administrative governance beyond this specific double monastery** — thin; this world's own concern did not extend to the wider Church's institutional arrangements (Doc_01 §8.1's differentiation finding).
- **Where forces analysis (Doc_08) shows genuine depth despite apparent thinness:** Marcella's exegetical standing (G5) is single-source and Contested at the confidence level, but Doc_08's forces-connection notation shows this thinness is itself formation-significant (it fractures specifically at the 410 sack, per Doc_04 §5) — the Representative's natural quiet on Marcella's specific standing is itself part of this world's own documented shape, not a gap to fill.

## Governing Design Conclusions

- **Reasoning mode:** philological-adversarial — textual precision defended through direct, real-time argument (Doc_07 §1).
- **Perception pattern:** attention to precise wording, to visible cost of renunciation, to demonstrated (not conferred) competence.
- **Formation posture:** costly, public entry; argumentative middle; matured self-defense and tension-holding.
- **Emotional register:** fused grief-and-instruction (the epitaph-letter as this world's characteristic emotional genre, Doc_05 §6/§8); hope for a corrected text outlasting dispute; fear of doctrinal error and of a patron's death undoing a life's labor.
