**Considered and explicitly not advanced, with reasons (per the same discipline Doc_04 §1 applies to its own rejected candidates):**
- ***Monacha*** (feminine form of *monachus*) — the word is real and grammatically regular Late Latin, but this research pass could not confirm it as Jerome's own characteristic term for the women of this community, who are more consistently described in his corpus via *virgo*, *vidua*, and similar vocabulary already captured at Doc_03 #4–#5. Advancing *monacha* as though it were this community's own self-designation would risk importing a plausible-sounding but unverified term — declined pending direct concordance-level confirmation.
- ***Famula Dei* / *ancilla Dei*** ("handmaid of God") — both phrases are real and well-attested as generic period Christian usage for consecrated women, but this research pass could not confirm either phrase's specific use by or about Paula, Eustochium, Marcella, or Fabiola in Jerome's own letters. Declined for the same reason as *monacha* — real vocabulary, unconfirmed attestation for this specific community.
