## 3. Balance Testing

**Has one dimension become disproportionately dominant?** This is the test most directly relevant to this world's Step-0 scoping mandate. Doc_04's Proportionality check and Doc_05 §0/§10's explicit proportionality assessments confirm G1 (*Hebraica veritas*, Jerome-centric) is Primary, but so are G2 and G3, both of which center the women's own ecological weight; the sole entirely Jerome-authored gravity is not permitted to dominate the classification outcome unchecked (Doc_04 §3). But this document does not claim that asymmetry is closed: Doc_05 §10 itself states G1 "receives the most direct textual-scholarly elaboration... because that is where this world's own surviving record concentrates," and Doc_04 §3 states plainly that "the sole gravity centering a woman's independent intellectual agency (G5-Marcella) alone is demoted to Tensional... The asymmetry is real." **Pass, on the Framework's actual test (no dimension improperly dominates; the proportionality is evidence-driven, not impression-driven) — this document names the voice/elaboration asymmetry, not only the geographic one (410–420 Bethlehem-concentration, Doc_01 §1.3), as the honest, still-open residual, consistent with how the cited documents themselves carry it.**

## 4. Reduction Testing
