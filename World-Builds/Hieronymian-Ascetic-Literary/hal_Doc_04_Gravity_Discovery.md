## 2. Six Gravity Tests (per candidate)

### G1 — *Hebraica veritas*

- **Repetition:** Recurs across Jerome's prefaces, his commentaries, and the Augustine correspondence — multiple evidence streams. **Pass.**
- **Dependency:** The Vulgate project, the community's Hebrew-study practice, and the Augustine/Oea controversy all depend on this principle. **Pass, strong.**
- **Formation:** Shapes Jerome's own scholarly identity and practice directly; shapes the women's engagement only indirectly (as dedicatees/patrons of the resulting texts, not as practitioners of Hebrew study themselves — no source attests any of the four women studying Hebrew). **Pass, but asymmetric — flagged for proportionality in Doc_05.**
- **Explanatory:** Explains the Vulgate project, the Augustine dispute, and Jerome's self-presentation as scholarly authority. **Pass, strong.**
- **Persistence:** Visible from the Rome period (Damasus-commissioned Gospel revision) through the full Bethlehem period to the Augustine correspondence (as late as the early 400s). **Pass.**
- **Interaction:** Reinforces G3 (patronage — the women's funding makes the Hebrew study and translation project materially possible) and G4 (letter-writing — the Augustine dispute is conducted entirely by letter); competes with the wider Latin scholarly consensus favoring the Septuagint (external, not a candidate gravity itself, but the friction is real); reshapes G6 (it is itself a source of controversy, feeding into the Augustine dispute specifically). **Pass — demonstrated relationships to G3, G4, G6.**

**Bipolar-geography test:** Rome and Bethlehem both — Rome as origin (Damasus commission) and ongoing dispute-partner (Augustine, external to Rome technically but part of the wider Latin ecclesiastical network centered there); Bethlehem as the site of the sustained Hebrew-based translation work itself. **Holds across both poles.**

### G2 — Voluntary ascetic self-impoverishment

- **Repetition:** Attested for Paula, Eustochium (implicitly, via her mother's example and her own virginity), and Fabiola — three of four named women, across three separate letters (Epp. 108, 22, 77), though these are one author's letters in one genre, not independent attestation in the technical sense; the pattern's actual corroboration is the independent social-historical scholarship on senatorial renunciation, per Doc_02 §6, not the letter count. **Pass**, on that basis.
- **Dependency:** The Bethlehem foundation's funding, the pilgrim hospice, and (per Fabiola) the Roman hospital foundation all depend on this practice. **Pass, strong.**
- **Formation:** Directly shapes the women's own formation (the central content of their asceticism) and indirectly Jerome's (as beneficiary and exhortor). **Pass, strong — arguably the strongest Formation-test result of any candidate for the women's half of the ecology.**
- **Explanatory:** Explains the funding structure (G3), the hospice, the hospital, and the family resistance named in Doc_02 §7.2. **Pass, strong.**
- **Persistence:** Visible from pre-382 Rome (Marcella's household) through Paula's, Fabiola's, and (implicitly) Eustochium's practice at Bethlehem/Rome across the full 382–420 span. **Pass.**
- **Interaction:** Reinforces G3 (it *is* the source of the wealth patronage redirects) and G5 (Marcella's independent household standing rests partly on her own unmarried, propertied position, itself a form of this practice); competes with conventional senatorial marriage-and-inheritance expectations (external, per Doc_01 §6). **Pass — demonstrated relationships to G3, G5.**

**Bipolar-geography test:** Rome (Marcella, Fabiola's ongoing Roman practice) and Bethlehem (Paula, Eustochium's residential practice) both show this. **Holds across both poles.**

### G3 — Patronage as operative authority structure

- **Repetition:** Attested in Doc_01 §3.3/§8.1 and independently in Rebenich's and Cain's scholarship as the interpretive frame for Jerome's whole career arc. **Pass.**
- **Dependency:** Jerome's travel, Hebrew study, and the entire Bethlehem foundation depend on it (G1 depends on G3 for material possibility); the pilgrim hospice and hospital (G2) are themselves patronage acts. **Pass, strong.**
- **Formation:** Shapes the entire community's authority self-understanding (Doc_01 §5.3 "Authority" pattern) — this is the clearest candidate for a Primary-level Formation-test result. **Pass, strong.**
- **Explanatory:** Explains why Jerome's authority is precarious (the 384–385 crisis, Doc_01 §8.1) and why the women hold real, not merely supportive, ecological weight. **Pass, strong.**
- **Persistence:** Visible across the entire 382–420 span and both geographic poles. **Pass.**
- **Interaction:** Reinforces G1, G2; competes with episcopal/territorial authority as a rival mode (the axis of Doc_01 §8.1's differentiation argument); reshapes G5 (Marcella's authority is best described, per Doc_01 §4, as this same logic operating from independent wealth rather than a rival mode — see the Interaction Matrix note below on this specific relationship, which requires care). **Pass — demonstrated relationships to G1, G2, G5.**

**Bipolar-geography test:** Rome (Paula's, Marcella's, Fabiola's wealth, pre- and post-385) and Bethlehem (the funded foundation itself) both show this. **Holds across both poles — arguably the strongest bipolar-holding candidate.**

### G4 — Letter-writing as formation/community-maintenance medium

- **Repetition:** The overwhelming majority of Doc_02's entire source base is epistolary. **Pass, trivially strong** (though this raises an evidentiary-circularity concern addressed in §4 below).
- **Dependency:** G1 (the Augustine dispute), G5 (Marcella's exegetical role, insofar as it is *known* through Jerome's letters — though the practice itself, per Ep. 127, is described as being conducted partly in person, in Rome), and general community cohesion across the Rome/Bethlehem divide all depend on this medium. **Pass.**
- **Formation:** Directly shapes how spiritual direction and instruction were delivered (Ep. 22 to Eustochium, the commentaries dedicated to the women). **Pass.**
- **Explanatory:** Explains how a geographically bipolar community maintained coherence at all. **Pass, strong.**
- **Persistence:** Visible across the entire span. **Pass.**
- **Interaction:** Reinforces G1, G3, G5; does not clearly compete with or reshape any other candidate — it functions more as an enabling medium than an organizing force in tension with anything. **Pass on Interaction, but the relationship is uniformly "reinforcing," never "competing" or "reshaping" — flagged below as a distinct concern from the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check.**

**Bipolar-geography test:** By definition, this candidate's whole significance is bridging Rome and Bethlehem. **Holds across both poles, definitionally.**

**Evidentiary-circularity flag:** G4's overwhelming Repetition-test result is partly an artifact of Doc_02's own source base being almost entirely epistolary (Author Gravity concern named at generation) — this document does not treat "the sources are letters" as independently proving "letter-writing organized the community's formation," though the Dependency and Explanatory results (Rome/Bethlehem coherence, the Augustine dispute) do independently support the latter claim beyond mere source-type circularity.

### G5 — Scriptural-exegetical authority as practiced recognition

- **Repetition:** Attested for Jerome pervasively and for Marcella specifically (Ep. 127) — but for Marcella, in exactly one source. **Pass for Jerome; weak/single-source pass for Marcella specifically.**
- **Dependency:** Jerome's entire career and the community's textual production depend on this; Marcella's specific attested exercise of it is more narrowly dependency-linked (Roman clergy's specific disputes), not shown to be depended upon by other ecological dimensions the way G1 or G3 are. **Pass for Jerome; Partial/weaker pass for Marcella's specific instance.**
- **Formation:** Central to Jerome's formation and self-understanding; for Marcella, the evidence shows her *exercising* recognized authority but not clear evidence of it *forming* others' practice beyond the specific clergy who consulted her. **Pass for Jerome; Partial for Marcella.**
- **Explanatory:** Explains Jerome's whole vocation; explains, for Marcella, why Doc_01's strand-determination tension exists at all (this is exactly the candidate that generates that tension). **Pass, though this is somewhat circular — the candidate explains a tension the candidate itself creates.**
- **Persistence:** Visible across the full span for Jerome; for Marcella, visible from 385 (Jerome's departure) to her 410 death — a real but shorter and single-attested window. **Pass for Jerome; weaker pass for Marcella.**
- **Interaction:** Reinforces G1 (Jerome's exegetical authority is inseparable from his Hebrew/textual-authority project) and relates to G3 in the specific, carefully-argued way Doc_01 §4 already established (same underlying logic, different position — not a rival authority structure). **Pass, with the Doc_01 §4 relationship carried forward exactly, not re-argued from scratch.**

**Bipolar-geography test — methodological caveat added following Round 1 review:** Testing "Marcella's instance" in isolation from Jerome's instance necessarily produces a single-pole result, since she alone never relocates to Bethlehem — this is **partly an artifact of the decomposition itself, not a neutral, independent empirical finding**, and this document no longer treats it as a freestanding reason bearing on the strand-determination question below (it remains relevant only to *this candidate's own* Primary/Supporting/Tensional classification, addressed in §3). The decomposition (testing Jerome's and Marcella's instances of "recognition-based, non-office scriptural authority" separately) is itself defensible — Jerome's exegesis is fused with his philological/translation project in a way Marcella's is not, and Open Issue #7 is specifically about Marcella — but the document must not let a result produced by its own choice of decomposition do double duty as independent evidence against reopening Doc_01's strand-singular finding. That would be circular.

**Resolution of Doc_01 Open Issue #7 — reasoned independently below, on its own confidence basis rather than by re-importing Doc_01 §4's prior reasoning or treating the bipolar-geography test above as bearing on it:**

Doc_01's actual reopening trigger (§4, Open Issue #7) is narrow: reopen the strand-singular finding *if evidence surfaces that Marcella's authority operates on a genuinely distinct basis — a difference in kind, not merely degree, from Jerome's authority mode.* This is a question about the **texture of the authority**, not about how broadly it organizes the ecology or how many geographic poles it reaches. Bipolar-geography and Primary-gravity breadth are answers to a different question (does this candidate organize broadly enough to be a world-level Primary gravity?) and do not bear directly on kind-vs-degree. This document corrects itself accordingly and reasons the strand question on its own terms:

- **The confidence basis, re-examined directly (not re-imported from Doc_01 §4):** The entirety of the evidence for Marcella's authority having a genuinely distinct basis rests on one source — Jerome's own retrospective obituary (Ep. 127) — written after her death, for his own purposes. Two features of Ep. 127 itself, read closely rather than summarized, cut *toward* "same kind, different position" rather than toward a distinct basis: first, Jerome's own account states that while he was still resident in Rome, Marcella disputed his exegetical answers "not for argument's sake but to learn" — placing her, by Jerome's own framing, *inside* his own recognition structure as a serious interlocutor rather than as an alternative source of authority operating on different terms; second, Cain's reading of Ep. 127 as partly serving Jerome's own exegetical-method vindication against Origenist critics (Doc_02 §1.4) means the letter's rhetorical purpose is to enlist Marcella's remembered authority *in support of* Jerome's own standing, not to document a rival account of what makes someone authoritative. Both of these considerations, examined on their own evidentiary merits, support "same underlying currency, different position" over "distinct basis" — they are not merely consistent with Doc_01 §4's prior conclusion by default; they actively argue for it.
- **The confidence level itself is dispositive independent of the above:** even setting the close reading aside, evidence resting on a single source, authored after the subject's death for the author's own vindicatory purposes, is Contested by this document's own five-level vocabulary. A difference in kind cannot be affirmatively established on Contested, single-source evidence — the reopening trigger requires evidence surfacing, and Contested evidence has not, by definition, risen to that threshold.

**Finding: Doc_01's strand-singular determination is not reopened.** This finding rests solely on the confidence/kind-vs-degree analysis above; Marcella's bipolar-geography failure is not treated as an additional, co-equal reason for this specific question — it remains relevant only to this candidate's own gravity classification (§3). Marcella's exegetical authority is real, ecologically significant, and carried forward as a genuine Tensional-level finding (see Classification below) — but it does not rise to the level that would fracture the strand-singular finding.

- **Interaction:** Reshapes G1 (the Augustine dispute is a direct consequence/test of the Hebrew-veritas principle) and G3 (the Origenist rupture with Rufinus was itself conducted through, and threatened, the patronage-and-reputation network — Pammachius and Marcella are named addressees of Jerome's own polemic, per Doc_02 §1.1). **Pass — demonstrated relationships to G1, G3.**

**Bipolar-geography test:** Rome (Rufinus's and later polemical circulation, the Augustine correspondence's wider Latin-network context) and Bethlehem (the site of the actual 416 violence, and Jerome's residence throughout the Origenist dispute). **Holds across both poles.**
