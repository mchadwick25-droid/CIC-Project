## Cluster 1 — Naming the Unnameable God

- **ousia / hypostasis** — being and concrete-subsistence: the paired words the era hammered out to say one God, three who are God, without division or confusion. Tier 1. [AS][DR][TC][RT][CT] (the "settlement" historiography is contested — contest type at Doc_06). All registers; see-weighted in articulation. AG flag: the technical articulation is the three authors' own labor.
- **homoousios** — "of the same being": Nicaea's contested word, received here as the faith's banner rather than coined here. Tier 2 (carried inside the ousia/hypostasis entry at depth). [SC][TC][RT].
- **akatalēptos / akatalēpsia** — the incomprehensible God / divine incomprehensibility: what the Eunomian fight forced into precision — the essence of God is not captured by any name. Tier 1. [AS][DR][TC][RT]. AG flag: sharpest articulation in the two Gregories.
- **epinoia** — conception/reflective concept: the mind's honest instruments, which reach toward God truly without seizing his essence (the anti-Eunomian epistemology). Tier 2. [TC]. AG flag: Basil's argument, extended by Nyssen.
- **energeia / energeiai** — the workings/operations: God known from what he does, not what he is ("from his energies, not his essence"). Tier 2. [TC][RT].
- **agennētos** — "unbegotten": the opponents' battle-standard — Eunomius' claim that this one word names God's very essence; the world's whole epistemology of God is its refusal. Tier 2. [TC][CT] (adversarially transmitted beyond the surviving Apology). See-weighted.
- **theologia / oikonomia** — the doctrine of God-in-himself vs. God's dealings (creation, incarnation, salvation) — the era's working distinction for what kind of speech is being attempted. Tier 2. [TC][DR] (moderns hear "theology" as any religious talk). **oikonomia** carries a second live sense — pastoral accommodation, measured reserve in speech (Basil's contested restraint about calling the Spirit "God") — both senses candidates. [CT]-adjacent on the reserve debate.

## Cluster 2 — Worship as Doctrine's Home

- **doxa / doxologia** — glory and glory-giving: the fight over one preposition in the doxology ("with the Spirit" vs. "in the Spirit") that occasioned the era's pneumatology; worship as the place where the faith is actually held. Tier 1. [AS][DR][RT]. Congregation-weighted.
- **paradosis (agraphos)** — the (unwritten) tradition: the customs of the churches — facing east, standing on the Lord's day, the threefold immersion, the invocation's words — argued as carrying the faith alongside Scripture. Tier 1 candidate. [AS][TC][DR][RT] (moderns hear "tradition vs. Scripture" polemics). AG flag: the explicit argument is Basil's (On the Holy Spirit).
- **baptisma / phōtisma** — baptism/illumination: into the Threefold Name — the community's doctrinal spine in ritual form; also the era's delay problem (exhortations against postponement). Tier 2. [SC][RT].
- **psalmōdia** — psalm-singing, including antiphonal night vigils: the shared prayer-culture the world defended as the churches' common custom. Tier 2. [AS][RT].
- **mystērion** — mystery: the hidden made present in rite and in the incomprehensible God confessed. Tier 3. [SC][TC].
- **panēgyris** — the festival assembly at the martyrs' shrines: liturgy, crowd, market, preaching — the countryside's Christian calendar. Tier 2. [AS][RT]. Congregation/countryside-weighted.
- **martys / martyrion** — the witness and the shrine: the last persecutions' dead (the Forty above all) as the region's patrons and formation exemplars. Tier 1 candidate. [SC][DR][RT].

## Cluster 3 — The Common Life and the Poor

- **koinōnia / to koinōnikon** — communion/community, the shared life: the world's word for what human beings are made for — argued explicitly against the solitary ideal ("whose feet will you wash, alone?"). Tier 1. [AS][DR][RT]. Brotherhood-weighted, congregation-wide reach.
- **adelphotēs** — the brotherhood/sisterhood: the world's own name for its ascetic communities (not yet "monastery" as a settled institution). Tier 2. [AS][TC][RT].
- **askēsis** — training/discipline: the athlete's word turned to the whole Christian life — renunciation ordered toward love, against its own excesses. Tier 2. [SC][DR][RT].
- **hēsychia** — stillness/quiet: the contemplative's desire — the Pontus retreat, the interior silence — forever in tension with the church's summons. Tier 1. [AS][DR][RT]. AG flag: articulated chiefly by Basil (early letters) and Nazianzen (whole career).
- **philoptōchia** — love of the poor: the era's named virtue (title of the era's own orations) — almsgiving become institution (the poorhouse, the leprosarium) and formation criterion. Tier 1. [AS][RT].
- **philanthrōpia** — God's love for humankind, and the virtue that imitates it: the theological ground under the poorhouse. Tier 2. [SC][RT].
- **ptōchotropheion / xenodocheion** — the poorhouse / guest-house: the "New City" outside Caesarea — the institutional face of philoptōchia. Tier 2. [AS][TC].
- **pleonexia** — greed/grasping-for-more: the vice the famine homilies anatomize — wealth as the poor's bread withheld. Tier 2. [SC][DR][RT].
- **eikōn (tou Theou)** — the image of God: the anthropology under everything — the beggar bears it; the slave bears it ("who can buy the image of God?"); the soul's kinship to its maker. Tier 1. [SC][DR][RT].

## Cluster 4 — Formation, Learning, and the Soul's Ascent

- **paideia** — formation/education: the Greek formation ladder the world climbed, converted, and disciplined — Athens used, never worshipped; the classics as bee-flowers (take the honey, leave the poison). Tier 1. [AS][DR][TC][RT]. AG flag: elite self-description; the plateau never saw Athens.
- **philosophia** — "philosophy" as the world speaks it: the ascetic Christian life itself — "the true philosophy" — a deliberate repossession of the pagan school's crown-word. Tier 2. [AS][DR][RT].
- **theōsis / theopoiēsis** — deification: becoming god by grace — the era's boldest word for salvation's end (coined in this circle in its noun form). Tier 1. [AS][DR][TC][RT]. AG flag: the noun is Nazianzen's; the doctrine wider.
- **epektasis** — the stretching-forward: perpetual progress into the inexhaustible God — no final arrival, ever-deeper desire. Tier 2 (not Tier 1: substantially one author's development). [AS][TC][PV — Nyssen-weighted; the label as a fixed noun is partly a modern convention on his participles — flagged].
- **parrhēsia** — bold speech/confident access: before God (the goal of purification) and before power (the bishop at the prefect's bench). Tier 2. [SC][RT].
- **apatheia** — passionlessness/freedom from disordered passion: shared with the wider Greek ascetic world; here disciplined toward love rather than mere absence. Tier 2. [SC][TC][PV] (usage varies across the three authors). Cross-world blur risk with Alexandria/Desert — [DR] for the build itself.
- **eusebeia** — reverence/right piety: the master-affect — precision of confession and rightness of life as one reverence; its opposite (asebeia) is what both Eunomius and the greedy rich are accused of. Tier 1. [AS][DR][RT].
- **diastēma** — the interval/gap: the creature's unbridgeable difference from the Creator — the metaphysical ground of incomprehensibility and endless progress. Tier 3. [TC][PV — Nyssen].

## Cluster 5 — Church Order and Its Contests

- **episkopos / chōrepiskopos** — the bishop and the country-bishop: the region's two-level pastoral structure — metropolitan sees and village oversight (the canonical letters discipline both). Tier 2. [SC][TC][RT].
- **kanōn / kanonikai** — the rule/canon and the enrolled women (canonical virgins/widows): discipline's vocabulary and the recognized female estates. Tier 2. [SC][TC].
- **presbytera? / diakonos** — (declined: insufficient in-world evidence for a distinct female-office vocabulary here; the enrolled estates are carried under kanonikai). Recorded as a considered-and-declined candidate rather than silently omitted.
- **koinōnikos bios / to koinobion** — the common life / community house: the ordered alternative to both the solitary flight and the radicals' anarchy. Tier 3 (carried inside koinōnia/adelphotēs). [TC].
- **oikonomos** — the steward: of the church's goods, of the poorhouse, of the mysteries — stewardship as office and as metaphor. Tier 3. [SC].
- **exoria / exile vocabulary** — the era's experience of depositions and banishments under hostile reigns. Tier 3. [SC].
- **symbolon / pistis (tōn 318)** — the creed / "the faith of the 318 fathers" (Nicaea): the confessed standard, received and fought for. Tier 2. [SC][TC][RT].

## Cluster 6 — The Affections, Grief, and Hope

- **penthos / paraklēsis** — grief and consolation: the era's dense corpus of funeral orations and consolation letters — tears permitted, despair refused, hope argued at the deathbed. Tier 2. [AS][RT].
- **anastasis** — resurrection: the hope that reorders grief — the deathbed dialogues' subject; the sisterhood's own name for its life ("the life of the resurrection" anticipated). Tier 2. [SC][RT].
- **phthonos** — envy: the vice the world's own homilies single out (envy as the diabolical sin — the era's social-moral preaching). Tier 3. [SC].
- **philotimia** — love of honor/ambition: the Greek city's engine and the clergy's named disease (the era's own disgust at episcopal ambition). Tier 2. [AS][DR][RT].
- **orgē** — anger: catalogued and disciplined in the moral homilies. Tier 3. [SC].

## Completeness note

Candidates were drawn from all Doc_02 evidence streams — dogmatic-polemical (Cluster 1), liturgical-customary (Cluster 2), ascetic-legislative and philanthropic (Cluster 3), paideutic and mystical (Cluster 4), canonical-institutional (Cluster 5), consolatory-moral (Cluster 6) — not from one voice; the AG flags mark where a term's articulation is nonetheless one author's labor. Continued search was producing restatements rather than genuinely new candidates, meeting LDF's sufficiency test. **Initial Tier 1 slate (subject to Doc_04/Doc_06 revision):** ousia/hypostasis, akatalēpsia, doxologia, paradosis agraphos, koinōnia, philoptōchia, eikōn tou Theou, paideia, theōsis, hēsychia, eusebeia, martys/martyrion — 12 candidates, to be pruned or confirmed by demonstrated ecological weight, not by interest. One deliberate exclusion recorded: **epektasis** is held at Tier 2 despite its modern fame, because in-period it is substantially one author's development — promoting it would be importing the twentieth century's Nyssen into the fourth (Doc_02 §5 caution applied).
