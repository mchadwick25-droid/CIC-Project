## Part A — World Scope: is the window and boundary right?

Doc_01 (recovered, built under CF V7.3) already argues this in full; restated here for the actual decision, not re-derived:

- **Self-description:** "the churches of Cappadocia and Pontus in the years the faith of Nicaea was fought for — from the peace after the last martyrs to the death of the last of the great household (c. 325 – c. 394)."
- **Beginning c. 325:** Widely Accepted (Nicaea; end of the Eastern persecutions).
- **Ending c. 394, soft edge:** Dominant Modern Reconstruction — a construction judgment (the 381–394 transformation window), flagged in Doc_01 itself for external review. The alternative endpoints considered and declined: c. 400, or 398 (Eunomius' books burned) — both judged as aftermath, not the world's own life.
- **Strand finding:** strand-singular (Article 21) — the tempting episcopal/ascetic division declined because the same persons and families inhabit both; two fracture lines named instead (radical-ascetic/Gangra; the pneumatological rupture). Doc_01 itself flags this classification as defensible-but-contestable — a reviewer could argue the radical-ascetic line is a strand, not a fracture.
- **Geographic/cultural scope:** Cappadocia, Pontus (Iris valley), Armenia Minor (Sebaste), with education/mission extension arcs to Athens and Constantinople. Countryside and non-Greek-speaking church life named as thin throughout (Inferential/Thin), not filled in.
- **The world's own named risk, carried since Doc_01 and never retired:** this is a smaller, more author-concentrated evidence base than any world in the current set — three authors, one family, one friendship network. Doc_01 and Doc_02 both argue the regional-world framing holds; Critic Finding 2 (recovered, `Critic_Checkpoint_1_Simulated.md`) calls this the build's single deepest exposure and recommends a Cappadocia specialist judge it at Article 31 external review.

**What Mark is actually deciding here:** not whether the reasoning is competent (it is) — whether the boundary calls he'd have to defend to a specialist are the ones he wants standing: the soft 394 cut, the strand-singular classification, and building a regional "world" this concentrated in one household's archive. Any of the three can be revised now, cheaply; none can be revised later without reopening Doc_04 and Doc_08.

### Excluded from this manifest — modern secondary scholarship (Doc_02 §5)

Rousseau, McGuckin, Silvas, Elm, Van Dam, Sterk, Holman, Gribomont, Caner, Ayres, Behr, Anatolios, Daley, Ludlow, Coakley, and Brown are all under active copyright. They inform the builder's own interpretive reasoning (already worked into Doc_02–Doc_09) but are **not acquisition targets** — nothing in the Register Bar-governed spoken record cites them as sources the Representative can quote, and none should be copied into `cic/texts/`. This is a rights exclusion, not a quality judgment; the manifest tracks primary texts the record store can license and cite, not the builder's reading list.

### Honest gaps — not filled, named instead

No verified open edition was located this session for: Basil's individual moral/social homilies (famine, the rich fool's barns, usury, envy, anger, drunkenness, named martyrs) or his Address to Young Men; Gregory of Nazianzus' verse corpus including *De Vita Sua*, and his full (not "select") letter collection; Gregory of Nyssa's homilies on the Song of Songs, Ecclesiastes (including the anti-slaveholding homily — Doc_02's most load-bearing single citation after the Vita), the Beatitudes, and the Lord's Prayer, plus the Life of Gregory Thaumaturgus and his consolation letters; Julian's school edict of 362 as an independent text. **This is a real scope reduction, not a gap to paper over**: several of Doc_09's Tier-1/Tier-2 stories and Doc_06's Tier-1 lexicon entries (eikōn's anti-slaveholding citation chief among them) currently rest on citations this manifest cannot yet back with an acquired open text. Options, for Mark's own call, not decided here: (a) proceed on the rows above and mark the ungrounded citations `do-not-cite-until-acquired` pending a further search pass (which may find these in smaller open anthologies not surfaced this session); (b) authorize a licensed-source purchase for the highest-value gap (the Ecclesiastes homilies, for the slaveholding citation specifically); (c) accept the citations as builder-memory-only with the existing unverified-caveat carried, scoped down at Doc_06/Doc_09 revision.
