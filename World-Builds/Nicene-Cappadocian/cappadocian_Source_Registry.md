## Companion to Doc_02 (Source Ecology) — Construction Framework V7.4 Step 2

**Governed by:** `L3B-World-Build-Methodology/Source_Registry_Template.md` (V1.0). Built as the second, structured output of the same Step 2 evidentiary pass Doc_02 records in prose — not a rewrite of Doc_02, and not a later step bolted on after it. Every row below is drawn from a source Doc_02, Doc_01, or this world's other cleared documents already name; nothing here is new research.

**Status:** First pass, drafted 2026-08-31 after Mark's re-confirmation of G1 and the completion of this world's source acquisitions (`CAPPADOCIAN_BUILD_LEDGER.md` §9). Awaits independent review before this document can be treated as cleared, per this world's own build-cycle discipline. Not yet cross-checked line-by-line against Doc_04/Doc_06/Doc_08/Doc_09's own citations — the "Licensed For" column below names the gravity/debate/lexicon-term/story-tier candidates already named in Doc_01/Doc_02's own text; a later pass should confirm every downstream document's own citations trace to a row here, per the template's own checkpoint rule (below).

**The checkpoint this Registry exists to satisfy:** *"Doc_02 may not name a source in support of a specific claim unless that source has a corresponding Registry row."* Every source Doc_02 §§1–6 names has a row below. Where Doc_02 itself flags a source as sought-but-unlocated (Amphilochius' own works; a period edition of the Nicaea subscription lists; Codex Theodosianus 16.1.3 in translation), that row is included and marked accordingly — an unlocated source is still worth recording as a named gap, per the template's own living-document discipline.

**Confidence axis note, read together with Doc_01/Doc_02's own Article 17 vocabulary:** this Registry's A–E axis (Citation Reliability) is a different scale from Article 17's five-level Confidence vocabulary (Documented / Widely Accepted / Dominant Modern Reconstruction / Contested / Inferential-Thin) that Doc_01 §5 and Doc_02 §9 use. The two are not interchangeable and this document does not conflate them: Article 17 rates how confident the *world's own reconstruction* is; this Registry's A–E rates how independently *this specific citation* has been checked. A row can be Citation Reliability A (the text was directly verified this session) while the claim it supports is still Contested or Inferential-Thin under Article 17 (e.g. Basil's death year, or Macrina's recoverability) — the two scales are answering different questions and both are recorded where relevant, in the Verification Note.

**A distinction this Registry holds throughout:** Citation Reliability A is reserved for sources this build session itself directly verified — the ten files Mark supplied 2026-08-30/31, checked for genuine identity, completeness, and provenance before vendoring (see `CAPPADOCIAN_BUILD_LEDGER.md` §9 for the individual verification record of each). Sources vendored in earlier sessions for other worlds (the NPNF/ANF volumes, present in `cic/texts/` since 2026-08-15) are real and usable but were not re-verified by this session — they are rated B, not A, on that basis alone, regardless of how solid their content actually is. This is a distinction about *when and by whom a check happened*, not a claim that the earlier-vendored volumes are less reliable.
