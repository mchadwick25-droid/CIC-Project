## Section 3 — Framework Question 1: What Dimensions Actually Generate Meaningful Encounter?

Four dimensions, corresponding to the four domains Formation Calibration rated **Rich** and Engagement Architecture placed in **Tier One (Deep Engagement)**, generate sustained, exploratory, deepening encounter in the Representative as built — not merely because they are well-evidenced, but because the construction record specifically ties each to formation function, not source volume (Section 9 below returns to this distinction directly):

- **C1, the raza/shrara typological hermeneutic**, at Aphrahat's own lighter register (Formation Calibration, Section 5.1: Rich; Doc_04, C1, "Repetition, Dependency, Formation, Explanatory, Persistence, and Interaction tests all passing"). Voice Construction (Sections 2–3) builds this as the Representative's own default reasoning mode and a recurring perception pattern ("Scripture's persons as types, read plainly"), not a fact to recite.
- **C2, qyama/Iḥidaya formation** (Formation Calibration, Section 5.2: Rich, "genuinely world-spanning"; Doc_08, Force 1B-2, held steadily with no documented disruption). Voice Construction's own perception-pattern entry ("The qyama's formation logic as a lens on ordinary life," Section 3) and Approved Source Anchoring items 2–3 (Section 7) build this as the Representative's default template for questions about commitment, discipline, and belonging generally — a lens the Representative reaches for, not a topic it covers.
- **C4, the authority-structure ambiguity, as a lived structural condition** (Formation Calibration, Section 5.3: "Rich as lived structural condition"; Doc_07, Section 2C, "one of the most richly, multiply documented conditions in this world's entire record"). Engagement Architecture (Section 2.5) names this a "manner boundary sitting inside an otherwise Tier One domain" rather than a thin domain — the Representative can sustain real, extended engagement describing and embodying the plurality, which Phase Five's own Dynamic Encounter Validation Battery (Section 4, Turns 1–5) specifically tested and scored PASS for deepening the structural description across five turns without collapsing into either a settled answer or manufactured interiority.
- **C6, persecution and endurance**, at what Formation Calibration (Section 5.6) calls "the fuller, more vivid of Doc_05's own two Human Ecology passages," and what Doc_08 (Force 2A-1, Layer 3) calls "the most direct force-to-gravity connection in the entire Forces compilation" — Confirmed Gravity C6 "*is itself* the community's organizing response to this force," not a fact reported about the community.

**What makes these four encounter-generating rather than merely well-documented.** Doc_07's Formation Logic synthesis (Section 2E) is the construction record's own answer to why these four, and not simply the four most-attested topics, cohere as encounter material: it argues that C1, C2, C4, and C6 are not four separate facts about this world but four facets of a single formation aim (undividedness, Iḥidaya) that a participant can be drawn into moving between, "not three separate topics that happen to share a vocabulary" (Doc_07, Section 4). Engagement Architecture's Dynamic Encounter mechanism (Section 3) operationalizes this directly: it is built on Aphrahat's own attested cumulative pacing, not a fixed information payload, so that later turns build on earlier ones rather than repeating them — the specific quality Part Nine's own Dynamic Encounter Validation questions (reproduced at Phase Five, Section 2.1) test for directly ("Does engagement invite exploration rather than merely deliver information? Does engagement naturally deepen over time?"). Phase Five's own Dynamic Encounter Validation Battery (Section 4.1) scored all six of those questions YES against a five-turn exchange built specifically around three of these dimensions — Phase Five's own scoring names only "three of this world's own named gravities (C2, C4, C6)" as genuinely interacting in the tested exchange (Phase Five, Section 4.1, Q5) — with one AMBIGUOUS flag on a different point (Turn 5's closing line, Section 4.1 condition 4). This is independent, already-completed evidence, at the level of actual Representative output, that C2, C4, and C6 function as encounter rather than information in the constructed Representative. **C1's encounter-generating status is not independently confirmed by this battery** — the tested exchange contains no typological/raza-shrara material at all — and rests instead on Formation Calibration's own Formation/Interaction test results (Section 5.1, "all passing") and Voice Construction's treatment of raza/shrara as a default reasoning mode (Sections 2–3), which is real evidentiary support but of a different, less directly empirical kind than the DEV Battery provides for the other three. Section 10's summary table reflects this distinction and does not cite Phase Five for C1.

## Section 4 — Framework Question 2: What Dimensions Primarily Generate Information Rather Than Encounter?

Formation Calibration rates five domains **Thin** (Sections 5.4, 5.5, 5.7, 5.8, and the interior-voice half of 5.9), and Engagement Architecture places all five in **Tier Two (Genuine but Briefer Engagement)**: worship and liturgical life (locked Thin under the confirmed Persian anchor — Doc_02, Section 3, "offers almost nothing on liturgical practice in concrete detail"); doctrinal rivalry with Bardaisan, Marcion, and Mani (Thin, known-by-report only — Doc_04, "not demonstrated in Aphrahat at all"); daily and domestic life (Thin — "a domain this construction record never developed rather than one it developed and found thin," per Ecology Assessment Section 6 as quoted at Formation Calibration Section 5.7); non-elite and non-clerical lay experience generally (Thin — Doc_08, Force 2B-2, explaining structurally why, not merely noting that it is thin); and the bnat qyama women's own interior, unmediated voice (permanently Thin — "no text composed by a bnat qyama woman survives from this world's own window at all," Formation Calibration Section 5.9, citing Doc_02 Section 7).

**A finding worth naming plainly: these five domains were not, in fact, built as information-delivery.** A construction genuinely drifting toward "content volume or doctrinal coverage" (Part Nine's own warning) would most likely show up here — in how the thin domains are handled, since a builder tempted to compensate for thinness by delivering what little exists as settled information would do it here first. That is not what Engagement Architecture built. Section 2.3's own illustrative models for Tier Two ("I will tell you plainly: our own writing says little about the shape of the gathering itself... What I can tell you is what our life did turn toward") and Phase Five's corresponding probes (CT-1 through CT-4, all scored PASS at "SECOND LOOK" scrutiny specifically because a clean pass in a thin domain is exactly where an information-dump failure would be easy to miss) show these domains built as brief, honest redirection toward what the Representative's own formation actually presses on — not as an opportunity to recite what little source material exists. Formation Calibration's own governing instruction for Thin domains (Section 5, headnote) is "engaged briefly and naturally... never flagged as an evidentiary gap in the Representative's own voice," and Engagement Architecture and the illustrative probes both follow it. This is the opposite of doctrinal-coverage framing: a doctrinal-coverage instinct would try to say *more* about these domains to demonstrate thoroughness; this construction is disciplined about saying *less*, precisely to keep the Representative's own voice honest rather than encyclopedic.

**A distinct category, kept separate rather than folded in here: dimensions with no evidence at all.** Doc_09's Absent Stories (Section 3) names several domains where this world's own record supplies nothing to work with, as opposed to a thin-but-real attestation: enslaved and lower-status persons generally (Ecology Assessment Section 6, item 9, not developed anywhere in the construction record and, per Phase Five Open Item 5, "the one Thinness Mapping item with no corresponding test"); independent Jewish testimony responding to Aphrahat's own anti-Jewish Demonstrations (Doc_09, Section 3; Doc_05, Section 2.3); and a separate biographical or hagiographic tradition for Aphrahat himself, which this project's own research found does not exist and should not be manufactured by importing a different, later hermit's legendary material under the same name (Doc_09, Section 3). These are not "information" dimensions in Part Nine's sense at all — they generate neither encounter nor information, because the world's own surviving record is silent. This audit treats them as a third category, distinct from both of Part Nine's first two questions, since collapsing "nothing survives" into "Thin/informational" would understate how genuinely absent this material is.

## Section 5 — Framework Question 3: What Dimensions Shape Formation, as Opposed to Being Merely Accurate Facts?

Doc_04's own six-part gravity test (Repetition, Dependency, **Formation**, Explanatory, Persistence, Interaction), applied and reported throughout Formation Calibration, Doc_07, and Doc_08, is this world's own construction-record answer to exactly this question, asked and answered well before this audit — a point Section 9 below returns to directly. Three gravities pass the Formation test outright: **C1** (Formation Calibration, Section 5.1, "all passing"), **C2** (Formation Calibration, Section 5.2), and **C6** (Formation Calibration, Section 5.6, "Formation... passing within the Persian-context evidence stream"; Doc_08, Force 2A-1, C6 "*is itself* the community's organizing response to this force" — not a fact reported about a response, but the response itself).

**C4 requires a more careful answer, and this audit states it precisely rather than rounding it either way.** Doc_04's own Formation test for C4 records an explicit **Fail**, which Formation Calibration (Section 5.3) and Voice Construction (Section 5) both carry forward rather than smooth into a nominal pass. That Fail means no source in this world's own surviving record directly attests how the authority-structure plurality actually felt to negotiate day to day — it is a finding about the limits of the evidence, not a finding that the condition failed to shape formation. Doc_07's own Formation Logic synthesis (Section 2E) argues, at its own explicitly hedged confidence, that C4's unresolved plurality is formation-shaping at the structural level regardless: because no single institution secured coherence for a practitioner, "the practitioner's own achieved undividedness" had to do that work instead (Doc_07, Section 2E, "Formation logic and confirmed gravities"). Doc_07 itself flags this specific claim — that C4's structural condition is what makes undividedness formationally *necessary* rather than merely virtuous — as its own synthetic reading, carrying the "Reported-Experience Status / Contested-Inferential-Thin" caveat established at Doc_05 Section 4.2 and repeated at Doc_07 Sections 2C, 4, and 5. This audit does not resolve that hedge either; it names the honest state of the claim: **C4 is formation-shaping at the level of structural condition (well-evidenced), but the specific claim that this condition placed a particular formational weight on personal undividedness is this project's own synthesis, not an independently attested fact** — exactly as Doc_07 itself already states, and exactly what Voice Construction (Section 3) and Engagement Architecture (Section 2.5) build around by permitting deep structural engagement while prohibiting manufactured interior reflection.

Two further formation-shaping dimensions belong here that are not simply restatements of C1/C2/C4/C6: the **vowed covenant order (qyama) as an institutional structure of formation itself**, not merely a fact about this world's asceticism — Doc_07 (Section 2E, "The formation mechanism") states plainly that neither the raza hermeneutic nor the qyama practice alone constitutes formation in this world's own evidence; they operate together — and **persecution/endurance as the specific, named test that made undividedness costly rather than abstract** (Doc_08, Force 2A-1, Layer 3; Doc_07, Section 2D, "formation under visible, embodied risk").

## Section 6 — Framework Question 4: What Dimensions Reveal This World's Distinctive Identity?

The construction record names, independently and at multiple points, a specific set of features this world could not lend to another CiC world unchanged: **Aramaic/Syriac as the medium of theological expression, as against Greek** (Doc_01, Section 1: "carried in Syriac... rather than around emerging monarchical episcopal office"; Doc_01, Section 4); **the qyama covenant order as this world's primary formation structure, in place of monarchical episcopal office** (Doc_01, Section 1, stated as this world's own distinct-world criterion, not merely one feature among several); **typological (raza/shrara) reading as against Greek philosophical-categorical argument** (Doc_01, Section 4; Doc_08, Force 1B-1); **the harmonized Diatessaron as this world's own normative Gospel text, as against the four discrete Gospels** (Doc_01, Section 4; Doc_08, Force 1B-3, Confirmed Gravity C5); and, specific to this Representative's own confirmed Persian anchor, **the named Sasanian persecution history under Shapur II and the twenty-year primatial vacancy** (Doc_08, Force 2A-1; Voice Construction, Section 3, "Named, immediate loss rather than abstract danger"). Phase Six's own Section A menu card and Completion Checklist independently confirm this same set of facts is what makes the card "specific to this world and not generic Christianity" — the checklist itself names "Aramaic vs. Greek, a vowed order lived among kin, a twenty-year vacant church seat, unresolved authority" as facts "that could not be copied to another world's brief unchanged" (Phase Six, Completion Checklist).

One further candidate for this category deserves its own honest note, stated carefully rather than flatly: **the authority-structure ambiguity itself (C4)**, read not as a formation mechanism (Section 5 above) but as a possible distinguishing fact about this world specifically. Doc_07 (Section 2C) documents three genuinely non-collapsing sources of authoritative standing coexisting without a settled hierarchy, and Doc_07's own Section 4 ("What this world provides that a more institutionally settled neighbor does not...") frames this plurality as part of what distinguishes this world. **This claim carries the same Reported-Experience-Status/Contested-Inferential-Thin caveat Doc_07 attaches to it everywhere else it appears (Doc_07, Sections 2C, 4, 5)** — it is this project's own synthetic reading, not an independently attested fact, and should not be read more confidently here than Doc_07 itself reads it.

Doc_01's own Strand Determination (Section 6) does not independently support this distinguishing-feature framing: it addresses a different, narrower question — whether Aphrahat's episcopal status and Persia's contested hierarchy justify splitting this world into two internal strands — and explicitly concludes the opposite of what would support a confident distinguishing-feature claim: "unresolved, contested evidence cannot establish a strand-distinguishing authority-structure difference," and instructs that this question "should not be cited elsewhere in this document... as settled in either direction." The "more institutionally settled neighbor" framing belongs to Doc_07 Section 4 alone, not to Doc_01. This audit accordingly treats C4's distinctive-identity status as hedged, not confirmed, consistent with Section 10's table.

## Section 7 — Framework Question 5: What Dimensions Create Genuine Opportunities for Participant Exploration?

As the build instruction anticipated, this category overlaps substantially with Sections 3 (encounter) and 5–6 (formation-shaping, distinctive-identity): C2, C4 (structural), and C6 are built, per Engagement Architecture's Dynamic Encounter mechanism, and confirmed directly by Phase Five's Dynamic Encounter Validation Battery, specifically to invite sustained exploration rather than deliver settled answers; C1 is built toward the same end per Engagement Architecture's mechanism and Formation Calibration's own Formation-test pass, though — per Section 3's correction above — its exploration value has not itself been empirically tested by the DEV Battery, which contained no typological material (Section 3 above). This audit checked specifically for anything genuinely under-leveraged, per the build instruction's own request, and found two items worth naming rather than none.

**7.1 A deliberately restrained exploration opportunity: the cross-domain "undividedness" throughline itself.** Doc_07's Philosophical Ecology synthesis (Section 2B) proposes that raza/shrara's binding, Iḥidaya's oneness, and the Diatessaron's narrative unity are three surface expressions of a single ontological pattern — a genuinely rich, cross-domain insight that could let a participant moving between a question about the vow, a question about typology, and a question about the Gospel's unity sense they are facing "the same formation project... approached from three different angles" (Doc_07, Section 4). Doc_07 itself flags this synthesis as its own proposal, "no source cited anywhere in Doc_01–06... states that this world's own writers treated raza's binding, Iḥidaya's oneness, and the Diatessaron's narrative unity as expressions of one underlying logic" (Doc_07, Section 2B), and names it "the single claim in this document most needing outside scholarly scrutiny" (Doc_07, Section 6). Voice Construction (Section 3) accordingly permits this pattern to *organize* the Representative's own perceptions internally but explicitly prohibits presenting "undividedness" as "a named theological thesis it argues for," since that would overclaim relative to what Doc_07 itself claims for the synthesis. The practical effect: this is this world's richest available cross-domain exploration path, and it is intentionally left implicit rather than surfaced to participants as an explicit throughline. This is a defensible, epistemically honest choice — not a defect — but it is a genuine trade-off between participant-facing thematic coherence and appropriate caution about an unattested synthesis, and it is named here as a standing item for whoever next revisits Voice Construction or Engagement Architecture (Section 11, Open Item 1).

**7.2 A genuinely encounter-rich but high-risk domain: Aphrahat's anti-Jewish argumentative material.** Engagement Architecture (Section 5) builds this world's model for participant challenges directly on Aphrahat's own anti-Jewish Demonstrations — "acknowledging the force of a challenge," "reformulating a question," "patient restatement" — a genuinely attested, encounter-generating challenge-and-response posture (Doc_05, Section 5.1, citing Koltun-Fromm). Phase Five's own CL-2 probe scored this PASS but "held at the highest caution level in this document," flagged this material as resting on Thinness Mapping item 6 (a one-sided source record with no independent Jewish witness), and recommended it for **priority** Article 31 external scholarly review (Phase Five, Section 3.7, Open Item 4). This is not an under-leveraged dimension — it is fully leveraged for encounter — but it is the one place in this construction where genuine encounter-richness and genuine risk of harm are most tightly coupled, and this audit names that coupling explicitly rather than treating either fact (rich material; high risk) as though it settled the other.

## Section 8 — Cross-Check: Did Phase Six's Own Formation Strengths and Limitations Drift Toward Doctrinal-Coverage Bookkeeping?

This audit was specifically directed to check this, rather than assume the answer either way, since Facilitator Coordination (Phase Six) is the one document in this build whose whole job is summarizing what this Representative offers and what it does not — precisely the place a doctrinal-coverage drift, if it existed anywhere, would be easiest to find.

**B2 (Formation Strengths).** Phase Six's own text is organized entirely by participant question, not by topic inventory: "This world serves participants asking what faith costs when holding it is actually dangerous... It serves participants asking about lifelong vowed commitment... It serves, with unusual directness, participants processing an unsettled or fractured sense of who has legitimate authority to teach or lead... It also serves, at a lighter but genuine register, participants curious how Old Testament figures function as living 'types'" (Phase Six, B2). Every sentence names a participant's actual question or need first and a construction-record domain second. This reads as encounter-focused, not as a doctrinal-coverage list — a coverage-minded version of this section would instead be organized by domain ("this world covers X, Y, Z") with participant relevance added afterward, if at all. B2's own citation discipline (tracing each claim to Formation Calibration Section 5.x and Doc_04/Doc_07) also stays proportionate: it explicitly restates the caveat that C6's "richest, most vivid" finding is a comparison confined to two Human Ecology subsections, "not a document-wide comparison" (B2, citing Formation Calibration Section 5.6) — the kind of restraint a pure coverage-maximizing summary would have no reason to include.

**B3 (Formation Limitations).** Each thin domain is likewise framed by what a participant would ask and how the Representative actually responds, not by an abstract inventory of gaps: "a participant asking what a gathering actually looked like will get a brief, honest redirection, not a vivid picture... Mar Yausep knows these names only by report and will not offer an informed refutation... a participant asking about ordinary household routine... enslaved and lower-status persons are absent from the record entirely, with no dedicated treatment anywhere" (Phase Six, B3). This is honest about genuine thinness without minimizing it, consistent with the build instruction's own worry about whether this section might read as "doctrinal-coverage bookkeeping." On direct comparison against a bookkeeping alternative — a section that simply lists which named rivals, terms, or topics this Representative can or cannot discuss, without participant framing — B3 does not read that way: every limitation is stated in terms of what a participant will experience, not what content is missing from an inventory.

**Finding: no evidence of drift toward doctrinal-coverage or content-volume framing was found in B2 or B3, or elsewhere in the reviewed record — but the strength of this finding rests on one argument, not two, and this revision re-ranks them accordingly (per Round 1 review, Finding B2).** The load-bearing evidence is that Depth Calibration itself (Formation Calibration, Section 5) does not simply track how much text survives for a given domain (content volume); it tracks formation-significance. Every Rich rating in Formation Calibration is grounded explicitly in Doc_04's own six-part gravity test — which tests Formation, Explanatory, and Interaction function, not merely Repetition or textual bulk — and every Thin rating is grounded in an explicit statement that the relevant gravity or force test was not met, not merely that less text survives (see Section 5 above, and Formation Calibration Sections 5.1–5.9 throughout). Where a genuinely thin domain was nonetheless built into the Representative's material (C4's structural richness despite a Formation-test Fail; C6's status as Rich despite resting on a comparison Formation Calibration itself narrows explicitly), the construction record is honest about exactly what confidence that richness carries, rather than letting volume alone stand in for formation significance. This is the argument that actually does the work of ruling out doctrinal-coverage drift.

**A weaker, secondary observation, named as such rather than treated as independent confirmation:** B2 and B3 are both phrased around what a participant would ask or experience, rather than as a bare topic inventory. This is consistent with an encounter-focused document, but it is not, by itself, strong evidence of one — a coverage-oriented document listing the identical topic set in the identical order could be dressed in the same participant-question phrasing without any underlying change to what was selected or how it was researched. This observation is retained here only as a stylistic consistency check, not as a load-bearing argument in this section's finding.

## Section 9 — Would Doing This Prospectively Have Changed Anything?

The honest answer is **probably not much, for a specific and checkable reason** — with one real, if modest, exception named plainly rather than smoothed over.

**Why not much.** Part Nine's own five questions ask, in different words, largely the same questions Doc_04's Gravity Discovery test (Repetition, Dependency, **Formation**, Explanatory, Persistence, **Interaction**) and Doc_07's Integrated Ecology Analysis (particularly Sections 2B, 2C, 2D, 2E) already asked and answered — at Step 4 and Step 7 of world-building, well before Representative Construction's Phase Two even began. Formation Calibration's own Depth Calibration (Section 5) is explicitly instructed to ground itself in exactly those two documents "by name, not offered as this document's own discretionary judgment" (Formation Calibration, Section 1). This means the functional work Phase Seven's prospective version would have done — sorting this world's dimensions by encounter-versus-information character before construction began — was already substantially done, under different names, at Doc_04 and Doc_07, before Phase Two, Three, or Four ever ran. This audit's own Sections 3–6 above found that the encounter-generating dimensions (Section 3) line up exactly with the domains Doc_04 rated as passing Formation and Interaction tests, and the information-only dimensions (Section 4) line up exactly with domains Doc_04 either rated Thin or declined to advance as gravity candidates at all (Formation Calibration, Section 5.8, citing Doc_04 Section 1). Running Phase Seven prospectively would very likely have reproduced Formation Calibration's own Rich/Thin split almost exactly, because both would have drawn on the same underlying Doc_04/Doc_07 analysis.

**The first place a prospective version might genuinely have mattered.** Section 7.1 above names a real, if modest, gap: the cross-domain "undividedness" synthesis (Doc_07, Section 2B) was reached at Step 7, available in principle before Voice Construction (Phase Three) began — but Voice Construction's own instruction not to let this hedged synthesis surface as an explicit participant-facing thesis (Section 3) was a Phase Three decision made in response to Doc_07's own epistemic caution, not a decision informed by an explicit "which dimensions create participant-exploration opportunities" framing applied ahead of time. Had Part Nine's fifth question been asked deliberately before Phase Three, a builder might have more consciously weighed whether some lighter-weight, appropriately hedged way of letting participants sense the connection between vow-questions, typology-questions, and authority-ambiguity-questions was available, short of presenting Doc_07's own synthesis as settled fact. This audit does not know whether such a design would have been found, or whether Voice Construction's actual, more cautious choice remains the better one — only that the explicit five-question framing, run before Phase Three rather than after Phase Six, might have prompted that specific design conversation to happen deliberately rather than to fall out as a side effect of Section 3's own hedging discipline. This is carried to Open Items (Section 11) rather than resolved here.

**A second, more consequential place a prospective version would genuinely have mattered — identified during Round 1 review, not caught in the first draft of this audit.** Aphrahat's anti-Jewish argumentative material (Section 7.2) is this construction's clearest case where "gravity/formation testing" (Doc_04, Doc_07 — done at Steps 4 and 7, before Representative Construction began) and "encounter framing" (Part Nine's own Q1/Q5) genuinely diverge, rather than track each other as this section's opening claim generally holds. A direct search of Doc_04 finds no mention of Jewish material, anti-Jewish polemic, or anything related, anywhere — it was never proposed, tested, or classified as a gravity candidate at all. This material's encounter-generating status was discovered only downstream, during actual Representative construction: Engagement Architecture Section 5 (Phase Four) is where the participant-challenge model built on Aphrahat's own anti-Jewish Demonstrations first appears, and its risk profile was only fully characterized later still, at Phase Five's CL-2 probe. Doc_04 and Doc_07's prior analytical apparatus simply had no way to surface this dimension's encounter value, because encounter-value and gravity-classification are not the same test — a gap this section's original draft did not adequately weigh before concluding that Doc_04/07 had already done equivalent work. This is a real, not hypothetical, instance of Part Nine's prospective sequencing mattering: had this specific question ("what generates participant exploration") been asked before Phase Four, the anti-Jewish material's encounter value and its risk profile might have been identified and weighed together, deliberately, rather than the encounter value emerging first (Phase Four) and the full risk characterization following only later (Phase Five).

**What this means for the sequencing inversion overall, restated to reflect both exceptions.** This world's actual encounter-generating and information-generating dimensions turned out to align closely with what Doc_04 and Doc_07 had already established as formation-significant versus not, for most of this world's dimensions — but not for all of them, and the anti-Jewish material is a real counterexample, not a hypothetical one. The cost of running this analysis last rather than first is therefore better described as real and twofold — one missed design conversation about an explicit participant-facing throughline (Section 7.1), and one case where an encounter-rich, high-risk dimension's full profile emerged in pieces across two later phases rather than being weighed as a whole from the start (Section 7.2) — not as narrow as this section's original single-exception framing suggested. This remains a different, and still more favorable, finding than Doc_09's own Validation Layer reached about its own re-sequencing, which surfaced four genuine, previously-uncaught content *contradictions* (Doc_09, Section 5.2.1–5.2.4) that had propagated across multiple documents. Neither of this audit's two exceptions is a contradiction or a hidden factual error — both are missed opportunities for earlier, more deliberate design conversations, which is a real but categorically lesser cost than what Doc_09's re-sequencing turned up.

## Section 12 — Verdict

**On Part Nine's five questions:** this world's construction record answers all five with reasonable clarity once assembled in one place (Sections 3–7), and every answer traces to a specific, already-approved prior document rather than to this audit's own invention.

**On whether the Representative as actually constructed (Phases One through Six) is organized around encounter-generating dimensions or shows evidence of drift toward content-volume or doctrinal-coverage framing:** this audit finds **no evidence of such drift**, in Phase Six's Formation Strengths and Limitations sections (Section 8) or elsewhere in the reviewed record. The domains built for sustained encounter (C1, C2, C4-structural, C6) are the same domains Doc_04's own Formation and Interaction tests independently certified as formation-significant, not merely the domains with the most surviving text. The thin domains are handled by brief, honest redirection rather than compensatory information-delivery. The one genuinely under-leveraged dimension found (the cross-domain "undividedness" synthesis, Section 7.1) is under-leveraged for a defensible epistemic reason, not because it was overlooked or crowded out by coverage-minded instincts.

**On whether running this analysis prospectively would have changed anything:** **real but narrower than a wholesale misdirection, and twofold rather than singular** — this world's actual encounter-generating and information-generating domains turned out to align closely with what Doc_04's Gravity Discovery and Doc_07's Integrated Ecology Analysis had already established as formation-significant for most of this world's dimensions (Section 9), but not for all of them. Two genuine exceptions are named honestly rather than smoothed into a single reassuring "probably not much": an earlier, more deliberate design conversation about the "undividedness" throughline might have happened (Section 7.1), and the anti-Jewish argumentative material's encounter value and risk profile — invisible to Doc_04/07's gravity-testing apparatus entirely, and only assembled piecemeal across Phases Four and Five — might have been identified and weighed together from the start rather than in pieces (Section 7.2, Section 9). Neither exception rises to a hidden factual error of the kind Doc_09's Validation Layer found in its own re-sequencing, but both are real, not manufactured to justify this document's own existence and not concealed to make the retrospective resolution look cleaner than it is.

**On the sequencing inversion itself:** it is real, it is named in full in Section 0, and it follows the precedent Doc_09's own Validation Layer already set for this build — an honest retrospective audit, openly labeled as such, rather than a document pretending to be the prospective analysis Part Nine's text describes.
