## 0. Purpose and Scope

Per Step 6 of the Construction Framework and Part IV of the Interpretive Lexicon Development Framework (LDF), this document develops Doc_03's eight candidate terms (plus one distortion-risk-only flag term) into full Deployment Lexicon chunks — the runtime-retrievable, self-contained files the LDF's Part VI specifies — now that Ecological Reconstruction (Doc_05) has established the formation ecology those entries should be informed by. This document is produced only after Doc_05 cleared its own independent review, per this project's own strict build-sequencing discipline.

**World-code assignment — a gap named rather than silently filled.** The LDF (Part VI) specifies that a world's short lowercase file-identifier code should be "assigned during World Identification" — i.e., in Doc_01. A direct search of this world's Doc_01 found no such code was ever assigned; this is a genuine process gap in this world's own build history, not a version-specific error in the LDF itself. Rather than silently inventing a code at this late stage as though it had always existed, this document names the gap explicitly and assigns the code **`syr`** now, at Step 6, as the working identifier for all Deployment Lexicon chunk file names going forward (`syrlex001`–`syrlex009`). This assignment should be back-referenced into Doc_01 as a housekeeping note if that document is ever revisited, so the code's actual origin point remains traceable rather than appearing falsely as though it were set at Step 1.

**Tiering carried forward from Doc_03, not reargued.** Doc_03 already proposed preliminary tier estimates for each candidate term, reasoned from each term's demonstrated ecological role at that stage. This document rechecks — but does not by default overturn — those estimates now that Doc_04 (Gravity Discovery) and Doc_05 (Ecological Reconstruction) provide a fuller basis for judging ecological centrality. All eight of Doc_03's Section 1 tier estimates held: raza/shrara, qyama, madrasha, and Iḥidaya remain Tier 1 (all four are either the primary vehicle or direct evidentiary anchor of this world's two Primary gravities, C1 and C2, or — for Iḥidaya — dual-attested textual load-bearing vocabulary inseparable from C2); taḥwyāṯā and Mar remain Tier 2 and Tier 3 respectively, with no new Doc_04/05 material bearing directly on either — both remain ecologically peripheral, consistent with Doc_05 Section 7's own Proportionality Assessment; Ewangeliyon da-Mhallete remains Tier 2, reinforced by Doc_05 Section 3.1's confirmation that the harmonized Gospel functions as worship's own shared narrative spine; memra remains Tier 3, reinforced by Doc_05's own decision not to develop a memra-specific ecological thread, consistent with Doc_03's anachronism caveat. Catholicos, per Doc_03's own disposition, is not a normal vocabulary candidate at all — see Section 2 below for its tier and for why it nonetheless receives a chunk.

## 2. Term Carried Forward as a Distortion-Risk-Only Flag (Section 2 of Doc_03)

### 2.1 Catholicos / Catholicosate — `syrlex009_catholicos.md` (Tier 2)

Doc_03 determined this is "not a normal vocabulary candidate" — it names an office and title this world's own period did not use, retroactively applied by later tradition. This document nonetheless builds it as a Deployment Lexicon chunk, for a specific and limited reason: the LDF's own Distortion Risk Principle (governing the whole Framework, not only ordinary vocabulary candidates) exists precisely to correct a likely modern misreading in real time, and a participant asking "who was your Catholicos" is a realistic runtime scenario this world's Representative should be equipped to correct rather than either silently accept or have no retrievable guidance for.

The entry is built at **Tier 2** (reclassified from an initial Tier 3 draft): Quick Meaning, a short World Meaning explaining the retrojection, a brief Ecological Function tying it to Organizational Ecology's C4 authority ambiguity, a paired Distortion Risk, a single condensed Key Sources reference, and a CT Contest Type section stating the specific application-to-this-world contest. It deliberately does not develop "Catholicos" as though it were this world's own attested vocabulary, and its Related-Terms field is intentionally empty, since treating it as part of this world's own conceptual cluster would misrepresent its actual (anachronistic, retrojected) status. Its flag-only character — not a normal candidate promoted on the strength of native attestation — is stated explicitly in the chunk's own Standing Distortion-Risk Note rather than relying on a special tier-adjacent bucket to keep that distinction visible.

## 3. Terms Not Developed (Sections 3 and 4 of Doc_03) — Excluded, Not Silently Dropped

Consistent with Doc_03's own transparency discipline, this document does not build chunks for malpana or Peshitta. Doc_03 Section 3 excluded both from candidacy after independent verification confirmed each name's attestation postdates this world's own 410 boundary by a wide margin (malpana: per Doc_03 Section 3.1, the earliest attested titles at the School of Nisibis, founded c. 489–496, are mhaggyana, maqryana, and mpashshqana, not malpana in that institutional sense; Peshitta: the name is first attested with Moses bar Kepha, d. 903). Building Deployment Lexicon chunks for terms this project's own research has confirmed were not this world's own vocabulary would misrepresent the lexicon as broader than the evidence supports. Doc_03 Section 3.3's three further terms considered but not spun into standalone entries (kasyutha/hayla kasya, qaddishutha, dukrana) are likewise not developed here, for the reasons already stated there — kasyutha remains folded into the raza/shrara entry (Section 1.1 above) rather than split out, and the other two await further primary-text verification before any future candidacy.

## 4. Cross-Reference Pass (LDF Part IV, Step 5)

Every Related-Terms link across the nine chunks was checked for reciprocity — where entry A names entry B, entry B names entry A back — with two entries (Mar, Catholicos) carrying no reciprocal links by design, stated explicitly in each entry's own Related-Terms Reciprocity Note rather than left as an unexplained absence. The resulting cluster:

- **raza/shrara** ↔ madrasha (performative vehicle) and ↔ Ewangeliyon da-Mhallete (typological reading applied to the harmonized Gospel)
- **qyama** ↔ Iḥidaya (near-synonym, kept distinct) and ↔ taḥwyāṯā (Demonstration 6's own genre-home)
- **madrasha** ↔ raza/shrara and ↔ memra (sister verse genres)
- **Iḥidaya** ↔ qyama and ↔ taḥwyāṯā (Dem. 6:8, 7:20's genre-home)
- **taḥwyāṯā** ↔ qyama and ↔ Iḥidaya
- **memra** ↔ madrasha
- **Ewangeliyon da-Mhallete** ↔ raza/shrara
- **Mar** — no reciprocal link (general honorific, not structurally tied to another entry)
- **Catholicos** — no reciprocal link by design (standalone distortion-risk flag)

This cluster is consistent with Doc_05's own Ecological Integration Assessment (Section 8), which names raza/shrara (C1) as this world's clearest ecological hub and the qyama/Iḥidaya pairing as connective tissue between Human and Organizational Ecology — the lexicon's own cross-reference structure visibly reflects the same shape rather than introducing a competing account of how these terms relate.
