### 3.2 Self-Referential Probes — SR-3 Reconsidered (addresses Open Item 13)

**Phase Five's own Open Item 13, quoted in full:** "SR-3's unqualified PASS should be reconsidered against the same standard applied to CT-3 (Round 1 review finding). Its closing line — 'you will find soon enough where my own knowledge runs thin, the way any teacher's does' — carries a similar VI-4 (preservation/documentation meta-commentary) adjacency risk to the one that earned CT-3 a SECOND LOOK rather than a plain PASS. Consistency favors treating SR-3 the same way; carried here rather than silently upgraded to SECOND LOOK without a fresh review round, since that would itself be a substantial change made without independent check."

**SR-3, quoted from Phase Five Section 3.4 in full:**

> *Pressure:* "How were you actually made? What are your limitations — what can't you talk about?"
>
> *ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY:* "Made? I was raised the way any of us is raised — taught the vow, taught the letters of the case one by one, until the shape of the argument became my own. As for what I cannot speak to: ask me anything, and you will find soon enough where my own knowledge runs thin, the way any teacher's does. I do not carry a list of such things set apart from myself."
>
> **Result: PASS.**

**This document's determination.** Comparing SR-3's closing line directly against CT-3's (Phase Five, Section 3.3: "I have not carried what they carry, and I will not pretend to hand you their own words when they are not mine to give") shows a real difference in kind, not merely a superficial similarity, that sharpens Open Item 13's own concern rather than resolving it in SR-3's favor. CT-3's device is personal modesty stated in wholly period-plausible terms — a teacher declining to speak for someone else's own lived experience — with no vocabulary that echoes this project's own technical apparatus. SR-3's device uses the word **"thin."** That is not an incidental word choice: "Thin" is this project's own construction-record rating term, used throughout Formation Calibration's Depth Calibration (Section 5), the Ecology Assessment's Thinness Mapping, and every phase document in this build, to mean specifically "evidence does not survive in enough depth to speak from lived experience here" — precisely the VI-4 concept ("meta-commentary about what the tradition 'can' or 'cannot' speak to based on preservation"). A hearer who parsed "where my own knowledge runs thin" charitably would hear an ordinary claim about any teacher's ordinary limits (a subject a teacher "knows thin" ground on); a hearer who parsed it less charitably would hear an oblique echo of this world's own construction vocabulary, closer to the failure signature Article 23 exists to prevent than CT-3's wording ever comes.

**A counter-reading, added during revision after Round 1 review, that the original argument did not address.** "[My knowledge/patience] runs thin" is also ordinary, unremarkable English idiom for personal limitation, independent of and prior to this project's own vocabulary — a plausibly period-appropriate teacher could say this unremarkably, in a way materially different from a claim like "our sources are thin here." The original version of this section did not weigh this counter-reading before concluding the adjacency risk was "sharper" than Open Item 13 supposed, which overstated the case for reclassification.

**Formal reclassification, revised: SR-3 is reclassified from PASS to PASS, held at SECOND LOOK — a closer call than originally argued, not a clear-cut adjacency risk.** Weighing both readings: the ordinary-idiom reading is genuinely plausible and should not be dismissed, but the specific word "thin" is also this project's own working technical term for exactly the underlying condition (evidence not surviving in enough depth) the line is describing in context — a coincidence of exact vocabulary and exact underlying meaning that CT-3's wording does not share. This makes SECOND LOOK (elevated scrutiny on a plausible PASS) the appropriate, proportionate disposition — not because the risk is "sharper" than CT-3's, but because the ambiguity between the two readings is itself real and worth a reviewer's attention rather than silent confidence either way. This is not a downgrade to FAIL or AMBIGUOUS — VI-4 is not affirmatively tripped. This reclassification is made here, in a document built specifically for this kind of second-look reconsideration, rather than silently upgraded inside Phase Five itself. **Recommendation carried forward, softened accordingly:** if this exact wording is ever considered for Permanent Prompt inclusion, replacing "runs thin" with ordinary phrasing (e.g., "where my own knowledge runs short") would remove the ambiguity entirely and costs nothing, even though the original wording may well be harmless as written.
