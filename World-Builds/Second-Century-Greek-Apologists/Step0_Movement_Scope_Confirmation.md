## 2. Section A — Movement-Scope Eligibility

### A1 — The floor

This candidate's entire window (124–200) predates the Creed itself by more than a century, so A1 is not the operative test in the primary sense; A2 is the real gate. **Correcting the prior draft's citation (Round 1 finding A9):** the prior draft attributed its A1/A2 routing to "the same reasoning IJC's own confirmation relied on," but IJC's actual conclusion was the opposite of a routing choice — IJC held that *both* A1 and A2 apply, to different phases of one movement straddling 325, expressly rejecting a forced either/or (and IJC's own Round 1 review flagged an earlier version of that reasoning as an invented rationale for excluding A2 outright). That precedent does not transfer to a window that never crosses 325 at all. The genuinely on-point precedent is LPC's own cleared Step 0 §2, which runs A2 as the sole governing test for Cyprian's wholly pre-Nicene phase and says of the Procedure's "as applicable" language "nothing else" — this candidate is in the same position, and A2 alone governs.

### A2 — Pre-Nicene continuity: clears, with two figures requiring honest disclosure rather than exclusion

A2 tests continuity with the strand that became Nicene orthodoxy, against divergence toward a rival current (Gnostic, Marcionite, Ebionite, or similar).

**Justin, Athenagoras, Theophilus, and Aristides clear cleanly and strongly.** Justin's Logos theology is not merely continuous with what became Nicene Trinitarian vocabulary — it is one of its direct historical sources; Athenagoras' *Plea* and *On the Resurrection* and Theophilus' *To Autolycus* are standard proto-orthodox texts with no divergence to test for.

**Tatian requires a disclosure, not an exclusion — and the disclosure this candidate would write already exists, in a built world, and is a better model than anything drafted here.** Tatian's *Address to the Greeks* is the pupil-of-Justin apologetic work vendored here — but Irenaeus reports directly (*Against Heresies* I.28) that after Justin's martyrdom Tatian went on to found or lead the Encratite current (rejecting marriage and wine on ascetic-dualist grounds), a genuine later divergence Irenaeus traces from Saturninus and Marcion rather than from any Phrygian or Eastern source. The *Address* is generally read as predating or independent of that later turn. **Round 1 finding A8 caught this document naming a discipline it did not follow:** Tatian is not merely a census-level "contested figure" precedent to imitate — he is a **built, live figure**, `records/syr/figure/syr.figure.tatian.md`, and his source record `records/syr/source/syr.source.tatian-address-to-greeks.md` already carries a section headed "THE ENCRATITE CHARGE, HANDLED HONESTLY," stating plainly that the charge is real, that the *Address* is not a confession of it, and that it predates the events Eusebius describes. Any Doc_02 disclosure this candidate writes should be built to the same standard that record already sets, not invented independently — and see A5 below and §3 B3 for the more consequential fact this same discovery exposes: Tatian is not only a disclosure question, he is a second figure-sharing question of the same kind as Justin's.

**Resolution: A2 clears**, with Tatian's specific and bounded caveat carried forward as a binding Doc_02 disclosure (§4), modeled on Syriac's own existing treatment rather than drafted from scratch.

### A5 — Interaction with Articles 20, 21, and 23

This candidate's own defining activity is arguing directly against named rivals — Justin against Marcion by name (1 Apol. 26, 58) and at length against Judaism's own claim on the Scriptures (the whole of the *Dialogue with Trypho*).

**Correcting the prior draft's statement of the rule and its citation (Round 1 finding A6).** The prior draft wrote that Marcion "is not separately screened here... rather than as an independent candidate" — inverting both the rule and the precedent it cited. A5's actual limiter is scoped "under this section": a movement's rivals are not separately assessed *inside this candidate's own Section A pass* — the rule says nothing against a rival having been screened in its own right elsewhere, and Marcion demonstrably was: census I.20, `Excluded - Doctrinal Floor (C1)`, whose own status note reads "Exclusion is not a judgment of unimportance." IJC's own confirmation states this correctly (Homoian Christianity "was already tested and excluded at the portfolio level... **as its own candidate world** — a floor effect, not a judgment of unimportance") and the prior draft's citation of that sentence said the opposite of what it says. The outcome is unaffected: Marcion belongs inside this candidate's own reconstruction as Justin's named opponent, using exactly 1 Apol. 26 and 58 — though see §3 B3 below for the fact that PAHC's own records already cite both passages for the identical purpose, which narrows what is genuinely unclaimed here.

Trypho is a different case, and the prior draft mis-routed the obligation it creates. **Correcting Round 1 finding A7:** this is not, as previously written, a matter outside "the Article 20/23 sense." Article 20 does not apply (it governs the suppressed *within* a community, not external opponents) — but Article 23 does apply, in full: "The Representative speaks about the world's opponents as the world understood them — honestly, without rehabilitating them into modern equals and without modern editorializing." Rabbinic Judaism as Justin's interlocutor is exactly the kind of external-opponent material Article 23 governs, and PAHC has already gone further than a general caution: `pahc.source.justin-dialogue.md` carries a named Doc_01 "Ways-That-Never-Parted" reading caution specifically for this material. Doc_02 should build on that standard, not invent a lighter one. There is also a further, real adjacency the prior draft missed entirely: Aristo of Pella's fragments are dual-assigned in this candidate's own corpus map to both `greek-apologists-second-century` and `ebionite-nazoraean-current` (census I.24, `Contested — Evidentiary`), on the map's own admission that "the Jewish-Christian current and pahc are both defensible; neither is demonstrated by the fragments" — and the *Dialogue*'s own corpus-map entry separately notes that its chapter 47 discussion of Torah-observant Christians is "one of the era's few direct witnesses to the ebionite-nazoraean-current material." Both are carried to §4 below.

**Correcting the prior draft's Article 21 finding (Round 1 finding A4) — the bundling it flagged as unresolved does not exist.** The prior draft asserted that "roughly a dozen works... are not uniform in genre," with six named fragmentary voices (Hegesippus, Dionysius of Corinth, Rhodon, Polycrates, Serapion, Apollonius) "currently bundled" with this candidate in `pahc.source.second-third-century-remains.md`, and made disentangling them a binding Doc_01 task. Direct inspection of `cic/corpus-map/greek-apologists-second-century.yaml` shows none of those six names on this candidate's own roster at all — they are PAHC's, not this candidate's, and PAHC's own source record already runs a careful per-author disposition of that fragment collection. What the corpus map actually contains, and what genuinely does raise a live Article 21 question, is narrower and different in kind: three works transmitted under Justin's name and flagged `confidence: provisional` with the note "transmitted under Justin, widely doubted" (the *Discourse to the Greeks*, the *Hortatory Address to the Greeks*, *On the Sole Government of God*); Aristo of Pella's fragments (dual-assigned, as above); and the Ambrose *hypomnemata* on Quadratus, itself `provisional` on a date question and dual-assigned with Syriac Christianity. The real Doc_01 question is whether these five items belong in this candidate's own figure roster as-is, or whether the pseudonymous-Justin works in particular should be held apart from Justin's own genuine corpus regardless of how the Justin question in §3 resolves. This replaces the prior, unfounded finding in §4.

**Strand Determination (Article 21), Tatian specifically — new, per Round 1 finding A1/C1.** Tatian's own dual claim (pahc's disclosed Justin-pupil-in-Rome phase against Syriac's own claim on him as "the man who wove the four Gospels into the one story these churches read for two hundred years") is not resolved by this document and is carried to §3 B3 and §4 as a second binding figure question, alongside Justin's.

## 3. Section B — Seed Screening and Tiering

### B1 — Sourcing

Strong pass, independently verified against the actual text library: Justin's two *Apologies* and the *Dialogue*, Athenagoras' *Plea* and *On the Resurrection*, Theophilus' *To Autolycus*, Tatian's *Address*, and the *Epistle to Diognetus* are all vendored whole (`anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-irenaeus.xml`, `anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml`). Aristides (recovered 1889) and the ten fragmentary sub-apostolic voices collected as "Remains of the Second and Third Centuries" (`anf08`) are also vendored, Melito's own fragment specifically at div2 x.v.

**Completing the roster (Round 1 finding A5).** Beyond the sixteen-work core, the corpus map also carries: three works transmitted under Justin's name at `confidence: provisional` ("widely doubted"); Aristo of Pella's fragments, dual-assigned with the Ebionite/Nazoraean current; and the Ambrose *hypomnemata* on Quadratus, `provisional` on its own date and dual-assigned with Syriac Christianity. The *Epistle to Diognetus* itself carries a live dating caveat in the corpus map — "some put it after the entry's 200 CE close." B1's "strong pass" stands, but the roster's own attribution and dating flags are named here rather than left implicit.

### B2 — Ecology

Real and uneven, in a specific and checkable way. The apologetic-argument register (engaging Greek philosophy, rebutting the atheism/cannibalism/sedition charges, arguing Christian ethics as superior) is exceptionally strong — this is what the genre *is*. Persecution and its stakes are load-bearing throughout (Justin's own martyrdom; the genre's whole occasion is legal jeopardy). Worship-practice texture is thinner than a typical built world's for a reason that must be stated precisely rather than as originally drafted (see §3 B3 immediately below for the correction to what, specifically, is and is not already claimed by PAHC): some of the material a builder might reach for first — Justin's own baptism and Sunday-worship description, 1 Apol. 61 and 65–67 — is already the basis of PAHC's own worship-practice ecology. A build of this candidate should not re-derive that specific lens from that specific passage; its own worship-practice picture will correspondingly lean on what remains, and Doc_05 must name that honestly. Ordinary lay formation, women's voices, and enslaved persons' own testimony are essentially absent — the apologetic genre itself selects for classically-trained male rhetoricians writing to power, more narrowly than PAHC's own noted skew.

**Passes B2**, with the worship-practice thinness named as a structural, disclosed limitation.

### B3 — Uniqueness

**The Justin bullet is substantially rewritten (Round 1 findings A2, A3).** The prior draft claimed PAHC "never" engages Justin's philosophical argument, his address to Roman power, or the Trypho debate, and offered that as evidence of large unclaimed territory. Each of those three claims is contradicted directly by PAHC's own records, checked file by file rather than assumed:

- **The philosophical argument is already claimed, at load-bearing, tier-1 weight.** `pahc.quote.moses-is-more-ancient.md` (1 Apol. XLIV, `evidentiary_weight: load-bearing`) is the antiquity-of-Moses apologetic argument itself, with its own `divergence_note` naming it plainly as "an apologetic claim about chronology made to a pagan audience." `pahc.quote.those-who-lived-reasonably-are-christians.md` (1 Apol. XLVI, `load-bearing`, retrieval tier 1) carries the Logos-spermatikos doctrine — the very philosophical move A2 above calls one of Nicene vocabulary's direct historical sources — opened specifically to serve `pahc.witness.god-and-argument`, "which cites this exact chapter for 'the Logos present in every race of men.'"
- **The address to Roman power is already named as the frame, not overlooked.** `pahc.quote.the-memoirs-of-the-apostles-are-read.md`'s own divergence note: "Justin is describing the practice of his own community to an outside audience, in an apology addressed to the emperor, so the account is shaped to look orderly and unthreatening to a Roman reader." PAHC's own figure record's `bridge_line` is built on the same fact: "He described our Sunday worship in an open letter to the emperor, and died for the name he defended."
- **The Trypho material is already engaged as argument, under a named Doc_01 caution**, not merely mined for scripture-reading evidence: `pahc.source.justin-dialogue.md` calls the *Dialogue* "this world's fullest display of how its scriptures were actually read — proof-from-prophecy... an argument in progress, not a report of a settled separation," under the "Ways-That-Never-Parted" caution named in §2 above. Justin's inside testimony against Marcion (1 Apol. 26, 58) is likewise already claimed, called in PAHC's own records "this world's clearest inside evidence that the rival movements were live, contemporary, and undefeated" — the exact citations §2's A5 above offers as this candidate's own rival-engagement territory.

**What is actually unclaimed is narrower than the prior draft stated, and this document does not manufacture false precision about it.** Candidates for genuinely open territory, not yet verified against a full read of PAHC's own records: the *Second Apology* in its own right (PAHC's roster names only the *First Apology* and *Dialogue*); Justin's own framing of Christianity as "the true philosophy" and his vocation as a philosopher-teacher running an actual school in Rome, as distinct from the conversion narrative PAHC already tells; and the *Dialogue*'s sustained argumentative structure taken as a whole work of persuasion, as distinct from the specific proof-from-prophecy citations PAHC has opened. Doc_01 must verify this residue directly against a complete read of PAHC's own figure, source, quote, and witness records for Justin before relying on it — this document has checked enough to know the prior claim was wrong, not enough to certify what remains.

**The precedent for a shared figure is also corrected (Round 1 finding A3).** The prior draft named Augustine as precedent for "a single figure serving two Representatives through two different projects," citing HAL and Donatism. Checked directly: `records/hal/figure/hal.figure.augustine.md` states outright that Augustine is "STRICTLY AN OUTSIDE VOICE: Augustine belongs to his own (not-yet-built) world; this record draws on him ONLY as the other side of the correspondence" — and Donatism's own Step 0 places him among that world's named *opponents* (Article 23 material), reconstructed from Donatism's own perspective, not as a second native voice. Augustine's actual native home is LPC. The pattern the project has actually built is: **native to one world; a declared outside voice, or a declared opponent, everywhere else** — not co-ownership. The genuinely on-point precedents, both uncited in the prior draft: (1) `alx.source.athanasius-vita-antonii.md` and `desert.source.athanasius-vita-antonii.md` hold the *Life of Antony* from opposite sides with an explicit bounding rule ("no Alexandrian record may treat desert formation logic as constitutively Alexandrian on this text's authority"); (2) Mark's own 2026-08-27 ruling in `cic/corpus-map/latin-apologists.yaml` states the project's actual mechanism plainly: **"THE RULING SPLITS THE AUTHOR, WHICH IS WHAT `per work` MEANS"** — one author, different works, different worlds. That mechanism is unavailable for Justin as currently recorded: PAHC's own figure record claims `locus: whole work` on both his *First Apology* and *Dialogue*, not a per-work split.

**Three options, not two, are therefore before Mark, and this document does not decide among them:**
(a) Justin stays native to PAHC, held here as a declared outside/boundary voice on the Athanasius/HAL model, with a written bounding rule scoping exactly what this candidate may draw on (the residue named above, pending Doc_01's own full verification) and what remains PAHC's alone;
(b) exclude Justin from this candidate's own figure roster entirely;
(c) narrow PAHC's own `locus: whole work` claim to a specific, named subset of Justin's corpus, freeing the remainder for a genuine per-work split on the Athanasius model — the heaviest option, since it requires revisiting a cleared, built world's own records, not just this candidate's.

**A second, equally consequential figure question — new in this revision, from Round 1 finding A1/C1 — sits alongside Justin's and was entirely absent from the prior draft.** Tatian is not only a disclosure question (§2 A2 above); he is a **built, live figure** in Syriac Christianity, with his own figure record and a `load-bearing`, `verified-direct` source record on the *Address to the Greeks* — the identical work this candidate names in B1. Syriac's own source record states its claim in terms that leave no room for treating this as settled in this candidate's favor: "Every argument for Tatian belonging to the Syriac East runs through the statement that he was an Assyrian." This candidate's own corpus map already flags the conflict in the work's own note ("Tatian sits awkwardly across two entries... **B3 does not pass unconditionally.** Two figure questions — Justin and, newly, Tatian — are binding pre-Doc_01 work, not vigilance notes. Both are carried to §4.

### B4 — Audiences

Strong, honestly-scoped fit with *academically minded users*, *thoughtful believers* working through faith-and-reason questions, and a real, currently underserved audience for this portfolio: people wanting a *rational, philosophically-argued* model of Christian defense rather than a devotional or formational one — including *deconstructing Christians* and skeptical seekers who want to see the case argued on philosophy's own terms rather than assumed. ### B5 — Scale

Athens, Rome, Antioch, and Sardis — major centers, though the candidate's own coherence as "one world" spanning four cities over eighty years is itself part of the genre-vs-community question flagged in §1/§4. Modest, non-decisive tiebreak factor only.

Clears Section A directly (§2); clears Sourcing strongly, with its own roster's attribution flags now named (B1); clears Ecology with a specific, corrected, structurally-caused thinness (B2); **does not cleanly clear B3**, because two figure-sharing questions — Justin and Tatian — are binding, unresolved decisions rather than vigilance notes; serves real, distinctive audiences honestly (B4).
