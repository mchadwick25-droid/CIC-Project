### A2 — Pre-Nicene continuity: clears, with one figure requiring honest disclosure rather than exclusion

**A note on counting, corrected here (Round 2 finding):** this heading concerns one continuity-disclosure question — Tatian's later turn — not the two figure-*sharing* questions (Justin, Tatian) that B3 below addresses. **Justin, Athenagoras, Theophilus, and Aristides clear cleanly and strongly.** Justin's Logos theology is not merely continuous with what became Nicene Trinitarian vocabulary — it is one of its direct historical sources; Athenagoras' *Plea* and *On the Resurrection* and Theophilus' *To Autolycus* are standard proto-orthodox texts with no divergence to test for.

**Tatian requires a disclosure, not an exclusion — and the disclosure this candidate would write already exists, in a built world, and is a better model than anything drafted here.** Tatian's *Address to the Greeks* is the pupil-of-Justin apologetic work vendored here — but Irenaeus reports directly (*Against Heresies* I.28) that after Justin's martyrdom Tatian went on to found or lead the Encratite current (rejecting marriage and wine on ascetic-dualist grounds), a genuine later divergence Irenaeus traces from Saturninus and Marcion rather than from any Phrygian or Eastern source. The *Address* is dated by the same standard this document applies to Commodian in its sibling case — as a matter of cited scholarly judgment, not settled fact: it is widely, though not universally, read as predating or independent of that later turn. **Round 1 finding A8 caught this document naming a discipline it did not follow:** Tatian is not merely a census-level "contested figure" precedent to imitate — he is a **built, live figure**, `records/syr/figure/syr.figure.tatian.md`, and his source record `records/syr/source/syr.source.tatian-address-to-greeks.md` already carries a section headed "THE ENCRATITE CHARGE, HANDLED HONESTLY." **Correcting an overstatement of that record's own content (Round 2 finding):** the section does not state plainly that the charge is real — it deliberately stops short of asserting the charge's truth, naming it as Irenaeus's report and declining to adjudicate it, while noting the *Address* itself is not a confession of it and that the work predates the events Eusebius describes. Any Doc_02 disclosure this candidate writes should be built to the same standard that record already sets, not invented independently — and see A5 below and §3 B3 for the more consequential fact this same discovery exposes: Tatian is not only a disclosure question, he is a second figure-sharing question of the same kind as Justin's.

**Resolution: A2 clears**, with Tatian's specific and bounded caveat carried forward as a binding Doc_02 disclosure (§4), modeled on Syriac's own existing treatment rather than drafted from scratch.

### B2 — Ecology

Real and uneven, in a specific and checkable way. The apologetic-argument register (engaging Greek philosophy, rebutting the atheism/cannibalism/sedition charges, arguing Christian ethics as superior) is exceptionally strong — this is what the genre *is*. Persecution and its stakes are load-bearing throughout (Justin's own martyrdom; the genre's whole occasion is legal jeopardy). Worship-practice texture is thinner than a typical built world's for a reason that must be stated precisely rather than as originally drafted (see §3 B3 immediately below for the correction to what, specifically, is and is not already claimed by PAHC): some of the material a builder might reach for first — Justin's own baptism and Sunday-worship description, 1 Apol. 61 and 65–67 — is already the basis of PAHC's own worship-practice ecology.
