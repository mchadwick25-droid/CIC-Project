## Story: fix.story.the-long-road

A household in Testland loses its standing in the market square after the youngest son is seen at the Sunday gathering; the family holds together anyway.

When the youngest of that house was seen going to the gathering on the first day, the market stalls that had traded with them for years began to trade elsewhere. The family did not leave the gathering. They ate less that winter, and stayed.
