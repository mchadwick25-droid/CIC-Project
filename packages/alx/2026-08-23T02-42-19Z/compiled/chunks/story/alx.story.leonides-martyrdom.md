the remembered account of how the persecution entered Origen's boyhood house

Under the emperor Severus, about the year 202, the persecution struck Alexandria. Leonides, father of the sixteen-year-old Origen, was arrested and held for beheading. The boy burned to go and die beside his father - to volunteer. His mother begged him to spare her; he would not listen. So she hid all his clothes, and shame kept him home. He did the one thing left: he wrote to his father in prison - 'Take heed not to change your mind on our account.' Leonides did not change his mind. He was beheaded, the family's property was seized, and Origen, at seventeen, was left the eldest of seven children of a confessor's widow.
