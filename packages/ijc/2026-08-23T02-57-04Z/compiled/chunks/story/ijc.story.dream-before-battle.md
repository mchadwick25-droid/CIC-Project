Lactantius's account of the dream before the Milvian Bridge - the earlier, plainer version

Lactantius tells it differently, and earlier. In his account Constantine was directed in a dream, the night before the battle, to mark the heavenly sign on his soldiers' shields - the letter-mark that stands for Christ - and so to proceed to battle. He did as commanded, and by that mark his army prevailed at the bridge. There is no daylight vision here, no army of witnesses, no sky-written words. A dream, a command, an obedience - and the victory that followed.
