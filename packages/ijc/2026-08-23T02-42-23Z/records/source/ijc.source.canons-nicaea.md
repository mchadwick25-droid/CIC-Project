The canons survive far more securely than any full conciliar acts for
325 (no complete acts exist - a fact stated, not smoothed). The
initiating council of the world's conciliar mechanism and the standing
precedent every later council in the window cites; Canon 6's "ancient
customs" language is the baseline against which Canon 3 of 381 and
Canon 28 of 451 innovate. Percival's apparatus (excursuses, ancient
epitomes) is a named 1900-era scholarly layer, distinguished from the
conciliar text itself wherever cited.
