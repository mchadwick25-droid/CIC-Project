The one continuous narrative of Ambrose's life; openly hagiographic in
genre and purpose (legacy Doc_02 SS2's five-dimension entry carried in
full): a genuine insider (Ambrose's own secretary) with an obvious
stake in his subject's posthumous reputation, writing to edify, with
legendary material (the bees) not marked off from documented biography
inside the text itself. Everything drawn from it in this build is Tier
3 (attributed tradition) at most, and nothing from it is quoted
verbatim - the translation is not vendored and its wording cannot be
checked this session. The dating (c. 412-413, not "shortly after"
Ambrose's death) carries the legacy build's own Round 2 correction.
