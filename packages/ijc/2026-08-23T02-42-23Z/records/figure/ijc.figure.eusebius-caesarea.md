The founding decade's most visible - and most interested - witness.
Full five-dimension Author Gravity per the reviewed Doc_02 SS2:
over-visible for 312-325; representative of a court-adjacent, moderate
position in real tension with the eventual Nicene settlement (his letter
to his own church explaining his hedged subscription survives in
Socrates I.8, verified); enormously influential on all later accounts;
a panegyrist writing about his own patron, with silences as informative
as content. His court theology is Strand B's seed (Doc_01 SS4). Note
the standing name-collision hazard: distinct from Eusebius of Nicomedia,
the Homoian-leaning court bishop who baptized Constantine - conflating
the two Eusebii is a classic error this record exists to prevent.
