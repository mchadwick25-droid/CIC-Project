canon_cells: F3-I,F3-E

"The emperor is within the Church, not over the Church." Ambrose's word from the basilica crisis. A ruler is a son of the Church, under her discipline like any believer. His soldiers are real - but he cannot command what belongs to the altar.

world_word: Imperator intra Ecclesiam, non supra Ecclesiam

The emperor belongs inside the Church like any believer - he does not stand above it.
