id: ijc.term.concilium
canon_cells: F1-I,F1-E

A council: bishops gathered, most often at the emperor's summons, to argue, vote, and issue rules meant to bind the whole church. This world's chief tool for settling disputes. It did not always keep them settled.

world_word: concilium (synodos)

A gathering of bishops, called to settle what the whole church must hold.
