This world's foundational witness to its own beginning - a direct
participant's account of the Constantinian alliance, written by the
emperor's own panegyrist. Author Gravity is severe and carried on every
use (legacy Doc_02 SS2): court proximity, providential framing, and

Constantine "long afterwards... confirmed... by an oath" - the edition's
own footnotes already flag the divergence from Lactantius's dream
account; carried as ijc.contested.constantine-conversion, never
harmonized. VC 4.24's "bishop of those outside" is Documented as a
textual claim; what it reveals about imperial self-understanding versus
