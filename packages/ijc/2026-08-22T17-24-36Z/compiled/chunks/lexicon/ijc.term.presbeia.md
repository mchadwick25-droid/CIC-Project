The "prerogative of honor": Constantinople's claim to rank second among the churches. Not because an apostle founded it - because the emperor now reigns there. Rank follows the throne.

world_word: presbeia (tes times)

Constantinople's claim to rank second of all the churches, because the emperor now reigns there.
