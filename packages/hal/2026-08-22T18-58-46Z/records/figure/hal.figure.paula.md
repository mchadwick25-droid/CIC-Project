The women's-pole anchor figure. Everything known of her comes through
Jerome's hand (Ep. 108 above all), and the record's own finding is that she
was the material and institutional FOUNDER of Bethlehem, not merely its
financier - she built, funded, and governed the women's community and the
hospice. Her interiority and quoted words in Ep. 108 are Jerome's literary
construction (Inferential-Thin as her actual speech). The scale and
totality of her self-impoverishment is Contested
(hal.contested.paula-renunciation-totality); the Palladius notice
('hindered by a certain Jerome... by his jealousy') is the one independent
near-contemporary view and cuts against Jerome's framing of the
partnership (hal.contested.paula-jerome-relationship). Claimed descent
from the Scipios and Gracchi is Jerome's report, unverifiable.
