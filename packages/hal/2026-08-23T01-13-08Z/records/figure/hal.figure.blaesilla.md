The human center of the 384-385 crisis (hal.force.clerical-precarity). Her
death after intense ascetic discipline generated public accusation against
Jerome specifically ('the detestable tribe of monks', in his own report of
the funeral muttering - wording to be collated at first quotation); with
Damasus's death that December, the protection was gone and Jerome left
within the year. This world's own record carries her death honestly as a
cost: the discipline could be, and in her case was, taken further than the
body could sustain. Her grief-stricken mother's collapse at the funeral is
Jerome's report. Everything is single-sourced to Jerome's letters.
