Rights verified from the file's own prepended header ('Rights: Public
Domain'; Clarke 1918, plus the transcriber's site-wide public-domain
declaration). Wording verified directly - closing the prior build's open
verification flag on this source: ch. 41 reads 'She was hindered by a
certain Jerome from Dalmatia. For though she was able to surpass all,
having great abilities, he hindered her by his jealousy, having induced
her to serve his own plan'; ch. 36 carries Posidonius's remark on Jerome's
temper and the prediction that Paula 'will die first and be freed from his
bad temper.'

Author-gravity in the OTHER direction (carried from Doc_02 section 3.4):
Palladius writes from an Origenist-adjacent, Evagrian-influenced milieu
about a community that defined itself against Origenism - hostile
testimony, valuable precisely for its independence, not an arbiter. His
'fifty virgins' for Eustochium's convent is the only extant headcount for
either Bethlehem community and is uncorroborated. See
hal.contested.paula-jerome-relationship.
