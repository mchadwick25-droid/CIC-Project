Doc_01 Open Issue 3, formalized at Doc_02 and carried at full strength: the
one text under the women's names is the one scholarship assigns to Jerome.
Consequence: no record in this corpus may quote Ep. 46 AS Paula's or
Eustochium's words; it may be quoted as the community's (Jerome-penned)
public voice with the attribution contest stated. Pairs with
hal.search.womens-own-texts and hal.limit.f5-women-own-words.
