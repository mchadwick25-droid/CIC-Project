SYSTEMIC AUTHOR-GRAVITY FLAG (operative through every later step, the hal
counterpart of Alexandria's Origen flag): Jerome authored, or is the
mediating hand behind, nearly the entire surviving record - including every
account of the women's agency. He also curated his own letter collection in
later life. The screening question - is this the ecology's pattern or
Jerome's framing? - stays active wherever he is the main witness, which is
almost everywhere. His self-presentation is embellished at known points
(Hebrew formation, per Williams - hal.contested.hebrew-fluency); his
polemical portraits (Rufinus, John of Jerusalem) require adversarial
discounting; his obituaries idealize by genre. Independent witnesses:
Sulpitius (admiring), Palladius (hostile), Augustine (disputing), Rufinus
(accusing) - triangulation, not arbiters. Birth-year contest carried in
dates rather than resolved.
