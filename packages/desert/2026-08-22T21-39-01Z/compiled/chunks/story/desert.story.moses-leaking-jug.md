canon_cells: F4-P

a jug leaking water behind him - my own sins run out behind me and I do not see them

The tradition tells that a brother at Scetis had done wrong, and the elders were gathered to judge him. Abba Moses would not come at first. When they sent for him again, he came carrying a jug full of water with a hole in it, so that the water ran out behind him the whole way. When the others asked what it meant, he said: my own sins run out behind me the same way, and I do not see them - and today I am coming to judge another man's fault. Hearing this, the elders said nothing more to the brother, and let him go.
