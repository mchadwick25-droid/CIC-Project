Re-derived from the prior build's cleared Doc_08 Cell 1A-i (three review
rounds), itself drawing on Doc_01 SS7's own framing of this world's
founding problem: "the practical and theological problem of how to
pursue total Christian formation once martyrdom was no longer the
available path" (Doc_01 SS7's own words, not paraphrased here). This is
the single most consequential force in this world's own ecology - it
directly generates gravity 1 (withdrawal) as the reinvented total-
offering practice and gravity 2 (spiritual combat) as the reinvented
site of total struggle, both already carrying, in their own records,
the identical framing ("Intensifies under the generating force
(martyrdom's unavailability): a direct, load-bearing response to that
specific historical pressure, not merely coexistence with it").

kind: initiating maps Doc_08's own Cell 1A (initiating/external); the
schema's three-value kind enum (initiating/ongoing/ending) does not
carry the internal/external axis Doc_08's six-cell matrix uses, so the
cell code is carried in this record's own name field instead, matching
the convention already established in the Alexandria build's own force
records (e.g. alx.force.persecution's "[2A - ongoing/external]").
