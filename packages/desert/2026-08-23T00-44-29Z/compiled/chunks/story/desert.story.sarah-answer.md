some monks came to test her, a woman - she answered them as one man answers another

The tradition tells that some elder monks once came to visit Amma Sarah meaning to humble her, and said as much to her directly - that she was, after all, only a woman. She answered them: by nature I am a woman, yes - but not by my own thoughts. It is I who am the man here, and you who are the women.
