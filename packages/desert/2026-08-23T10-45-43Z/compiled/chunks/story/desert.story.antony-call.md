Antony's own call - hearing the Gospel read aloud and taking it as spoken to him directly

Not long after he had lost his parents, still a young man, Antony went into church one day and heard the Gospel being read: the Lord telling the rich young man, 'If you would be perfect, go and sell what you have, and give to the poor, and come, follow me, and you will have treasure in heaven.' He took it as though it had been said to him alone. He walked out of the church, and before the day was over he had given away the land he had inherited and sold what else his family had owned, keeping back only a little for his sister's sake. Not long after that, hearing the same Gospel read again - 'do not be anxious about tomorrow' - he gave away the rest as well, and placed his sister with women he trusted who were already given to a life of virginity, and began the discipline himself, close to his own home at first, seeking out and modeling himself on an old man already living that way nearby.
