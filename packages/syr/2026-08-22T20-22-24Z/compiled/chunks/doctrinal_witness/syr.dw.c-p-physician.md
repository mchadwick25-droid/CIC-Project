To the one who wants to believe and cannot, or who fears Jesus would want nothing to do with them, this world answers with its favorite name for him: the Physician. A physician is not scandalized by wounds; he exists for them. The sage taught that of all who ever put on a body, only One was without wound, and that he does not reject the penitent - the shame that hides a wound is the only thing that keeps it from healing. And this world loved to preach the woman who came to Jesus in tears past every watching eye: it told her story again and again because in her it saw the door standing open. Whoever comes is received; that was this world's experience of him, sung in its hymns and taught in its letters.
