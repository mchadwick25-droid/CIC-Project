the tradition's memory of its own bishop standing at the great council

When the bishops of the whole world gathered at Nicaea in the year 325 to answer Arius, the tradition remembers that Jacob, bishop of Nisibis, was among them - one voice from the Syriac-speaking frontier standing with those who confessed the Son as true God. The lists that record the council's signers, pieced together by later hands, carry his name among the assembled bishops. His own city remembered him afterward as its father and guardian, and Ephrem's hymns for Nisibis sing of Jacob first among its bishops.
