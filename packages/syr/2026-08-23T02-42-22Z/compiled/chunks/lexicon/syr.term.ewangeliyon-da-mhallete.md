The 'Gospel of the Mixed': the one continuous Gospel story this world read and heard in worship. Tatian wove the four accounts into a single narrative around 172. For some two hundred years, 'the Gospel' in these churches meant that one unfolding story, not four separate books.

The one woven Gospel story this world read in worship. The four accounts were joined into a single telling, and people simply called it 'the Gospel'.
