id: syr.term.ihidaya
canon_cells: F4-I

The Single One. One word does double duty in this world. It names the vowed celibate - single-minded, undivided for God - and it is also the Syriac title of Christ as the Only-Begotten. The two senses share one root: to live single is to be joined, by your own name, to Christ's own oneness.

world_word: ihidaya (pl. ihidaye)
