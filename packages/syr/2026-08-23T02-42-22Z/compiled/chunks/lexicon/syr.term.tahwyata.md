Aphrahat's own name for his writings: the Demonstrations. Each one works through its subject step by step, as a reasoned showing of a point. He also called them letters. Many follow the Syriac alphabet, one letter per piece, so the alphabet holds the argument in memory.

world_word: tahwyata (sing. tahwitha)

Tahwyata means Demonstrations. It is Aphrahat's own name for his teaching letters. Each one shows one subject, step by step.
