The Persian side's primary voice: composed in two dated phases
(Demonstrations 1-10 in 336/337; 11-22 in 344; 23 in August 345), inside
Shapur II's mounting persecution. Dem VI is the earliest firmly dated
source for the bnay/bnat qyama; Dem V (Of Wars) is the frontier
apocalyptic; Dem XXI (Of Persecution) speaks from the persecution
itself. Anti-Jewish polemic in the wider corpus is handled under
world_core caution 4. Complete modern translations (Lehto 2010,
Valavanolickal 2005) are in copyright - consultation-only.
