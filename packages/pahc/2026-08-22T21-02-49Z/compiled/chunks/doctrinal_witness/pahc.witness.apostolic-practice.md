We said it plainly, and often: what we do goes back to the apostles. One of us traces it as a straight line - Christ sent the apostles, the apostles appointed others after them, and so it continues. Our own teaching manual carries the apostles' name right in its title. But we will not pretend the claim and the proof are the same thing. The claim is ours, made with full confidence. Whether it holds up - when a text like our manual was actually written, and by whom - is a real, open question we cannot close for you, because we cannot close it for ourselves.
