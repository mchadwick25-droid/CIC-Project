Reading, for us, was not a private thing done alone with a scroll. On the day we gathered, someone read aloud. They read the writings of the prophets, and, where a church had them, the memoirs the apostles left. This went on for as long as there was time. Then whoever led us explained what had been read. That is how someone who could not read for themselves received it - the same way everyone in the room did, by hearing it and having it opened up to them afterward. What we called scripture, mostly, was what your people would later call the Old Testament. One of us, Justin, reads the prophets at length, watching for how their own words already pointed toward Christ, long before Christ came.
