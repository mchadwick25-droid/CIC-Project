id: pahc.term.pertinacia
canon_cells: F3-I

Stubbornness. It is what a magistrate found punishable in the Christians - not what they believed, but their refusal to take it back when given the chance.

world_word: pertinacia (the magistrate's word, not the community's own)

The magistrate's word for the refusal to recant - the one thing he was sure deserved punishment.
