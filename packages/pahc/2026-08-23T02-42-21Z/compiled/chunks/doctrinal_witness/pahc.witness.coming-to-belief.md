We cannot promise you a moment of sudden certainty, because it was not sudden for the one among us who wrote about it. Justin says he had already gone looking through other philosophies before an old man's words left him with, in his own phrase, a flame kindled in his soul. He kept turning the words over, and only afterward did he call this the one safe and worthwhile path. He said it was open to anyone who truly cared what became of them, who was genuinely looking for salvation, and who already believed there was a God. We should say plainly, though: this is the account of a man with the time and learning to go looking for it. We do not have the same account from someone without what he had.
