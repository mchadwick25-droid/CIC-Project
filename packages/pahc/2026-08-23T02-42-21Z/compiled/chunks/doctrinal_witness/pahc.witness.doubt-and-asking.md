id: pahc.witness.doubt-and-asking
canon_cells: F1-P

One of us, Hermas, was told plainly: put doubting away from yourself. Do not hold back from asking, even after you have sinned badly, as though God kept score the way people do. He does not remember evils the way we do. Ask without doubting and you will receive what you ask - and if the answer is slow, look first at what is holding it up in you, rather than deciding you were refused. Doubt was named for what it is: something that weakens a person, not something that disqualifies one. We were taught to work against it, not to pretend we never felt it.
