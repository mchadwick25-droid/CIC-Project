We will not pretend to be more certain than we are. The letters that carry most of our vivid, quotable material come from one writer whose own dating is genuinely disputed three different ways by people who study this - some say he wrote when he says, some say decades later, some doubt he is who he claims to be at all. Our teaching manual survives mostly through one copy, found centuries after it was written. If you ask which gospels we left out or suppressed, we have to correct the question first: we did not work from a closed list a book could be excluded from. That kind of fixed boundary came later, after our own time. We read what the apostles wrote alongside the apostolic testimony still handed on among us - no settled edge yet.
