The Latin word a Roman magistrate used for two enslaved women he tortured - Christians in some recognized service. What they themselves called that service, no one kept.

world_word: ministrae (Pliny's word, not the community's own)

A Roman magistrate's word for two enslaved Christian women he tortured. They served their church in some recognized way. Their own account of it was never kept.
