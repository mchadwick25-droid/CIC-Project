Rights verified from both files' own DC.Rights headers (Public Domain).
Two vendored editions deliberately: the anf01 text is the classic
Roberts-Donaldson translation but predates the full recovery of the
letter's ending; anf09's Keith revision carries the complete text
(chapters through 65 in modern numbering, including the long closing
prayer) from Codex Hierosolymitanus. Quotes from the recovered portion
must cite the anf09 file, not anf01.

Transmission (Doc_02 SS1.2): Codex Alexandrinus (5th c.) preserves the
letter but loses a leaf (57:7-63); Codex Hierosolymitanus (1056 CE)
supplies the complete text; Syriac (Cambridge Add. 1700), two Akhmimic
Coptic witnesses, and an 11th-c. Latin copy of a plausibly much earlier
translation also survive - a thicker base than the Didache's.

WEIGHT: the best-attested single act of translocal corrective authority
in this world's early decades (Rome writing to Corinth over deposed
presbyters), and the single strongest textual anchor for Strand B's
plural, interchangeable episkopos/presbyteros college (chs. 42, 44).
Rhetorical-purpose caveat (Doc_02 SS1.2): written to settle a dispute -
its picture of settled order may be persuasive overstatement.
