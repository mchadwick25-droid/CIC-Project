THE EUSEBIUS SCREEN, binding on every record citing this row (the same
discipline Alexandria's build applies): Eusebius writes generations
after this world closed, with a documented apologetic interest in tidy
successions and settled institutions. His QUOTED DOCUMENTS (the Greek of
Polycarp ch. 13; the Lyons letter; the anonymous anti-Montanist) rate
better than his own institutional/succession narration, which stays at
reduced confidence and must never be used to smuggle a settled
monepiscopal or successionist picture back into this world's own argued,
unsettled window. His inconsistent dates for Polycarp's martyrdom
(Chronicon: 167 CE vs. the usual 155-156 derivation) are part of this
world's dating-uncertainty record, not a problem to resolve silently.
