Collective figure: speaker for Martyrdom-of-Polycarp quote records. Its
account is community testimony already shaped by hagiographic and
martyrological convention - the formation ideal is the evidence, the
staged details are not historical reporting - and its own concluding
chapters are not a clean single-layer witness. The community's caution
about volunteering for death (ch. 4, Quintus) is as much its voice as
the bone-collection devotion (ch. 18); both belong to any honest use.
