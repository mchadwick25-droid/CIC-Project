A collective figure, deliberately: the letter's own voice is a
community's, and attributing quotes to "Clement" as an individual would
assert an attribution the text itself never makes. The traditional name
appears only in the scholarly tag. Speaker for all 1 Clement quote
records. Strand B's founding voice: the plural, interchangeable
episkopos/presbyteros college (chs. 42, 44) and the best-attested act of
translocal corrective authority in this world's early decades.
