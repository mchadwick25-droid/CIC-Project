The figure who spans both registers of this world's record: his own
letter's modest self-naming ("Polycarp, and the presbyters with him" -
never a bishop's title, though Ignatius separately addresses him as
Smyrna's bishop: the world's own disclosed tension, held, never
resolved) and the community's commemorative martyrology (Tier 3 story
discipline - the hagiographic genre named whenever told). His ch. 13
forwarding of Ignatius's letters is one of the correspondence network's
three founding data points. The Harrison two-letter-splice question
rides the letter's dating wherever it is used.
