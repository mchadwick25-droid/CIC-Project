quick_meaning: 'The mark in every human being that makes formation possible. Not something earned, and not something
  lost. It is the ground of the soul''s capacity for God - damaged by sin, never destroyed. The whole of
  our formation exists to restore and fulfil it.'
world_meaning: 'Everything we say about the human person — the soul''s structure, the nous''s reach, the
  freedom to turn, the possibility of being transformed — rests on one conviction: we are made in the
  image of God. This is not a graceful way of saying that we are impressive among the animals, or that
  we carry rights other creatures lack. It names something more exact and more astonishing. We are constituted
  by a real likeness to God, given in our making, and that likeness is what lets a creature genuinely
  receive and share in the life of its Creator.


  The image is specifically the image of the Logos. We are not made in the image of God-in-general, as
  though God were an undifferentiated divine stuff; we are made in the image of the eternal Word, the
  one through whom all things were made and who is himself the Image of the Father. So our capacity for
  reason, for love, for wisdom, for the direct perception of divine reality — these are not extras added
  onto what we are. They are what the image of the Logos looks like in a creature. To be human is to be
  shaped toward the very reality the Logos is.


  We read the Genesis words with care: "Let us make humankind in our image (eikon), according to our likeness
  (homoiosis)." Two words, and they are not the same word. The image is what we are by creation — the
  mark that cannot be scrubbed out. The likeness is what we are becoming through formation. The image
  is given; the likeness is that same image brought to its fullness. Hold the two apart and the whole
  shape of our life makes sense; collapse them and it does not.


  When we turned away, the image was wounded, darkened, obscured — but not erased. This is the hinge of
  everything. A soul whose image had been wholly destroyed could not be formed toward God; it would have
  to be made again from nothing. But the image is damaged, not absent. The freedom with which the soul
  turned away is the same freedom with which, under grace, it can turn back; the reason that chased lesser
  goods is the same reason that can be illumined toward the highest. Formation is not the manufacture
  of something new. It is the restoring and fulfilling of what has been there, and hurt, all along. This
  is why we can speak of being brought back to our archetype — the image clarified until it reflects what
  it was always made to reflect — and why we can say the Logos entered our nature to begin, from inside,
  the healing our nature was made for but could not accomplish alone.'
distortion_risk: '**World Hearing:**

  The image is first of all the ontological ground of the soul''s capacity *for* God — the reason formation,
  illumination, gnosis, and real participation in divine life are possible rather than fictional. It says
  what we are in our deepest structure: constituted by a likeness to the Logos that makes us capable of
  genuine communion with God. The dignity is real, but it follows from this; it is not the point.'
