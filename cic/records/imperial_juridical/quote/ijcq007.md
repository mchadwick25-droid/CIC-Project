Added 2026-08-15 at Mark's explicit request ("add a quote from Letter CV to Pulcheria too"), completing
the full trio from Leo's dated dispatch of 22 May 452 rejecting Canon 28: Letter CIV to Marcian
(ijcq006), Letter CV to Pulcheria (this record), and Letter CVI to Anatolius (ijcq005) are now all
quoted.

Avoided the same header-vs-body trap caught while sourcing ijcq006: this letter's own section II and
III carry Percival/Feltoe's editorial summary headings ("The Nicene canons are unalterable and binding
universally," paragraph id="ii.iv.c-p7"; "Only by imitating his predecessor will he regain Leo's
confidence: the assent of the bishops is declared null and void," paragraph id="ii.iv.c-p10") - checked
first and confirmed to be editorial paraphrase, not Leo's translated text, the same as srcIJC47's
earlier mistake with Letter CIV. This record quotes the letter's own body (paragraph id="ii.iv.c-p11"),
verified directly.

Thematically distinct from both earlier Leo quotes: ijcq005 makes the procedural argument (a decree
contrary to Nicaea is void from the outset); ijcq006 makes the status distinction (royal rank is not
apostolic rank); this one names the SOURCE of Leo's authority to act - not a personal claim, but "the
blessed Apostle Peter's authority," the same Petrine ground the Chalcedon bishops themselves invoked
when they cried "Peter has spoken thus through Leo" (noted but not quoted at ijcq004, since that
acclamation has no figure in this world's registry to attribute it to). This quote is Leo's own
first-person version of that same claim.
