This is the healing itself, following directly from the martyrs' translation to the Ambrosian Basilica
that ijcq009 quotes: a blind, "well-known citizen of that city," on hearing the crowd's commotion,
asked to be led to the relics and touched a handkerchief to the bier, then to his own eyes - Augustine
reports his sight was restored immediately.
