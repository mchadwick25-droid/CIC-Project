This is imperial_juridical's SECOND eyewitness voice on the 386 basilica standoff, after ijcq002
(Ambrose's own sermon, preached during the standoff itself). Augustine was in Milan at the time as a
still-unconverted young man - he says so in this same passage ("We, still unmelted by the heat of Thy
Spirit") - and wrote of it roughly a decade later in the Confessions, a different kind of witness than
Ambrose's own first-person sermon or Paulinus's hagiography written a generation later still (ijcstory003).

theology or corpus, which stays Excluded from this world per srcIJC08's own Comparandum Note.
