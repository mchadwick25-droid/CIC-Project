  canals, harvest and tax seasons, boats working the river. Travelers came out from the valley to visit
  the settlements, hiring guides, carrying water, sometimes losing the track; accounts of such journeys
  count the days between settlements and name the dangers - thirst, robbers, wrong turnings. Monks went
  the other way for markets and festivals and were known figures in the villages. The two worlds faced
  each other across that line every day, each entirely visible to the other.'
