Added 2026-08-15 at Mark's explicit request ("add a quote for Palladius too"). Closes srcDES028's
"not yet wording-verified" status - the first quote to cite this row, and the first quote in this
world drawn from Palladius's own authorial voice rather than from a narrated story about someone else.

Wording transcribed directly from cic/texts/palladius_lausiac-history_clarke1918.txt (confirmed at
line 193 of the vendored plain-text file, the closing sentence of the Prologue, immediately before
Chapter I begins). Selected because it is Palladius's own explicit statement of method - he will not
sort his subjects by where they lived (city, village, or desert), only by "the fashion of their plan
of life" - which matches this world's own organizing principle (formation as a way of living, not a
location) more directly than any single narrated episode would.

No figure for Palladius existed before this record; desertfig014 was created alongside it (see that
record's attribution_note) rather than misattributing his own words to one of the ascetics whose
stories he tells.
