Licensing record added 2026-08-15 (four-reader review, scholar finding 15): the compare-view prayer answer's "Lord, help" previously circulated unattributed. The saying is Macarius' answer to the question "how should one pray?"; the attribution belongs in the spoken answer. NOTE for voicing: Agathon 9 (prayer as warfare to the last breath) was also voiced in the same answer and has no record home here - it was dropped from the rendered answer rather than licensed, since this build carries no Agathon figure.
