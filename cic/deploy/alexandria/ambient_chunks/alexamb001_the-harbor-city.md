## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. Alexandria faced the sea. Two harbors worked day and night beneath the Pharos, whose fire could be seen far out over the water; the grain of Egypt came down the Nile and left these docks in fleets for Rome, and later for Constantinople. Two great colonnaded streets crossed the city, and the quarters between them each had their own life - Greeks, Egyptians, Jews, and every trading people of the sea mixed in the markets. It was a city of size and noise and rivalry, proud of its learning and quick to riot. A person of this world walked to gatherings through streets where every language of the Mediterranean could be heard and every god of it had an altar somewhere.
