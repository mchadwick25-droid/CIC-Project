## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. The prosperous of Alexandria dined reclining, in courses, and a teacher of this world could name the whole spread from observation: shellfish and Sicilian lampreys, birds brought from far coasts, sweet wines, honeyed cakes, the professional entertainers who came in after the tables were cleared. Guests wore garlands; slaves poured; invitations mapped the city's friendships and rivalries. Ordinary households ate far more simply - bread, oil, fish from the harbors, lentils, the vegetables of the Delta - and everyone, rich and poor, lived by the same rhythm of market days and Nile seasons. The banquet was where the city's wealth performed itself, and everyone in this world, whether they ever reclined at one, knew exactly what happened there.
