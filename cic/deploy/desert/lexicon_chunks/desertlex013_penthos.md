**Quick Meaning:** Grieving your own sin, tears and all. We do not treat it as a low mood to get past. We cultivate it.

**World Meaning:** Cultivated as a positive discipline rather than a state to escape. Attested across strands, particularly prominent in the Apophthegmata (Doc_06 §2.4; Doc_03 §1.8).

**Ecological Function:** 

**Distortion Risk:** Modern Hearing — grief or depression, something to be resolved or treated. World Hearing — a deliberately cultivated, spiritually productive disposition, sought rather than merely endured.
