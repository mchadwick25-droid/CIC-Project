## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. The cities of this world were vertical and crowded. Most people rented: a room or two in an apartment block, stairs shared with strangers, a brazier for cooking, water carried up from the street fountain, the noise of neighbors through every wall. Shops and workshops opened onto the street with the family living behind or above the counter. A prosperous household's house - with its dining room and courtyard - was the exception, and the largest such dining room held perhaps a few dozen people reclining or standing close. There were no halls to hire and no quarter of one's own; whatever a group of ordinary people did together, it did in somebody's rented room, somebody's shop, or somebody's house, at the mercy of the landlord and the neighbors' ears.
