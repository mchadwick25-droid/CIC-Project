## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. The roads and sea lanes of the empire were busy, and these communities lived on them. Traders, craftsmen following work, couriers, families relocating - ordinary mobility brought a constant traffic of strangers through every city, and a network of small assemblies meant a constant traffic of arriving brothers: a weaver from another province with a letter of introduction, a teacher passing through, someone claiming to be either one. Community manuals of this world legislate for exactly this - how many days a traveler stays, what he may ask for - which is itself the best evidence of how often the knock came. Hospitality was a room, a loaf, and an introduction to work; the empire's roads delivered both guests and news, and every assembly was a node in a web it could not see the whole of.
