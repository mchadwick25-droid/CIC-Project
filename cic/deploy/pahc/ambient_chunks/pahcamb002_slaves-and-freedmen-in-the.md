## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. Slavery was the ordinary machinery of these cities. A large share of everyone a person passed in the street was enslaved or had been: household servants, workshop labor, secretaries, nurses, managers of their owners' businesses. Slaves could hold responsibility and money in practice while owning nothing in law; freedmen carried their former owner's name and often kept working in the same trade, some rising to real prosperity. Families were interwoven with it at every level - children raised by enslaved nurses, marriages spanning statuses, a household's slaves present at everything the household did. One of this world's own books was written by a man who had been sold in childhood and freed; nothing about that biography would have surprised any reader. It was how the cities worked.
