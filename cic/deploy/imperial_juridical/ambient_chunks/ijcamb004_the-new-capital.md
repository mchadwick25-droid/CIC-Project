## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. A new capital was built inside a single lifetime. Constantinople rose on the straits with everything a capital needs made to order: a senate, a hippodrome for the races, a palace, harbors, and a grain fleet from Egypt whose arrival the city watched the way farmers watch weather - the bread dole depended on it. Everything flowed toward the new center: marble, officials, petitioners, relics, ambitious men of every profession. The court's presence made the city a machine for careers; the hippodrome factions gave it its riots; the bread ships gave it its anxieties. For this world the city's rise mattered doubly, because rank follows the capital - but the city itself, the roaring, subsidized, brand-new metropolis, was simply where the empire now happened.

## Period Note

Fourth to fifth century: Constantinople as civic machine. The see's ecclesial CLAIMS are Native-Constitutive.
