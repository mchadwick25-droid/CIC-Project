## Ambient Text

Common life of the place, not the teaching of any one voice. Children of the lettered classes learned to read at home, and the methods were domestic and gentle: alphabet letters carved in boxwood or ivory given as toys, named in songs, jumbled and reassembled as a game; a child praised for right guesses and coaxed, not beaten, at that age; writing taught by guiding the small hand over letters cut into a tablet. The nurse mattered - her accent and grammar would become the child's - and households chose her accordingly. A tutor followed for grammar proper, then the established authors. All of it assumed a household with leisure, staff, and books. This world's own advice on raising a girl works entirely with these ordinary tools, redirected to a new curriculum - which is itself evidence of how standard the tools were.
