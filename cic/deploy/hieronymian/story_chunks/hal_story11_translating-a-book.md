## Story Text

This is how the work typically went - not a single remembered event, but the pattern this household's scholar followed across many books. First, he consulted a Hebrew teacher over a hard word or passage. Next came the work of translating it into Latin, checked against both the received Greek and the Hebrew itself. At the end came a preface. It defended the choices made, answered objections in advance, and dedicated the finished work to the household member whose request had prompted it.
