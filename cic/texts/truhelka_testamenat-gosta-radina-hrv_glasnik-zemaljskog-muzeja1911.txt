Content note: Gost Radin, the senior churchman and counsellor of Duke Stephen Vukčić, whom the census names as the only voice from inside the church, left a testament in Dubrovnik in 1466; Truhelka found it in the Ragusan archive (Testamenta Notariae, volume 20, last four pages) and printed it here for the first time with a long commentary. In the extract: the finding and Radin's career (from file line 26); Radin as a witness to Duke Stephen's own testament of 21 May 1466 (614); the manuscript's description (682); the large cross at the head of the text and the opening words "Va ime oca i sina i svetago duha. Amen." (818 to 820); the argument that the church's own official name is "crkva bosanska" and that no Bosnian or Ragusan document calls its members Bogomils (809); the discussion of the "krsna slava" (family patron-saint feast) (923 to 968); then "Tekst testamenta Radina gosta" (1095) with the text in Bosnian Cyrillic to its close, "писано ... у Дубровнику" (1300). The testament's wording is NOT quotable from this OCR: use the plates and the page images. Truhelka writes in 1911 as an editor with a thesis (that the church was Patarene, not Bogomil); his Croatian is scholarship, not Radin's voice. No English translation found.
