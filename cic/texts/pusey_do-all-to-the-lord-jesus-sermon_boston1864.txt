DO  ALL  TO  THE  LORD  JESUS. 

“  Whatsoever  ye  do  in  word  or  deed,  do  all  in  the  Name  of  the  Lord 
Jesus,  giving  thanks  to  God  and  the  Father  by  Him.” — Col.  iii.  17. 

All,  probably,  have  felt  at  times  a  painful  void, 
after  they  had  been  wholly  taken  up  with  some 
work  of  their  calling  or  active  duty.  The  soul  has 
seemed  to  come  to  itself,  and  found  itself  empty 
and  exhausted.  What  it  has  done,  it  has,  accord¬ 
ing  to  its  infirmity,  done  according  to  the  will  of 
God.  At  least,  it  does  not,  on  examining  itself, 
find  anything,  in  any  marked  way,  done  contrary 
to  the  law  and  will  of  God.  On  the  contrary,  it 
has  even  been  employed  in  His  service ;  it  set  out, 
perhaps,  with  some  brief  prayer  that  it  might  do  it 
as  God  willed,  or  that  God  be  thereby  glorified ;  at 
least,  at  the  outset  of  the  day,  it  commended  all  its 
works  to  God,  that  they  “  might  be  ordered  by  His 
governance  to  do  always  that  is  righteous  in  His 
sight  ”  ;  and  yet,  its  work  over,  all  which  it  has 
done  often  seems  one  great  void.  It  has  not,  per¬ 
haps,  labored  in  vain  ;  it  has  done  what  was  given 
it  to  do  ;  its  work  may  have  been  for  the  good  of
