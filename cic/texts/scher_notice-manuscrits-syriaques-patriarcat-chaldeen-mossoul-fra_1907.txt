Its value here is the colophons it summarises, which date copies and name the patriarch of the day: for example Cod. 80, finished 1550 at the monastery of Rabban Hormezd "du temps de Mar Siméon patr., et de Mar Elia, héritier du siège" (file lines 1278-1279); a Nomocanon note of 1564 at Amid "du temps de Mar Abdisô' patr." (line 1072); a note on the death of Mar Abdisho in 1570 (line 1044); and copies of 1578 at Gazarta and 1588 at Abnâyé "du temps de Mar Elia, patr." (lines 884 and 412). Which patriarchal line each named patriarch belongs to is not stated in the catalogue and is not decided here. The colophons are the nearest this library holds to the community's own record of the period; Scher writes in French as the visiting cataloguer of the Chaldean patriarchate's library.
