Content note: A second volume of papers from the Chinese Rites case, following the Historia cultus Sinensium of the same year. Title page at OCR lines 17-47; the printer's note to readers at 73-100. First piece: 'Vindiciae scriptorum Nicolai Charmot' against the Jesuits' Italian 'Dilucidationes', offered to the Holy Office on 2 September 1699, from 123 to 'Finis' at 3210; inside it a letter of the Paris Seminary at 2948 and an 'Expositio' at 3059. Second piece: 'Secundae vindiciae', offered 14 November 1699, from 3224: preface at 3251; answers to four Jesuit writings at 3502, 5089, 5625 and 6556, the first on the Chinese words Tien and Xangti; 'Finis' at 7017. A letter of the superior and directors of the Paris Seminary, dated 16 November 1699, at 7031. Index from 7186. Maigrot's Mandate of 1693 is discussed (for example at 296-300 and 4781), not printed; the main volume holds it.
