Title: R. D. Patriarchae Orientalium Assyriorum de sacro oecumenico Tridentini Concilio approbatio et professio, et Literae illustrissimi Domini Marciantonii Cardinalis Amulii ad legatos sacri Concilii Tridentini, MDLXII
Creator: Abdisho IV Maron (Abdisu), patriarch of the Assyrians of the East, d. 1578 (the profession of faith); Marcantonio Amulio, Cardinal (the covering letter to the legates at Trent)
Publisher: 1562 print (no printer named in the text; the Henry Charles Lea Library copy at the University of Pennsylvania is catalogued as printed at Riva)
Publication Date: 1562
Rights: Public Domain (published 1562; public domain by date; host carries the Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0)
Language: lat
Source: Internet Archive item bub_gb_p5REpLdbsbAC, file bub_gb_p5REpLdbsbAC_djvu.txt, fetched directly by the library/source-research thread (Era VII session, one-world agent for church-of-the-east-schism-of-1552), 2026-10-23; host rights statement: licenseurl http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/ (contributor National Central Library of Rome, Google-digitised).
Provenance note: Header prepended; the OCR text follows unchanged (no characters altered; the raw file contains 0 long-s glyphs, so no long-s normalisation was done; the OCR renders long s as f or / in many words). Rights basis restated: a 1562 print, the year read in the OCR of the title page ("MDLXII"), is public domain by date, and the host marks the item Public Domain.
Content note: The whole print, about 11,000 characters. Cardinal Amulio's letter to the legates at Trent dated Rome, August 1562 (the day is garbled in the OCR); then the profession of faith of Abdisho IV (the successor line to Yohannan Sulaqa), sworn at Rome on 7 March 1562 in the presence of Cardinals Michele (Alessandrino) and Amulio, with the later certification of 23 July 1562 and his oath to receive the decrees of the councils and of Trent. SECOND WITNESS for exact wording: the OCR is readable but noisy (long s read as f and /, some broken words), sampled at five points; verify any quote against the page images (Mark's OCR ruling, 2026-09-25). A second scan of the same print is Internet Archive item LeaBX8301545A232 (University of Pennsylvania, Public Domain Mark; OCR noisier, not vendored). This is Abdisho IV's profession, not Sulaqa's own of 1553.
