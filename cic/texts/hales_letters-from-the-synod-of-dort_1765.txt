Content note: The outside witness to the Synod: the letters of an English observer to the ambassador, datelined at Dort from November 1618 into January 1619 (the OCR datelines read "Novem.", "Decemb.", "Jan. 1619"). They report the sessions, the speeches, the Remonstrants' summons and the English delegates' dealings with the foreign divines. Hales writes as the ambassador's chaplain, not as a party to either side. The 1765 printing is a later edition of letters written in 1618-1619.
