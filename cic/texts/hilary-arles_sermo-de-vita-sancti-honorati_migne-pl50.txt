Title: Sermo de Vita Sancti Honorati Episcopi Arelatensis, cum reliquis Opusculis Genuinis (Sermon on the Life of St. Honoratus, Bishop of Arles, with the Remaining Genuine Minor Works)
Creator: Hilary, bishop of Arles (c. 401 - 449) -- Latin text, ed. J.-P. Migne
Publisher: Patrologia Latina, vol. 50 (Paris: Migne, 1846), section headed "SANCTI HILARII ARELATENSIS EPISCOPI OPUSCULA GENUINA"
Publication Date: 1846
Rights: Public Domain
Rights basis: "Public Domain Mark 1.0" per archive.org; 1846 publication, long out of US copyright.
Source: archive.org item `patrologiae_cursus_completus_lat_vol_050`, OCR text (DjVuTXT), downloaded directly 2026-09-09. Extracted from the full volume (see the companion De Laude Eremi file's own header for why the whole volume was not vendored).
Language: lat
Provenance note: this header was prepended by the build thread. Boundaries: extracted from Migne's own section heading "SANCTI HILARII ARELATENSIS EPISCOPI OPUSCULA GENUINA" (col. approx. 1249) through the last line before the next section heading, "OPUSCULA DUBIA -- SERMO DE MIRACULO S. GENESII" (col. approx. 1275) -- i.e. every work Migne himself classifies as a genuine (non-disputed) work of Hilary of Arles, not only the Vita Honorati sermon this Registry row was originally scoped to. This includes, after the Vita Honorati sermon itself, at least one further short genuine letter (addressed to a "beatissime papa," discussing Eucherius's son's own Instructiones) that this pass did not separately title or bound -- flagged for whoever next reads this file closely to give its own heading/boundary if it is cited as a distinct work. The disputed "Opuscula Dubia" material (the Sermo de Miraculo S. Genesii and whatever follows it) is deliberately NOT included -- out of scope for this Registry row, and its attribution to Hilary is itself contested by Migne's own apparatus. Not otherwise re-transcribed or corrected -- OCR quality is the same rougher two-column-interleaved quality as the companion Eucherius file, preserved as scanned.
Content note: Hilary of Arles's own funeral sermon for Honoratus, founder and second abbot of Lerins and Hilary's own predecessor as bishop of Arles -- this world's own founding narrative for Lerins, previously entirely absent from the vendored library (independently corroborated to exist by Gennadius's own text, already vendored, npnf203 ch. LXX: "his Life of Saint Honoratus, his predecessor"). Vendored for Gallic Monastic-Ascetic Christianity (era 2, c. 360-450) -- see gallic_Source_Registry.md row 27 and gallic_G1_Scope_and_Source_Acquisition_Manifest.md.
