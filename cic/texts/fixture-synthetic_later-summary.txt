A LATER SUMMARY OF TESTLAND PRACTICE
(a fixture text - Testland, synthetic; a later hand's account, not the
community's own)

A generation after the households of Testland first gathered, a summarizer
named B set down what she had been told of their practice, at second hand
and in her own words rather than theirs.

She wrote of the daily bread the households kept, of the rite of water, and
of the teaching kept for the household's own initiates and never spoken to
a wider crowd: "What is written for the initiate alone is not for the
crowd, and not for the voice to speak."

She dated the founding of the community a full season later than the
scroll's own witness does, a divergence the households themselves never
resolved.
