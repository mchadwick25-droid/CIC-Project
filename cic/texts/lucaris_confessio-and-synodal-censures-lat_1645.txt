Creator: Cyril Lucaris, Patriarch of Constantinople (Confession in Latin, printed some sixteen years before this edition per its own preface; this printing follows the Geneva edition of 1633); with the synodal censures of Cyril of Beroea (1639 per the preface) and Parthenius (1642 per the preface), both Patriarchs of Constantinople

Content note: Latin only in practice. Order in the file: the publisher's preface to the reader (which says the Confession follows the Geneva edition of 1633 by Ioannes Tornesius, with the long scriptural quotations omitted and only cited, and that the two Censures were added so that readers could see the Greek churches had rejected it, since some doubted it was Lucaris's own work); a second preface, addressed to the Christian reader, recalling the first printing of the Confession at the request of Cornelius Haga, the ambassador of the United Netherlands at the Porte; the Confession of Faith; then the two Censures.
