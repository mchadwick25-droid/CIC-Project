Creator: Issued in the name of King Christian III of Denmark and Norway (the ordinance opens with his preface); catalogued by the host under "Danske folkekirke"; printer Johannes Vinitor (Hans Vingaard)
Publisher: Copenhagen: Ex officina literaria Joannis Vinitoris Stutgardiani in novo claustrali vico Haffniae, die Luciae virginis 1537 (imprint from the colophon)
Publication Date: 1537
Rights: Public Domain (published 1537; public domain by date)
Language: lat
Source: Internet Archive item ordinatioecclesi00dans, file ordinatioecclesi00dans_djvu.txt, fetched directly by the library/source-research thread (Era VII session, world VI.27 agent), 2026-10-23; host rights statement: the item carries no rights field and no access restriction; contributor Boston Public Library (Rare Books Department copy); collections bostonpubliclibrary, americana.
Provenance note: Header prepended; the OCR text follows unchanged (no characters altered, no long-s normalisation done: the raw file holds 4,791 long-s glyphs and 959 r-rotunda glyphs, which the quote gate must normalise or the registry must map before any quote is checked). The title page and colophon in the OCR give the year 1537. Rights basis restated: a book printed at Copenhagen in 1537 is public domain by date.
Content note: The first Lutheran church ordinance of Denmark-Norway (and so of Iceland), the Latin text drawn up with Johannes Bugenhagen, whom the preface names as "Doct: Pomerano". The OCR begins with front-matter fragments from the library binding and ends with a bookseller's description and page noise; the ordinance runs between them. Gothic type: OCR is readable Latin with systematic glyph substitutions (long s, r-rotunda as ꝛ after p, c and h) and scattered noise, and a few Danish liturgical lines appear near the end. Second witness for exact wording until each quote is checked against the page image. A second scan (Royal Library Copenhagen, den-kbd-pil-130018152623-001) has a worse OCR and was not vendored.
