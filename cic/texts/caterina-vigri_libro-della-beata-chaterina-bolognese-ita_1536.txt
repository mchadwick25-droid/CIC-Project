Title: Libro della Beata Chaterina Bolognese del Ordine del Seraphico Santo Francesco, elquale essa lasciò scritto de sua propria mano (two parts in one volume; part two is Dionisio Paleotti's life of her)
Creator: Caterina Vigri of Bologna (1413-1463), Poor Clare; second part by Dionisio Paleotti
Publisher: Bologna: per li Heredi de Hieronymo de Benedetti, 1536
Publication Date: 1536
Rights: Public Domain
Rights basis: the host record gives Bologna, 1536, Heredi de Hieronymo de Benedetti; the OCR colophon reads 'Stampata in Bologna per li Heredi de' and carries no year. The author died in 1463. The year rests on the host record.
Language: ita
Source: Internet Archive item wotb_8080280 (archive.org/details/wotb_8080280), full-text file wotb_8080280_djvu.txt, fetched directly by the Library thread, October 2026.
Provenance note: Johns Hopkins (Women of the Book) scan of a sixteenth-century print. The long s is read by the OCR as 'f' throughout; it is not converted. The body is legible but noisy, with stray marks at the tail. Header prepended by the Library thread; the OCR text follows unchanged apart from this header. Nothing stripped, normalised, corrected, re-spaced or retyped.
Content note: Part one is Caterina's book, which holds the Seven Spiritual Weapons (Sette armi spirituali), with her prologue to the sisters at Ferrara, and verse. Part two is Paleotti's life of her, which is not her own voice. A second witness for exact wording because of the long-s reading. The 1926 English translation (The Spiritual Armour) was not found in the public domain; see the dossier.
