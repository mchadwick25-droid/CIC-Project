THE WITNESS SCROLL
(a fixture text - Testland, synthetic)

1. The Elder's Testimony

1.1 In the days when the households of Testland first gathered, the elder
called Vera stood before them and spoke of what she had seen.

1.2 She said: this is not a claim to have walked beside him, only a claim to
have watched what could not be silenced afterward.

1.3 The households wrote down her words, and this scroll is what remains of
that writing.

1.4 "I did not see him. I only saw what his witnesses could not stop
telling."

2. On the Water

2.1 Of the rite of water the elder taught plainly, without ornament, to any
household that asked.

3. On the Three

3.1 The households named three things worth guarding: the bread, the water,
and the word spoken aloud.

4. On Who May Enter

4.1 "We did not ask what a person had been before the water. We asked only
what they carried after it."

4.2 This was the whole of the teaching on entry, and the households kept no
fuller version of it.
