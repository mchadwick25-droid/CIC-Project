Title: Terza raccolta di lettere inedite del glorioso S. Carlo Borromeo, arcivescovo di Milano, in cui a' suoi luoghi sono citate le lettere gia stampate, e si da in fine un indice cronologico degli argomenti di tutte le lettere contenute si nelle prime due, che nella presente raccolta
Creator: Carlo Borromeo (1538-1584); edited and dedicated by the printer Giovanni Battista Agnelli to Francesco d'Almada e Mendonca, formerly Portuguese minister plenipotentiary at Rome (dedication dated Lugano, 4 November)
Publisher: Per gli Agnelli, Lugano
Publication Date: 1763
Rights: Public Domain (published 1763; public domain by date)
Language: ita
Source: Internet Archive item bub_gb_HtDZzDNx5nIC, file bub_gb_HtDZzDNx5nIC_djvu.txt, fetched directly by the library/source-research thread (Era VII session), 2026-10-16; host rights statement: licenseurl http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/ (Public Domain Mark), no possible-copyright-status field, access-restricted-item not set; contributor National Central Library of Rome (Google Books scan). A second scan of the same edition is on archive.org as bub_gb_hW2jkztc0pgC.
Provenance note: Header prepended; the OCR text follows unchanged (long s count 0, so no long-s normalisation; nothing retyped, nothing dropped, nothing corrected). Printed at Lugano in 1763, so public domain by date.
Content note: The third collection of Borromeo's letters on the Milan seminary and the Jesuits, 1567-1584, with letters to and from his agent Cesare Speciano and others, the printer's dedication and address to the reader (which quote Fleury on publishing uncomfortable facts), and at the end an "INDICE UNIVERSALE" listing every letter in all three collections in date order with a one-line summary. The dedication states openly that the collection is offered as evidence against the Society of Jesus; it is a purposeful selection of 1762-63, not Borromeo's whole correspondence. OCR sampled at 10/30/50/70/90 % and found legible, with the long s read as f throughout and some broken words.
