Title: Manga Panalanging Pagtatagobilin sa Caloloua nang tauong naghihingalo (the Recomendacion del alma of Tomas de Villacastin, S.J., translated into Tagalog by Gaspar Aquino de Belen), at ysinonod dito ang Mahal na Passion ni Iesu Christong Panginoon natin na tola (and following it the Passion of Jesus Christ our Lord in verse)
Creator: Gaspar Aquino de Belen (translator of Villacastin's prayers for the dying, and author of the Tagalog verse Passion that follows them); Tomas de Villacastin, S.J. (1570-1649), author of the Spanish Recomendacion del alma
Publisher: Manila: Imprenta de la Compania de Jesus, por D. Nicolas de la Cruz Bagay
Publication Date: 1760 (this printing, which its title page calls the fifth edition; the host catalogue records a 1703 edition containing Villacastin's prayers without the poem, and no record of a second, third or fourth edition)
Rights: Public Domain (published 1760, public domain by date; the host item, a John Carter Brown Library scan, carries no rights field and is not access-restricted)
Language: tgl (the prayers and the Passion in Tagalog; the licences and approbations at the front in Spanish)
Source: Internet Archive item magapanalagingpa00vill (John Carter Brown Library), file magapanalagingpa00vill_djvu.txt, fetched directly by the library/source-research thread (Era VII session), 2026-10-16; host rights statement: none given on the item (no possible-copyright-status field); the basis is the 1760 imprint.
Provenance note: Header prepended; the OCR text follows unchanged (no normalisation of any kind). Rights basis restated: the Manila 1760 printing is more than 95 years old and is public domain by date.
Content note: Second witness under the Library's OCR ruling (2026-09-25), not quotable from this file alone: the OCR of this 1760 Manila print reads the long s as f throughout the Tagalog ("fa" for "sa", "Pafsion" for "Passion"), drops and garbles many letters, and carries marginal noise on most pages; the title page and the Spanish approbation (which describes the book as Villacastin's Recomendacion del alma put into Tagalog by Aquino de Belen, printer of books, with the Passion of Our Lord in Tagalog verse inserted by the translator) are readable with effort. Order of contents: title page, licences and approbations (Spanish), the prayers for the dying, the Litany of the Saints, then the verse Passion (the first "Pafsiong" of the poem falls a little under half way through the file) running to "LAVS DEO" at the end. The poem is the earliest surviving Tagalog pasyon; its first printing (1704) is after this world's window, so for the 1565-1650 entry it is a successor text, and it is placed to the Era-8 Philippine entry as that entry's own voice.
