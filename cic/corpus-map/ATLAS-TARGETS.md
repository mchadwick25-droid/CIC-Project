# Atlas census - every assignment target

Generated by `cic/engine/atlas_targets.py` from `cic-website/data/world-census.json`. **Assign to the `id` column** - it is also the filename of the corpus-map bucket, which is the whole alignment mechanism.

All 274 entries are valid targets. That includes the 215 marked *Pre-Survey Candidate* (Mark, 2026-08-26: material plainly belonging to one of them belongs there rather than held back - placing a source is not a claim that the era's survey has run), and the four marked ⛔ *Excluded - Doctrinal Floor*, which will never be built as worlds but still need a home for the heresiological material the corpus carries about them. A built world may draw on those only as `register: etic` - described from outside, never as its own voice.

The six *Built & Live* entries are the six worlds that exist today. There is one taxonomy here, not two.
