Article-by-article — read and flag

Article I — Name

The name of the corporation is Church in Conversation.

Article II — Duration and Type

Nonprofit corporation under the Colorado Revised Nonprofit Corporation Act, perpetual duration, no members — governance vested in the Board of Directors.

Article III — Purpose

Organized exclusively for charitable, religious, and educational purposes under 501(c)(3). Specific purpose: helping people experience Jesus in new ways through the living traditions and practices of His Church, throughout history and today — including reconstructing historical Christian communities with scholarly rigor, making them available through honest conversational encounter, supporting religious/historical education, and related research and publication.

Article IV — Foundational Commitments (Mission Lock) ⚠️ already flagged for counsel

Six binding commitments: (1) Access — core encounter always free to individual seekers. (2) Witness, not recruitment — no persuasion design, no conversion-tracking. (3) Transparency never gated — sources/confidence/limitations open at every tier. (4) No engagement optimization — never designed to maximize duration/return/dependency. (5) Independent accountability — subject to external scholarly review, honestly disclosed. (6) Money buys access, never voice — no gift alters content; Board maintains a gift-acceptance policy. Amendment protection: Article IV/VIII changeable only by 4/5 vote of all directors including a majority of independent directors.

Article V — Limitations (IRS-Required Language)

No private inurement except reasonable compensation; no substantial lobbying; no political campaign intervention; activities bounded by 501(c)(3) exemption and 170(c)(2) deductibility requirements.

Article VI — Directors

No fewer than 5 directors; majority must be independent (not family of, nor in a compensated/business relationship with, the founder or any officer). Initial Board seated by the incorporator's organizational action after incorporation.
