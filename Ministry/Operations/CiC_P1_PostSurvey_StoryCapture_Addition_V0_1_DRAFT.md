# P1 Post-Survey Addition — Story-Capture Consent (DRAFT V0.1)

**Date:** 2026-07-16 · **Status:** DRAFT for Mark's review — proposed addition to the
Pilot Plan's Piece 4 post-survey (Section 8.3), per the Telling the Story plan's
recommendation (2026-07-15): capture 2–3 real tester quotes and one real story
*during* P1, with consent built into the survey — don't wait until after.

**Why now:** these quotes/stories feed both the public story (landing page quote slot,
Letter to Friends refresh) and Proposal Packet V0.2's asset list. Consent captured in
the survey itself means nothing has to be re-requested later, and nothing is ever used
without permission. This is additive only — questions 1–8 stay exactly as written.

> **9.** Was there a moment in the conversation that stayed with you — something the
> voice said, or something you felt or realized while talking with it? In a sentence
> or two, what happened?
>
> **10.** May we quote your answers here (or the moment you just described) when we
> tell this project's story — on its website, or in updates to friends and partners
> of the project?
> *( yes, with my first name · yes, anonymously · no )*
> Nothing is ever quoted without this permission. Answering "no" changes nothing
> about your access or how welcome your honest feedback is.
