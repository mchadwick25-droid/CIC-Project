## 1. Opinionated presence — qualified positive

Casual 8-turn conversations: all six worlds take real stances, no
neutral-surveying. Under real pushback (the sustained-disagreement
battery), 3 of 6 worlds (Desert, Imperial Juridical, Hieronymian)
conceded ground their own record holds; the other 3 were uncertain, not
clean holds either.

**Mark's verdict:** "do a really good job of holding the belief... yes,
there are subtleties and breakdowns, but it is better than it could be."

## 2. Uptake of the participant's actual words — strong, with one flagged failure mode

Consistent pattern of mirroring the participant's own image/phrasing back
(Papnoute/"boy," Marius/"traveler," Albina/"surface") wherever checked —
not systematically verified across all 96 turns.

**Real finding, Mark's own catch:** Albina's answer to "what would people
today get most wrong about you" (and the same question-type answered by
Papnoute and Marius) speaks with confident, direct knowledge of what
"modern people" think — which requires an anachronistic awareness of
"today" the Representative shouldn't have. Better shape: answer from
uncertainty about *any* outside era looking in ("I don't know what people
from another age would make of us, but if they took it as X...") rather
than characterizing "today" as a known audience. **Logged as a fleet-wide
pattern for Phase 1A** — this question type recurs across worlds, not
just Albina's instance.

**Mark's verdict:** mostly present, with this one known failure mode.

## 3. Candidate-offer when ambiguous — not sure / no clean test case

No genuinely ambiguous participant questions in the 8-turn probes (they're
all direct); zero hits for the literal "I'll take that to mean X" pattern
anywhere. The related mechanism (`restricted_offer` repair events) does
fire under pushback in the sustained-disagreement battery — a bounded,
qualified answer instead of a flat yes/no — and holds in 5 of 6 worlds,
but slides into an actual concession in the 6th (Hieronymian, matching the
sustained-disagreement battery's own already-known finding). This is a
related mechanism, not a direct test of what the category actually asks.

**Mark's verdict:** not sure — fair, since there's no real test case for
it in the current baseline data.

## 4. Honest edge-speech — solid

Two halves: (a) breaking-frame failure mode ("historians say," "scholars
debate") — zero instances, clean fleet-wide. (b) The positive version
(naming the record's own limit, in-voice) — clearly present and frequent
in Syriac and Hieronymian; not observed in the PAHC or Imperial Juridical
8-turn probes, which may mean it's thinner there or may just mean those
particular 8 questions never hit the edge of the record. Couldn't
distinguish which from what's on hand.

**Mark's verdict:** "this is good, let's let pilot readers find things if
they are there, this seems to be solid." — read as the signal to stop
digging further on this category and the two not yet covered.

## 5. World-particular imagery / 6. Length restraint — not covered
