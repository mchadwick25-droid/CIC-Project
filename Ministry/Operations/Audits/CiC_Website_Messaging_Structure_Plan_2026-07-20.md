# Website Messaging & Structure — Working Plan

**Status: DRAFT, awaiting Mark's markup.** Also published as an artifact for full-page review: see the System Hub Decision Log's 2026-07-20 entry for the link.

**What this is:** the plan Mark asked for — a look through every page as it stands today, what's built from negative or contrast framing that shouldn't be, and a minimal, positive-only structure to bring it up to: welcome, vision, why, what, how, with the Table at the center.

**What this isn't:** the deeper messaging pass. That's still the step-by-step process, section by section, together — Mark's own words. Nothing below is live; no website file has been touched as part of this plan.

---

## One thing, before anything else

The Messaging & Branding Kit already has a strong rule against most of this — heavy "not X, it's Y" contrast is named directly as "a dead AI giveaway" (Kit §1.1, 5c). But the Kit also explicitly *protects* a few negative-shaped lines as intentional, calling them clarifying contrast rather than denial: *"It will never try to convert you"* and *"A doorway, not a home."* Mark's direction today reads stricter than that carve-out. Below, both are left alone and flagged rather than quietly decided either way — needs his call on whether they go too.

## Part 1 — The audit: what's on the site today

Every instance of negative framing, "not X, it's Y" contrast, or a retired catch-all word (the Kit already retired "honest/honestly" as a tell), found by reading each live page plus the one drafted-but-unpublished landing page copy.

**Home (`index.html`)**
- *"showing their own confidence... speaking honestly"* — **retired word.** "Honestly" was retired as the catch-all adverb; the Kit maps it to "openly" in conversational contexts.
- *"built to point you back toward real community, not toward itself"* — **negative tail.** The sentence already said what it does; the "not toward itself" adds a denial nobody asked for.
- *"The First Centuries is where we're starting, not where we're stopping."* — **not-X-it's-Y contrast.** Repeats verbatim on the Atlas page.
- *"No product like this exists anywhere else we could find"* — **worth a call, not an automatic strike.** This exact phrase was previously decided as the Kit's approved alternative to "only/first" overclaims. Under today's stricter rule it may still need to go — flagging rather than assuming.

**About (`about.html`)**
- *"No single movement exhausts the richness of Christ"* — appears twice (mission statement, Conviction 1). **Negative-shaped, and repeated.**
- *"allowed to speak honestly in its own voice"* — **retired word.**
- *"without modernizing, harmonizing, defending, or critiquing it into someone else's argument"* — **stacked negative list.** Four things it doesn't do, none of what it does.
- *"governed by a written charter that forbids designing for persuasion, conversion-tracking, or engagement-maximization"* — **prohibition-framed.** Real, necessary information, written as a list of bans.
- *"They are not derived from the technology — the technology is derived from them."* — **textbook not-X-it's-Y**, the exact shape the Kit's own §5c names as the tell.

**Atlas (`atlas.html`)**
- *"every era honestly labeled"* · *"not a summary of it"* · *"is where we're starting, not where we're stopping"* — **retired word, negative tail, and the Home contrast pattern**, three instances in one short hero.

**Support (`support.html`)**
- *"aren't tax-deductible yet on their own"* · *"That external review hasn't happened yet."* — **necessary disclosure, wrong order.** This is the page needing structural work, not line edits — see Part 4.

**Landing page copy (drafted, never published — `Ministry/Communication/CiC_Landing_Page_Copy_V0_1_DRAFT.md`)**
- *"Not a chatbot answering questions, and not a real person impersonated, but a carefully built representative voice"* — **triple negation before the actual sentence.**
- The whole "What It Refuses To Do" section, three refusals each written as "It never…" — **the architecture itself is negative**, not just the wording. Worth rebuilding the section's shape, not just its lines. (Same protected-line exception noted above applies to one of the three.)
- *"No fundraising asks, no spam"* — **negative list** where a plain positive statement would do.

## Part 2 — The proposed shape: five beats, the Table at the center

Welcome, vision, why, what, how — in that order, minimal at each step. The five existing pages already map onto this almost exactly; nothing needs inventing, mostly trimming and reordering. Every draft line below leans on language already decided in the Brand Kit rather than inventing new phrasing.

| # | Page | Beat | Job | Draft line (short, for markup) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | **Welcome** | Arrival at the Table; the hook, one door in. | "Twenty centuries of the church. One table. A chair pulled out for you. Come and join us." |
| 2 | About, opening | **Vision** | Why this exists at all. | "The whole church, across every century, holds more of Christ than any single doorway ever could. Church in Conversation gathers many of its movements at one table, in their own words." |
| 3 | About, continued | **Why** | Why this approach — conversation, not reading. | "You don't just read about these communities — you sit with them. Ask a question, and a voice built from that community's own letters and records answers, in its own words." |
| 4 | Atlas + About "how it works" | **What** | What it actually is — the features. | "Five communities are live today, each rebuilt from its own sources. Seat more than one at a time, and hear them answer the same question, each in its own voice." |
| 5 | Support + Pilot | **How** | How it's built, and how to join — the program. | "Each community passes through months of scholarship before anyone meets it — sources cataloged, claims tested, confidence shown at every answer. Join the pilot, or follow along as it grows." |

**The Table as centerpiece — not a sixth section, a rule for all five.** The hook already carries it: "one table, a chair pulled out for you." Worth carrying the same image (or the Arriving mark's own seat-at-threshold motion) visually through Welcome, What, and How, so a visitor is never more than a scroll from the same picture — the Table — rather than meeting five different metaphors on five different pages.

## Part 3 — Ready-to-use rewrites (the reframe key)

The worst offenders from the audit, each paired with a plain, positive alternative — concrete enough to drop in once the deeper pass reaches that section.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "…built to point you back toward real community, not toward itself." | "…built to point you back toward real, living community." |
| "The First Centuries is where we're starting, not where we're stopping." | "The First Centuries is where we're starting — later chapters of the Church's story are already planned." |
| "No single movement exhausts the richness of Christ — together they form a vast testimony." | "Together, Christ's many movements across history form one vast testimony to His ongoing work." |
| "…without modernizing, harmonizing, defending, or critiquing it into someone else's argument." | "…speaking plainly, in its own voice, on its own terms." |
| "They are not derived from the technology — the technology is derived from them." | "These convictions came first. The technology exists to serve them." |
| "Not a chatbot answering questions, and not a real person impersonated, but a carefully built representative voice…" | "A carefully built representative voice, anchored to that community's own real record." |
| "It won't smooth over the hard parts." | "Where the record holds real tension, it stays open — the argument, not a tidy answer." |
| "No fundraising asks, no spam." | "Just an occasional honest update on how the work is going." |

## Part 4 — The one page that needs more than lines

**Support isn't a wording problem.** The nonprofit status and tax-deductibility content is real disclosure, not marketing copy — it can't just disappear. What can change is the order: lead with what a visitor can actually do today (give directly, or give through the partner-church arrangement for an immediate deduction), and let the federal-determination footnote follow, instead of opening on what isn't finished yet. Same information, welcome first.
