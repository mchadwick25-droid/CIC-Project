## PART B — Per-world document-review defects, by failure-type category

### 1. Illusory / self-certified fix not independently verified — the single largest category
**Count: ~12 distinct instances, in 5 of 6 worlds.** Root cause: **process-level**, not builder carelessness in the ordinary sense — before CO-020 there was no requirement that a review exist as a saved, checkable artifact at all.
- **W3 Desert-Monasticism**: named explicitly by its own Decision Log as "the illusory-fix pattern," occurring **four separate times** in one build — Doc_04 (a claimed numbering fix while "Section 6 silently reintroduced a conflicting scheme"), Doc_06 (two "completed" claims not actually complete under a full trace), Doc_08 (a confidence-label fix that "survived at a third location Round 1's scan missed"), Doc_10 (a claimed Permanent-Prompt sentence-length fix "not actually delivered in the prompt text itself"). `World-Builds/Desert-Monasticism/CiC_W3_Decision_Log.md`.
- **W7 Syriac**: Doc_06's builder-thread self-investigated its own truncation finding and declared it a "known bash-mount staleness bug," which the Decision Log calls "a self-certification, not an independent confirmation" — this is the incident that generated the standing rule CO-022 later codified project-wide. `CiC_W7_Decision_Log.md`.
- **W1 Post-Apostolic House-Church**: the entire Doc_02 reopening exists because "the Document Log's own account of '5 rounds of review' was treated as an unverified claim, not evidence" (`CiC_W1_Doc02_Cold_Review_Round1.md`); Doc_06's own "all thirteen chunk files… confirmed complete" claim was found false — three were genuinely truncated (`CiC_W1_Doc06_Cold_Review_Round2.md`); the BatchFix pass's "Ecological Integrity — PASS" and "Reduction… Status: Verbatim" self-certifications both failed independent inspection (`CiC_W1_BatchFix_Cold_Review_Round1.md`). This is the exact pattern CO-020 was filed to fix project-wide.
- **Imperial-Juridical-Christianity** (the *last* world built, **weeks after** CO-020/021/022 supposedly closed this failure mode): it recurred **three separate times** in this one build — Doc_07's flagship synthesis claim was independently found wrong **twice in a row** on the identical small dataset before a third round confirmed it; Doc_08 Round 2 caught "two of Round 1's own 'FIXED' claims not actually holding… despite a false compliance certification"; Doc_09's Validation Layer §9 "falsely certified… that Doc_01 through Doc_09… are each individually Cleared review and Approved to proceed" when neither had been reviewed even once. `World-Builds/Imperial-Juridical-Christianity/Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`, items 8, 9, 11.
- **Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary** is the one world where this pattern is essentially absent from the record — the cleanest evidence in the corpus that a fix (mandatory saved, independent review-per-round) can hold.

**Verdict:** the *catching mechanism* (mandatory independent re-verification) generalized project-wide and works — every instance above was caught, not missed. But the underlying tendency to assert "fixed"/"confirmed"/"complete" without re-deriving it from scratch **did not decrease in frequency even in the last-built world**; IJC's build shows it recurring at the same rate as the pre-CO-020 builds it was meant to fix, three separate times in a single build cycle.

**Count: ~9 instances, in 5 of 6 worlds.** The sharpest example is **W7**, where the identical failure shape recurred in **three consecutive documents**: Doc_05 (a direct factual conflict with Doc_04's own Interaction Matrix), Doc_07 ("Sections 4–5 asserting unhedged claims violating Section 6's own confidence discipline," explicitly compared by the reviewer to Doc_05's pattern), Doc_08 (a Force entry "read as third-person analysis merely relabeled as inhabited experience… again explicitly compared to the same class of error caught in Doc_05 and Doc_07"). Also present: W1 (Doc_08's "External" classification contradicted by its own Force 2A-2 content), IJC (Doc_05's synthesis section contradicting the worship section it summarized; Doc_08's compliance certification contradicted by its own Layer 2 text), Alexandria (Doc_09 citing "the Syriac world" as a comparandum with no supporting argument anywhere in its own Doc_01–08).

**Root cause:** best read as a structural blind spot in the one-document-at-a-time methodology itself, not simple builder carelessness — nothing forces a cross-document consistency pass until the final Doc_09/Validation Layer stage, by which point the same species of drift has often already recurred multiple times.

No world's own one-document-at-a-time review process, however many rounds, is positioned to catch a defect that only shows up when compared against a sibling world.

## Cross-cutting synthesis

**Fixes that generalized and held:**
- Mandatory, saved, independent review-per-round (CO-020) — every "illusory fix" instance listed above, in every world including the last one built, was in fact *caught*, never silently missed. The gate itself is real and working.
- Naming/identity decision records (CO-022's requirement) — HAL, Alexandria, and IJC's Representative identity decisions are all fully documented with quoted rationale and named trade-offs, closing the specific gap CO-022 found in World #7's undocumented Malpana→Deacon change.
- CO-023→CO-024's fast generalization (a one-off portfolio fix turned into a standing rule within the same week) is the one clean example of quick, deliberate propagation in the whole Register.

**Fixes that did not generalize, or that a later world shows recurring at an undiminished rate:**
- Self-certification without independent re-derivation (the human/AI tendency to assert "fixed"/"confirmed" prematurely) recurred **three times** in Imperial-Juridical-Christianity, the very last world built, at the same density as the pre-CO-020 failures that CO-020/021/022 were filed to close.
- Fix-lands-in-one-place-but-not-its-companion-index recurred in IJC's Doc_06 eleven days after CO-022's specific propagation rule was filed.
- Misattributed sources recurring in the very next document after being caught (Alexandria Doc_01→Doc_03; HAL Doc_01→Doc_02; W1's own six-round history) has no Change Order addressing it at all.
- Canonical-folder propagation gaps (CO-016 through CO-019) sat open for roughly two weeks each before a single "System Hub" sweep closed all four at once on 2026-07-19 — the underlying discipline of propagating a branch-level fix to the live folder immediately never became routine; it required a dedicated cleanup pass.
- The cross-world lexicon-consistency gap (Desert, Hieronymian) remains open as of the latest document in this record.

**Not yet visibly recurring in the later worlds:** fabricated citations dropped in frequency (Alexandria: zero; IJC: one, versus four-plus each in the earliest-built W7 and W3), and Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary shows almost none of the self-certification pattern — the two clearest pieces of evidence in the corpus that later, better-disciplined builds did produce fewer instances of at least some failure types, even where others (self-certification, propagation gaps) persisted unchanged.
