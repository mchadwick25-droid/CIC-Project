## Finding, by process step

### Step 0 — Movement Scope (`CiC_L3B_Step0_Movement_Scope_Methodology_V1.0.docx`)
- **Rule stated clearly?** Yes, unusually precisely — Section A gives a hard floor test with named exceptions (A1–A5), Section B gives five scored criteria with citations to source documents for each.
- **Verification specified?** Partial. A4 (hand-selected adjacent inclusion) has a real mechanical gate: "no more than one of the five Article 4 commitments waived," logged in the Change Orders Register, and "flagged for external review if hand-selected inclusions exceed five total or 10%." That's a genuine numeric audit trigger. But Section B scoring itself has no equivalent — "score each candidate... recording citation-grounded reasoning" has no check that the reasoning was actually citation-grounded before a seed advances.
- **Consistent across versions?** Only one version exists (V1.0); no drift to check.
- **Silence found?** None significant — this is the most tightly specified document in the set.

### Doc_01 — World Identification
- No dedicated L3B/L4 template exists for Doc_01 itself. It's governed by Construction Framework Part I (`CiC_L3B_Formation_World_Construction_Framework_V7.3.docx` / V7.4 draft) and Blueprint Sections 1–2, both phrased as open questions ("What beginning point is appropriate?"), not required fields.
- **Verification specified?** No completion checklist for Doc_01 anywhere. Strand Determination is the one sub-item with a hard requirement ("Record the finding explicitly: strand-singular, or strands identified with named evidence" — Construction Framework Part I), but nothing checks that this recording actually happened before Step 2 begins.

### Doc_02 — Source Ecology & Author Gravity
- **Rule stated clearly?** Yes, extensively — Author Gravity Assessment (visibility, representativeness, influence, limitations, plus Transmission History added as a fifth dimension in "v1.6"), Source Asymmetry, Missing Voices/Affirmative Duty (Constitution Article 20).
- **Verification specified?** This is the one area with a genuine, explicit mechanical gate, and it shows real iteration: `Doc_02B_Approved_Source_Database_Template_V2.md` and the superseded `Cross_World_Source_Registry.md` (both marked "**SUPERSEDED — 2026-07-04**" at the top, redirecting to `Source_Registry_Template.md`) built a hard "Checkpoint, not just intent" rule: *"Doc_02 may not name a source in support of a specific claim unless that source has a corresponding Registry row. If a claim in Doc_02 cannot be traced to a Registry entry, this section is not finished, regardless of how complete the narrative reads."* `Source_Registry_Template.md` carries this forward as the live design. Construction Framework V7.4 draft goes further, adding a Freeze Criterion: *"Source Registry complete... and the deployed Permanent Prompt's grounding-anchor paragraph verified to be drawn from it."* This is real, checkable, gate language — a clear positive counter-example to the lexicon-chunk gap.
- **Consistent across versions?** V7.3 Construction Framework has Source Ecology and Source Registry as separate/sequenced; V7.4 draft explicitly restructures this ("a briefly-added separate 'Step 2B' was merged back into Step 2") and documents why. The superseded files are marked, not silently left live — an example of the project's own Record Integrity Principle being followed here.
- **Silence found?** None major — this is the best-governed step in the pipeline, likely *because* a real defect (the Theon/Gregory-of-Nyssa borrowing, described in `Doc_02B...V2.md` and `Source_Registry_Template.md`) forced a mechanical fix.

### Doc_03 — Lexicon Candidate List
- Governed by `CiC_L3B_Interpretive_Lexicon_Development_Framework_V2.1.docx` Part I. Rule is stated ("Candidates should be drawn from... recurring terminology... vocabulary within Historical Gravities..."), with a soft completion heuristic ("complete enough to proceed... when continued search produces substantially diminishing returns"). No checklist, no verification step — this is explicitly a "working reference document, not a formal scholarly output" per the Framework's own text, so the absence of a gate may be intentional rather than an oversight.

### Doc_04 — Gravity Discovery (`Doc_04_Gravity_Discovery_Template_V1.0.md`)
- This document is uniquely self-aware about exactly the pattern Mark is asking about. Its own preamble states: *"This is the first L4 template Doc_04 has ever had — every world built before this one... produced its own Doc_04 without one, each inventing its own structure independently,"* and reports that a coach-thread review of four completed worlds' Doc_04s found "Section counts... range from 6 to 9. No two worlds use the same section names... Only one of four worlds gives the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check and the Forces test their own dedicated sections... with no way to confirm from the table of contents alone that every candidate actually received both checks."
- **Verification specified?** V1.0 responds by adding one concrete, structural fix: a *required index table* (Section 4, Classification Summary) with columns for Six-Test verdict, Cross-Check result, Cross-Strand status, and Classification — "the actual fix for the 'hard to verify' problem... not by forcing every world into one prose layout, but by guaranteeing one place a coach or downstream builder can go to confirm... without reading the full document." This is a template retroactively adding a verification mechanism after the fact — i.e., direct evidence that this project's pattern is *reactive* fixes (built only after a defect is found across multiple worlds), not prospective ones.
- **Consistent across versions?** N/A — V1.0 is the first version, built explicitly to reconcile four independently-invented structures.
- **Silence found?** The template explicitly says it "has not yet been tested against a live build," so whether the index table actually gets filled in correctly, or is itself audited, is still unverified as of this document.

### Doc_05 — Ecological Reconstruction
- No dedicated L4 template exists. Governed by Construction Framework Part III/IV and Template V1.5's dimension lists (Formation Ecology, Meaning Transmission, Emotional/Philosophical/Authority/Boundary lenses, etc.). All phrased as exploration prompts ("Questions may include..."), explicitly non-checklist by the Template's own "Template Flexibility Principle." No completion gate.

### Doc_06 — Full Interpretive Lexicon + Deployment Lexicon Chunks — **the core finding**
Two documents govern this: `CiC_L3B_Interpretive_Lexicon_Development_Framework_V2.1.docx` (construction-time rules) and `L4-Templates/Deployment_Lexicon_Chunk_Template.md` (the per-chunk deployment template — the one Mark found 40–56% non-compliant).

- **Rule stated clearly?** Yes. The Framework states the "Confidence Inheritance Principle" (lexicon claims "should be calibrated using the constitutional five-level confidence vocabulary... the same as any other claim about the formation world, Constitution Article 17"). The chunk template's Key Sources section is marked **"Include for Tier 1 and Tier 2. Omit for Tier 3"** — an unconditional structural requirement, not optional — and instructs: *"If a source carries Author Gravity risk — if it dominates the evidence for this term — note it: 'Note: [source] dominates the surviving evidence for this term's usage; see Author Gravity Assessment in Doc_01.'"* The two governing documents are internally consistent with each other on required front-matter fields and content sections (Framework Part VI's chunk-structure list matches the template file field-for-field) — no drift there.
- **Verification specified? No.** The chunk template's own "Final Assembly Instruction" is a 6-item checklist: replace brackets; remove builder notes; check World Meaning for analytical-distance markers; confirm CT Contest Type completed where CT tag applies; confirm Reported-Experience Status marker text present where applicable; save with correct filename. **Nothing in that list checks that Key Sources is populated for Tier 1/2 entries, that confidence-vocabulary language actually appears, or that an Author Gravity disclosure note appears where a source dominates.** The Framework's Part IV workflow lists "Confidence Review" as a step ("checking each entry's claims against the... confidence vocabulary") but specifies no mechanism — no audit table, no sampling requirement, no sign-off gate, no equivalent of Doc_04's index table.
- **Structural gap vs. its sibling documents:** Doc_07 (`Integrated_Ecology_Analysis_Template.md`) has a ten-item "Doc_07 Completion Certification" with COMPLETE/INCOMPLETE status. Doc_08 (`[world-code]_Forces_Document.md`) has a twelve-item "Doc_08 Completion Certification." `World_Profile_Template.md` has an eleven-item "World Profile Completion Status." **No equivalent aggregate completion-certification document exists for the lexicon as a whole** — the per-chunk template's 6-item list is the only self-check, and it applies to one chunk being freshly authored, not to auditing a completed set of chunks against the template's own rules before the world is called done.
- **Freeze Criteria don't catch it either.** Construction Framework Part VI Freeze Criteria (both V7.3 and V7.4 draft) list: ecologies reconstructed, confidence levels visible, Forces Document complete, Story Inventory complete, Living Tradition Status confirmed, External Scholarly Review complete, and (V7.4 addition) Source Registry complete + grounding-anchor paragraph verified. **The Deployment Lexicon does not appear in Freeze Criteria at all.** It is instead gated separately, at deployment, by Blueprint Section 18 ("Deployment Package Readiness"), which only checks *structural* completeness — "Each chunk must carry retrieval front-matter and follow the naming convention" — not the deeper content-quality rules the Lexicon Development Framework itself states.
- **Silence found — the length/depth question directly.** Compare stated length targets across L4 templates: World Capsule Core "3,000–5,000 tokens," World Context Layer Chunk "300–600 tokens," Doc_07 "3,000–6,000 words," World Profile "2,000–4,000 words," Representative Permanent Prompt "1,500–3,000 tokens." **`Deployment_Lexicon_Chunk_Template.md` states no length target at all** — no word count, no token budget, for the chunk overall or even for World Meaning specifically. The only depth guidance is qualitative and tier-relative: Quick Meaning is "[ONE SENTENCE]"; World Meaning is "several paragraphs" for Tier 1, "one to two paragraphs" for Tier 2, and "may be brief" for Tier 3. `Story_Repository_Chunk_Template.md` has the identical silence pattern ("Length should reflect what the story requires... Do not abbreviate... do not extend it beyond what the ecology supports" — no numeric anchor at all). This is a genuine, confirmed silence on exactly the question Mark raised: nothing in the governing documents specifies a target that would keep one world's chunks from averaging 217 words while another's average 1,200 — both would satisfy every stated rule.

### Doc_07 — Integrated Ecology Analysis (`Integrated_Ecology_Analysis_Template.md`)
- **Rule stated + verified.** This is one of the best-gated documents in the set. Section 1 requires an input-completion table (Doc_01–Doc_06 checklist with a "Gate confirmation: YES/NO" and an explicit instruction to stop if NO). Section 7 is a ten-item Completion Certification. Final Assembly Instruction has seven specific, checkable steps (e.g., "Confirm every lens draws on specific named evidence... A lens entry that could have been written without consulting the construction documents is not an integrated analysis"). Consistent with Construction Framework Step 7 text in both versions.

### Doc_08 — Forces Document (`[world-code]_Forces_Document.md`)
- **Rule stated + verified**, similarly strong. Twelve-item Doc_08 Completion Certification (Section 9), explicit per-cell/per-layer requirements, five "Governing Principles Applied" each with its own CONFIRMED/NOTE EXCEPTIONS status field. Construction Framework Part VI Freeze Criteria requires "Forces Document complete (Doc_08 — required)" as a hard gate, consistently stated in both V7.3 and V7.4.

### Doc_09a/b/c — Story Inventory, World Profile, Validation Documentation
- **Story chunks** (`Story_Repository_Chunk_Template.md`): same per-chunk-only pattern as lexicon chunks — a 6-item Final Assembly Instruction for one chunk, no world-level "Story Repository Completion Certification" aggregating compliance across all chunks. The Tier 4 sourcing rule is strict and clearly stated ("every element... must have a source identified... If any element cannot be sourced, it must be removed") but verification again rests entirely on the individual chunk's own self-check, not an audit gate.
- **World Profile** (`World_Profile_Template.md`): strongly gated (eleven-item completion checklist, explicit "usability test" for Formation Logic, verbatim-copy requirement for the Integrative Observation with a Verbatim/Modified status field).
- **Naming/scope drift worth flagging:** Construction Framework Step 9 text says "Output: Doc_09 (Story Inventory) and the World Profile" (two things), but the real file-naming convention observed in built worlds (`CiC_W3_Doc09a_Story_Inventory.md`, `Doc09b_World_Profile.md`, `Doc09c_Validation_Documentation.md`) treats Doc_09 as three sub-parts. Neither Blueprint nor Construction Framework documents the a/b/c split explicitly — it exists only as convention in the actual world-build folders, not in the governing text.
- **"Validation" is used three separate ways with overlapping names but different scope and timing**, and nothing reconciles them: Blueprint Section 17 "Validation Documentation" (world-level historical/ecological plausibility, done pre-freeze); Construction Framework Part VI "Validation & Testing" (the same world-level ecological-integrity tests, run at Step 9); and Representative Construction Framework V3.2 Part Eight "Validation Testing" (Representative *voice* probes — Source-Awareness, Anachronism, Confidence-Under-Thinness, etc. — run at Step 10, *after* the world is already frozen). A reader moving between documents has to infer from context which "validation" is meant; none of the three documents cross-references the others to disambiguate.

### Doc_10 — Representative Emergence (RCF V3.2 + `Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Template.txt` + `Representative_Construction_Notes_Template.md`)
- **This is the strongest-verified step in the entire methodology**, and instructively so. `Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Template.txt`'s Final Assembly Instruction has grown, version by version (documented in its own Version History, v1.0→v2.4), into an extremely specific checklist: explicit search-and-remove word lists (step 5), a dedicated register-fidelity check (5b) that requires comparing delivered prose against what Section 2 *documented* as evidenced register, a readability check with numeric Flesch-Kincaid targets (5c: "grade 8-10... Reading Ease 60 or above"), and a check for a specific previously-found failure pattern (5d, brought forward from another branch's testing). RCF V3.2 Part Eight adds nine named probe categories (Source-Awareness, Anachronism, Confidence-Under-Thinness, Self-Referential, Scholarly-Framework, Relational Safety, Claim-Laundering, Register-Fidelity, Sustained Engagement) with explicit Violation Indicators. Construction Framework V7.4 draft's new "Validation Protocol Rigor" subsection goes further still — it requires *two independent trials* (one held-out probe set), *fresh-context generation* (no same-session author-grades-own-work), and *blind grading*, explicitly because a same-session, single-sample check had previously been mis-reported as "fully clean."
- **The contrast is the finding.** The methodology clearly *can* produce rigorous, specific, checkable verification mechanisms — it did so for the Representative's voice (after adversarial testing found real failures) and for Source Registry compliance (after the Theon/Nyssa defect). It has not yet done the equivalent for Doc_06 lexicon chunks or Doc_09a story chunks, where no comparable failure had yet been found *before* Mark's audit. The pattern across the whole set is retroactive, not prospective: verification mechanisms appear where a specific defect was already caught (Doc_02 Source Registry, Doc_04's index table, the Permanent Prompt's iterative checklist), and are absent where none had yet surfaced (Doc_06, Doc_09a chunks) — until now.

## Cross-cutting patterns across the whole set

1. **Modal-verb pattern.** Most requirements throughout L3B/L4 are phrased as "should" (advisory) rather than "must/required" (mandatory). The exceptions — explicitly marked MANDATORY or "required" — are: the Permanent Prompt's three vision sections (Sections 6/7/8), Doc_08, Doc_09, Living Tradition Status Confirmation, External Scholarly Review, and (V7.4 draft) Source Registry completeness + grounding-anchor verification. Lexicon-chunk Key Sources/confidence-disclosure is a structural "Include for Tier 1/2" instruction but was never elevated into this small "mandatory/gated" tier the way those other items were.

2. **Verification mechanisms cluster where a defect was already found, not where a rule was first written.** Doc_02 (Theon/Nyssa borrowing), Doc_04 (four-world structural divergence), and the Permanent Prompt (multiple named regressions in its own version history) all got hard, specific, checkable gates *after* the fact. Doc_06 lexicon chunks, Doc_09a story chunks, and Doc_01 have no comparable gates because — until Mark's audit — no comparable defect had surfaced there yet.

3. **Version-drift handling is inconsistent but not absent.** Some superseded documents are explicitly marked (`Doc_02B_Approved_Source_Database_Template_V2.md`, `Cross_World_Source_Registry.md` both carry "SUPERSEDED — 2026-07-04" banners redirecting to the live document). The V7.4 Construction Framework draft explicitly documents what changed from V7.3 and why (Step 2 restructuring, new Fix-the-Mechanism and Record-Integrity principles, new Freeze Criteria). But other drift is unreconciled and only surfaces in project-status documents outside the L3B/L4 set itself (Deployment Standards document referenced-but-missing; System Operations V1.2 possibly stale against CO-013's World Priority Layer).

4. **Terminology reused across scopes without cross-reference**: "Validation" (three distinct processes at three different pipeline stages), "Doc_09" (one step producing what is treated informally as three sub-documents), "Freeze" vs. "Deployment Package Readiness" (two separate gates, only one of which — Freeze — is enumerated with hard criteria in the Construction Framework; the other delegates to a document that doesn't exist).

5. **The length-target silence is real and generalizable**, not specific to lexicon chunks: every L4 template that governs an *always-present, context-budget-constrained* artifact (Capsule Core, Context Layer chunk, Permanent Prompt) states a hard token/word range. Every L4 template that governs a *retrievable, per-term-or-story* artifact (Deployment Lexicon chunk, Story Repository chunk) states none — only qualitative, tier-relative guidance. This is a consistent category-level gap, not a one-off omission in a single file.
