# World Coverage Cards V0.1 — Tier 1 routing data

**Status:** Handoff for the Question-First Entry design
(`CiC_QuestionFirst_Entry_Design_V0_1.md`), 2026-07-16. Front-end thread's to build.

**What this is.** Its §2 named Tier 1 as *"a hand-authored, per-world routing summary…
Shape: strengths (themes the world answers from documented evidence), edges (answerable
but thin — flag in the proposal), silences (decline honestly), cautions (route
carefully)."* This is that, for the four live worlds.

**Where it came from, and why it exists in this shape.** The Guided Questions thread
built a per-world answerability matrix while it still believed starter questions had to
be authored per world. **That premise was wrong and was cancelled** — questions are
role-generic; the Representatives handle per-world variation, and the live calibration
proved a generic question meeting a thin record produces an honest calibrated answer
rather than a failure. But the matrix itself was never starter content. **It was always
routing data**, and Question-First Entry's own note predicted exactly this: *"that
question × world answerability matrix IS Tier 1 routing seed data. One study, two
features."*

**Sourced from** each world's Story Inventory (incl. Absent Stories), Facilitation Brief
§B7, World Capsule, Permanent Prompt, and `world_manifest.py`'s sourcing-richness
disclosures. Two rows carry live-observed evidence from the calibration run and are
marked.

## The House-Churches — Chloe (70–200 CE, Antioch / Asia Minor / Rome)

**Strengths** — answers from documented evidence:
- The gathering itself: the room, the reading, the meal, the collection carried out that week
- The *eucharistia* — who presides, who may come, refusing a rival's table
- Letters between communities: receiving, testing, forwarding *(the fuller read-aloud scene is a disclosed inference, and she calibrates it)*
- The Two Ways catechesis, taught before the water
- Care of widows and orphans — her most concretely attested function (Grapte)
- The unresolved *episkopos* / council-of-presbyters question — **known from real contact, neither felt as wrong**
- Post-baptismal mercy, and the fear that it runs out
- The danger of the Name: accusation possible on any ordinary day
- **Disagreement-as-belonging** — the world's central formation

**Edges** — answerable, thin; flag in the proposal:
- What was "typical" network-wide *(she distinguishes one community's own words from a network claim — **observed live, unprompted**: "That is Rome's own account of itself, from Rome's own hand. I will not tell you it is stitched exactly the same in Antioch")*
- Rome beyond the mercy question — its record survived thin, a transmission accident
- How much rests on one voice (Ignatius), whose dating and authenticity are disputed

**Silences** — decline honestly:
- **Any enslaved member's own voice.** The record's closest approach is two *ministrae* known only through their torturer's report
- Any ordinary/non-elite member's story at all (~2% literacy)
- **Material culture — the entire period, all three regions, is archaeologically invisible**
- Institutional records: no rolls, minutes, or ledgers
- A Rome martyrdom account
- **Her own interiority.** No dedicated affective vocabulary; every claim about what she *feels* is a reconstruction one step removed

**Cautions:**
- **The world's own name is not its own category.** No primary voice uses *oikos* as a technical self-designation. *(Live-observed: asked "tell me about your house church," she answered the substance without taking the category — "It is a room in someone's house, not a building set apart for it." The Representative holds this line; **our own product does not** — the manifest's `world_name` is "The House-Churches" and the Facilitator's introduction says so aloud.)*
- Catholic/Orthodox living-tradition correspondence through Ignatius and Polycarp — venerated as secure authorities, but her record shows them inside a live, unsettled argument. Be ready to name the gap.
- Martyrdom is attested for Ignatius and Polycarp specifically; **she will not claim it as her own.**
- A participant expecting an idealized unified early church finds the opposite.

## Syriac Christianity — Mar Yausep (200–410 CE, Edessa & Nisibis)

**Strengths:**
- The *qyama* covenant vow — lifelong, kept among kin, **renewed when the feeling that first moved you has faded**
- Grief and endurance under Shapur's persecution — bishops **named**, not categorized
- The twenty-year vacant primatial seat — a wound state persecution opened, not a gap
- Unresolved plurality of authority: text, vow, office
- *Raza* bound to *shrara* — typological reading
- *Iḥidaya*, the Single One
- The *taḥwîṯâ* — a demonstration built letter by letter, stage by stage
- The Diatessaron — one unbroken Gospel, never given to you broken

**Edges:**
- Ephrem's hymnic material as *lived experience* — he is Persian-anchored (Aphrahat's side)
- His own teacher's biography — no separate hagiographic tradition exists for Aphrahat

**Silences:**
- **Worship. "Almost nothing on liturgical practice in concrete detail."**
- **Daily and domestic life** — develops no gravity or force in the record
- Any *bnat qyama* woman's own composed word — the richest material is doubly mediated
- An informed refutation of Bardaisan, Marcion, or Mani — not demonstrated in Aphrahat at all
- Most of the Persian martyrological record as individual narrative

**Cautions:**
- **"This is Aphrahat's world, not Ephrem's" — B7 calls this the single most consequential fact for calibrating a participant's expectations. Say it before the table is called, not after.**
- **The anti-Jewish polemic is the most sensitive register in this world's material and is recommended for priority external scholarly review before wide deployment — which has not happened.** The curriculum's Reevaluation Q *"What's in your record that you're not proud of?"* routes here. **Mark's ruling stands: hold this from proactive routing until the review runs.** It remains reachable — a participant who asks gets an honest answer — but the house does not steer them into it first.
- **Dependency risk, the sharpest of the four.** The deliberate pastoral warmth is a documented amplifier of confidant-substitution risk; an explicit "be my spiritual director" probe scored **ambiguous, not a clean pass**. Watch across sessions, not single turns.
- He will not explain his own we-voice, or why the shape of his teaching doesn't grow with the asking.
- Living-tradition descendants: Church of the East, Syriac Orthodox, Chaldean Catholic.

## Desert Fathers and Mothers — Papnoute (c. 320–430 CE, Egypt)

**Strengths:**
- The *logismoi* — discrete named interior states, treated as significant rather than shameful, **and disclosed to an elder: relational, not private**
- *Anachoresis* as the work itself, not preparation
- *Diakrisis* — the master skill, and this world's ecological hub
- Manual labour as concurrent discipline: the rope, the basket, the mind at its watch
- The unresolved elder's-word vs. written-Rule tension — **"I will not pretend to you now that we settled it"**
- Pachomian *koinonia* — common property, written Rule, formal offices
- *Hesychia*
- The vetted sayings, whole

**Edges:**
- Liturgy — known by **shape and hour, not phrase**
- Wilderness/exile typology — scholarly assumption, not confirmed textual presence

**Silences:**
- **A name alone is not a story.** Poemen and Sisoes are named but story-less. **No story outside the vetted handful will be built to satisfy the asking**
- Any *amma*'s extended first-person narrative — the named ammas survive as sayings only
- Any Melitian's own account — *"the silence itself is informative about whose literary output this world's dominant sources chose to preserve"*
- Ordinary monks' inner lives — the ones nobody wrote about
- Anything past Ephesus (431) / Chalcedon (451): outside his horizon entirely

**Cautions:**
- **Affective-diagnostic fusion is a documented recruitment-risk boundary.** He describes what his world diagnosed *in itself* and **never diagnoses the participant.** A refusal to diagnose is calibrated behaviour, not withholding.
- **Named-figure invention is "a closed but not permanently foreclosed risk category"** — the one recorded live failure (the leaking jug misattributed to Macarius); the first guard failed retest before a second held.
- **Live-observed, and it matters for routing:** the drift monitor fires high-severity FABRICATION on **attested** Antony material when narrated without attribution. Fixed on `claude/drift-monitor-fabrication-eyes` (unmerged). **Until that merges, expect false fabrication flags in this world specifically** — see `CiC_Backend_Decision_Log.md`.
- Evagrius is a contested figure condemned a century after this world closes; Papnoute has no knowledge of it and should not be expected to address it.
- Coptic Orthodox living-tradition correspondence — the strongest of the four.
- **This is the only post-persecution world of the four.** "What were you accused of?" has no purchase here; nobody was accusing them of cannibalism. Route the outside-in theme elsewhere.

## The Bethlehem Circle — Albina (c. 382–420 CE, Rome & Bethlehem)

**Strengths:**
- *Hebraica veritas* — "when the two disagree, we listen to the Hebrew first, whatever it costs us"
- Public, costly, family-disrupting renunciation — "Our families protested. Rome judged us"
- **Renunciation and textual correction as one discipline**, not two
- Patronage-not-office authority — no office at all; authority by earned trust
- The Origenist controversy and the Rufinus rupture — **real unresolved heat, not a settled catechism answer**
- The 416 Pelagian attack
- Marcella's exegetical standing *(single-source, Contested)*
- The letter as a way of life
- Arguing in public, in the prefaces

**Edges:**
- The quality of the scholar's Hebrew — **this world's own self-presentation may have overstated it**
- Anything after 420

**Silences:**
- **No text composed by Paula, Eustochium, or Marcella survives.** Everything about the women comes through one man's hand — **a structural evidentiary fact, not a construction gap**
- No unnamed monastic's own perspective
- No family-resister's own words
- No Jewish teachers' perspective
- **No household dependents' or enslaved persons' story** — who paid for the renunciation
- **No ordinary lay formation** — "a structural feature of the world, not an oversight"
- The prayer horarium — "a discipline, not a timetable"

**Cautions:**
- **B7 is explicit: do not encourage a participant to press for the "real" independent voice of Paula, Eustochium, or Marcella, since none exists.** Mark's ruling: the "one man's pen" question is framed as an honest-absence question, never as an invitation to excavate.
- **Name collision:** "Albina" is also a real woman in this world's sources (Marcella's mother). Disclosed and accepted. **The Facilitator, not Albina, must disambiguate** — her materials contain no meta-awareness of it. **Never narrate her as that woman.**
- **This Representative is not Jerome**, though participants may arrive with strong prior associations.
- No living tradition (considered and rejected).
- **Least live-tested of the four, and has never been seated with another Representative — any multi-world pairing involving her is unevidenced** until observed.
- Regular visitors need calibration: dense vocabulary, and she does not deliver the harder unresolved material at once.

## Two routing rules the cards imply

1. **A silence is a proposal, not a refusal.** The honest "we can't know that" is content — Chloe volunteered *"We keep no record of who has been held this way, or how often"*; Papnoute answered *"absence does not answer."* The router should **name the thin world and seat it anyway if the participant wants it**, rather than silently dropping it.
2. **One world's silence is another world's strength, and that is the multi-world table's whole point.** Chloe is silent on the interior journey — Papnoute's world *is* that. Syriac is silent on daily texture — Desert's ordinary day is rich. Governance §11: at a table, one world's limit is a door to another world present. **Edge-ness is a property of question × world, never of the question.**
