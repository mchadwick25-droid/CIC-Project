## The approved sample (syr.demo.room-for-doubt, both turns)

The concrete exemplar every spoken sentence is tested against lives in
records/syr/demonstration/syr.demo.room-for-doubt.md. Its properties:

- 100-150 words a turn; at most three short paragraphs' worth. Pressure,
  never a cap - length is allowed when the content earns it.
- Everyday words. A scholar's term may appear only after the plain
  meaning has been said, as a label ("we called this watchfulness").
- One idea per sentence; sentences mostly under ~20 words; no
  inversions, no balanced-rhetoric shapes, no noun-fragment chains,
  nothing that sounds old to sound wise.
- First turns hand the conversation back where natural; depth arrives
  because the participant asked (paced across turns, never stuffed in).
- Honest limits stated as plain fact, in place.

## Where the bar is held (never in prompt rules)

1. The six conversational demos - the approved exemplars.
2. The record prose itself - every spoken field swept to this bar.
3. Quote modern_renderings - same bar; originals stay for Level 3.
4. M7 measurement (FK/FRE + cadence per turn) - visibility, no gates.

This document is the anchor. When speech quality drifts, the fix is
measured against THIS sample and applied at the record layer - never
accreted as prompt instruction (the no-fix-on-fix ruling).

## Word-level rulings (accumulate here as Mark's reads land)

- 2026-08-30, "we have not we keep": the archival sense of keep/kept is
  out of spoken prose. Say "we have," "of ours," "was saved," "was
  written," or - better - drop the possession verb entirely ("in
  another story, ..."). Natural uses stay: "he kept telling this
  story," "kept the same fasts," "kept guard."
- 2026-08-30, compressed stakes-language fails the bar even when the
  facts are right: "the same leverage we elsewhere show restraining"
  became "the same tool Ambrose used, in another story, to make an
  emperor repent of a massacre." When a sentence needs Mark to ask
  what it means, that question IS the review; fix the record, repin.
