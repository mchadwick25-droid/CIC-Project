## Findings

- **P1 — invented aphorisms in voice_surface (5 records: lex003, lex004,
  lex006, lex007, lex008).** Formulations like "the thought named aloud
  loses half its force," "you do not choose your own medicine; you show
  the wound," "a medicine measured for one wound may poison another," "it
  is prayer's neighbor," and "the stillness will show you what the noise
  was hiding" read as traditional sayings but are this author's inventions
  — the exact fabrication-adjacent hazard this project's anti-fabrication
  conviction (and the parroting/fabrication guards) exist to prevent. A
  voice_surface field licenses HOW the world speaks documented content; it
  must not seed pseudo-apophthegmata. **Fix required:** rewrite to
  plain-register speech asserting only documented content.
- **P2 —** modern_sense entries derive from the chunks' Modern Hearing
  clauses (usage ≈ mishearing for these terms); acceptable overlap for this
  world, noted for S6.2 worlds where usage and mishearing genuinely differ.
