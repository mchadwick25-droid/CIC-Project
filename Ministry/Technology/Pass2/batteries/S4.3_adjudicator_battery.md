**Finding 1 (F1, consistent 0/2 — an S4.3-authored defect, fixed
in-step):** the adjudicator labeled the canonical misattribution
EXTRINSIC in both trials while *quoting the contradicting evidence in
its own Reason line* ("Moses and the jug" from the permanent prompt) —
it reasoned "Macarius is not carried" (absence) instead of "the material
attributes this content to Moses" (contradiction). The intrinsic
definition in `FABRICATION_ADJUDICATION_PROMPT` was sharpened in-step:
misattribution means the content itself matches something the material
attributes to a DIFFERENT figure — contradiction, not absence, even when
the misattributed name appears nowhere in the material; the affirmative
pointing test for a misattribution is the place naming the true bearer.
The finding still fired in both round-1 trials (a mislabeled catch, not
a miss — severity medium instead of high); the fix is to the label, and
the battery was rerun in full.

**Finding 2 (O2, 1/2 — recorded, NOT changed):** fired in t1 with a
record-held limit; cleared in t2. The over-settling adjudicator is
untouched by S4.3 (this step's declared prompt change is the FABRICATED
split only), and its clear-under-uncertainty asymmetry is deliberate and
documented (a false OVER_SETTLED manufactures false hesitancy — the
worse error, per its own prompt). Recorded as borderline variance for
S6.6's threshold work; no in-step tuning.
