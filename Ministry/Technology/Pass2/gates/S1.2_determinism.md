## What the baseline measures (scoping, stated per the harness docstring)

The one non-deterministic stage of today's pipeline — the Haiku relevance
vote — is bracketed by two fixed policies (`vote=retrieve` / `vote=skip`);
the real system lies between the brackets, and the bracket width is itself
a measurement of how much of today's behavior hangs on the vote R6 replaces.

## Headline baseline findings (numbers in the committed JSON)

1. **Verbatim-term dense retrieval is strong**: MRR = 1.0 in 5 of 6 worlds
   (IJC 0.83). The exact-term path works today.
2. **The dead-guard class generalizes beyond madrasha.** Multiple worlds
   show `must_not` chunks retrieved even under `vote=skip` — i.e., Tier-1
   chunks ranking in the top 2 auto-retrieve with their
   Do-Not-Retrieve-When never read (IJC negative-condition 2/2, PAHC 1/1,
   Desert 2 under skip). Only 2 chunks fleet-wide carry `force_llm_vote`.
   Alexandria is maximally exposed: **all 45 of its lexicon chunks are
   Tier 1**, so whatever ranks top-2 always short-circuits.
3. **The already-discussed de-dup proxy structurally fails on composite
   Term strings** — every world shows a deterministic (both-policies)
   duplicate retrieval when context names the term plainly
   ("qyama", "gnosis", "episkopos", "Vulgata") but metadata `Term:` is
   composite ("qyama (ܩܝܡܐ) / bnay qyama / bnat qyama"). The guard's best
   case (exact simple Term, e.g. "Theosis", "homoousios") still only
   reroutes to the LLM vote. Previously undocumented; S3.3's ID-keyed
   session exclusion set is the fix, and this baseline is its before.
4. **Reactive-turn dilution is real and measured**: the current
   concatenation shape buries the right chunk in 3 of 8 reactive cases
   (PAHC's episkopos falls to rank 6 under desert-content concatenation;
   Syriac's madrasha to rank 4). R9's before-numbers.
5. **Thematic misses that R1/R2 target**: 2 thematic cases fleet-wide have
   their target chunk absent from candidates entirely (ALX-TH-02,
   DES-TH-04) — the embedding never sees `Retrieve-When`, exactly Pass 1's
   diagnosis.
6. **Cross-world isolation: 0 violations across all 630 candidate slots**
   (structural per-world-index invariant, asserted on every case).
7. **Metadata regression checks: 0 failures** (incl. the madrasha
   `force_llm_vote` flag — the S1.2 regression case for the live-verified
   fix).
