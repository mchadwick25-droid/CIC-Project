## The change

- **Direct-address detection before the holistic selector**
  (`nodes.detect_direct_address` / `detect_rep_to_rep_address`): cheap,
  deterministic, conservative (exactly one seated representative named,
  no whole-table marker; ambiguity always falls through to the
  selector). Wired inside `determine_turn_type` (the plain endpoint's
  router LLM call disappears on determined turns) and
  `select_next_speaker` (the streaming selector call disappears on
  determined selections). Enforces FG §8 verbatim: "When the participant
  addresses a specific Representative, you route accordingly.
  Immediately, completely, without editorial intervention."
- **The REASON line becomes a private directive** — a new consumer of
  output the selector already generated and discarded: parsed into a
  `reason_sink`, delivered to the selected speaker via the turn-local
  `state.private_directive` (never persisted, never participant-visible)
  as "A Private Word from the Facilitator" in the dynamic prompt.
  Direct-address selections carry a deterministic directive. The
  moderation literature's Confronting act, with the room never acquiring
  a voice.
- **Question-stacking rebuilt on outstanding first-pair-parts**
  (`outstanding_first_pair_parts`): derived deterministically from the
  transcript (no stored counter to drift); a question answered in-round
  no longer stacks — ending the internal contradiction where
  REACTIVE_TURN_GUIDANCE's "Ask, Don't Just Answer" licensed exactly the
  act the old ends-with-"?" count penalized. Threshold (>2 outstanding)
  unchanged.
- **Block truncation with a stable prefix** (`build_public_transcript`):
  the opening frame (first 2 spoken events) pinned, middle elided with a
  counted marker, recent window depth 10 kept; short conversations
  rendered whole exactly as before.
- **F9 — the named first runtime consumer of `wrs/parameters.yaml`:**
  `MIN_MULTI_WORLD_TURNS` now reads `turn_floor_multi_world.value`
  (fail-open to the historical 2 — deployability). M3 pending → floor 2
  stands; S4.4b retires it only after Mark decides.
- `table_discourse.py`: unchanged — its "Ask, Don't Just Answer" text is
  now consistent with the rebuilt check; the contradiction was
  code-side.

**Touches vs blueprint:** nodes.py, main.py, state.py
(`private_directive`, turn-local), wrs/parameters.yaml (read-only
consumer; file unedited) — the declared set minus the S4.4b-only items
