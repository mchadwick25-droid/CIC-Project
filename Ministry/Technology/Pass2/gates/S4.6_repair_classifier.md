## The change

- **`app/graph/repair_classifier.py` (new)** — the SIXTH intercept,
  mirroring epistemology_bridge's classify-then-route shape with one
  structural difference: a hit never produces a Facilitator turn. The
  narrow initiation classifier (open request / restricted request /
  restricted offer / bare pushback; fails open to NOT_REPAIR, with an
  explicit distress rule) extracts the challenged claim; the evidence
  adjudicator — reusing `_gather_world_evidence`, plus the world's own
  contested_claim records with their `pressure_response` ("how this
  world characteristically holds it") — decides HOLD or CONCEDE; the
  strategy is SELECTED from a closed two-template set, never composed;
  and it rides inside the challenged Representative's own answering turn
  as the S4.4a private directive. Genuinely UNCERTAIN adjudications
  select nothing — the turn proceeds exactly as today (a false hold
  hardens a possible fabrication; a false concede talks a world out of
  its conviction; silence costs only today's behavior).
- **Chain placement LAST** (`governance.classify_pre_turn`,
  `include_repair`, both endpoints): runs only when safety, the
  epistemology bridge, the closing sequence, and the modern-term bridge
  have all declined.
- **The challenged Representative answers** — round-opening selection
  goes to them without a selector call (a repair initiation is addressed
  to its target as surely as a direct address); plain multi-world "all"
  path keeps current behavior (working states don't carry the
  directive — declared).
- **`challenge_adjudicated` events** carry each firing's world / kind /
  verdict / matched record — the datum §10's rates are computed from.
- **Compatibility classification:** gated on world_core presence — runs
  for migrated worlds only (Desert); unmigrated worlds keep current
  behavior exactly, zero added calls.
- **State.py note:** no field change needed — challenge events live in
