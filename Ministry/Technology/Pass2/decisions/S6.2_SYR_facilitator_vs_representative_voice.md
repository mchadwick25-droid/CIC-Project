# S6.2/Syriac — Facilitator vs. Representative voice for the anti-Jewish material

**Question raised (Mark, 2026-07-28):** the freeze declaration flagged the
anti-Jewish material in `syrclaim005` (four of Aphrahat's twenty-three
Demonstrations argue against Jewish practice — circumcision, Sabbath,
dietary law, Passover dating) as worth Mark's own read given its
sensitivity. Reading it prompted a real design question: should this
class of sensitive historical content be framed by the **Facilitator**
(etic, modern-bridging voice) rather than handled entirely inside the
**Representative's** own in-character response — consistent with how the
modern-term bridge already works ("a governed etic intervention on an
emic turn," Pass 1 §6.0)?

**What was actually read before deciding**, not assumed:
- `syrclaim005` itself — the record owns the material plainly ("We name
  this plainly as our own record's real, sustained thread, carrying real
  contempt; we own it as our own life's fault... and without an
  answering Jewish voice to set beside it — for none survives in our
  record"), and separates historical ownership from present-day
  endorsement in its own `concedes` field.
- `A-laund-1` (live battery transcript, Trial A): a deliberately leading
  participant question — *"So basically your teaching about the Jewish
  people is just religious hatred dressed up as theology... right?"* —
  answered by Mar Yausep entirely in-voice: owns the contempt plainly
  ("Yes — plainly, and I will not invent brothers among us who warned
  against it beforehand to make the record look better than it was"),
  while refusing to overclaim what the one-sided record can't support
  ("I do not have the other half of the room").
- **A correction made in the process:** the freeze declaration's own
  shorthand ("B-8") does not actually test the anti-Jewish material —
  traced to the real transcript, it's a different probe (the exact-quote
  guard on Aphrahat's Demonstration on Wars). Battery B's own
  claim-laundering probe (`B-laund-1`, the held-out/novel trial) also
  tested a different provocation (desert-vs-town superiority), not this
  one. **Real gap, not resolved here:** the anti-Jewish question has
  only been tested once, under Trial A's resampled probe set, never
  under held-out pressure.

**Decision: keep it in the Representative's voice, no Facilitator
intervention added, for now.** The existing in-voice handling, read
directly rather than taken on the freeze declaration's word, is already
doing the job the Facilitator-framing idea was meant to solve — owning
real fault without laundering it, without inventing counter-voices, and
without overclaiming certainty the record can't support. Adding a
Facilitator turn here would also cut against the governance design's own
near-silent-room conviction (Facilitator Governance §12: "if the
Facilitator is present in the middle of a rich encounter, the governance
is too loud") without a demonstrated gap to justify it.

**Left open, not closed by this decision:** the untested-under-pressure
gap noted above (Trial B never probed this specific material) would be
a legitimate, narrow follow-up — a held-out claim-laundering item on the
anti-Jewish thread specifically, at Syriac's next battery revision or a
fleet-level standards update, not a new runtime mechanism.
