# Table Pairings V1 — the C6 record

**Written:** 2026-08-28, the Table-build thread, under Mark's delegation of
the full C6 range. This is the reasoned pairing set for the first live
Tables: which of the six built formation worlds sit together, and why.
Governed by `CiC_L3D_The_Table_Design_Document_V2.3` (any 1–3 combination
is *legal*; the Facilitator's curatorial judgment — and before launch,
this document — decides which are *offered first*). The no-foreknowledge
rule (Mark, 2026-08-28) changes what pairing analysis has to weigh: voices
know each other only through the Table's own words, so historical
acquaintance between traditions no longer matters to the mechanics — what
matters is **contrast of formation**, which is what the participant came
to witness.

## Recommended launch set

**P1 — alx + desert** *(the archetype, proven).* Intellectual-textual
against ascetic-embodied — the exact contrast the design document itself
keeps reaching for, and the pairing both live runs have now exercised
(zero isolation violations, balanced word-share, genuine cross-voice
engagement). The known-good default.

**P2 — pahc + ijc** *(the arc of the church).* The sharpest historical
span any two seats can hold: the faith as a meal in somebody's house,
before empire noticed it, against the faith as an institution the empire
writes to. Strongest for the questions participants actually bring about
belonging, authority, and what "church" is. Bridging note: `pahc` is the
one formation world for which `_fleet.modern.trinity` is anachronistic, so
at any mixed table the term is *not* round-anachronistic and Chloe fields
it un-bridged — her own compiled honest-limits craft is the handling, and
the selector should favor the in-horizon voice; flagged for live review,
not a blocker.

**P3 — syr + alx** *(two ways of knowing).* Greek philosophical reasoning
against Syriac poetic-symbolic reasoning — the same Scripture met through
argument and through image. The cleanest demonstration that "genuinely
different ways of thinking" means reasoning *mode*, not just conclusions.

**P4 — hal + desert** *(the convergence stress case — run under review).*
Two asceticisms, near-identical decades (382–420 / 320–430): aristocratic
household renunciation against desert solitude. Deliberately the hardest
pairing for irenic flattening — similar formations must stay distinct or
the Table fails its own test (V2.3 §10: the Table reveals construction).
Scheduled for battery-accompanied runs, not first public offering.

**F1 — alx + desert + pahc** *(the flagship three-seat).* School, desert,
and household across the whole ancient arc (70–430): three formation modes
with no two alike, anchored by the two proven voices. This is the seating
the C5 battery runs against.

## Held back from the first offering, deliberately

- **ijc + desert** — the adversarial review's friction ground
  (post-Constantine Egypt, institution against withdrawal). A genuinely
  valuable table *once* validation can grade whether real difference
  is being held rather than smoothed — the convergence check needs a
  track record first.
- **hal + alx**, **syr + ijc**, and the remaining combinations — nothing
  wrong with them; they simply add contrast the launch set already covers.
  The three-seat ceiling and the Facilitator's restraint principle (V2.3
  §5: "the right two or three voices rather than every available voice")
  both counsel starting narrow.

## What ratifies this

These are offerings, not mechanics — the engine seats any legal 2–3
combination regardless. Before any pairing is participant-facing: its
battery run (Artifact-7 SS8) green on the AUTO probes and Mark's read on
the RECORDED ones. P1 and F1 have live evidence as of this date; P2–P4
await their runs. Mark's own read of this document is the C6 sign-off.
