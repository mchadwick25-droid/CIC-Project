# The Church in Conversation — Full System Feature Analysis & Market Comparison V0.1

**Status:** Strategy document for Mark, 2026-07-16. Part 1 inventories everything
the system now is (built, in construction, and designed), as one product. Part 2
compares it against five best-in-class tools that bracket its feature space.
Part 3 answers the question asked: do we have the right features, what should we
adopt, and what must we refuse.

---

## Part 1 — The full system, inventoried

**What this is, said once:** a formation-encounter system in which a participant
orients across two thousand years of Christian history, chooses historically
reconstructed community voices, and enters governed, transparent conversation
with them — every claim carrying its evidence, every absence explained, every
experience bounded by what the sources actually support.

### Layer 1 — The truth layer (construction & scholarship)
*The invisible 80% that makes everything else trustworthy.*

- **Step 0 phase-level scoping:** the Movement-Scope eligibility floor (A1 plain
  confession, A2 pre-Nicene continuity, A3 interpretive fidelity, A4 bounded
  hand-selected inclusion, A5 rivals-inside-worlds) plus the person-defined
  (Criterion 2) test, feeding tiered seed lists (B1 sourcing, B2 ecology, B3
  uniqueness, B4 audience fit, B5 scale-as-tiebreak).
- **The construction pipeline (Doc_01–09):** world identification and boundaries;
  Source Ecology with Author Gravity (including transmission history) and the
  marginalized-voices affirmative duty; lexicon candidates → full three-level
  lexicon; Gravity Discovery under six tests with confidence cross-checks;
  ecological reconstruction written from inside; Forces analysis; Story
  Inventory in four evidence tiers — **and no Tier 5: invented narrative is
  prohibited; silence in thin areas is the designed response.**
- **The five-level confidence vocabulary** (Documented → Inferential/Thin)
  carried from construction through deployment to the participant's screen.
- **Gates:** Living-Tradition Status confirmation before freeze; external
  scholarly review (Article 31); the one-document-at-a-time, review-gated build
  protocol; the validation suite (probe batteries, drift testing, adversarial
  resistance, ecology assessment).

### Layer 2 — The encounter layer (the product's beating heart)

- **The Table:** conversation with one to three Representatives (design ceiling
  five), each a *community's* voice — never an impersonated individual — with
  multi-world orchestration, per-world reactive-turn discipline (crowded tables
  stay readable), and streaming, pinned-scroll chat UX.
- **Two conversation shapes:** Deep Interview (one voice, room to go deep) and
  Compare Worlds (2–3 voices answering the same question) — emerging naturally
  from seat count.
- **The Facilitator:** a classify-then-route governed host with twelve
  fidelity-drift signals, vulnerable-participant triggers (acute distress;
  dependency/harmful dynamic), the gracious close with participant-controlled
  closing resources (Article 34's door outward), and pre-threshold
  Ask-the-Facilitator Q&A for the skeptical visitor.
- **Representative Modes (in construction):** four participant roles (general,
  pastor/teacher, academic, deconstructing/reconstructing) tailoring register,
  examples, and depth under the hard invariant *one truth, four registers* —
  role changes what's offered first, never what's reachable.
- **Tours (in construction):** the Representative guiding a source-bounded walk
  through their world — with honest declines where evidence runs out.

### Layer 3 — The transparency layer (the differentiator)

- **Three-Level Transparency (Article 30, constitutional):** conversation →
  on-request plain explanation (10th-grade floor, universal) → full scholarly
  apparatus; Levels 2–3 always reachable, never gated, for anyone.
- **One interaction grammar everywhere:** hover for the short honest
  explanation, click for full depth — lexicon terms (inline, first-occurrence
  highlighting, per-speaker vocabularies), citations, stories, closing
  resources, map entries, artwork sources.
- **Honesty-of-absence, systematized:** per-world sourcing-richness disclosures
  on the selection tiles; the map's status system (Live / Selected / Deferred /
  Pre-Survey / Excluded-with-grounds / Contested); the Beyond-the-Floor stream
  with research briefs; founder-prophet (✦/✧) marks; wet-ink rendering for
  still-forming currents; confidence-styled relationship edges that are never
  drawn beyond the evidence.
- **Planned:** Academic Documents (the raw construction corpus, browsable).

### Layer 4 — The orientation layer (this year's addition)

- **The World Atlas:** 178 entries across ten user-facing eras and eight lanes —
  every candidate world in Christian history with sourcing signals, floor notes
  (questions, never verdicts), relations, and participant-facing briefs; census
  spreadsheet + filterable browser.
- **The interactive map:** wall-chart aesthetic (parchment, Cinzel, engraved
  bands, PD-art era medallions with source credits), three semantic zoom tiers
  (landscape → era → world-with-figures), lineage edges on demand, context
  strip of church and world history, guided demo tour, app-handoff integration
  (verified working on its branch), recordings (GIF/slideshow) for showing.

### Layer 5 — Experience & operations

- Pre-encounter onboarding (once per tester); refresh-warning banner; session
  caps; consented transcript capture for the pilot; hosting plan (Render);
  the exploration-branch discipline for integration work; demo/recording
  machinery; designed-but-future: voice casting (cost-gated), group/classroom
  mode, institutional embed, the free-core / paid-professional funding shape
  with its named Article 30 boundary.

## Part 2 — Five comparators that bracket our feature space

| Tool | Pole it represents | Scale signal |
|---|---|---|
| Character.AI | Engagement / persona conversation | 20M+ MAU, ~75 min/day |
| Khanmigo (Khan Academy) | Pedagogical guardrails on AI chat | K-12 institutional adoption |
| Google Arts & Culture | Immersive cultural orientation & tours | 80+ countries' collections |
| Logos | Source rigor & study depth (faith market) | The pastor/scholar standard |
| Duolingo | Habit, retention, simplicity | 10x DAU since 2019; retention 12%→55% |

### Character.AI — the engagement pole, and the cautionary tale
What it proves: people will spend *over an hour a day* in persona conversation —
the appetite CiC serves is enormous and real. How it fails: engagement is
engineered through emotional mirroring, memory, and open-ended attachment
loops; historical figures are user-created with no curation ("no accuracy
checking… whitewashing documented views" — literally remorseful Nazi bots);
parasocial dependency became lawsuits, teen deaths, and an under-18 ban.
**Lesson for us:** our dependency triggers, worlds-not-persons rule, and
Doorway-Not-a-Home article aren't drag on engagement — they are the difference
between presence and predation. The market has now proven the harm we designed
against. Never import its retention mechanics.

### Khanmigo — guardrails as the product
What it does well: Socratic method enforced (never gives the answer), content
grounded in verified curriculum, teacher visibility — "the guardrails are the
product," which is precisely our own thesis. Where it breaks, instructively:
its historical-figure chats are documented as "speculations at best," sanitized
and feel-good — because guardrails without a *construction pipeline* have
nothing solid to stand on. **Lesson:** our Doc_01–09 pipeline is what Khanmigo's
history feature is missing; also worth adopting — its guiding-question habit
(see Part 3, Adopt #1).

### Google Arts & Culture — orientation and tours, validated
Its 2025 "Talking Tours" (AI-guided walks through 360° heritage sites, ask
questions as you go) is *exactly* our Tours concept — market-validated by
Google this year, minus our source-boundedness. Its explore-by-time, high-res
artifact viewing, and playful entry points (Art Selfie) show how orientation
becomes delight. **Lesson:** our map/tour direction is right; add high-res
artifact viewing to tours (we already have the PD-art-with-credits discipline);
keep one playful, zero-cost entry moment in mind for Phase 2.

### Logos — the rigor pole in our own market
Its AI Study Assistant answers questions **with citations that click through to
the exact location in the source** — the gold standard our citation/Academic
Documents path should match. Smart Search in natural language lowered its
complexity barrier. Its standing criticisms — expensive, overwhelming,
power-user-shaped — mark the ditch on the rigor side. **Lesson:** rigor must
never cost simplicity; our three-level transparency (plain first, apparatus on
request) is the right inversion of Logos's apparatus-first shape. Match its
click-to-exact-source; refuse its complexity.

### Duolingo — engagement mechanics, and their edge
Streaks and leagues lifted retention from 12% to 55% — but the research
documents the divergence: loss-aversion streaks produce *compulsive usage
without learning* at the edges; users speed-run to protect streaks. **Lesson:**
we want its *simplicity* (one obvious next action, tiny sessions possible, warm
brand voice) and refuse its *compulsion loops*. Formation cannot be
streak-farmed; a "sitting" is the wrong unit for loss-aversion. Our continuity
answer must be invitation-shaped, not anxiety-shaped.

### What none of them have (the moat, stated plainly)
No tool in this bracket combines: (1) a scholarly construction pipeline behind
every voice, (2) confidence calibration surfaced to the end user, (3) honesty
about absence as a designed feature (the map's whole thesis), (4) a governed
facilitator with pastoral—not merely safety-compliance—triggers, and (5)
community voices rather than impersonated individuals. Position in one line:
**Logos-grade rigor under Character.AI-grade presence, governed by convictions
neither of them has.**

## Part 3 — Do we have the right features?

**Core verdict: yes.** The encounter, truth, transparency, and orientation
layers form a complete and differentiated system; nothing in the comparator
set exposes a missing *pillar*. What the comparison exposes is thinner
scaffolding around the encounter: how a participant **returns**, how a novice
knows **what to ask**, and how an encounter **travels** to other people. All
three are addressable without new pillars.

### Adopt (fits our grammar, strengthens without diluting)

1. **Suggested next questions** (from Khanmigo's Socratic craft): after a
   Representative's turn, the Facilitator can offer 2–3 tappable follow-ups
   ("Ask her what the meal meant" / "Ask how they chose leaders"). Serves the
   general user who doesn't know what to ask; role-aware once Modes land; pure
   prompt-layer work. *Effort: small. Risk to simplicity: none — it removes
   blank-box paralysis.*
2. **The pilgrim's map** (our answer to progress mechanics): worlds you've
   visited gently distinguish themselves on the map — where you've been, not
   what you owe. No streaks, no loss. Continuity as memory, not obligation.
   *Effort: small once accounts exist; the accounts question (already open)
   is the real gate.*
3. **Themed trails** (Arts & Culture's curated routes, our content): the
   cross-era threads already named in the atlas — persecution-formation, Bible
   translation, women's voices — become guided map routes: "walk the
   translation thread from Ulfila to Carey." *Effort: modest; content already
   exists in the census relations.*
4. **Click-to-exact-source** (Logos's standard): when Academic Documents
   arrives, citations open the passage, not the document. Design it in now via
   the data contract. *Effort: design note now, build later.*
5. **A closing reflection beat** (aligned with Article 34, absent from every
   comparator): at the gracious close, one optional question — "what stayed
   with you?" — kept private to the participant (and, with consent, to pilot
   transcripts). The formation analog of a learning check. *Effort: small.*
6. **High-res artifact moments in tours** (Arts & Culture): the Chloe tour's
   images zoomable, each with its source credit — already our artwork
   discipline, applied at tour resolution. *Hand to the hospitality thread.*
7. **Pastor's take-away** (Logos-market insight + Modes synergy): in
   pastor/teacher mode, "export a discussion guide from this conversation" —
   the conversation's themes, sources, and questions in a printable page.
   *Effort: moderate; belongs to the Modes thread's Tier list.*

### Adapt with care

- **Session memory across visits** (Character.AI's stickiest feature): genuine
  formation value ("last time you asked about suffering…") but it is also the
  parasocial accelerant. If adopted: facilitator-held (not representative-held)
  memory, visible and erasable by the participant, and framed as continuity of
  *inquiry*, not relationship intimacy. Needs the accounts decision plus a
  governance pass against the dependency triggers.
- **A playful first-touch moment** (Art-Selfie energy): something light at the
  threshold — e.g., "which era are your questions from?" — only if it can be
  done without cuteness corroding the register. Prototype-test before
  committing.

### Refuse, by name (and say why in the record)

- **Streaks, leagues, XP, loss-aversion loops** — compulsion is the opposite of
  formation; Duolingo's own edge-case data is the argument.
- **Emotional-mirroring retention mechanics; companionship framing** — the
  Character.AIs of the market have proven where this ends; our Facilitator
  exists to interrupt dependency, not to cultivate it.
- **User-created personas / uncurated worlds** — the construction pipeline IS
  the product; opening the persona factory would delete the moat and the
  integrity in one act.
- **Single-figure impersonation** ("talk to Augustine") — already refused with
  reasons on the record; every comparator that tried it produced documented
  fabrication.
- **Engagement-optimized notifications** — the project's one honest
  notification is an answered question, not a nag.

### The guard on simplicity (the real risk)

The comparison's sharpest warning isn't a missing feature — it's Logos-style
sprawl. The system already holds its complexity behind two disciplines worth
naming as law: **one interaction grammar** (hover/click, everywhere) and
**progressive disclosure** (plain first, depth on request). Every adopted
feature above rides the existing grammar; nothing adds a new one. Proposed
feature gate for everything future: *does it serve the sitting, the
orientation, or the trust? If none of the three — refuse it, whatever the
market does.*
