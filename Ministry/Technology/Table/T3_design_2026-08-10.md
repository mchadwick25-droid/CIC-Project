## 4. What the conversation is designed to be

The governing documents stand unchanged (the four VR_1A decisions,
Facilitator Governance §8, the palette, the Goodhart rule). The table-
specific design points, each with its instrument:

- **Rounds have shapes, not slots.** One answer + one real response is
  a complete round; a third voice enters when it genuinely differs.
  Instrument: the table checkpoint's shape counters (report-only) and
  the convergence/manufactured-resolution governance.
- **The differing voice must get in — this round or the next.** The
  cap at 4 makes this the selector's job, and it is the one quality
  risk the cost shape carries. Instrument: pilot transcripts + the
  seeded-divergence probes (blueprint B5's battery re-run); if a seated
  world's real difference goes silent, the cap rises before the voice
  is compromised.
- **The participant stays in the room.** The reactive block's own test
  ("if they could only watch, speak to them") + reading-load bounded by
  the cap.
- **Transparency per speaker** — citations, glosses, name-bridge pills
  render per voice at the table; human-verified once (blueprint B8).
- **The pilot watch carries the WATCH classes:** pattern-grounded
  abstractions in dialectic, the desert FIRST_PERSON signals, Haiku's
  fuller measure, question-back and gloss rates. All observational,
  never targets.
