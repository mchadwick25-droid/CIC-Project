# Notices of Uncertificated Share Issuance

**Faithways Studio, Inc., a Colorado public benefit corporation** · Draft for review, 2026-07-27
· Required by C.R.S. § 7-106-207 (uncertificated share notice) and § 7-101-505 (public benefit
corporation disclosure), per the Organizational Resolutions' own directive to the President and
Treasurer. Two separate notices below, one per shareholder — not legal advice, a plain-language
document meant to be sent directly, same spirit as everything else in this folder.

---

## Notice to Mark Chadwick

**To:** Mark Chadwick
**From:** Faithways Studio, Inc.
**Date:** July 27, 2026
**Re: Notice of Issuance of Uncertificated Shares**

This notice confirms that Faithways Studio, Inc. has issued **3,500,000 shares of common stock**
to you, in exchange for cash payment of $20.00, received and cleared July 27, 2026. These shares
are **uncertificated** — no paper stock certificate has been or will be issued unless the board
later determines otherwise — and your ownership is reflected in the corporation's share register.

**Public benefit corporation disclosure (C.R.S. § 7-101-505).** Faithways Studio, Inc. is a
Colorado public benefit corporation. As a shareholder, you should know that the corporation's
board of directors is required by law to manage the corporation in a manner that balances your
pecuniary interest as a shareholder, the interests of those materially affected by the
corporation's conduct, and the corporation's specific public benefit — presently, to create safe
spaces to explore faith and the story of Jesus (Article III of the Articles of Incorporation).
This means the board's duty is not limited to maximizing shareholder financial return alone.
Shareholders of a public benefit corporation may, under Colorado law, have standing to bring or
join a derivative action addressing the board's failure to pursue the corporation's stated public
benefit or its balancing duty under C.R.S. § 7-101-506.

Faithways Studio, Inc.
