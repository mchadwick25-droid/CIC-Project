## 1. Background

Prior to the Incorporation Date, Assignor personally developed the intellectual property and
related assets described in Section 2 (collectively, the "**Assigned Property**"), in connection
with the project and product known as "Church in Conversation." The parties intend that, as of
the Incorporation Date, Assignor contributes the Assigned Property to the Assignee as a
**capital contribution** — additional paid-in capital, increasing Assignor's basis in shares
Assignor already holds — and not in exchange for any additional shares of stock. This is a
separate transaction from, and not part of, the founders' cash purchase of stock addressed in the
Organizational Resolutions. The shares purchased for cash are issued immediately prior to, and
are not conditioned upon, this contribution — the corporation exists as a newly formed
corporation with no material assets at the moment of that issuance, and receives the Assigned
Property only afterward, as a separate and subsequent transaction.

## 3. Assignment

Effective as of the Incorporation Date, Assignor irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to
Assignee all of Assignor's right, title, and interest in and to the Assigned Property, free and
clear of all liens and encumbrances, together with all rights to sue for past, present, and
future infringement.

## 4. Consideration

The Assigned Property is contributed to Assignee as a capital contribution, without the issuance
of any additional shares of stock in exchange. The Assigned Property becomes an asset of the
Assignee, and the contribution increases Assignor's basis in the shares of common stock Assignor
already holds, consistent with treatment under Section 118 (corporate income exclusion) and
Section 362(a)(2) (carryover basis) of the Internal Revenue Code. Consistent with the
corporation's own determination that it has no operating history, revenue, or material assets at
incorporation (see the Organizational Resolutions), the Assigned Property is recorded at $0.00
value for basis purposes — a reasonable, defensible figure for a pre-revenue contribution of
self-created property with no established market value, and simple to document without a paid
appraisal.

## 5. Representations

Assignor represents that Assignor is the sole owner of the Assigned Property, has the full right
and authority to assign it, and that the Assigned Property is not subject to any third-party
license, claim, or encumbrance that would limit Assignee's ownership or use.

## 6. Further Assurances

Assignor agrees to execute any further documents and take any further actions reasonably
requested by Assignee to perfect or evidence the assignment made here, including any assignments
needed to record trademark or domain-name transfers with the applicable registrars or the USPTO.
