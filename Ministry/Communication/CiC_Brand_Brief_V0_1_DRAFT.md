## The big idea (the test every public sentence answers to)

**An open invitation into conversation with the whole church.**
*(Wording revised at Mark's direction 2026-07-17: "honest/honestly" retired as the brand's catch-all word — replaced by function: open/openly, transparent, or the concrete thing. "Open invitation" also carries its everyday meaning: standing, anyone welcome.)*

- **The offer — invitation, conversation:** a chair at the Table; twenty centuries of the church you can actually talk with.
- **The guard — trust, safety, witness:** no pressure, nothing invented, uncertainty said out loud, the mission stated plainly and never pushed.
- **The test:** *does this sound like an open invitation to a real conversation — never a pitch, never a lecture, never a museum?*

## Purpose

To help people experience Jesus in new ways through engagement with the living traditions and practices of His Church throughout history and today. *(The Mission, verbatim and unchangeable — the brand compresses it, never edits it.)* The brand posture beneath it, in Mark's words: a safe, trustworthy, engaging place to explore the church, *"trusting Jesus will be revealed the way Israel revealed Jesus in the grand narrative of the kingdom of God — Jesus at the center, now and not yet."*

## Who it's for (in order)

1. **The unraveling** — on a hard journey, making sense of the church as experience or assumptions come apart. *(The founder is mourner #1: his own deconstruction-to-reconstruction arc through church history is the thesis lived once — public testimony, approved.)*
2. **The stuck** — one point of view, never shown that other ways of knowing Jesus exist.
3. Behind them: pastors & teachers, scholars, donors. **Written rule: the seeker voice is never outranked by the donor voice.**

## The onliness — FINAL (internal strategy test; public form stays "no product we could find")

*The only place you can sit with the church's many movements as they agree and disagree openly across twenty centuries — and ask them anything, with no agenda for where you land.*

*(Mark's flip, 2026-07-17: participant-first — "the only place YOU can sit with…" — the onliness leads with what the person gets to do, not with what the product contains.)*

**The lock:** the disagreement is the credibility. Recruitment tools harmonize their witnesses; CiC lets them disagree in front of you — the built-in answer to the agenda-suspicious skeptic.

## Positioning statement (revised per Mark's direction: engagement framed positively, the door wide)

**For** anyone who wants to discover how differently Christians across twenty centuries have known and followed Jesus — the curious, the searching, the rebuilding — **Church in Conversation is** a table where the church's many movements, rebuilt from their own words, talk with you and with each other: agreeing, disagreeing, and answering openly. **It** shows its sources and its confidence as you talk, and it has no agenda for where you land — a doorway back to living community, not a home. **Unlike** chatbots, AI companions, and historical-figure apps, **it** is governed by a written constitution that forbids persuasion and engagement design. Come and join us at the Table.

*(Note the rule this encodes, per Mark: the public **address** is positive and open — "a way to see differences," no one defined by their wound and no one eliminated — while the internal audience **priority** (the unraveling and the stuck first) still governs design and voice decisions. Address wide, prioritize deep.)*

**"Church in Conversation," no "The,"** is the organization and masterbrand. **Series titles are public-facing packaging** — "the big and then a way to package it": *Conversations with the Early Church* now, siblings per era later (Reformation, etc.), always with the masterbrand. Sanctioned short forms: **CiC** (internal), **"the Table"** (experiential handle). *(Unblocks the Kit thread's wordmark. Flag to the front-end thread: its 2026-07-07 "The Church in Conversation" correction is reversed — L1 usage stands.)*

## Three words

**Transparent · Inviting · Experiential** *(Mark's revision, 2026-07-17 — "honest" → "transparent," which is Conviction 4's own name and the more distinctive claim: every brand says honest; this one shows its work. Later the same day Mark retired "honest/honestly" brand-wide as overused and unclear — replaced by function with open/openly, transparent, or the concrete phrase. Rooted lives on inside the voice's scholarly floor.)*

## Voice (Mark's five pairs)

**Witness but not recruiting · Rigorous but not distracting · Accessible but not shallow · Technology but not seen · Relational but not human**

Register: the **BibleProject balance** — everyday English surface, scholarly floor; conversational ("you don't just read about them — come sit at the table"); one notch playful (relational, never academically dry); modern carrier holding ancient things; strongly welcoming; personal in warmth. North-star voices: **N.T. Wright, Tim Mackie, Justo González** — scholar-hosts; warmth through shared discovery, not comfort.

## Proof (why anyone should believe it)

The build process is the product: review-gated months-long reconstruction from primary sources · participant-visible confidence, down to the footnote · adversarial testing before anyone meets a voice · external scholarly review required — and until complete, every participant is told exactly that · a written constitution that forbids persuasion and engagement design · the witnesses' own open disagreement.

## Never (top of the never-say list)

**"Finding the right answers"** — the wince-line; CiC offers encounter and real questions, never answer-delivery. Then: promised transformation ("this will change your faith" — testified only, never promised; *the brand prays for what it refuses to promise*) · manufactured urgency · "the only" in public copy · hype superlatives · "we'll reconstruct your faith" · engagement-metric boasting · the founder in the spotlight.

## Protected verbal assets (used verbatim, never paraphrased)

"A doorway, not a home" · **"We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. How it impacts the kingdom belongs to our Lord. That's bigger than us."** *(FINAL — 2026-07-18, Mark's own line, replacing "honest and free" through four iterations: "free" read as no-charge; adjective-pair versions stayed foggy; the Holy Spirit tail was cut by Mark's own Christianese ban)* · **"Come and join us at the Table"** (DECIDED 2026-07-17 as the primary CTA — Mark: "sitting isn't of value"; joining is the action, participation is the point. "Come sit at the Table" retires from CTA use; the Kit thread's gap list carries the corpus-wide swap) · the doorway/house hook · "Twenty centuries of the church. One table. A chair pulled out for you." (CONFIRMED by Mark 2026-07-17: the chair stays — the welcome gesture; "come and join us" is the ask)

## The three failure modes (what the brand steers between)

**Recruitment tool** (it pushes) · **entertainment** (it charms and changes nothing) · **neutral museum** (so busy proving no agenda it stops being about Jesus and the kingdom). The witness stands in the middle: testimony stated plainly, never pushed, never traded for respectability.

## Founder

**Backstage.** Visible: his journey (the approved reconstruction testimony) and God's provision. The project's voice is canonical; the Letter to Friends stays a letter.

## The one P1 tester question — DECIDED

**"What, if anything, did you walk away thinking differently about?"**
*(Mark's construction: simple, and deliberately non-leading — "if anything" keeps even the question from pressuring an answer into existence. Hands to the prototype-testing thread's interview protocol.)*
