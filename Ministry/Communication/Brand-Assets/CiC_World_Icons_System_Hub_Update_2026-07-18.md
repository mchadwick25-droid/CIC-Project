## 1. Where the icon work actually stands

**All five live worlds now have a Representative icon, built to one governed family
template.** Each is a connected-bust line emblem in the manuscript register (iron-gall ink
on the era ground), gender- and object-differentiated, with **each world's object and
appearance verified against that world's OWN construction record** before drawing — the
discipline that produced Papnoute's cracked jug, Theon's shared scroll, Yausep's one
Gospel, and Albina's wax tablet rather than generic guesses. Appearance stays emblematic
and is flagged INFERENCE wherever a world's record is silent; the eventual
demographic-reference artifact remains the formal authority for complexions.

Four of five are **locked**; the fifth (Albina) is **approved and one step from locked**.
The **era-ground colour system that the Era-2 icons sit on is approved** and is now the
canonical per-era palette for the whole product (icons + atlas).
