## The mark

The C-as-table — a single ring stroke, opening facing right — with one madder dot
outside the mouth, at the threshold. Ink on parchment. Never any third element.

**The public sentence (the only public meaning, said once, never elaborated in
donor material):** *"The mark is a table; the opening is the way in — and it never
closes."* The mark never appears at first contact without this one plain sentence
beside it. "The red dot is you" is internal rationale, never copy.

## Geometry (refined per R1–R7)

ViewBox 100 · ring center (50,50) r 31 · **opening ±50°** (R1 — widened from ±40°;
doorways are wide) · dot r 6.5 at (88.5, 50) (R2 — threshold, not pellet; never
inside the ring in any state) · madder locked muted `#A13E2B` (R3 — brightened
toward alert-red it becomes a notification badge) · ink `#2A2521` · modulated
master carries the pen logic (R4). R5 favicon testing: the optically-compensated
tile is the source; formal 16/24/32 raster check rides with rasterization. R6: the
wordmark is Alegreya (the decided old-style humanist); lockup rules below. R7
(period-app adjacency) is largely resolved by R1/R2 — confirm in final lockup
contexts.

## Wordmark & lockups (R6)

**Church *in* Conversation** set in Alegreya — roman, italic "in," no "The." The
masterbrand is present on everything public; series titles (*Conversations with
the Early Church*) only ever beneath it, subordinate, never alone. In most
contexts the wordmark carries more identity than the mark — lead with it wherever
words fit. Mark-plus-wordmark lockup: mark at cap-height ×1.6, clearspace between
mark and wordmark = one dot diameter.

## Clearspace & minimums

- **Clearspace:** the ring's stroke width (11/100 of the mark) on all sides,
  minimum. Nothing enters it — including the wordmark outside the lockup spacing
  above.
- **Minimum sizes:** mark alone 16px (from the favicon master below 48px); the
  modulated master never below 48px; lockup minimum 120px wide.

## The never-list

Never a cross, dove, flame, or speech bubble; nothing tech-flavored · the dot
never inside the ring, never duplicated, never another color · the opening never
narrowed, closed, or pointed anywhere but right · madder never brightened · never
on photography without a parchment or ink plate · never spun, bounced, pulsed at
alert speed, or moved to attract a click · the motion never replays unbidden and
never plays the joining at a visitor · `prefers-reduced-motion` receives the
completed still mark, table built, guest seated.

## The companion illustration

The table-and-chair glyph (`CiC_Mark_TableChair_*.svg`, spec in
`CiC_Brand_Mark_TableChair_V0_1.md`) is **illustration, not identity**: empty
states, tour art, print corners — wherever "a chair pulled out for you" deserves
a picture. Never presented as the logo; never in the favicon slot; never adjacent
to the mark in the same composition (two table-metaphors side by side read as two
brands). **It carries its own motion** (Mark, 2026-07-18): the table already
there, the chair *pulled up* from off-right, settling at the decided gap — once,
then stillness; the chair never breathes (the logo's madder is the guest, alive;
this madder is the invitation, furniture) and the pull never tucks the chair
under. Reference: `CiC_Mark_TableChair_Motion_Reference.html`. The adjacency
guard extends to motion — the two motions never play in one view.
