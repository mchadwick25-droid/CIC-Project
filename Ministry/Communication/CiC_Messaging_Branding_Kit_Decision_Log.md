## 2026-07-17 (later) — DECIDED: "honest/honestly" retired as the brand's catch-all word; function-mapped replacement applied

**Mark's direction (verbatim):** "the only thing i struggle with is the honestly,
its a overused and unclea word, can we global replace it with something else,,
authenticly, transparently, or something else."

**Decided:** "honest/honestly" is retired as the brand's catch-all. The replacement
is **function-mapped, not one-for-one**, because the word was doing three jobs:
- Conversational contexts → **open / openly / in the open** (the primary
  replacement: short, warm, invitational — and "an open invitation" gains the
  standing-invitation double meaning; it is also Alpha's own brand value).
- Shows-its-work contexts → **transparent(ly)** (already one of the three words —
  used where it means exactly that, not overloaded everywhere).
- Hard-history truthfulness → **the concrete phrase** ("won't smooth over the hard
  parts," "nothing hidden," "where the record is thin, it says so").
**Rejected: "authentic/authentically"** — the sector's single most overused brand
word; differentiates nothing.

**Applied across the four living brand documents** (Kit, QuickRef, Brand Brief,
Handoff Package): the test is now "an open invitation to a real conversation"; the
big idea "an open invitation into conversation with the whole church"; the onliness
"agree and disagree openly"; M1 retitled "The whole church, in the open"; the
boilerplate and one-liner re-rendered; lexicon row and never-say entries added
banning the catch-all uses.

**One deliberate exception, flagged not assumed:** the measurement line "we measure
whether the encounter was honest and free — never whether you changed" — Mark's own
historic phrase, quoted across the governance-adjacent corpus. It stands verbatim
unless he directs "open and free." **Governance quotations (Article 6 etc.) are
never edited — they are the Constitution's words, not brand copy.**

**Gap list gained item 10:** the corpus-wide honesty-word audit (landing "How is
this honest?" → "What makes this trustworthy?"; elevator one-liner; About
narrative), exempting the flagged measurement line pending Mark's call.

**Next action:** Mark's call on "honest and free" vs. "open and free"; otherwise
the kit review proceeds as before.

## 2026-07-17 (later) — DECIDED: the measurement line becomes "transparent and unforced"

**The exchange, kept because the reasoning matters:** Mark rejected both "honest and
free" and "open and free," proposing "accessible and transparent." The consultant
pushback: "free" is the agency word the "never whether you changed" clause leans on —
an encounter can be accessible and transparent and still coercive. Mark's
clarification (verbatim): "ok it comes accross as free (no charge) that was what i
was responding to" — his objection was the price ambiguity, not the agency claim,
and the ambiguity is real: the product actually is free of charge, inviting exactly
the wrong parse.

**DECIDED (applied):** *"We measure whether the encounter was transparent and
unforced — never whether you changed."* — "transparent" is the brand's own word for
the truth half; "unforced" keeps the agency claim unmistakably about pressure, not
price. (Runner-up offered and standing by if Mark prefers warmth over precision:
"transparent and *yours*.") Applied to the Kit, QuickRef, and Brand Brief; the
corpus-wide swap (elevator speeches, positioning brief, packet material) rides
gap-list item 10.

**Next action:** kit review proceeds; the honesty-word retirement is now complete
with no exceptions.

## 2026-07-17 (later) — FINAL: the measurement line is Mark's own — "represented accurately and engagingly; impact left to the Holy Spirit"

**The path (kept because it teaches how the line was found):** "honest and free"
(historic) → Mark flags "honestly" as foggy → "transparent and unforced" applied →
still foggy; Mark: "do we need an and to say the same thing, we are measuring if the
program accuratly represented the world with no agenda, no recruitment, no bias, no
manipulation" → three no-adjective-pair options offered → **Mark writes the line
himself (verbatim):** "we measure that the christian movement was represented in an
engaging and accurate way, and not its impact, we leave that to the Holy Spirit."

**FINAL (lightly polished, applied to Kit, QuickRef, Brief):**
*"We measure whether each movement was represented accurately and engagingly —
never its impact. We leave that to the Holy Spirit."*

**Why this one works where four drafts didn't:** "engaging and accurate" is not an
adjective pair saying one thing twice — it is the brand's two poles (engaging = the
experiential offer; accurate = the transparent guard), so the "and" earns its
place. And the tail names *who* impact belongs to — sharpening Article 6's
"belongs to them and to God" into the ministry's own voice, fully consistent with
mission-stated-plainly-never-hidden. Register note recorded in the kit: where the
Spirit clause can't travel (academic abstract), quote the first clause and stop —
never paraphrase.

**Next action:** corpus-wide swap rides gap-list item 10; kit review proceeds
(RECOMMENDED visual directions and M1–M4 still awaiting Mark).

## 2026-07-17 (later) — "Christian movement" chosen; the visit/represent lexicon distinction added

**Mark's question:** movement, Christian movement, or Christian world?
**DECIDED: "each Christian movement"** in the measurement line (and portable lines
generally). Reasoning: a protected line travels out of context, so bare "movement"
floats; "Christian world" collides both with the product vocabulary (a world is
what a participant visits) and with the Christendom idiom. "Christian movement" is
self-contained anywhere it lands — and was Mark's own original dictation.

**Lexicon distinction added to the kit (a keeper):** *a world is what you visit; a
Christian movement is what it represents.* The line measures representation, so
movement is its noun.

**The measurement line, complete and final:** *"We measure whether each Christian
movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way — never its impact. That's
bigger than us."*

## 2026-07-17 (later) — DECIDED: the three-word system — tradition / Christian movement / world

**Mark's ruling (verbatim):** "i think it has to be Christian movement" — movement
keeps the claims register.

  "Choose a Tradition" stays; "one tradition, one century" is the hook; the

  only with its definition (as onboarding does), never as the outward category
  term.

(tradition in UI/warm registers, movement in claims, world defined where taught).

## 2026-07-19 — DECIDED: two new binding rules from a real catch on a demo-video draft (BR-33, BR-34)

**Context:** a system-hub thread producing a demonstration video sent copy
containing "you're talking with them, not texting." Mark's reply to that
thread named the problems and directed: "I want to make these principles in

**BR-33 — DECIDED: never clarify a medium (or anything) by negating the
alternative nobody asked about.** Mark, verbatim: "we have a principle, tell
them what it is, not what its not (like your talking with them, you don't
need the not texting.)" This is the existing positive-framing rule (BR-30),
now confirmed to apply beyond "denial of moral suspicion" (won't convert you)
to **any** unprompted negation, including mundane mode-clarification. Added

**BR-34 — DECIDED: "conversation," not "talking," as the default word for
the interaction — and minimize "not X, it's Y" as a sentence shape,
independent of content.** Mark: "lets not over humanize the experiencing,
your are having a conversation, not talking… make sure it doesn't leave a
strong impression that you are talking with a real person. You don't have to
go to your talking to a computer… choose words that can bridge the
connection and the technology aspects." And separately, on stacked negation
specifically: "its a dead AI giveaway." Two distinct findings, both binding:
1. **"Conversation" is the sanctioned default noun** — medium-agnostic

   human speech. A three-column bridge table (too human / bridge / too
   clinical) added to the Kit and the standalone parameters doc.
2. **Heavy "not X, it's Y" phrasing is now flagged as a craft problem, not
   only a tone problem** — it's a recognized AI-writing tic; using it

**What is NOT touched, stated explicitly per Mark's own boundary from
yesterday:** clarifying contrast that names a category ("a doorway, not a
