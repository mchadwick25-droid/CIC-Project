**Recommended (NOT decided — naming is exactly the kind of call this project reserves
for Mark):** confirm the architecture explicitly — "Church in Conversation" as
organization/platform masterbrand on everything public; "Conversations with the Early
Church" as the era-bound series title for the 70–430 CE offering, always tethered to

**Reasoning, including the heart:** the era-bound title is honest about what exists

organization name stay honest forever. This resolves the scope-claim tension without

## 2026-07-17 (later) — Mark completed the center of gravity: two poles, offer and guard

**Mark's direction (recorded):** alongside trust / safety / witness-not-recruitment,
"the other is invitation, conversation." The brand's center is two-poled:

- **The offer — invitation, conversation.** The open chair at the Table; twenty
  centuries of the church you can actually talk with. Already carried by the
  project's own name ("Church in *Conversation*"), the hero copy ("a chair pulled
  out for you"), and the standing CTA ("come sit at the Table").
- **The guard — trust, safety, witness-not-recruitment.** The refusals, visible
  confidence, doorway-not-home — what makes the invitation safe to accept.

**Why this is the right shape (finding, from the benchmark):** the strongest centers
in the space are exactly this pair — charity: water offers hope, guards with proof;
Habitat offers "together, we build," guards with printed research; Alpha offers the
conversation ("Join the Conversation"), guards with no-pressure. An offer without a
guard is hype; a guard without an offer is a compliance document. Sequencing rule the
corpus already practices and the kit should encode: **the invitation leads, the trust
answer stands one breath behind it** (the landing page's hero → "How is this honest?"
anchor is the pattern).

**The heart of it:** trust language alone would make CiC sound like a safety
data sheet for an experience nobody was drawn to; invitation language alone would
make it another warm pitch. The chair pulled out is why anyone comes; the honest
Table is why anyone stays. The name has held both poles all along.

**Applied:** benchmark §1 and §3 updated to state the two-poled center; artifact
republished. This completes the center-of-gravity direction recorded earlier today;
formal confirmation still travels with the §2.3 naming decisions.

**Next action:** the Kit thread's message architecture should be built around the
two poles (offer leads, guard answers) — added to its inputs.

## 2026-07-17 (later) — The mission clarification: the third failure mode named; the brand is never neutral and never coercive

**Mark's words (verbatim, kept):** "we offer a place to explore the church in a safe,
trustworthy and engaging way, trusting Jesus will be revealed in the same way Isreal
revealed Jesus in the grand narritive of the kingdom of God with Jesus as the center,
now and not yet. We do have a mission, we have faith God will use the expereince.
but we still want it to be about the kingdom not an accedemic or historical
experience."

**The finding:** this completes the failure-mode map. The brand can fail three ways:
as a **recruitment tool** (fear 1), as **entertainment that changes nothing**
(fear 2), or as a **neutral museum** — a religious-history exhibit so busy proving it
has no agenda that it stops being about Jesus and the kingdom. The fix for the
agenda-suspicion fear must never be neutrality-performance. The witness stands
between recruitment and museum: it has a testimony and refuses to push it —
Conviction 3 verbatim, now with Mark's biblical warrant (the witnesses reveal Jesus
the way Israel's story does: by testimony, Jesus at the center, kingdom now and not
yet).

**Brand consequence (decided as a finding):** the mission is **stated plainly, never
hidden and never pushed**. What the agenda-suspicious fear is a *hidden* agenda; a
stated mission paired with structural refusals is the only posture honest in both
directions. Softening the Jesus-centeredness to appear neutral would be its own
dishonesty. Mark's verbatim is deliverable-grade for the kit's
theology-of-communication section.

**Applied:** two voice pairs added to the workshop drafts — **expectant but not
engineering**; **kingdom-centered but not academic**. Instrument updated and
republished.

**Next action:** Session 2 remainder still open (pairs confirmation, sliders,
spokesperson, never-say line).
