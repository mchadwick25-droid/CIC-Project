# Church in Conversation — Writer's Quick Reference

**V0.1 · 2026-07-17 · One page. The five-minute version of the full Kit — open this before writing anything public.**

## The test

*Does this sound like an open invitation to a real conversation — never a pitch, never a lecture, never a museum?*

## Say it in one breath

*Church in Conversation rebuilds documented Christian movements from their own words — and you don't just read about them: you join them at a table where they talk with you, and with each other — openly.*

## Three words

**Transparent · Inviting · Experiential**

## The voice (every sentence survives all five)

**Witness but not recruiting** — state the mission plainly; never push.
**Rigorous but not distracting** — scholarship underneath, one click away.
**Accessible but not shallow** — everyday English; full depth reachable.
**Technology but not seen** — experience first, governance second, mechanism third.
**Relational but not human** — real conversation; never pretends to be a person.

## Sound like

N.T. Wright · Tim Mackie · Justo González — a scholar-host at a table. Warmth through shared discovery, not comfort. BibleProject balance; 10th-grade floor.

## The four messages (in order)

1. **The whole church, in the open** — many movements, one table, real disagreement.
2. **It shows its work** — sources and confidence visible; "where the record is thin, it says so."
3. **Witness, never recruitment** — "it will never try to convert you"; "a doorway, not a home"; we measure the representation, never the impact — that's bigger than us.
4. **The build is the product** — months of review-gated scholarship; free to any seeker; "help us build the next world."

## Use verbatim (never paraphrase)

"A doorway, not a home" · "We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. How it impacts the kingdom belongs to our Lord. That's bigger than us." · "Come and join us at the Table" · "Twenty centuries of the church. One table. A chair pulled out for you." · "Where the record is thin, it says so."

## Never

"Finding the right answers" (or any promised answers) · promised transformation (testify — real quotes only — never promise) · urgency or scarcity · "the only / the first" · hype and exclamation marks · engagement numbers as success · the founder in the spotlight · a single tradition as the face of the project · Christianese or project jargon · "users" · "honest/honestly" as the catch-all word (say *open*, *transparent*, or the concrete thing) · "authentic/authentically" · denying a suspicion nobody raised ("not texting," "not a scam") — say what it is, once, and stop · "talking" as the primary word for the interaction (say *conversation* — medium-agnostic, doesn't over- or under-claim) · stacked "not X, it's Y" contrast as a sentence shape, even when the content is true — it reads as generated, not crafted.

## Always

Raise the AI trust question yourself, first — then answer it (locked to sources, shows confidence, tested, reviewed — and what's unfinished, said plainly). Show the work before the caveat. Real dates, no deadlines. Participation verbs: join, ask, meet, explore. Numbers: check with Mark before print. Say "conversation," not "talking" — warm without overclaiming human presence, plain without going clinical ("interacting with an interface"). A clarifying contrast naming what kind of thing this is ("a doorway, not a home") stays fine — the rule targets *denial*, not *category*.

## The name

**Church in Conversation** (no "The") on everything; era titles only in lockup beneath it (*Conversations with the Early Church*). Short forms: CiC (internal), "the Table" (experiential).

## Who you're writing to (address wide, prioritize deep)

Publicly: anyone curious how differently Christians have known Jesus — the curious, the searching, the rebuilding. Silently prioritized: the person whose faith is unraveling, and the person who's never seen past one doorway. **The seeker's voice always outranks the donor's.**
