## 2. The motion (final — Mark: "this is it")

**Alone · built · seated · breathing**, once per page arrival, then stillness:
the red dot alone on empty parchment (~0.8s; mind the phantom round-cap-dot
rendering trap) → the C drawn in one stroke from the *lower lip* around to the
*upper lip* — the doorway completes last, beside the guest (~1.3s; never
both-ends growth: it reads as enveloping) → the weighted sit (~0.6s) → the
candle-breath (opacity-only, 5.5s cycle). Working pure-CSS reference:
`Archive/Ministry-Early-Days-2026-07/Communication/CiC_Logo_Motion_Study_V0_1.html` (V0.8) — lift it.

**Binding motion rules:** the dot never enters the circle (no animation plays
the joining at a visitor) · the opening is constant — the mark never reacts to
the cursor · never spin / bounce / pulse-at-alert-speed / move-to-attract-a-click
· `prefers-reduced-motion` gets the completed still mark as a full experience.

## 3. Confirmed convergences (your decisions, now brand record — build on them)

- **Typefaces: Alegreya + Alegreya Sans** (OFL) — adopted into the brand record;
  exactly the humanist old-style direction the Foundations specified. Wordmark
  treatment "Church *in* Conversation" (italic "in") stands. Never drift to a
  geometric sans (the personas: "the bookishness is half the trust").
- **Palette: your hex values stand** as the manuscript pigments made real —
  parchment `#F7F3EB` · iron-gall `#2A2521` · madder `#A13E2B` (the one action
  accent — **locked muted**; brightened toward alert-red it becomes a
  notification badge) · gold-leaf `#B45309` · lapis `#1E40AF` · Tyrian `#6B3FA0`
  · graphite `#8A837C`. The rubrication story (the red mark beside black text is
  where reading begins) is yours to use in the rationale — never in donor copy.
- **The app's role-color language** (distinct hue per speaker role) is endorsed —
  converge the three visual systems (app, World Map, Chloe Tour) onto this one
  palette, per your own thread's "one visual identity, not two."

## 4. UI copy rules (the ones that bite screens; full set in the Kit + QuickRef)

- **Lockups:** the masterbrand "Church in Conversation" (no "The") on everything;
  "Conversations with the Early Church" only ever beneath it, subordinate; never
  the series title alone.
- **The three-word system:** *tradition* = outward chooser word ("Choose a
  Tradition" stays) · *Christian movement* = claims register · *world* = inward,
  surfaces only with its definition.
- **Voices:** Representatives = "we" (capital-R in UI); the Facilitator = "I,"
  always. People are *participants*, never users. Every voice is anchored —
  "representative voice," never a bare floating "voice."
- **CTA:** "Come and join us at the Table" (invitation slots only; functional
  buttons stay functional). The chair pulled out = welcome gesture, kept.
- **The mark never appears at first contact without one plain sentence beside
  it.** The approved public sentence: *"The mark is a table; the opening is the
  way in — and it never closes."* Nothing more; "the red dot is you" is internal.
- **Never in UI copy:** "finding the right answers" · promised transformation ·
  urgency/scarcity · "honest/honestly" as a device (retired; use open /
  transparent / the concrete phrase) · "AI" as a headline · a single tradition as
  the face of anything.
- **Imagery governance:** period art or plainly labeled reconstructions only;
  never AI-generated scenes presented as the world. (The Chloe Tour's concrete
  caption system — "A REAL PLACE, AS IT SURVIVES" — is the pattern.)
