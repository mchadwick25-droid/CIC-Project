## Pahc / Chloe — merged from the built Hosted-Tour demo + the current (2026-09-01) reconstruction approach

**Status:** DRAFT, shaped in conversation with Mark, not yet built, not yet reviewed line-by-line. The prime test case for the zero-credit engagement layer.

**What this is:** not a rewrite from scratch. `Hosted-Tour/Design/CiC_Chloe_Tour_Interactive_Demo.html` (V0.2, 2026-07-16) is a real, already-built, already-licensed 9-stop demo of this exact scene — screenshot-verified in this session as genuinely well produced: manuscript aesthetic, a working source-cartouche pattern, real credited photography, honest captioning throughout. Mark's direction: use it as a source. Its *assets and structure* carry forward wherever they already do the job. Its *rule* — no reconstructed imagery under any circumstance, because this world's own record is archaeologically invisible — is superseded. Mark's own words, this session: *"i dont have a nothing invented ever rule... the phrase came from conversation boundaries and containing ai to a rigorous standard in creating dialogue, not a general rule that dictates whether we use... an old building as inspiration to build a replica."* Williamsburg is the governing analogy: informed, honestly-labeled reconstruction is legitimate; nothing here changes what the live Representative may claim in a real conversation.

**What updates, concretely:** informed AI-grounded reconstruction is now allowed for tour visuals (Williamsburg model — real evidence in, honestly captioned as reconstruction, never claimed as a photograph); tour sourcing may draw on real material outside the pinned world records, and may merge sources to build a picture, as long as origin stays visibly tagged per element; narration moves from the demo's neural edge-tts to ElevenLabs; static photography may gain real motion (Ken Burns on real photos; Luma Ray3 / Kling for grounded reconstruction shots) rather than staying still.

## Stops that change under the current approach

### Stop 0 — The Door (rebuilt)

**Old approach:** the House of the Menander at Pompeii, real, licensed, explicitly labeled *"the setting culture's house, not a gathering place of this community."*

**Current approach:** a grounded reconstruction, produced in Luma Ray3, image-to-video, built from real reference stacked three ways — (1) the **Casa di Diana at Ostia**, a real, dated (c. 130 CE), well-documented insula squarely inside this world's own window; (2) Justin's own biographical detail, a rented room above a bathhouse, from his trial record; (3) general Roman housing scholarship on insula life (shops below, modest apartments above, most residents living exactly this way, not in a private domus). Motion: a slow entry, camera settling where Chloe places the participant — "by the wall, where you can see the whole room."

**Cartouche behavior (per this session's UI discussion):** the label appears on entry — *"A reconstruction, not a photograph"* — then fades within a second or two to a small quiet mark in the Tyrian-purple transparency-apparatus family, sitting in the frame's corner. Hover (tap on phone) resurfaces the short form; click/tap-through gives the full trail — Casa di Diana, Justin's own record, the housing scholarship, named separately, not blended into one unlabeled claim.

**Voiceover (ElevenLabs, Facilitator + Chloe, tagged by origin per this session's convention):**
> **[Facilitator — WORLD RECORD]** Justin taught in a rented room above a bathhouse here in Rome. **[OUTSIDE SOURCE — Roman housing scholarship]** A room like his sat above shops, one floor of many, holding far more families than any single house could. **[Facilitator, on the image]** What you're seeing is built to match that — real proportions, drawn from a building that still stands in Ostia — but it's a reconstruction. Nobody photographed Justin's room. This is our best honest guess at its shape.
>
> **[Chloe]** Come in — stand there, where you can see the whole room. We keep it the way it's always kept before anyone arrives.

### Stop 7 — What We Cannot Show You (reframed, not removed)

Still a first-class stop, still the world's own record running out exactly where a visitor most wants to go — but its opening premise shifts slightly now that Stop 0 shows *something*:

> **[Chloe]** What you just walked into is a reconstruction, honestly. Here is what even that can't give you. The exact words of the prayers and the thanksgiving — Justin says only "according to his ability"; no fixed text was ever kept. And of everyone who might have filled a room like this, across this whole world's record, two names survive: Tavia, and the wife of Epitropus. A name and a greeting. Nothing more.

The accent decline and the singing decline (Pliny, the ministrae, their cost) carry forward exactly as built.
