A Sunday Gathering in Rome

as Justin describes it · a hosted tour of The Scattered Churches, 70–200 CE

Χ Your host: Chloe, Host of the Assembly  ·  leave whenever you wish — the door stays open
The tour pauses — it does not ignore you

“Who is the one presiding — a bishop?”

CHLOE
You have asked the question we ourselves have not settled. In some households one bishop stands at the center. In others a council of presbyters governs together, and nothing feels missing. I have real contact with both. Justin says only: the one presiding. I will not close for you what our own life has not closed.
