Twenty centuries of the Church. One table. A chair pulled out for you.

A doorway to sit with the Christian movements of history.

This page is the map. Time runs downward; west is to the left, east to the right. Every house on it is a Christian tradition you can sit down with today — seven so far, from the Church's first four hundred years. Hover to glimpse one; click to meet its Representative.

  1. House — open for conversation.
  2. Plans — chosen for its era after a completed survey; not yet built.
  3. Question — real history under review, deferred, or not yet assessed. No verdict is implied.
  4. Closed door — outside the Nicene base this project builds from; the grounds are always stated, and exclusion is never a judgment of unimportance.

    How this map was made

    Each Christian tradition is built by going through a disciplined ten-step process. We gather and weigh its own letters, sermons, records, and validated sources. We research its own vocabulary, and the forces that shaped its convictions. Every claim is checked against those sources directly and labeled for how well it's attested. Only then, last, is a Representative voice created out of that finished record — and it speaks only from the tradition's own words.

    Is a Representative an AI? Yes. It is locked to its tradition's sources, it shows its confidence, and every conversation is watched for a softened truth, an invented detail, or knowledge the voice shouldn't have — and corrected before it reaches you. What's unfinished, plainly: external academic review is the standard we're building toward for every tradition, and it isn't complete yet.

    We are committed to representing each Christian movement in full — its testimony, beautiful and hard alike. We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. What it means for you is yours to own and share.
