## 2. The floor test (Constitution Article 4 — Movement-Scope Principle)

The census's own `floorNote`, which the Era 8 gate cited approvingly "as a
clean example that the floor is the Creed and not liturgical correctness":

> "No question of doctrine arises here at all. Old Believers held exactly
> the same creed as the church they left — the split was about ritual:
> how to make the sign of the cross, how to spell the name of Jesus,
> which way a procession should walk around a church."

Avvakum's own dialogue with the Eastern patriarchs at the Chudov
Monastery (`avvakum_life-of-archpriest-avvakum_harrison-mirrlees1924.txt`,
pp. 120-121) is fought entirely on the ground of inherited ritual
practice and the authority to alter it — that narrower claim, scoped to
this one exchange, is accurate. But twelve pages earlier in the same
file (p. 34), Avvakum argues the wording of the Nicene Creed's own eighth
article directly, in the register of divine essence: the Nikonian
removal of "the True" from "the Lord, the True and Life-giving" empties
the Creed, on his own argument, of "the essence of God." That is a claim
about the Creed's own text and content, not about ritual gesture, made by
this world's central voice at the opening of its own central text.

**The floor, stated at the precision the source supports:** the schism
was not a collision between two different confessions of faith — neither
side charged the other with a new doctrine of God or of Christ, and both
professed the same Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed as their own. The
dispute, across the bulk of the movement's own record, is over
corrections to inherited practice and the authority to make them. **But
the disputed corrections included the Creed's own wording, and Avvakum
argues that particular change in explicitly theological terms.** This
world clears Constitution Article 4's floor test comfortably, on the
Creed's overall content and both sides' shared profession of it, on this
accurate, qualified statement. Doc_01 §9 carries this in full, with both
passages quoted, and registers the Creed-wording dispute as a
`contested_claim` candidate rather than suppressing it to keep the floor
claim clean.

**This is a ritual-and-textual schism, not a doctrinal one in the sense
of a rival confession of faith.** Doc_01's own boundary and orientation
work carries this framing forward.

## 3. Second screening criterion (individual-revelation test)

Per the corrected Section A framing on record (`CiC_Step0_Conclusion_
FINAL_v2.docx`, Phase One's own document, cited here only for the general
screening principle it states, not as authority over Era 8's own separate
gate): a movement can clear the doctrinal floor and still warrant scrutiny
if its whole authority claim rests on a small number of named individuals'
personal revelation rather than a broader communal interpretive
tradition. The Old Believers do not fail this test. Avvakum is the
movement's central voice and martyr, but the movement's own claim to
legitimacy rests on continuity with the received Muscovite liturgical
tradition itself — the position is that THEY kept what the whole Russian
church held before 1653-1667, not that Avvakum revealed something new.
The Solovetsky petitioners, the Vyg community's Pomorian Answers (1723),
and the broader communal argument all rest on the same claim of
inheritance and continuity, not on one man's personal authority. Clears
this test.

## 4. Open scope question carried forward, not decided here

The movement divided, within this world's own window, between those who
retained priests (popovtsy) and those who concluded a valid priesthood
could no longer be had (bezpopovtsy) — a real internal division with
consequences for ecclesiology. This is a distinct question from §2: §2
concerns the Creed's own wording in the ritual-and-textual dispute
specifically, evidenced in Avvakum's own generation; the strand question
here is about the later, separate ecclesiological argument that a valid
priesthood could no longer be had at all — neither question resolves the
other. Whether this world is single-stranded (built around the
founding-generation, Avvakum-centered voice, with the later branching
noted as a later development) or strand-plural (requiring full Article-21
strand analysis, per the PAHC/IJC precedent) is **not decided at this
stage**. Doc_01 states the question and this Step 0's own reasoning for
leaving it open; a firm decision is deferred until closer to the M1
Representative-identity checkpoint, when the actual weight of each
strand's own evidentiary base is better known. Logged in this world's
`Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` (entry 1).
