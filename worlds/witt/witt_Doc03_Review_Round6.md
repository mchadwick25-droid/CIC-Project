# Root cause

Round 5 named the root cause precisely and Revision 5 acted on it precisely: the fix was scoped to *the claim type* rather than to *where the last finding landed*, across all four locations, and on the read side it worked — verifiably, from the files, including in the one place with a route into another canonical document.

The reconciliation was built around one question: **is every statement that something was read true?** It answered that question completely. It never asked the complementary one: **is every statement that something was *not* read true?** So §11 item 2's three *new* LC entries are exact complements of §13's declared ranges — they were derived that way, as the log says — while the *pre-existing* sentences in the same list, written by hand across Rounds 1–4, were carried forward untested. Both R6-1 and R6-2 live in those carried-forward sentences, and R6-3 is the same asymmetry reaching §11 item 8.

The claim type, stated at full width, is: **any statement in this document about whether a named structural unit was read — in either direction.** Round 5 got the first half of that. This round is the second half. A pass that closes it would derive §11 item 2 wholly as the arithmetic complement of §13's declared ranges — every entry, not only the three added at Revision 5 — reconcile §11 item 8 against the Registry rows it proposes to extend rather than only against the files, and check the affirmative "not cited by any entry" statements by grep rather than by reasoning about newly-marked zones.

Two things are worth saying against any inference that this is a sixth recurrence:

1. **It is not the escalated class.** Five rounds found overclaims. This round looked for a sixth overclaim, in the reconciled locations and outside them, and found none. The three findings here all understate coverage. Nothing is unwarranted, nothing false travels to the Registry, and no participant-facing or downstream claim rests on anything that was not read.
2. **The defects are older than the pass that was supposed to catch them.** R6-1's two sentences date from Round 3's R3-1 fix and survived Rounds 4 and 5 unremarked; R6-2's LC sentence is older still. Revision 5 did not introduce them — its scope simply did not reach them, and neither did any prior round's.

That said, the plain answer to the question the brief put is: **the reconciliation does not yet hold across all four locations.** §11 item 2 disagrees with §13 about Hymn I and about the Fourth, Fifth and Sixth Commandments; §11 item 8 disagrees with the Source Registry about the AC Conclusion and about the LC's read extent; §12 inherits the first disagreement. Whether that warrants a further pass, a change order, or disclosure and carry-forward is not this review's to decide.
