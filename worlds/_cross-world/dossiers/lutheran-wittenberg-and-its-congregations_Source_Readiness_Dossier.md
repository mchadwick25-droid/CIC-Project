## Step 0 scope notes (for whoever drafts Doc_01/the Step 0 confirmation)

**Doctrinal floor clears cleanly.** Augsburg Confession Article I explicitly affirms the Nicene decree by name (one divine essence, three persons, condemning Arians/Manichaeans/etc.); Article III affirms full deity and full humanity of Christ, virgin birth, true death/burial, descent, resurrection, ascension, session, and return to judge. Luther's catechisms and Melanchthon's Apology are consistent throughout. Unlike most patristic-era candidates, this is not a live question.

**Real, disclosable complications** (name in Doc_02, don't smooth over): (1) Luther's late-life antisemitic writings (*On the Jews and Their Lies*, 1543) — serious, well-documented, needs explicit disclosure discipline. (2) The Eucharistic break with Zwingli/the Reformed (Marburg Colloquy, 1529) — a sibling-rival tension the census's own `relationsSummary` already flags, and which this same research batch's Reformed-cities candidate independently confirms from its own side. (3) The Peasants' War (1525) and *Against the Murderous, Thieving Hordes of Peasants* — politically fraught, needs careful framing. (4) Conflict with the Anabaptists — persecution this same batch's Anabaptist dossier also flags from its own side. None of these threaten doctrinal-floor clearance; all are within-tradition disclosure obligations of the same kind the Latin Apologists' own Step 0 models for Minucius Felix, Lactantius, and Arnobius.
