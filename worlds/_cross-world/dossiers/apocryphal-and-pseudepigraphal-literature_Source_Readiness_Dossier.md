| M.R. James, *The Apocryphal New Testament* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1924) | The standard English translation/collection of NT apocrypha; a prior pass flagged it as "a new find" | **Correction to that earlier characterization.** Three archive.org items checked directly: two library-scanned copies (`apocryphalnewtes0000unse_b9n3`, `apocryphalnewtes0000mont_n1i9`) carry Controlled Digital Lending markers (`urn:acs6:`/`urn:lcp:`) and no public-domain declaration — borrow-only, not downloadable. The one freely-downloadable copy (`JAMESApocryphalNewTestament1924`) is community-uploaded under a **Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0** license — not a public-domain mark, and NC-ND is not reconcilable with genuine 1924-vintage PD status as an authoritative rights determination. The 1924 date likely does put the underlying work in US public domain by the standard rule, but that has to be established independently of what archive.org itself is asserting on any of these three items — not yet done. Do not add to the queue on the strength of the earlier characterization. |

## 5. Open cross-world questions

**Genuinely unresolved, not decided here, per the source-readiness process's own limits:** whether "Apocryphal and Pseudepigraphal Literature" is coherent as a single formation "movement" at all, the way this project's other candidate worlds are — or whether it's a text-classification label spanning many disconnected authors, communities, and centuries (its own time window, c. 100–900, is wider than every other candidate combined). The corpus-map's own staging notes flag this repeatedly. A strong, well-linked source base doesn't resolve whether this is a viable single world — that's a scope/governance question for a Step 0, not a sourcing question this dossier can close.
