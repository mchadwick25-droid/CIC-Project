# Cold Review, Round 1 — Representative Voice Construction (Phase Three), World #1

**Reviewed document:** `CiC_W1_Representative_Voice_Construction.md`

**Method:** Independent review, zero visibility into drafting. Every direct quotation and every specific citation in the document was checked against the cited source document directly (not against the drafting document's own paraphrase of that source), including: the Ecology Assessment, Formation Calibration, Identity Preliminary Decision, Doc_06 (Full Interpretive Lexicon, including its tier/tag table and its cross-reference-cluster description), Doc_07 (Integrated Ecology Analysis), Doc_08 (Forces Document), Doc_09 story chunk `pahcstory001_ignatius-guarded-journey.md` and `pahcstory007_hermas-visions.md`, the World Profile, Doc_02 (Source Ecology), the governing Representative Construction Framework V3.2 (Part Five and Part Nine, converted from the docx and read directly), and the L4 Representative Construction Notes Template v2.2. Particular attention was given to the three items the document's own Document Log flags as new synthesis, since these are not inherited findings and carry no prior review history.

---

## Verdict: QUALIFIED PASS

**What this means:** the document's core structure is sound, its disclosure discipline is generally strong (it consistently distinguishes inherited findings from its own new reasoning, and it flags its own three synthesis items honestly), and no fabricated quotation or reversed/inverted claim was found. However, this review found multiple real, independently verifiable defects — two of them tag-citation errors bearing directly on one of the document's own flagged new-synthesis arguments, one a source misattribution, and one an unsupported characterization used as load-bearing evidence inside the document's hardest-scrutiny synthesis item. These should be fixed before this document is treated as clean, on the same pattern as the two prior phase documents in this build (both cleared as Qualified Pass with defects fixed).

## Findings

### 1. Two Doc_06 tag-citation errors in the Tier 2 "native vs. requiring caution" sorting (Section 3)

**What:** Section 3 ("Tier 2 — native to Chloe's own household/pastoral-instructional voice") cites specific tag strings from Doc_06's tag table (§3) for two terms, and both are wrong against that table.

- *Ministrae* is cited as "AS/SC/RT tagged" (line: "*Ministrae.* AS/SC/RT tagged Latin outside-vocabulary..."). Doc_06 §3's actual tag table (`CiC_W1_Doc06_Interpretive_Lexicon_FINAL.md`, line 75) shows *ministrae* tagged **AS and DR only** — not SC, and not RT.
- The "agape" label is cited as "SC/TC/RT/CT tagged." Doc_06 §3's actual table (line 76) shows the "agape" label tagged **SC, DR, RT, CT** — the document substitutes TC for the actual DR tag.

**Why it matters:** this is precisely the evidentiary mechanism Section 3 uses to justify its own flagged new-synthesis sorting of Tier 2 terms into "native" versus "requiring caution" for Chloe specifically (Document Log, item 2). Both errors happen to fall on terms placed in the "native" column, and both errors involve a tag genuinely present in the row (DR) being silently dropped or replaced with a tag not present (SC/TC) — not a wholesale fabrication, but a real transcription error in exactly the data this section holds out as its own precise, source-checked basis for sorting. It does not appear to change the "native" classification's ultimate defensibility, but the document's own explicit tag citations are inaccurate and should not be republished as-is.

**Severity: significant.**

### 2. Source misattribution: "the church of God" quote cited to the wrong document (Section 3, Register derivation)

Doc_02 §1.2 is the 1 Clement entry in the Source Registry (visibility, representativeness, influence, limitations, transmission history) and does **not** contain this phrase anywhere. The phrase "the church of God" (1 Clement's own opening salutation) actually originates in `CiC_W1_Doc03_Lexicon_Candidate_List_FINAL.md` (line 50: "'the church of God which sojourns at Rome, to the church of God which sojourns at Corinth'") and is also used, correctly cited to different sources, in Doc_07 and the World Profile §1. Doc_02 §1.2 is simply the wrong citation for this specific quote.

**Severity: significant** (a real citation-to-wrong-document error, of the kind the review brief specifically asked to check for).

### 3. Unsupported characterization inside the document's own hardest-scrutiny synthesis item: "Grapte's own plain instructional function" (Section 3, Register derivation)

**What:** The register-derivation argument — the first of the three items the document's own Document Log flags as new synthesis requiring the hardest scrutiny — states that Chloe's base register "should sit closer to the Two Ways tradition's own terse, practical, teaching-oriented mode and to **Grapte's own plain instructional function**" than to Ignatius's or 1 Clement's registers. No source anywhere in this build characterizes Grapte's or Hermas's material as "plain." To the contrary:

- The same document, four paragraphs earlier in the same section, characterizes Hermas's own material as **"visionary and instructional rather than argumentative"** (quoting World Profile §7's "Hermas's own visionary literature").
- Doc_09's own chunk file for the Grapte material (`pahcstory007_hermas-visions.md`, Tier Justification) explicitly requires a "genre distinction" disclosure because the material is **apocalyptic/visionary** in form — an elderly woman who is secretly the Church, a Shepherd-figure delivering angelic instruction — not because it is plain or unadorned.
- Nowhere in the Ecology Assessment, Formation Calibration, Doc_07, Doc_08, or Doc_09 is Grapte's or Hermas's own register described as "plain."

**Why it matters:** this word is doing real argumentative work — it is one of two named anchors (alongside the Two Ways tradition) for the register-derivation argument's central claim that Chloe's base register should NOT default to Ignatius's elaborated rhetoric. But the actual source genre for the Grapte material is visionary/apocalyptic, which is not self-evidently "plain" in the way Two Ways catechesis is (terse, procedural, non-narrative) — if anything it cuts the other way. This is exactly the kind of overreach the review brief asked to test the register-derivation argument for, and it is a real one: an invented descriptor, used as load-bearing evidence, that is not only unsourced but arguably inconsistent with the document's own genre characterization stated a few paragraphs earlier.

**Severity: significant.**

### 4. Framework's "formation axis" determination is never made explicit (Section 1, Reasoning Mode — Framework compliance)

**What:** Framework V3.2 Part Five states that a Representative's mode of engagement is "shaped by the world's own formation axis, which may be reasoning, worship, communal practice, ascetic discipline, or affective devotion rather than intellectual argument," that "where a world's formation axis is intellectual, its reasoning mode carries this weight; where the axis is worship, communal life, ascetic practice, or affective devotion, that axis carries it instead," and that "these formation lenses are co-equal, and which one is paramount is world-derived." This is a specific, named determination the Framework asks a builder to make.

This world's own construction record (World Profile §3, "The Formation Mechanism") states directly: "Two mechanisms did nearly all of this world's formative work, and they are precisely this world's only two Primary gravities. The correspondence network (G02) transmitted... The eucharist (G07) did the parallel work ritually." These are explicitly presented as **co-equal**, not as reasoning-primary-with-worship-secondary. Yet Voice Construction's Section 1 (Reasoning Mode) is built entirely around the "argued, not inherited" reasoning/argument frame (Doc_07 §2E) without ever stating whether reasoning is in fact this world's paramount formation axis for Chloe specifically, or whether — given Chloe's own role as eucharistic host and the eucharist's status as an equally Primary, equally rich domain for her — the worship/ritual axis should carry equal or greater weight in shaping how she "takes up a question" (the Framework's own phrase for what the formation axis governs). The document never surfaces this Framework-named choice at all; it simply builds "Reasoning Mode" as labeled by the L4 template's Section 2 heading, without addressing whether that label's weighting assumption fits this specific world.

**Severity: moderate** — a real, specifically-named Framework requirement that goes unaddressed, though not one that necessarily changes any specific claim already made in Sections 1–4.

### 6. Quotation fidelity: Doc_07 §2G's "three postures" characterization presented as one continuous verbatim quote (Section 1, item 3 of "what was considered and rejected")

**What:** The document quotes: "Doc_07 §2G's own account of this world's revelatory interpretive posture describes Hermas as one of three distinct postures 'existing side by side, none... subordinated to the others.'" Checked against Doc_07 §2G directly: the actual text is "three postures existing side by side, 'none of these subordinated to the others within this world's own lifetime' (Doc_05 §6.1)" — where only "none of these subordinated to the others within this world's own lifetime" is itself a quotation (from Doc_05 §6.1, nested inside Doc_07's own prose), and "three postures existing side by side" is Doc_07's own unquoted framing, not a quotation of anything. Voice Construction's version folds both fragments into a single quoted string, obscuring that only part of it is verbatim and silently dropping "of these" and "within this world's own lifetime" without an ellipsis marking that specific internal cut cleanly.

**Why it matters:** this project's own build history has flagged quotation-not-verbatim as a recurring defect requiring vigilance (a Doc_04 G04 issue was found and fixed in both prior phase documents). This is a smaller instance of the same pattern — not fabrication, but blending paraphrase and verbatim text into what reads as one clean quotation.

**Severity: moderate.**

### 7. Quote's referent silently shifted: Formation Calibration's "within and alongside" language re-applied from function to vocabulary usage (Section 3, presbyterion discussion)

**What:** Section 3 states that when Chloe's household sits inside a Strand A configuration, "her use of this term [presbyterion] should be voiced 'within and alongside' that office's own vocabulary... (Formation Calibration §1)." The actual Formation Calibration §1 sentence this quotes reads: "her instructional function there should be voiced as exercised *within and alongside* that office, not as replacing or equaling it" — i.e., the quoted phrase governs how Chloe's Grapte-derived **instructional function** (teaching authority) should be exercised relative to a bishop's office, not specifically how she should use the **word** "presbyterion." Voice Construction quotes the words verbatim but re-applies them to a different, narrower referent (term usage rather than exercised function) than the one Formation Calibration actually addressed.

**Severity: minor** (the underlying register-caution point about presbyterion is independently well-supported by Doc_06's own PV-tag rationale, quoted accurately elsewhere in the same paragraph; this is a secondary, supporting citation whose context has been stretched).

## Summary

This is a well-disciplined document that generally does what it claims to do, and no fabricated quotation, inverted claim, or dishonest confidence-inflation was found. But the review surfaced a real cluster of defects concentrated exactly where the document itself asked for the hardest scrutiny: two Doc_06 tag-citation errors inside the Tier 2 native/caution sorting, a source misattribution for a quoted phrase, and — most significant — an unsupported "plain" characterization of Grapte's instructional register that is used as load-bearing evidence in the register-derivation argument and that arguably contradicts the document's own "visionary" characterization of the same material stated a few paragraphs earlier. A further Framework-compliance gap (the unaddressed "formation axis" determination) and a handful of smaller quotation-fidelity and citation-precision issues round out the findings. None of this appears to overturn the document's core conclusions, but each item is independently verifiable, several sit inside the document's own flagged new-synthesis claims, and all should be corrected before this document is treated as clean — consistent with the Qualified Pass disposition both prior phase documents in this build received.
