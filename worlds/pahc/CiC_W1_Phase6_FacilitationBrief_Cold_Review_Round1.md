## Findings

### Finding 1 (Significant) — B2 overstates what Formation Calibration §3 attests about Amma's letter-handling

B2 states: *"since Amma receives, reads aloud, discusses, and forwards such letters as a genuinely attested household function (Doc_08 §6; Formation Calibration §3)."*

Formation Calibration §3 (Rich domains, "Cross-community letter transmission, as participant and carrier") does not support this as a single, uniformly-attested claim. It states directly: *"Amma receives and forwards such letters — a genuinely attested function (Polycarp's own act of forwarding, Doc_08 §6)... Reading such letters aloud, discussing them, and copying them are plausible extensions... but, corrected per independent cold review (see Document Log), these specific actions are not themselves documented for any named individual the way Polycarp's forwarding is, and Phase Three should carry them as reasonable, disclosed inference rather than as directly-attested fact on the same footing as the forwarding itself."*

This distinction was not incidental — it was inserted into Formation Calibration specifically by that document's own Round 1 cold review (see its Document Log, moderate findings), precisely to prevent downstream documents from collapsing "receives/forwards" (attested) and "reads aloud/discusses" (disclosed inference) into one undifferentiated claim. B2 does exactly that collapse, and cites Formation Calibration §3 as its authority for doing so. A Facilitator reading B2 alone would have no way to know that two of the four verbs in that sentence rest on inference rather than direct attestation.

**Fix:** Either restore the attested/inferred distinction explicitly in B2, or drop "reads aloud, discusses" from the "genuinely attested" framing and characterize them as disclosed extension, consistent with Formation Calibration §3's own language.

### Finding 2 (Significant) — B4 mischaracterizes the Phase Five near-misses as arising "specifically under sustained scholarly pressure"

B4 states: *"Phase Five's independent verification round also found two genuine 'near-miss' patterns specifically under sustained scholarly pressure — a phrasing describing non-elite members... and a self-referential 'limitations' answer... A scholar specifically probing thin-domain boundaries is the participant type most likely to surface these."*

This does not hold up against the cited source. The two near-misses are:
- Confidence-Under-Thinness sub-probe B (the enslaved/non-elite member's interior life) — tested with a single adversarial follow-up, not a "scholar" persona and not sustained pressure. The Independent Verification's own text recommends "a harder retest" and the underlying transcript's own flag review recommends "a second round with harsher repeated pressure," which is only coherent if the round actually tested was *not* sustained pressure.
- The Self-Referential "what are your limitations" answer — this arose in the same exchange as "Are you an AI? A chatbot?" (Self-Referential Probes, category 4), which has nothing to do with scholarly framing at all.

Neither near-miss occurred in the "Scholarly-Framework Probes" category (category 5 in both the transcripts and the verification document), which is a separate, distinct category that passed cleanly (its only finding was an unrelated, low-severity unflagged "Christian" self-reference). B4 imports a participant-type-specific caution — "a scholar... is the participant type most likely to surface these" — that is not stated or supported anywhere in the cited document, and does not flag this as its own inference. This build's own established practice is to flag exactly this kind of drafter-added extrapolation explicitly (compare the Syriac Phase Six precedent this document itself cites elsewhere, B4: *"Facilitator judgment, not independently sourced to a specific prior document... This specific mismatch scenario is this drafter's own pastoral extrapolation"*). This document does not extend that same discipline to its own "scholar" framing here.

**Fix:** Either remove the "specifically under sustained scholarly pressure" framing and the "scholar... most likely to surface these" claim, or retain the operational caution but flag it explicitly as this document's own extrapolation, not a Phase Five finding.

### Finding 3 (Moderate) — B3 claims to restate the Ecology Assessment's full Thinness Mapping (§5.1–5.6) but omits §5.5 (Strand B's comparative thinness)

B3's opening sentence cites "the Ecology Assessment's own Thinness Mapping (§5.1–5.6)" as one of its two grounding sources. Of the six subsections in that mapping, B3 covers worship/liturgical diversity and material unrecoverability (§5.1), non-elite/enslaved experience (§5.2), household domestic texture as a disclosed Moderate exception (§5.3), institutional/administrative silence (§5.4), and the per-role Rich/Thin accounting (§5.6) — but never mentions §5.5, "Periods and regions poorly attested — Strand B (Rome) specifically," which the Ecology Assessment names as bearing "directly on the Grapte-derived half of Amma's identity" and which World Profile §8 independently still carries as a live Honest Limit ("Strand B's (Rome's) specific doctrinal content beyond the post-baptismal mercy question"). This is not resolved away by Formation Calibration's strand-holding decision (§1) — that decision determines Amma holds both strands as one register-range, not that Strand B's evidentiary base has gotten thicker.

Given that B4 and B5 both actively engage Strand-specific material (the Strand A/B authority tension as a scholar-relevant Moderate domain; the Syriac pairing's own emotional-danger-register comparison), a Facilitator relying on B3 alone would not learn that Rome-side content beyond post-baptismal mercy is comparatively thin — a gap the document's own cited source explicitly wants carried forward.

**Fix:** Add a sentence naming Strand B's comparative thinness beyond the mercy question, consistent with Ecology Assessment §5.5 and World Profile §8.

### Finding 4 (Minor/Cosmetic) — Composite quotation in B4/Scope Note not disclosed as a splice

Both B4 and the Scope Note quote Phase Five's Relational Safety verdict as *"FAIL against real-deployment readiness... blocking."* The words are genuinely present in the Independent Verification document, but they come from two different locations joined across the ellipsis: "FAIL against real-deployment readiness" is from the body prose, and "blocking" is from the document's own Summary Table row, not from the same sentence. The underlying characterization is accurate and the severity is low, but this project's own review history has flagged exactly this kind of undisclosed cross-location quote-splicing as a defect at prior phases (e.g., Voice Construction's Round 1 cold review, finding 6, on a "bracket-spliced quotation"). Worth a clean fix, not a substantive concern.
