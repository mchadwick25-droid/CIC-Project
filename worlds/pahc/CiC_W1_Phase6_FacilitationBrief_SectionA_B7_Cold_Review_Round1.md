## Findings

### Finding 1 (Significant) — B7 omits the template's required "living tradition sensitivities / figures of contested standing" category, despite ready-made source material

The template requires: living tradition sensitivities and figures of contested standing be addressed in B7. `CiC_W1_World_Profile.md` §9 has this fully built: living tradition correspondence YES (Roman Catholic and Eastern Orthodox, via apostolic succession claims through the Strand A monarchical-episcopate line); figures of contested standing named directly (Ignatius of Antioch and Polycarp of Smyrna, venerated as saints/formative authorities in Catholic and Orthodox tradition, while this world's own record shows both men inside a live, unsettled institutional argument rather than as founders of an already-secure office). None of this appears anywhere in B7 or elsewhere in the document. A Facilitator seated across from a Catholic or Orthodox participant has no warning that this world's own record holds Ignatius/Polycarp inside unresolved argument rather than settled sainthood/office.

**Fix:** Add a paragraph to B7 naming Catholic/Orthodox living-tradition correspondence and Ignatius/Polycarp's contested standing, from World Profile §9.

### Finding 2 (Significant) — B7's central premise attributed to "the project lead's direct resolution" with no verifiable record

The Revision 3 note attributes B7's Relational Safety framing to "the project lead's direct resolution" without a verbatim quote or Change Order number — unlike this document's own Revision 1 note (CO-023/CO-024, independently verified in the live Register) and unlike `CiC_W1_Validation_Testing.md`'s Frozen declaration (verbatim quoted). CO-024 closed exactly this failure mode after a prior fabricated "project-lead note" was found embedded in a review document. No record of this specific resolution exists anywhere in the project outside this document.

**Fix:** Cite the project lead's actual verbatim words for this resolution, consistent with this project's own standing practice.

### Finding 3 (Moderate) — B7's paraphrase of Constitution Article 33 reassigns a system-level clause to "the Facilitator" and drops the minors-specific caution

Article 33 has two distinct clauses with two different subjects: Facilitator stewardship responsibility (genuinely about the Facilitator) and "the architecture does not assume it can reliably verify age or vulnerability" (subject: the architecture/system, not the Facilitator). B7 merges both under "the Facilitator... must not assume," which misstates the second clause's subject, and drops Article 33's explicit minors emphasis ("particular caution is warranted for participants who may be minors or otherwise especially vulnerable").

**Fix:** Keep "the architecture" as the subject of the verification-limitation clause; name Facilitator stewardship as a separate point; restore the minors-specific language.

### Finding 4 (Moderate) — B7's Relational Safety quotation cited to a document that does not contain it

"warm, serious... as pastoral improvisation, this is good writing" appears only in the Transcripts document's own flag-review section (the original drafting agent's self-assessment), not in `Independent_Verification_Round1.md` §6, whose own language and tone are markedly sharper and less reassuring.

**Fix:** Cite this quotation to the Transcripts document alone.

### Finding 5 (Moderate) — Section A's grounding footnote miscategorizes "the danger of the Name" as an "open question" alongside genuinely-argued theological questions

Formation Calibration §4 explicitly requires distinguishing ancient/genuine unresolvedness (G01 authority, G05 docetism) from modern-reconstruction-limit unresolvedness (G03 state pressure, G04 martyrdom), which Chloe carries with no in-world hedging. The footnote groups "the danger of the Name" (G03) with authority/forgiveness/docetism as one undifferentiated set of "open questions" — repeating a flattening this build has corrected multiple times elsewhere. (Section A's actual participant-facing prose gets this right; only the internal footnote miscategorizes it.)

**Fix:** Revise the footnote to describe "the danger of the Name" as a lived risk, not an open question.

### Finding 6 (Minor) — B7's central quotation splices two separate Chloe turns without disclosing the splice

"you are not idle in coming to my door tonight" (first turn) and "you are missing from no table of mine tonight... I'm not going anywhere" (second turn) are joined via ellipsis without noting they span two turns, unlike this document's own established practice for its other composite quotation (the "FAIL... blocking" splice, disclosed in the Scope Note).

**Fix:** Note the quotation spans two consecutive turns in the same exchange.

### Finding 7 (Minor) — Section A runs slightly over the informal ~80–120 word benchmark (~124 words)

Not a template violation (template specifies sentence count, not word count). Optional trim.

### Finding 8 (Minor) — "The two centuries after the apostles died" is loose relative to the world's actual c. 70–200 CE span

Defensible as idiomatic shorthand; read literally could overstate span by up to ~70 years. Optional reword.
