## Findings

### Finding 1 — Misattributed quotation: a Part Two principle cited as Part Six [SIGNIFICANT]

**Location:** Section 1.4, "Source-awareness and confidence-level, addressed directly," fourth bullet ("Engagement Depth Follows the World's Own Gravity, Not Documentation").

**What the document says:** it quotes, verbatim and accurately, "The Representative itself experiences its depth as the world's own weight of concern, never as documentation: it speaks longest and richest about what shaped and pressed on its community most, and briefly or not at all about what mattered less to it — because this is what it lived, argued over, and was formed by, never because 'more was written down'" and cites it as **"(Framework Part Six)."**

**What the source actually shows:** this text appears in the governing Framework docx only under **Part Two: Representative Boundary Principles**, as the eighth of the nine named principles ("Engagement Depth Follows the World's Own Gravity, Not Documentation"). It does not appear anywhere in Part Six. Confirmed by direct inspection of the converted framework text (Part Two runs lines 133–219 of the plain-text conversion used for this review; the "Engagement Depth Follows..." principle sits at the end of that block, immediately before Part Three begins).

**Why this matters:** the document's own bullet list is explicitly introduced as "**Four of the nine** [Part Two Boundary Principles]... quoted here in full because this document's own instruction rests on their precise wording, not a paraphrase" — and this specific bullet is one of those four, correctly named and correctly quoted, but then mis-cited to the wrong Part in the very next parenthetical. This is the identical defect class the project's own history has already flagged twice in adjacent documents (a quotation attributed to the wrong source, found in Voice Construction's own cold review; a Doc_04 quotation later found to be a paraphrase-as-verbatim in Formation Calibration and its own source, the Ecology Assessment). Section 1.4's own confidence-level line for this subsection states "**Documented** throughout this subsection — all four principles are quoted directly and verbatim from the governing Framework text, not summarized" — an accuracy claim this specific citation error undermines.

### Finding 2 — Selective quotation omits the complicating clause in its own key evidentiary basis for a load-bearing new-synthesis claim [SIGNIFICANT]

**Location:** Section 2, "What Dynamic Encounter is not, for Chloe specifically, disclosed as a deliberate exclusion" — the Doc_07 §2E "formation arc" quotation, and Document Log item 4.

**What the document says:** it quotes Doc_07 §2E's "formation arc" paragraph as: "Entry via Two Ways catechesis and baptism → incorporation into a local *ekklesia*'s own already-argued answer to the authority question → ongoing re-formation through repeated eucharistic gathering and reception of translocal correspondence**...** No settled terminal stage is evidenced anywhere in this world's own record" — and uses this quotation as the direct evidentiary basis for excluding a "graduated-access model" from Chloe's Dynamic Encounter design, arguing instead that her engagement deepens through rapport and occasion.

**What the source actually shows:** Doc_07 §2E's actual sentence, in full, is: "Entry via Two Ways catechesis and baptism → incorporation into a local *ekklesia*'s own already-argued answer to the authority question → ongoing re-formation through repeated eucharistic gathering and reception of translocal correspondence **→ for a minority, direct testing through state exposure, met in Strand A with an intensified, martyrdom-oriented formation logic modeled on one bishop's own self-presentation.** No settled terminal stage is evidenced anywhere in this world's own record." The ellipsis is present and technically discloses that something was cut, but the specific clause removed — a further, differentiated formation stage that only "a minority" reach, met with an "intensified" formation logic — is exactly the kind of complicating detail a reviewer would need to see to assess the very claim the quotation is offered to support. The document's own argument is that this world shows a "single-threshold formation arc" with no "further sequence" beyond baptismal incorporation; the sentence it quotes to prove this contains, in the part it cuts, a further sequence for a subset of the community. The document never engages with this clause, explains why it does not count as a form of graduated/tiered formation, or discloses that its own source sentence contains material in tension with its conclusion.

**Why this matters:** this is precisely the risk the task brief for this review flagged — whether "the Doc_07 §2E 'formation arc' citation this rests on actually saying what the document claims, and is excluding the graduated-access reading actually justified" — and on inspection, the citation as quoted does not fully support the exclusion without further argument the document does not supply. This does not mean the underlying conclusion is wrong (martyrdom-testing is arguably not "teaching a participant earns access to" in Part Six's sense, and a case could be made distinguishing it from a graduated-access model), but that case is not made; the complicating clause is simply cut. This affects Document Log item 4, one of the document's own seven self-flagged new-synthesis items, and is the single most consequential finding in this review.

### Finding 3 — Unverified provenance claim: the L4 template's Role quote is not "already quoted" anywhere upstream [MODERATE]

**Location:** Section 1.5, "Questions about Chloe herself" — "Per the L4 template's own governing instruction on role (carried forward from CO-014 and **already quoted in the identity chain this document inherits**): 'Role is a register-shaping label for the participant's benefit...'"

**What the document claims:** that this specific L4 template quotation has already appeared somewhere in the "identity chain" — i.e., in the Identity Preliminary Decision, Ecology Assessment, Formation Calibration, or Voice Construction documents this document is built from.

**What a direct search shows:** the phrase "register-shaping label" and this specific L4 Section 1 Role quotation do not appear anywhere in `CiC_W1_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md`, `CiC_W1_Representative_Ecology_Assessment.md`, `CiC_W1_Representative_Formation_Calibration.md`, or `CiC_W1_Representative_Voice_Construction.md` (confirmed by direct text search across all four files and the entire worlds folder for this world — the phrase occurs nowhere except in this Engagement Architecture document itself and, separately, in the L4 template's own version-history note about CO-014). This is, in fact, the first time this specific L4 quotation is used anywhere in this Representative's construction record, not a repetition of an already-established citation.

**Why this matters:** this is a small but checkable factual claim about what prior, already-cleared documents contain, and it does not hold up. It does not affect the substance of Chloe's role framing (which is independently and correctly sourced to the Identity Preliminary Decision elsewhere in the same sentence), but it is a disclosure-discipline slip — claiming a citation is "already" established when it is being introduced for the first time understates how much new sourcing work this document is actually doing at this specific point.

### Finding 4 — L4 correspondence claim omits a weaker but real partial anchor [MODERATE]

**Location:** Front matter ("On this document's relationship to the L4 Representative Construction Notes Template") and Document Log ("L4 template correspondence, disclosed rather than assumed").

**What the document claims:** that L4 Section 1's "Formation Posture" item is "**the one genuine, direct point of correspondence**" anywhere in the L4 template for this document's subject matter, and that Sections 1 and 2 (How Chloe Receives Questions; Dynamic Encounter) "have no L4 anchor" at all.

**What the L4 template actually contains:** confirmed true that no L4 section is titled "Engagement Architecture," and that Section 1's "Formation Posture" item (within "Ecology Assessment Summary") is worded closely to Part Six's third subsection. However, L4 Template Section 7 includes a named validation probe, **"Sustained Engagement Testing,"** whose own template language — "how sustained engagement was tested — number of turns, types of exchanges, what drift or consistency was assessed across longer conversation arcs" and "whether the Representative's stability and consistency across extended encounters" was maintained — is the closest behavioral analogue anywhere in the L4 template to Part Six's "Dynamic Encounter" concept (Section 2 of this document). It is a testing record rather than a specification, so the document's underlying point (no L4 section documents this behavior "as its own object") is defensible, but the flat claim that Sections 1–2 have "no L4 anchor" and that Formation Posture is "the one" correspondence is incomplete: a weaker, disclosed correspondence exists and this build's own stated disclosure discipline ("this gap is named explicitly rather than papered over") would call for naming it rather than omitting it.

### Finding 5 — Quotation-fidelity slip: a dropped word changes a quoted phrase [MINOR]

**Location:** Section 2, "What makes Dynamic Encounter concretely available to Chloe" — "'why letters carry weight, why catechesis matters, why office had to be argued rather than assumed' (Ecology Assessment §6, citing Doc_05 §5.2)."

**What the source actually says:** Ecology Assessment §6 reads "it is why letters carry weight, why catechesis matters, why office had to be **argued for** rather than assumed (Doc_05 §5.2)." The word "for" is dropped from the quotation without ellipsis or bracket notation.

**Why this matters:** a small deviation from verbatim in a passage explicitly presented as a direct quotation. The meaning is not substantially altered, but it is not, as presented, a verbatim quotation.

### Finding 6 — Two quotations silently truncated at the closing quotation mark, without ellipsis, where the source sentence continues [MINOR-COSMETIC]

**Location A:** Front matter, "On this document's relationship to the L4 Representative Construction Notes Template" — the L4 Section 1 "Formation Posture" item is quoted as ending "...How this shaped the Representative's relational and formation posture" (L4 Template, Section 1)." The actual template text continues: "...How this shaped the Representative's relational and formation posture **in the Permanent Prompt.**" The quotation is closed one clause early, with no ellipsis marking the cut, giving the appearance of a complete sentence.

**Location B:** Section 2, "What Dynamic Encounter is not, for Chloe specifically" — 'this world "explicitly lacked any formal teaching institution"' (Ecology Assessment §1). The source (Ecology Assessment §1, itself quoting Doc_07 §2E) actually reads: "this world 'explicitly lacked any formal teaching institution **of the kind that would define World #2's own formation logic just past this world's own close**' (Doc_07 §2E...)." Again closed early without ellipsis.

**Why this matters:** neither omission changes the substantive claim being made, and both are individually trivial, but together with Finding 5 they form a recognizable pattern of small, undisclosed trims to quotations this document elsewhere insists are being "quoted directly," "quoted here in full," or "not paraphrased." Given this project's own repeated finding of quotation-fidelity defects in the three documents immediately preceding this one, this pattern is worth tightening even though no individual instance is severe.

### Finding 7 — Quotation-splicing via bracket-insertion and mid-clause ellipsis, a technique this build otherwise avoids [MINOR-COSMETIC]

**Location:** Section 3, "How understanding is transmitted in this tradition" — "the correspondence network (letters shaped by 'a specific writer['s]... response to a specific, local argument,' Doc_05 §2.1, quoted at Ecology Assessment §1)."

**What the source actually says:** Doc_05 §2.1 (as quoted consistently elsewhere in this build, including in Doc_07 and Voice Construction) reads: "...they encountered it as a specific writer **had already shaped it, in** response to a specific, local argument." The Engagement Architecture document inserts a bracketed possessive ("writer['s]") and elides "had already shaped it, in" to compress the quotation into a noun phrase. The resulting meaning is not distorted, but this is a different quotation technique than the clean verbatim-with-ellipsis approach used everywhere else in this document and its predecessors, and it produces a quotation that reads slightly awkwardly as a result ("a specific writer's... response to a specific, local argument"). Given that a genuinely blended verbatim/paraphrase quotation was an identified defect in the immediately preceding document (Voice Construction's Doc_07 §2G quote, per that document's own Document Log), this kind of grammatical splicing — even though disclosed via brackets and ellipsis rather than silently blended — is worth flagging for tightening to a cleaner verbatim excerpt or an unquoted paraphrase.

### Finding 8 — Ambiguous citation bundling two different kinds of reference [MINOR-COSMETIC]

**Location:** Section 3, "What she assumes about a questioner" — "hospitality itself is the structural unit this world's identity rests on (Doc_01 §4, Identity Preliminary Decision)."

**What is unclear:** the citation bundles a section-numbered reference to Doc_01 (§4, Cultural Scope — confirmed to actually discuss the household/oikos as "the basic organizational and gathering unit," so the underlying claim is grounded) together with "Identity Preliminary Decision," a document that has no numbered sections and is not the source of a §4. As written, a reader cannot tell whether "Identity Preliminary Decision" is meant as a second, separate citation or as a (mistaken) qualifier on "Doc_01 §4." The underlying claim is accurate and well-sourced; only the citation's formatting is unclear.

## What was checked and found sound

- **No fabricated quotation was found anywhere in this document.** Every direct quotation checked against its cited source — the Part Six governing texts opening each of the three main sections (all three verified verbatim against the converted Framework docx), the four Part Two Boundary Principle quotes in Section 1.4 (three of four correctly attributed; see Finding 1 for the fourth), the Doc_04 G04 quote in Section 1.3 (verified verbatim against Doc_04's actual G04 entry, including the "(Ignatius, Polycarp)" parenthetical, which is genuinely part of the source text, not an editorial insertion), the Doc_09 Story 004 usage-guidance quote in Section 1.3 (verified verbatim), the Framework Part Nine Phase Four description in the header (verified verbatim), and the Voice Construction §5 strand-consistency quote in Section 2 (verified verbatim) — all check out as accurate, with the exceptions above.
- **The seven self-flagged new-synthesis items are honestly disclosed as new synthesis**, are not presented as direct findings, and (with the exception of Finding 2 above) are reasonably supported by what they cite. The Story 004 generalization (item 2) does not overreach — Formation Calibration's own Thin-domains section already ties the Pliny/*ministrae* material to the "non-elite and enslaved members' experience" Thin domain, so the Engagement Architecture document's synthesis is a defensible extension, not an invented connection.
- **The Rich/Moderate/Thin domain lists in Section 1** are accurate, complete restatements of Formation Calibration §3 (checked item-by-item against that document).
- **Internal consistency** with the Identity Preliminary Decision, Ecology Assessment, Formation Calibration, and Voice Construction was checked throughout and no contradiction or drift was found — the document correctly treats identity, ecology, calibration, and voice as settled inputs and does not re-litigate or silently revise any of them.
- **Confidence-level calibration** (Documented / Widely Accepted / Dominant Modern Reconstruction) is applied consistently and conservatively throughout; no claim was found upgraded beyond its evidentiary support.
- **Framework compliance**: the document's three sections map exactly onto Part Six's three named subsections (confirmed by direct inspection of the Framework text), and nothing Part Six requires for Engagement Architecture appears to be missing.
- **The construction-record illustration in Section 3** (the "how do I know if I belong" example) is clearly and correctly marked as illustration, not an attested claim or tested transcript, consistent with the precedent set in Voice Construction's own header note.
- **The claim that no L4 section is titled "Engagement Architecture"** is confirmed true by direct inspection of the L4 template's eight section headers.

## Summary

This document is structurally sound, framework-compliant, and free of fabrication or confidence-inflation, and its extensive self-disclosure of new synthesis is, with one exception, both honest and well-supported. The defects found are real but narrow: one misattributed quotation (a Part Two principle cited as Part Six, inside a subsection whose own confidence claim depends on all four quotes being accurate), one selectively-quoted evidentiary basis that omits the one clause most relevant to assessing the document's own central new-synthesis exclusion in Section 2 (the graduated-access model question), one inaccurate claim about a quotation's prior appearance in this build, one incomplete account of L4 template correspondence, and a handful of small quotation-fidelity slips consistent with a pattern this build's own prior three Representative Construction documents have each needed a review round to catch and fix. None of these defects touches the document's core structure or its treatment of Chloe's identity, ecology, or voice as settled; all are fixable without reopening any prior decision. Recommend: fix Findings 1–8 directly (consistent with this project's own revision-decision precedent for narrow, non-substance-changing corrections), with particular attention to Finding 2, which is the one finding that bears on whether a load-bearing design decision (no graduated-access model for Dynamic Encounter) is as fully justified as the document currently presents it.
