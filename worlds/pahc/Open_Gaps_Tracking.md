## Two build eras — read this first, it explains most of what follows

different times, that were never reconciled into one another on the record:

1. **The old build cycle** (files prefixed `CiC_W1_...`, all dated **2026-07-07 to
   2026-07-09**): Construction Framework V7.3, Doc_01 through Doc_09 plus a full
   Representative Construction stack (Phases One–Four, Permanent Prompt, World Capsule

   dated **2026-08-21 to 2026-08-22** for the steps checked here): the schema/engine

   This is the system that actually compiles into `packages/pahc/` — four packages exist,
   the newest built **2026-09-20T03-05-29Z** (today, per `records/worlds/pahc.yaml`,
   `state: admitted`).
