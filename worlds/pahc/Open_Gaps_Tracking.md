# Open Gaps — World #1 (Post-Apostolic/Sub-Apostolic House-Church Christianity, world code `pahc`)

Tracked from this world's build discrepancies as they are found. This file did not exist before 2026-09-19; created following the header/append shape already established by `worlds/syr/Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`, per this project's own standing convention for this document.

1. **Phase Six's Grapte "cross-community transmission role" claim for this Representative is contradicted by the current, real construction record — surfaced 2026-09-19 while authoring this world's `facilitator_brief` record, not yet resolved either way.** Phase Six (`worlds/pahc/CiC_W1_Phase6_Facilitation_Brief_B1-B6_DRAFT.md`, cleared review across two rounds and awaiting the project lead's own disposition) states, in its own header and B1/B2: this Representative's "documented function is combined with Grapte's pastoral-instructional **and cross-community transmission role** (Hermas, *Vision* 2.4.3)," and that she "receives and forwards such letters — a genuinely attested function (Polycarp's own act of forwarding, Doc_08 §6) — and reads them aloud and discusses them as a plausible, disclosed extension of that attested function." The same claim appears in the document's own Section A menu-card prose ("reading aloud whatever letter has just arrived from a sister community across the sea") and is one of the two stated reasons the upstream `CiC_W1_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md` gives for selecting the combined Chloe/Grapte role over a plain household-host option.

   The current, live `records/pahc/voice_craft/pahc.craft.chloe-voice.md` record states the opposite, in its own trailing note, dated to this build's own Step 11 round-1 review: "Checked directly against `cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml`: the cross-community sending in Vision 2.4.3 is Clement's own function, marked as his by the passage's own warrant clause ('permission has been granted to him'); Grapte's own function is a copy of the text and the charge to admonish the widows and orphans with it. Corrected here, and the 'cross-community transmission role' phrase removed from the role description accordingly." That record's own `identity` field, as it stands today, describes only the pastoral-instructional (admonish-the-widows-and-orphans) role — no transmission function. `records/pahc/demonstration/pahc.demo.identity-collision-womens-authority.md` independently confirms the identical correction, verified against the same vendored text, in its own words: "the cross-community sending in the same passage belongs to Clement, not Grapte, per the text's own explicit warrant clause."

   Both records explicitly flag, at the point of their own correction, that the upstream Phase Six document (and its own Preliminary Decision document) were not corrected along with them — the voice_craft record's own words: "This record is now correct against the vendored text; the upstream approved decision document is not, and correcting it is the project lead's own call, not this build thread's — carried forward to the closing summary rather than silently edited." That closing-summary carry-forward does not appear to have reached Phase Six's own text by the time Phase Six cleared its two independent review rounds (2026-07-09): both rounds checked citation accuracy and disclosure discipline but did not catch this specific record-vs-document drift, since the correction was made in a separate build step's own record rather than in Phase Six's own draft.

   Because this bears on a genuinely load-bearing description of what this Representative's own documented function actually is — not merely a stylistic choice — this was logged as an open discrepancy: `pahc.facilitator_brief.post-apostolic-house-church.md`'s own `world_identity` and `formation_strengths` fields were built to state only what the current `pahc.craft.chloe-voice` record actually supports (the admonish-the-widows-and-orphans function alone), and deliberately do not carry forward Phase Six's own cross-community-transmission framing or its "Chloe receives and forwards such letters" claim, since grounding either would mean citing a claim the two real records on point now contradict. The letters/translocal-network material that genuinely is a strength of this world is instead grounded directly in `pahc.gravity.translocal-network` (attested by Rome/1 Clement, Ignatius, and Polycarp's own forwarding act — none of them this Representative), stated as the world's own general lived mechanism rather than this Representative's own personal function.

   **RESOLVED 2026-09-20 — not a judgment call, a checkable textual fact.** Read the primary source directly (`cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml`, *Vision* 2.4.3, lines 1005–1025): "you will send the one to Clemens and the other to Grapte... And Clemens will send his to foreign countries, for permission has been granted to him to do so... And Grapte will admonish the widows and the orphans." The text itself gives the cross-community-sending function to Clemens by its own explicit warrant clause, and gives Grapte only the widows-and-orphans charge — confirming, independently and a third time, exactly what `pahc.craft.chloe-voice` and `pahc.demo.identity-collision-womens-authority` already found. Phase Six's B1/B2/Section A attribution of a "cross-community transmission role" to Grapte (and by extension to this Representative) is simply incorrect against the source it cites. Per this project's own "keep the live/canonical surfaces clean" discipline and the identical precedent already set for `worlds/desert/Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` entries 4–5 (a construction-phase draft is not corrected retroactively once a later record build supersedes it), `CiC_W1_Phase6_Facilitation_Brief_B1-B6_DRAFT.md` itself is left unedited — it should be read as superseded on this specific point by `pahc.craft.chloe-voice`, `pahc.demo.identity-collision-womens-authority`, and `pahc.facilitator_brief.post-apostolic-house-church.md`, all three of which now agree. No further action needed on this entry.
