## 1. Scope, stated plainly before any category is tested

The Framework's own Part VI names fifteen validation categories (Historical Plausibility; Ecological Integrity Testing and its five named sub-tests — Balance, Reduction, Complexity, Emergence, Worship Integration; Differentiation; Author Dominance; Anachronism; Relational Safety; Living Tradition; Adversarial Resistance; Encounter; Revision — not sixteen; "Validation Protocol Rigor" is Part VI's own governing subsection for how any category may be reported "confirmed," not itself a sixteenth category), several of which — Adversarial Resistance Testing, Encounter Testing, and much of Relational Safety Testing — are, by their own nature, tests of a *Representative in live conversation*. No Representative exists for this world; per this thread's own launch instructions, Step 10 (Representative Emergence) is explicitly out of scope and has not begun, "not even Phase One (Ecology Assessment), since that phase already assumes an identified figure/voice to assess." This document therefore validates what exists — the construction record, Doc_01 through Doc_09's own Story Inventory and World Profile — and names plainly, category by category, which validation work genuinely cannot be performed yet rather than either skipping those categories silently or performing a hollow substitute against material that doesn't exist. This is a considered scope boundary, not an oversight, and it means this document does **not** conclude the world is Freeze-eligible (§9 below states this explicitly).
