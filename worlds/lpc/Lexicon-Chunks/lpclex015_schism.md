---

## Quick Meaning

For us, schism is division within the one episcopate over discipline or doctrine — a tearing of the body rather than a disagreement within it.

---

## World Meaning

Disagreement is not this. Men argue here, hard, and stay. Schism is the other thing: when the arguing stops being argument and becomes a separate altar, a separate bishop, a separate people in the same town.

The difference is not the intensity of the dispute. It is whether anyone walked out. A church that keeps two men who cannot agree has not suffered schism; a church where one of them consecrates a rival has.

---

## Ecological Function

Schism is the negative boundary of communion and the thing the one episcopate exists to make intelligible. It is also where this world draws its own line against its neighbours — the conviction that disagreement is survivable and separation is not.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A modern participant is likely to hear denominational difference — a normal, unremarkable feature of a plural religious landscape, carrying no great weight.

**World Hearing:**
A tearing of a single body, with real consequences for standing, sacraments and ordinary life in a town where both parties are present. Not one option among many but the outcome this world organizes itself to avoid.

## CT Contest Type

**Application to this world**, and secondarily **Historical scope**.

The live contest is over whether the heresy/schism distinction was as stable and as clearly drawn in this period as later usage implies, and over how far the category was in practice a description of a real ecclesial situation as against a polemical instrument used by the party that eventually prevailed. The North African case is the field's central example: whether Donatism is best understood as a schism in the terms this world's own sources supply, or whether that framing already encodes the victors' account, is actively argued.

**What this entry therefore does not do:** it does not present the distinction as a neutral technical taxonomy, and it does not settle whether this world's own application of the category to its rivals was accurate. Doc_03 assigned the [CT] tag to this term and this document confirms it and supplies the contest type Doc_03 did not.
