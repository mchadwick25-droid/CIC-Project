**(a) `v.iv.ii` is not "the *Retractations* preface."** Extracted this session by walking the markup:

The `div3` is titled **"Preface."** and is NPNF's own preface to *On Baptism*. That is a verification claim asserted as checked and wrong about what was checked — the failure mode Round 1's own H1 established as the one this project treats most seriously — landing on the single most load-bearing piece of new material in the revision.

**(d) It is the fourth instance of the pattern the Decision Log has now named three times.** Round 3's review artifact reads: *"**Retractations preface** (`v.iv.ii`, line 10796)."* The fix pass adopted that phrase verbatim, into two documents, without re-deriving what `v.iv.ii` is — which the `div3` title states in one word. `lpc_Decision_Log.md`'s fourth entry closes by saying the pattern is "named here plainly rather than left to be inferred… which is the discipline working as designed, not a sign it can be relaxed." It was relaxed in the same commit that wrote the sentence.
