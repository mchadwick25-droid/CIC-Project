**Location:** §6, "the explicit fallback instruction" paragraph: *"And **the Passion of Perpetua and Felicitas** — vendored (Registry row 204) but Excluded as outside this world's own *c.* 246 start by 66 years (Doc_02 §1; Doc_09 §6 item 1)."*

**Checked against:** `Source_Registry.md` row 204 (Perpetua's Passion, c. 203 CE, excluded because it's *assigned to a different world's corpus* — `tertullian-s-voice`, per Mark's 2026-08-26 ruling — not excluded on a date-boundary at all); `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` §1 (the "66 years" / "predates by 66 years" language belongs explicitly to the **Scillitan Martyrs** (180 CE), a different excluded text, per row 28); and `Doc_09_Story_Inventory.md` line 126, the document's own cited §6 item 1: *"The one North African woman who did — Perpetua — sits outside the horizon by four decades."* Four decades (246−203=43) ≠ 66 years. The document conflates two separate exclusions (Scillitan Martyrs' date-boundary exclusion vs. Perpetua's cross-world-assignment exclusion) and states a specific numeric fact that both of its own cited sources contradict.

**Independently re-verified against `Source_Registry.md` row 204 directly before applying** — confirmed: row 204 carries both texts, with two distinct exclusion reasons stated separately (Scillitan Martyrs: date-boundary, 66 years, row 28's own reason mirrored; Perpetua: Out-of-Boundary by prior corpus-assignment ruling, a different reason entirely). Fixed by restating both exclusions correctly and attributing each fact to its actual source.
