# Doc_09 — Correction History

## Latin Pastoral-Congregational Christianity (`lpc`)

**What this is.** Every correction notice that was written inline in Doc_09, the seven story chunks and `lpc_Story_Index.md` during review rounds 1–7, moved here verbatim on 2026-09-15.

**Why it moved.** `CLAUDE.md` is explicit that change history *"belong[s] in `Ministry/` … never inline"* in a canonical surface, and that *"if you find commentary, changelog cruft, or leftover process notes in a live/canonical file, treat that as corruption: remove it, don't add to it."* The deliverables had accumulated **76 notice spans and roughly 3,100 words of build history** — `lpcstory007` was 23% correction apparatus and Doc_09 17%. Story Text is the field Doc_10 consumes as **deployable narrative**, and a chunk that is a quarter build history is not deployable.

**The second reason, which is the sharper one.** Keeping corrections inline created a *mention versus use* problem: a corrected phrase survives inside the notice that records it, so any closure audit has to tell the two apart. `scripts/notice_strip.py` existed solely to do that, and **it produced a HIGH finding in three of the last four review rounds — each time inside the fix for the previous round's finding** (Round 5's over-strip; Round 7's sibling absorption, introduced by the fix for Round 6's LOW-8). Removing the notices removes the ambiguity, and the tool built to cope with it is deleted rather than fixed again.

**Nothing here is discarded.** Each entry records the file, line and section it was written in, and the live sentence it attached to. The corrections themselves remain in force in the deliverables; what moved is the narration *about* them.
