### M1. Four Registry rows (70, 86, 87, 127) carry Exclusion Reason "Out-of-Boundary" for sources whose own stated rationale is not a temporal or geographic mismatch at all — contradicting the Source Registry Template's own binary definition of that field, a defect the Template's own author already recognized and fixed once (row 28) but that recurred four more times across five different review rounds and was never generalized

`Source_Registry.md`, rows 70 (line 88), 86 (line 113), 87 (line 114), and 127 (line 169).

`Source_Registry_Template.md` (§ "For every Excluded source, record an Exclusion Reason") defines exactly two values, and defines "Out-of-Boundary" narrowly: **"A straightforward temporal or geographic mismatch against Doc_01. The source was never a serious candidate for this world's own voice."** The Template is explicit that the Boundary Check is never a relevance or redundancy judgment: *"Boundary Status is never assessed by the date a piece of scholarship happened to be written... A source... whose own subject or origin belongs to a different era, place, or tradition is Excluded, however well-regarded or however recently written."*

Four rows use "Out-of-Boundary" for something else entirely:

- **Row 70** (R. A. Markus, *Saeculum*): Verification Note states plainly the actual reason is topical, not chronological — *"a foundational and widely-cited study, but of Augustine's own political theology and philosophy of history specifically, **not of this world's own pastoral-congregational subject matter**."* Markus's own subject is Augustine of Hippo, this world's own anchor figure, writing within its own 395–430 window. There is no era, place, or tradition mismatch; the row was declined because its *angle* on an in-boundary figure isn't the angle this Registry currently wants, which the Template does not treat as a Boundary Check failure at all.
- **Row 86** (Robert Dodaro, *Christ and the Just Society in the Thought of Augustine*): Verification Note reads *"the same ground row 70 (Markus) is excluded on; excluded for the same stated reason"* — inheriting row 70's own mislabeling rather than independently applying the Template.
- **Row 87** (Henry Chadwick, *Augustine of Hippo: A Life*): Verification Note states the real reason directly — *"A short biography, **declined as duplicative** of rows 30 (Brown) and 31 (Lancel), both already Native."* Redundancy with an already-held Native source is not a Boundary Check failure under the Template either; a source can be Native and simply not licensed for anything (row 6 is the Registry's own working example of exactly this: Native, "not currently licensed for any specific claim — noted for completeness").
- **Row 127** (Miles Hollingworth, *Saint Augustine of Hippo: An Intellectual Biography*, added this world's own most recent revision, Round 9): Verification Note again gives the real reason as redundancy — *"duplicative of rows 30 (Brown) and 31 (Lancel)... the same ground row 87 (Chadwick) was already excluded on"* — but the row's own Boundary Status/Exclusion Reason columns still read **Excluded / Out-of-Boundary**. This is a direct mismatch with `Doc02_Round9_Review.md`'s own text (line 117), which correctly separated its two exclusion recommendations by reason — *"item 9 — duplicative of rows 30 (Brown) and 31 (Lancel)... Item 10 — **Out-of-Boundary**"* — meaning Round 9's own review never called Hollingworth's exclusion "Out-of-Boundary"; that label was introduced only when the recommendation was converted into a Registry row, and no round since has caught it.

**This is not a first occurrence of the pattern — it is a pattern the project already fixed once and then repeated.** Row 28 (*The Passion of the Scillitan Martyrs*) originally carried "Out-of-Boundary" and was corrected to "Named Comparandum" at Round 1/2 (`Doc02_Round2_Review.md`'s own M15, per that round's own "Other Round 1 findings verified fixed" list: *"row 28 re-labelled Named Comparandum, correctly, since its own note is a temptation statement"*) — establishing, as early as Round 2, that the Exclusion Reason field's own semantic content matters and must match the row's actual rationale, not just belong to the allowed value set. That lesson was never converted into a check applied to future additions: rows 70 and 86 (Round 4/5) and row 127 (Round 9) all repeat the identical class of error the row 28 fix already named and corrected. Row 98 (Maier's prosopography, extending "well past this world's own 430 boundary") and row 103 (Clark's Monica biography, whose subject genuinely sits mostly outside the Carthage/Hippo focus and predates Augustine's ordained ministry) are, by contrast, defensible uses of "Out-of-Boundary" — this finding is about rows 70, 86, 87, and 127 specifically, not the category as a whole.

**Why this is substantial, not cosmetic, under CO-022's own test.** Boundary Status and Exclusion Reason are not narrative prose — they are the Registry's own binding classification fields, the ones `Source_Registry_Template.md` says downstream steps rely on directly ("To Doc_03... the Registry hands forward only its Native entries as the legitimate evidentiary base for a lexicon term, a gravity, a force, or a story"). Mislabeling four in-boundary, on-subject sources as failing a temporal/geographic check they do not actually fail is a scope-boundary and sourcing-conclusion error on the Template's own terms, not a wording issue. The correct fix, on the Template's own logic, is not merely relabeling the Exclusion Reason (no third value exists for "duplicative" or "off-angle") — it is recognizing that these four sources should most likely be **Native**, at Confidence C, with "not currently licensed for any specific claim" in Licensed-For, the same pattern row 6 already establishes, since none of the four actually fails the Boundary Check the Template defines. That is a real reclassification, not a typo fix, which is why it lands here as MEDIUM rather than COSMETIC.

**Required:** either reclassify rows 70, 86, 87, and 127 from Excluded to Native (Confidence C, unlicensed, per the row-6 pattern), correcting the substantive miscategorization; or, if the build thread judges "declined as redundant/off-topic" should remain a recorded Exclusion, propose that as a genuine third Exclusion Reason value to `Source_Registry_Template.md` (a cross-document, methodology-level change, which would itself need escalation-category review under CO-022's second category before this world's own Registry could adopt it unilaterally). Either way, the current state — four rows asserting a boundary mismatch that does not exist — does not hold under the Template as it is currently written.
