**Why this matters:** The chunk file itself (`donlex018_agonistici.md`) gets this exactly right — its own Key Sources note states plainly that "the group's **character**, scale, and typical conduct are **HIGH Author-Gravity risk**," distinct from the Documented bare-existence fact — so no participant-facing content is wrong. **Fix:** Either narrow the parenthetical to the five terms it actually supports and add a term-specific sentence addressing Church/Ecclesia (no tested gravity, but corroborated directly per Doc_04 §7) and Agonistici (existence Documented; character/self-designation carried at lower confidence, disclosed via the CT tag and the Key Sources Author-Gravity note rather than Reported-Experience Status, and state why that discipline is judged sufficient) — or drop the "Documented at its core... for G1–G5" framing entirely and argue term-by-term why Reported-Experience Status is inapplicable to each of the seven.
