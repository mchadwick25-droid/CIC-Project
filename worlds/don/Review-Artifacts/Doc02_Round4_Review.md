## HIGH

### H1. The state-assistance sentence added to Doc_02 §1 this revision misidentifies its own actor, mislabels the evidence's independence in the one paragraph a binding obligation forbids that label, and cites a Registry row outside its Licensed-For — all in one sentence, all refuted by files already open.

**Doc_02 §1, new this revision (`git diff 9caf7bea c915ed69`):**

> "State assistance in suppressing the schism **is independently attested** — Augustine's own account of **a military commander** 'sucking back Felicianus and Prætextatus once again within their pale' (*Answer to the Letters of Petilian*, **Registry row 4**) and the Prolegomena's summary of Letter 70's account that 'the state was called upon to enforce his ejection' (**Registry row 31**) — not merely asserted here."

**(a) The actor was not a military commander. He was this world's own bishop, and the vendored file says so three times.** The quoted clause's subject is Optatus Gildonianus. In the same chapter of *Contra litteras Petiliani*, four sentences earlier, Augustine asks: "what answer will they make about **Optatus, whom they were afraid to condemn** though they had long known his wickedness? **Why are those who were baptized by him** not said to have been baptized by one dead?" Elsewhere in the same volume: "you too are free from pollution by **the sacrifice of Optatus**… See therefore what kind of sacrifice he offers **whom you hold to be a priest**"; and "In the time of Gildo, because **one of your colleagues** — the vendored file's own editorial note supplies the identification, "**Optatus of Thaumugade (Thamogade), the friend of Gildo**" — was his intimate friend, let the followers of Maximianus be our witnesses to their sufferings. For if one might appeal to Felicianus himself… **whether Optatus did not compel him against his will to return to your communion**."

So, on the vendored corpus alone, without reaching for any external field knowledge: Optatus Gildonianus is the Donatist bishop of Thamugadi, a colleague of Petilian's, who baptizes, who offers the sacrifice, whom the Donatists were afraid to condemn, and who compelled Felicianus back into the Donatist communion — advancing, on Augustine's telling, "with a military force." Calling him "a military commander" is not a loose gloss; it changes who the actor is, and it changes the character of the fact. The mainstream Donatist party's own bishop leading armed men against the Maximianists is a materially different — and considerably sharper — datum than an imperial officer enforcing a decision, and it is the one Doc_01 §4 already gets right when it says the mainstream party "suppressed them, invoking existing imperial and proconsular anti-heretical legislation."

**(b) "Independently attested" is false in this document's own vocabulary, contradicts itself one clause later, and collides head-on with a binding obligation.** Both named attestations are Augustine's: the *Contra litteras Petiliani* passage is his, and the Prolegomena's Letter 70 summary is a nineteenth-century editor's précis of a letter of Alypius and Augustine ("Alypius and Augustin… urged in criticism [*Ep*. lxx.]… the state was called upon to enforce his ejection"). The sentence itself concedes this — "**Augustine's own account** of…" — one clause after asserting independence.

This is not a free-floating imprecision. Doc_02 §6 uses the same phrase in its strong, technical sense two sections later: "the group's *existence* **is independently attested outside hostile polemic** — imperial legislation names them directly." And **Doc_01 §7 item 1, binding on this document**, says of the Maximianist episode that it "reaches us almost entirely through Augustine's own quotation and **must be marked as such, not treated as independent attestation**." Doc_02 §10 asserts that this obligation "is discharged as set out above." `don_Decision_Log.md` records that this precise mischaracterization — "the Maximianist episode's evidence was mischaracterized as independently attested when it reaches the record almost entirely through Augustine's own polemic" — was one of Doc_01 Round 1's four high findings in this same world.

**(c) Row 31 is cited for a claim its Licensed-For does not cover.** Row 31 is licensed for three things: "The dating of *Contra Cresconium* and *Contra epistulam Parmeniani*; the earlier *Psalmus contra Partem Donati* rhetorical parallel (row 37); a Donatist apologetic account of the Maximianist reception, reported and refuted (Doc_02 §1). **Not** the primary evidence for the reception itself." The Letter 70 state-enforcement material is none of these. The Licensed-For field is not decoration in this Registry — row 6's Ep. 87 constraint is the document set's best piece of discipline precisely because it is treated as binding. Citing a row past its licence, three rows away from the one that models the discipline, undercuts it.

Name the actor as the vendored text does: *"Augustine's own account of the Donatist bishop Optatus of Thamugadi, Gildo's ally, 'advancing with a military force' and 'sucking back Felicianus and Prætextatus once again within their pale' (row 4)."* This is a better fact than the one it replaces, and it belongs in §6's Author Gravity reasoning too.
