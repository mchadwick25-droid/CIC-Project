## MEDIUM SEVERITY

### M1. §0 item 1 states D-C (the Numidian native-social-protest substrate) "was tested" — Doc_04 is explicit that it was deliberately *not* tested, unlike D-B

**Claim checked:** Doc_05 §0 item 1: "Tyconius's own hermeneutics (D-B) and the Numidian native-social-protest substrate (D-C) were **tested** and not advanced (Doc_04 §2) and are treated below only where their own thinness or contested standing is itself part of this world's honest ecological account (§6.5, §6.3)."

**What I found:** Doc_04 draws a sharp, repeatedly-emphasized distinction between these two candidates that Doc_05's sentence erases. Doc_04 §2's own heading is "Candidates Considered and Not Advanced" (not "Tested and Not Advanced"), and its two bullets use different verbs on purpose:

- D-B: "→ **not a gravity**. Fails Repetition... fails Persistence... fails Dependency..." — a candidate that was run through the six tests and failed them.
- D-C: "→ **not tested as an independent gravity**. Doc_02 §5 itself treats this at Confidence C, explicitly contested... 'pending independent verification of the underlying distributional data against a primary source.' A candidate this evidentially unsettled **should not be run through gravity testing** as though it were settled; **testing it now would manufacture false precision** about a question Doc_02 itself left open."

Doc_04 §4's Classification Summary and its Required Index Table make the same distinction in the verdict column: D-B's row reads "Fails Repetition, Persistence, Dependency"; D-C's row reads "**Not tested** (Confidence C, contested)." Doc_04 §1's own generation table likewise marks D-B "→ not advanced, §2" but D-C only "→ not advanced" with no six-test verdict ever entered for it anywhere in the document — because none was run.

Doc_05's own §6.3 and §6.5 get this right when they discuss the two candidates individually: §6.5 correctly says of D-B specifically, "his own dissent **was tested** at Doc_04 and not advanced as a gravity: single-voice, no following, failing Persistence and Dependency"; §6.3's construction note correctly restates D-A's (a different candidate) Cross-Check "precisely," and never claims D-C was tested. The error is confined to the one blanket sentence at §0 item 1, which flattens Doc_04's own deliberate distinction back into "were tested and not advanced" for both.

**Why this matters:** This is exactly the kind of "fidelity slip" the Doc_04 Round 1 review itself flagged as M2 when Doc_04 briefly flattened Doc_03's three-way existence/self-designation/character distinction for D-A back into a two-way split — a document's own method section restating a prior document's finding less precisely than the prior document stated it, on a point that prior document explicitly cared about getting right (Doc_04 §2 states in terms that testing D-C "would manufacture false precision" — the exact failure mode a careless restatement risks reproducing at one remove). It does not change any classification, but a reader relying on Doc_05's §0 for a summary of the "not advanced" candidates' history would come away believing D-C failed six-test testing, when Doc_04's own point is that it was never run through it.

**Fix:** Reword §0 item 1 to distinguish the two, e.g.: "Tyconius's own hermeneutics (D-B) was tested and failed to advance; the Numidian native-social-protest substrate (D-C) was, by Doc_04's own deliberate discipline, not tested at all, being too evidentially unsettled to test without manufacturing false precision (Doc_04 §2)."

### L3. Worship Ecology's inhabited-passage attribution of the *Deo laudes* "sign and token" framing to "this world's own opponents" traces, in Doc_02, to the modern CIL editors — but independent primary-source checking this session found genuine ancient corroboration Doc_02 itself does not cite

**Why this matters:** Low severity and arguably favorable to the document's own claim rather than damaging to it — this is not a fabricated attribution, since ancient-opponent corroboration for the substance of the claim does exist, just not the specific one Doc_02 cited or Doc_05 carries forward. Flagged for the record because (a) the specific "signum ac tessera" wording Doc_05's inhabited passage leans on is, precisely, the modern editors' phrase, not an ancient one, and (b) this is exactly the kind of independent primary-source spot-check the review brief calls for, and it surfaced material neither Doc_02 nor Doc_04 currently cite — worth flagging to the build thread as a possible strengthening addition for a future pass, not a correction Doc_05 is obligated to make on its own initiative (Doc_02 is already Approved to proceed and this review's scope is Doc_05, not reopening Doc_02).
