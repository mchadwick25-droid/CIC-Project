## Findings

### Finding 1 — [COSMETIC] Teacher–student relationship mis-cites Tensional T1 as grounding
**Location:** §3, RICH table, row 6 — "The teacher–student formation relationship | Supporting **C5**, Tensional **T1**; force 2B-3 (transmission Mechanism One)."
**What's wrong:** T1 (Doc_04 §3.6) is the **Teacher–Bishop Authority Tension** — teacher-authority vs. bishop-authority. That is a different axis from the *teacher–student* formation relationship. The teacher–student relationship is correctly grounded in **C5** (Learning-Formation Integration) and **2B-3 Mechanism One** (Doc_08: "the teacher-student formation relationship … genuinely transmissible only through accompaniment"), both already cited in the row. The T1 add conflates the teacher–student accompaniment channel with the teacher–bishop authority tension.
**Why cosmetic, not substantial:** the row's load-bearing grounding (C5 + 2B-3) is correct and fully supports the RICH placement; T1 is a spurious *additional* citation that does not change the depth tier. T1 also appears — correctly — in the MODERATE row "Authority structures (the teacher–bishop question)," which is its proper home.
**Fix:** delete "Tensional **T1**" from the RICH row-6 grounding cell, leaving "Supporting C5; force 2B-3 (transmission Mechanism One)."

### Finding 2 — [COSMETIC] "Daily domestic / material practice" cites Doc_05 §6.2, which is the Spatial Ecology lens
**Location:** §3, THIN table, row 5 — "Daily domestic / material practice | Doc_05 §6.2 (thin) | brief."
**What's wrong:** Doc_05 §6.2 is the **Spatial Ecology** lens, not a dedicated domestic/material-practice section. The household is named *within* §6.2 ("the household (the everyday primary place of formation for most)"), so the citation is not baseless, but the label reads as though §6.2 is a domestic-practice reconstruction, which it is not (§6.2's own construction note treats the reconstruction as DMR on fragmentary archaeology).
**Why cosmetic:** the thinness placement itself is correct (Phase One §2 already carried daily domestic practice as THIN, "reachable only obliquely"), and the household genuinely appears in §6.2. Only the citation's precision is loose.
**Fix:** reword to "Doc_05 §6.2 (household within the Spatial lens; thin/oblique)" or cite the household reference explicitly, so the tag does not imply a dedicated section.

### Finding 3 — [COSMETIC] RICH teacher–student row does not acknowledge C5's documented late-horizon attenuation
**Location:** §3, RICH table, row 6 (as above).
**What's wrong:** C5 is classified **Supporting and temporally qualified** — it attenuates/terminates as an active organizing force in the late horizon under the post-Nicene authority shift (Doc_04 §3.5; Doc_08 3B-1/3B-2). Because Theon carries the *whole-span* horizon, a RICH grounding resting partly on C5 carries a hidden temporal qualification: the teacher–student formation relationship is richest in the early/mid school period, thinner (and, per 3B-3, increasingly *lost*) in the late horizon.
**Why cosmetic:** the RICH placement is still defensible — 2B-3 Mechanism One is Widely Accepted across the horizon and Gregory's *Address* strongly attests the accompaniment relationship (Phase One §1.3) — so the domain genuinely supports RICH engagement. This is a completeness note, not a mis-placement.
**Fix (optional):** add a half-clause noting the relationship is richest in the school period and attenuates late, so the whole-span voice does not over-weight the late horizon on this domain.
