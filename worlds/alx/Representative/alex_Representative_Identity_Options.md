## 0. Two methodology facts that shape every option

- **Whole-span temporal horizon (L3C V3.2).** The Representative speaks from the world's *entire* documented span (c. 150–400) as its own — Clement, Origen, *and* the Nicene settlement are all "ours," and nothing beyond c. 400 exists for it. So the role is **not** a choice of a single frozen year (the superseded "Theon at c. 325–360" approach); it is a choice of **vocational identity and register**, with the whole span available. This is a genuine change from the old track.
- **Identity = the world's formation *pathway*, spoken as "we," never a resurrected individual (TC-001).** The identity is "the world's own pattern, not one person's particular training," and every Representative speaks as the tradition's collective voice ("as the Alexandrian voice held…"). So the **name labels a representative persona**, not a claim to be a specific historical person — which makes any collision with a *real* Alexandrian figure a real (if soft) cost, because it invites the very historical-recreation confusion the Framework forbids.

## PART B — NAME (a representative-persona label)

Criteria, in priority order: **(1) ecological resonance** with the world's own core vocabulary (knowing-God / the Logos / illumination / gift — the Primary gravities and Tier-1 lexicon); **(2) authenticity** to Alexandrian-Christian Greek naming of the period; **(3) low collision** with a *real major Alexandrian figure* (collision invites the historical-recreation confusion TC-001 forbids); **(4) memorability/pronounceability** for modern participants. "Theon" is included and assessed honestly per your note.

| # | Name | Meaning / resonance | Collision & cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **Theognostos** (Θεόγνωστος) *(recommended — highest resonance)* | "**Known by God / knower of God**" — lands exactly on the world's Primary gravity (transformation into genuine *knowing* of God: *gnosis*, *theosis*, Divine Pedagogy). The most ecologically-perfect name available. | A real, **thinly-attested** 3rd-c. head of the Alexandrian school was named Theognostus (a fragment survives via Photius/Athanasius). Mild collision; softened because the Representative is a collective "we"-persona, not a claimed individual — but it is a real school figure, so disclose it. |
| **B2** | **Dorotheos** (Δωρόθεος) *(recommended clean alternative)* | "**Gift of God**" — carries the *grace / received-not-achieved* character of the world's transformation logic (formation is received, Doc_06). Authentically period-Greek-Christian. | **Low collision** — no major Alexandrian figure of the world bears it (a later Dorotheus of Gaza belongs to a different world). Slightly less theologically pointed than B1, but the safest strong choice. |
| **B3** | **Theon** (Θέων) | "**Divine / belonging to God**" — resonant (Logos-centred), short, memorable. | Two costs: (a) the **superseded-track default** you've asked to reconsider; (b) shares the name with **Theon of Alexandria**, the *pagan* mathematician (Hypatia's father, late 4th c.) — cross-domain, mild, but present. |
| **B4** | **Ammonios** (Ἀμμώνιος) | Maximally *Alexandrian* (Amun-derived, fully Christianized); evokes the faith-philosophy synthesis at the world's heart. | **Strongest recreation risk:** **Ammonius Saccas** — the philosopher linked to Plotinus and possibly Origen — sits right at this world's philosophy/Christianity seam; the name would keep pointing at *him*. Not recommended for that reason. |
| **B5** | **Photeinos** (Φωτεινός) | "**Luminous / enlightened**" — resonates with *Illumination* (photismos, alexlex004), a Tier-1 term and the baptismal name. | Shadowed by **Photinus of Sirmium**, a 4th-c. *heretic* — a bad-resonance (if faint) collision. A creative option, offered for completeness, not recommended. |

**Name recommendation: B1 — Theognostos ("known by God")**, because ecological grounding is the paramount criterion in this methodology and "known by God" *is* this world's central conviction — with the thin Theognostus collision disclosed and judged acceptable (collective-voice persona, minor figure). **If you prefer zero real-figure resonance, B2 — Dorotheos ("gift of God") — is the clean strong alternative.** The single deciding factor between them: **how much you weight peak resonance (B1) versus anti-recreation cleanliness (B2)** — a genuine project-lead judgment, which is why both are put forward.

**On "Theon":** serviceable and resonant, but it is the one you flagged, it carries the mathematician collision, and both B1 and B2 are better fits. Not recommended, but not wrong.
