## Verdict: **Substantial revision required**

This document's citation discipline on the load-bearing quotations is genuinely strong: every specific quotation named in the review brief as a known fabrication risk for this world's build was re-verified character-for-character against the actual vendored file and found accurate, including the two quotations this world's own history has already had to correct once each (Zwingli's "beatifies," not "[justification]"; the Second Helvetic Confession's "mirror" quotation at the corrected line 684, not the wrong 677). No new fabricated or altered quotation was found anywhere in this document. That is real, load-bearing good news given this world's demonstrated pattern.

However, the document's own highest-risk defect class for this specific step — cross-reference accuracy to Doc_01–Doc_04 — is not clean: the Doc_08 handoff section (§11) misattributes a specific, already-once-corrected forces-cell placement, actually *reintroducing* a defect that Doc_04's own Round 1 review found and Doc_04's own Round 2 review confirmed fixed. Separately, the document's central self-discipline — the "genre asymmetry" rule it states in §0 item 4 and enforces conscientiously almost everywhere else — is violated twice in inhabited-voice prose describing Geneva's Consistory, inventing specific operational and experiential detail (household-level enforcement, and the disciplinary institution's "daily practice" as a *felt* ground of assurance) that no vendored source supports and that Doc_02 explicitly says this corpus cannot supply. This directly contradicts the Tier 4 traceability note's own claim that "no element is invented beyond these attested sources." Neither defect is a fabricated primary-source quotation (the class this world has been hit hardest by before), but both are real, correctable, and belong to exactly the defect families this build has a demonstrated pattern of missing on a first pass.

## Findings

### High

**1. Doc_08 handoff (§11) reintroduces a forces-cell placement error that Doc_04's own Round 1 review found and Round 2 review confirmed corrected — a genuine regression, not a new independent error.**

**Checked:** Doc_05 §11, "To Doc_08 (Forces Document)": *"the forces integrated per lens here map to Doc_01's own six-cell matrix using the cell assignments Doc_04 §3 already established — 1A the late-medieval sacramental order refused (G2's own deep root, corrected to this cell at Doc_04 Round 1 review); 1B the two independent local origins (G1, G2, G3's own roots) and the Consistory's 1541 founding (G4); ..."*

**What I found, against Doc_04 §3.1–§3.4 directly (not Doc_05's own paraphrase of it):**
- Doc_04 §3.1 (G1) states its Forces-connection is **Cell 1A** (the Augustinian inheritance, an external antecedent) **and Cell 2B** only. It does *not* place G1 in Cell 1B — and it says so explicitly: *"Cell 1B is reserved there for this world's own two specific local founding acts"* (i.e., reserved for G2/G3's own roots, not G1's). This exact G1 placement (Cell 1A, not 1B) is itself the outcome of a Doc_04 Round 1 review Medium finding (#9, "Questionable forces-connection cell placement for G1's Augustinian-inheritance citation"), independently re-verified fixed at Doc_04 Round 2 review (finding 9: *"Genuinely fixed... Doc_04 §3.1's forces-connection now places this in Cell 1A, explicitly correcting the earlier Cell 1B placement"*).
- Doc_04 §3.2 (G2) states its own Forces-connection is **Cell 1A/1B** (the late-medieval sacramental order refused) **and Cell 2B** — with no mention anywhere of a Round 1 correction to G2's own cell placement. The "corrected to this cell at Doc_04 Round 1 review" note that actually belongs to *G1's* move into Cell 1A has been transplanted onto *G2's* Cell 1A/1B placement, which was never contested and never corrected.
- Doc_05's own Cell 1B line — *"1B the two independent local origins (G1, G2, G3's own roots)"* — lists G1 as one of Cell 1B's occupants. Doc_04 does not support this: G1 is Cell 1A/2B only.

**Why this matters:** this is precisely the cross-reference/misattribution defect class this world's build has been caught on repeatedly (twice at Doc_03, once at Doc_04's own Round 1 review, on this *exact same* Cell 1A/1B question). Doc_05's own §11 is the direct handoff instruction Doc_08's own six-cell matrix will be built from; an incoming builder following Doc_05's instruction as written would put G1 back in the cell Doc_04 explicitly moved it out of, undoing a fix already made and confirmed.

**Fix required:** correct §11's Cell 1A/1B lines to match Doc_04 §3.1–§3.4 exactly: Cell 1A = the Augustinian inheritance (G1) and the late-medieval sacramental order refused (G2); Cell 1B = the two independent local origins (G2, G3) and the Consistory's 1541 founding (G4) — G1 does not belong in Cell 1B. Drop or correct the misattributed "corrected... at Doc_04 Round 1 review" parenthetical so it attaches to G1's Cell 1A placement, not G2's.

**2. The genre-asymmetry discipline the document states and mostly enforces is twice violated by invented Consistory operational/experiential detail in inhabited-voice prose — in tension with the document's own Tier 4 traceability claim that "no element is invented beyond these attested sources."**

**Checked:** every inhabited-voice passage and the §10 Inhabited Synthesis body text against Doc_02 §5/§6 (no institutional-practice, material-culture, or consistory-register source is vendored at all) and against Doc_05's own repeated, explicit disclaimers to the same effect (§0 item 4; the §4 construction note: *"The Consistory's own actual weekly case-by-case operation — what discipline looked like to the person summoned before it — is Confidence E, unvendored, and is not narrated here beyond Calvin's own general doctrinal statement of the office"*; the §10 closing "Named absence" note: *"the ordinary weight of a consistory summons... the surviving evidence... does not let us say, and it is not narrated here"*).

**What I found:**
- §5, the "Consistorial discipline as Geneva's own further step" inhabited passage: *"someone must ask that question directly, **house by house if need be**, and answer for it before the whole body of pastors and elders together."* "House by house" is a specific operational/enforcement detail (home-level visitation) that is not in Calvin's own general doctrinal statement (Institutes IV.3.8: elders "unite with the bishops in pronouncing censures and exercising discipline") and is not attested anywhere in this corpus — Doc_02 §5 states explicitly there is no vendored institutional-practice source for how discipline was actually administered.
- §10, the Geneva-formation paragraph: *"to have one's own daily conduct answerable... to a body of pastors and elders who might **summon a household directly**"* and *"held out not as a threat but, **in the Consistory's own daily practice** and in the confession's own comforting cadence alike, as the ground of a settled life."* The first phrase again invents a specific enforcement mechanism (household summons) beyond the vendored general doctrine. The second phrase is worse: it asserts, as if known, how predestination doctrine actually functioned *in the Consistory's day-to-day operation* — a specific claim about lived institutional experience — immediately before the same section's own closing bracket states plainly that "what any of this actually felt like... is not narrated here" and that institutional daily-practice content is a "named absence, not a filled silence." The body text and the document's own disclaimer directly contradict each other within the same section.

**Why this matters:** Doc_02 §5/§6 is unambiguous that no Ecclesiastical Ordinances, no consistory registers, and no material-culture source of any kind is vendored for this world — this is the single most emphasized evidentiary gap in this world's entire source ecology, and Doc_05's own §0 item 4 makes "marking plainly where [a lens] is reconstructing from doctrine about practice rather than from practice itself" a named governing discipline. Both instances above cross from "doctrine about practice" into asserting practice-level and experience-level specifics with no source, inside inhabited-voice prose that Article 23's own quarantine rule keeps free of in-line caveats — meaning a reader encounters these as confidently narrated facts about how Geneva's Consistory actually operated. The §10 traceability note's blanket claim, *"No element is invented beyond these attested sources,"* is not accurate as written; neither phrase is in the traceability list, and neither is derivable from any listed source.

**Fix required:** remove "house by house if need be" and "who might summon a household directly" (or replace with language that stays at the level of the general doctrinal statement — e.g., "answer for it before the whole body of pastors and elders," without specifying the mechanism of summons). Remove or substantially qualify "in the Consistory's own daily practice... as the ground of a settled life," which asserts an experiential/operational claim the document's own closing paragraph says cannot be made. Revise the traceability note's closing sentence so it does not overclaim completeness once the above is fixed, or add these elements to a construction-note caveat if any operational specificity is retained.

### Low / Cosmetic

**4. Arithmetic imprecision in the "five years apart" figure for the two strands' independent origins, inherited from Doc_01 and restated without correction.**

**Checked:** Doc_05 §1 ("Zwingli's own Erasmian-humanist scholarship, catalyzed at Zurich by the 1522 Sausage Affair; and, **five years later**... Geneva's own 1526 alliance with Bern") and §6.1 ("The two strands' own independent origins — **five years apart**... Doc_01 §4 names directly").

**What I found:** Doc_01 §4 itself states the two origins are "five years apart" while comparing Zwingli's 1519 preaching start to Geneva's 1526 alliance/1536 break — a gap that is actually seven years (1519→1526) or seventeen (1519→1536), not five. (The "five years" figure that *is* accurate elsewhere in Doc_01 concerns a different pair: Zwingli's 1531 death to Calvin's 1536 arrival.) Doc_05 §6.1 directly echoes Doc_01 §4's own figure and citation, which is faithful to Doc_01's own (already-approved, not-to-be-reopened) text. Doc_05 §1 restates the same "five years" figure independently, anchored loosely to the 1522 Sausage Affair rather than 1519, which is off by a different, smaller margin (four years to 1526) but still not clearly "five."

**Disposition:** this is an inherited Doc_01-level imprecision, not a new fabrication by Doc_05, and Doc_01 is out of scope for reopening in this review. Flagged for awareness only; no action required of Doc_05 beyond optionally not restating the figure a second time in its own voice where it is not needed for §1's argument.

**5. The Zurich-after-Kappel inhabited passage narrates Zwingli's death with slightly more specific circumstance than Doc_01 itself states.**

**Checked:** §6.3 inhabited passage: *"Our own pastor fell with the city's own soldiers, not apart from them — reform here was never a private conviction sheltered from the world's own violence..."* against Doc_01 §2 ("the Second War of Kappel... in whose fighting Zwingli was killed") and §7.

**What I found:** Doc_01 states only that Zwingli was killed in the fighting of the Second War of Kappel; it does not characterize the manner of his death as being "with the city's own soldiers, not apart from them" as opposed to, e.g., serving in a chaplain's role. This is a defensible Tier 4 inference from the documented fact (he died in that battle) rather than an invented personal anecdote (no invented family, age, or named companion), and it does not violate the CLAUDE.md "no invented personal history" rule in the way a fabricated biographical detail would — but it does add interpretive color about the manner of death beyond what Doc_01 itself commits to, without a construction-note caveat.

**Disposition:** Low. No correction strictly required; consider softening to stay closer to Doc_01's own "killed in the fighting" formulation, or add a brief construction-note caveat if the stronger phrasing is kept.
