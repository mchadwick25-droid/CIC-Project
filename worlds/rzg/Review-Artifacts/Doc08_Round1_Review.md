## Verdict: **Substantial revision required**

The document's cell-assignment discipline on the world's single most-litigated question — where G1's forces-connection sits — is *mostly* right and represents real, hard-won progress: both vendored quotations reused from Doc_04/Doc_05/Doc_07 (the Consensus Tigurinus 9th Head and Institutes IV.3.8) check out exactly against the source files, with no regression on the "Wherefore" elision this world's build fixed six times over at Doc_07 Round 1. First, the document's own self-certifications do not match its own data: it claims "seventeen identified forces" four separate times (Sections 7, 8, and the closing line) when Section 3 and the Force Index table both actually contain **nineteen**, and its Proportionality Principle claims only one force (2B-6) lacks a gravity connection when the document's own Force Index shows **five** do. None of these three requires re-deriving the document's substantive analysis; all three are fixable without touching the underlying six-cell architecture.

### Finding 1 — HIGH — The document's own force count is wrong: it claims "seventeen identified forces" four times; Section 3 and the Force Index both contain nineteen

**Checked:** Section 7 ("None of the seventeen identified forces is itself contested..."); Section 8, Proportionality Principle ("All seventeen identified forces received full three-layer treatment... Sixteen of these seventeen forces have a genuine, traceable connection to at least one classified gravity... the remaining one (Force 2B-6)..."); the closing line ("*Six cells, seventeen identified forces...*").

**Found:** Section 3's own `#### Force` headers, counted directly, total **nineteen**, not seventeen: Cell 1A (1A-1, 1A-2 — 2), Cell 1B (1B-1, 1B-2, 1B-3 — 3), Cell 2A (2A-1 through 2A-5 — 5), Cell 2B (2B-1 through 2B-6 — 6), Cell 3A (3A-1 — 1), Cell 3B (3B-1, 3B-2 — 2). 2+3+5+6+1+2 = 19. The Force Index table (Section, "Force Index") independently confirms this: it has exactly nineteen rows, one per force ID, matching Section 3 one-for-one.

The Proportionality Principle's second claim is also wrong on its own terms, not just on the total. The Force Index's own "Connected Gravities" column shows **five** forces, not one, marked cross-cutting with no gravity connection: Force 2A-2 ("— (cross-cutting; grounds Force 2B-5)"), Force 2A-4 ("— (cross-cutting; Boundary Ecology)"), Force 2B-6 ("— (cross-cutting; Section 6)"), Force 3A-1 ("— (cross-cutting)"), and Force 3B-2 ("— (cross-cutting; Section 6)"). That means fourteen forces connect to a gravity, not sixteen, and five lack one, not one — Force 2B-6 is not even alone in being a required Transmission entry (Force 3B-2 is the other required Transmission entry per Section 9's own checklist item, and the document elsewhere is careful to treat 2B-6 and 3B-2 as a matched pair, e.g. Section 8's Transmission Specificity Principle discussion). The Proportionality paragraph singling out only Force 2B-6 as the exception is wrong twice over: it undercounts the total and undercounts the exceptions by exactly the same margin (two forces each), suggesting the paragraph was drafted against an earlier, shorter version of Section 3 and never reconciled after Section 3 grew to its current nineteen-force form.

A related, smaller completeness gap: Section 7's own "Forces at Documented or Widely Accepted Level" paragraph explicitly names fourteen forces plus separate treatment for Force 1B-3 and Force 2B-6 (sixteen total), but never states a confidence level in prose for Force 2A-4 or Force 2A-5 anywhere in Section 7 — their "Documented" ratings appear only in the Force Index table. This is consistent with, and likely caused by the same underlying drift as, the miscount above.

**Why it matters:** these are load-bearing self-certifications, not incidental phrasing — the Proportionality Principle's own "Status: CONFIRMED" rests explicitly on these two numbers, and Section 9's completion checklist implicitly inherits them. A "checked, not merely asserted" governing-principle status that gets its own document's basic arithmetic wrong is exactly the category of self-certification failure this project's review discipline exists to catch (cf. CLAUDE.md: "A blocking review finding can't be dismissed by self-certification").

**Fix required:** correct "seventeen" to "nineteen" in all four locations (Section 7, Section 8's Proportionality Principle, the closing line, and anywhere else the count is repeated — check `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` for whether this document's own draft process logged an intermediate 17-force count). Rewrite the Proportionality Principle's second sentence to state the actual figures (fourteen forces connect to a gravity; five — 2A-2, 2A-4, 2B-6, 3A-1, 3B-2 — are treated as cross-cutting) and explain, rather than merely assert, why each of the three non-Transmission cross-cutting forces (2A-2, 2A-4, 3A-1) legitimately has no gravity connection despite not being a Transmission entry. Add explicit prose confidence statements for Force 2A-4 and Force 2A-5 to Section 7.

### Finding 3 — HIGH — G1 is reconnected to Force 1B-1 (Cell 1B), contradicting Doc_04 §3.1's explicit forces-connection statement and Doc_05 §11's corrected handoff — a softer recurrence of this world's twice-caught G1/Cell-1B error

### Finding 4 — MEDIUM — Force 1B-3's Layer 2 entry mislabels a spliced, extended passage as "carried directly"

**Checked:** Force 1B-3, Layer 2: "The magistrate governs bodies and property; he does not sit in judgment on whether a soul may come to the Lord's own table (Doc_05 §4, carried directly) — a discipline answerable to the city's own shifting politics is no discipline at all, which is why this body had to be its own thing from the start, not an arm of the council that had ratified the Sixty-Seven Articles at Zurich."

**Found:** Doc_05 §4's actual inhabited passage (the one Force 2B-4's own Layer 2, three cells over, correctly and verbatim reuses) reads in full: *"The magistrate governs bodies and property; he does not sit in judgment on whether a soul may come to the Lord's own table. Doc_08's own Force 1B-3 entry drops the middle sentence (the actual Perrinist-crisis content: "the council's own men tried to claim...") without an ellipsis, and appends an entirely new clause not present anywhere in Doc_05 — "which is why this body had to be its own thing from the start, not an arm of the council that had ratified the Sixty-Seven Articles at Zurich" — while still labeling the whole thing "(Doc_05 §4, carried directly)." The appended clause is new interpretive/causal content (a claim about *why* the Consistory "had to be its own thing from the start"), invented for this entry and not sourced to any prior document, presented inside inhabited Layer 2 prose under a label that specifically claims direct, unmodified carry-over.

**Why it matters:** this is exactly the kind of reuse-accuracy failure the review brief asked to be checked ("checked for accurate reuse... and for citation-attribution accuracy") — a passage doing double duty for two different forces, decades apart, under a citation tag that overstates its own fidelity, with new content quietly appended inside what a reader is told is a verbatim carry-over. The underlying comparative fact (Zurich's council ratified the Sixty-Seven Articles) is true and previously established, so this is not a fabrication of substance, but it is a misrepresentation of provenance inside inhabited prose — a related but distinct problem from Finding 2's inline-citation issue.

**Fix required:** either write Force 1B-3's own Layer 2 in the document's own words without a "carried directly" label (since it is not, in fact, a direct carry), or select a genuinely applicable Doc_05 passage about the 1541 founding specifically and quote it verbatim, unmodified, with the citation in a header outside the prose per Finding 2's fix.

### Finding 7 — LOW / COSMETIC — Connection 8 names a contrast, not a produces/intensifies/reacts-to relationship

**Checked:** Section 4, Connection 8: *"Force 2A-4 (Counter-Reformation, sustained across the whole window) → Force 3A-1 (no external ending force attested)... Naming this contrast explicitly is itself a finding: sustained external pressure does not, on its own, predict a world's own ending."*

The document's own arrow notation (`→`) implies a directional causal relationship that does not, in fact, hold here — there is no Force 3A-1 for 2A-4 to produce, intensify, or trigger.

**Why it matters:** minor — the underlying observation (sustained pressure did not predict an ending here, unlike Donatism) is sound and honestly framed in the prose immediately following. But the Connection's own header format overstates what is actually being shown.

**Fix required:** none strictly necessary — the prose itself already discloses the contrastive, rather than causal, nature of the finding. Consider relabeling this as a "Named Contrast" rather than a numbered Connection alongside the other seven, or softening the arrow to a different notation, if a future revision pass touches Section 4 anyway.
