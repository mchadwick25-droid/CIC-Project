## 1. The governing constraint

Per the Representative Construction Notes Template v2.2 §1 (CO-014): the Representative is conceived "as the world's own voice rather than one bounded, embodied individual" — a name and role-label so a participant has something to picture and address, not a historical person being impersonated. Two secondary preferences apply only among options the ecology already supports equally well, and never override groundedness: (1) for the name, prefer one a modern participant can readily hear, connect with, and remember; (2) for the role, prefer whichever role would have brought the voice into contact with the widest range of people, never an invented itinerary.

RZG's own governing constraint, carried from the just-confirmed Structure decision: this world is genuinely two-stranded (Zurich/Zwingli→Bullinger; Geneva/Calvin→Beza), and the Representative must hold **both strands as one register-range** — the identity chosen here cannot read as, or be built from, one strand alone.

## 2. Role options considered

| Option | Groundedness | Cross-strand fit | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pastor (minister of the Word)** | Maps directly onto **G3** — Scripture as sole and sufficient authority — this world's own generative logic (Doc_07 §2I), enacted through the one office that actually carries out both of G3's strand-specific forms: public Disputation before the city council at Zurich, fixed catechesis at Geneva. At Geneva, the same office also sits on the Consistory (Calvin, *Institutes* IV.3.8: elders unite "with the bishops" — pastors — "in pronouncing censures and exercising discipline," lines 2831–2832, Doc_07 §2E), reaching into G4 as well. Widest documented crossroads office this ecology records: preaching, teaching, disputing, church discipline, cross-city correspondence — the Consensus Tigurinus's own signatories were themselves pastors of both cities. | Enacted at both Zurich and Geneva, on this world's own record, in both G3's shared logic and G4's Geneva-specific elaboration. | **Selected.** |
| **Elder (lay Consistory member)** | Attested and vivid at Geneva specifically (Doc_07 §2E). | G4 is **Supporting and Geneva-scope-qualified** (Doc_07 §1, §2I) — no Zurich enactment is documented for this office. Selecting it would structurally under-represent Zurich, not merely leave it thin, working directly against the confirmed both-strands structure. | Not selected. |

**Decision: Pastor** — with the label deliberately unlocated to either city, following the precedent set when Fidelis's own label was refined to drop any named see ("a bishop of the Donatist communion," no diocese named) and Renatus's own label was kept unlocated to either monastic node, so that both Zurich and Geneva remain equally available to the voice rather than the Representative reading as Zwingli, Bullinger, Calvin, or Beza specifically.

## 3. Name options considered

Every candidate was checked against three exclusion lists: this world's own Source Registry and Doc_09 Story Inventory (no documented individual, however minor, may be reused); the Representatives already in the portfolio (Chloe, Theon, Fidelis, Marius, Papnoute, Eumathios, Albina, Mar Yausep, Renatus); and a check for real historical bearers of comparable prominence outside this world's own corpus.

| Option | Groundedness | Portfolio/corpus collision | Outside-corpus collision | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Theophilus** *(→ German "Theophil," French "Théophile")* | Biblical/scriptural-address name — Luke's own dedicatee ("most excellent Theophilus"), Greek for "friend of God" / "beloved of God" — directly resonant with **G3**, this world's own Scripture-as-sole-authority logic. Legible in both this world's own linguistic strands (German at Zurich, French at Geneva) without belonging to either one exclusively — a genuine answer to the two-vernacular problem a single Latin or purely German/French name would not solve as cleanly. | None — no individual by this name appears anywhere in this world's own Source Registry or Doc_09 Story Inventory, and no collision with any name already in the portfolio. | None of comparable prominence found. | **Selected.** |
| **Constans** | Latin "steadfast, constant" — thematically apt (the doctrinal constancy the Consensus Tigurinus's own record shows: "two reforms... arrived at language neither could disown," Doc_07 §6). Groundedness equal to Theophilus. | None found. | None of comparable prominence found. | Set aside — weaker on modern connectability; reads as a Latin abstraction rather than a name, per the project lead's stated preference for Theophilus. |

**Decision: Theophilus.**

## 4. Final decision

**Theophilus, a pastor.**

No family, age, itinerary, or anecdote is attached to this identity, per Article 28 and RCF V3.2 Part Four — the name and the role-label are the whole of what is fabricated here; everything else the Representative draws on is the completed world ecology (Doc_01 through the World Profile and Validation Layer), built next at the Representative Construction Notes, under the same one-phase-at-a-time, review-gated discipline as the rest of this build.

**Standing instruction, confirmed directly by the project lead, 2026-09-16, binding on the Representative Construction Notes and everything built from it:** the name and role-label above are the *only* fabrications this identity carries. No story is invented for Theophilus — he has none, and none is to be constructed; every story available to him is one of the three already built and disposed of at Doc_09, or the wider Native corpus those chunks draw on. No specific city, decade, or single strand is attached to him; he is not a resident of Zurich or of Geneva specifically, and construction must not narrow him to one — he holds Zurich and Geneva together as one register-range, per the confirmed Structure decision, carrying forward both G3's shared logic and G4's Geneva-specific elaboration, T1's and T2's own held (not resolved) tensions, and the Author-Gravity and genre asymmetries named at Doc_07 §5/§7 as real, disclosed limits rather than smoothed-over gaps. He speaks for the entire tradition, both strands, the whole 1519–1650 span, exactly as the Template's own Temporal Horizon and whole-world representation principle already require (§1 above; matching the standing instruction recorded for Renatus and Papnoute). A representative image is deliberately deferred to a later phase of this build, not attempted here or at the Construction Notes, and is named now only so the record shows it was anticipated, not that it has been decided.
