## Quick Meaning

The precise formula both Zurich and Geneva agreed to in 1549 — that the sign (bread and wine) is distinguished from, but never disjoined from, the reality it signifies — is the technical language that settled, in words if not beyond all dispute, what neither city's own prior formula had settled alone.

---

## World Meaning

We do not say the bread simply is the body, the way Rome and Wittenberg, in their own different ways, both still say. But we also refuse to say the bread is merely bread, a picture with nothing behind it. Both cities' own pastors put their names to the same careful sentence: though we distinguish, as we ought, between the signs and the things signified, yet we do not disjoin the reality from the signs. Faith receives, truly, what the sign points to — not the sign alone, and not something located inside the sign, but the reality itself, given by the Spirit's own power to those who come believing.

This sentence did not exist before 1549, in exactly this form, in either city's own prior confession. It is the specific language the Consensus Tigurinus was negotiated to produce, and its own two signatories — "the Ministers of the Church of Zurich" and "John Calvin, Minister of the Church of Geneva" — stood behind it together, each recognizing in the other's own confession something they could not disown as their own.

---

## Ecological Function

This is the specific textual proof that the two strands' own shared confessional core is real, not merely asserted by a later historian's classification — the one document literally co-authored across the strand boundary. It is also the formula every future confessional restatement of this world's own Supper doctrine (the Second Helvetic Confession, later Reformed confessions generally) draws on directly, whether or not it quotes the exact words.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A precise, almost legalistic form of words — the kind of careful compromise language that suggests both sides were more interested in signing something than in genuinely agreeing, since real agreement rarely needs sentences this exact.

**World Hearing:**
Exactly that suspicion is a live, unresolved contest this world's own record does not settle (see CT Contest Type below) — but the formula itself was not treated by either signatory as empty diplomacy. It was the specific, hard-won language each side judged it could actually mean, not merely sign.

## CT Contest Type

**Meaning.** Whether this formula represents a genuine theological synthesis of Zwingli's earlier memorial view and Calvin's own developed spiritual-presence doctrine into one coherent position, or a deliberately elastic form of words that let both parties sign without either fully conceding, is live scholarly contest in Reformation historiography. No secondary scholarship on the Consensus Tigurinus is vendored for this world, so this characterization rests on the builder's own general knowledge of the historiography, disclosed as such rather than presented as text-grounded (Doc_03 §4). This document does not adjudicate the contest; a future encounter should carry both readings forward, tagged, rather than resolve to one.
