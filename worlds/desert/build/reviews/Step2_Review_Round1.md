## B. Findings

### SUBSTANTIAL

**1. SUBSTANTIAL — The vendored Cassian is materially incomplete, and the records affirmatively claim the opposite of what the file says.** The npnf211 file contains only stubs for three whole units: Conference XII prints, in full, "**Not translated.**" (file lines 37466–37474, nine lines total); Conference XXII prints "**This Conference is omitted.**" (lines 46037–46047); Institutes Book VI prints "**We have thought best to omit altogether the translation of this book.**" (lines 21559–21568). Against this:
- `desert.source.cassian-conferences.md` says "a drafting-pass claim that the NPNF edition omits Conference XII was checked against the file and **found wrong** at the division level" — the drafting-pass claim was **right in substance**; the check counted `div3` elements without reading the nine lines inside the division, which state the omission in so many words.
- `desert.search.cassian-npnf211.md` says "checked directly after a drafting-pass memory claimed Conference XII was omitted **(it is not)**" — flatly false.
- `desert.source.cassian-institutes.md` describes "The Twelve Books" with no disclosure that the vendored edition translates only eleven of them (Book VI, on fornication — squarely relevant to this world's eight-faults psychology — is untranslated).
- Manifest §2 row: "all 24 Conferences present (checked)".

The hedge in both Cassian records ("whether Gibson left individual passages untranslated inside the chastity/nocturnal-illusions material is NOT yet verified") is itself falsified by the file: it is not passages but the entire Conferences XII and XXII, and the file states it explicitly at the very divisions the drafter opened. This is exactly the repeated-pattern verification failure this project's history warns about. Fix: correct all four locations; the edition fields should state the three omissions as edition facts (they are formation-relevant: the Victorian translator's excisions are themselves a transmission screen).

**2. SUBSTANTIAL — Palladius formation_confidence silently upgraded against the cleared Doc_02.** `desert.source.palladius-lausiac-history.md` assigns `formation_confidence: Documented`. The cleared Doc_02 §2.1 and §9 assign **Widely Accepted** "as to authorship, approximate date, and general content" (and the prior build's migrated row srcDES007 carries that same verification note verbatim). The record set's own pattern elsewhere mirrors Doc_02's assignments exactly (Vita = Documented per §1.1; Apophthegmata, Pachomian corpus, Evagrius, Historia Monachorum = Widely Accepted per §§1.2–2.2), so Palladius is the one divergence — and it is unmarked, in a record set that elsewhere flags every carried correction by name. Possessing the vendored Clarke text documents the edition, not fourth-century authorship/date. Either revert to Widely Accepted or state the upgrade and its argument explicitly (note that Documented ≠ vendored elsewhere in this set: Kellia and Nepheros are Documented without files).

**3. SUBSTANTIAL — Socrates record misfiles the anthropomorphite affair into IV.23–24.** `desert.source.socrates-historia-ecclesiastica.md` describes "IV.23–24 (the monastic digression - lives of the Nitrian and Scetiote ascetics, **the anthropomorphite affair**, and English excerpts from Evagrius…)". The IV.23–24 digression (file lines 13377–13719) contains no anthropomorphite material — I grepped: the anthropomorphite affair appears in Book VI (first hit at file line 17687, in the Theophilus sequence the record's own "VI.7-17 area" clause correctly covers). A step-4 builder citing "the anthropomorphite affair, Socrates IV.23" from this record would cite a chapter that does not contain it. Fix: delete the phrase from the IV.23–24 clause.

**4. SUBSTANTIAL — Graham Gould is load-bearing for two Contested ratings but unregistered, against the deliverable's own stated discipline.** `desert.source.antony-letters.md` and `desert.source.rubenson-letters.md` both rest their Contested formation_confidence on Gould's "published counter-position" ("credentialed, not fringe") — yet no source record registers Gould, while `desert.source.brakke-demons.md`'s own body states the governing principle: records "can cite a real registered source instead of a floating name." The prior build registered exactly this work (srcDES017, *The Desert Fathers on Monastic Community*, OUP). Step 3's contested_claim record (`held_against[]`) will need this registration; creating it is step-2 work. Fix: add a consult-only Gould source record (and consider whether Veilleux, named as the second pole of the version-priority debate and formerly srcDES018, warrants the same — the manifest G2 lists him for acquisition but no record registers him).

**5. SUBSTANTIAL, PLAUSIBLE - needs check — Paieous attributed to the Kramer-Shelton edition.** `desert.source.nepheros-archive.md` author field: "The monks Paieous and Nepheros and their correspondents (4th c. CE); modern edition by B. Kramer and J. C. Shelton (Das Archiv des Nepheros, 1987)". To my knowledge the Paieous correspondence (monastery of Hathor, 330s–340s) is the London Melitian archive, P.Lond. VI (ed. H. I. Bell, 1924), while the 1987 Kramer-Shelton edition covers the later Nepheros dossier — two related but distinct archives. The cleared Doc_02 §5.2 says "centered on the letters of the monks Paieous and Nepheros" without tying Paieous to the 1987 edition; the new record's juxtaposition tightens that into an edition-attribution claim I believe is wrong. I could not consult the editions from this session, so: PLAUSIBLE - needs check before any figure or quote record leans on it.

### COSMETIC

**6.
