## A. SUBSTANTIAL findings

**S1 — both new Sozomen dependence-screen additions assert that Sozomen III.14 tracks Socrates. The vendored edition's own source-note for that chapter says otherwise, and Round 2's M2 handed the fix pass the correct fact.** Two places, both written by this commit:
- `desert.gravity.koinonia.md:35` (manifestation): "…Sozomen's own account draws on **Socrates** and the wider monastic literary tradition **here**…"
- `desert.gravity.authority-tension.md:24` (Sozomen locus): "III.14 … corroborating weight only, since Sozomen **here** tracks **Socrates** and the wider monastic literary tradition, not independent testimony"

"Here" scopes both to III.14 specifically. Read in `cic/texts/npnf202_socrates-sozomen-ecclesiastical-histories.xml`, the chapter's own editorial source-note (immediately after the III.14 division heading, "Of the Holy Men who flourished about this time in Egypt…") reads in full: *"This chapter is made up from a great variety of sources, as well as personal observation. Vita Antonii; Timotheus' collection of monastic biography, mentioned in Soz. vi. 29; Hieron. de vir. illust.; Evagrius Ponticus, Gnosticus; Philippus of Side, Historia Christiana; Sulp. Sev. de Vita Martini."* Socrates is not in the list (grepped the note's whole span: no "Socr" token).

The screen on `desert.source.sozomen-historia-ecclesiastica.md:40-41` is conditional — "**where Sozomen tracks Socrates**, he is not a second witness — corroborating weight only" — and the work-level characterisation at `:20` ("largely dependent on Socrates, the monastic literary tradition, and oral report") is a statement about the *Historia* as a whole. Applying the screen requires establishing its antecedent for the passage cited. The fix pass asserted the antecedent instead of checking it, and it is false at III.14.

This is not a pedantic mismatch, because the true dependency is the one that matters most for `koinonia`'s own claim. That manifestation offers Palladius and Sozomen as the two witnesses to the angel-tablet rule; III.14's declared sources include *Palladius's Lausiac History itself*, so the two are not independent of each other — which is exactly the sharper point Round 2's M2 stated in terms ("Palladius's *Lausiac History* is among the chapter's own declared sources"). The fix pass had the correct fact in front of it, wrote a different and wrong one, and thereby also lost the only reason the caution is genuinely needed for this particular pair. Same defect class as Round 1's S1 and Round 2's S1: an unchecked, checkable assertion about registered material, doing justificatory work — this time about a **vendored** file, the strictest checkability tier this build has.
