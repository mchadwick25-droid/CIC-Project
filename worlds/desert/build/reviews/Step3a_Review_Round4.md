Worse: the record reports "keeping only provision for his sister" as the passage's upshot, but §3 — inside the cited span — has Antony give that reserve away too and place his sister in a convent. Three prior rounds verified the quoted words sat at the cited lines without checking the record's summary against what the full passage says.

**S6 — `desert.term.cheironaxia` drops a standing caution its own source record requires on every use.** `desert.source.nepheros-archive.md` states two cautions travel with every use of that source: the community is Melitian, and its representativeness for the mainstream strands rests on an unverified working assumption. `cheironaxia`'s evidential and informational senses build the lexicon's single strongest corroboration claim ("most solidly corroborated... three independent evidence streams converge... rare density") on that source without either caution.
