# Open Gaps — Desert Fathers and Mothers (Desert Monasticism)

No file existed for this world prior to 2026-09-20 despite the project-wide convention (CLAUDE.md: "Every known gap, open question, or review outcome belongs in that world's `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`"). Created now with its first entry, found during an Opus adversarial review of an unrelated content-system redesign document that spot-checked this world's records against the live site as part of that review.

1. **Sibling record contradiction: `desert.story.sarah-answer` shipped a clause its own sibling `desert.quote.sarah-man-among-you` had already declared unquotable — CLOSED 2026-09-20.** `desert.quote.sarah-man-among-you` was corrected 2026-08-27 to drop "By nature I am a woman, but not by my own thoughts" from Amma Sarah's saying: that clause belongs only to the Greek alphabetical collection (Sarah 4), which has no public-domain English translation to verify it against, and the record's own divergence note declared it "unquotable." `desert.story.sarah-answer` — a separate record narrating the same encounter, related to the quote record via `associated-with` — was never updated to match, and still carried the barred clause in its own `text` field. A tradition-page drafting pass on 2026-09-19 (this session, commit `5ffa278`) pulled the story record's `text` into `cic-website/traditions/desert-monasticism.html`, rendered it in direct quotation marks, and cited it as "verified against the actual record" — which was true of the record it checked, but that record itself was wrong. No existing gate catches this: `gate_reciprocity` checks relations exist, `gate_confidence_crosscheck` checks a Documented/verification pairing, and nothing compares free-text content across related records for consistency. **Fixed 2026-09-20:** `desert.story.sarah-answer.text` now matches `desert.quote.sarah-man-among-you`'s own `modern_rendering` ("It is I who am the man, and you who are the women"); the site copy was corrected to match. **Not yet fixed, and broader than this world:** the underlying gate gap (nothing checks cross-record consistency of related records' free-text claims) is a fleet-level defect, not specific to desert — flagged for the content-system redesign work in progress (`Ministry/Operations/...` review doc) rather than closed here, since it needs a real gate, not a one-off correction.

2. **Site trim of `desert.limit.communal-wrong-unrepaired` broadened the claim beyond what the record supports — CLOSED 2026-09-20.** Found by a targeted Opus recheck of the in-progress content-system redesign, which re-verified the original adversarial review's finding was still live. The record's `statement` reads "...a community of ours that harmed someone and then made it right: named the harm, went to the one harmed, restored what was taken." The same tradition-page drafting pass (`5ffa278`) dropped the clause after "made it right" when rendering this text on `cic-website/traditions/desert-monasticism.html`. This is not a harmless shortening: the record disclaims a specific, three-part definition of repair (naming the harm, approaching the one harmed, restoring what was taken); the trimmed site copy disclaims repair full stop, a broader and less accurate claim than the record makes. **Fixed 2026-09-20:** the site copy now matches the record's statement in full. **Not yet fixed, and broader than this world:** this is the same class of defect as entry 1 (a rendering diverges from its source record with nothing catching it) but on the "trim/shorten" side rather than the "carry over a stale clause" side — both are being folded into the in-progress content-system redesign's rendering-mode design (a length-constrained trim of record prose now requires an LLM-judged check that the trim doesn't change the claim, not a deterministic string match, per Mark's decision 2026-09-20).

3. **`desert.demo.identity-collision-womens-authority` still voiced the barred Sarah clause, in the Representative's own first-person exchange text — CLOSED 2026-09-20.** Found by the desert `world_front` pilot migration (content-system redesign, Stage 2), which checked this demonstration record against its two already-corrected sibling records while sourcing `narrative.questions`. This is the same underlying defect as entries 1 and 2 (the "By nature I am a woman, but not by my own thoughts" clause, established 2026-08-27 as belonging only to an unverifiable Greek recension and barred from being voiced at all), but the most serious instance found so far: this record's `exchange` field is a scripted turn in the Representative's own voice, not site-rendered HTML — meaning this exact wording could have been spoken by the live Representative in a real conversation, not just displayed on a page a participant reads. A prior review round (documented in this record's own body note, "Step5, Round 1 review Finding S4") had already caught and fixed an *adjacent* defect here — the clause was wrongly placed in quotation marks, which the record's own sourcing rules reserve for verified verbatim quotes — but that fix removed the quotation marks and kept the barred words themselves, missing that the underlying clause was never sourceable at all. Neither the sibling `story` nor `quote` record's 2026-08-27/2026-09-20 corrections were ever propagated here. **Fixed 2026-09-20:** the exchange text now reads "It is I who am the man, and you who are the women," matching `desert.quote.sarah-man-among-you`'s own `modern_rendering` exactly. **Not yet fixed, and broader than this world:** this is now the third confirmed instance of a correction to one record failing to propagate to a related record with no gate catching it — direct, repeated evidence for the content-system redesign's own cross-record-consistency gate (`flag_cross_record_consistency`, added Stage 1, not yet registered in the live gate battery). It also demonstrates the risk isn't confined to site HTML: a `demonstration` record can carry the same defect straight into the Representative's own assembled voice, which is a higher-stakes surface than a web page.
