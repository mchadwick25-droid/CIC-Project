## 1. Historical Plausibility Summary

This world's core chronology (c. 320s–c. 430), its founding figures (Antony, Pachomius, Amoun, Macarius, Evagrius), and its three-strand organizational structure rest on Documented/Widely Accepted confidence across independent evidence types — literary (Athanasius, the *Apophthegmata*, the Pachomian corpus), papyrological (the Nepheros archive), and archaeological (the Kellia excavations) — a genuinely unusual density of independent corroboration for a formation world of this period (Doc_02 §§5.1–5.2; Doc_08 throughout). Historical plausibility is assessed as **strong** for the world's broad shape and **appropriately calibrated, not overstated**, for its interior/incident-level claims, where this build has repeatedly and consistently flagged genuine scholarly contest (the *Vita*'s reliability, Theophilus's motives, the *Lives*' recension priority) rather than resolving it for narrative convenience.

## 2. Ecological Integrity Summary

- **Balance:** the ecology gives proportionate weight to its best-evidenced dimensions (formation practice, gravity structure) and does not pad its thinnest dimensions (liturgical content, typological interpretation) with invented specificity (Doc_05 §10; Doc_09a §4's explicit weakest-element flag).
- **Reduction:** this world is not reduced to a single gravity or figure — six Primary, two Supporting, two Tensional gravities are independently tested and cross-strand-verified (Doc_04), and Antony's own centrality is explicitly qualified as generative-but-not-exhaustive (Doc_01 §2.1).
- **Complexity:** the three-strand structure, the authority tension (gravity 10), and the Melitian boundary question are all preserved as genuine, unresolved complexity rather than flattened into a single coherent narrative.
- **Emergence:** this world's own gravities are shown emerging from documented forces (Doc_08's Forces-and-Gravities Synthesis), not asserted as free-standing facts.
- **Worship Integration:** treated honestly as this world's thinnest well-developed dimension (Doc_05 §5; Doc_09b §2) — structure and rhythm are documented, content is not invented to fill the gap.
- **Author Dominance:** actively tested and corrected throughout this build — the Evagrian systematization's single-author concentration was identified at Doc_03 and held to Supporting rather than Primary classification specifically because of it (Doc_04 §3, §6), the clearest demonstration that this world's construction did not let one voice's evidentiary richness stand in for the whole ecology.
- **Anachronism:** actively guarded against — this document's own construction repeatedly distinguished this world's c. 320s–c. 430 usage from later, more systematized homonymous traditions (the Byzantine hesychast movement's full apparatus, explicitly not retrojected onto this world's own *hēsychia*/*nēpsis*, Doc_06 §§1.3, 2.5).
- **Adversarial Resistance Testing:** this is the area where this document can make its strongest claim, and that claim must itself be held to the same accuracy standard it describes. Across eighteen review rounds spanning Doc_01 through Doc_08 (Doc_01: 2 rounds; Doc_02: 2; Doc_03: 2; Doc_04: 3; Doc_05: 1; Doc_06: 3; Doc_07: 2; Doc_08: 3), cold independent adversarial review found real defects at seven of the eight prior stages: an inverted historical claim in Doc_01 (Theophilus's 399 Festal Letter, stamped "well-established" when the claim itself was backwards); a missing required Formation Narrative Sources assessment in Doc_02 (a Step 2 deliverable-completeness gap, not a fabrication); a fabricated Doc_02 citation supporting an inverted transmission-history claim in Doc_03 (one integrity-level finding, not two); a genuinely broken candidate-numbering scheme requiring three review rounds in Doc_04, including an illusory "fixed" claim that a second review round caught; Doc_05 cleared with only cosmetic findings in a single round, no substantial defect; a Framework-structure violation and a recurring incomplete-fix pattern requiring three rounds in Doc_06; an analytic overreach in Doc_07 (an architecture claim contradicting the document's own synthesis); and a missing-force synthesis gap plus a recurring non-conforming confidence label requiring three rounds in Doc_08. This is offered as evidence the adversarial testing was genuine, not pro forma — a construction process that found nothing wrong across eight documents would itself be the more suspicious outcome — and that same review discipline extends to catching this document's own self-referential claims about itself, not only claims about the historical world.
