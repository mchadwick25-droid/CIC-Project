## Named acquisition gaps (not yet Registry rows — no source exists to classify)

Per the Boundary Check's own logic, a row requires an actual source to classify; these are named here, outside the numbered table, as open acquisitions rather than given a row with no source behind it. **Expanded this revision (Round 1 review, MEDIUM finding):** the first draft named only the two items below and declined, on principle, to name items with no vendored source at all — corrected here, since the Template's own "every source currently known" instruction, and this Registry's own rows 17–18 (Young, Kelly, neither vendored either), already establish that a named-but-unheld source belongs in this document.

- **Palladius, *The Dialogue of Palladius Concerning the Life of Chrysostom*, tr. Moore (1921).** Verified acquisition lead, `not-yet-downloaded`, already logged in `worlds/_cross-world/download-queue-seed.yaml` (verified 2026-09-13).
- **Diodore of Tarsus's own exegetical works.** No public-domain English translation path exists (Source Readiness Dossier §4; Doc_01 §7).
- **Theodore of Mopsuestia's *Commentary on the Twelve Prophets*** (his earlier, Antiochene-presbyterate work — the piece that would actually anchor this world, as distinct from his later episcopal-Mopsuestia writing). No public-domain translation identified; the Mingana 1932 translation of a different work (his *Commentary on the Nicene Creed*) carries its own open 2028 rights question (Source Readiness Dossier §4).
- **Paul W. Harkins, *Discourses Against Judaizing Christians* (Fathers of the Church, 1979).** Named in Doc_02 §9 as the standard modern translation of the *Adversus Judaeos* material; confirmed well within copyright, not a PD candidate.
- **Robert L. Wilken, *John Chrysostom and the Jews* (University of California Press, 1983), and Wayne Meeks and Robert Wilken, *Jews and Christians in Antioch* (Scholars Press, 1978).** Census-named secondary scholarship on this world's own Jewish-community material; not vendored, not independently read this session — named here so a future pass does not treat their absence as an oversight rather than a disclosed gap.
- **Athanasius's *Tomus ad Antiochenos*, the Serapion of Antioch fragments (`anf08`), and Malchion's synodal letter against Paul of Samosata (`anf06`).** Each already considered and correctly declined as a cross-link to this world by the prior source-research pass (Source Readiness Dossier §2: wrong schism/era in each case) — named here as declined candidates, not overlooked ones, per V1.8's own instruction that every dossier §2 item receive a disposition line.
