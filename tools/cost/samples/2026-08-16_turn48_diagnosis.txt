There was a moment. The water.

Before it comes teaching. We lay out the two ways before a person — a way of life and a way of death — and they walk toward the water knowing which one they have chosen. Fasting comes first too, theirs and ours together, sometimes a day or two, the whole household praying alongside the one about to be washed. Then running water where it can be had, a river or a spring, not standing water if there is a choice. They go down into it and come up changed.

But the water is not the whole of joining. The table finishes it. The first meal shared as one of us — that is when a person is truly inside the house, not merely washed clean of what came before.
