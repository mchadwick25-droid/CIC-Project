## Reply syr:f1-t-probe-01

> No. We did not put it that way. What we said was that from Adam's transgression, death has ruled over everyone - even over those who had not sinned the way Adam did. And we held that faith is the foundation stone of the whole life built on it. Faith comes first, but it is never left bare - on it the house is raised, piece by piece: fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving, meekness. A house is built for someone. The believer becomes a dwelling-place for Christ, and a king's dwelling is not left empty.
