## 4. Checked and closed

| candidate | why it looked promising | why it's closed |
|---|---|---|
| H.J. Lawlor, "The Sayings of Paul of Samosata," *Journal of Theological Studies* os-XIX (1917–18) | The obvious scholarly starting point on Paul of Samosata | Genuinely freely accessible (full-text on archive.org, `sim_journal-of-theological-studies_1917-10_19_73`, confirmed via direct fetch) — but it's source-critical scholarship, not a translation: Lawlor quotes the reconstructed sayings in Greek, with English limited to his own commentary. Not corpus-eligible as a primary-source text. The follow-up "Additional Notes" (JTS os-XIX, 74–75) wasn't found among currently-indexed archive.org scans. |
| Roger Pearse's 2023 PD translation of "Ps.-Dionysius of Alexandria's Letter to Paul of Samosata" | A translated primary letter, explicitly PD-marked | Per Pearse's own site and cited scholarship (de Riedmatten 1952), the letter is spurious — likely a late-4th-century Apollinarian forgery, possibly later. Not genuine 264–268 Antioch-synod material; would file as pseudepigraphal, not as this world's own tradition. |
