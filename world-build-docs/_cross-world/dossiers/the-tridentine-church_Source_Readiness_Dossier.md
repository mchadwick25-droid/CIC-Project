## 5. Open cross-world questions

**Vs. Society of Jesus (VI.11), researched separately this same pass:** real but not disqualifying overlap. See that dossier's own §5 for the mirror of this note — both worlds would likely cite the Waterworth Canons/Decrees, but this world holds native/foundational claim (the body that convened and enacted them) while the Jesuits hold implementing/corroborating claim. Flag for whichever Doc_02 runs first.

**Vs. Borromean Milan (VI.12), a separate, narrower census entry not yet researched:** narrower risk than it looks. Since Borromeo's own primary voice has no PD English translation at all (§4 above), neither candidate can claim him as a "native voice" source — both are limited to secondary/biographical material if either wants him (e.g., Giussano's *Life of St. Charles Borromeo*, PD, on archive.org). This actually lowers collision risk. Recommend this world treat Borromeo only as a named figure inside the broader implementation narrative, not attempt his own voice; the real VI.22/VI.12 distinction is scale (whole-church vs. one diocese), not competing sources. VI.12's own census sourcing note ("rich institutional + visitation") should be verified separately — likely diocesan/visitation scholarship, not Borromeo's own primary voice.

## 6. Step 0 scope notes (for whoever drafts Doc_01/the Step 0 confirmation)

**Doctrinal floor clears without question** — this is the Catholic Church itself, uncontroversially Nicene/Chalcedonian.

**Real gravity risk, disclosed rather than smoothed over:** the inverse of the Jesuits' "founder-corpus" problem. This candidate's real gravity risk is thinness of interior/pastoral voice (no Pole, no Borromeo in English), not domination by one figure's writing. Its available corpus is institutional, doctrinal, and narrative-history in character — the Canons, the Catechism, and a hostile external narrative (Sarpi) — rather than a personal formation voice the way Luther's, Calvin's, or Ignatius's own corpora are. Doc_02 should name this plainly as this world's own distinctive ecological shape, not a defect to paper over.
