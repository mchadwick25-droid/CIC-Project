# Cascade Check: Do the Fragile Step 2 Entries Actually Carry Weight Downstream?

**Author:** Fable (model), follow-up to `CiC_Fable_Analysis_Disciplined_Rebuild_vs_Current_2026-07-04.md`, same date.
**Occasion:** The project lead correctly objected that my prior report's "small/modest marginal gain" ratings for Steps 1, 4, 5, 7, 8 were **process-quality judgments, not evidentiary-base judgments**. The Construction Framework is a downward-dependency chain; I had flagged specific Step 2 entries as thin, memory-resting, or unverified without checking whether those entries are actually *cited in support of specific claims* in Doc_04 (Gravity Discovery), Doc_05 (Ecological Reconstruction), Doc_07 (Integrated Ecology Analysis), Doc_08 (Forces), or Doc_09 (Story Inventory). This report does that check: a survey across all five worlds, then a full trace for Early-Communal (Chloe), then corrected per-step estimates for Chloe, then a revised bottom line. All claims below come from re-reading the actual files on `CiC-Fable-Experiment`.

**The objection was right, and it changes the map — unevenly.** Three worlds mostly confirm containment. One (Desert) has a genuine cascade my prior report missed entirely. And the pilot-candidate world (Chloe) turns out to be the *opposite* of contained: nearly every fragile Step 2 entry there is load-bearing downstream, because Chloe's fragile entries *are* its core corpus.

---

## Part 1 — Dependency-Cascade Survey, All Five Worlds

### Alexandria / Theon — 0 of 2 flagged entries load-bearing downstream. Containment confirmed.

- **Daniélou (Doc_02B entry 28, Confidence C, memory-resting).** Its function is *defensive/negative*: it grounds why theosis must be framed as eschatological-completion and NOT as Nyssa's epektasis. Doc_04 and Doc_05 discuss theosis extensively — but never cite Daniélou, never mention epektasis, and never argue the framing against the Nyssa alternative. If Daniélou were excluded, no downstream gravity or lens finding changes; what weakens is the justification for the exclusion rule itself — a Step 2/2A-layer problem, exactly where the prior report already located it.
- **The unlocated Origen burning-bush reading (possible fabrication).** Absent from Doc_04, Doc_05, the Integrated Ecology Analysis, and the Forces Document entirely. It lives in the deployment layer, not the analytical chain — still a live defect, but not a cascade through Docs 04-08.
- The other Confidence-C entries (Chadwick, Young, and the comparanda Tertullian/Cyril/Perpetua): none appear downstream at all.

### Early Latin / Cordus — 1 of 7 fragile entries load-bearing (properly hedged). Containment essentially confirmed.

- **"Mundus senescit": 0 load-bearing downstream uses.** The phrase does not appear in Doc_04 or the Forces Document at all. Its appearances elsewhere are all register/color, not argued *from*. The correction needed is real but surgical: fix the verbatim presentation in five files — no gravity re-derivation follows.
- **Tyconius (entry 16)** is the one load-bearing case, part of the evidence stream for the "Scripture becoming Latin" gravity, used with the adversarial-transmission flag properly attached.
- Two Doc_04 stream-sources (Prudentius, Salvian) lack Doc_02B rows — a coverage gap, but neither carries a claim alone.

### Desert / Kimon — 2-3 genuine cascades, including one the prior report missed. Containment NOT confirmed here.

- **Antony D. Rich, *Discernment in the Desert Fathers* — an admittedly unverified source with NO Doc_02B row sits inside a gravity classification.** Supporting Gravity 5 (diakrisis as master-virtue) carries Rich as "a named specialist warrant... unverified against the edition in this run" — material adopted from the V6 corpus, which the build's own Final Report admits is compounded memory, worse than fresh recall. This is exactly the cascade shape the project lead's objection predicted.
- **Palladius (entry 17, C, no locus checked)** underwrites Doc_05's three-position women's social ecology.
- **Wipszycka (entry 20, D — lowest-graded entry in the table)** is load-bearing in Doc_05 and Doc_08, though as a restrictive caution (safe failure direction).
- The malformed Doc_02B table row is entry 7, Evagrius's *Praktikos* — a formatting defect on a Confidence-A, heavily load-bearing entry.

### Nicene-Cappadocian / Eumathios — 1 of 4 fragile secondary-scholarship entries load-bearing, in a mediated way. Mostly contained, one honest complication.

- **Ayres/Behr (entry 19, C, memory-resting).** Never cited downstream directly, but operationalized: Doc_06 carries its content as a contest-flag, Doc_04's "blur discipline" is the caution applied as a construction mandate — but by Doc_07 the flag is dropped, presenting the ontology as settled. A real, if low-probability, cascade: if the memory-characterization were wrong, the correction would have to chase an uncited, absorbed discipline through three documents.
- Rousseau, Silvas, Holman: genuinely contained.

## Part 2 — Deep Cascade Trace: Early-Communal (Chloe)

Chloe's Doc_02B has 20 entries: 9 at A, 6 at B, 5 at C. Unlike Alexandria, where fragile entries were peripheral scholarship, **Chloe's fragile entries are Pliny, 1 Clement, Diognetus, Hermas, Polycarp's Philippians, Lampe, Bradshaw, and Moss — the world's core evidence and its three governing modern reconstructions.**

**Entry 11 — Pliny, Ep. 10.96-97 (B; the dual-citation artifact).** The most load-bearing single source in the entire build, and the one the two Step 2 artifacts grade divergently (Ledger: Tier A; Doc_02B: B — in a document whose header falsely asserts no Ledger exists). It is the decisive witness for a Doc_04 gravity-interaction test, the stated ground for declining "martyrdom as formation center" as a gravity, the sole outside-witness for a persistence claim, two full sections of Doc_05, Doc_07's self-declared "highest-integration point," Doc_08's central ongoing force, two story entries, and four participant-facing prompt lines. **The two Step 2 artifacts currently disagree about the confidence of the single node the most downstream weight hangs from, and one contains a false statement about the other's existence.** A gated Step 2 resolves this in an hour; nothing else in the pipeline ever forces it.

**Entry 13 — 1 Clement (B).** The gravity classified Primary-with-reservation (G4, the Deposit) is argued *from* this row; it also underwrites Doc_07's central contestation site, two Forces items, a Tier 1 story, and the deployed prompt's second grounding anchor.

**Entry 14 — Epistle to Diognetus (B, dating Contested, minority view places it outside the world's span).** The sharpest participant-facing finding: the deployed prompt says, near-verbatim and uncaveated, "Every foreign land is our fatherland, and every fatherland foreign" — a participant hears a line that, on the minority dating, comes from outside the world the voice claims to speak from. A gated Step 2 would force an explicit licensing decision here.

**Entry 15 — Hermas Vis. 2.4.3 (B).** A single verse held at B, never re-fetched, is the whole evidentiary basis for a Doc_09 story and a women's-teaching claim in Doc_05.

**Entry 16 — Polycarp, To the Philippians (B).** The deployed prompt's defense against the world's own known misdating failure (the anti-Novatian guardrail) is built on a B-grade row never independently re-fetched.

**Entry 17 — Aristides (C).** Contained in effect — one of several parallel streams; if excluded, the gravity it supports stands unchanged.

**Entry 18 — Lampe (C, no page checked).** The entire organizational ecology of the world — "Rome is a constellation of cells," the NOT-approved-for flag against monarchical episcopacy — hangs on a page-unverified characterization of one monograph.

**Entry 19 — Bradshaw (C).** An absorbed discipline governing Doc_05's entire worship-claims structure, resting on an unverified characterization — Chloe's own version of Eumathios's Ayres/Behr problem.

**Entry 20 — Moss/Ste.Croix/Barnes (C).** Shapes a span-wide Forces characterization, a declined gravity, and — participant-facing — the prompt's proportionality register: "Most days the Name costs a little. Some days, in some cities, it has cost everything" is this characterization rendered in-voice.

**Cross-reference items — Eusebius and Acts of Justin (both C).** Both feed Doc_09 Tier 1 story assignments, exposing a tier/grade mismatch: Tier 1 means Documented at narrative level, but the sole sources are graded C.

**Beyond the flagged entries.** Verified sources cited downstream in support of specific claims with **no registry row at all**: Lucian, Tacitus, Celsus, Justin 1 Apol. 61/65, Papias, 2 Clement, Barnabas, Justin's Dialogue, Melito, Irenaeus, the Acts of the Scillitan Martyrs, Tertullian, Hurtado, Didache chapters 13-16. **Five of Doc_09's seventeen stories rest wholly on sources with no Step 2 row.** This is exactly what the new checkpoint rule exists to prevent — for Chloe the uncovered set is roughly 25-30 sources.

**Honest calibration:** dense dependency is not the same as expected error. Nearly all of Chloe's B-grade rows are famous, stable, easily fetched texts; the likely outcome of a real Step 2 redo is verify-and-upgrade, not discovery of falsehood. The real, high-probability revisions: the Pliny grading reconciliation and false header; the Diognetus licensing decision (possibly editing a deployed prompt line); the story tier reconciliation; ~25-30 new registry rows; page-level checks of Lampe/Bradshaw/Moss with possible label adjustments; fetching 1 Clem. 42/44 to resolve G4's flagged status. None of this is currently visible from inside any single document.

## Part 4 — Bottom Line

**Does the cascade check change the recommendation? It changes its shape in one important way, and the project lead's objection is what found it.**

1. **Alexandria, Early Latin, Nicene-Cappadocian: containment confirmed by the trace, not merely asserted.** Targeted remediation (Option B) remains right for these three, now on checked rather than impressionistic grounds.

2. **Desert/Kimon: the prior report was wrong to imply containment.** An unverified source with no registry row sits inside a gravity classification. Option B still covers this *if* Desert's registry work explicitly includes re-pointing Doc_04 Gravity 5 and Doc_05 — add that to Kimon's named defect list.

3. **Chloe: do the full ten-step disciplined pilot, not a Step-2-only fix — a stronger recommendation than before, for a better reason.** The trace shows Chloe's downstream chain is densely dependent on exactly its fragile Step 2 layer. A Step-2-only remediation would build the correct registry and then leave the re-pointing of Doc_04/05/08, the story tier reconciliation, and the prompt-line licensing decisions undone — diagnosis without half the treatment. The full pilot would surface and close real, currently-invisible revisions, and makes the pilot more informative: Chloe is the world where gating has the most to catch, so a gated pass that still finds little would be strong evidence for closing the case on full rebuilds of the other four; a pass that finds what this trace predicts gives a measured, per-step cost/yield record to decide with.

4. **Honesty about magnitude:** dense dependency does not mean the world is likely wrong. The expected outcome of the pilot is a handful of genuine corrections, not overturned gravities. The pilot's value is converting "almost certainly right but never checked" into "checked," on the one world where checking demonstrably touches what participants hear.

5. **Unchanged regardless:** commissioning the first Article 31 external human review as soon as one registry exists, and the deployment-layer workstream, stand exactly as before. The participant-facing Cordus items (verbatim tag, Fidentius briefs) remain the only defects live in deployment-facing files today and should still be sequenced first.

**One sentence for the record:** the prior report's "small gain" ratings for Steps 4, 5, and 8 were process-quality judgments that happened to be right for three worlds and wrong for the pilot world and partially wrong for Desert; the corrected recommendation is targeted remediation for Alexandria, Early Latin, and Nicene-Cappadocian, targeted remediation plus two named Doc_04/Doc_05 re-points for Desert, and the full ten-step gated pilot — not a Step-2-only fix — for Chloe.
