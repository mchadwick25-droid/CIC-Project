# Source Registry — The Old Believers (obel)

Companion to `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`, built together with it in one pass to `Build/reference/L3B-World-Build-Methodology/Source_Registry_Template.md`. The world's corpus-map bucket (`cic/corpus-map/the-old-believers.yaml`) holds two work-rows, the two vendored Avvakum files, and both are covered by rows 1 and 2 below. Boundary checked against this world's own Doc_01 (`Doc_01_World_Identification_Boundaries_Orientation.md`): 1666-1815; central and northern Muscovite Russia and Siberia; Russian and Church Slavonic; a ritual-and-textual (not doctrinal) schism (Doc_01 §9); strand determination not yet decided (Doc_01 §6).

Corpus figures, counted two ways. The English file row 1 holds 36,763 words by `wc -w` under the `POSIX` locale and 36,938 under `C.UTF-8`; splitting the text on whitespace in Python 3 gives 36,938. The Russian file row 2 holds 5,330 words by `wc -w` under `POSIX`, which miscounts Cyrillic, and 20,660 under `C.UTF-8` and in Python 3. The counts include each file's provenance header. The 116 bracketed glosses in row 2 are counted by a Python 3 regular expression and by `grep -o` on the body under both locales.
