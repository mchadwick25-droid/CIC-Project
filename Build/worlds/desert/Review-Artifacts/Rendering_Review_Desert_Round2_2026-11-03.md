# Rendering Review: desert quote records, round 2 (9 records)

- **Reviewer:** Opus 5.5 (independent; did not draft, render or review these records before)
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Method (short)

This is a targeted recheck of the nine renderings that failed round 1 (Rendering_Review_Desert_Batch1, Batch2 and Batch3, 2026-11-03). For each record, the reviewer reads the edition text in `cic/texts/` at the round-1 span and compares the new `modern_rendering` with it clause by clause. The bar is: every clause rendered; nothing added; one thought per sentence; no sentence past about 25 words; no word whose modern sense misleads; hedges, tense and reported-speech frames kept; editors' bracketed insertions kept marked or left out; plain English; consistent with `use_note.not_for` (none of the nine records has a `divergence_note`). Sentence lengths are counted by script and by hand. The reviewer also checks the antony `text` change against the edition at line 47574, and the nous record's `use_note.means` and `modern_lens_note` changes.
