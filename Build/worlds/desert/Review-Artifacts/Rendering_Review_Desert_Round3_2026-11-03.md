# Rendering Review: desert quote records, round 3 (3 records)

- **Reviewer:** Opus 5.5 (independent; did not render or review these records before)
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Method (short)

This is a targeted recheck of the three records that failed P2 in the bracketed-supplements review of 2026-11-03. For each record, the reviewer reads the edition at its locus and confirms that `text` matches it. The reviewer then checks the new `modern_rendering` against the full bar: every clause rendered, nothing added, editor's bracketed supplements kept in brackets or left out where they add meaning, one thought per sentence, no sentence past about 25 words (counted), no misleading modern word, hedges, tense and frames kept, plain English. The changed `use_note.means` and `modern_lens_note` are checked for accuracy and for attributing bracketed matter to the editor. The bar is something wrong, missing, added or misleading, not style.

Edition lines read: `cic/texts/evagrius_praktikos_dysinger.txt` lines 158-161 (pr 8) and 187-196 (ch. 6); `cic/texts/pachomius_rules-ethiopic_schodde1885.txt` line 206. Each record's `text` matches its edition.
