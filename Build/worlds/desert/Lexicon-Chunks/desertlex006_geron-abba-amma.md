**Quick Meaning:** Honorific address for a spiritually authoritative elder, male (abba) or female (amma), whose sayings and example carry teaching authority without formal ecclesiastical office.

**World Meaning:** This vocabulary cluster names the entire authority structure of this world's more solitary and semi-communal ascetics. Authority is earned through recognized discernment, not conferred by office, and is transmitted through direct, personal relationship rather than formal instruction. Plural-voices flag: the ammas' own share of surviving material is thin relative to the male-authored and male-centered bulk of the tradition — named ammas are genuinely attested, not merely inferred, but this entry does not overstate the evidential balance.

**Ecological Function:** The primary transmission mechanism for spiritual combat and discernment teaching; stands in this world's own documented tension against the communal-rule strand's formal, office-based authority model.

**Distortion Risk:** Modern Hearing — a generic "spiritual mentor" relationship resembling contemporary coaching or therapy. World Hearing — a relationship carrying real, if informal, authority: a disciple's disclosure of interior thoughts to an elder was itself considered spiritually necessary, not merely helpful, and the elder's counsel was not one input among many but authoritative guidance.

**Key Sources:** The *Apophthegmata Patrum* — Widely Accepted as to the corpus's general origin in this world's own oral teaching tradition; Contested as to how faithfully any individual saying preserves its original strand-specific context versus reflecting later compilers' own arrangement. The named *ammas* — Syncletica, Theodora, Sarah — thin relative to the male-authored and male-centered bulk of the surviving material, but real and specifically named, not merely inferred: Widely Accepted that named *ammas* and their sayings are a genuine, if thin, part of the *Apophthegmata* tradition; Inferential / Thin for any claim beyond what the surviving sayings themselves state. Their material is transmitted entirely through the same later-compiled apparatus as the male-attributed sayings, compounded by a much smaller sample size for any individual cross-check.
