# Doc_04 — Gravity Discovery — Independent Adversarial Review (Round 1)

**World #3: Desert Monasticism**
**Reviewer:** cold, independent pass. No drafting context. Every cross-reference checked against the cited section's actual content.
**Ground truth consulted in full:** Doc_01, Doc_02, Doc_03 (all Approved), and Framework V7.3 Part III / Step 4 including the verbatim six-test and Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check definitions.

## (a) Overall verdict

**SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED.**

The document is fluent, well-organized, and does real work — its candidate generation is genuinely traceable, its single-voice flags are correctly placed at generation per the Framework, and its cross-strand table is largely faithful to Doc_01. But it fails on several load-bearing points, including the two the document stakes its methodological credibility on:

1. Its showcase Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check (candidate 9) is **mislabeled** — it dresses a Persistence-test failure as a thin-evidence problem, when the evidence is in fact Widely Accepted, so the Cross-Check as the Framework defines it does *not* bar candidate 9 from Primary. The right answer (Supporting) is reached for the wrong stated reason.
2. Its forces-test "fracture" claim **conflates the 399–400 Origenist controversy with the 553 condemnation** and misattributes Doc_02 §1.4, importing a Doc_08-level ending/transmission force into a Doc_04 structural classification.
3. It operates **two conflicting candidate-numbering schemes**, and cross-references break across them.
4. One of its two Tensional gravities (**#10, authority tension**) is classified **without ever being generated as a candidate or run through any of the six tests, the Cross-Check, the forces test, or cross-strand testing.**
5. It **drops three hand-offs that Doc_01 and Doc_02 explicitly reserved for "Doc_04"** — most notably the Rubenson/Antony-literacy re-flag that both upstream docs routed specifically to "Doc_04's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check."

Plus a cluster of citation-fidelity errors. Several findings interlock with this step's missing L4 template: the document has substituted a plausible narrative shape for the missing scaffolding in exactly the places rigor was supposed to live (the Cross-Check, the forces test, and the traceable candidate-to-classification chain).

## (b) Findings

### SUBSTANTIAL

**Finding 1 (substantial) — Two conflicting candidate-numbering schemes; cross-references break across them.**
**Sections:** 1–5 vs. 6, 8.
Sections 1–5 number candidates 6 = koinōnia, 7 = practical scriptural engagement, 8 = economic embeddedness, 9 = Evagrian. Section 6 silently renumbers into a fresh 1–10 sequence in which 6 = scriptural, 7 = koinōnia, 8 = Evagrian, 9 = economic, 10 = a new authority tension. The two schemes then collide inside single cross-references: Section 6's Tensional #10 describes "the tension between candidate 3's informal authority mode and candidate 7's formal one" — under the Sections 1–5 numbering a reader has been using, candidate 7 is scriptural engagement, not an authority mode at all. Section 8's open items 4 and 5 use two incompatible numbering systems for the same integers in adjacent sentences. **Fix:** one stable candidate index throughout.

**Finding 2 (substantial) — The showcase Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check (candidate 9) is mislabeled; it reaches the right classification via the wrong stated basis.**
**Section:** 3 (and Section 6, item 8).
The Framework's Cross-Check bars Primary only when evidence "rests primarily on Inferential/Thin evidence." Doc_02 §1.4 rates Evagrius's ascetic-practical corpus "Widely Accepted as to authorship and general content" — which meets the Primary confidence floor. The Cross-Check as the Framework defines it does not bar candidate 9. What actually disqualifies it is that it "Fails cross-strand persistence" (the document's own Section 2 wording) — a Persistence/Formation-test failure, not a confidence failure. The document conflates single-author concentration (an Author Gravity axis) with low evidential confidence (the Cross-Check axis) and labels the result a Cross-Check outcome. Candidate 6 (koinōnia) is handled correctly in the same section ("the confidence level itself is not the limiting factor... the limiting factor is Persistence"); candidate 9 deserved identical treatment. **Fix:** classify candidate 9 Supporting on Persistence/cross-strand grounds; state plainly that its confidence (Widely Accepted) would not by itself bar Primary, and that concentration is an Author Gravity concern distinct from the Cross-Check.

**Finding 3 (substantial) — Forces-test "fracture" conflates the 399–400 controversy with the 553 condemnation, misattributes Doc_02 §1.4, and imports Doc_08-level material into a Doc_04 classification.**
**Section:** 4 (and Section 6, item 8).
Section 4 pins Evagrius's pseudonymous survival on "the 399–400 Origenist controversy... broke its transmission under its own name, surviving only pseudonymously thereafter (Doc_02 §1.4)." But Doc_02 §1.4 attributes the pseudonymous transmission specifically to the 553 condemnation (Second Council of Constantinople), not 399–400. The document collapses two events roughly 150 years apart into one "external doctrinal force" and cites the §1.4 passage (about 553) to support a claim pinned to 399–400. Separately, the Framework's forces test asks about "the forces identified in Steps 1 and 2" — the 399–400 controversy is not among Doc_01 §7's four Step-1 forces, and Doc_02 §8 explicitly reserves this pattern for Doc_08's Forces Document. Using this transmission-history event as "decisive corroboration" of a Doc_04 structural classification is premature and conflates transmission vulnerability with the gravity's organizing role during the world's actual life. **Fix:** correct the 399/553 attribution; demote the transmission material from "decisive corroboration" to a flagged lead for Doc_08.

**Finding 4 (substantial) — Tensional gravity #10 (person- vs. office-based authority) is classified without ever being generated or tested.**
**Sections:** 6 (item 10), 8 (item 5).
Section 1 generates candidates 1–9. The authority tension is never generated there, never appears in the Section 2 six-test table, never gets a Section 3 Cross-Check, never runs the Section 4 forces test, and never appears in the Section 5 cross-strand table — it materializes for the first time in Section 6 as a co-equal Tensional gravity. The raw material is real (Section 2's Interaction cells for candidates 3 and 6 name this tension; Doc_01 §6 supports it), but promoting an interaction between two already-tested candidates into a standalone classified gravity while bypassing the entire six-test pipeline applied to everything else is the "new gravity added to fill out the classification scheme" risk. **Fix:** either generate it as a proper candidate and run it through Sections 2–5, or present it explicitly as a derived tension between candidates 3 and 6 rather than a tenth gravity on equal footing with tested candidate 8.

**Finding 5 (substantial) — Three hand-offs that Doc_01/Doc_02 explicitly reserved for "Doc_04" are dropped.**
**Sections:** 3, 5, 8.
- **Rubenson/Antony-literacy re-flag (missing entirely).** Doc_01 §10 and §11 item 4, and Doc_02 §1.3, all reserve this specifically for "Doc_04's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check." Doc_04 Section 3 never mentions it. This bears directly on candidates 1 and 3 (the *agrammatos*/oral-elder characterization).
- **Nepheros "fourth pattern" question (dropped, and resolved by silent absorption against an explicit upstream warning).** Doc_02 §5.2 flags Nepheros as possibly not mapping onto any of Doc_01's three strands and expressly reserves the question for "Doc_04's cross-strand gravity testing" (Doc_02 §11 item 2). Doc_04 Section 5 simply slots Nepheros into Strand C without addressing the reserved question.
- **Nag Hammadi–Pachomian proximity (dropped).** Doc_02 §5.3/§11 item 3 flagged this "for further specialized attention before Doc_04... bearing on Doc_04's gravity discovery." Doc_04 is silent. Not adopting either position is correct, but a one-line acknowledgment was owed.

**Finding 6 (substantial, cumulative) — Citation-fidelity errors across the evidence table and Cross-Check.**
- §1, candidate 7 cites "Doc_01 §8.1" for the World #2 Alexandrian comparison; §8.1 is actually the World #9 comparison. The World #2 reference is in Doc_01 §4.
- §3, candidate 8 claims "Kellia excavation, Nepheros archive — both Documented per Doc_02 §9"; Doc_02 §9's Documented tier lists Kellia but not Nepheros, which carries no assigned confidence level and a heavy Melitian-representativeness caveat.
- §3, candidate 8 cites "Doc_02 §10" for the Goehring-claims-unverified point; the actual reservation is Doc_02 §11 item 4 / §7 (and Doc_01 §11 item 6).
- §2 table, candidate 5 Dependency cites "Cassian's later 'mother of all virtues' framing, Doc_03 §1.9" — Doc_03 §1.9 says "master virtue governing all others" and does not contain a "mother of all virtues" phrase or attribute anything to Cassian there.
