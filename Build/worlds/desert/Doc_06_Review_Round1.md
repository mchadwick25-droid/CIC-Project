**Reviewer stance:** Cold, isolated, adversarial. No drafting context. Every claim treated as unverified until checked against the governing Framework and the Approved ground-truth documents (Doc_01–Doc_05) and the nine deployment chunk files.

## (a) OVERALL VERDICT: **SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED**

The document is strong where it is most often weak: cross-document citation fidelity is excellent — every gravity number, Doc_05 draw, and spot-checked Doc_02 Key Source resolved correctly, with no fabrication found. The nine Tier 1 construction entries are fully structurally compliant, and the nine deployment chunks are faithful, non-drifted derivatives in the correct format.

However, the document fails Framework structural compliance at the Tier 2 layer specifically, in exactly the way the Framework's language was written to forbid. All seven Tier 2 entries drop the required Quick Meaning section, and six of seven drop Ecological Function — sections the Framework states Tier 2 "should not omit." "Depth should compress; structure should not fragment." Here, structure fragmented. Combined with a misclassified [CT] contest type and an overstated tiering-discipline defense, the document cannot clear as-is.

## (b) NUMBERED FINDINGS

### SUBSTANTIAL

**S1 — All seven Tier 2 entries omit Quick Meaning.** Framework Tier 2 requires "the same structural sections as a Tier 1 entry, at reduced depth... should not omit sections present in Tier 1 entries." Entries 2.1–2.7 open directly with World Meaning. Aggravated by Part V: Quick Meaning "exists specifically to serve runtime retrieval," and two affected terms (2.6 *synaxis*, 2.7 *kellion*) carry [RT].

**S2 — Six of seven Tier 2 entries omit Ecological Function.** Only 2.1 (*xeniteia*) includes one. Same violation class as S1.

**S3 — The apatheia [CT] contest type is misclassified, and the tension is relocated from where Doc_04 placed it without disclosure.** The stated contest type ("application to this specific formation world") should be "historical scope (whether it applied as broadly as claimed)" per the actual Rubenson/Gould dispute (a generalization dispute, per Doc_02 §1.3 and Doc_01 §10). Separately, Doc_04 §3 routes the Rubenson/Antony-literacy tension to candidates 1 and 3 specifically, stating it does *not* threaten their attestation; Doc_06 instead attaches [CT] to *apatheia* without building the bridge or noting the relocation. The citations themselves are accurate; the defect is the type label and the silent relocation.

**S4 — Tier-proportion / tiering-discipline defense is overstated.** Tier 1 is 50% of the lexicon (Framework expects Tier 2 to be the majority). The intro claims the nine Tier 1 terms "track Doc_04's six Primary gravities plus *koinōnia*" — but only six anchor a gravity; *apotagē* (1.2) and *hēsychia* (1.3) anchor no gravity and are Tier 1 on the Framework's separate "central conceptual clusters" criterion, which is legitimate but misdescribed by the intro's framing.
