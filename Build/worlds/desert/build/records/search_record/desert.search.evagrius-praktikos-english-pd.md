answered it correctly. The right question was "is there a vendorable
one", and public domain is only one of the ways a text can be that.

Sinkewicz (2003), the Philokalia translations and Guillaumont's critical
editions are all in copyright and remain consult-only, and the Socrates
HE IV.23 excerpts in npnf202 remain the corroborating second witness.
The Chapters on Prayer and the Antirrhetikos are still unvendored.
