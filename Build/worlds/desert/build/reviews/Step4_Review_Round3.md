## A. SUBSTANTIAL findings

**S1 — `desert.limit.f6-t-outsiders-and-narrowness`, restored this pass to close Round 2's flagship S2, declares a *Vita* silence that nine sections of the *Vita* fill, in the compiled `statement`, in `why_sources_cannot_answer`, and in its only `sources[].locus`.** The record's single source entry:

> locus: "**the Vita's own silence on the standing of those wholly outside the Christian community**"

and the compiled `statement` (emitted by `build_prompt()` as "Honest limit (F6-T)"):

> "Did we think people outside the faith were condemned? **Is Christianity too narrow, one way among the world's many? I do not have an honest answer to either question.** We drew a hard line against other Christians we judged to have gone wrong… That is a line drawn inside the Christian community. It is not a statement about anyone entirely outside it."

The fleet's own F6-T question this addresses, dumped from the loader, is `f6-t-02`: "**Isn't Christianity too narrow — one way, out of all the world's ways?**" I read *Vita* §§72–80 in full. They are a sustained, public engagement with non-Christians, and §80 answers `f6-t-02` in Antony's own reported words:

> §80: "'**And these signs are sufficient to prove that the faith of Christ alone is the true religion.** But see! you still do not believe and are seeking for arguments… Believe, therefore, also yourselves…'"

and around it: §72 "'if you to me, become as I am, **for I am a Christian**'"; §74 "men who were deemed wise among the Greeks… asked him a reason for our faith in Christ… **first pitying their ignorance**"; §76 "**you do not worship God Himself, but serve the creature rather than God who created all things**"; §78 "our faith is extending everywhere. **You by your arguments and quibbles have converted none from Christianity to Paganism**"; §79 "your religion was never persecuted… **What is yours, though praised and honoured, perishes**, while the faith and teaching of Christ… has filled the world"; §70 "**as many became Christians in those few days as one would have seen made in a year**"; and the *Vita*'s own closing §94, "read this among the **heathen**, that even in this way they may learn that our Lord Jesus Christ is not only God and the Son of God…".

Three things make this substantial rather than a wording quibble. First, the field it lands in: `honest_limit.statement` is one of two fields `build_prompt()` compiles as the Representative's own admission of ignorance, and F6-T reads `substantive` to the coverage gate either way, so nothing mechanical can see it. Second, the *narrowness* half of the deleted predecessor is the half Round 1 explicitly declined to challenge, and this record's own body note says so — "the outsiders/narrowness half **Round 1 had explicitly declined to challenge** ('does not stretch one to answer the other'… **and Round 1 raised no accuracy objection to it**)". A prior review's silence is being used as the check. Round 1 never opened §§72–80 for this claim; Round 2 opened them for a different one. Third, and most sharply: **this same commit wrote §§72–80 into `desert.dw.f1-i-god.positions[1]`** — "this world's own record also shows public argument more than once, against the Arians at episcopal summons and **against pagan philosophers at Antony's own mountain**" — as the fix for Round 2's M2. The pass had the passage in hand, in the same edit session, and still wrote "the *Vita*'s own silence" into a restored honest_limit's only locus.

The half that does hold is `f6-t-01` ("are people outside your community going to hell"): `hell` occurs **0** times in the whole *Vita* span, and the four `condemn` hits are unrelated (sin condemned in the flesh; the devil condemned; not condemning one's neighbours). A limit narrowed to damnation-language specifically would be sound. What is here is not.
