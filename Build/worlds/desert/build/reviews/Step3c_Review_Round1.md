## A. SUBSTANTIAL findings

**S1 — `desert.contested.alexandria-continuity`'s single concession — the whole point of the record — asserts an Origenist/Alexandrian *conceptual affinity* for Evagrius that no record in this corpus carries; the claim is then propped up by two further statements this step wrote, one in a `sources[].locus` and one back-written into a cleared gravity record, each citing the other.**

Three statements, all new at `6ec971a9`:

1. `desert.contested.alexandria-continuity.md:26` (`concedes`): "The systematized interior psychology one of this world's own participants developed carries **genuinely Origenist-adjacent conceptual vocabulary** … and this is the one place this world's own evidence shows **a real conceptual affinity with Alexandria's intellectual tradition**."
2. `desert.contested.alexandria-continuity.md:19` (`sources[].locus` for `desert.source.evagrius-praktikos`): "the one register where this world's own evidence shows a genuine conceptual affinity with Alexandria's intellectual tradition."
3. `desert.gravity.evagrian-systematization.md:74–76` (body, added this commit): "the Origenist-adjacent conceptual affinity **this record's own transmission-history note flags** is now the one genuine, narrow point of contact conceded at `desert.contested.alexandria-continuity`."

Statement 3 is checkable against the record it describes and is false. `desert.gravity.evagrian-systematization.md:46–49` — the transmission-history note it names — reads in full: "Its later transmission history (the 399-400 Origenist controversy; the 553 pseudonymous-survival pattern) is Doc_04's own flagged lead for Doc_08 … **not grounds for this record's own classification.**" That is a *reception* history: Evagrius's works were caught in a controversy and later condemned. It flags no conceptual affinity, asserts nothing about Evagrius's own vocabulary, and explicitly declines to let the reception decide anything. Statement 2 is checkable against `desert.source.evagrius-praktikos.md` and is equally unsupported: that record's `work` field (`:20`) describes the eight logismoi, apatheia, and antirrhesis, and the post-553 Syriac/Armenian survival — Alexandria is not mentioned anywhere in the record, and neither is Origen except as the date-marker "the 553 condemnation."

I grepped every desert record for "Origen": the corpus's entire Origenist thread is (a) the 399–400 controversy and the 553 condemnation as *events*, and (b) Rubenson's contested reading of the **Letters of Antony** as "substantively Origenist" (`desert.source.rubenson-letters.md:22`; `desert.source.antony-letters.md:24`; `desert.term.apatheia.md:48`). Nothing anywhere attributes Origenist conceptual vocabulary to Evagrius, and Doc_07 §2 — this build's own cleared explanation of where the Evagrian scheme came from — attributes it to Greek philosophical training under the Cappadocians, i.e. to a channel that is not Alexandria: "systematic conceptual structure emerged specifically where a Greek-philosophically-trained participant … organized it using intellectual tools this world did not otherwise generate internally."

This is substantial rather than a wording slip for four reasons. First, it is the **only** thing the record concedes, and the record's own body (`:63–66`) sells it as the difference between an honest reading and "a flat denial of any relationship at all" — remove it and the record is a flat denial. Second, the record's body (`:44–46`) and the index (`STEP3C-INDEX.md:45`) both certify that this record is "built entirely from this corpus's own registered sources," which is precisely what the concession is not. Third, it is the same undetectable move Round 5's S1 and Round 2's S1 named: an assertion about registered material, doing argumentative work, that no gate can catch and that fails against a file already in the repository. Fourth, the three statements were written to corroborate each other — the contested_claim cites the gravity record's note, the gravity record's note cites the contested_claim — so a reader checking either end finds the other end agreeing, and only opening `evagrian-systematization`'s actual note breaks the loop.

**S2 — `desert.contested.strand-porousness` cites `desert.source.athanasius-vita-antonii` as the source for the Amoun/Antony founding link. The *Vita* contains no Kellia material at all, and its one Amoun section says nothing about founding or advice.** `desert.contested.strand-porousness.md:16–18`:

```
- source_id: desert.source.athanasius-vita-antonii
  locus: "the Amoun/Antony founding link (Strand A's own paradigmatic figure advising Strand C's second settlement into being)"
  license: public-domain
```

This is the only source cited for `held_against[0]` (`:25`), the record's first and most concrete counter-argument: "Amoun founded Kellia (the semi-anchoritic pattern) on Antony's own advice."

I read the *Vita*'s own Table of Contents and §60. §60 is titled "Of the death of Amun, and Antony's vision thereof," and its content is exactly that: Antony sees a soul borne upward, hears "This is the soul of Amun, the monk at Nitria," and the chapter continues with the Lycus-crossing miracle. It records that Amun "had persevered in the discipline up to old age" and that "the distance from Nitria to the mountain where Antony was, was thirteen days' journey." There is no founding, no advice, and no Kellia. Grepping the whole of `npnf204` for "Cellia"/"Kellia" returns two hits, both in the editors' introductory apparatus, none in the *Vita*.

For completeness I checked the other vendored candidates before charging this: Sozomen I.14 gives Ammon's marriage, the Lycus crossing and Antony's vision of his soul, and says "This divine man was the founder of the monasteries there" — of the Nitrian region, with no mention of Antony's advice and no mention of Cellia; Sozomen VI.31 describes Cellia's dispersed dwellings and weekly assembly with no founder attribution; Palladius ch. VIII has Amoun settling "in the inner part of the mount of Nitria … and he made himself two round cells," again with no Antony advice and no Kellia. The corpus's actual home for the claim is Doc_01 §2.2's own hedged sentence ("Kellia — established c. 338 by Amoun, **reportedly** on Antony's own advice") and, behind it, the *Apophthegmata* tradition — which is already in this record's `sources[]` at `:19`, has **no vendored edition**, and is paraphrase-only under the compiler screen. So the correct citation was in the record's own source list and the pass reached past it for a vendored one that does not carry the claim.

Two aggravating details. `held_against[0]` also drops Doc_01's "reportedly," asserting a hagiographic tradition as fact in a compiled-facing field. And this is the same defect class as Step3b Round 1's mis-cited *Vita* locus, which that round charged substantial — the third occurrence in this build of a *Vita* locus asserting what the *Vita* does not say.

**S3 — `STEP3C-INDEX.md:15`'s roster justification is falsified by four chapters of a vendored, `verified-direct`, `load-bearing` source, and it is the sole stated ground for excluding two of the four names.** The index reads: "Amoun and Macarius are named as settlement founders in Doc_01 §2.2 but **with no comparable individual source base in this corpus**; **Pambo is quoted once (Palladius ch. Same class as Step3b Round 1's flagship ("the index asserting things the records did not say") and Round 5's S1.

What the step has instead is a single, coherent failure mode, and it is this build's own: **checkable assertions about registered material, written into fields that do argumentative work, that fail against files already in the repository.** Four of the ten substantial findings are one file open away from being right, and in three of those four the correct material was in the record's own hand. `desert.contested.alexandria-continuity`'s only concession claims an Origenist conceptual affinity for Evagrius while this corpus's actual Origenist evidence sits in the Antony Letters thread the same commit built a record for; the supporting statement it wrote back into `desert.gravity.evagrian-systematization` says that record's note "flags" something the note explicitly declines to flag. `desert.contested.strand-porousness` cites the *Vita* for the Amoun/Kellia link when the *Vita* has no Kellia in it, while the correct source — the *Apophthegmata* — was already the entry directly beneath it.
