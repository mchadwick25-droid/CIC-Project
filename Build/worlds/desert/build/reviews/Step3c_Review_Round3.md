**S3 — Criterion A, the roster's newly written first bar, anchors Antony on "Jerome, *De viris illustribus* 88, vendored — 'Born 251, died 356.'" Jerome's text says no such thing: the years are an NPNF editorial endnote, and `desert.source.jerome-de-viris`'s own cleared body already flags the identical kind of note, in the identical volume, as "the edition's note, not ancient testimony."** `STEP3C-INDEX.md:18`:

> "**Criterion A … absolute personal birth and/or death *years* stated in a registered source record** (not merely an age or an interval, and not a settlement's own founding date). Antony: Jerome, *De viris illustribus* 88, vendored — 'Born 251, died 356.'"

I opened `cic/texts/npnf203_theodoret-jerome-gennadius-rufinus.xml` at the chapter itself and read it both as raw XML and as plain text. The chapter is "Chapter LXXXVIII" (so the number 88 is right), and the markup is:

```
<span class="c12">Anthony<note place="end" n="2513"><p class="endnote"> Born 251, died 356.</p></note></span> the monk, whose
life Athanasius bishop of Alexandria wrote a long work upon, sent seven letters…
```

"Born 251, died 356" is an **endnote attached to the name** by the NPNF editor. Jerome's own dating clause in that chapter is "He flourished during the reign of Constantinus and his sons" — no years at all.

This corpus already knows the difference and has written it down. `desert.source.jerome-de-viris.md`'s body: "Gennadius's 'Pachomius the presbyter-monk' at line 42250 (**with the volume's own note 'Born about 292, died 348' at the same locus - a dating witness for the figure record, flagged as the edition's note, not ancient testimony**). … chapter numbering follows the vendored edition, re-check before printing a chapter number in any compiled-facing text." So the same volume supplies the same class of datum for Pachomius, and the corpus's own source record explicitly refuses to treat it as testimony. Criterion A now applies two incompatible standards to two endnotes on facing pages of one file — and the one it credits is the one written this round.

The consequence is not that Antony's dates are wrong (c. 251–356 is Documented at Doc_01 §2.1 and in the figure record's own marked derivation from *Vita* §89 plus an external death year). It is that **Criterion A, applied to the evidence the paragraph itself cites, does not do what the paragraph says it does.** For Pachomius and Evagrius the criterion is satisfied exactly as stated — `desert.source.pachomian-corpus`'s `author` field gives "c. 292-346" and `desert.source.evagrius-praktikos`'s gives "c. 345-399 CE," both verified — but for Antony no registered source record states either year; the corpus's non-editorial anchor is Doc_01 §2.1, a cleared prior-build document rather than a source record, and the figure record's own `dates.born` derives 251 from an *age* ("near a hundred and five years old") plus an unnamed "external chronology," which is the very construction Criterion A's parenthesis rules out ("not merely an age or an interval"). This is the **third consecutive round** in which the roster paragraph has been rewritten and its own anchor has turned out not to be what the paragraph says it is: Round 1's S3 (a source-base claim four Palladius chapters falsify), Round 2's S4 (an age-at-death sold as an absolute year), and now an editorial endnote sold as Jerome. The roster decision remains right; its justification has now failed verification three times running.
