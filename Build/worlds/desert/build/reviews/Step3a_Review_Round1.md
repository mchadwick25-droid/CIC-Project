## B. Findings

**1. SUBSTANTIAL — Build-infrastructure vocabulary inside `senses` fields, a repeated pattern (7 records, 8 instances).** The four-register senses shape mirrors the participant question registers (informational/evidential/personal/translational — `Build/reference/Redesign-Spec/CiC-Program-Spec.md` §"Four registers"), i.e., the evidential sense is the term's answer-ground for "did it happen, how do you know" — in-world evidence talk, not corpus-operations talk (compare the fixture world's own evidential senses in `records/fix/term/`, and the clean instances here: cheironaxia's and kellion's evidential senses do this correctly). The following senses instead carry this build's file-management jargon:
- `desert.term.antirrhesis` evidential: "The **vendored** Socrates... a **directly-checked** witness; the work itself is **consult-only**"
- `desert.term.apatheia` evidential: "(**consult-only**; the **vendored** Socrates excerpts witness his works by name)"
- `desert.term.theoria` evidential: "**consult-only**, with the **vendored** Socrates excerpts as the one directly checkable witness"
- `desert.term.koinonia` evidential: "cannot be quoted from any **vendorable** English; what **this corpus** holds directly are the one-remove reports... plus **consult-only** scholarship"
- `desert.term.synaxis` evidential: "a **vendored, directly-checked** witness"
- `desert.term.puritas-cordis` evidential: "Directly verified in the **vendored** Cassian"
- `desert.term.apotage` evidential: "not the Rule's own **vendorable** text"
- `desert.term.logismoi` evidential: "a one-author elaboration **this record** keeps distinct" — record self-reference.

The gate battery cannot catch this (the no-build-attribution gate deliberately scopes term checks to plain_meaning/quick_meaning/world_word, and M2's `build_prompt` does not currently compile senses), so it will never fail mechanically — which is exactly why it needs to be caught here, before the senses become retrieval content. The historical substance in each case is correct and should be kept; the fix is rewording the transmission facts in-world ("survives chiefly in Syriac and Armenian; in English we can check only the passages Socrates quotes" carries the same honesty without the corpus's licensing vocabulary). Vendoring/verification status already lives in the right places: `sources[].locus`, the confidence block, and the record bodies.

**2. SUBSTANTIAL — `desert.term.kellion` presents a paraphrase-only apophthegm inside quotation marks, contradicting its own body note.** The personal sense reads: "'sit in your cell and it will teach you everything' is the tradition's own remembered counsel," while the body states "it is deliberately not marked as a quotation here." Typographically it *is* marked as a quotation, and the wording is a paraphrase (no vendored Apophthegmata text exists; the standard rendering differs). The apophthegmata source record's verbatim discipline is explicit: until Budge is vendored, "no verbatim-quote claim anywhere." Fix: drop the quotation marks and render as reported counsel ("the tradition's remembered counsel that the cell, sat in, teaches everything"), or equivalent. Same pattern, milder, in `desert.term.apophthegma` personal sense ("'Give me a word' was a real request") — fix together.
