## (b) Numbered findings

### Finding 1 — Inhabited passage overstates the Nitria–Kellia distance *(minor)*
**Section 1**, first `[Inhabited]` block: "A newly arrived brother sits outside an elder's cell at Kellia, having walked two days from Nitria." The attested distance is ~19 km / 12 miles — a single day's walk at most (the founding tradition has Antony and Amoun reaching the site on "an after-dinner stroll" until sunset). Verified via Wikipedia "Kellia" and "Nitria (monastic site)"; Doc_01 §2.2 places the whole cluster "within roughly fifty miles of one another." Recommend "a day's walk from Nitria" or removing the day-count.

### Finding 2 — The Evagrian eight-fold *logismoi* catalogue is presented under gravity 2 rather than gravity 9 *(cosmetic)*
**Section 3**, Emotional/affective ecology: "Dominant fears center on the logismoi themselves (gravity 2) — ... the specific, catalogued anxieties of gluttony, lust, avarice, sadness, anger, acedia, vainglory, and pride (Doc_03 §1.5)." The general experience of demonic thoughts is correctly gravity 2, but the eight-fold enumeration itself is the Evagrian systematization — gravity 9, Strand-C-bound (Doc_03 §1.5 explicitly says so). This is the only instance of slippage in an otherwise clean document (everywhere else the 2-vs-9 distinction is kept correctly). Recommend attributing the eight-fold list to gravity 9.

### Finding 3 — Citation slippage in the Rule-to-Benedict transmission chain *(cosmetic)*
**Section 9**: "the cenobitic model (gravity 6) becomes, via the transmission chain already noted in Doc_02 §1.2 and Doc_03 §1.18 (Cassian's own translation work)..." Doc_03 §1.18 is Cassian's *apatheia → puritas cordis* vocabulary substitution, not his transmission of monastic rule/structure. The substantive Benedict-lineage claim is accurate and fully supported by Doc_02 §1.2 alone; only the §1.18 anchor is misapplied (conflating two different Cassian translation acts). Recommend dropping §1.18 from this citation.

### Finding 4 — Emotional/Affective Ecology is folded, not standalone; the lightest of the required Step 5 dimensions *(cosmetic/observational)*
Framework Step 5 names this as a dimension in its own right; Doc_05 folds it into Human Ecology as one substantive paragraph — adequate for Step 5, but the most compressed dimension. Flagged so the disposition owner can decide whether it warrants fuller treatment now or deferral to Doc_07. Not a gap that blocks the document.

### Finding 5 — Forces integration is concentrated, not evenly distributed across every lens *(cosmetic/observational)*
Strongest in Sections 1, 4, 9; carried more implicitly in Worship, Organizational, and Ministry Ecology. All substantive forces are integrated somewhere and the requirement is met in substance — flagged only as an evenness observation.
