    text: >-
      The desert communities held the same Nicene faith as the churches
      they came out of. What set them apart was a way of life, not a
      different doctrine. When it mattered, one of their own stated the

      beginning. He was the Eternal Word of the Father's own being. One

      desert's most systematic scheme. Part of his writing was formally

      earlier, inside this world's own span. It scattered his circle
