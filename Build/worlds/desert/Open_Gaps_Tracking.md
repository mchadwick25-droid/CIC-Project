## Standing open items — carried, not resolved by either build thread

### OG-1. Representative identity — Papnoute, Abba (Elder). RESOLVED, project-lead decision, 2026-07-11; re-checked (not re-decided), 2026-08-22.

Per `CiC_W3_Decision_Log.md`'s "Representative Identity decision (Doc_10 gate)" entry:
Strand C (semi-anchoritic, Nitria/Kellia/Scetis) confirmed as strand of origin; "Abba"
confirmed as role label on the project lead's own stated reasoning ("the tradition is
popularly known as 'the Desert Fathers'"). Name candidate **Isaac was proposed then
withdrawn by the project lead** — rejected specifically because it names a real,
documented individual elsewhere in the wider *Apophthegmata* tradition, even though no
figure by that name appears in this build's own Doc_09a inventory. **Papnoute** (the
Coptic form underlying the Hellenized "Paphnutius") was confirmed instead, on the
project lead's own reasoning that it is more resonant with this world's Coptic-majority
participant base (Doc_02 §6).

**Named trade-off carried honestly, not hidden:** a moderately known Nicene-era
"Paphnutius" exists in wider church history outside this build's own documents. The
new-spec build's own Step 3c/Step 5 work (see Build Log below) found this collision
risk to be deeper than first known: by Step 5 (2026-08-22) the apparatus held **three**
distinct historical men named Paphnutius in this world's own source/force material
(never compiled-facing); a further discovery pass on 2026-09-13 found a **fourth**
(Budge's Historia Monachorum contents list, ch. 12) and then a **fifth** the same day
(a 334 CE Bell papyrus witness list, "Papnutius the deacon of Paminpesla"). None of
this leaked into compiled-facing content at any point checked; each discovery is
disclosed in `build/DECISION-LOG.md`'s own entries rather than smoothed into an
existing count.

**Binding instruction, still in force:** the project lead directed that although
strand/role/name are drawn from Strand C, Papnoute must speak for the whole living
movement (Strands A, B, and C together), not a Strand C snapshot. Re-checked at Step 5
(2026-08-22, `voice_craft` review) and found to hold on every axis it could check, with
one honest exception disclosed rather than certified away: the **demonstration set
itself draws exclusively on Strand A/C material, with zero Strand B/Pachomian
representation** — named in `desert.voice.craft.md`'s own trailing confirmation note
(Step 5 Review Round 1, Finding S9). Not blocking (every required canon cell is
satisfied), but a real, disclosed gap, not a closed one.
