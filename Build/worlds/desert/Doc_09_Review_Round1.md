## (a) Overall verdict

**SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED — narrow.**

This is an exceptionally disciplined and honest deliverable. The critical fabrication check comes back completely clean: all four narrated stories/sayings are genuine, accurately worded, and correctly attributed. Tier classification is sound; the Absent Stories section is substantive; the Freeze Assessment correctly refuses self-assigned Frozen status; Doc_09c is genuinely honest about what cannot be assessed because Doc_10 doesn't exist. Citation fidelity across dozens of cross-references is remarkably high.

The verdict is nonetheless substantial on one narrow ground: Doc_09c §2's "Adversarial Resistance Testing" subsection — explicitly billed as the document's strongest validation claim — misstates the build's own review history, attributing a "fabricated citation" to Doc_02 (which had no such finding; its actual finding was a missing required assessment) when the fabricated citation actually occurred in Doc_03, where it is the same finding as the "inverted transmission-history claim" Doc_09c lists as a separate item. A self-referential accuracy error in the section staking the strongest validation claim should not clear on a cosmetic disposition.

Freeze Assessment correctly refuses self-assigned Frozen; Doc_09c honestly marks Representative/Conversational Readiness and Encounter Success Assessment as "cannot be assessed," and honestly states External Scholarly Review and Living Tradition Differentiation review have not occurred.

### Finding 5 — NOT A DEFECT (verified). The Doc_06 §2.2 [CT]-tag citation, which looked suspicious on its face, is actually correct on inspection. Broader citation audit of all high-risk references and all gravity numbers found zero errors in Doc_09 itself.

### Finding 6 — SUBSTANTIAL. Doc_09c §2's "Adversarial Resistance Testing" section misstates the build's own review history: it lists "a fabricated citation in Doc_02" (never happened — Doc_02's actual Round 1 finding was a missing required "Assess Formation Narrative Sources" assessment, not mentioned anywhere in Doc_09c's list) and separately lists "an inverted transmission-history claim in Doc_03" — but these are the same finding, both located in Doc_03 (the fabricated citation supported the inverted Sogdian-transmission claim; `Doc_03_Review_Round1.md`, Finding F1). The section overstates the breadth of caught defects and gets the location of the fabricated-citation defect wrong, in the exact subsection framed as the document's strongest evidence that validation was genuine. The Doc_04, Doc_06, Doc_07, and Doc_08 characterizations in the same list are all accurate; only the Doc_02/Doc_03 pairing is wrong.

### Finding 7 — COSMETIC — two further overstatements in Doc_09c §2. "Every one of Doc_01 through Doc_08 underwent multiple rounds... and did find real defects at every stage" is inaccurate for Doc_05 specifically, which underwent a single review round with only cosmetic findings. "Eight documents and three-hundred-plus review passes" has no basis in any artifact (the actual total across all eight documents is 18 review rounds: Doc_01: 2, Doc_02: 2, Doc_03: 2, Doc_04: 3, Doc_05: 1, Doc_06: 3, Doc_07: 2, Doc_08: 3) and reads as rhetorical inflation in a section whose purpose is to demonstrate rigor.

### Finding 8 — NOT A DEFECT (verified). Absent Stories (Doc_09a §5) is substantive, not perfunctory — all three entries name a specific expectable story, explain concretely why it cannot be told, and ground the silence in already-evidenced source-ecology reasoning.
