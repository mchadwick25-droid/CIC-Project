## Top failure causes

1. **Engine post-draft removal ships the remainder.** The voice copies a quote record's words, or a quoted line from a doctrinal_witness, into its own sentences. The engine strips those sentences, then ships what is left. That damaged c-i (P1) and left orphans in c-p, c-t, f2-t, f4-t, f5-i and f6-t. The root is in the records too: `witt.dw.truly-god-and-truly-man` and `witt.dw.how-the-promise-reached-us` carry quote text inline, without a placeholder.
2. **Wrong or missing record wording.** The voice_craft exemplar "Works follow it, uncommanded" contradicts `witt.term.good-works` (f1-i). AC Article I on the Trinity is vendored but has no record, and `witt.dw.truly-god-and-truly-man` says the opposite of it (c-t). `witt.demo.the-way-in` leaves baptism out of joining (f4-i, P2).
3. **Build vocabulary in the voice, with no caution or output check against it.** f2-e (P1) talks about vendored texts, record ids and "the system". f5-e ("No force in the evidence") and f3-t ("in ways we do not tell here") are milder.
4. **Misleading adjacency.** A story is placed as the example for a claim it does not support (f5-p). No check tests a story's lead-in against its not_for.
5. **Repetition and recital.** Record text is read out word for word and recurs across answers. Examples: "how-the-promise" in five answers, the married-clergy paragraph in f4-e and f5-t, and "The Saxon church inspected its own parishes from 1527... Those reports exist. We do not hold them." in f1-e, f1-p, f3-p and f6-p. Paragraphs run long in 27 of 28 answers.

Also: the automatic checks passed all 28. They do not test record agreement, build vocabulary, removal residue or story context, so "28 of 28 passed" says little about these answers.
