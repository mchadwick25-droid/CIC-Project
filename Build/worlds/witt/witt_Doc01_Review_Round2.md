#### N1 — §4: the Contested definition is attributed to Constitution Article 17, which does not contain it. This is S1's own error, one document over.

**Where:** §4, "Social" paragraph, line 65.

**What the revision says:** "…is correctly named **[Contested]** — Constitution Article 17's own definition (*'live scholarly disagreement; no dominant position… carry the tension; do not resolve it'*) applies directly…"

**What I found.** Constitution V2.2, Article 17 ("Source Transparency, Confidence and Evidential Integrity Principle," paras 373–407) contains no such wording. It does not enumerate the five levels at all. It says only that "Every reconstruction carries a calibrated, visible level of evidential confidence drawn from a single fixed vocabulary."

The quoted words are the **Construction Framework V7.4**'s, paras 202–204:

> "Contested / Live scholarly disagreement; no dominant position. Named scholarly tensions are carried at full strength and never quietly resolved. / Permitted language: 'contested,' 'scholars disagree.' Carry the tension; do not resolve it."

Round 1 attributed this correctly, to "the Framework." The revision moved the attribution onto the Constitution and got it wrong. This is precisely S1 — a methodology quotation printed under the wrong governing document — reintroduced in a sentence written to close a Round 1 finding.
