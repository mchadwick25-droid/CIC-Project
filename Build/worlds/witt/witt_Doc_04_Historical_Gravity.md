## 0. What this document is, and the disciplines it applies

**What it is.** The Framework's Step 4 output for this world: a candidate list generated from traceable Doc_02 evidence, every candidate run through the six named tests, classified Primary / Supporting / Tensional / did-not-reach-gravity-status against the Framework's own definitions, cross-checked against evidential confidence, tested under a declared substitute for cross-strand testing (this world is strand-singular), and given a forces-connection notation. The Gravity Index (§7) and the Interaction Matrix (§5) were built alongside the testing, not after it. **This document's own claim to have already reconciled every §3 line against the matrix by direct comparison was tested by independent review and, after two rounds of fixes, now holds.** Every one of the 156 off-diagonal cells was checked against its §3 line at two levels — relationship *code*, re-parsed and compared by script, and, for every reshaping ("S") cell, *direction against the cell's own stated basis*, re-derived by hand — and every mismatch found at either level was resolved in the cell's favor. That two-level, full-matrix check is what is now claimed, and only that.

**Discipline 1 — a gravity organizes; a topic recurs (CF Part III).** Candidates were generated only from elements recurring across *multiple* Doc_02 evidence streams (§1 names the streams), or from documented Attention Presence / Attention Absence patterns. Doc_03's fifteen §10.2 clusters were a starting map, not a shortlist: four of them (items 5, 6, 7, 15) are folded into other candidates rather than advanced (§2.2), and one candidate advanced here (G13) is not a Doc_03 cluster at all but an attention pattern Doc_02 §3.1 and §11 documented.

**Discipline 2 — read, don't infer (Doc_01 §10, Doc_02 preamble, Doc_03 §0).** Every quotation this document makes in its own voice was located and read in the vendored file this pass, at the line given (§9 lists the ranges). Nothing below draws on general knowledge of Luther or the Reformation: where a true thing about Lutheranism could not be traced to this world's own vendored evidence, it was cut or named as a gap. R94's Named-Comparandum bar is observed — no "Here I stand," no *pecca fortiter*, nothing from the 1525 or 1543 texts, nothing from the *Deutsche Messe* beyond R59's named absence.

**Discipline 3 — name the layer (Doc_02 §2, Doc_03 Discipline 2).** Editors' introductions and sidenotes (Jacobs, Steinhaeuser, Lambert, Schindel, Steimle, Schmauk, Neve; Bacon; Morley; Cole), Bente/Dau's bracketed German-text insertions, and Smith's modern idiom are named as layer wherever drawn on and never counted as a voice. "The Word must do this thing, and not we poor sinners" is Luther's text (v2 14917–14918; C1, corrected this revision from the compressed "The Word must do it"); "The Priesthood of Believers" is Jacobs's sidenote (v2 2171).

**Discipline 4 — flag Author Gravity at generation, in this world's own shape.** This world's Author Gravity problem is not competing authors but one voice in nine registers plus a second voice under princes' signatures (Doc_02 §3, Doc_03 Discipline 3). The flag set at §2.1 therefore records, for each candidate, which registers and which of the two voices attest it *as read*, and whether any non-founder trace does. A candidate visible only in the founder's argued-doctrine registers, or only in the founder's complaint about others, is flagged there, before testing.

**Discipline 5 — the founder-prescription / congregational-reception line is drawn on every candidate that crosses it.** Doc_02 §14 instructs this document directly: "Doc_04 may confirm gravities from the founder corpus but must mark any claim about the congregations' *holding* of a gravity as Inferential/Thin until the congregational register enters the library." That instruction is applied at the Formation Test of every candidate, and it is the single largest source of Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check divergence in this document (§6).

**Discipline 6 — strand-singular, with a declared substitute (Doc_01 §6; Template §5).** No candidate is split by strand. In place of Article 21's cross-strand test, every candidate is scored on a **register-and-voice spread** and on its **reception-side status** (§4 states the substitute, argues why it serves Article 21's purpose here, and says plainly what it cannot certify). §4 also answers whether anything found in this pass gives an argued basis to reopen Doc_01 §6, and finds none.

**Discipline 7 — forces-connection is a separate, required field, not a seventh test.** Per Forces Framework §4 Step 4, every classified gravity carries a notation of how it held, shifted, intensified, or fractured under the forces already identified at Doc_01 §7 and Doc_02 §13. The forces are cited by the codes at §1. Doc_08 does not yet exist and is not cited.

## 1. Evidence streams and forces used for candidate generation

**Doc_02 evidence streams, as this document codes them** (reused in §2.1, §3 and the §7 Index):

- **[D]** Academic disputation — the Ninety-Five Theses and the 1517 letter [R2].
- **[T]** The 1519–1523 vernacular and Latin treatises addressed to named readers — sacramental treatises, *Good Works*, *Christian Nobility*, *Babylonian Captivity*, *Christian Liberty*, *Papacy at Rome*, *Doctrines of Men*, *Secular Authority*, *Earnest Exhortation*, Teutonic Order [R3–R14, R16, R19–R20, R24].
- **[P]** Polemic against a named opponent — Alveld, Emser, the bull [R8, R17, R21–R23].
- **[S]** Spoken pastoral address to Wittenberg's own congregation — the Eight Sermons [R15].
- **[K]** Catechesis in three stages — *Kurze Form*, LC, SC [R14, R25, R26].
- **[H]** Hymnody and the hymnal prefaces [R27–R30].
- **[C]** Conversation — Table Talk, triple-filtered [R31–R32], Contested as verbatim for any single saying.
- **[B]** Sustained argument against Erasmus — *Bondage* [R34–R36], OCR-degraded.
- **[M]** Melanchthon's confessional voice — the AC (corporate princely "we") and the Apology (his own name) [R37–R38].
- **[N]** Non-founder traces at one remove — Karsthans, Walter, Kessler, Spangenberg, Katharina von Bora's one sentence [R44–R47, R31].
- **[I]** Institutional evidence (Doc_02 §4) — Diets, bull, edicts, bans, the Teutonic secularization, the AC's signatories.
- **[Mat]** Material evidence by report (Doc_02 §6) — print editions and printers, household stratification presupposed by the catechisms, the saints' functional specialisms.
- **[E]** Editorial apparatus (Doc_02 §2) — used for dated events and as attention-pattern evidence, never as a voice.
- **[AP/AA]** Attention Presence / Attention Absence (Doc_02 §3.3, §11, §13): what the library keeps returning to (the Word, faith, the conscience, the devil, the household, the founder's own assessment of reception) and what it conspicuously avoids or lacks (the Reformed rival — "Marburg"/"Zwingli" zero hits; the parish; women's own voices; 1546–1580).

**Forces, as this document codes them** (from Doc_01 §7 and Doc_02 §13; Doc_08 will compile the full matrix):

- **[F-gen]** What generated the world: a friar-professor's conviction, argued first over indulgences, that the church's practice rested on an authority Scripture does not support (Doc_01 §7).
- **[F-emb]** What it was embedded in: late-medieval Christendom under a contested papal authority; the Holy Roman Empire's fragmentation (Doc_01 §7).
- **[F-resp]** What it was responding to: a laity taught to rely on indulgences, confession, and the sacramental system for assurance (Doc_01 §7).
- **[F-pap]** The papal force in its documented forms: the bull, the Leipzig/Emser/Alveld polemic, the Confutation (Doc_02 §13).
- **[F-imp]** The imperial force: Worms, the Diets, the council appeal (Doc_02 §13).
- **[F-ter]** The territorial-princely force, in both directions: Frederick's protection; Duke George's bans; the Teutonic Order's secularization (Doc_02 §4, §13).
- **[F-pop]** The popular-insurrectionary force, 1521–22 as documented; 1525 by named absence [R48] (Doc_02 §12.4, §13).
- **[F-rad]** The internal-radical force: Karlstadt 1522; the "enthusiasts," "new spirits," "fanatics," and Anabaptists of 1529–31 (Doc_02 §12.5, §13).
- **[F-ref]** The Reformed rival, refused at Marburg 1529 — a force this library holds only by absence and by one in-voice word (Doc_01 §7; Doc_02 §13 item 1).
- **[F-turk]** The military-external force, the Turk (Doc_02 §6, §13).
- **[F-print]** Print as medium and market (Doc_02 §6, §13).
- **[F-inh]** The inheritance refused: the Augustinian Hermits as seedbed; the seven-sacrament, monastic, penitential apparatus (Doc_01 §5, §8.0, §8.6).
- **[F-sel]** Survivorship as selection, not suppression: the founder's own reluctance to be collected, the editors' principles, the modern rights/hosting economy that shaped this library (Doc_02 §13).

### 2.2 Candidates considered and NOT advanced, with reasons

- **The papacy / Antichrist as its own gravity** (Doc_03 §10.2 item 15; 6.7 Tier 1, both voices, every register). Recurs everywhere, but as the thing the ecology organizes *against*, not around: it is Doc_01 §7's "what it was refusing," a force [F-pap] for Doc_08. Its organizing effects on the world's self-understanding are carried in G2 (the authority it usurped) and G12 (the adversary it is named as). Doc_03 6.7's demonstrated plurality — "Antichrist" in the polemical register, "the Church of Rome" in the confessional — is carried at G12's forces notation as a real register shift under [F-imp]. Not advanced; the closest call among the folds, because it is the world's most-named noun.
- **Print** (Doc_02 §6, §13). Richly documented, but almost entirely in the editorial layer [E, Mat] — an Author-Gravity "layer-only" candidate — and Doc_02 §13 already lists it among the forces. Its organizing effect on formation is carried by G11 (the medium's vernacular content) and at G13's forces notation (the market corrupting the hymns). Not advanced; a Doc_08 force [F-print].
- **Lay experience — "what villagers actually knew."** No evidence stream carries it: Doc_02 §7 "Not present: any visitation protocol; any parish register; any lay letter…"; §10's last row, "Not tellable from this library." It cannot be generated from evidence and so cannot be tested. G13 is the *documented* form of this world's attention to it — the founder's — and §3 G13 is explicit that G13 is not this candidate under another name.
- **Luther himself — the founder's person as organizing force.** The census's own instruction ("Founder corpus kept distinct… so founder-gravity discipline can run," Doc_02 §3.1) exists to prevent exactly this. The world's own texts refuse it: "I would gladly have seen all my books forgotten and destroyed" (v1 260–261); "I did nothing; the Word did it all" (v2 14931). Recorded as the Article 26 trap, not advanced.
- **Law and Gospel as a separate gravity** (Doc_03 2.6, [AS], Tier 1; §10.2 item 2). The strongest fold in this list, and the one most exposed to its own evidence: the document's own Evidence sections show G4 and G5 depending on it, not only G1 and G2. "All the Scriptures of God are divided into two parts — commands and promises" (v2 11782–11783); "All Scripture ought to be distributed into these two principal topics, the Law and the promises" (Ap 579–580); the *Kurze Form*'s illness/remedy (v2 13198–13212); LC 2967–2975. It recurs in both voices and three registers, and read fully it structures more than G1 and G2: **G5** is, at its centre, this distinction applied to the person — the commands "teach a man to know himself… and may despair of his powers," then "the second part of the Scriptures stands ready — the promises of God" (v2 11786–11807, quoted at §3 G5) — and **G4**'s Creed-over-Commandments order — "the Creed is a doctrine quite different from the Ten Commandments; for the latter teaches indeed what we ought to do, but the former tells what God does for us" (LC 2967–2975, quoted at §3 G1 and G4) — is the same distinction supplying the catechism's own structure. Under the Dependency Test, a distinction four candidates state or presuppose is not disposed of by an argument addressed to two, and the fold is re-argued here on a narrower and more defensible ground than the one first given: G1, G2, G4 and G5 each state the distinction in their own words, in their own registers, for their own purposes — justification's content, the Word's twofold address, the catechism's order, the conscience's sequence — and no vendored text treats "Law and Gospel" as a topic in its own right, examined, preached on, or catechized as such, the way G1's "faith alone" or G4's three parts are. It is the ecology's shared hermeneutic grammar, present everywhere it is used and nowhere as its own subject — which is why it is folded rather than a fifth thing four candidates depend on, not because only two do. G4's and G5's Dependency tests are updated to name it (§3). Folded; Doc_06 may keep it Tier 1 regardless — a lexicon tier is not a gravity classification.

- **Repentance/penance, indulgence, and the keys — "the founding vocabulary of 1517–20"** (Doc_03 1.1, 1.2, 1.6, all Tier 1; §10.2 item 5). Folded here, on a full accounting rather than a silent omission. ### G1 — Justified by faith alone

**Evidence.**

- *Confession.* AC IV, "men cannot be justified before God by their own strength, merits, or works, but are freely justified for Christ's sake, through faith… This faith God imputes for righteousness" (AC 234–239); Ap IV, "in this controversy the chief topic of Christian doctrine is treated" (Ap 561–562); "By faith alone in Christ, not through love, not because of love or works, do we acquire the remission of sins" (Ap 1118–1120).

**Register-and-voice spread:** 4/4 — treatise/polemic, congregational-facing (sermon, catechesis, hymn), Melanchthon, and one non-founder trace scored on presence in the household register rather than on this gravity specifically (Kate's question presupposes a household "we" — thin, and about coldness *in prayer*, not in faith, TT 3147–3148; corrected this revision, C11). **Reception-side status:** founder-attested as received (1522, Wittenberg only).

**Evidential confidence:** **Documented** — multiple independent texts, both voices, verified at the loci.

**Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check:** organizing strength and evidential confidence agree. No divergence.

## 4. Cross-strand gravity testing (Constitution Article 21) — a declared, justified substitute for a strand-singular world
