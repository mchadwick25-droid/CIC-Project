# Source Registry — The Latin Apologists

Per `Source_Registry_Template.md` V1.0. Co-equal Step 2 output with `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`. **Living document: append-only.** A row is never renumbered or deleted. Doc_02 may not name a source in support of a specific claim unless that source has a row here. Rows are entered for works that the corpus map assigns to this world, and for works that are not vendored. A vendored work assigned only to another world is recorded as a candidate in `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`, not here.

**Status:** Draft. Not approved to proceed. The saturation statement at the end is not closed.
**World file-code:** `latap`. Registered on 2026-10-29 as a candidate world (`Build/Ministry/Operations/Standing/WORLDS_REGISTRY_LOG.md`).
**Boundary checked against:** `Doc_01_World_Identification_Boundaries_Orientation.md` (c. 197–320; Carthage, Rome and Ostia, Sicca, Nicomedia and Trier; strand-singular as a recommendation pending the project lead). Boundary Status is judged by what a source speaks for, never by the publication date of a modern edition.

**Type:** P (Primary) / S (Secondary) / M (Material/External) / L (Period Lexicon).
**Confidence (citation reliability):** A = the Licensed-For passages were read and matched in the vendored file by a structure marker (a chapter heading, a division id, a heading line, or the edition's own title). For an Excluded row, A means that the passage which makes it a Named Comparandum was read and matched. B = a specific work or locus is named and exists in the vendored file, but the Licensed-For content was not itself re-read. C = a real author and work, no locus checked, not vendored. D = a body or genre of material, no single text named. No row is E; a source with no traceable basis is not entered. A C row for a work that has since been vendored keeps its letter, and its note names the row that holds the vendored file.
**Structure markers:** for the ANF and NPNF volumes, `div2:` and `div3:` are the file's own division ids, and chapter references follow the ANF chapter headings. For the TEI files, the CTS urn and the `textpart` hierarchy are the file's own. For plain-text scans, `line N` is the line of the vendored file, which counts the intake header, and the text after it is the heading as the scan prints it, OCR errors included.
**Scan-quality note:** every scan is OCR text and carries OCR errors. Quotations in this Registry and in Doc_02 were matched against the files after whitespace and line-break-hyphen normalisation, and after reading long s as s, which is the quote gate's own rule. No other letter of any quotation was corrected. The Latin scans' headers say that wording must be checked against the page image before it is quoted. No page image was available to this pass, so every Latin and German quotation is verified against the scan text only.
**Rights:** the ANF and NPNF volumes carry the Public Domain rights line in their own metadata. The raw OCR scans of Latin editions carry a Public Domain line from the intake header (printed before 1930). The TEI files are CC BY-SA 4.0 as digital editions (Open Greek and Latin, University of Leipzig, 2014): attribution and share-alike travel with any quotation, and the printed editions they transcribe are public domain by date. The German and French scholarship is public domain by date (1901 to 1922). Each file's own header is the authority.
**Witness status:** where a work has a TEI file, the TEI is the preferred base for quoting and the scan is the check on the printed page. The Oehler 1853 volumes are an older recension than CSEL and are the only public-domain Latin for fifteen of Tertullian's works. Where a work is also in CSEL 20 or 47, the CSEL text is the better base. A garbled scan stays a second witness until a clean witness of the same work is vendored.
**What an A attests where there is no Licensed-For:** a row with no Licensed-For content does not carry an A.
**Mixed voices:** where one work mixes voices, the Verification Note says whose voice is whose. An opponent's words in a work of this shelf are never quoted as the shelf's own.

## Saturation statement

**Not closed.** The Registry holds every source the builder could identify from four channels: the corpus map and `cic/texts/` (the assigned files, and a full-text search of the whole shelf), the census entry's own `voices` and `sourcing` fields, the Source Readiness Dossier, and the scholarship that the vendored Harnack, Monceaux and Schanz-Hosius-Krueger cite for dates. The V7.4 field-bibliography sweep has **not** been run against any external instrument. Instruments not reached: the bibliographies of the modern works on Tertullian and the Latin apologists after 1930 (Step 0 §4 items 1, 2 and 8 record that post-1930 revisions of these dates exist and were not consulted); Lamirande-style or later handbooks; the *Cambridge History of Early Christian Literature*; the *Dictionary of Christian Biography* entries.

**The shelf search.** `cic/texts/` holds 1,525 text files (1,487 `.txt` and 38 `.xml`). A case-insensitive search for the regular expression `Tertulli|Minucius Felix|Arnobi|Lactanti|Commodian`, applied to every file in the directory, matches 452 files. 34 of them are files this shelf assigns. The other 418 are mostly single mentions by editors and authors of other worlds. They were not each opened. The files that hold real material on the five authors and are not assigned here are the two Cyprian-and-LPC volumes already named, Jerome's other letters, and the Church histories; none was read for a claim in this build.

**Not yet counted.** The word counts of Step 0 §3 B1 stand as that document states them. They cover the ANF English text of 40 works and were derived by a method written out there. This Registry adds no corpus count of its own. It records one correction to them: the ANF07 file prints, under the heading "Fragments of Lactantius", two Latin poems of uncertain authorship in English, not fragments of lost works; the 3,932 words Step 0 counts for "fragments" are the poems (rows 44). `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` carries the finding.
