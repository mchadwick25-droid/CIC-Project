# Open Gaps — Donatism ("The Church of the Martyrs") Formation World

Running, dated ledger for the Donatism world build (world-code `don`). Same discipline as
`Build/worlds/alx/Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`: dated entries, disclosed-recovery notes, honest status,
project-lead-anchored where confirmation is a project-lead act. This file is the durable
record; no real decision, review outcome, or open question lives only in a build thread's
own conversation history.

**A note on provenance, stated plainly.** Donatism has never carried a standalone
`Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` before this file. Its own build has used `don_Decision_Log.md`
(1,027 lines, 68 dated entries, 2026-09-01 through 2026-09-17) as that record throughout —
this is stated explicitly inside the Decision Log itself (merge-reconciliation entry,
2026-09-10: "this world does not carry a separate `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`... adding a
second, parallel tracking file now would fragment the convention rather than serve it").
This file is created now per a portfolio-level repo-structure instruction (`CLAUDE.md`'s
own "Track gaps and exceptions explicitly" rule, and the fleet convention every sibling
world uses), and it works **from** the Decision Log and the ~64 review/decision artifacts
on disk, not around them — every entry below traces to a specific dated Decision Log
section, a named `Review-Artifacts/` file, or a directly-verified file on disk. Nothing
here is invented to fill a gap in what the record actually shows; where the record itself
is silent, that silence is stated rather than papered over.

## Thread scope and governing documents

Donatism is built under the same `cic-build-cycle` discipline as every other CiC world:
one document at a time, review-gated, self-governing within four escalation categories
(Representative identity/title, portfolio-level/cross-world, governance/methodology, and
unresolved tensions the pipeline cannot close on its own), never self-assigning Frozen
status. World-code `don` was assigned at Step 0, this world's first document
(`Step0_Movement_Scope_Confirmation.md`).

**Construction window:** 311/312–439 CE, Roman North Africa (Carthage, Numidia, Africa
Proconsularis, Byzacena, Mauretania). **Strand determination:** strand-singular (Doc_01
§4, confirmed against Constitution Article 21's own definitional test — the Maximianist
schism does not clear the strand-status bar; the mainstream Donatist party's own reception
of returning Maximianist clergy without reordination or rebaptism is evidence of a shared
formation pattern, not a distinct strand). **Representative:** Fidelis, "a bishop of the
Donatist communion" (name and role-label refined live from an initial "Restitutus, a
Donatist bishop of the parallel hierarchy" — see the Representative build log below).

Governing documents used throughout, per the Decision Log's own citations: the Constitution
(Articles 20, 21, 23, 24, 29, 31, 33–35, 36 all invoked by name at various points);
`CiC_L3B_Formation_World_Construction_Framework_V7.4` and its companion Blueprint V7.3;
`CiC_L3C_Representative_Construction_Framework_V3.2` (Parts Three through Nine); the Forces
Framework V1.1; the M4 Change Order (Smart's seven-dimension lens spine for Doc_07,
escalated to and resolved by the project lead — see the Doc_07 build-log entry below); and
