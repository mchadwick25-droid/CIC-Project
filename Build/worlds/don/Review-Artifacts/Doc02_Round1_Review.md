### H1. §2 says Optatus's revision added "an eighth book." There is no eighth book — and §1 and Registry row 1 say so.

**Doc_02 §2, Optatus, Limitations:**

> "the work's revision (c. 385, adding an eighth book responding to Parmenian's own reply) shows Optatus's own argument developing under pressure, not fixed from the outset."

**Doc_02 §1, sixteen lines earlier:** "*Against the Donatists* (Books I–VII, c. 366–367, revised c. 385)." **Registry row 1:** "Books I–VII (c. 366–367, rev. c. 385)." **Step0 §3 B1:** "(c. 366–367, revised c. 385)," no book count claim.

Checked against the vendored text this world is built on, `cic/texts/optatus_against-the-donatists.txt`: the work runs `BOOK THE FIRST` through `BOOK THE SEVENTH` and stops. There is no eighth book. The same file's own apparatus states what the second edition actually changed: "In the first edition of St. Optatus written about 370 a.d. the list of Popes ended with Damasus. The name of Siricius who became Pope in 383 was added in the second edition (cf. Preface to Book VII)," and, on the Donatist anti-pope list, "he evidently added in his second edition the names of Lucian and Claudian, at the same time that he added the name of Siricius to that of the Popes." The second-edition addition is Book VII and a set of list updates — not an eighth book, and not a reply to a Parmenian reply. No "Parmenian's reply" to Optatus is attested; Optatus's whole work is itself the reply to Parmenian.

Why it is high, not medium: this is a factual error inside an Author Gravity entry, it contradicts two other statements in the same pair of documents, and it is refuted by the vendored file the entry is describing. It also does load-bearing work — "shows Optatus's own argument developing under pressure" is a Limitations claim resting entirely on the false detail.

**Fix.** Replace with what the vendored text supports: *"the work's second edition (after 383, on the evidence of the added name of Pope Siricius) updates the papal and Donatist episcopal lists and carries Book VII, showing Optatus's own argument extended after the fact rather than fixed at the first edition."* Do not assert a motive for the revision that no source attests.
