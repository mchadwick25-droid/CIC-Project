### R1. (Low) The new Maximianist bracket labels 393–398 "the suppression campaign," which contradicts §4's own chronology one paragraph earlier

The fix rewrote the label to "the suppression campaign proper runs 393–398" without moving the start year with it, and propagated the same mislabelled bracket into §4's disposition paragraph. The word "proper" makes it a precision claim, so this is not readable as loose shorthand.

**Fix (two loci).** Either "the suppression campaign proper runs 394–398," or, keeping the fracture's own span visible: "the fracture opens in 393 and the suppression campaign proper runs to 398."
