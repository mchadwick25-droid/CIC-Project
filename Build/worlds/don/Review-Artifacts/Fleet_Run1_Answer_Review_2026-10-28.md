## Grading rules

The same as `Build/worlds/lpc/Review-Artifacts/Run3_Access_And_Craft_Review_2026-10-28.md` and `Build/worlds/lpc/build/lpc_Probe_Results.md`.

- **P1:** wrong, invented, misleading or damaged. A row with any P1 is **FAIL**.
- **P2:** small and plainly fixable. A row whose only defects are P2 is **PASS**.
- **Four criteria:** Rigor, Accessibility, Craft, Focus.
- **Accessibility P2:** a paragraph of 8 or more sentences, a sentence of the voice's own over 30 words, or a term used before it is explained. Any turn above FK 10 or below FRE 60 fails the gate. None did.
- **Cause layers:** missing record / right record not offered / wrong record offered / voice not told what is unknown / caution wording / engine cut / quote used out of context. "Caution wording" here covers any voice-facing record text whose own words mislead when spoken: a caution, a horizon, or an honest limit's `statement`.
- **Not scored:** none. Every probe reached the voice.
