## HIGH SEVERITY

### H1 (Round 2). §3.3's own Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check for G3 still says "four" texts and "three" Primaries — both numbers wrong, both left over from before the Round 1 fix, in the exact paragraph the fix was supposed to correct

**Claim checked:** Task item 1 (H3 re-verification) and task item 4 (new inconsistencies the fix itself may have introduced) together.

**What I found:** §3.3's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check bullet reads, in full:

> "**Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check — CONSISTENT, and the least Author-Gravity-encumbered Primary in this document.** This gravity's core reality is **Documented** on grounds substantially independent of Optatus's and Augustine's own hostile framing: **four separate Donatist-voiced or -authored texts**, plus non-textual epigraphic corroboration (the *Deo laudes* acclamation, stone, no literary mediation at any point), converge on it. This is worth stating as a positive, deliberate finding rather than merely noting the absence of a caveat: **of the three Primary gravities in this document**, this is the one whose specific evidentiary texture, not merely its bare existence, escapes the hostile-mediation problem that qualifies G1 and (to a lesser extent) G5." (§3.3, emphasis added)

Both bolded numbers are wrong, and both are checkable against the rest of the same document:

1. **"Four separate ... texts" should be three.** Round 1's H3 finding was that G3's evidentiary base double-counted Macrobius's one letter (present in the vendored Migne file under two different headings — the *Passio Isaac et Maximiani* and the separately-titled *Fragmentum epistolae Macrobii Donatistae*) as two distinct texts, and that the correct, non-duplicated count is three (the sermon, the *Passio Marculi*, and Macrobius's letter). This fix was applied correctly and consistently in §1's candidate table ("three distinct Donatist-authored/voiced texts"), in §3.3's own Repetition test two bullets above this one ("three distinct Donatist-voiced or Donatist-authored texts"), in §4's index table ("Attested in three distinct Donatist-voiced texts plus independent epigraphy"), in §5's Cross-Voice bullet ("three distinct texts plus epigraphy"), and in the companion `Gravity_Index.xlsx` Candidates sheet's G3 row ("three distinct Donatist-voiced/authored texts"). This one instance — in the Cross-Check bullet, the specific place Round 1 called out as where the inflated count was doing the most rhetorical work ("presented as a 'positive, deliberate finding'") — was missed.
2. **"Of the three Primary gravities" should be four.** This document now classifies four Primary gravities (G1, G2, G3, G5 — stated correctly at line 136's "Primary (4)," line 149's and 169's "the four Primaries," and the closing line 222's "4 Primary"). This specific sentence is a direct, internal self-contradiction: it names G1 and G5 by name as the two Primaries whose evidentiary texture is more hostile-encumbered than G3's, but calls the total "three" rather than four (silently omitting G2, which — like G3 — carries a "CONSISTENT, no significant divergence" Cross-Check with no comparable hostile-mediation caveat, so its omission from the *comparison* is defensible, but the *count* "three" is not).

**Independent verification of the correct count.** I did not accept either round's own recount; I re-derived it myself. `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` §6 states that the single vendored Migne file "delivers **four** texts that speak as Donatists" — the *Passio Donati* sermon, the *Passio Marculi*, Macrobius's letter (present under two headings, counted once), **and** Tyconius's *Liber Regularum* — and I independently confirmed this by reading the vendored file's own front-matter table of contents (`cic/texts/monumenta-vetera-donatistarum_migne-pl8.txt`, lines 1–22), which states explicitly that Mabillon's *Monumenta Vetera* comprises "**three items, in sequence**": (1) the sermon, (2) the *Passio Marculi*, (3) the *Passio Isaac et Maximiani*, "**with the appended epistle of the martyr Macrobius**" — i.e., the letter is presented in the file's own front matter as part of item 3, not as a fourth independent item. I further confirmed by direct grep that "PASSIO MARCULI" appears once (line 665), the Macrobius material appears under both of its titles (*Passio Maximiani et Isaac*, line 1367/1495; *Fragmentum epistolae Macrobii Donatistae*, line 2196) referring to the same letter, and the sermon is described separately in the front matter. Doc_02's own reasoning for reaching "four" specifically depends on adding Tyconius's *Liber Regularum* from a different acquisition (G1) — material that bears on G1/interpretation, not on martyr-cult. Doc_04's own distinction (three texts for G3 specifically, since Tyconius doesn't bear on martyr-cult) is therefore sound and well-reasoned everywhere it was actually applied — this finding is only about the one place it wasn't.

**Why this matters:** This is not a new defect in kind — it is H3 from Round 1, not fully fixed, sitting in the specific paragraph whose entire function is to state, precisely, an evidentiary-confidence finding for a Primary gravity. A future reader trusting this specific bullet (rather than cross-checking every other instance of the same figure in the document) would be told a factually wrong number in the one place the Framework's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check is supposed to be most exact. **Fix:** change "four separate Donatist-voiced or -authored texts" to "three" and "of the three Primary gravities in this document" to "of the four Primary gravities in this document" in §3.3's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check bullet. No companion-workbook change is needed — the xlsx Candidates sheet's G3 row does not repeat either error.
