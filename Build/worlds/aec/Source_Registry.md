# Source Registry — Antiochene Exegetical Christianity (Chrysostom-centered)

Companion to `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`, built to `Build/reference/L3B-World-Build-Methodology/Source_Registry_Template.md`. The world's corpus-map bucket (`cic/corpus-map/antiochene-exegetical-christianity-chrysostom-ce.yaml`) holds 66 work-rows (counted in October 2026 by parsing the file as YAML and by `grep -c '^- row_id:'`, which agree). This Registry groups them by source file and genre where their Type, Confidence, Boundary Status and Licensed-For treatment is uniform, and gives a row of its own to any work with a distinct disposition. The corpus-map file stays the record of work-level assignment. Every one of the 66 rows is covered by a row below. Row 14a splits row 14 and refers to the same two corpus-map rows.

Corpus figures, counted two ways. Counted in October 2026, the six Chrysostom volumes `npnf109` to `npnf114` hold 3,332,586 words by `wc -w` under the `POSIX` locale and 3,351,124 under `C.UTF-8`. With each XML tag replaced by a space and the text split on whitespace in Python 3, the same six files hold 3,032,271 words. The `wc -w` figures include markup and editorial apparatus.

**Status:** Not approved to proceed. The Round 3 review and the cap-ruling review did not clear the world's Steps 0 to 2, the three-round cap is reached, and the project lead decides the next step. See `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` section 10.
