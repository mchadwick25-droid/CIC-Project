# Source Registry — Antiochene Exegetical Christianity (Chrysostom-centered)

Companion to `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`, built to `Build/reference/L3B-World-Build-Methodology/Source_Registry_Template.md`. The world's corpus-map bucket (`cic/corpus-map/antiochene-exegetical-christianity-chrysostom-ce.yaml`) holds 50 work-rows. This Registry groups them by source file and genre where their Type, Confidence, Boundary Status and Licensed-For treatment is uniform, and gives a row of its own to any work with a distinct disposition. The corpus-map file stays the record of work-level assignment. Every one of the 50 rows is covered by exactly one row below.

Corpus figures, counted two ways. The six Chrysostom volumes `npnf109` to `npnf114` hold 3,339,085 words by `wc -w` under the `POSIX` locale and 3,357,623 under `C.UTF-8`. With the XML tags stripped and the text split on whitespace in Python 3, the same six files hold 3,035,294 words. The `wc -w` figures include markup and editorial apparatus.

## Named acquisition gaps (not yet Registry rows: no source exists to classify)

A row needs an actual source to classify. These are open acquisitions, kept outside the numbered table.

- **The eight *Adversus Judaeos* homilies.** No public-domain English translation search has been run (Doc_02 §4, §9). This is the world's most consequential open acquisition question.
- **Diodore of Tarsus's own exegetical works.** No public-domain English translation path exists (Source Readiness Dossier §4; Doc_01 §7).
- **Theodore of Mopsuestia's *Commentary on the Twelve Prophets*.** It is his earlier, Antiochene-presbyterate work, the piece that would anchor this world, as distinct from his later episcopal writing at Mopsuestia. No public-domain translation is identified. The Mingana 1932 translation of a different work, his *Commentary on the Nicene Creed*, carries an open 2028 rights question (Source Readiness Dossier §4).
- **Paul W. Harkins, *Discourses Against Judaizing Christians* (Fathers of the Church, 1979).** The standard modern translation of the *Adversus Judaeos* material (Doc_02 §9). It is well within copyright and is not a public-domain candidate.
- **Robert L. Wilken, *John Chrysostom and the Jews* (University of California Press, 1983), and Wayne Meeks and Robert Wilken, *Jews and Christians in Antioch* (Scholars Press, 1978).** Census-named secondary scholarship on this world's Jewish-community material. Not vendored and not read.

## Living-document protocol

Append-only, no renumbering, no deletion, per the Template. Sub-lettered rows (6a, 8a, 11a, 11b, 14a) split a single original numbered row into per-work dispositions; the letter suffix marks a split, not a renumbering. Rows 1, 3, 5 to 12, 14, 15 to 20 are Confidence B or C and are listed in `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` §9. No later document treats them as Confidence A without the same direct verification used for rows 2, 4, 13, 14a and 21.
