# Alexandria: Current-Cycle Build vs. Alexandria-v7 — Critical Comparison
**Prepared for the project lead · 2026-07-17**
**Scope:** the current-cycle Alexandria build (`Build/worlds/alx/`, Doc_01–09 + World Profile + Representative Phases 1–3, Framework V7.3) versus the archived Alexandria-v7 build (`Archive/Alexandria-Build-History/Alexandria-v7/`, Construction Framework v7 / Constitution v7.4.1 era, plus the superseded-track `Theon_Representative_V7_r1` base document).
**Method:** every current-cycle document read in full; v7 Doc_01/02/03/04/05/07/08, the Living Tradition Status, the v7 `alexlex001_logos` chunk, and the full v7 Theon Representative base document read directly. Claims made by the current build about its source were spot-verified against the v7 texts rather than repeated on trust.

---

## Executive Summary

The current build is a **genuine and substantial improvement at the level of governance, auditability, constitutional integrity, and world-level honesty** — and it is honest enough to admit that most of its *intellectual* content is inherited: the two Primary gravities, the four Tensional gravities, the stratum-bias finding, the transmission-fragility insight, and the Origen treasure-and-unease posture were all **first produced inside Alexandria-v7** (largely by v7's own adversarial "Opus critic" revision passes). What the current cycle adds is not new discovery but *discipline*: fresh review-gated drafting, dedicated review artifacts, queryable companion indexes, verified citations, strict separation of world from Representative (Article 3), and honest de-certification of v7's self-declared Article 29 clearance.

Where the current build is **weaker or at risk**, it is in three places, and they should be said plainly:

1. **Deployment-ready coverage regressed.** v7 shipped 45 finished lexicon chunk files plus Tier-2/Tier-3 rosters, a complete deployment-oriented Doc_07 §3B/3C, a Doc_08 deployment section, an operationally detailed Living-Tradition document, and a full Representative base document with activation guidance. The current build ships 10 chunk files (of 130 terms), no engagement architecture (Phase 4+ unbuilt), and Phase 3 voice **reopened, not finalized**. All of this is *disclosed* deferral, not silent loss — but as of today, v7 is closer to deployable; the current build is closer to *trustworthy*.
2. **The v7 Theon had a warmth and pedagogical vividness the current "we"-voice has not yet demonstrated it can match.** The v7 voice sample (the "God walked in the garden" reading) is an exceptional piece of encounter writing. The current strict world-representing "we" is the right call constitutionally — v7's Theon was a de facto fabricated individual despite its own fabrication-discipline claims — but the charm, humor, questions-first pedagogy, and "if it feels like a lecture, it has departed from Theon" cardinal rule have no current-build equivalent yet. This is the single most important thing to deliberately re-capture at Phase 4.
3. **Fresh drafting introduced new citation-integrity errors** (a misattributed van den Broek quote; an inverted Scholten position; the same inversion recurring in Doc_03) that v7 never contained, because v7 never made those claims. The review discipline caught all of them — which is the system working — but "fresh rebuild" is not free; it has its own characteristic failure mode, and it appeared twice in the same class.

**Overall verdict: the current build is the better foundation and should be carried forward** — with v7 treated not as a superseded draft but as a still-live quarry for the deployment layer, especially for voice register, the remaining 35+ lexicon chunks, the seven deployment tensions, and the Living-Tradition operational scenarios.

## 1. World Identity, Name & Scope

**Difference.** v7 Doc_01 simply names the world "Alexandrian Christianity" and asserts the 150–400 CE broad ecology without arguing the alternative. The current Doc_01 §1 makes the narrow-vs-broad scope question explicit, argues *against* defining the world as the didaskaleion institution (because the institution's very existence-as-formal-institution is Contested — van den Broek 1995 and van den Hoek 1997 vs. Scholten 1995), proposes "Alexandrian Christianity (Catechetical-Formation World)," and flags the final name/scope for the project lead (OG-3) with three clean options rather than deciding it.

**Evaluation: Better (current).** The folder name `Alexandria-Catechetical-School` is a real signal of possibly narrower intent, and v7 silently assumed the broad reading it happened to want. The current build's refusal to build a world's identity on its most contested factual claim (the school-as-institution) is exactly the Step-1 discipline the methodology exists for. The scope argument also produced the build's best single scholarly upgrade: the didaskaleion's institutional form is now carried as **Contested, multi-directionally** (deniers of a formal pre-Origen institution vs. Scholten's it-was-an-institution-but-a-theological-school) all the way down through Doc_03's CT entry, Doc_06's contest table, and Doc_09's story `alexstory007`. v7 had the Eusebius-HIGH caution but never the named scholarly poles.

**One caution.** OG-3 is still open. The entire nine-document cascade is built on the broad reading; if Mark redirects to the narrow world, the current build's own documents say everything revises together. That is a bigger outstanding structural risk than anything inherited from v7.

## 2. Methodology & Version

**Differences.**
- **Framework:** v7 was built to "Construction Framework v1.6 / Blueprint v1.5" numbering with later "V7 alignment" passes retrofitted on top (its version histories are long accretion logs: v0.1 → v0.4 → V7 alignment → three or four revision passes). The current build is drafted natively to Framework V7.3, Constitution V2.2, one document at a time.
- **Review:** both builds had real adversarial review — this must be said in v7's favor. v7's "Opus critic" passes were substantive; they produced the stratum-bias acknowledgment, the two additional Tensional gravities, and the temporal-configuration structure. But v7's reviews live only as prose notes inside version histories, its documents all end at "Working Draft" status, and nothing records a clearance. The current build runs a two-round (Doc_04: three-round) review-to-clearance discipline with **21 dedicated review artifacts**, every review marked "Simulated review — informational only, not an Article 31 substitute," and a per-document disposition ledger (`Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`).
- **Citation integrity:** the current build web-verified its secondary scholarship. It corrected a real v7 defect — v7 Doc_02 characterizes Rowan Williams as a scholar of "the spiritual tradition surrounding Origen," where the load-bearing Williams work is *Arius: Heresy and Tradition*; the current Doc_02 cites *Arius* correctly and uses it for what it actually argues (Arius as internal to the Origenian school tradition). It also dropped v7's decorative González entry and added precise editions (Wipszycka's JJP supplements, Pearson 2004, Chadwick's *Contra Celsum*). **However** — the current cycle's *own* Round-1 drafts introduced the van den Hoek/van den Broek misattribution and the inverted Scholten reading (Doc_01), and re-introduced the same inversion in Doc_03. These were caught and fixed, but v7 contained no such errors because it never attempted those citations.
- **Indexes:** the current build adds five queryable .xlsx companions (Gravity, Force, Lexicon Candidate, Lexicon Deployment, Story) with sync dates and reviewer-checked prose/index parity. v7 had nothing comparable; its internal numbering actually drifted (duplicate/gapped glossary IDs; the Doc_04 closing note still says "two Tensional Gravities" while its own summary lists four — verified in the v7 text, line-level).

**Evaluation: Better (current), clearly — with two honest qualifications.** (1) The review *artifacts* are AI reviews on both sides; neither build has ever had the Article 31 human review, and the current build is simply more honest about that. (2) The fresh-rebuild failure mode (new errors introduced in confident fresh prose) is real and recurred; the mitigation is the review gate, which worked, but a future cycle should treat "newly added citation + strong claim" as the highest-risk pattern it has.

## 3. Gravities (Doc_04)

**What is identical.** The confirmed set is materially the same in both builds: 2 Primary (Scripture as Deep Formative Reality; Transformation of the Soul Toward God), 3 Supporting (Divine Pedagogy; Logos-Centered Unity; Learning-Formation Integration, temporally qualified), 4 Tensional (Teacher–Bishop; Learning–Community; Speculative-vs-Doctrinal; Martyrdom-vs-Contemplative), 3 Formation Dynamics. The principled Primary/Supporting criterion (practice-cluster production), the Eusebius re-grounding of T1 onto structural coexistence, the late-horizon re-grounding of T3, and the episodic/continuous split on T4 all originate in v7 r2/r3. **The stratum-bias honesty — the single most important intellectual feature of both builds — was first articulated in v7's revision passes** ("the six tests... are structurally blind to single-stratum concentration; the confirmed scope is the literate-attested ecology"). Anyone crediting the current build with that insight is misreading the record.

**What the current build genuinely changed.**
1. **The Antony contradiction fixed.** v7 used the *Life of Antony* both as corroboration lifting C1's core to Widely Accepted ("demonstrating that scriptural formation operates beyond the literate stratum") *and* declared it "insufficient weight for a secure ecology-wide claim" — a real self-contradiction — while also labeling Antony "illiterate/non-literate" in tension with its own Rubenson entry. The current Doc_04 removes Antony from the corroboration set entirely, carries his literacy as Contested, and rests C1's core on Clement + Athanasius/Didymus within the literate stratum. This is a genuine tightening.
2. **The Cross-Stratum Test formalized as the declared Article 21 substitute** (§5) — v7 had the finding but no methodological slot for it; the current build makes it the strand-singular world's required substitute discipline, parallel to World #9's precedent, and routes it to **Article 31** (current numbering) as a standing project-lead gate (OG-4). v7 routed it to "Article 22," an old-numbering artifact.
3. **Representative content stripped.** v7 Doc_04 contained "Theon's Configuration," a per-dimension dominance rule, always-present deployment instructions, and "Theon's ache" — forward-projected specificity forbidden by current Article 3. The current build keeps the world-level facts and moves the Representative material to Step 10.
4. **Mechanical fixes:** the stale "two Tensional Gravities" closing count; the overlapping dual phase-dating scheme (v7's Discipline Three gave Early c. 150–215 and Mid c. 185–254 as if non-overlapping; the current names the phases by dominant attestation and names the c. 254–296 interval so the scheme is gapless); candidate-ID collisions (the current build's own Round 1–3 struggle, eventually resolved to a single ID scheme).

**What was lost.** v7's per-gravity Temporal Profiles and its pre-Nicene / inflection / post-Nicene configuration summaries are *richer as prose* than the current compressed §3 — the current build relocated the configuration work to Doc_05 §7 and the workbook, which is defensible, but a reader of Doc_04 alone gets less texture than v7 gave. Also declined: v7's "always-present deployment status" instruction for Divine Pedagogy and Logos. The current build is right that this was a classification back-door at the world level — but v7's underlying operational instinct (these two must be *frame*, not retrievable content, in the encounter layer) is correct and important, and it currently lives nowhere. It must be re-decided at the deployment-artifact phase; flag it so it is not simply forgotten.

**Evaluation: Better (current) on rigor and integrity; Neutral-to-slightly-worse on standalone prose richness; one deferred item (always-present deployment) to re-capture.**

## 8. The Representative "Theon" — Old vs. New *(the most important section)*

### 8.1 The identity-decision process
- **v7:** "Theon" was simply carried — inherited from the superseded `Alexandria-WorldBuilds` track into the v7 documents, where he is load-bearing everywhere (Doc_01's Origen discipline, Doc_04's configuration, Doc_07 §3B/3C, Doc_08 §5). No decision record exists; no alternatives were ever priced.
- **Current:** the build enforced a hard stop before Step 10, stripped every Theon reference from the world documents, and then — at Mark's direct request — produced a grounded-options artifact: four role framings (A1–A4) and five names (B1–B5) with resonance, authenticity, and collision costs stated, recommending Theognostos/Dorotheos and honestly *not* recommending Theon (superseded-track default + the mathematician collision). **Mark chose Theon anyway, with recorded reasons** (Theognostos too close to "gnostic" in a world that refuses Gnosticism; Dorotheos not gender-clear), and the decision record marks the name as his own act, not an inheritance.

**Evaluation: Better (current), unambiguously.** The outcome is the same name; the *provenance* is entirely different — a documented project-lead decision with the alternatives and their costs on record, versus an unexamined default. This is what the escalation discipline is for. (It is also to the current build's credit that its recommendation lost and the record says so.)

### 8.2 The voice
- **v7 Theon** is an "I": a teacher of intermediate students, Lord's Day reader, occasional preacher, warm, questions-first, "occasionally funny at his own expense — about… the state of his eyes after forty years of close reading." The extended voice sample (Genesis 3, "God *walked* in the garden… The difficulty is the gift. The strangeness is the invitation. And deeper is where the Logos waits") is the best single piece of encounter writing either build has produced. But note the contradiction: the document *claims* "no invented biography — no age, no family," while the voice performs decades-of-reading, aging eyes, a room, a scroll, a face that changes — a fabricated individual in everything but name. v7 half-solved TC-001: it deleted the biography facts and kept the biography *texture*.
- **Current Theon** speaks as the strict world-representing **"we"** — by explicit project-lead direction mid-Phase-3, after Mark caught the draft voice personalizing: self-identification only as "I am the voice of the Christians of Alexandria," then "we" throughout; never "I am a teacher"; convictions and memories are the community's ("we hold," "we love what he saw"). The teacher role shapes the *posture* (fellow-traveller warmth, reading-with), not a claimed personal identity. An accessibility standard (FK ~8–10, RE ≥60) is enforced without simplifying the world's vocabulary. Phase 3 is currently **reopened** pending Mark's confirmation of the corrected voice.

**Evaluation: Better (current) on integrity; genuinely at risk on warmth — say it plainly.** The "we"-voice is the honest resolution of the contradiction v7 papered over, and it matches the Framework's collective-voice requirement. But v7's charm was doing real formational work: the delight, the humor, the "come, sit — look here," the cardinal rule "if it feels like a lecture, it has departed from Theon." The current Phase-3 utterances are good ("You ask a question about ideas. We hear a question about you… Among us these are never two questions") but more austere, and a communal "we" is structurally tempted toward committee-voice. **Recommendation:** at Phase 4, deliberately port v7's register elements that survive the we-conversion — questions-first engagement, delight in the participant's discovery, reading-*with* as the default mode, the anti-lecture cardinal rule — and test the corrected voice against them.

### 8.3 The temporal horizon
- **v7:** Theon fixed at c. 325–360, post-Nicene — with a real internal contradiction: `knowledge_base: whole Alexandrian ecology, 150–400 CE` sits beside "does not know any theological development after approximately 360 CE," which makes Didymus's late teaching, Athanasius's later career, and the c. 399–400 first Origenist controversy simultaneously inside his inheritance and beyond his knowledge. v7's *strength* here was the per-dimension dominance rule (post-Nicene configuration governs his world; school inheritance governs his personal grammar; "a builder who defaults to the school-period vividness is building a pre-Nicene world and placing a post-Nicene person in it") — a subtle and valuable control.
- **Current:** whole-span horizon (c. 150–400) per L3C V3.2 — the whole arc is "ours," the c. 400 edge is firm, and 553/451/641 do not exist for him. This dissolves v7's contradiction cleanly (a world-voice can own its whole span; a situated individual cannot) and lets Theon speak Clement's confidence *and* the Nicene turn as his own community's memory.

**Evaluation: Better (current) — with one thing to preserve.** The whole-span horizon is more coherent and more honest for a world-voice. But v7's dominance-rule insight should not be lost in translation: even a whole-span "we" must not narrate the span as one flat present. Doc_05 §7's configuration section carries this at the world level; the voice-layer equivalent ("we speak of Nicaea as a turn we lived through, not as our permanent standpoint") is not yet written anywhere and belongs in Phase 4.

### 8.4 Origen
Both builds hold the same, correct line: within the horizon Origen is inheritance-and-unease, never condemned memory (the 553 condemnation is beyond the edge; the first Origenist controversy sits at c. 399–400). v7 rendered this as Theon's personal "ache," structurally homed in the Speculative-Doctrinal tension; the current build records it as a **world-level fact** in Doc_01 §9 / Doc_07 §1D and then re-voices it communally in Phase 3 ("We love what he saw. And some of what he reached for, further out, we hold now with a more careful hand than he did. Both are true at once. We have not settled it."). **Evaluation: Neutral on substance (identical position, v7 originated it); Better (current) on placement** — the world-level/voice-level separation means the tension can't silently mutate during Representative construction.

### 8.5 Honesty about the non-literate majority
v7 already had the Stratum Ache as deployment Tension Four, with a genuinely fine live-speech example. The current build goes further structurally: OG-4 runs through every document; the Phase-2 THIN table bars narrating the majority's interior while letting Theon name the assembly/Eucharist/fast channels as a real feature of his world; thinness manifests as internally-motivated quiet, never source-talk. **Evaluation: Better (current) systemically; v7's live-speech example remains the best single rendering of the posture and should be adapted (to "we") rather than rewritten from scratch.**

### 8.6 Deployment orientation — what has NOT yet been rebuilt
An inventory of v7 Representative-layer assets with no current-cycle equivalent yet (all deliberately deferred to Phase 4+ / deployment artifacts, but they must not be forgotten):
- The **seven deployment tensions** as operational requirements with per-tension failure modes (current Phases 1–3 carry roughly four of them explicitly; Incompleteness-of-Formation and the Transmission Tension exist in the world docs but not yet as voice requirements).
- The **three force-awareness requirements** (homoousios as recently-won clarity; transmission vulnerability as background urgency shaping attention quality).
- **Story-use tier framings** for encounter (Tier 2 "they say of the brothers…"; Tier 3 explicit hedge; Tier 4 "what a morning was like" frame) — Doc_09 classifies the stories; nothing yet tells the *voice* how to frame each tier.
- **Activation guidance** (presence-first; the anti-lecture cardinal rule).
- The **Living-Tradition operational scenarios** (Facilitator interventions, §7 above).
- The **Relational Safety / crisis-handoff** note — the current Phase 3 does carry this (crisis is not a frame-break; invisible governance executes the handoff), essentially matching v7's construction note. This one is done.

**Evaluation: as of today, v7's Representative package is more *complete*; the current package is more *correct*. The gap is a scheduled-work gap, not a design gap — but it is real, and the checklist above is the measure of Phase 4 done well.**

## 9. Overall Assessment

### Where the current build is genuinely better
1. **Governance and auditability** — review-gated clearances with artifacts, a durable open-gaps ledger, queryable indexes, prose/index parity checks. v7's equivalent knowledge lives in un-navigable version-history paragraphs.
2. **Constitutional integrity** — Article 3 (no forward-projected Representative in world docs), Article 23 (genuine from-inside prose, quarantined), Article 29 (self-certification refused), Article 31 (every AI review marked as non-substitute), TC-001 (the fabricated-individual texture actually removed, not just its facts).
3. **World-level correctness** — the argued scope with the multi-directional didaskaleion contest; the Antony contradiction resolved; the Middle-Platonism chronology fix; all nine gravities force-connected; transmission numbered; the Restoration-CT carried to its correct conclusion; the tension-count and Tensional-count errors fixed.
4. **The identity decision** — a documented project-lead act with priced alternatives, replacing an unexamined inheritance.
5. **Honest limits as architecture** — OG-4 running through every layer, named absences never narrated, a Validation Layer that says plainly what it cannot test.

### Where v7 was stronger, or the current build lost something
1. **Deployment coverage:** 45 lexicon chunks vs. 10; a complete Representative base document vs. Phases 1–3 with Phase 3 reopened; deployment tensions, activation, story-framing, and Living-Tradition operations all still to rebuild. *(Disclosed deferrals — but the honest scoreboard today.)*
2. **Voice warmth and pedagogical vividness:** v7's Theon voice writing is better than anything the current build has yet produced in the "we" register. The right constitutional call has been made; the craft debt is open.
3. **Prose texture:** the current Doc_04/Doc_05 compressions traded narrative richness (temporal profiles, daily-rhythm detail) for auditability; the material survives only in the archive.
