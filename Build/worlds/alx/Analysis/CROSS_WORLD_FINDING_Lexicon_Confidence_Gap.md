# Cross-World Finding: Lexicon deployment chunks in Desert-Monasticism and Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary systematically omit required confidence-vocabulary and Author-Gravity disclosure

**Prepared by:** the Alexandria (Catechetical-School) build thread, 2026-07-19, at the project lead's direction ("do what's best for the academic rigor and quality of the program regardless of others... note that so we can fix it in other worlds if it is truncated and shouldn't be").
**Scope:** this is a finding about **Desert-Monasticism** and **Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary**'s own deliverables, not Alexandria's. It is written here, in Alexandria's own Analysis folder, because it surfaced during this session's portfolio consistency audit and this thread has no authority to edit another world's build directly. **This document is the actionable handoff** — quote-sourced, quantified, and specific enough to act on without re-deriving the investigation.
**Status:** finding only. No files outside Alexandria's own folder were touched.

---

## The determination

Alexandria's lexicon deployment chunks average ~1,200 words each; Desert-Monasticism's average ~217; Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary's average ~307. A prior version of this session's own consistency audit treated this purely as a "house-style difference" not worth adjudicating. **On closer, evidence-based inspection at the project lead's direction, that framing was too neutral.** The length gap is not stylistic — it tracks a real, measurable, and governing-template-mandated content omission in the two shorter siblings: **the Article 17 confidence-vocabulary grading and the Author-Gravity risk disclosure that ground every claim's epistemic weight are systematically missing.**

This is not a preference call. It is the exact discipline the project's own anti-fabrication apparatus exists to enforce — the same discipline this session's Doc_09 Round 2 review (`Review-Artifacts/Doc_09_Round2_Review.md`) just caught Alexandria itself under-applying in one story and fixed. **Alexandria's fuller format is being kept as-is; it is not being trimmed to match the siblings.** The siblings' shorter format is the one that needs the fix.

---

## The evidence

### 1. The governing template requires this, explicitly

`Build/reference/L4-Templates/Deployment_Lexicon_Chunk_Template.md`, Key Sources section (line 160–162), the instruction every world's lexicon chunks are built against:

> *"If a source carries Author Gravity risk — if it dominates the evidence for this term — note it: 'Note: \[source] dominates the surviving evidence for this term's usage; see Author Gravity Assessment in Doc_01.'"*

This is not optional elaboration. It is the template's own stated requirement for any term whose evidentiary base is dominated or mediated by a single risky author.

### 2. The quantified gap, checked systematically (not cherry-picked)

Grepped every lexicon chunk in each world for (a) any Article 17 confidence-vocabulary term (Documented / Widely Accepted / Dominant Modern Reconstruction / Inferential-Thin / Contested) and (b) any Author-Gravity / mediation-risk language:

| World | Chunks with confidence vocabulary | Chunks with Author-Gravity/mediation language |
|---|---|---|
| **Desert-Monasticism** | 5 of 9 (56%) | 2 of 9 (22%) |
| **Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary** | 6 of 15 (40%) | 1 of 15 (7%) |
| **Alexandria** (after this session's own 3-chunk fix, see below) | 45 of 45 (100%) | present throughout, via explicit DMR/Origen-concentration/[CT]-routing language even where the literal phrase "Author Gravity" doesn't appear |

### 3. Concrete before/after — same discipline, two different outcomes on comparable material

**Desert's `desertlex005_diakrisis.md` (entire Key Sources section):**
> *"Apophthegmata Patrum, scattered sayings across named elders."*

No confidence level stated anywhere in the chunk. No mediation caution — despite the fact that Desert's **own** Doc_02 (per its story chunk `desertstory004_moses-leaking-jug.md`, which does carry this discipline) flags the *Apophthegmata* as a "5th–6th century compilation" carrying a "compiler-mediation caveat." The lexicon chunk for the identical source category drops a caution the world's own story chunk for the same source category correctly keeps. This is an internal inconsistency within Desert-Monasticism's own deliverables, not just a cross-world gap.

**Desert's `desertlex001_anachoresis.md` (entire Key Sources section):**
> *"Athanasius's* Life of Antony *(the primary narrative source for the outer-mountain progression); independent archaeological corroboration of the settlement pattern withdrawal produced."*

Names what corroborates the claim but never states what confidence level the claim itself carries.

**Alexandria's `alexlex001_logos.md` (Key Sources Note, for comparison — the template's own worked pattern):**
> *"Note: Origen dominates the surviving systematic evidence for the Logos in this world; the underlying conviction is broadly Alexandrian and independently attested in Clement (earlier) and Athanasius (later), so the core is Widely Accepted while the specific Origenian systematization is Dominant Modern Reconstruction (Doc_02 §3.1; Doc_04 C4)."*

This is the template's own instruction executed as written: names the dominant/risky source, states the confidence level, splits the core (broadly attested) from the systematization (author-concentrated), and cites the exact grounding document-section. Every one of Alexandria's 45 chunks now does this (see §4 below — three of Alexandria's own chunks were themselves missing this and were fixed today, in the same pass that produced this finding).

**Hieronymian's `hal_lex08_origenism.md`** is the strongest of the sibling examples — it does carry a genuine CT Contest Type section and an Author-Gravity-adjacent caution in Key Sources ("Note: both sides are adversarial; neither account of the dispute's substance is privileged over the other") — but even here, no Article 17 confidence-vocabulary word appears anywhere in the chunk. The narrative register is confident throughout with no calibration marker distinguishing what is Documented from what is inferred.

### 4. Alexandria held itself to the same standard, found and fixed its own 3 gaps first

Before writing this finding, the same grep was run against Alexandria's own 45 chunks. Three (`alexlex007_participation`, `alexlex008_theosis`, `alexlex021_transformation`) had the same omission — solid Author-Gravity/CT-routing discipline in their Key Sources Notes, but no explicit confidence-level word. **Fixed directly, today**, before this finding was written, so the standard being asked of the sibling worlds is one Alexandria's own corpus now fully meets (45 of 45), not one it merely gestures at.

## Why this matters (not just "more detail is nicer")

The whole point of Article 17's five-level confidence vocabulary and the Author-Gravity screening discipline (Doc_01/02's own governing apparatus, present in every world) is to prevent a claim resting on one dominant or risky source from being presented with the same epistemic weight as a claim independently corroborated across multiple sources. A lexicon chunk that states a claim without a confidence level, and without flagging that its sole source is compiled, mediated, or dominant, is not simply terser — it is **silently dropping the exact safeguard this project's anti-fabrication discipline exists to provide at the point where a Representative actually retrieves and speaks the claim.** This is a runtime-facing artifact; the gap isn't academic-only, it reaches the participant.

## What this finding does NOT recommend

This thread has no authority over Desert-Monasticism's or Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary's own build records, and did not touch either world's files. **Do not treat this as instruction to unilaterally rewrite those chunks from outside their own coach thread** — the fix requires re-grounding each claim against those worlds' own Doc_02 Author-Gravity assessments, which is that thread's own material to check, not something to import from Alexandria's conventions wholesale.

## Recommended action

Route this finding to the System Hub / to whoever owns the Desert-Monasticism and Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary build threads, with the concrete ask: **audit each world's lexicon chunks against its own Doc_02 Author-Gravity assessments and the L4 template's own Key Sources instruction; add the missing confidence-vocabulary grounding and Author-Gravity disclosure per chunk, the same way Alexandria's `alexlex001_logos.md` (and, after today, all 45 of its chunks) already does it.** This is the template's own bar, not an imported one.
