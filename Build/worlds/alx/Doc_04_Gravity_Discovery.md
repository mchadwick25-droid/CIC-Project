## 0. Method, Phase Scheme, and the Two Governing Disciplines

**Purpose.** Gravity Discovery identifies the forces the Alexandrian ecology actually *organizes around* — a gravity organizes; a topic merely recurs (Framework Part III). Candidates are generated only from traceable recurrence across Doc_02 evidence streams (never general impression), run through the six tests (Repetition, Dependency, Formation, Explanatory, Persistence, Interaction), cross-checked against the five-level confidence vocabulary, tested for cross-stratum status (the declared Article 21 substitute, §5), connected to the forces (Forces Framework Step 4), and classified Primary / Supporting / Tensional / not-a-gravity.

**Single phase scheme (used consistently throughout).** Gravities are dynamic across the world's own span; each is assessed across three phases by *dominant attestation* (the phases overlap in calendar years because the figures' floruits overlap — the phase names track whose evidence dominates, not hard non-overlapping boundaries):
- **Early / formative** — dominated by **Clement** (to c. 215).
- **Mid** — dominated by **Origen** (c. 215–254, plus his earlier work).
- **Late / post-Nicene** — dominated by **Athanasius and Didymus** (c. 296–400).
- *Inter-phase interval c. 254–296* — dominated by no single surviving major voice; Dionysius of Alexandria's episcopate (c. 248–264) and the post-Origen teachers sit here. **Heraclas** reaches us through Eusebius (*HE* VI.26; HIGH-risk). **Theognostus does not**: he is absent from Eusebius entirely (`grep -c "Theognostus"` against the vendored `npnf201` returns 0, and ANF6's own notice opens "Of this Theognostus we have no account by either Eusebius or Jerome"), and his three *Hypotyposes* fragments reach us through **Athanasius** (*De Decretis* 25; *Ep.* 4 *ad Serapionem* 11) — which carries its own screen, since Athanasius quotes him selectively in post-Nicene anti-Arian polemic (Doc_02 Stream 12). **Pierus** likewise sits here (Eusebius *HE* VII.32, which places **Achillas** — not Pierus — over the catechetical school). Treated as a transitional interval within the Mid→Late movement, so the scheme is gapless.
- **Nicaea (325 CE)** is the named inflection point assessed for every confirmed gravity (Doc_01 §2.3 found it an internal development, not a world boundary — gravity discovery must show *how* each gravity inflected there).

**Discipline One — the Origen SYSTEMIC screen (Author Gravity, Article 26; Doc_02 §3.1).** Origen is the single dominant source for most candidate vocabulary. For every gravity, the screen asks: *is this the ecology's pattern or Origen's?* The answer is given at **two levels**: the **core claim** (does the pattern hold independently of Origen — corroborated by Clement earlier and Athanasius/Didymus later, and by any non-literate attestation?) versus the **Origen-concentrated mechanism claim** (the specific systematized form Origen gives it). The core is carried at its independently-supported confidence; the mechanism is capped at **Dominant Modern Reconstruction** where it rests on Origen. **Eusebius** is a second, distinct HIGH-risk dependency for institutional/succession claims (Doc_02 §3.6) and is screened separately.

**Discipline Two — the cross-build (desert) constraint (Constitution Article 3; Doc_01 §3.3).** No ecology-wide gravity is confirmed on desert-attributed evidence (the *Apophthegmata*, the Pachomian corpus, Evagrius, the cell/*abba* logic). Where a candidate's *developed* form is desert-attributed, only its **urban** attestation counts toward gravity status, and the desert form's world-attribution stays held open for Doc_04-of-the-Desert-build, not resolved here.

**The stratum-bias limit (stated up front because it governs every Primary classification).** The entire surviving corpus is literate, Greek-speaking, and educated. The six tests and the Author-Gravity discipline are **structurally blind to single-stratum concentration**: multi-stream, multi-figure attestation *within one stratum* reinforces rather than corrects the bias. Therefore **no gravity below is confirmed Primary for the whole ecology** — only for the **literate-attested ecology**. Whether the Alexandrian *majority* (non-literate, Coptic-speaking, rural) organized around these same gravities is **unconfirmable on the available evidence** and is carried as an explicit held-open gap (deferred to Article 31 external scholarly review). This is the central Confidence/Gravity divergence of the whole document (§5).

## 2. Candidates Considered and Not Advanced (to gravity status)

Named and reasoned through rather than silently dropped (Template §2):

- **D-A Participation↔Perception Dynamic → Formation Dynamic (not a gravity).** Fails the Dependency test as an *independent* force: it is what mutual Scripture-engagement (C1) and soul-transformation (C2) *produce*, not what produces them. Removed from C1/C2 it has no independent organizing power. Retained as the named **mechanism** of the C1↔C2 pairing (§3.1, §6).
- **D-B Theosis as Horizon → Formation Dynamic.** Genuinely attested (Clement, Origen, Athanasius) but names *where* transformation is going, not *how* it organizes practice — the eschatological dimension of C2, not an independent organizer. Its developed systematic form (Evagrius) is desert-attributed and set aside (Discipline Two); the concept survives at Widely Accepted on the urban trio.
- **Individual–Communal → Formation Dynamic (integrated, not tensional).** Declined as a Tensional Gravity: it lacks the population/institutional/practice-cluster *separation* a tensional pole requires — it is a polarity held *within* each believer, which the ecology integrates (catechumenate, the Sunday assembly) rather than holds unresolved.
- **Logikos / Rational Nature → not a gravity.** The **anthropological presupposition** *of* the confirmed gravities (the human as rational, made for God), not an independent organizing force; fails Dependency. (Remains a Doc_03 [CT] lexicon candidate.)
- **Askesis / Asceticism → not a gravity (urban supporting practice only).** Cannot be confirmed as an organizing gravity on urban evidence alone; its developed, systematic form is desert-attributed and constraint-blocked (Discipline Two). Clement's *Paedagogus* attests a genuine urban ascetic *practice*, but as a practice serving C2, not an organizer in its own right.
- **Restoration (Apokatastasis-adjacent) → not an independent gravity; a dimension of C2.** Resolves the Doc_03 §6 separability handoff: the broadly-shared conviction that God's work is genuinely *restorative* is real, ecology-wide, and separable from Origen's condemned *apokatastasis* (corroborated by Clement and Athanasius) — but it is the **restorative character of the Transformation-toward-God primary (C2)**, not a separate organizing force. As a lexicon term it stands (separable from Apokatastasis, which stays [CT]); as a gravity it folds into C2. *(Doc_03 §4 posed the fold-target alternatively as Salvation; here it folds into the C2 Transformation-toward-God primary as its restorative character — Salvation and Transformation being tightly linked in the Doc_03 lexicon — while remaining a standalone lexicon term.)*

## 3. Per-Candidate Testing

Each confirmed gravity is run through the six tests, the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check, and a forces-connection notation. Per-test verdicts are stated explicitly for the five organizing-force candidates (C1–C5, §3.1–3.5); the four Tensional gravities (§3.6) are carried at summary verdict here, with their full explicit six-test grid in the companion `Gravity_Index.xlsx` (correcting a gap in the source, where one candidate's Repetition verdict was left implicit).

### 3.1 C1 — Scripture as Deep Formative Reality → **PRIMARY** (literate-attested ecology)

- **Repetition — PASS (strong).** Recurs across Streams 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; four partly-independent figures (Clement, Origen, Didymus, Athanasius); all three phases.
- **Dependency — PASS (strong).** The teaching tradition, the hermeneutical habit, catechetical formation, the worship-lectionary, and the Rule of Faith all collapse without Scripture as the formative instrument.
- **Formation — PASS (strong).** Scripture read at depth is *how* participants are shaped — the reader is transformed by the reading, not merely informed.
- **Explanatory — PASS.** Explains the allegorical habit, the learning tradition, the worship shape, and the boundary disputes (which are largely disputes over *reading*).
- **Persistence — PASS (strong).** Visible across all three phases; at Nicaea it *acquires* a doctrinal-witness dimension (Scripture as the ground of the homoousian argument) — an inflection, not a fracture.
- **Interaction — PASS (strong).** The primary *vehicle* of C2, C3, C4, C5 (reinforcing/instrumental; see §6).
- **Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check — CONSISTENT, with two flagged divergences.** Core claim ("Scripture is deeply formative") **Widely Accepted** — carried by Stream 4 and by the *non-Origen* evidence **within the literate stratum** (Clement earlier, Athanasius/Didymus later). The **systematized multilevel allegorical method** is Origen-concentrated → capped at **Dominant Modern Reconstruction** for any ecology-wide method claim (Discipline One, two-level result). **Stratum divergence:** confirmed Primary for the *literate-attested* ecology only; ecology-wide primacy unconfirmable (the one gesture beyond the literate stratum, the Antony material, is Tier-3 hagiography and is *itself Contested as to literacy* per Rubenson, Doc_01 §3.3 — so not securely beyond-literate — and is not relied on).
- **Forces-connection.** Internal-initiating (Scripture inherited as the authoritative formative instrument, via the Philonic-Jewish and apostolic inheritance); ongoing-internal (continuous practice); **ongoing-external response** — the *allegorical method* sharpened partly in response to Gnostic and pagan-philosophical challenge (Doc_02 §7). **Intensifies** mid-horizon (Origen); at Nicaea gains a doctrinal-witness dimension (holds, does not fracture).

### 3.2 C2 — Transformation of the Soul Toward God → **PRIMARY** (literate-attested ecology)

- **Repetition — PASS (strong).** Streams 4, 2, 3, 1, 12; three figures, all phases.

- **Persistence — PASS (strong).** All phases; at Nicaea the mechanism **shifts** from a contemplative-ascent frame toward an Incarnation-grounded one (under Arian pressure) — becoming more theologically robust and more communally accessible.
- **Interaction — PASS (strong).** Reinforcing/instrumental pairing with C1 (the organizing spine, §6).

## 5. Cross-Strand Testing (Article 21) — Declared Substitute: the Cross-Stratum Test

Doc_01 found this world **strand-singular**, so standard cross-strand testing does not apply.
