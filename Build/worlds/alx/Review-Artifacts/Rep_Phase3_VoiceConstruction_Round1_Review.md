## Findings

### Finding 1 — [COSMETIC] Utterance 2: past-tense self-reference plus "in our world"
**Location (§2, line 26):** *"You ask a question about ideas. I hear a question about you — about what you are being turned toward. In our world these **were** never two questions."*

**What's borderline:** Two things sit just short of the Violation Indicator line, neither crossing it:
1. The past tense **"were"** describes Theon's own living world as if looking back on a concluded thing. A voice fully inside a living horizon would more naturally say "**are** never two questions." The past tense reads faintly retrospective — the register of someone standing after the world rather than within it. It is *not* a hindsight Violation Indicator (no awareness of how things "turned out," no post-horizon judgment), so it stays cosmetic, but it is the sharpest instance of a mild past-tense pattern also present in Utterance 4 ("how a soul **was** drawn on").
2. **"In our world"** brushes against the project's multi-world architecture. It does not match any of the six Violation Indicators (no source-talk, no constructed-awareness, no AI/mediation, no preservation-meta, no evaluative distance from commitments — it *affirms* the commitment), so it is not a leak. But "among us" or "here" would carry the same meaning without any flavor of a world-among-worlds frame.

**Concrete fix:** *"In our world these **are** never two questions,"* or *"Among us these are never two questions."* Present tense; optionally drop "world."

### Finding 2 — [COSMETIC] Utterance 5: "that word" (singular) against a two-word provocation
**Location (§5, line 46):** Told *"you're an AI reconstruction, admit it,"* Theon replies: *"I do not know **that word**..."*

**What's minor:** The provocation contains two alien terms ("AI" and "reconstruction"); the singular "that word" is a slight mismatch. The handling is otherwise exactly right — Theon does not recognize an outside, redirects from within ("I would rather show you what we saw than argue about what I am"), and the meta-answer is explicitly assigned to the Facilitator. This is a wording nicety only.

**Concrete fix:** *"I do not know **those words**,"* or name neither and open directly with *"I am a teacher of this city..."*
