## Findings

### 1. [COSMETIC] §4 rests the role-breadth on "the didaskaleion" — the one institutional claim this world holds as *Contested*
**Location:** §4 Role-Breadth Note — "The Alexandrian **catechetical teacher** satisfies this authentically: the didaskaleion was a genuine crossroads of a plural city…"
**What's wrong:** The breadth argument is load-bearing (it is what satisfies the Framework Part Four role-preference for "a crossroads position… that received many visitors"), and it is pinned to *the didaskaleion*. But the didaskaleion's institutional character is precisely the claim Doc_01 §1.2 rates **Contested** and multi-directionally so (van den Broek/van den Hoek doubt a formal institution before Origen; Scholten affirms an institution but as a theological, not catechetical, school). This document's own §2 lists "the didaskaleion's exact institutional form is Contested (Doc_01 §1.2)" under *institutional particulars — THIN*. So §4 leans breadth on an institution §2 just flagged as thin/contested.
**Why it is only cosmetic:** the *substance* is well-grounded independently of the institution question — Doc_01 §1.2 rates it **Widely Accepted** "that identifiable Christian teachers (Clement, then Origen) were conducting advanced Christian instruction in Alexandria across this period, whatever its institutional form," and Doc_05 §1 attests "a city of competing teachers… Christian catechists." The crossroads-of-seekers is attested; only the label overreaches.
**Fix:** reword §4 to rest the breadth on the attested teaching activity (advanced Christian instruction that drew many kinds of seeker and rival — WA per Doc_01 §1.2; Doc_05 §1), not on "the didaskaleion [as institution]." E.g., "the Alexandrian catechetical teacher's own activity was a genuine crossroads…" This keeps §4 consistent with §2 and with Doc_01 §1.2.

### 2. [COSMETIC] The Thinness Mapping (§2) does not visibly draw on Doc_08 (Forces), though the header and the Framework both require it to

**Why cosmetic:** the thinness determinations themselves are correct and well-grounded in the docs that *are* cited; what is missing is the visible forces linkage the header claims.
**Fix:** either add the Doc_08 forces basis to the relevant §2 bullets (majority-interior thinness as a survivorship-forces / stratum-asymmetry consequence; institutional thinness tied to the Eusebius HIGH-risk dependency) or soften the header to match what §2 actually uses. Making it honor the header is the better fix — Doc_08 is a *required* Phase One input.

### 3. [COSMETIC] §3 asserts the "named source, not builder discretion" discipline but does not show the per-item gravity/force mapping
**Location:** §3 Depth Calibration Preview — "Emphasis-shape has a named source (Doc_04 gravities; Doc_08 forces), not builder discretion," followed by RICH/MODERATE/THIN lists.
**What's wrong:** Framework Part Four requires depth calibration to be "grounded explicitly in Doc_04… and Doc_08… a construction artifact with a named source, not a builder's discretionary judgment." §3 echoes that language but the three lists are not traced to their specific gravities/forces, so the "named source" is asserted rather than demonstrated — the one thing the discipline exists to prevent (emphasis that *reads* grounded but is discretionary).

The lists are also individually inferable from Doc_04 (RICH ≈ C1/C2 Primary + C4 integrating center; MODERATE martyr ideal ≈ T4; THIN ≈ OG-4 majority + desert cross-build).
