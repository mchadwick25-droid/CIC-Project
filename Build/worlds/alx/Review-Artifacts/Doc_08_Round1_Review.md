## Findings

### F1 — [SUBSTANTIAL] Index asserts gravity-connections the §5 prose does not carry
**Location:** `Force_Index.xlsx` → `By Connected Gravity` (C1, C4, C5 rows) and `Forces` (2B-3, 3B-3, 1A-3 rows) vs. Doc_08 §5 (lines 95, 98, 99).
**What's wrong:** The prose §5 synthesis and the index disagree on which forces connect to three gravities:

| Gravity | §5 prose connected forces | Index `By Connected Gravity` | Index-only additions |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Scripture | 1A-2, 1B-2, 2B-1 | 1A-2, 1B-2, 2B-1, **2B-3, 3B-3** | 2B-3, 3B-3 |
| C4 Logos-Centered Unity | 1A-1, 1B-2, 2A-2, 2A-4 | 1A-1, **1A-3**, 1B-2, 2A-2, 2A-4 | 1A-3 |
| C5 Learning-Formation | 1B-1, 1B-3, 3B-1, 3B-2 | 1B-1, 1B-3, **2B-3**, 3B-1, 3B-2, **3B-3** | 2B-3, 3B-3 |

The `Forces` sheet is internally consistent with `By Connected Gravity` (2B-3 → C1,C5; 3B-3 → C1,C5,T3; 1A-3 → C4,T3), so the drift is squarely index-vs-prose, not intra-index.
**Evidence:** §5 C1 row reads "1A-2 + 1B-2 (produced); 2B-1 (sustained)" — no 2B-3/3B-3. §5 C4 row reads "1A-1 + 1B-2 (produced); 2A-2 …; 2A-4 …" — no 1A-3. §5 C5 row reads "1B-3 + 1B-1 (produced); 3B-1 + 3B-2 (attenuated/terminated late)" — no 2B-3/3B-3. The index adds all five.
**Why it matters:** The whole point of the companion index (cic-forces-index bar) is look-up parity — a reviewer must be able to trust the index without re-reading the prose. Here the index would tell a reader that transmission (2B-3/3B-3) connects to Scripture and Learning-Formation and that the Neoplatonic environment (1A-3) connects to Logos-Unity, and the prose synthesis would not corroborate it. This is not a completion failure (all 9 gravities connect in both), but it is a genuine drift.
**Note on direction:** The index's additions are substantively defensible — 2B-3/3B-3 Layer 3 explicitly turn on the survival of the sacramental/interpretive channels (C1 Scripture-transmission, C5 learning-formation), and 1A-3 relates to C4 transitively via the §4 map (1A-3 → 2A-2 → C4). So the cleaner fix is likely to *complete the prose*, not prune the index.
**Concrete fix:** Reconcile the two so they are identical. Preferred: add to §5 — C1 "…; 2B-3/3B-3 (transmitted onward)"; C4 "…; 1A-3 (the philosophical environment it integrates against)"; C5 "…; 2B-3/3B-3 (the school channel's transmission and its loss)". Alternatively, remove those attributions from the index. Then re-stamp the "Last confirmed synced" line.

### F2 — [COSMETIC] `By Confidence` sheet buckets 2B-1 / 2B-3 / 3B-3 differently than §6 prose
**Location:** `Force_Index.xlsx` → `By Confidence` vs. Doc_08 §6 (lines 111–112).
**What's wrong:** The index puts 2B-1, 2B-3, 3B-3 in the "WA + partial DMR / thinner" bucket. §6's first bullet lists the same forces under "Widely Accepted (core force claims)" ("the ongoing internal Scripture force (2B-1) … and the broad transmission lines (2B-3, 3B-3)"), with the DMR component noted separately. Both acknowledge WA-core + a DMR component, so this is emphasis, not contradiction — but it is a visible mismatch a cross-checker will notice.
**Fix:** Pick one placement convention and apply it to both artifacts (recommend: WA-core with an explicit DMR-component annotation, matching how 2B-1's method-claim and the succession particulars are already handled).

### F3 — [COSMETIC] 1A-3 labels the *initiating* philosophical environment as "Neoplatonism, systematized by Plotinus/Porphyry" — chronologically it is the ongoing-period maturation
**Location:** Doc_08 §1, force 1A-3 (line 28).
**What's wrong:** The dates given are correct (Plotinus c.204–270; Porphyry c.234–305). But the *initiating* period is c.150–early-3rd c. (Clement arrives c.180; Origen's peak c.220s–230s). Mature Neoplatonism — Plotinus teaching from c.244, Enneads edited by Porphyry c.301 — postdates the world's initiation; the genuinely initiating philosophical substrate was **Middle Platonism** (Numenius, Albinus, and Ammonius Saccas, the shared Alexandrian teacher). The document's own §4 map already routes 1A-3 → 2A-2 ("matures into"), which is exactly where mature Neoplatonism belongs. So the naming, not the dating, is anachronistic.
**Why only cosmetic:** No Layer 2 and no gravity connection rests on the label; the maturation is correctly captured at 2A-2.
**Fix:** In 1A-3 Layer 1, name Middle Platonism / Ammonius Saccas as the initiating-period substrate the ecology formed within, and present Plotinus/Porphyry as the systematization it *becomes* (2A-2), rather than as the initiating environment itself.

### F4 — [COSMETIC] 1A-1 Layer 2 carries a mild comparative-analytical register (borderline from-within)
**Location:** Doc_08 §1, force 1A-1, Layer 2 (line 24).
**What's wrong:** "…near enough to what was needed to be usable, different enough that naming the Word as the one who *entered* human nature changed what Philo had seen rather than merely repeating it." The "near enough / different enough … changed what Philo had seen rather than merely repeating it" framing reads as the analyst's differentiation of two syntheses, not the community's own consciousness. (Naming Philo is itself defensible — Clement and Origen cite him — but the meta-comparative calibration is the soft leak.)
**Fix:** Render in the community's own register, e.g.: the words were already to hand, given to be used; and in naming the Word who entered flesh they were put to a use they had not had before — without the "near/different" calibration or the "rather than merely repeating it" comparison.

### F5 — [COSMETIC] 3A-1 Layer 2 uses "doctrinal contest" — mild analytical term in a from-within passage
**Location:** Doc_08 §1, force 3A-1, Layer 2 (line 58).
**What's wrong:** "the community knew only that doctrinal contest, once begun, did not simply resolve." "Doctrinal contest" leans analytical inside a Reported-Experience Layer 2. Very minor.
**Fix:** Optional softening to the world's own register (e.g., "that contest over the confession, once begun, did not simply resolve — that a line drawn could divide as well as defend").
