## 1. The Six-Cell Matrix

### Cell 1A — Initiating, External

**1A-1: Roman aristocratic ascetic movement predating this world's own bounded span**
- **Historical Event Layer:** A senatorial-class ascetic movement among Roman Christian women, already active by the 360s–370s (Marcella's household), predating Jerome's 382 arrival. Confidence: Widely Accepted (existence); Dominant Modern Reconstruction (specific dating of functioning household status, per Doc_01 §1.1's corrected finding).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A household already gathered around fasting, plain dress, and study before the scholar from the East ever arrived — a movement this world's own participants understood themselves as joining and extending, not founding from nothing.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Established the social and devotional template (renunciation as aristocratic Christian practice) that this world's whole formation ecology (G2) presupposes and extends, per Doc_01 §1.1.

**1A-2: Jerome's arrival in Rome with papal-adjacent scholarly credentials**
- **Historical Event Layer:** Jerome arrives in Rome 382 as secretary/scriptural adviser to Pope Damasus I, possessing classical rhetorical/grammatical training and biblical scholarship. Confidence: Widely Accepted.
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A trained scholar arriving with exactly the philological competence the Aventine circle had lacked.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Generated G1 and G3 directly — without this convergence, no Hebrew-based translation project and no patronage-authority structure exist in this world.

### Cell 1B — Initiating, Internal

**1B-1: Marcella's household reading practice, pre-Jerome**
- **Historical Event Layer:** Marcella's personal ascetic conversion (via exposure to Athanasius's Roman exile, per Ep. 127) dates to the 340s; her household's transition to a functioning multi-woman formation site is best placed 360s–370s (Doc_01 §1.1's corrected, Dominant-Modern-Reconstruction finding).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A widow who had already chosen, on her own initiative, the discipline that later became this world's shared practice — before any outside teacher named or systematized it for her.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** The internal seed from which G2 (ascetic self-impoverishment) and, specifically, G5-Marcella (exegetical authority as independent recognition) both grow — Marcella's own prior initiative is the internal-origin counterpart to Jerome's external arrival (1A-2).

### Cell 2A — Ongoing, External

**2A-1: Pagan aristocratic backlash and the wider Roman senatorial culture**
- **Historical Event Layer:** A resilient pagan senatorial culture (the Symmachus circle, the Altar of Victory controversy) coexisting with and resisting Christian ascetic renunciation throughout the 380s–390s. Confidence: Widely Accepted (general pattern); Doc_01 §6.
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A world in which choosing renunciation invited real social judgment from one's own class — the mockery and suspicion of neighbors who read the choice as a betrayal of family and rank, not admiration.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Pressed directly on the boundary structure (Doc_07 §3) — made the renunciation-boundary genuinely costly to cross, not merely symbolically so.

**2A-2: The Jerome-Augustine correspondence and the wider Latin scriptural-authority debate**
- **Historical Event Layer:** Ongoing exchange (Ep. 112 = Augustine's Ep. 75, and related letters) contesting the Hebrew-based translation's abandonment of the Septuagint's received authority; concretely tested in the Oea "ivy/gourd" incident (Jonah 4:6). Confidence: Documented.
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A running argument, carried by letter across a real distance, with a respected fellow scholar who would not simply concede the point.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Directly shaped G1's conceptual structure (Doc_07 §1) — the *Hebraica veritas* commitment was articulated and refined specifically *in response to* this ongoing external contest.

**2A-4: The clerical-reputational precarity of Rome-based, papal-adjacent standing, with the 384–385 crisis as its sharpest instance**
- **Historical Event Layer:** An ongoing structural exposure — Jerome's Rome-period standing depended on the continued, personal favor of a single patron of a different kind than Paula's material funding: Pope Damasus I's clerical protection. This exposure became acute in 384: Blaesilla's death from ascetic excess generated public accusation against Jerome specifically; Damasus's own death that December removed the protection that had shielded him from it; resulting clerical hostility drove his August 385 departure. Confidence: Documented (the sequence of events); Inferential/Thin (a formal conciliar "synod" reviewing his conduct, per Doc_01 §9 Open Issue #4 — not asserted here).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* Favor held at one man's pleasure, and gone the moment that man was gone — a kind of standing this world's own central scholar had, and then, within a single year, did not.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** This force — not the pagan backlash of 2A-1, a different force entirely with its own genuinely pagan-aristocratic content (see 2A-1's own entry above) — is what shifted G3 (patronage) from a Rome-centered to a Bethlehem-centered arrangement (Doc_05 §4); what made the 385 relocation — and thus G4's intensification (Doc_04 §5) — necessary in the first place; and what created the specific vacancy in Rome that gave G5-Marcella's independent standing room to be exercised without competing against Jerome's own continued Roman presence. **Explicitly distinguished from Paula's material funding chain, which did not fail during this crisis and is what carried Jerome through relocation rather than into destitution**, consistent with Doc_07's own treatment of the two chains as separate.

**2A-3: Egyptian/Palestinian monastic movement as ongoing institutional template**
- **Historical Event Layer:** The Nitrian ascetic communities (Ep. 108's attested encounter with Bishop Isidore and an unnamed monastic multitude, per Doc_01 §6's corrected finding) provided an ongoing organizational and devotional model this world drew on throughout its span. Confidence: Widely Accepted for the general influence; Documented for the specific Nitria encounter.
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A pattern of desert withdrawal and communal discipline, witnessed firsthand on the founding journey, carried back and adapted — not copied wholesale, but genuinely formative of how this world understood what a monastic community should look like.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Shaped Organizational Ecology (Doc_05 §4) and the *monasterium duplex* pattern (Doc_06, Part B entry 1), while producing a distinctly Latin-literary variant rather than a copy.

### Cell 2B — Ongoing, Internal

**2B-1: Origenist controversy**
- **Historical Event Layer:** Theological dispute over Origen's teachings, c. 393–403, rupturing Jerome's friendship with Rufinus and his relationship with Bishop John of Jerusalem. Confidence: Documented (occurrence); Contested (relative doctrinal vs. personal/political weight, per Doc_06 entry 8's CT tag).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A former friend and fellow translator becoming the fiercest of opponents; a doctrinal inheritance this world had absorbed without quite noticing, suddenly requiring urgent, public renunciation.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Anchors G6 (Supporting gravity); reshapes G1 and G3 directly (Doc_04 Interaction Matrix; Pammachius and Marcella named addressees of Jerome's polemic).

**2B-2 (required — Transmission as ongoing internal force, per Transmission Specificity Principle): The *praefatio* and *epistula* apparatus as the community's own internal transmission mechanism**
- **Historical Event Layer:** Jerome's extensive corpus of translation prefaces and letters, produced continuously throughout the 382–420 span, functioning as the primary vehicle by which this world transmitted its scholarly and formation content both within itself (Rome to Bethlehem) and outward. Confidence: Documented (Doc_06 entries 1, 7, 13).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* Every finished piece of translation labor accompanied by its own defense, sent onward to the very household whose funding and whose questions had occasioned it.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** This is the internal transmission mechanism underlying G4 (Supporting gravity) and, critically, the vehicle by which this world's *content* (G1, the theological patterns of Doc_05 §9) actually reached its own dispersed community and, eventually, a wider readership — distinguished explicitly here as its own named force per the Transmission Specificity Principle, not folded into the general "letter-writing" gravity discussion already covered at Doc_04/Doc_06.

### Cell 3A — Ending-or-Transforming, External

**3A-1: The 410 Gothic sack of Rome**
- **Historical Event Layer:** Alaric's forces sack Rome, 24 August 410; Marcella dies from resulting injuries/deprivation, her Aventine household destroyed as a site of the wider network. Confidence: Widely Accepted (via Jerome's Ep. 127; no independent corroboration, per Doc_02 §1.4).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* Soldiers demanding treasure at the door of a household that had already given its treasure away years before.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Fractures G5-Marcella (Doc_04 §5); ends the Rome-based pole's independent center of gravity; the single clearest ending-force in this world's own bounded span (Doc_01 §1.3's final-decade-concentration finding).

**3A-2: The 416 Pelagian mob attack on the Bethlehem monastery** — classified here in Cell 3A (External), consistent with Doc_04 §5 and Doc_06 entry 9, both of which classify this force by actor-origin as "ending-adjacent external." This document defers to that already-established actor-origin criterion rather than introduce a second, competing classification basis for one entry alone.
- **Historical Event Layer:** Violent attack on the monastery, buildings burned, at least one death reported; Jerome's own account (letter to Riparius) notably vague on specifics. Confidence: Documented (occurrence); Inferential/Thin (details).
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* A theological argument that had, until then, stayed on paper, arriving instead as fire at the community's own door.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Anchors G6 alongside Origenism; an ending-adjacent external force pressing on this world's final years, absorbed by an already-established organizing pattern (Doc_04 §5's corrected G6 forces-notation) rather than generating a new one. This world's own prior doctrinal position-taking is part of why this particular external force found this particular target — a genuine, secondary observation retained here even though it does not override the actor-origin classification above.
- **Pelagianism CT-tag decision, closing the handoff Doc_06 entry 9 explicitly deferred here:** Doc_06 entry 9 itself frames this attack's causation with a doctrinal-vs-political ambiguity structurally similar to what earned Origenism its CT tag. This document considered that parallel directly and still declines the CT tag, for a reason specific to the difference between the two cases: Origenism's CT tag rests on a genuine, named, ongoing scholarly literature actively debating whether the controversy was doctrinal or political in substance (Doc_06 entry 8's Contest Type). No comparable body of scholarly contest exists in this build's own research for the Pelagian attack specifically — the "combination of theological conviction and local grievance" language in Doc_06 entry 9 names an *acknowledged uncertainty in this document's own account*, not an identified *external scholarly dispute* of the kind Constitution Article 26's CT framework requires. **Decision stands: no CT tag for Pelagianism**, on this narrower and now-complete basis — an acknowledged internal uncertainty is not automatically a Contested Tradition without an identifiable contest to point to.

### Cell 3B — Ending-or-Transforming, Internal

**3B-2 (required — Transmission as ending/transforming internal force, per Transmission Specificity Principle): The specific loss of the *praefatio*-defense mechanism (2B-2) upon the death of its sole practitioner**
- **Historical Event Layer:** this world's ongoing transmission apparatus (2B-2 — the *praefatio*/*epistula* practice of defending each translated book on its own terms, book by book, against live objection) had exactly one practitioner throughout this world's entire span: Jerome himself. No successor scholar, editor, or defender of the method is attested anywhere in this world's own record. When Jerome died (420), this was not merely "the makers could no longer witness reception" in a generic sense — it was the concrete cessation of the *specific mechanism* (2B-2) by which this world had continuously argued for and adjusted its own textual-authority claims in real time. Confidence: Documented that no successor is named in the surviving record (an absence, not an inference); Inferential/Thin for what specifically would have happened had a successor existed.
- **World's Own Experience Layer:** *[From-Within]* Every book so far had come with its own defense, argued fresh against whoever objected. After the last preface, no one remained who could write the next one in the same voice, answering the same way.
- **Formation Impact Layer:** Marks the transformation of G1 from a live, actively-defended, ongoing project (2A-2, 2B-2 — contested and re-argued in real time throughout this world's span) into a fixed, undefended text whose subsequent reception (Doc_01 §7's gradual, uneven displacement of the Vetus Latina) proceeds without the specific real-time-defense mechanism that had shaped it throughout — a concrete transmission-mechanism ending, not merely a generic "the makers died" observation, and the direct internal-transmission counterpart to 3A-1's external ending force.

## 2. Cross-Cell Connections

- **1A-2 → 2A-2 → 3B-2**: Jerome's arrival with scholarly credentials (initiating) generates the ongoing Augustine dispute (ongoing external), which in turn shapes how the translation project's eventual, uncontrolled transmission (ending internal) will be received — a single throughline from origin to unresolved legacy.
- **1B-1 → 3A-1, directly (not routed through 2B-2)**: the *praefatio*/*epistula* apparatus (2B-2) is a mechanism specific to Jerome's Bethlehem scholarly output, not to Marcella's own independent, in-person Roman standing (Doc_06 entry 11: her authority was exercised "in person," not primarily by letter). Marcella's initiative (1B-1) persists on its own terms, independent of 2B-2's apparatus, until it is terminated directly by an external ending force entirely outside this world's own control (the 410 sack, 3A-1) — internal initiative, externally severed, with no intervening transmission mechanism required to explain either its persistence or its end.
- **2A-4 (clerical-reputational precarity) and 2B-1 (Origenist controversy) are structurally parallel, not merely coincidental**: both show this world's authority as vulnerable specifically through *relationship and reputation* rather than office — one tested by the loss of a single patron's protection (2A-4), the other by the loss of a single friend's alliance (2B-1). The pattern is that this world's authority structure (G3) had no institutional buffer against either kind of relational rupture, external or internal.
- **2A-3 (Egyptian monastic template) shapes 3A-2's context**: the desert-withdrawal model this world adapted (2A-3) is precisely what a violent mob attack (3A-2) demonstrates was never fully insulating — even a community modeled on desert isolation remained reachable by organized local violence.
