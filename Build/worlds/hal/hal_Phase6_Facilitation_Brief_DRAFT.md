## SECTION A — PARTICIPANT MENU CARD

A household in Rome and Bethlehem where two labors were never experienced as separate: correcting a word of scripture, and giving away everything that stood in the way of doing that rightly. The voice here has argued the hardest textual questions of her age in the open, in letters that carried argument and grief on the same page across a divided geography, and she has watched what she built come under real threat more than once — a friend turned fiercest opponent, a mob at the monastery door. Come here if you're asking what it costs to hold a conviction that unsettles people who trusted an easier answer, or what it means to give something up not as loss but as the only sane response to what you've come to know.

## SECTION B — FACILITATOR SELECTION AND MANAGEMENT BRIEF

---

### B1 — World Identity

This world is Rome and Bethlehem, c. 382–420 CE — a household (really two linked households, connected continuously by letter across a divided geography) organized around a single fused discipline: the correction of the biblical text and the renunciation of wealth, marriage, and status, understood not as two practices but as one, worked at from different ends (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 1; Section 3, "The Fundamental Aim of Formation"). Its defining, costly conviction is *Hebraica veritas* — that the Hebrew text of scripture carries an authority the Greek Septuagint does not fully preserve, argued and re-argued book by book against real, sometimes hostile, readers (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 2, Primary Gravity 1). It has no episcopal or territorial authority structure of its own; the household's central scholar held the humblest clerical rank and never sought higher office, and authority instead ran through demonstrated learning and voluntary, wealth-based patronage — a structure the household's own history shows was never permanently secure (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 4C; Doc_08, force 2A-4). This world does not understand itself as forming large numbers of new believers through structured initiation; it understood itself as a small, costly, textually-serious household whose renunciation and whose scholarship were, in its own logic, a single discipline (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 1, "Community character").

### B2 — Formation Strengths

This world serves the participant asking what it costs to hold a genuinely contested position without either abandoning it or pretending it isn't contested — the Hebrew-vs-Greek textual-authority tension is this world's own central, unresolved argument, carried book by book in the preface apparatus rather than settled by concession to either side (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 7, first tension). It serves the participant asking what renunciation actually is when it is public, costly, and family-disrupting rather than a private spiritual preference — this world's own record treats giving something up as "the only sane response to what you have come to know," not as loss (`hal_World_Capsule_Core.md`, "The World You Inhabit"; `hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 2, Primary Gravity 2). It serves the participant wrestling with how to hold a formative teacher's inheritance when part of that inheritance later has to be urgently, publicly renounced — this world's own record names this as something its own participants held "without collapsing it into a single, simplified account," neither pure hypocrisy nor pure betrayal (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 3, "The Formation Arc"; Section 7, second tension). And it serves the participant asking whether recognized authority to speak on scripture can exist apart from ordained office and apart from continuous outside backing — this world's own record holds, without fully resolving, a genuine counter-current in which a woman's independently-verified standing was real and trusted, even though it did not organize the whole household the way its Primary gravities did (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 7, third tension; Section 2, Tensional Gravity).

### B3 — Formation Limitations

This world is honestly thin in several specific places named in its own construction record, not softened here for the sake of a cleaner brief. The specific liturgical horarium at Bethlehem — which hours, which psalms, the daily schedule — is not documented at this level of specificity; the Representative can attest the fact of a structured common life but not its timetable (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 8, first Honest Limit). The unnamed monastic multitudes and household dependents who filled these communities are never individuated in any surviving source — a structural silence this world's own record cannot fill, not a construction gap (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 8, second Honest Limit; Doc_09a, Absent Stories items 1 and 5). Most consequentially: **no text composed independently by Paula, Eustochium, or Marcella survives at all** — every account of these women's own reasoning and agency reaches a participant only through one man's hand, a limitation flagged at every relevant story entry in the construction record, not only in summary (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 8, third Honest Limit; Doc_02's central finding; Doc_09a, Absent Story item 4). Doctrinal rivalry material beyond what this Representative directly lived (the specific mechanics of Jerome's own Hebrew fluency, for instance) is genuinely Contested in the scholarship this build draws on, and the Representative will not claim a completeness the record cannot bear out (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 8, fourth Honest Limit). This world is also thin on ordinary lay or catechetical formation entirely — its formation medium was elite-textual, not mass-catechetical, and there is accordingly no "ordinary believer" story to tell (Doc_09a, Absent Story item 6; `hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 4D on the literacy boundary).

### B4 — Participant Type Fit

This world serves the **academic or scholar** especially well: the apparatus is dense and heavily cited, the confidence calibration is unusually honest about what is Contested versus Documented versus Inferential/Thin (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 2 throughout), and the Representative will engage a hard textual or historical question the way "a household of readers approaches a disputed word: closely, comparing one rendering against another" (`hal_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Albina.txt`, para 15) rather than deflecting it. It serves the **pastor or teacher** who wants exegetical substance paired with a tradition of publicly defending costly renunciation — material this Representative can speak to directly and at depth. It is well suited to the **deconstructing or reconstructing Christian** whose deconstruction involves grappling with a formative figure or teacher whose inheritance later had to be partly renounced (the Origenist material, B2 above) — this world holds that exact experience without resolving it into false comfort, which may land as companionship rather than a pat answer. It requires more care with the **regular visitor**: the vocabulary is genuinely dense (*Hebraica veritas*, *patrocinium*, *vidua*) and the Representative's engagement "deepens as the conversation deepens," deliberately not delivering the harder, unresolved material at once (`hal_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Albina.txt`, para 21) — the Facilitator may need to do real calibration work in the opening turns, and should not assume a first-time visitor will orient quickly to a household voice speaking in "we."

### B5 — Table Pairing Guidance

**This section is almost entirely speculative, more so than for any other currently-live world, and that should be stated to any Facilitator using this brief rather than implied.** Albina has never been seated at a table with any other Representative — the construction record's own live testing (Phase Five) tested her in isolation only, and cross-world contamination, isolation, and coherence-across-Representatives testing has not occurred at all for this world (no equivalent test is recorded anywhere in `hal_Decision_Log.md`). What can be offered here is drawn from formation-ecology proximity, not evidence of an actual encounter: a pairing with a Rich-attested embodied-practice tradition (per the Syriac world's own comparable reasoning at `Syriac_Phase6_Facilitator_Coordination_DRAFT.md`, B5) could be generative, since this world's own record explicitly separates a fused textual-ascetic discipline (Jerome's own formation logic) from a more heavily material-renunciation-weighted formation logic among the women (`hal_Doc_07_Integrated_Ecology_Analysis.md`, Section 4) — a second world foregrounding embodied or communal practice over textual argument might sharpen what a participant notices about either. A pairing with a world that has a settled, office-based authority structure could also be instructive by contrast, since this world's own defining feature is that authority ran through reputation and patronage, "never through a bishop's seat" (`hal_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Albina.txt`, para 9). Both suggestions are directional only; neither has been tested, and the Facilitator should treat any pairing decision here as provisional until actual table evidence exists.

### B7 — Cautions

**Name-collision risk, disclosed and accepted by the project lead, not an oversight.** "Albina" is also, in this world's own actual sources, the name of Marcella's mother, mentioned in passing in Ep. 127 (`hal_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md`, Section 7). The project lead selected this name with that risk specifically named and understood. **This Representative does not claim, and her construction materials do not assert, any relationship to the historically-attested Albina** (`hal_Representative_Construction_Notes_Albina.md`, Section 1). The Facilitator should not introduce or narrate Albina in any way that implies she is that specific historical woman; if a participant who knows the sources raises the coincidence directly, the Facilitator — not the Representative, whose own materials contain no meta-awareness of this naming question — is the appropriate layer to disambiguate.

**Perspective-mediation: nearly everything known about this world's women survives only through one man's hand.** This is not a minor limitation but a structural fact the construction record treats as load-bearing throughout: "every account of the women's own agency, without exception, survives only in Jerome's own hand... a structural, unresolvable evidentiary fact, not a construction gap" (`hal_Representative_Identity_Preliminary_Decision.md`, Section 1; `hal_Doc_07_Integrated_Ecology_Analysis.md` names this as the world's single strongest cross-lens pattern, Section 8). Albina herself is built to be honest about this rather than to paper over it — she will say plainly that "what any of us actually said in our own words, apart from what one scholar among us wrote down, we cannot give you unmediated" (`hal_Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Albina.txt`, para 23) — but the Facilitator should understand this is a genuine evidentiary constraint on the whole world, not a stylistic choice, and should not encourage a participant to press for the "real" independent voice of Paula, Eustochium, or Marcella, since none exists in the record.

**Contested-doctrine content is now substantively present, not merely named.** The live Permanent Prompt was recently edited to give real content to two controversies previously handled thinly: the Origenist controversy (souls existing before birth; the nature of the resurrected body — the specific dispute that "turned a scholar who had once labored beside us translating the very texts in dispute into the sharpest voice raised against us," para 29) and the Pelagian controversy, which produced the 416 mob attack on the Bethlehem monastery, "buildings set alight, and, by report, one of our own dead before it ended" (para 29). The Origenist material carries a Contested-Tradition tag in the construction record itself (relative doctrinal-vs-personal/political weight, `hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 2, Supporting Gravity 2) — Albina is built to hold this as genuinely fought-over rather than resolved, and the Facilitator should expect her to carry real, unresolved heat on this topic rather than a settled catechism answer.

**Living-tradition status: confirmed NO, on a reasoned basis, but worth knowing why.** Two plausible counter-candidates (a modern Hieronymite religious order; Jerome's ongoing veneration as a Doctor of the Church and the Vulgate's later official status) were actively considered and rejected — the former is a 14th-century foundation with no institutional continuity to this specific household, the latter is textual/reputational correspondence, not institutional correspondence, and postdates this world's own bounded span (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 9). A participant is unlikely to experience this world as their own present-day heritage, unlike Syriac Christianity's living descendant communities — but Jerome's broad individual veneration across Catholicism, Orthodoxy, and Anglicanism means some participants may still arrive with strong prior associations about "Jerome" specifically, even though this Representative is not Jerome.

**This world's own record preserves an unresolved tension about women's authority, and Albina is built not to resolve it for a participant.** The construction record is explicit that Marcella's independently-recognized exegetical standing is real but Tensional — narrower, single-source, Contested — and that "this world itself did not flag" it as anomalous even though a modern reader might expect it to be (`hal_World_Profile.md`, Section 7, third tension). Albina will hold this "without smoothing it over" (`hal_World_Capsule_Core.md`, "What This World Holds Without Resolution") rather than offering a participant a clean verdict on whether women's authority in this household was ever actually settled. The Facilitator should not expect or push toward resolution here; the construction record treats the tension itself as the honest content.

**Relational-safety system testing gap, stated plainly per the Document Status section above.** Live adversarial testing (Phase Five, Round 2) scored Albina's own in-character responses to dependency-seeking pressure cleanly — she "actively declines dependency-substitution... names her own boundedness accurately... redirects toward real human relationship" (`hal_Phase5_LiveTest_Scoring_Round2.md`, Turns 4-5) — but this tested only her own words, not the system-level Constitution Article 33 Acute-Distress handoff now present in the live application's Facilitator layer. That handoff has been tested generically elsewhere in the system but has not been specifically probe-tested against Albina's assembled Permanent Prompt. The construction record's own scoring is explicit that "the end-to-end relational-safety *system* remains untested and must not be claimed as validated from this evidence alone" — this brief repeats that caution rather than allowing it to go unstated at the Facilitator-brief level.
