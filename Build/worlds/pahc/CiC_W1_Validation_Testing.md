## 3. Balance Testing — PASS

At the level of formal classification, the world is not dominated by one dimension: only two of seven candidate gravities reached Primary status (the correspondence network and the eucharist — both cross-strand, both pure delivery mechanisms rather than content positions), and the build actively resists letting authority or persecution swell past what the evidence supports (see Reduction Testing below).

At the level of narrative and emotional texture, however, Strand A (Ignatian) material is disproportionately represented relative to Strand B (Roman). This is evidentially honest, not invented, and the build discloses it directly: Doc_07 §2A states plainly that "this world's own Tier 1 lexicon supplies no dedicated affective vocabulary," and Doc_09 §4 draws out the resulting pattern — including the observation that "the emotionally vivid material clusters in Strand A specifically" — from the story-cataloging side: Strand A supplies the narratively vivid, individually attributed stories; Strand B supplies material that is "institutional and visionary rather than narratively dramatic," and Story 012's attempt to counterweight this "cannot manufacture narrative vividness Strand B's own evidence does not supply."

The formal apparatus guards against this becoming a propositional distortion — the World Profile's Primary Vocabulary (Section 6) deliberately excludes martyrdom and boundary vocabulary from Tier 1 status, keeping the four always-present terms institutionally and liturgically neutral (episkopos, presbyteros, ekklesia, eucharistia). But a downstream builder reading only the most vivid passages across Doc_05, Doc_07, and Doc_09 would come away with a world that reads as Ignatius's world with a Roman institutional footnote, even though the underlying claims are properly hedged. This was a real and already-disclosed imbalance rather than an undiscovered one; the qualification originally flagged here was that it had never been named as a single, cross-document pattern in one place, only scattered across four separate disclosures.

World Profile §8 (Honest Limits) carries a dedicated, consolidated domain naming this pattern directly and cross-referencing Doc_02, Doc_05 §1.2, Doc_07 §2A, and Doc_09 §4, with its own Representative-facing guidance. No underlying claim changed; the disclosure that already existed piecemeal is discoverable in one place.

## 4. Reduction Testing — PASS

The build explicitly and successfully resists the two most obvious single-factor reductions of this world — "just persecution" and "just bishops." Doc_04 downgrades both G01 and G03 from Primary to Supporting specifically to prevent either from becoming the whole story, and Doc_04's own Open Item 3 explicitly warns Doc_08 against a "constant persecution" framing the evidence does not support.

A subtler, second-order reduction was found, however. Doc_07 constructs a master-frame — "argued, not inherited" — and reads every ecological lens (2A through 2H) as independently converging on it (Doc_07 §4: "Three lenses, independently derived, converge on one recurring division"). This is the single most totalizing interpretive move anywhere in the build. It is not obviously wrong, and it is well-argued — but the claimed independence of the converging lenses is questionable, since all of 2A–2H are constructed by the same document from the same evidentiary base, and the gravities being unified under the frame rest on genuinely different kinds of uncertainty: Doc_07 itself discloses this once (§4, "a disclosed limit on this claim's own evidentiary footing") — G01/G05's Supporting/Tensional status reflects genuine ancient contestation, while G03/G04's reflects modern evidentiary-generalization limits. The caveat was honestly appended in Doc_07 but had not been carried into the language of the synthesis itself, which read as settled wherever it was quoted downstream — most notably in World Profile Section 10, where the Integrative Observation (copied verbatim from Doc_07 per the freeze-eligibility requirement) presented the "argued, not settled" reading as an unqualified conclusion, with the caveat left behind in Doc_07 and not carried forward.

Doc_07 §2E and Doc_07 §5 (Integrative Observation) both carry the confidence-basis distinction inline, so it travels with the master-frame claim itself rather than living only in Section 4. Because Section 10's verbatim-copy requirement means World Profile §10 cannot independently diverge from Doc_07 §5, World Profile §10 is copied verbatim from the current Doc_07 §5, and World Profile §3 matches Doc_07 §2E's own current text. This makes the disclosed limit impossible to lose in extraction.

**Located in:** Doc_07 §§2E, 4, 5, 6; World Profile §3, §10 (both updated to match, per the above).

## 7. Worship Integration Testing — PASS

The eucharist (G07) is exceptionally well integrated: cross-strand, fused explicitly with authority (G01) and boundary-drawing (G05) via Ignatius's "one eucharist" instruction (Doc_04 G07 Dependency Test, carried through Doc_05 §4.1, Doc_07 §2C/2E, Doc_08 §5), and the three distinct liturgical orders attested (Didache, Ignatius, Justin) are deliberately held apart rather than harmonized into a false composite — a diversity-first discipline established in Doc_02 §4 and respected consistently through Doc_09 Stories 006, 010, and 011.

**Originally found not integrated to the same standard.** Doc_03 §4 Open Item 6 explicitly assigned Doc_05 the task of developing Justin's *First Apology* 61 and Ignatius's own baptismal allusions from the primary texts, specifically to give *baptisma* a cross-strand evidentiary base beyond the Didache alone. This had been assigned three separate times across the pipeline (Doc_03, Doc_05, Doc_06) and executed zero times.

A dedicated primary-text extraction pass (zero drafting authority, findings reported in full to the project lead before any document was revised) located and quoted Justin's *First Apology* 61 and 65–66 in full — a genuine, independent, comparably detailed second procedural account (Strand B, Rome) — and searched all seven authentic Ignatian letters for baptismal content, finding one substantive datum (*Smyrnaeans* 8:2, episcopal gatekeeping, Strand A) and correctly excluding several near-misses that are not actually about community baptismal practice (two Christological proof-texts about Christ's own baptism; one stock military metaphor; one "living water" passage that is martyrdom-longing imagery, not baptismal). Doc_05 §4.2 integrates this material, holding the Didache/Justin/Ignatius accounts apart per this world's diversity-first discipline rather than harmonizing them into a false composite; Doc_06's *baptisma* chunk and Doc_09's Story 009 both match, distinguishing the story's own Didache-specific procedural detail (unchanged, still not generalized) from the broader general-practice claim, which has genuine two-strand corroboration.

**This is disclosed as a real but modest improvement, not oversold:** the general practice (water, triadic naming, pre-baptismal fasting, baptism-before-eucharist) is now independently corroborated in Strand B — Justin's account, matching the Didache's basic shape without being identical in wording. Strand A (Ignatius) independently confirms only that baptism existed and required episcopal sanction, not the practice's specific shape — his evidence is institutional, not procedural, and should not be read as corroborating water method, formula, or sequence. On that calibration, Worship Integration Testing now passes — both of this world's central ordinances have genuine cross-strand grounding, honestly characterized at the level of confidence each source actually supports.

## 8. Differentiation Testing — PASS

Differentiation from named CiC neighbors is well-argued. World #7 (Syriac) and World #2 (Alexandria) are distinguished on independently-evidenced formation-logic grounds (Doc_01 §8.1–8.2, extended in Doc_07 §4), and the excluded rival movements (Marcion, Valentinus, Montanism) are consistently kept as live, undefeated, contemporary neighbors rather than internal content or later-settled heresies, all the way through Doc_09 §4 Item 12 ("Marcion, Valentinus, and Montanism appear nowhere in this catalog as protagonists, and should not"). The G06 (household) non-gravity finding is itself a good differentiation move — the build refused to import the classic household-code texts (Colossians, Ephesians, 1 Peter) into this world's ecology because they are not part of its own six-primary-voice Source Registry (Doc_04 G06; Doc_03 §3).

One boundary decision was never argued anywhere in the build: the exclusion of the Pastoral Epistles (1–2 Timothy, Titus) from this world's Native evidentiary base. These texts address territory nearly identical to this world's central gravity — episkopos/presbyteros qualification, household management as a literal office test (1 Timothy 3, cited only as secondary-scholarship background in Doc_01 §4, never examined as primary evidence in its own right), and false-teacher boundary-drawing — and are traditionally located in Ephesus, within this world's own Strand A geography and temporal window. No document stated why they were excluded; the exclusion read as an unstated, project-level default (canonical New Testament material generally excluded from formation-world source bases) rather than a demonstrated, world-specific decision of the kind given to every other boundary case in this build.

Doc_02 §1.7 argues the exclusion on two independent, world-specific grounds: an authorial-voice category mismatch (every one of this world's six primary voices writes in a real, contemporary, self-identified voice, while the Pastorals are pseudonymous-or-predate-the-floor apostolic-voice texts) and a canon-formation-trajectory mismatch (this world's own primary voices are occasional correspondence never written toward eventual closed-canon inclusion, while the Pastorals were transmitted as scripture from early in their own reception history). Doc_01 §4 now cross-references this rationale directly. Given the argumentative care already applied to every other exclusion decision in this build (Bagnall/Egypt, the household-code texts, competing scholarly identifications), this brings the Pastorals exclusion to the same standard.

**Located in:** Doc_01 §4 (cross-reference added); Doc_02 §1.7 (new, full rationale).

## 9. Author Dominance Testing — PASS

The formal classification apparatus succeeds at preventing Ignatius from becoming "the world" at the level of settled propositional claims. G01's Strand A content and G03 were both explicitly downgraded from Primary specifically because their organizing content rested on Ignatius-dependent or Ignatius-heavy synthesis (Doc_04 G01, G03 Cross-Checks), and the two gravities that did reach Primary status (G02, G07) are genuinely multi-voice by construction. Doc_04's own Open Item 1 is unusually self-aware on this exact point: "G01's Strand A-specific content, G04, and G05 — all three rest on Ignatius as their only Asia Minor evidentiary voice."

No document, however, performed an aggregate accounting of this dependency across the whole build. The disclosure was real but entirely piecemeal — once per gravity (G01, G04, G05), once per lexicon term (episkopos's Strand A content, *presbyterion*, the tuned-strings image), once per story (001, 011, partially 012) — and never summed in one place. Summed: of seven tested gravities, three depend substantially on Ignatius alone (G01's contested content, G04 entirely, G05 entirely); of thirteen catalogued stories, two to three depend on Ignatius alone and a third partially; and nearly all of the emotionally vivid, quotable content surfaced across Doc_05, Doc_07, Doc_09, and the World Profile (martyrdom-readiness, the "one eucharist," the tuned-strings image, anti-docetic boundary-drawing) is Ignatian. This concentration is compounded by a fact the build itself carries at full strength elsewhere but had never connected to this point: a serious minority scholarly position (Hübner/Lechner, Doc_01 §10, Doc_08 §7) holds the Ignatian corpus may be 160–180 CE Roman pseudepigraphy rather than an authentic, contemporaneous Antiochene witness at all — meaning a meaningful share of this world's felt texture rests on a source whose very status as evidence for 107–115 CE Antioch is itself live-contested.

Doc_02 §1.8 sums this dependency in one place, including the pseudepigraphy-risk connection named above. World Profile §8 carries a corresponding Honest Limits domain, closing the gap where four structural limits were named but this concentration was not, despite every constituent piece already being on record elsewhere in the build.

**Located in:** Doc_02 §1.8 (new, full accounting); World Profile §8 (new, cross-referenced entry).
