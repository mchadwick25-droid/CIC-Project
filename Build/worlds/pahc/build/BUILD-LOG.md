## Source-form fragment re-author — 2026-09-25

`pahc.quote.first-concerning-the-cup`: the sentence-completeness check (P3
Decision-Log Entry 29) flagged "Now about the Thanksgiving - the
Eucharist." as a fragment made by splitting one source sentence ("Now
concerning the Thanksgiving (Eucharist), thus give thanks."). Under Mark's
R44 ruling of 2026-09-24, a split that leaves a clause without its own
subject and verb is never acceptable, so the heading and its instruction
are rejoined as one imperative sentence: "Now, about the Thanksgiving -
the Eucharist - give thanks this way." Only `modern_rendering` changed;
every other field is byte-identical. Authored by Opus. Reads "translation"
on two consecutive runs of Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 4.6. P3 Decision-Log Entry
37 carries the fleet-wide record of this pass.
