## Findings

**H (high) — none.**

**M (medium) — one, found and fixed directly during this same pass, logged rather than silently corrected:**

- M1. §2 A1 originally stated this world's window sits "over 1,350 years after Chalcedon and over 1,300 after Nicaea/Constantinople" — arithmetically backwards (Chalcedon, 451, is *later* than Constantinople, 381, so the gap to Chalcedon must be the *smaller* number, not the larger one). Corrected in the same drafting pass to the precise, checked figures: "1,357 years after the Council of Constantinople (381) and 1,287 years after Chalcedon (451)." This does not change the test's own outcome (A2 is still plainly not applicable at either figure) — a cosmetic/arithmetic fix, not a substantive one, applied directly per `cic-build-cycle`'s own rule rather than treated as a finding requiring a new round.

**L (low) — three:**

- L1. The Aldersgate quotation ("one was reading Luther's preface to the Epistle to the Romans") was checked word-for-word against `cic/texts/wesley-j_journal-v1_curnock1909.txt` lines 26957–26958. Exact match, including the comma placement. Confirmed clean.
- L2. §2 A3.2 states Wesley traveled to Herrnhut "in August–September 1738" and attributes this to Widely Accepted historical record, explicitly flagged as not independently checked against a primary vendored source. This is the correct confidence level — no Herrnhut travel account is vendored in this pass's own corpus (Vol. I of the Curnock Journal ends before this trip is narrated at length; the trip itself is covered in Vol. I per most editions, but this pass did not locate and re-verify the passage against the vendored file before this review's own deadline). **Recommendation, not a blocking finding:** Doc_02 should attempt to locate and cite the actual Vol. I passage narrating the Herrnhut visit directly, since Vol. I (which covers 1738) is already vendored and may well contain it — a real, low-cost improvement Doc_02 can make that this round does not require before Step 0 clears.
- L3. §3 B1's own "genuine host-verification correction" paragraph is a valuable disclosure but is written as backward-looking narration of a mid-session correction rather than a forward-looking sourcing claim. This is process narration adjacent to the CLAUDE.md rule against embedding process commentary in canonical documents — however, it is retained here (not moved to `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` alone) because it directly documents *how a specific sourcing claim was verified*, which is itself the sourcing claim's own evidentiary basis, not mere status narration. Judgment call, disclosed; not treated as requiring removal.
