# Source Registry — The Methodist Revival

Companion to `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`, built to `Build/reference/L3B-World-Build-Methodology/Source_Registry_Template.md`. The world's corpus-map bucket (`cic/corpus-map/the-methodist-revival.yaml`) holds 12 work-rows across seven vendored files. This Registry groups them by source file and genre where their Type, Confidence, Boundary Status and Licensed-For treatment is uniform, and gives a row of its own to any work with a distinct disposition. Every one of the 12 rows is covered by a row below. Rows 9 to 19 are leads not yet vendored; rows 20 to 22 are secondary scholarship; row 25 is a dossier lead that is not used.

**Living document: append-only.** A row is never renumbered or deleted; a later-discredited source drops to Confidence E and is marked removed-from-use, and the row stays. Doc_02 does not name a source in support of a specific claim unless that source has a corresponding row here.

**Type:** P (Primary) / S (Secondary) / M (Material/External) / L (Period Lexicon).
**Confidence** (citation reliability, distinct from Doc_02's five-level narrative-confidence vocabulary), one letter per row: A (the Licensed-For content was read and verified at the source by a structure marker) / B (locus named, not re-checked line by line, or a whole-volume row not itemized by entry) / C (tied to a real author and work, no specific locus) / D (tradition or genre level only) / E (no traceable source; not acquired, not a resting tier, must be re-grounded before use).
**Boundary Status:** Native / Excluded, judged by subject, tradition and target against Doc_01's boundary (1738–1815, the Methodist Revival in Britain and America), never by the publication date of a modern edition. **Pending** is used only for row 13, where Doc_01 §9 escalates the boundary question to the project lead and this table does not pre-judge the ruling. Row 18 is marked era-edge. The Exclusion Reason column is blank for every row not Excluded; no row is Excluded.
**Voice** is stated at the start of each Licensed For cell: own (this world's own voice) / opponent (a rival or contemporary movement's own voice, quoted for contrast) / editorial (a later editor's or scholar's voice, not this world's own) / institutional (the Conference's own voice).

Corpus figures, counted two ways. The seven vendored files hold 1,005,750 words by `wc -w` under the `POSIX` locale and 1,013,823 under `C.UTF-8`; a Python 3 whitespace split gives the `C.UTF-8` figure. The counts include Gutenberg and Internet Archive header text and Curnock's editorial apparatus.

**Status:** Not approved to proceed. See `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md` section 10.
