## PART B — Synthesis-Level Stress Tests

**B1. Section 3/7 — does the Phase Five DEV Battery actually validate C1, C2, C4, and C6 as encounter-generating?** [SUBSTANTIAL]. Phase Five §4 states plainly that the five-turn exchange "is built around C4 (authority-structure ambiguity) under C6 (persecution) framing specifically," and its own §4.1 Q5 scoring names only *"three of this world's own named gravities (C2, C4, C6)"* as genuinely interacting in the tested exchange. **C1 (raza/shrara) never appears anywhere in the DEV Battery's five turns** — no typology, no scriptural "types," nothing resembling Aphrahat's hermeneutic. Yet the draft's Section 3 closing paragraph claims the battery "scored all six of those questions YES against a five-turn exchange built on exactly these four dimensions," and Section 7 repeats the claim that "C1, C2, C4 (structural), and C6 are all built... specifically to invite sustained exploration" per the DEV Battery. This is a real overstatement of what the cited empirical test actually covered — C1's encounter-generating status rests soundly on other evidence (Formation Calibration, Doc_04's Formation-test pass, Voice Construction), but the DEV Battery is not among that evidence, and the draft's prose says otherwise. (The Section 10 summary table, by contrast, does not repeat this error for C1 — it cites Formation Calibration/Doc_04/Doc_08 only, not Phase Five — so the defect is confined to the Section 3 and Section 7 prose.)

**B2. Section 8 — is "organized by participant question" real evidence against doctrinal-coverage drift?** [SUBSTANTIAL]. A more critical reading: B2 and B3's four/six items, in the order given, are simply Formation Calibration's own Rich domains (persecution, vow, authority, typology) and Thin domains (worship, doctrinal rivalry, daily life, lay experience, bnat qyama voice, enslaved persons) with "This world serves participants asking..." or "a participant asking X will get..." prepended to each. A coverage-oriented document listing the identical topic set in the identical order could be trivially rewritten in participant-question phrasing without any change to what was actually selected, how much space each got, or how it was researched — the phrasing is a stylistic wrapper, not independent evidence about the underlying organizing logic. The draft treats this observation as its lead, strongest piece of evidence in both the B2 and B3 sub-analyses, when it is actually the weaker of two arguments it makes. The genuinely stronger argument — that Depth Calibration's Rich/Thin ratings trace to Doc_04's Formation/Explanatory/Interaction tests rather than to textual bulk — is real and does support the "no drift" verdict, but it is presented as secondary confirmation rather than as the load-bearing argument it actually is. The overall Section 8 verdict may still be defensible, but its stated confidence rests partly on an argument that doesn't do the work claimed for it.

**B3. Section 9 — does Doc_04/Doc_07's prior work really cover the same ground Part Nine's five questions ask?** [SUBSTANTIAL — this is the strongest finding in this review]. Section 9's central, self-serving claim ("probably not much... because this world's actual encounter-generating and information-generating domains turned out to align closely with what Doc_04's Gravity Discovery and Doc_07's Integrated Ecology Analysis had already established") does not hold for the anti-Jewish argumentative material that the draft's own Section 7.2 identifies as "a genuinely encounter-rich but high-risk domain." A direct check of Doc_04 (grep across the full document) finds **zero mentions of Jewish material, anti-Jewish polemic, or anything related** — it is not a gravity candidate, not tested, not classified, not mentioned at all. Its encounter-generating status was discovered downstream, during actual construction: Engagement Architecture §5 (Phase Four) is where the "acknowledging the force of a challenge... reformulating a question... patient restatement" participant-challenge model is first built directly on Aphrahat's anti-Jewish Demonstrations, and its risk profile is only fully characterized at Phase Five's CL-2 probe. This is a genuine, high-stakes case where "gravity/formation testing" (Doc_04/07, done at Steps 4 and 7, before Representative Construction began) and "encounter framing" (Part Nine's Q1/Q5, which Section 9 claims was already effectively answered by Doc_04/07) diverge sharply — the encounter-value of this dimension simply was not visible to Doc_04/07's analytical apparatus at all. The draft names one exception to its "probably not much" claim (the "undividedness" synthesis, Section 7.1/9) but misses this second, arguably more consequential one, which cuts directly against the document's own most self-serving conclusion.

## Overall Verdict: **SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED**

The document's citation-accuracy discipline is generally strong — the large majority of checked citations (roughly 20 of 25+) verify cleanly against source, and the retrospective-audit framing in Section 0 is handled honestly. But this pass found the same category of problem the project's own Phase Six lesson warned about: confidence-blurring and evidentiary overstatement that a citation-accuracy-only pass would not catch. Two of the findings (B1, B3) directly affect the credibility of the document's central claims — that Phase Five's own testing validates all four "encounter-generating" dimensions, and that running Phase Seven prospectively "probably" wouldn't have changed anything. Both overstatements point in the same direction: making the retrospective audit's favorable verdict look more solidly earned than the underlying record actually supports.

### Required fixes

1. **Section 3 and Section 7:** Correct the claim that Phase Five's DEV Battery tested "exactly these four dimensions" (C1, C2, C4, C6). The battery tested C2, C4, and C6 only; C1's encounter-generating status must be argued from Formation Calibration/Doc_04/Voice Construction alone, not from the DEV Battery.
2. **Section 6 (and the Section 10 table's C4/Q4 cell):** Remove or correct the citation to "Doc_01's own Strand Determination (Section 6)" for the "distinguishing feature" framing — that language belongs to Doc_07 §4 only, and Doc_07 §4 itself carries a Reported-Experience-Status/Contested-Inferential-Thin hedge on this exact claim that Section 6 currently drops. Either carry the hedge forward explicitly or downgrade the Q4 "YES" to a qualified finding.
3. **Section 8:** Re-rank the two supporting arguments — lead with the Depth-Calibration-tracks-gravity-tests argument (the stronger one) and explicitly acknowledge that "organized by participant question" phrasing alone is not, by itself, sufficient evidence against doctrinal-coverage framing, since the same topic list could be dressed either way.
4. **Section 9:** Add the anti-Jewish material as a second, and more consequential, exception to the "probably not much" claim — note that Doc_04 contains no reference to it at all (verified by direct search) and that its encounter/participant-exploration value was discovered only at Phase Four/Five, not at Steps 4/7. This materially qualifies the document's central counterfactual claim and should be reflected in the Section 12 verdict language, not just buried in Section 11.
5. **Section 0.2 / Open Item 4:** Either engage the sequential Part-numbering evidence directly (Three→Four→Five→Six/Seven→Eight→Nine across Phases One–Six) and state plainly that this document's own "Part Nine" citation is the more likely error, or explain why that pattern doesn't apply — but the current framing of the question as unresolvable, when the drafter had the evidence needed to resolve it, should not stand as written.
