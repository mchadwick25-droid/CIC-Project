# Rendering Review: syr quote records, round 2 (12 records)

- **Reviewer:** Opus 5.5 (independent; did not draft, render or review these records before)
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Method

This is a targeted recheck of the twelve renderings that failed in round 1 (`Rendering_Review_Syr_Batch1_2026-11-03.md` and `Rendering_Review_Syr_Batch2_2026-11-03.md`). For each record, the round-1 finding is read first. Then the edition text is read at its span in `cic/texts/`, along with the record's `text`, its new `modern_rendering` (from the working-tree diff), its `use_note` and its `modern_lens_note`. The check covers the round-1 finding and the full bar: every clause rendered, nothing added, editors' supplements and glosses kept marked where they add meaning, one thought per sentence, no sentence past about 25 words, no misleading modern word, hedges, tense, reported-speech frames and contrasts kept, plain English. Sentence lengths are counted by script and confirmed by eye. Dashes and ellipses count as no word. Where a note changed, the change is checked against the edition too.
