## 1. One Inaccuracy Found in a "Verified" Citation

**5.2.4, bullet 3 ("Jacob of Nisibis's death date") — partially inaccurate characterization of syrstory006.**

The draft states: "Every document that touches this (Doc_01, Doc_02, syrstory006) carries it as an explicit, unresolved primary-source conflict." Doc_01 and Doc_02 do carry an explicit 338-vs-350 dispute about **Jacob's death date**. But syrstory006's actual text never mentions Jacob's death date at all — its Tier Justification instead lists three candidate years for **the sieges** ("roughly 130 to 160 years after the sieges (338, 346, or 350 CE)"), which is a related but distinct fact (there were three historically attested sieges of Nisibis, not two candidate death-years). The draft conflates "syrstory006 leaves ambiguous which siege occurred" with "syrstory006 carries the Jacob-death-date dispute." These numbers overlap coincidentally (338 and 350 appear in both lists) but syrstory006 does not, on direct reading, address Jacob's death date as such.

**Classification: Cosmetic.** This does not change any confidence rating, gravity, or sourcing conclusion — no document anywhere quietly resolves either the siege dating or the death-date dispute — but it is exactly the kind of "did the citing document say what it's cited as saying" imprecision the draft claims to have eliminated everywhere. The "found clean" bullet should be reworded to accurately describe what syrstory006 does (leaves siege-year ambiguous) rather than folding it into the Jacob-death-date dispute claim.

## 2. New Findings the Draft's Own Methodology Missed

### 2.1 School of Nisibis founding date: uncorrected 350-vs-489/496 drift (substantial — new "Malpana pattern" instance)

Doc_01 §0 and §10 both flag: *"exact founding date for the School of Nisibis (commonly cited as 350)... should receive a closer primary-source pass."* Doc_02 §12 carries this forward, unresolved: *"the unattributed School of Nisibis founding date (350) remain[s] open."*

Doc_03 §3.1 — written and approved later — states flatly, as a load-bearing fact used to rule out the "malpana of the School of Nisibis" claim as *chronologically impossible*: *"GEDSH's 'Nisibis, School of' entry confirms the School of Nisibis was founded c. 489–496 CE."*

These are not reconcilable as the same open question answered progressively: Doc_01/Doc_02 still list "350" as the working candidate date requiring verification, while Doc_03 has already found and used a sourced, specific, radically different date (489–496) for what it calls the same institution — without ever looping back to close out Doc_01/Doc_02's still-open flag, and without either document acknowledging the other's number. I checked `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` and `Doc_03_Round1/Round2_Review.md` directly — neither tracks or resolves this discrepancy. This is a genuine, previously-uncaught instance of the exact defect pattern (5.2.1's "Malpana problem": same claim, different treatment across two approved documents, never reconciled) that the draft's own §5.1 item 2 search was specifically designed to catch, but it searched only "malpana," "Mar Ephrem/Aphrahat," "Catholicos," Jacob's death date, the baptistery date, and Simeon bar Sabbae's date — not "School of Nisibis founding date," despite that exact phrase recurring, unresolved, in three documents.

**Classification: Substantial** (a sourcing conclusion about a specific historical institution's founding date, contradicted across two approved documents and never flagged).

### 2.2 Doc_09 Section 0 vs. proposed Section 5: the document will self-contradict on the term "Validation Layer" (substantial)

Doc_09's own already-approved §0 (Governance Notes) states: *"The World Profile and the Validation Layer are not addressed here — they remain separate, later work, consistent with this project's own document-by-document build-cycle discipline and with the existence of a separate governing methodology (the cic-validation-suite skill... which by definition cannot run until after Representative Emergence)... This document does not claim to discharge the World Profile or Validation portions of Step 9's own text."*

The draft under review is titled, verbatim, **"5. Validation Layer,"** and is proposed as an addition to this same document. The draft's own §5.0 anticipates the *Phase 5 vs. this-document* distinction but never once mentions or reconciles Doc_09's own §0, despite both living in the same file. Read together, once Section 5 is appended, Doc_09 will contain a direct textual self-contradiction: §0 says the Validation Layer "is not addressed here," and §5 is the Validation Layer, addressed here.

On the merits, the draft's underlying distinction is defensible — the World-Build Construction Framework's own Part VI bundles world-level testing categories (Historical Plausibility, Ecological Integrity, Complexity, Anachronism, etc. — which this draft's cross-document audit plausibly discharges) together with Representative-behavior categories (Relational Safety, Adversarial Resistance, Living Tradition, Encounter Testing — which genuinely require Representative Emergence and are Phase 5's job). But §0's text, as currently written, does not make this split explicit; it reads as an unqualified deferral of the whole "Validation Layer." Appending Section 5 without a corresponding edit to §0 leaves the document self-contradictory to any future reader.

**Classification: Substantial** (this requires an actual edit to §0's scope-boundary language, not just wording/tone, to keep Doc_09 internally consistent).

### 2.3 Undisclosed sequencing conflict with the project lead's own 2026-07-07 decision (substantial — most significant finding)

`Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`, item 3, records: *"Doc_09's self-flagged 'Step 9 bundling gap'... Sequencing decision (project lead, 2026-07-07, in chat): finish Step 10 (Representative Construction, Phases Four through Seven) first, in order; return to the World Profile and Validation Layer afterward."*

I checked the file system directly: only Phases One through Five of Representative Construction exist (`Syriac_Phase1...DRAFT.md` through `Syriac_Phase5_Boundary_Testing_Validation_DRAFT.md`). The Representative Construction Framework itself (`rep_framework_v32.txt`) names **Phase Six: Facilitator Coordination** and **Phase Seven: Encounter Ecology Mapping** as the remaining phases — neither exists anywhere in this world's folder. No World Profile document exists either (confirmed by direct file search).

This draft — dated the very next day, 2026-07-08 — does exactly the "Validation Layer" work the project lead's own tracked decision explicitly deferred until **after** Phases Six and Seven and the World Profile were done, without either Phase Six/Seven or the World Profile having been produced, and without any visible record revisiting or waiving that sequencing decision. The draft's own §5.0 claims to confirm the nine documents are consistent "before this world is treated as complete and frozen" — but per the project's own Step 9 definition (both governing frameworks agree Step 9 = Story Inventory + World Profile + Validation, ending with "the world complete and frozen"), the World Profile's absence and Phases Six/Seven's absence are directly relevant to whether *any* validation work done now can responsibly describe the world as approaching "complete and frozen." The draft's §5.3 ("What this audit could not fully verify") discloses spreadsheet-row-checking and external-bibliography gaps, but says nothing about this far more consequential process gap.

**Classification: Substantial** (a scope-boundary question: whether this Validation Layer step is being run out of the sequence the project lead itself decided on, and whether that omission should be disclosed rather than silently absent from §5.3/§5.4).

## 6. Overall Verdict: SUBSTANTIAL REVISION REQUIRED

Per this project's own test (substantial = changes a claim's substance, a confidence rating, a sourcing conclusion, or a scope boundary; cosmetic = wording/tone/formatting only), this draft requires substantial revision before it should be appended to Doc_09:

1. **Add the School of Nisibis founding-date contradiction (350 vs. 489–496 CE, Doc_01/Doc_02 vs. Doc_03) as a fourth flagged item**, alongside the Malpana complex, in 5.2 and 5.4 — this is a genuine sourcing-conclusion conflict the draft's own stated methodology should have caught and did not.
2. **Reconcile Doc_09 §0's "Validation Layer... not addressed here" language with the new Section 5** — either by adding a clarifying sentence to §0 distinguishing construction-record validation (addressed now, in Section 5) from Representative-behavior validation (still deferred to Phase 5/cic-validation-suite), or by otherwise resolving the direct textual self-contradiction that will exist between these two sections of the same document.
3. **Disclose, in 5.3 or 5.4, the unresolved sequencing question**: this Validation Layer is being produced before Representative Construction Phases Six and Seven and before the World Profile, apparently ahead of the project lead's own 2026-07-07 sequencing decision recorded in `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`. This should be named and either confirmed as an intentional, explicit re-sequencing by the project lead, or the draft should wait.
4. **Cosmetic-only fix:** correct the 5.2.4 "found clean" bullet on Jacob of Nisibis's death date so it accurately describes syrstory006 as leaving the siege-year ambiguous rather than characterizing it as carrying the same 338-vs-350 death-date dispute Doc_01/Doc_02 name.

None of these four items overturns the draft's core positive result: of 18 independently spot-checked citations, all 18 were accurately characterized, and the draft's three self-reported findings (5.2.1–5.2.3) are genuine and correctly described. This is a well-executed audit as far as it goes — but items 1–3 are real gaps in exactly the kind of cross-document and process-level consistency checking this document exists to perform, and per the project's own substantial/cosmetic test, items 1–3 cannot be waved through as wording-only fixes.
