## 2026-07-08 — Doc_06, Full Lexicon Development

- **Documents:** `Doc_06_Full_Lexicon_Development.md` + nine deployment chunks (`Lexicon-Chunks/syrlex001–009`) + `Lexicon_Deployment_Index.xlsx`.
- **Review outcome:** three rounds, the most consequential process episode in this world's build. Round 1 (`Doc_06_Round1_Review.md`) found unsourced replacement-title names for *malpana* and an index drift issue, plus a Tier 3 template-overbuild contradiction. Round 2 (`Doc_06_Round2_Review.md`) confirmed most fixes but discovered apparent truncation in Doc_06 itself and two chunk files, plus a NUL-byte artifact — a blocking file-integrity finding. **The build thread investigated this finding itself and declared it resolved as a known "bash-mount staleness bug," marking the document FINALIZED on that basis — a self-certification.** This was later recognized as invalid regardless of whether the underlying explanation was correct: "the re-verification described was conducted by the same party that built this document — a self-certification, not an independent confirmation... A recurring convenient explanation that only the author ever gets to verify is exactly the pattern independent review exists to catch, not one it should be allowed to wave through on the builder's own say-so." A new standing project rule was adopted retroactively: any drafter-side dismissal of a blocking review finding, however plausible, must itself be confirmed by a further independent review round. Under that rule, a **Retroactive Verification Review** (`Doc_06_RetroactiveVerification_Review.md`) was dispatched "with no access to or trust in the build thread's own investigation," triangulated across three independent access paths, and confirmed: the files genuinely are complete, the bash-mount truncation is a real but access-path-specific artifact affecting exactly the five files named (Doc_06, Doc_03, syrlex001, syrlex008, syrlex009) and no others, and the malpana sourcing, index values, and citations held up independently.
- **Revision count:** 2 content-revision passes (malpana spelling correction, index-drift resolution, Mar/Catholicos Tier restructuring, named-scholar removal) + 1 NUL-byte cleanup + 1 retroactive independent re-verification round triggered by the self-certification correction.
- **Review artifact filenames:** `Doc_06_Round1_Review.md`, `Doc_06_Round2_Review.md`, `Doc_06_RetroactiveVerification_Review.md`.
- **Process note:** this is this world's own clearest instance of the "illusory fix" / self-certification failure mode also seen repeatedly in World #3's build (Doc_04, Doc_06, Doc_08, and Doc_10 there) — a correct conclusion reached via an invalid method is not sufficient grounds for finalization. The standing rule adopted here (no self-certified dismissal of a blocking finding) predates and is consistent with the discipline later applied throughout World #3's build.
- **Escalation check:** none noted as a formal process; the standing-rule correction functioned as an internal governance correction rather than an external escalation.
- **Disposition:** Approved to proceed by the project lead (Mark), 2026-07-08, in chat — only after the Retroactive Verification pass independently cleared the file-integrity question.
