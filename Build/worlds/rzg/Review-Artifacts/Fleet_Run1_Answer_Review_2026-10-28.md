# rzg fleet graded run 1: answer review

## Verdict

**Revise.**

Theophilus is often clear and his own. At his best he says plainly what the record does not hold (f5-i, f6-p, f2-p) and refuses a false premise without fuss (f3-e, f6-e). Every placed quote matches its record word for word.

But 16 of 28 answers fail. Nine fail on content. Seven more fail only on the per-turn reading-level gate. The automatic checks passed all 28.

Three causes account for most of the content failures:

- **The records do not answer the fleet's questions in several cells.** This world tagged its records to its own build questions, not to the fleet canon. So for original sin, being born again, apostolic warrant, outsiders' fate, the Trinity and "a church today", the voice has no record. It fills the gap from general knowledge, and three times it gets it wrong. Yet the vendored corpus answers most of these directly.
- **The voice is not told what is unknown, or what is known.** With no witness and no honest limit for those cells, it either invents (f4-t) or claims an absence that the world's own sources contradict (f6-t).
- **Engine cuts and leaks.** Quotes whose lead-in says "our founder" instead of a name are removed with their sentence (five quotes in three answers). Two raw tags reach the participant.

The same as `Build/worlds/lpc/Review-Artifacts/Run3_Access_And_Craft_Review_2026-10-28.md` and `Build/worlds/lpc/build/lpc_Probe_Results.md`.
