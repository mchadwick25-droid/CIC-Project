## Verdict: **Substantial revision required**

The great majority of this document is accurate, disciplined, and carefully cross-checked: Section 2's gravity classifications, confidences, and Article 21 cross-strand characterizations match Doc_04 §4/§5 exactly; Section 5's Forces Summary correctly restates eleven of Doc_08's nineteen forces with no recurrence of this world's own twice-caught G1/Cell-1B placement error; Section 6's vocabulary tags match every lexicon chunk's own front matter exactly; and Section 10's Integrative Observation is a genuine, verified, character-for-character verbatim copy of Doc_07 §6. This is real, careful synthesis work in most of its parts.

But the review found **two High-severity findings that are exactly the citation-fidelity failure mode this world's build has been caught on at every prior document from Doc_04 through Doc_09** — one a claim with a fabricated citation in the document's own opening substantive line (Section 1), the other a misattributed and silently-altered quotation underpinning the Method Note's own central governance argument for *not* re-escalating a portfolio-level question. The second finding also bears directly on this review's own most novel and highest-stakes question: whether the Method Note's decision to apply Donatism's precedent rather than escalate fresh is sound. It is **not soundly executed as written**, even though the underlying substantive conclusion (cover all nine Doc_07 lenses) is very likely correct on independent grounds. Three further Medium findings and three Low findings round out the review.

## Findings

### Finding 1 — HIGH — Section 1's claim that "Zwingli's own circle called its own project simply preaching and defending 'the gospel'" is fabricated, with a citation to content that does not exist in Doc_01

**Checked:** Section 1, World Identity: *"No single self-name is attested distinct from 'the Reformed' or 'the gospel' — Zwingli's own circle called its own project simply preaching and defending 'the gospel' (Doc_01 §1, §2)..."*

**Found:** The word "gospel" does not appear anywhere in Doc_01, nor anywhere in Doc_02 through Doc_09 (confirmed by direct search of every `Doc_0*.md` file in this world's build folder — zero occurrences). Doc_01 §1 and §2 discuss World Name, Short Description, Core Identity, Distinctive Contribution, and Historical Boundaries, and name no self-naming convention involving "the gospel" at all. This is not a paraphrase of something Doc_01 says in different words — it is a specific historical claim (that Zwingli's own circle used this specific self-description) attached to a specific citation, and neither the claim nor the citation is supported anywhere in this world's own approved upstream record. This is precisely the kind of "fresh claim not already established upstream" the World Profile's own job is to avoid introducing, and it sits in the document's very first substantive sentence.

**Why it matters:** This is a research-grade historical claim (what a specific historical community called itself) presented with a specific pinpoint citation, in a document whose entire warrant is that it synthesizes rather than originates. A reader trusting the citation would find nothing at Doc_01 §1 or §2 to support it. This is the same shape of defect — a claim dressed in a citation that does not actually contain it — that Doc_03 Round 1 review, Doc_04 Round 1 review, and Doc_07 Round 1 review each already caught and corrected in this world's build.

**Fix required:** Either locate and cite an actual upstream source for this specific self-naming claim, or remove it and state plainly (as the rest of the sentence already does correctly) that no self-name is attested in this world's vendored corpus, without inventing what the unattested name might have been.

### Finding 2 — HIGH — The Method Note's central precedent-application quotation is misattributed to the wrong Donatism document, silently extended beyond what either source actually says, and overstates what the record shows the project lead actually ruled

**Checked:** Method Note: *"Donatism's own World Profile already put this exact question to the project lead directly (`don_Decision_Log.md`, 2026-09-03), on the correct grounds that it is portfolio-wide, not world-specific: 'every other M4-era world's own Doc_07 will yield the identical nine-versus-four mismatch, since M4 is a portfolio-wide Change Order, not a Donatism-specific finding.' **The project lead's direction, already obtained and binding across the portfolio: cover all nine of Doc_07's actually-built lenses.**"*

**Found, on direct inspection of both cited Donatism artifacts:**

1. **Misattribution.** The quoted sentence, in full, does **not** appear in `don_Decision_Log.md`. The Decision Log's 2026-09-03 entry says only: *"the World Profile's own scope question carries the same portfolio-wide reach (every other M4-era world's own Doc_07 will yield the identical nine-versus-four mismatch), squarely matching `cic-build-cycle`'s own Portfolio-level and Governance/Methodology escalation categories."* — no "since M4 is a portfolio-wide Change Order, not a Donatism-specific finding" clause anywhere in that entry. That exact clause instead appears in `don_World_Profile.md` §10 (Disposition): *"a decision with plain portfolio-wide reach (every other M4-era world's own Doc_07 will yield the identical mismatch, since M4 is a portfolio-wide Change Order, not a Donatism-specific finding)..."* The quotation the RZG Method Note presents as a single sentence from the Decision Log is actually assembled from — and cited to the wrong one of — two different Donatism documents.
2. **Not the project lead's own words.** In both source documents, the "portfolio-wide" language is the Donatism build thread's own stated *reason for escalating* the question, not a statement the project lead made or ratified in those terms. What the project lead is recorded as actually saying, in both documents, is narrow and specific: *"The project lead's direction: cover all nine of Doc_07's actually-built lenses"* — with no reference to "the portfolio" or to binding force on other worlds in the recorded directive itself.
3. **Overstated characterization.** The RZG Method Note's own gloss — *"already obtained and binding across the portfolio"* — is this document's own inference, not a fact the Decision Log or the Donatism World Profile actually records. What the record shows is: (a) the Donatism build thread argued the question was portfolio-wide as grounds for escalating rather than self-resolving, and (b) the project lead then answered the specific question put to them (how should Donatism's own World Profile treat the four-vs-nine mismatch). Nothing in either document shows the project lead being asked, or affirming, "does this ruling bind every future M4-era world's World Profile."

**Why this matters:** This is the load-bearing citation for the single most consequential judgment call in this document — the decision to decline a live escalation category (`cic-build-cycle`'s Portfolio-level/Governance-Methodology categories, which `CLAUDE.md`'s own default-action table marks "Always ask") on the strength of an already-settled precedent. A misattributed, silently-extended quotation is a weak foundation for that judgment, regardless of whether the judgment's conclusion turns out to be right. It also means this document's own claim, two sentences earlier, that it is "applying that world's own already-obtained project-lead ruling directly, rather than re-escalating an identical question" rests on a citation this review cannot verify says what the document claims it says.

**On the substantive question this bears on (see also Finding 6, below, on whether re-escalation was actually warranted):** independent support for treating M4 as genuinely portfolio-wide *does* exist, and is stronger than the Donatism precedent this Method Note leans on — Doc_07 §1 itself quotes the Construction Framework's own live text: M4 (adopted 2026-07-27) applies its nine-lens spine "always... for every world." A Change Order to a shared governing Framework document is portfolio-wide by definition, independent of any one world's own Decision Log entry. The Method Note would be on firmer, directly-verifiable ground citing that Framework text (which it already correctly does, earlier in the same Method Note, for the *Doc_07 lens-lettering* question) than leaning on a garbled cross-reference to a different world's own Decision Log for the *World Profile template's* four-versus-nine question specifically.

**Fix required:** Correct the citation to actually quote whichever source contains the exact words used (most likely `don_World_Profile.md` §10, not the Decision Log), without extending either quotation beyond what its own source says. Replace "already obtained and binding across the portfolio" with an accurate characterization of what was actually established (a specific ruling for Donatism's own document, reasonably generalizable and independently supported by M4's own portfolio-wide Framework text, but not itself a ruling on RZG's own case). Given how cheap a confirmatory check would be and how directly this implicates a named escalation category, an actual brief confirmation from the project lead — rather than an inferential reading of another world's precedent — would be the more disciplined path; see Finding 6.

### Finding 3 — MEDIUM — The Method Note's claim about Doc_07's actual current §2 title is wrong for this world's own Doc_07, and appears copied verbatim from Donatism's analogous Method Note without being re-verified against this world's own document

**Checked:** Method Note: *"This world's own Doc_07's actual current top-level structure is: `§1` Document Identity, `§2` Integration Lenses (`2A`–`2I`), `§3` Additional Lenses..."*

**Found:** This world's own `Doc_07_Integrated_Ecology_Analysis.md` §2 is actually titled **"The Required Lenses"** — the string "Integration Lenses" does not appear anywhere in this world's own Doc_07. Direct comparison shows the World Profile's Method Note text is a verbatim match, word for word, to Donatism's own World Profile Method Note describing *Donatism's* Doc_07 — and Donatism's own Doc_07 §2 genuinely is titled "Integration Lenses" (confirmed directly). This is a case of this world's own Method Note reusing another world's precedent text without re-checking it against this world's own governing document — the identical discipline failure the Method Note itself exists to guard against ("per `cic-build-cycle`'s standing instruction to re-read a governing document's actual current section numbering rather than assume it"). The section *lettering* (2A–2I) and every subsequent top-level section title (§3 through §8) are correctly verified and accurate; only §2's own name is wrong, and §1's own name ("Document Identity," dropping "and Confirmed Inputs") is imprecise in the same inherited way.

**Why it matters:** No cross-reference in this document is actually broken by this — every citation to "Doc_07 §2I," "§2A," etc. throughout Sections 3 and 4 uses the correct letters and resolves correctly. The defect is narrower than a functional citation break, but it is a concrete, checkable factual error inside the one section of this document whose entire stated purpose is asserting exact, freshly-verified structural accuracy — and it is direct evidence that at least one piece of this Method Note's precedent-based reasoning was carried over from Donatism without independent verification, which should raise the bar for re-checking Finding 2's own precedent claim as well.

**Fix required:** Correct "Integration Lenses" to "The Required Lenses" and "Document Identity" to "Document Identity and Confirmed Inputs."

### Finding 5 — MEDIUM — Section 1 asserts a specific temporal vantage point for a future Representative that is not grounded in any upstream document and edges into a decision Doc_07 §3 and this world's own escalation categories explicitly reserve for Representative Emergence

**Checked:** Section 1, Temporal scope: *"A Representative formed in this world inhabits the two-strand ecology at a point after the Consensus Tigurinus (1549) has already bridged the doctrinal divide but before either city's own confessional statements (the Second Helvetic Confession, 1566) have yet fixed the founding generation's own oral method in permanent written form."*

**Found:** No document from Doc_01 through Doc_09 states or implies a specific temporal window (post-1549/pre-1566) at which a future Representative is understood to stand. **Why it matters:** This is exactly the kind of claim the World Profile's own job description forbids introducing — a fresh interpretive decision, not a synthesis of an already-established finding — and it touches the one category (Representative construction) every other document in this build has been unusually careful to leave untouched pending its own dedicated escalation.

### Finding 6 — On the Method Note's precedent-application judgment itself (not a separate citation defect, but the governance question this review was specifically asked to weigh)

Is RZG's own case different enough from Donatism's to warrant a fresh escalation rather than applying precedent? On the narrow substantive question — should an M4-era World Profile's Ecological Summary cover Doc_07's actual nine lenses rather than the template's stale four — RZG's own case is, if anything, **less** ambiguous than Donatism's: this document's own Method Note correctly notes that RZG's own Doc_07 was built under M4's nine-lens spine from its own first draft, unlike Donatism's Doc_07, which was reconciling a mid-construction change. There is no live reason to expect a different substantive answer here.

But *how* this document reaches that answer is the problem Findings 2 and 3 document: it leans on a misattributed, silently-extended quotation rather than the stronger, directly-available ground it already correctly uses elsewhere in the same Method Note (Doc_07 §1's own quotation of the Framework's M4 text applying "for every world"). Given `CLAUDE.md`'s own explicit default-action table lists "Cross-world or portfolio-level decision: Always ask," and given this project's own stated view that "a blocking review finding can't be dismissed by self-certification," the more disciplined path here would have been either (a) a brief, cheap confirmatory question to the project lead specifically about whether the Donatism ruling is intended to generalize, or (b) grounding the non-escalation decision explicitly and solely in the independently-verifiable M4 Framework text (which does not require Donatism's precedent at all), rather than resting weight on a citation that, on inspection, does not say what this document claims it says. As written, this section's reasoning is not sound; its conclusion is probably right regardless.

## Summary

This World Profile does the synthesis job Step 9 requires competently across most of its eleven sections, and several of its most consequential claims — the gravity classifications, the Article 21 cross-strand test results, the forces summary, and the verbatim Integrative Observation — are accurate and carefully cross-checked against their upstream sources. But the review found two High-severity findings of the exact citation-fidelity shape this world's build has been caught on at every prior document from Doc_04 through Doc_09: a fabricated claim with a fake citation in Section 1's own opening line, and a misattributed, silently-extended quotation underpinning the Method Note's own decision not to re-escalate a live portfolio-level question. A third, Medium finding shows the Method Note's own precedent-based section-structure claim was copied from Donatism's analogous document without being re-verified against this world's own Doc_07 — direct evidence of exactly the discipline lapse the other two findings independently suggest. None of this requires re-deriving this document's own gravity, forces, or vocabulary synthesis, which is largely sound; it does require re-grounding the Method Note's own central citation and fixing the Section 1 and Section 6 citation errors before this document can be considered complete.

**Escalation categories:** none apply to this review's own findings. This is ordinary citation-fidelity, sourcing-precision, and scope-discipline work — fixable directly by the build thread. Finding 6 (whether the Method Note's underlying non-escalation judgment is itself sound) is a governance-adjacent observation this review was specifically asked to weigh, not a new escalation this reviewer is initiating; the recommendation there is that the build thread either re-ground that judgment in independently-verifiable text (Doc_07 §1's own M4 quotation) or put a brief confirmatory question to the project lead, at the build thread's own discretion.
