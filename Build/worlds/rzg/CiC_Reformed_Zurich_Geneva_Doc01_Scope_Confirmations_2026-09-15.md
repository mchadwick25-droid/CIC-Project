## Question 1 — Dort scope

**Presented:** "Does the Synod of Dort (1618–19) belong in this world's (Zurich & Geneva) own story, or does it stay with the not-yet-built Dutch worlds (VI.9/VI.26)? The census itself leaves this genuinely open across three intersecting entries."

**Options presented:**
1. *International Reformed dimension only (recommended)* — "Cite the Canons of Dort and the documented Zurich/Geneva delegate participation (Beza's theological lineage, Diodati/Tronchin from Geneva, a Swiss cantonal delegation) in this world's own Doc_02. Leave the Dutch domestic controversy itself (Arminius, the Remonstrance, Oldenbarnevelt, the exiled Brotherhood) to VI.26. Keeps real, evidenced material without claiming a story that's really the Dutch Republic's own."
2. *Dort entirely out of this world's story* — "Cleanest boundary — this world only transmits an abstract theological lineage (Beza) toward Dort, no in-world content about the synod itself. Drops the documented delegate presence from this world's own record even though both strands were physically represented there."
3. *Defer the whole question* — "Leave it untouched until VI.9 and/or VI.26 actually enter build. Avoids any coordination cost now, at the price of leaving Doc_01's own transitional-development question formally unanswered indefinitely."

**Selected:** Option 1, "International Reformed dimension only (recommended)."

## Question 2 — World architecture

**Presented:** "Are Zurich and Geneva better modeled as one formation world with two internal strands (current architecture), or as two separate formation worlds? Running the Framework's own World Separation Criteria honestly turned this into a real close call, not a comfortable single-world conclusion."

**Options presented:**
1. *One world, two strands (tentative reading, recommended to keep)* — "Zwingli/Bullinger (Zurich) and Calvin/Beza (Geneva) as two strands within The Reformed Cities, bridged by the Consensus Tigurinus. Favored by shared gravities (no doctrine gained or lost) and shared interpretive method, plus the historical convention of treating both as one 'Reformed' family. Real counter-evidence exists (independent origins, unconverged authority structures) but doesn't reach the bar of a new/lost gravity."
2. *Two separate worlds* — "Split into a Zwinglian/Bullingerian Zurich world and a Calvinist/Bezan Geneva world. Better honors the independent origins (five years apart, no contact), the permanently-diverged authority structures, and the genuinely different formation logics — but is a real portfolio-level restructuring (new Atlas entries, a second Doc_01, likely doubling most of this build's remaining work)."
3. *Need more discussion before deciding* — "The evidence as reasoned in Doc_01 §4 is presented for your own read — if you want to look at the actual document section or talk through the trade-offs further before committing either way."

**Selected:** Option 1, "One world, two strands (tentative reading, recommended to keep)."
