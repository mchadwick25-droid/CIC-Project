- **Balance Testing.** This world's own ecology is deliberately *not* balanced across all lenses, and the imbalance is disclosed and evidence-tracked, not an unexamined skew: Doc_07 §5 finds three lenses thin for one single, named, shared reason — the genre asymmetry between richly-vendored doctrine/confession and almost entirely absent daily-practice/first-person material — surfacing identically in Experiential/Emotional (§2B), Narrative/Mythic (§2C), and Material (§2G), while Ethical/Legal (§2E) is, in this document's own characterization of Doc_07's own content, unusually well-evidenced rather than a thin add-on — Doc_07 §2E itself states the lens is "substantively present in this world's own record, distinct from the doctrinal lens above," with Geneva's Consistory named as "this world's own most concretely legal-ecclesial institution." **PASS** on the Framework's own terms (balance proportional to what the evidence actually supports, not artificially equalized across lenses that were never equally documented).
- **Reduction Testing.** This world does not reduce to a single explanatory factor — Doc_04 confirmed three Primary gravities (G1, G2, G3), one Supporting (G4, strand-scope-qualified), and two Tensional (T1, T2), with G3 named explicitly as the *generative logic underneath* G1 and G2 rather than a fourth undifferentiated factor collapsing the whole ecology into one thing (Doc_07 §2I). **PASS.**
- **Complexity Testing.** Tensions and ambiguities are preserved, not resolved by hindsight: Doc_07 §7 and §2I state directly that T1 and T2 are held, not resolved — "what the ecology actually lived rather than resolved" — and the [CT]-tagged Sign and the Thing Signified contest (whether the 1549 Consensus is genuine synthesis or diplomatic elasticity) is carried, tagged, through every document that touches it (Doc_04, Doc_06, Doc_07, Doc_08, the World Profile) and adjudicated nowhere. - **Emergence Testing.** Dimensions interact meaningfully, and this world's own record shows genuine cross-lens findings not visible at any single earlier lens: Doc_07 §5's own first finding — that this world can be doctrinally unified and institutionally divergent at the same time — and its own second finding — that the same genre-asymmetry pattern surfaces at three separate lens scales as one underlying cause rather than three unrelated caveats — were each arrived at only by holding multiple lenses together, not stated at any single lens in isolation. **PASS.**
