## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

**Build.** The census's "moderate-to-rich institutionally, ordinary parish voice thinner" holds exactly. The already-vendored Jerusalem 1672 corpus (Robertson, Kimmel, Schaff) and the Sathas volume give the doctrinal and chronicle core. This pass adds two inside voices: the Patriarchate's own legislation, in Gedeon's Greek, and an Orthodox churchman's eyewitness of the 1650s, in Paul of Aleppo's English. It also adds a Slav monk's protest against Greek control (Paisii) and two English eyewitnesses who frame the window, Covel at the start and Walsh at Gregory V's death. The thin spots are the neomartyr acts and Phanariot writing in public-domain print: Nikodemos's Neon Martyrologion and Hypselantes were not found, and Dositheos's 1715 history and Chrysanthos are unreadable scans. Most own voice is Greek; English is outside witness or translation. No floor question.
