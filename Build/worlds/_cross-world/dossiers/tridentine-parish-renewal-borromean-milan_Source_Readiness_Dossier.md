## 5. Open cross-world questions

- **Vs. The Tridentine Church (VI.22).** Its dossier (§5, September 2026) said neither world could claim Borromeo's own voice and that the only real distinction was scale. That premise no longer holds: under the September 2026 ruling the Acta, the three letter collections, the sermons and the Ricordi are Borromeo's own primary voice in Latin and Italian, and they sit naturally here, not in VI.22. The question passed on undecided: does VI.22 keep the Acta as `tradition` (as now) with this world holding the same text as `tradition`, or does the Acta become `context` for VI.22 once this world exists? Both rows are written as `tradition` for now. See §6 for the build-or-fold question.
- **Vs. The Society of Jesus (VI.11).** The 1762–63 letter collections are, by their editors' own statement, published as evidence against the Jesuits, and much of their content is Borromeo's quarrel with the Milan Jesuits over the seminary. The brief's rule (map to VI.11 only where a text is a Jesuit's) was followed, so they are staged to this world only. VI.11's Doc_02 should know they exist as a hostile outside witness about the Milan college in 1567–84, and that the Monumenta Borgia carries the Jesuit side (§2). Passed on, not decided.
- **The Acta's header.** The file header's "second witness, never primary evidence" sentence contradicts the staging file and the September 2026 ruling. An existing vendored file; the lead decides how to correct it.
- **Author slugs.** New prefixes `giussano`, `bascape`, `possevino-giovanni-battista` need entries wherever `AUTHOR-IDS.yaml` is maintained; `borromeo` already exists. `possevino-giovanni-battista` is spelled out in full so that the Jesuit Antonio Possevino can take `possevino-antonio` later without collision.

## 6. Step 0 scope notes and recommendation

**Floor (A1).** Census floorNote: "No question." Nothing found this pass changes that. The one floor-adjacent thing to disclose is the Inquisition sentence of 1564 in Borromeo's name (Gibbings, §3): a fact about the man, not a creedal question.

**Sourcing (B1).** Strong, and far stronger than the census's own list implied once the September 2026 ruling is applied: the world's own legislation (4.5 MB Latin), Borromeo's own letters, sermons and lay counsels (about 0.9 MB Italian), three early Lives in Italian and Latin, and a clean English Life (2 MB). The gaps are specific: no visitation acts, no Schools' rules or rosters, no canonisation process beyond Peña's summary, and no public-domain English of anything in Borromeo's own words. The English-quotable voice is Giussano alone.

**Ecology (B2).** Heavy author gravity toward one man, and toward hagiography about him: three of the Lives were written for the canonisation. The counterweights are real: the legislation is institutional, not personal; the letters show him in dispute and in administration, not in a halo (the 1762–63 editors chose them for exactly that); the sermons and Ricordi are addressed to nuns and lay people, not about him. A Doc_02 should name the hagiographic pull and set the letters and Acta against it.

**Uniqueness (B3).** The census frames this world as "the Catholic mirror of Wittenberg's parish question", a diocese-level, parish-level ecology (seminary, visitation, confessional, lay catechesis, plague). VI.22's own dossier describes that world's corpus as institutional, doctrinal and narrative-history, thin in pastoral and interior voice. The two are complementary, not duplicates: VI.22 holds the council and the whole church; this world holds what one province did with it, in its own words.

**Audiences (B4).** Not assessed here beyond noting that the public-domain English witness is one two-volume Life; everything else a participant hears in English will be an Opus rendering from Italian or Latin, marked as such.

**Scale (B5).** About 11 MB vendored for this world in total (including the Acta), comparable to a mid-size built world; the Italian and Latin share is large.

**Recommendation: build** (as its own world, VI.12), not as part of the Tridentine Church (VI.22). Reasoning from the tests above: B1 now clears with the world's own primary voice, which the earlier VI.22 dossier thought did not exist; B3 is the census's own distinction and this pass confirms it in the sources (one province's legislation, letters and parish practice against VI.22's conciliar and doctrinal texts); B2's hagiographic gravity is a design risk to name, not a reason to fold the world away. The honest alternative, folding VI.12 into VI.22 as its pastoral half, would hand VI.22 the pastoral voice its own dossier says it lacks, but at the cost of the parish-level ecology the census built this entry to carry, and of an English-quotable base that is already thin. This is a recommendation for Mark, not a decision.
