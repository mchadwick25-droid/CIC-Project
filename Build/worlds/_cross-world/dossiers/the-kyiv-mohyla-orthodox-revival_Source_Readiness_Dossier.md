- **The Confession** now has three witnesses to its wording (Kimmel, Schaff for Part I only, and the English of 1762 and 1898) and is the world's charter. What the second pass adds, honestly:

- **Mohyla in his own hand.** The Arkhiv vol. 7 prints the notes Mohyla kept himself: the miracle stories he gathered from the Ruthenian, Moldavian and Greek churches, the canons he composed, his reasoning on the monastic life, what he spent rebuilding St Sophia and the Tithe church, and the books he bought in Warsaw and Krakow in 1632-1633. This is the friendly inside voice the first pass said was thin, in readable Cyrillic, with the editor marking what is not in his hand.
- **The documentary spine.** AZR vol. 4 runs from Jeremias II's visit through the union to Mohyla's years and is clean; AUZR vol. 2 covers 1599-1637 and is a second witness. Together they are what a printed public-domain series can give of the "academy records, brotherhood statutes" the census names; the Collegium's own statutes are still not in print (§4).
- **The Lithos, unreadable as text.** The 1644 answer to Sakovych is in the corpus, but the OCR read its Latin type as Cyrillic look-alikes and nothing in it can be quoted without the page images. Golubev's Russian study of it, and his summary of Sakovych's charges, read well.
- **Vyshensky.** The Athonite's letters are pre-window (about 1600-1616) and placed as antecedent; the Kyiv thread may prefer context.

Everything new is Ruthenian, Slavonic, Polish or Russian; no English was found for any of it.

## 5. Open cross-world questions

- **The Confession is shared with VI.17 (Orthodoxy under the Ottomans, 1453–1650) and VI.18.** It is the Ottoman world's charter against Lucaris and the Kyiv world's charter as its own metropolitan's book. All the Confession files already carry rows for both. Which world holds the "native" claim, and which holds the corroborating claim, is the two builds' call. This dossier does not decide it.
- **The Union of Brest (VI.20) owns the other side of the argument.** Krevza's *Oborona uni* and the Filippovich diary are in this world's file but are Brest rows too (§1). Potii, Smotrycki and the Lviv brotherhood stay with the Brest pass. Whether this world uses Krevza as an outside witness or leaves him to Brest is for Doc_02.
- **The Russian Church (VI.23, then Nikon to Holy Synod).** Two items reach Moscow. The epistles ascribed to Artemii are placed provisionally at VI.23 (the elder tried at the Stoglav council of 1551; the epistles were written in Lithuania). The Synopsis is placed provisionally at the Nikon-to-Synod entry because Russia reprinted it. Philaret's catechism (Schaff) is placed at the Synodal Century.
