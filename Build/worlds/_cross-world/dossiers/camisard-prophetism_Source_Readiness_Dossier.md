## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

Part of another world, as the census already says. The own-voice layer is real but narrow: the London depositions in French and English, Cavalier's memoir, Marion's warnings and the 1703 manifesto, almost all produced in exile and as advocacy. The hostile Catholic side (Brueys, Louvreleul, Fléchier) is rich, and the census itself says the story is largely reconstructed from persecutors' records. The material reads best inside the Church of the Desert world, where Court's history and the synods' later rejection of the inspirations give it its frame. French carries the primary evidence; the English of 1707 makes the depositions accessible. Vendoring it now serves both worlds at once.
