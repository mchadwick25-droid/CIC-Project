## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

Build. The world's own voice is unusually well documented for a hidden church: three volumes of its synod acts from 1715 to 1796, Antoine Court's own history and sermons, the Rabaut family's toleration writing, and the Tour de Constance record with prisoners' letter fragments. Everything primary is in French; English exists only for Rabaut Saint-Étienne's story, so the build will lean on French readings. Coquerel's 1841 history is scholarship, kept because its appendices print the Rabaut papers. The gap is personal letters: Court's memoirs and Paul Rabaut's letters (1884) are not on the Internet Archive, and Marie Durand's letters survive here only in fragments. The world is distinct from `the-huguenots` (which ends in 1685) and absorbs the Camisard episode cleanly.
