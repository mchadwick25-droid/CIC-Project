What kind of witness each is:

- **The Spirituals' voice.** Clareno's Expositio is clean Latin in his own words. Ehrle's Archiv Bd 1 to 4 print Latin extracts of Clareno's Historia septem tribulationum, Raymund of Fronsac's collection of acts, Ubertino's answers of 1310 and Bonagratia's opinion, inside Ehrle's study. Ubertino's major work, the Arbor vitae, exists only as a 1485 print whose OCR is unusable and over the size limit (section 4).
- **The papal side.** Eubel's Bullarium V holds the bulls of 1303–1334, among them Sancta Romana and the entries on Cum inter nonnullos, Ad conditorem canonum and Quia quorundam. It is institutional and hostile in the census's terms, and it is the library's text for John XXII.
- **The Michaelists.** Carlini's study prints Michael of Cesena's own protest of 1338 in Latin in the appendix. Scholz Part 2 prints Ockham's treatises against John XXII and Benedict XII and on imperial and papal power. Both are placed needs-ruling.
- **The Observant wing.** Glassberger's chronicle is an Observant voice in Latin; the Bullarium nova series holds the papal letters for 1431–1455.
- **The hostile and secondary record.** Bernard Gui's handbook in Mollat's Latin and French, Limborch's register as a second witness, and Lea's third volume in English. Lea is the only public-domain English text in the set that tells the story, and it is scholarship, not source.
- **The Francis-circle texts.** The English Mirror of Perfection and Fioretti, the Latin Actus, Jacopone's laude and Underhill's volume are placed needs-ruling, because their date and attribution are contested and they straddle the Franciscan Movement world to 1300.

## 5. Open cross-world questions

- **The Fraticelli (V.12) and the Observance (V.9) are this world's two named branches.** What this world takes from each, and where the poverty quarrel ends and the Observant reform begins, is a Step 0 question for all three worlds. The shared files are listed in section 1.
- **The Michaelists (Ockham, Michael of Cesena).** The census names both as voices. Their writing is political and ecclesiological as much as Franciscan, and the Avignon papal-church world (the Latin Papal Church, Avignon to Fifth Lateran) has the other side of the same quarrel. The placements of Carlini and Scholz Part 2 are needs-ruling for that reason.
- **The Francis-circle texts.** The Mirror of Perfection (Sabatier's date and attribution to Leo are contested), the Actus and Fioretti (compiled in the fourteenth century in the March of Ancona, per the English introduction) and Jacopone (died 1306) all lie between the Franciscan Movement world (to 1300) and this one. They are placed needs-ruling on all three, not decided.
- **The Conventual side and the 1517 division.** The census names the Conventual records and Ite vos. Eubel VI and VII, the later nova-series volumes and Ite vos were not found in usable form (section 4). Bartholomew of Pisa (section 3) is the Conventual-side lead and is not vendored. The Conventual voice in the library is therefore thin, and Lea and the bulls carry it.
- **Ubertino's own voice.** His 1310 answers are in Ehrle Bd 3. His major work is not usable. Step 0 should say which of the two the world treats as his voice.
- **Olivi.** No edition was found. Olivi enters through Ehrle Bd 3 and Lea, and through the Joachimite Current world if it claims him.
- **Rendering.** Every primary voice here is Latin or Italian. An Opus rendering is needed. The only public-domain English that covers the story is Lea, who is secondary, and the English of the Francis-circle texts.

## 6. Step 0 scope notes and recommendation

**Floor.** The census says no question: the poverty condemnations were about obedience and property, not the Creed. The bulls in Eubel's volume fit that, and nothing sampled touches the Creed.

**Sourcing.** The census says "Rich", with the poverty-controversy dossiers, the Observant and Conventual records, Ite vos, and Clareno and Olivi's internal literature. In Latin, that is largely borne out for the first half of the period: the bulls of 1303–1334 are in the library, Clareno's Expositio and the Spirituals' documents in Ehrle's volumes are in the library, Michael's protest of 1338 and Ockham's treatises are in the library, and the Observant chronicle and the papal letters of 1431–1455 are in the library. It is thin at the other end. Ite vos and the Conventual records are not in the library, Olivi's works are not, and Ubertino's major work is not usable. There is no public-domain English leg beyond Lea.

**Ecology.** Five named voices. John XXII speaks through his bulls. Clareno speaks in his own Latin. Ubertino speaks in the documents of 1310. Michael of Cesena speaks in one protest. Ockham speaks in his treatises, which are the most abundant Michaelist text. The shape is a quarrel with both sides on the shelf, which is good for a conversation that tests who gets heard. The weak side is the Conventuals, whose own voice the library does not hold.

**Uniqueness.** Against the Franciscan Movement world (to 1300) the line is the year 1300, and the Francis-circle texts straddle it. Against the Fraticelli world the sources overlap almost wholly. Against the Observance world the overlap is the Observant wing. Against the Avignon papal-church world the overlap is John XXII and the Michaelist polemic.

**Audiences.** The Order is still here and still divided along the line drawn in 1517, as the census says. The audience is large and reachable.

**Scale.** 18 files are placed here (28.0 MB by characters). 12 are tradition rows: five assigned or provisional (Clareno, Ehrle Bd 1 to 3, Glassberger) and seven needs-ruling (Ockham, Michael of Cesena, and the five Francis-circle texts). The other 6 are context.

**Recommendation: build.** The movement speaks in its own voice in vendored Latin texts (Clareno, the Spirituals' documents, Michael's protest, Ockham, the Observant chronicle), and both sides of the quarrel are on the shelf. The cost to say up front is that the library has no public-domain English for any of them, so every Representative quote is an Opus rendering of Latin checked against the scan, and the Conventual voice, Ite vos, Olivi and Ubertino's major work are not yet in the library. A Doc_02 should run one more acquisition pass on those before the build counts the world's five named voices as secure. This world is also the natural home for the Fraticelli, which the census already marks as within another world. This is a recommendation for Mark to weigh, not a decision.
