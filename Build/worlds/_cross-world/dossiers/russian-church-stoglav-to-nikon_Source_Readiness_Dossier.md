## 5. Open cross-world questions

- **The Stoglav is placed to three worlds.** Primary here; antecedent to the Old Believers (VII.7) and to Nikon to Holy Synod (VII.21). Whether the Old Believers thread wants a primary claim on the Stoglav's sign-of-the-cross passages is that thread's call; the editor's preface in the 1863 edition (file lines 302 and 442) already speaks of the schism.
- **Avvakum's early chapters.** The Old Believers bucket holds the Life. This dossier proposes it as context here (§2). Whether the pre-1652 chapters belong to this world or only to VII.7 is for the lead to rule.
- **Kyiv (VI.21).** The Stoglav in French (Duchesne) names Kyiv about thirty times; the Mogila confession and Golubev sit in the Kyiv bucket. The question of how Muscovy received Kyivan learning before 1650 belongs to the Kyiv and Nikon-era worlds, not here. Recorded, not decided.
- **Ottoman Orthodoxy (VI.17).** Jeremias II's 1589 visit and the Constantinople-Moscow patriarchal quarrel sit across VI.17 and this world. The only pointer in the corpus is one editorial note in the Fletcher file (about line 10166).
- **Language tag for the Stoglav.** The 1863 file is tagged `chu` (the Russian recension of Church Slavonic) in its header. The Avvakum Wikisource file uses `orv` loosely for modernized Russian. The lead should confirm the tag convention for sixteenth-century Russian church texts.
- **The Kurbsky–Ivan correspondence** is a church-and-tsar voice in Ivan's reign, and its authenticity is contested (a point of scholarship the build would have to carry, not a gap in the corpus). If the build wants it, a `contested_claim` record is warranted. Not decided here.

## 6. Step 0 scope notes and recommendation

**Floor question.** The census floorNote is "No question". Nothing read in this pass contradicts it: the council is a church council of an Orthodox church, and the Domostroi is devotional and household discipline. The quarrels recorded are about ritual, discipline and the church's relation to the state, not about the creed. Fletcher's chapter XXIII is a Protestant list of "errours", not a doctrinal finding.

**Uniqueness and overlap.** Four sibling worlds touch this one. Nikon to the Holy Synod (VII.21, 1652–1815) begins where this world ends. The Old Believers (VII.7, 1666–1815) are the schism's other side. Kyiv-Mohyla (VI.21, 1615–1700) is the contemporary learning in a different polity. Ottoman Orthodoxy (VI.17, 1453–1650) is the patriarchal source of the 1589 patriarchate. None covers Muscovy's church from the Stoglav to the Time of Troubles and the Filaret years. The overlap with the Old Believers is in the sources (the Stoglav, the Domostroi, Avvakum's preface), not in the formation story.

**Scale.** Seven vendored files, about 1.0 million words by `wc -w`, of which about 87,000 tokens are the three Russian files and the rest English and French. The three inside voices are small in word count: the Stoglav and the Domostroi are each under 25,000 Russian words by `wc -w` (the Cyrillic OCR counts as long tokens), and the AAE volume is large but mixed with secular documents.

**What is thin.** No named theologian speaks in the corpus yet: Maxim the Greek is on the host in a weak scan (§3), Joseph of Volokolamsk and Nil Sorsky were not found (§4), and Hermogenes appears only in AAE documents that must be read to see whether the words are his. Chronicles and saints' lives (PSRL, the Great Menaia) are on the host but unopened. No inside voice is in English. The census itself calls ordinary lay voices thin; the Domostroi is the nearest to a lay text.

**Recommendation, for Mark to decide, not a decision: build.** Floor (A1) clears. Sourcing (B1) now has a council text in its own language and in a clean French translation, a lay discipline manual, and the Time of Troubles documents, with three outside witnesses to read against them; it is institutional rather than personal, as the census says. Ecology (B2) will be lopsided toward the council and the state-church documents, which Doc_02 can say plainly. Uniqueness (B3) holds against the four siblings. Audiences (B4) and scale (B5) were not assessed in this pass. One condition on the build: a second library pass on the PSRL volumes and Maxim the Greek (a cleaner scan) before Doc_02, since those are the places a named voice and a saint's life would come from. If that pass finds nothing readable, the fallback is **open question** on whether the world can carry a personal voice at all.
