## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

**Build.** The own-voice floor is now met in two registers: the Catholic Armenian layman Serpos writing to Rome inside the window (1783, 1786, four volumes, Italian), and the Mechitarist congregation's own record of its founder (Nurikhan, with documents) and of its church and rite (Issaverdens, Chamich already vendored). Outside witnesses frame it from both ends of the encounter: Tournefort's eyewitness of 1700-1702 and the Jesuit missionaries' memoirs on how to deal with the Armenians. The gap is Armenian-language text: Mekhitar's own writings, Simeon of Erevan's Jambr and the Catholic patriarchate's records exist only as unreadable Armenian scans, so the Apostolic side of the millet quarrel is thin (Abraham of Crete in French is the one voice). Against the sibling worlds the entry is distinct: New Julfa holds nothing Mekhitarist, and Zartonk begins after this window's sources.
