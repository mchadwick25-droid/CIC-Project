## 5. Open cross-world questions

- Nikon himself: the census places him here, as the establishment side, but he was deposed by the same council. His Replies are tradition here and context for the Old Believers (provisional rows given).
- Ligarides: a Greek prelate in the tsar's service who ran the council. Do his History and Answers count as this church's own voice (spec: tradition, provisional) or as an outside witness?
- The 1686 transfer of the Kyiv metropolia and Dimitrii of Rostov: Kyivan Mohylan learning carried into Moscow. Shared with `the-kyiv-mohyla-orthodox-revival` (transmission row given for Dimitrii).
- Mouravieff and the 1856 Regulation reprint both serve `russian-church-synodal-century` as well; the Uniate reunion documents in Mouravieff serve `ruthenian-greek-catholic-polotsk-to-galicia`.
- The Valaam mission to Alaska (1794) has no primary text in this pass; it may belong to `alaskan-mission-herman-veniaminov` for sourcing.
- Paisii Velichkovskii's circle is a separate census world (`the-hesychast-revival-paisius-and-the-philokalia`); no overlap was vendored here.

## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

Build. The church's own voice is now present in both languages and at each turning point: the patriarch of the reforms in his own long defence (Palmer vol. I), the council's case against him (Ligarides), the Spiritual Regulation and its Supplement in English of 1729 and in the Synod's own Russian print, Feofan preaching the Petrine settlement, Platon's catechetical theology, and Dimitrii and Tikhon for the devotional side. The floor is not in question. Against its siblings it is distinct: the Old Believers are the other side of the same split, and the Synodal century continues it after 1815. The thin points are real and should be named in the build. Tikhon is represented by one small tract. There is no primary text yet for the 1764 secularization or the Alaska mission. The ordinary believer is almost silent, as the census's own sourcing note warns. A follow-up pass should target Tikhon's collected works and Dimitrii's Chet'i-Minei (OCR test first), and the attendants' recollections of Tikhon behind the third census story.
