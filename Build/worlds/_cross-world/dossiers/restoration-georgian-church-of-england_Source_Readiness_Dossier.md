## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

Build. The church's own voice is deep and varied in public-domain English. The spec has its liturgy in a printing from inside the window, a parish priest's daily diary, an archbishop's visitation charges, the missionary society's own history and accounts, a nonjuring bishop's letters, the era's most read devotional book, its chief apologetic answer to deism, and both wings of its late evangelicalism. Every census story now has a primary witness vendored or listed as a lead: Ken and Hawkins for the Seven Bishops, Humphreys and the 1772 SPG accounts for Codrington, Woodforde for the Christmas dinners. The floor is not in question and everything is in English. Against its sibling worlds (Old Dissent, Methodism, the Quakers) this is the established side of each quarrel, and nothing else holds it. The weakest point is the ordinary lay voice outside the clergy. The Cheap Repository is written for the poor, not by them; Woodforde's parishioners speak only through him.
