## 6. Step 0 scope notes and recommendation

**Trinity/Christology vs. the anti-Trinitarian current (VI.14):** mainstream Anabaptism (Swiss Brethren, Hubmaier, Menno) is doctrinally distinct from and not conflated with VI.14's anti-Trinitarian current; no confirmed source above denies the Trinity. But there is a genuine, disclosable floor-adjacent complication unique to the Dutch/Menno wing, not present among the Swiss Brethren: Menno (following Melchior Hoffman) taught "celestial flesh" Christology, that Christ's flesh originated in heaven and merely passed through Mary "as through a channel", rather than being derived from her. This bears directly on the floor test's third commitment (Christ "truly human... incarnate of... the Virgin Mary") and needs the same bounded-disclosure treatment the Latin Apologists' Step 0 gives Minucius Felix's Christological absence: a real complication, not disqualifying, and distinct in kind from VI.14's outright Trinity rejection. (Recorded September 2026; Step 0 §2 A1 and §4 item 1 now carry it, grounded in Menno's, Dirk's and the Martyrs Mirror's own words.)

**Believer's baptism/rebaptism** is a discipline/practice question, not a floor question, the same shape as Tertullian's Montanism in the Latin Apologists precedent.

**Münster** is excluded as outside "mainstream"; none of the sources above are Münsterite. Cornelius 1853 (§3) is available if Doc_01 wants a primary witness for the exclusion.

**Scale, restated October 2026.** Fifteen files, about 4.5 million words in full, of which roughly 1.9 million are German. The named-theologian range is still thinner than the patristic worlds (Marpeck and Denck absent; Grebel only through hostile records), but the inside voice the census promises is now actually in the corpus in every genre it names: court testimony (Zurich, Württemberg), prison hymns (Ausbund, clean), the martyr book, the Hutterite Chronicle (clean), and the movement's confessions in both Dutch-wing (Dordrecht, Waterlander) and Moravian (Riedemann, Schleitheim-German) forms.

**Uniqueness and overlap.** Siblings on the Atlas are the Reformed Cities (VI.2, the persecutor and the shared Zurich archive), Lutheran Wittenberg (VI.1, the Augsburg condemnations), and the anti-Trinitarian current (VI.14, kept separate by the floor note). The overlap with the Reformed Cities is in the sources (Egli, Zwingli), not in the formation story: Zurich's records show the same events from the bench, this world from the dock. No sibling carries the free-church line this world is parent of.

**Recommendation, for Mark to decide, not a decision: build.** Floor (A1) clears on the movement's own confessions, with the celestial-flesh complication disclosed rather than hidden. Sourcing (B1) now carries every genre the census names in the movement's own voice, with clean scans where quotation needs them. Ecology (B2) is lopsided toward testimony and martyrology and thin in named theologians, which Doc_02 can state as an absence rather than fill. Uniqueness (B3) holds: the persecuted free-church formation logic is not told by any sibling. Audiences (B4) and scale (B5) are already argued in Step 0 and Doc_01, which exist and have each been through three review rounds.
