## 5. Open cross-world questions

- **Gottfried Arnold.** Raised in the Brethren dossier (VII.3): Arnold's *Erste Liebe* and *Ketzer-Historie* are German Pietism's own literature and the radical Pietists' founding reading. Placement (VII.2, VII.3, or both) is for the project lead; the available scans are garbled and over 170 MB.
- **The Petersen household.** Johann Wilhelm Petersen's Leben is placed provisional `tradition` here and provisional `context` in VII.3. The Petersens stood between Spener's circle and the radicals; which world owns them is a Doc_01 question.
- **Halle and Herrnhut.** Zinzendorf and Spangenberg were both Halle pupils; the Moravian files are in their own bucket and only Spangenberg's Halle chapters are linked here. The boundary between VII.2 and VII.4 after 1727 is clear in the census; the library follows it.
- **Württemberg.** Bengel sits inside this entry per the census; nothing here changes that.
- **Tranquebar.** Ziegenbalg's letters are placed here as the Halle mission's own record. The Tamil congregation has no census entry; the question whether a mission-born Christianity of the eighteenth century deserves one is the same portfolio question the Moravian dossier raises.

## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

**Build.** The own voice is present in every register the census asks for: Spener's programme in his own German (two printings), his catechism of the priesthood of believers, Francke's Halle narrative in German and in two contemporary English printings, three of his devotional works in English made in his lifetime, the Halle hymnal, Bengel's apocalyptic and exegesis in German and English, the lay conversion narratives in Reitz's own anthology, and the Tranquebar missionaries' letters. The floor is clear (Step 0 A1: confessional Lutherans). The census's own tiering (Viable, secondary) stands: the weakness is language. Spener has no public-domain English, Reitz and Freylinghausen are German only, and the women's voice the census names (Petersen) is represented by her husband's book. A Doc_02 should say that the Representative's Spener would be the Library's own rendering from the German, and that the Halle correspondence network is archival and absent.
