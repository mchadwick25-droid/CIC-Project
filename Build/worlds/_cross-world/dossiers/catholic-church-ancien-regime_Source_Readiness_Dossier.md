## 6. Recommendation (portfolio decision for the project lead; does not block library work)

Build. The institutional voice is now present in the papacy's own acts across the whole arc the census tells (Unigenitus, the suppression and restoration of the Jesuits, the condemnation of the Civil Constitution, Auctorem fidei), and the state's side is in Anderson's English. The devotional heart of the period is strong: Liguori, Montfort, Bossuet, Challoner and Muratori give the era's distinct temperaments in their own words. The Revolution's martyrs speak through the Compiègne survivor and Barruel, and Pius VI's captivity through Baldassari and his jailer Merck. French and Latin carry most acts, with English for the Jesuit bulls, Unigenitus and the revolutionary laws. The main thin spot is the Latin bullarium itself, left as a lead for size, and the boundary with Port-Royal and Utrecht needs a ruling.
