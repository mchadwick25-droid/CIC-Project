## Adding to this queue

**Proactive finds** (searched for and verified before any record cites them): add a row to `download-queue-seed.yaml` directly, with a real url and a rights_basis actually checked against the host — never guessed. **Reactive wants** need no action here at all: they come from `WANTS-REGISTER.md`, which is itself generated from records/ every run.

After downloading and vendoring an item from this queue, move its seed-file row's `status` to `downloaded` rather than deleting it — the row is now a record of what was already found and why, the same no-rewrite-history discipline `cic/engine/texts_registry.py` follows for its own ENTRIES.
