## Section 2 — Tier Categories, Re-Confirmed Against the Current Governing Text

Re-read directly from the Construction Framework V7.4 extracted text, **Part II — Evidence Development**, for this document. The bullets below are CF V7.4's own text, condensed but not paraphrased into new wording; where a chunk quotes this section, it now quotes CF V7.4 directly.

- **Tier 1 — Documented Historical Narrative.** "Direct textual attestation within or close to the world's horizon. Named author with identifiable social location. Datable with reasonable confidence. The story claims to describe specific events or persons and the claim is historically credible with appropriate caveats for the author's perspective and genre." Confidence: Documented to Widely Accepted at the narrative level.
- **Tier 2 — Collected and Traditional Material.** "Stories transmitted in collected form with identifiable collection history, traceable to the world's own community memory... Academic consensus treats the tradition as authentic even where individual attribution or detail cannot be verified. The collection itself is evidence of what the community remembered and valued." Confidence: Widely Accepted to Dominant Modern Reconstruction; specific attributions Contested.
- **Tier 3 — Attributed Tradition.** "Material attributed to specific figures or moments but resting on collected tradition rather than direct documentation... Hagiographic narrative is a specific type within this tier... Individual details follow genre conventions and cannot be treated as historical reporting, but the account of what a formed life looks like is itself genuine formation ecology evidence." Confidence: Contested for the portrait/attribution; Inferential/Thin for specific details. "The formation ideal communicated is credible evidence; the specific events claimed are not."
- **Tier 4 — Historically Grounded Reconstruction.** "A composite reconstruction of what regularly happened within the ecology — not a story about a specific named individual, but a narration of typical practice built from multiple attested sources. Every element must be traceable to attested evidence within the world's horizon." Confidence: Inferential/Thin regardless of how well-sourced the individual elements are.
- **No Tier 5.** "Generated or illustrative narrative — stories invented to illuminate formation truths but not traceable to the world's own evidence — is not permitted within this framework. If the evidence does not support a story, the story does not exist for this world."

**How this document applied the tiers, stated once.** The tier is assigned to each story *as its own source tells it*, by genre analysis of that text, not by author or by book. Sulpitius therefore anchors five Tier 1 stories and two Tier 3 stories; Cassian anchors two Tier 1 stories (his own first-person encounters) and four Tier 2 stories (elders' *exempla* and an origin-tradition he transmits). Where a Tier 1 narrative carries its author's supernatural framing (an angel, a demon, a providential surrender), the framing is carried at Contested strength as the world's own self-understanding and named as such in the chunk — the discipline Donatism's donstory003 established for Macrobius's omen and vision — rather than letting it drop the whole account to Tier 3. Where the wonder *is* the narrative claim (a resurrection; a Christophany; serpents that flee), the story is Tier 3. Section 7 records the two hardest calls.
