## Method (short)

Each record's current `text` and `modern_rendering` are read against the vendored edition, cic/texts/npnf211_sulpitius-severus-vincent-lerins-cassian.xml, with `<note>` blocks set aside. The round-1 and round-2 findings come from Rendering_Review_Gallic_Batch4_2026-11-03.md and Rendering_Review_Gallic_Round2_2026-11-03.md. The check covers every clause, anything added, one thought per sentence, sentence length (counted by script on the parsed `modern_rendering`), misleading modern senses, kept hedges and reported-belief frames, and fit with `divergence_note` and `use_note.not_for`. For the second record, the metadata change is read with `git diff` and checked against the edition's headings. A record fails only for something wrong, missing, added or misleading, not for style.
