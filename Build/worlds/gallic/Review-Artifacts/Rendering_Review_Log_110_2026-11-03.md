# gallic quote renderings: per-rendering review log (110 records)

Date: 2026-11-03. Records: the 110 `gallic.quote.*` records, each with a `modern_rendering` and a `text` taken from a vendored source.

What this log is for: it lets the claim "reviewed" be checked, one record at a time. Each row gives the record id (the `gallic.quote.` prefix is dropped), the review file or files that read the rendering, and the verdict. All review files are in `Build/worlds/gallic/Review-Artifacts/`.

How it was made. Four independent Opus 5.5 reviewers each read one batch: `Rendering_Review_Gallic_Batch1_2026-11-03.md` to `Batch4`. Each rendering was read against the record's `text` and against the edition text at its locus in `cic/texts/`. The bar: every clause rendered, nothing added, one thought per sentence, no sentence past about 25 words, no word whose modern sense misleads, and the source's own hedges kept. Round 1 failed six renderings. Five were re-rendered by Opus at the source record. A second, independent Opus reader rechecked all six in `Rendering_Review_Gallic_Round2_2026-11-03.md`. That reader found the round 1 finding on `postumianus-you-have-conquered-all-the-eremites` unfounded: the text begins at the edition's second "you have conquered", verbatim apart from the first capital. One rendering failed again, was re-rendered, and passed a third independent Opus reader in `Rendering_Review_Gallic_Round3_2026-11-03.md`. That round also checked a speaker correction on `benjamin-relied-on-his-own-judgment`. One record whose text carries a translator's bracketed supplement was then read against the bracket rule (the supplement must not be voiced as the source's own where it adds meaning) by a further independent Opus reader, in `Rendering_Review_Gallic_Brackets_2026-11-03.md`.
