## HIGH

### H1 — Doc_09 §2's tier definitions are copied verbatim from Donatism's Doc_09, and six chunks then quote a sibling document's paraphrase in quotation marks as the Construction Framework's own words

Doc_09 §2 states it re-read CF V7.4 directly "rather than from a remembered list," and its "Built from" note says Donatism's Doc_09 was read "for structure... never for content." All five tier bullets in Doc_09 §2 are byte-identical to Donatism's Doc_09. Two of those bullets contain phrases that do not occur in CF V7.4 at all (grep: 0 hits each) — "the formation ideal it communicates is the evidence, not the specific events claimed" and "individual attribution may be uncertain, but the tradition itself is treated as authentic." Both are then quoted inside quotation marks and attributed to "the governing text" in six chunks: gallicstory001, 003, 014 (Tier 3 phrase) and 008, 010, 012 (Tier 2 phrase).

### H2 — Four Story Texts / Node notes assert place-names that are not at the cited locus, three of them drawn from the editorial layer; Section 4's No-Tier-5 audit lists none of them

- gallicstory004: "the monk of **Ligugé**" — "Ligugé" occurs once in all of npnf211, in Gibson's prolegomena to Cassian, nowhere in Sulpitius.
- gallicstory006: "what happened one day **at Marmoutier**" — same Gibson prolegomena line; *Dial.* III.15 names no location beyond "the small open court" and "the lofty rock."
- gallicstory007: "Of the funeral **at Tours**" — Letter III never names Tours.
- gallicstory003 Node note: "at **Ligugé**... near **Poitiers**" — both editorial (Gibson; Roberts's footnote on "the city Pictava").

Section 4's audit certifies "every element traces to..." for each of these four without flagging any of them, while correctly refusing Roberts's "Worms" and "Cosne" elsewhere.

### H3 — The Egyptian/Eastern reception disclosure fails in two of the six Marseilles chunks, at two of the three places the document says it is made

gallicstory010's Story Text names only "the fathers," "the monks of Gaul," "the hermit of the desert" — no Egypt/East. gallicstory013's Story Text names Chæremon and Germanus but never Egypt or Panephysis, though its own Node note shows the drafter knew ("Cassian's own experience in Egypt, at Chæremon's cell near Panephysis"). 013 is the consequential one: it is the grace argument's own origin story, and without the disclosure a Representative narrating it presents the argument's origin as a southern-house episode rather than received Egyptian experience — precisely the conflation Doc_04 §7/Doc_05 §7.2/Doc_06 §4(a) exist to prevent.

### H4 — The "flattened working copy" line ranges are not reproducible and contradict Doc_05's own review-corrected ranges for the same file

Doc_05's §11 search_record (ranges specifically audited/fixed at Review Round 1 C13) gives *Inst.* II.1–18+III.1–12 = lines 14947–16292 and *Inst.* IV.1–9 = lines 16296–16530 in the flattened npnf211. Doc_09's gallicstory008 gives *Inst.* II.5–6 = 16294–16417 — inside Doc_05's *Inst.* IV range, outside its *Inst.* II range. gallicstory004's *Vita* IX = 771–833 doesn't reconcile with Doc_05's own passages from the same chapter at 742–747/766–787. Independently rebuilding a flattened copy gives ratios that don't match Doc_09's numbering at all, while Doc_05's stay flat at ≈1.02. The div ids, by contrast, are all independently verified correct. Half the verification handle Section 10(a) points reviewers to is broken, and the divergence from an Approved sibling document is nowhere disclosed.
