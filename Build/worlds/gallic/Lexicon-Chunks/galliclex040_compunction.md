## Quick Meaning

For this world compunction is the salutary pricking of heart from which the monastic life springs and by which it advances — "From the fear of the Lord arises salutary compunction. From compunction of heart springs renunciation" — weighted as the sign of progress, not of failure: "their compunction for their faults increases day by day in proportion as their purity of soul advances."

---

## World Meaning

Cassian sets it at the root of the ladder and again near its top. At the root: "From the fear of the Lord arises salutary compunction. From compunction of heart springs renunciation, i.e. nakedness and contempt of all possessions." It is one of the ways God visits the mind — when we have been slothful "He chastens us with most salutary compunction" — and it is what a true discipline produces where a false one cannot: from careless satiety, "the intervention of a healthy compunction will raise us to the right measure of strictness." The picture is Serapion at the after-supper conference: "first I was moved to secret sighs, and then my heart's compunction increased and I openly burst into sobs and tears," and the stolen biscuit comes out. Near the top, in the book on pride, the perfect are marked by it: they "recognize more and more that they are burdened with sin (for their compunction for their faults increases day by day in proportion as their purity of soul advances), and ... sigh continually from the bottom of their heart." The holier the monk, the more he weeps; the tears are not a symptom of distance from God but of nearness.

At Tours the word is heard once, in heaven's mouth. After Martin has been forced into communion with the Ithacian bishops and grieves that his power is lessened, "suddenly, an angel stood by him and said, 'Justly, O Martin, do you feel compunction, but you could not otherwise get out of your difficulty. Renew your virtue, resume your courage, lest you not only now expose your fame, but your very salvation, to danger.'" The pricking is right; the remedy is not despair but renewal. In both places compunction is what a healthy heart does, and what a cooled one has stopped doing.

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
Guilt, scrupulosity, or shame as psychological burdens to be relieved; tears as breakdown; compunction as a sign that something has gone wrong.

**World Hearing:**
A salutary pricking that begins the monastic life and deepens as purity grows — the healthier the heart, the more it weeps — sent by God, produced by true discipline, and confirmed by an angel as "just."
