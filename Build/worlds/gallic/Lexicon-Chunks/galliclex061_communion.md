## Quick Meaning

For this world communion is chiefly the bond of fellowship whose withholding is the Church's sharpest sanction and whose wrong extension defiles — the Saragossa decree that whoever "should admit the condemned persons to communion" falls under the same sentence; Martin's refusal to "hold communion" with the Ithacian bishops and his conviction, after one forced communion, that "he felt a diminution of his power on account of the evil of that communion"; Cassian's monk "suspended from prayer" with whom "no one has any liberty of praying"; Vincent's "unity of communion and of the faith."

---

## World Meaning

At Tours communion is a thing one refuses, is forced into, and pays for. The synod at Saragossa condemned the Priscillianists and "added that if any one should admit the condemned persons to communion, he should understand that the same sentence would be pronounced against himself." At Trier "those bishops with whom Martin would not hold communion went in terror to the king." To save lives Martin at last "held communion" with them for a moment — and afterwards, "when it happened that he cured some of the possessed more slowly and with less grace than usual, he at once confessed to us with tears that he felt a diminution of his power on account of the evil of that communion in which he had taken part for a moment through necessity." An angel confirms the grief and bids him renew his virtue; and he "never again did he attend a synod." Communion here is fellowship among bishops with consequences in the saint's body.

At Marseilles it is the right to pray with the brethren, and its loss is the house's sharpest sanction short of expulsion. "If one of them has been suspended from prayer for some fault which he has committed, no one has any liberty of praying with him before he performs his penance on the ground," because "one who is suspended from prayer is, as the Apostle says, 'delivered unto Satan'"; and whoever "dares to hold communion with him in prayer before he has been received by the elder" shares his condemnation. The Egyptians meet on Saturday and Sunday "for the purpose of Holy Communion," and Sunday's single office is "the Lord's communion"; what Gaul did about the sacrament's frequency, Cassian's own text does not say. At Lérins Vincent names the whole: the approved masters are those "remaining in the unity of communion and of the faith," and a part that cuts itself off "from the communion of the universal faith" is to be left for the whole body.

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
"Communion" as the sacrament only (the eucharist, "taking communion"); or as a denominational body ("the Anglican Communion"); excommunication as a legal penalty.

**World Hearing:**
Ecclesial fellowship with spiritual weight — refused to bishops who urged the sword, forced once and paid for in diminished power; the right to pray with the brethren, lost by a fault and restored by penance on the ground; "the unity of communion and of the faith" as the mark of an approved master; the sacrament inside this, not the whole of it.
