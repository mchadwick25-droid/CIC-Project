## Quick Meaning

In Cassian's Marseilles, Abbot Piamun's name for the "reprehensible" third kind of monk — "rightly named in the Egyptian language Sarabaites" — who make "their renunciation only as a public profession, i.e., before the face of men," live "two or three together," build houses "calling them monasteries," and remain "perfectly free and their own masters," "never submitting to the precepts of the gospel" or "to the will of the Elders."

---

## World Meaning

The Sarabaite is what the world calls a monk without a boundary. He has withdrawn "from the congregations of the Cœnobites," is not "subject to the will of the Elders, or, taught by their traditions," and has made "renunciation only as a public profession, i.e., before the face of men"; such men "either continue in their homes devoted to the same occupations as before," or build "cells for themselves and calling them monasteries remain in them perfectly free and their own masters." Those who "shirk the severity of the monastery, and live two or three together in their cells, not satisfied to be under the charge and rule of an Abbot," are the same. Against the coenobites "crucified daily to this world and made living martyrs," "the others are cast down into hell by the lukewarmness of their purpose" — and, Piamun says, in other provinces "we have found that this third class of Sarabaites flourishes." A fourth kind, the false anchorite, takes the name of the desert without its training. Gaul hears the category as Egypt's; no one at Tours uses it, though Anatolius wears the profession falsely there.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:** a technical term for a recognized monastic order or rule, or a later judgment that any monk living outside a large communal house was doing it wrong by Benedictine-era standards.

**World Hearing:** Egypt's own name — used inside the world, not imposed on it later — for a specific and already-recognized failure: renunciation performed publicly but never submitted to an elder's tradition. The category exists to be avoided, not classified.
