## Quick Meaning

For this world the formative bond is a named man and those formed by watching him — Martin's "eighty disciples, who were being disciplined after the example of the saintly master," Clarus "a disciple of Martin's," Sulpitius mourning "our master, Martin" — which the south keeps as elder and junior, and which Vincent turns on doctrine: the true Catholic is "not a teacher but a disciple, not a leader but a follower."

---

## World Meaning

At Tours the whole house is a master and his disciples. "There were altogether eighty disciples, who were being disciplined after the example of the saintly master"; Clarus, "a disciple of Martin's who had lately died," is seen in vision ascending "in the same way as I had seen his master"; and Sulpitius's second letter is written because he had not described "the death of our master, Martin." Martin himself points his disciple to a further model — Paulinus "was the man who should be made the object of our imitation, adding that the present age was fortunate in possessing such a model of faith and virtue." Gallus explains Martin's miracles as those of "that disciple of Christ, imitating the miracles performed by the Saviour." A disciple is formed by what he sees his master do, and becomes in turn what other men see.

In the south the same bond is stated as a rule. The junior takes his examples "from a very few or indeed from one or two only and not from too many"; Paul himself was sent to Ananias, and "the Lord does not Himself show the way of perfection to anyone who having the opportunity of learning" despises "the teaching and training of the Elders." Abbot Chaeremon, asked why he does not relax the rule for guests, gives the master's side: "the authority of a teacher will never be strong unless he fixes it in the heart of his hearer by the actual performance of his duty." At Lérins Vincent takes the word out of the cell and applies it to the faith itself: whoever keeps the deposit is bound "to be not an author but a keeper, not a teacher but a disciple, not a leader but a follower." In this world even the doctor of the Church is a disciple first, and a master's word is worth only what his life has already done.

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A student and a teacher of doctrine; "discipleship" as a program or curriculum; "master" as a degree or as domination.

**World Hearing:**
A man formed by living beside a named master and watching what he does — his power at Tours, his rule at Marseilles — whose authority is only as strong as his own performance of it; and, for the faith, a keeper who refuses to be an author.
