## Failed point: corrected wording

### gregory-nyssa-on-becoming-god (FAIL)

Failing point: the round 3 sentence "For this last form of God's presence among us is not the same as that former presence." It turns the edition's concessive clause ("For although this last form ... is not the same as that former presence") into a grounding statement. "Although" is lost, and the "For" moves from the main claim onto the concession.

Faithful wording: keep the concession and the main clause in one sentence, so that "For" governs both, as in the edition. It replaces the two sentences "For this last form ... former presence. Even so, ... both then and now.":

> For although this last form of God's presence among us differs from that former one, his existence among us is still shown equally, then and now.

That sentence has 26 words, which is within "about 25". "Differs from that former one" renders "is not the same as that former presence". "Is still shown equally, then and now" renders "still ... equally both then and now is evidenced". Nothing is added.

The first restored sentence ("For who, surveying the universe...") passes as proposed. Everything else in the round 3 corrected rendering is outside this read and stays as it is.
