## Section 1 — World Identity

**World name (as the world would name itself):** the churches of Cappadocia and Pontus that held the faith of Nicaea, from the years after the great persecutions to the death of the last of the great household — years that cost us Eupsychius too, at Caesarea, under the last of the persecuting emperors (c. 325 – c. 394) (scholarly registry label: Cappadocian Christianity, world_id `cappadocian-trinitarian`).
**World code:** cappadocian
**Temporal scope:** c. 325 – c. 394 (the ecology is richest c. 360–383; the ending is a transformation window, 381–394 — Doc_01)
**Geographic scope:** Cappadocia (Caesarea, Nazianzus, Nyssa, Tyana, the plateau villages), Pontus along the Iris (Annisa, Neocaesarea, Ibora), Armenia Minor (Sebaste); extension arcs to Athens (education) and Constantinople (the 379–381 mission)
**Community character:** a provincial church of fresh martyr-memory and old founding-lineage, gentry households and plateau villages, which — pressed for two reigns by a court confessing otherwise — bound its whole life into the exact glorification of the God no mind can compass, and built, out of an ascetic ferment and a famine, a common life with a guest-door and a poorhouse

## Section 3 — Formation Logic (from Doc_07 2E; usability-tested)

**Fundamental aim:** the eusebēs — the reverent one: the Name held exactly, the tongue schooled to reach without seizing, the psalms by heart, the door open and the surplus already the poor's, tears permitted and despair refused, stillness sought and the summons obeyed — the whole aimed at likeness to God (theōsis) without terminus. Intensified form: the brother or sister of the common life; public form: the trustworthy conscript; baseline: the reverent householder.
**Mechanism:** reception before analysis, repetition before articulation. The formula said over you before you can weigh it; psalms sung until they interpret the singer; customs kept because handed down; feasts and fasts training the affections by the calendar's repetition; doctrine arriving as the explanation of what you already do — argument honored, always downstream of worship, always disciplined by the creature-Creator interval.
**Arc:** the household's lamp → hearer → baptism into the Name (commonly after adult delay, scolded) → long fidelity (psalmody, feasts, alms, the canons' graded road back after failure) → for some, the brotherhood's intensification → for a conscripted few, office fled and accepted → the deathbed inside the resurrection's grammar, and the horizon of endless approach.
**Register note (world is strand-singular):** the arc runs through nested registers — household, congregation, brotherhood, see — inhabited by the same families and persons; the interpenetration is the world's signature (Doc_01 §3).
**Logic ↔ gravities:** Gravity 1 supplies the charter and the epistemic spine; Gravity 2 the social body; Gravity 3 the weather it was built to survive — the system is **doxology institutionalized under hostile weather** (Doc_07's central finding), its instruments portable (formula, psalm, custom, alms) because for two generations nothing fixed could be trusted to stay in friendly hands.

## Section 4 — Ecological Summary

**4A Emotional texture:** doxological awe with tender grief — the same trained capacity holding fullness and limit together at both poles: "true, and not enough" said of a word for God and of a deathbed hope. Festal joy (panēgyris), hēsychia's ache, friendship's extravagant warmth and documented wounds. Dangerous states: presumption before God above all (the confident definer as emblematic disaster); envy, ambition, anger (the world's own homily-catalogue); despair in grief; the hoarder's deadness.
**4B Philosophical ecology:** reality divides once — Uncreated and created, nothing between (the era's fight was precisely against populating the middle); the interval (diastēma) uncrossable from below, inhabited from above (energeiai; theōsis as participation without fusion); the person as eikōn — social by constitution, royal down to the leper and the slave, unbounded in calling; time as contest-era with a direction the reigns cannot alter.
**4C Authority structures:** inherited, weighed, and suffered-for — Scripture supreme; the baptismal formula as the one authority every register shares; the creed of the 318; the unwritten customs (theorized as carriers — paradosis); founders' lineage; recognized holiness; household elders; the episcopate and councils. No functioning center (hostile court, distant sees) — authority distributed and portable, which is why exile fails against it. Ordination against the will as authority's credential (sought = suspect; fled and accepted = trustworthy).
**4D Boundary structures:** nearly every boundary a *near* boundary — the Homoian court-church (a preposition apart, the sword mostly on that side), the Eunomians (rebaptism at the far edge; graded reception rules answering), the radicals (Gangra's line defending household, marriage, feast), the old religion (calendar and classroom more than battle-line), the frontier churches (porous, pastoral). Boundary logic: precision without cruelty — distinctions made finely so mercy can be administered exactly (graded penance, graded reception, graded speech).

## Section 7 — The World's Tensions (held as held)

**Hēsychia against the summons** — the true desire for stillness against love's conscription; enacted in the world's most consequential careers (the apology for flight; Sasima; the resignation at the council). Cost of stillness: the church's emergency unmanned; cost of the summons: the interior life spent. Never resolved — the world's greatest friendship broke on it and its greatest orations argue it. Formation: the trustworthy conscript as ideal.
**Precision against reserve** — everything true may not be said aloud today: the legislator's measured economy about the Spirit against allies' impatience for candor; defended by his friend, resented by others; motives Contested. Formation: speech-registers as moral discipline — and a live question about truthfulness the world did not close.
**Renunciation against order** — the fire and the hearth: Gangra's censures against the radicals; the ordered brotherhood as answer; the fierce ones unreconciled. Formation: askēsis refereed by the church, resented by the fiery.
**Athens against the fishermen** — the schooled instrument against the simple faith it serves: Julian pressing from outside (the classics reclaimed for the gods), the Eunomian spectacle warning from within (logic worshipped), and the world's own answer — dialectic humbled by the interval — never quite quieting either side.
**The victory question (inside 1 and 3):** the world's confession became the empire's law in its last years, and the world's record of *being pressed* must not be mistaken for a settled innocence about power — its own uses of power (Sasima's jurisdictional politics; canonical coercion; the naming that erased opponents) are carried undefended (Critic Finding 7), and what its bishops did as the establishment's touchstones after 381 is thinly documented (named gap).

## Section 10 — Integrative Observation (verbatim from Doc_07 Section 5 — Status: Verbatim)

> In this world, reverence is one skill exercised at every scale: eusebeia is the trained refusal to grasp — the tongue does not grasp at God's essence (it says what was given and bows), the hand does not grasp at the surplus (it opens toward the image-bearer at the door), the heart does not grasp at office (it flees, and serves when seized), and grief does not grasp at the beloved (it weeps, and lets the resurrection keep them). The world's enemies in every register are the graspers — the definer, the hoarder, the ambitious, the despairing — and its formation is the long schooling of a creature to live open-handed before the God it cannot compass and the neighbor it cannot own: precision without possession, all the way down. Lose this and every practice in the world falls apart into unrelated pieces — dogmatics here, charity there, monasticism elsewhere; hold it and the doxology, the granary, the guest-door, and the deathbed are one motion.
