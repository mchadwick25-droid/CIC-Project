## 1. Candidate Generation (traceable to Doc_02 evidence streams)

Candidates were generated from recurrence across multiple evidence streams, not general impression. Each lists its streams; single-stream, single-author, or asymmetrically transmitted candidates are AG-flagged at generation, per the Framework.

1. **The Triune God confessed at the edge of the knowable** (in the world's own words: the akatalēptos God rightly glorified — right doxa against those who would define him and those who would demote the Spirit). Streams: dogmatic-polemical corpus (both Contra Eunomium projects; the Theological Orations; On the Holy Spirit); liturgical-customary evidence (the doxology controversy; the baptismal formula argued as doctrine's warrant; the unwritten-customs argument); conciliar-credal stream (Nicaea's defense; 381 and its creed; the communion law); catechetical stream (the Catechetical Oration; baptismal orations); even the crowd stream (Nyssen's money-changers complaint — the argument reached the marketplace).
2. **The ascetic reordering of life toward koinōnia and the poor** (renunciation ordered into common life with a guest-door: household → sisterhood/brotherhood → poorhouse). Streams: ascetic-legislative corpus (the Asketikon; the embedded community questions); the Gangra canons (the disorder that ordering answered); philanthropic institutions (the Basileias — letters and Or. 43); the poverty homilies (famine, barns, usury — Basil and Nyssen; Nazianzen's On the Love of the Poor); formation narrative (the Vita's household-become-community).
3. **Paideia converted** (the Greek formation ladder repossessed for Christ: Athens used, the classics as bee-flowers, rhetoric in the pulpit; the counter-pressures — Julian's edict outside, "fishermen's simplicity" impatience inside). Streams: Address to Young Men; the invectives against Julian and the school-edict crisis; the Libanius correspondence; the whole corpus's rhetorical fabric; the anti-Eunomian method itself (logic disciplined by reverence against logic worshipped).
4. **The martyrs' land** (the last persecutions' dead as patrons: the Forty, Gordius, Mamas, Julitta; the panēgyris calendar; family relics). Streams: festal homilies (multiple authors); the Vita's estate-shrine and relic notices; the region's founding confessor-memory (grandparents' generation).
5. **The household as formation lineage** (the Christian gentry family as the world's seminary: grandmother to mother to sister to brothers; leadership emerging from converted households; the funeral orations' domestic saints). Streams: Vita Macrinae; the funeral orations on family (Caesarius, Gorgonia, the elder Gregory); Basil's own account of his formation inheritance (the grandmother's Thaumaturgan teaching — letters at work level); the sheer prosopography of the world's leadership (one family: three bishops, one didaskalos, one monk). **AG flag at generation** (added at Revision 1 — the original run raised this concern only at testing, against the Framework's point-of-generation rule): the evidence base is substantially one family's and one circle's self-memorialization (the Vita, the funeral orations, Basil's own inheritance claims) — a single-network sourcing risk flagged before testing; the modest against-the-grain controls are weighed at §3.1.
6. **The bishop as public patron under famine and empire** (grain, tax appeals, the poorhouse, the prefect's bench, the governor's mercy sought). Streams: famine homilies; correspondence with officials; Or. 43's public-Basil narrative; the canonical letters (discipline as governance).
7. **Hēsychia against the summons** (the pull between stillness and office: the Pontus retreat against the metropolis; flight from ordination; the philosopher conscripted). Streams: Basil's early letters (the retreat ideal) against his career; Nazianzen's Oration 2, the flight narratives, De vita sua, the Sasima wound; Nyssen the unwilling administrator (Basil's own complaints of his brother's unfitness). AG flag at generation: articulated almost wholly by the three authors about themselves — but it is also *enacted* in their documented careers, which is evidence of a different kind than self-description.
8. **The contested church under the contested empire** (the situation: reign-by-reign policy swings — Constantius, Julian, Valens, Theodosius; exiles and depositions; the provincial division of 372; the Nicene minority's long endurance to sudden victory). Streams: imperial-legal stream; the exile correspondence; the Constantinople mission; the invectives; Or. 43's Valens material.
9. **Grief schooled by resurrection** (the era's dense consolation culture: funeral orations, deathbed dialogues, consolation letters, infant-death treatises). Streams: consolatory corpus across all three authors; the Vita's deathbed; the family's serial bereavements as the texts' occasions. AG flag: genre-dense but circle-internal — is it the world's or this bereaved family's?

**On Candidate 1's Formation score (the decisive positive case).** The risk was that Trinitarian doctrine would test as a *conclusion* the world reached rather than a force that *formed* people. The evidence says otherwise, in an unusually direct chain: the fight was fought over the words ordinary worship uses (a doxology's preposition; the baptismal formula), argued *from* worship ("we must believe as we are baptized, and glorify as we believe" — the era's own logic, work-level), and pressed down to the marketplace (the money-changers complaint — hostile witness to popular doctrinal talk); catechesis, hymn-practice, festal preaching, and canonical discipline all carry it. Whoever was baptized, sang, or kept a feast in this world was being formed *by* the contested confession, not merely alongside it. The Trinitarian-theological labor was itself a lived formation practice — this build's assignment named that as a candidate distinctive, and the tests confirm it, with the streams listed above as the warrant.

**On Candidate 8's Formation score (the decisive moderation).** The imperial contest is everywhere (repetition, dependency, explanatory, persistence all strong): it made exiles, forced the ecumenical diplomacy, conditioned every public act. But the Formation test asks whether the ordinary Christian's *becoming* runs through it — and the formation instruments the evidence shows (household teaching, baptism, psalmody, festival, brotherhood, alms) are conditioned by the contest, not constituted by it. This mirrors, structurally, the Early Latin build's finding about its establishment gravity — noted here as a convergent *pattern across builds* (situational gravities recur), not as a template imported: the scoring was done on this world's evidence first, and the parallel observed after.

**On Candidate 5 (the assignment's family-network candidate).** Repetition is strong but the *visibility* is suspect: the family-formation evidence is overwhelmingly one family's self-memorialization (Vita; funeral orations; Basil on his grandmother). Is the household-lineage the world's formation pattern, or this family's — projected onto the world by the accident that this family wrote the record? Against-the-grain controls exist but are modest: the elder Gregory's household (a different family — Nonna forming her Hypsistarian husband and her children); Basil's canonical and homiletic asides assuming household religious formation; the enrolled widows/virgins implying household-based female estates. Verdict: the *pattern* (formation begins in the believing household, and the region's leadership rose from such households) is Widely Accepted; its *centrality as an organizing gravity* cannot be established beyond the writing families — the Persistence test scores moderate for exactly this reason, and the classification below reflects it. The family texture is real, distinctive, and partly a transmission artifact — the distinction Doc_01 §5 demanded is hereby made rather than collapsed.

**On Candidate 7 (hēsychia against the summons).** The AG risk named at generation stands: as *discourse*, it is three authors writing about themselves. But the Interaction and Explanatory tests rescue it: it is enacted in documented careers (retreats really taken, ordinations really fled and really submitted to, the Sasima rupture really suffered); it explains otherwise-odd public facts (why the world's greatest rhetor spent his prime in flight; why its metropolitan kept writing longingly of a riverbank he never returned to); and it interacts with nearly every other candidate (it is the ascetic candidate's interior cost, the paideia candidate's renunciation problem, the patron-bishop candidate's shadow). It is a genuine tensional force of the world's formed core — with its social breadth honestly bounded (the plateau farmer was not fleeing ordination).

**On Candidate 10 (precision against reserve — tested at Revision 1).** The risk ran both ways: that testing would either dissolve this candidate into candidate 1 (mere texture of the confession-gravity), or ratify it because the classification had already asserted it. Neither happened. *Repetition:* strong — the tension recurs across four genuinely distinct streams (the treatise's own architecture: equal glorification argued to the edge of the word and the word withheld; the correspondence's documented quarrel over that withholding; the encomium's posthumous defense; the council's doxological-form article). *Dependency:* moderate — the shape of the world's central pneumatological instruments (the treatise; the 381 article) depends on it, as does Basil's communion-diplomacy with the wavering (holding the weak in rather than forcing the word); but the formation system's ordinary instruments do not run through it. *Formation:* moderate, with a genuine two-sided structure — as practice it belongs to the formed core (a discipline of speech: what may be said, to whom, when — the Theological Orations open with exactly this discipline), while its objects are the whole congregation (the reserve's stated logic is pastoral: the weak not driven off by a word they cannot yet bear). *Explanatory:* strong — it explains the central treatise's strangest feature (a work everywhere implying what it nowhere plainly says), explains 381's article-form, explains the documented strain between the two friends' practices (the metropolitan's economy against the Theologian's boldness at Constantinople), and explains accusations arriving from both flanks. *Persistence:* moderate, honestly bounded — concentrated in the pneumatological decade (c. 372–381) and among clergy and ascetics; visible across regions (Caesarea, the Nazianzen circle, Constantinople, the council) but not across the social ecology. *Interaction:* strong — reshapes candidate 1 from inside (it is the confession-gravity's tempo question), is fed by candidate 2's constituency (the impatient critics are ascetics — the movement's zeal pressing on the bishop's economy), draws its craft from candidate 3 (audience-fitted speech is rhetoric's own discipline turned pastoral), and is counseled by candidate 8 (a hostile court made economy prudent as well as pastoral). **Verdict: survives as Tensional** — a documented argument within the winning side, distinct from the fight with opponents, unresolved in-period (the sources leave 381's form as either reserve's vindication or its monument, and so does this document). It does not survive as anything more: too decade- and circle-bounded for Supporting. The original classification is confirmed — now with a testing record instead of an assertion.

**On Candidate 11 (renunciation against order — tested at Revision 1).** The strongest of the three late-named tensions, and the one whose missing test record was least defensible, since its evidence is largely conciliar and legislative — the build's own preferred against-the-grain material. *Repetition:* strong — censures (Gangra's twenty canons), legislation (the Asketikon's ordering answers and embedded questions), correspondence (the Eustathius arc; the canonical letters' ascetic cases), and the homiletic record (renunciation preached and its excesses walked back), across the world's whole span (Gangra in the c. 340s to the Rules' developing recensions). *Dependency:* strong–moderate — Primary 2's entire institutional form is the answer to this tension (Doc_01 names the ordering of the ferment as one of the world's principal labors); the household-boundary rules, the slave-reception provisions, and the fast-and-feast discipline all depend on where this line is drawn. *Formation:* strong where the line operates — obedience against self-willed askesis is the ordered community's formative core, and the Gangra line also formed the ordinary parish (the canons defend the married priest, the household, the feast: the village's ordinary formation was precisely what the radicals disrupted). *Explanatory:* strong — explains why the Asketikon exists at all (a movement that needed legislating), the guest-door design (renunciation aimed toward the poor instead of against the social bond), the Eustathius relationship's whole arc, and the movement's breadth and its policing. *Persistence:* strong–moderate — region-wide (the ferment is Anatolian: Sebaste through Pontus and Cappadocia) and span-long, with the honest asymmetry that one side is voiceless (the radicals survive only in hostile catalogues; their self-understanding is Inferential/Thin). *Interaction:* strong — competes with candidate 2 definitionally while sharing its root conviction (the fracture is over the ordering, not over renunciation — Doc_01's fracture line 1); competes with candidates 4 and 5 (fasts kept against the feasts; households dissolved); is policed by candidate 6 (councils and canons are episcopal instruments); reinforces and is reinforced by candidate 7 (one renunciatory pull feeds both the retreat and the separatist temptation — the Basilian retreat itself began under Eustathian inspiration, per the carried Gribomont line); and overlaps candidate 1 in persons (the ascetic mentor become the pneumatological adversary). **Verdict: survives as Tensional, comfortably** — a persistent counter-force never fully retired in-period (its live phase ends only with the institutionalization the forces analysis maps at Doc_08 3B-3). Confidence split named at §3.2: the tension's existence and the ordering's response are Documented; the radical side's inner life is Inferential/Thin — a divergence carried, not resolved, and itself constitutive of the classification (a voiceless party cannot anchor more than a counter-force finding).

**On Candidate 12 (Athens against the fishermen — tested at Revision 1).** The test that mattered most here was whether this is a gravity at all or merely candidate 3's interior texture — the original run split it out at classification without showing why. The testing gives a real but bounded answer. *Repetition:* strong — the tension recurs in the paideia corpus (the Address is a defense, and a defense implies live suspicion), the imperial-legal stream (the edict — documented in the adversary's own voice), the dogmatic-polemical stream (the double posture: refuting the dialectician while denouncing dialectic worshipped; the money-changers complaint), and the ascetic-legislative stream (the Rules' subordination of secular learning). *Dependency:* moderate — Supporting Gravity 4 (paideia converted) is constituted by the management of this tension, and the anti-Eunomian method depends on its resolution-in-practice (use disciplined by reverence); the wider ecology's instruments do not. *Formation:* moderate–strong within its register — the Address is literally a formation document (what to take, what to leave — the bee-flowers discipline as taught practice), and the brotherhoods' book-discipline is formative; the plateau's formation does not pass through it (the same elite boundary as its parent candidate, named rather than resolved). *Explanatory:* strong — explains why the Address exists, why the world's most rhetorical preachers keep praising unlettered simplicity in high style, why Julian's edict struck where it struck (the enemy's testimony to the instrument's strategic weight), and why the era's most dangerous opponent was read as the instrument worshipped (Eunomius as the cautionary spectacle). *Persistence:* moderate — elite-register-bounded, but temporally persistent (the edict of 362 through the late anti-Eunomian works) and visible along the whole education arc (Caesarea–Constantinople–Athens). *Interaction:* strong — it is candidate 3's interior countercurrent ("converted" paideia is precisely paideia held under this tension); reshapes candidate 1's method-question (how the confession may be argued); is weighted by candidate 2 (the ordered communities institutionalize the simplicity pole while being founded and legislated by Athens men — the world living both poles at once); meets candidate 4 at the shrine (the learned panegyric serving the unlettered martyr — the register-crossing §5 maps); and shares an axis with candidate 10 (both discipline theological speech, along distinct axes — 10 governs how much of the confession is said, 12 governs the instrument it is said with; the Theological Orations' opening discipline carries both at once). **Verdict: survives as Tensional — but narrower than the original classification implied, and the boundary must be carried:** this is the formed-and-lettered world's tension, real wherever the ladder reached and enacted from both directions (edict without, impatience within), not a plateau-wide pull. The alternative disposition — folding it back into Supporting 4 as that gravity's internal texture — was seriously considered at this revision and declined on two grounds: the tension has its own force-history (Julian's edict attacks the instrument, not the conversion project; the internal impatience attacks from the opposite side — Supporting 4 is the achieved synthesis, this is the pressure the synthesis never dissolves), and its interaction row is distinct from Supporting 4's (§6's matrix shows candidate 3 *competing* with candidate 2 at this same junction while candidate 12 only *reshapes* it — a different relationship, not a mirrored one). The fold-in alternative is recorded at §9 for the external reviewer rather than erased.

### 3.3 Forces Test (how candidates behave under the Doc_01/Doc_02 forces; aligned with Doc_08 Section 5)

- **The Triune confession (1)** sharpens under every hostile force and relaxes under none: Homoian court pressure produces the doxology stand and the treatise; Eunomian confidence produces the incomprehensibility precision; the ascetic rupture (Eustathius) produces the pneumatological line in the sand. Under the Theodosian settlement it wins — and the world's distinct militancy dissolves with the victory (see Doc_08 Cell 3A): the gravity's own success ends the world. That full life-cycle marks an organizing force of the first order.
- **The ascetic-philanthropic reordering (2)** absorbs the radical ferment (Gangra's disorder becomes the ordered brotherhood), answers the famine (the poorhouse), and disciplines wealth (the homilies) — it takes every social force the period throws and converts it into formation structure. It also survives the world's end (the Rules outlive the region's moment) — the mark of substance rather than situation.
- **Paideia converted (3)** is forced into self-consciousness by Julian (the edict makes the classics a battlefield) and by Eunomius (dialectic worshipped requires dialectic disciplined) — its two great crises are both external provocations, confirming it as a real force while explaining its elite boundary.
- **The martyrs' land (4)** is the last persecutions' direct deposit; the imperial peace transposes it (dying remembered, not expected) — same transposition-structure the Latin build found, but with a regional inflection: here the memory is *fresh* (grandparents, not great-great-grandparents) and *familial* (relics in the estate chapel).
- **The household as formation lineage (5)** *(notation added at Revision 1)*: held throughout the span but never directly tested by a hostile force — the forces that touch it are generative (Doc_08 1B-2) and archival (2B-2: the transmission force amplifies its visibility — the structural confirmation of §3.1's caveat) — and it closes with the generation itself (3B-1: the world's ending is a series of family funerals). A gravity whose force-history is amplification-by-its-own-archive is exactly what the Supporting-with-caveat classification says it is.

## 7. Differentiation Adjudication (the Phase Status blur risk, addressed on the evidence)

**Against Alexandria, the divergence is structural, not decorative.** (a) **Formation axis:** Alexandria's documented axis is school-exegetical (text, teacher, ascent through reading); this world's is doxological-communal (worship's words, household, brotherhood, festival) — the lexicons barely overlap at their signature terms (this world's Tier 1 slate has no hermeneutical term in it; the finding emerged from the evidence, not by design). (b) **The Teacher-Bishop tension resolves oppositely.** Alexandria holds the didaskalos and the bishop in institutional tension (a school the church must manage). Here there is no school to manage: the teacher, the ascetic, and the bishop fuse in the same persons — and the tension goes *inward*, becoming this world's Tensional Gravity 8 (the philosopher dragged to the chair; the flight literature; Sasima). Alexandria's tension is between institutions; Cappadocia's is inside vocations. (c) **Shared inheritance, different function:** Origen is present here as instrument and quarry (anthologized, corrected, used against Eunomius), not as founding contested patrimony; allegory serves preaching and consolation, not a school's curriculum. (d) **Blur discipline for the build:** the shared Nicene confession is a genuine overlap — the differentiator is *where the confession lives* (here: in a doxology's preposition and a baptismal formula fought for under a hostile court; the marketplace arguing it), and every downstream document must keep that located texture rather than generic "Nicene theology."
