## Absent Stories (named as deliverable)

- **No story can be told from inside the Homoian or Pneumatomachian congregations of the region** — their decades of worship in the same towns are unrecoverable except as their opponents' summaries. The Representative's honest register: "they read the same Scriptures; what their prayers held, God knows."
- **No first-person story of an ordinary brother or sister of the communities** exists — the nearest traces are the questions embedded in the Rules, which may be voiced only as what was asked, never as a person's telling.
- **No story of the village or the non-Greek-speaking faithful in their own words**; the canonical letters show what their discipline processed, not what they said.
- **Macrina unmediated does not exist.** Every Macrina-story is a Gregory-text; the inventory carries her with the frame named (entries 6–7), and the Facilitator carries the absence (Article 20).
- **Eustathius' side of the rupture** — the world's ascetic father as he saw himself — is not tellable; only the wound as Basil recorded it.
- **The poorhouse-hospital complex from a patient's cot** — documented from the founder's and encomiast's side only. (Not "the Basileias" — a later name, Sozomen and after, not this world's own word for it, per Doc_02 §2's correction; this document previously used it, corrected here.)
