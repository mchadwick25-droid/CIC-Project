# Source Registry — The Hussite and Bohemian Brethren Movement

Per `Source_Registry_Template.md` V1.0. Co-equal Step 2 output with `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`. **Living document: append-only.** A row is never renumbered or deleted. Doc_02 may not name a source in support of a specific claim unless that source has a row here. Rows are entered only for works that the corpus map assigns to this world, and for works that are not vendored. A vendored work assigned only to another world is recorded as a candidate in `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` (section E), not here. Numbers 25 to 28 are not used, and they are not reused.

**Status:** Approved to proceed with Doc_02, after independent review (`Review-Artifacts/Round3_Recheck_Review.md`, clear). The saturation statement at the end is not closed (see "Saturation statement").
**Boundary checked against:** `Doc_01_World_Identification_Boundaries_Orientation.md` (c. 1402–1517; Bohemia and Moravia; three strands: Utraquist, Taborite, Unity). Boundary Status is judged by what a source speaks for, never by the publication date of a modern edition.

**Type:** P (Primary) / S (Secondary) / M (Material/External) / L (Period Lexicon).
**Confidence (citation reliability):** A = the Licensed-For passages were read and matched in the vendored file by a structure marker (letter number, chapter heading, running-head page, or chapter title). For an Excluded row, A means that the passage which makes it a Named Comparandum was read and matched. B = a specific work or locus is named and exists in the vendored file, but the Licensed-For content was not itself re-read. C = a real author and work, no locus checked, not vendored. D = a body or genre of material, no single text named. No row is E; a source with no traceable basis is not entered.
**Structure markers:** line numbers are lines of the vendored file in `cic/texts/`. "Letter" numbers are Workman & Pope's own roman numerals. "p." numbers for *De Ecclesia* are read from the running heads and were checked against the printed chapter headings.
**Scan-quality note:** all vendored files are OCR text and carry OCR errors. Quotations in this Registry and in Doc_02 were matched against the files after whitespace and line-break-hyphen normalisation only. No OCR correction was applied to any quoted word.
**Rights:** each vendored row states its rights basis from the file's own header. For a work that is not vendored, the row states the basis as far as the builder knows it. The Library must verify it before any acquisition.

Instruments not reached: the bibliographies of Kaminsky (1967), Fudge (1998, 2002, 2010) and Šmahel (2002); the *Cambridge History of Christianity* vol. 4 bibliographies; Czech national and library catalogues; archive.org, HathiTrust and Google Books full-text search; the bibliographies in Goll and in Lützow's other works.

**The shelf search.** The shelf holds 315 text files (277 `.txt` and 38 `.xml`). A whole-word, case-sensitive search for "Hus" or "Huss" matches 53 files. A case-insensitive search matches 106, and the extra matches were not read. Many matches are false ones, such as the biblical name Uz spelled "Hus" in the Wycliffite Bible. Files with real Hus or Hussite material beyond the eight assigned works are all assigned to other worlds: Foxe, Vol. III; the Loserth introduction to Wyclif's *De Ecclesia*; Luther's *Works*, vols. 2 and 3, and the Wace–Buchheim volume; Van Braght's *Martyrs Mirror*; Vedder on Hubmaier; Kempis, *Founders of the New Devotion*; the *Fasciculi Zizaniorum*; and Sarpi. They are recorded as candidates in `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` (section E), and none is a row here. A search for "Bohemian Brethren", "Unity of the Brethren", "Unitas Fratrum" and "Bohemian Confession" matches eight files. Three are false matches ("community of the brethren" and similar phrases in monastic texts). Five are real: the Seifferth volume, Luther's *Works* vols. 2 and 3, Van Braght, and Zwingli's *Latin Works* vol. 3 (Heller, 1929).
