**Confidence (citation reliability):** A = the Licensed-For passages were read and matched in the vendored file by a structure marker (letter number, chapter heading, running-head page, or chapter title). B = a specific work or locus is named and exists in the vendored file, but the Licensed-For content was not itself re-read. C = a real author and work, no locus checked, not vendored. D = a body or genre of material, no single text named. No row is E; a source with no traceable basis is not entered.
