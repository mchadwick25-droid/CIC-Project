## 2. Question 1 — What dimensions actually generate meaningful encounter?

The four Primary and one Tensional gravity Phase Two §3 rates RICH, each independently confirmed by Doc_04's own six-test assessment:

- **G1, Pastoral Office as Territorial Flock-Keeping** (Doc_04 §3 Candidate 1: passes all six tests strongly). This world's own ecological hub (Doc_05 §9.1) — every other gravity is exercised by, or tests, this office.
- **G2, Penitential Discipline** (Doc_04 §3 Candidate 2, Cyprian-phase-grounded). The examined, graded road by which a failed member is walked home.
- **G3, Collegial Communion Preserved Despite Disagreement** (Doc_04 §3 Candidate 3, passes all six; the cross-phase synthesis is Widely Accepted, the underlying facts Documented). This world's own clearest structural signature.
- **G6, Sacramental and Ordination Validity Across the Boundary** (Doc_04 §3 Candidate 6, passes all six). The question answered twice, oppositely, a century apart.
- **G8, Confessor-Authority vs. Episcopal-Regulated Peace** (Doc_04 §3 Candidate 8, "passes all six as a persistent, bounded counter-force" — Tensional, Cyprian-phase-bound on a positive check, Doc_05 §2.3).

**What makes these encounter-generating rather than merely well-documented, on the design record's own argument.** Doc_07 §2I (Formation Logic, the convergence lens) is the construction record's own answer: "Every other lens converges here, and each converges on the same shape: a rite that ends in restoration... a pastor who will not stand apart from those who failed... a refusal of any single decisive test... a graded road back rather than a verdict... one named man answerable for these particular people... and a boundary that disagreement does not breach." These are not four separate facts a participant learns in sequence; they are facets of one formation aim. Phase Four §2's own Dynamic Encounter mechanism — Answerability → the Argued Case → the Road Back — operationalizes this directly, grounded in Doc_05 §5.1's own attested formation arc rather than a conversational technique laid over it.

**Empirical confirmation, not design-argument alone.** Unlike a purely prospective mapping, lpc's own Phase Five already tested these dimensions directly. Probes 1, 2, and 9 (Source-Awareness, Scholarly-Framework) all PASSED cleanly and, on this audit's own reading of their content rather than an explicit gravity tag in Phase Five's own table, are G1/G2/G3-territory (the 256 preface, the certificate process, the office's own answerability) — flagged here as this document's own synthesis, per §1's disclosure discipline, since Phase Five itself does not tag these three probes by gravity. **Probe 13's own pass criteria, stated explicitly in Phase Five's own table, name G6 directly** ("holds G6's own two-phase structure — the question persists, the answer reverses"), not G1/G2/G3, and it PASSED cleanly. **Probe 10's own pass criteria are G8's own defining content** ("responds from within the world's own actual, lived use of the term — the first-phase claim to grant peace"), and its actual generated response is squarely G8 material; it too PASSED cleanly, closing the gap this audit would otherwise leave for G8's own empirical confirmation. ## 3. Question 2 — What dimensions primarily generate information rather than encounter?

Phase One §2's Thinness Mapping names the domains directly, and Phase Six §2 (drawing on the same mapping) restates them in Facilitator-facing terms: the ordinary believer's interior life; the lapsed's own account of themselves (the single most consequential omission, tied directly to Primary gravity G2); a woman's own narrating voice; rural, Punic- and Berber-speaking congregational life; the physical setting and material conditions of worship (administrative rather than architectural); a founding narrative or origin myth (absent, and a positive finding about this world's own self-understanding as the ordinary church, per Doc_07 §2C); and, distinctly, the 133-year interval 258–391 — an absence by design, never to be filled from the neighboring Donatism world.

**One domain does not belong in this list uniformly, and this audit names the distinction Phase One §1.3 and §2 already state rather than flattening it — and names a real gap this checking surfaced, rather than asserting the gap closed.** Worship and liturgical life is rated "Thin, no longer empty" as of a 2026-09-19 finding: Phase One's own record holds that Datus can describe what was *done* — the catechumenate's stages, the renunciation formula, the hand laid on the head, the graded penitential road and its reconciliation ruling — in real, specific detail, with only what was *said* remaining thin (no fixed order of service, consecration formula, or catechetical curriculum survives). **Checked directly against both deployed artifacts rather than assumed carried forward: none of this specific liturgical content — the catechumenate's own stages, the renunciation formula, the imposition of the hand — actually appears in either the Permanent Prompt or the World Capsule Core.** Both artifacts state only the general shape ("taught... in the creed... then baptized," "the creed said back, then the water"), not the richer, specific content Phase One's own 2026-09-19 finding established. This is a genuine, disclosed integration gap between an already-Approved-to-proceed Phase One finding and the deployed artifacts built afterward, not a preserved distinction this audit can credit as accomplished — carried forward at §6.3 below rather than corrected here, consistent with this document's own retrospective-audit scope: this document reports on the construction record, it does not amend already-disposed artifacts.

**A finding worth naming plainly: these domains were not built as information-delivery, and Phase Five's own testing confirms it rather than resting on design-argument alone.** The Permanent Prompt's own governing instruction states the discipline directly: "That was never yours to narrate for them, and you do not tell the listener it is missing." Probes 5 and 6 (Confidence-Under-Thinness) both PASSED under exactly this discipline — Probe 6's own generated response reaches for "not something that came down to us" rather than "the record does not preserve this," the exact meta-commentary form Part Eight's Violation Indicators bar. This is the opposite of doctrinal-coverage framing: a coverage-minded instinct would try to say *more* about these domains to demonstrate thoroughness; this construction is disciplined, at the level of explicit prompt rules, about saying less.

## 5. Question 4 — What dimensions reveal this world's distinctive identity?

The construction record names, independently and at multiple points, the specific features that make this world legible as itself and not generic early Christianity: **answerability itself as the organizing conviction** — "not that the church is pure, nor that it is right, but that it is answerable" (Phase One §1.2; Doc_07 §6, the Integrative Observation); **the capacity to hold ferocious argument and unbroken communion in the same breath** (G3, this world's clearest structural signature, Doc_04 §3 Candidate 3); **the two-phase structure itself with its genuine 133-year silence**, distinguishing this world's own evidentiary shape from any world with a continuous record; **the reversed answer to one persisting question** — what a font gives outside the one church, argued oppositely a century apart by the same office (G6); and **the refusal of the single determining factor** as a recurring shape of reasoning rather than a ninth gravity (Doc_04 §2; Doc_07 §2D).

Doc_07's Cross-Lens Synthesis (§5) independently confirms this same set of facts is what distinguishes lpc from its neighbors: against World #6 (Imperial-Juridical Christianity), the law's *object* differs — readmitting a failed local member versus ordering sees and the state relationship; against World #4 (Donatism), the sharpest contrast this project has, since the two worlds share a corpus, a province, and a century and answer one question oppositely — "when a bishop disagrees irreconcilably, does he preserve communion or build a rival hierarchy?"; against Worlds #9 and #2, formation happens in a congregation on a Sunday, not in a study or a school.

## 6. Question 5 — What dimensions create genuine opportunities for participant exploration?

This category overlaps substantially with §2 and §4 above: G1, G2, G3, G6, and G8-in-its-own-phase are built, per Phase Four's own Dynamic Encounter mechanism, specifically to invite sustained exploration. This audit checked for anything genuinely under-leveraged and found two items worth naming.

**6.1 Two named open construction items, already disclosed rather than newly discovered.** Phase Four §5 names, rather than silently works around, two real, currently unbuilt exploration opportunities: the *Acta Proconsularia Sancti Cypriani*, which if read would move `lpcstory006` (the death of Cyprian) from Tier 3 to Tier 1; and Possidius *Vita Augustini* XIX–XXVII, read at source but not yet built into a Tier 1 story of the ordinary, uneventful pastorate. **Checked directly against the deployed Permanent Prompt rather than assumed carried forward: neither item appears there** — construction-only disclosure of this kind is correctly excluded from the deployed runtime artifact, which contains no construction-only notes of any kind, so this is not itself a defect in the Permanent Prompt. Neither open item is a source-availability absence — both are integration tasks, named precisely as such in Phase Four, and are not resolved here.

**6.2 A genuinely encounter-rich domain where exploration and a real, disclosed risk are tightly coupled, not merely adjacent.** Phase Six §3 develops this fully: the same content that generates this world's richest encounter — the road back, examined and walked toward before being received home — is the content most likely to be engaged by a participant disclosing genuine self-forgiveness distress, and is where a Facilitator's own crisis-redirect register carries the sharpest risk of landing as "prove yourself first" rather than unconditional warmth. This is not a case of an under-leveraged dimension — Phase Four's mechanism fully and deliberately leverages it — but it is the one place in this construction where genuine encounter-richness and a real, named risk are produced by the same mechanism, not two separate facts sitting beside each other.

**6.3 A genuine, unresolved integration gap this audit's own checking surfaced, named rather than corrected here.** §3 above found that Phase One's own 2026-09-19 liturgical-richness finding (the catechumenate's stages, the renunciation formula, the imposition of the hand) does not actually appear in either deployed artifact, which state only the general shape of baptismal preparation. This is not a case of the construction correctly declining to overstate a thin domain — Phase One's own record establishes this content is genuinely available, not thin — it is unbuilt richness that a future revision pass to the Permanent Prompt and World Capsule Core should draw on directly from Phase One §1.3, the same way this construction already draws G1 through G8's own richer content from their own originating documents. Not resolved by this document, consistent with its own retrospective-audit scope.

## 8. Verdict

**On whether the Representative as actually constructed is organized around encounter-generating dimensions or shows evidence of drift toward content-volume or doctrinal coverage:** this audit finds no evidence of such drift. The RICH/MODERATE/THIN calibration set at Phase Two §3 is preserved without erosion through every subsequent phase and into both deployed artifacts; the one domain requiring a corrective distinction (worship and liturgical life, §3 above) was a citation-precision issue caught and fixed at Phase Six's own review, not a drift in the underlying construction; and Phase Five's own adversarial testing empirically confirms, rather than merely asserts, that the RICH gravities function as encounter in practice.

**On whether running this analysis prospectively would have changed anything:** probably not much. The functional work Phase Seven's prospective version would have done was already done at Doc_04's own six-test gravity assessment and Doc_07's own Integrated Ecology Analysis, both completed well before Representative Construction began — this audit's own five-question pass over that record confirms it rather than surfacing anything Doc_04/Doc_07 missed. The two genuinely open exploration opportunities named at §6.1 were already disclosed, by name, in documents this audit is not the first to name them in.

**On the sequencing inversion itself:** it is real, it is named in full at §0, and this document follows the same honest-retrospective-audit resolution the sibling `don` build and Syriac Christianity's own Phase Seven documents already established for this project, rather than pretending to be the prospective document Part Nine's text describes.
