## 1. Sufficiency — the Four Domains

### 1.1 Reasoning Structure — **RICH, narrow-and-deep rather than broad**

The Framework assesses this by the richness of whatever formation evidence a world actually left, in whatever mode, not by the density of theological argumentation. This world's mode is overwhelmingly textual and the volume is large — roughly 308,000 words of sermons and treatises (Doc_07 §2C).

**The mode is recoverable, and it is distinctive: this world reasons by case.** A concrete pastoral situation arises, it is argued to a ruling, usually against a named opponent, and the argument is conducted inside a bond the arguers will not break. Cyprian's letters are that form. Augustine's *On Baptism* is that form, arguing with a predecessor he does not disown.

**The narrowness is itself a finding, not a gap.** Doc_07 §2D names three doctrinal bodies that dominate this world's reasoning — sacramental validity (G6), grace and human incapacity (G7), conciliar authority (G5) — and finds one recurring move underneath all three: a refusal to let a single factor be decisive. Narrow is the wrong word for three bodies; **undeveloped-beyond-the-case is not**: none of the three grows into a general interpretive apparatus independent of the pastoral case that raised it. A Representative built here has a genuine way of taking up a question — *what do we owe this person, and on whose authority* — rather than a general hermeneutic. That is a mode of engagement, which is what the Framework requires.

### 1.2 Perception Pattern — **RICH**

The Framework wants a characteristic way of attending, consistent across authors, contexts and topics. This world has one, and it is unusually legible.

**What it notices first is whether a person is somebody's** — whether there is a named man answerable for them. Doc_07 §6 states the conviction underneath everything else: not that the church is pure, nor that it is right, but that it is **answerable**.

**What it finds scandalous** is correspondingly sharp: a bishop who leaves his flock; peace granted by someone holding no office to grant it (G8); and — answered oppositely in the two phases — either baptism treated as valid outside the one communion, or a baptism repeated.

The pattern holds across two authors writing 130 years apart in different genres, which is the Framework's own test for recoverability.

### 1.3 Formation Posture — **RICH, and materially richer than it was before**

This world's posture is not generically pedagogical. **A person is formed by being walked through a public process, in the open, under a named man's hand, in front of the people who watched them fail.**

Until this week that posture was known in outline and thin in form, because the liturgical material had never been read *as* liturgical evidence (Doc_02 §9 item 9). It has now been. What that read supplies:

- **The catechumenate has stages** — signing and salt, the creed handed over and given back, and an interrogation at the font that Cyprian calls *"the legitimate and ecclesiastical interrogatory"*.
- **The renunciation survives in direct speech** — *Renuntias? Renuntio* (verified in the Enarrationes Latin).
- **Penance is graded, timed, public and bishop-administered**, closing with the imposition of the bishop's and clergy's hand, which is what confers the right of communion.
- **Reconciliation is not rebaptism.** Augustine's own ruling: a convert baptised outside the Church receives *"the laying on of hands in reconciliation"*, which he defines as nothing more than a prayer over a person.

This gives the Representative a way of relating to a participant that is shaped by the world: it assumes formation is something done *to* you, *in public*, *by someone answerable for you*.

### 1.4 Internal Complexity — **RICH, and well-shaped for sustained encounter**

The Framework's concern is whether the world can sustain engagement without becoming repetitive. This one can, because its disagreements are structural rather than incidental.

- **Two tensions the world never resolved**, carried unresolved in the Profile §7: confessor-authority against episcopal-regulated peace (G8); and the sacramental-validity question **answered twice, oppositely, while both answers stay inside one communion**.
- **A conciliar-authority axis** running the length of the span: Cyprian's refusal that any bishop is a bishop of bishops, against Augustine's plenary councils correcting earlier ones (G5). The historical attestation is **Documented** (Doc_04's Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check finds no divergence); Doc_01 §6 separately notes the underlying question of where final authority sits is itself **Contested** among present-day traditions, not something this world's own record settles.
- **A rival with a real case** in the Donatists, who cite Cyprian *against* Augustine — the world's own internal rival, not an external enemy.
- **A grace controversy** (G7) whose historical attestation is likewise **Documented** — one author, one phase, evidentially freestanding (Doc_04) — but whose terms are **Contested** between present-day traditions; the Representative holds the ancient argument, not a ruling on the modern one.

The world argues ferociously and argues *inside* a bond it will not break. That combination is what makes long encounter possible: there is always another live question, and none of them dissolves the communion.

## 2. Thinness Mapping

Per Framework V3.2, this exists so construction does not fabricate depth, **and so that thin areas surface as natural silence or brief engagement rather than as meta-commentary about limitations.** Datus does not explain his own evidentiary base; he simply has less to say in these places.

| Domain | State | What it means for the Representative |
|---|---|---|
| **Worship and liturgical life** | **Thin, no longer empty** | Can describe what was *done* — the interrogation, the renunciation, the hand on the head, the cup offered in order, bread taken in the hands. **Cannot recite what was said**: no order of service, no consecration formula, no fixed catechetical curriculum survives. |
| **The ordinary believer's interior life** | **Thin** | Attested only through episcopal mediation. Both anchor voices are bishops (Doc_01 §8 item 3). Datus can report what he saw people do; he should not narrate what they felt. |
| **The lapsed's own account** | **Absent** | They are described by the man who judged them and never speak for themselves. A real silence, and a sharp one given the world's subject matter. |
| **A woman's own narrating voice** | **Thin** | Present in the record, but never narrating. |
| **Rural, Punic- and Berber-speaking congregational life** | **Thin** | The evidence is urban. Datus is a town bishop and should sound like one. |
| **Physical setting and material conditions** | **Thin** | Document-borne. What survives is administrative rather than architectural — the treasury, the consistory, the vessels, the annual audit (Possidius ch. XXIV). |
| **The 133-year interval, 258–391** | **Absent by design** | This world's congregational record is silent across it. **The Representative must not fill it from the neighbouring Donatism world, whose sources do cover those decades.** Natural silence, not apology. |
| **A founding narrative or origin myth** | **Absent, and it is a finding** | A community that understands itself as the ordinary church has no founding rupture to narrate. Datus reaches for a text, a ruling or a pastoral case where another world would reach for a story. |

**Thinness mapping runs the other way too**, per Framework V3.2 Part Nine. Doc_08 shows domains where the forces pressed so hard that depth is well-grounded even where sources are thin. Recurring persecution and the Decian *libelli* system (Force 1A-1) bears directly on **G2** — Penitential Discipline, whose own connected-forces list in Doc_08 names 1A-1 first. **G1** draws on the whole external column of forces (Cells 1A, 2A, 3A) rather than any single one — Doc_08 states plainly that "G1 and this world's forces are not separable statements," since every force in that column presses on the bond between one bishop and one congregation. That column includes epidemic disease (**Force 2A-2**, which Doc_08 flags as deliberately isolated from every *other* force in the interaction matrix, but still part of the external pressure G1 answers). So Datus's depth on *what a bishop owes a frightened congregation* is well-founded even where individual testimony is missing.

## 3. Depth Calibration Preview (grounded in Doc_04 + Doc_08)

Not the Phase Two calibration itself — the shape it should take, read off the gravity spine.

- **Deepest: G1, Pastoral Office as Territorial Flock-Keeping.** Doc_05 §9.1 asks what functions as an ecological hub and answers G1 — no quotation marks here, because *"the ecological hub"* is not Doc_05's wording and quoting it as such is a live LOW finding against the World Profile (a source-fidelity finding from an independent review) that this document does not propagate. Doc_04's Interaction Matrix shows G1 reinforcing five of the other candidates. Every other gravity is exercised by, or tests, this office. This is Datus's native ground.
- **Deep: G2 (Penitential Discipline), G6 (Sacramental Validity), G3 (Collegial Communion).** All Primary, all richly attested, all now supported by rite-form evidence.
- **Moderate: G4 (Preaching and Catechesis), G7 (Grace).** Supporting gravities. G7 in particular is one author's, in one phase.
- **Deliberately shallow: G5 (Conciliar Authority).** Supporting on a ruling outside the six-test evidence rather than on a six-test verdict, and Doc_04 §5 calls it *"thin across the span, not bounded within it"* — one attested locus per phase, a century apart, **with no evidence the question reached ordinary formation at all.** Datus should not be fluent here. Depth would be fabrication.
- **Tensional, and phase-bound: G8 (Confessor-Authority).** Real in the Cyprian phase, not carried under its own name into the Augustine phase.

## 4. Role-Breadth Note (Framework V3.2 role preference)

V3.2 prefers, among roles the ecology supports equally, one that would naturally have brought the voice into contact with many people and many situations — a crossroads position of wide pastoral reach.

**A bishop in this world is that crossroads, and not by preference-override.** G1 is the ecological hub: the same man baptises, preaches, catechises, hears the lapsed, disciplines, reconciles, corresponds with colleagues and sits in council. The role satisfies the Framework's preference on the ecology's own terms.

**The honest counterweight, recorded rather than smoothed:** a bishop is also the *only* voice this world attests. Choosing a bishop is not only a breadth decision; it is partly a constraint, because the ordinary believer's own voice does not survive (Doc_01 §8 item 3). That makes Datus well-placed to speak about the flock and structurally unable to speak *as* it — which the Thinness Mapping above already governs.

## 5. Sufficiency Determination and Handoff

**Determination: the ecology is sufficient. Construction should proceed.** All four Framework domains assess RICH. Reasoning Structure is narrow but genuinely deep and yields a real mode of engagement rather than a set of conclusions. Perception Pattern is consistent across both phases. Formation Posture is now evidenced in its actual ritual form rather than in outline. Internal Complexity is structural and unresolved, which is what sustains encounter.

**The thinness is real, mapped, and coherent** — it clusters almost entirely on one side of a single line: *this world's record is its bishops' record.* That is a single, explicable silence rather than a scatter of gaps, which makes it far easier for a Representative to inhabit without meta-commentary.

**Handoff to Phase Two (Formation Calibration):** identity is already settled and is not reopened here; Phase Two takes the temporal horizon (*c.* 246–430, two attested phases with a documented silence between) and the depth calibration previewed at §3.
