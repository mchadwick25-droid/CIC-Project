## Quick Meaning

The certificates issued to those who complied with the Decian edict's sacrifice requirement — the administrative mechanism that created the category of the lapsed, including for people who never sacrificed but paid to be recorded as having done so.

---

## World Meaning

The empire did not ask anyone to renounce Christ in writing. It asked for a sacrifice, and then it wrote down that you had made one.

That is the whole mechanism, and it is why the crisis took the shape it did. A demand you could satisfy with money rather than incense produced a class of people who had not, in their own minds, worshipped anything — and who nonetheless held a document saying they had. The paper is the problem. Without it, failure under pressure is a private matter between a person and God. With it, failure is filed.

---

## Ecological Function

This is the administrative fact underneath the lapsed, and therefore underneath penitential discipline and the whole confessor-authority tension. A participant who understands this term understands why the crisis was documentary rather than merely moral: the community was not guessing who had complied.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A modern participant may picture a formal identity document or licence.

**World Hearing:**
A compliance record produced by an empire-wide administrative demand, whose existence is precisely what made failure documentary and public rather than private.
