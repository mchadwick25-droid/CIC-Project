---

## Quick Meaning

For us, catechesis is the instruction of those not yet baptized — the creed and the basic content of belief taught before the water, not after it.

---

## World Meaning

You are taught before you are washed. That order is not an accident and it is not bureaucracy: you are told what you are joining before you join it, and you say it back before anyone washes you.

There is enough of this work that a manual gets written for the people who do it — not a record of one instance but a book about how to do it at all, including what to do when the person in front of you is bored, or clever, or frightened. That is the measure of how ordinary this was.

---

## Ecological Function

Catechesis is the other half of the formation medium, distinguished from preaching by audience. It is where the creed enters, which makes it the practical route by which the church's boundary questions and its account of grace first reach a person. A participant who understands this term understands that entry to this community was taught, staged and examined rather than immediate.

---

## Distortion Risk

**Modern Hearing:**
A modern participant is likely to hear a children's catechism — rote question-and-answer, memorized young, faintly dry.

**World Hearing:**
Adult instruction, before baptism, of people making a consequential change of standing, delivered seriously enough that a working manual for instructors exists.

---

## Key Sources

*On the Catechising of the Uninstructed* (Registry row 15) is a manual for the practice itself, not merely an instance of it; the creedal works (row 18) preach the baptismal creed to catechumens directly; the *Confessions* (row 9) narrates a catechumenate and baptism from the inside; *On the Lord's Prayer* (row 5) is catechetical exposition of the church's own set prayer.

**Evidentiary footing differs from preaching's and is not weaker.** This term is attested through dedicated treatises rather than raw recurrence of a headword — a manual written to instruct instructors is stronger evidence of a practice's centrality than word-frequency would be.
