## 1. What Article 29 actually asks

Quoted from `L1-Foundation/CiC_L1_Constitution_V2_2.docx`, Article 29, read at source:

> **Living Tradition Status Confirmation.** Where a formation world's reconstruction touches a tradition that continues into the present — including where that contact is mediated through a specific figure, practice, institution, or lineage of contested standing among present-day traditions — that world's Living Tradition Status must be formally confirmed before the world is advanced toward freeze. Confirmation establishes how the historical reconstruction is distinguished from any living tradition's present-day self-understanding, identity, or doctrine, per the Scholarly Integrity and Representational Accountability Principle (Article 28). This confirmation is a freeze-eligibility gate… The method of confirmation is governed by the build documents; the requirement that it occur is constitutional.

**Two limbs, and they are not alternatives — the second is a subset the Article names so it cannot be missed:**
- **First limb:** does the reconstruction touch a tradition that continues into the present?
- **Second limb:** is that contact mediated through a specific **figure, practice, institution, or lineage of contested standing among present-day traditions**?

**Note what confirmation produces.** It is not only a yes/no. Article 29 says confirmation *"establishes how the historical reconstruction is distinguished from any living tradition's present-day self-understanding, identity, or doctrine."* The registry's `living_tradition_flag` is the yes/no; **the distinguishing statement is the substance**, and it is what Article 28 and the Permanent Prompt's Living Traditions section will draw on.

## 2. First limb — the answer is yes, and the difficulty is the opposite of scarcity

`Doc_01` §1 already made the observation, and it is the right one:

> this world's core content — an ordinary bishop's territorial, sacramental, congregational care of a local flock — is, **if anything, more diffusely and more widely continuous into the present than any single-see primacy claim**: it is close to the default self-understanding of the parish or diocesan ministry of **most historic Christian communions that have retained the office of bishop or pastor at all**, not a claim particular to one see's own succession.

**That is a yes to the first limb, stated as a difficulty about naming rather than a doubt about touching.** The build has never converted it into an analysis, which is the gap this document closes.

**Where the reconstruction touches present-day traditions, by gravity, from this build's own findings:**

| Gravity | Touchpoint continuing into the present |
|---|---|
| **G1** Pastoral Office as Territorial Flock-Keeping *(Primary)* | The parish and the diocese. A named minister answerable for a bounded local congregation is the ordinary shape of ministry in the Roman Catholic, Orthodox, Anglican, Lutheran and Methodist communions, and in congregational polities without the territorial claim. |
| **G2** Penitential Discipline *(Primary)* | The Roman Catholic sacrament of Penance and Reconciliation stands in direct line from the practice this world argued into existence. The Reformation traditions' rejection of it is equally a relation to this material. |
| **G6** Sacramental Validity *(Primary)* | **Augustine's position is the settled Western one** — a sacrament performed outside the communion is valid though unfruitful — and it is why baptisms are not repeated between communions today. **Cyprian's position lost.** |
| **G5** Conciliar Authority *(Supporting)* | Cyprian's refusal that any bishop is *"a bishop of bishops"* bears directly on the papal-primacy question as present-day traditions contest it. |
| **G7** Grace and Human Incapacity *(Supporting)* | Augustine's grace doctrine is foundational to Catholic, Lutheran and Reformed self-understandings, which do not agree about it. |

**No single tradition can be named as *the* corresponding one.** This world is ancestral to the Western church as a whole, before its divisions. That is a finding about the world, not an evasion: `lpc` is upstream of the fracture lines, so every Western communion has a claim on it and none has an exclusive one.

## 3. Second limb — engaged, and this build's own record already documents it

Article 29's second limb asks about a figure, practice, institution or lineage **of contested standing among present-day traditions**. Three qualify on this build's own evidence.

**(a) Augustine himself.** `Doc_06` §3's contest type for *grace*, assigned and verified in this build:

> The contest is not principally about what Augustine wrote but about **how that position stands to living traditions' use of the word, across the Reformation-era appropriations** and the modern reassessment of whether the position Augustine attacks is the one Pelagius held.

Augustine is claimed by communions that disagree with each other about what claiming him means. That is contested standing in Article 29's exact sense.

**(b) *"Compel them to come in."*** `Doc_06` §3's contest type:

> **The most-cited patristic warrant in the modern historiography of religious coercion**; the argument runs over its relation to later practice, over how far the pastoral-correction framing should be credited, and over whether the three-phase development is a genuine change of mind or a retrospective self-presentation.

This world's Section 8 and its Representative will be asked about this. It is the sharpest reputational exposure in the world.

**(c) Cyprian, whose own ruling was overturned by the tradition that venerates him.** This build established at Doc_01 §5 that Augustine argues *at book length* that Cyprian's specific rebaptism ruling was **wrong**, while claiming his communion — and that the Donatists claimed Cyprian as their own precedent on the same question. A figure held in common by a communion that rejected his position and a communion that kept it is contested standing by definition.

**One precision, because it prevents a false negative.** This build's `[CT]` tags do **not** answer this limb and must not be read as doing so. `Doc_06` §3 records that Article 26 and LDF Part II restrict `[CT]` to *"live scholarly contest within the relevant subfield, not in-world disagreement."* Article 29's contest is **between present-day traditions**. Three different axes; the build's three CT terms happen to overlap two of them, which is informative but not determinative.

## 5. What this analysis concludes, and what it leaves to the project lead

**Concluded, on the build's own evidence:** both limbs of Article 29 are engaged. The first limb is engaged diffusely and widely rather than through one nameable tradition; the second is engaged through three specific and documented instances.

**Left entirely to the project lead — this is the Article 29 determination and a build thread does not make it:**

1. **The flag.** On this analysis the expected value is `true`, but the value is not set here.
2. **The distinguishing statement**, which is the substance Article 29 asks for: *how* this reconstruction is to be distinguished from the present-day self-understanding of the traditions it touches. Two shapes are available and the choice is a real one:
   - **Name a set** — the Western communions that descend from this material, distinguishing the reconstruction from each. Precise, and risks implying the list is exhaustive or that the world belongs more to the named than the unnamed.
   - **Name the relation** — state that this world is ancestral to the Western church before its divisions, that no present-day communion owns it exclusively, and that the reconstruction speaks for the fifth-century world and not for any present-day communion's account of itself. Matches Doc_01's own diffuse-continuity finding, and is the shape `lpc`'s actual evidence supports.
3. **Whether the three second-limb instances need handling beyond the general statement** — particularly *"compel them to come in,"* which is the one a participant is most likely to raise and the one where a careless answer does the most damage.

**This analysis does not perform the confirmation and does not advance the world toward freeze.** Article 29's gate remains closed until the project lead confirms.
