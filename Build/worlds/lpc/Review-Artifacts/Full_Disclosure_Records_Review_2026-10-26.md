That is a cross-world decision for the project lead, and it should be registered as an open gap. This review does not make it.
- **D6-4 (P1, safety gap: bereavement by suicide).** The guards route a participant who shows "personal risk or distress" to the Facilitator. They do not cover a participant who calmly says that someone close to them died by suicide and asks what Augustine would say. As written, that participant would be given "a homicide" and Judas's "no place for a healing penitence" about their own loved one. Bereavement by suicide is safety-adjacent, and the standing rule is caution over assumed resilience. Add as the second guard on the witness and both quotes (record file; s24): `If a participant says that someone close to them died by suicide, this record is not used; the turn goes to the Facilitator, and the Representative is not called.` In the witness, set `do_not_retrieve_when` to `["participant speaks of wanting to die or of harming themselves", "participant says someone close to them died by suicide"]`. Add to the witness body: `If a participant says someone close to them died this way, the record is not used and the turn goes to the Facilitator.`
- **D6-5 (P2, witness `positions[0]` and `text`, record file).** "He wrote of Christian virgins who were violated in captivity. Some of them, he said, killed themselves 'to avoid such disgrace.'" makes the women who killed themselves to avoid violation into the women who were violated, and those are not the same women (*ne quidquam huius modi paterentur*). Replace with: `He wrote of Christian virgins taken captive, some of whom were violated. Some, he said, killed themselves "to avoid such disgrace."`
- **D6-6 (P2, `lpc.quote.who-that-has-any-human-feeling-would-refuse-to-forgive-them`, `modern_rendering`, in s24).** "Whoever kills himself is a killer" is close to a tautology and loses the legal charge of *homicida*. Replace `If so, then surely whoever kills himself is a killer.` with `If so, then surely whoever kills himself commits homicide.` This also matches the witness's `means`.
- **D6-7 (P2, `lpc.quote.no-place-for-a-healing-penitence`, `modern_rendering`, in s24).** *detestamur*, "execrate", means to abhor. The modern sense of "curse" is to swear at or to lay a curse on. Replace `Do we rightly curse the deed of Judas?` with `Do we rightly abhor the deed of Judas?`
- **Judas and the distress question.** With D6-4 in place, the Judas quote needs no further guard. Its own Facilitator guard, the guard keeping despair to Judas, and the requirement to give it with the compassion sentence are enough. The infant and hell records do not need the Representative silenced. The per-turn Facilitator check covers acute distress. D1-5 and D4-3 stop the teaching being pressed onto a participant's own dead.
