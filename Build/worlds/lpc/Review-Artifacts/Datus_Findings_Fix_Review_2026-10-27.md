## Findings

P1 means wrong, unsupported, misleading, softened or hardened, or a safety gap. P2 means a plainly correct small fix.

- **P1-1 (witness `text` and `positions[0]`, record file `records/lpc/doctrinal_witness/lpc.witness.peace-for-the-dying-penitent.md`).** Ep. XII is a directive: they "should ... be able to make confession ... that ... they should come to the Lord with the peace" (*ut ... ueniant*). The witness tells it as an event: "Hands were laid on them for repentance, and they went to the Lord with the peace the martyrs had asked for." That says the rule was used and people died reconciled. The record's own guard says the source does not say this ("does not say how many used the rule or what became of any who died"), and `text` is what the voice reads. In both `text` and `positions[0]`, replace `Hands were laid on them for repentance, and they went to the Lord with the peace the martyrs had asked for.` with `Then hands would be laid on them for repentance, and they would go to the Lord with the peace the martyrs had asked for.` (FK 7.5, FRE 83.8, 24 words.)
- **P1-2 (witness `use_note.means`, generator `wb_lpc_s2u_use_notes.py` WITNESSES, and the record file).** "Cyprian said sick penitents in danger could confess to a presbyter or deacon and get peace." This merges the two passages into a claim neither makes: that every sick penitent could confess to a deacon. The deacon provision is only for those with a martyr's certificate, and the witness's first guard says the speaker "must not say it covers every penitent". Replace with: `Cyprian let the sick with a martyr's letter confess to a presbyter or deacon, and a council ruled the sick should have peace.` (FK 9.8, FRE 65.8, 23 words.)
- **P2-1 (world_core caution 13, generator `wb_lpc_s21.py` CAUTIONS, then the world_core record).** FK 11.5 and FRE 50.4. The voice says so plainly, in its own words. It points the participant to the world of the Reformed Cities: Zurich and Geneva. That world speaks for the Reformed churches of those two cities. The voice does not answer from what later ages made of Augustine, and it does not say what Luther or Calvin taught.` (FK 7.4, FRE 69.0.) "The voice" matches cautions 14-17.

- **P2-3 (`lpc.quote.before-even-a-deacon`, `modern_rendering`, generator `wb_lpc_s24.py` `build_penitent_peril_quotes`).** "Should be dealt with" carries the ANF wording into a modern sense of handling a problem or a culprit.
