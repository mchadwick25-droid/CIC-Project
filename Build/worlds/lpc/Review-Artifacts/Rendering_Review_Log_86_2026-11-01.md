How it was made. A file counts only if it read the rendering against the text; reviews of `use_note.means` only, and mentions in lists, do not count. Quote texts were re-checked against the vendored source by two methods in the 2026-11-01 review for the 28 records.

Open limit: for `o-blessed-people-of-the-church` the round 2 fix was the reviewer's exact corrected text, and was not read a third time (review cap: three rounds, then Mark).
