**Telos.** Discussed under F3. Sermon 88 with *Ep.* LIV §14 is a defensible reading. It is not a stated continuity, and it needs its own confidence tag. "Someone who went looking" has no record behind it.

**Safety.** The guard is not a redirect. It is not conditional on the participant. It gives no outside-help instruction. Code keeps the voice silent on safety routes (`SAFETY_ROUTES` in `engine/m4/turn.py`). The two residual risks are F5 (wording) and F2 (an exemplar that asks for disclosure). Doc_10's account of Facilitator ownership matches the governance the model Doc_10s summarise. `witt` §7 gives the same narrow textual confirmation. The `desert` Doc_10 has an in-voice redirect to human help, which the current rule forbids; `lpc` correctly does not follow it.

**The C-P demonstration (scope).** It belongs. Craft bar (a) presupposes a Center-Personal demonstration. The L4 spot-check runs C-P live. Its first sentence answers directly, with no composite-voice framing. Its grounding (Sermon 88, Enchiridion 53, Sermon 87) is Documented. Keep it once F1, F2 and F18 are fixed.

**Identity change (a), the fleet self-identification sentence.** This is not an escalation-category decision. The fleet `pronoun_rule` already sanctions exactly "I am a representative of" plus the world name from "Who we are" (`display_name` "Latin Pastoral-Congregational Christianity"). The old line conflicted with that rule and carried the label withdrawn in D2 (2026-10-23). Applying a fleet rule and D2 is execution, not a new identity choice. §8 Judgment 1's alternative ("keep the earlier line… accept a conflict") is not open to a build thread. Present it as compliance, not a judgment call. It is listed below for visibility only.

**Identity change (b), no proper names.** This is not an identity, name or title decision. Datus's name, office, object and image are unchanged, and the discipline goes back to Phase Three (the Decision Log's Voice Configuration entry discloses "zero occurrences"). It does run against the fleet citation contract and the name-bridge design (F6). If it is kept, it is a methodology departure for the project lead.

**Writing at FK 4–7.** The voice largely survives the plainness. The world's own images carry the turns, for example: "Cut from the vine, a branch keeps its shape. It does not keep its life." The prisoners shut in two cells to weaken them with hunger and thirst. "The Lord felt the pulse of his heart." The confessor's condition that each case go before the bishop. Even the shortest turns (compel turn 4, FK 3.9: "The first was his own town. It had once stood wholly with the other side.") are concrete and specific, not generic. Where the voice goes flat or generic, it is not the grade level. It is the signposting and the composed summary lines (F7, F8, F18).
