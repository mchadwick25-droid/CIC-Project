### R4-1 (Medium) — the NEW-1/NEW-4 fix stranded the sentence after it, which now asserts a cross-document agreement that does not hold

**Where.** §2 A5, line 70, the sentence immediately following the rewritten boundary statement.

> "Both worlds' Step 0 confirmations, drafted the same day, independently name this boundary."

**What changed.** This sentence is unedited from the prior revision. In the prior revision it followed the blanket statement (*"never 'the schism-crisis angle'"*), and it was accurate: both documents stated the boundary in that same blanket form. The Round-3-driven edit replaced the sentence it refers back to. "This boundary" now most immediately names **the scoped reading** — "stated precisely, not as a world-level rule… that parenthetical qualifies *Cyprian's own characterization*" — and on that reading the sentence is false.

**The divergence is real and checkable.** I read the Donatism sibling-branch draft directly (`git show origin/claude/record-native-world-build-v2-e2s0dt:World-Builds/Donatism/Step0_Movement_Scope_Confirmation.md`, 132 lines). Its §3 B3, line 94:

> "World #8's own entry states the boundary directly: **Cyprian and Augustine's ordinary pastoral office and sacramental care**, 'not the schism-crisis angle, which belongs to world #4,' with Donatism explicitly named as 'occupying and contesting the interval' between them."

Donatism attaches the parenthetical to **both** anchor figures — the world-level reading this document now explicitly rejects and, at §3 B3, says would wrongly exclude *On Baptism, Against the Donatists*, *Answer to the Letters of Petilian*, and *The Correction of the Donatists* (all three re-verified as `confidence: assigned` to this world in `cic/corpus-map/latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity.yaml`). And Donatism's §4 item 4 makes its version **binding**: *"The schism-crisis / ordinary-pastoral-office distinction named in §3 above and in the Step 0 Conclusion itself must be stated explicitly in Doc_01."*

So the two same-day Step 0 confirmations now read the same source sentence two different ways, and one of them has already bound its own Doc_01 to its reading.

**Why it matters.** §2 A5 is the paragraph that establishes the reciprocal Article 23 obligation with the Donatism build, and it explicitly claims *"a genuine, sourced cross-check is now possible."* Having run that cross-check in one direction, the document asserts concordance in the other where there is a scope divergence. This is the same defect class as Round 1's H1, Round 2's N1 and Round 3's NEW-1 — a cross-document claim that is true at a looser scope and false at the scope the surrounding text sets — recurring at the site of the previous round's fix, which is the pattern every round of this review has found.

**Counter-argument, stated fairly.** "This boundary" can also be read as pointing back to the paragraph's grammatical subject, *"The Step 0 Conclusion's own boundary"*, and on that reading both documents do name it and the sentence is true. The harm is also partly mitigated: §3 B3 already tells a Doc_02 builder that Donatism's own B1 lists Optatus first among its documentary assets, so the Optatus collision is surfaced elsewhere even though the reading divergence is not. A reviewer could reasonably rate this **Low** and apply it directly. I rate it Medium because the ambiguity is created by the new text, one of the two available readings is false, and — independent of the wording — the underlying divergence is a live cross-world item that this document surfaces nothing about while claiming to have cross-checked.

**No new escalation.** This does not open a fifth CO-022 category-4 item: Donatism's draft is not an already-cleared master document (it is an unreviewed draft on an unmerged branch), and this is not two reviews disagreeing. It is a carry-forward, not an escalation.

**Fix.** Two options, either sufficient:
1. Replace the sentence with one that names the divergence, e.g.: *"Both worlds' Step 0 confirmations, drafted the same day, independently name this boundary — though Donatism's own draft attaches the parenthetical to both anchor figures rather than to Cyprian alone, a divergence in reading that Doc_02's Optatus decision and Doc_01's boundary statement should resolve rather than inherit."*
2. Or keep the sentence and add the divergence as a sub-item under §4 item 2, alongside (a)–(d).
