**Read by:** `python -m engine.m10.cli claims lpc`.

Every absence or exclusivity claim that the deliverables and record files of `lpc` make about the record. The command derives the claims and halts on any claim without a row and on any row without a claim. Registration shows a claim is known, listed and answerable. It does not show the claim is true. `VERIFIED` means checked at source with the check named. `UNVERIFIED` means listed, not yet checked. `JUDGEMENT` means not decidable by a string search and resting on a named reading.

Ids derive from the claim's normalised text, so rewording a claim makes it a new claim.
