## 7. Editorial-Apparatus Register

**Five entries rest near 19th-century editorial matter printed inside or beside the primary text, and each names it rather than absorbing it.** Doc_05 §11 item 11 found the problem is corpus-wide and routed it to the project lead; this register is the local instance list.

| Entry | The editorial matter | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| the flock | *"[Note this decree, 'by all of us,' and what follows.]"* earlier in the sentence of Epistle LIV §14 that the entry quotes | an editor's note beside the quoted sentence, outside the quotation |
| bishop of bishops (negated) | *"Of course this implies a rebuke to the assumption of Stephen…"* inside the 256 preface | a substantive claim about Cyprian's **motive** |
| the people | in the vendored English edition of Cyprian, the three occurrences of *plebs* are in the editor's prose | the English volume cannot show the word; Cyprian's Latin letters carry *plebs* in running text, so the entry's label is a working handle and the question of a world word is open |
| compel them to come in | the volume's own preface calls the anti-Donatist works as a body *"least satisfactory to Protestant readers"* and then says the coercion doctrine rests on *"a false exegesis"* | a 19th-century Protestant editor's **theological verdict** |
| **certificates (letters of peace)** | *"thousands of certificates were given, against the Gospel law"* — an **editorial endnote's** wording; Cyprian's own text reads *"thousands of certificates were **daily** given, **contrary to the law of the Gospel**"* (`anf05` line 30204; Hartel's Latin *libellorum milia*) | the entry quotes Cyprian's wording and not the endnote's |

The two-way classification of the lapsed (*libellatici* / *sacrificati*) is not on the register. Cyprian states it himself (Hartel letter 55 §13 and §17; ANF Epistle LI), and the anonymous treatise's endnote that states the same split is not relied on. **The control cannot stop at the vendored sources: a quotation arriving from a review artifact needs checking against the marked-up text exactly as one arriving from memory does.** Mechanically: mark the `<note>` spans **before** stripping tags, since stripping first makes the apparatus indistinguishable from the text. The same method yields this world's certificate count at **38 in Cyprian's own text against 42 on a naive sweep**.
