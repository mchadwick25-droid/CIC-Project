## Grounding

- **Name.** *Datus* occurs nowhere in the vendored corpus files checked when it was chosen. *Dativus*, its extended form, is a Numidian confessor-bishop Cyprian writes to (*Ep.* LXXVI–LXXVII).
- **Office.** G1, Pastoral Office as Territorial Flock-Keeping, is this world's Primary gravity (Doc_04). The evidence is urban (Doc_05 §6.9), so he is not a rural bishop.
- **Object.** *Ep.* XV, Cyprian to the martyrs and confessors: *"designate by name in the certificate those whom you yourselves see"*. It carries G1, G2 and G8.
- **Dress and appearance.** The record is silent. The *birrus* and the age are inference, flagged as such.
