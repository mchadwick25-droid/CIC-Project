## Step 1 — independent ten-item recall list

Ten sources a specialist bibliography on this world would be expected to hold. Seven primary,
three modern.

1. **Cyprian, *Epistulae*** (the letter corpus, with the letters to him). The working record of
   a bishop managing a congregation under persecution: the lapsed, the *libelli*, confessors'
   intercession, readmission.
2. **Cyprian, *De lapsis* and *De ecclesiae catholicae unitate*** (251). The penitential
   discipline and the doctrine of the one church, both written for congregations in crisis.
3. **The *Sententiae episcoporum* of the Council of Carthage, 256.** The eighty-seven bishops'
   votes on rebaptism. Anchors "councils" and "baptism".
4. **Pontius, *Vita Cypriani*, with the *Acta proconsularia*.** How a congregation remembered
   its bishop, and the official record of his death.
5. **Augustine, *Sermones* and the *Enarrationes in Psalmos*.** The brief names Augustine's
   sermons; the congregation speaks back in them.
6. **Augustine, *Epistulae*** (22, 29, 36, 54–55, 98, 185, and the Divjak letters). Pastoral
   practice: the Parentalia, fasting, infant baptism, discipline of clergy and laity.
7. **Possidius, *Vita Augustini*.** The contemporary biography of Augustine as bishop.
8. **Peter Brown, *Augustine of Hippo: A Biography*** (1967; rev. 2000).
9. **F. van der Meer, *Augustine the Bishop*** (1961). Closest match to "pastoral-congregational".
10. **J. Patout Burns, *Cyprian the Bishop*** (2002).

**Near-misses written at Step 1 and left off:** Augustine, *De baptismo contra Donatistas*;
the later African councils and the *Codex canonum ecclesiae Africanae*; *Confessions*;
*De catechizandis rudibus*; Tertullian, *De baptismo* and *De paenitentia*; Cyprian's other
treatises; Novatian and the Cornelius letters; Sage 1975; Brent 2010; Clarke's ACW *Letters*;
Rebillard 2012; Lancel 1999; Frend; Dolbeau's sermons.

## Step 3 — the PRESS question, asked verbatim

*"Name up to three sources you would expect a bibliography of this world to contain that this
registry does not hold. If you can name none, say so explicitly."*

**Answered — three named.** Live web search was available and was used (WebSearch, standard
mode) to confirm bibliographic details. Search records are listed under B-1a.

**(1) François Decret, *Early Christianity in North Africa*, trans. Edward Smither (Eugene,
OR: Cascade Books, 2009; James Clarke edition, ISBN 978-0-227-17356-5, xii + 228 pp.; French
original *Le christianisme en Afrique du Nord ancienne*, 1996).** The standard short survey of
the whole African church across this world's window, with chapters on the mid-third-century
persecution and Cyprian. A search of the 308-row Registry finds no Decret. The Registry's
modern layer is rich on Cyprian (rows 32, 54, 55, 77) and on Augustine, and has no general
survey of the African church to orient a reader. Search record:
`lpc.search.web-decret-early-christianity-north-africa`. Catalogues disagree on the James Clarke
date (2009 or 2011); I saw a search summary, not the book.

**(2) W. H. C. Frend, *Martyrdom and Persecution in the Early Church* (Oxford: Blackwell, 1965;
reissued 1981, 1993, 2008).** Its chapters include "The great divide: Alexandria, Carthage and
Rome, 190–240" and "Decius". It is the standard study of the persecution that produced the
lapsed. The Registry names Frend once, in its list of reference bibliographies that "could not
be opened". Doc_02 (line 77) says no claim rests on Frend and no row is required, but that
passage is about the Donatist literature; it does not address this book. Search record:
`lpc.search.web-frend-martyrdom-and-persecution`. I did not see the book's text.

**(3) Eusebius, *Church History*, VI.43 (the letter of Cornelius on Novatian) and VII.2–9
(Dionysius of Alexandria and the rebaptism dispute).** This one needs no acquisition. It is
vendored at `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`: the Cornelius
chapter is headed "Novatus, his Manner of Life and his Heresy" (file line 38196); the chapter
on Cyprian's rebaptism position is at line 39303; "The Ungodly Baptism of the Heretics" is at
line 39803. The Registry's holdings table gives the file one line, "Deferred", and the reason
speaks only to VII.3 ("Cyprian's own letters (row 1) and council record (row 4) already hold
the position first-hand"). That reason does not reach VI.43 or the Dionysius chapters, which
are the only contemporary account of the Roman and Alexandrian side not written by Cyprian or
an African. Row 8 holds one Roman-side text (*De rebaptismate*), but the Registry's Doc_02 does
not set Eusebius beside it. I read chapter headings and line positions only.

**Considered and not named:** J. B. Rives, *Religion and Authority in Roman Carthage* (Oxford,
1995; search record `lpc.search.web-rives-religion-and-authority-roman-carthage`). Confirmed to
