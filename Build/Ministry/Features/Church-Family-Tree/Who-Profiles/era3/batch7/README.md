# Who profiles: era 3, batch 7

Eight profiles, one id each: `avitus-of-vienne`, `philoxenus-of-mabbug`, `boethius`, `fulgentius-of-ruspe`, `eugippius`, `john-of-beth-aphthonia`, `ciaran-of-clonmacnois`, `primasius-of-hadrumetum`.

They are the first eight of the fifteen remaining era 3 WHO rows of the supplement Who scan, `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-12_who-scan-supplement/Who_Scan_Supplement.md`, in order of the best date the held sources give for death or last mention: Avitus of Vienne (no held source gives a year. Peiper's index puts his death after late 525 or early 526, by Binding's date for a homily at Geneva, and before 533, when Julian signs as bishop of Vienne at Orléans. Peiper's own preface dates the Geneva pieces to 513 to 516, which leaves only his correction of the Life's emperor to Justin, whose reign Victor of Tunnuna begins under Mommsen's 518. The batch orders him by that 518, the Library header's year; by the index's window he would sort after Boethius), Philoxenus of Mabbug (523, by the Library header and François Nau), Boethius (524, Mommsen's year for the consuls under whom Marius of Avenches puts his death), Fulgentius of Ruspe (529 or 533, as Andrea Gallandi reports Baronius and Chifflet), Eugippius (about 535, by the Library header only), John of Beth Aphthonia (538, by the Library header, which gives the year to the writer; the held sources date only the archimandrite John bar Aphthonia, whom he may or may not be, to the entry for Severus's death in the year 849 of the Greeks, which E. W. Brooks gives as 538, or, with Elias of Nisibis, to the next year of that count), Ciaran of Clonmacnois (544, John Williams ab Ithel's margin to the Annales Cambriae) and Primasius of Hadrumetum (last seen in May 553, at his signature to Vigilius's Constitutum). None is a live person entry or was written before. The other seven, Dionysius Exiguus, Liberatus of Carthage, Brendan of Clonfert, Martin of Braga, Marius of Avenches, Barhadbeshabba 'Arbaya and John Climacus, go to batch 8. Many of the scan's years rest on no held line, so the cards give the years the held sources give and say whose they are.

The coordinator confirmed the major marks on 2026-11-14: Boethius, Philoxenus of Mabbug and Fulgentius of Ruspe are major; Avitus of Vienne, Eugippius, John of Beth Aphthonia, Ciaran of Clonmacnois and Primasius of Hadrumetum are not. Eugippius and John of Beth Aphthonia are Whos as recorders. Ciaran of Clonmacnois is a founder known through a late Life, which the brief marks as the house's own telling. Primasius's surrender and death are known only from Victor of Tunnuna, who wrote against him, and the brief says so.

John of Beth Aphthonia is the author of the later of the two Lives of Severus of Antioch that M.-A. Kugener edits. Whether he is the archimandrite John bar Aphthonia of Kenneshre is disputed: Kugener calls that attribution false; J.-B. Chabot and Rubens Duval write of the Life as his, and E. W. Brooks names its author the archimandrite of Kenneshre. The brief and card state the dispute and keep the two men's facts apart.

Card traditions are census movement names. Fulgentius of Ruspe is told within Gothic, Vandal & Lombard Homoian Christianity, the movement of the Vandal kingdom he lived under, as the Victor of Vita card is; the census has no movement for the Catholic church of Vandal Africa, and Latin African Christianity under Byzantium, where Primasius is told, begins in 533. Boethius is told within The Byzantine Imperial Church (Justinianic), the scan's first placing, since his treatises take part in the Chalcedonian quarrel over Eutyches and Nestorius.
