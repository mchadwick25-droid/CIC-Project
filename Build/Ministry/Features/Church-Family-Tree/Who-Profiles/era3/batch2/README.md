# Who profiles: era 3, batch 2

Ten profiles, one id each: `joshua-the-stylite`, `john-rufus`, `brigid-of-kildare`, `zachariah-rhetor`, `severus-of-antioch`, `caesaria-of-arles-the-elder`, `caesarius-of-arles`, `julian-missionary-to-the-nobadae`, `ferrandus-of-carthage`, `benedict-of-nursia`.

They are the next era 3 WHO rows of `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-11_who-scan-era3/Who_Scan_Era3.md`, as cleaned in `2026-11-12_who-scan-dedupe/`, after batch 1, in order of death or last attested year as the scan gives it: Joshua the Stylite (about 507), John Rufus (about 515), Brigid of Kildare (518 or 521), Zachariah Rhetor (after 536), Severus of Antioch (538), Caesaria of Arles the Elder (before 542), Caesarius of Arles (542), Julian, missionary to the Nobadae (about 545), Ferrandus of Carthage (about 546) and Benedict of Nursia (about 547). None is a live person entry or was written before. Simeon of Beth Arsham (about 530) falls in the same years and is skipped as a live person entry. Two rows in the same years, Vahan Mamikonian (about 505) and Kaleb of Aksum (about 540), were rescored STORY by the coordinator on 2026-11-14 under Mark's era 3 ruling "Story unless own voice", since no words of their own are held: Vahan's speeches and letters survive only inside Lazar of Pharp's History, and Kaleb's prayer only in the Book of the Himyarites and the Synaxarium. Ferrandus of Carthage and Benedict of Nursia took their places. Many of the scan's years rest on no held line, so the order is the scan's, and the cards give the years the held sources give and say whose they are.

The coordinator confirmed the major marks on 2026-11-14, under Mark's full-auto instruction: Severus of Antioch, Caesarius of Arles, Julian, missionary to the Nobadae, and Benedict of Nursia are major; Joshua the Stylite, John Rufus, Brigid of Kildare, Zachariah Rhetor, Caesaria of Arles the Elder and Ferrandus of Carthage are not. Joshua the Stylite and Zachariah Rhetor are Whos as recorders. Brigid of Kildare is known through Cogitosus's Life, written long after her, and Benedict of Nursia through Gregory the Great's Dialogues; both briefs mark them as books of miracles. Julian's whole story is John of Ephesus's, and the brief says so.

Three names follow the held sources. Julian has no place in any held source, so his card name gives his role and John's own name for the people, "the Nobadae"; "Nobadia" is in no held file. Zachariah keeps "Zachariah Rhetor", the epithet the chronicle's headings use, since "of Mitylene" rests on E. W. Brooks's identification; it is on the card as another name. Caesaria's "the Elder" is the scan's form, which keeps her apart from the second Caesaria, the abbess for whom the Life of Caesarius was written.

Three card traditions are the scan's and name a record the person appears in, not a party shown in his own words: Joshua the Stylite and Zachariah Rhetor "Syriac Orthodox (West Syriac) Christianity", and John Rufus "The Church of Alexandria and Miaphysite Egypt", though he worked in Palestine and at Antioch. They are to be settled with the placing.

A field with no content is left out. |
