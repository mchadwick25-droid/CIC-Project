# Who profiles: era 3, batch 3

Ten profiles, one id each: `theodora-wife-of-justinian`, `marcellinus-comes`, `cosmas-indicopleustes`, `barsanuphius-and-john-of-gaza`, `mar-aba-i`, `vigilius-of-rome`, `romanos-the-melodist`, `cyril-of-scythopolis`, `procopius-of-caesarea`, `samson-of-dol`.

They are the next era 3 WHO rows of `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-11_who-scan-era3/Who_Scan_Era3.md`, as cleaned in `2026-11-12_who-scan-dedupe/`, after batch 2, in order of death or last attested year as the scan gives it: Theodora (548), Marcellinus Comes (fl. to 548), Cosmas Indicopleustes (fl. to about 550), Barsanuphius and John of Gaza (first half of the sixth century), Mar Aba I (552), Vigilius of Rome (555), Romanos the Melodist (about 556), Cyril of Scythopolis (about 559), Procopius of Caesarea (about 565) and Samson of Dol (565). None is a live person entry or was written before. Justinian I falls in the same years and is skipped as a live person entry. Dorotheus of Gaza, "mid-6th c." in the scan, does not fall in this run: Migne heads his notice "ANNO DOMINI DXCVI" and reports the Bollandists placing him at the end of the sixth century, with his death year unknown. Many of the scan's years rest on no held line, so the order is the scan's, and the cards give the years the held sources give and say whose they are.

Theodora is a Who on Mark's named ruling in the era 3 scan, "Theodora: Who" (2026-11-11), beside his rule for rulers, "Story unless own voice". No words of her own are held: the speech in the Nika revolt is Procopius's composition, and the brief, card and records say so. The coordinator confirmed the major marks on 2026-11-14, under Mark's full-auto instruction: Theodora, Mar Aba I and Vigilius of Rome are major; the other seven are not. Marcellinus Comes, Cosmas Indicopleustes and Cyril of Scythopolis are Whos as recorders; Marcellinus carries no label, by Mark's ruling. Procopius of Caesarea carries the Outside witness label, and his card's Tradition line reads "Outside the church". Barsanuphius and John of Gaza are one paired profile, on Mark's pairing ruling of 2026-11-11.

Two names follow the held sources. Theodora has no place in the held sources that identifies her, so her card name gives her role, "wife of Justinian", as Julian's did in batch 2. Marcellinus keeps "Marcellinus Comes", the title he gives himself.

Card traditions are the scan's placings, except Cosmas, whose card follows the census's "The Church of the East under Persia"; McCrindle leaves his church an open question, and the card says so. They are to be settled with the placing.
