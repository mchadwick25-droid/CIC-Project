In 399 the bishop of Constantinople, the eastern capital, was John, a preacher from Antioch in Syria. He came from its plain way of reading Scripture, the tradition the tree calls Antiochene Exegetical Christianity. That year the emperor's chief eunuch, Eutropius, had a law passed that stripped refugees of the right to shelter in churches. Then he fell from favor and fled into the cathedral himself. As he lay trembling under the altar table, John climbed into the pulpit and preached against him. Socrates of Constantinople says some thought John added insult to cruelty. John himself said he spoke to soften the people's hearts toward him, for the church, like a mother, had hidden her enemy under her cloak.

At Antioch John had trained under Diodore, who kept to the literal sense of Scripture. In 398 the court had chosen John, and Eutropius made Theophilus of Alexandria ordain him. Palladius of Helenopolis, who stood by John to the end, says he moved the bishop's extravagant spending to the hospital, and built more hospitals. In a sermon on Matthew, John told his listeners that if they wanted to honor the body of Christ, they must not neglect Him when He is naked.

He rebuked men in office too. Socrates says that turned the tide against him, and that the sermon over Eutropius made it worse. Theophilus held a grudge, since his own favorite had lost the see to John. In 403 he and his bishops, meeting at the Oak near Chalcedon, cited John to appear. When John refused them as his enemies, they deposed him for refusing. The people rose, and he was recalled. After a clash with the empress Eudoxia, a second council silenced him, and in 404 an imperial decree sent him into exile. In 407, Palladius says, soldiers hurried him toward Pityus on the Black Sea, hoping he would die. He died on the way, with his usual words, "Glory to God for all things."

In 438 Bishop Proclus of Constantinople carried John's body back to the city in procession, and by this, Socrates says, he brought back to the church's communion the followers who had left it over John's deposition.
