# Who profiles: era 2, batch 6

Nine profiles, one id each: `liberius-of-rome`, `marcellus-of-ancyra`, `moses-the-black`, `germanus-of-auxerre`, `hilary-of-arles`, `marius-mercator`, `eutyches`, `prosper-of-aquitaine`, `eznik-of-kolb`.

They are the last nine of the twelve era 2 WHO rows of the supplement Who scan (Open-Gaps 236), after the three in era 2 batch 5, in the scan's order: Liberius of Rome, Marcellus of Ancyra, Moses the Black, Germanus of Auxerre, Hilary of Arles, Marius Mercator, Eutyches, Prosper of Aquitaine and Eznik of Kolb. None is a live person entry or was written before. Several of the scan's years rest on no held line, so that order is the scan's, and the cards give the years the held sources give and say whose they are. This batch completes era 2.

The coordinator confirmed the major marks on 2026-11-13, under Mark's full-auto instruction: Eutyches is major; the other eight are not. Marius Mercator is a Who as a recorder and translator. Prosper of Aquitaine is described as a theologian and chronicler, and Hilary of Arles as a monk and bishop, as the held sources show them (the coordinator's care point). Moses the Black and Germanus of Auxerre are known through Lives and sayings, which the briefs mark as such.

Four card traditions are the scans' and name a record the person appears in, not a party he belonged to: Marcellus of Ancyra "Cappadocian Christianity", Marius Mercator "Pelagianism", Prosper of Aquitaine "Gallic Monastic-Ascetic Christianity" and Eutyches "The Church of Alexandria and Miaphysite Egypt". They are to be settled with the placing.

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `<id>.brief.txt` | The brief as text, with no audio tags. Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. |

Its fields are `name`, `otherNames`, `label` (outside witness only), `knownFor`, `dates`, `place`, `tradition`, `role`, `briefLength`, `keyWorks` (title, about), `legacy` (`traces`, `remembered`), `uncertain`, and `sources` (`ownWords`, `about`; each entry gives a title, an author where one applies, a language and its file under `cic/texts/`). A field with no content is left out. |
| `audio/<id>.audio.txt` | The text sent to the voice: the brief with `[calmly]` at the start and `[warmly]` before the closing line. |
| `records.json` | For each id, `claims` (claim, witness, `formation_confidence`), `contested_claims` (claim, sides), and `absent_details` (detail, note) for the honest limits. |
