# Who profiles: era 2, batch 2

Nine profiles, one id each: `macrina-the-younger`, `auxentius-of-durostorum`, `ulfila`, `damasus-of-rome`, `priscillian`, `cyril-of-jerusalem`, `apollinaris-of-laodicea`, `tyconius`, `eunomius-of-cyzicus`.

These are the next nine era 2 WHO rows of the era 2 Who scan, as cleaned by the 2026-11-12 duplicate clean-up, after era 2 batch 1, ordered by death or last attested year: Macrina (379), Auxentius (fl. about 380), Ulfila (383), Damasus (384), Priscillian (385), Cyril (386), Apollinaris (about 390), Tyconius (fl. to about 390) and Eunomius (about 394). Athanasius, Ephrem, Basil and Gregory of Nazianzus fall in the same years and are skipped as live person entries. The coordinator confirmed the major marks on 2026-11-13, under Mark's full-auto instruction: Ulfila, Damasus of Rome, Priscillian, Cyril of Jerusalem, Apollinaris of Laodicea and Eunomius of Cyzicus are major; Macrina the Younger, Tyconius and Auxentius of Durostorum are not. Auxentius is a Who as a recorder, the form Pontius the Deacon set in era 1 batch 2. His card's tradition is the one movement whose people list holds him, "Gothic, Vandal & Lombard Homoian Christianity".

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `<id>.brief.txt` | The brief as text, with no audio tags. Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. |
| `<id>.card.json` | The card. Its fields are `name`, `otherNames`, `label` (outside witness only), `knownFor`, `dates`, `place`, `tradition`, `role`, `briefLength`, `keyWorks` (title, about), `legacy` (`traces`, `remembered`), `uncertain`, and `sources` (`ownWords`, `about`; each entry gives a title, an author where one applies, a language and its file under `cic/texts/`). A field with no content is left out. |
| `audio/<id>.audio.txt` | The text sent to the voice: the brief with `[calmly]` at the start and `[warmly]` before the closing line. |
| `records.json` | For each id, `claims` (claim, witness, `formation_confidence`), `contested_claims` (claim, sides), and `absent_details` (detail, note) for the honest limits. |
