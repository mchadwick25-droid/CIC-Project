# Who profiles: era 2, batch 5

Ten profiles, one id each: `socrates-of-constantinople`, `mesrop-mashtots`, `cyril-of-alexandria`, `sozomen`, `theodoret-of-cyrrhus`, `leo-of-rome`, `koriun`, `alexander-of-alexandria`, `eusebius-of-nicomedia`, `ossius-of-cordoba`.

The first seven are the last seven era 2 WHO rows of the era 2 Who scan, as cleaned by the 2026-11-12 duplicate clean-up, after era 2 batch 4, ordered by death or last attested year as the scan gives it: Socrates of Constantinople, Mesrop Mashtots, Cyril of Alexandria, Sozomen, Theodoret of Cyrrhus, Leo of Rome and Koriun. Augustine of Hippo and Nestorius fall in the same years and are skipped as live person entries. The last three are the first three, by death or last attested year, of the twelve era 2 WHO rows of the supplement Who scan (Open-Gaps 236): Alexander of Alexandria (326, an editor's note), Eusebius of Nicomedia (about 341, a note in Zenos's edition of Socrates) and Ossius of Cordoba (last attested about 357, by Archibald Robertson's notes to Athanasius's account of his detention at Sirmium at "a hundred years old"). Several of the scan's years rest on no held line; the cards give the years the held sources give, and say whose they are. The coordinator confirmed the major marks on 2026-11-13, under Mark's full-auto instruction: Cyril of Alexandria, Leo of Rome, Theodoret of Cyrrhus, Mesrop Mashtots and Eusebius of Nicomedia are major; Alexander of Alexandria, Ossius of Cordoba, Socrates of Constantinople, Sozomen and Koriun are not. Socrates, Sozomen and Koriun are Whos as recorders, the form Auxentius of Durostorum followed in era 2 batch 2 and Palladius of Helenopolis in batch 4. Mesrop Mashtots is a founder known through a disciple's record, Koriun's Life, which the brief marks as hagiography.

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `<id>.brief.txt` | The brief as text, with no audio tags. Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. |
| `<id>.card.json` | The card. Its fields are `name`, `otherNames`, `label` (outside witness only), `knownFor`, `dates`, `place`, `tradition`, `role`, `briefLength`, `keyWorks` (title, about), `legacy` (`traces`, `remembered`), `uncertain`, and `sources` (`ownWords`, `about`; each entry gives a title, an author where one applies, a language and its file under `cic/texts/`). A field with no content is left out. |
| `audio/<id>.audio.txt` | The text sent to the voice: the brief with `[calmly]` at the start and `[warmly]` before the closing line. |
| `records.json` | For each id, `claims` (claim, witness, `formation_confidence`), `contested_claims` (claim, sides), and `absent_details` (detail, note) for the honest limits. |
