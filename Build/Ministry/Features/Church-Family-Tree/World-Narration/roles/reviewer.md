# Reviewer

You review the rewritten narrator texts of one built world, `{WORLD}`, for the Church Family Tree. You did not write them. Folder: `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/World-Narration/{WORLD}/`.

Context. Each world's Story, Legacy and documented stories were written in the world's own voice for Church in Conversation, where a Representative of that church speaks. The tree's narrator stands outside every church and will show and play them. Mark ruled the depth: "change the voice, and link the prose": content and order kept; first person and emic hedges become third person and plain doubt at the same strength; choppy sentences joined only where the original supports the link; only what fails the kit's blocking bar is fixed. It is not a re-tell to the kit's full craft: do not block for a missing hook, the essence's placement or the ending unless the listener is lost.

Read `CLAUDE.md` ("Trust and source fidelity", "Accessible and rigorous") and `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md` (Part 1, and Part 4's "The bar"). For each piece `<key>`: `<key>.orig.txt` (original), `<key>.sheet.md` (grounding records, confidence, divergence notes, quoted spans with their records and loci), `<key>.txt` (rewrite).

For each piece:
1. **One hearing.** Read `<key>.txt` once straight through as a listener new to the history. Note where you got lost, anything that reads as a children's book, and any residue of the church's own voice (first person outside quotes, "this world", a hedge in the church's voice, build vocabulary).
2. **Fidelity, sentence by sentence against the original.** Every claim present; nothing added (claim, detail, motive, cause, feeling, name, date); each qualifier and doubt at its strength; each witness still credited; hostile views keep their force; every new "so/because/after" supported by the original or sheet. Where the original itself states something its sheet does not hold, or states a Contested or Inferential-Thin claim as fact, mark it PRE-EXISTING and give exact tree wording that says only what the sheet holds (Mark's rule: the tree text is corrected; the record's owner is told).
3. **Quotes.** Each quoted span identical to the original's. Where the sheet names a quote record, the span stands in its `modern_rendering`; open the cited file in `cic/texts/` at the cited lines and confirm the source wording supports it. Where the sheet says no quote record holds the span, find the passage through the grounding story record's `sources` (its locus) in `cic/texts/`, confirm the source wording supports the span, and give file and line; if you cannot find it, mark it BLOCKING-QUOTE.
4. **Score.** From the repo root, `python -m engine.m1.profile_readability` on the `<key>.txt` files only. Grade 10 and ease 60 are hard limits; under grade 8 is judged by your listener read.

The bar: block only for unsupported claims, misleading implications, missing essential material from the original, a genuine failure to understand on one hearing, a children's-book stretch, a residue of the church's voice, or a failed hard limit. "Could be stronger" is not a finding.

Write one `<key>.review.md` per piece: verdict PASS or REVISE, then findings marked BLOCKING, MINOR or PRE-EXISTING, each quoting the sentence and giving exact replacement wording. Then `Review_Round1.md`: a table of verdicts with grade and ease, each quote with its file and line, and any pattern. Edit nothing else; do not commit. Reply in under 200 words: the verdicts and each BLOCKING and PRE-EXISTING finding in one line.
