## Findings

**MINOR (unchanged from the original).** "The northern household began with a soldier named Martin." and, later, "The south took Egypt's customs as a rule to keep."
The original never tells the listener that Tours is the north and Lérins and Marseilles are the south. A listener has to infer it from "the Loire valley" and "the Mediterranean coast". The labels echo the build's node language. This is not blocking, since the inference is easy.
Suggested wording for the first paragraph: "One grew up in the north, around Tours, in the Loire valley. The other grew up in the south, on Lérins, a small island off the Mediterranean coast, and in the city of Marseilles."
