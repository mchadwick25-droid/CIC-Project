## Findings

**BLOCKING (added detail).** "He used to tell Martin's household what had happened to him, Sulpitius says."
The original's "used to tell us" was the world's own first person. The rewrite replaced it with a recipient, "Martin's household", that nothing holds. The source says only "The same man was wont to relate that, when he left the body, he was brought before the tribunal of the Judge" (cic/texts/npnf211_sulpitius-severus-vincent-lerins-cassian.xml, line 1013), with no listener named. The story record's text has "The man himself used to tell what had happened to him", also with no listener. The quote record gallic.quote.the-catechumens-testimony has "offered himself to us" (line 1012), but that "us" is Sulpitius as author, and it is about the man as a witness, not about whom he told. Naming Martin's household also suggests Sulpitius had the account through Martin's community, which is not stated at this locus.
Replace with: "The man himself used to tell what had happened to him, Sulpitius says."
