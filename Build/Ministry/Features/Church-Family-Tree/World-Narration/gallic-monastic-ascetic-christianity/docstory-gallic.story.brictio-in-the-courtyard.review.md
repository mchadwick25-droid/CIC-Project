## Findings

**BLOCKING (added detail).** "One day Martin sat on his wooden seat, the one his household all knew, in the little court outside his door."
This is the same pattern as the catechumen story: the world's "we all knew" became a group that nothing holds. In the source, Gallus is speaking to his listeners in the Dialogues: "that wooden seat of his (which you all know)" (cic/texts/npnf211_sulpitius-severus-vincent-lerins-cassian.xml, lines 5290-5291). The quote record's modern_rendering has "which you all know". Neither says Martin's household.
Replace the first two sentences with: "One day, as his disciple Gallus tells it, Martin sat on his wooden seat, the one Gallus's listeners all knew, in the little court outside his door. Gallus says Martin saw two demons on the rock above the monastery, calling out with glee, \"Come here, Brictio, come here, Brictio.\""
