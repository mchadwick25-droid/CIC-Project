## Findings

**MINOR.** "He had served Caesar long enough, he said, and now he asked to serve God, since it was not lawful for Christ's soldier to fight."
"Christ's soldier" now sounds like a general rule. In the source and the original, Martin calls himself Christ's soldier: "I am the soldier of Christ: it is not lawful for me to fight" (cic/texts/npnf211_sulpitius-severus-vincent-lerins-cassian.xml, lines 841-842). The "since" is supported, because that sentence is his reason.
Suggested wording: "He had served Caesar long enough, he said, and now he asked to serve God, since, as Christ's soldier, he could not lawfully fight."
