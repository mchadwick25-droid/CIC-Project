# Review: legacy

**Verdict: REVISE**

Score: grade 8.4 (names out 8.0), reading ease 61.3. Passes the hard limits.

**One hearing.** "the last translator" is puzzling right after Jerome is named; the listener must work out that it is Jerome.

**Fidelity.** In the original, the limit is the world's own record and the world's own Bible ("This world's own record", "this world's own settled Bible"). The rewrite replaced both with wider terms, and that changes the claim.

## Findings

- BLOCKING (misleading implication). "The surviving record does not follow Jerome's great labor past his own lifetime." and "Did the Hebrew-based translation ever become these churches' settled Bible? The surviving evidence cannot answer that question. Their own witness ends with the argument still unresolved." Heard right after Augustine's "the churches of Christ", "these churches" means the Latin churches at large, and "the surviving record" means all evidence. Both now say the later fate of Jerome's translation is unknowable, which is false: `hal.term.vulgata` gives its "later standard status" as belonging "to centuries after this world's close", and `hal.force.transmission-ending` limits the absence to the circle's own record ("no successor ... attested anywhere in the record" of the world). "great" is also an added judgment for "central". Replace the first and last paragraphs with:

  "The circle's own record does not follow Jerome's central labor past his lifetime. It shows an argument that was still open when he died."

  "Did the Hebrew-based translation ever become the circle's own settled Bible? Its surviving writings cannot answer that question. They end with the argument still unresolved."

  The middle paragraph stands.
