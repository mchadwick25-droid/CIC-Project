## Blocking

- legacy: "The surviving record does not follow Jerome's great labor past his own lifetime" and "Did the Hebrew-based translation ever become these churches' settled Bible? The surviving evidence cannot answer that question." The original's "this world's own record" and "this world's own settled Bible" became wider terms. The result says the translation's later fate cannot be known, which contradicts `hal.term.vulgata` (its later standard status belongs to centuries after the world's close). Exact replacement in legacy.review.md, scoped to "the circle's own record" and "the circle's own settled Bible".

## Pre-existing

None. Every Contested claim (Marcella's standing, Paula's epitaph) stays credited to Jerome's memorial in both original and rewrite.

## Patterns

1. **The voice change is clean but leaves "us" behind.** No "we", "our" or "this world" remains in the narrator's voice. Three idioms kept a first-person "us" from the originals: "he tells us" (ciceronian-dream), "Jerome tells us" (rome-crisis), "comes down to us" (marcella-standing). Each is MINOR, with replacement wording given.
2. **Replacing "this world" needs a scope, not a general term.** The legacy failure came from turning "this world's" into "the surviving" and "these churches". Where the original limits a claim to the world, the rewrite should name the circle.
3. **Joins are well supported.** All "and", "but", "though" and "so" joins were checked against originals and sheets. One join (ciceronian-dream) pushed a sentence to 31 words; MINOR.
