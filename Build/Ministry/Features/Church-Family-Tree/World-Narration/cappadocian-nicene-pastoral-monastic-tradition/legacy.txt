The teachers of these churches did not stay local. Their names and arguments became curriculum, liturgy and law for later traditions. Those traditions trace their roots through the councils these churches lived through and helped write.

Orthodox, Catholic and, more distantly, Protestant teaching all still argue in vocabulary this circle fought to place. It is the vocabulary of one God in three persons. Basil set out two Greek terms in a letter to Count Terentius. Ousia, the essence, is common, like goodness or Godhead. Hypostasis is what is particular to each: Fatherhood, Sonship, the power to sanctify.
