**Verdict: REVISE**

One hearing: clear apart from the last sentence. The voice is changed cleanly: "This world's own teachers" is now "The teachers of these churches". No residue. Grade 8.3, ease 60.6 (passes, near the limit). No quoted spans (sheet: none).

## Findings

**PRE-EXISTING.** "Even the letter most often cited to prove their agreement is disputed between two of them."
On one hearing this says that two of the teachers argued over the letter. What the record holds is that its authorship is disputed. `cappadocian.contested.settlement-historiography` says that Basil's Epistle 38 is "attribution-contested between Basil and his brother Gregory of Nyssa - a substantial body of modern scholarship ... reassigns it to Nyssen". The original carries the same misleading wording. Replace with: "Even the letter most often cited to prove their agreement is in doubt, since many scholars now think it was written not by Basil but by his brother Gregory." (25 words.)

**MINOR.** "Did the three teachers ever share one settled scheme, or were they three related projects that later tradition flattened into one tidy story?"
"They" now means the teachers, so the teachers are heard as "projects". Replace with: "Did the three teachers ever share one settled scheme, or did they pursue three related projects that later tradition flattened into one tidy story?"

Fidelity otherwise: every claim is present at the same strength. "Historians now dispute this" keeps the Contested status. Plain doubt is kept and not settled.
