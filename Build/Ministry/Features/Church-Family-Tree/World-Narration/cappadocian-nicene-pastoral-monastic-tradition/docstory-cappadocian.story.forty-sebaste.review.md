# Review: docstory-cappadocian.story.forty-sebaste

**Verdict: PASS**

One hearing: clear, and not flat at grade 7.1 (ease 77.8). No quoted spans (sheet: none). The traditional passion details stay credited ("The tradition says", "It says"), as the record's divergence note requires.

## Findings

**MINOR.** "As the church remembers it, they were stripped and made to stand through a winter night on a frozen pond."
"Our church" has become "the church". From a narrator who stands outside every church, "the church" can sound like the narrator's own or a universal church. Replace with: "As the churches of Basil's day remembered it, they were stripped and made to stand through a winter night on a frozen pond."

Fidelity otherwise: "offered to any man who would deny the faith" and "confessing the faith the other man had just abandoned, so the martyrs still numbered forty" match the record ("so that the number of martyrs stayed forty").
