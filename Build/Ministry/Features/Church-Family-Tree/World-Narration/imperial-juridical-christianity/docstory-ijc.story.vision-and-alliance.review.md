## Findings

- **PRE-EXISTING.** "Constantine stopped hunting the church, and within a year toleration was law."
  Told in the narrator's own voice, this says that Constantine had been persecuting the church. The record's own source corpus says otherwise. Lactantius, *De Mortibus* 24 (`cic/texts/anf07_lactantius-apostolic-constitutions-didache-liturgies.xml`, line 10337), says that Constantine, "having assumed the government, made it his first care to restore the Christians to the exercise of their worship". `ijc.force.constantine-alliance` itself notes that persecution's legal end "had already begun with Galerius's own edict of 311". The record's wording ("an emperor ... stopped hunting the church") is the church's own view of the empire, and the narrator should not carry it forward as fact about one man.
  Replace with: "The empire stopped hunting the church, and within a year toleration was law."
  The record's owner should be told. The same sentence is in `ijc.story.vision-and-alliance`'s text and telling.
