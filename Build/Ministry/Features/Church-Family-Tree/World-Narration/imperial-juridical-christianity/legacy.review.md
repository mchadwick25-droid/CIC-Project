# Review: legacy

**Verdict: REVISE**

Grade 7.8, reading ease 66.1 (names out). The listener read is not flat.

## One hearing

The voice change is mostly clean: "this world's own final council" becomes "the last council of this period", and the Contested matters (`ijc.contested.primacy-reception`, `ijc.contested.canon-28-meaning`) stay framed as open questions, as the records require. The new paragraph break before "Constantinople's rank" helps.

One residue remains. "This world's own record" has become "the record used here", which is build vocabulary and a report about the record. A listener new to the history cannot tell what "used here" points to.

## Fidelity

The content and order are kept. The joins ("...accepted it, but whole regions rejected it"; "...one way, and the see that refused it...") are contrasts the original already makes. "At the council itself" in Egypt is held by `ijc.force.chalcedon-failed-consensus` at the level of the standard scholarship (its `divergence_note`), and is told without overstatement.

None.

## Findings

- **BLOCKING (residue / failure to understand).** "The record used here ends just as that fracture opens, and it says nothing of how the fracture would end."
  Replace with: "The story of this period ends just as that fracture opens, and its documents say nothing of how the fracture would end."
- **MINOR.** "The record opens that disagreement and does not close it."
  Replace with: "These documents open that disagreement and do not close it."
- **MINOR.** "The two rival claims these churches argued over have been argued over ever since." The phrase "these churches" has no clear referent for a listener.
  Replace with: "The two rival claims argued over in this period have been argued over ever since."
