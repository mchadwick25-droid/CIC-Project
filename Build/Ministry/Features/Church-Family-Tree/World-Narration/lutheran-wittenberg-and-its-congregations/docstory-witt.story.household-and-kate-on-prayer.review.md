## Findings

**BLOCKING (unsupported narrowing; it changes whom Luther blamed).** "They are diligent in their false worship, he said, while he and his household were ice cold and negligent in prayer." The source (lines 3146-3151, "Of the Power of Prayer") reads: "they are diligent, and take great pains in their false worshipping, but we, indeed, are ice cold therein, and negligent." Luther's "we" is set against the devil's servants "in Popedom", not against another household. The world's own doctrinal-witness record on this exchange (`witt.dw.cold-and-careless-among-us`, text) reads it as "we, at the true one", meaning those under the new faith. "He and his household" makes a general self-accusation of his own side into a remark about one family. The tense also slips from "are" to "were" inside the same reported speech. Replace with:

"They are diligent in their false worship, he said, while his own side is ice cold and negligent in prayer."

**MINOR (for the record's owner, not this rewrite).** Katharina's own "we" in the source ("in Popedom they pray so often with great vehemence, but we are very cold and careless in praying", lines 3147-3149) is read as "their own household" in the grounding story record's `tellable_as` and `telling`, but as "we, under the new one" in `witt.dw.cold-and-careless-among-us`. The tree text follows its grounding record, so it is not marked PRE-EXISTING. The two records should agree.
