## Findings

**On "against the Jews".** The rewrite on disk (and as committed in c8e6093e) does not say "against the Jews". It reads: "Luther's 1543 treatise is real and part of this history, but this account does not say how the churches that came after have answered for it." Adding "against the Jews" is not something the rule forbids. It clarifies something the listener needs. It is supported by this piece's own grounding record, `witt.limit.1543-treatise-not-held`, whose `statement` opens "Our founder wrote a treatise against the Jews in 1543. It is part of our history, and we do not hide it." The phrase names the treatise's subject. It does not describe its content, quote it or judge its effect, which are the only things that record's `not_for` rules out. Leaving the subject out is the real fault, for two reasons.

**BLOCKING (failure to understand on one hearing; the record's force softened).** The legacy is its own audio file (`legacy.mp3`, per `Build/tools/generate_world_narration.mjs`), so it can be heard without the Story. A listener who hears only "Luther's 1543 treatise" cannot tell what it was or why later churches would have to answer for it. The original's "the 1543 treatise" relied on the Story. The grounding record's whole point is that the treatise is not hidden, and an unnamed treatise hides it. "The founder's death" in the sentence before also comes before Luther is named in this piece.
