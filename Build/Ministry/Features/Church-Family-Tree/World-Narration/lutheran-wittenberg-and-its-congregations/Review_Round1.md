## Blocking

- story: the doctrine paragraph's frame "These churches taught that..." is right, but "A sinner is freely justified..." and "In the Supper, Christ's body and blood are truly present..." are still told as the narrator's own present-tense fact. The Supper is the very point the Confession fought over, so the narrator carries one side's teaching. Full replacement paragraph in `story.review.md`.
- legacy: "Luther's 1543 treatise" is heard without its subject. The rewrite on disk does not say "against the Jews". Adding it is a clarification the rule allows, not an addition: the piece's own grounding record, `witt.limit.1543-treatise-not-held`, opens "Our founder wrote a treatise against the Jews in 1543", and naming the subject is not describing its content. Legacy is its own audio file, so without the subject the sentence cannot be followed, and the record's "we do not hide it" is softened. Wording: "His 1543 treatise against the Jews is real and part of this history, but this account does not say how the churches that came after have answered for it.", with "the founder's death" made "Luther's death".
- household-and-kate-on-prayer: "while he and his household were ice cold" narrows Luther's "we" (set against the devil's servants "in Popedom", Table Talk lines 3149-3151) to one family. Wording: "They are diligent in their false worship, he said, while his own side is ice cold and negligent in prayer."

## Patterns

1. **The voice change is clean in the stories.** No "we/our/us" remains outside quotes, and no "this world" or church-voice hedge. "The library holds" became "the sources behind this account hold", at the same strength.
2. **Witnesses stay credited.** Every Table Talk piece keeps its teller ("By his account", "Luther said", "as his students recorded it"), which carries the Contested wording at its strength. Two rewrites (Cajetan, Worms) moved the witness to the front, which helps a first-time listener.
3. **The links hold.** Every new "and", "but", "yet" and "though" restates a link or contrast the original already made. None adds a cause or motive.
4. **Where first person had to go, the replacement must keep its reach.** The two "we" fixes that needed judgment (the story's doctrine, Kate's answer) are where the round's findings sit. A frame like "These churches taught" has to cover every sentence of doctrine, and a "we" has to become a group as wide as the source's.
5. **For the record's owner.** The story record and `witt.dw.cold-and-careless-among-us` read Katharina's "we" differently (household against the new faith as a whole). They should agree.
