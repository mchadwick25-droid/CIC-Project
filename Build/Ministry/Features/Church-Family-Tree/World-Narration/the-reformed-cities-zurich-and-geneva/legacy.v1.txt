The beliefs of these two cities passed on, unbroken, to four later churches: the Scottish Kirk, the Huguenots, the Dutch Reformed, and English Puritans. No force from outside or inside ended the Reformed churches of Zurich and Geneva between 1519 and 1650. Those dates are a boundary drawn by editors around a living tradition. They are not a historical ending.

At the Synod of Dort in 1618 and 1619, Reformed delegates from many countries pressed the teaching on election to its sharpest form yet. It came to Dort at one remove, carried forward from Geneva through the teaching of Theodore Beza. Dort sharpened a belief already held. It did not close or complete it. Dort falls thirty-two years before this account ends.
