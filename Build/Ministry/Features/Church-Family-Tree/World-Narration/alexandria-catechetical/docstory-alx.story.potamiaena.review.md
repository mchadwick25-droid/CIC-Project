# Review: docstory-alx.story.potamiaena

**Verdict: PASS**

Score: grade 6.5 (names out 5.5), reading ease 80.2. Under 8; the listener read finds it vivid and adult, not childish.

**One hearing.** Clear. "Our church baptized him" is now "The church baptized him". "The tradition says" stays audible at the pitch, the promise and the vision, as the record's absent_detail requires.

**Fidelity.** All claims present, in order. Joins only ("but she stood fast", "the reason: Potamiaena", "and then he was beheaded"); "but" restates the original's own contrast. Checked against Eusebius HE VI.5 (`cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`, lines 32954-33002). No quoted spans.

## Findings

1. PRE-EXISTING (who heard the reason). "At first they thought he was joking, but he was not. The tradition says he told them the reason: Potamiaena had come to him in the night and set a crown on his head." In the paragraph, "them" can only be his fellow soldiers. In Eusebius it was not the soldiers: Basilides was taken to the judge and imprisoned, and "the brethren in God coming to him and inquiring the reason" heard of the vision (lines 32983-32995). The original carries the same slip; the rewrite kept it. Tree wording: "At first they thought he was joking, but he was not, and he was put in prison. The tradition says that when fellow Christians came to ask him why, he told them: Potamiaena had come to him in the night and set a crown on his head."
   The record's owner should be told: alx.story.potamiaena's text runs the vision straight on from the soldiers.

2. MINOR. "She was threatened with what ancient cruelty kept for women" is oblique on one hearing. Eusebius says the judge "threatened to hand her over to the gladiators for bodily abuse" (line 32965). The original has the same phrase and an adult listener can follow it; optional: "She was threatened with rape, the cruelty the age kept for women, but she stood fast."
