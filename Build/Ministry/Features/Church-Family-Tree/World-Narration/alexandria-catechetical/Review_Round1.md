## Blocking

- origen-demetrius: "Nearly all we know of what came next comes from Eusebius, ..." First person outside a quote. Replace with: "Nearly everything known of what came next comes from Eusebius, who admired Origen and was hostile to the memory of Demetrius."

## Pre-existing (the original states more or other than the record or source holds)

- origen-daring-deed: "Eusebius says Origen wanted no one to accuse him of wrong, so he took a hard verse ... in the most literal sense." Eusebius gives two purposes, to fulfill the Saviour's word and to remove grounds for scandal; the "so" makes reputation the sole cause. Wording in the review.
- arsinoite-conference: the Dionysius quote is the 1890 translation's "should", not the record's rendering "must". Tree wording: "the truth must be loved and honored most of all."
- plague-nursing: "The church buried its dead with open hands." Now asserted by the narrator, though only Dionysius reports it, and the image is literal in the source (bare hands on the bodies). Tree wording: "He wrote that they took the bodies of the dead in their open hands, washed and dressed them, and carried them away."
- potamiaena: "he told them the reason" makes the soldiers hear of the vision; in Eusebius it was fellow Christians who visited him in prison. Wording in the review.

Each needs its record's owner told; the tree text is corrected per Mark's rule.

## Patterns

1. **The voice change is clean but for one sentence.** "our church", "our bishop", "one of our teachers", "we cannot hear", "lost to us" and "Eusebius tells us" all became third person at the same strength. Only origen-demetrius kept a "we".
2. **The links hold.** Every new join restates a link or contrast the original already made; none adds a cause or motive. No sentence passes 28 words.
3. **The pre-existing faults sit in the record texts, not the rewrites.** Three are drifts from the source in what the Eusebius-based tellings say (a narrowed motive, the wrong audience for a vision, a literal image heard as a figure); one is a quote carried in source wording rather than its rendering.
4. **Minor, optional:** legacy's "that age" and "interested translators", gregory-formation's "not settled who gave that speech", potamiaena's oblique "what ancient cruelty kept for women". Wording in each review.
