# Sheet: desert-monasticism / docstory-desert.story.moses-leaking-jug

## Original text (as narrated now)

Our sayings tell of a brother at Scete, the camp of the monks, who committed an offense. A congregation met on the matter. They sent for Abba Moses, but he refused to come. Then they sent the priest of the church to him, saying, "Come. All the people are waiting for you." Moses got up and came.

He took a basket with a hole in it, filled it with sand, and carried it on his shoulders. Those who went out to meet him asked, "What does this mean, father?" He said the sand was "my sins, running down behind me. I cannot see them." And today, he said, he had come to judge faults that were not his own.

When they heard this, they set the brother free and said nothing more to him.
