Athanasius writes that Antony was about eighteen or twenty when his parents died, which left him with a little sister and the care of the house.

Less than six months later, as he went into church as he always did, he was thinking of how the apostles had left everything to follow the Savior. The Gospel was being read. He heard the Lord say to the rich man: "If you want to be perfect, go and sell what you have and give to the poor. Then come and follow me, and you will have treasure in heaven."

Antony went out of the church at once, as though the passage had been read on his account. He gave the villagers his family's land, three hundred Egyptian field measures of good ground. He sold everything else he could move and gave the money to the poor, keeping back a little for his sister.

Later he went into church again and heard the Lord say, "Do not be anxious about tomorrow." He could stay no longer. He went out and gave the rest to the poor as well, and he placed his sister with known and faithful virgins to be brought up. From then on he gave himself to a life of discipline outside his house, training himself with patience.

In the next village lived an old man who had been a hermit from his youth, and after Antony had seen him, he copied his devotion.
