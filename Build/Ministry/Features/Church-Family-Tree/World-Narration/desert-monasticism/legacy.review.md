Clear. The split sentences read as an adult narrator, and the two paragraphs each make one point. No residue: "This world's own" and "this one's own" are gone.

## Fidelity: checked two ways

Method 1, clause by clause against the original. Method 2, a word-level comparison of the two texts (every word in one and not the other). Both show the same result. Paragraph 1 is complete: eight named kinds, passed west through Cassian's Conferences, narrowed two centuries later by Gregory the Great, became the seven deadly sins. The new "and they became" carries the original's own sequence. Paragraph 2 loses two qualifiers, "technique" and "systematized", which are the substance of the point.

## Findings

**BLOCKING (lost qualifier, misleading implication).** "People often assume it names the same practice as the later Byzantine Hesychast tradition, with its Jesus Prayer. But that method was worked out by a separate, later tradition."

The original says the *Jesus Prayer technique* and the *systematized method* belong to the later tradition. The record (desert.term.hesychia) draws the same line: the practice of stillness is attested in the desert but is "unsystematized"; what does not belong is "the later Byzantine hesychast method (the Jesus Prayer technique)". Without the two qualifiers, the listener hears that the prayer itself, not its later systematized method, has nothing to do with the desert. Replace with:

"People often assume it names the same practice as the later Byzantine Hesychast tradition, with its Jesus Prayer technique. But that systematized method belongs to a separate, later tradition."

**MINOR.** "The desert monks had eight named kinds of tempting thought, and these did not stay in the desert."

"The desert monks had" widens the original's "this world's own" toward the whole movement, while desert.gravity.evagrian-systematization holds that the eight-kind scheme was Evagrius's and his circle's and "did not organize the wider movement" (the Story says so too). Replace with:

"The eight named kinds of tempting thought taught in the desert did not stay there."
