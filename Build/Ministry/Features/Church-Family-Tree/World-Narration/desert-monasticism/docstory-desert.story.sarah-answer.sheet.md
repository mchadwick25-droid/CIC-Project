# Sheet: desert-monasticism / docstory-desert.story.sarah-answer

## Original text (as narrated now)

Amma Sarah is one of a few women our teaching tradition remembers by name. The Syriac collection keeps six sayings under her name. In our speech, steadfast courage was called manly, and weakness womanly. Amma Sarah used to say to her brothers, "It is I who am a man, and you who are women."
