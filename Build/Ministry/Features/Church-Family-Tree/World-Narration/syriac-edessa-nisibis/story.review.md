# Review: story

**Verdict: REVISE**

One hearing: clear and well linked; the joins add contrast and time only where the original's order carries them. Grade 9.1, ease 67.3.

## Findings

- PRE-EXISTING (unsupported witness). "Ephrem's hymns show that women of the covenant sang them in choirs." No grounding record says Ephrem's hymns are the witness. syr.term.qyama (evidential sense), syr.term.madrasha, syr.gravity.covenant-life and syr.core.syriac all credit the choirs' singing to later accounts, and the world's own choirs piece opens "Later accounts say". Tree wording: "Later accounts say that women of the covenant sang Ephrem's hymns in choirs." The owner of the world Story text should be told.

## Quotes

- "One day, my brothers, I picked up a pearl." Identical to the original; stands in the modern_rendering of syr.quote.a-pearl-upon-the-palm, syr.quote.on-a-certain-day-a-pearl and syr.quote.pearl-mysteries. Source: cic/texts/npnf213_gregory-great-ephraim-syrus-aphrahat.xml, lines 21794-21795 ("On a certain day a pearl did I take up, my brethren"). Supported.
