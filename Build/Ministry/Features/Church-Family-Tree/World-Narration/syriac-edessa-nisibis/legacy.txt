The woven Gospel of these churches did not last forever. In the 400s, bishops moved against it. Rabbula of Edessa ordered every church to read the Distinct Gospel, meaning the separate Gospels. Theodoret of Cyrrhus said the harmony cut out the passages showing that Jesus was born of David's line. He took more than two hundred copies from his own churches and put the four Gospels in their place. The harmony's Syriac text did not survive. It is known only through quotations and translations into other languages.
