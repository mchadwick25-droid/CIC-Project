The woven Gospel of these churches did not last forever. Within a generation after 410, a new translation split the four Gospels apart again. Tradition credits Bishop Rabbula of Edessa with the change, but the sources leave open how much of it was really his doing. Once the harmony stopped being copied, its original Syriac text did not survive. It is known today only through fragments, quotations, and translations made from it in other languages.
