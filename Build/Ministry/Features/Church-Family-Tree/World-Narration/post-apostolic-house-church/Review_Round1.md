## Blocking

- legacy: "Only the quiet disappearance of the older, argued option is visible." This is build vocabulary ("a live, argued option" in the forces record) and does not say on one hearing what disappeared. Replace with: "All that can be seen is that the older way quietly dropped out of the argument."

## Patterns

1. **The voice change is clean.** No first person outside quotes, no "this world", and no church-voice hedge remains in any piece. Each "we cannot be sure" became "it is not certain" or "no one can say for certain" at the same strength. Justin's piece had nothing to change and was correctly left word for word.
2. **The links hold.** Every new "so", "because", "but", "yet" and "even as" restates a link or contrast the original already made. None adds a cause or motive.
3. **Joining can push past about 30 words.** In story (33) and legacy (32) a join made a sentence longer than the kit allows (MINOR; splits given).
4. **The pre-existing faults sit in legacy and Ignatius.** The originals state some Inferential-Thin or ungrounded claims flat: legacy's "with nothing felt to be missing", "prove they were genuine" and "what a later settlement found useful to keep"; Ignatius's "because that unity was still being argued over" and "He believed his coming death gave his words unusual weight". The rewrite carried these at the original's strength, as the ruling asked. They are not the rewrite's fault. But they fail the blocking bar upstream (unsupported claim or lost doubt), so they need Mark's call: fix them in this pass, or in the source records and world front. Wording is given in each review.
