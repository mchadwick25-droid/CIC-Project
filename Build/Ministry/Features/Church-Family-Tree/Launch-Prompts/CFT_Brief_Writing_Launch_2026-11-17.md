# Church Family Tree brief writing: launch

Read `CLAUDE.md`. You write Church Family Tree Who briefs and cards. Do exactly what this says, the same way for every person. Add no rules, rewrite nothing yourself, and change nothing here without Mark.

**The standard is the approved Polycarp brief:** `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-16_polycarp-clean-write/Polycarp_approved.brief.txt`. The paragraph jobs are in `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-16_ignatius-calibration/Writing_from_Polycarp.md`. A narrator who knows the record tells what happened. A witness is named only where trust turns on it.

**For each person, in order:**
1. The researcher (Opus) builds the source sheet from the person's own record. The sheet opens with a story spine: what happened, in order, in plain words, with each witness noted beside it.
2. The writer (Fable) gets only Polycarp, `Writing_from_Polycarp.md` and the sheet, with the same prompt every time.
3. A script checks every draft against Polycarp before anyone sees it:
   - quotation near 17%;
   - attributions ("said / wrote / called") near 6;
   - 520 words or fewer;
   - grade 10 or below, and reading ease 60 or above;
   - American spelling;
   - an opening type different from the briefs around it.
   A draft that fails goes back to the writer once.
4. Only the quotes used get the blind second rendering (Opus) and the comparison.
5. One Opus agent checks every sentence against the primary texts and makes the required fixes. A short recheck covers what it changed.
6. The existing card is reworked: its fact fields are checked, legacy and uncertainty are rewritten from the sheet, and "References for this brief" is added. The card is checked like the brief.
7. Recordings are made only when a brief is final and ready to publish (voice: Neo for Who briefs).

**Batches:** 4 to 6 people per era. At the end of each batch, give Mark one plain-text file with the briefs and cards, for his Perplexity check, and a drift report with the step 3 numbers for each brief.
