# Who worker brief

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-15). The paragraph below goes, word for word, into the brief of every worker who drafts or revises a Who profile. It replaces any instruction to copy an earlier batch. The decision is in `Decision-Log.md`, "The Who writing standard restored to the eight early-church briefs" (2026-11-15).

## The paragraph

Before you draft, read `Who_Profile_Pattern.md` in full and all eight briefs in `Who-Profiles/bar/`. They are the standard. Do not open an earlier batch to see how a brief is written, and do not match its length, its shape or its phrasing; earlier batches are being rewritten to this same bar. Mark's definition of craft is the test: "good story telling and flow, no ai tells hanging sentences and everything is relevant to the story." Write each brief as a story an adult would hear on BBC News or read in National Geographic. Open in a sourced moment, follow the life, put the reason in the sentence with the act, and say in a sentence or two why this person mattered. Leave no sentence standing alone, and end where the story ends. A last line picked because it is quotable is a fault. Name ancient witnesses inside the story with their leaning in a few words. Keep editors, editions and dating arguments for the card's Sources and What is uncertain. A date, count or judgment that only a modern scholar gives is named to him in a clause or left to the card, and is told flat nowhere. Invent nothing, check every quote against the vendored file, and tell a dispute as a dispute. Aim at about two minutes; go shorter where the sources are thin, and longer only where the story needs it and your batch note says why. When you finish a draft, read it beside two of the eight and apply Mark's five craft tests from checklist item 10 in your batch note, quoting your own sentences. Then score it: grade 8.0 to 10.0 on the full text, reading ease 60 or above with names left out. If it scores below grade 8, join and explain; do not reach for longer words. In review, a fix may join sentences as well as split them.
