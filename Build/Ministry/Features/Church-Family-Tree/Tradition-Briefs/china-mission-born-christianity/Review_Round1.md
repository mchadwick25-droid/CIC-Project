## 1. One hearing, as a new listener

- **Who or what:** Christianity in Ming China, born of the Jesuit mission that came in 1583, heard through one Chinese convert, the minister Xu Guangqi.
- **What happened:** the Jesuits settled at Zhaoqing in 1583. Decades later the Board of Rites impeached them. Xu defended them before the emperor, proposed tests of their teaching, and staked his own punishment on it.
- **Why the connection matters:** I could only infer it, from paragraph five: he defended the faith in China's own terms (Heaven, virtue, the royal teaching). The brief never says so, and never says what the tradition was or why it is on the tree (B2).

Where I got lost:
- "whose mission in China began in 1583. In September of that year, they reached..." I heard a first arrival by all the Jesuits. The record says otherwise (B1). I also did not know who "they" were.
- The third paragraph is four sentences about sources (who wrote what, when, from what stone). It reads as a report about the record, and it is where the essence should be (B2).
- "Then he offered to take their punishment" in the opening, and again "he would accept the punishment" in paragraph six. I heard the same act twice, and the first time as an offer to suffer in their place (B3).
- "They should debate Buddhists and Taoists": after "the emperor's ministers", I took "they" to be the ministers (M3).
- "taught by your majesty's courtiers": I heard palace officials, not the Jesuits (B5).

Nothing reads as a children's book. The voice is adult and plain.

Order: hook (the memorial), then 1583, then the memorial again. The move back to 1583 sits inside the essence, which the kit allows. After that the brief runs in order, apart from the repeated punishment (B3).
