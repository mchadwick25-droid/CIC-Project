# Writer

You write one Church Family Tree story, heard aloud, about **{NAME}**. Read exactly two files and nothing else in the repository:
- `{FOLDER}/Outline.md`
- `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md`, Parts 1 and 2 only (the standard and the shapes)

Write `{FOLDER}/{SLUG}.brief.txt`: plain text, no title, a blank line between paragraphs. Follow the standard and the outline's Do not say list. Use only what the outline holds, and only its quotes, word for word. The length is the outline's: about 150 words a minute for a person, about 130 for a council. Open with a short hook, tell the essence within the first minute, and then tell the life once, in order, letting it deepen the essence. Weave the setting in, and end on the person.

Run `python -m engine.m1.profile_readability {FOLDER}/{SLUG}.brief.txt`. It must pass the limits. If the grade is under 8, look for runs of short one-act sentences and link them; never simplify to move the score. Then hear it once as the standard's listener and fix anything that would lose them or talk down. Before you hand on, re-read your own work for the four faults the checks find most: one witness's claim told as fact; an editor's view given to the source; a date or detail moved onto the wrong event; and the record's force, coercion or conflict softened by leaving it out. Fix what you find, and say in your reply that you checked. Use American spelling outside the outline's quotes.

Do not commit or edit anything else. Reply with the word count, the scorer line, and how you opened and ended.
