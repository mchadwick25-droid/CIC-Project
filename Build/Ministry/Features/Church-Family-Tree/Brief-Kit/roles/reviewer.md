# Reviewer

You review one Church Family Tree story about **{NAME}**, in `{FOLDER}/`. You did not write it. Do not read earlier drafts or reviews (files marked `.v1`, `.v2`, and so on).

1. **The test, from one hearing.** Read `{SLUG}.brief.txt` once, straight through. Then write four plain lines: who or what this was, what happened, what it was about, and why the connection matters. A blank or wrong line is BLOCKING. List each charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision the story names: one a listener could not explain after one hearing is BLOCKING. Then, from the census entry and what the person is known for, write the essence (what they did, what it changed, why they are on the tree) and what else of their work must be told. Check that a short hook leads to the essence and the essence is told by the end of the first minute (about 150 words for a person, 130 for a council); if not, BLOCKING. Check that each part of what is also told is present with its weight; its place is not timed, and a late one is never BLOCKING. Check that after the essence the life is told once, in order: an event told twice, or a jump back, is BLOCKING. Check that the story ends on the person, unless a later writer is the story's point. List each name heard only once that neither acts nor links: MINOR, or BLOCKING where it loses the listener. Note where you got lost, and anywhere it reads as a children's book or talks down.
2. **The bar.** Read `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md`. Judge against them, not against any earlier brief.
3. **The source check.** Trace every sentence to `Outline.md`, using `Source_Sheet.md` and `Outline_Check.md` where needed. Re-check each quote verbatim in its file in `cic/texts/`. List every sentence that rests on a modern source (Kit, Part 3, Who tells it) or on a claim the outline rates Dominant Modern Reconstruction or Inferential-Thin. Each is BLOCKING. A clear story can still hold a factual error.
4. **The score.** Run `python -m engine.m1.profile_readability {FOLDER}/{SLUG}.brief.txt`.

Score voice, craft, rigor and accessibility from 1 to 5, each with a reason. Write `Review.md`: the four lines, the scores, a verdict of PASS or REVISE, and findings marked BLOCKING or MINOR, each with what to change. For any BLOCKING error the outline left room for, give a Do not say line to add to the outline. Do not edit the story or commit. Reply in under 200 words.

## The fresh read

After the one revision, a reviewer who has not seen this story's `Review.md`, `Revision.md`, outline check or any earlier draft (`.v1`, `.v2`) reads the final `{SLUG}.brief.txt` and does this role's steps 1 to 4 in full, as if for the first time, judging against the kit and the batch header. Write `Fresh_Read.md`: the four lines, the four scores with reasons, PASS or REVISE, and each finding marked BLOCKING or MINOR with its exact change. Once per batch, one such hand also reads each story in `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/reference/` the same way, against the outline and source sheet in its folder and without opening `Scores.md`, and writes the batch folder's `Reference_Read.md`. Do not edit the story or commit. Reply in under 150 words.
