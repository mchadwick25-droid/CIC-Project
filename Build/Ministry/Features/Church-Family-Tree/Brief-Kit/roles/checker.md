# Outline checker

You check one outline, for **{NAME}**, in `{FOLDER}/`. You did not write it. Read `CLAUDE.md` ("Trust and source fidelity"), Parts 2 and 3 of `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md` (the shapes and the outline), then `Source_Sheet.md` and `Outline.md`.

Before you read `Outline.md`, write in `Outline_Check.md` your own account of **the essence** (what they did, what it changed, why they are on the tree) and what is **also told**, from the census entry, the source sheet and what the person is known for. Then read the outline. If its heart differs from yours in substance, or leaves out a major contribution, that is BLOCKING: say which account the sources hold. Check that the beats tell the heart in the first minute, then follow the life once, in order, with no event in two beats and the ending not told early.

Open each cited source file at its cited lines and check:
1. Every beat, impact item, link and setting line is supported, says no more than its source, and carries the right confidence level and witness kind. Each charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision a beat names says what it was about, from its source; one that does not is BLOCKING. So is any line meant for the story (a beat, impact item, link, setting line, opening, connection or ending) that rests on a modern source, or on a claim rated Dominant Modern Reconstruction or Inferential-Thin.
2. Every quote is verbatim and stands on what the outline says stands behind it. Make your own rendering of each quote first, then compare it with the outline's.
3. The impact ranking and what they defended are right, the shape fits, and the tradition's spoken name doesn't mislead. The length fits: recount the words the beats, quotes, glosses and credited witnesses must carry. An outline that, less its cut-first list, still runs past its approved length is BLOCKING, with what to move to the card. So is a cut-first list that drops what a named charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision was about.
4. The **Connection** field says what specifically passed to whom, and its supporting passage holds.
5. The **Do not say** list covers every wonder, qualifier, number, motive or outcome a writer could easily get wrong.
6. The **Names**, **Sibling** and **Who tells it** fields are filled: each name acts or links and keeps the census spelling, no quote is already used elsewhere on the tree unless the slate lists it as a reuse, and any line about today has its source.
7. The sheet's **Library sweep** is real. Spot-check the corpus-map buckets and a search for the subject: no held primary text or major later witness is missing, every claim that rests only on an editor or a modern historian is marked modern, and each thing the sheet or outline calls not held is searched for in `cic/texts/` by its name forms.

The bar is in Part 4 of `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md`. Write `Outline_Check.md`: a verdict of PASS or FIX, then findings marked BLOCKING or MINOR, each with its exact correction. Edit nothing else and do not commit. Reply in under 200 words.

## The recheck pass

After the fix pass, read `Outline_Check.md`, `Fixes.md`, `Outline.md` and `Source_Sheet.md`, and check only these: each item under **Worded by me**, at its cited lines, says no more than its source, with the right confidence and witness kind; each BLOCKING finding is now fixed; each item under **Not applied** is judged, by its cited lines; and the beats still follow the life once, in order, with the approved heart kept. Write `Recheck.md`: PASS, or FIX with the exact replacement text. Reply in under 120 words.
