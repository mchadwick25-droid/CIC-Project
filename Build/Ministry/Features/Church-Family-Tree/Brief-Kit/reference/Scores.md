# The reference stories and their fixed counts

Each story here is frozen with its brief, its outline and its source sheet once it meets the kit; its kind kit names it. Its fixed counts are the reviewer role's reads of the frozen brief: the trust read's marks and the two listeners' faults. Each fresh read counts its kind's references the same way (Kit, Part 3, step 5), against the outline and source sheet in each folder.

The person references (Basil of Caesarea, Epiphanius of Salamis, and John Chrysostom, still to be added) are being brought to the kit of 2026-11-24 (Open-Gaps, "The three person references, brought to the 2026-11-24 kit", 2026-11-24). Their counts are set here when that is done. Until then, a fresh read records the references' counts in its batch's `Reference_Read.md` with nothing to set them beside.
