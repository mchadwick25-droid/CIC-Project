# Kind kit: a council

**Purpose.** Let a listener meet one council: why it met, what it fought over and decided, and what its decisions did to the churches.

**The test, for a council.** What this council was, what happened at it, what its main issue was about, and why the connection matters.

## The heart

- **The essence:** the major issue, what the council decided on it, and why it is on the tree.
- **Also told:** what called it, the story of the major issue, the other essential business, and each decision.

## Shape and traps

It should include what called it, the major issue and the story of it, what else it did, its decisions, and its impact, in whatever order tells it best, but lead with the major issue and keep it as the spine. Include other essential business from the outline so that one dispute does not misleadingly stand for the whole council. The impact is in whether its decisions held, and who they divided. Trap: one dispute swallowing the council, or a roll of names. Follow two or three voices through it.

## Length

Aim at about three minutes (about 390 words), up to about four and a half (585) where the content calls for it. A council may run past that only where its business truly needs it; the outline says why, and Mark rules on that length with the hearts.
