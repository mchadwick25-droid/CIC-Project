# Kind kit: a story

**Purpose.** Tell one happening from the record, and what it shows of the person or tradition it belongs to. A documented story sits on its entry's card; a map story stands on the map by itself.

**The test, for a story.** What happened, who was there, what it was about, and why it matters to the person or tradition it belongs to.

## The heart

- **The essence:** what happened, and what it shows of its person or tradition.
- **Also told:** who was there and what they did and said; what it was about; what came of it.
- **For a map story:** where it happened and the tradition it belongs to come through in the telling, since it stands alone on the map.

## Length

As the record holds it: most run one to two minutes.

## Traps

Retelling the whole life around the happening; a happening told only through what a later writer made of it; a scene the old sources do not describe.

## Steps

Every step. A documented story's card is its entry's card; a map story has none. Both are recorded.
