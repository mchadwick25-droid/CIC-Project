# Kind kit: a connection note

**Purpose.** Tell a visitor, in one or two sentences, what passed between two entries on the tree.

**The test, for a note.** What the tie was, what passed along it, and what that was about.

## The heart

- **The tie:** its kind on the tree (continued as, gave rise to, broke with, argued against, drew on), and the old line that shows it.
- **What passed:** the teaching, practice, text or people that went from one to the other, and what it was about.

## Form

One or two sentences, about 25 to 50 words, in the narrator's voice. Part 1's rules for every text apply; its rules for a narrated story do not.

## Steps

Research (a source sheet and an outline of the tie and what passed, with their source lines), outline check, write, brief review and one revision, the final check, Mark's reading of the brief, and release. A note has no hearts page, no card, no second reading unless it quotes, and no recording.
