# Stage 3 change order amendment: words per answer becomes a depth reading

Named change order. It replaces one row of the quote-door numbers table in `Stage3_Change_Order.md`. Coordination-level, AI-reviewed (not Mark's own words), made at the coordination thread's instruction of 2026-11-01 and resting on Settled row 10 ("yes we do not want thin or flat answers by stripping all the meat out because of technical problems. the richness and depth is what makes this unique").

## What changes

| Row | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Words per answer | lpc today 233; target "no worse than today" | Depth reading of the sealed set: one independent Opus reader, limited to the lpc shelf and records, classes each sealed answer rich, adequate, thin or flat and says whether a record in the ground answered. Reported with the counts. Target: no more thin and flat together than the first reading of the new path (3 of 8 sealed answers: thin 1, flat 2), and each thin or flat answer is read for whether the ground held a record that answered it. |

## Why

The 233 is the mean words per answer of lpc's 28 sealed probes in the saved drafts of fleet graded run 1. The old drafts held sentences the gates later cut, so a word count is not a depth reading, and the Stage 4 proof run showed words per answer falling (144 on the sealed 28) while the reading of the 8 sealed answers was rich 1, adequate 5, thin 2, flat 0. A count that can be met by padding or missed by removing unsupported sentences does not measure richness.

## What does not change

- Readability stays at the project target of grade 8 to 10 and reading ease at least 60 (CLAUDE.md, NorthStar), not the older figure in the table (6.5 and 74.0). The proof run's 28 sealed answers read 9.8 and 62.2.
- Every other row of the change order stands.
