## The sheet

Every reading and every proposed change under Stage 4 reports on all three axes.

| Axis | What it is | What a reading or change reports |
|---|---|---|
| Rigor | Academic rigor and acceptability: accuracy, context, the world's living ecology said correctly. | Serious and acceptable counts under Mark's bar; for each answer, whether it says the world's own situation correctly (its voice, who it speaks for, its limits in its own terms). |
| Craft | Depth, richness, accessibility, readability, engagement. | A depth rating per answer (rich, adequate, thin, flat); mean words; answers under 80 words; reading grade and ease against the target in CLAUDE.md. |
| Cost | API costs kept down: the production cost per answer. | Extra live model calls per answer and rough tokens against today's one call; which steps are code, which are batched, which can be skipped for low-risk turns. |

## Rule

No change is accepted that improves one axis by deleting from another unless that is stated and ruled. A fix that works by removing supportable content is not accepted while a bind or a repair is possible: bind or repair first, withhold last. Retrieval, which decides the material available, is part of craft.
