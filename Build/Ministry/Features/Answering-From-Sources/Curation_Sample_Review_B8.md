## Recurring pattern

- In the gazetteers, earlier rounds named only running heads and map references as non-entries. The wider class is any line after a place-name heading that the explicit list took as a new entry: the "Évêché. More ..." or "More., ..." line, a bishop-name line, a location line, a row of a footnote table. A quick test finds most of them: a division of 1 to 3 words followed by a division whose title is not a place name. Both scans need the whole sweep, not the cases listed above alone.
- An `allow_oversize` sentence of the form "no numbered heading line inside" can hold true and still hide an unnumbered printed heading that the scheme promises to address (Benson Intercalary). Check such divisions against the Contents, not only for numbered lines.

## Tooling observation

`check` cannot see either defect class. Two warnings would help: a warn on a division of 3 or fewer words followed by a division whose title starts with a lower-case word, "More", "Évêché" or an all-caps personal name; and, where a Contents range is declared, a warn on a Contents entry that has a body heading line but no division. Both are suggestions only.
