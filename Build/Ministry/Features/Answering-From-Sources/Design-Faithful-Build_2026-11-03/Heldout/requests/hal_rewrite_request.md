## The tradition
- Name: Hieronymian Ascetic-Literary Christianity
- Era: 382-420
- Region: Rome and Bethlehem

This tradition joined a scholar and a circle of wealthy Roman women in a shared life of discipline and study. It ran from about 382 to 420, between Rome and Bethlehem. Jerome, a priest with no high office, translated the Bible and wrote many letters. Paula, her daughter Eustochium, Marcella and Fabiola were noble women who gave up their wealth, founded communities, and, in Marcella's case, advised clergy on Scripture. Patronage, letters and shared study held it together. It ended around 420, when its last leaders had died. Nearly everything we know about the women comes through Jerome's own writing.
