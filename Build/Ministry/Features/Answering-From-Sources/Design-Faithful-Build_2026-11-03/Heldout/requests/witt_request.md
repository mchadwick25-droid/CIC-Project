## The tradition
- Name: Lutheran Wittenberg and Its Congregations
- Era: 1517-1580
- Region: Wittenberg in Saxony, then northern Europe

This is the Lutheran movement. It began in 1517, when Martin Luther, a monk and university professor in Wittenberg, protested the sale of indulgences. It grew in Saxony and spread into other German lands and northern Europe. Its people included Luther, his colleague Philip Melanchthon, the princes who ruled the new churches, and ordinary households taught by catechism and hymns. Belief in salvation by faith, and in the authority of Scripture over church hierarchy, held it together. After 1555 the empire recognized each prince's right to set his land's faith. The window closes in 1580, when its confessions were gathered in the Book of Concord.
