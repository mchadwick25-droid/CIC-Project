## The tradition
- Name: Desert Monasticism
- Era: 320-430
- Region: Egypt (Nile valley and desert edge)

Desert monasticism was a Christian movement in Egypt, running from about 320 to about 430. Men and women left village life to live on the desert edge, alone, in small groups, or in organized communities under a written rule. Most were Coptic-speaking villagers who wove rope and baskets to support themselves. Teaching passed by word of mouth, from an elder to a disciple, in short sayings. Weekly shared prayer and meals, and the struggle against demons, held them together. The movement's boundary is set around 430, when its teaching was being written down and exported, and bishops had begun settling its disputes. That end date is the least certain part.
