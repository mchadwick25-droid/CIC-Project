## The tradition
- Name: The Reformed Cities: Zurich and Geneva
- Era: 1519-1650
- Region: Zurich and Geneva, in Switzerland

This is the Swiss branch of the Protestant Reformation, from 1519 to 1650. It began when Ulrich Zwingli started preaching reform in Zurich, and Heinrich Bullinger led the city after him. John Calvin joined in Geneva in 1536, and the two cities agreed on the Lord's Supper in 1549. City councils, not princes or bishops, remade church life. Geneva added a Consistory of pastors and lay elders who oversaw everyday conduct. Its people held that God's sovereignty reaches to predestination and that Christ is present in the Supper in spirit, not in the bread. The end date is an administrative cutoff, since the Reformed tradition lives on in later churches.
