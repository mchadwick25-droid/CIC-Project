## The tradition
- Name: Donatism
- Era: 311-439
- Region: Roman North Africa, centered on Carthage and Numidia

Donatism was a rival church in Roman North Africa. It began around 311 to 312, when Carthage's new bishop, Caecilian, was accused of being consecrated by a man who had handed over scripture during the Diocletian persecution. Strict Christians rejected him and set up their own bishops, creating a parallel church in town after town. They held the same basic beliefs as their rivals but insisted that only a pure clergy could give valid sacraments, so they rebaptized newcomers. They honored martyrs and saw themselves as the true church. This study ends at the Vandal capture of Carthage in 439, though the movement lingered for centuries.
