## The tradition
- Name: Cappadocian Christianity
- Era: 325-394
- Region: Cappadocia and Pontus, highlands of Anatolia

This is the Christianity of Cappadocia and Pontus, highlands of Anatolia, from about 325 to 394. Its people held the faith of the Council of Nicaea about the Trinity. They were landowning families, village congregations, the poor who came to the church for help, and monks and nuns. Basil, his brother Gregory of Nyssa and their friend Gregory of Nazianzus are its main voices. Shared worship, household life, care for the poor and the fight over the Trinity held it together. Its tension broke in 381, when the empire made the Nicene faith law; the last great witness fell silent about 394.
