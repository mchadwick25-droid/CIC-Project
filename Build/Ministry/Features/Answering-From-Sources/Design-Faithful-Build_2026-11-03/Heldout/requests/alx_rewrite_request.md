## The tradition
- Name: Alexandrian Christianity
- Era: 150-400
- Region: Alexandria and Egypt

This is Christianity in Alexandria and the Egyptian lands around it, from about 150 to 400. It was known for learned teaching: teachers such as Clement and Origen read Scripture for deeper meaning and used Greek philosophy to explain the faith. Its best-recorded people were Greek-speaking city dwellers; Coptic-speaking villagers and early monks appear far less in the records. Bishops held growing authority, and teaching, worship and deep reading of Scripture shaped its believers. The account stops about 400, before the Council of Chalcedon in 451 split Egyptian Christians into separate communities.
