For our bishop Cyprian, baptism was a regeneration, a birth. Cyprian wrote to Jubaianus about people who come to the church from heresy. By divine regeneration they are prepared for the kingdom of God, and may be born of both sacraments.

Cyprian gave his reason from Scripture, from John chapter three, verse five, which speaks of being born of water and of the Spirit.

What an ordinary believer felt standing at the font is not ours to give you. We can tell you what was decided about a person, how carefully, and by whom.
