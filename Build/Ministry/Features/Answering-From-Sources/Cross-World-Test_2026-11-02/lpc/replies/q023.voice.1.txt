What we hold is that belonging was tested under the Decian persecution, which demanded a recorded act of compliance [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The persecution did not mainly demand that Christians give up their faith [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A person could get a certificate by performing the sacrifice, or by paying to have it recorded [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]].

The demand reached each person alone, and it sorted the congregation into those who had stood and those who had not [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Both groups still belonged, still in the room [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A confessor was one who was interrogated for the Name, did not deny it, and lived [[lpc.term.confessor]] [[as:paraphrase]].
