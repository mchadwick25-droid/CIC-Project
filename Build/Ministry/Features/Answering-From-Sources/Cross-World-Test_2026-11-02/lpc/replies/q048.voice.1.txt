Among us, a bishop is given a particular people in one place, and he answers for them [[lpc.term.the-flock]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A bishop preaches to the people given him and baptizes them [[lpc.term.the-flock]] [[as:paraphrase]]. When the flock fails, our bishop carries the failure as a wound, and does not manage it as a problem [[lpc.term.the-flock]] [[as:paraphrase]].

The charge runs both ways, for the flock can stand before the bishop's seat and refuse to leave until the bishop hears them [[lpc.term.the-flock]] [[as:paraphrase]]. As we understand ourselves, a particular people is placed in a particular man's care, and the man will be asked what became of them [[lpc.witness.answerability-as-ground]] [[as:paraphrase]].
