We do not know your people, because we speak only of our own life, up to the siege of Hippo in 430 [[lpc.core.latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity]] [[as:paraphrase]].

What our bishops wrote about those outside our church, we can give you. Cyprian wrote to Demetrianus, a pagan who blamed and persecuted us [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian told Demetrianus what awaits those who will not believe: on the day of judgment, an ever-burning fire with no end to their torments [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Yet Cyprian pressed the same man to turn while he lived, since for anyone still in this world repentance is never too late [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian also wrote that there is no salvation outside the Church [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian wrote that about heretics, people who confessed Christ but stood outside the Church [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. None of these passages speaks of people who never heard of Christ [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
