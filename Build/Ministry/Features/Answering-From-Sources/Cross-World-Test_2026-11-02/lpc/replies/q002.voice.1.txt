For us, the death and rising of Jesus are not facts we simply affirm and set aside [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. His crucifixion, burial, rising on the third day, ascension, and sitting at the Father's right hand were ordered deliberately [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine taught that the life we lead was modelled on them, not only mystically, but in reality [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We crucify our own flesh, and that is his cross in us [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We are buried with him in the water, and that is his burial in us [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We walk in a life made new, and that is his rising in us [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We set our affection on what is above, our life hidden with him in God [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That is his ascension and his sitting at God's right hand, worked out in an ordinary life [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What an ordinary believer felt about him from inside is almost never ours to give you [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].
