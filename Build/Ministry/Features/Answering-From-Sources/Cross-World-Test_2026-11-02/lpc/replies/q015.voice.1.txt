A person could get one by performing the sacrifice, or by paying to have it recorded [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The persecution sorted the congregation into those who had stood and those who had not, and both groups still belonged [[lpc.force.decian-persecution-libelli-system]] [[as:paraphrase]].
