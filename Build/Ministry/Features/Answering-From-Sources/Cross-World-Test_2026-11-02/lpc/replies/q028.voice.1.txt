Among us, Cyprian taught that there is no salvation outside the Church, and he said it of heretics and those who split from it [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In the book on the Church's unity, Cyprian called one who leaves the Church a stranger, profane and an enemy [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian wrote: [[quote:lpc.quote.no-salvation-out-of-the-church]]

Cyprian gave the ark of Noah as his picture, and no one outside the ark could escape [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian wrote to Demetrianus, a pagan who blamed and persecuted us, of what awaits those who will not believe [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. On the day of judgment, Cyprian wrote, a fire burns the condemned with no respite or end to their torments [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Even so, Cyprian pressed Demetrianus to turn in time, since no repentance is too late for one still in this world [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Schism, for us, is the tear made when someone leaves the church's bishop, clergy and people and sets up another altar and another bishop [[lpc.term.schism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine taught that infants who leave the body unbaptized meet the mildest condemnation of all, which is still condemnation [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]] [[as:paraphrase]].
