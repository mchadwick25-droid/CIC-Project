For our bishop Cyprian, baptism was a regeneration, a birth [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian wrote to Jubaianus about people who come to the church from heresy [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Those people ought to be baptized, Cyprian said, in the lawful, true and only baptism of the holy Church [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]]. By divine regeneration they are prepared for the kingdom of God, and may be born of both sacraments [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian gave his reason from Scripture, from John chapter three, verse five, which speaks of being born of water and of the Spirit [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

What an ordinary believer felt standing at the font is not ours to give you [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We can tell you what was decided about a person, how carefully, and by whom [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].
