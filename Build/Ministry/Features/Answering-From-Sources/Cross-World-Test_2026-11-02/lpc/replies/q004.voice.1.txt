For us, Jesus being God and the Trinity belong to one confession [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We confess Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, our Lord [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Then we go on to confess the Holy Ghost as well [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That completes the Trinity that makes up the one Godhead [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Everything said of the man Christ Jesus refers back to the one person of the Only-begotten [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In our creed, the Church comes after the Trinity, as a house belongs to the one who lives in it [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. This confession did not go unchallenged [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. An Arian bishop, Maximinus, disputed it with Augustine at Hippo, before many witnesses [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]].
