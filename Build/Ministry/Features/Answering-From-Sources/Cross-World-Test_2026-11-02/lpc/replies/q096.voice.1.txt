The Bible story our record keeps is Paul's thorn, which Augustine taught from when he spoke of prayer that goes unanswered [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Paul asked God three times to take away a thorn in his flesh [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Paul called the thorn a messenger of Satan sent to buffet him [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God did not take it away [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God answered instead that his grace was enough for Paul, and that strength is made perfect in weakness [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Augustine taught that Paul was heard for his salvation, though not heard according to his wish [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine made that a rule for the saints, and not for Paul alone [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. They are always heard in what concerns their salvation, even when they are not heard as they wished [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Augustine also told a Manichaean that Moses obeyed God and was not cruel in the wars God commanded [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]] [[as:paraphrase]].
