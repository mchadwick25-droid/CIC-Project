For us, God gives first: no effort of ours is enough on its own, and any good a person does was given to them before they did it [[lpc.term.grace]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine taught that God hears the saints in what concerns their salvation, even when he does not hear them as they wished [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Paul asked God three times to take away a thorn in his flesh, and God did not take it away [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God answered instead that his grace was sufficient, and that strength is made perfect in weakness [[lpc.witness.heard-for-salvation-not-for-wish]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We also held God to be just [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]] [[as:paraphrase]].
