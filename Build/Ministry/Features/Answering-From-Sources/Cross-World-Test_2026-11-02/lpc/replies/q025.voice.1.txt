Much of what we know of Cyprian's life comes from his own deacon, Pontius, who wrote in praise of his bishop and says so himself [[lpc.story.election-of-cyprian]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Pontius is the only witness for the claim that Cyprian was a new convert when he was chosen, and no other witness of ours confirms or corrects it [[lpc.story.election-of-cyprian]] [[as:paraphrase]]. No source outside Pontius describes the plague relief in Carthage [[lpc.story.the-plague-and-the-enemies]] [[as:paraphrase]].

What comes down to us is mostly what a bishop saw and decided, not what a plain believer was feeling [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].
