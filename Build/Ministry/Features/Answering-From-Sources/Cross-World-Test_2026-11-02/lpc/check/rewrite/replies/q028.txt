Among us, Cyprian taught that there is no salvation outside the Church, and Cyprian said so of heretics and those who split from the Church [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
In the book on the Church's unity, Cyprian called one who leaves the Church a stranger, profane and an enemy [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Cyprian gave the ark of Noah as his picture, and no one outside the ark could escape [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Cyprian wrote to Demetrianus, a pagan who blamed and persecuted us, of what awaits those who will not believe [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Even so, Cyprian pressed Demetrianus to turn in time, since no repentance is too late for one still in this world [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Schism, for us, is the tear made when someone leaves the church's bishop, clergy and people and sets up another altar and another bishop [[lpc.term.schism]] [[as:paraphrase]].
