(1) We baptized infants, and we would not make a newborn wait.

(6) The newborn, they wrote, "has not sinned, except in that" he was "born after the flesh according to Adam." Because of that he "has contracted the contagion of the ancient death." So "not his own sins, but the sins of another" are forgiven him.
