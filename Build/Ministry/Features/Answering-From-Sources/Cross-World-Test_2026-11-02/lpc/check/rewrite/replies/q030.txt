Many around Augustine found the endless punishment hard to bear, and Augustine said those doubters softened everything that seemed hard, without arguing against Scripture [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Augustine allowed the doubters to suppose, if the thought gave them pleasure, that the pains of the damned are at times eased [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Augustine would not let the doubters say that the punishment ends [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].
In the next chapter Augustine wrote that the death of the wicked will abide for ever [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].
