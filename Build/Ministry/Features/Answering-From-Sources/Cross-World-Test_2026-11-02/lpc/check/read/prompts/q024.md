Each record may carry a MUST NOT ASSERT line: a sentence that asserts what any bound record says must not be asserted is not supported, and when a sentence joins content from more than one record it must stay within the narrower scope of them (years, persons, cases covered), the MUST NOT ASSERT line winning.
