The sentence is supported only if every assertion in it is stated in the records or follows closely from them, with the same speaker and the same force. It is not supported if it adds a detail, drops or adds a negation, states a Contested or inferential point as settled, widens the years or the place a record limits itself to, states as the whole tradition's what one author says, or uses a word like "the bishop" for a person the records do not name. A sentence bound to several records is judged against all of them taken together: it is supported only if every assertion in it is stated in those records together or follows closely from them together; whatever they together do not support is not supported. A sentence that only frames the answer and claims nothing is supported. A word that refers back to something (he, they, "them", "the others", "it") must name, through the sentence before it, the very person or thing the records say it of; if the records cannot settle it, or the sentence before points to something else, it is not supported (unresolved_referent). A claim of exclusivity or absence ("only", "never", "none", "nothing") is supported only if a bound record states that exclusivity or absence; a record that merely names the thing does not.
