We held a person who had failed as one of our own, to be taken back and not shut out for good. The lapsed were our own people, not strangers and not enemies, and the lapsed sat where we sit. The lapsed came back by a public road, walked in steps under the bishop. The public road had an order, took time, and everyone could see the road. Taking back the failed was central in Cyprian's time, and our record places the concern of taking back the failed in Cyprian's phase alone.
