Among us a person was taught first and washed afterward. We told the person what the person was joining before the person joined. The person said the creed back, and then we baptized the person. Catechesis was the teaching given before baptism, including the creed. Augustine wrote a guide to catechesis. Both bishops preached to shape believers, and Augustine's works for those preparing for baptism have the same aim. Cyprian's council said a baptism given outside our one church counted for nothing. Augustine said a baptism given outside our one church was given, though it did no good there. Augustine would not rebaptize someone already baptized among heretics.
