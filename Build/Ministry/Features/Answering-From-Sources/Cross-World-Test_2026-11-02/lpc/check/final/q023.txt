The Decian persecution did not mainly demand that Christians give up their faith. A person could get a certificate by performing the sacrifice, or by paying to have the sacrifice recorded. The demand reached each person alone, and the Decian persecution sorted the congregation into those who had stood and those who had not. Both those who had stood and those who had not still belonged, still in the room. A confessor was one who was interrogated for the Name, did not deny the Name, and lived.
