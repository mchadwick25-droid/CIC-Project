We held that a newborn comes with no sins of his own, yet has taken on the contagion of the ancient death, being born after the flesh according to Adam. We would not make a newborn wait for baptism. Cyprian and his colleagues met in council, sixty-six in number, and judged that God's mercy and grace are not to be refused to any one born of man. Augustine went on to the hard end of the question of newborns. Augustine wrote that in baptism infants die only to original sin. The mildest condemnation Augustine named for unbaptized infants is still condemnation.
