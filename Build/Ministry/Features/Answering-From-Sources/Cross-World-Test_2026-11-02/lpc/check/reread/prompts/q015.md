It is not supported if it adds a detail, drops or adds a negation, states a Contested or inferential point as settled, widens the years or the place a record limits itself to, states as the whole tradition's what one author says, or uses a word like "the bishop" for a person the records do not name. A word that refers back to something (he, they, "them", "the others", "it") must name, through the sentence before it, the very person or thing the records say it of; if the records cannot settle it, or the sentence before points to something else, it is not supported (unresolved_referent).
