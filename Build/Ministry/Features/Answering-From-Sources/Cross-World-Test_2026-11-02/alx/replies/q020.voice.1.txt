In our own words, a person's change was a wound healed and an image restored [[alx.term.eikon]] [[alx.term.hamartia]] [[alx.term.psyche]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Hamartia, our word for sin, meant the soul turned from God toward lesser goods, more a wound than a debt owed or a law broken [[alx.term.hamartia]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Every person bears the mark God gave at creation, which sin can damage but never destroy [[alx.term.eikon]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The psyche, the whole person made for God, is being restored toward the likeness of the one whose image the person bears [[alx.term.psyche]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Psyche did not mean a ghost caged in flesh, for the person was alive and acting through the body [[alx.term.psyche]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In the school tradition, learning was the very path of the soul's transformation, not preparation for it [[alx.gravity.learning-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
