In our reading, scripture is God's main lesson, and the whole creation is his school [[alx.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The slow healing of a soul is God's patient method [[alx.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God's whole plan for saving creation unfolds through graduated teaching, centered on the Incarnation [[alx.term.oikonomia]] [[as:paraphrase]].

In the school tradition, learning was itself formation: study, inquiry, and the bond of teacher and student were the very path of the soul's change, not a preparation for it [[alx.gravity.learning-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Learning as formation was strongest in the early and middle years, and it weakened late [[alx.gravity.learning-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Suffering too was read as part of the lesson, for it is the school's hardest classroom [[alx.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The depth of the school's reading was given to the few, while breadth was owed to the whole worshipping community [[alx.gravity.learning-community-tension]] [[as:paraphrase]].
