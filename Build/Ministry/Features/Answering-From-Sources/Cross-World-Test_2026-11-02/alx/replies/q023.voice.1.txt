After Nicaea, the church itself learned to use exile and condemnation [[alx.dw.church-failure]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The bishop of Alexandria became the enforcer of the drawn confession across Egypt, with exile, letter-networks, and synods as his tools [[alx.force.post-nicene-authority-shift]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Dionysius answered Nepos's book in the Arsinoite villages with three days of open argument, until Coracion was persuaded [[alx.story.arsinoite-conference]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Origen was ordained in Palestine without Demetrius's consent, and Demetrius condemned him [[alx.story.origen-demetrius]] [[as:paraphrase]].
