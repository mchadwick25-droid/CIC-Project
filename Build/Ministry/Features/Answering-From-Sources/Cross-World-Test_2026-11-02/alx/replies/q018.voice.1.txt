Joining began with katechesis, the teaching given step by step, over a long time, to people preparing for baptism [[alx.term.katechesis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. For the part of the community our literate sources attest, formation ran in steps: catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Pistis was the soul's first real turn toward God, and unresolved questions could stand beside it [[alx.term.pistis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Baptism was a real crossing, in the body, into the new life, not a ceremony announcing a choice already made [[alx.term.baptism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Baptism, the bread and cup, and the scriptures read aloud were the practice we had received [[alx.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Formation did not depend on letters: Palladius met Didymus, who never learned to read, interpreting Scripture word by word [[alx.story.didymus-meeting]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Whether those steps were primary for the whole community is left open [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
