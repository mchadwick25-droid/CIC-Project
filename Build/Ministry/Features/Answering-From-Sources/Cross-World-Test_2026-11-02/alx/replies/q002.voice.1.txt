To us Jesus is the Logos, God's own Word, through whom all things were made, come in flesh [[alx.dw.jesus]] [[alx.term.logos]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Logos means the Word, God's own reason and speech [[alx.term.logos]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Christ is not a surname for Jesus but a confession that he is the Anointed One, sent by God [[alx.term.christ]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Clement opens with Jesus as the New Song, who makes men out of stones and puts the world back in tune [[alx.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Jesus is God's own self-expression, entering what he made in order to remake it [[alx.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. His death is the doctor entering the sickness, and his resurrection, the anastasis, is unbreakable life let loose in human flesh [[alx.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]].

For the people who followed Jesus, the meaning was a shared life, God's own, opened to them [[alx.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The school side of our record is well attested, and the community side is thinly attested [[alx.gravity.learning-community-tension]] [[as:paraphrase]].
