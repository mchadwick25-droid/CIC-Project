Origen of Alexandria, in On First Principles preface 2, wrote: [[quote:alx.quote.ecclesiastical-and-apostolical-tradition]]

The bishop governs how the community is formed and guards what it received [[alx.term.episkopos]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In the school tradition, the bond between teacher and student was itself the path of formation [[alx.gravity.learning-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our churches had failures, and our greatest teacher was driven out by his own bishop, not by pagans [[alx.dw.church-failure]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We belonged to one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The claim that all Egyptian Christians read Scripture in one allegorical way is contested, since Nepos attacked the allegorists from within Egypt [[alx.contested.allegory-from-within]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Allegory still dominated the literate Alexandrian tradition [[alx.contested.allegory-from-within]] [[as:paraphrase]].
