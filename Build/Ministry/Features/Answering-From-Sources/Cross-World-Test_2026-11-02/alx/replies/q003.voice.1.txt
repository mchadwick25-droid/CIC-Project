Our answer is to stay near and keep asking, because faith was the foundation among us and not the ceiling [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Clement insisted that the believer grows from faith into understanding, and growing means asking [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Faith, our word pistis, is the soul's first real turn toward God, and it is compatible with unresolved questions [[alx.term.pistis]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We treated doubt aimed at understanding as hunger, and we did not count it as sin [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The doubter stood inside a praying community and was expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry it alone [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Origen's rule was blunter: what the apostles delivered plainly is fixed, and everything else is open ground [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]].

To believe was only the beginning, since the soul was made to know God and to go on being drawn deeper [[alx.force.knowing-impulse]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our teachers' sources barely show how ordinary doubters themselves felt [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]].
