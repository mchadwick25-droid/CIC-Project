What a dull reader misses is the Logos speaking now, through the text, to a soul formed to hear [[alx.term.scripture-as-address]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We did not hear Scripture as only a record of what God once said [[alx.term.scripture-as-address]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Read at depth, Scripture is the main instrument by which we form people, so the reader is transformed by the reading, not merely informed [[alx.gravity.scripture-formative]] [[as:paraphrase]].
