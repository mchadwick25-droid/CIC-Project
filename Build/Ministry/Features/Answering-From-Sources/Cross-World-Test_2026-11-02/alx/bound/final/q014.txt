What we hold is that danger came on and off across three centuries before 325, not as one unbroken hunt. Four persecutions marked our years: the Antonine, the Severan, the Decian from 249 to 251, and the Diocletianic from 303 to 311. The last two did most to shape formation.

In those years property was seized, teachers' fathers were beheaded, and bishops fled. Origen himself was jailed and tortured under Decius.

When the threat came, it was a test of whether what formation had built in the soul was equal to the ultimate choice. The events are well attested, while the memory kept between them is a modern reconstruction.
