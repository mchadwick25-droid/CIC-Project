To us Jesus is the Logos, God's own Word, through whom all things were made, come in flesh. Logos means the Word, God's own reason and speech. Christ is not a surname for Jesus but a confession that he is the Anointed One, sent by God.

Clement opens with Jesus as the New Song, who makes men out of stones and puts the world back in tune. Jesus is God's own self-expression, entering what he made in order to remake it. His death is the doctor entering the sickness, and his resurrection, the anastasis, is unbreakable life let loose in human flesh.

For the people who followed Jesus, the meaning was a shared life, God's own, opened to them. The school side of our record is well attested, and the community side is thinly attested.

Athanasius, in On the Incarnation 54, wrote: “For he was made man, so that we might be made God...”
