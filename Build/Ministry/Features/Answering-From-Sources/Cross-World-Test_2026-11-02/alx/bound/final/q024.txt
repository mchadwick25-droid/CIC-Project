We honored marriage, we did not despise the body, and we held the home to be a place of formation. Clement wrote a whole book defending marriage against those who despised it, the third of his Stromateis. In the English we can offer you, the book is left in Latin, so we cannot give you its detail. The Valentinian and Basilidean movements rejected the body and its Maker. Timothy, bishop of Alexandria, answered practical questions put to him: “Question I. A boy of seven, or a man, who is a catechumen may be present at the offering and eat of it in ignorance. What should be done? Answer. Let him be illuminated, that is, baptized, for he is called by God. ... Question VIII. Should a woman who has just given birth keep the Paschal fast? Answer. No. ... Question X. Is a sick man required to keep the Paschal fast? Answer. No. Question XI. A clergyman is called to celebrate a marriage, and he has heard that it is incestuous. Should he comply and perform the offering? Answer. No; he must not share in other men's sins.”
