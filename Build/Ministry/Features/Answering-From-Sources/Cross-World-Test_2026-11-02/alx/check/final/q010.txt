In our reading, scripture is God's main lesson, and the whole creation is God's school. The slow healing of a soul is God's patient method. God's whole plan for saving creation unfolds through graduated teaching, centered on the Incarnation. In the school tradition, learning was itself formation: study, inquiry, and the bond of teacher and student were the very path of the soul's change, not a preparation for the soul's change. Learning as formation was strongest in the early and middle years, and weakened late. Suffering too was read as part of the lesson, for suffering is the school's hardest classroom. The school side of our record is well attested, and the community side is thinly attested.
