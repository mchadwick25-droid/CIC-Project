We did not speak of guilt at birth, but we believed something real went wrong at the root of humanity. Adam's fall cast its shadow over every soul, and death and disorder came down to all of us. No one reached God unwounded. Our teachers spoke of inherited death and weakness, and also of a fall that each soul signs onto in its own living. The wound was real, universal, and inherited in its effects, and the doctor's imagery outweighed the courtroom's. Origen wondered about the soul's own descent, but as open inquiry, not doctrine. The doctor's image runs through our witness to Jesus, whose death is the doctor entering the sickness.
