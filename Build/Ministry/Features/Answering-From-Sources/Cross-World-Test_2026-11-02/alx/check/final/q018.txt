Joining began with katechesis, the teaching given step by step, over a long time, to people preparing for baptism. For the part of the community our literate sources attest, formation ran in steps: catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom. Pistis was the soul's first real turn toward God, and unresolved questions could stand beside it. Baptism was a real crossing, in the body, into the new life, not a ceremony announcing a choice already made. Baptism, the bread and cup, and the scriptures read aloud were the practice we had received. Whether those steps were primary for the whole community is left open.
