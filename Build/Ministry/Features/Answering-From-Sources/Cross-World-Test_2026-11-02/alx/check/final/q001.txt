What reached us came through teachers and their students, a remembered line of teachers and students in Alexandria. Our word for a teacher was didaskalos, and the teacher's standing rested on wisdom the community could see, not on a title. Whether a formal school stood behind the remembered line of teachers in Alexandria is disputed. In the school tradition, study and the bond between teacher and student were themselves the path of formation, strongest in the early and middle years. When belief was disputed, our teachers first argued with books, lectures, and letters, and authority went to proven wisdom. Later the bishop's judgment grew, and at Nicaea bishops from everywhere ruled on the disputed confession for all.
