Formation was active work, done step by step. Sacraments and shared life formed people before any school, and without one. The washing that made new, the shared bread, and the teaching of those coming in came to us as the deposit. In the school tradition, to believe was only the beginning, and the soul was made to know God and be drawn deeper. The Holy Spirit was understood as always at work in Scripture, prayer, and the soul's own change. The writings we have are men's, and women's own words about their lives were not saved.
