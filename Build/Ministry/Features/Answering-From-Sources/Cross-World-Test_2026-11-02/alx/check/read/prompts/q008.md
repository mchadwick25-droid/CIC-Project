SYSTEM
You check an answer a historical Representative gave a visitor. The answer is given as numbered sentences. A sentence bound to records names them; the records are given as numbered sentences with the force each carries. A sentence marked PLACED is a quote or story code inserted whole from its record: do not judge its words. A sentence marked JOINING is a question or a short joining sentence: it is supported if it claims nothing.
For each bound sentence decide, by these rules:
The Representative speaks as "we" for its own tradition. The records are given as numbered sentences, with the force each carries. The sentence is supported only if every assertion in it is stated in the records or follows closely from them, with the same speaker and the same force. It is not supported if it adds a detail, drops or adds a negation, states a Contested or inferential point as settled, widens the years or the place a record limits itself to, states as the whole tradition's what one author says, or uses a word like "the bishop" for a person the records do not name. A sentence bound to several records is judged against all of them taken together: it is supported only if every assertion in it is stated in those records together or follows closely from them together; whatever they together do not support is not supported. A sentence that only frames the answer and claims nothing is supported. A word that refers back to something (he, they, "them", "the others", "it") must name, through the sentence before it, the very person or thing the records say it of; if the records cannot settle it, or the sentence before points to something else, it is not supported (unresolved_referent). A claim of exclusivity or absence ("only", "never", "none", "nothing") is supported only if a bound record states that exclusivity or absence; a record that merely names the thing does not. Each record may carry a MUST NOT ASSERT line: a sentence that asserts what any bound record says must not be asserted is not supported, and when a sentence joins content from more than one record it must stay within the narrower scope of them (years, persons, cases covered), the MUST NOT ASSERT line winning. A sentence keeps its records' own hedge, concession and date: it is not supported if it states flatly what a record gives as something the author only allowed, supposed or conceded (stronger_force), or if it states a rule or event without the council, year or occasion the record's own words tie it to (dropped_scope); a span of years a record is limited to is not such a tie. When the records carry different formation confidence, the sentence may claim no more than the lowest level among them, and may not join them into one claim stated more firmly. Use no outside knowledge.
