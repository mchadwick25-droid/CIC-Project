We held that Christ is truly given, by the Spirit, to whoever receives him believing. We refused Rome's claim that the Mass repeats Christ's own sacrifice. We also refused Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits locally in the bread. Zurich's refusal of the Mass in 1523 matured at Marburg in 1529 into a further refusal of corporeal presence, aimed at Wittenberg. In 1549 the two cities stated together what we affirm. In today's English, the Ministers of the Church of Zurich and John Calvin says: “Therefore, though we distinguish signs from the things signified, as we ought, we do not separate the thing itself from the signs...”
