What we can tell you plainly is how we handled a person who wronged the community. At Geneva our Consistory, pastors and lay elders together, held the standing power of censure. Its harshest step was excommunication, which shut a person out of the Supper and the visible church for sin not repented. Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. John Calvin wrote: “By these governors I mean elders chosen from the people. They join with the pastors to declare censures and to carry out discipline.”

What we cannot give you is a real case, with a named person and a real hearing. At Zurich our record shows no such body, because the council ruled church and city directly.
