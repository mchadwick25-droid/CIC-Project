We claim no living chain back to Jesus, because no one among us met anyone who met him. What we have is the text, and we hold the text to be enough on its own terms. So our test for any practice was whether Scripture warrants it, and we let go of what would not stand. Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require, and whatever Scripture does yield, no pope, council or custom may set aside. Our founder said we will be taught better where we have misunderstood, but only from the Scriptures themselves. Calvin's Institutes drew heavily on Augustine, a line across eleven centuries with no institutional continuity, so influence and not identity. The Anabaptists pressed a rival reading from inside our own founding circle: we do not deny they read Scripture, we deny they read it whole.
