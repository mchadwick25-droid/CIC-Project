At Geneva, our Consistory answered someone who wronged the community with censure, and its harshest step was excommunication. Excommunication meant exclusion from the Supper and from the visible church, answering sin that was not repented. Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. The Consistory was pastors and lay elders together, and Zurich's council ruled church and city directly instead. John Calvin wrote: “By these governors I mean elders chosen from the people. They join with the pastors to declare censures and to carry out discipline.”

What we cannot give you is a single real case: a named person, a real hearing, what was said, or what happened to the person afterward. That record exists in Geneva's consistory registers, and the registers have not reached us.
