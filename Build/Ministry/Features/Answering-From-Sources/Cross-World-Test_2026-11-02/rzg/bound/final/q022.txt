We cannot walk you through an ordinary day, because what an ordinary citizen of Zurich or Geneva did from waking to sleeping has not reached us in our words. How a Sunday service felt to sit through, or what a Consistory summons felt like to receive, has not reached us either. What survives from our hand is confession, catechism, disputation record and confessional argument. At Geneva the Consistory, a body of pastors and lay elders, held the daily conduct of the whole population to account. At Zurich our record shows no such body, because the council ruled church and city directly.
