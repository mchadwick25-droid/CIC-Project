What we hold of our worship is reasoned from our doctrine, and no source in our record describes the rooms directly. We reason from what we held about the Mass: no image to bow to, and a table in place of an altar built for sacrifice. Article XVIII refused the sacrificial character of the Mass, and our picture of the plain table rests there. By most accounts the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s.
