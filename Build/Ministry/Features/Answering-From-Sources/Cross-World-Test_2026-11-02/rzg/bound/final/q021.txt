Our churches kept a plain table where an altar once stood. No image stood to bow to, and no altar was built for a sacrifice we no longer offered. Article XVIII refused the sacrificial character of the Mass, and the doctrine behind the plain table is drawn from there. By most accounts the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s, and no instrument answered the voice in worship for years after. We reason all of this from what we held about the Mass, and no source in our record describes the rooms directly. Catholic pressure bore on us through those years, with Jesuits at Fribourg from 1580 and a reconquest fought on Geneva's own doorstep in the 1590s.
