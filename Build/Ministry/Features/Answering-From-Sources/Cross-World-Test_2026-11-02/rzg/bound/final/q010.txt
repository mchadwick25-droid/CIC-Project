We read Scripture as the final authority for our doctrine and our church order, above church tradition or a pope's decree.

Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require.

Whatever Scripture does yield, no pope, council, or custom may set aside.

At Zurich we put this into practice through public Disputation, argued aloud before the city council until the council judged what the text had yielded.

At Geneva we used sustained catechesis, with doctrine built up book by book and taught to the whole population.

Our two cities never borrowed each other's method.

Each time a rival claim came, we asked the same question: does this reading hold against Scripture?

The Anabaptist schism of 1525 tested our method from inside our own founding circle.

We do not deny the Anabaptists read Scripture, but we deny they read it whole.

Nothing in our record reflects on whether the violent content of Scripture troubled our people.
