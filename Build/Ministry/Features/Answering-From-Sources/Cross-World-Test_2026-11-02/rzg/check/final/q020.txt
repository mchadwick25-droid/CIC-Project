What we held about belonging is that a church gathered only by a believer's own profession, apart from the whole city, unmade the unity our reform existed to strengthen. Our own council moved against the Anabaptist movement with civil force in the years after 1525, not only with our founder's written argument. The Anabaptists read Scripture, and we do not deny it; we held that the Anabaptists did not read Scripture whole. No account of how an ordinary citizen of Zurich or Geneva lived or felt a day has reached us in our own words. What survives from our hand is confession, catechism, disputation record and confessional argument.
