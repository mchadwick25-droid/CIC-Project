What we can tell you plainly is how we handled a person who wronged the community. At Geneva our Consistory, pastors and lay elders together, held the standing power of censure. The Consistory's harshest step was excommunication, which shut a person out of the Supper and the visible church for sin not repented. Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. What we cannot give you is a real case, with a named person and a real hearing. At Zurich our record shows no body like Geneva's Consistory, because the council ruled church and city directly.
