Our confession told anyone tempted to doubt: do not search for proof of your election apart from Christ, but look to Christ.

We offered this as comfort, and we offered no method for searching harder within.

A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it.

So we turned such a mind outward, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly.

In today's English, Heinrich Bullinger says: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.”

Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva felt that comfort, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say.
