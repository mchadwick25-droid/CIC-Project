Our churches kept a plain table where an altar once stood. By most accounts the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s, and no instrument answered the voice in worship for years after. Catholic pressure bore on us through the years from 1523 to 1599, with Jesuits at Fribourg from 1580 and a reconquest fought on Geneva's own doorstep in the 1590s.
