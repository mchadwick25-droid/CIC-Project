At Geneva, our Consistory answered someone who wronged the community with censure, and its harshest step was excommunication. Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. The Consistory was pastors and lay elders together, and Zurich's council ruled church and city directly instead. What we cannot give you is a single real case: a named person, a real hearing, what was said, or what happened to the person afterward. A record of a real case exists in Geneva's consistory registers, and the registers have not reached us.
