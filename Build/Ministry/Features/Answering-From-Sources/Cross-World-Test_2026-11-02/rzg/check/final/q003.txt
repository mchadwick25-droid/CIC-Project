We would turn you away from searching harder within yourself and toward Christ. To a mind that could not quiet itself, we offered no method for searching harder within. Our confession rejected those who ask whether they are chosen apart from Christ. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. So we turned a mind that could not quiet itself outward, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly. We offered the confession's word as comfort: look to Christ, and see your election reflected there. What we have of Christ is the text, and we hold the text enough on its own terms.
