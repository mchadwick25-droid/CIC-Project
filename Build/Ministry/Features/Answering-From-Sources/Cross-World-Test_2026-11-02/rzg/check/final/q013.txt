What our record holds is Catholic pressure, sustained across the whole of our window.

The Jesuits, founded in 1540, were established at Fribourg from 1580, directly facing Reformed Bern and Geneva.

In the 1590s the Catholic reconquest of the Chablais was fought on Geneva's own doorstep.

Those named episodes kept our refusal of Rome alive across the whole window, without ever ending our world.

With Rome, our quarrel was never over who Jesus is.

To us Jesus is what the whole church says he is.

What is distinctly ours is what we were taught about who is chosen in him.
