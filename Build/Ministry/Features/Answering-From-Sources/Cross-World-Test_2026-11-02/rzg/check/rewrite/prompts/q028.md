Record rzg.gravity.spiritual-presence-rejection-of-corporeal-sacrificial-mediation [classification: primary, formation confidence: Documented, limited to the years 1523 to 1650]
(1) Spiritual Presence and the Rejection of Corporeal/Sacrificial Mediation We refuse Rome's claim that the Mass repeats Christ's own sacrifice, and we refuse Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits locally in the bread.
(2) What we affirm - that Christ is truly given, by the Spirit, to whoever receives him believing - took both cities and a 1549 agreement to state together.
(3) Zurich's own founding refusal of the Mass (1523) matured, at Marburg (1529), into a further refusal of corporeal presence specifically aimed at Wittenberg, not Rome; the Consensus Tigurinus (1549) then supplies this doctrine's own strand-bridging positive formula, resolving our own internal tension (T2) in text without settling, in our own words, whether that resolution is genuine synthesis or diplomatic elasticity.
(4) The cities refuse both Rome's claim that the Mass repeats Christ's sacrifice and Wittenberg's claim that his body sits in the bread, affirming Christ truly given by the Spirit.
means: The cities refuse both Rome's claim that the Mass repeats Christ's sacrifice and Wittenberg's claim that his body sits in the bread, affirming Christ truly given by the Spirit.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a claim that the cities accepted the Mass as a sacrifice; a claim that the cities accepted Wittenberg's local presence of Christ's body
