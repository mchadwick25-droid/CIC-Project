QUESTION
Someone tells you they cannot manage to believe in Jesus - what is your response to them?

RECORDS

Record rzg.witness.a-quieted-mind [formation confidence: Documented, limited to the years 1566 to 1566]
(1) To someone who could not quiet their own mind, we offered no method for searching harder within.
(2) Our confession rejected those who ask whether they are chosen apart from Christ.
(3) It named Christ as the mirror in which we see our election.
(4) A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it.
(5) So we turned it outward instead, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly.
(6) If your own mind will not settle, we do not tell you to look harder inside yourself.
(7) We tell you to look away from yourself entirely.
(8) Our own confession says it plainly: we learn whether we are chosen by looking to Christ, not apart from him.
(9) This is not a trick of words.
(10) Self-examination, pressed far enough, finds only more of itself -- doubt answering doubt.
(11) Our own confession points the searching mind somewhere outside its own churning: to Christ, once, rather than to the self, endlessly.
(12) For a mind that will not settle, our confession turns attention away from endless self-examination toward Christ as the mirror of election.
means: For a mind that will not settle, our confession turns attention away from endless self-examination toward Christ as the mirror of election.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a claim that this teaching actually quieted every anxious believer; a claim that this confession prescribed searching harder within oneself; a claim about this world's teaching on forgiving the unrepentant or unanswered prayer

Record rzg.limit.doubt-and-assurance [formation confidence: Documented, limited to the years 1519 to 1650]
(1) Was there room among us for doubt?
(2) We can give you our doctrine's answer.
(3) Our confession tells anyone tempted to doubt: do not search for proof of your election apart from Christ.
(4) Look to Christ, and see your election reflected there.
(5) It offers this as comfort.
(6) Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva actually felt that comfort, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say.
(7) We have given you what we taught.
(8) We cannot give you what was felt.
(9) The record gives the doctrine's answer that assurance is found by looking to Christ, but not whether ordinary believers actually felt settled or anxious.
means: The record gives the doctrine's answer that assurance is found by looking to Christ, but not whether ordinary believers actually felt settled or anxious.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a claim that ordinary believers at Zurich or Geneva experienced election as comfort; a claim that ordinary believers experienced it as anxiety

Record rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ [formation confidence: Documented, limited to the years 1523 to 1523]
(1) What we actually have of Christ is not a living chain.
(2) No one among us met anyone who met him; fifteen hundred years stand between his own life and ours.
(3) What we have is the text, and we hold that it is enough on its own terms.
(4) Our own founder said so of himself, and we made it our own rule: where we have not rightly understood the Scriptures, we will be taught better -- but only from the Scriptures themselves, not from a chain of memory we do not possess and do not need.
(5) We will not pretend to a chain we do not have.
(6) Fifteen hundred years separate us from Christ.
(7) No one among us knew anyone who saw him, and we do not claim otherwise.
(8) What we have is the text itself, and we hold that the text is enough: 'where I have not now correctly understood said Scriptures I shall allow myself to be taught better, but only from said Scriptures' -- our own founder's own confession, and our own standing rule.
(9) We do not treat the text as a substitute for a living memory we lack.
(10) We treat it as sufficient in itself, tested against itself, generation after generation, rather than handed down through a chain of witnesses we would otherwise need and do not have.
(11) We claim no living chain of memory back to Jesus, only Scripture, held sufficient and corrected only by itself.
means: We claim no living chain of memory back to Jesus, only Scripture, held sufficient and corrected only by itself.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a claim that this world possessed an unbroken chain of witnesses to Jesus; a claim that a text alone was enough for everyone who held it; a claim that Scripture alone meant private individual interpretation
