(2) We will not dodge it: our own council moved against the Anabaptist movement with civil force in the years after 1525, not only with our founder's own written argument.

(9) We will not pretend it did not happen.

(11) What we will say in our own defense is this: a church gathered only by a believer's own profession, apart from the whole city, was not, to us, a smaller version of what we had built.
(12) It unmade the very thing our own reform was built to strengthen -- one city, one church, answerable together to the same tested Scripture.
(13) We deny they read Scripture wrong.
(14) We deny they read it whole.
(15) We admit civil force against the Anabaptists and defend refusing them because a profession-only church unmade the one city-church.
