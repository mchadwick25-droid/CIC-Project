At Geneva, our Consistory answered someone who wronged the community with censure, and its harshest step was excommunication [[rzg.limit.consistory-case-narrative]] [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Excommunication meant exclusion from the Supper and from the visible church, answering sin that was not repented [[rzg.limit.consistory-case-narrative]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance [[rzg.limit.consistory-case-narrative]] [[as:paraphrase]].
The Consistory was pastors and lay elders together, and Zurich's council ruled church and city directly instead [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[as:paraphrase]].
