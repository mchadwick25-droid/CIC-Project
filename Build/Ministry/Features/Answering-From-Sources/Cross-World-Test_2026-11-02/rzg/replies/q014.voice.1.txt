At Zurich the city council governed church and city together, while at Geneva a body of pastors and lay elders held the whole population's conduct answerable [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[rzg.gravity.consistorial-church-discipline]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Scripture alone, not church tradition or a pope's decree, settled how we governed our churches [[rzg.term.sola-scriptura]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Bullinger held the title of antistes, Zurich's chief pastor, after Zwingli died, and the office answered to the city council [[rzg.term.antistes]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Calvin's 1541 recall to Geneva produced the Ecclesiastical Ordinances, which created a Consistory of pastors and lay elders with power of censure [[rzg.force.1541-ecclesiastical-ordinances-consistory-founding]] [[as:paraphrase]].
