We refused Rome's claim that the Mass repeats Christ's own sacrifice [[rzg.gravity.spiritual-presence-rejection-of-corporeal-sacrificial-mediation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
We also refused Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits locally in the bread [[rzg.gravity.spiritual-presence-rejection-of-corporeal-sacrificial-mediation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
