Our confession told anyone tempted to doubt: do not search for proof of your election apart from Christ, but look to Christ [[rzg.limit.doubt-and-assurance]] [[rzg.witness.a-quieted-mind]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We offered this as comfort, and we offered no method for searching harder within [[rzg.limit.doubt-and-assurance]] [[rzg.witness.a-quieted-mind]] [[as:paraphrase]].

A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it [[rzg.witness.a-quieted-mind]] [[as:paraphrase]].

So we turned such a mind outward, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly [[rzg.witness.a-quieted-mind]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva felt that comfort, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say [[rzg.limit.doubt-and-assurance]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We can give you what we taught, but we cannot give you what was felt [[rzg.limit.doubt-and-assurance]] [[as:paraphrase]].
