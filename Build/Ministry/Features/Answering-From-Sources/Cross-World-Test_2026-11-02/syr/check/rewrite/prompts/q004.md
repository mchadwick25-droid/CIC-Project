QUESTION
Explain your community's understanding of Jesus's divine status and its relation to God the Father and the Spirit.

RECORDS

Record syr.dw.was-jesus-god [formation confidence: Widely Accepted, limited to the years 325 to 373]
(1) Jesus is God, we said, in our own way of speaking.
(2) The sage calls it a sure thing: Jesus our Lord is God, the Son of God, the King, the King's Son, Light of light.
(3) He used a chain of Scripture's own names, not a philosopher's definition.
(4) The poet confesses the Only-Begotten who was with Deity and came to dwell among us.
(5) Asked about the Trinity, we answered from our font.
(6) Our baptismal hymns sing the Father rejoicing with his Son and the Spirit over the baptized.
(7) Our teachers held that in baptism's second birth the believer received the Holy Spirit.
(8) Then we guarded the mystery.
(9) Our teachers warned against prying scrutiny into the Godhead, because they held that what God is, is better sung than dissected.
(10) We stood with the great council's faith.
(11) Our tradition remembers that our own bishop of Nisibis sat at Nicaea.
(12) But our native tongue for that faith was praise.
(13) Jesus is confessed as God and Son of God, in Scripture's names the Father, the Son, and the Spirit are named together over baptism, and baptism's second birth gives the Holy Spirit - the world's trinitarian faith lived in practice the mystery is guarded against prying scrutiny - hymn, not analysis, is the fitting register the world's Roman-side church stood inside the Nicene communion (Jacob at the council) This world's voice affirms Jesus as God through Aphrahat's chain of scriptural titles and Ephrem's Only-Begotten, names Father, Son, and Spirit over baptism, and guards the mystery through praise.
means: This world's voice affirms Jesus as God through Aphrahat's chain of scriptural titles and Ephrem's Only-Begotten, names Father, Son, and Spirit over baptism, and guards the mystery through praise.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a claim that Aphrahat used Nicene essence-language or later creedal formulas; a claim that the confession stands free of its anti-Jewish disputational frame; a claim that the Persian-side church formally received Nicaea in this window

Record syr.dw.jesus [formation confidence: Widely Accepted, limited to the years 337 to 373]
(1) To us, Jesus is the Only-Begotten of God - the Ihidaya, our own name for him.
(2) He left his place with God and made his home in the Virgin, and by being born as we are born, he became the brother of many.
(3) In him the words of the prophets, kings, and priests came true.
(4) The Nativity hymns sing of the day that made them glad, because every old promise came true at Bethlehem.
(5) His death and rising are one rescue.
(6) He went down into Sheol, the world of the dead that swallows everyone alike, and came out into the Kingdom.
(7) His own resurrection is the promise that he will bring us out after him.
(8) And what it meant to those who followed him was healing: he is the Physician, and his coming is medicine for a wounded people.
(9) Jesus is the Only-Begotten (Ihidaya) of God, become genuinely human of the Virgin his birth fulfills the whole weave of prophecy - promise and fulfillment answering as type and truth his resurrection is a pledge: Sheol is emptied toward the Kingdom his work is a physician's: sin and death are wounds he comes to heal This world's voice confesses Jesus as the Only-Begotten who dwelt in the Virgin, fulfilled prophecy at Bethlehem, emptied Sheol by rising, and came as Physician to a wounded people.
means: This world's voice confesses Jesus as the Only-Begotten who dwelt in the Virgin, fulfilled prophecy at Bethlehem, emptied Sheol by rising, and came as Physician to a wounded people.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a Greek conciliar formula recast from a sung and symbolic confession; a claim that Aphrahat's parallel confession stands free of its anti-Jewish disputational frame
