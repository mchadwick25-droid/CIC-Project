Record syr.quote.warned-before-baptism [formation confidence: Documented, limited to the years 337 to 337]
quote from Aphrahat, Demonstration VII.20
(1) For this reason it is right for the preachers of the Church, who sound the trumpet, to warn everyone in God's covenant before baptism.
(2) They are also to warn those who choose for themselves virginity and holiness: the young men, the virgins, and those wishing to become holy.
(3) The preachers are to warn them and say this.
(4) If your heart is set on marriage, then marry before your baptism.
(5) Otherwise you may fall in the fight and be killed.
(6) And if you are afraid of this part of the fight, turn back now, so that you do not break your brothers' hearts along with your own.
(7) And if you love your possessions, turn back from the army too.
(8) Otherwise, when the battle goes against you, you may remember your possessions and turn back to them.
(9) For there is disgrace for the one who turns back from the fight.
means: Aphrahat, in Demonstration VII, has the church's preachers warn candidates before baptism to marry first if they wish to marry, and to turn back if afraid or attached to possessions.
MUST NOT ASSERT: a ruling for or against infant baptism, which the passage does not address; a description of how every baptism in this world was actually conducted; a modern born-again conversion experience

(1) We would recognize the words "born again" at once.

(12) Sheol gives up its captives, and the Kingdom receives the invited.
