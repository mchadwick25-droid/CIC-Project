Record syr.dw.god [formation confidence: Widely Accepted, limited to the years 200 to 373]
(1) We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person.
(2) And we believed this out loud, against neighbors who taught otherwise.
(3) Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus, not a lesser or harsher god.
(4) Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing, not from eternal elements that drifted into order.
(5) Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers.
(6) And with Bardaisan, notably, we agreed on one great point: the stars do not rule us; free will is God's gift and cannot be compelled.
(7) Among ourselves we argued about exactly these things: creation, freedom, the body's worth, the resurrection.
(8) The arguments happened in the same streets, often in the same verse forms, against teachers everyone knew by name.
(9) one God, maker of all things visible and invisible, both good and just creation from nothing - against eternal pre-existent elements free will is real: neither nature nor fate compels the soul the body is God's work and will rise - against every teaching that despised it This world's voice says one good and just God made all things from nothing, against Marcion, Bardaisan and Mani, while agreeing with Bardaisan that the stars do not compel freedom.
