Record syr.term.ihidaya [formation confidence: Widely Accepted, limited to the years 337 to 373]
(1) The Single One.
(2) One word names the vowed celibate, single-minded and undivided for God.
(3) It is also the Syriac title of Christ as the Only-Begotten.
(4) The two senses share one root, so the one who lives single shares a name with Christ.
(5) Aphrahat sets the two side by side.
(6) He writes that the Only Begotten, from the bosom of the Father, will make all the solitaries rejoice.
(7) The Single One: our word for the vowed celibate - and, at the same time, our title for Christ the Only-Begotten.
(8) ihidaya (pl.
(9) ihidaye) Ihidaya, the Single One, names both the vowed celibate and Christ as the Only-Begotten, two senses sharing the root yhd and attested together in Aphrahat and Ephrem.
