Record syr.term.raza-shrara [formation confidence: Widely Accepted, limited to the years 337 to 373]
(1) Symbol and truth, held as one pair.
(2) A raza is a thing in Scripture or in nature that shows a hidden truth, the shrara.
(3) The raza is bound to the truth it shows.
(4) It is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will.
(5) A raza is a symbol that is bound to the truth it shows.
(6) It is not a stand-in chosen at will.
(7) raza / shrara A raza is a symbol in Scripture or creation bound to the truth (shrara) it discloses, the pair at the center of Ephrem's method of reading.
