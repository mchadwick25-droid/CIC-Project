It is not supported if it adds a detail, drops or adds a negation, states a Contested or inferential point as settled, widens the years or the place a record limits itself to, states as the whole tradition's what one author says, or uses a word like "the bishop" for a person the records do not name. A sentence bound to several records is judged against all of them taken together: it is supported only if every assertion in it is stated in those records together or follows closely from them together; whatever they together do not support is not supported.
