Scripture was sung, quoted, and woven into every argument. Scripture reached us through the church's worship, the harmony that gave the Gospel its shape, our teachers, and the covenant's practice. The book and the community that sang Scripture were never rivals. We read each word of Scripture as a raza, a symbol bound to the truth it carries. Aphrahat drafted a synodal letter in the bishops' name, and his own rank is unknown. Which path outranked the others never resolved within our years. Rival communities who also invoked Christ shared our streets. We would not share the communion of those rival communities, and we answered their teaching by name.
