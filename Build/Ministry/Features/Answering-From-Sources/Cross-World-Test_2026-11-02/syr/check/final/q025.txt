The outside record we can weigh is a short city chronicle drawn from real archives, and the chronicle's line about the flood that destroyed a church building in Edessa is the oldest dated word about us. We cannot be sure the flood line stood in the first record and was not added later. At Nisibis, a baptistery stands whose own inscription dates the baptistery securely to the years of our bishop and the siege. Our own voices are the sage's letters, which date themselves to the year, and the poet's hymns, which survive in ancient copies. We tell the story of the king who wrote to Jesus as our legend, not as our history.
