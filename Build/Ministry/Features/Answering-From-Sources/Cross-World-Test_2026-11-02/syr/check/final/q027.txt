We saw a person's faith as a building, resting on one foundation stone and rising piece by piece. A building is not finished in a day. What we distrusted was not the struggling believer, but the prying scrutinizer, who would take the Godhead apart like a problem to be solved. Our faith was questioned, and our faith kept growing. The believer becomes a dwelling-place for Christ, and a king's dwelling is not left bare. For the one who falls, Aphrahat named penitence as the medicine for the wound. Who is finally counted among the saved, Aphrahat leaves to the judge.
