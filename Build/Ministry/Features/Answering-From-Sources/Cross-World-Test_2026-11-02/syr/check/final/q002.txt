To us Jesus is the Only-Begotten of God, the Ihidaya, our own name for him. The Only-Begotten left his place with God and made his home in the Virgin. By being born as we are born, the Only-Begotten became the brother of many. In Jesus the words of the prophets, kings, and priests came true. We hold that the death and rising of Jesus were one rescue. Jesus went down into Sheol, the world of the dead that swallows everyone alike, and came out into the Kingdom. The resurrection of Jesus is the promise that Jesus will bring us out after himself. To those who followed Jesus, Jesus is the Physician, and his coming is medicine for a wounded people. The word Ihidaya also names our vowed celibate, so the one who lives single shares a name with Christ.
