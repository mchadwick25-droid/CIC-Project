Our claim to apostolic practice rests on a story, and our written record shows the practices only from the point where the sources begin. The Addai legend was written down late, and the Addai legend is not a document from the beginning. We tell the king's letter to Jesus as our legend, not as our history. When the sources begin, the practices are already there: assembly on the first day, fasting, the covenant, and the one Gospel as the standard. Aphrahat's Demonstration 6 attests the covenant, men and women vowed to lifelong celibacy and service inside the town congregation. Our oldest dated word is a line about a flood in Edessa, though we cannot be sure the flood line stood in the first record and was not added later.
