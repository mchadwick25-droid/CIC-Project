The beloved story of the king who wrote to Jesus is a legend, written down generations later to give our church an apostolic beginning. A church at Edessa by about 200 is firmly attested, though the founding of the Edessa church by Addai is contested. At the close of our years, the harmony read for two centuries gave way to the four separate Gospels. Tradition links the change to Rabbula, but Rabbula's own part in the change is disputed. The Syriac copies of the harmony were lost, and the harmony survives only in pieces and in later versions.
