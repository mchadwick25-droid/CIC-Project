We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person. Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus, not a lesser or harsher god. Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing. Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers. With Bardaisan we agreed that the stars do not rule us, and that free will is God's gift and cannot be compelled. God was with the persecuted, as he was with his Son. Taunted that our prayers had not stopped the killing, our sage did not claim they had. God was with the persecuted, and promised the persecuted resurrection. The persecuted, our sage meant, were not abandoned.
