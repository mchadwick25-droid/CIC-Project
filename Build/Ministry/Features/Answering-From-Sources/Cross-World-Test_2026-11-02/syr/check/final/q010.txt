We read Scripture and the world through the raza, a symbol that shows a hidden truth we call the shrara. The raza is bound to the truth it shows. The raza is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will. The pair of raza and shrara stands at the center of Ephrem's method of reading. Our practices were already in place when our sources begin. We kept assembly on the first day and fasting, and the one Gospel was already the standard. We lived our inheritance as apostolic. Our written record shows our inheritance only from the point where the sources begin.
