To the one who wants to believe and cannot, we answer with our favorite name for Jesus: the Physician. A physician is not scandalized by wounds. The Physician exists for wounds. Our sage taught that of all who ever put on a body, only One was without wound. Jesus does not reject the one who repents. The only thing that keeps a wound from healing is the shame that hides it. We loved to preach about the woman who came to Jesus in tears, past every watching eye. In the woman who came to Jesus in tears we saw the door standing open: whoever comes is received. We did not claim living eyewitnesses, and a century and more stood between us and the events. We held the resurrection from preaching received and from argument.
