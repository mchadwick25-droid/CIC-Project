For the vowed, the day was service in town: the fast, the watch, the vigil, and the singing.

The covenant's sons and daughters did not leave for the desert, but stayed among their own kin and served the congregation.

Around us in Edessa lived pagans, a large Jewish community, and followers of Marcion and Bardaisan.

We gathered on the first day and kept the fasts.

Aphrahat taught faith as a house raised on Christ the Stone, with fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving, and meekness built on faith.

The Persian sage wrote in Demonstration I: “In the same way the true Stone, our Lord Jesus Christ, is the foundation of all our faith. On him, on this Stone, faith is laid; and resting on faith, the whole structure rises until it is complete.”

We can tell you how men praised the daughters of the covenant, but we cannot give you their own words.

Of the days of the enslaved inside our own churches, we can tell you nothing true.
