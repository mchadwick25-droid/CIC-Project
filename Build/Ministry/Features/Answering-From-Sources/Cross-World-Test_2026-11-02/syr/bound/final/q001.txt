Our word Ihidaya, the Single One, names both the vowed celibate and Christ the Only-Begotten. So the one who lives single shares a name with Christ. The covenant of vowed men and women stood at our heart early, and its first datable witness is Demonstration 6, from 337. No source says how much earlier the covenant began.

At Edessa the modern city sits on the ancient one, and the church the flood destroyed lives only in a chronicler's line. Our hymns were copied by later hands into books that still exist. Ephrem sang in his first Nativity hymn: “This is the day that made the Prophets, the Kings, and the Priests glad. On this day their words came true - all of them. For on this day the Virgin gave birth to Immanuel in Bethlehem.”
