We answered need with service, as the covenant's sons and daughters stayed in town and served the congregation.

The covenant's work was the fast, the watch, the vigil, and the singing.

We spoke of sin as a wounding, a sickness in the human race, and we preached that our Lord took sin and nailed it to his cross.

The Persian sage wrote in the covenant exhortation of Demonstration VI: “Let us visit our Lord in the persons of the sick.”
