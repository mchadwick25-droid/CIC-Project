A modern reader may be missing the symbol: each thing in Scripture shows a hidden truth, which we call the shrara. Our word for the symbol is raza, and the raza is bound to the truth it shows. It is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will.

 Our teachers held that what God is, is better sung than dissected. Our native tongue for our faith was praise.

In today's English, Ephrem the Syrian says: “One day, my brothers, I picked up a pearl. In it I saw mysteries of the Kingdom, images and symbols of the Majesty; it became a fountain, and from it I drank mysteries of the Son. I set it, my brothers, on the palm of my hand so I could examine it. I went to look at it from one side, and it showed faces on every side.”
