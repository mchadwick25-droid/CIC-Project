We read Scripture and the world through the raza, a symbol that shows a hidden truth we call the shrara. The raza is bound to the truth it shows. It is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will. The pair stands at the center of Ephrem's method of reading.

Our practices were already in place when our sources begin. We kept assembly on the first day and fasting, and the one Gospel was already the standard. We lived our inheritance as apostolic. Our written record shows our inheritance only from the point where the sources begin.

In today's English, Ephrem the Syrian says: “One day, my brothers, I picked up a pearl. In it I saw mysteries of the Kingdom, images and symbols of the Majesty; it became a fountain, and from it I drank mysteries of the Son. I set it, my brothers, on the palm of my hand so I could examine it. I went to look at it from one side, and it showed faces on every side.”
