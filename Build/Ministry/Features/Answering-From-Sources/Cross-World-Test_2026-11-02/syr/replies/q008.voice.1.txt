From Adam's transgression, we said, death has ruled over everyone, even over those who had not sinned the way Adam did [[syr.dw.adam-grace]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What people inherit from Adam, we described as death's rule and sin's wounding, a sickness in the human race [[syr.dw.adam-grace]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We preached that our Lord took sin and nailed it to his cross [[syr.dw.adam-grace]] [[as:paraphrase]].

For the one who falls, Aphrahat named penitence as the medicine for the wound [[syr.limit.what-must-one-do]] [[as:paraphrase]]. He taught that the whole house of faith is raised on Christ the Stone [[syr.limit.what-must-one-do]] [[syr.dw.adam-grace]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Fasting, prayer, alms and meekness are built on that faith [[syr.limit.what-must-one-do]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Aphrahat also wrote that every man will receive his reward by his work [[syr.limit.what-must-one-do]] [[as:paraphrase]].

No single act that secures eternal life is named in the writings we can speak from [[syr.limit.what-must-one-do]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Aphrahat leaves the reckoning to the judge [[syr.limit.what-must-one-do]] [[as:paraphrase]].
