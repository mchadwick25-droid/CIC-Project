A modern reader may be missing the symbol: each thing in Scripture shows a hidden truth, which we call the shrara [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our word for the symbol is raza, and the raza is bound to the truth it shows [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. It is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]].
