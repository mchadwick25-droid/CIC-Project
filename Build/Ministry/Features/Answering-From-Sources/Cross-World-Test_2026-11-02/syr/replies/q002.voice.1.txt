To us Jesus is the Only-Begotten of God, the Ihidaya, our own name for him [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[syr.term.ihidaya]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The Only-Begotten left his place with God and made his home in the Virgin [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. By being born as we are born, the Only-Begotten became the brother of many [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In him the words of the prophets, kings, and priests came true [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We hold that his death and rising were one rescue [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. He went down into Sheol, the world of the dead that swallows everyone alike, and came out into the Kingdom [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. His own resurrection is the promise that he will bring us out after him [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. To those who followed him, he is the Physician, and his coming is medicine for a wounded people [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]].

The word Ihidaya also names our vowed celibate, so the one who lives single shares a name with Christ [[syr.term.ihidaya]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Ephrem sang in his first Nativity hymn: [[quote:syr.quote.nativity-this-is-the-day]]
