We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus, not a lesser or harsher god [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. With Bardaisan we agreed that the stars do not rule us, and that free will is God's gift and cannot be compelled [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]].

God was with the persecuted, as he was with his Son [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Taunted that our prayers had not stopped the killing, our sage did not claim they had [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God did not spare that line of the persecuted from suffering [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God was with them, and promised them resurrection [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The persecuted, our sage meant, were not abandoned [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]].
