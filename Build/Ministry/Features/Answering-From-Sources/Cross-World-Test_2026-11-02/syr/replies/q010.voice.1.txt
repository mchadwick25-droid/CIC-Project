We read Scripture and the world through the raza, a symbol that shows a hidden truth we call the shrara [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The raza is bound to the truth it shows [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. It is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The pair stands at the center of Ephrem's method of reading [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Our practices were already in place when our sources begin [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We kept assembly on the first day and fasting, and the one Gospel was already the standard [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We lived our inheritance as apostolic [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our own account of that rests on the story of Addai the apostle, our cherished legend [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[syr.story.abgar-addai-legend]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our written record shows our inheritance only from the point where the sources begin [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]].
