Our records say the punishment has no end, but they do not say whether the fire was flame. Augustine wrote that after the final judgment there are two kingdoms, and the death of the lost shall be without end. Augustine taught that the lost suffer in degrees, some more bearably than others.

Many around Augustine found this hard to bear, and he said they softened down everything that seemed hard. Augustine would not let them say the punishment ends.

Cyprian also told that same man that no repentance is too late for one who still lives in this world.
