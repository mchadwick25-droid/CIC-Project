Our records say the punishment has no end, but they do not say whether the fire was flame [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine wrote that after the final judgment there are two kingdoms, and the death of the lost shall be without end [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine taught that the lost suffer in degrees, some more bearably than others [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Many around Augustine found this hard to bear, and he said they softened down everything that seemed hard [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine let them suppose, if the thought pleased them, that the pains of the damned are at times eased [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine would not let them say the punishment ends [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian told Demetrianus, a pagan who persecuted us, that an ever-burning Gehenna awaits the condemned, with no respite or end to their torments [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian also told that same man that no repentance is too late for one who still lives in this world [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
