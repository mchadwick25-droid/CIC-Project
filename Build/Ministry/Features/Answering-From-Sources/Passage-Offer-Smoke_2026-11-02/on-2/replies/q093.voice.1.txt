Yes, communion of the Lord's body was part of our life, and Augustine would not have the dying cheated of it [[lpc.witness.the-dying-and-the-pastors-post]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine wrote to Honoratus, a bishop in Mauritania, in 428 or 429, that when ministers stay at their posts in danger, none are defrauded of that communion [[lpc.witness.the-dying-and-the-pastors-post]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A writing we hold writes that some dare not take the sacrament every day, and others dare not omit it any day [[lpc.passage.d8219731a60f]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A writing we hold writes that both kinds are moved by a wish to honour it [[lpc.passage.d8219731a60f]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A writing we hold writes that the Christians of Carthage call baptism salvation, and the sacrament of the body of Christ life [[lpc.passage.a4ee6140ff47]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That writing supposes this comes from an old apostolic tradition, that without baptism and the Lord's supper no one reaches the kingdom of God [[lpc.passage.a4ee6140ff47]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A writing we hold writes that the apostle faults those who ate unworthily, by not telling it from other food or giving it the special honour due [[lpc.passage.d8219731a60f]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A writing we hold writes of the sacrament as the body and blood of the Lord, and goes no further than those names [[lpc.passage.d8219731a60f]] [[lpc.passage.a4ee6140ff47]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Among us the altar was also where we remembered our dead, and Augustine's mother, Monica, asked her sons to remember her there [[lpc.witness.remembered-at-the-altar]] [[as:paraphrase]].
