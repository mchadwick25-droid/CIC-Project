As we understand ourselves, a particular people is placed in a particular man's care, and he will be asked what became of them. The flock is the particular people one bishop is given in one place, and the flock can press him face to face.

When a member had failed, we wanted the name set down, then examined before the whole congregation and weighed with its judgment. Cyprian wanted each certificate of peace to name the person. Some certificates named one person and then asked peace for his friends too, without naming them.
