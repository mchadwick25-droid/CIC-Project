On hard sayings, what we hold is Augustine's answer to a Manichaean about the wars of Moses, carried out on God's own command. Augustine wrote of those wars without flinching from that command. We held that the earthly wars of the old order were also a kind of picture, for a time. Even so, Augustine's answer does not claim that God never rightly commanded the sword at all. Is it the fire of the last judgment you want us to speak to, or how we read hard words in general?
