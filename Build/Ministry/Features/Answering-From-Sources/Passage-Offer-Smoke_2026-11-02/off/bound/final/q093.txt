Among us, communion is a formal standing within the one body, which a person can lose and be given back. At the council of 256, Cyprian said no one would be put out of communion for thinking differently. A century and a half later, Augustine argued at book length that the ruling of that council was wrong. Augustine never put Cyprian outside our communion. Augustine wrote: “The Winnower will come and divide the bad from the good. Then there will be a bodily separation too. A separation in spirit comes before it now. In heart, always keep apart from the bad. In body, stay joined with them for a time, but with caution. Yet do not neglect to correct your own people, those who in any way are in your care. Do it by warning, by teaching, by urging, or by threats.”

Is it the rite of the table you are asking about, or the standing we call communion?
