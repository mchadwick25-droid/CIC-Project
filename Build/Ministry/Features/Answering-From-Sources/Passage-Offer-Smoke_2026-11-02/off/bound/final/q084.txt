Yes, we can read together, as among us a bishop preached to the people given to him. The charge ran both ways, and the bishop's flock could stand before the bishop's seat and refuse to leave until the bishop heard them. Augustine wrote: “‘Jesus answered and said to them, This is the work of God, that you believe in Him whom He has sent.’ This, then, is to eat the food: not the food that perishes, but the food that lasts to eternal life. Why do you get your teeth and stomach ready? Believe, and you have eaten already.”
