# Stage 3 change order amendment: the quote card's content may change, its function stays

Named change order. It narrows Settled register row 11 and Decision-Log entry 16, and answers the open question held in `Stage3_Change_Order.md` ("whether the quote card may carry the edition words").

## Mark's words

Mark, 2026-11-01, relayed by the coordination thread: "the function of the card stays, the content can be updated into a strong template."

## What changes

- Entry 16 and row 11 said the quote card stays as it is today, with no edition or original wording. They now say: the card keeps its function, which is the spoken modern rendering and the source reference with the three-level transparency (row 12), and its content may be upgraded into a template Mark chooses.
- The rule that a card carries no edition or original wording is no longer a ruling. It is today's content. It changes only when Mark picks a template that says otherwise.

## What does not change

- Three-level transparency stays everywhere (row 12): marked text, hover for brief context, click for the full page with links to the source references and documents.
- Today's card, and `test_a_quote_card_carries_the_modern_rendering_and_source_reference_and_no_original_wording`, stand until a template is chosen. When one is, the test is rewritten to the chosen template in the same pull request that changes the card, not before.
- No layout or styling work starts until the system works (row 3). The options are shown to Mark as an Artifact; no code.
- No gate is retired and no other world is touched.

## Done when

Mark has chosen a template from the options put to him, entry 50 of the Decision-Log records the choice, and the card and its test are changed together under that choice.

## Outcome

Mark chose template B on 2026-11-01 (Decision-Log 52): hover with speaker, work, locus, confidence phrase and the record's one-line meaning; click page with three labelled layers (today's English, the edition, the original). The quote card's test is rewritten to template B in the pull request that builds it.
