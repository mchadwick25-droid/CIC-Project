## Recurring patterns

- A reason that passes the checker can still state something false. `leaf_missing` verifies only the neighbouring lines, not that any leaf is missing. The `allow_oversize` scan does not see bare margin section numbers, because they do not look like headings. In both cases the reviewer has to read the page.
- Damaged printed numerals read through a `fix` with a quote are not sequence-only readings when the glyphs back them. Raulx 12790 is the weakest case. It still passes, because the numeral is printed and only its middle glyph is damaged.

## Tooling observations

- The reason forms have no honest way to record a head that is not printed while its text is present inside the division before (Optatus Book II XXV). A fifth form needs a change order. It belongs to the Library thread or to Mark, not to a drafter.
- The `allow_oversize` sub-heading scan misses margin section numbers that sit at the start of a body line (Knoll caput25).
