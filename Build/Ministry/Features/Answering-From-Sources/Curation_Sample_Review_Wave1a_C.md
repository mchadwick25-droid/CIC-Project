## Recurring patterns

- Printed headings the pattern misses because of OCR damage, so the next division swallows them. The checker reports these as numeral gaps, and the drafter left the gaps unexplained: Raulx (4 headings), Harnack (testimonies 27 and 28). In Monceaux tome 2 a whole printed section with a lost numeral is missing, although the drafter added the same kind of division elsewhere in that file.
- An `allow_oversize` sentence that says "no numbered heading inside" when the body does print numbered parts: Benson ch. X (both sections), and Monceaux tome 2 l3/c6/s4. Before accepting such a claim, check it against the Contents and the body.
- Gazetteer files: running heads, map references and OCR debris from a bibliography table taken as entries (Mesnage, both scans).
- Appendices that are the author's own essays, declared `kind: notes` apparatus (Benson A-L, Dales I-V), while Monceaux's appendices are body divisions. The format offers only front, index and notes. Mark or the curation thread should decide whether an author's appendix counts as body. Until then I have recorded this as a note, not a defect.

## Tooling observations

- All 25 curations in the group, and all 13 wave-1b curations, pass `check` (exit 0) against main at the time of review. Every defect above passes the checker. Gaps are reported only as `info`, and allow_oversize sentences are taken on trust.
- An `ocr_map` entry that maps one glyph to several numerals (Duchesne tome 3: 'm': 'III', 'n': 'II') applies to every numeral the rule reads. In this file each reading is confirmed by the TOC, but the map is broader than one fix would be.
