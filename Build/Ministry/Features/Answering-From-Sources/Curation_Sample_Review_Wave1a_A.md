## Recurring patterns

- The `check` result hides gaps in the numbering. Both sampled files pass every threshold. The Bruder file's `info` line nonetheless shows 36 gaps across its five rules, and none of them is explained. A gap in an `info` line is a missed heading until shown otherwise.
- `allow_oversize` text should be checked against the page. In PL38 and PL39 the same justification text was pasted in, and the printed `CAPUT` heads disprove it.
- A numeral fixed from its place in the sequence, not from its glyphs (PL39 66478).
- Sermons inside a series section that are not divided at their own printed heads (Morin, the Casinenses).

## Tooling observations

- `check` prints only the first 8 gaps per rule. Gaps should be failures, or listed in full, unless the yaml explains them.
