We said that from Adam's transgression, death has ruled over everyone, even over those who had not sinned as Adam did. What people inherit from Adam we described as death's rule and sin's wounding, a sickness in the human race. We preached that our Lord took sin and nailed it to his cross. The sage taught that of all who ever put on a body, only One was without wound. For the one who falls, he named penitence as the medicine for the wound. The Persian sage wrote in Demonstration I: “In the same way the true Stone, our Lord Jesus Christ, is the foundation of all our faith. On him, on this Stone, faith is laid; and resting on faith, the whole structure rises until it is complete.”

On faith the whole house was raised: fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving, and meekness. Aphrahat leaves the final reckoning to the judge.
