What we can show is practice older than proof. When our sources begin, the practices are already there: assembly on the first day, fasting, the covenant, and the one Gospel as the standard. Our earliest hard trace is a line in a city chronicle, which The Chronicle of Edessa gives as: “And they destroyed the temple of the church of the Christians.”

We cannot be sure that line stood in the first record and was not added later. We claimed no living eyewitnesses, and the Gospel our churches read in worship was the one woven story. No order of worship survives from us, so we cannot walk you through a service step by step. What we kept are the pieces: the font sung as a womb, the sealing, and the covenant vow taken before the water.
