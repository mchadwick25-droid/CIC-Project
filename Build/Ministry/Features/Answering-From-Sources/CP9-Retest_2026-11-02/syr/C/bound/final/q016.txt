Among us, we knew ourselves as one people across two empires and beyond, called after the one name of Christ. Bardaisan spoke of Christians in Philip's record, the Book of the Laws of Countries: “And what shall we say of the new race of us Christians, whom Christ at his coming planted in every country and in every region? For see - wherever we are, we are all called by the one name of Christ: Christians. On one day, the first of the week, we gather together.”. Our streets held rival communities who all invoked Christ, and we would not share their communion. What we can show you of our own practice is older than any proof we have: assembly on the first day, fasting, the covenant, and the one Gospel as the standard. Our written record shows these only from the point where our sources begin. Which church of your own day do you have in mind?
