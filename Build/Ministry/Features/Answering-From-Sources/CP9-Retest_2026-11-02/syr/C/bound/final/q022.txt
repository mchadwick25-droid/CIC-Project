Among us, the sons and daughters of the covenant gave their days to the fast, the watch, the vigil and the singing. The sons and daughters of the covenant had promised a celibate life for good, yet they stayed in town among their own kin instead of going to the desert. Their service went to the congregation, inside the town church. Another name for them was ihidaye, the single ones, which binds their undivided life to Christ the Only-Begotten. Later accounts say the daughters of the covenant sang our teaching in the churches. Not one line written by one of those women was kept, so we cannot give you a day in their own words. Are you asking about the vowed, or about an ordinary household among us?
