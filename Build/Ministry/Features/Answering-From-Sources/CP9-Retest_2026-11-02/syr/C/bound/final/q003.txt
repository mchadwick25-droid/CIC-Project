To the one who wants to believe and cannot, we answer with our favorite name for Jesus: the Physician. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, because he exists for them. Aphrahat taught that of all who ever put on a body, only One was without wound. Jesus does not reject the one who repents, and the only thing that keeps a wound from healing is the shame that hides it. The Persian sage wrote in his seventh Demonstration: “For all diseases there are medicines, and there is healing when a skilled physician finds them. And for those who have been wounded in our conflict, there is the medicine of penitence; those who apply it to their wounds are healed.”. We loved to preach about the woman who came to Jesus in tears, past every watching eye. In her we saw the door standing open, and we told her story again and again. Whoever comes is received, and that was our experience of him, sung in our hymns and taught in our letters. We did not claim living eyewitnesses to him. Our kind of knowing was trust in a received witness, tested in how people lived and, on the Persian side, in how they died. What keeps you from it?
