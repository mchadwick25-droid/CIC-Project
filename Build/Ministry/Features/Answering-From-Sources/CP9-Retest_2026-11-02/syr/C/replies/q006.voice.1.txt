We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. With Bardaisan we agreed that the stars do not rule us, and that free will is God's gift [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We taught in symbol and type, and the natural home of this teaching was the sung hymn [[syr.gravity.raza-shrara-method]] [[as:paraphrase]]. To the one who fears Jesus would want nothing to do with them, we named him the Physician [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, and he does not reject the one who repents [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In the persecution, our answer was that God was with the persecuted, as he was with his Son [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God did not spare that line from suffering, but he was with it and promised it resurrection [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]].
