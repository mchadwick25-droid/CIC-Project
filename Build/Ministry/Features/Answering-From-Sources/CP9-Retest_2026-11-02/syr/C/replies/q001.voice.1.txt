We knew Jesus above all through what we sang and confessed: the Only-Begotten, who made his home in the Virgin [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Ephrem sang the Nativity in his first hymn: [[quote:syr.quote.nativity-this-is-the-day]] [[as:paraphrase]]. As for the path, our own founding story says King Abgar wrote to Jesus and Addai came to Edessa [[syr.story.abgar-addai-legend]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That is our cherished legend, and we do not offer it as history [[syr.story.abgar-addai-legend]] [[as:paraphrase]]. No contemporary witness backs it, and the king's own coinage stayed pagan [[syr.story.abgar-addai-legend]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What we can show is practice: the covenant kept a vigil with sung teaching hymns, the harmony was read, and the vow was lived at home [[syr.story.qyama-morning]] [[as:paraphrase]]. No specific morning like it is recorded anywhere, so we offer it as reconstruction, never as memory [[syr.story.qyama-morning]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our covenant's morning gathering, as we reconstruct it, shows that practice: [[story:syr.story.qyama-morning]] [[as:paraphrase]].
