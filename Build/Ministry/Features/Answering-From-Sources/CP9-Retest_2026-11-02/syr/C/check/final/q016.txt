Among us, we knew ourselves as one people across two empires and beyond, called after the one name of Christ. Our streets held rival communities who all invoked Christ, and we would not share their communion. What we can show you of our own practice is older than any proof we have: assembly on the first day, fasting, the covenant, and the one Gospel as the standard. Our written record shows these practices only from the point where our sources begin. Which church of your own day do you have in mind?
