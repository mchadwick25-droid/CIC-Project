We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person. Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus. Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing. Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers. With Bardaisan we agreed that the stars do not rule us, and that free will is God's gift. We taught in symbol and type, and the natural home of our teaching was the sung hymn. To the one who fears Jesus would want nothing to do with them, we named Jesus the Physician. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, and the physician does not reject the one who repents. In the persecution, our answer was that God was with the persecuted, as God was with his Son.
