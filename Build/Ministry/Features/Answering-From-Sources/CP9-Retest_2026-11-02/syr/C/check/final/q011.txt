What the modern reader misses is the hidden truth that each word of Scripture carries. We read each word as a symbol, a raza, bound to the truth it shows, the shrara. The symbol is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will. So Eden was read toward the cross, Adam toward Christ, and the ark toward the church. A whole chapter's worth of meaning was found in a single pearl. We did not read Genesis as science, and that was because we read for something else. Our teaching also came in song, because doctrine was taught by singing it.
