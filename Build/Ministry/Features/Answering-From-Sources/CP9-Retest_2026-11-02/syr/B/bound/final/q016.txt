We knew ourselves as one people across two empires, wherever Christians lived, called after the one name of Christ. We did not have denominations, not as the word is now meant. Our streets held rival communities who also invoked Christ: Marcion's people, Bardaisan's circle, and later Mani's missionaries. We would not share their communion, and we answered their teaching by name, in prose and in song. We held to one God, the whole of Scripture, and the body's resurrection. Bardaisan, as his disciple Philip records him, spoke of that one name: “And what shall we say of the new race of us Christians, whom Christ at his coming planted in every country and in every region? For see - wherever we are, we are all called by the one name of Christ: Christians. On one day, the first of the week, we gather together.”. Our Persian bishops were many and contested, and no one used the title Catholicos in our years. Which church do you have in mind?
