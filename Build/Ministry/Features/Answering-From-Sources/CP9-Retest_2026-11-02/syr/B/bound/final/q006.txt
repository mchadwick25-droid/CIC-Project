We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person. We believed this out loud, against neighbors who taught otherwise. Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus, not a lesser or harsher god. Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing. Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers. With Bardaisan we agreed that the stars do not rule us, and that free will is God's gift and cannot be compelled. God was also with the persecuted, as he was with his Son. His answer was never exemption, but presence and the promise of resurrection. The Persian sage wrote in Demonstration XXI: “Hear, my beloved, these names of martyrs, confessors, and the persecuted. Abel was murdered, and his blood cried out from the earth. Jacob was persecuted, and he fled and became an exile. Joseph was persecuted, and he was sold and thrown into the pit. Moses was persecuted, and he fled to Midian.”

To the one who wants to believe and cannot, we answered with our favorite name for Jesus, the Physician. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, because he exists for them.
