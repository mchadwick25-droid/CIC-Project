We would have met you as a believer still under construction, because faith, our sage taught, is a building that goes up piece by piece. A building is not finished in a day. What we distrusted was not the struggling believer but the prying scrutinizer, who would take the Godhead apart like a problem.. On faith the whole house was raised, with fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving, and meekness. For the one who falls, Aphrahat named penitence as the medicine for the wound. Whether you would be rewarded, he left to the judge. Ordinary believers appear in our writings only as those addressed, so how any one town would have received you is not in our record.
