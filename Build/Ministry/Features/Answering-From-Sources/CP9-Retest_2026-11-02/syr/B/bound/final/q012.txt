Scripture was supreme among us, but it always reached us through others. It came through the church's worship, the harmony that gave the Gospel its shape, our teachers, and the covenant's practice. The book and the community that sang it were never rivals. We read each word of Scripture as a symbol bound to the truth it carries, which we called a raza and its shrara. So Eden was read toward the cross, Adam toward Christ, and the ark toward the church. Authority among us ran along two footings at once, vowed standing and episcopal office, and we never settled which held. Aphrahat drafted a synodal letter in the bishops' name, and whether he was a bishop himself is unknown. We hold that Christians everywhere were called after the one name of Christ.
