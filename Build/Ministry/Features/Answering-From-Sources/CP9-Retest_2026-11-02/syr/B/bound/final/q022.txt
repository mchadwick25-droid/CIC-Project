For the sons and daughters of the covenant, a day was lived in town among their own kin, serving the congregation by the fast, the watch, the vigil and the singing. They promised a celibate life for good, yet they did not leave for the desert. They were men and women vowed to lifelong celibacy and service inside the town congregation. Aphrahat's Demonstration 6, from 337, treats the order as already set up, needing correction more than founding. Our sage taught that fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving and meekness were raised on faith, like a house on a foundation stone. The Persian sage wrote in Demonstration I: “In the same way the true Stone, our Lord Jesus Christ, is the foundation of all our faith. On him, on this Stone, faith is laid; and resting on faith, the whole structure rises until it is complete.”

Not one line written by a woman of the covenant was kept, and we can tell you only how men praised them. Of the days of enslaved people inside our churches, we can tell you nothing true.
