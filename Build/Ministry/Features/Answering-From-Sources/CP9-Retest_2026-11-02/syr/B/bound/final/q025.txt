Among the sources we can check, none is a record taken under torture: they are our sage's letters, our poet's hymns and a short city chronicle. Our sage's letters date themselves to the year, and our poet's hymns survive in ancient copies. The chronicle was drawn from real archives and records that a flood destroyed a church building in Edessa. The chronicle was compiled later, so it is no eyewitness account, and we cannot be sure the church line stood in the first record. Theodoret, an outside historian, tells of Jacob's prayer that brought gnats on the siege of Nisibis, and we hold that as hagiographic tradition, not attested event. Which record do you have in mind?
