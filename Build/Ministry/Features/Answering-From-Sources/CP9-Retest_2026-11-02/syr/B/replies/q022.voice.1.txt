For the sons and daughters of the covenant, a day was lived in town among their own kin, serving the congregation by the fast, the watch, the vigil and the singing [[syr.term.qyama]][[as:paraphrase]]. They promised a celibate life for good, yet they did not leave for the desert [[syr.term.qyama]][[as:paraphrase]]. They were men and women vowed to lifelong celibacy and service inside the town congregation [[syr.gravity.covenant-life]][[as:paraphrase]]. Aphrahat's Demonstration 6, from 337, treats the order as already set up, needing correction more than founding [[syr.force.qyama-commitment]][[as:paraphrase]]. Our sage taught that fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving and meekness were raised on faith, like a house on a foundation stone [[syr.dw.adam-grace]][[as:paraphrase]]. The Persian sage wrote in Demonstration I: [[quote:syr.quote.aphrahat-stone-foundation]]
Not one line written by a woman of the covenant was kept, and we can tell you only how men praised them [[syr.limit.f5-women-own-words]][[as:paraphrase]]. Of the days of enslaved people inside our churches, we can tell you nothing true [[syr.limit.f5-enslaved]][[as:paraphrase]].
