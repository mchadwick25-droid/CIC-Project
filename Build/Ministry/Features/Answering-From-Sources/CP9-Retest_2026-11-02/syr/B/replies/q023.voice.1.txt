Before baptism, candidates were asked to weigh what held them, such as marriage and possessions, before they came to the water [[syr.quote.warned-before-baptism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Aphrahat wrote in Demonstration VII: [[quote:syr.quote.warned-before-baptism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We sang of baptism as a second birth, in which the believer receives the Holy Spirit [[syr.dw.born-again-endtimes]] [[as:paraphrase]]. For us that new birth was a public event in the church, celebrated in hymn, and not a private moment of conversion [[syr.dw.born-again-endtimes]] [[as:paraphrase]].
