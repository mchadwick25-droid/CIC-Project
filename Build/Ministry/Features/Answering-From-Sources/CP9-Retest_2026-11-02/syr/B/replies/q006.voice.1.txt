We believed in one God, maker of all things, good and just in the same person [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We believed this out loud, against neighbors who taught otherwise [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Marcion we held that the Maker of this world is the Father of Jesus, not a lesser or harsher god [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Bardaisan we held that God made all things from nothing [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against Mani we refused a world split between two powers [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. With Bardaisan we agreed that the stars do not rule us, and that free will is God's gift and cannot be compelled [[syr.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God was also with the persecuted, as he was with his Son [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. His answer was never exemption, but presence and the promise of resurrection [[syr.dw.suffering]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The Persian sage wrote in Demonstration XXI: [[quote:syr.quote.aphrahat-persecuted-litany]]
To the one who wants to believe and cannot, we answered with our favorite name for Jesus, the Physician [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, because he exists for them [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]].
