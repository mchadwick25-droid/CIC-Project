We read a thing in Scripture or in nature as a sign that shows a hidden truth [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Such a sign we call a raza, and the truth it shows we call the shrara [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The raza is bound to its truth, and no reader may pick it at will as a stand-in [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The pair stands at the center of Ephrem's method of reading [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Ephrem the Syrian wrote of a pearl in his first Pearl hymn: [[quote:syr.quote.on-a-certain-day-a-pearl]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Beside this way of reading stood the one Gospel, which was already the standard when our sources begin [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]].
