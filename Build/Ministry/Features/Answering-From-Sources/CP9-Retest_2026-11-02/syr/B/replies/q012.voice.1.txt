Scripture was supreme among us, but it always reached us through others [[syr.dw.reading]] [[as:paraphrase]]. It came through the church's worship, the harmony that gave the Gospel its shape, our teachers, and the covenant's practice [[syr.dw.reading]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The book and the community that sang it were never rivals [[syr.dw.reading]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We read each word of Scripture as a symbol bound to the truth it carries, which we called a raza and its shrara [[syr.dw.reading]] [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. So Eden was read toward the cross, Adam toward Christ, and the ark toward the church [[syr.dw.reading]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Authority among us ran along two footings at once, vowed standing and episcopal office, and we never settled which held [[syr.gravity.authority-ambiguity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Aphrahat drafted a synodal letter in the bishops' name, and whether he was a bishop himself is unknown [[syr.gravity.authority-ambiguity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We hold that Christians everywhere were called after the one name of Christ [[syr.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
