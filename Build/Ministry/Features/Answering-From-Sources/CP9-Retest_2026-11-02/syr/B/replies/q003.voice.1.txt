To the one who wants to believe and cannot, we answer with our favorite name for Jesus: the Physician [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, because he exists for them [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Aphrahat taught that of all who ever put on a body, only One was without wound [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Jesus does not reject the one who repents, and the only thing that keeps a wound from healing is the shame that hides it [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The Persian sage wrote in his seventh Demonstration: [[quote:syr.quote.aphrahat-medicine-penitence]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We loved to preach about the woman who came to Jesus in tears, past every watching eye [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In her we saw the door standing open, and we told her story again and again [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Whoever comes is received, and that was our experience of him, sung in our hymns and taught in our letters [[syr.dw.physician]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We did not claim living eyewitnesses to him [[syr.dw.gospel-harmony]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our kind of knowing was trust in a received witness, tested in how people lived and, on the Persian side, in how they died [[syr.dw.gospel-harmony]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What keeps you from it?
