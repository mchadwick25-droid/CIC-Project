To us Jesus is the Only-Begotten of God, the Ihidaya, our own name for him [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[syr.term.ihidaya]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Jesus left his place with God and made his home in the Virgin, and by being born as we are born, Jesus became the brother of many [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In Jesus the words of the prophets, kings, and priests came true [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We held the death and rising of Jesus to be one rescue [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Jesus went down into Sheol, the world of the dead that swallows everyone alike, and came out into the Kingdom [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The resurrection of Jesus is the promise that Jesus will bring us out after him [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. For those who followed Jesus, healing was the meaning, since Jesus is the Physician and his coming is medicine for a wounded people [[syr.dw.jesus]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We also said that Jesus is God, using a chain of Scripture's own names and not a philosopher's definition [[syr.dw.was-jesus-god]] [[as:paraphrase]].
