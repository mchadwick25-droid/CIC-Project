We read a thing in Scripture or in nature as a sign that shows a hidden truth [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We call a sign of that kind a raza, and the truth shown by a raza we call the shrara [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The raza is bound to its truth, and no reader may pick a raza at will as a stand-in [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The pair of raza and shrara stands at the center of Ephrem's method of reading [[syr.term.raza-shrara]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Beside the reading of raza and shrara stood the one Gospel, which was already the standard when our sources begin [[syr.dw.apostolic]] [[as:paraphrase]].
