We knew ourselves as one people across two empires, wherever Christians lived, called after the one name of Christ [[syr.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We did not have denominations, not as the word is now meant [[syr.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our streets held rival communities who also invoked Christ: Marcion's people, Bardaisan's circle, and later Mani's missionaries [[syr.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We would not share the communion of those rival communities, and we answered their teaching by name, in prose and in song [[syr.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We held to one God, the whole of Scripture, and the body's resurrection [[syr.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
