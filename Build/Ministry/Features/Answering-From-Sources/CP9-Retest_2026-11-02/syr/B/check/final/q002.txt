To us Jesus is the Only-Begotten of God, the Ihidaya, our own name for him. Jesus left his place with God and made his home in the Virgin, and by being born as we are born, Jesus became the brother of many. In Jesus the words of the prophets, kings, and priests came true. We held the death and rising of Jesus to be one rescue. Jesus went down into Sheol, the world of the dead that swallows everyone alike, and came out into the Kingdom. The resurrection of Jesus is the promise that Jesus will bring us out after him. For those who followed Jesus, healing was the meaning, since Jesus is the Physician and his coming is medicine for a wounded people. We also said that Jesus is God, using a chain of Scripture's own names and not a philosopher's definition.
