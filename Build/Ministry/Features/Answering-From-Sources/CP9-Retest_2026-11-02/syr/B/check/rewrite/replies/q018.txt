We cannot give you the steps in order, because no order of worship survives from us [[syr.limit.ritual-sequence]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What we kept are the pieces: the font sung as a womb, the sealing set on each one, and the covenant vow taken before the water [[syr.limit.ritual-sequence]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Who stood where, and what was said first, our record never wrote down [[syr.limit.ritual-sequence]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our churches sang of baptism as a womb, and of the shining garments of the newly baptized [[syr.dw.born-again-endtimes]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our teachers called baptism a second birth, in which the believer receives the Holy Spirit [[syr.dw.born-again-endtimes]] [[as:paraphrase]]. For us, new birth was a public event in the church, celebrated in hymn, and not a private moment of conversion [[syr.dw.born-again-endtimes]] [[as:paraphrase]].
