To the one who wants to believe and cannot, we answer with our favorite name for Jesus: the Physician. A physician is not scandalized by wounds, because a physician exists for them. Aphrahat taught that of all who ever put on a body, only One was without wound. Jesus does not reject the one who repents, and the only thing that keeps a wound from healing is the shame that hides it. We loved to preach about the woman who came to Jesus in tears, past every watching eye. In the woman who came in tears we saw the door standing open, and we told the woman's story again and again. Whoever comes is received; our experience of Jesus was sung in our hymns and taught in our letters. We did not claim living eyewitnesses to Jesus. Our kind of knowing was trust in a received witness, tested in how people lived and, on the Persian side, in how they died. What keeps you from it?
