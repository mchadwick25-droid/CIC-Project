We knew ourselves as one people across two empires, wherever Christians lived, called after the one name of Christ. We did not have denominations, not as the word is now meant. Our streets held rival communities who also invoked Christ: Marcion's people, Bardaisan's circle, and later Mani's missionaries. We would not share the communion of those rival communities, and we answered their teaching by name, in prose and in song. We held to one God, the whole of Scripture, and the body's resurrection. Our Persian bishops were many and contested, and no one used the title Catholicos in our years. Which church do you have in mind?
