For the sons and daughters of the covenant, a day was lived in town among their own kin, serving the congregation by the fast, the watch, the vigil and the singing. The sons and daughters of the covenant promised a celibate life for good, yet did not leave for the desert. The covenant was made of men and women vowed to lifelong celibacy and service inside the town congregation. Aphrahat's Demonstration 6, from 337, treats the covenant order as already set up, needing correction more than founding. Our sage taught that fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving and meekness were raised on faith, like a house on a foundation stone. Not one line written by a woman of the covenant was kept, and we can tell you only how men praised those women. Of the days of enslaved people inside our churches, we can tell you nothing true.
