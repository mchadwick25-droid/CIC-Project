From Adam's transgression, death has ruled over everyone, even over those who had not sinned the way Adam did. We preached that our Lord took sin and nailed it to his cross. The sage taught faith with a picture: Christ was the foundation stone, and faith rested on him. On faith the whole house was raised, with fasting, prayer, love, almsgiving and meekness. For the one who falls, Aphrahat named penitence as the medicine for the wound. Aphrahat also wrote that every man will receive his reward by his work, and left the reckoning to the judge.
