At Nisibis, a baptistery still stands where we worshipped, and its own inscription dates it securely to the years of our bishop and the siege. It is the only building of ours from those years that can be dated securely. The church the flood destroyed lives only in a chronicler's line. The Chronicle of Edessa records, in its entry for the year 513 of the Greeks: “And they destroyed the temple of the church of the Christians.”. We cannot be sure that line stood in the first record and was not added later. What is known of us comes from letters that date themselves to the year, and from hymns copied by later hands into books that still exist.
