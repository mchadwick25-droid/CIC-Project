We read a thing in Scripture or in nature as a sign that shows a hidden truth. We call a sign of that kind a raza, and the truth shown by a raza we call the shrara. The raza is bound to its truth, and no reader may pick a raza at will as a stand-in. The pair of raza and shrara stands at the center of Ephrem's method of reading. Beside the reading of raza and shrara stood the one Gospel, which was already the standard when our sources begin.
