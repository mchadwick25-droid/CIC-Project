What we have of Christ is not a living chain, but the text [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]] [[as:paraphrase]]. No one among us met anyone who met him, and fifteen hundred years stand between his life and ours [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We hold that the text is enough on its own terms [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Where we have not understood the Scriptures rightly, we will be taught better, but only from the Scriptures themselves [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Scripture alone, not church tradition or a pope's decree, was the final authority for our doctrine and our church order [[rzg.term.sola-scriptura]] [[as:paraphrase]]. At Zurich that conviction was argued aloud before the council, until the council said what the text had yielded [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[as:paraphrase]]. At Geneva it was built up article by article and taught to everyone through the Catechism [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Zurich's reform opened on 1 January 1519, when Zwingli began to preach straight through the Gospel of Matthew [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]] [[as:paraphrase]].
