Our claims stand firmest where our record is richest, in confession and doctrine [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. We hold Calvin's Institutes and Catechism, Zwingli's founding statements, the Consensus Tigurinus and the Second Helvetic Confession directly, and they are verified [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. Much of our record rests on one author, and Calvin's writings run roughly four times Zwingli's [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. The core of election doctrine is attested in both cities, but its fully worked-out later form rests mostly on Calvin and Beza [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. We do not claim that both cities teach that later form equally [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. Whether the 1549 Supper formula is real synthesis or only careful accommodation, our record does not settle [[rzg.contested.sign-and-the-thing-signified]][[as:paraphrase]]. One reading holds that the 1549 statement deepens and completes Zwingli's 1523 remembrance doctrine rather than replacing it, and our record does not settle that either [[rzg.contested.zwinglis-remembrance-vs-negotiated-consensus]][[as:paraphrase]]. That the 1549 text softened Zwingli's language is a judgment of our own record, not a finding handed down by scholars [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. Of an ordinary Sunday service or an ordinary citizen's life, no surviving account has reached us [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. For the Bolsec dispute and the Perrin quarrels we have Calvin's letters, which give one side's view, and for Marburg we have no primary narrative [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]]. The 1541 Ordinances and Geneva's consistory registers are not in hand, so the Consistory's weekly discipline rests on thin evidence [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]][[as:paraphrase]].
