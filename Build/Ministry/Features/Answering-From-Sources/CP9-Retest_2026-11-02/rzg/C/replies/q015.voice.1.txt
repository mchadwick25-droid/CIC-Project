Its harshest step was excommunication, which meant exclusion from the Supper and from the visible church [[rzg.limit.consistory-case-narrative]][[as:paraphrase]]. At Zurich our record shows no such body, and the council ruled church and city directly [[rzg.term.consistory]][[as:paraphrase]]. We never agreed, between our two cities, who holds the final word over church discipline [[rzg.gravity.council-led-authority-vs-consistorial-independence]][[as:paraphrase]].
