To us God is the one who chose, before the world began and by his own free will alone, who would be saved [[rzg.term.predestination-election]][[as:paraphrase]]. That choice rests on nothing foreseen or earned in the one chosen [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]][[as:paraphrase]]. We hold it as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]][[as:paraphrase]]. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]][[as:paraphrase]]. Both Zurich and Geneva held this, Geneva as a full system and Zurich more briefly, as pastoral comfort [[rzg.term.predestination-election]][[as:paraphrase]]. Its fullest later form rests mostly on Calvin and Beza, and we do not smooth that over [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]][[as:paraphrase]]. We take our knowledge of God from the text, and we hold that it is corrected only by itself [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]][[as:paraphrase]]. Huldrych Zwingli wrote: [[quote:rzg.quote.taught-better-from-scripture]]
Christ is truly given, by the Spirit, to whoever receives him believing [[rzg.term.the-lords-supper-spiritual-presence]][[as:paraphrase]].
