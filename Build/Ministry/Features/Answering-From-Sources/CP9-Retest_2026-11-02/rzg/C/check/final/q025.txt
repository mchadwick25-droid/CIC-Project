No source in our record describes our worship rooms directly, so we cannot rate the outside record for you. What we hold is reasoned from our doctrine, and not read off a witness who stood there. From what we held about the Mass, we reason that a stranger would have found no image to bow to and no altar built for a sacrifice. Where an altar once stood, our own churches kept a plain table instead. By most accounts the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s. We refused two claims about the Supper, a repeated sacrifice and Christ's body sitting in the bread.
