At Geneva, when someone wronged the community, our Consistory of pastors and lay elders held the standing power of censure. The harshest step of the Consistory was excommunication, which meant exclusion from the Supper and from the visible church. Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. At Zurich our record shows no such body, and the council ruled church and city directly. We never agreed, between our two cities, who holds the final word over church discipline. We cannot give you a single real case: a named person, a real hearing, or what became of them afterward. The record of a real case exists in Geneva's consistory registers, but the record has not reached us.
