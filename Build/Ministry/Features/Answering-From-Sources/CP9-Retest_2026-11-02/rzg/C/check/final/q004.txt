To us Jesus is what the whole church says he is. Who Jesus is was never our own quarrel, not with Rome and not between our two cities. What is distinctly ours is what we were taught about who is chosen in Christ. God's own free choice, made before the world began, decides who is saved. Our confession called Christ the mirror in which we see whether we are chosen, and our confession rejected looking for that apart from Christ. What we have of Christ is the text, since no one among us met anyone who met Christ and fifteen hundred years stand between. We hold the text to be enough on its own terms.
