We held that God's own free choice, made before the world began, decides who is saved. Nothing foreseen or earned in the one chosen decides who is saved. We hold God's free choice as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given. The fullest, systematized form of election rests mostly on Calvin and Beza, and we do not smooth that divergence into both cities teaching election equally. We held God's care over everything he made to be continuous and active, not that of a distant first cause. We counted the positions we refused as wrong, not merely different: Rome's repeated sacrifice and its images, Wittenberg's body in the bread, and a church gathered only by a believer's own chosen profession. Each time we put one test to the reading, whether it holds against Scripture.
