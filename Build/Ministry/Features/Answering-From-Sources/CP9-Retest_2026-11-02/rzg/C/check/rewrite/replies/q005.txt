At Zurich, a claim was argued aloud until the council itself said what the text had yielded, and at Geneva doctrine was built up and taught to everyone through the Catechism [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In both cities we held that Scripture alone could bind a church [[rzg.term.sola-scriptura]] [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[as:paraphrase]]. [[story:rzg.story.first-zurich-disputation]] Zurich's council governed church and city together [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Geneva's Consistory fought for its independence from council control of the kind Zurich's council held, and only by 1555 substantially won it [[rzg.gravity.council-led-authority-vs-consistorial-independence]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We never agreed between our two cities who holds the final word over church discipline [[rzg.gravity.council-led-authority-vs-consistorial-independence]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Against the Anabaptists, our own council turned to civil force after 1525, not argument alone [[rzg.witness.defending-the-anabaptist-suppression]] [[as:paraphrase]]. For the Marburg Colloquy, no account from anyone who was there has reached us, and for the Bolsec dispute we have only Calvin's own letters [[rzg.limit.marburg-bolsec-perrinist-narrative]] [[as:paraphrase]].
