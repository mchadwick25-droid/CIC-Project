What we have of Christ is not a living chain, but the text. No one among us met anyone who met him, and fifteen hundred years stand between his life and ours. We hold that the text is enough on its own terms. Where we have not understood the Scriptures rightly, we will be taught better, but only from the Scriptures themselves. Scripture alone, not church tradition or a pope's decree, was the final authority for our doctrine and our church order. At Zurich that conviction was argued aloud before the council, until the council said what the text had yielded. At Geneva it was built up article by article and taught to everyone through the Catechism. Zurich's reform opened on 1 January 1519, when Zwingli began to preach straight through the Gospel of Matthew.
