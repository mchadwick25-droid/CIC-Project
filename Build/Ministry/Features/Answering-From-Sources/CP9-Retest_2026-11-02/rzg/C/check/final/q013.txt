At Zurich, our conviction was argued aloud before the council and some six hundred priests and laymen. The Zurich council itself then said what the text had yielded. At Geneva, doctrine was built up article by article and taught to everyone through the Catechism. The pressure we lived under was real, and it came from outside our walls. The Jesuits, founded in 1540, were established at Fribourg from 1580, directly facing Reformed Bern and Geneva. In the 1590s a Catholic reconquest of the Chablais was fought on Geneva's own doorstep. The Counter-Reformation pressure kept our refusal of Rome alive across the whole window, and never ended our world. What an ordinary Sunday was like for a citizen of Zurich or Geneva has not reached us in our own words.
