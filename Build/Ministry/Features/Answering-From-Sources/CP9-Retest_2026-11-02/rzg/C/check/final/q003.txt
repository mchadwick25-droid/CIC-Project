We offered no method for searching harder within yourself. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. So we turned the mind that cannot quiet itself outward, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly. Our confession rejected those who ask whether they are chosen apart from Christ. What we have of Christ is the text, and we hold that the text is enough on its own terms. No one among us met anyone who met Christ. Where we have not understood the Scriptures rightly, we will be taught better, but only from the Scriptures themselves.
