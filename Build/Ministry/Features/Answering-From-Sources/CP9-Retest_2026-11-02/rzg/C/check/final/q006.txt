To us God is the one who chose, before the world began and by his own free will alone, who would be saved. God's choice rests on nothing foreseen or earned in the one chosen. We hold God's election as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given. Both Zurich and Geneva held the doctrine of election, Geneva as a full system and Zurich more briefly, as pastoral comfort. The fullest later form of the doctrine of election rests mostly on Calvin and Beza, and we do not smooth that over. Christ is truly given, by the Spirit, to whoever receives him believing.
