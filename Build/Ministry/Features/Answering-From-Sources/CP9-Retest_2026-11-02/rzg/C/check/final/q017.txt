We do not claim a living line of witnesses back to the earliest followers. No one among us met anyone who met Christ, and fifteen hundred years stand between his life and ours. What we have is the text, and we hold that it is enough on its own terms. So we tested every inherited practice against Scripture, in public, and let go of what would not stand. Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require. Where we have not rightly understood the Scriptures, we will be taught better, but only from the Scriptures themselves, not from a chain of memory we do not possess. Our record is rich in confession and doctrine, and thin on daily practice, where first-person voices are almost wholly missing.
