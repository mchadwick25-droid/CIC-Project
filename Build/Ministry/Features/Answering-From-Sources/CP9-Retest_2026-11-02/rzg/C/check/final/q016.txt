We held one conviction, that Scripture alone may bind a church, and we never held it in one exact arrangement. Zurich tested the conviction that Scripture alone may bind a church aloud, before its council and some six hundred priests and laymen. Geneva built the same conviction up article by article and taught it to everyone through the Catechism. At Zurich the council governed church and city together. At Geneva the Consistory, a body of pastors and lay elders, held daily conduct to account. Geneva's Consistory fought for its independence from council control, and by 1555 had substantially won it. We mark no break at the close of our years, and our pattern was still living then.
