To us God is the one who chose, before the world began and by his own free will alone, who would be saved [[rzg.term.predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God's choice rests on nothing foreseen or earned in the one chosen [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We hold God's election as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Both Zurich and Geneva held the doctrine of election, Geneva as a full system and Zurich more briefly, as pastoral comfort [[rzg.term.predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The fullest later form of the doctrine of election rests mostly on Calvin and Beza, and we do not smooth that over [[rzg.gravity.sovereignty-of-god-predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]].
