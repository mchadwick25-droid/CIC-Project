How an ordinary citizen of Zurich or Geneva lived or felt a day, a service, or a summons has not reached us in our words. What we can tell you is what we refused: a church gathered only by a believer's own profession, apart from the whole city. We held that a church gathered only by a believer's own profession unmade the very unity our reform existed to strengthen. The Anabaptists read Scripture, and we do not deny it, but we held that the Anabaptists did not read Scripture whole. Our own council moved against the Anabaptists with civil force in the years after 1525, and not with argument alone.
