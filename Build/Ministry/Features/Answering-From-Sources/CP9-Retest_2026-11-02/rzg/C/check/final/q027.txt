Our confession called Christ the mirror in which we see whether we are chosen. We offered God's free choice of who is saved as settled ground, not as a threat. At Geneva, a body of pastors and lay elders held the whole population's ordinary conduct answerable to what we profess. The Consistory's harshest step was excommunication, which answered sin that was not repented, and readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. How a Consistory summons felt to the person who received it has not reached us.
