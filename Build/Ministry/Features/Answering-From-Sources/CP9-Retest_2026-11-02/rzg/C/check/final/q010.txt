Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require. Whatever Scripture does yield, no pope, council, or custom may set aside. At Zurich we argued aloud before the city council until the council judged what the text had yielded. At Geneva we built doctrine book by book and taught it to the whole population. Neither city borrowed the other's method. The Anabaptist break in 1525 tested our scriptural method from inside our own circle. We do not deny that the Anabaptists read Scripture, but we deny that the Anabaptists read it whole. You ask what a modern reader might miss, or whether the violence in some texts troubled our people, and our record gives no answer to either.
