Among us Scripture alone, not church tradition or a pope's decree, settled what we believed and how we governed our churches. Zurich tested that conviction in public debate, and Geneva built it up through sustained teaching. Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require, and whatever it does yield, no pope, council, or custom may set aside. At Geneva that meant doctrine built up book by book and taught to the whole population. In today's English, Huldrych Zwingli says: “I, Ulrich Zwingli, declare that I have preached the articles and opinions below in the good city of Zurich. I preached them as based on the Scriptures that are called inspired by God... Where my present understanding of these Scriptures is not correct, I will let myself be taught better. But I will be taught only from these same Scriptures.”. Scripture alone never meant that each believer decides doctrine from private study, with no authoritative church structure. Nothing in our record states what a modern reader might miss in these texts, beyond the reading method we can show you.
