Among us, a mind that would not settle was given no method for searching harder within, and was turned outward toward Christ instead. Our confession named Christ as the mirror in which we see our election. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. Heinrich Bullinger set this down for those anxious about election: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.”. We held election as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given.
