No source in our record describes our worship rooms directly, so we cannot rate that outside record for you. What we hold is reasoned from our doctrine, and not read off a witness who stood there. From what we held about the Mass, we reason that a stranger would have found no image to bow to and no altar built for a sacrifice. A plain table stood in its place. By most accounts the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s. Huldrych Zwingli held in 1523: “Christ sacrificed himself once. He is forever a sure and valid sacrifice for the sins of all the faithful. From this it follows that the mass is not a sacrifice. It is a remembrance of the sacrifice and an assurance of the salvation which Christ has given us.”. We refused two claims about the Supper, a repeated sacrifice and Christ's body sitting in the bread.
