You would find mostly what we took away: no image to bow to, and a plain table where an altar once stood. By most accounts, the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s. No voice or instrument answered it in worship for years after. We also draw it from our confession's chapter on idols and images, which holds that no image can represent God. That is reasoned from our doctrine, not read off a witness who stood in the room. Its size, its furnishing, and what its walls looked like, no source in our record describes.
