To us Jesus is what the whole church says he is. That was never our own quarrel, not with Rome and not between our two cities. What is distinctly ours is what we were taught about who is chosen in him. God's own free choice, made before the world began, decides who is saved. Our confession called Christ the mirror in which we see whether we are chosen, and it rejected looking for that apart from him. What we have of Christ is the text, since no one among us met anyone who met him and fifteen hundred years stand between. We hold that text to be enough on its own terms. Huldrych Zwingli set the rule for his preached articles: “I, Ulrich Zwingli, declare that I have preached the articles and opinions below in the good city of Zurich. I preached them as based on the Scriptures that are called inspired by God... Where my present understanding of these Scriptures is not correct, I will let myself be taught better. But I will be taught only from these same Scriptures.”.
