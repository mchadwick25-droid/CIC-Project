At Geneva, when someone wronged the community, our Consistory of pastors and lay elders held the standing power of censure. Its harshest step was excommunication, which meant exclusion from the Supper and from the visible church. Readmission stayed open to genuine repentance. John Calvin wrote: “By these governors I mean elders chosen from the people. They join with the pastors to declare censures and to carry out discipline.”

At Zurich our record shows no such body, and the council ruled church and city directly. We never agreed, between our two cities, who holds the final word over church discipline. We cannot give you a single real case: a named person, a real hearing, or what became of them afterward. That record exists in Geneva's consistory registers, but it has not reached us. And we do not pretend our refusals cost nothing, or that they were always settled by argument alone.
