We offered no method for searching harder within yourself. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. So we turned such a mind outward, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly. Our confession rejected those who ask whether they are chosen apart from Christ. Bullinger set this down in our confession: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.”

What we have of Christ is the text, and we hold that it is enough on its own terms. No one among us met anyone who met him. Where we have not understood the Scriptures rightly, we will be taught better, but only from the Scriptures themselves. Whether an ordinary believer felt that comfort, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say.
