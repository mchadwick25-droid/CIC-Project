Yes: to someone who could not quiet their own mind, we offered no method for searching harder within, and turned that mind toward Christ instead. Our confession rejected asking whether one is chosen apart from Christ, and named Christ as the mirror in which we see our election. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva actually felt that comfort, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say. Heinrich Bullinger gave this word in his confession: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.”.
