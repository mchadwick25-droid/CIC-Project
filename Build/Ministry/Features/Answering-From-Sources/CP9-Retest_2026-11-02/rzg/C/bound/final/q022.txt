We cannot walk you through an ordinary day, because what survives from our hand is confession, catechism, disputation record, and argument. How a Sunday service felt to sit through, or what a Consistory summons felt like to receive, has not reached us. What we can show is the frame around such a day, and at Geneva that frame was the Consistory, our body of pastors and lay elders. The Consistory held everyone to account for how they lived each day. At Zurich the council ruled church and city directly. We never settled between our two cities who holds the final word over church discipline. At the Supper, the bread and cup were not a sacrifice repeated, and not Christ's body confined to what you can chew.
