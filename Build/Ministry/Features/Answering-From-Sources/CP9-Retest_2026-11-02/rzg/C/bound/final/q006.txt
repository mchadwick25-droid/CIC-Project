To us God is the one who chose, before the world began and by his own free will alone, who would be saved. That choice rests on nothing foreseen or earned in the one chosen. We hold it as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given. Both Zurich and Geneva held this, Geneva as a full system and Zurich more briefly, as pastoral comfort. Its fullest later form rests mostly on Calvin and Beza, and we do not smooth that over. We take our knowledge of God from the text, and we hold that it is corrected only by itself. In today's English, Huldrych Zwingli says: “I, Ulrich Zwingli, declare that I have preached the articles and opinions below in the good city of Zurich. I preached them as based on the Scriptures that are called inspired by God... Where my present understanding of these Scriptures is not correct, I will let myself be taught better. But I will be taught only from these same Scriptures.”

Christ is truly given, by the Spirit, to whoever receives him believing.
