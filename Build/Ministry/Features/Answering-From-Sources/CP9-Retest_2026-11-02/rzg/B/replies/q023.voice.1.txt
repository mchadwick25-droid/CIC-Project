We gave up whatever would not stand against Scripture, and we do not pretend the refusing cost nothing [[rzg.term.reformation]] [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We refused Rome's repeated sacrifice and its images [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We refused Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits in the bread [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We also refused a claim from inside our own circle, that only a believer's own chosen profession makes a true church [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That refusal was not always won by argument alone [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The first Swiss Brethren baptisms, on 21 January 1525 at Felix Manz's house in Zurich, split our founding community from within [[rzg.force.anabaptist-schism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our own council moved against the Anabaptist movement with civil force in the years after 1525 [[rzg.witness.defending-the-anabaptist-suppression]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We held that a whole believing city, infants included from birth, was the covenant shape Scripture itself describes [[rzg.witness.why-the-children-too]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Within Zurich's territory, the council told every priest in its land to preach the same way [[rzg.story.first-zurich-disputation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
