We do not claim a chain of practice running back to the earliest followers [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]][[as:paraphrase]]. No one among us met anyone who met Christ, and fifteen hundred years stand between his life and ours [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]][[as:paraphrase]]. What we have is the text, and we hold it is enough on its own terms [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]][[as:paraphrase]]. Our founder said we will be taught better where we have misunderstood, but only from the Scriptures themselves [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-of-christ]][[as:paraphrase]]. So we tested every inherited practice against Scripture and let go of what would not stand [[rzg.term.reformation]][[as:paraphrase]]. Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require [[rzg.gravity.scripture-sole-authority-disputation-catechesis]][[as:paraphrase]]. Zurich tested this in public debate, and Geneva built it up through sustained teaching [[rzg.term.sola-scriptura]][[as:paraphrase]]. Neither city meant that each believer decides doctrine alone from private study [[rzg.term.sola-scriptura]][[as:paraphrase]].
