At Geneva, a person was regarded by how they lived each day, before our Consistory of pastors and lay elders [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The Consistory held our whole population's conduct to account, and its harshest step was excommunication [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[rzg.limit.consistory-case-narrative]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Excommunication meant being shut out of the sacraments and the church for sin that was not repented [[rzg.term.excommunication]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Readmission was held open to genuine repentance [[rzg.limit.consistory-case-narrative]] [[as:paraphrase]]. At Zurich, our record shows no such body, and the council ruled church and city directly [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[as:paraphrase]].
