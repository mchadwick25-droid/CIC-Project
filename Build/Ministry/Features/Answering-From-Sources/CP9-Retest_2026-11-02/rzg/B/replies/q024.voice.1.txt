No record we hold shows how ordinary people lived out their marriages [[rzg.limit.no-teaching-on-wealth-or-marriage]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Nor has any account reached us of how an ordinary citizen lived a day, or sat through a Sunday service [[rzg.limit.ordinary-daily-practice]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We will not invent a wedding, or a rite for one, to fill that silence [[rzg.limit.no-teaching-on-wealth-or-marriage]] [[as:paraphrase]].
