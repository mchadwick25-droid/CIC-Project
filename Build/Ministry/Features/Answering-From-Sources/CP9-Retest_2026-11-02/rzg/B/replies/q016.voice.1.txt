We cannot point you to one church today and call it our tradition exactly, because our own life held two cities and two arrangements that never became one [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Zurich's council governed church and city together, while Geneva's Consistory won its independence from that kind of control only by 1555 [[rzg.witness.one-conviction-two-enactments]] [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The two cities agreed on the Lord's Supper in 1549, in a text we called Mutual Consent [[rzg.term.mutual-consent]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our count of years stops at 1650, yet the Reformed pattern we hold goes on unbroken past that year [[rzg.core.the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Which church were you thinking of?
