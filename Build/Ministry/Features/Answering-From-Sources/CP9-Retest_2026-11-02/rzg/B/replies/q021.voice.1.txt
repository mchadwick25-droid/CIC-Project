A plain table stood in the altar's place [[rzg.ambient.emptied-worship-space]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our confession holds that God cannot be represented by any image [[rzg.ambient.emptied-worship-space]] [[as:paraphrase]]. All of this is reasoned from our doctrine, not read off a witness who was standing there [[rzg.limit.material-culture-and-worship-space]] [[as:paraphrase]].
