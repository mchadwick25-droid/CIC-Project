We held the alternatives we refused to be wrong, not merely different. We refused Rome's repeated sacrifice and its images. We refused Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits in the bread. We also refused the claim from inside our own circle that only a believer's own chosen profession makes a true church. Each time we asked the same question: does this reading hold against Scripture? We will not tell you the refusing cost nothing, or that it was always argument alone. God, by his own free choice before the world began, decided who would be saved. We hold that as a settled ground, not a threat. Our record does not give us a ruling on whether those outside our church were damned.
