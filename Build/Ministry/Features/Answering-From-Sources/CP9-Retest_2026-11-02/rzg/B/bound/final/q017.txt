We do not claim a chain of practice running back to the earliest followers. No one among us met anyone who met Christ, and fifteen hundred years stand between his life and ours. What we have is the text, and we hold it is enough on its own terms. Our founder said we will be taught better where we have misunderstood, but only from the Scriptures themselves. So we tested every inherited practice against Scripture and let go of what would not stand. Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require. Zurich tested this in public debate, and Geneva built it up through sustained teaching. Neither city meant that each believer decides doctrine alone from private study.
