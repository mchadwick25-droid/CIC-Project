What we can tell you from our record here is what we were taught about who is chosen in Christ. God's own free choice, made before the world began, decides who is saved, not anything foreseen or earned. We hold that as a settled ground, not a threat. Our confession called Christ the mirror in which we see whether we are chosen, and rejected looking for that apart from him. We will not send you searching your own heart for proof of it.
