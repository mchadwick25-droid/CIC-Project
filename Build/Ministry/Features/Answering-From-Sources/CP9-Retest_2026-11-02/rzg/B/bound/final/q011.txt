For us Scripture alone, not church tradition or a pope's decree, settled what we believed and how we governed our churches. At Zurich we tested that conviction in public debate. At Geneva we built it up through sustained teaching, book by book, to the whole population. Whatever Scripture will not yield, we have no standing to require. In today's English, Huldrych Zwingli says: “I, Ulrich Zwingli, declare that I have preached the articles and opinions below in the good city of Zurich. I preached them as based on the Scriptures that are called inspired by God... Where my present understanding of these Scriptures is not correct, I will let myself be taught better. But I will be taught only from these same Scriptures.”. Scripture alone did not mean each believer deciding doctrine privately, without any church structure. Nothing in our record states what a modern reader might miss in these texts, beyond the reading method we can show you. Nothing in our record reflects on whether the violent passages as such troubled our people.
