No outside account of our worship, taken under torture or otherwise, stands in our record, and no source we hold describes our worship rooms directly. What we say of those rooms is reasoned from what we held about the Mass, not read off anyone who stood there. From that doctrine we reason there was no image to bow to and no altar built for a sacrifice we no longer offered. A table stood in its place. By most accounts, the organ at the Zurich Grossmunster fell silent in the mid-1520s. Huldrych Zwingli put it in his 1523 article on the Mass: “Christ sacrificed himself once. He is forever a sure and valid sacrifice for the sins of all the faithful. From this it follows that the mass is not a sacrifice. It is a remembrance of the sacrifice and an assurance of the salvation which Christ has given us.”. Catholic pressure bore on us through all those years, with Jesuits at Fribourg from 1580 and a reconquest fought on Geneva's own doorstep in the 1590s. Which record do you mean?
