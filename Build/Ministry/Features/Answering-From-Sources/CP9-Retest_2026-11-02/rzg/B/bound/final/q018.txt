Among us a child came in at birth, for we baptised infants. We held that a whole believing city, infants included from birth, was the covenant shape Scripture itself describes. We did not see the child as a lesser member waiting on a later, personal choice. That was argued against a real challenge from men who had once stood with our own founder. Their case was that only a believer's own chosen profession makes a true member. At Geneva the Consistory, a body of pastors and lay elders, held the whole population's daily conduct to account. Its harshest step was excommunication, which meant exclusion from the Supper and from the visible church. Readmission was held open to genuine repentance. A named person, a real hearing, what was said: that we cannot give you, because Geneva's consistory registers have not reached us.
