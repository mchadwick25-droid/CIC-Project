An ordinary day among us is not something our record can show you; what survives from our hand is confession, catechism, disputation record and doctrinal argument. How a Sunday service felt, or what a Consistory summons felt like to receive, has not reached us in our words. At Geneva, the Consistory was our body of pastors and lay elders, and it held everyone to account for how they lived each day. At Zurich, no such body appears in our record, and the council ruled church and city directly.
