We gave up whatever would not stand against Scripture, and we do not pretend the refusing cost nothing. We refused Rome's repeated sacrifice and its images. We refused Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits in the bread. We also refused a claim from inside our own circle, that only a believer's own chosen profession makes a true church. That refusal was not always won by argument alone. The first Swiss Brethren baptisms, on 21 January 1525 at Felix Manz's house in Zurich, split our founding community from within. Our own council moved against the Anabaptist movement with civil force in the years after 1525. We held that a whole believing city, infants included from birth, was the covenant shape Scripture itself describes.
