Zurich's reform was argued in the open, when six hundred people met in the Town Hall to test our founder's case against Scripture. The council ruled for it and told every priest in its land to preach the same way. Catholic pressure was sustained across the whole window. The Jesuits were established at Fribourg from 1580, facing Bern and Geneva directly. In the 1590s a Catholic reconquest of the Chablais was fought on Geneva's own doorstep. What we cannot give you is the look of our worship spaces, because no source in our record describes them directly. Nor has any account reached us of how an ordinary citizen lived a day or sat through a service.
