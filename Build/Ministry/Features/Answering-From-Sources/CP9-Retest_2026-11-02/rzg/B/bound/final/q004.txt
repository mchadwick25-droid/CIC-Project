To us Jesus is what the whole church says he is, and that was never our own quarrel, not with Rome and not between our two cities. What is distinctly ours is what we were taught about who is chosen in him. God's own free choice, made before the world began, decides who is saved, and not anything foreseen or earned in the one chosen. We hold that as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith already has, in that faith itself, the only evidence of election anyone is given. Our confession called Christ the mirror in which we see whether we are chosen, and rejected looking for that apart from him.
