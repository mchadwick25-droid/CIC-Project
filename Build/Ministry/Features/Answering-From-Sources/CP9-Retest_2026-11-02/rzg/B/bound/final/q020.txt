What survives from our hand is confession, catechism, disputation record, and argument, and not any account of how an ordinary believer lived or felt a turning of the heart. What we can show you is a refusal, from inside our own circle, of a church gathered only by a believer's own profession, apart from the whole city. We held that such a church unmade the very unity our reform existed to strengthen. Those who held it read Scripture, and we do not deny it, but we held that they did not read it whole. Our council also moved against them with civil force in the years after 1525, not only with argument.
