At Geneva, a person was regarded by how they lived each day, before our Consistory of pastors and lay elders. The Consistory held our whole population's conduct to account, and its harshest step was excommunication. Excommunication meant being shut out of the sacraments and the church for sin that was not repented. Readmission was held open to genuine repentance. At Zurich, our record shows no such body, and the council ruled church and city directly. Calvin wrote: “By these governors I mean elders chosen from the people. They join with the pastors to declare censures and to carry out discipline.”. What we cannot give you is a single real case, with a named person and a real hearing.
