We would not send you searching harder inside yourself for a belief you cannot find. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. So we turned such a mind outward toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly. In today's English, Heinrich Bullinger says: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.”. To us Jesus is what the whole church says he is, and that was never our own quarrel. What we have of him is the text, since no one among us met anyone who met him. We hold that text enough on its own terms. Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva felt comfort from this, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say.
