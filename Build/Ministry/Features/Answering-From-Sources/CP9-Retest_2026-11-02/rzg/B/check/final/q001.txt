What we have is the text, and we hold that the text is enough on its own terms. Our founder held that where the Scriptures are not rightly understood, correction must come from the Scriptures themselves, not from a chain of memory we do not possess. At Geneva, a body of pastors and lay elders held the whole population's ordinary conduct answerable to what we profess. The first Swiss Brethren baptisms in 1525, only two years after the First Zurich Disputation, tested our scriptural method against a rival reading from inside our own circle. How an ordinary citizen lived with the text from waking to sleeping has not reached us in an ordinary citizen's own words.
