We cannot describe our worship space, because no source in our record describes it directly. The size of our worship space, its furnishing, and the look of its walls are not given. What we can tell you is what we refused: no image to bow to, and no altar built for a sacrifice we no longer offered. A plain table stood in the altar's place. By most accounts, the organ at the Grossmunster in Zurich fell silent in the mid-1520s, and no voice or instrument answered the organ in worship for years after. What we say of our worship space is reasoned from our doctrine, not read off a witness who was standing there.
