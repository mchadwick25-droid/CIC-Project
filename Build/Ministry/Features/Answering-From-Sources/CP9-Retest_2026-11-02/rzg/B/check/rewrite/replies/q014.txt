Authority ran differently in our two cities, and we never agreed between them who holds the final word over church discipline [[rzg.witness.what-we-have-not-agreed]] [[rzg.gravity.council-led-authority-vs-consistorial-independence]] [[as:paraphrase]].
At Zurich the council governed church and city together, from our first days [[rzg.gravity.council-led-authority-vs-consistorial-independence]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Zurich's own title for its chief pastor was antistes, which Bullinger held after Zwingli, and the antistes answered to the city council [[rzg.term.antistes]] [[as:paraphrase]].
At Geneva, the Consistory was a body of pastors and lay elders that held the whole population's daily conduct to account [[rzg.term.consistory]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Calvin's recall in 1541 produced the Ecclesiastical Ordinances, which created the Geneva Consistory with the power of censure [[rzg.force.1541-ecclesiastical-ordinances-consistory-founding]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Geneva's Consistory fought for its independence from the magistrate, and only by 1555 substantially won it [[rzg.gravity.consistorial-church-discipline]] [[as:paraphrase]].
