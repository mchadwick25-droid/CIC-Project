The Consistory held our whole population's conduct to account, and the Consistory's harshest step was excommunication. Excommunication meant being shut out of the sacraments and the church for sin that was not repented. At Geneva, readmission was held open to genuine repentance. At Zurich, our record shows no such body, and the council ruled church and city directly. What we cannot give you is a single real case, with a named person and a real hearing.
