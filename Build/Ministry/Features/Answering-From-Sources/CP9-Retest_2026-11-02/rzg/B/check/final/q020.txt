What we can show you is a refusal, from inside our own circle, of a church gathered only by a believer's own profession, apart from the whole city. We held that a church gathered only by a believer's own profession unmade the very unity our reform existed to strengthen. The Anabaptists read Scripture, and we do not deny it, but we held that the Anabaptists did not read it whole. Our council also moved against the Anabaptists with civil force in the years after 1525, not only with argument.
