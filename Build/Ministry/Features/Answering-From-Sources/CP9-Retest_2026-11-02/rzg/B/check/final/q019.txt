We offered no method for searching harder within yourself. A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. So we turned such a mind outward, toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly. Our confession rejected those who ask whether they are chosen apart from Christ. We hold God's free choice as the ground of a settled life, not as a threat. Whoever is joined to Christ by true faith has in that faith the only evidence of election anyone is given. Bullinger set this down in our confession: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.”
