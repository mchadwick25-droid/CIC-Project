To someone tempted to doubt, our confession said: do not look for proof of your election apart from Christ. Our confession named Christ as the mirror in which we see our election. Heinrich Bullinger's confession turns the anxious this way, and here is how it puts the matter: “We reject those who try to find out, apart from Christ, whether they are chosen. ... Let ... Christ be the mirror in which we look at our predestination.” A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself and no bottom to it, so we turned the mind outward toward Christ. We offered our confession's answer as comfort. Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva felt that comfort, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say.
