At Geneva the Consistory was pastors and lay elders together, and the elders held real power beside the pastors. The harshest step of the Consistory was excommunication, which shut a person out of the Supper and the visible church for sin that was not repented. What we cannot give you is one real case, with a named person, a real hearing, and what was said. The registers that hold real cases exist at Geneva, but the registers have not reached us. How a Consistory summons felt to the person who received the summons has not reached us either. Our own council moved against the Anabaptists with civil force in the years after 1525, and we will not tell you the suppression was argument alone.
