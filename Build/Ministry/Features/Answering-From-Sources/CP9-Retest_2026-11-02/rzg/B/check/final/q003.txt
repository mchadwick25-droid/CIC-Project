A mind turned only on itself finds more of itself, and no bottom to it. So we turned a mind that will not settle outward toward Christ, once, rather than toward the self, endlessly. To us Jesus is what the whole church says he is, and who Jesus is was never our own quarrel. What we have of Christ is the text, since no one among us met anyone who met Christ. We hold the text enough on its own terms. Whether an ordinary believer at Zurich or Geneva felt comfort from our confession's teaching, or felt anxious instead, our record does not say.
