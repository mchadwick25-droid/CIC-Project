We held the alternatives we refused to be wrong, not merely different [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We refused Rome's repeated sacrifice and its images [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We refused Wittenberg's claim that Christ's body sits in the bread [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We also refused the claim from inside our own circle that only a believer's own chosen profession makes a true church [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Each time we asked the same question: does this reading hold against Scripture [[as:paraphrase]]? We will not tell you the refusing cost nothing, or that it was always argument alone [[rzg.witness.a-narrow-true-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God, by his own free choice before the world began, decided who would be saved [[rzg.term.predestination-election]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We hold God's free choice of who is saved as a settled ground, not a threat [[rzg.witness.one-thing-not-in-dispute]] [[as:paraphrase]].
