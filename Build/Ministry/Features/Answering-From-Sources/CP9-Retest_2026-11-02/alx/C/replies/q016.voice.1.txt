We cannot say that any present-day church is our church, because we speak from before the splits that made today's map [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In our own time we called ourselves part of the catholic church, meaning the whole church spread everywhere as against the sects, not the name of a later denomination [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What we belonged to was one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We received baptism, the shared bread and the teaching of those coming in as a deposit handed down, and did not invent them in Alexandria [[alx.force.apostolic-tradition]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The rule of faith was the short summary of what the apostles handed down [[alx.term.kanon-pisteos]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The boundary with Gnostic schools and, after 318, with the Arian churches was real to us [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
