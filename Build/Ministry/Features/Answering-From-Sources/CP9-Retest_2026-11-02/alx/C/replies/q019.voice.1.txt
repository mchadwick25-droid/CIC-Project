We offered a restless mind a teacher, a long path, and a patient God, because God is always teaching through Scripture's difficulty, through catechesis, and through suffering [[alx.gravity.divine-pedagogy]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Formation was active work, done step by step [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The steps were catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Prayer meant converse with God, spoken or silent, the soul turning toward him without distraction [[alx.term.prayer]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Turning back was metanoia, a change of mind, and not only feeling sorry [[alx.term.metanoia]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Clement wrote of the Word as Instructor: [[quote:alx.quote.clement-instructor]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our teachers left room for questions, and a doubter was expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry it alone [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We have one student's account of how a teacher led him through a staged course [[alx.story.gregory-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That account comes from Caesarea, after the Alexandrian rupture, and it is not the ordinary Alexandrian experience [[alx.story.gregory-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
