We kept a real boundary around what we belonged to: one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, and the rule of faith. The boundary was kept by argument and exclusion from communion, and after Nicaea by harder tools. Whether Origen held the restoration of all things as settled doctrine is contested, since Origen's texts survive through Rufinus and Jerome. Our teachers spoke of both faith and works, and said we must love God and our neighbour and do the Father's will. The soul has a real power to turn toward God or away, and God does not compel the soul. For our own who fell under persecution, the tradition that won here brought the repentant back in, even at the deathbed.
