We did not speak of guilt at birth, but we believed something real went wrong at the root of humanity. Our teachers spoke of inherited death and weakness, and also of a fall that each soul signs onto in its own living. The wound was real, universal, and inherited in its effects. Origen wondered about the soul's own descent, but as open inquiry, not doctrine. We called the soul's turning from God toward lesser goods hamartia, more a wound than only a debt owed or a law broken. So the doctor's imagery outweighed the courtroom's.
