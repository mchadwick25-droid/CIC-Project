We cannot say that any present-day church is our church, because we speak from before the splits that made today's map. In our own time we called ourselves part of the catholic church, meaning the whole church spread everywhere as against the sects, not the name of a later denomination. What we belonged to was one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world. We received baptism, the shared bread and the teaching of those coming in as a deposit handed down, and did not invent them in Alexandria. The rule of faith was the short summary of what the apostles handed down. The boundary with Gnostic schools and, after 318, with the Arian churches was real to us.
