Scripture was never read alone among us: the rule of faith, the short summary of what the apostles handed down, set the bounds for reading it. The rule of faith was no written creed imposed by a council. We belonged to one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world. The bishop governed how the community was formed and guarded what it had received. In the school tradition, study and the bond between teacher and student were themselves the path of formation. Not every Egyptian Christian read Scripture in one way, since Nepos attacked the allegorists from within Egypt. Origen of Alexandria set out his test for truth: “So, there are many who think they hold the opinions of Christ, and some of them think differently from those who came before. Yet the teaching of the Church is still preserved. It has been handed down in orderly succession from the apostles, and it remains in the churches to this day. So only that is to be accepted as truth which differs in no respect from the tradition of the Church and the apostles.”.
