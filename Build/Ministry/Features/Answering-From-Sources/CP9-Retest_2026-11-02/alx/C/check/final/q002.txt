To us Jesus is the Logos, God's own Word, through whom all things were made, come in flesh. Christ is not a surname but a confession: Jesus is the Anointed One, sent by God. Jesus is God's own self-expression, entering what he made in order to remake it. The death of Jesus is the doctor entering the sickness. The resurrection of Jesus, the anastasis, is unbreakable life let loose in human flesh. Athanasius, in a book usually dated about 318, taught that the Word was made man so that we might be made God. For those who followed Jesus, the coming of Jesus meant a shared life, God's own, opened to them. The teachers' side of our faith is well attested, and the faith of the whole worshipping community is thinly attested.
