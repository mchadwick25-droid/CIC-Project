For the whole community, a life was formed by the washing that made new, the shared bread, and the teaching of those coming in. Scripture was heard as formative address, and the community shared its life. Sacraments and shared life form people before any school, and without one. The steps of formation were catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom. We call formation primary only for the part of the community that our literate sources attest, and we leave open whether it is primary for the whole community.
