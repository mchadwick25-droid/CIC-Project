We read Scripture as God's main lesson, in a school that was the whole of creation [[alx.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. So reading was a practice of formation: meeting the Logos at the depth the reader's formation allowed [[alx.term.interpretation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We read at more than one level, the plain sense and deeper senses about Christ and the soul, a method contested within Egypt itself [[alx.term.allegoria]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What the apostles delivered plainly stood fixed, and everything else was open ground, where asking questions was itself a way of loving God [[alx.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In the school tradition, study and the bond between teacher and student were the very path of the soul's change [[alx.gravity.learning-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
