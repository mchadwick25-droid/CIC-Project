The steps were catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Prayer meant converse with God, spoken or silent, the soul turning toward God without distraction [[alx.term.prayer]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Turning back was metanoia, a change of mind, and not only feeling sorry [[alx.term.metanoia]] [[as:paraphrase]].
