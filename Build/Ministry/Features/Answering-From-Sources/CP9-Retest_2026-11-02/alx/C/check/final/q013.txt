What our record holds is danger that came and went over three centuries before 325. Property was seized, teachers' fathers were beheaded, and bishops fled. There were four persecutions: the Antonine, the Severan, the Decian from 249 to 251, and the Diocletianic from 303 to 311. The Decian and the Diocletianic persecutions did most to shape formation. Origen himself was jailed and tortured under Decius. Before 325, the church's whole authority was persuasion.
