If you dug where we met, we could not tell you what you would find. Our own writings describe souls and books far more than rooms and walls. The city itself has kept little, because it was rebuilt age after age, and much of the old shore now lies under the sea. For us the ekklesia was not a building or a membership list, but the assembly gathered around the Logos who calls it. What we can show you is the practice we received: baptism, the bread and cup, and the scriptures read aloud. The eucharistia, our thanksgiving meal of bread and cup, was open to members whether or not they could read.
