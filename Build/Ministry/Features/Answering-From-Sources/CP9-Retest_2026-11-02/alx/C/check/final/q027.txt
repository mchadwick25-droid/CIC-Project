Our teachers met a questioner with room to ask, not with suspicion. We treated doubt aimed at understanding as hunger, and we did not count it as sin. Clement insisted that faith is the foundation and not the ceiling. A person with a question was expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry the question alone. The asking stayed inside the rule of faith, and what the apostles delivered plainly stayed fixed. When the churches in the Arsinoite villages split over Nepos's reading of the promises, the bishop answered with three days of open argument, not a condemnation. Two kinds of formation stood side by side among us: depth for the few the school could teach, and breadth owed to the whole worshipping community. The school side of the two kinds of formation is well attested, while the community side is thinly attested.
