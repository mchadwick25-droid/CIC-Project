We believed in one God, the maker of all things, good and beyond every image and every clever description. God's Word and God's Spirit are God's own, not lesser gods and not created things. God is known the way light is known: you do not stare at the sun, you see everything else by it. Our deepest habit was to speak of God as a teacher. The whole creation is God's school, scripture is God's main lesson, and the slow healing of a soul is God's patient method. Our teachers held that God's hand in pain is a physician's. We refuse the idea that suffering proves God absent.
