Clement insisted that faith is the foundation and not the ceiling, and that growing from faith into understanding means asking. Pistis was the soul's first real turn toward God, and pistis was compatible with unresolved questions. What the apostles delivered plainly was fixed, and everything else was open ground, where asking, testing, being wrong, and correcting formed a soul. A doubter stood inside a praying community and was expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry the question alone. We did not have the modern language of a private crisis of faith.
