Among us, a person joined through long, staged teaching that led to baptism, and baptism was a real crossing into the new life [[alx.term.katechesis]] [[alx.term.baptism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our teachers named the steps of formation as catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The practice around the steps of formation was contemplation, asceticism, prayer, and teaching for baptism [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Baptism was not a ceremony announcing a choice already made, but a crossing made in the body [[alx.term.baptism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Formation by these steps is the path our literate sources show, and whether it was the path for the whole community we leave open [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]] [[as:paraphrase]].
