For the whole community, a life was formed by the washing that made new, the shared bread, and the teaching of those coming in. Scripture was heard as formative address, and the community shared its life. Sacraments and shared life form people before any school, and without one. In the city, that formation held contemplation, asceticism, prayer, and teaching for baptism. Its steps were catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom. We say this for the part of the community that our literate sources attest, and we leave open whether it held for everyone. To believe was only the beginning, because the soul was made to know God and to go on being drawn deeper. In today's English, Timothy says: “Question I. A boy of seven, or a man, who is a catechumen may be present at the offering and eat of it in ignorance. What should be done? Answer. Let him be illuminated, that is, baptized, for he is called by God. ... Question VIII. Should a woman who has just given birth keep the Paschal fast? Answer. No. ... Question X. Is a sick man required to keep the Paschal fast? Answer. No. Question XI. A clergyman is called to celebrate a marriage, and he has heard that it is incestuous. Should he comply and perform the offering? Answer. No; he must not share in other men's sins.”.
