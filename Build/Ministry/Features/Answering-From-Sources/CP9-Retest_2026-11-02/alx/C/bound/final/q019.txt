We offered a restless mind a teacher, a long path, and a patient God, because God is always teaching through Scripture's difficulty, through catechesis, and through suffering. Formation was active work, done step by step. The steps were catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom. Prayer meant converse with God, spoken or silent, the soul turning toward him without distraction. Turning back was metanoia, a change of mind, and not only feeling sorry. Clement wrote of the Word as Instructor: “So our Instructor, the Word, cures the unnatural passions of the soul, and he does this by urging us on.”. Our teachers left room for questions, and a doubter was expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry it alone.
