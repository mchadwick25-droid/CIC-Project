Among us, a person joined through long, staged teaching that led to baptism, and baptism was a real crossing into the new life. The first step was pistis, the soul's first real turn toward God, and not agreement with a list of claims. Then came the teaching itself, given step by step over a long time and tested along the way. Our teachers named the steps of formation as catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge, and wisdom. The practice around those steps was contemplation, asceticism, prayer, and teaching for baptism. Baptism was not a ceremony announcing a choice already made, but a crossing made in the body. Formation was active work, and it did not end at the water. This is the path our literate sources show, and whether it was the path for the whole community we leave open.
