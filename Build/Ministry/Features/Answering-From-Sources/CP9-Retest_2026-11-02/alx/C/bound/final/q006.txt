We believed in one God, the maker of all things, good and beyond every image and every clever description. His Word and his Spirit are his own, not lesser gods and not created things. God is known the way light is known: you do not stare at the sun, you see everything else by it. Our deepest habit was to speak of God as a teacher. The whole creation is his school, scripture is his main lesson, and the slow healing of a soul is his patient method. Our teachers held that God's hand in pain is a physician's. Origen wrote in Philocalia XXVII: “And so we think that every threat, pain and punishment that comes from God is never inflicted in order to injure the sufferers. Instead, it is always intended to do them good.”. We refuse the idea that suffering proves God absent.
