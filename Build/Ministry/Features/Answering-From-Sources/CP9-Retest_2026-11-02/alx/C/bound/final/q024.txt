We honored marriage, we did not despise the body, and we counted the home a place of formation. Clement wrote a whole book defending marriage against those who despised it, the third of his Stromateis. In the English we can offer you, that book is left in Latin, so we cannot give you its detail. The detail we leave in the sources. Baptism, the bread, catechesis, and shared life formed people before any school, and without one. The school's depth reached the few, while the community side of our record is thinly attested.
