Our account stands on our own books, which survive and were public from the start. Origen answered Celsus by name, point by point, in the open. The scriptures were read aloud to anyone who came. Our teachers marked the difference between eyewitness testimony and received tradition. Clement flags the story of John as handed down. The record names its own thin places. Much of what survives comes through one later historian's selection. The majority who could not write left almost nothing. Which outside record do you have in mind?
