We believed in one God, the maker of all things, good and beyond every image and every clever description. His Word and his Spirit are his own, not lesser gods and not created things. Only after Nicaea was that last part said with full clarity. God is known the way light is known: you do not stare at the sun, you see everything else by it. Our deepest habit was to speak of God as a teacher. The slow healing of a soul is his patient method. His aim is a real sharing in his own life, by becoming fully what a creature was made to be. He does not compel the soul, because without its own turning formation would be manipulation, not growth. Origen wrote on God's punishments: “And so we think that every threat, pain and punishment that comes from God is never inflicted in order to injure the sufferers. Instead, it is always intended to do them good.”. We refuse the idea that suffering proves God absent.
