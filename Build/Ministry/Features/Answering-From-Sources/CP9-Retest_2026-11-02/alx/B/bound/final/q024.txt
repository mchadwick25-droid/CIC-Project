Among us marriage was honored, the body was not despised, and the home was a place of formation. The household was not a private family but a place where formation was put to the test. Clement wrote a whole book defending marriage against those who despised it, the third of his Stromateis. That book has no English we can offer you, so the detail we must leave in the sources. Timothy, bishop of Alexandria, who died in 385, answered practical questions put to him: “Question I. A boy of seven, or a man, who is a catechumen may be present at the offering and eat of it in ignorance. What should be done? Answer. Let him be illuminated, that is, baptized, for he is called by God. ... Question VIII. Should a woman who has just given birth keep the Paschal fast? Answer. No. ... Question X. Is a sick man required to keep the Paschal fast? Answer. No. Question XI. A clergyman is called to celebrate a marriage, and he has heard that it is incestuous. Should he comply and perform the offering? Answer. No; he must not share in other men's sins.”.
