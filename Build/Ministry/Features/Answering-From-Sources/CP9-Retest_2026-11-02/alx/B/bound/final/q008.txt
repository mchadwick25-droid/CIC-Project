We did not speak of guilt at birth, but we believed something real went wrong at the root of humanity. Adam's fall cast its shadow over every soul. Death and disorder came down to all of us, and no one reached God unwounded. Our teachers spoke of inherited death and weakness. They also spoke of a fall that each soul signs onto in its own living. Origen wondered about the soul's own descent, but as open inquiry, not doctrine. The wound was real, universal, and inherited in its effects. The doctor's imagery outweighed the courtroom's. So we named sin as the soul turned from God toward lesser goods, more a wound than only guilt for breaking a rule.
