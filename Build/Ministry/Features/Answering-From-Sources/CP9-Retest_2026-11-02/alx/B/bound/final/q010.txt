We read Scripture as a lesson from God the teacher, and for us study was itself the soul's formation. Our teachers called the whole creation God's school, and Scripture his main lesson. They drew one careful line. What the apostles delivered plainly stood fixed. Everything else was open ground, where asking questions was itself a way of loving God. We saw this teaching as God's whole plan for saving creation, given in graduated steps and centered on the Incarnation. Clement wrote of philosophy and the Greeks: “Perhaps, too, philosophy was given to the Greeks directly and in the first place, until the Lord should call the Greeks. For philosophy was a guardian to bring the Greek mind to Christ, as the law was for the Hebrews. So philosophy was a preparation. It paved the way for the one who is made perfect in Christ.”. This depth of study was the school's gift to the few, and the breadth owed to the whole worshipping community was a second pole we never gave up. The school side of our record is well attested, and the community side is thinly attested.
