Scripture is not only a record of what God once said, and a reader who treats it so misses the main thing. To us it is the Logos speaking now, through the text, to a soul formed to hear. So we read for change, and a reader of depth is transformed by the reading, not merely informed. We read at more than one level: the plain sense, and deeper senses about Christ and the soul. Origen wrote in Philocalia I.11: “Just as a human being consists of body, soul and spirit, so too does Scripture. God granted it for the salvation of humankind.”. Beneath every level lay one way of listening, which was to hear the Logos speaking. Not every teacher among us read as Origen did, and the method was contested within Egypt itself. What we say here holds for readers who could read, since the villages appear in our record only through their bishop's account.
