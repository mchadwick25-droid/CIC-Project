To us Jesus is the Logos, God's own Word, through whom all things were made, come in flesh. We called him the Christ, which is a confession that he is the Anointed One, sent by God, and not a surname. Clement opens with him as the New Song that makes men out of stones and puts the world back in tune. Jesus is God's own self-expression, entering what he made in order to remake it. His death is the doctor entering the sickness. His resurrection, the anastasis, is unbreakable life let loose in human flesh. For the people who followed him, it meant a shared life, God's own, opened to them. Athanasius wrote in On the Incarnation: “For he was made man, so that we might be made God...”. What we can show you most fully is how the school's teachers held him, since the school side is well attested. The community side is thinly attested.
