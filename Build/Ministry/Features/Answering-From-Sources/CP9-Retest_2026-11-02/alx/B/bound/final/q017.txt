Our evidence is real, but it is not a paper chain back to the apostles. Baptism, the bread and cup, the scriptures read aloud, and the rule of faith were the tradition's general, unobjected practice. Our practice ran unbroken as far back as our own memory reached. We agreed with the other churches we were in communion with. And our rule of faith matched the apostolic writings we read. Our teachers named that rule outright as the apostles' deposit, handed down. In today's English, Origen of Alexandria says: “So, there are many who think they hold the opinions of Christ, and some of them think differently from those who came before. Yet the teaching of the Church is still preserved. It has been handed down in orderly succession from the apostles, and it remains in the churches to this day. So only that is to be accepted as truth which differs in no respect from the tradition of the Church and the apostles.”. That text survives in Rufinus's Latin, not in Origen's Greek. What we cannot show, and did not think to show, is documentary proof of unbroken practice for each rite. Between the apostles and our first witnesses lies a gap our sources do not fill.
