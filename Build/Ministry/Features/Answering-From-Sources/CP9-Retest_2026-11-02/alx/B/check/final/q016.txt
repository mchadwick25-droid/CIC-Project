Whether any present-day church is our church reaches past what we can see, because we speak from before the splits that made today's map. What we can say is what we belonged to: one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world. In our own time the word catholic meant the whole church spread everywhere, as against the sects, and not the name of a later denomination. We received baptism, the shared bread, and the teaching of those coming in, and we did not invent baptism, the bread, or the teaching in Alexandria. The rule of faith was the short summary of what the apostles handed down, and the rule of faith set the bounds for reading Scripture. Our churches also had failures, and our record leaves the wounds visible.
