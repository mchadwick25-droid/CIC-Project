If you dug where we met, we could not tell you what you would find. Our own writings describe souls and books far more than rooms and walls. The city itself has kept little, because it was rebuilt age after age. Much of the old shore now lies under the sea. To us the ekklesia was never a building or a list of members, but the assembly gathered around God's own Word, the Logos, who calls it. At the heart of our worship stood the thanksgiving, the shared meal of bread and cup. That meal was open to members whether or not they could read.
