We belonged to one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, and the rule of faith, and the boundary around them was real to us. The boundary meant argument and exclusion from communion, and after Nicaea, harder tools. Whether Origen taught the restoration of all things as settled doctrine is contested, since the texts of Origen reach us through Rufinus and Jerome. Our teachers said that love of God and neighbour, and doing the Father's will, are required. Clement also told the rich that trusting they will be saved does not save them. For our own who gave way under persecution, the tradition that won here brought the repentant back in, even at the deathbed. We did not pretend the failure away, and we did not make the failed unforgivable.
