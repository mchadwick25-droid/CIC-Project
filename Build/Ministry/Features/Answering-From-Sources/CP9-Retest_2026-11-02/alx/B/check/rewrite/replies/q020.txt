We saw sin as the soul turned away from God toward lesser goods, more a wound than a debt [[alx.term.hamartia]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We saw the whole person as made for God, being restored toward the likeness of the one whose image the person bears [[alx.term.psyche]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The image of God is a mark given to every person at creation, and sin can damage the image but never destroy it [[alx.term.eikon]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In the school tradition learning was itself formation, and learning as formation weakened late, after the Origen rupture [[alx.gravity.learning-formation]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our teachers also treated doubt aimed at understanding as hunger, not as sin [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]].
