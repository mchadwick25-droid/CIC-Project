We saw sin as the soul turned away from God toward lesser goods, more a wound than a debt. We saw the whole person as made for God, being restored toward the likeness of the one whose image the person bears. The image of God is a mark given to every person at creation, and sin can damage the image but never destroy it. In the school tradition learning was itself formation, and learning as formation weakened late, after the Origen rupture. Our teachers also treated doubt aimed at understanding as hunger, not as sin.
