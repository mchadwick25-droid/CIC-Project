By Logos we meant God's own reason and speech, through whom all things were made and who became flesh in Jesus [[alx.term.logos]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Clement and Origen called the Word God without embarrassment [[alx.dw.was-jesus-god]] [[as:paraphrase]].
God's Word and Spirit are his own, not lesser gods and not created things [[alx.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Only after Nicaea was the claim that God's Word and Spirit are his own said with full clarity [[alx.dw.god]] [[as:paraphrase]].
When Arius taught that the Son was the first and highest creature, Nicaea answered with homoousios, of one substance with the Father [[alx.dw.was-jesus-god]] [[alx.term.homoousios]] [[as:paraphrase]].
Athanasius explained that the fathers reached for a word not found in scripture because the other side had swallowed every scriptural phrase with a private meaning [[alx.dw.was-jesus-god]] [[as:paraphrase]].
What we hold is the worship, the baptismal formula, and the fought-for conviction that only God can join us to God [[alx.dw.was-jesus-god]] [[as:paraphrase]].
