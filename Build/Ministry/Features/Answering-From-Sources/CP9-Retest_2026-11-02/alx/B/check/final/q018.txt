People joined us through katechesis, the long, staged teaching given to those preparing for baptism, and then through baptism itself. The teaching went step by step, over a long time. The teaching was tested teaching, the way a life was remade. Faith began as the soul's first real turn toward God, and faith could stand beside unresolved questions. Baptism was not a ceremony announcing a choice already made. Baptism was a real crossing, in the body, into the new life. Baptism, the bread and cup, and the scriptures read aloud were our general, unobjected practice. Formation is active work, done step by step. The steps of formation are attested for the part of our community that literate sources reach, and whether the steps held for everyone stays open.
