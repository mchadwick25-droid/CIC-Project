Our link to Jesus ran through what the apostles handed down: baptism, the bread and cup, the scriptures read aloud, and the rule of faith, which we received and kept visible. The rule of faith is the short summary of what the apostles handed down. We cannot show documentary proof of unbroken practice for each rite, and between the apostles and our first witnesses lies a gap our sources do not fill. We belonged to one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world. What is well attested is a line of teachers and students, while whether a formal school stood behind the line is disputed.
