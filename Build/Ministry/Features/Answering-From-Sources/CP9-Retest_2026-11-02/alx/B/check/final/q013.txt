What our record shows is danger and flight: property was seized, bishops fled, and teachers' fathers were beheaded. Four persecutions came, the Antonine, the Severan, the Decian and the Diocletianic. Origen himself was jailed and tortured under Decius, and Origen's father Leonides died a martyr under Severus. The majority who could not write left almost nothing, so how ordinary believers lived through those years we cannot say from their own words.
