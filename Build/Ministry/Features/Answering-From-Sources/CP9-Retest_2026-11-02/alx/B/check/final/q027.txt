Our teachers welcomed a person with questions, and they did not count doubt aimed at understanding as sin. We took doubt aimed at understanding for hunger. Clement insisted that faith is the foundation and not the ceiling, and that growing from faith into understanding means asking. A person with questions was expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry it alone. Origen's rule was that what the apostles delivered plainly is fixed, and everything else is open ground. Our teachers also spoke of both faith and works, and they said that faith lacks nothing and that love of God and neighbour is needed too. Depth-formation was what the school could give the few, and breadth-formation was owed to the whole worshipping community. In that tension between school and community, the school side is well attested, while the community side is thinly attested.
