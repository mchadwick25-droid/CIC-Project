We would tell them that our teachers treated doubt aimed at understanding as hunger, and did not count it as sin. Faith, as our teachers meant it, is not agreeing with a list of claims but the soul's first real turn toward God. Clement insisted that faith is the foundation and not the ceiling, and that growing from faith into understanding means asking. Origen's rule was that what the apostles delivered plainly is fixed, and everything else is open ground for asking, testing and correcting. Our doubters stood inside a praying community, and they were expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry it alone..
