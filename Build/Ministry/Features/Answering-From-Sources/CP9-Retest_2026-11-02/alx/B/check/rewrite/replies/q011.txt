Scripture is not only a record of what God once said, and a reader who treats Scripture so misses the main thing [[alx.term.scripture-as-address]] [[as:paraphrase]].

To us Scripture is the Logos speaking now, through the text, to a soul formed to hear [[alx.term.scripture-as-address]] [[as:paraphrase]].

So we read for change, and a reader of depth is transformed by the reading, not merely informed [[alx.gravity.scripture-formative]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We read at more than one level: the plain sense, and deeper senses about Christ and the soul [[alx.term.allegoria]] [[as:paraphrase]].
