Whether any present-day church is our church reaches past what we can see, because we speak from before the splits that made today's map [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What we can say is what we belonged to: one baptism, one bread, the scriptures, the rule of faith, and communion with churches across the whole world [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In our own time the word catholic meant the whole church spread everywhere, as against the sects, and not the name of a later denomination [[alx.dw.one-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We received baptism, the shared bread, and the teaching of those coming in, and we did not invent them in Alexandria [[alx.force.apostolic-tradition]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The rule of faith was the short summary of what the apostles handed down, and it set the bounds for reading Scripture [[alx.term.kanon-pisteos]] [[as:paraphrase]].
