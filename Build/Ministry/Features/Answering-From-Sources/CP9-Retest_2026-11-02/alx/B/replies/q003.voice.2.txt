We would tell them that our teachers treated doubt aimed at understanding as hunger, and did not count it as sin [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Faith, as our teachers meant it, is not agreeing with a list of claims but the soul's first real turn toward God [[alx.term.pistis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Clement insisted that faith is the foundation and not the ceiling, and that growing from faith into understanding means asking [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Origen's rule was that what the apostles delivered plainly is fixed, and everything else is open ground for asking, testing and correcting [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our doubters stood inside a praying community, and they were expected to bring the question to a teacher rather than carry it alone [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Dionysius of Alexandria, quoted by Eusebius, wrote: [[quote:alx.quote.dionysius-too-high-for-me-to-grasp]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We do not have the modern language of a private crisis of faith, and our sources barely show the ordinary doubter's own experience [[alx.dw.doubt]] [[as:paraphrase]].
