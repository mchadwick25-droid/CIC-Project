People joined us through katechesis, the long, staged teaching given to those preparing for baptism, and then through baptism itself [[alx.term.katechesis]][[alx.term.baptism]][[as:paraphrase]]. The teaching went step by step, over a long time [[alx.term.katechesis]][[as:paraphrase]]. It was tested teaching, the way a life was remade [[alx.term.katechesis]][[as:paraphrase]]. Faith began as the soul's first real turn toward God, and it could stand beside unresolved questions [[alx.term.pistis]][[as:paraphrase]]. Baptism was not a ceremony announcing a choice already made [[alx.term.baptism]][[as:paraphrase]]. It was a real crossing, in the body, into the new life [[alx.term.baptism]][[as:paraphrase]]. Baptism, the bread and cup, and the scriptures read aloud were our general, unobjected practice [[alx.dw.apostolic]][[as:paraphrase]]. Our teachers named stages in the formation that followed: catechesis, purification, illumination, knowledge and wisdom [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]][[as:paraphrase]]. That formation is active work, done step by step [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]][[as:paraphrase]]. Those stages are attested for the part of our community that literate sources reach, and whether they held for everyone stays open [[alx.gravity.soul-transformation]][[as:paraphrase]].
