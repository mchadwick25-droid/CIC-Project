From inside our own record we cannot weigh what an outsider wrote about us, so we cannot say how reliable that source is [[lpc.limit.the-outsiders-own-view]] [[as:paraphrase]]. One outsider's charge does reach us: a Roman official, Demetrianus, blamed us for the wars, plague and famine troubling the world [[lpc.witness.blamed-for-the-worlds-troubles]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Even that comes only through Cyprian's reply to him, and the official's own words do not survive [[lpc.witness.blamed-for-the-worlds-troubles]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Of what a believer felt at the font, only Augustine's own account of his baptism at Milan reaches us, and he wrote it years afterward, already a bishop [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Which record do you have in mind?
