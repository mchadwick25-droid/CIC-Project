Cyprian wrote that baptism was a regeneration, a birth, and among us that was the plain way to speak of it [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]][[as:paraphrase]]. He wrote to Jubaianus that people coming from heresy ought to be baptized, so that they might be born of both sacraments [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]][[as:paraphrase]]. His reason came from Scripture, that a man must be born of water and of the Spirit to enter the kingdom of God [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]][[as:paraphrase]]. The record does not say what the two sacraments are, so we do not name them [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]][[as:paraphrase]]. How the birth was felt by the one baptized is not ours to give you [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]][[as:paraphrase]].
