Among us, belonging asked a share of what a person owned, and for some it asked suffering [[lpc.witness.almsgiving-quenches-sin]][[lpc.witness.confessor-claim-vs-regulated-peace]][[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian wrote a whole treatise on almsgiving, much of it aimed at believers who had money [[lpc.witness.almsgiving-quenches-sin]][[as:paraphrase]]. He told them their wealth was for sharing with Christ, and he called almsgiving the ordinary remedy for sin after baptism [[lpc.witness.almsgiving-quenches-sin]][[as:paraphrase]]. Those who confessed Christ under questioning and suffered for it held that their suffering gave them standing to ask peace for others [[lpc.witness.confessor-claim-vs-regulated-peace]][[lpc.term.confessor]][[as:paraphrase]].
