What we never settled is the nearest answer: whether a baptism given outside our one church counts at all [[lpc.term.heresy]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We answered it twice, a century and a half apart, in opposite ways [[lpc.term.heresy]] [[lpc.witness.answerability-as-ground]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian's council said it does not count, and Augustine said it was given, though it did no good there [[lpc.term.heresy]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our later bishop judged the earlier answer wrong, but he never put the man who gave it outside our communion [[lpc.witness.answerability-as-ground]] [[as:paraphrase]]. A second question stayed open as well: the confessors held that their suffering gave them standing to ask peace for the lapsed, and the church answered with a name set down and examined before the congregation [[lpc.witness.confessor-claim-vs-regulated-peace]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We have never found where those two become one settled rule, and we do not expect to [[lpc.witness.confessor-claim-vs-regulated-peace]] [[as:paraphrase]].
