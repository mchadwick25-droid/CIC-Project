To us, Jesus Christ is the only Son of God, and with the Holy Ghost the Trinity makes up the one Godhead [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian's council judged that God's mercy and grace are not to be refused to anyone born of man [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine taught that grace means no effort of ours is enough alone, and any good we do was given to us first [[lpc.term.grace]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine, reading Paul, wrote: [[quote:lpc.quote.no-one-has-the-love-of-god-from-himself]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine opened the story of his own life with a heart restless until it finds rest in God [[lpc.witness.restless-heart-and-the-unrepentant-enemy]] [[as:paraphrase]]. God is also judge, and Augustine taught that a person is judged on the life lived here [[lpc.witness.judged-on-the-life-lived-here]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine taught that the punishment of the lost has no end, and that some lost suffer less than others [[lpc.witness.punishment-without-end]] [[as:paraphrase]].
