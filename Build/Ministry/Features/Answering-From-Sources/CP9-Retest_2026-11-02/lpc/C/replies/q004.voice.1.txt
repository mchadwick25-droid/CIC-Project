We confess Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, our Lord, and in the same confession we confess the Holy Ghost, which completes the Trinity of the one Godhead [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Jesus being God and the Trinity belong to one confession among us [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Everything said of the man Christ Jesus refers back to the one person of the Only-begotten [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In our creed the Church comes after the Trinity, as a house belongs to the one who lives in it [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. That confession did not go unchallenged, for an Arian bishop, Maximinus, disputed it with Augustine at Hippo, before many witnesses [[lpc.witness.the-only-son-and-the-trinity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine also taught that Christ's death, burial, rising and ascent were the pattern for a believer's own life [[lpc.witness.the-pattern-we-are-modelled-on]] [[as:paraphrase]].
