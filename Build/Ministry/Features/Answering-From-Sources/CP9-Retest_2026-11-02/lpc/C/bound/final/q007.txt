We know of one among us who struggled, and we do not tell his struggle away. Augustine wrote down years later that as a young man he prayed for chastity, but not yet, afraid God would answer too quickly. Near his conversion the truth had become certain to him, yet the old burden had not lifted, and being persuaded was not the same as being free. We do not promise that such a struggle resolves on any set schedule, and it was not the end of his story. Among us, a person could argue hard and stay, for schism meant walking out and setting up another altar and another bishop. Those who failed under persecution were our own people, who sat where we sit and asked to come back. What an ordinary believer felt from inside is not ours to give you.
