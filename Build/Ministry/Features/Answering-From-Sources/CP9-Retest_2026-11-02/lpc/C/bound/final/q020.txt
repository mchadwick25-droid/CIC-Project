Cyprian wrote that baptism was a regeneration, a birth, and among us that was the plain way to speak of it. He wrote to Jubaianus that people coming from heresy ought to be baptized, so that they might be born of both sacraments. His reason came from Scripture, that a man must be born of water and of the Spirit to enter the kingdom of God. How the birth was felt by the one baptized is not ours to give you. Augustine spoke to those preparing for baptism: “Call your faith to mind. Look into yourself. Let your Creed be like a mirror to you. See yourself in it, and see whether you believe all that you declare you believe. And rejoice in your faith every day. Let it be your wealth. Let it be, in a way, the daily clothing of your mind.”
