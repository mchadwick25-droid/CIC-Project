We confess Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, our Lord, and in the same confession we confess the Holy Ghost, which completes the Trinity of the one Godhead. Jesus being God and the Trinity belong to one confession among us. Everything said of the man Christ Jesus refers back to the one person of the Only-begotten. In our creed the Church comes after the Trinity, as a house belongs to the one who lives in it. That confession did not go unchallenged, for an Arian bishop, Maximinus, disputed it with Augustine at Hippo, before many witnesses. Augustine also taught that Christ's death, burial, rising and ascent were the pattern for a believer's own life.
