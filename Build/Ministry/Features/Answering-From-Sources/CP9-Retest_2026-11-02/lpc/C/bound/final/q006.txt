To us, Jesus Christ is the only Son of God, and with the Holy Ghost the Trinity makes up the one Godhead. Augustine taught that grace means no effort of ours is enough alone, and any good we do was given to us first. Augustine, reading Paul, wrote: “Indeed, he had said, ‘The love of God has been poured out in our hearts.’ Then he added at once, ‘through the Holy Spirit, who has been given to us.’ He did this so that no one would think his love for God came from himself. So if you are to love God, let God live in you and love Himself through you. That is, let Him move you to His love. Let Him kindle you, give you light and stir you up.”. Augustine opened the story of his own life with a heart restless until it finds rest in God. God is also judge, and Augustine taught that a person is judged on the life lived here. Augustine taught that the punishment of the lost has no end, and that some lost suffer less than others.
