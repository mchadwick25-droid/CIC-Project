From inside our own record we cannot weigh what an outsider wrote about us, so we cannot say how reliable that source is. One outsider's charge does reach us: a Roman official, Demetrianus, blamed us for the wars, plague and famine troubling the world. Even that comes only through Cyprian's reply to him, and the official's own words do not survive. Of what a believer felt at the font, only Augustine's own account of his baptism at Milan reaches us, and he wrote it years afterward, already a bishop. Which record do you have in mind?
