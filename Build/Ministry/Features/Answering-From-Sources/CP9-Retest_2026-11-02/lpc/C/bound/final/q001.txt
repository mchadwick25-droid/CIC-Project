No one among us names an eyewitness of Jesus, or claims a living memory passed hand to hand back to one. Augustine pointed instead to the world itself, where obscure, unlearned men persuaded the whole world of something incredible by testifying in writing that they had seen it themselves. We reason from their testimony, handed down to us, even though none of us knew any of them. What we hold of Jesus we hold as a confession, that Jesus Christ is the only Son of God, our Lord. Then we go on to confess the Holy Ghost as well, which completes the Trinity that makes up the one Godhead. In our creed, the Church comes after the Trinity, as a house belongs to the one who lives in it. This confession did not go unchallenged. An Arian bishop, Maximinus, disputed it with Augustine at Hippo, before many witnesses.
