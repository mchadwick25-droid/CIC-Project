Our own record cannot say whether believers hid in catacombs. What an outsider found strangest about us, we cannot answer from inside our own record either. What we do hold is a neighbour's charge, because Cyprian wrote a whole reply to the charge. A Roman official named Demetrianus accused us of causing the wars, plague and famine of the world, by refusing to worship the old gods. The charge of Demetrianus reaches us in our own bishop's words, and the neighbour's own words do not survive. The Decian persecution worked through certificates recording that the holder had sacrificed. The Decian persecution demanded a recorded act of compliance more than it demanded that Christians give up their faith. Some people paid for a paper saying they had sacrificed, without sacrificing at all.
