To us Jesus is the one whose death and rising our own life is cut to fit. We confess him as Jesus Christ, the only Son of God, our Lord. His crucifixion, burial, rising on the third day, ascension, and sitting at the Father's right hand were ordered deliberately. We crucify our own flesh, and that is his cross in us. We are buried with him in the water, and we walk in a life made new. We set our affection on what is above, our life hidden with him in God, worked out in an ordinary life.
