We read Scripture as the canon that stands far above every later letter of a bishop, Old Testament and New. Augustine said there can be no doubt or argument about what the canon contains. A bishop's letter can be corrected, and a council by a later, greater council, but Scripture cannot be corrected that way at all. Our bishops and councils still held real authority of their own, over real disputes among us. Genesis reports three days before the sun was made on the fourth, and Augustine named that difficulty honestly rather than explaining it away. Augustine wrote that we cannot understand how those first days were, and yet we must believe it. When a Manichaean pressed Augustine on the wars of Moses, Augustine answered that Moses showed obedience, not ferocity, since the command was God's own. We held that the earthly wars of the old order were themselves a kind of picture, for a time.
