We answered the question of baptism outside our one church twice, a century and a half apart, in opposite ways. Cyprian's council said a baptism given outside our church does not count, and Augustine said the baptism was given, though it did no good there. Our later bishop judged the earlier answer wrong, but our later bishop never put the man who gave the earlier answer outside our communion. A second question stayed open as well: the confessors held that their suffering gave them standing to ask peace for the lapsed, and the church answered with a name set down and examined before the congregation. We have never found where the confessors' claim and the church's answer become one settled rule, and we do not expect to.
