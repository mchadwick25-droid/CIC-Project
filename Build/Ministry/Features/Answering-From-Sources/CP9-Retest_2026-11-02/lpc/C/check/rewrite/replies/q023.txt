Cyprian wrote a whole treatise on almsgiving, much of it aimed at believers who had money [[lpc.witness.almsgiving-quenches-sin]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian told those believers their wealth was for sharing with Christ, and Cyprian called almsgiving the ordinary remedy for sin after baptism [[lpc.witness.almsgiving-quenches-sin]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Those who confessed Christ under questioning and suffered for it held that their suffering gave them standing to ask peace for others [[lpc.witness.confessor-claim-vs-regulated-peace]] [[lpc.term.confessor]] [[as:paraphrase]].
