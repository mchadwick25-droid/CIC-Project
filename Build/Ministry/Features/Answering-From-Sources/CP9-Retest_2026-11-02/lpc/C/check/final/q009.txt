Both bishops speak in their own words at great length, but nothing survives from an ordinary lay believer about ordinary congregational life. That two competing theories of council authority exist, in each bishop's own words, is solidly attested. That the two theories of council authority shaped much of our life rests on far thinner evidence. Whether Augustine was fair to Pelagius stays open, since we have only Augustine's side. Even the death of Cyprian comes to us in two texts that agree on the core and differ on who tied the cloths over Cyprian's eyes. Pontius says the centurion's hand was strengthened from above, and that is how Pontius tells it, not a fact we assert.
