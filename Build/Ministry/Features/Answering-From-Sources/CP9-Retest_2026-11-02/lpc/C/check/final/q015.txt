What our record holds is what was decided about a member who failed, and how fiercely, and by whom, because every account we have of the lapsed is a bishop's. The way back was a public road, walked in steps under the bishop, by which a failed member came home. The road had an order, the order took time, and everyone could see the road. The lapsed left nothing in their own words, so we cannot tell you why a person gave way, or whether the person judged the terms of return just.
