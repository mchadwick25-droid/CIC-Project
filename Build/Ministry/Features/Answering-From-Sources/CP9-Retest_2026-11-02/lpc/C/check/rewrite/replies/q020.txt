Cyprian wrote to Jubaianus that people coming from heresy ought to be baptized, so that the people might be born of both sacraments [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian's reason came from Scripture, that a man must be born of water and of the Spirit to enter the kingdom of God [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

How the birth was felt by the one baptized is not ours to give you [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].
