Among us, a man came to the office of bishop through the voice of the people together with God's judgment [[lpc.term.suffrage]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian named our suffrage and God's judgment side by side as the two things that put him in office [[lpc.term.suffrage]] [[lpc.story.election-of-cyprian]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Suffrage means the voice of the people, and suffrage can go against the man's own wishes [[lpc.term.suffrage]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Pontius, our bishop's own deacon, tells of the election of Cyprian in his account of Cyprian's life [[lpc.story.election-of-cyprian]] [[as:paraphrase]]:

[[story:lpc.story.election-of-cyprian]]

A bishop is given a people in one place, and the bishop preaches to the people and baptizes the people [[lpc.term.the-flock]] [[as:paraphrase]].

A bishop answers for the people given to him, and those people can stand before the bishop's seat and refuse to leave until the bishop hears them [[lpc.term.the-flock]] [[as:paraphrase]].

As we understand ourselves, a particular people is placed in a particular man's care, and the man will be asked what became of the people [[lpc.witness.answerability-as-ground]] [[as:paraphrase]].
