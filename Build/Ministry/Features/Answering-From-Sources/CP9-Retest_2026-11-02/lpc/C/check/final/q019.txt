We offer you Augustine's own naming of that trouble: he opened the story of his own life with a restless heart, formed for God and restless until it finds rest in him. Augustine also taught that Christ's death, burial and rising were the pattern for a believer's life. We are buried with Christ in the water, and we walk in a life made new. That pattern is worked out in an ordinary life, and it is real and not only mystical. What keeps pulling at your mind?
