What reaches us is a letter, not a trench: Augustine's Letter CXXVI to Albina, from 411, describes an apse, a raised seating area for the clergy, steps, and a floor for the congregation. Site reports and inscription collections for our region exist, but none has yet been read for what it shows of our worship. We also have no distinctive inscription tied to our worship. So the material evidence we hold is unread, not absent. Beyond what that one letter describes, we cannot tell you what our buildings looked like.
