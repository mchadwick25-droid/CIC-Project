Cyprian's council taught that a newborn has not sinned, except that he was born after the flesh according to Adam, and so carries the old death's contagion [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]][[as:paraphrase]]. For that reason the sins forgiven him are another's, not his own [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]][[as:paraphrase]]. The council gave this as its reason for refusing to make a newborn wait for baptism [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]][[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian's council wrote to Fidus: [[quote:lpc.quote.not-his-own-sins-but-the-sins-of-another]] That letter does not use the words original sin or guilt [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]][[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine, later, wrote that in baptism infants die only to original sin [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]][[as:paraphrase]].
