We would say a reader is missing what the hard words are for, and Augustine himself began as that reader [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]][[as:paraphrase]]. As a young man drawn to a rival teaching, he was much disturbed by the violence in our older Scriptures [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]][[as:paraphrase]]. Once he held our faith, he answered that Moses showed obedience, not ferocity, and that God in giving the command acted in righteous retribution [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]][[as:paraphrase]]. He also held that the earthly wars of the old order were a picture for a time [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]][[as:paraphrase]]. Christ's command about the other cheek asks for an inward disposition, not only a bodily act [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]][[as:paraphrase]].
