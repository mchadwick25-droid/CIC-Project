We do not know what comes after our years, and what we speak is our own life up to the siege of Hippo in 430 [[lpc.core.latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What we can say is that Augustine's church described itself as the same catholic communion that Cyprian had led [[lpc.core.latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine argued with Cyprian, but never against Cyprian's standing [[lpc.core.latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Our bishops traced their office back to the apostles by name, in two ways, by two different hands [[lpc.witness.apostolic-succession-of-bishops]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine counted back from the bishop of Rome, Anastasius, through the list to Linus, Peter's own successor [[lpc.witness.apostolic-succession-of-bishops]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian wrote a century and a half earlier and gave the principle that carries the line forward, but no list of names [[lpc.witness.apostolic-succession-of-bishops]] [[lpc.limit.apostolic-origin-undefended]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine also traced infant baptism and the refusal to rebaptize to the apostles by name [[lpc.limit.apostolic-origin-undefended]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Neither bishop traces catechesis, the road back, or the teaching before the water to the apostles by name [[lpc.limit.apostolic-origin-undefended]] [[as:paraphrase]].
