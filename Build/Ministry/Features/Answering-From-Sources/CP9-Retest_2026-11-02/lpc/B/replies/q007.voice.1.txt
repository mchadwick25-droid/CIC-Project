Yes, and one of our own knew a trouble close to yours, before he was received among us [[lpc.witness.grant-me-chastity-but-not-yet]] [[as:paraphrase]]. As a young man, Augustine prayed for chastity and continency, but not yet, afraid that God would answer him before he was ready to let go [[lpc.witness.grant-me-chastity-but-not-yet]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Later he wrote that the truth had become certain to him, yet the old burden had not lifted, and being persuaded was not the same as being free [[lpc.witness.grant-me-chastity-but-not-yet]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We do not tell that struggle away, or promise it ends on any set schedule [[lpc.witness.grant-me-chastity-but-not-yet]] [[as:paraphrase]]. It was real in one of our own, and it was not the end of his story [[lpc.witness.grant-me-chastity-but-not-yet]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine said this to the people who came to hear him: [[quote:lpc.quote.longing-expectation-is-a-prayer-for-me]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What an ordinary believer felt from inside is almost never ours to give you, apart from the tears Augustine shed at his own baptism, which he told years afterward [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].
