Among us, Cyprian taught that none are saved outside the Church [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian said it of heretics, people who confessed Christ but stood outside the Church [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian wrote: [[quote:lpc.quote.no-salvation-out-of-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In his book on the Church's unity, Cyprian called anyone who leaves the Church a stranger, profane, and an enemy [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian gave Noah's ark as his picture of this [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. To Demetrianus, a pagan who blamed and persecuted us, Cyprian wrote that an ever-burning Gehenna will burn up the condemned, with no respite or end to their torments [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Yet Cyprian pressed that same Demetrianus to turn while he lived, since for anyone still in this world no repentance is too late [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Heresy raised one sharp question among us: whether a baptism given outside our one church counts at all [[lpc.term.heresy]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Schism is the tear made when someone leaves the church's bishop, clergy and people, and sets up another altar and another bishop [[lpc.term.schism]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Cyprian's words went to heretics, to those who split from the Church, and to a pagan who heard and would not believe [[lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church]] [[as:paraphrase]].
