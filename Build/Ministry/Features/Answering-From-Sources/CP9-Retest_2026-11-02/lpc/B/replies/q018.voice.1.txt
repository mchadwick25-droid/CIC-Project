Among us, a person was taught first and washed afterward [[lpc.term.catechesis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We told the person what they were joining before they joined it [[lpc.term.catechesis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. The person said the creed back, and then we baptized them [[lpc.term.catechesis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine wrote a guide to this teaching, and he wrote works to teach those preparing for baptism [[lpc.term.catechesis]] [[lpc.gravity.preaching-and-catechesis]] [[as:paraphrase]]. We baptized infants too, a custom held everywhere and instituted by no council [[lpc.witness.baptism-traced-to-the-apostles]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine held that it was rightly believed to have come down by apostolic authority [[lpc.witness.baptism-traced-to-the-apostles]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine would not baptize again someone already baptized among heretics, while Cyprian's council held that a baptism given outside our church did not count [[lpc.witness.baptism-traced-to-the-apostles]] [[lpc.term.heresy]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine wrote: [[quote:lpc.quote.peter-may-baptize-but-this-is-he-that-baptizeth]] [[as:paraphrase]]. In 428 or 429, Augustine told Honoratus, a bishop in Mauritania, that ministers must not leave behind people who depend on them in danger [[lpc.witness.the-dying-and-the-pastors-post]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Then the dying who crowd into the church can ask for baptism and be baptized [[lpc.witness.the-dying-and-the-pastors-post]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Neither Augustine nor Cyprian traces the teaching before the water to the apostles by name [[lpc.limit.apostolic-origin-undefended]] [[as:paraphrase]].
