The first is that the confessors' claim to ask peace for the lapsed and the bishop's regulated road back never became one settled rule. Their suffering was real, and so was what it carried. The second is the font, for our bishops answered the question of a baptism given outside the church twice, in opposite ways. Our later bishop judged the earlier answer wrong, yet never put the man who gave it outside our communion. We do not pretend that holding the bond makes either question easy.
