We met a failure with a public road back, walked in steps under the bishop. There is an order to that road, the order takes time, and everyone can see it. The Decian persecution demanded a recorded act of compliance, and a person could get a certificate by sacrificing or by paying to have it recorded. In one year many lapsed and many confessors stood in one congregation, and both groups still belonged, still in the room. The confessors issued written requests that named lapsed persons to be received back. Cyprian wrote that thousands of certificates were being given every day, against the law of the Gospel. Both sides spoke in earnest, and that was the difficulty. Cyprian wrote: “I think we must come to the help of our brothers in this way. Some have received letters of peace from the martyrs, and may be helped by the martyrs' standing with God. If misfortune and the danger of sickness seize them, they should not wait for me to be present. They should be able to confess their sin before any presbyter who is there. If no presbyter can be found and death begins to draw near, they may confess before even a deacon. Then hands are laid on them for repentance. In this way they may come to the Lord with the peace the martyrs asked for them in their letters to us.”. In 428 or 429, Augustine wrote to Honoratus, a bishop in Mauritania, that when the ministers are at their posts in extreme danger, some are baptized and others reconciled to the Church. We can tell you what was decided about a person, and how carefully, and by whom. We cannot tell you what an ordinary believer felt at the font or in the congregation.
