Augustine traced two of our practices to the apostles by name, infant baptism and the refusal to rebaptize, though no apostle wrote either down. His reasoning was that a custom this old and this universal, with no council behind it, is rightly believed to come from the apostles. The office of bishop is traced too, because Augustine counted the line of Rome's bishops back to Peter. Cyprian gave no list of his own, but he stated the principle that carries that line forward. Neither bishop traces catechesis, the road back, or the teaching before the water to the apostles by name. That gap is real, and we do not close it. The oneness of the bishop's office is an argument Cyprian drew from what the Lord said to Peter, not a second named succession.
