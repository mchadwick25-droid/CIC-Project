The council of Cyprian and his colleagues gave the following as its reason for refusing to make a newborn wait for baptism [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]] [[as:paraphrase]].

[[quote:lpc.quote.not-his-own-sins-but-the-sins-of-another]]

The letter of the council to Fidus does not use the words original sin or guilt [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Augustine, later, wrote that in baptism infants die only to original sin [[lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another]] [[as:paraphrase]].
