Our own record cannot say whether believers hid in catacombs. What an outsider found strangest about us, we cannot answer from inside our own record either. What we do hold is a neighbour's charge, because Cyprian wrote a whole reply to the charge. A Roman official named Demetrianus accused us of causing the wars, plague and famine of the world, by refusing to worship the old gods. The charge of Demetrianus reaches us in our own bishop's words, and the neighbour's own words do not survive. Rome ordered a sacrifice, and some people paid for a paper saying they had made it, without sacrificing at all. Cyprian called the paper saying a person had sacrificed a denial, since the hands of those who held the paper were clean but their conscience was not.
