To us, Jesus's death and rising are the pattern our own life is modelled on, in reality and not only in mystery. We crucify our own flesh, and that is Jesus's cross in us. We are buried with Jesus in the water, and that is Jesus's burial in us. We walk in a life made new, and that is Jesus's rising in us. We set our affection on what is above, and that is Jesus's ascension and Jesus's sitting at God's right hand, worked out in an ordinary life.
