Cyprian wrote to Jubaianus about people who come to the church from heresy. Cyprian held that the people ought to be baptized, so that divine regeneration would prepare them for the kingdom of God. Cyprian rested the argument on Scripture, on the saying that a man must be born of water and of the Spirit to enter the kingdom of God. So for Cyprian, our bishop, baptism was a regeneration, a birth. What a baptized infant or a catechumen understood by the birth, our record does not give. We can tell you what was decided at the font, and by whom, but not what an ordinary believer felt standing at the font.
