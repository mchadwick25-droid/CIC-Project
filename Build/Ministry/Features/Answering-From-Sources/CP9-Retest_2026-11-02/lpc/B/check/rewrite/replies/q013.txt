The charge of Demetrianus reaches us in our own bishop's words, and the neighbour's own words do not survive [[lpc.witness.blamed-for-the-worlds-troubles]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Rome ordered a sacrifice, and some people paid for a paper saying they had made it, without sacrificing at all [[lpc.term.libelli]] [[as:paraphrase]].
