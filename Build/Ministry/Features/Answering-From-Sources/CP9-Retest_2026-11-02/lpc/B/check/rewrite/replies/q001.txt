No one among us names an eyewitness of Jesus, or claims a living memory passed hand to hand back to one [[lpc.limit.no-chain-of-witnesses]] [[as:paraphrase]]. What reached us was the testimony of the apostles, written down, and we reason from the apostles' testimony even though none of us knew any of the apostles [[lpc.limit.no-chain-of-witnesses]] [[as:paraphrase]]. Augustine pointed to the world itself, where obscure, unlearned men persuaded everyone of something incredible by testifying in writing that they had seen it themselves [[lpc.limit.no-chain-of-witnesses]] [[as:paraphrase]]. For the path of our bishops, Augustine counted back through the bishops of Rome to Peter, and Cyprian gave the principle but no list of names [[lpc.witness.apostolic-succession-of-bishops]] [[as:paraphrase]].
