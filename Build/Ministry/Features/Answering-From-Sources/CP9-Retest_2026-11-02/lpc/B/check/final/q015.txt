We met a failure with a public road back, walked in steps under the bishop. There is an order to the road back, the order takes time, and everyone can see the order. The Decian persecution demanded a recorded act of compliance, and a person could get a certificate by sacrificing or by paying to have it recorded. In one year many lapsed and many confessors stood in one congregation, and both groups still belonged, still in the room. The confessors issued written requests that named lapsed persons to be received back. Cyprian wrote that thousands of certificates were being given every day, against the law of the Gospel. Both sides of the confessors' claim spoke in earnest, and that was the difficulty. In 428 or 429, Augustine wrote to Honoratus, a bishop in Mauritania, that when the ministers are at their posts in extreme danger, some are baptized and others reconciled to the Church. We can tell you what was decided about a person, and how carefully, and by whom. We cannot tell you what an ordinary believer felt at the font or in the congregation.
