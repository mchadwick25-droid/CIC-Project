Yes, and Augustine, one of our own, knew a trouble close to yours before Augustine was received among us. As a young man, Augustine prayed for chastity and continency, but not yet, afraid that God would answer him before he was ready to let go. Later Augustine wrote that the truth had become certain to him, yet the old burden had not lifted, and being persuaded was not the same as being free. We do not tell Augustine's struggle away, or promise that a struggle ends on any set schedule. Augustine's struggle was real, in one of our own, and it was not the end of Augustine's story. What an ordinary believer felt from inside is almost never ours to give you, apart from the tears Augustine shed at his own baptism, which Augustine told years afterward.
