No one among us names an eyewitness of Jesus, or claims a living memory passed hand to hand back to one. What reached us was the testimony of the apostles, written down, and we reason from the apostles' testimony even though none of us knew any of the apostles. Augustine pointed to the world itself, where obscure, unlearned men persuaded everyone of something incredible by testifying in writing that they had seen it themselves. For the path of our bishops, Augustine counted back through the bishops of Rome to Peter, and Cyprian gave the principle but no list of names.
