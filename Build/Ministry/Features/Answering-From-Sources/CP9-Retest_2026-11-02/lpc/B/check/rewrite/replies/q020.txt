Cyprian wrote to Jubaianus about people who come to the church from heresy [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian held that the people ought to be baptized, so that divine regeneration would prepare them for the kingdom of God [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Cyprian rested the argument on Scripture, on the saying that a man must be born of water and of the Spirit to enter the kingdom of God [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

So for Cyprian, our bishop, baptism was a regeneration, a birth [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[as:paraphrase]].

What a baptized infant or a catechumen understood by the birth, our record does not give [[lpc.witness.baptism-as-new-birth]] [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].

We can tell you what was decided at the font, and by whom, but not what an ordinary believer felt standing at the font [[lpc.limit.ordinary-interior-life]] [[as:paraphrase]].
