As a young man drawn to a rival teaching, Augustine was much disturbed by the violence in our older Scriptures [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Once Augustine held our faith, Augustine answered that Moses showed obedience, not ferocity, and that God in giving the command acted in righteous retribution [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]] [[as:paraphrase]].

Augustine also held that the earthly wars of the old order were a picture for a time [[lpc.witness.violence-commanded-not-cruel]] [[as:paraphrase]].
