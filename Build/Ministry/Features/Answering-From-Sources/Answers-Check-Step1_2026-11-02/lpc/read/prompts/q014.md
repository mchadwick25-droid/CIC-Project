What it means: Augustine says feeding the Lord's flock is the duty of love, and warns against feeding it as one's own for glory, power or gain.
Must not be used for: a claim that the passage gives Peter authority over the other apostles or the whole Church; a claim that it names bishops, priests or any rank; a claim that it quotes the words 'Feed my sheep', which come just after it
The passage:
Let it be the duty of love to feed the Lord’s flock, if it was the mark of fear to deny the Shepherd. Some feed the flock of Christ with this aim: to have the sheep as their own, and not as Christ’s. They are shown to love themselves, and not Christ. They act from the wish to boast, or to hold power, or to make gain. They do not act from the love of obeying, serving and pleasing God.
