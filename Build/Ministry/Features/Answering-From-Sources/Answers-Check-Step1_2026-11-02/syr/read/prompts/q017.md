A sentence bound to several records is judged against all of them taken together: it is supported only if every assertion in it is stated in those records together or follows closely from them together; whatever they together do not support is not supported. A sentence that only frames the answer and claims nothing is supported. A word that refers back to something (he, they, "them", "the others", "it") must name, through the sentence before it, the very person or thing the records say it of; if the records cannot settle it, or the sentence before points to something else, it is not supported (unresolved_referent). Each record may carry a MUST NOT ASSERT line: a sentence that asserts what any bound record says must not be asserted is not supported, and when a sentence joins content from more than one record it must stay within the narrower scope of them (years, persons, cases covered), the MUST NOT ASSERT line winning.
