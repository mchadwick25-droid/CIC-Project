## 3. What the sources can carry, part by part

**1. Why they met.** Carried: Marcellinus's two edicts and the Catholics' replies (Letters 128-129, CSEL 44 1609, 1828; summarized in npnf104 10433-10445); the Breviculus (CSEL 66061; French 1749-1762); Mansi's summary (1425-1440); Possidius 13 (2511, 2545).

**2. Who came and who was missing.** Carried: the roll call and the mandates in the Gesta (Mansi 2633-3001; PL 11 from 117271), the Donatist disputants named (Mansi 1425-1440: Primianus, Petilianus, Emeritus, Montanus, Gaudentius, Adeodatus, Protasius), the count (Mansi 3247; the don contested record on 286/279). Missing: the Donatist laity, barred by the edict; absent bishops noted in the subscriptions.

**3. What was argued.** Carried in the Donatist bishops' own recorded words and the Catholics' (section 4), and in Augustine's summary (Breviculus; PL 43 40419-42543).

**4. What was decided, quoted from the source.** Carried: "SENTENTIA COGNITORIS" in Mansi (26585 onward); CTh 16.5.52 (7200) as the law that followed. PL 11's acts break off before it, so Mansi is the only vendored witness to the verdict's text.

**5. Who disagreed and why.** Carried; see section 4.

**6. The legacy.** Carried: Ad Donatistas post collationem (PL 43 42982), the bribery charge and Augustine's answer (CSEL 60650-60656), Gesta cum Emerito of 418 (72034), Contra Gaudentium (73262), CTh 16.5.52 and 16.5.55 (7200, 7220), Possidius (2545). Monceaux VII for the modern reading (38913-39034).

**7. Stories from inside the event's own sources.** Carried: the refusal of seats and the wheat-and-tares exchange already read by the don build (acts 144-145, 265-271 in PL 11; 130257-130363); Emeritus's later silence at Caesarea (Gesta cum Emerito, 72034); the sentence given late in the day (Mansi 1437-1440 as the Donatists' complaint).

**8. How each tradition remembers it.** See section 6.

## 4. The dissenting side in its own words

- **In the Gesta.** Petilian of Constantina (PL 11 117590, 118192, 118247, 125513; Mansi 8275, 9061, 9125, 9225), Emeritus of Caesarea (PL 11 117271, 129616, 130257, 130354; Mansi 9443), Adeodatus (130363), Primian of Carthage (Mansi 1514), the Donatist mandatum (Mansi 3001). These are a notary's transcript, not an opponent's quotation: the strongest Donatist self-testimony the Library holds.
- **After the conference, only in Augustine.** The Donatist pamphlets against the verdict and the charge that Marcellinus was bribed survive only as Augustine answers them (Ad Donatistas post collationem, PL 43 42982; CSEL preface 60650-60656). Gaudentius of Thamugadi's two letters survive inside Contra Gaudentium (CSEL 73262-73276). Emeritus's words of 418 are inside Gesta cum Emerito (72034).
- **Missing.** No independent Donatist narrative of 411; no Donatist text on the verdict apart from quotation; the Liber genealogus, the one Donatist chronicle held, gives nothing on the conference by grep.

## 5. Legends

None known, and none found. Checked: (a) the charge that Marcellinus was bribed and gave sentence at night is a contemporary Donatist accusation, not a later legend, and is held (Mansi 1437-1440; CSEL 60650-60656): tag it as a contested claim; (b) the bishop count is a contested number already modelled in `don.contested.bishop-count-411-conference`; (c) Marcellinus's later veneration as a martyr (executed 413) is memory, carried faintly by CTh 16.5.55 "spectabilis memoriae" (7220) and Augustine's letters after his death; no vendored text carries a legend about it. Prosper's chronicle was searched for a 411 entry and none was located in the body text.

## 9. Gaps and escalations

- The verdict's text is held only in Mansi 4 (26585), in a long-s OCR; PL 11 breaks off before it. Quotation of the sentence needs the Mansi page image.
- PL 11's OCR is the corpus's worst for a text of this weight (the don build's own finding); every Donatist quotation should be cross-read against Mansi 4.
- No Donatist account of the conference survives independent of the Gesta and Augustine; part 5 is strong on the day itself and dependent on the opponent afterwards.
- No non-Latin memory of the event was located. This is the state of the evidence.
