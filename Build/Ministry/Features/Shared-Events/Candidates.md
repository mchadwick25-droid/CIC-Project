# Shared-events candidate list

Candidate councils, gatherings and confessions that involved or changed more than one tradition, earliest church to present. Read-only research from the repo; nothing was edited and no paid API was called. 77 candidates in 10 era tables, date order. Dates marked "?" are not firmly known to me; names and dates are general knowledge, every repo fact comes from the files named.

## How to read the tables

- **census movements**: ids from `cic-website/data/world-census.json`. A trailing `*` means that movement's own census text (or one of its site stories) names the event. Ids without `*` are my placing by date, region or doctrine, not something the census says. The line after the ids says whether the event is in an edge note or an era keyEvent, and names any census site story already about it. Only 69 edges exist and most are early, so most events are in no edge.
- **built traditions**: worlds with `records/<code>/story/` files: alx, cappadocian, desert, don, gallic, hal, ijc, lpc, pahc, rzg, syr, witt (fix is the fixture). Worlds hus, jes, meth, obel, rcg, wsyr, aec exist but have no story records yet, so they count as not built. Record kinds: (S) story, (W) doctrinal_witness, (C) contested_claim, (F) world_front. "Telling it" means a story record; the rest only draw on the event.
- **vendored sources**: files in `cic/texts/`, checked against `cic/corpus-map/_staging/` and by grep. "Strong/Good" means acts, canons, creed, or a contemporary narrative is on hand. "Thin/Partial" means indirect or a later account. "none vendored" means exactly that.
- **episodes in the sources**: narrated scenes, with file and rough line number. I sampled with grep and did not read whole files; line numbers are approximate. "canons/text only" means the sources hold no narrative; "none vendored" means no sources.
- **known legends**: a popular story attached to the event, then what the vendored files show about it. "-" means I know of none. Where a file does not mention a legend I say so; I have not given origins from memory, and no vendored file was found that records where any of these legends first appears (except where noted).
- **queue**: `Build/worlds/_cross-world/download-queue-seed.yaml` (rows numbered from 0 in file order), `DOWNLOAD-QUEUE.md`, `WANTS-REGISTER.md`. Mostly "none": the queue is dominated by lpc, jes and hus items.
