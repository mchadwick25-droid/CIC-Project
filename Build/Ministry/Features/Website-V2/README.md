# Website V2 — the design sandbox workstream

**Charter (Mark, 2026-09-01):** upgrade the website for growth,
accessibility, clear storytelling, easy feature access, and
professional top-tier design — through "a divergent, struggle and
convergent process," not "a jump to decisions straight line."
Quality and rigor take precedence over cost. Good professional design
as an expert is key.

## The sandbox

- **This branch (`claude/website-v2-sandbox`) IS the sandbox.** Merges
  to main auto-deploy to the live site, so nothing merges until Mark
  rules a converged, built, read increment ready to ship.
- `Sandbox/` is the workshop: divergent mockups, dead ends, competing
  directions, struggle artifacts. Messiness is allowed HERE and only
  here — it is never compiled, served, or merged into deliverable
  docs. Everything outside `Sandbox/` follows the standing file
  discipline.
- Deliverables that leave this folder (Design V2 doc, storyboard,
  decision log entries, site copy) follow every standing rule:
  register bar, draft-and-approve, dated decisions.

## The funnel

D0 Immersion → D1 DIVERGE (3–5 genuinely different directions; no
converging allowed) → D2 STRUGGLE (adversarial reviews as files;
directions are torn down, defended, killed or strengthened;
hybridization only after critique) → Mark picks → D3 CONVERGE (Design
V2 + storyboard, frozen by Mark) → D4 BUILD (increments, each read by
Mark before it ships).

## Model routing (Mark's ruling, 2026-09-01)

Sonnet coordinates, follows designs to build, keeps the ledger. Opus
does the critical reviews. Fable does deep research, complex planning,
and design synthesis. Briefs to subagents are pointers, not summaries.
Mindful of cost; quality and rigor take precedence.

Decisions land in `Decision-Log.md` here (workstream-local), with
cross-links to `Build/Ministry/Technology/CiC_FrontEnd_Decision_Log.md` for
anything that binds beyond this workstream.
