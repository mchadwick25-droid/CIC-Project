The way in

Start with your question

Write it in your own words. It stays with you — nothing is sent ahead. You will ask it yourself, in the room, of whichever voice you choose.draft

You will be in conversation with an AI system — a voice built from one tradition's own letters and records. It shows its sources as it speaks, and it will tell you where its record runs out.draft

Or begin with one of these — questions people bring

  • I grew up being told doubt was sin. Was there room among your people for doubt?
  • Did any of you ever want to leave?
  • I pray and nothing happens. Did your people know that silence?
  • The people who taught me the faith turned out to be hypocrites. Did that happen among you?
  • Why does God allow suffering like this? Where was he when it happened to your people — and to mine?
  • I want to believe in Jesus, but I can't. What would you say to me?

These are from the project's own set of questions, the same set every voice is prepared to meet. Any of them, or your own, is asked in the room.draft · seed-status questions

Who's at the table

Who would you like to ask?

Seven Christian traditions from the Early Church and Imperial Church eras, each with one voice that speaks for it from its own record. Ask any of them. You can bring the same question to more than one.draft

This is a pilot. We're intentionally looking for a limited number of participants across four perspectives — general, pastor or teacher, academic, and anyone re-examining their faith.

Because of cost, we're asking each participant to keep to about five conversations for now — we can't enforce this yet, only ask.

Before you sit down

This is an AI system. Here is what holds it to the record, and what is still unfinished.

What holds it

  • Locked to sources. A voice is built last, from a completed record of one tradition's own letters, sermons, and primary sources, through a ten-step, review-gated build — and speaks only from that record.
  • Shows its confidence. Every claim carries how well it is attested, in the same five words inside every conversation, never adjusted for who is asking.
  • Watched while it speaks. Every conversation is watched in real time for a softened truth, an invented detail, or knowledge the voice shouldn't have — and corrected before it reaches you.
  • Witness, never recruitment. A voice of its tradition, in real conversation — it does not pretend to be a person. What it means for you is yours to own and share.

What is unfinished, said plainly

  • Independent academic review is the standard we are building toward. It has not begun; an advisory board is being formed and needs funding and volunteers.
  • Every conversation runs on one plain voice. The modes designed for a pastor, a scholar, or someone re-examining faith have not shipped.
  • Later centuries are planned, not built. What is live is the Church's own first four hundred years; each later era is its own careful build.
  • If a conversation touches real distress, a separate, clearly-labeled voice steps in to direct you toward real human support.

We are committed to representing each Christian movement in full — its testimony, beautiful and hard alike.

We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. What it means for you is yours to own and share.
