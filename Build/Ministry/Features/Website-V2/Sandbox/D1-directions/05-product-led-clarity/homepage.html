Before you sit down

Three things to know

1

This is an AI system.

Each Representative is a voice built from its tradition's own letters, sermons, and records, through a ten-step, review-gated build. It speaks only from that record, and never pretends to be a person.

Independent academic review is the standard we're building toward. It isn't complete yet.

2

It shows its work.

Sources and confidence stay visible inside every answer. Every conversation is watched in real time for a softened truth, an invented detail, or knowledge the voice shouldn't have — and corrected before it reaches you.

Where the historical record is thin, we let the silence stand.

3

Witness, never recruitment.

We are committed to representing each Christian movement in full — its testimony, beautiful and hard alike.

We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. What it means for you is yours to own and share.

Get involved

Help us open the door a little wider

Every conversation has a real cost, turn by turn — and multi-voice Table conversations increase that cost, since each additional voice generates its own response. That's not the whole picture, either: keeping this running also means hosting, storage, and the ongoing work of maintaining it — and as we grow, adding more Christian traditions and reaching more people, those costs grow with us. We've worked diligently to bring what we can down, so the door can stay open to as many people as possible. Keeping it that way is where we need your help.
