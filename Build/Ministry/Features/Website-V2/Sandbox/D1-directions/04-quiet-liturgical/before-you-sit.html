I · What this is

You will be in conversation with an AI system.

Each Representative is a built voice: made from one Christian tradition's own letters, sermons, and records, and speaking from those and from nothing else. It is a conversation with a tradition's witness, not with a person.

You can ask it anything. It answers from what its tradition left behind, shows you where each answer comes from, and says so when the record runs out.

II · What it is built from

Months of review-gated work, one tradition at a time.

Each Christian tradition is built by going through a disciplined ten-step process. We gather and weigh its own letters, sermons, records, and validated sources. We research its own vocabulary, and the internal and external forces that shaped its beliefs and convictions. Every claim is checked against those sources directly and labeled for how well it's attested. External academic review is the standard we're building toward for every tradition — not yet complete, since it isn't funded yet. Only then, last, is a representative voice created out of that finished, self-contained record. It speaks only from the tradition's own letters, sermons, and primary sources.

Where the historical record is thin, we let the silence stand.

III · What it will not claim

It bears witness, and leaves the rest to you.

We are committed to representing each Christian movement in full — its testimony, beautiful and hard alike.

We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way. What it means for you is yours to own and share.

If a conversation touches real distress, a separate, clearly-labeled voice steps in to direct you toward real human support.

Nothing here is finished. What is unfinished, we say plainly.

Silence is kept.
