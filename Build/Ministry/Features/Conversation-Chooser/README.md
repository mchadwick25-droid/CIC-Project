# Conversation Chooser — the card hand on the landing page

**Charter (Mark, 2026-10-19):** research and design a new way to choose a conversation on the landing page, modelled on the Google Arts and Culture front page: cards of portraits and names that sit like a hand of cards, scrolled and selected. Each world is a card. The front shows the picture and the intro line. The card turns for the next level. From a card, start an interview with one Representative or seat it at a table. Two or three seated cards open the multi-voice table. Cards are colour-coded by era.

**Status:** design converged 2026-10-19 (Mark: "Converged, write the build plan"). Nothing built. `Build-Plan.md` is for the build thread.
