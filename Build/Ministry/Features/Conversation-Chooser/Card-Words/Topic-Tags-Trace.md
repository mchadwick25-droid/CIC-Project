# Topic tags and alternate names — audit trace — 2026-10-20

Each live world's `card.topics` in `records/worlds/<code>.yaml` lists the everyday words (from a shared list of 35) on which that world's own records hold a view of their own. This file records which record supports each tag. The record ids are kept here and not in the public census, so a visitor's search shows the note and the reason, not the source. Sources come out in conversation.

## How the tags were made

1. The shared list of 35 words was drafted for a visitor typing everyday words (Mark chose a shared list over per-world phrases, 2026-10-20).
2. One Sonnet 5.5 drafter per world tagged the words the world's records are substantial on, with a note of at most 14 words (217 tags).
3. Three Opus 5.5 reviewers checked every tag against its traced record under a stricter test from Mark: the world must hold a significant view of its own, not a mention. They kept, fixed or dropped each tag and rewrote notes that claimed more than the record. 184 tags remain.
4. A script checked every final tag: the word is on the list, no duplicates, every note is 14 words or fewer, every trace is a real record file.
5. Mark's call on 2026-10-20: distinction, not perfection. A word held by many worlds is kept, because each note says how that world differs. Converged, then written to the registry.

## Alternate names

Names come only from the world's own records, each labelled own, outsider, scholarly or later, with the record's own caution. "Sacramentarians" was left out for Zurich and Geneva because it comes from a Wittenberg record. Everyday names the records do not carry (Lutheran, Calvinist, Church of the East) are not in the registry. They are for search-only keywords, which Mark approves one by one; none is written yet.
