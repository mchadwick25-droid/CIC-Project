# Build plan — the conversation chooser — 2026-10-19

Deliverable 4 of 4, for a later build thread. Written after Mark converged (see `Decision-Log.md`, "Converged", 2026-10-19). Nothing here is built. Line numbers are from `main` at commit `97f80128` and must be re-checked before editing.

## 1. What is being built

One component, the **chooser**, mounted in two places on the public site:

- On the landing page, in place of the three era strips of chairs under "Who would you like to talk with?".
- On the Build a Table page, in place of the seat ring and its side panel, with the seat tray open.

The component has four parts: the **hand** (five cards dealt from the deck), the **deck** controls (era chips, a search box, the written count, "Deal another hand", "See all"), the **tray** (seats, count, "Go to the table"), and the **list** (the same cards as a vertical list, used on phones and under reduced motion). Every action resolves to the existing `talk.html?worlds=<census ids>&mode=interview|table&from=<page>` link, so the room's welcome, disclosure and safety handling are untouched.

## 3. What is deliberately not built

- The fan of all eleven (direction A). It hides half the cards and the evidence runs against it.
- Any automatic rotation, autoplay or scroll-linked motion.
- A flip on hover alone.
- Colour as the only era cue.
- Any new words before the room's own welcome and disclosure.

## 5. Open items the build thread settles first

1. **Why a world matched.** A search for "Augustine" matches Donatism through its `voices` text, because Augustine wrote against that church. The result must say so, for example "Augustine: argued against this church", taken from the first sentence of the matching voice string. Checked: the voice strings do not carry that sentence in every world (Decision-Log, 2026-10-19 re-check), so add a `card.matches` list to the registry.
2. **Where the story teasers sit.** Decided 2026-10-19 (Mark): above the hand. Landing page order is headline, story teasers, the hand with its deck controls, then the three doors as one line each. The build mounts the chooser below the teasers' section once that section exists; until the story pool is written, the chooser mounts where the chairs section is today.
3. **The era tones.** Measured 2026-10-19; all three clear the thresholds (VII narrowly).
4. **A deck of ten.** Closed 2026-10-19: Mark keeps Donatism as a live conversation, so the deck is eleven. (The hand and Shuffle would work unchanged at ten if a world ever leaves.)
5. **The landing page's own tab order.** Today thirty-three stops for eleven chairs; the target is one named stop per card plus the controls.
6. **Per-portrait crop point.** The prototype crops every portrait at one point, anchored to the top of the head, and uses the 192 px thumbnails. The build uses the full portraits and sets the crop point per world in the registry `card:` block, so each face sits in the frame.
