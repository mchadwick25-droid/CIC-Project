## 1. Aligned — and in several places better than the brief asked

The execution is faithful, and twice it improved on its instructions:

- **R1–R3 are exact.** Opening ±50° in all three forms (verified: dasharray 260/100
  offset −50; master arc 50°→310°). Dot r 6.5 at (88.5, 50), outside the ring in
  every state and every file. Madder `#A13E2B` everywhere on light grounds.
- **R4 is genuinely pen-true — verified, not vibes.** I measured the modulated
  master's stroke around the ring: thin ≈8.5–8.7 at visual 1–2 o'clock *and*
  7–8 o'clock, full ≈11.5–11.8 at 10–11 and 4–5 o'clock, ~10.2 at the cardinal
  points — a smooth old-style oblique stress with the thin axis exactly where a
  broad-nib pen held at the humanist angle thins. No fake texture, no faux
  roughness: modulation without ornament, which is precisely what "pen logic, not
  fake brushwork" meant.
- **The motion implementation preserves the grammar in full** — V0.8 sequence,
  single-ended build from the lower lip (doorway completes last), phantom-dot trap
  fixed, reduced-motion complete, binding rules carried as comments in the file
  itself so future editors inherit them.
- **The usage sheet is the one-pager the brief ordered** and correctly carries the
  public sentence, clearspace, minimums, the never-list, and the chair's
  illustration role.
- **The never-list sweep passes** across all assets: no third element anywhere; the
  opening never narrowed, re-aimed, or cursor-reactive; the dot never inside;
  nothing spins, bounces, or pulses.
- **The palette/typography convergence already ruled stands:** §2.1–2.2 went from
  proposal to brand record without a value changing; graphite-as-Facilitator and
  madder-vs-gold-leaf separation are information design serving Conviction 5.
- **The copy strings reviewed are almost uniformly excellent** (details §3) — the
  tour invitation card in particular (*"…as Justin describes it — every stop names
  its source"* + a final "Not now") is a model of consent-explicit, source-anchored
  invitation.

## 2. Rulings (the brand authority's calls, made now, logged)

1. **The asymmetric lips are a feature — keep them.** A real pen exiting near the
   thin axis cuts a thin upper lip (~9.1) and near the thick axis a full lower
   (~11.8). Correcting them to symmetry would *break* the pen logic R4 asked for.
2. **The two-form practice is ENDORSED as brand policy — "optical forms."** Type
   founding has cut display and text optical sizes for five centuries; the
   modulated master is the display cut, the geometric stroke the text cut, one
   mark in two drawings by size domain. Conditions (already in the sheet, now
   ruled): modulated never below 48px; geometric is also the sole animation form;
   the two forms never appear in one composition.
3. **The favicon is still "Arriving."** Stroke 14 / dot r 8 / mark shifted left is
   optical compensation, not drift: same gap, same relation, same story. One
   condition for the raster export (R5, build thread): **test the dot at 16px
   specifically** — at 8/100 of the tile it renders ≈1.3px, and a dot that
   vanishes takes the meaning with it. Per-size raster adjustment of the dot
   (r 9–10 at 16px only) is sanctioned in advance if needed — that is refining
   the drawing, not the decisions.
4. **The adjacency guard is ENDORSED as written:** the chair illustration never
   sits beside the mark in one composition. Two table-metaphors read as two
   brands; the guard is exactly the one-mark discipline the benchmark demanded.
5. **The dark-register madder `#CB6E52` is SANCTIONED, with its reason recorded:**
   the never-brightened rule guards against alert-red drift *within a register*;
   lifting madder for contrast on the leather-dark ground is token practice, the
   same move as lifting the ink to `#EDE5D6`. It is a contrast adaptation, not a
   brightening violation — and this sanction closes the question before anyone
   relitigates it.
6. **The role-pigment story is ENDORSED into the brand record — with a
   telling-place rule.** Gold-leaf for the witnesses, lapis for the participant,
   Tyrian for the apparatus, graphite for the Facilitator — "the unpigmented
   voice that represents no one" is the visual form of the I-voice rule, and it
   is beautiful. Where it may be told: in-product (About, design notes) and
   internal documents. Where it may not: donor material — it sits beside the
   rubrication story in the rationale bank, and rationale spoken to donors reads
   as managed poetry.
7. **Cinzel's confinement is CONFIRMED as brand intent going forward:** the
   engraved register belongs to the map and tour artifacts only — never app
   chrome, never print, never the Table. Monumentality at the Table is the museum
   register the brand refuses; bounded to the wall-chart artifacts it is a
   sanctioned sub-register, named and fenced.
8. **Chrome speaks project-we; the Facilitator speaks I — assign every string a
   speaker.** "Optional: tell **us** where you're starting from…" is fine as
   *chrome* (project voice, like onboarding's "We're cataloging"). The same words
   in the Facilitator's mouth would violate the I-rule. Ruling: every
   participant-facing string carries an owner (chrome / Facilitator /
   Representative), and pronouns follow the owner. Route: a one-pass speaker
   audit of the artifact's strings (small; most are already correct).

## 3. Findings — ranked by participant visibility

**F1 (the one real gap): the public sentence has no S0 home.** The rule — the mark
never appears at first contact without *"The mark is a table; the opening is the
way in — and it never closes"* beside it — is correctly stated in the usage sheet,
but the S0 threshold design (§5.1) shows the masthead and hero without placing the
sentence anywhere. If the masthead carries the mark (it should), the sentence
needs one quiet home at S0 — candidates: the footer line, or the first line of the
About overlay. **Route: UX thread proposes placement; Mark's taste call on which.**

**F2: mockup Representative copy needs the "illustrative" label.** Body copy
voiced as Chloe/Papnoute in the artifact mockups is design fiction unless drawn
from validated transcripts. The anti-fabrication discipline extends to mockups
the moment one is shown outside the project (a screenshot travels). Route: the
artifact marks sample Representative text as *illustrative — not validated world
output*, one line in its header. (Brand review does not judge the Representative
voice itself — that is the validation battery's jurisdiction, not mine.)

**F3 (watch, not violation): error red lives near brand red.** `--error #C0392B`
is a brighter cousin of madder `#A13E2B`. "Rare by design" already limits
exposure; add one rule at build time: an error line never renders adjacent to a
madder CTA with color as the only differentiator (the error treatment should
carry its hairline/icon form anyway, for accessibility).

**F4 (watch): "Type what you're carrying…" on the question door.** "Carrying"
is corpus-true and lands hard for the unraveling — and reads gently enough for
the merely curious. It passes. The watch is only that the *set* of three door
descriptions, taken together, stays address-wide (the curious, the searching,
the rebuilding) rather than presuming woundedness — confirm at the artifact
string pass in F2's sweep.

**Everything else quoted passes cleanly:** the consolidated status line (plain,
concrete, better than the banner it retires) · "A walk, not a menu — start
anywhere. Typing closes this." (affordance honesty) · "Don't know what to ask?"
(invitational, motionless, question-form — curiosity ordering as chrome) · "If
you want somewhere to go next" + "Stop suggesting for this conversation."
(conditional offer, one-action off — deference drawn) · the tour card (exemplary)
· the proposed-table pattern (sourcing reasons only, absences named — approve the
pattern; per-string check rides F2's sweep) · the hook and CTA at S0 (verbatim,
correct slots).

## 5. Consultation — before the raster export and Increment 1 consume these assets

1. **Do the 16px dot test first** (Ruling 3's condition) — it is the only place
   the mark's meaning could silently die in export.
2. **Run the speaker audit + illustrative-label pass** (Ruling 8, F2) in one
   artifact sweep — an hour's work, closes both findings.
3. **Give the public sentence its S0 home** (F1) before the threshold screen is
   built — retrofitting copy into a built hero is how sentences get orphaned.
4. **Wordmark outlines:** when the Alegreya vector outlines are cut, watch the
   italic "in" at small lockup sizes — italics fail before romans; the 120px
   lockup minimum may need to rise, or the "in" may need a size-specific roman
   fallback. Test, don't assume.
5. **Nothing else blocks.** Increment 1's token swap, table-bar consolidation,
   and Level-3 panels are brand-clean as designed.
