## 1. Scope, and what "living" means

This addendum governs **A1.E10** (Era 10, 1906–present): the census's 32
era-10 entries (IX.1–IX.32 — twenty-seven Pre-Survey Candidates, the four
Floor Question register rows IX.15/IX.16/IX.17/IX.26, and the IX.18 A3 label
row), whatever candidates the E10 sweep drafts, and the VIII.26/VIII.27
era-10 segments banked by the Frozen E9 Q3 fork (created at the E10 gate, not
by the sweep). After E10 it governs **any Step 0 assessment touching a living
movement** — including the Eras 1–2 revalidation wherever a finding reaches a
living successor, and any future re-run on a Frozen era's living tails.

**Definition [E]: an entry is *living* when the tradition it names is alive
today — carried in the census's `living` field as set on the merits** (the
Frozen E9 practice: living flags set "per draft on the merits," mismatches
fixed as findings at gates). A tail open at the present edge is a *symptom*
the run uses to find mismatches, never the definition — the roster's date
strings include known window-fill artifacts (the IX.16/IX.17 pair is banked
as such in the Frozen E9 forward flags), and law anchored to artifact data
fails at exactly the rows this addendum most needs to govern (IX.15, living
with a recorded end of 1993, must be *inside* the regime; IX.17, marked not
living, must not be dragged in by its 2026 window-fill end). **The E10 run is
charged to flag every `living`/`end` mismatch for the gate** — IX.15, IX.17,
and IX.28 at minimum, with the eight closed-end/living-true rows (IX.1,
IX.2, IX.3, IX.8, IX.9, IX.12, IX.21, IX.22) flagged for classification
alongside under R5's rule — each classified there, not pre-shaped here.

An entry whose tradition is genuinely dead (IX.6 Confessing Church,
1933–1945) is *historical, assessed under the standing method unchanged* —
era 10 is not uniformly living, and this addendum claims none of its dead. A
living entry's *early history* (Azusa 1906–1920s as events) is likewise
historical; the addendum governs claims about the movement's **present** —
its current confession, current authority claim, current scale, current
trajectory, and its end.

## 2. The failure modes this addendum exists to prevent

**(a) Rigor-inflation.** The classical confidence vocabulary (Documented /
Widely Accepted / Contested — the census's validator-enforced edge grades,
and B1's Documented-or-Widely-Accepted screening bar) assumes a *weathered
record*: sources that have survived, been criticized, been cross-checked by
people with no stake in the outcome. The living have no weathered record —
they have self-descriptions, journalism, statistics of varying independence,
and scholarship still in first draft. Stamping "Documented" on a living
movement's account of itself launders freshness into settledness.

**(b) Neighbor-harm.** Every claim the atlas makes about a living body — a
floor or register finding, an edge, a relations line, a teaser — is a claim
about living neighbors who can read it today, on a participant-facing atlas.
The historical eras judge the dead by their own surviving confessions; era 10
describes the confessions of communities that are still speaking. The claim
must therefore be *dated, sourced to the body's own published words, and
phrased as a description of a text — never as a verdict on the faith of the
people.*

**(c) Endangerment.** The roster itself names a third mode the historical
eras never face: some living traditions are persecuted now, and accurate,
dated, participant-facing specificity about them can be unsafe to publish at
all (IX.5's and IX.31's own rows say so). R4's safety clause carries the
rule.

## 3. The rules

**R1 — A3 runs for every living entry [E].** A3 (Contemporary movements and
interpretive fidelity) ran live for the first time at the Frozen E9 gate —
VIII.16, the register's first A3-led case, whose per-body machinery
(assessment on the body's own hermeneutic texts) the E9 record banks as era
10's main tool. **The [E] part is the mandate: at E10, A3 runs for each
living entry**, not only where flagged. Per A3's own Frozen-methodology text:
assessment is against **the specific candidate community's own confession,
never a label**. An A3 finding cites a **dated, own-voice current statement**
(official confession, statement of faith, catechism, published
self-description — with its year). Where the determination is genuinely
unclear, it is recorded as an open question for Mark, per the Methodology's
general practice.

*Composite and label rows [E]:* a **label row (IX.18) carries no entry-level
A3 finding, ever** — its own Frozen-adjacent text forbids it ("assessment is
only ever of a specific body's specific confession"); it carries per-body
findings only where a specific body is drafted as its own entry or named
line. **Composite/family rows** (IX.16's four packed bodies; the IX.15,
IX.4, IX.7 families) carry **per-body sub-findings, each with its own dated
own-voice citation** — for a family, the cited body is named as the family's
carrier for that finding, never silently generalized. Where per-body findings
diverge, the run may propose row splits at the gate (IX.16 is the
predictable first case). **Culture/phenomenon rows** — living entries naming
a current rather than a confession-bearing body (IX.23, IX.25, IX.29,
IX.30, IX.32 on the current roster) — carry A3 findings **only via the same
carrier device**: a named carrier body or formation text, cited dated, never
a finding on the current as such; where no carrier exists, the
no-single-confession shape is recorded explicitly and the entry moves on —
five boilerplate open questions are noise, not assessment.

**R2 — Self-description sourcing, dated [E].** Every living entry's own-voice
fields draw on the tradition's **own current statements, dated**. Where the
current self-description and the founding-era self-description diverge, the
entry carries **both, each dated** — divergence is disclosed, never
harmonized away in either direction.

**R3 — Contemporary-attestation vocabulary [E].** For claims about a living
movement's *present state*, two new grades:

> **Contemporary-Documented [CD]** — corroborated by at least one present-day
> source independent of the movement (official statistics, scholarly
> fieldwork, an independent census of religion), beyond the movement's own
> publications.
> **Contemporary-Reported [CR]** — resting on self-report, a single stream,
> or journalism alone.

Rules of use [all E]: (i) **The boundary**: a claim *about the state of
things now* ("the body today affirms X"; "600 million Pentecostals") takes
CD/CR; a claim about a **dated past event** takes the classical ladder with
its recency disclosed — "the 2019 statement was adopted in 2019" can be
Documented; "the body today affirms X" can never be. (ii) **B1 screening**:
CD satisfies B1's Documented-or-Widely-Accepted screening threshold for
present-state core claims; CR does not. (iii) **No promotion**: no
present-state claim is ever graded Documented, and no self-report reaches CD
without independent corroboration. Scale claims are the canonical case: the
number is carried with its counter named, CD only if the counter is
independent. (iv) **Recording surface**: CD/CR are era-doc and census-prose
vocabulary (statusDescription / sourcing / floorNote text), **never schema
enum values** — the validator's confidence set (Documented / Widely Accepted
/ Contested) is untouched. An edge touching a living body's present carries
a classical grade **only on its documented textual basis, dated in the
note** (the anticipated fundamentalist–modernist "in tension with" edge
grades on the historical rupture texts; any present-tense continuation is
phrased per R4).

**R4 — Present-tense humility [E].** **Floor and register findings, and
every other participant-facing statement about a living body — relations
lines, edge notes, teasers, status copy, A4 divergence logs alike:** (i)
describe **published texts, not people** — "its current statement of faith
(2019) affirms/states…," never "they deny…"; (ii) carry **the date of the
statement assessed**; (iii) carry the standing acknowledgment that **a
living body can change its confession** — the finding is a dated reading,
not an eternal verdict (the finding's census text says so where space
allows; the era doc says so always); (iv) never characterize the faith of
individual members — per Methodology A1's own law ("a factual description of
the confessional difference, not a value judgment about the movement or its
adherents") and the standing factual-descriptive register posture. C2 runs
on living bodies cite the body's own **current** authority claim, per the
Frozen C2 citation discipline with "current" made explicit [E]. **Safety
[E]:** where a roster row names safety or consent as an assessment dimension
(IX.5's "unsafe to publish... real people could be affected"; IX.31's
"consent and safety care alongside sourcing"), participant-facing copy and
citations carry the row's safety posture — **no identification of non-public
persons or communities**; where honest assessment would require unsafe
specificity (which network, which line, which city), the finding is recorded
as an open question with the safety constraint named (the R9 pattern) —
endangerment is the living era's third failure mode, named by the roster
itself. **The E10 run is charged to sweep the 32 existing rows' prose fields
against this standard** — IX.11's present-tense teaser is the known first
hit.

**R5 — Ends stay honestly open.** The Frozen E9 register-cap convention
("living register rows cap at their carrying era's boundary"), together with
the census's own wider practice for living lanes, already rules the dating:
a living row runs to its carrying era's boundary, and era 10's boundary is
the present. This addendum adds the guards [E]:

- **Movement-end vs episode-end.** Ending a living *movement's* row requires
  a **documented institutional terminus** — dissolution, merger, formal
  supersession — never an assessment of decline. Declining is not ended; no
  obituaries for the living. But a still-living tradition's **subsided
  episode** (a revival wave, a school, an era-bounded form) may keep its
  dated episode window — provided the row's copy says the tradition lives
  on. The two are distinguished explicitly in every finding.
- **Retroactivity.** Every existing non-boundary end on a living era-10 row
  is flagged to the E10 run and classified at the gate as one of: **(a)**
  documented institutional terminus (keep; write the rationale — IX.21's
  1989 legalization is the model), **(b)** episode-end of a still-living
  tradition (keep the window; the copy names the living continuation), or
  **(c)** unsupported artifact (propose the fix — IX.15's 1993, with no
  event stated anywhere on the row, is the known first case).
- **Segmentation.** Segmenting a living lane (the VIII.26/VIII.27 fork's
  era-10 halves) happens only at a documented institutional break.

**R6 — Dates for the living [E].** Starts follow the standing rules
(documented event anchors; disclosed reach-backs on the IX.29 shape — which
is also the mandate's named-as-likely shape for the Social Gospel candidate;
the shape itself is E10's question).
Interior gravity windows (a peak, an inflection) are named **only on a
verified anchor — an [S]-flagged source does not suffice** (in this
program's usage [S] is the unverified-debt marker, and the review precedent
treats grading over [S] as asserted-not-verified); an interior window whose
anchor is unverified waits for the verification, and the string stays
start–present with the start's own rationale. The temptation to narrate a
living arc is declined.

**R7 — Recency floor [E] — the stability marker.** How recent is too recent
to be a movement rather than a current event? Proposed rule: **an own-entry
candidate — and any existing pre-survey row whose persistence is under the
marker (IX.31, Deconstruction, c. 2010s–, is the roster's live case) — must
show one institutional generation of persistence**: roughly twenty years, OR
a survived founder-to-successor transition, OR formal institutionalization
legible across independent sources. A *candidate* failing the marker enters
only by Mark's explicit hand-selection with the recency named (the A4
pattern applied to time instead of doctrine, same logging discipline). An
*existing row* failing the marker is not silently retained: the run proposes
one of **(a)** retention by Mark's hand-selection with the recency named and
logged, **(b)** demotion to named lines inside the parent rows, or **(c)**
removal — the gate chooses. Pre-survey rows are not Frozen; proposing (b) or
(c) is legal. **IX.31 argues under this rule at the E10 run — flagged, not
pre-ruled here.**

**R8 — The conversation-system asset, bounded [E].** For living worlds the
program's unique asset is real: "internal voice" can include the tradition's
own present answer, properly framed via the Construction Framework. The
boundary: **the census and the Step 0 record never cite CiC's own
conversation system as evidence.** The asset serves world-building;
attestation comes only from the world.

**R9 — Published sources only [E].** The run performs no outreach: no
interviews, no correspondence, no contacting living bodies for clarification.
A question only the body itself could answer is recorded as open, not asked.

**R10 — Review-round teeth [E].** The two adversarial review rounds on the
E10 era doc are additionally charged to hunt this addendum's own failure
modes: any classical-ladder grade on a present-state claim (per R3's stated
boundary), any undated own-voice citation, any present-tense verdict phrased
against people rather than texts — on any participant-facing surface, per
R4's widened scope — any speculative end or arc, and any interior gravity
window resting on an unverified anchor. Each is a substantial finding, not a
cosmetic one.

## 4. What does not change

The ten-era frame; the Frozen C2 text and its citation discipline; the
Frozen Era 3–9 records; the succession conventions (continuesAs = identity
only; the validator enforces same-or-adjacent era); the
two-review-rounds-as-files discipline; gate packages stating leans;
dateRationale stamps on every date change; **only Mark freezes, and
ambiguous replies never freeze what they don't name.** Where this addendum
and any Frozen ruling conflict, the Frozen ruling wins and the conflict is
flagged rather than resolved silently.
