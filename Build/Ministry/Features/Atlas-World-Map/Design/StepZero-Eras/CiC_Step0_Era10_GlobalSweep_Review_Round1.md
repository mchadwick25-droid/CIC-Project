## Substantial findings

### S1. LA-4 (Cristero War) gets "enter" without the movement's own dated founding text
- **Location:** §4 LA-4 (Recommendation; G-LA4); §2 table row LA-4 (class "enter"); §7 G-93.
- **Evidence:** The change order "evidence for recent movements" (Decision-Log, 2026-11-08) says a "defined movement" verdict needs **both** the movement's own dated founding texts **and** a scholarly reference work. LA-4's own evidence list says item 4, "the League's 1925 manifesto or program", is "**wanted, not found**". G-93 states the rec "shows all four marks on verified items except an own founding text". The items it does have are not the movement's own texts: the bishops' suspension of worship (31 July 1926) is an episcopal act seen through ACI Prensa and a Dialnet article, and John Paul II's 2000 canonization homily is a later church text. The episode-row precedent (IX.6) does not waive the evidence rule.
- **Fix:** Reclassify LA-4 as "enter once" (condition: one dated own text of the movement staged, such as the Liga Nacional Defensora de la Libertad Religiosa's 1925 program or a dated *cristero* manifesto), or as a named line in LA-1 (option b). Rewrite G-93's recommendation to match. Update the §2 counts.

### S2. Three rows classed "enter" while Section A is "not yet assessable"
- **Location:** §2 table rows LA-2, GS-A8, GS-A11; §4 LA-2 (Section A, Recommendation); §5 GS-A8 and GS-A11 (Section A, Recommendation).
- **Evidence:** LA-2: "not yet assessable on a dated statement … no dated pre-2010 statement was seen", but Rec "enter" (Tier 1). GS-A8: "Evangelical Church: … not yet assessable", and G-109 itself says "the Evangelical wing's Section A must be shown before entry", but class "enter". GS-A11: "Not yet assessable as one row: the member churches' own statements were not seen", but class "enter". The same state is classed "enter once" for C3, LA-3, GS-A2 and E4. So the sweep applies one standard two ways, and the §2 counts are off.
- **Fix:** Reclassify LA-2, GS-A8 and GS-A11 as "enter once (Section A settled on a dated statement)". In LA-2, name the statement, for example a dated CGADB/CPAD *Cremos* or the CCB's *Pontos de Doutrina e da Fé*. Restate the counts: enter 18 → 14 (LA 4 → 2 once S1 is also applied, Asia 7 → 5), enter once 7 → 11 (or 10 if LA-4 becomes a named line, with named lines 20 → 21). Recompute the "All" column.

### S3. LA-2 double-counts IX.11, and the sweep never asks what IX.11 becomes after four regions pull its content out
- **Location:** §4 LA-2 (B3, Overlap; G-LA2); §4 §4 doc-hygiene flags; §7 Cross-cutting (G-132 to G-135).
- **Evidence:** IX.11's census record already tells the Assembleias de Deus founding as a Tier 1 documented story, "Two Swedes Sail for Pará" (November 1910 – June 1911). It cites the Igreja Batista de Belém records of June 1911 and Gedeon Freire de Alencar, *Assembleias de Deus: Origem, implantação e militância (1911–1946)* (São Paulo, 2010), as "the standard Brazilian" reference. It also names the Igreja Universal (1977, Macedo) as a voice. Its placeLabel is "Brazil · Nigeria and beyond". LA-2's B3 ("IX.11 … by its own rule cannot tell one body's story") and its Overlap line (which flags only the 1950s/1911 date mismatch) leave this out. G-91(a) ("IX.11 keeps one pointer line") would move a documented story out of an existing row, and neither the candidate nor the question says so. The same row is also drawn on by C3 (Nigeria), C5 (a Church of Pentecost line), GS-A6 (El Shaddai moves out), GS-A12 (an Indian line moves in) and LA-3. No question asks what IX.11 is once all of this is done.
- **Fix:** (1) In LA-2, rewrite B3 and Overlap to name the Pará story and Alencar 2010 as IX.11 content, and say which row keeps the story. (2) Replace G-132 and G-133, which only repeat G-105 and G-107 (see M10), with one cross-cutting question on IX.11's scope after the sweep. Give real options, for example: (a) IX.11 stays the multi-country umbrella and the new country rows cross-reference it; (b) IX.11 narrows to what no country row holds, and its Brazil story and El Shaddai move with a change order; (c) country rows are held until IX.11's scope is ruled. Add a recommendation.

### S4. The living marks are graded against R3
- **Location:** §5 GS-A9, GS-A11, GS-A13, GS-A15 (CD); §5 GS-A8, GS-A10, GS-A12 and §4 LA-5 (CR on undated items); §3 C6 (CR on an item with an [S] year).
- **Evidence:** R3 gives CD only for a verified source independent of the body. It gives CR for the body's own word, journalism, or an unverified independent source, and only dated items after 2010 count. Against that rule:
  - GS-A9 is graded **CD** on "uccj.org publishes both confessions as current standards (page undated in this run)". That is the body's own word, with no date.
  - GS-A11 is graded **CD** on the "PCC website (current)" and a year-book page, with "newest dated evidence: not fixed in this run". That is the body's own word, with no date.
  - GS-A13 is graded **CD** on the "FABC 50 General Conference volume", which is FABC's own text, so at most CR.
  - GS-A15 is graded **CD** on "uniting.church (current)", with "not fixed". That is the body's own word, with no date.
  - GS-A8 ("undated in this run"), GS-A10 ("undated"), GS-A12 ("undated") and LA-5 ("CR, undated") cite no dated item, so the grade is "none".
  - C6's CR rests on a Holder Rich book whose year is [S].
- **Fix:** Regrade GS-A9, GS-A11 and GS-A15 to "none (no dated item after 2010)". Regrade GS-A13 to CR (2023), after the volume's date is verified. Regrade GS-A8, GS-A10, GS-A12, LA-5 and C6 to "none" until a dated item is staged. Put the dated items needed on the §8 wanted list.

I could not open the VJOL page (the server dropped the connection twice), so the item could not be confirmed in this review either. The quote built on it (Hội Tin Lành Đông Pháp, 1927) is correctly marked [S].
- **Fix:** Relabel the item [S]. It stays on the wanted list until the issue, year and author are read on the page.
