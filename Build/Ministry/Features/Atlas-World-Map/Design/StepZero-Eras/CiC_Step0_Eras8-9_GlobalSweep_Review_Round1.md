## Substantial findings

### S1. The Middle East pass marks six Library files V that it did not read, and a Section A statement and a quote rest on them
- **Location:** §6 ME-4 evidence 3 and 4, and its Section A line ("Paul of Aleppo's journal (V, Library)"); §6 ME-2 evidence 4 (Duwayhī); §6 ME-5 evidence 3 (Denisov); §6 ME-10 evidence 2 (Theiner); §6 ME-13 (Paisii); §6 §4 and §2b, the Melkite row ("Named line in VII.23 (V: Paul of Aleppo, Fortescue)").
- **Evidence:** The pass's own legend says V means "I opened the item in this run ... and read the detail there". These six items carry V with a qualifier that says they were not read:
  - Paul of Aleppo, *Travels of Macarius*: "**V** (held; the registry describes the 1650s journey)". It is then cited as a dated own statement under ME-4's Section A, and as V support for the 1650-1724 Melkite line in §2b.
  - Volney vol. 2 for ME-4: "**V** (registry content note: the Melkite Basilian monastery at Shuwayr and its Arabic press)".
  - Paisii, *Istoriia slavianobolgarskaia*: "**V** (held; the registry says he complains 'of Greek domination of their church')". The quoted words are the file's own content-note header (`cic/texts/paisii-hilendarski_istoriia-slavianobolgarskaia-bul_ivanov1914.txt`, line 9), not Paisii's text. A summary is being quoted under V.
  - Denisov, *Pomorskie otvety*: "**V** (held ...)". Theiner vol. 4: "**V** as a held file; its coverage ... was not checked". Duwayhī: "**V** as a held file ... No sentence can rest on it".
  A registry note is a summary. Under every pass's legend, and the brief's, it is [S]. The Asia pass handles the same case correctly: Raulin and Sebastiani are "present in the Library ... **[S]** for content". §0's "Reading across" does not mention this use of V, so the record presents it as verified.
- **Fix:** Relabel all six [S] ("held, not read"). In ME-4, drop Paul of Aleppo from Section A (the 1848 berat in Charon, V, carries it), and change the §2b Melkite answer to "(V: Fortescue; Paul of Aleppo [S])", or open the 1650s passage in the Library file and cite its lines. In ME-13, remove the registry wording from quotation marks, or replace it with Paisii's own words from the file. Related, at the lower level: the Africa pass gives Merolla and Zucchelli "**V-s** (Library content notes ...)" (§3 AF-3 evidence 3); under Africa's legend V-s is a study's details, so these primaries on content notes are [S] (see C4).

### S2. AP3 is classed "enter" while its Section A is "not assessable on a dated statement"
- **Location:** §5 G89-AP3 (Section A; Recommendation); §2 table row AP3 (class "enter"); §8 G89-26.
- **Evidence:** AP3's Section A reads: "Not assessable on a dated statement of the period ... no dated confession of theirs was opened. Assessed at the world level per A5: they are the continuation of a church that clears (VI.13b)." Its own B1 names the communities' scripture, *Tenchi hajimari no koto*, as "wanted", and says the later Kakure "still clear" is "a separate question". The brief gives two Section A states, a dated statement or "not yet assessable". The era 10 Round 1 review made exactly this pattern its S2 (LA-2, GS-A8, GS-A11: "not yet assessable", classed "enter"), and the fix was "enter once". A5 governs strands inside an already-included movement (Article 21). Whether a separate row for two centuries of priestless, orally kept faith inherits VI.13b's clearance is a reading Mark should rule. The record states it as settled.
- **Fix:** Either reclassify AP3 "enter once (Section A settled)" and name the condition (Mark rules the A5 continuity reading, or a dated text of the communities is read), or keep "enter" and add the A5 reading to G89-26 as an explicit sub-question with options. Recount §2: enter 24 → 23 (Asia 6 → 5), enter once 2 → 3 (Asia 0 → 1).

## Moderate findings

### M1. The Jesuit court mission in China has two homes
- **Location:** §8 G89-1(b) and G89-23(a); §7 §1a option (b) and Recommendation; §2 rows AP2 and RV-1; §2b China row.
- **Evidence:** G89-1(b) puts "the Jesuit side of the court mission" in RV-1. G89-23(a) puts "the Jesuit court mission and the Rites as named lines" in AP2. §1a says only the Rites get one home ("RV-1 names the Jesuit side in one line and links to AP2"). The court mission itself is placed in both. Mark's test ("jesuites moving around the world, that is a single movement") is the reason RV-1 exists, so the record needs to say which row tells the court mission and which only points to it. The same holds, more lightly, for the 1698 union: RV-1 lists Nilles 1885 ("the 1698 union act naming the Jesuits as brokers") as its evidence item 6, while "ME-8 keeps its own Jesuit line".
- **Fix:** In G89-1 and G89-23, and in §2b, name one home for the court mission's narrative (RV-1 or AP2) and make the other a one-line pointer with a link. State the same for ME-8's Jesuit line.

### M2. Two claims about existing "continued as" links are wrong

The same applies to ME-1: `VI.30 continued as VIII.40` (Widely Accepted) exists, and ME-1's option (a) adds "continued as" links on both sides without saying the old link goes. In the other direction, VI.24's link to VII.4 is "gave rise to" (Documented), not a continuation.

In AF-2, ME-1 and G89-6, name the re-routing of IV.26 → VIII.13 and VI.30 → VIII.40 as part of the change order. Change "VI.24 `continuesAs` VII.4" to "VI.24 gave rise to VII.4" in §7.

### M3. The 1650-1724 Melkite line is placed in a row that starts in 1724

### M4. AF-2 enters from 1517, outside the sweep's scope, and the era-line rule is applied two ways
- **Location:** §3 AF-2; §2 row AF-2 ("enter, from 1517"); §8 G89-8 (Rec. (a) 1517).

In G89-8, show that option (a) reaches into era 7, outside the "eras 8 - 10" scope, and say why (b), 1650 with 1517-1650 logged for the era 7 owner, is or is not preferred.

### M6. Living marks are written "yes", or graded, with no dated item

### M7. Bleby's quote on Sharpe is not verbatim
- **Location:** §4 LA8-1 evidence 1 (record line 645).
- **Evidence:** The record quotes Sharpe "had learnt from his Bible, that the whites had no more right to hold black people in slavery ...". The raw text (archive.org `b24870109`, OCR lines ~5788-5791) reads: "But he thought, and he learnt from his Bible, that the whites had no more right ...". "had learnt" is not in the source. - **Fix:** Requote it as "he learnt from his Bible, that the whites had no more right to hold black people in slavery, than the black people had to make the white people slaves", in the pass file and in the record.
