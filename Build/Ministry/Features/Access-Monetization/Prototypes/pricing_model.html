Pack Pricing Model

Free is 3 conversations of 3 rounds. A paid conversation runs up to 7 rounds. Packs are priced on their own costs. The free allowance is a separate bucket, funded apart from pack sales. Change any number below and everything recomputes. Prices are not set, and they go to the funding thread before they are final.

What this model does not settle

Rounds. I read a round as one participant message and the Representative's reply. Tell me if you mean something else.

Who funds the free bucket. I treated it as funded by contributions, as the door works today. If pack sales carry part of it, the last card above shows how many packs that takes. The split belongs to the funding thread.

Voice length. Costs use the replies from the 15-turn run. Decision 12 aims for shorter replies, which would lower every figure.

Not included. Hosting, support, tax, and development time. The operating target above stands in for them until the funding thread gives the real figures.
