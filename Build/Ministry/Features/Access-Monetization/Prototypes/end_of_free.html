End of Free Prototype

Six screens for the moment a visitor reaches the end of the free allowance, in the Facilitator's voice. Prices come from the candidate ladder and are not final. Nothing here is wired to the engine, and no real payment can happen.

The copy, scored

What I need you to react to

  • Direction. This is the research's top pair: a closing line at the end of every conversation, and a dismissible sheet only when someone starts another after the free ones are used. Do you want this, or one of the other four directions?
  • Three claims on screen need to be true before launch. "They do not expire," "a reply that fails does not count," and "a conversation you leave unfinished is waiting where you stopped." The ledger supports the first two. The resume window is not built yet.
  • No refresh date. The free allowance is 3 conversations, not a weekly one, so "Not now" gives no return date. Opening the door further through gifts is mentioned only in the notes, not on screen, because I did not want to promise it.
  • Safety. None of these screens may appear after a safety event or stand between a message and the safety gate (decision 1). The sheet appears only when a visitor starts a new conversation, never in the middle of one.
  • The old text. The existing session-cap close in engine/m4/facilitator_turns.py says "ten exchanges" and points to the support page. It would be replaced. I have not edited it.
