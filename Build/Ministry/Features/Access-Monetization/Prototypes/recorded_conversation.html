Term explanations
  • Logos: The Word: God's own reason and speech, through whom all things were made, and who became flesh in Jesus.
  • the Incarnation: The Logos entering human nature - not to teach or model, but to heal it from within.
  • baptism: Not a ceremony announcing a choice already made. A real crossing - the body sharing in Christ's death and rising.
  • Scripture: Scripture is not a historical record of what God once said - it is the Logos speaking now, through the text, to a soul formed to hear.
