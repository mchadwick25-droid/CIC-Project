# Commentaries worklist, Eras 7 to 10 (1517 to present)

Read-only research for Church in Conversation, 2026-10-26. Mark's instruction: every commentary we can find, to help pastors. Eras 1 to 6 are covered by other agents. Nothing in the repo was edited, no model calls, no downloads. Companion file: commentaries-eras-7-10.yaml (same rows, expanded sub-question lists, computed fields).

**Counts.** 131 works or sets listed. Already vendored: 10 fully, 26 partly, 95 not at all. Serving at least one of the six scored worlds: 66. Serving later worlds only: 65. Rows with rights that block use or limit it: see the rights section. Rows marked unverified: 23.

## Method

- Worlds scored: witt, rzg (records exist), hus, jes, meth, obel (world files exist, records empty).
- Sub-questions: the 109 sub-questions of clusters C1 to C14 in 07-subquestions.yaml. C15 to C17 are excluded.
- Open cell: for witt, a sub-question the coverage maps rate L, I, N or '-' (24 cells, including Trinity and Spirit as Mark flagged). For rzg the same test gives 62 cells. For hus, jes, meth and obel every one of the 109 cells counts as open, because no record exists.
- A work serves a sub-question when the biblical books or doctrine it covers carry a direct answer, for example Romans for C4.S1 to S5, Genesis for C5.S4, Job and Psalms for C14. Each work's list was set by hand from the books it covers; it is written in the yaml as macros and as an expanded list.
- A work serves a world when the author belongs to that world's tradition and window, or is the opponent voice the world's records cite (Calvin for meth, Rheims for jes). Judgement call; the yaml lists the worlds.
- Priority = sum over served worlds of (work's sub-questions that are open cells in that world). Ties break on native voice (the world's own author), then witt+rzg cells, then count of later Atlas worlds.
- Because four worlds have no records, a whole-Bible set scores about 76 for each unbuilt world it serves. The 'built cells' figure (witt plus rzg) is shown separately; use it to rank gap-filling for the two worlds that already have records.
- Later worlds: the number of other Era 7 to 10 Atlas entries in the traditions listed for the work (groups defined in the yaml). It is a tie-break only and was not used in the priority.

Sub-question lists in the table read as cluster then sub-question numbers, for example C4.S1-5 means C4.S1 to C4.S5.
