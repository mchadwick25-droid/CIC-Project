## World by world

**Alexandria (alx).** Nothing on the topic: no devil, demon, miracle, angel or saint record in 179. The library has plenty: Antony on the weakness of magic and charms (`npnf204` 33129-33161), Antony healing "by the name" (33320), Hermas's two angels (`anf02` 2196-2231), Origen in `anf04`. Must add: a miracle-discernment record from Origen against Celsus, an angel record, a demons record. Origen's Homilies on Luke (angel and demon attending each soul) are OTHER. S7: nothing traced.

**Cappadocians (cappadocian).** The only strong ground is the martyr shrine: `martys-martyrion`, the Forty of Sebaste, the panegyris reconstruction (Inferential-Thin, honestly flagged) and `no-stones-to-show`. Nothing on demons, angels or magic, though Laodicea canon 36 (`npnf214` 12513-12514) and Nazianzen's exorcism notes (`npnf207` 1654-2054) are vendored. Caution: the term's not_for says this world "denied" worship of martyrs, and the Basil and Nyssa martyr homilies that would show it are IDENTIFIED, unvendored. The healings at shrines are recorded as self-understanding only. Must add: a source for that denial, or soften the line; an angels term (Basil on the angel of each believer is OTHER).

**Desert (desert).** The best-rendered Eastern world. Verified words: Antony to the beast-demons (Vita 9), the noonday demon (Evagrius), Antony healing "by the name" and "Christ worketh them, not we". The tomb story is Contested. Risk: its absent_detail says the beasts are "this world's own chosen register for interior struggle... not a claim about literal events". That is a modern reading, and C13-19 asks exactly whether they took it literally; no vendored line settles it. The Sayings (`anan-isho_paradise-v2` 82, Sisoes) and Lausiac History are vendored and unused for demons. Must add: an honest_limit on literal belief; a story from the Sayings on a demon or a vision; relics beyond Antony's secret burial. Historia Monachorum is IDENTIFIED.

**Donatists (don).** `macrobius-letter` is a good martyr story (bodies thrown into the sea so none could be venerated), but it exists twice (`macrobius-letter` and `macrobius-letter-isaac-maximianus`). That is duplicate drift to fix, not a second witness. Everything from the movement's own martyr acts (Tilley, Abitinian Acts, Duval) is IDENTIFIED. Augustine on the tombs and feasting (Letter 29) is OTHER. Must add: a martyr-cult honest_limit, since the church of the martyrs is this world's centre and no record says what happened at their graves.

**Gallic monastics (gallic).** The fullest world: devil, demons, exorcism, miracle, angels and the tomb test are all rendered, mostly by verified quotes from Sulpitius and Cassian. Martin's silent exorcism, the catechumen raised, Cassian refusing to tell miracle tales, Martin's test of the unattested tomb (`npnf211` 1176), the angel of the twelve psalms. Gaps: Cassian Conferences VII and VIII (demons; "On Principalities", `npnf211` 32132, 33442) are vendored and cited by no record;. Agnes, Thecla and Mary visiting Martin (4554) is the only near-S7 material. Must add: a Conf. VII-VIII record; a relics record from Victricius (OTHER).

**Jerome's circle (hal).** The witness `hal.dw.hell` carries the one devil position: restoration of the devil renounced. `desert-romances` frames the Hilarion and Malchus tales as "stories we told", a good guard. The vendored Letter 109 to Riparius (`npnf206` 22242, the Vigilantius row) and Letter 108 on Paula at the holy places (20791) have no record; the Paula quote is about pilgrimage, not relics. Must add: a relics and intercession record from Ep. 109; Hilarion's demons (OTHER).

**Imperial-juridical (ijc).** Two adjacent hits only: the Lactantius dream and the Milvian vision, told side by side, and `martyrium` as shrine. `jerome-damasus-verses` is retrieved on "tombs of the martyrs" but its text is about verse-making, so the retrieval is a trap. Ambrose's finding of Gervasius and Protasius (Ep. 22, `npnf210` 42690) is vendored and unrecorded. Must add: an Ambrose relics record; fix the Damasus retrieve_when.

**Latin pastoral (lpc, building).** 330 records, none on any C13 topic. The library is rich and mapped: Augustine's miracles at Hippo (City of God XXII.8, `npnf102` 45632-45679), amulets and incantations with Christ's name (`npnf107` 1649, 1701), Monica's request, and On Care for the Dead (`npnf103` 44050, the dead appearing in dreams). `ye-belong-to-my-care` (draft) mentions martyrs' shrines but is retrieved on a bishop's duty. Belongs to the LPC thread.

**Post-apostolic house church (pahc).** The Didache's test of prophets (verified, `anf07` 13026) plus the `prophetes` term answer the "true prophet from a fraud" half of C13-12. `martyrdom-of-polycarp` guards against "medieval-style relic veneration"; the Two Ways story omits magic. Perpetua's vision of her dead brother Dinocrates (`anf03` 59700-59740) is vendored and mapped here, the best S7 text in the fleet, unrecorded. Justin on exorcism (`anf01` 16966) and Ignatius, "all magic was dissolved" (5496), are also unrecorded.

**Zurich and Geneva (rzg).** Three stories, none on topic; no record on any C13 question. Calvin's Institutes I.14 on devil and angels (12881-12932) and ch. XX on intercession of saints (vol. 3, 23294), and Zwingli on invocation of saints (vol. 3), are vendored. Calvin's Treatise on Relics and Zwingli's Short Instruction are OTHER. Must add: records for all of S1, S4, S5, S6.

**Syriac (syr).** Strongest on S3: `theodoret-gnats` is retrieved on "can the miracle stories be believed", `jacob-deliverance` says the miracle is "the tradition's own telling", the Abgar story is "told as our story, never as history". Roll-and-vine gives angels in a dream as a warrant. `dw.unsettled` guards the polemic that made neighbours "demons' tools". Egeria on the shrine of Thomas at Edessa is vendored and mapped (152 hits), no record. Brock's Luminous Eye is IDENTIFIED.

**Wittenberg (witt).** Devil and saints are fully rendered: `term.the-devil` (a real, personal enemy; the commandments as "true holy water"), `temptation`, Small Catechism quote, `term.saints` (remembered, never prayed to), `images`. Gaps: the Apology's article on invocation of saints is vendored (7377-7491) and unrecorded; Table Talk is a selection (43 devil lines), the full "Of the Devil" chapters are OTHER.
