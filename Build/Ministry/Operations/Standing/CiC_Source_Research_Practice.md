# Source research — proactive acquisition practice

How the dedicated source-research thread works. `CLAUDE.md` 3.4 points here. Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-10-19 with no change in substance.

## Search ahead of need

- Source new worlds proactively, not reactively. Search, rights-verify, and download against upcoming worlds' time windows and regions *before* their Doc_02 stage starts.
- Add verified rows to `Build/worlds/_cross-world/download-queue-seed.yaml` (never to the generated `DOWNLOAD-QUEUE.md`) and vendor into `cic/texts/` ahead of need. `DOWNLOAD-QUEUE.md` already draws the line between its proactive half and its reactive half (`WANTS-REGISTER.md`).
- One research pass serves every future world whose window it covers, because the corpus is shared. That is higher leverage than a per-world manifest.

## Check what is already in hand first

Before searching, check `CORPUS-USE.md`'s tier method against the new worlds: named-never-opened, same time and place, same time and different region, out of window. Much of what's needed may already sit unused among the vendored files.

## Known blocker

The sandbox blocks the patristic text hosts outright, regardless of real scarcity. Widening that network policy is a live open item.

## Keep sessions fresh

The research thread runs long and accumulates enormous context; multi-day sessions have run past a billion cached tokens. Split its work into fresh sessions per batch, one era or region at a time. An ever-larger context makes every later turn more expensive, however good the research is.
