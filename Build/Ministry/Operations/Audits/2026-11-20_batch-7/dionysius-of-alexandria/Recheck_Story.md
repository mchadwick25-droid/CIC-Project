"Some of his own church, Athanasius of Alexandria says, went over his head to the bishop of Rome, who wrote against both errors and told Dionysius what was said against him."

"Some of his own church, Athanasius of Alexandria says, went over his head to the bishop of Rome. The bishop wrote against both errors and told Dionysius what was said against him."
