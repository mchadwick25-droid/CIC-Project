## Quoted spans

Six entries cover the brief's seven quoted spans (the first two are one quotation broken around "he wrote"). Each `modern` is the brief's words exactly. Every one is **awaiting second reading**.

1. "If it is a joy ... his own blood." Printed English JER 40966-40968; awaiting second reading. Latin original left out: Migne's print has letter faults (letitia, que, vestigiis5, Cresi) and no held print confirms the reading (Batch_Originals_Ruling rule).
2. "Who among those eager to learn ... whenever need arose." Printed English JER 51430-51437; awaiting second reading. Latin left out for the same reason (Seripturas, prebebat, qus, poposcissel, a Greek "αὖ" for ut). The brief's wording differs from the English; the entry's note says so.
3. "jointly". Printed English EUS 36975-36976; original Greek GRK 5261-5262, checked by hand, clean; awaiting second reading.
4. "When he explains these things ... wholly and perfectly." Printed Latin APOL-L 14211-14215 (clean; APOL-M 42051-42056 agrees with faults). No English held. Awaiting second reading.
5. "is said to have written". Printed English JER 45146-45147; original Latin APOL-L 13961-13962 (clean; APOL-M 41897 agrees). Awaiting second reading.
6. "since they think a martyr praised him." Printed English JER 51456; Latin left out (JER-L 31320-31321 reads "ἃ martyre predicatum", a fault). Awaiting second reading.

## Conflicts with the entry's own fields

None found. Checked against `sourcing`, `voices`, `legacy`, `why`, `floorNote`, `relationsSummary`, `dates`, `placeLabel`:
- `dates` 307-310 and `why` ("jailed in 307 ... 309 or 310") agree with the card's arrest late 307 and death 309 or 310.
- `voices` says Jerome "kept twenty-five volumes in Pamphilus's hand". Jerome's Latin says only "manu ejus"; the card records that the editors read it as Pamphilus's (uncertain 9).
- `legacy` says Jerome calls him the founder of the library. Jerome's letter says he wished to establish one (JER 49611-49614). The card says gathered, as the brief does.
- `relationsSummary` "is said to have studied at Alexandria" agrees with the card's credited report (uncertain 6).
- The census says "Apology"; the card and brief say "Defense", the same book.
