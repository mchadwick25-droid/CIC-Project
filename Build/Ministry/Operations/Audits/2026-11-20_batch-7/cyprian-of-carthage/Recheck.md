## 2. Blocking findings

- **B1 "under guard".** The gloss is gone and the Place line is added. The phrase still appears once, at O 121, inside the Do-not-say prohibition the check itself prescribed. **FIX 3** (so the outline does not carry the words at all), replace O 121 with:
  "- Not that he was guarded, imprisoned or held at Curubis. The held texts say he was sent there and stayed there; Pontius says the brethren visited him and the townspeople supplied his needs (ANF 28046–28050). Say 'sent away to live in Curubis, a small town on the coast'."
- **B2 the last hiding.** Partly fixed: his letter and Pontius are credited, and the Do not say line no longer corrects Pontius silently. Not fixed: three held witnesses speak to the last hiding, and the outline credits two. The *Acta* say that after his recall "ex sacro praecepto in suis hortis manebat et inde cotidie sperabat ueniri ad se" (KNOPF 4853–4856): he stayed at his gardens by a holy command, expecting each day that they would come for him. That is a third account, differing from Ep. 81's withdrawal (EP 17369–17373, "de hortis nostris interim secederem"). Mark's ruling (2026-11-19) is to credit each.
  **FIX 1.** In beat 6 (O 45), replace from "Pontius tells it differently" to the end of the beat with:
  "Pontius tells it differently: friends offered him places to retire to, and he would not go (ANF 28142–28149; Documented as Pontius's account). The account of his death says that after his return he stayed at his gardens by a holy command, expecting each day that they would come for him (KNOPF 4853–4856; Documented as the *Acta*'s account). Credit each in turn: his own letter, then his deacon, then the account of his death."
  Replace O 120 with:

His last letter says that on friends' advice he left his gardens for a while to die at Carthage, not Utica (EP 17369–17377); Pontius says he refused his friends' offers of a refuge (ANF 28142–28149); the account of his death says he stayed at his gardens by a holy command, waiting each day for them to come (KNOPF 4853–4856) (D4).
