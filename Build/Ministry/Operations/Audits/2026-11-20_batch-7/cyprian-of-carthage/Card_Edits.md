## Conflicts with the entry's own fields

- `teaser` says he was made bishop two years after his baptism as fact. The card gives it as a modern reckoning, since no ancient source states it (BEN 2628; ANF 27513-27525). Not an entry field on the list, but it reads against the card.
- `dates` 248-258 stands. The card adds that the start is 247 or 248 (EUS note), 248 (ANF editor) or June 248 to April 249 (Benson).
- `why` says he read Tertullian every day and called him the master. The card keeps this and says it is Jerome's hearsay at two removes (NPNF3 40500-40505). This is not a contradiction.
- `legacy` has Facundus citing him about 550. No source on the sheet holds this; the card does not carry or deny it.
- `sourcing`, `voices`, `floorNote`, `relationsSummary`, `placeLabel`: no conflict.

## Claims not found or not carried

- Source_Sheet E6 says Ep. 15.4 (EP 2465-2473) shows others got certificates. That passage is about martyrs' letters of peace, not certificates of sacrifice. The card takes the certificates from LAPS 1028-1029 instead.
- Source_Sheet line numbers corrected: UNIT 364-365 (not 363-364); EP 15426-15428 for error and proud (not 15425-15427); KOCH 8076-8098 for Koch's doubt of Chapman.
- Source_Sheet E4 says the Roman clergy wrote that Cyprian had withdrawn for a certain reason (EP 1055-1056); the card says only what the held lines give.
- Not carried: Lactantius's criticism of To Demetrianus (L7 4571, not re-read) and the Coprianus mockery; Dionysius of Alexandria; Prudentius (not held); the Confessions oratory (the line breaks before the name).
- Sulpicius Severus: the held line (npnf211 2657-2661) says the traveler wished to worship at the tomb, not that he did.
- Whether Stephen's death fell in 257 rests on EUS 39406-39407 and 39421-39423 (an editor's reckoning); the card says so.
