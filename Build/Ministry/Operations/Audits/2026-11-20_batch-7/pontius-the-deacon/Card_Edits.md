## 2. New: uncertain (appended after the four existing entries)

1. **Name and office** (new). Evidence: HAR 331-341 (no manuscript and not the Cheltenham list gives the name; neither name nor office can be found in the text; Jerome followed a tradition whose source is unknown); SHK 31626-31630 (most accept Jerome; Dessau's Curubis Pontius; Corssen against); DEL 3936-3942 (the inscription honors a magistrate, C. Helvius Honoratus, surnamed Pontius; Corssen showed the identification poorly founded). Brief P1.
2. **Kind of book** (new). HAR 1909-1910; MON 11030-11032; DEL 4097-4102 (Corssen, and the defense overstated); DEL 4079-4083 and SHK 31630-31633 (Reitzenstein: deaths of famous men, a poor rhetorician); DEL 4312-4320 (Delehaye: as weighty as the Acta of Perpetua, biography in the tone of a speech of praise). Brief P2, P9.
3. **Eyewitness** (new). VE 28055-28059 and 28183-28184 (the claim); SHK 31630-31631 (Reitzenstein); DEL 4264-4277 (a forger would not have played the part so discreetly, and would have told more than the dream). Brief P2.
4. **The dream's meaning** (new). VE 28098-28102 (a day as a year, the same day a year later); VE 28125-28131 (church business, the poor); HAR 1379-1380 (not convincing, not Cyprian's meaning); HAR 1335-1337 (private affairs, awkward for Pontius). Brief P5.
5. **The last withdrawal** (new). VE 28142-28149 (did not consent); EP 17368-17373 (left the gardens for a while, on friends' advice, so as not to be taken to Utica, and a bishop should confess in his own city). Brief P6.
6. **What he left out** (new). DEL 4168-4173 (strange silence on the councils and Rome); HAR 2087-2093 (not "opera ac merita" in his sense, so it did not belong); MON 11092-11103 (no word on the baptism of heretics or the councils; he wished to paint the Christian and the cleric, not the church leader). Brief P8.
7. **Preface phrase** (new). VE 27709-27712 ("any of his more illustrious labours"); VL 40364 and HAR 558-559 (si qua de antiquioribus comperi); HAR 589-592 (an eyewitness, but not from the start). The words I used for the Latin ("what he learned of earlier things") are a plain gloss of the comparative, not a quotation.
8. **The court record** (new). VE 28001-28003 and VL 40900-40901 (there are Acts which relate); HAR 1829-1833 (Monceaux; knew a report of the first hearing and the sentence, not the Acts as we have them); DEL 4251-4252 (more probable he knew them); SHK 31640-31641 (von Soden: meant to replace them). Source for the first hearing, brief P6, is Cyprian's story; kept to one entry here.
9. **Style** (new). BEN 27384-27386 (devoted grandiloquent deacon, affected lines); DEL 4298-4299 (un esprit sterile; sees nothing beyond the written document). Both credited, neither used as a fact.
10. **Jerome's date** (new). JE 38785 and 41789 (fourteenth year of Theodosius); JE 38785 note (a.d. 492); JE 15402 note (Theodosius named Augustus 19 January 379). Supports brief P1, "about 130 years later".
