# Second Readings, Blind C (batch 7, B7-41 to B7-59)

## B7-41
His book, which he is said to have written in Greek in defence of Origen.

## B7-42
Since they think that a martyr has praised him.

## B7-43
For they did not come to this by choice. They were betrayed by the weakness of the flesh. They also show the marks of Jesus on their bodies.

## B7-44
Peter's argument was for mercy and kindness. The argument of Meletius and those with him was for truth and zeal.

## B7-45
He was seized for no reason and quite unexpectedly. As if on Maximinus's order, he was beheaded at once and without any explanation.

## B7-46
A voluntary exile.

## B7-47
He spread out his hand and held it flat, like the blade of a sword. He copied the usual stroke of execution, and so, as clearly as in plain speech, he showed what he wanted me to understand. I understood that the sentence was a death sentence, still to come.

## B7-48
I decided to write a few things in brief. This is not because anyone, even among the pagans, could be unaware of the life of so great a man. It is so that a great and unmatched example may be handed down to our descendants too, as an undying memory.

## B7-49
Shall I grieve that I was not his companion? But his victory is to be celebrated. Shall I celebrate his victory? But I grieve that I am not his companion. Still, I must tell you plainly, as you know yourselves, that this was how I felt. I rejoice greatly, indeed too greatly, in his glory, but I grieve more that I stayed behind.

## B7-50
Surely you cannot forbid the Truth to reach your ears by the quiet, hidden path of a silent book.

## B7-51
Meanwhile she asks one thing anxiously of earthly rulers: not to be condemned without being known.

## B7-52
The blood of Christians is seed.

## B7-53
Marcion, by what right do you cut down my wood? Valentinus, by whose permission do you turn aside the streams of my spring?

## B7-54
This is my property. I have owned it for a long time. I owned it before you.

## B7-55
I am the heir of the apostles.

## B7-56
How you should understand "another" I have already declared: as a name of person, not of substance, for distinction, not for division. Yet although I hold one substance everywhere, in three joined together, I must still call "another" the one who commands.

## B7-57
The church is not a number of bishops.

## B7-58
Give me the master.

## B7-59
And if it is such a joy to have one letter of a martyr, how much more to have so many thousand lines that seem to me to be traced in his blood.
