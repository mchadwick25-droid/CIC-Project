## Quoted spans (all awaiting second reading; no `secondReading` written)

1. "Spurred on by their proud ungodliness ... I took up this task." Modern rendering is the brief's, word for word. `printed` Fletcher, AN 4567 (English); `original` DI-L 23206-23210 (Brandt, Latin). A second scan of the edition (second-scan file 32136-32140) confirms the reading, with scan faults. Awaiting second reading.
2. "Religion must be defended not by killing but by dying ... faithfulness." `printed` Fletcher, AN 5158; `original` DI-L 26240-26242. Confirmed by the other scan of Brandt's edition (19163-19165). Awaiting second reading.
3. "He was the most eloquent man of his time ... the necessities." No English held. `printed` is the Latin as Prosper copies Jerome's entry, TE 11251-11252, which is clean. Jerome's own entry (JC 44985-44987) has a margin number and the word olymp inside the last line, which would stop the validator's substring check, and no entry for it exists in `tools/quote_scan_readings.json`; I did not edit that shared file. If the entry should cite Jerome's own print, it needs a reading there. Awaiting second reading.

## Conflicts with the census entry's existing fields

- `dates` "c. 303-325" and `end` 325: the card says no death year is held. The only 325 is Fletcher's "believed" (AN-ed 476) and the translation editor's gloss on Jerome (JE 41033 endnote); Monceaux says nothing is known (MO 25248-25249).
- `why` ("His Divine Institutes open with an address to Constantine") and `teaser` ("addressed his defense of the faith to Constantine"): the card says only some copies carry the address and its authorship is disputed (DI-L 285-297; MO 25825-25852; SH 39678-39696).
- Not contradicted, not found: the entry's `legacy` says a letter in Calvin's collected letters calls Lactantius "an ancient doctor of the Church". Nothing in the sheet or outline holds it, so the card leaves it out.
- `sourcing`, `voices`, `floorNote`, `relationsSummary`, `placeLabel`: consistent.

## Claims not found, or not carried

- The outline's "Institutes written about 303 to before 311": not found in this form. Monceaux says conceived about the start of the persecution and written between early 307 and early 311 (MO 25872-25876, 25928-25930); the card uses that.
- Sheet line numbers that did not hold and were corrected: MO 24921-24932 for "about 290" (it is MO 24954-24955); MO 28738-28744 for "more and more" (the note is MO 28726-28736); MO 25192-25193 for March 1, 317 (it is MO 25190-25191); "c. 314-315" for the Persecutors (Monceaux says about 314; MO 26030-26050).
- Schanz arguing for his authorship of the Persecutors (SH 40249-40250): the scan runs two columns together and is unreadable, so the card carries only the readable SH 39060-39062.
- Left out as the outline says: the poems, the Epitome, the Anger of God, Coxe's and McGiffert's sympathies beyond the two noted, Monceaux's portraits of the emperors, the interrupted sacrifice, Galerius's mother, the census, Maximin's priests.
