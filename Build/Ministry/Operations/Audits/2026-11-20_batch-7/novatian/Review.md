## The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Novatian, a leading elder and writer of the church at Rome in the middle of the third century, made a rival bishop of Rome against Cornelius in 251.
- **What happened:** after the persecution under Decius he led a church that would not take back to communion those who had given way, and left their pardon to God. His enemies charged him with cowardice, a forced ordination and an oath at the communion table. Dionysius urged him to step down. His church spread and lasted for centuries.
- **Why the connection matters:** his church kept the common faith but its own rule. At Nicaea its bishop Acesius accepted the creed and stood apart only over that rule. Constantine answered with the ladder, and the council set the terms on which his clergy could come back.

All three lines come clear from one hearing.

## Findings

1. **BLOCKING: the essence is complete only at word 195, past about 150, and the hook runs six sentences.** *On the Trinity* and what it sets out belong to the approved essence, but line 5 gives only "a writer on the faith", and the book comes in line 7. Put the book into the essence and take it out of line 7.
   - Line 5, replace "Novatian was a leading elder at Rome and a writer on the faith. He was later made a rival bishop of Rome, set up against the first." with: "Novatian was a leading elder at Rome, and his book On the Trinity set out what the church believed of Father, Son and Spirit. Later he was made a rival bishop of Rome, set up against the first."
   - Line 7, replace "His book On the Trinity follows the rule of truth, the church's short summary of belief about God as Father, Son and Holy Spirit." with: "The book follows the rule of truth, the church's short summary of belief."

   This brings the essence to word about 182 at no net cost in length.

   The hook (116 words) still holds the lapsed gloss, Cyprian's credit, the dying clause, Quote 1 and its plain sense, so it cannot reach about 150 without losing one of them. The reviser can quote only Quote 1's first sentence, "Let them knock at the doors, by all means, but certainly not break them down.", which is word for word and ends at a full stop. Then shorten line 3's plain sense to "They might plead, not force their way in before the church judged." That reaches about 158. If the reviser keeps the full quote, the essence at about 1:13 comes to Mark under the 2026-11-20 length ruling.

   The outline left room for this, because it planned the hook at 70 words before its glosses. **Do not say line:** "The book held back past the essence. Name On the Trinity, and what it sets out, inside the essence sentence."
2. **BLOCKING: line 7 implies the opposite of what the book argues.** "and the Son, truly man and truly God, two and not one person under two names; then the Spirit." Heard once, "two and not one person" attaches to the Son, as if the Son were two persons. The book's point is that the Father and the Son are two, not one person under two names (Outline, Also told; Source Sheet E2: "the Father is One, the Son Another").
   - Replace with: "and the Son, truly man and truly God. Father and Son are two, not one person under two names. Then it turns to the Spirit."

   The outline's run-on phrasing left room for this. **Do not say line:** Not "the Son ... two and not one person". Say "Father and Son are two, not one person under two names".
3. **MINOR: one charge is softened.** Line 11, "had them lay hands on him". Cornelius says "he compelled them by force to confer on him the episcopate" (`npnf201_...` 38531–38533), and the outline says "made them lay hands on him". Change "had them lay hands on him" to "forced them to lay hands on him". The word count is the same.

Valesius reports that the Novatians' own *Martyrdom of Novatian* made him only a confessor (SOC-ed 14134–14141, on the card). The held sources differ, and this clause carries one of them. Cut ", as his church remembered him" (5 words saved). The sentence still credits Socrates, and the next sentence keeps the doubt.
5. **MINOR: a name heard once that neither acts nor links.** Line 25, "under the emperor Valerian". Valerian is not in the outline's Names. Change it to "in a later persecution".
6. **MINOR: cuts toward the 4:00 aim.** Cut "writing to Antioch" from line 9 (", writing to Antioch": 3 words). "above all his rival Cornelius" carries the witness.
