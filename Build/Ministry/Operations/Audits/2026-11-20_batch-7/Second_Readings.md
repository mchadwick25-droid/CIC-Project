# Second Readings (batch 7, B7-01 to B7-59)

## Method

For each id I set our wording (`Second_Reading_Key.md`, as it stands in the brief) beside the blind rendering (`Second_Readings_Blind_A.md`, `_B.md`, `_C.md`). I first confirmed that each key wording stands word for word in the brief and equals its card entry's `modern`. Where the readings differed, I opened the cited lines in `cic/texts/`, read the story's card `quotationOriginals` entry (`printed`, `original`, `uncertain`), and read the sentence of the brief that holds the quote. Where the blind reader rendered past the end of our span, I judged only the words we use, and whether the cut changes the sense in the brief's sentence. Following Mark's guidance (Decision-Log, "Batch 6: where sources disagree", 2026-11-19), where two held sources differ, a wording held by either is credited; where the card's English is made from a Latin the Library holds, I read that Latin.

- **SAME**: same meaning.
- **MINOR**: a small difference a listener would not take differently.
- **MEANING**: a listener would understand something different.

The four CARRIED spans (`origen` two, `methodius-of-olympus` one) were not read again and their cards are untouched.
