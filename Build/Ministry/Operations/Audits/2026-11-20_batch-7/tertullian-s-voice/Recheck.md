## 1. Worded by me

- **B1, essence (O 5) and Do not say (O 134): FIX.** Neither cited source carries "the one who left the deepest mark". Vincent says "as Origen holds by far the first place among the Greeks, so does Tertullian among the Latins" (VIN 13555–13557). Coxe says "Tertullian is the founder of Latin Christianity" (ANF4 17041–17042, not 17042 alone). In the same notice Coxe gives Minucius "its earliest clothing in Latinity" (ANF4 17042–17043), which supports "rank, not order". The plainest wording both carry is "first among the Latins" and "the founder of Latin Christianity". Neither says anything about a mark left.
  - O 5, replace "was the first great writer of the faith in Latin, and the one who left the deepest mark, around the year 200" with "was the first great writer of the faith in Latin, around the year 200". In the note, replace "ANF4 17042" with "ANF4 17041–17042".
  - O 134, replace `Say "the first great writer of the faith in Latin, and the one who left the deepest mark" (D4; rank, not order).` with `Say "the first great writer of the faith in Latin" (D4; rank, not order: Vincent's "first ... among the Latins", Coxe's "founder of Latin Christianity").`
- **"whose works are lost" (O 17, O 100; S 126): FIX.** JER 40495 says only "chief of the Latin writers after Victor and Apollonius". Jerome's own chapters on the two men list their works and do not say they are lost (Victor, JER 40157–40160; Apollonius, JER 40348–40354). No held source cited says so. The clause also brings back by implication the "first whose books survive" claim that B1 bars, because the Octavius may be older. Cut it:
  - O 17: "Latin; Jerome names only two Latin Christian writers before him (JER 40495); a few works in Greek are lost (EUS-ed 10260–10262)."
  - O 100: "First, why it was new: Jerome names only two Latin Christian writers before him (JER 40495), and he had to make Latin carry it, ..."
  - S 126: delete ", whose Latin works are lost".
- **B1 D4, B2 Links and I2, B4 What came 3, the Length recount, the B6 cuts (beats 1, 2 and 5, and Quotes 1–3), M3, M6, M8–M11, M13, M14: PASS.** Each holds at its lines: HARN 22546–22548; ANF3 2710–2712, 3997–4003, 4000–4001, 21747–21749, 51014–51016, 51037–51041; KRO 12559; OEH2 43866; MON 17466–17469; JER 40496, 40514–40516; EUS 10279–10280, 10302–10306; ANF3-ed 1443–1446; ANF4 17138; LACT-E 4569; Jerome's Chronicle 43480–43482.
