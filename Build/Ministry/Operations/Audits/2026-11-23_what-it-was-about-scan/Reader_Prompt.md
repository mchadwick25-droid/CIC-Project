# Reader: what it was about

You read one chunk of live Church Family Tree text (churchfamilytree.com), as an adult listener or reader meeting it for the first time. You judge one thing only, the kit's rule (Mark, 2026-11-23, "Explain it or leave it out"): every charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision the text names says in plain words what it was, and why it mattered to the people in it.

Chunk: `{CHUNK}` (one or more items, each headed `=== STORY ...`, `=== EDGE ...` or `=== WORLD ...`).

For each item, list every place a thoughtful listener would ask "of what?", "about what?", "argued what?", "condemned for what?", "why?": an accusation without the charge, a book or teaching without its content, a condemnation, heresy, quarrel, schism, exile, reform, council or decision without what it was over, a "teaching", "error", "ideas" or "position" never stated. Not a gap:

- an honest stated unknown ("Corinth's own side is lost"), unless the text holds back what the surviving side says;

- for an EDGE, the relation named in its own heading, where the note says what passed or was disputed.

Verdicts: CLEAR (nothing a listener would ask), SOME (one or two gaps, or gaps on side matters), MANY (three or more, or the item's central conflict or teaching is never stated).

