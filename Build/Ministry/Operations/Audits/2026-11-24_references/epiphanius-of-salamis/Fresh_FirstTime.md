## 3. Sentences that fail on one hearing

**F2 (MINOR).** "...and that Theophilus saw no danger in following his example, because the people praised him." Meaning lost: "him" could be Theophilus or Epiphanius. Change "because the people praised him" to "because the people praised Epiphanius".

**F3 (MINOR).** "A book written to answer the council's argument replied with his own curtain letter." Meaning lost: "curtain letter" is a label the listener never heard. The curtain story was told, but not as a letter by that name. Change "replied with his own curtain letter" to "replied with his own letter about the torn curtain".

**F4 (MINOR).** "Origen, he writes, taught that souls were angels who fell into bodies, denied that this flesh would rise again, and read the garden of Eden as hidden meanings." Meaning lost: with no church background, "this flesh would rise again" does not say rising from the dead. Change "denied that this flesh would rise again" to "denied that this flesh would rise from the dead".
