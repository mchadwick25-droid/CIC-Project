### MINOR

**First-time read, F1 (MINOR).** "Augustine wrote his own book on the heresies in the order of his list." Meaning lost: "his" follows Augustine, so it sounds like Augustine's own list. Change to: "Augustine wrote his own book on the heresies in the order of Epiphanius's list."

**First-time read, F2 (MINOR).** "...and that Theophilus saw no danger in following his example, because the people praised him." Meaning lost: "him" could be Theophilus or Epiphanius. Change "because the people praised him" to "because the people praised Epiphanius".

**First-time read, F3 (MINOR).** "A book written to answer the council's argument replied with his own curtain letter." Meaning lost: "curtain letter" is a label the listener never heard. The curtain story was told, but not as a letter by that name. Change "replied with his own curtain letter" to "replied with his own letter about the torn curtain".

**First-time read, F4 (MINOR).** "Origen, he writes, taught that souls were angels who fell into bodies, denied that this flesh would rise again, and read the garden of Eden as hidden meanings." Meaning lost: with no church background, "this flesh would rise again" does not say rising from the dead. Change "denied that this flesh would rise again" to "denied that this flesh would rise from the dead".

**First-time read, F5 (MINOR).** "deacons", first in "he had deacons seize the man and hold his mouth shut". The word returns in "made him a deacon, then a priest" and in Constantinople. A listener with no church background does not know it is a church rank below priest. Change "he had deacons seize the man" to "he had deacons, the clergy who serve under priests, seize the man".

**First-time read, F6 (MINOR).** "celebrated the sacraments", in "no one celebrated the sacraments for them". Church jargon, not known to this listener. Change to "no one celebrated the sacraments, the church's holy rites, for them".
