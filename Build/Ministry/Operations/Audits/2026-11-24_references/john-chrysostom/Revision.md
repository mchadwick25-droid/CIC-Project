## Findings and what was done

- **B1. Socrates and Palladius not identified.** Socrates, at his first mention: "a church historian of the city writing a generation later" (Who tells it). Palladius, at his only mention: "who wrote John's life and took his side" (Do not say gives these words). The second "Socrates says" (Beat 5) follows the first and needs no gloss.
- **B2. Clergy corrected for what.** The trust read found the reason (SS 17262-17267, "designing to correct the morals of the clergy"). Added "over how they lived". The expulsions stand as told: Socrates gives "some for one cause, and some for another", no single reason.
- **B3. Rebukes for what.** Added the outline's one example, the Gothic general's request for a church for the Arians, with the outline's gloss, then "He rebuked many others in office too", so he is one of many (Do not say). This restores what Cut first 15 had sent to the card, the only content the outline holds for these rebukes.
- **B4. Why recalled.** No change. Trust.md has no reason line, so the finding drops under the review's lead-in. The outline's Leave out puts the causes on the card (Socrates and Theodoret differ).
- **B5. Sermons out of order.** Moved the two sentences, word for word, to the end of the Antioch paragraph (Listen_FirstTime's exact change).
- **M1. Which empire.** "the cathedral of Constantinople, the emperor's city" (Setting). The outline does not say "Roman", so the word is not added.
- **M2.** "left the church over his fall" (the review's wording).
- **M3.** "which Christians call Christ's body" (the review's wording). The sentence is split at "silk", since the gloss took it past 40 words.
- **M4 and M6.** "The accounts differ." cut. "A later bishop of Constantinople summed up the meeting's own minutes, and they tell it differently: some charges were heard..." "Bishop of Constantinople" is right for Photius (Links, "Photius of Constantinople").

- **M7.** "chief chamberlain" (Opening 1's word) for "chief eunuch"; "a hospital" for "the hospital". "ordain" and "white clothes" stand ("may stand").
- **M8.** No change required. Items 3 and 4 are met by B3 and B5. "His sermons were read for centuries" is the outline's (Cut first 14).
