"His sermons were read for centuries, and the followers who left the church over his fall came back only when his body came home." **SUMMARY HOLDS.** SS 17304-17308: "Those who desire to form an adequate idea of them, must read for themselves". Later witnesses show the centuries, which Mark's ruling allows: Cassian (CASS 55143-55144); AUGC 880-881, "Chrysostom says that the priest stands daily at the altar"; CALV1 18508-18509, "the words of Chrysostom, 'The foundation of our philosophy is humility'". The return: SS 21459-21469, "Proclus the bishop brought back to the Church those who had separated themselves from it on account of Bishop John's deposition ... By this means the admirers of that prelate were conciliated." Note on "only": Atticus put John's name back in the prayers hoping "many would be induced to return" (SS 20198-20206), and Socrates does not say whether any did. But in 438 Socrates still has a separated group, brought back "by this means". So "only when" holds for the followers the sentence names.
