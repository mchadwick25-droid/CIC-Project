## Worded by me: checked, holding

- 1 (no year for bishop) and 2 (no year for death): hold. "Eight years after" is Gregory of Nyssa's (Macrina 180). His "after this" follows Basil's election and his ordaining of Peter, so the sheet's "after his election" is a fair reading, told only "if needed". The council of 381 is a settled date (Kit Part 1). Its creed's words hold (`npnf214...xml` 13395).
- 3 (Sasima, not Nyssa): holds. Or. 43.59 gives "the multiplication of the Bishops" and "appendage to this scheme", with the pain "even time has not obliterated". Socrates names Sasima (`npnf202...xml` 21065-21066). The confidence note is right.
- 5 (Beat 6): holds. The book's sentence is at `npnf208...xml` 14168-14170. "Teach him" narrows Basil's "us", but the plain-sense line restores the church, so it is left alone.
- 6 ("Gregory says", defending him): holds (Or. 43.68-69, 42927-42971).
- 7 (Letter 223): holds ("my slanderers ... sullying my reputation among the brethren", 39105-39112).
- 8 (Letter 94): holds ("those who are besieging your impartial ears ... what harm the government suffers from me", 30010-30016). It gives no charge beyond this, as Do not say requires.
- 9 (the bishops' envy): holds ("the envious struggles of the prelates of his native land", 42050-42052). The credit sits on the blame.
- 10, 11, 13, 14: hold. They are directions to the writer and add no claim.
- 12 (fewer credits): holds by Kit Part 1. Pontus, the famine, the old bishop's death, the house and the lepers are facts Gregory reports, so they are told plainly. The credits stay on the blame, the dialogue, the complaint, the defence, the funeral deaths, the differing accounts and the late witness.
- 15 (Words to explain): holds. The Sp4 gloss is the negation of the opponents' view (10556-10558). "A meeting of bishops" is a plain gloss.
- 16 (Who tells it): holds, apart from finding 1.
