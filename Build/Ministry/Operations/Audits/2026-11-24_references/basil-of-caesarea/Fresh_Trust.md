## MINOR, noted, no change

1. **Pontus before the priesthood.** Gregory's line on founding and leading (43.29) comes at the withdrawal after the quarrel. The brief tells it before the priesthood, then "went back to Pontus". The order still stands. Basil's own Letter 14 (`208` 23988-23996: "I departed into Pontus in quest of a place to live in") and Rufinus (30043-30050: "Basilius Ponti urbes et rura circumiens… monasteria construere") put an earlier life in Pontus, and community building there, before he was bishop. No change.
2. **"What turned him" in his own telling.** In Letter 223 the reading of the gospel comes after the waking, as its next step. Since the opening has already told the waking, the compression is fair. If the reviser touches it anyway, the exact change is "In his own telling, it was the gospel: he read in it of selling goods and sharing with the poor." Under the batch ruling, a note only.
