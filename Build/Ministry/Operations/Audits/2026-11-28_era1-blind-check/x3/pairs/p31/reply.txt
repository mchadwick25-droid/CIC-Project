Verdict: Y is clearly better.

Reasons:
1. Y tells one story, in order, built around a single scene. The dream ("held his hand open and flat, like a sword blade") is set up early and pays off at the death: "on the very day of the year... on which his sentence had been shown him." X is a list of episodes.
2. Y credits its sources cleanly where they differ: "Pontius says he turned down friends' offers... But Cyprian's own last letter says..." X does this too ("The old writers differ on what turned him"), but it names three witnesses in two sentences, two of them called Gregory.
3. Y ends on its subject's own words of grief. X ends on a later writer, Benedict.

X faults:
- It tells things twice. "he died before a council... declared it in its creed" comes back as "In 381... a council at Constantinople declared." The lodging appears in paragraph 2 and again in paragraph 6.
- The opening sentence is overloaded: "defending himself in a letter against men who, he wrote, were spreading lies about him."
- "His accusers were filling the governor's ears" comes with no reason or context.
- The doxology paragraph ("with the Son, together with the Holy Spirit, and through the Son, in the Holy Spirit") is too dense to follow in one hearing.
- It has too many names (Macrina, Rufinus, Valens, Benedict, two Gregorys).

Y faults:
- Paragraph 2 lists the coming events in advance ("went into exile by choice, heard Cyprian's dream there, and was with him on his last night").
- "Back from Curubis" never says why he came back.
- "a bishop should confess his faith in the city where he leads the church" doesn't make clear why that meant leaving.
- "a notable volume" is a weak quote that adds little.

Grades: X C+, Y B+.
