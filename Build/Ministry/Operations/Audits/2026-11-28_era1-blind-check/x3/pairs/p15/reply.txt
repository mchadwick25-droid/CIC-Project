Y is clearly better.

Reasons:
1. Y opens on a real scene ("As he lay trembling under the altar, the bishop, John Chrysostom, climbed into the pulpit") and names John inside it. X opens on Noetus and holds Hippolytus back until paragraph two.
2. Y tells one story in order, Antioch to Constantinople to the Oak to exile to his death, and credits each source where they differ ("Socrates says they removed him from office only for refusing to come... they tell it differently"). X jumps around and repeats itself.
3. Y explains what it names ("the Arians, who taught that the Son of God had a beginning"). X leaves its Easter point murky: "some keeping the moon's fourteenth day."

Faults in X: it tells the Sardinia exile twice ("In 235 he was sent to Sardinia" / "In 235... were deported to Sardinia"). It also tells the book twice and the martyr's day twice ("Rome kept his day among its martyrs" / "kept them both on 13 August"). The summary paragraph runs ahead of the story. "a later bishop there quoted him" is vague. The Damasus close is tangled ("Yet that split came later..."), and it ends on a later writer. Sentences are dense and abstract by ear ("one God, in two persons, with the grace of the Holy Spirit as a third").

Faults in Y: the return of his body is told twice ("came back only when his body came home" / "those who had left the church... came back"). In "They decked the altar," it is unclear who "they" are, and so is "they" in "they tell it differently." "trembling" may be a detail added for effect. The Gothic-general episode is never tied to his fall.

Grades: X: C-. Y: B+.
