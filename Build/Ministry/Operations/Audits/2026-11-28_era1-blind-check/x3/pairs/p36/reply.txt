Y is clearly better.

Reasons:
1. Y opens on a living scene, "As he lay trembling under the altar, the bishop, John Chrysostom, climbed into the pulpit", and then tells one story in order through to the body's return. X's story moves in a loop: doctrine, break, letter, break, doctrine, split, books.
2. Y explains what it names, in a clause each time: "the Arians, who taught that the Son of God had a beginning"; "the altar, where the bread of Communion lies". X names "Unspeakable and Silence... Father and Truth, then to Word and Life" but never says why they mattered to anyone.
3. Both credit their sources well where the sources differ. Y does it inside the action: "Socrates says they removed him from office only for refusing to come... they tell it differently."

X faults: things are told twice. The break comes in "Tertullian alone says he broke with the church" and again in paragraph 7. The two branches come in "carried the school on in two branches" and again in "split into an Italian branch". The doctrine is in paragraphs 2 and 9. It gives the ending before going back: "until writings... were found in Egypt in 1945." The letter quote is split across paragraphs 1 and 5. "Under one bishop and stayed until the time of a third" is unclear to a listener. "Almost everything known of Valentinus comes from men who wrote against him" is a how-we-know sentence, and it comes late.

Y faults: paragraph 2 gives the ending and then goes back: "came back only when his body came home". The return is then told again in paragraph 17. "His sermons were read for centuries" is a summing-up. "Some thought" gives no source. "John was silenced" is vague. In "they tell it differently", "they" is unclear.

Grades: X C, Y B+.
