Y is clearly better.

Reasons:
1. Y tells a story people act in. "He had deacons... seize the man and hold his mouth shut" and the Euphrates line ("real water, the kind you can touch with your hand") hold a listener. X is mostly a report on the record: "Irenaeus says... Tertullian says... Hippolytus says."
2. Y credits differing sources well: "The historian Socrates says... Another historian, Sozomen, says instead..."
3. Y explains what it names ("deacons, the clergy who serve under priests"; "the sacraments, the church's holy rites"). X lists "Unspeakable and Silence... Father and Truth... Word and Life" without saying what these powers meant to anyone.

Faults, X:
- Paragraph 2 summarizes, then the script tells it all again: "Tertullian alone says he broke with the church" is followed by the break in full later, and "two branches" comes back as "an Italian branch... and an Eastern one."
- "For eighteen centuries... found in Egypt in 1945" jumps to the ending, then goes back.
- "almost everything known of Valentinus comes from men who wrote against him" comes late, not early.
- Paragraphs 13–15 are a list of books.
- The close ("Valentinus himself was now nowhere") trails off on followers.

Faults, Y:
- "Later writers, from Augustine to a council at Nicaea..." is told twice (paragraphs 3 and 15).
- The order breaks: "In old age he carried the same fight..." comes before "He wrote the Panarion about 375."
- "about 375" may rest on an editor's dating.
- "He had not, and had judged them by report" is told as fact with no witness credited.

Grades: X C; Y B.
