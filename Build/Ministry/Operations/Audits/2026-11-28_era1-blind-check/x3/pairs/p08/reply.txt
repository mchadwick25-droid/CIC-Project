Y is clearly better.

Reasons:
1. Y's opening image ("a medicine chest") runs through the whole script and comes back at the close ("in his chest of cures: the church"). X opens on a good line, then tells the ending in paragraph 3 ("tortured, held two years in prison and put to death about 310") and goes back to his life.
2. Y credits sources where they differ: "Socrates says Theophilus wanted revenge... Sozomen says instead..." X does it once ("Photius... says Pamphilus wrote most of it in prison"), but its long money-changer quote is hard to follow by ear: "at once they drench him in the shame of the heretics."
3. Y is concrete and vivid: "It was not spiritual water but real water," and "hold his mouth shut."

X faults: the death and the Defense are both told twice ("the two friends wrote a Defense of Origen together" / "Eusebius says he and Pamphilus wrote the Defense"). "In the persecution" is never explained. "He refused and ignored his threats" has an unclear pronoun. "Eusebius took Pamphilus's name as a surname" is never explained. Paragraph 5 is a loose aside. The last two paragraphs, on Jerome, run out of order and end on a later writer.

Y faults: paragraph 3 previews things told later ("Augustine to a council at Nicaea", "Jerusalem and Constantinople"), so they are told twice. "people led astray ever since Adam" is unclear. It names a lot of people (Theophilus, Socrates, Sozomen, John Chrysostom, the Long Brothers, Augustine, Jerome). "about 375" may rest on a modern reckoning.

X: C+
Y: B+
