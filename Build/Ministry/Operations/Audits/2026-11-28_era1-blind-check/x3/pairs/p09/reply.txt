Y is clearly better.

Reasons:
1. Y opens on a live scene with a clear question: "As he lay trembling under the altar, the bishop, John Chrysostom, climbed into the pulpit and preached against him." X opens on a long quote set up as a report ("Eusebius of Caesarea wrote of how...").
2. Y credits its witnesses where they differ: "Socrates says they removed him from office only for refusing to come... and they tell it differently." It keeps Palladius's bias in view: "who wrote John's life and took his side."
3. X gives the ending first and then goes back: "tortured, held two years in prison and put to death about 310" comes before the persecution scene. Y moves forward in order.

Faults in X: the death and the torture are told twice (para 2, then paras 7 and 11). Jerome's book-buying and copying are spread over three places. The pronouns in "He refused and ignored his threats" are unclear. The money-changer passage is dense and hard to follow by ear ("But Origen's accusers think that they alone have the skill..."). "Eusebius took Pamphilus's name as a surname" is never explained. "about 310" may rest on an editor's dating. It ends on a later writer, Jerome.

Faults in Y: the essence paragraph gives away the ending, "came back only when his body came home," and the last line then repeats it. "His sermons were read for centuries" is summary. "They decked the altar" has an unclear "they", and the sentence around it is nested. "Some thought" names no witness. "they tell it differently" has an unclear "they".

X: C-
Y: B+
