Verdict: Y (Basil) is somewhat better than X (Josephus).

Reasons
1. Y tells one life in order and keeps its conflict going: the emperor's threat, "a man who owns only rags and a few books cannot be robbed," and the rival bishop who "once blocked his road with armed men." X breaks the order. Paragraph 2 jumps to Rome and the year 93, then goes "Back in the cave." That is the ending told first and then a step back.
2. Y credits its sources where they differ, cleanly: "The old writers differ on what turned him… His brother, Gregory of Nyssa, says… while the historian Rufinus says…" X's crediting is just as careful ("by chance or by God's providence, he writes"). But its second half turns into a report about the record (Origen, Eusebius, Jerome) rather than a story.
3. Y explains what it names in passing: "rhetoric, the art of public speaking," and "a council, a meeting of bishops." X's glosses interrupt the story: "Its key word, the Christ, means…" comes before the quote, and "The Greeks are those who were not Jewish" comes after it.

Faults in X
- "foretold that the general would be emperor. When that came true": a wonder told in the narrator's voice.
- "a few lines tell of John… James… Jesus": each passage is then told again, so the content comes twice.
- The quarrel over the Jesus passage is previewed ("give in different words") and only resolved much later with Jerome.
- "in the north of the country": the country is never named.
- "The passage itself says he was the Christ": repeats the quote.
- "sent out in his own day": unclear whose day.

Faults in Y
- The 381 creed is told twice: "died before a council… declared it" and "In 381… declared in its creed."
- The lodging for the poor and sick is told twice.
- "out of envy" is credited, but "Lepers… Basil greeted them as brothers and tended them himself" is praise told as fact.
- "The rich gave to it at his urging" has no source.
- The two-form doxology line is hard to follow on one hearing.
- It ends on a later writer, Benedict, rather than on Basil.

Grades: X: C+. Y: B−.
