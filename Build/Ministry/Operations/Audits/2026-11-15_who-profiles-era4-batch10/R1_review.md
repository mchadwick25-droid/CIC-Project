**Verdict: passes with minor fixes.** The coin scene, the cupped hand and the death follow the Life closely. The dates are owned by name and the partisan Life is marked once.

Fix: "After eleven months, on a feast day, Constantine cried out in the palace that another man was emperor, Stephen of Auxentius." (+4)

**R1-M8 (brief para 5: "some with noses, eyes or hands cut away").** PG100 l.95761-95762: "nares abscissæ … effossi oculi … amputatæ manus". Eyes were put out, not cut away. Fix: "some with noses or hands cut off or eyes put out, for refusing to sign." (+3) With M7 the brief runs 371 words.

**R1-M9 (brief para 7: Theophanes's year).** The brief says Theophanes "gives 20 November" with no year. He places it in the annal Goar's Latin numbers A.C. 757 (THEOPH l.78706), which the card gives. Leaving the year to the card is acceptable, since the brief already shows four placings by name. If the coordinator wants it in the brief: "Theophanes the Confessor, a chronicler, gives 20 November, under the year his Latin edition numbers 757." (+8)

**R1-M10 (card `dates` and `uncertain[2]`: "his fifty-third year").** The edition notes a variant at the line, PG100 l.97224: "Regius Codex non habet" τρίτῳ (the scan garbles the Greek word as "tptrty"), so one manuscript reads his fiftieth year. **R1-M11 (card `uncertain[1]`: two witnesses' words merged).** "Theophanes and Nikephoros's Short History both say he was accused of drawing people from their homes, their kin and the palace into the monastic life." Only the Short History says homes, kin and palaces (PG100 l.81475-81481). Fix: "Theophanes says he was accused of urging many into the monastic life and away from court honors and riches. Nikephoros's Short History says he was accused of drawing people from their homes, their kin and the palace into it."

**R1-M12 (card `uncertain[3]`: a reason the editors do not give).** "The editors of Theophanes reject this, since the Life has him die in his fifty-third year." The editors cite the Acta Sanctorum, not the Life, THEOPH l.131112-131115: "(non vero post annos LX in clausura exactos, ut scribit Theophanes) … Vide Acta SS., tom. VIII Octobris". Fix: "The editors of Theophanes reject this and place his death in his fifty-third year."

**R1-M13 (card `legacy.traces[0]`: "tells most of the history of the iconoclasts").** PG100 l.88605: "pleraque ad historiam Iconoclastarum spectantia … luculenter enarrantur", much that bears on that history. Fix: "…tells much of the history of the iconoclasts clearly, say its Benedictine editors…"

**R1-M14 (brief para 6: "by Saint Pelagius's church").** The Life says the church had once stood there, and Constantine made the ruin a graveyard for the condemned, PG100 l.97176-97180: "ubi nimirum sancti martyris Pelagii templum olim erat, quod cum lapsum … damnatorum sepulcrum effecit". Fix: "His body was thrown into the criminals' pit where Saint Pelagius's church had stood." (+1)

## nikephoros-of-constantinople

**Verdict: two blocking points (B3, B4) and minor fixes.** The crowning scene, the debate with Leo and the night of the exile are faithful. The brief places Ignatius's lapse early. But three dates are told as settled where held editors disagree.

**R1-B3. The crowning, the exile and the death: held placings missing (brief paras 1, 2, 6 and 8; card `dates`, `uncertain`; records).**
- Brief: "In 813 Patriarch Nikephoros crowned…"; "led the church of Constantinople from 806 to 815"; "One night in March 815"; "He died in exile in 828." Card `dates` gives only 813, 815 and 828.
- Exile. Goar's Notitia puts the deposition at the end of Lent 816, THEOPH l.364-365: "Ad quadragesimæ finem anno 816 indict. 9 Nicephoro tendit insidias, et sede abdicat". The Bollandists' own note to the Life of Methodios gives 814, PG100 l.102222-102223: "Sanctus Nicephorus in exsilium pulsus est anno 814 die 13 Martii". Fabricius (PG100 l.305) and the Bollandists' commentary (l.1672) give 815.
- Crowning. Goar reports Baronius and an anonymous Greek chronicle giving 814, in June, THEOPH l.350-353: "Nicephoro diadema capiti imponente, tyrannidem invaserat anno Christi 814, Junii die 42, indictione septima, prout Baronius, et Anonymus Græcus … affirmant, nonnihil a Theophane nostro variantes". The scan reads "42". The Bollandists reckon 813 from Theophanes (PG100 l.4643-4644), and Mai has 813 (l.14465).
- Death. Goar, in his preface to Nikephoros's short chronology, PG100 l.85133-85138: "Baronius … ab anno nimirum 806 ad 828, hoc postremo mortuum scribit, qui tamen sequentis 829 mensem Junium attigit". The writer compares his text with Camerarius, Contius and Scaliger (l.85153-85160), which l.82718-82724 names as Goar's edition of 1652.
- Fix, brief para 1: "In 813 Patriarch Nikephoros crowned the new emperor, Leo the Armenian, in the Great Church." → "When Leo the Armenian took the throne, Patriarch Nikephoros crowned him in the Great Church." (0)
- Fix, brief para 2: "Nikephoros led the church of Constantinople from 806 to 815." → "Nikephoros had led the church of Constantinople since 806." (−1)
- Fix, brief para 6: "One night in March 815, Leo sent soldiers to take the sick patriarch into exile. Ignatius says he rose" → "One night in March, Leo sent soldiers to take the sick patriarch into exile. The Bollandists, editors of saints' Lives, date it to 815, and Jacques Goar, who edited Theophanes, to 816. Ignatius says the patriarch rose" (+18). The patriarch replaces "he", which would otherwise point at Goar.
- Fix, brief para 8: "He died in exile in 828." → "He died in exile in 828, or in 829 by Goar's count." (+6)
- To hold the length: para 3, cut "Nikephoros became an imperial secretary too." (−6; the card's `knownFor` keeps it) and change "which restored the images, he spoke for them" to "which restored the images, Nikephoros spoke for them" (otherwise "he" points at his father). Para 7: "In exile, in monasteries he had built, he wrote against the iconoclasts." → "In exile he wrote books against the iconoclasts." (−4; "books" gives "he asks in one" its antecedent; the card's `place` keeps the monasteries). With B4 the brief runs 377 words, FK 8.04, Flesch 62.66.
- Fix, card `dates`: "Born about 758, by the Bollandists. Consecrated patriarch on Easter Day, 12 April 806, by Fabricius and the Bollandists. Crowned Leo V on 11 July, in the sixth indiction, by Theophanes the Confessor, which the Bollandists reckon as 813; Jacques Goar reports Baronius and an anonymous Greek chronicle as giving June 814. Exiled in 815 by Fabricius, on 13 March 815 by the Bollandists; their notes to the Life of Methodios give 13 March 814, and Goar the end of Lent 816. Died in exile on 2 June 828, in the fourteenth year of his exile, by the Bollandists; Fabricius and Angelo Mai also give 828, Mai at seventy, and Goar counts on into June 829. His body was brought back to the city in 846, by the Bollandists; Theophanes the priest says it had lain nineteen years"
- Fix, card `uncertain`: add after the first item: "His dates. The Bollandists give 815 for his exile and 828 for his death. Their own note to the Life of Methodios gives 814 for the exile. Jacques Goar puts the exile in 816 and the death in June 829, and reports Baronius and an anonymous chronicle as putting the crowning in 814, not 813."
