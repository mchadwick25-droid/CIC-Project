# Opus check of the Who scan, era 3 (2026-11-11)

Five drafting workers (Sonnet) sorted the candidates by region and opened the cited lines. One Opus subagent then opened the cited lines in `cic/texts/` for every WHO, STORY, WANTED and LISTED row (116 rows) and the 33 draft councils. ## Result

- Rows: 98 confirmed, 18 corrected, 0 not supported. No invented person-detail found.
