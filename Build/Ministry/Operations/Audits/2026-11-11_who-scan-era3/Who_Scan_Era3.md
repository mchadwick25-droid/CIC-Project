# Who scan, era 3 (2026-11-11)

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-11). Read-only scan of era 3 (451–622 CE) of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Final counts, 98 people: **WHO 64, STORY 17, LISTED 11, WANTED 6**. Judgment-call rows: 63. Sixteen further rows are carried to the era 4 scan and are not counted (see "Carried to era 4"). Councils are not people and carry no verdict (see Councils below). All five live person entries (Simeon of Beth Arsham, Peter the Iberian, Gregory the Great, Patrick, Justinian I) are WHO, so no live profile is at risk.

## How the list was built

- Era 3 has 24 census entries: 19 movements and 5 person entries (Simeon of Beth Arsham, Peter the Iberian, Gregory the Great, Patrick, Justinian I).
- Named voices and people lists of the 19 movements, merged by alias. The Armenian Christianity before the Bagratids entry names nobody in its text fields.
- Carried from era 2 by date: Reccared (LISTED in era 2, now WHO) and Euthymius the Great (LISTED in era 2, now WHO as a hagiographer's record).
- Held under the old rules and re-sorted: Kaleb (WHO, judgment call), Heraclius (STORY; the Ekthesis is held in Latin in Mansi 10, l.100125-100714).
- Found in text: Procopius, Evagrius Scholasticus, Cassiodorus, Baudonivia, Brunhild, Médard, Jonas of Bobbio, John of Biclaro, Anastasius I, Elias of Jerusalem, Pelagius II, Marcellinus Comes, Agathias, Zachariah Rhetor, Joshua the Stylite, Sophronius, George and Antony of Choziba, and others the movement texts name.
- Left out: Nino (fourth century), Koriun (writes before 451), Tirechan (era 4 by date, not named in an era 3 entry). Elfoddw (d. 809) stays LISTED, outside the window.

## Rulings applied (Mark, 2026-11-11)

- Standard as in eras 1 and 2, including recorders, women, founders through disciples, and the standing OUTSIDE WITNESS label.
- Rulers: "Story unless own voice". Official texts alone make a STORY; an own corpus or a personal confession makes a WHO. Justinian and Reccared are WHO; Anastasius I, Heraclius, Theodoric, Huneric, Clovis and Geiseric are STORY.
- People named in era 3 entries who fall after 622: "Carry to era 4". Thirteen provisional rows, scored in the era 4 scan.
- Marcellinus Comes: "Recorder, no label". WHO as a recorder; the OUTSIDE WITNESS label stays on Procopius, Agathias and al-Baladhuri.
- Theodora: "Who".
- Coordinator's call under settled rules (Mark may overrule): Moses of Khoren is WHO as a recorder with his date tagged Contested.
- Pairing (Mark, 2026-11-11, after this scan merged): "if two people are tied together in their sources with similar identity its ok to make that a single who". Barsanuphius and John of Gaza are now one paired WHO row (previously two rows).
- Moved to era 4 (same date): Mennas (brother of Benjamin I, STORY), al-Tabari and Ibn Ishaq (both WANTED), all era 4 by date. Counts before the move and the merge: 104 people, WHO 65, STORY 19, LISTED 12, WANTED 8; judgment-call rows 67.
- Councils: "counsels are treated like a tradition with a different icon and title. but people who are a part of it should be on the who click" (Mark, era 2).

- Adomnan of Iona vs Adamnan of Coldingham (Bede, bede_...giles1903.txt ~11317).
- Jonas of Bobbio vs Jonas of Orleans (not held, but same name) and the prophet Jonah.
- David of Wales vs King David of the Psalms (psalm files, welsh hymns).
- Elbodug of Gwynedd (d.809) vs the earlier Elvod bishop of Bangor 755 (nennius_history-of-the-britons-giles.txt line 46 note).
