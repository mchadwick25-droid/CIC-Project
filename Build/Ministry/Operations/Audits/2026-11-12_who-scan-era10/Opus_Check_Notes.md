# Era 10 Who scan: independent check notes (Opus)

Read-only check of rows_A.md to rows_E.md (142 rows: A 30, B 29, C 31, D 35, E 17). No repo edits, no paid calls. Rows are named by batch and person (no row numbers in the files).

Method. Every file key used by a WHO, STORY or LISTED row was resolved in cic/texts (all resolve). The cited lines were read with sed/grep and quotes compared verbatim (OCR spelling as printed; line-break hyphens and doubled spaces counted as CONFIRMED). For WANTED, about 180 name and work strings (incl. Cyrillic forms of the Russian and Ukrainian names, Polish and Spanish diacritics) were searched in cic/texts contents, cic/texts filenames and cic/corpus-map. Every non-zero hit was opened: all are other people, ordinary words, the Mass prayer "Orate Fratres", or ATLAS-TARGETS.md rows naming the era 10 traditions as survey candidates (no source). Earlier-era overlap: grep of the era 1-7 and 9 Who_Scan files and Era8_Carry_Rows.md.
