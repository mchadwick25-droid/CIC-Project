# Who scan, era 10 (2026-11-12)

Approved to proceed (coordinator, under Mark's full-auto instruction of 2026-11-12). Read-only scan of era 10 (1906 to about 2010, "The Global Church Era") of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Counts, 244 rows (a paired row counts once): **WHO 6, STORY 2, LISTED 108, WANTED 128**. Of the LISTED rows, 102 are living people or people of unknown status, listed only until death under Mark's ruling of 2026-11-12; the other LISTED rows are scored as before.

Judgment-call rows: 57. Major "yes" marks: 48 on WANTED rows (none can reach the map until a source is held) and 1 on WHO rows. Era 10 has no live person entries in the census (all 71 entries are movements, every `people` list is empty), so nothing is flagged on that count. Councils carry no table; gatherings are named in the rows of their participants and new ones are listed as leads. The Library holds almost nothing after 1906: 128 of the 244 rows are WANTED. No row carries the OUTSIDE WITNESS label, because no secular, Jewish, Muslim, Asian or African historian of these churches is held for the period.

## How the list was built

- Era 10 has 71 census entries (all movements). Named voices and `people` lists of the entries (old rules), merged by alias.
- Carried forward from the era 9 audit: Oldham, Azariah, Cheng Jingyi, Mott, Tikhon (Bellavin), Kivebulaya (a WANTED row here), E. Kolodziejczyk (1911) and Underwood's Call of Korea (1908; his row stays in era 9).
- Found in text and corpus-map: shapers named in the era 10 census texts and the corpus-map rows of these entries.
- Five drafting workers (Sonnet) sorted the candidates by tradition family and opened the cited lines. One Opus subagent then opened the cited lines for every WHO, STORY and WANTED row, read every LISTED row, and checked every death tag (`Opus_Check_Notes.md`).

## Rulings applied

- The standard of eras 1 to 9 (Decision-Log 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12), including the Melanchthon rule: where a person's own held work plus a following or held witnesses carries a tellable life, the row is WHO with a thin life flagged, not WANTED.
- Recorders are WHO with a thin own life flagged (era 2). Hostile inside writers are WHO as recorders only where a life can be told. They are never an outside witness. Popes and rulers are STORY unless they have their own voice and a held text. Pairs are one judgment-call row.
- Era is by date of main work: works mainly before 1906 go to era 9, listed under "Other eras by date" with no verdict.
- Living people and recent deaths (Mark's rulings of 2026-11-12, see "Mark's rulings" below): a living person, or a person whose death cannot be confirmed, is LISTED only until death. A person whose death in 1990 or later is stated in a held source or in the census is scored like anyone else. A person certainly dead before 1990 by common knowledge is scored and tagged "death not in a held source (Widely Accepted)" (coordinator call, kept).
- "Major?" column (proposal only). The map shows up to 4 names per tradition and 5 per river, chosen from Whos. WANTED "yes" marks cannot reach the map until a source is held.
