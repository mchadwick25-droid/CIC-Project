# R1 changes: era 4 batch 8

Every round 1 finding is listed with what was done. The drafter reopened each cited line before applying the fix. Briefs were rescored with `engine.m1.gates.grade_text`, and the audio was rebuilt from each brief with `[calmly] ` at the start and `[warmly] ` at the start of the last paragraph. Every quotation in the briefs and cards was checked again by script against its file, whitespace- and hyphen-tolerant. The one mismatch left is a page break and running head between "my" and "mind" in Bugga's closing line (BONI l.2451-2459), which the records note.

Length. While round 1 ran, Mark ruled (2026-11-14, relayed by the coordinator) that non-major briefs run to about 2:15, about 365 words, at most: "no stop, how much of the story will we loose, i would rather have a 2;15 good story than a 2;00 cramped but flat card". An earlier relay of a 2:00 limit was withdrawn. The Bugga, Adam and Yahya briefs, over the limit after the fixes, were tightened without dropping a scene or a turning point. All six now run 350 to 368 words.
