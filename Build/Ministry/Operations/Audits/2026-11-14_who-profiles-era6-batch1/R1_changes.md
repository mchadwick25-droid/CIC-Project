# R1 changes: era 6 batch 1

Round 1 found 8 blocking and 100 minor points. Every point was applied. Before each fix the cited lines were reopened in `cic/texts/`. After the fixes the briefs were rescored with `engine.m1.gates.grade_text`, the audio scripts were rebuilt from the briefs, and every quotation in the briefs and cards was rechecked by script against its file, allowing for whitespace, case, hyphen line-breaks and punctuation. Four quotations need an allowance: two for OCR forms ("naturalhj", "tlie") and two for footnote marks in the edition. Where a fix took a brief past the 2:30 cap of about 405 words, other words were cut and no scene was lost. Those cuts, and every wording that differs from the reviewer's, are listed below.
