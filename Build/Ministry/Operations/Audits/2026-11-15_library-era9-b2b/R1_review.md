## Blocking

- **B1. `roberts-robert_christendom-astray_1884.txt`.** The header called the OCR's preface year "garbled"; line
  290 prints "December 18, 1888" cleanly. 1883 is supported inside the file (line 82; line 24946). The agreeing
  date is the archive.org record's, a reader's upload, not a library catalogue's.
- **B2. `cabot_memoir-of-ralph-waldo-emerson-v1_1887.txt`.** The Publisher line named Houghton, Mifflin; the
  title page prints only "Cambridge, Printed at the Riverside Press, 1887".
- **B3. `smith-hannah-whitall_unselfishness-of-god_1903.txt`.** The contents list starts at line 83, not 95.
- **B4. `marchant_dr-john-clifford_1924.txt`.** The Rights line called 1924 the imprint date. The title page
  prints no year; 1924 comes from "First published 1924" (line 252) and the introduction's date (line 720).
- **B5. `cartwright_autobiography-of-peter-cartwright_strickland1856.txt`.** The title page names Hitchcock and
  Walden, and Nelson and Phillips; the notice of 1856 is Carlton & Porter's. A later printing by another house,
  with no date of its own, presented as 1856.

## Not blocking

- Taylor, the Kaiserswerth account, Monod in French, Elaw: a publisher's name or address in the header is not in

- Elaw: OCR poor in stretches; the break at the end is as stated.
- Graves: the record says Texarkana 1928, the scan shows Memphis 1880, as the header says.
- Tiffany: the Rights line said both "the title page prints no year" and "the scan has no title page".
- Wiseman's Appeal: a later impression ("nth Thousand") under the 1850 title page.
- Dialogues on Prophecy: a short Google notice remains at lines 13-21, as "nothing was stripped" says.
- Cardale: the "Part III" heading is at line 19882; its title page starts at 19870.
