Numbering: one number per sentence of narration. A quoted speech that runs to two sentences inside one tag is one unit (units 35 and 36).

## Quotation check

All 20 quotations were checked against the RC final readings, by eye and by an exact-substring test. All 20 match character for character. A full stop is turned into a comma before a speech tag in five places; that is normal.

One quotation hides a cut by ear: unit 24 ("So I too expect to be plotted against,"). The settled sentence goes on: "and fastened to the wood by one of those I have named. Or perhaps by Crescens". The brief stops the quotation mid-sentence. It then adds "perhaps by Crescens" in its own voice, so the omitted words "by one of those I have named. Or" disappear. A listener hears one unbroken sentence in which Justin names only Crescens. See unit 24.

No other quotation has a cut inside it.

## Listener read, heard once through

1. **Where a global listener gets lost.** "Flavia Neapolis" and "the Samaritans" come with no anchor. Nobody is told who the Samaritans are, or that the city is today's Nablus (the modern name is only in the ANF introduction, ANF 13851). "Marcion" in the last paragraph is never explained, so the line about the lost book means nothing to the ear. In paragraph 7, "he wrote, perhaps by Crescens" blurs Justin's voice and the narrator's. In unit 31 the quotation has no tag. "Rusticus, the prefect of Rome" needs one word ("the city's governor").
2. **The trial.** By ear the trial is clear and in the right order. The two compressions (units 34 and 36) make the dialogue sound tighter than the record, but they do not mislead about what was said.
3. **Legacy is thin.** The brief ends on the burial and then two bookkeeping lines: a lost book was quoted, and other books survived. Neither says what came of him. Nothing in the close tells the listener that his pupils carried his teaching. Tatian was "a hearer of Justin's" (Irenaeus, ANF 33663–33668), and Euelpistus heard him gladly. Nothing says that Eusebius held that he "stood out above the rest" (RC Q30). Nothing says that he taught that the Word was in every people, so that whatever was said well belongs to Christians (RC Q17, Q18). Nothing mentions his account of Sunday worship (RC Q19). `Writer_Sheet.md` ranks these as I2–I9. The ending has no weight. The emotional peak is the burial, and the legacy paragraph is the weakest in the brief.
