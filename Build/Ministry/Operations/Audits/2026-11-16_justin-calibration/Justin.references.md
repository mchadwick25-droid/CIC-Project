## What is uncertain

- **Whether Crescens caused his death.** Justin expected a plot. His hearer Tatian says Crescens tried to have him killed. Eusebius and Jerome say Crescens succeeded. The record of the trial names no accuser and does not name Crescens. Contested (D2).
- **When he died.** The trial record names the prefect, Rusticus, but no emperor and no year. Eusebius sets the death under Marcus Aurelius and Lucius Verus; Jerome's *Chronicle* puts the Crescens entry under Antoninus Pius; Epiphanius says Hadrian. Contested (D1). Epiphanius alone gives an age, thirty, which clashes with Justin's own works. Inferential-Thin (D15).
- **His people.** He calls the Samaritans "my people" and calls himself uncircumcised. Whether his family were Samaritans in religion, or Greeks or Romans living in Samaria, he does not say. The story keeps to his words (D5).
- **The century.** "Second century" is modern reckoning. The "hundred and fifty years" is his own figure (S3, C10).
- **Two accounts of his turning.** The old man by the sea, and the Christians unafraid of death. He gives both and does not join them (D7). The old man and the place are not named (N2).
- **The words against Marcion.** The book is lost. Its words reach us second-hand, through the English of Irenaeus's Latin (Q25, N11).
- **The record of the trial.** Anonymous, its date not stated; Widely Accepted for the hearing before Rusticus and the beheading at Rome (M12, M16). The text questions six companions; the printed heading names four (D9). The bath by his lodging has different names in the witnesses, so the story leaves it out (D10). One word in his answer about heaven is unsure in the manuscripts; the story does not use it (D8).
- **"Plotted against."** Justin's sentence goes on to name a punishment, which may be the stake or the stocks (D11). The story ends the quotation before it.
- **Whether the emperor answered.** Not recorded (N13).
- **The opening quotation.** Tertullian's phrase, in the sheet's final reading (Q29), has the same wording as one of the sheet's headings; it is quoted as the final reading.
