# Opus checks of the Who scan, era 2 (2026-11-11)

Four Opus subagent passes read the cited vendored passages in `cic/texts/`. Each row got CONFIRMED, CORRECTED or NOT SUPPORTED. Corrections were applied to the table before the final rulings.

## Pass 3: the outside witnesses (10 rows)

Josephus, Pliny, Suetonius and Ammianus were corrected, and Libanius and Julian were corrected in their evidence. The "about OCR line" numbers in the vendored file headers run 10 to 12 lines below the real lines; the table uses the real lines.

- Josephus: Testimonium at lines 81405–81419, James at 91005–91010. Whiston prints the full traditional wording with nothing marked as added; the file's header calls it disputed and Whiston's Dissertation I (from line 138231) argues it genuine.
- Pliny: no Pliny file, but the full English of Ep. 10.96 and Trajan's reply is inside McGiffert's note 891 to Eusebius III.33 (npnf201, lines 19451–19533). The source wanted is an edition of Pliny, Book 10.
- Suetonius: Claudius 25.4 English at 25908, Nero 16.2 Latin at 28459 and English at 28524 (Rolfe).
- Ammianus: XXII.5 is Julian restoring the temples and summoning the divided Christian bishops, not the recall of exiles.
- Libanius: letters "Libanius to Basil", genuineness questioned, are held in npnf208 (Letters 335–359).
- Celsus: Origen is unsure who Celsus was (two of the name, both Epicureans).
- Porphyry: the direct fragment is Eusebius HE VI.19; Augustine's material is from Porphyry's other works.
- Confirmed: Tacitus (Annals 15.44), Lucian of Samosata (Peregrine, line 1033), Julian (Against the Galilaeans is not held).

## Pass 4: the councils section (12 councils)

4 confirmed (Tyre, Sardica, Constantinople 381, Seleucia-Ctesiphon), 8 corrected.

- Nicaea: Jacob of Nisibis is in the held subscription lists, so attendance is attested.
- Ariminum and Seleucia: Aetius was deposed at the Constantinople synod of 360, not at Seleucia; Hilary's De synodis (npnf209) dates before both councils.
- Aquileia: npnf210 holds only the editor's summary of Letters 9–12; the acts are in Mansi tomus 3 and partly in Kauffmann.
- Carthage 411: Bruns's Codex canonum does not hold the acts; Mansi tomus 4 does.
- Diospolis: Jerome and Orosius were absent; the accusers (Heros, Lazarus) were absent too. The acts are lost; only Augustine's De gestis is held.
- Ephesus 431 and 449: Pulcheria was not a participant. Chalcedon: no correction.
- Tyre: its own acts are not held, only the documents in Athanasius, Socrates, Sozomen and Mansi.
- Missing councils named in era 2 census text were added to a list: Gangra, Rome 377, Paris, the Donatist council on Tyconius, Saragossa, Toledo 400, Constantinople 383, the African councils of 418 and the Persian synod of 424. Constantinople 360 was added as a row.

## Systematic findings

1. Header line numbers in the vendored files run 10 to 12 lines off from the real lines.
2. "Held" and "not held" claims were wrong in several rows (Koriun held; Lactantius De mortibus held; Damasus's letters in Theodoret; the Avellana holds no Damasus letters).
3. Two witnesses are often one: Socrates and Sozomen copy Rufinus; Jerome and Augustine worked together against Pelagius.
4. Namesake traps in `cic/texts/`: Evagrius Scholasticus, Theophilus of Antioch, Julian of Norwich, Sarah Osborn, Gennadius Scholarius, Marcellinus Comes, the Ephrata files, the Basilian files, Antony of Choziba; and inside the scan, the Melitian Callistus (not Callistus of Rome) and Donatus of Carthage (not Donatus of Casae Nigrae).
