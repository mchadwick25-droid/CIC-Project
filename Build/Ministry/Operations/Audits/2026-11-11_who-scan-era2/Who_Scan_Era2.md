# Who scan, era 2 (2026-11-11)

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-11). Read-only scan of era 2 (312–451 CE) of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Final counts, 111 people: **WHO 56, STORY 7, LISTED 46, WANTED 2**. Judgment-call rows: 51. Councils are not people and carry no verdict (see Councils below). The era 1 addendum (outside witnesses, Pontius the Deacon) is in `Who_Scan_Era1.md`.

## How the list was built

- Era 2 has 36 census entries: 23 movements and 13 person entries (Augustine, Jovinian, Ambrose, Jerome, Athanasius, Gregory of Nyssa, Ephrem, John Chrysostom, Nestorius, Paula of Rome, Constantine, Basil, Gregory of Nazianzus).
- All named voices in the people lists of era 2 movements, and the names in their voices lines, merged by alias.
- People held or excluded under the old rules who fall in era 2: Priscillian, Hilarion, Apollinaris, Mesrop, Constantius II.
- The era 1 rows tagged "era 2 by date" are rescored. Ten rows carry the tag, not 11; Eusebius of Caesarea (dates run into era 2) is rescored with them.
- Found in text: Arius, Cyril of Alexandria, Damasus, Theophilus, Meletius, Siricius, Optatus, Caecilian, Eusebius of Vercelli, Julian, Euthymius, Epiphanius.
- Shemon bar Sabbae is the era 1 Simeon bar Sabbae (STORY) and is not rescored. Reccared (589) and Euthymius fall in era 3 by date.
- Not scored, because no era 2 census text names them: Ossius, Alexander of Alexandria, Liberius, Marcellus of Ancyra, Eusebius of Nicomedia, Eutyches, Dioscorus, Moses the Robber.

## Rulings applied (Mark, 2026-11-11)

- Standard as in era 1: WHO, STORY, LISTED, WANTED; founders known only through enemies need two independent witnesses.
- Recorders: "we need Palladius as a who as a biographer and other things, we need to include people like him as who's". Palladius: "he needs to be a who". A recorder whose work became a main source is WHO; a thin life is flagged as a judgment call.
- Women: "women who have 1 source and that source is a who, then that qualifies"; chosen reading "Who if she shaped" (WHO if she founded, taught or led; STORY otherwise).
- Emperors: "By the standard". Constantine is WHO as a named exception; Theodosius I is STORY.
- Founders known through a disciple's or biographer's record: "Who". Founders with their own words and a following: "Own words + following".
- Jovinian: "Take it down". LISTED; the live profile comes down in a later release.
- Outside witnesses: "they are who but clearly mentioned as outside witnesses"; "this should be all that are significant witnesses outside the church through the eras"; "these are historians, not doctrine or athiests or others fighting the church, just objective historians". Suetonius and Ammianus Marcellinus are WHO, labelled OUTSIDE WITNESS (the coordinator's reading of the rulings). Polemicists (Celsus, Porphyry, Lucian of Samosata, Julian, Libanius) are sorted by the standard.
- Councils: "counsels are treated like a tradition with a different icon and title. but people who are a part of it should be on the who click".
