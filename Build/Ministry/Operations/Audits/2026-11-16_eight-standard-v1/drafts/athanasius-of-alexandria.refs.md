## (a) Moved to the card

- "By the count of Archibald Robertson ... more than half of what survives was written in those years of hiding" (Robertson, N4 5900-5922): out of the brief, to What is uncertain with his name.
- "In one, the emperor's agents searched towns, monasteries and tombs for him, and no one gave him up": Robertson's own prose (N4 5900-5922), not an ancient witness. Out of the brief; card may keep it under Robertson's name.
- "At about thirty": Robertson's inference, "not much beyond his thirtieth year" (N4 1513-1519). Out; card, with the note that his opponents said he was under the canonical age.
- "Four more exiles": Robertson's count (N4 7104, 1493). The brief now rests the count on the Festal Index (below).
- "Named Peter as his successor": Robertson's narrative (N4 7262-7266); out, to the card. (Jerome, Letter 127, calls Peter "the successor of Athanasius" only in an editor's note, J6 26088-26089.)
- "Died in May of 373": the month is the editor's bracket on the Index's "seventh of Pachon"; the brief keeps the year only.
- "Sixty years after his death" stays: 373 to the reunion letter of 433. The year 433 rests on the npnf203 introduction (lines 4410-4413) and Pusey's content note, a widely accepted date; card keeps 433 and its basis.

## Notes for the coordinator

- Gregory is a panegyrist; the brief says he "praised him in a sermon" to mark it. His "first ... or with the concurrence of but a few" is told as his claim.
- The Festal Index is an ancient record kept with the Easter letters (in Syriac, printed in N4 with Robertson's notes); the brief names it as "the index kept with his Easter letters".
