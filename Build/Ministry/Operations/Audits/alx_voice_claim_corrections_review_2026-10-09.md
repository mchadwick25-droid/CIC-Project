## Source verification

- Eusebius, Church History VI.6 (`cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`, line 33043): Clement, "having succeeded Pantænus, had charge at that time of the catechetical instruction in Alexandria, so that Origen also, while still a boy, ... was one of his pupils". This is the source of the Clement to Origen link.
- Jerome, On Illustrious Men 38 (`cic/texts/` npnf203, lines 40250-40251): "a pupil of the Pantaenus mentioned above, led the theological school at Alexandria after the death of his master". The `alx.figure.clement` paraphrase is accurate. Jerome here follows Eusebius.
- `alx.figure.pantaenus` and `alx.quote.clement-bee` both record that Clement does not name his final teacher and that the identification with Pantaenus runs through Eusebius (HE V.11).

So both named links rest on Eusebius. "That line rests almost entirely on Eusebius" is accurate, and it is the governing record's own phrase ("the orderly succession derives almost entirely from Eusebius").

## Per-item checks

1. What `alx.contested.didaskaleion-institution` holds. Its `claim` (formation_confidence Contested) is a formal school with an unbroken line of heads, "Pantaenus, Clement, Origen, Heraclas, Dionysius". Its `held_against` says the orderly succession derives almost entirely from Eusebius. Its `concedes` keeps "a real tradition of learned Christian teaching ... teachers, pupils, succession of influence", and names the institutional form and continuity as what is contested. The concession is about a teaching tradition in general. It does not attest the named links, and `alx.term.catechetical-school` says the same: Clement and Gregory attest "the teaching relationship itself", which is Clement's own unnamed teachers and Origen teaching Gregory, not Clement teaching Origen. Calling the named three-person line contested therefore matches the record's `claim` and OG-20's reading of it. It does not contradict the concession.

## Findings

### 1. NOT SUBSTANTIAL: `alx.force.transmission-ongoing` now grades the succession two ways
The new `description` calls the Pantaenus, Clement, Origen line "contested". The record's own notes still end "Mechanisms Widely Accepted; succession particulars DMR." Dominant Modern Reconstruction and Contested are separate levels, and the governing record, `alx.contested.didaskaleion-institution`, rates the succession Contested. The notes are not in `compiled/repository.json`, so the voice does not see them. But a builder reading the record finds two grades for one claim.
Fix: "succession particulars DMR." → "succession particulars Contested (alx.contested.didaskaleion-institution)." This edits a record, so the package must be rebuilt and repinned, and OG-21's pin and hash updated to match.

### 2. NOT SUBSTANTIAL, OUTSIDE THE RULING: `alx.figure.pantaenus` `bridge_line` states the Eusebian identification as fact
`bridge_line`: "His pupil remembered him as the bee who gathered honey from the prophets and apostles." This field is in the compiled package. It treats Clement's "Sicilian bee" as Pantaenus, which is the Pantaenus to Clement link stated as plain fact. The same record's notes say "Clement does NOT name his final teacher - the identification with Pantaenus runs through Eusebius (HE V.11), so any use of the tribute must say 'the teacher scholarship identifies as Pantaenus'". `alx.quote.clement-bee`'s `not_for` rules out "a claim that Clement names Pantaenus here". This record is not part of the branch, and it is not about Origen, so it is not in item (8). It is a known defect, though, and must not be left only in a review file.
Fix: add one sentence to OG-21's "Not changed" paragraph, which has not merged yet: "`alx.figure.pantaenus` `bridge_line` ("His pupil remembered him as the bee who gathered honey from the prophets and apostles.") states as fact the identification of Clement's 'Sicilian bee' with Pantaenus, which its own notes and `alx.quote.clement-bee` say runs through Eusebius. It is outside this ruling and stays OPEN for the build thread." The record itself is left for the build thread to correct under its own ruling.
