## Shown dates (Open-Gaps 429)

Census `dates`: `1st-4th c.`. Proposed: **`mid-2nd c. to after 404`**, a change, by the date rule and the batch header's "Dates from the sources' own clues".

- Start. The first witness of their own time is Justin, in the middle of the 2nd century (Source_Sheet A2, Widely Accepted; ANF1 19893-19905). The 1st century rests only on later, hostile witnesses: Epiphanius's two beginnings at Pella, "after the capture of Jerusalem" (Pan 25029-25034, 25335-25345; Source_Sheet H2-H3, Contested), and Jerome's report that John wrote against "the then growing dogma of the Ebionites" (VI 39566-39572). Both stand in their witnesses' voices in timeline period 1 and in `uncertain` 13. They are not the shown date.
- End. No witness gives one. The last dated witness is Jerome's letter to Augustine of 404 ("usque hodie", to this day, CSEL34 23235; the year is Widely Accepted, Source_Sheet A8; the letter names none). Augustine says the sect "still continues" (AugF 6787). So the end is "after 404", the form the Manichaeism check set ("216 to after 1000", `Card_Check_E.md` N1). An end in the 4th or 5th century would be one no source gives.
- `start` 70 and `end` 400 only place the entry on the map and are not edited. For Mark (Open-Gaps 429): `start` 150 would sit on Justin's middle of the century; `end` 404 on Jerome's letter, or 400 kept as the brief's "to about 400".
- `region`, `placeLabel`, `placeQualifiers` and `modernCountries`: under Entry fields.

## shortDescription

> The Ebionites and Nazoraeans were Jews who believed that Jesus was the Christ and went on keeping the Law of Moses: circumcision, the Sabbath and the food laws. Later church writers placed them in Syria and beyond the Jordan.

Two sentences, 39 words. It opens on their names, says what they were, and ends on where the record puts them. No negative, contrast, dash or placeholder opening. It names both groups without settling whether they were one people or two (`uncertain` 2). Readability alone: grade 9.6 (names out 7.5), ease 76.2: pass. The Montanism example scores grade 17.5 and ease 38.1 (`Card_Check_E.md`), so this is simpler.

## timeline
