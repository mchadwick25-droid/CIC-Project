### Shown dates and the year 410

The start, "c. 201", passes. What the card may show at the end: 410 meets the date rule as Widely Accepted. It is not a year an editor supplied where the record gives none. The acts date the grant to "la 11e année du règne de Yezdegerd" (Chabot 16155), and the meeting to the Epiphany and "le mardi premier du mois de Shebat" (16412, 16422). Chabot converts by the standard count of Yazdegerd's years, "à partir du 14 août 399", which he takes from Nöldeke (16175-16177, 16211-16212), giving 6 January 410 (16480).

That is the same footing as the other converted years these cards show: 201 from Seleucid 513 (CE 53), 258 and 430 from consuls, 363, and Athanasius's 367 (npnf204 68714). What the rule bars is a year the old sources do not fix, such as Gwynn's 350 for the siege.

Mark's ruling of 2026-11-26 (`Header.md`, "Dates from the sources' own clues") settles it. A date may be worked out from the sources' own clues, with the working shown. Here the acts give the regnal year and the month, so the card shows "about 410", with the working on the timeline. The held "410"s in `uncertain` items 6 and 8 and in `quotationOriginals[4].work` stand. See S9 for the exact change.
