## Listed for the fix (the note; not changed here)

1. **"a writer of about 192"**: a modern reckoning. The year comes from the translator's footnote to HE V.16.19 (McGiffert's note 1599, which works the date out from the thirteen years without war). The anonymous writer gives no year: he says only that it is "more than thirteen years since the woman died" (HE lines 29887-29888). Fault 2 (a modern view, here a date, told as the record). Suggested: "a writer of the time, whose name is lost".
2. **"met many times to test"**: the source says "met often in many places throughout Asia" (HE lines 29721-29722; ANF 7 line 11308, "met together often and in many places"). Close; the source's wording keeps the "many places". Already logged in the batch-11 Montanism Source_Sheet (G-list).
3. **"Apolinarius, bishop of Hierapolis"**: true, but the edge's cited line (HE IV.27, line 26112) does not call him bishop. Serapion's letter does (HE V.19.2, lines 30561-30562); that source could be added to the edge.
4. **The edge's first source** cites ANF 7 line 11308 only; the McGiffert English of the same passage is at HE lines 29721-29738, which the card adds.

The enemy's words ("frenzy", "profane", "revile") are credited to the anonymous writer on each line; the prophets' side ("slayers of the prophets") is given beside them. No year is told that the record does not give. The expulsion is kept at full force.
