### Findings

**P1. Period 1 `when`: BLOCKING.** "While Commodus reigned" moves the Rhossus visit onto Commodus's reign. Eusebius dates only Serapion's being bishop to that reign: "At this time ... Serapion ... was still well known as bishop" (npnf201 31060-31061). He tells of Serapion's death in Book VI (33559), after the opening of Severus's persecution (32397). The letter to Rhossus has no date. The card also contradicts the brief, which opens "About the year 200". This is a date moved onto the wrong event. The writer's own record says the date belongs on "Serapion's episcopate, not on the visit", but the period head does not hold to that.
- Old: "Late 2nd century, while Commodus reigned". New: "Around 200, while Serapion was bishop of Antioch".
- Support: npnf201 31003, 31060-31061, 33559; the brief, paragraph 1.

**P2. Timeline line 17: BLOCKING.** The angel does not promise a daughter. It promises that Anna will conceive and bear a child, and Anna vows the child, "male or female". The daughter is told only at the birth. As written, the line is an unsupported claim.
- Old: "A book under James's name tells of Joachim and Anna, a couple with no child, and of an angel's promise to Anna of a daughter, Mary."
- New: "A book under James's name tells of Joachim and Anna, a couple with no child, of an angel's promise to Anna that she will conceive, and of the birth of their daughter, Mary."
- Support: ANF8 36694-36697 ("thou shalt conceive, and shall bring forth ... if I beget either male or female"); 36729-36736 ("A girl ... and called her name Mary").

**P3. Line 2: MINOR.** "A parish in his charge" goes past the record. Eusebius gives "the parish of Rhossus", and Serapion says only that he visited.
- Old: "On a visit to Rhossus, a parish in his charge, Serapion supposes all there hold the true faith."
- New: "On a visit to the parish of Rhossus, Serapion supposes all there hold the true faith."
- Support: npnf201 33697-33698 ("some in the parish of Rhossus"), 33710 ("When I visited you"); Schwartz 2504-2505.

**P4. Line 27 `reference`: MINOR.** The same as G7: give the base year.
- Old tail: "which takes the work down to the fourteenth year of the emperor Theodosius, line 38785". New tail: "which takes the work down to the fourteenth year of the emperor Theodosius, line 38785, counting his reign from 379".
