## Shown dates

Census `dates`: `c. 185-312`. Proposed: **`early 3rd to early 4th c.`**, a change, by the date rule.

- Start. The first held events are Alexander's coming to Jerusalem to share Narcissus's office (E 33426-33532) and Origen's first stay at Caesarea, when a war broke out in Alexandria (E 35303-35322). Eusebius gives neither a year; the early 3rd century is Widely Accepted for both. 185 rests on no held line.
- End. The Martyrs of Palestine runs to the end of the seventh year of the persecution and its easing in the eighth (E 47405-47408), the persecution having begun in Diocletian's nineteenth year (E 45893). The early 4th century is Widely Accepted. 312 rests on no held line. Jerome's notice of Eusebius (period 5) is what came of the church, not its own span.
- `start` 185 and `end` 312 only place the entry on the map and are not edited (flagged for Mark).
