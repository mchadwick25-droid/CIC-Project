- Start. Eusebius sets Zephyrinus's accession "about the ninth year of the reign of Severus" (E 32210-32211). Counting Severus's reign from 193 (Widely Accepted), that is about 201, a conversion, shown with its working on timeline line 1. Victor before him belongs to the House Church entry.

## shortDescription

> In Rome, Callistus, once a slave, became bishop, and Hippolytus, a writer of his day, said he let people off for sins of the body. In 251, after Decius made Christians sacrifice, bishops at Rome agreed to take back those who gave way, if they repented.

Two sentences, 45 words. Each person named by what he was then. Hippolytus's charge stays in his voice ("said"). No negative, contrast, dash or placeholder opening. Grade 10.0 (names out 8.6), ease 73.4: pass. Simpler than the Montanism example.
