## 3. Sentences that fail on one hearing

1. **BLOCKING.** S17: "Cyprian's own last letter says when officers came to take him to another city, he left his gardens for a while, on their advice, to be tried in the city where he led the church."
   Meaning lost: the last place the listener heard was Curubis, a year earlier. Nothing says Cyprian came back from exile, so "his gardens" and "another city" have no setting. "On their advice" reads as the officers' advice, which makes no sense; the listener cannot tell whose advice it was. S16 and S17 together also sound like a contradiction (he refused to go anywhere; he left for a while) with no signal that two witnesses tell it differently. The listener loses where Cyprian is and why he hid, right before the arrest.
   **Change, for the reviser from the outline:** first, one clause that brings Cyprian back from Curubis to his gardens near Carthage, as the outline gives it. Then name whose advice it was, as the letter gives it. Then make the difference between the two witnesses audible, for example: "Pontius says Cyprian turned down friends' offers of places to hide. Cyprian's own last letter adds that when officers came to take him to another city, he left his gardens for a while, on [whose] advice, so that he could be tried in the city where he led the church." Use only what the outline holds for the return, the gardens and the advice.
2. **MINOR.** S10: "The young man behind him read it and showed it by a sign." "Behind him" could mean behind Cyprian or behind the governor, so the listener loses the picture of the dream for a moment. Change: say whom he stood behind, as the outline gives it (e.g. "The young man, standing behind the governor, read it...").
3. **MINOR.** S25: "a list of his works made about a hundred years after his death already ends with it." "Ends with it" is unclear heard once: does the list end with the Life, or end because of it? Change: "...and a list of his works made about a hundred years after his death already includes it, as its last item."
