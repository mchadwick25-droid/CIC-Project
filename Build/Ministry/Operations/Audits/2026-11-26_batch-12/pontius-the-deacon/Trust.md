Three changes:

    - In the brief, "on their advice" points back to the officers. The letter has his dearest friends advise him.

## The heart

The heart is right for the subject. Almost everything held of Pontius is the one book. The record carries what the brief puts first:
- he chose to go into exile;
- he heard the dream;
- he was there on the last night;
- he wrote so that later readers would have the example;
- he grieved to be left behind.

It is told in time. The essence ends at word 166: the deacon, the exile, the dream and the last night, then the book and why he wrote it. That is about 1 minute 6 seconds, within "about 150", so it is not late. The opening scene and the name come first, in the first three sentences.
