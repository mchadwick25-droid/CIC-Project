## 3. Impact, heaviest first (what later witnesses carried on and used)

The era has no single subject, so "impact" here is the questions the age handed on, ranked by how much the next age's witnesses fought over them.

1. **Who may come back after giving way, and who decides.** Asked first in mass after Decius (E11, E12; the `councils-of-rome-and-carthage-251` and `novatianism` siblings), asked again after 303 (Peter of Alexandria's canons, in that outline; CYP-L 1028–1029 for certificates). The next era's quarrel at Carthage over a bishop said to have handed over the Scriptures (`latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity`) grows from E23. Documented.
2. **What Christians owe an emperor who stops hunting them.** The 311 edict asks for prayer in return (E28, EUS 45669–45672); an emperor already judged a church house (E19); Constantine follows (E29). Documented.
3. **How to speak of the Son beside the Father.** Fought at Rome and Antioch in the 200s (siblings `modalist-monarchianism`, `antiochene-church-third-century`). Named only, in a clause. Documented in the siblings' sources.
4. **Which teachers and books carry what the apostles handed down.** The bishops' synods (E4) and the siblings on Marcion, Valentinus, the New Prophecy and the apocrypha. Named only. Documented in the siblings' sources.
