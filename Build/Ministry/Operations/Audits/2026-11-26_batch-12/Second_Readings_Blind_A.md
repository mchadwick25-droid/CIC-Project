# Second Readings, Blind A (SR2-01 to SR2-06)

## SR2-01
Source: `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`, lines 42736-42738. English translation.

"I advise you to send the extra people, those who are of no use to us, such as old women, children and old men, out through the gates."

Reading note: the passage ends at "outside the gates" (27 words); the next clause, "to go wherever they may please", is outside it and not rendered. "Supernumeraries" means the extra people, those beyond the number needed.

## SR2-02
Source: `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`, line 42760. English translation.

"like a father and a doctor,"

Reading note: a fragment. In context, Eusebius receives the people who escaped the siege. "Physician" here means a doctor.

## SR2-03
Source: `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`, lines 42816-42817. English translation.

"make no small or ordinary mistake."

Reading note: a fragment. The subject is the people who put the first month in the twelfth segment and fix the fourteenth day of the Passover by it. In context the phrase is understatement: they make a serious mistake.

## SR2-05
Source: `cic/texts/anf05_hippolytus-cyprian-caius-novatian.xml`, lines 23614-23626. English translation; the inline endnotes (1660-1661) are left out.

"But when I say 'another', I do not mean that there are two Gods. I mean it only in the way that light comes from light, or water from a spring, or a ray from the sun. For there is only one power, and it comes from the All. The Father is the All, and from him comes this Power, the Word."

Reading note: "cometh" is old English for "comes". "Fountain" means a spring or source of water. The passage ends at "the Word" (60 words).

## SR2-06
Source: `cic/texts/hippolytus_refutatio-omnium-haeresium-grc_wendland-gcs26-1916.txt`, lines 1348-1351. Greek.

"We are their successors. We share in the same grace, and in the high priesthood and the teaching. We are counted as guardians of the church. So we do not let our eyes close in sleep, and we do not keep silent about the right teaching."

Reading note: "their" (ὧν) refers back to the apostles in the sentence before. They received the Holy Spirit and passed it on to those who believed rightly. "High priesthood" translates ἀρχιερατεία. "Right teaching" translates λόγον ὀρθόν, literally "the right word". The τε ... τε ... καί pattern can also be read as "sharing in the same grace, high priesthood and teaching", with all three as one list; the sense is the same. The scan is damaged at "καί": line 1349 reads "χαὶ" and "zal". Both are clearly OCR errors for καί. The scan uses "ϑ" for θ throughout. The passage ends at σιωπῶμεν (25 words).
