# Second Readings (SR1)

Comparison of the blind second readings against our wording, batch 12. The comparer did not do the blind readings.

## Method

For each id in `Second_Reading_Key_SR1.md` (SR1-01 to SR1-27):

1. Our wording was checked word for word against the piece's brief (`<slug>.brief.txt`) and against the card entry's `modern` (`<slug>.card.json`, the `quotationOriginals` index the key names). All 27 stand word for word in the brief and equal the card's `modern`.
2. Our wording was set beside the blind rendering in `Second_Readings_Blind_A_SR1.md` (SR1-01 to 09), `_B.md` (SR1-10 to 18) or `_C.md` (SR1-19 to 27).
3. Where the two differ, the cited lines in `cic/texts/` and the entry's `printed` / `original` were opened. Each card `printed`, `original` and `englishPrinted` was also matched against its cited lines, allowing for line-break hyphens, edition line numbers and markup.
4. Grades: SAME (same meaning), MINOR (a difference a listener would not take differently), MEANING (a listener would understand something different).

Two blind notes, checked at the source:

- **SR1-08.** Kroymann prints "sperauerat" (had hoped). His apparatus (line 9647) gives "sperauerat R, separauerat P (corr. R) MF". "separauerat" makes no sense with "episcopatum" as its object. Oehler's print, also held, reads "Speraverat" (`tertullian_quae-supersunt-t2-polemica-lat_oehler1853.txt` line 21632). Our wording and the blind reading both follow "sperauerat". Separately, the scan reads "Yalentinus" (line 9634) and "aliura" (line 9652). The card's `original` prints "Valentinus" and "alium" but does not say so. Oehler's print confirms both readings (lines 21632-21636).
- **SR1-18.** The request asks for 20 words. The first sentence has 16 words and ends at "βούλονται" (line 13704). The card's `original` and our wording run on through "τοῦτο πέπονθε πρῶτος Μεθόδιος", which is 4 more words, 20 in all. The brief quotes both sentences, so its span ends at "Μεθόδιος". The Greek sentence goes on ("τῆς ἐν Λυκίᾳ πόλεως λεγομένης Ὀλύμπου ἐπίσκοπος"), and the brief gives that only in its own words. The blind reader gave the extra four words in a note: "This is what happened first with Methodius". That says the same as our "Methodius was the first to fall into this".
