## Worded by me

- **B1, the charge of seeming (Beat 4, OL 40; Words to explain, OL 22).** Holds. APL 17666-17670 reads "quod dicunt eum dicere, δοκήσει, id est, putative tantum, et per allegoriam, non etiam secundum ea, quae per historiam ... gesta esse omnia quae a Salvatore gesta sunt". The researcher is right: the line names no body and no second group, and "caeca enim est malitia" points at the same accusers. The new clause, "that he made all the Saviour's deeds happen only in seeming, not in history", says no more than this. The mere-man charge stands at 17661-17664 and the soul charge at 20291-20293. Confidence (Documented as the Defense's report) and witness (contemporary; whose words, D1) are right.
- **B2, sheet row I6a (SS 120).** Holds. JERL 35093-35100 is printed as the row gives it ("Medis inter / has provincie Palestinos ecodiceslegunt ... Pamphilius vulga. / verunt : totusque orbis hac inter se trifaria varie- / late compugnat"). APL 9981-9983 gives the clean reading. JERL 35034 heads the section "26. Ex prologo in Paralipomena". LATER, Documented as Jerome's report: right.
- **M2, Beat 1 (OL 37).** Holds. E1 (EUS 47072-47080) has "sharing his possessions with the needy".
- **The recount cut in Beat 6 (OL 42). Says more than its source.** When Jerome's clause went to the card, the beat kept "Eusebius called himself after him: Eusebius Pamphili". On the sheet, "calls himself Eusebius Pamphili" rests only on the Letter 84 translator's endnote (JER6 19417-19419). The fix pass marked that note modern (E11). Jerome says only "His surname Pamphilus arose from his friendship for Pamphilus the martyr" (JER 41076-41077). The beat's "Documented as Jerome's report" does not cover "called himself" or the form "Pamphili". **Replace** OL 42's sentence

  "Eusebius called himself after him: Eusebius Pamphili, and wrote his Life in three books, now lost (E11)."

  with

  "Eusebius took the surname Pamphilus from him, and wrote his Life in three books, now lost (E11, JER 41076-41077; EUS 47082-47085)."

  The count stays the same. Jerome's reason, the friendship, stays on the card.
