## Worded by me

- **B1, the charge about seeming (Beat 4, OL 40; Words to explain, OL 22).** The check's "while others charged the opposite, that Christ only seemed to have a body" goes past the Latin. APL 17666-17670 does not speak of the body, and it does not name a second group: "Post, ista est, quae istis omnibus adversatur, (caeca enim est malitia) quod dicunt eum dicere, δοκήσει, id est, putative tantum, et per allegoriam, non etiam secundum ea, quae per historiam ... gesta esse omnia quae a Salvatore gesta sunt." The same "dicunt" runs through the list, and "caeca enim est malitia" points at the same accusers contradicting themselves. The rest of the check's sentence is kept word for word. My new text in Beat 4: "and also the opposite, that he made all the Saviour's deeds happen only in seeming, not in history (E9a, APL 17661-17670, 20291-20293)". The citation reaches 17670 because the clause ends there. In the gloss: "made Christ either a mere man or one whose deeds happened only in seeming". The lines that hold it are APL 17661-17670 (mere man at 17663, seeming at 17667-17669) and 20291-20293 (the soul).
- **B2, the sheet row (SS 120).** The check's Latin, "Medias inter has provincias Palaestinos codices", matches no held print. I quote JERL 35093-35100 as printed and give the reading, confirmed by APL 9981-9983 ("Mediae inter has provinciae Palaestinos Codices legunt; quos ab Origene elaboratos Eusebius et Pamphilus vulgaverunt") and the Gothic-Bible file at 1700-1705. The check's APL 9979-9980 is widened to 9979-9983, because the words stand at 9981-9983. Migne numbers the section 26 (JERL 35034, "26. Ex prologo in Paralipomena"); the row says so beside the check's "II.27". The outline item keeps the check's JERL 35093-35098.
- **M2, Beat 1 (OL 37).** The check left it to me to keep or drop "He shared his possessions with the needy". New text: "He shared his possessions with the needy (MP's wider giving; drop it if Opening 1 has told the need, card)". E1 holds it (MP 11).
