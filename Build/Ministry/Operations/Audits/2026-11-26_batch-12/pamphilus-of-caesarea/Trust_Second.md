## The heart

**Right heart.** The record supports it. Eusebius knew Pamphilus as a collector of Origen's books and a giver of Scripture (EUS 36890-36894; JERL 31259-31264). Jerome knew him as Origen's copyist (JERL 58486-58489). Eusebius records the stand and the death (EUS 46700-46709, 47191-47198), and the Defense is their joint work (EUS 36973-36976). Later readers worked from his books and argued over his name (JER 51453-51456). The Martyr shape is kept without the death swallowing the story.
