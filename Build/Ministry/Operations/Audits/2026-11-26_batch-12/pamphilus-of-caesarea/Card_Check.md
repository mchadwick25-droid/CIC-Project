## The codex 117 correction: confirmed at the lines

`cic/texts/photius_library-v1_freese1920.txt`:
- Codex 117 begins at line 11361: "Read an anonymous work defending Origen and his abominable writings, in five volumes." Lines 11377-11378 say it "chiefly relies upon Pamphilus the martyr and Eusebius". So Pamphilus and Eusebius are its authorities, not its authors.
- The verdict follows at lines 11379-11381: "This apology is not a refutation of the charges against Origen for the most part, but rather supports the accusation". "This apology" is the anonymous work.
- The fifteen counts are at lines 11400-11428. The proofs come "in his fourth book" and "in the fifth", which fits a work in five volumes and not the six-book Defense.
- Codex 118 begins at line 11437: "Read the Defence of Origen by Pamphilus the martyr and Eusebius. It is in six books". Here Photius gives no verdict and no list of charges.

So the writer is right. Source Sheet D8, E9 (last bullet) and X6 are wrong, and so is L7, which rests on E9. The starting card's item "Photius's view of the Defense" is wrong too. Outline lines 65, 92 and 106 rest on the same error.

The card's open question "Another book and another apology" now credits both the verdict and the counts to the anonymous work. It matches lines 11361-11428: "migrations from one body into another" and "neither eternal punishment nor resurrection of the flesh". Its "as a separate work" fairly reads the two headings, five anonymous volumes against six named books. It stands.
