## BLOCKING findings

**1. SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** "Irenaeus puts him among the Encratites, the self-controlled, who refused marriage and meat, a group he says sprang from earlier teachers."
- Source, IR 33656-33660: "those who are called Encratites (self-controlled) preached against marriage ... Some of those reckoned among them have also introduced abstinence from animal food".
- Irenaeus gives the refusal of meat to some of the group, not to all. The outline's Words to explain carries the same widening ("they refused marriage and animal food").
- Change: "Irenaeus puts him among the Encratites, the self-controlled, who refused marriage, and some of them meat, a group he says sprang from earlier teachers." Do not say, for the outline: "The Encratites refused marriage and meat" as true of all; say "some of them" refused animal food (IR 33659-33660).

**2. MODERN ONLY.** "Some two centuries later Epiphanius, the only writer to name the place, says Tatian crossed to the East and set up his school in Mesopotamia."
- The report holds: EP 15996-16000, "τὸ δὲ αὐτοῦ διδασκαλεῖον προεστήσατο ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς μὲν ἐν τῇ Μέσῃ τῶν ποταμῶν ... ἀπὸ Ῥώμης γὰρ μετὰ τὴν τοῦ ἁγίου Ἰουστίνου τελείωσιν διελθὼν ἐπὶ τὰ τῆς ἀνατολῆς μέρη".
- "The only writer to name the place" rests on Holl's apparatus alone (EP-ed 16018, "ἐν τῇ Μέσῃ τῶν ποταμῶν nur durch Epiph. überliefert"): a modern editor's judgment about the record, which Part 1 keeps out.
- Change: cut the clause. "Some two centuries later Epiphanius says Tatian crossed to the East and set up his school in Mesopotamia." The point stays on the card. Do not say, for the outline: "the only writer to name the place" / "he alone names it" (Holl's judgment, EP-ed 16018).

**3. Live-text echo.** "Irenaeus, his opponent, says he heard the teacher Justin and left the church after Justin died."
- Live: "he became a Christian and heard the teacher Justin." The outline has "heard Justin teach" and "a hearer of Justin's", never "the teacher Justin".
- Change: "Irenaeus, his opponent, says he was one of Justin's hearers, and left the church after Justin died." (IR 33664, "He was a hearer of Justin's".)

**4. Live-text echo.** "And Moses is older than Homer, he argues, citing Greek, Babylonian, Phoenician and Egyptian writers rather than Christian ones."
- Live: "from Greek, Babylonian and Egyptian writers, that Moses was older than Homer." The outline has "Greek, Chaldean, Phoenician and Egyptian writers" (beat 3); the source calls them Chaldeans (AG 7282, 7290).
- Change: "And Moses is older than Homer, he argues, citing writers of the Greeks, Chaldeans, Phoenicians and Egyptians rather than Christian ones." If "Chaldeans" is thought unclear on one hearing, "the Chaldeans of Babylon" (AG 7293, Berosus "a Babylonian").
