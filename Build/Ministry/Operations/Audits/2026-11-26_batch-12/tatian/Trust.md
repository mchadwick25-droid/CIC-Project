## BLOCKING findings

**1. SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** "Irenaeus puts him among the Encratites, the self-controlled, who refused marriage and meat, a group he says sprang from earlier teachers."
- IR 33656-33661: "those who are called Encratites (self-controlled) preached against marriage ... Some of those reckoned among them have also introduced abstinence from animal food".
- Irenaeus gives the refusal of meat to *some* of them only. The brief gives it to the whole group, so the qualifier is lost.
- Change: "who refused marriage and meat," becomes "who refused marriage, and some of them meat as well,".

**2. MODERN ONLY.** "Some two centuries later Epiphanius, the only writer to name the place, says Tatian crossed to the East and set up his school in Mesopotamia."
- The rest of the sentence is HELD. EP 15996-15999: "τὸ δὲ αὐτοῦ διδασκαλεῖον προεστήσατο ἀπ᾽ ἀρχῆς μὲν ἐν τῇ Μέσῃ τῶν ποταμῶν ... ἀπὸ Ῥώμης γὰρ μετὰ τὴν τοῦ ἁγίου Ἰουστίνου τελείωσιν διελθὼν ἐπὶ τὰ τῆς ἀνατολῆς μέρη".
- "The only writer to name the place" rests only on Holl's apparatus (EP-ed 16018: "ἐν τῇ Μέσῃ τῶν ποταμῶν nur durch Epiph. überliefert"). No old source says it. It is also a clause that only says what is missing.
- Change: cut ", the only writer to name the place,". The sentence becomes "Some two centuries later Epiphanius says Tatian crossed to the East and set up his school in Mesopotamia."

**3. Heart: one clause of the approved essence is late.**
- The approved essence (Outline, The heart) ends: "in the 400s one bishop, Theodoret, gathered the copies he found in his own churches and brought in the four Gospels instead."
- The first minute (paragraphs 1-2, 117 words) carries everything else: his book, Justin, the break by Irenaeus, and the Gospel book read in the Syriac-speaking churches. Theodoret appears only in the last paragraph.
- The rest of the heart is right for the subject and is told on time. The one gap is this clause.
- Change: at the end of paragraph 2, "read in worship by the Syriac-speaking churches of the East." becomes "read in worship by the Syriac-speaking churches of the East, until a bishop in the 400s put his copies away." That adds 9 words, to 682, which is past the 675 cap. Finding 2 takes away 6 words (676 net). If the cap governs, the Review decides whether to keep this clause or rule that the closing paragraph carries this part of the heart.
