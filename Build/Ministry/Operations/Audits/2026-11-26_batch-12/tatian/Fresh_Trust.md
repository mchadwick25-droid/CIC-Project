## Findings, with exact changes

**Finding 1. BLOCKING. Sentence 2, SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** The brief makes an inward act a journey. The Greek is "καὶ ἐμαυτὸν γενόμενος ἐξήτουν ὅτῳ τρόπῳ τἀληθὲς ἐξευρεῖν δύνωμαι" (AG-G 10387-10388), and Otto's Latin on the same page gives it "in me ipsum descendens inquisivi quomodo verum invenire possem", going down into myself (AG-G 10457-10458). Ryland's "retiring by myself, I sought" (AG 6970-6971) is the older English for the same withdrawal into himself. "He went off alone" tells a physical going-away that neither the Greek nor the Latin gives. The quote must be checked against the original, and here the original turns inward.

Exact change: replace "He went off alone and asked how he might find the truth." with "He withdrew into himself and asked how he might find the truth." (Net +1 word; the brief goes to 676. If the 675 ceiling must hold, use "He turned inward and asked how he might find the truth.", net 0.)

Do not say line for the outline (Opening 1 left room for it with "he went off by himself"): "He went off alone" / "he went away by himself." Say "he withdrew into himself" (AG-G 10387-10388, "ἐμαυτὸν γενόμενος"; Otto's Latin "in me ipsum descendens").

**Finding 2. MINOR. Sentence 19.** The sentence is held, but Tatian gives it as the reason for Crescens's plot ("because by proclaiming the truth he convicted the philosophers", AG 6554-6555), and the brief sets it apart without the "because". Optional exact change, if it fits: "Justin, he says, had shown the philosophers to be gluttons and cheats." to "This was because Justin, he says, had shown the philosophers to be gluttons and cheats." Otherwise note only; the two sentences sit side by side and the reason is heard.

**Finding 3. MINOR. Sentence 25.** Irenaeus charges that Tatian invented the aeons ("He invented a system of certain invisible Æons", IR 33668; μυϑολογήσας, HI-G 17442); "spoke of" is milder but keeps the charge's content. Optional exact change, same length: "Irenaeus says Tatian spoke of aeons," to "Irenaeus says Tatian invented aeons,". Otherwise note only.

**Finding 4. MINOR, note only. Sentence 32.** "I know not how" (οὐκ οἶδ᾽ ὅπως, EU 26425) is told as plain ignorance. That is the words' plain sense; that it might instead mark disapproval is a modern editor's debate (McGiffert's note on Lightfoot, EU-ed 26427-26432) and belongs on the card. No change.
