### The two quotations

- **Q2.** Modern: the brief's span, "When I say 'another,' ... the Word." Printed: HIP 23614-23626, Salmond's English, "But when I say another, I do not mean that there are two Gods, but that it is only as light of light, or as water from a fountain, or as a ray from the sun. For there is but one power, which is from the All; and the Father is the All, from whom cometh this Power, the Word." (the endnotes at 23615-23620 and 23623-23625 fall between; the validator reads past them). Greek, NG 3705-3708, checked by hand: ἕτερον δὲ λέγων οὐ δύο θεοὺς λέγω ("saying another, I do not say two Gods"), ἀλλ᾽ ὡς φῶς ἐκ φωτὸς ἢ ὡς ὕδωρ ἐκ πηγῆς ἢ ὡς ἀκτῖνα ἀπὸ ἡλίου ("but as light from light, or water from a spring, or a ray from the sun"), δύναμις γὰρ μία ἡ ἐκ τοῦ παντός ("for one power, the one from the whole"), τὸ δὲ πᾶν πατήρ, ἐξ οὗ δύναμις λόγος ("the whole is the Father, from whom the power, the Word"). The rendering holds. The scan is damaged inside the span ("ix", "Ἡ", "ἡ", "ἀκτῆα"), and line 3706, a row of stray accent marks, falls inside the sentence, so no `original` can stand verbatim at its lines. HIP confirms the reading, so the Greek is carried by hand in the entry's `uncertain`, not as `original`.
- **Q3.** Modern: the brief's span, "We are their successors. ... silent." Printed: HIP 1129-1140, MacMahon's English. Original: RG 1348-1351, printed as the scan reads it, with `uncertain` giving καὶ for "χαὶ" and "zal". Greek checked by hand: ὧν ἡμεῖς διάδοχοι τυγχάνοντες ("we, being their successors"), τῆς τε αὐτῆς χάριτος μετέχοντες ἀρχιερατείας τε καὶ διδασκαλίας ("sharing in the same grace, the high priesthood and the teaching"), καὶ φρουροὶ τῆς ἐκκλησίας λελογισμένοι ("and counted guardians of the church"), οὐκ ὀφθαλμῷ νυστάζομεν οὐδὲ λόγον ὀρθὸν σιωπῶμεν ("we do not doze with the eye, nor keep the right word silent"), present tense as Wendland prints it (MacMahon's note, HIP 1137-1139: the common reading is future). The rendering holds.
