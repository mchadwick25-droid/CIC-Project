Theodoret, bishop in the East, quotes "Hippolytus, Bishop and Martyr" in his florilegia on the faith (JER 20468, 23357, 27188), and names "Hippolytus and Methodius, bishops and martyrs" (JER 20260). The Eusebius clause is unchanged.

9. "Novatus is his name for Novatian, whose party split from the church at Rome in 251, sixteen years after the exile, refusing to take back those who had given way in persecution." **ASSUMPTION ADDED. BLOCKING.**
   - What holds: The split is the one at Rome in 251. Rome's list names "Novatus" as its maker: "supervenit Novatus ex Africa et separavit de ecclesia Novatianum" (LP 66825-66826), and under Cornelius, "Novatus extra ecclesiam ordinavit Novatianum in urbe Roma" (LP 66891-66893). The refusal is in Eusebius: "as if there was no longer for them a hope of salvation" (EUS 38265-38267). The years 235 and 251 are the list's consular dates, and the gap is arithmetic.
   - What does not hold: The sentence says Damasus's "Novatus" is a name for Novatian himself. No held source says what Damasus meant. Eusebius and the Greeks do call Novatian Νοουάτος (EUSG 7517, 7618; EUS 38265). But the Roman list of 354, from Damasus's own city and time, names Novatus as a different man, the one who split Novatian off. So the identification is a reading. Damasus's "scisma … Novati" (LP 109167-109168) is the same split either way, so the gloss does not need the identification.
   - Exact change: replace the sentence with "This was the split that Novatian's party made from the church at Rome in 251, sixteen years after the exile, refusing to take back those who had given way in persecution."
   - Suggested Do not say line for the outline: "'Novatus is his name for Novatian' / 'Damasus means Novatian' → 'the split that Novatian's party made from the church at Rome in 251' (LP 66825-66826, 66891-66893: Rome's list names Novatus as the man who split Novatian off)."
   - Note: the outline's Words to explain holds the current wording, so the writer followed the outline. The finding is against the source, and it blocks under the bar until it is fixed or a third reader shows from a source that Damasus meant Novatian.
