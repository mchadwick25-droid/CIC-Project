**Heart.** The heart is the right one for what the sources show: he answered Noetus, he would not give way to Rome's bishops, his own book is the record of the quarrel, and the later witnesses could not agree whether he was an elder, a bishop or a man who had stood in a split. By about word 150 (the end of paragraph 2) the story has told that he answered Noetus, would not give way to Zephyrinus and Callistus, wrote the book, was exiled in 235 and was kept among Rome's martyrs. The heart is told in time.

## MINOR (noted, not blocking)

1. *Against Noetus* 14 makes the third a third "in the ordering" (οἰκονομίᾳ), not a third person, while the Son and the Father are "two persons". "as a third" keeps the text's words, but a listener may hear "a third person". Optional, word for word: "with the grace of the Holy Spirit as a third, in God's ordering of things".
2. Rome's own list (LP 66821-66823) makes Novatus a man "ex Africa" who split Novatian off. So Damasus's "Novatus" may be that man, not Novatian. The split is the same either way. Optional: "The split of Novatus was the one at Rome in 251, sixteen years after the exile, when Novatian's party broke away, refusing to take back those who had given way in persecution."
3. Where the verse stood is known only from the editors of the stone (LP 109007-109009; De Rossi as cited), as the Source Sheet's "Not held" list says. That is allowed as the edition of an old text. No change.
