## 1. The kind kit's test

- **Who this was:** filled. "Hippolytus ... taught in the church at Rome in the early 200s and wrote in Greek."
- **What happened:** filled. He quarrelled with Zephyrinus and Callistus, wrote the Refutation, and "In 235 he was sent to Sardinia with Rome's bishop."
- **What it was about:** filled. The Son is "another beside the Father, yet one God with him", and "how far the church could forgive". The second part is made concrete in paragraph 6.
- **Why the connection matters:** filled, but weakly. I can say that later bishops in the East quoted him, that his Easter table led to Eusebius's cycle, and that Rome later claimed him as an elder who called people back from Novatian's split. That last link is the one the story ends on. On one hearing it reads as a contradiction (see the failing sentences, item 3), so the listener comes away unsure what the tie to Novatian was.

A gap a listener feels but that is not a finding: "Rome kept his day among its martyrs" makes me ask how he died, and the story never says. If the old sources give only the exile and the calendar, that is told correctly. I note it here only for the reviewer.

## 2. The opening

The first three sentences: Noetus's teaching, Noetus called twice before the elders and put out, and "Hippolytus, who tells that story, taught in the church at Rome in the early 200s and wrote in Greek."

- Who, where and when for Hippolytus all come by sentence 3. The name comes in where the moment allows. It is not held back for a reveal.

## 3. Sentences that fail on one hearing

1. "He and his side stood against them for the truth." (paragraph 3) The sentence before it ends on Zephyrinus "with Callistus as his adviser", so "He" sounds like Zephyrinus or Callistus. The meaning lost is that Hippolytus is the one who resisted. MINOR. Change: "Hippolytus and his side, he says, stood against them for the truth."
2. "In 235, Rome's list of bishops records, Pontianus, the bishop, and Hippolytus, whom it calls an elder, one of the priests who led the church with its bishop, were deported to Sardinia." (paragraph 9, 35 words) Four inserted clauses sit between the subject and "were deported". By the verb I had lost who was deported. MINOR. Change: "Rome's list of bishops records that in 235 its bishop, Pontianus, and Hippolytus were deported to Sardinia. It calls Hippolytus an elder, one of the priests who led the church with its bishop."
3. "In the East, Eusebius wrote that Hippolytus had led another church, without saying which, and later bishops there quoted him for the faith and called him bishop and martyr." I cannot tell whether "another church" means a rival church at Rome or a church in another city. "In the East" and "quoted him for the faith" are also unclear. The meaning lost is what Eusebius actually claimed. MINOR. Change (for the trust reader to check): "Eusebius, writing in the East, calls him the bishop of a church but does not say which one. Later bishops in the East quoted him as a witness to the true faith and called him bishop and martyr."
4. "Hippolytus, an elder, it was said, had always stayed in the split of Novatus while a tyrant's orders pressed." Two sentences earlier I heard that Hippolytus was sent away in 235. The next sentence tells me Novatian's split came in 251. Heard once, that is a contradiction: how could he be in a split that began sixteen years after his exile? "Novatus" heard beside "Novatian" adds a second muddle, and "a tyrant's orders pressed" is opaque. This loses the thread at the close, where the story carries its link to Novatianism, so I mark it BLOCKING. Change: say "Novatian" once. The narrator states the two dates already in the story side by side without a judgment, for example, "a split that, by the story's own dates, began sixteen years after he was sent to Sardinia". The trust reader must confirm this adds nothing. If the reviewer prefers, the verse can instead be placed so that the listener hears it as Damasus's report of a tradition, not as a fact about 235.
5. "As Hippolytus went to the kingdom of the faithful, the people asked where to go ..." I did not take in that "went to the kingdom of the faithful" means he was dying. The meaning lost is that this is his deathbed word. MINOR. Change: "As Hippolytus went to his death, to the kingdom of the faithful, the people asked ..."
