## What the cuts touch

- **Nearest the heart: item 32.** The heart's What came of him reads "used against rival teachers, from Irenaeus and Tertullian…; defended … the church's teaching against the heresies". With Tertullian's line and the heresies book on the card, that use is carried by Irenaeus against Marcion alone, and Tertullian appears only as the ending's name. Without item 32 the draft runs about 687, over 675.
- **80 is not reachable without the heart.** These cuts reach about 673, two under the limit. The next cuts take heart lines: "The account of his trial was kept too" (8, the heart's I2), to about 665; then Justin's reply when told to sacrifice (18, the stand before the prefect). Dropping the second writing's join and "By his account" (about 4) would leave the husband's spite, a judgment of blame, uncredited. A redraft that runs over 675 goes to Mark with these named.
