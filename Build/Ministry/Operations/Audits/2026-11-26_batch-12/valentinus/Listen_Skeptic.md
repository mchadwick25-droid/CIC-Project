## 1. The kind kit's test

- **Who he was:** filled. A teacher at Rome in the middle of the second century who founded a school. It arrives late (see 2).
- **What happened:** filled. He taught at Rome, broke with the church there (by Tertullian's account, after losing a bishop's place, and put out more than once), and his school went on in two branches.
- **What it was about:** filled. Heavenly powers in pairs, the fall of one, a lesser maker of the world; people immortal or "saved by nature". Clement's reply shows the stakes: commandments useless, salvation without the Savior.
- **Why the connection matters:** filled, but thin. I can say what the church defended against him (one God who made the world). I cannot say what Irenaeus's, Tertullian's or Hippolytus's books did, beyond that they exist (see 4).

## 3. Sentences that fail on one hearing (MINOR)

1. "His students carried the school on in two branches, while the church's writers who answered it set against it the faith in one God who made the world." The inverted "set against it the faith" doesn't parse on one hearing. I lose what was set against what.
2. "But another man won the place, by the claim his suffering for the faith gave him." The custom (a man who suffered for the faith had a claim to office) is not given. I hear "claim" and lose why suffering would win a bishop's seat.
3. "In Irenaeus's account, a pair beyond any name, Unspeakable and Silence, gave rise to Father and Truth, then to Word and Life, and Man and Church, and to more powers after them." The names in a list run together. "and Man and Church" loses the pairing, so I can't tell which names are pairs.
4. "The Italian branch, Ptolemy's, said it was a natural body, of soul and not of spirit, on which the Spirit came down at his baptism." "Of soul and not of spirit" carries no meaning for me. I don't know the difference this school drew, so the split means nothing.
5. "Irenaeus wrote five books against what he called knowledge falsely so called…" "What he called … so called" doubles up. I lose whether "knowledge" is the school's word or his.
6. "By Tertullian's day, he says, Valentinus himself was now nowhere." "Now nowhere" is unclear: dead, forgotten, or without followers? The next sentence ("only one man … kept his rules whole") leaves "his rules" undefined. The ending lands blurred.
