### B3. "Tertullian alone says he broke with the church." (Trust SOFTENED; Trust_Second HELD.) Heart wording (report).

The readers disagree. Trust: "he is not the only witness. Epiphanius says that… in Cyprus 'τῆς πίστεως ἐξέστη' (he left the faith) (HOLL 29801–29803)." Trust_Second: HELD, from KR 9652–9653 and the absence of a break in Irenaeus III.4.3. Trust_Second did not check Epiphanius.

Judged by the bar: BLOCKING. I read HOLL 29801–29803 ("εἰς Κύπρον δὲ ἐληλυϑώς… τῆς πίστεως ἐξέστη"). A held old source does report that he left the faith. Epiphanius is late (about 375) and on hearsay, and the outline rightly leaves his report to the card (S4, E15, D5). But "alone" is a flat claim about the record, and the record does not support it, so it is an unsupported claim. Source_Sheet D3 holds the narrower claim: "Tertullian alone gives reasons". Irenaeus gives Valentinus's years at Rome but tells no break. So the qualified claim holds.

Change (Trust's wording): "Tertullian alone says he broke with the church." → "Of the writers near his time, Tertullian alone says he broke with the church." This is the approved essence's wording, so it is applied as a checked fix and reported to Mark.
