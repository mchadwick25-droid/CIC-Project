## Listener faults the bar blocks

**A. The split over Jesus's body (paragraph 7).** "Hippolytus says the school split over what kind of body Jesus had, into an Italian branch, Ptolemy's, and an Eastern one." The quarrel is named, but the story never says what it was or why it mattered (Explain it, so missing essential material). The outline cannot supply this: Beat 5 says "the held lines do not say why the difference mattered", and the Do not say list sends the two positions to the card. Explain it or leave it out, so the quarrel is cut:

- Old: "Hippolytus says the school split over what kind of body Jesus had, into an Italian branch, Ptolemy's, and an Eastern one."
- New: "Hippolytus says the school split into an Italian branch, Ptolemy's, and an Eastern one."
- This is a cut. The cause goes to the card with the two positions. At the source: the outline's Do not say entry "tell only the split, its cause and the two branches" should read "tell only the split and the two branches", and Beat 5 should drop "over what kind of body Jesus had".

**B. "Knowledge falsely so called" (paragraph 8).** This is a label with no sense attached. The story never says the school claimed a special knowledge, so the jab means nothing. The label is the title of Irenaeus's books, not essential material, so it is cut:

- Old: "Irenaeus wrote five books against what he called knowledge falsely so called, aimed above all at the school of Ptolemy, which he calls a bud of Valentinus's."
- New: "Irenaeus wrote five books against them, aimed above all at the school of Ptolemy, which he calls a bud of Valentinus's."
- This is a cut. The word "them" is worded by the gatherer (it points to the school and its branches told just before). The rest is the outline's own wording (Beat 6). It also clears listener MINOR 3.
