## tree/eusebius-of-caesarea
Heart: Eusebius, bishop of Caesarea, was the first to write the church's history from Christ to his own day, building it from earlier Christian writings. He lived through the great persecution, signed the Nicene creed only after asking what its new word meant, and at Tyre was accused by a fellow prisoner of having bought his release by giving way.
