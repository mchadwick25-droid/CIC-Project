## Entry fields

The card is changed here; these entry fields (outside `card`) need release to change them. The census entry has no `why`, `relationsSummary` or `voices`, so none of those changes.

1. **teaser.** Old: "Bishop of Caesarea in Palestine who wrote the first history of the church and a Life of Constantine." The old line says "wrote the first history", which the brief does not. New: "Bishop of Caesarea in Palestine who set out to write the church's story from its own letters and lists, wrote the emperor Constantine's Life, and was charged at Tyre with having got out of prison by giving way." (the card's `knownFor`).
2. **dates.** Old: "c. 260-339", which rests on McGiffert's reckoning (Source_Sheet S4). Suggested: "Alive in 303; died after Constantine". `start` 260, `end` 339 and `activeYear` 325 only place the entry on the map (Stage Two Kit section 3); release decides whether to move them.
3. **longDescription.** Replace with the finished brief, final newline removed.
4. **quote_renderings.json.** The old listings for this entry (seven spans) no longer stand in the brief; release lists the three new spans from `quotationOriginals`, with the final check's blind readings.
5. **modernWorks** (new field; title and author required). Tools that clarify the old texts, not witnesses:
   - Title: "Eusebius Pamphilius: Church History, Life of Constantine, Oration in Praise of Constantine (Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 1)". Author: Arthur Cushman McGiffert (translator of the Church History and Martyrs of Palestine, with prolegomena and notes) and Ernest Cushing Richardson (revised translation of the Life of Constantine); editor Philip Schaff. Year: 1890. Note: English translations of Eusebius's works with introduction and notes.
   - Title: "Socrates and Sozomenus Ecclesiastical Histories (Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 2)". Author: A. C. Zenos (Socrates) and Chester D. Hartranft (Sozomen); editor Philip Schaff. Year: 1890. Note: English translations of the two church histories, with notes.
   - Title: "Theodoret, Jerome, Gennadius and Rufinus: Historical Writings (Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 3)". Author: Blomfield Jackson (Theodoret), Ernest Cushing Richardson and W. H. Fremantle (Jerome's Lives of Illustrious Men and Apology); editor Philip Schaff. Year: 1892. Note: English translations with notes.
   - Title: "Athanasius: Select Works and Letters (Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 4)". Author: Archibald Robertson; editor Philip Schaff. Year: 1892. Note: English translation of Athanasius's treatises and letters, with notes.
   - Title: "The Principal Works of St. Jerome (Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, second series, volume 6)". Author: W. H. Fremantle. Year: 1893. Note: English translation of Jerome's letters, with notes.
   - Title: "A History of the Councils of the Church, from the Original Documents, volume 2: A.D. 326 to A.D. 429". Author: Karl Joseph von Hefele; translated by Henry Nutcombe Oxenham. Year: 1876. Publisher: T. and T. Clark, Edinburgh. Note: a reference history of the councils from 326 to 429, including the councils of Tyre and Jerusalem in 335. Moved from the card's `sources.about` word for word in title and author.
   Initials and years for the Nicene and Post-Nicene volumes follow the Source Sheet's file notes (McGiffert 1890; Robertson 1892; Fremantle 1893); the first names of Zenos, Hartranft and Jackson are not in the held files and should be checked against the volumes before release.
6. **Modern views kept out of the card**, for Modern works only: McGiffert's birth about 260 and death 339, the "Father of Church History" title, Hefele's views on Tyre, Rufinus and Eusebius's middle way, McGiffert and Robertson on Eusebius's innocence, Gibbon, the Abgar forgery verdict.
