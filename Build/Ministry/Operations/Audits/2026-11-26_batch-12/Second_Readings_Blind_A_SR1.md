# Second Readings, Blind (A)

Ids SR1-01 to SR1-09.

## SR1-01
When I came upon the last of them, the first in power, I had tracked him down where he was hidden away in Egypt, and there I found rest.

## SR1-02
Perhaps philosophy was also given to the Greeks directly and as a primary gift, until the Lord should call them. For philosophy was a tutor that led the Greek mind to Christ, just as the law led the Hebrews to him.

## SR1-03
Latin. What in those books is unlearned? Or rather, what in them does not come from the very heart of philosophy?

## SR1-04
From Syria all the way to Rome I am fighting with wild beasts, on land and at sea, by night and by day. I am chained to ten leopards, by which I mean a squad of soldiers. Even when they are treated kindly, they only become worse.

## SR1-05
Some who are without God, that is, unbelievers, say that he only appeared to suffer. (In truth it is they who only appear to exist.) But if that is so, why am I in chains? Why do I long to face the wild beasts? Then am I dying for nothing?

## SR1-06
Greek. "I am God's wheat, and I am ground by the teeth of wild beasts, so that I may be found pure bread" (Eusebius, Church History).
Scan note: the first word prints as "ΒΊτος". I read it as "Σῖτος" (wheat), damaged in the scan; the context ("pure bread") supports this. "ἀλήϑομα!" is read as "ἀλήθομαι" (I am ground). The book number in the Eusebius reference is unreadable ("}. 30").

## SR1-07
The same is true of the heart, as long as no one cares for it. It stays unclean, a home for many demons. But when the only good Father visits it, it is made holy and shines with light.

## SR1-08
Latin. Valentinus had hoped to become a bishop, since he had both the talent and the eloquence for it. But another man won the office on the strength of having confessed the faith under persecution. Angry at this, Valentinus broke away from the church of the true rule of faith.
Note: the 22 words end at "abrupit" (broke away). "praerogatiua" means a prior claim or privilege; here, the claim another man gained from his witness as a martyr-confessor. The page break (and an apparatus line, "sperauerat R, separauerat P") falls inside the passage; I followed the printed text "sperauerat" (had hoped).

## SR1-09
You are immortal from the beginning, children of eternal life. You wanted death shared out to you so that you might spend it and use it up, and so that death might die in you and through you.
