## What stays

The approved heart stands whole: the essence; his three reasons for writing; the three passages, each with what it says and why the act in it happened; the doubt, with Jerome's reading, Origen's word and "This man was the Christ" side by side; and what came of him, with Eusebius setting the lines against the forged records. Quote 1 and the lots, the scene that carries the craft, are untouched. The record's force stays: both charges of treason (the men's sword and his answer in Quote 1), the stoning of James and the charge against the high priest, the crucifixion at the leading men's urging, John's killing for fear of a rising. Every Explain it the remaining beats need stays: the Antiquities, the Christ, the Greeks, the council of judges, the forged records, why he refused to kill himself.

## What the cuts touch

- The Romans' charge of treachery at the walls goes with Opening 1. The story's question, traitor or God's servant, now rests on the men in the cave and Quote 1. The Do not say line "both sides called him a traitor" no longer has a Roman scene behind it; the writer should not use it.
- Suetonius, Titus and Domitian are no longer named. The tree link to `suetonius` stays in Links.
- The next cut, if the redraft runs over, would take the forged records (about 22 words). That sentence carries the connection's first part, why Christians wanted his lines, so it goes to Mark rather than to the card.
