**B2. His books' purpose, and the rival historian.** Para 4, s1: "Nearly everything known of his life comes from his own books, and he wrote them partly to defend himself." - Change: replace the whole of paragraph 4 with: "At Rome he wrote the books that tell nearly everything known of his life. Also: "in his Life he answered a rival historian" alone → "In his Life, a book on his own life, he defended himself against a rival historian who, he says, had written falsely of him".
