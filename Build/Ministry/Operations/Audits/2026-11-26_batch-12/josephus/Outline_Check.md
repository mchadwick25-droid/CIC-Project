# Outline check: Josephus

## My account of the heart (written before reading Outline.md)

From the census entry (`knownFor`: a Jewish priest and historian at Rome, whose *Antiquities* names Jesus and his brother James), the slate job, and Source_Sheet.md:

- **Essence:** Josephus was a Jewish priest of Jerusalem who commanded in Galilee in the rising against Rome, surrendered and went over to the Romans, and at Rome wrote his people's history. In it, a few short lines name John the Baptist, James "the brother of Jesus, who was called Christ", and Jesus himself; he is on the tree because these are the only lines on them by a Jewish writer of that century, and Christians kept and argued over them.
- **Also told:** the surrender at Jotapata and his own account of it (the prayer, the lots, the prediction to Vespasian, confirmed by Suetonius); both sides calling him a traitor; why he wrote (the *War* to correct false accounts, the *Antiquities* for Greek-speaking readers); what the three passages say, each briefly and exactly: John as a good man Herod killed for fear of a rising; James stoned by the high priest Ananus, who lost office for it; the passage on Jesus, about 110 words, with "He was the Christ".
- **What came of him:** (1) Eusebius quoted all three and used the Jesus passage against the forged Acts of Pilate (the earliest held quotation of it); (2) Origen cited him on John and James and said he did not accept Jesus as the Christ; (3) the copies differ at the key clause (Jerome "he was believed to be the Christ", Michael the Syrian "people think he was the Messiah"), so how much is his own is Contested; Sozomen read him as near belief. Defended: none by Josephus himself for the church; he defended himself (his *Life*) against charges of treachery. Shape: Outside witness: how short the passages are, and what they can and cannot tell.
