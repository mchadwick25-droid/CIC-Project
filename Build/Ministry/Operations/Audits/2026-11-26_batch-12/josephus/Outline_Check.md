# Outline check: Josephus

## My account of the heart (written before reading Outline.md)

From the census entry (`knownFor`: a Jewish priest and historian at Rome, whose *Antiquities* names Jesus and his brother James), the slate job, and Source_Sheet.md:

- **Essence:** Josephus was a Jewish priest of Jerusalem who commanded in Galilee in the rising against Rome, surrendered and went over to the Romans, and at Rome wrote his people's history. In it, a few short lines name John the Baptist, James "the brother of Jesus, who was called Christ", and Jesus himself; he is on the tree because these are the only lines on them by a Jewish writer of that century, and Christians kept and argued over them.
- **Also told:** the surrender at Jotapata and his own account of it (the prayer, the lots, the prediction to Vespasian, confirmed by Suetonius); both sides calling him a traitor; why he wrote (the *War* to correct false accounts, the *Antiquities* for Greek-speaking readers); what the three passages say, each briefly and exactly: John as a good man Herod killed for fear of a rising; James stoned by the high priest Ananus, who lost office for it; the passage on Jesus, about 110 words, with "He was the Christ".
- **What came of him:** (1) Eusebius quoted all three and used the Jesus passage against the forged Acts of Pilate (the earliest held quotation of it); (2) Origen cited him on John and James and said he did not accept Jesus as the Christ; (3) the copies differ at the key clause (Jerome "he was believed to be the Christ", Michael the Syrian "people think he was the Messiah"), so how much is his own is Contested; Sozomen read him as near belief. Defended: none by Josephus himself for the church; he defended himself (his *Life*) against charges of treachery. Shape: Outside witness: how short the passages are, and what they can and cannot tell.

## Verdict: FIX

The outline's heart matches mine in substance (priest and commander, surrender, the books at Rome, the three passages, what Origen, Eusebius and Jerome did with them, the open question). No major contribution is missing. The quotes are sound, the length fits (about 625 after Cut first, against 675 approved; Cut first drops no reason or meaning), the Library sweep holds up on spot-check. Six things must change before the writer starts.

Disclosure: a census grep for quote reuse printed the `josephus` entry's live `longDescription`. Nothing in this check rests on it; the reuse check found none of Quotes 1 to 3 in any other entry.

## Findings

### 1. BLOCKING: the passages are told out of order (beats 5 and 6)

Beat 5 tells James's death (Antiquities XX, under Albinus), then beat 6 tells Jesus crucified under Pilate. That runs backward in time and in the book (Jesus JW 81405 and John JW 82087 are both in Book XVIII; James JW 91000 is in Book XX). Heard once, the listener hears Jesus crucified after his brother is stoned. The note "it is the third of the three passages told, not a return" does not cure it.

Correction: replace beats 5 and 6 with three beats in the book's own order for the deaths, which is also time order:
- **5. John.** The current beat 5's first two sentences and the John half of its Explain it (E12).
- **6. The passage on Jesus.** The current beat 6, with its first sentence changed to: "In the same eighteenth book, between a riot under Pilate and another disaster, stands a passage on Jesus: about a hundred words (E14)." Delete the sentence beginning "Do not say "earlier"".
- **7. James.** The current beat 5 from "Later the high priest" to its end, with the James half of its Explain it (E13), and "Later" changed to "In the twentieth book, the high priest". Quote 3 then lands straight after Quote 2, which is the job Quote 3 is given.
Renumber Cut first items 4 and 5: item 4 is beat 7, item 5 is beat 5.

### 2. BLOCKING: "copies" asserts a manuscript difference no old source states (essence, heart "The doubt", What came 3 heading, Connection, Do not say)

The held texts show that Jerome's Latin (JVL 50907) and Michael's French (MS 11233) word the clause differently from Eusebius's Greek. They do not show whether Jerome had a different Greek text or rendered it freely; the old editor at JVL-ed 50948–50952 reads Jerome as giving the sense. "Jerome's copy says" and "their own copies give differently" turn that open question into a claim.

Corrections:
- Essence: "which their own copies give differently" → "which the Christian writers who quote it give in different words".
- Heart, The doubt: "Jerome's copy says only that he was believed to be the Christ" → "Jerome's Latin says only that he was believed to be the Christ".
- What came 3 heading: "The copies disagree at the key clause." → "The old writers who quote it word the key clause differently."
- Connection: "and Jerome's copy reads that he was believed to be the Christ" → "and Jerome's Latin reads that he was believed to be the Christ".
- Do not say, the "Testimonium Flavianum" line: → "the passage on Jesus", "the old writers who quote it". Add: "Jerome's copy" or "Jerome had a different text" → "Jerome's Latin reads" (JVL 50907).

### 3. BLOCKING: the forged Acts. Eusebius does not say the emperor gave the order (Words to explain, What came 1, Connection)

EH 47945–47951: "they sent them with the emperor's approval to the whole of the empire ... with written commands that ... the schoolmasters should give them to their scholars ... to be studied and learned by heart." The commands are "theirs"; the emperor approved.

Corrections:
- Words to explain, forged acts: → "forged records of Pilate's dealings with Jesus, sent across the empire with the emperor's approval for schoolboys to learn by heart (I1; EH 47945–47951)".
- What came 1: "that an emperor of his own day had ordered schoolboys to learn by heart" → "that, he says, had been sent out in his own day with the emperor's approval for schoolboys to learn by heart".
- Connection: "against forged records of Jesus that an emperor had spread" → "against forged records of Jesus sent out with an emperor's approval".
- What came 1, Defended: "the Gospel account of Jesus" → "Jesus, whom the forged records were 'full of every kind of blasphemy against' (EH 47945–47946)". Eusebius's sentence (EH 9242–9246) sets John's and Jesus's passages together against the forgers; say "the passages on John and Jesus", not "the one on Jesus".
- Do not say, add: "The emperor ordered the schoolboys" → "sent out with the emperor's approval" (EH 47945–47951).

### 4. BLOCKING: Origen's "witness to James's great justice" points to a line our text lacks (What came 2)

Origen's praise (OM 30620–30629; OMG 3727–3733) is attached to the claim that Jerusalem fell for James, which the held Antiquities XX does not hold (D3). After beat 7, a listener will take "bore witness to James's great justice" as describing the stoning passage they have just heard.

Correction: What came 2 → "**Origen, a Christian scholar of the third century, knew the lines on John and the phrase on James** ("the brother of Jesus called Christ", OMG 3729–3730), and wrote that Josephus did not accept Jesus as the Christ (I2; CC 37933–37934; OMG 3731–3733). In the passages where he cites Josephus, he quotes nothing of a passage on Jesus (I2, D2). One clause. Documented." Do not say, add: "Josephus praised James's justice" → leave out; Origen says it of a line not in our text (D3).

### 5. BLOCKING: "and his son after him" (beat 3)

JW 111188–111190: "Thou, O Vespasian, art Caesar and emperor, thou, and this thy son." There is no "after him". Suetonius (SUET 36662–36665) names only Vespasian.

Correction: beat 3, "told him he would be emperor, and his son after him" → "told him that he, and his son with him, would be emperor". Do not say, add: "his son after him" → "he, and his son with him" (JW 111188–111190).

### 6. BLOCKING: "Sadducees: a party of priests" rests only on Whiston's footnote

JW 90995–90996 says only "the sect of the Sadducees, who are very rigid in judging offenders, above all the rest of the Jews". "Priests" comes from Whiston's note (JW-ed, footnote at JW 91025–91028), an editor's claim.

Correction: Words to explain, Sadducees → "one of the Jewish schools of his day (S3), harsher judges than the rest, Josephus says (E13)". Source Sheet E13's plain words "the priestly party" are struck for the story.

### 7. MINOR: Quote 1 drops "from thee"

My rendering of JW 110852–110856: "I give them my hands willingly, and am content to live. And I declare openly that I do not go over to the Romans as a deserter from the Jews, but as a servant sent by you." Whiston's "a minister from thee" is a servant sent by God, not only God's servant. Correction: Quote 1, "but as your servant" → "but as a servant sent by you". Words to explain, minister: "rendered 'servant sent by you'".

### 8. MINOR: "the Christ" gloss adds "king"

Whiston's gloss (JW-ed 139288–139290) is "the Christ of God, or the true Messias of the Jews". Correction: Words to explain → "the Christ, the Messiah, the anointed one the Jews awaited".

### 9. MINOR: John's washing. The outline drops Whiston's qualifier

JW 82097–82099 prints "not in order to the putting away [or the remission] of some sins [only], but for the purification of the body". With Whiston's "[only]" (kept, per the sheet), Josephus's English does not flatly deny a washing for sins. Corrections:
- Words to explain, baptism: replace the last two sentences with "Do not give it the Christian sense of a washing away of sins. Josephus says it was for the cleansing of the body, the soul already made clean by right living."
- Do not say, John's baptism line: → "Josephus says the washing was for the cleansing of the body, the soul already made clean by right living (E12)".

### 10. MINOR: Herod by role

JW 82009 calls him "Herod the tetrarch", and the outline gives no plain word for him; a listener may take him for the Herod of the Christmas story. Correction: Names, Herod → "acts; first heard as 'the ruler Herod' (JW 82009)".

### 11. MINOR: Library sweep name forms

The Patrologia Orientalis French OCR drops accents ("Josephe"), which is not among the sweep's name forms. I searched `chronicle-of-seert_...patrologia-orientalis-5...` and `po08_...` for it: the hits are editors' notes and a list of historians of Jerusalem's fall (PO 5, line 4455), none on the three passages. Agapius's version is not held, as the sheet says. Correction: add "Josephe" to the sweep's searched forms, with this result.
