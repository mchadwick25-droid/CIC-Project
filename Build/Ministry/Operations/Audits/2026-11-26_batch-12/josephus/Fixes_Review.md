## Worded by me

1. **B3, beat 5** (Outline.md line 67). "who urged the Jews to live justly and to come to his washing (E12)" → "who urged the Jews to deal justly with one another and to honor God, and to come to his washing (E12)". The wording is the review's own story change, so beat 5 and the Do not say line match. Source, JW 82092–82095: "him, who was a good man, and commanded / the Jews to exercise virtue, both as to / righteousness toward one another, and / piety toward God, and so to come to bap-".
2. **B3, Cut first 13's Keep line** (Outline.md line 182). "a good man who urged the Jews to live justly" → "a good man who urged the Jews to deal justly with one another and to honor God". Same source lines.
3. **B3, Do not say** (Outline.md line 141). Added: "urged the Jews to live justly" alone → "urged the Jews to deal justly with one another and to honor God" (JW 82093–82095). This is the review's line word for word except the citation. The review cites JW 82091–82093. Those lines read "was called the Baptist; for Herod slew / him, who was a good man, and commanded / the Jews to exercise virtue, both as to". They do not hold the duty to one another (JW 82094) or "piety toward God" (JW 82095). The citation is corrected to the lines that hold the words.
4. **MINOR 6, Words to explain** (Outline.md line 35). Added before "the Christ": **Pilate:** "the Roman governor of Judea" (E14; EH 8924–8925). Source, EH 8924–8925: "Pontius Pilate was entrusted with the / government of Judea". The source sheet holds the gloss at E14.
