## Rulings on the two BLOCKING findings of the second trust read

**Sentence 8, "The first was a history of the war in Greek": MINOR, not BLOCKING.**
- The second reader cites JW 91748–91751: Josephus says he "formerly composed" the *War* "in the language of our country".
- The fresh trust reader counted the sentence SUMMARY HOLDS. "The first" is ordered within the books the sentence before names, those written at Rome that tell his life (JW 131974–131978). In that set the Greek *War* does come first. Cut first 17 left out "first in his own language".
- So the sentence is held as written. A listener may still hear it as his first book of all. The change below costs a word and is fully held, so it is applied as MINOR.

**Sentence 16, "The passage itself says he was the Christ": not upheld. HELD.**
- The second reader says no copy of Josephus's own text is held. That is not so. Whiston's *Antiquities* (JW 81405–81421) is the text of the *Antiquities* as printed, not a quotation by a Christian writer. It reads "He was [the] Christ". The outline rates it "the text as printed, Documented; how much is his, Contested" (beat 6).
- The wording is the outline's own Do not say line: "the passage's own words" → "the passage itself says he was the Christ".
- The sentence does not claim that Josephus wrote the clause. It sets the printed passage beside Jerome's Latin, in the sentence before, and Origen's word. Then it says how much Josephus wrote is uncertain. That is the outline's scene, "the three readings side by side, each credited".
- The proposed change ("The copies in Greek, Eusebius's among them") would drop the printed *Antiquities* text as the passage and credit the reading to the Christian writers who quote it. That is not what the held lines show. No change.
