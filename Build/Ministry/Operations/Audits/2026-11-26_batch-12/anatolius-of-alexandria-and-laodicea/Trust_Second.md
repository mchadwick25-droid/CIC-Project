## Heart

The right heart. Eusebius's whole account of him turns on the siege and the vote (EUS 42718–42762), then Laodicea and the Easter extract (EUS 42764–42940); the later witnesses who use him (Columbanus, Colman, Wilfrid, Bede) all use him on Easter. It is told by the end of paragraph 2, about 105 words in: inside the first minute.
