## SUMMARY HOLDS sentences

1. **"Ignatius, bishop of the church at Antioch in Syria, was a prisoner bound for death in the arena at Rome when he wrote: [Q1]"**: Eph. 1, ANF 4703–4705: "I came bound from Syria … trusting … to be permitted to fight with beasts at Rome". Rom. 2, ANF 6961: "the bishop of Syria". EUS 19740: "chosen bishop of Antioch".
2. **"On that road he wrote seven letters, six to churches and one to a fellow bishop, and they tell nearly all we know of him."**: The seven are Ephesians, Magnesians, Trallians, Romans, Philadelphians, Smyrnaeans and Polycarp (EUS 19961–20101 names them). No other early account of his life is held. Fair.
3. **"Three messages run through them: keep together around your bishop, hold that Christ truly suffered, and, to Rome, do not save me."**: Eph. 4 (ANF 4828); Trall. 9–10 (ANF 6617–6676); Rom. 1–4 (ANF 6917–6922, 7036–7038: "I shall willingly die for God, unless ye hinder me").
4. **"Polycarp of Smyrna gathered the letters he held and sent them on, and later bishops quoted them for centuries."**: *Phil.* 13, ANF 3613–3618: "all the rest [of his Epistles] which we have by us, we have sent to you". Bishops quoting him: Irenaeus (ANF 53556–53560; bishop of Lyon, EUS 27953), Eusebius (EUS 19994–20071), Athanasius (NPNF4 59204–59206), Theodoret (NPNF3 20264–20270).
5. **"Across Asia, the Roman province that is now western Turkey, churches came out city by city to greet him, even those off his route."**: Rom. 9, ANF 7280–7287: "the Churches that have received me … not as a mere passer-by. For even those Churches which were not near to me in the way … have gone before me, city by city, [to meet me.]" "Asia" places the cities he names (Smyrna, Ephesus, Magnesia, Tralles, Troas). Fair.
6. **"Nothing should be done without the bishop: one thanksgiving meal of one bread and one cup, at one altar, as there is one bishop."**: Magn. 7, ANF 5895–5897: "neither do ye anything without the bishop and presbyters". Philad. 4, ANF 7497–7502: "one Eucharist … one cup … one altar; as there is one bishop". Eph. 20, ANF 5547: "breaking one and the same bread". A fair joining of the three.
7. **"They thought his own sufferings only seemed real too, he wrote, and he charged that they cared nothing for the widow, the orphan or the hungry."**: Smyrn. 5, ANF 8134–8137: "nor the sufferings we have individually endured. For they think also the same thing regarding us." Smyrn. 6, ANF 8184–8186: "no care for the widow, or the orphan, or the oppressed; … of the hungry". "The same thing" is what they say of Christ, so the sense holds.
8. **"He was afraid, he wrote, that the love of the church there would try to spare him."**: Rom. 1, ANF 6919–6922: "I am afraid of your love, lest it should do me an injury … it is difficult for me to attain to God, if ye spare me."
9. **"The bread was an offering: by the teeth of the beasts, he wrote, he would be found a sacrifice to God."**: Rom. 4, ANF 7046–7047: "Entreat Christ for me, that by these instruments [i.e., by the teeth of the wild beasts] I may be found a sacrifice [to God]." Rom. 2, ANF 6958: "that I be sacrificed to God while the altar is still prepared". His prayer, compressed to what he hoped for. Fair.
10. **"Later Polycarp wrote to the church in the city of Philippi, which had seen Ignatius pass, and counted him among those whose endurance they had witnessed."**: *Phil.* 9, ANF 3484–3486: "patience, such as ye have seen [set] before your eyes, not only in the case of the blessed Ignatius". *Phil.* 1: "have accompanied … those who were bound in chains".
11. **"At their request he sent on the letters of Ignatius he held, because they spoke of faith and patience, and asked for surer news of him and those with him."**: *Phil.* 13, ANF 3613–3630: "we have sent to you, as you requested … by them ye may be greatly profited; for they treat of faith and patience … Any more certain information you may have obtained respecting both Ignatius himself, and those that were with him, have the goodness to make known". Polycarp gives faith and patience as why the letters will profit them; telling it as why he sent them is a fair compression.
