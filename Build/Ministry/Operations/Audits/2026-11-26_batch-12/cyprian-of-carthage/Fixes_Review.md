Checked at ANF 27905–27908: "Do you wish to be assured that the cause of his withdrawal was not fear? to allege nothing else, he did suffer subsequently, and this suffering he assuredly would have evaded as usual, if he had evaded it before."

- **B5.** Beat 5 (line 82): "The question was whether a washing given by a group outside the church counted when the one washed came over to it."
- **B6.** Opening 1 (line 71): "says that soon after Cyprian's baptism the whole people of Carthage wanted him as their bishop. A crowd, he says, besieged the doors of his house, while he stepped back to give place to older men".
- **B7, "In the end".** Beat 7 (line 84): "In the end he was taken at his gardens". Checked at KNOPF 4856–4858 ("in suis hortis manebat … uenerunt ad eum principes duo").
- **Do not say lines, B1 to B7,** added word for word at lines 177–183.
