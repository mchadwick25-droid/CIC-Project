# Outline check: Cyprian of Carthage

## My own account of the heart (written before reading Outline.md)

From the census entry (`why`, `teaser`, `relationsSummary`, `legacy`, fields other than `longDescription`), the source sheet and what he is known for.

- **Essence:** Cyprian, a teacher of public speaking at Carthage, was made bishop while still a new Christian, and led the Latin-speaking church of Roman Africa through persecution, plague and split in the 250s, holding it together by his letters and his teaching that the church is one, a mother outside whom no one has God for Father. He died by the sword in 258, the first bishop of Carthage to die a martyr (Pontius).
- **Also told:** his withdrawal under Decius, the public notice against him and the question it raised (Rome's clergy on shepherds who stay, his own answer, Pontius's defence); *On the Unity* written at the Novatian split (one church, one episcopate held in full by each bishop, the mother line); the plague and *On the Mortality* (the plague as a test of who tends the sick; Pontius for care of pagans); the baptism quarrel with Stephen (no baptism outside the church; Stephen's handed-down laying on of hands; Stephen's refusal of the envoys, by Firmilian; the council of 1 September with its "no bishop of bishops", each bishop free under Christ's judgment); exile in 257 and the trial and death in 258 ("Deo gratias").
- **What came of him, heaviest first:** (1) the Donatists claimed his letters and council for their break, and Augustine answered with Cyprian's own example of unity, saying he was wrong on baptism but never left the church; (2) Arles 314 settled the West against his rule on baptism; (3) his feast and table at Carthage, kept for centuries (Augustine, Procopius); (4) read and praised (Lactantius, Jerome). **Defended:** the unity of the one church; each bishop's freedom to judge in his own church; his faith before the proconsul.

**Compared with the outline's heart:** the same in substance. Its essence names the rhetoric, the new Christian made bishop, persecution, plague and division, the one church as mother, each bishop answerable to Christ, the refusal of Stephen's ruling and the sword in 258; its ranking of what came of him (Donatists and Augustine, Arles, the feast) matches mine. Nothing major is left out. Pontius's "first bishop of Carthage to die a martyr" is rightly kept as his claim (card).

### MINOR

**M1. Beat 2 names Fabianus; Names gives him by role.** Replace "whose bishop Fabianus had just been killed in the same persecution (EUS 37413–37416)" with "whose own bishop had been killed in the same persecution (EUS 37413–37416)". "Just" is not in the source.

**M2. Beat 2, "said that he had done so rightly".** In the Latin, the "rightly" clause stands inside what Rome learned from Crementius, the subdeacon sent from Carthage (EP 1055–1056, 1077). The ANF English prints it in quotation marks (ANF 28797–28801), and Benson reads it as Carthage's own justification (D1). Replace it with: "They wrote that they had learned, from a subdeacon who came from Carthage, that he had withdrawn for a set reason, and had done rightly, being a man of standing with a struggle at hand (EP 1055–1056, 1077; ANF 28797–28801)."

**M3. Words to explain, laying on of hands.** "received with a bishop's hands laid on them as penitents" adds "a bishop's". Stephen's extract has only "ut manus illis inponatur in paenitentiam" (EP 15430–15433). Replace it with "received with hands laid on them for repentance, without a new washing". Add to **Do not say**: "a bishop laid hands on them" → "hands were laid on them for repentance".

**M4. Words to explain, persecution under Decius.** "the emperor's order that all sacrifice to the gods" is wrong on two counts. Its "all" comes only from McGiffert's note (EUS-ed, modern), and LAPS 349 does not state the order. Replace it with: "the emperor's persecution, in which Christians were called before the magistrates to sacrifice to the gods; one who gave way 'obeyed his edict' (LAPS 345–351, 1050–1051; EUS 37413–37416)". In Beat 1, add LAPS 1050–1051 beside "ordered sacrifice".

**M5. Beat 5, "and Stephen died before Cyprian".** No cited line holds this. Eusebius says only that Stephen held office two years and "was succeeded by Xystus" (EUS 39406–39407). Replace it with: "and Stephen was succeeded by Xystus, whom Pontius calls 'the good and peace-making priest' (EUS 39406–39407; ANF 28137–28138): card."

**M6. Firmilian is named in Beat 5 but given by role in Names.** In Beat 5, replace "Firmilian, a bishop in Cappadocia who took Cyprian's side," with "A bishop in Cappadocia who took Cyprian's side (Firmilian, Ep. 75)". This applies only if the report survives B1.

**M7. Beat 6, no confidence level for 257.** After "In 257", add "(the year from the consuls, Valerian for the fourth time and Gallienus for the third, KNOPF 4823; Widely Accepted)".

**M8. Do not say, one addition.** "He was exiled for refusing to name his presbyters" → in the *Acts* he is sent to Curubis after he will not take part in the rites, and the question about his presbyters comes afterwards (KNOPF 4825–4840).
