## Non-HELD sentences

### BLOCKING

**1. ASSUMPTION ADDED.** "His going brought a charge against him: a shepherd had left his flock."
- No old source states a charge in those terms. The Roman clergy's letter speaks of the hired man who leaves the sheep, and it addresses the clergy of Carthage: "Nolumus ergo, fratres dilectissimi, mercennarios inueniri, sed bonos pastores" (EP 1099–1100). It also says his withdrawal was "recta" (EP 1077). Whether the hireling lines were aimed at Cyprian is Contested (Source_Sheet D1). The only old witness to a charge against him gives it as fear: "Do you wish to be assured that the cause of his withdrawal was not fear?" (Pontius, ANF 27905–27906). Cyprian himself says only that what he did had been reported to Rome "minus simpliciter et minus fideliter" (EP 2967–2968). The sentence gives the charge a content no source gives, and frames the Roman letter as that charge, which leans to one side of D1.
- Change: cut the sentence. If the slate's "charge it brought" must be told, tell it as a witness gives it, e.g. Pontius's: "Pontius later asked whether the cause could have been fear, since in the end he suffered." That is Cut first 14 (now card), so it needs the outline restored first.
- Do not say (outline): "the charge that a shepherd had fled / left his flock" as the record. The outline's heart line "faced the charge that a shepherd had fled" carries the same error.

**2. ASSUMPTION ADDED (date).** "A few years later a dreadful plague swept the city."
- Pontius gives only "Afterwards there broke out a dreadful plague … over the whole city" (ANF 27939–27946). Jerome's *Chronicle* puts the plague in the same Olympiad entry as Novatus coming to Rome and Novatian (CHRON 43961–43970). Nothing old sets it "a few years" after Novatian's rise.
- Change: "Afterwards a dreadful plague swept the city."
- Do not say (outline): "a few years later" for the plague. Say "afterwards".

**3. SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** "He began: 'It remains for each of us to say what we think on this matter. …'"
- The record shows he began otherwise, with the letters of Jubaianus: "Cyprianus dixit: audistis, collegae dilectissimi, quid mihi Iubaianus coepiscopus noster scripserit …" (SENT 111–113). The quoted words come after: "superest ut de hac ipsa re singuli quid sentiamus proferamus" (SENT 121). "It remains" itself shows it was not his opening.
- Change: "He said:" (or "After the letters on the question were read, he said:").
- Do not say (outline): "He began:" before Quote 3.
