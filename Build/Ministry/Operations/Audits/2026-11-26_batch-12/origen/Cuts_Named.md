## The laymen-preaching conflict

The outline's Length line lists "laymen preaching before bishops" among Demetrius's charges that must carry. Cut first item 2 takes out that whole first quarrel (E14), and the measuring draft has it out. **My named cuts keep it out, on the card.** The Never cut list asks only for "Demetrius's charge beside the break". The draft carries that as his letter calling the deed of Origen's youth most foolish (E17), told beside the ordination and beside Jerome's other account. The Also told line, "Demetrius first praised him, then wrote against him", is carried by "he admired his daring and urged him on" (E9) set against that letter.

So the Length line's listing is the slip: it was counted before the Cut first list was applied. If Mark wants the quarrel carried, it costs about 45 words by the outline's estimate. It is not in the 1031, and it is in none of the counts above.
