The heart is not changed in substance. Checked: EUSG 1840-1841 reads "ὡς καὶ τὸν ᾿Ωριγένην τῶν φοιτητῶν γενέσθαι αὐτοῦ", without παῖδα ὄντα. Its apparatus (EUSG 1883-1885) shows the manuscripts split on "παῖδα ὄντα". McGiffert's note (EUS 33044-33050) says two important manuscripts and Rufinus leave the words out.

## Worded by me

- **B2, "boy" in the rest of the outline.** Each holds in every reading of EUSG 1840-1841 and EUS 33043-33044.
  - The essence (line 5): "Eusebius says Origen was among his pupils".
  - Sibling, `origen` (line 30): "Origen was among his pupils (E7)."
  - Beat 1 (line 37): "Among his pupils, Eusebius says, was Origen (E7)."
  - What came of it 1 (line 45): "Origen, by Eusebius's account (E7),".
  - Connection (line 56): "Origen was his pupil (Eusebius, E7)."
  - Do not say on Origen (line 83): "→ "Eusebius says Origen was among his pupils" (E7);".
- **B4 and B5, Also told (line 8):** "The two groups he answered, each in its own place: Christians who feared philosophy like children fearing masks and wanted "bare faith alone", whose own words survive only in his account; and the followers of Valentinus, who left faith to the simple and claimed knowledge for themselves as men saved by nature, a claim Irenaeus also reports (ANF1 30755-30763)."
  - The two groups are answered in separate books: T1 is Strom. VI.10 (ANF2 47241-47247), T2 is Strom. I.9 (ANF2 28313-28320) and T17 is Strom. II.3 (ANF2 32104-32111).
  - Irenaeus's report is at ANF1 30761-30763: "they shall be entirely and undoubtedly saved, not by means of conduct, but because they are spiritual by nature".
- **B5, Beat 2 (line 38):** "Clement also wrote, in another book, against the followers of Valentinus, a teacher he opposed: they left faith…". Same file lines as above.
- **B4 and B9, Who tells it, Clement (line 63):** "Credited as a witness only for the views he answers (Beat 2: the fearful believers survive only in his account; Valentinus's followers' claim is also reported by Irenaeus, ANF1 30755-30763) and the objectors' question (Beat 5)."
- **B8, Also told (line 10):** "martyrs he and his fellow believers saw burned and beheaded before their own eyes every day,". From ANF2 34507-34509: "we have exhibited before our eyes every day abundant sources of martyrs that are burnt, impaled, beheaded".
- **B9, "the persecutors' charge" replaced with "the objectors' question".** This is the question at ANF2 38903-38904: "But, say they, if God cares for you, why are you persecuted and put to death?"
  - Length (line 14), which also changes "Clement's account of the other side" to "Clement's account of the groups he answered" (B5).
  - Cut first 7 (line 113).
  - The Never cut list (line 119).
- **MINOR 1, Jerome's reply (line 59).** Ending 1 now agrees with the approved essence, which is left as it is: "the essence names it in one clause only, with Jerome's reply in three words ("pointed to his books") and no quote, so the scene is not told twice."
- **MINOR 4, "first in power" (line 50, Quote 1):** "Plain sense of "first in power" (optional, only within the 475), in the setup: the first in ability (McGiffert, EUS 28756-28757: "in ability truly he was first")."
- **MINOR 5, "the Law" (line 25, Words to explain):** ""the Law" in Quote 2 (optional, only within the 475): the law God gave the Hebrews through Moses (Strom. I.26, ANF2 31207-31208: "the law was divinely given by Moses"). Say it inside the setup to Quote 2, never as a line after it."
