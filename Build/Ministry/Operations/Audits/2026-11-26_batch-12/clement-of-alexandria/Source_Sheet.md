# Source sheet: Clement of Alexandria (`clement-of-alexandria`)

Batch 12, Part B. The evidence behind `Outline.md`. Every row gives the file (under `cic/texts/` unless marked), its own line numbers, the witness kind, the confidence level, and the text verbatim. Line ranges count the raw file; where an inline footnote interrupts a passage in the XML, the range spans it and the footnote text is left out of the quotation. Read afresh for this batch; the batch 4 sheet was used only to find passages.

## Library sweep

- **Card sources** (`Card.existing.json`): ANF2; EUS, Book V chapters 10-11 and Book VI chapters 3, 6, 11, 13, 14; NP3 (Lives 38); JER (Letter 70). All read in their files.
- **Corpus map:** `cic/corpus-map/alexandria-catechetical.yaml` (the census `toldWithin`) assigns ANF2's four Clement works (rows at lines 2037-3093), the Pantaenus fragments in `anf08_…` (7402-7416), the pseudo-Clementine Excerpts of Theodotus (4553-4607, which the ANF editor disowns as Clement's), and Jerome's Chronicle entries (4692-4696). `WORKS.yaml` and `AUTHOR-IDS.yaml` hold no Clement of Alexandria entry. `RETRIEVAL-HINTS.md` holds no Clement name forms; name forms searched were taken from the texts.
- **Name search** of `cic/texts/` for "Clement of Alexandria", "Clemens Alexandrinus", "Clement the Alexandrian", Κλήμη-/Κλήμεντ- and "Stromat-": **233 files** match (the pattern also catches Origen's own Stromateis and Clement of Rome in Greek; most are modern notes or passing mentions). Heaviest: EUS (91 lines), ANF2 (72), the Synaxaristes in Greek (38), Routh's Reliquiae Sacrae (36), Harnack's Chronologie (24), the Pedalion (22), Otto's Corpus Apologetarum (22), the Migne Origen with Pamphilus's Apology (19), PL23 (17), NPNF II/7 (16), EUSG (15), THD (14).
- **Used:** ANF2, EUS, EUSG, NP3, PL23, JER, JERL, PE, PEG, THD, PHO, CHR.
- **Set aside, and why:**
  - The Synaxaristes (`nikodemos-hagiorite_synaxaristes-vol1-sep-feb-grc_1900.txt`) and the Pedalion: the hits checked are other Clements (of Rome, of Ancyra) or canon notes; no commemoration of this Clement was found in the lines read.
  - Routh, Otto, Harnack, Hefele, Duchesne: modern editions and histories; they lead to sources already held, and their judgments are not used.
  - The Migne Origen and Lommatzsch's Pamphilus/Rufinus: the hits are editors' notes on Rufinus's plan to translate Clement; no old claim about Clement's life.
  - Epiphanius's Panarion (Holl): the hits are Holl's apparatus marking silent borrowings from the Exhortation; a modern identification, not Epiphanius naming him.
  - Eusebius III.23 (EUS 17100 onward), Clement's story of John and the young robber from What Rich Man is Saved?: a side story about John, not about Clement.
  - The pseudo-Clementine and Theodotus material in `anf08_…`: not his, by the ANF editor's own notice.
  - Cyril of Alexandria: the held volumes (`cyril-alexandria_…pusey1872/1881`) do not hold the Against Julian passage Coxe quotes; not used.
  - Photius's Library is used for the conflict but marked Card first: its harshest charge is against a book Photius doubts is Clement's.
- **Not held:** Clement's Greek (any work); Eusebius's Greek of Book V; the Hypotyposes themselves; Alexander's letters outside Eusebius.
