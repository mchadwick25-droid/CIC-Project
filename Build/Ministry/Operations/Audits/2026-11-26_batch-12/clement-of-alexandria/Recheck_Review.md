## Findings

**BLOCKING 1. Alexander as Clement's "pupil" (lines 28, 45, 56, 64; sheet line 19, I1).** This breaks Part 1's modern-work rule: an identification that only a modern work makes stays out of the story and the card. The old text never makes Alexander a pupil. Eusebius has only "ὡς δὴ γνωρίμων αὐτῷ γενομένων" (EUSG 2940), which McGiffert renders "familiar acquaintances" (EUS 34328-34331). Alexander's "κύριόν μου γενόμενον καὶ ὠφελήσαντά με" (EUSG 3044-3052) is "my master and benefactor", and he uses κύριον of Origen too. McGiffert himself says the pupil reading is an inference: "The epistle does not state this directly" (EUS note 1879). The Connection and the impact are story lines, so the word must go. Replacements:
- Line 28, Shape: replace "Who learned it: Origen and Alexander as pupils, and Jerome as a later reader." with "Who learned it: Origen, his pupil by Eusebius's account; Alexander, a bishop of his own time who calls him his master and helper; and Jerome as a later reader."
- Line 45, What came of it 1: replace "**His pupils.** Origen, by Eusebius's account (E7), and Alexander, who calls him master and came to know Origen through him (E16). (I1)" with "**His pupil, and a bishop who honored him.** Origen, his pupil by Eusebius's account (E7), and Alexander, who calls him "the holy Clement", his master and helper, and came to know Origen through him (E16). (I1)"
- Line 56, Connection: replace "Alexander, his pupil and friend, came to know Origen through Pantaenus and Clement (E16)." with "Alexander, who calls him his master and helper, came to know Origen through him and another blessed man (E16)."
- Line 64, Who tells it: replace "Alexander (old; his pupil and friend, in Clement's own time)" with "Alexander (old; a bishop in Clement's own time, who calls him his master and helper)". Add to Do not say: "Alexander was Clement's pupil" or "his pupil and friend" → "Alexander, who calls him his master and helper" (E16; the pupil reading is McGiffert's inference, M1, and is not told).
- `Source_Sheet.md` line 19: replace "**contemporary** (Alexander, his pupil and friend)" with "**contemporary** (Alexander, a bishop of his own time)". Row I1: replace "His pupils: Origen as a boy (E7);" with "His pupil and a bishop who honored him: Origen, by Eusebius's account (E7);".
