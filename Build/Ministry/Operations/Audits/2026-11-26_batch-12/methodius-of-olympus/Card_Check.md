## Fixes

1. **The "list of Nikephoros" is an editor's Latin chapter summary, and it says "Myra or Patara".** NIK 1335 is in Migne's Latin argument to Nikephoros's works ("Cap. XI. Testimonium Methodii Myrorum, vel Patarorum Lyciae episcopi ..."), which the source sheet itself calls "the editor's chapter list". The card gives it as Nikephoros's own witness and drops "Myra or". It cannot stand as an old source, so Patara rests on John of Damascus alone (JD 108853, "Sancti Methodii episcopi Patarorum et martyris").
   - `uncertain[1]`. Old: "Much later writers, John of Damascus and the list of Nikephoros, call him bishop of Patara, also in Lycia." New: "John of Damascus, writing much later, calls him bishop of Patara, also in Lycia."
   - `timeline[0].lines[2].text`. Old: "John of Damascus, and the list of Nikephoros, call him bishop of Patara." New: "John of Damascus calls him bishop of Patara."
   - `timeline[0].lines[2].reference`. Old: "John of Damascus, cic/texts/john-damascus_opera-omnia-t1-with-vita-pg94-grc-lat_migne1860.txt lines 108853; Nikephoros, cic/texts/nikephoros-cp_opera-with-vita-stephani-iunioris-pg100-grc-lat_migne1860.txt lines 1335". New: "John of Damascus, Against the Jacobites, cic/texts/john-damascus_opera-omnia-t1-with-vita-pg94-grc-lat_migne1860.txt line 108853".
2. **`legacy.traces[4]`, second sentence** (same ground as Fix 1). Old: "Later writers on the use of images in worship cited him too, John of Damascus among them, and, by a list of Nikephoros of Constantinople, those who opposed images." New: "John of Damascus, writing on the use of images in worship, cited him too."
3. **`sources.about`, the Nikephoros entry** ("Works, the editor's chapter list", Nikephoros of Constantinople, NIK file): remove it. No card line cites it after Fixes 1 and 2.
4. **`sources.about`, the John of Damascus entry, `title`.** The card cites the file only at line 108853, which stands in the testimonies of the Fathers in Against the Jacobites (heading at JD 108466, "CONTRA JACOBITAS"), not in the Orations on Images. The images citation rests on A6 35841-35843. Old: "Orations on Images". New: "Against the Jacobites, testimonies of the Fathers".
5. **`quotationOriginals[1].uncertain`, Holl's apparatus lines.** "ὄντι <MU" and "χυϑῇ Pa" stand at EPI 35750; 35747-35748 hold only the lemma's start. Old: "(lines 35747-35748)". New: "(lines 35747-35750)".
6. **`quotationOriginals[2].work`.** Nothing held shows that Lietzmann reprints Hussey's edition; his text differs from Hussey's in punctuation (LIE 13702-13704; SOCG 12877-12881). Old: "Greek ed. Robert Hussey, as Hans Lietzmann reprints it". New: "Greek as Hans Lietzmann prints it, and ed. Robert Hussey".
7. **`timeline[4].lines[4].reference`, the Syriac chronicle.** The oil poured under the statue's feet runs to ZR 923-924. Old: "cic/texts/zachariah-rhetor_chronicle_hamiltonbrooks1899.txt lines 899-921". New: "cic/texts/zachariah-rhetor_chronicle_hamiltonbrooks1899.txt lines 899-924".
