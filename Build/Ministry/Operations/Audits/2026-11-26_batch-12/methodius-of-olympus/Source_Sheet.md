Line ranges count the raw file; where an inline footnote or editor's bracket interrupts a passage in the XML, the range spans it and that text is left out of the quotation (marked "note left out"). **Explain it: Origen's teaching, from each side.** Methodius's side: R1, R3, R4 (the body a fetter given after the fall; the soul alone the person; only the shape rises, on another body). Origen's own side, held only in Rufinus's Latin (O1): the dead rise in their own bodies, made spiritual. The batch sets Origen's teaching to be told here only as Methodius answers it; O1 is for the card and the Do not say list.
