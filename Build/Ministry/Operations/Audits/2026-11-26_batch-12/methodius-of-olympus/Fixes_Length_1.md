## What stays

The approved heart stands whole. The essence is untouched: the bishop in Lycia, the Banquet, On the Resurrection against Origen, Epiphanius's copying and Jerome's word on the martyrdom. Also told keeps the book as a story one friend begs another to repeat, the line on marriage with Quote 1, Virtue's line on the whole person and the closing hymn; Origen's teaching as Methodius gave it, with its credit to the later summary; the answer, with Cain, soul and body joined, and the statue (Quote 2), the scene that carries the craft; and the quarrel, with Eusebius's question through Jerome and Socrates's scorn (Quote 3) and the Xenon. The record's force stays: Methodius's refusal to bear those who make the resurrection fleshless, Eusebius's challenge, Socrates's charge in its own words. Every Explain it the remaining beats need stays: chastity, Virtue, the fall, the chain, Origen, Eusebius's book, the Xenon, the Bridegroom as Christ. Jerome's two dates for the death stay.
