The first read's own reason shows the move: the word was carried over from man to the statue. That is a detail moved onto the wrong thing, one of Part 1's four faults.
- Change: "In his telling, a craftsman finds that an envious man has spoiled a beautiful statue he made, so he melts it down and casts it again." The parallel with paragraph 5's last sentence ("God, seeing his finest work spoiled by envy") stays, now in its right place.
