## Sentence by sentence

1. "In that writing, a sermon on repentance, Peter gave each of them a time of fasting and prayer, and its rules are known as his canons." **HELD.** ANF6 26600–26601: "The Canons of the Blessed Peter, Archbishop of Alexandria, as They are Given in His Sermon on Penitence"; canon 1 opens "since the fourth passover of the persecution has arrived" (ANF6 26608), so it is the Easter writing of paragraph 1; fasting and prayer, ANF6 26625–26628 ("constantly fasted, then let them watch in prayer").

2. "The tortured would fast forty more days, the days Christ fasted, and those jailed without torture would wait a year more." **HELD.** ANF6 26623–26625 ("other forty days ... during which ... our Lord ... had fasted"); ANF6 26681–26683 ("without the conflict of torments, were led captive ... a year in addition").

3. "Clergy who gave themselves up to the persecutors unbidden, fell, and then took up the struggle again could share communion, but not return to their office." **SUMMARY HOLDS** on the facts. PED 50115: "τοὺς ἀπὸ Κλήρου αὐτομολήσαντας, ἐκπεπτωχότας, καὶ ἀναπαλαίσαντας, ἔτι ἐν τῇ λειτουργία εἶναι"; ANF6 27162–27164 ("deserted of their own accord, and have lapsed, and taken up the contest afresh"); the sense of αὐτομολήσαντας is fixed by canon 9, the same verb, "would not have us go over to the ministers and satellites of the devil" (ANF6 27074–27075), and by Balsamon, "if any clergyman hath of his own accord sought the contest" (ANF6 27205–27206); "communion is sufficient for them" (ANF6 27192–27193). **But see Finding 1:** "return to their office" is the live story's wording.

4. "Four bishops of Egypt, writing from prison, rebuked Melitius, a bishop of Upper Egypt, in the south, for making priests and deacons in churches that were not his." **SUMMARY HOLDS.** "Meanwhile" is gone, as the outline's Do not say asks. "in the south" glosses Epiphanius's "ἐπισκόπου ἐν τῇ Θηβαΐδι" (EPI 43601), the Thebaid being Upper, southern Egypt; a plain gloss, no new claim.

5. "The priests Peter had appointed to visit the churches were then in hiding, and Melitius set them aside and put men of his own in their place." **HELD** as the account's claim (the paragraph opens "An account kept with that letter says"). ANF6 16836–16840: "certain presbyters, then in hiding, to whom the blessed Peter had given power to act as parish-visitors ... suspended them for the time, and by his own authority ordained two persons in their place".

6. "Peter wrote to his people that Melitius was invading his church and trying to pull its priests from his authority." **SUMMARY HOLDS.** ANF6 27793–27797: "invading my parish, hath assumed so much to himself as to endeavour to separate from my authority the priests"; the editor's note (ANF6 27794–27795) that the parish "was greater than the diocese" supports "church" over the modern "parish".

7. "Eusebius of Caesarea, who wrote at the time, gave it in these words: ..." **SUMMARY HOLDS.** Eusebius writes of "the persecution in our day" (EUS `npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml` 43312) and sets Peter's death among events of his own time (EUS 47990–47998). The outline's Do not say holds this wording.

8. "The split outlived him, and in 325 the bishops gathered at the Council of Nicaea let Melitius keep his city but took away his power to ordain." **HELD.** SOC 3206–3209: "It was decreed, the Synod being moved to great clemency towards Melitius ... that he remain in his own city but exercise no authority either to ordain or nominate for ordination".

## Finding 1 (BLOCKING): live wording in the clergy sentence

Live: "Fallen clergy kept communion, but were not to return to their office." New: "... could share communion, but not return to their office." The outline holds "communion, but not their office again" (B2), not "return". The source also says *remain*, not *return*: "should remain any longer in their sacred office" (ANF6 27163–27164), "they can no longer discharge their sacred ministry" (ANF6 27190–27191); Greek "ἔτι ἐν τῇ λειτουργία εἶναι" (PED 50115).

**Exact change:** "... and then took up the struggle again could share communion, but could no longer serve in their office."
