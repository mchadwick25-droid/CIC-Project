# Fixes from the review: Peter of Alexandria

Applies the review's "needs the outline" findings (B1, M7) to `Outline.md` only. Nothing else in the outline was changed.

## Applied verbatim

- **B1, Do not say.** The review's line was added to Do not say (Outline.md line 158), word for word, with its bracket filled in with my wording for αὐτομολήσαντας ("gave themselves up to the persecutors unbidden"): Not "Clergy who fell could share communion but never return to their office" as a rule for every fallen cleric. Canon 10 rules only on clergy who gave themselves up to the persecutors unbidden, fell, and took up the struggle again: communion, not their office (E11, ANF6 27162–27193).

## Worded by me

- **B1, beat B2's clergy line (Outline.md line 56).** Old: "Clergy who fell: communion, but not their office again (E11, ANF6 27162–27193)." New: "Clergy who gave themselves up to the persecutors unbidden, fell, and then took up the struggle again: communion, but not their office again (E11, ANF6 27162–27165, 27192–27193; Greek PED 50115–50116, "τοὺς ἀπὸ Κλήρου αὐτομολήσαντας, ἐκπεπτωχότας, καὶ ἀναπαλαίσαντας"). αὐτομολήσαντας = gave themselves up unbidden: the canon follows from canon 9 ("Ὄθεν", "Whence", PED 50115, ANF6 27162), where Christ "commanded His disciples ... not of their own accord to give themselves up to the tormentors" (ANF6 27151–27153; PED 50045–50046, "οὐ γὰρ θέλει αὐτομολεῖν ἡμᾶς πρὸς τοὺς τοῦ διαθόλου ὑπασπιστὰς"), and Balsamon (12th c.) reads it so: "if any clergyman hath of his own accord sought the contest" (ANF6 27205–27206). Leaving "destitute the flock of the Lord" is the canon's reason for the loss of office, not the word's sense (ANF6 27165)."
  - Checked in the files: ANF6 = `cic/texts/anf06_gregory-thaumaturgus-dionysius-julius-africanus-methodius-arnobius.xml` (27151–27153 canon 9; 27162–27166 and 27192–27193 canon 10; 27202–27207 Balsamon, "The father having spoken of those who of their own accord went over to the contest of martyrdom, now also speaks of those of the clergy who are in such a case"). PED = `cic/texts/nikodemos-hagiorite_pedalion-canons-grc_1886.txt` (50045–50046 canon 9; 50115–50116 canon 10). The Greek is quoted as the OCR prints it ("ἐκπεπτωχότας", "διαθόλου"); the review's "ἐκπεπτωκότας" is the normalized form of the same word.
  - Why "gave themselves up", not "left their flock": canon 10 opens "Whence", drawing its rule from canon 9's charge against those who go to the persecutors of their own accord, where the same verb (αὐτομολεῖν) names that act; Balsamon reads canon 10 the same way. The flock is the reason the canon gives for taking away office (ANF6 27165–27166, "inasmuch as they have left destitute the flock of the Lord"). Nikodemos's own reading (PED 50150–50152, "ἐπῆγαν μὲν ἀφ ἑαυτοῦ των εἰς τὸ μαρτύριον") agrees, but he is modern (1800) and is not cited in the beat.
  - The review's suggested brief line ("Clergy who fell and then stood firm again could share communion, but not return to their office.") still leaves out the unbidden surrender; the writer should carry the beat's wording, for example "Clergy who gave themselves up unbidden, fell, and then stood firm again could share communion, but not return to their office." That is the writer's change; the brief is not edited here.

## Not applied

- **M7, a plain-sense clause for "as if Maximinus had ordered it".** None added. No held line gives a plain sense beyond the "as if" itself. The Greek is "ὡς ἂν Μαξιμίνου προστάξαντος" (EUS-G 17558–17560, OCR "διαξιμίνου"); McGiffert's English keeps it, "as if by command of Maximinus" (EUS 47996–47997, `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`), with no note on it (his note 2736 is on the date only, EUS 47991–47993); Rufinus's Latin keeps it too, "tamquam ex Maximini praecepto" (EUS-G 17618). A clause saying either that Maximinus did order it or that he did not would add a reading no held text gives. The review allows "or none"; Q3 stands as it is.

It is Mark's approved heart wording, and the review asked for beat B2 only, so it is not changed here. Flagged: the beat and the new Do not say line now govern the telling.
