# Outline check: Peter of Alexandria

## My account of the heart (written before reading Outline.md)

From the census entry (card `knownFor`: bishop of Alexandria through the Great Persecution, wrote the rules for the lapsed, broke with Melitius, beheaded 311), the slate job, and the source sheet:

- **Essence.** Peter led the churches of Egypt and Libya as bishop of Alexandria through the Great Persecution. At its fourth Easter he set out, in a sermon on repentance, how each kind of Christian who had given way could come back to communion, weighed by what each had suffered; he broke with Melitius, who ordained in other bishops' churches and in Peter's own; and he was beheaded, with no reason given, in the ninth year of the persecution (311).
- **Also told.** The canons as substance: the tortured who fell "betrayed by the frailty of the flesh" and bearing "the marks of Jesus" get forty days; lighter or heavier terms by degree (prison without torture, fear alone, tricks, slaves and masters); those who fled or paid are not blamed; fallen clergy keep communion but not office. The break: the four imprisoned bishops' letter on ordaining outside one's own church, the account of Melitius entering Alexandria, Peter's letter telling his people to hold no communion with him; Athanasius's charge (deposition by council, including sacrifice) and Epiphanius's prison quarrel over mercy, each credited. The death: seized "for no cause", beheaded, with many Egyptian bishops (Eusebius).
- **What came of him.** Heaviest: his canons ratified at Trullo (692) and carried in every canon collection with Balsamon and Zonaras (defended: the measured return of the fallen). Then: the Melitian split outlived him and came to Nicaea (defended: one bishop's order in each church, Alexandria over Egypt). Then: remembered as "Bishop and Martyr" (Athanasius, Socrates). Card: his teaching read at Ephesus.
