## Findings with exact changes (MINOR, apply word for word)

### Finding 1. Which writing (listener 3.1)

The sermon two paragraphs after the Easter writing is not placed as the same text. The trust readers confirm it is: canon 1 opens "since the fourth passover of the persecution has arrived" (ANF6 26608), under the heading "as They are Given in His Sermon on Penitence" (ANF6 26600–26601).

- Old: `In that writing, a sermon on repentance,`
- New: `In that Easter writing, a sermon on repentance,`

### Finding 2. "unbidden" (listener 3.2, ruled by the trust readers' evidence)

The listener did not know "unbidden", the word that carries the reason these clergy lose their office. Ruled by the trust readers' evidence: both rest the sense of αὐτομολήσαντας (PED 50115) on canon 9's use of the same verb and on Balsamon, "if any clergyman hath of his own accord sought the contest" (ANF6 27205–27206), and the 1886 English of canon 10 itself reads "those of the clergy who have deserted of their own accord" (ANF6 27163). "Of their own accord" keeps the source's force exactly and is the translator's own phrase. It does not echo the live text.

- Old: `gave themselves up to the persecutors unbidden, fell,`
- New: `gave themselves up to the persecutors of their own accord, fell,`

### Finding 3. "return to their office" (second trust read, Finding 1; ruled MINOR)

The second trust reader marked this BLOCKING as a live-text echo. The bar does not block an echo: it blocks unsupported claims, misleading implications, missing material, failure on one hearing, a children's-book stretch and the tells (Kit Part 3). The echo rule is this batch's (slate decision 2). The claim holds: the fresh trust read rests "not return" on "seek for that which they have left" (ANF6 27181–27182). But the canon's own verb is *remain* and *discharge*: "should remain any longer in their sacred office" (ANF6 27164–27165), "they can no longer discharge their sacred ministry" (ANF6 27190–27191), Greek "ἔτι ἐν τῇ λειτουργίᾳ εἶναι" (PED 50115). The change sits closer to the source and clears the live wording, so it is applied.

- Old: `could share communion, but not return to their office.`
- New: `could share communion, but could no longer serve in their office.`
