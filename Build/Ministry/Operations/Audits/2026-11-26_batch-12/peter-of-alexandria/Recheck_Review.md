## BLOCKING

**R1. Beat B2 credits a 12th-century commentator's words to canon 9 (Outline.md line 56).** The quote "commanded His disciples ... not of their own accord to give themselves up to the tormentors" (ANF6 27151–27153) is not canon 9. It sits in Zonaras's comment on canon 9 (his paragraph opens at ANF6 27131, "Zonaras. Those who have recently arisen from sleep"). The beat presents it as the canon ("where Christ 'commanded ...'"), so a writer could carry a commentator's paraphrase as Peter's word: wrong witness kind. Canon 9's own English for the Greek it cites is at ANF6 27074–27075. (`Fixes_Review.md`'s "27151–27153 canon 9" carries the same error.)

Replace, in Outline.md line 56:

> where Christ "commanded His disciples ... not of their own accord to give themselves up to the tormentors" (ANF6 27151–27153; PED 50045–50046, "οὐ γὰρ θέλει αὐτομολεῖν ἡμᾶς πρὸς τοὺς τοῦ διαθόλου ὑπασπιστὰς"),

with:

> where Peter writes that Christ "would not have us go over to the ministers and satellites of the devil" (ANF6 27074–27075; PED 50045–50046, "οὐ γὰρ θέλει αὐτομολεῖν ἡμᾶς πρὸς τοὺς τοῦ διαθόλου ὑπασπιστὰς"), the same verb; Zonaras (12th c.) reads canon 9 so: Christ "commanded His disciples ... not of their own accord to give themselves up to the tormentors" (ANF6 27151–27153);

The scope of the beat's clergy line does not change; it stands on canon 9's own words and Balsamon.

**R2. The heart's "Also told" line still states the clergy rule for every fallen cleric (Outline.md line 8).** The checked beat (line 56) and canon 10 (ANF6 27162–27164, 27185–27187) rule only on clergy who went to the contest unbidden, fell, and took up the struggle again. The heart line governs the writer and widens the canon, the very fault of review B1. This is Mark's approved wording; the replacement is reported to Mark with the brief.

Replace, in Outline.md line 8:

with:

> communion but not office for clergy who gave themselves up to the persecutors unbidden, fell, and took up the struggle again,
