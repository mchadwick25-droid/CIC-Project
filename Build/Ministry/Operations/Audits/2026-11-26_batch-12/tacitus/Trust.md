## The one BLOCKING sentence

**S17. ASSUMPTION ADDED.** "He put on chariot races there too, mixing with the crowd dressed as a charioteer, or standing in a chariot himself."

- Source, MIR 2562–2564 (lines "Hortos suos ei spectaculo Nero obtulerat et circense ludicrum edebat, habitu aurigae permixtus plebi vel curriculo insistens"): Nero gave his gardens for the show *and* put on a circus show. The Latin does not say where the circus show was held. TAC 68 s11 agrees: "Nero offered his gardens for the spectacle, and was exhibiting a show in the circus".
- "there too" puts the races in the gardens. That is a place the source does not give. It leans toward the modern placing the outline bars ("in the Vatican", Duchesne, Do not say).
- Change, told as the source gives it: "He also put on chariot races, mixing with the crowd dressed as a charioteer, or standing in a chariot himself."
- Do not say line for the outline: Not that the chariot races were held in the gardens ("there"). Tacitus gives the gardens and the races side by side and names no place for the races (C6).
