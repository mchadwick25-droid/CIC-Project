# Batch 12 length count (independent)

You count, for each of twenty outlines, the words its story must carry. You did not write them and you change nothing in them. Read Part 2 ("Length") of `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md` and the Length section of `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/kinds/person.md` first.

The outlines: `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-26_batch-12/<slug>/Outline.md` for josephus, tacitus, suetonius, justin-martyr, tatian, ignatius-of-antioch, valentinus, bardaisan-of-edessa, hippolytus-of-rome, clement-of-alexandria, tertullian-s-voice, cyprian-of-carthage, pontius-the-deacon, lactantius, origen, methodius-of-olympus, pamphilus-of-caesarea, eusebius-of-caesarea, anatolius-of-alexandria-and-laodicea, peter-of-alexandria. Read nothing else.

For each, count from the outline itself, not from its own stated count:
- **Quotes:** the words of each modern rendering it plans to use.
- **Glosses:** each entry under Words to explain, at about 8 words each where it is worked into a sentence.
- **Witnesses:** each witness the story must credit by name or role (Who tells it, and every "credit" or "say so" in the beats and Do not say), at about 6 words each.
- **Narration:** every distinct fact the hook, essence, beats, what came of them, connection and ending must tell, at about 11 words each. Items the outline marks optional, or puts on its Cut first list, are counted separately, not in the total.

A person is heard at about 150 words a minute: at least about 300 words (2:00) unless the record is too thin, aimed about 450 (3:00), at most about 675 (4:30). Verdict: AIM (480 or under), LONG (up to 675), OVER (above it).

Write `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-26_batch-12/Length_Count.md`: one table (Slug, Outline plans, Quotes, Glosses, Witnesses, Narration with fact count, Total, Time, Verdict, After Cut first: the Total with the Cut first items kept, and their words in brackets), then for each LONG or OVER story one line naming what carries the weight (which beats, how many points). Reply in under 150 words with the table's totals only.
