## BLOCKING

**S17.** "In his life of Nero, Suetonius lists measures by which, he says, abuses were punished and new rules made."
- ROL 28454–28455 (*Nero* 16.2): "Multa sub eo et animadversa severe et coercita nec minus instituta"; ROL 28516–28518: "many abuses were severely punished and put down, and no fewer new laws were made".
- SOFTENED: the source says *severely* punished and put down. It is the one word he gives on the manner of the measures in the list that holds the Christians. The brief then says he names no "kind of punishment", so dropping "severely" also softens his force. (The outline, beat 4, carries the same dropped word.)
- Change: "In his life of Nero, Suetonius lists measures by which, he says, abuses were severely punished and put down, and new rules made."
- Do not say (for the outline): Not "abuses were punished" without "severely". His Latin is "animadversa severe et coercita" (E3).
