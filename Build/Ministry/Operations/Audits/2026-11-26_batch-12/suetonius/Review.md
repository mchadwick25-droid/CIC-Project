- Evidence: ROL 28454–28455 (*Nero* 16.2), "Multa sub eo et animadversa severe et coercita nec minus instituta"; ROL 28516–28518, "many abuses were severely punished and put down, and no fewer new laws were made". The brief has "abuses were punished and new rules made".
- Judgment: blocking. "Severe" is the only word Suetonius gives on the manner of the measures in the list that holds the Christians. The brief then says he names no "kind of punishment", so dropping it softens his force. Part 1 bars softening the record by leaving out its force, and the bar counts a softened sentence as unsupported. The first read's HELD does not show from the source that the sentence holds, so the second read's finding stands.
- Change: "In his life of Nero, Suetonius lists measures by which, he says, abuses were severely punished and put down, and new rules made."
- **Needs the outline.** Beat 4 carries the same dropped word. `Source_Sheet.md` E3 holds "severe" and the English, so the researcher restores it in beat 4 before the revision.
- Do not say (add to the outline): Not "abuses were punished" without "severely". His Latin is "animadversa severe et coercita": "severely punished and put down" (E3).
