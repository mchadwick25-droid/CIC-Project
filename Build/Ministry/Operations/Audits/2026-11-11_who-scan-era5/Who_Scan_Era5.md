# Who scan, era 5 (2026-11-11)

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-11). Read-only scan of era 5 (1054–1300 CE, "The High Medieval Era") of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Counts, 101 rows (a paired row counts once): **WHO 14, STORY 9, LISTED 35, WANTED 43**. Judgment-call rows: 62. Councils are not people and carry no verdict (see Councils below). Of the three live person entries, Gregory VII and Bonaventure are WHO and Innocent III is WANTED, so Innocent III is flagged (Mark: "Wanted, keep live"). The Library is thin for this era: of the 43 WANTED rows, most are people whose named source is not held at all. No Muslim, Jewish or Western secular chronicler of 1054–1300 is held in his own text, so the only OUTSIDE WITNESS rows are WANTED (Anna Komnene, Marco Polo).

## How the list was built

- Era 5 has 30 census entries: 27 movements and 3 person entries (Gregory VII, Innocent III, Bonaventure). Four movements (Humiliati, Free Spirit, Carthusian, Church of the East under the Mongols) name few or no individuals; their shapers come from their sources notes and the corpus-map rows.
- Named voices and `people` lists of the movements (old rules), merged by alias.
- Carried from era 4: Adam of Bremen (WANTED) and Nestor the chronicler (WANTED).
- Found in text (census longDescription, legacy, relationsSummary and sources): Adam of Eynsham, Walter Daniel, William of St Thierry, Thomas of Celano, Agnes of Prague, Albert the Great, Thomas Aquinas, Anselm of Canterbury, Thomas Becket, Gratian, Cardinal Deusdedit, Lucius III, Mari ibn Sulayman and Abu Salih (corpus-map), and others marked "found in text".
- Five drafting workers (Sonnet) sorted the candidates by region and opened the cited lines. One Opus subagent then opened the cited lines for every WHO, STORY and WANTED row, 18 LISTED rows and the draft councils (`Opus_Check_Notes.md`). A targeted Opus recheck read the 12 rows that changed afterwards (the second part of the same file).

## Rulings applied (Mark, 2026-11-11)

- The standard of eras 1 to 4: WHO is church-shaping with a body of work or a following and enough life to tell; recorders are WHO with a thin own life flagged; women are WHO if they founded, taught or led; rulers are STORY unless they have their own voice; founders known through enemies need two witnesses for the teaching and may rest the life on one; OUTSIDE WITNESS is a WHO label for objective historians outside the church.
- Pairing (Mark, 2026-11-11): "if two people are tied together in their sources with similar identity its ok to make that a single who". Paired rows are judgment calls.
- A later retelling alone gives LISTED until an early source is held (Vladimir and Olga precedent). A WANTED source must be able to carry a life, not only writing (coordinator's routine call).
- Era is by date of work: work after 1300 is carried to era 6; work before 1054 goes to an era 4 supplement unless an era 4 row exists.
- "Major?" column (proposal only): the map shows up to 4 names per tradition and 5 per river per era, chosen from the major Whos. Mark confirms it in each batch.
- Editions tagged "[suggested; to confirm]" were named from the drafting workers' or checker's memory, not from the census or the Library. The Library thread confirms them.
