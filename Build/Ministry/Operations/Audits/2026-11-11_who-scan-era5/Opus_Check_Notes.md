# Opus check of the Who scan, era 5 (2026-11-11)

Read-only. I opened the cited lines in `cic/texts/` (each file's own line numbers, as `rg -n` prints them) for all 67 WHO, STORY and WANTED rows in `check_rows.md`, all 15 council rows in `check_councils.md`, and 18 LISTED rows from `check_listed.md`. For every WANTED row I grepped the file names and the text for the named source and for the person. No paid calls were made and nothing in the repo was changed.

## 1. Summary counts

- **Rows checked: 67.** 49 confirmed, 18 corrected, 0 not supported.
  - WHO (16): 9 confirmed, 7 corrected.
  - STORY (8): 7 confirmed, 1 corrected.
  - WANTED (43): 33 confirmed, 10 corrected.
- **Invented person-detail: none found.** Two fact errors, neither invented: Agnes of Prague's death day is mistranslated ("sexto nonas Martii" is 2 March, not 6 March). Yahya ibn Said's row says no held line dates his work, but his own preface does (PO18 44779-44789). I found no OCR column run-together. I found no namesake mix-up. One form note: Racki writes "car Boris" for Boril (RACKI-8 3342).
- **Recommended verdict and era changes (17):**
  - Athanasios the Athonite: **remove from era 5.** An era 4 row already exists (WHO, confirmed by the era 4 check: `Who_Scan_Era4.md` l.48). The workers' grep missed it.
  - Yahya ibn Said: **carry back to era 4.** In his own words he wrote the book, rewrote it, then moved to Antioch in AH 405 (1014-15) and revised it (PO18 44779-44789). The work is before 1054. No era 4 row exists, so it goes to an era 4 supplement.
  - Symeon the New Theologian: **era 4 by date of work.** No era 4 row exists, so he goes to an era 4 supplement. If Mark prefers he stays here, WANTED stands.
  - Innocent III: **WHO to WANTED, judgment call.** FLAGGED: live person entry. No life is held, only his letters. This follows the era 4 John of Damascus precedent and the Nicholas I row. Source wanted: the *Gesta Innocentii III*, tr. James M. Powell, *The Deeds of Pope Innocent III* (Catholic University of America Press, 2004) [my recollection; Library thread to confirm]. Alternative: keep WHO as a named exception (Mark's call).
  - Sava of Serbia: **WHO to WANTED, judgment call.** Apart from his own rule, the life is Meyer (1894) summarising Shafarik (1865), a modern retelling. This matches the George the Hagiorite row. Source wanted: Domentijan's Life of Sava. RACKI-8 3437 cites its edition, "Žitie sv. Save od Domentijana, izd. Gj. Daničić, Beograd".
  - Henry IV: **STORY to WHO, judgment call.** His own first-person letters are held: to his son (FASC 13059-13107) and to the "king of the Celts" (FASC 12926-13056, "Cum ego ad locum qui Binga vocatur pervenissem", 12977; forced to give up the regalia, 13009). These are personal narratives of his fall, not chancery formulas. Alternative: STORY.
  - Gerard of Borgo San Donnino: **WANTED to WHO, judgment call.** A held Spiritual narrative tells his trial and prison (EHRLE-2 15898-15920). His book is held in the Anagni extracts. Alternative: WANTED.
  - Durand of Osca: **WANTED to LISTED.** The named sources carry writing, not a life (coordinator's rule). A held line exists: the profession of faith prescribed to him (DENZ 13694-13716).
  - Toros Roslin: **WANTED to LISTED.** The named source is short colophons with no edition known in translation. It cannot carry a life.
  - Alberic and Stephen Harding: **split, judgment call.** Stephen WANTED. Alberic LISTED, or both kept with Robert as one founders' row. See the pairs section below.
  - LISTED spot-checks: Frederick II LISTED to STORY; Lalibela LISTED to STORY (judgment call); William of St Thierry LISTED to WANTED (recorder; judgment call); John of Rila to era 4 (by date); Humbert of Romans arguable, WANTED for consistency (see section 5).
- **Councils: 15 checked.** 6 confirmed, 9 corrected. Three councils are entered twice or three times: Verona/Ad abolendam, Fourth Lateran and Second Lyon. Several rows wrongly say "acts not held". Denzinger holds canon extracts of Lateran I, II, III and IV, Verona 1184 and Lyon II.
- **LISTED spot-checks: 18.** 13 right as LISTED: Guigo II, Albert of Jerusalem, Simon Stock, Rufino and Angelo, Albert the Great, Zigabenos, Alexios I, Stefan Nemanja, Boril, the Awlad al-Assal, Sorghaghtani, Demetre I, and Lucius III (but his evidence is wrong). John of Rila is right as LISTED but sits in the wrong era. 4 should change: Frederick II, Lalibela, William of St Thierry, Humbert of Romans (arguable).
- **Pairs (Mark's ruling):**
  - **Yahballaha III and Rabban Sauma.** One Syriac Life tells both men and its title names both. Both are Uighur monks from Khan-balik who set out together (YS-EN 2257-2263). Chabot says their stories are "intimement liée" (YS-FR 11600-11604). The pair holds, as a judgment call. Their identities diverge from ch. IV (catholicos; envoy and Visitor). Alternative: two rows.
  - **Domentijan and Teodosije.** Both are Hilandar monks who wrote Lives of Sava. They have a similar identity and the pair holds. Note that the "Theodosije" in ATHOS 2649 (d. 1716) is a later namesake.
  - **Rufino and Angelo.** Both are companions named with Leo (SPEC 4886-4892, 6380-6384; FIOR 935, 979). The pair holds.
  - **Awlad al-Assal.** No held source ties the brothers. The only held line is "the Coptic canonist Ibn Assal" (BRAUN 1519), which is al-Safi alone. The pair rests on the census. Their identities are close (scholars of one family), so it can stand as a judgment call, LISTED.
  - **Alberic and Stephen Harding.** No founding text is held, so the tie cannot be checked. Stephen has his own work (the Carta caritatis, by tradition); Alberic has almost no individual record. Their identities differ, so I recommend two rows (or one row with Robert).
- **Era scan:** Athanasios has an era 4 row. Yahya, Symeon and John of Rila are era 4 by date of work. Marguerite Porete sits on the edge: the Mirror is c.1290s-1306 and the trial is 1308-10. Keep her here, flagged; alternative: carry to era 6. Yahballaha's career runs 1281-1317, mostly before 1300, and stays. No other row duplicates an era 1-4 row: I grepped every era 5 name against the four earlier audits.
- **Held candidates outside the census (for a supplement, not scored):**
  - Elizabeth of Thuringia (d. 1231). Her confessor Conrad of Marburg's *Summa vitae* (1232) is held in MIRBT 12954-13034. She had a hospital built (12972), so she is a likely WHO under the women rule.
  - Berengar of Tours. His oath of 1079 is held at DENZ 12533-12553.
  - Peter Abelard. The errors condemned at Sens are held, with a short life note (DENZ 12863-12961; 12889).
