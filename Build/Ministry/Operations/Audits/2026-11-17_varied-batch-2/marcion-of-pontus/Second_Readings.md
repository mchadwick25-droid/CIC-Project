# Second Readings: marcion-of-pontus

## 1. Do you know me?
Source: cic/texts/anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-irenaeus.xml 39883
Second rendering: Do you know me?
Ours: Do you know me?
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning, updated from "Dost thou".

## 2. I do know you: the firstborn
Source: cic/texts/anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-irenaeus.xml 39883-39884
Second rendering: I do know you, the firstborn of Satan.
Ours: I do know you: the firstborn of Satan.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning, updated from "thee".

## 3. Even now he is still teaching
Source: cic/texts/justin-martyr_first-apology-grc_rauschen1911.txt 730-731
Second rendering: Even now he is still teaching those who listen to him to believe in some other god, greater than the Creator.
Ours: Even now he is still teaching the people he has won over. He teaches them to believe in some other god, greater than the Creator.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning; "the people he has won over" fairly carries τοὺς πειθομένους, and splitting it into two sentences adds nothing.

## 4. Separating the law from the gospel
Source: cic/texts/tertullian_quae-supersunt-t2-polemica-lat_oehler1853.txt 4337-4338
Second rendering: Marcion's own distinctive work, and his chief one, is to separate the Law from the Gospel.
Ours: Separating the law from the gospel is Marcion's own work, and his chief one.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning; proprium (his own) and principale (chief) are both carried.
