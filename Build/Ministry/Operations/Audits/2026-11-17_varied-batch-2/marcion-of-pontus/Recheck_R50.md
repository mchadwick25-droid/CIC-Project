## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Marcion, a shipmaster from Pontus on the Black Sea, who joined the church at Rome in the middle of the second century and gave it money.
2. **What happened:** he taught that the Creator of the Old Testament was not the good God revealed in Christ, set the gospel against the law, kept a shortened Luke and Paul (his opponents say; he said he was restoring them), was expelled from Rome with his money, and founded churches of his own.
3. **Why the connection matters:** his opponents (Justin, Irenaeus, Tertullian, Polycarp) answered him book after book for one God and four Gospels, while his churches spread from Rome to Persia and lasted at least two centuries; all we know of him comes from those opponents.
