# Outline check: Marcion of Pontus

Checker: independent outline check, 2026-11-17. I read the kit's template and shapes, the source sheet and the outline, then opened every cited passage in `cic/texts/` at its lines (ANF 13958–13966, 14940–14948, 16128–16137, 33570–33600, 39855–39890, 41140–41151, 44905–44918; JG 726–733; T 21328–21364, 22505–22513, 22550–22640, 23720–23742, 23765–23771, 24530–24538, 29085–29105, 29145–29160, 29200–29260, 29320–29331, 40388–40396; OEH 4335–4339; EUS 23654–23683, 25257–25305, 28922–28929, 29926–29931, 40470–40475; EPI 7462–7474).

## Verdict: FIX

Two blocking findings, both quick to fix. All quotes are verbatim. The renderings are sound. The setting, shape, length, tradition name and leave-outs all fit the record. The outline is careful about the late legends (D3, D4), the money, the dates and the bishops. What it misses is that several beats hand the writer the opponents' charges with no "they say" attached.

## Findings

**1. BLOCKING: beats 5 and 6 state the opponents' charges as plain fact.** Beat 2 tells the writer to say "Tertullian says". Beats 5 and 6 do not, even though everything in them comes from Irenaeus and Tertullian, who were hostile witnesses. "He kept one Gospel, Luke's, shortened … a shortened set of Paul's letters. He dropped the letters to Timothy and Titus" is the charge Irenaeus makes ("mutilates", "dismembered", ANF 33581–33590) and Tertullian makes ("I wonder … that he rejected the two epistles to Timothy and the one to Titus", T 40391–40395). "Dropped" means he knew those letters and removed them. That is Tertullian's charge, and the text gives it as Tertullian's wondering, not as a fact he shows. The same goes for "His churches baptised only the unmarried …" (T 24533–24537, "according to Marcion" in Tertullian's report). "Documented as report" in the tag does not reach the writer's sentence.
Correction. Beat 5: "His opponents say he cut down Luke's Gospel and Paul's letters (Irenaeus, Tertullian). Tertullian says he rejected the letters to Timothy and Titus. Marcion's own claim, as Tertullian reports it, was the opposite: 'the defenders of Judaism' had added to the Gospel, and he was taking it back to what it was." Beat 6, the baptism sentence: begin "Tertullian says …". Add to **Do not say**: WRONG the narrator saying as fact "Marcion cut / mutilated / dropped / rejected …", or "he forbade marriage". RIGHT "Irenaeus says …", "Tertullian says …", or "his opponents say …", followed by his own claim to be restoring the Gospel (M6).

**2. BLOCKING: Impact 2 drops Eusebius's qualifiers.** The outline says "Eusebius names one burned at Smyrna, and a woman martyr at Caesarea". Eusebius says Metrodorus "appears to have been a proselyte of the Marcionitic sect" (EUS 23680–23681). Of the woman he says "it is reported that she belonged to the sect of Marcion" (EUS 40473–40474). Metrodorus also sits in Eusebius's Polycarp chapter, so a writer could easily place him in Marcion's own day. The editor's note says he died under Decius in 250 (EUS 23656–23658).
Correction. Impact 2: "Eusebius tells of a man burned at Smyrna who 'appears to have been' a Marcionite, and a woman martyr at Caesarea who, 'it is reported', belonged to Marcion's sect." Add to **Do not say**: WRONG "a Marcionite martyr died with Polycarp" or any date in Marcion's lifetime; "Eusebius names Marcionite martyrs" with no hedge. RIGHT keep "appears" and "it is reported", and give no date, or say "a century later".

**3. MINOR: who speaks in the Polycarp exchange.** Irenaeus says Marcion "met him on one occasion, and said, 'Dost thou know me?'" (ANF 39882–39883). Marcion asks and Polycarp answers. Opening (1) reads "Polycarp … meets Marcion. 'Do you know me?'", which reverses who met whom and leaves the speaker open. Correction: "Marcion meets Polycarp once and asks, 'Do you know me?' Polycarp answers …". Add to **Do not say**: WRONG Polycarp asking "Do you know me?". RIGHT Marcion asks; Polycarp replies. "Old bishop" is also colour, since Irenaeus gives no date for the meeting. Prefer "the bishop of Smyrna".

**4. MINOR: "point by point" (beat 4) is added.** The text says only "Antitheses, or contradictory propositions, which aim at committing the gospel to a variance with the law" (T 23738–23741). Correction: "setting the gospel against the law".

**5. MINOR: Irenaeus and Polycarp.** "Who tells it" says Irenaeus "had known Polycarp as a boy". Irenaeus says "whom I also saw in my early youth" (ANF 39860). Correction: "who saw Polycarp in his early youth". Q2's "who knew Polycarp in his youth" needs the same fix.

**6. MINOR: Justin's place.** Opening (2) has "Justin, writing in Rome". The held line (ANF 13961–13965) shows only that he addressed the emperor, the Senate and the people of Rome. Correction: "Justin, writing to the emperor at Rome, in Marcion's lifetime". (That Justin lived at Rome is widely accepted but is not in the held lines.)

**7. MINOR: Epiphanius's list.** Impact 1 leaves out "the Thebaid" and "other places" (EPI 7464–7473). Read aloud as written, the list sounds complete. Correction: end it "… Cyprus, Upper Egypt, even Persia, and other places".

**8. MINOR: Do not say, how he was expelled.** Tertullian gives the reason as "their ever restless curiosity, with which they even infected the brethren" (T 21337–21338) and names no meeting. Add: WRONG "a synod / council at Rome condemned him", "a trial". RIGHT "Rome expelled him more than once, Tertullian says".

**9. MINOR: Galatians.** Beat 5 says "Paul rebuking Peter". Tertullian says Paul "rebukes even apostles" (T 29149–29151) and later names Peter, John and James. "Peter" is a fair gloss of Gal 2. "Paul rebuking even apostles" stays closer to the text.

## Checks that passed

- **Quotes (two independent reads: printed sheet text against the file, and the file re-read line by line).** Q1 Greek (JG 729–731) and English (ANF 14943–14945) are verbatim. Q2 (ANF 39882–39884) is verbatim. Q3 Latin (OEH 4337–4338; "pro-prium" is joined across the line break) and English (T 23734–23736) are verbatim. The Impact quotes (ANF 44915–44917; T 29329–29330) are verbatim.
- **Renderings.** My own renderings, made before I read theirs:
  - Q1: "There is also a certain Marcion of Pontus. Even now he is still teaching those who listen to him to think there is another god, greater than the Maker."
  - Q3: "Separating the law and the gospel is Marcion's own and chief work."
  - Q2 (from the English): "'Do you know me?' 'I do know you: the firstborn of Satan.'"

  Theirs keep every clause and add nothing, and every sentence stays under 25 words. In Q1, "won over" for τοὺς πειθομένους (those being persuaded, present tense) and "believe in" for νομίζειν are acceptable.
- **Beats 1–4 and 7, the setting, the links and "Who tells it"** match the cited lines and carry the right confidence. Beat 2's "Tertullian says" and Inferential-Thin tag are right. Beat 7's Inferential-Thin is right (one witness; wrong bishop, D1). "Middle of the second century" is correctly marked as modern chronology.
- **Impact ranking.** The spread and survival of his churches, attested from Justin to Epiphanius, is a defensible heaviest item. What the church defended (one God; four Gospels; the older faith) is marked.
- **Shape and length.** Teacher or thinker fits. Two minutes, with the stated reason (no word of his own survives; few facts), fits the record.
- **Tradition name.** "The church at Rome" and "the churches of the Marcionites" do not mislead. The outline avoids "the Marcionite Church" as one body.
- **Do not say** already covers Sinope, wealth, the sum, the money not being returned, dates and bishops, "twice", the place of the Polycarp meeting, the Polycarp letter, "evil god", anti-Jewish motive, counting the letters, the canon, reconciliation, the bishop's son, quoting Marcion, the 115 years, "Gnostic", docetism, an end date and invented feelings. Findings 1, 2, 3 and 8 add the rest.
- **Leave out** is right. Nothing in the sheet that the story needs is wrongly left out.
