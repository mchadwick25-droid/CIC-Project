# Card edits: Marcion of Pontus

## Edit 1: uncertain[2]
- Old: "the shipmaster, the money returned, and the late offer to repent."
- New: "the shipmaster, the money cast out with him, and the late offer to repent."
- Evidence: Tertullian, "contributed money to the Catholic church, which along with himself was afterwards rejected" (T 29239–29243); Latin "proiectam mox cum ipso" (OEH 8454–8455); Source_Sheet M1 and D6 ("No held text says it was returned"). Brief: "The money Marcion had given in his first faith was cast out with him."

## Left alone (not present in this card)
- Sinope: already credited ("Marcion of Sinope, in Epiphanius of Salamis"; uncertain line on Sinope).
- "wealthy shipowner": not in the card; it says "shipmaster".
- A year such as 144: none in the card.
- "ten letters": not in the card.
- "known only through Tertullian": the card says "known through Tertullian of Carthage's answer", without "only".
