# Second Readings: Council of Nicaea (325)

## 1. We are persecuted because we said
Source: cic/texts/theodoret_opera-omnia-t3-historia-religiosa-pg82-grc-lat_migne1864.txt 70615-70616; English cic/texts/npnf203_theodoret-jerome-gennadius-rufinus.xml 6045-6046
Second rendering: We are persecuted because we said, "The Son has a beginning, but God has no beginning."
Ours: We are persecuted because we said, 'The Son has a beginning, but God has no beginning.'
Verdict: same
Reason: Word for word the same rendering of the Greek, with "εἴπαμεν" correctly as past.

## 2. For I count civil strife
Source: cic/texts/eusebius_werke-bd1-vita-constantini-oratio-ad-sanctos-grc_heikel1902.txt 11167-11168; English cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml 64468-64470
Second rendering: In my judgment, civil strife within God's Church is harder to bear than any war or fierce battle.
Ours: For I count civil strife in the church of God worse than any war or fierce battle.
Verdict: same
Reason: "Count" carries "νενόμισται", "civil strife" carries "ἐμφύλιος στάσις", and "worse" carries "χαλεπωτέρα". No shift.

## 12. He won some over and shamed others
Source: cic/texts/eusebius_werke-bd1-vita-constantini-oratio-ad-sanctos-grc_heikel1902.txt 11238; English cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml 64512-64513

## 14. It seemed good ... about the faith
Source: cic/texts/athanasius_opera-omnia-pg26-grc-lat_migne1857.txt 57165-57169; English cic/texts/npnf204_athanasius-select-works-letters.xml 56538-56542
Second rendering: About Easter they wrote, "It was decided as follows," for then it was decided that all should comply. But about the faith they did not write, "It was decided." They wrote, "This is what the catholic Church believes."
Ours: It seemed good ... about the faith they did not write, 'It seemed good.' They wrote, 'This is what the universal Church believes.'
Verdict: same
Reason: The elision drops the aside on general compliance, and the brief supplies "about Easter"; the contrast Athanasius draws is intact. "Universal" for "καθολική" is sound.
