# Review: Martin of Tours brief

Reviewer: independent (did not write the brief). Brief judged on its own; no earlier brief or review was read.

**Verdict: REVISE** (two BLOCKING, four MINOR).

## 1. Listener read (one pass, before the bar)

**Where I got lost or drifted.**
- Paragraph 2, "his story belongs among the monks and monk-bishops of Gaul". This sounds like a catalogue label, not a narrator. I paused on what "belongs among" meant.
- Paragraph 4. He "offered to stand unarmed before the battle line", but I did not know why he would offer that. Nothing said anyone had doubted him.
- Paragraph 5. Hilary makes him "an exorcist", then the people of Tours make him bishop, then "as bishop he still lived as a monk". I was never told he had become a monk, so "still" landed on nothing. The soldier-to-monk turn, the heart of the story, went past without being told.
- Paragraph 8, "Sulpicius claims his book was read". For a second I heard "his" as Martin's book. The "since" also seemed to make the book's readers a reason his way of life spread.

**Where it sounds like a children's book or talks down.**
- Paragraph 5, first sentence: "Hilary ... made him an exorcist, and later the people of Tours made him their bishop." Two appointments in a row, with no act, no cost and no sense of what was at stake: he refused higher office, he was beaten and driven out by Arians, he built a monastery, and he was tricked into the bishopric. This is titles in place of acts.
- Otherwise the register holds. The opening, the Trier paragraph and the ending read as an adult documentary narrator. Sentences are linked by cause and contrast, and there is no run of short one-act sentences.

## 2. Trace to the outline

- P4: beat 3, M6. "The Caesar Julian" as required. "A peace Sulpicius saw as Martin's victory" as required. Julian's charge of cowardice, which is what makes the unarmed offer meaningful, is left out (Finding 4).
- P5: beat 4 reduced to two offices; beat 5 (M11) the wooden cell by the Loire, "eighty disciples". The monastery near Poitiers (M9) and the trick at Tours (M10) are left out (Finding 2).
- P6: beat 6, M15 and M16, inside every Do not say line (did not defend his teaching; trial ended after he left; one day's communion; refused to sign).

- P9: impact item 4 (I4), the outline's suggested ending, with Gregory's point (Maximus's punishment) and not a "stand against the emperor".

Beat 7 (Candes, Q3) is omitted. That is a fair choice for this shape and is not a finding.

## 4. Scores

- **Voice: 4.** An adult documentary register almost throughout, with cause and contrast inside the sentence and no definitions tacked on. It drops once into a list of offices (P5) and once into a catalogue label (P2).
- **Craft: 3.** Strong hook, setting in the second paragraph, and an ending drawn from the record's own act. But the soldier-to-monk turn, the core of the Monk shape and half the thread, is never told. Two scenes (the unarmed offer, the Tours election) lose their stakes.
- **Rigor: 5.** Every sentence traces; every Do not say line is respected; wonders and claims stay in Sulpicius's and Gregory's voices; quotes are verbatim; witnesses are named only where needed.
- **Accessibility: 4.** Grade 8.3, ease 72.7, well inside the limits. On one hearing it is easy to follow, apart from the "still lived as a monk" gap and the "his book" ambiguity.

## 5. Findings

1. **BLOCKING: past the 3-minute limit.** 459 words, about 3:04, over Mark's 3-minute limit and the outline's 450-word ceiling. Finding 2 adds about 13 words net and Finding 4 about 5, so the revision must cut about 30 elsewhere to land at or under 440 (about 2:55). What to cut:
   - P2: replace "and his story belongs among the monks and monk-bishops of Gaul" with a shorter form of the outline's setting line, e.g. "...in Roman Gaul, in what is now France, among its monks and monk-bishops." (about 5 words saved; also fixes Finding 3). Do not upgrade it to "the first monk-bishop", which is not held.
   - P3: "He was baptised, yet stayed nearly two more years in the army, for his tribune's sake" can become "He was baptised, but stayed in the army nearly two more years" (about 4 saved). The tribune is a side detail with no stake as told.
   - P7: fold Gregory's introduction into the ending. Cut "Gregory, bishop of Tours almost two hundred years later, calls him 'our blessed patron'" (15 words) and open P9 with "Generations later, Gregory, bishop of Tours almost two hundred years after Martin, ..." (about 8 added; about 7 saved net). The ending then also carries the "Who tells it" line for Gregory.
   - P8: "His way of life spread, since many of his monks became bishops" becomes "Many of his monks became bishops" (about 5 saved; also fixes Finding 5).
   - P6: "Later, to save other lives, he shared communion for one day with the bishops behind those deaths, though he refused to sign his name" can lose "Later," and "his name" can become "it" (about 3 saved).
   - If still over: drop "because miracles were reported there" in P7 (5), or drop Finding 4's addition.

2. **BLOCKING: the soldier-to-monk turn is never told, and P5 reads as titles in place of acts.** The outline's shape is Monk or ascetic: "the turn away from ordinary life, the life they chose instead". The thread is "a soldier who would not fight became a bishop who never stopped living as a monk". The brief tells the soldier and the bishop but never the monk. "Hilary ... made him an exorcist, and later the people of Tours made him their bishop" is two appointments with no act or stake, and then "still lived as a monk" refers to something the listener never heard. Change: replace the first sentence of P5 with the turn and the unsought bishopric, from beat 4 (M9, M10). For example, in about 30 words where the current sentence has 17: "Out of the army, he joined Hilary, bishop of Poitiers, and founded a monastery nearby. When Tours wanted him as bishop, a citizen lured him out with a story of a sick wife, and crowds brought him in under a kind of guard." Then "As bishop he still lived as a monk..." has something to stand on. Keep within the Do not say lines (no "kidnapped", no monastery names, no "first monastery in Gaul", no "released at once").
   - **Do not say** (add to outline): Wrong: "Hilary made him an exorcist, and the people of Tours made him bishop" as the whole of beat 4. Right: tell the monastery near Poitiers before "still lived as a monk", and tell the bishopric as one he did not seek (M9, M10).

3. **MINOR: P2's tradition line sounds like a label.** "His story belongs among the monks and monk-bishops of Gaul" reads as catalogue language. See Finding 1 for a shorter form in the narrator's voice.

4. **MINOR: the unarmed offer has no stake.** Without Julian's charge, the listener does not know why Martin offered to stand unarmed. Change: "Julian called it cowardice, so Martin offered to stand unarmed before the battle line, and was jailed" (M6, NP11 842–852). This adds 5 words; take them from Finding 1's cuts.

5. **MINOR: "his book" and "since" in P8.** On one hearing "Sulpicius claims his book was read" can be heard as Martin's book (no writing of Martin's survives; outline Do not say). "Since" also makes the book's readers a cause of the way of life spreading. Change: split the causes. For example: "Many of his monks became bishops. Sulpicius claims the Life he wrote was read in Rome, Carthage and Egypt."

6. **MINOR: the Trier compromise lacks its cost.** "To save other lives" is vague, and the outline's cost (he grieved and never again attended a gathering of bishops, M16) is left out, though it is what makes Gregory's "forced Martin to yield" land at the end. If room allows after Finding 1, name whose lives (those the emperor meant to hunt down in Spain) or add "and he never went to a gathering of bishops again." Not required.
