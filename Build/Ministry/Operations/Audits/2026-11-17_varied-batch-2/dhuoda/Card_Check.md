## The removal you asked about

**"Chapter 68 tells William to read the book and later give it to his younger brother": removal confirmed.** Chapter 68 says only that she gathered the book in haste for him and his brother: "Istum tibi et fratri, ut prosit, quod collegi festinans" (DH 11140). It urges him to keep reading it (DH 11142–11143). It does not tell him to give it to his brother. That instruction comes in the opening part, in a passage held only in the Paris copy: "cùm perfectum loquendi vel legendi acceperit tempus, illi ostende, et admone legendo" (DH 3176–3178; sheet T8). The new wording, "She asks William to show the book to his younger brother, and to read it to him, when the boy is old enough", renders that passage faithfully and puts no chapter on it.
