# Card edits: Dhuoda

## Edit
1. keyWorks[1].about. Old: "Chapter 68 tells William to read the book and later give it to his younger brother". New: "She tells William to read the book and later give it to his younger brother". Evidence: the instruction is in the opening part, before chapter VIII, "illi ostende, et admone legendo" (DH 3175–3178; sheet T8). Chapter 68 says only that she gathered it for him and his brother (DH 11140–11143).

## Left alone
- "Married in 824, in Léopold Delisle's date": Delisle, quoted by Bondurand, says "avait épousé en 824" (DH 286–287). Bondurand's own reckoning agrees (DH 2239). Not wrong.
- Her death: dates says no source gives the year, and uncertain[0] gives the rest as what Bondurand thinks (DH 1053–1055, 1782–1784). Not stated as fact.
- "hostage" or William's age: the card has no "hostage" and no age at the commendation. "Turned sixteen" in uncertain[5] is from her verses, "annis praeteritis octobinis" (DH 11450–11454).

## At release 50

The new brief's fields, made to pass the census check. Nothing else on the card moved.

- Quotation 3: the printed Latin is read through its listed scan fault ("jussioiie" for "jussione").
- Quotation 5: the brief's "a respectable monument of literature and piety" becomes "a monument of literature and piety worthy of respect". The second reading found "respectable" heard today as faint praise (`Second_Readings.md`).
