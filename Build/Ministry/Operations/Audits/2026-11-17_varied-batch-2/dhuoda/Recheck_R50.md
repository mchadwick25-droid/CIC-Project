## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Dhuoda, a Frankish noblewoman, wife of the great lord Bernard, living at Uzès in the south of France in the 840s.
2. **What happened:** while Charlemagne's grandsons fought over the empire, Bernard took their newborn second son away before baptism and her elder son William was sent to King Charles; kept at Uzès, she wrote William a handbook of prayer, faith and service; Bernard was executed for treason in 844 and William was killed in 850.
3. **Why the connection matters:** hers is a rare surviving lay voice, a mother claiming her place as her son's teacher, and the book was copied and kept, and later named among the works of Christian instruction from the ninth century on.
