# Second Readings: Hild of Whitby

## 1. All who knew her used to call her Mother
Source: cic/texts/bede_opera-historica-v1-historia-abbatum-lat_plummer1896.txt 24994-24995; English cic/texts/bede_ecclesiastical-history-english-people_giles1903.txt 11014-11016
Second rendering: Everyone who knew her used to call her Mother, because of her outstanding devotion and grace.
Ours: All who knew her used to call her Mother, for her outstanding devotion and grace.
Verdict: same
Reason: Every clause matches the Latin, including "consuerant" as "used to" and "insigne" as "outstanding".

## 2. Later we saw five bishops
Source: cic/texts/bede_opera-historica-v1-historia-abbatum-lat_plummer1896.txt 24946-24948; English cic/texts/bede_ecclesiastical-history-english-people_giles1903.txt 10973-10975
Second rendering: In fact, we later saw five bishops come from that same monastery. All of them were men of rare merit and holiness.
Ours: Later we saw five bishops come from that same monastery. All of them were men of rare merit and holiness.
Verdict: minor
Reason: Ours leaves out the connective "Denique" (in short, in fact), which marks this as Bede's summing-up proof. The listener's takeaway is unchanged.

### 4. Oftfor
Source: cic/texts/bede_opera-historica-v1-historia-abbatum-lat_plummer1896.txt 24955-24957; English cic/texts/bede_ecclesiastical-history-english-people_giles1903.txt 10984-10990
Second rendering: Of the middle one, Oftfor: he gave himself to reading and keeping the Scriptures in both of Abbess Hild's monasteries; then, wanting something more perfect, he came to Kent, to Archbishop Theodore, and after more study resolved to go to Rome.
Ours: One man of her house, Oftfor, studied the Scriptures in both her monasteries, then went to Archbishop Theodore in Kent, and on to Rome.
Verdict: minor
Reason: "Studied" compresses "reading and keeping" (lectio et observatio), and "desiring greater perfection" is left out. The route and the order are right. Bede tells this of Oftfor as one of the five bishops (10973-10977), which ours leaves the listener to infer (see Recheck_R50, finding 1).
