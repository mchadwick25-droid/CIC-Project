## Listener read (one pass, before anything else)

**Where I got lost or drifted**
- Paragraph 3: "intending to live in Gaul as an exile for the Lord's sake, until Bishop Aidan called her home." For a moment it sounds as if she went to Gaul and was called back from there. "Intending" saves it, but only just.
- Paragraph 6: the opening's unnamed man comes back as "the man", and the name "Caedmon" first appears halfway through the next sentence. I made the link, but it came late. The opening never says he did sing in the dream, so "his verses" arrives with no set-up.
- Paragraph 8: after a sentence about Caedmon ("none could match him"), the Mother quote begins with "her", with no lead-in. It took a beat to know it was Hild again and Bede speaking.

**Where it sounds like a children's book or talks down**
- Nothing sustained. The sentences are linked by cause and contrast ("because", "although", "until", "but"), and there is no run of one-act sentences. Nothing is defined after a comma; "the Scots" is worked into the setting sentence as Bede's name for the Irish monks.
- The nearest thing is the synod (paragraph 5). It says what happened, but not much about why it mattered. "Kept Easter on different dates" is given, but the listener does not feel what that difference meant for the church at the time. It does not talk down, but it is thin.
- The last paragraph is four separate items stacked one after another (Caedmon's followers, Mother, the York prayers, the five bishops), with no links between them. It reads a little like a list, not a list for children.

## Findings

1. **MINOR: impact under a third.** The "what came of her" material is about 70–90 of 413 words. **Change:** give it more room, from the outline's own items. For example, add one sentence on Oftfor going from her houses to Archbishop Theodore in Kent and then to Rome (I1), or on her house going on, with Oswy and Edwin buried there (I3). Trim the setting or beat 3 to make the room.
2. **MINOR: the synod's stakes are thin.** **Change:** say what the difference meant at the time, using Opening 2 / M13 in its qualified form. Bede says it was told that at times, at court, the king was feasting for Easter while the queen was still fasting.
3. **MINOR: the closing paragraph is a stack.** **Change:** link the items. Lead into the Mother quote with Bede as speaker, or move it before the Caedmon sentence, so that "her" does not follow "him".
4. **MINOR: the Caedmon link comes late.** **Change:** let the opening say he sang in his sleep (M17, "he presently began to sing verses"), and name him, or call him "the man of the feast", when he is brought to her. That gives "his verses" something to refer to.
5. **MINOR: "intending to live in Gaul ... until Bishop Aidan called her home."** For a moment it can sound as if she reached Gaul. **Change:** say she went to the East Angles meaning to cross to Gaul (beat 2, M4), and that Aidan called her home from there.
