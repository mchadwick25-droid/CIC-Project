**2. Attribute "All who knew her used to call her Mother…" to Bede.**
- Old (in the closer, no speaker): ""All who knew her used to call her Mother, for her outstanding devotion and grace.""
- New (end of the Whitby paragraph): "Bede writes: "All who knew her used to call her Mother, for her outstanding devotion and grace.""
- Source: Outline Quote 3; sheet I4; HEL 24994–24995, HE 11014–11016 (checked). Bede says it in his account of her life at Whitby, before her illness, so it now sits there.

**3. Cut the repeated "Because she had made her people study the Scriptures…".**
- Removed: "Because she had made her people study the Scriptures, the young English church took bishops from her house." The Whitby paragraph still says she made her people study the Scriptures so that many became fit to be priests (M11). The closer gives the bishops in Bede's words, with no narrator "because".

**4. Explain "Paulinus" in a clause.**
- Old: "at the preaching of Paulinus."
- New: "at the preaching of Paulinus, the first bishop of the Northumbrians."
- Source: Outline beat 1; sheet M2; HE 10901–10906 (checked).

**Across the set: the witness line.**
- Old: "Almost all we know of Hild comes from Bede, a monk in the same kingdom, who wrote about fifty years after she died."
- New: "Her story comes to us almost entirely from Bede, a monk in the same kingdom, who wrote about fifty years after she died."
- The other set rules already held: the setting is in the outline's spoken words, there is no "in what is now", no "Yet", and no uncertainty tag. Nothing in the brief softened the synod. It still says plainly that she held with the Scots and that the king ruled against her side.
