# Second Readings: Benedict of Nursia

## 1. So we must found a school
Source: cic/texts/benedict_rule-of-saint-benedict_gasquet1925.txt 768-770; Latin cic/texts/benedict_rule-of-st-benedict-lat-eng_hunter-blair1907.txt 1240-1245
Second rendering: So we must set up a school for the Lord's service. In setting it up, we hope to lay down nothing harsh and nothing heavy.
Ours: So we must found a school for the Lord's service. In founding it, we hope to lay down nothing harsh and nothing heavy.
Verdict: same
Reason: Both carry "constituenda est", "Dominici", "in qua institutione" as the act of founding, and both negatives. "Found" and "set up" are interchangeable here.

## 2. Let him so temper everything
Source: cic/texts/benedict_rule-of-saint-benedict_gasquet1925.txt 3960-3961; Latin cic/texts/benedict_rule-of-st-benedict-lat-eng_hunter-blair1907.txt 9527-9528
Second rendering: He should temper everything, so that the strong have something to strive for and the weak do not run away.
Ours: Let him so temper everything that the strong have something to long for, and the weak do not run away.
Verdict: same
Reason: "Long for" renders "cupiant" at least as closely as "strive for"; "do not run away" matches "non refugiant". No difference in sense.

## 3. carrying the manuscript of the Holy Rule
Source: cic/texts/paul-the-deacon_history-of-the-langobards_foulke1907.txt 7832-7835
Second rendering: They carried with them the handwritten copy of the Holy Rule that this father had composed.
Ours: carrying the manuscript of the Holy Rule
Verdict: same
Reason: Verbatim excerpt of the printed English with "with them" and the relative clause left out; nothing in the kept words shifts sense.
