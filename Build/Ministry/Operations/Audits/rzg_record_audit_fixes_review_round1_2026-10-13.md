## Resolution

- Blocking, fixed: `rzg.demo.consensus-contest` (invented doctrine and a lost hedge restored); provenance notes of the three demonstrations (they claimed no substantive change); `rzg.quote.seniors-selected-from-the-people` (reviewer B asked for 'bishops'; the rendering keeps 'pastors', since Calvin uses the words as one name in IV.3.8 and 'bishops' would mislead a modern hearer, which is also what the 2026-10-12 quote-rendering entry decided); `rzg.limit.no-teaching-on-wealth-or-marriage` (the absence claim still contradicted Calvin's Institutes II.8.41-44); `rzg.term.sixty-seven-articles` `plain_meaning` and `quick_meaning` ('first statement' was wrong against the cited volume).
- Minor, fixed: the rest of the lines above, each with the reviewer's suggested wording, except `rzg.quote.christ-the-mirror-of-election` ('look at' in place of 'gaze on' and 'see') and `rzg.quote.taught-better-from-scripture` ('declare' alone).
- Unchanged fields that now sit beside corrected wording are registered in `Build/worlds/rzg/Open_Gaps_Tracking.md` under the 2026-10-13 entry 'Defects found by the 2026-10-13 wording pass and not fixed'.
