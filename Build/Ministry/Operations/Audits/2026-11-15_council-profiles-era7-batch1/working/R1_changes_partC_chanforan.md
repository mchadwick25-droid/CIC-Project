**Synod of Chanforan: census differences and gaps (2026-11-15).** The era 7 council profile `synod-of-chanforan-1532` differs from the live census in three places. The census has parties at the gathering with no tradition at the date. Owner: the census thread. The census is not changed by the council profile.

The held witnesses have him invited, or present: GIL81 l.2446-2448, MUS l.6402-6405 and CRESP1 l.49905-49911. - `the-waldensian-reformed-union`, `sources[0].note`: "survive only through later copies and transcriptions, not an original protocol." This settles a point the held lines leave open. Muston calls the Dublin manuscript "the contemporary manuscript" (MUS l.6470-6471), and Montet calls it "les actes du Synode d'Angrogne" (MON l.939-940).
- Parties in 1532-1533 with no census tradition:
  - the dissenting barbes, who have no Refused tradition (`waldensians-in-the-alps` ends in 1517);
  - the Waldensians of Provence and Dauphiné, who sent Morel (PER19 l.11976-11978);
  - the Basel, Strasbourg and Berne reformers, who fit `the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva` only in part, since Geneva was not yet Reformed;
  - the Calabrian Waldensians (MUS l.6381).
