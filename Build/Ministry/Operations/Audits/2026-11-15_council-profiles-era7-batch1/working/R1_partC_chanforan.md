## Blocking

**C-B1. Blocking (the dissent shrunk to two, with the reasons given to the two; a partisan witness misplaced; a role for a named witness).** Brief paragraph 6: "Two barbes, Daniel de Valence and Jean de Molines, would not go along. Gilles, who opposed them, says they thought the new rules not wholly necessary and a slight to the leaders before them. They went to the Brethren in Bohemia. The Brethren's chronicle, as its editor reads it, says the two stayed six months and left on good terms."
- Evidence, the size of the dissent. GIL81 l.2030-2031: "Il est vray que quelques-uns des Barbes ne consentirent pas à toutes les conclusions de cette Assemblée, estimans que ces règlemens nouveaux n'estoyent pas totalement nécessaires". The two are then "deux des principaux contredisans, assavoir Daniel de Valence, et Jean de Molines" (l.2047-2049). So the reasons belong to "some of the barbes", and Valence and Molines are two of their leaders. MUS l.6540-6542: "there were, says Gilles, a number who dissented, and two pastors having refused to sign, withdrew". MON l.9152: "Une faible minorité s'opposa à ces décisions". ZEZ l.11554-11555: "Zwei Geistliche und Führer jener Minorität verliessen daher die Synode".
- Evidence, a contest left unmarked. The paragraph before says the articles were "signed and sworn by all present" (Perrin, PER19 l.12007-12012, "vnanimement ... sans aucun contredit"; MOR l.7944-7946). - Evidence, "who opposed them". Gilles was born in 1571 (GIL81 header l.2, "Pierre Gilles (1571-1646)"). He wrote in 1644 and could not oppose them in person. They stayed "per integrum semestre unum", praised all they saw, "et ad suos in Gallias revertuntur" (l.7179-7183). "Left on good terms" is supported: COMEN l.2815 ("Discesserant tandem dilecti hospites, mutuis vtrinque solatiis", OCR) and ZEZ l.11571 ("bis zum Ende im besten Einvernehmen").

Some barbes would not consent, he says, thinking the new rules not wholly necessary and a slight to the leaders before them. **C-B2. Blocking (a contested point told in one side's frame, in the brief).** Brief paragraph 4: "A guard later told a ducal inquiry he had kept watch for ministers at the hamlet of Chanforans.
