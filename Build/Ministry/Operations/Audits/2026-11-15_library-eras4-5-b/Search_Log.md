# Library search log: eras 4-5 (622-1300), third fill, 2026-11-15

The search record for the Library thread's third pass over eras 4 and 5. The second pass is in
`2026-11-15_library-eras4-5/Search_Log.md`. The wants this time are the Library items the Who batches added after
that pass was scoped: Open-Gaps 269 (era 4 Who batch 9) and Open-Gaps 279 (era 4 Who batch 11). The vendored files,
their state and their shelves are recorded in `Build/Ministry/Features/Source-Library/CFT-Wants.md` (the "Eras 4-5,
third fill" section). This file says where the batch searched, what it found, what it did not find, and what it did
not take.

Rights rule for this batch: only a printed imprint of 1930 or earlier, read on the title page itself or its page
image; a reprint counts by its own date; no NC or ND licence, no permission notice, no access-restricted item.

## Searches

Two searchers ran in parallel (Sonnet), one for the Latin, Greek and Slavic wants and one for the Armenian, Arabic
and Syriac wants. Each pick was then checked by the Library thread: the title page, the byte count against the
host's live record, the wanted passage, the end of the file. Where the OCR could not give the imprint year, the
Library thread read the scan's page image.

**Open-Gaps 269 (John of Damascus, Nicaea II, Cyril and Methodius, Clement of Ohrid, Rimbert).**
- John of Damascus on the images, in English: Mary H. Allies (London: Thomas Baker, 1898), the Toronto scan, taken.
  Five more records of the same book (a Gutenberg text, a lending copy, two later uploads) not taken.
- The acts of Nicaea II in English: John Mendham (London: Painter, 1850) taken. Mendham is a Protestant
  controversialist; his introduction and notes stand on the pages of the acts.
- A complete Life of Rimbert: Pertz, Monumenta Germaniae historica, Scriptores II (Hannover 1829), a Google scan,
  taken. The Life runs to 25 chapters and ends "regnat in aeternum". The only Waitz (1884) scan found is the held
  one, which stops in chapter 23. A Getty scan of the same Pertz volume has cleaner OCR but no year on its OCR title
  page (the year is in the colophon only); not taken.
- Clement of Ohrid: Tunitskii's Materialy, fascicle 1 (Sergiev Posad 1918) found, imprint readable. It holds the
  Greek long Life (Theophylact's, held in PG 126) with a Russian introduction and apparatus; the Greek and the
  Cyrillic are garbled for most of the file, and the short Life is named in it but not printed. Not taken. Not
  found: Tunitskii (1913), Balaschev (1898), Stojanović (1905), Undolskii (1895), Miklosich's Vita S. Clementis
  (1847), any print of the short Life ascribed to Demetrios Chomatenos. No work by Clement himself was found.
- The Life of Methodius: Miklosich's Vita sancti Methodii (1870) not found. Dümmler's Pannonische Legende is in
  the Archiv für Kunde österreichischer Geschichts-Quellen 13 (1854); archive.org has many volumes of the Archiv
  (1848-1853, 1856, 1857, 1859-1861, 1864, 1865) but not volume 13. Not found.

**Open-Gaps 279 (Stepanos of Taron, Matthew of Edessa, Eulogius and Alvarus, Abu Qurrah, Stephen of Mar Saba).**
- Stepanos, the Armenian text: one upload found (the Bedrosian library's), with no imprint that can be read and
  Armenian OCR that is noise. Not taken. The record also carries a 2010 edition, out by date. Malkhasiants (1885)
  not found. Dulaurier's French part 1 (1883) not found.
- Matthew of Edessa: Dulaurier's French (Paris: Durand, 1858) taken. The OCR garbles the year on the title page;
  the Library thread read "M DCCC LVIII" on the page image (leaf n5). A second scan of the same edition not taken.
  The Armenian text (an upload labelled 1898): no imprint can be read, only a Russian censor's date of 1897, and
  the OCR is noise. Not taken. Dulaurier's extracts on the First Crusade (1850) seen, not read.
- Abu Qurrah: Arendzen's De cultu imaginum (Bonn 1897), Arabic with a Latin translation, taken; only the Latin can
  be read. Bacha's Un traité des oeuvres arabes (Tripoli and Rome): the title page, read on the page image (leaf
  n4), names place and publisher and no year; the year 1905 is the date of the preface only. Not taken; for Mark.
  Bacha's Mayamir (Beirut 1904): the colophon names the press and no year, and the Arabic OCR is broken. Not
  taken. Cheikho in al-Machriq (1912) not found.
- Michael the Syrian, tome III (Paris: Leroux, 1905), the French of Books XII to XXI, taken from the record's
  French-only file. Tome IV, the Syriac (1910): found, the OCR is noise, not taken. A second record of tome IV is
  a photographic reprint of 1963, out by date.
- The Life of Stephen the Sabaite by Leontius: Acta Sanctorum, July III, in Palmé's reprint (Paris and Rome 1868),
  taken, for Boschius's Latin. The host's date field reads 1863; the title page reads 1868. Four Google scans of
  the Venice reprint are other July volumes, and two Palmé records numbered 30 are July IV.
- Eulogius and Alvarus in English: none printed by 1930 was found, as expected.
