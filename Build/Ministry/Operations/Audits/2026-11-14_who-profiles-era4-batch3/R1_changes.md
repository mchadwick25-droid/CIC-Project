## Length cuts made with the fixes

- Theodore: "Soon after reaching Canterbury he visited all the English peoples, teaching the canons' way of keeping Easter." (shortened); the dream sentence removed (it stays in the records).

- Muirchu: the two Armagh sentences joined into one.
- Tirechan: "Tirechan often speaks as a witness." removed; "and the clergy he appointed" removed.
- Cogitosus: the city-of-refuge sentence removed (on the card); the painted-boards sentence shortened.
- Wilfrid: "Bede is briefer."; "Kept out by the king's enmity, Bede says, he preached".
- Cuthbert: B-M27's clause removed again; the Boisil-prior sentence removed (on the card's role).
