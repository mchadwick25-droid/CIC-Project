The other hits are period "All rights reserved" lines (Wilberforce, Hodder, Cabot and Cox), Lasserre's undated "(The Copyright is reserved.)" and the 1894 copyright notice in Corwin. ## Verdict

The batch is close but does not pass Round 1 as it stands. There are five substantial findings:

- R1-01: the content note invents later numbers of the Morning Watch.
- R1-02: a printed place is called absent.
- R1-03: the 1916 date is presented as a printed fact, and the rights basis is not one the rule names.
- R1-04: Bradlaugh's revision of the report is overstated in the header and the staging row.
- R1-05: Ward's own appendix is credited to Errington.

There are also eleven minor findings. No file has a rights notice that blocks it. Every title-page year that was checked on an image agrees with the header. The text files themselves need no change; all corrections are to headers and staging rows. R1-03 needs a ruling from the owner of the rights rule, not only an edit.
