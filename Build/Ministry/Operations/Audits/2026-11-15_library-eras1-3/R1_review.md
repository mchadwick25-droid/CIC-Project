# R1 review: Library eras 1-3 batch (25 archive.org texts), 2026-11-15

What was checked and how. All 25 new files in `cic/texts/` were checked for rights: the header's Rights, Publisher and Publication Date lines; the title page in the OCR (read by line range; page images read where the OCR is unusable); a whole-file search for copyright, reprint, Nachdruck, réimpression, permission and non-commercial wording; the live archive.org metadata of each item (`licenseurl`, `rights`, `possible-copyright-status`, `access-restricted-item`, `is_dark`); and a script comparison of the header's Rights text with the REGISTRY.yaml row and the download-queue `rights_basis`. For all 25, the file size minus the header (the rule line and one blank line) equals the djvu.txt size the item's file list gives. Two files (Nau part 1, Loofs) were downloaded again and are byte-identical to the vendored text. Twelve files were checked in depth: every line the header and the staging `locus` cite, the volume's end, and OCR quality by sample and by a count of Greek-script lines. The shelf choices for those twelve were checked against the entry windows. Line numbers below are this file's own lines unless marked "OCR".

Not checked: the mapping of each file to its Wanted Sources row; Content notes of the 13 unsampled files (except quick checks of Lactantius page 411, the Nau part 1 introduction and the Naironus passages on Maron, which are where the headers say); the staging files of the unsampled files; whether Theodoret's PG 82 text is "after Schulze" (the volume's own Theodoret title page was not found among the page images read); the Malalas header's claim that the PG 97 Greek OCR is poorer; and the Bardy staging note's claim that Bardy disagrees with Loofs on several fragments.

Every host record has no `access-restricted-item`, no `is_dark`, no `rights` field and no NC or ND licence. In every case the header's Rights text appears word for word in the REGISTRY.yaml row and equals the queue row's `rights_basis` (25 of 25, script check).

## Sampled files

**Epiphanius, Holl 1915.** The edition is GCS Epiphanius vol. I, Ancoratus and Panarion haer. 1-33 (title pages 951-1016). The size matches. The volume ends on p. 464 with the close of haer. 33; the apparatus at 35242 records the subscription "κατὰ Πτολεμαίων". So it ends with haer. 33, as stated. The Greek OCR is good (13,922 lines in Greek script). Shelf: valentinian 120-300, tradition. The letter was written inside the window. Correct.

## Findings

1. **BLOCKING.** theophylact-simocatta_historiae-with-genesius-grc-lat_bekker1834.txt, line 2 (Creator): "Theophylact Simocatta; Genesius; edited by Immanuel Bekker (title page)". The Genesius title page at 19753-19760 reads "GENESIUS EX RECOGNITIONE CAROLI LACHMANNI". Only Simocatta is Bekker's (82-97). The Creator line should credit Lachmann for Genesius. The registry and queue titles should not imply that Bekker edited both.
2. **BLOCKING.** agathangelos_..._lagarde1887.txt, line 8 (OCR quality: "the Greek is fair to garbled"). The registry note and the staging note say the same. The Greek is read wholly as Latin letters, for example 199-205 ("jTTovp.&von Tod Ösonörod. Aal radra eipnang ...") and 5000-5004. The file has no line with more than 20 Greek-script letters. The Greek is garbled throughout and cannot be searched or quoted from the OCR.
3. **BLOCKING.** paul-samosata_paul-de-samosate-etude-historique-fra-grc_bardy1923.txt, line 8 (OCR quality: "the Greek is fair to garbled"). The registry note says the same. The Greek is read as Latin letters throughout, for example 16216 ("6 xptores yap da copias péyas éyévero"). The file has no line with more than 20 Greek-script letters. The Greek is garbled.
4. **BLOCKING.** theodoret_opera-omnia-t3-historia-religiosa-pg82-grc-lat_migne1864.txt, line 8 (OCR quality: "the Latin column is poor near the chapter on Maron"). The registry note says the same. The Latin column is read as Greek glyphs in the whole volume, for example 30000, 90000 ("ΤΠΕΟΘΡΌΟΘΆΒΕΤΙ ἘΡΙΒΟΟΡῚ"), 105000 and the title-page block at 40-110. A count finds no line with ordinary Latin words in 125,605 lines. Say that the Latin is unusable throughout.
5. **MINOR.** cic/corpus-map/_staging/mansi_..._tomus-8-492-536-lat_welter-facsimile1901.yaml, locus "the acts from about OCR line 90500 to 127424". The Constantinople synod ends before "CONCILIUM HIEROSOLYMITANUM" at 116792 (OCR 116780; the Jerusalem synod of 536). The volume's indices run from 118250 (OCR 118238). The locus should end at about OCR 116780.
6. **MINOR.** mansi_..._tomus-8_..._welter-facsimile1901.txt, line 8 (OCR quality). The host record's notes say "cut off texts on some pages due to tight binding/margin; skewed texts on some pages inherent from the source; inherent erratic pages". The header does not mention this, although the Surius header carries the same kind of host note.
7. **MINOR.** ezana_..._littmann1913.txt, line 9 ("Nr. 11 (the campaign against the Noba, with the Christian formula)"). The registry note says the same, and the staging note says "pagan and then Christian in their opening words". Nr. 11 opens "In der Kraft des Herrn des Himmels" (3123). Littmann calls it Christian (3445), but his own Nachträge (10029) record Ditlef Nielsen (ZDMG 66) reading the inscription as largely pagan. The Christian reading of Nr. 11 is disputed and should not be stated as settled.
8. **MINOR.** cyril-scythopolis_vita-sabae_..._1880.txt, line 4: "the host record's 1875 is the set's first year". Neither the file nor the host record supports this. The record gives date 1875 and "13 v." with no explanation. Say only that the host record gives 1875 and the title page gives 1880.
9. **MINOR.** langlois_..._1867-1869.txt, line 9: "Agathangelos from 13550 (the translators' preface)". The registry note says the same. At 13562-13586 there is the section title "traduite ... par Victor Langlois" and then Langlois's own "Introduction". It is one translator's introduction, not a translators' preface.
10. **MINOR.** cic/corpus-map/_staging/agathangelos_..._lagarde1887.yaml, note. The work is placed as `tradition` on armenian-christianity-conversion-era (314-451) with needs-ruling, but the note gives no date reason ("Placed as the French of Langlois is"). The Greek version is not shown to fall inside the window. The note should state its date problem, as the Langlois note does.
11. **MINOR.** naironus_..._1679.txt, line 1 (Title: "auctore Fausto Nairono Banesio Maronita"). The OCR of the title page is unreadable at this word ("FAVSTO 'NAH^O^O VANSWSl", 92). The host record reads "Banensi". The header's "Banesio" is not supported by anything checked. Check the page image and correct it.

Counts: 4 blocking, 7 minor.
