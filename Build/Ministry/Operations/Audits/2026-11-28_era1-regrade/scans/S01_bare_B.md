## tree/methodius-of-olympus
Heart: Methodius, a bishop of Lycia who died a martyr, wrote the Banquet, where ten young women praise the virgin life without scorning marriage, and On the Resurrection, which argued against Origen that soul and body are one and the same body will rise. Later writers used his work against Origen or scorned him for it.

## tree/suetonius
Heart: Suetonius, a Roman writer of emperors' lives, left two passing lines, one on Claudius driving out Jews rioting "at the urging of Chrestus" and one on Nero punishing Christians, which later Christians read, corrected and joined to the Acts.

## tree/hippolytus-of-rome
Heart: Hippolytus, a Greek-writing teacher at Rome in the early 200s, held against Noetus that the Son is another beside the Father yet one God with him, fought bishops Zephyrinus and Callistus over that teaching and over forgiveness, wrote the Refutation of All Heresies, and was exiled to Sardinia and kept by Rome as a martyr.

## tree/tacitus
Heart: Tacitus, a Roman historian who despised Christians, gave the fullest outside account of Nero blaming and cruelly killing them after the fire of 64, and named Christ as put to death under Pilate; later Christians answered or retold him.

## tree/josephus
Heart: Josephus, a Jewish priest and general who surrendered to Rome and lived on there, wrote the histories of the war and of his people, in which a few lines on John the Baptist, James and Jesus were copied and argued over by Christians for centuries.

## tree/cyprian-of-carthage
Heart: Cyprian, bishop of Carthage in the mid-third century, led his church through persecution, plague and division, taught that the church is one mother outside which no one has God as Father, opposed Stephen of Rome on baptism, and died a martyr in 258; Donatists and Augustine both claimed him.
