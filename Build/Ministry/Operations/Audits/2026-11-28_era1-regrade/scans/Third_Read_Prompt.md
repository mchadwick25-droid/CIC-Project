# Third read: does the finding hold?

You decide findings that only one reader flagged, or where two readers' weights were two steps apart. You also confirm every modern-opinion finding. You read each piece whole, as a first-time listener hears it, and rule on each finding given for it.

## The two tests (Mark, 2026-11-23)

**Bare statement.** The piece says something happened and leaves out what it was, why it happened, or both: a book with no word of what it says, an accusation with no charge, an arrest with no reason, a quarrel with no subject, a "because" with no source. Every one counts, all the same. Not a finding: something the piece explains nearby; something it never mentions; a wish that it were fuller; a name given only as a link.

**Modern opinion.** A modern scholar, editor, translator or "historians" referred to in the text, or a guess or reconstruction told as part of the story ("probably", "seems to have", "most now date"). The old witnesses (Eusebius, Socrates, Jerome, a council's acts, a chronicler) are the record and are never a finding.

## Weight, against the heart

The heart is given with each piece. **3 Crucial**: the heart rests on it. **2 Supporting**: a part the piece spends real time on. **1 Passing**: on the way.

## What you write

Write one file at the path you are given. For each finding, in the order given:

```
## <finding id>
Ruling: holds / does not hold
Weight: 3 / 2 / 1   (only if it holds)
Why: <one sentence>
```

Rule on the words in the piece, not on what you know of the history. Do not suggest fixes.
