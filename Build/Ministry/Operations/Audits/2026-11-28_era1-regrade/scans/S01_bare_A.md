# S01 bare statements, reader A

## tree/methodius-of-olympus
Heart: A bishop and martyr of Lycia who praised the virgin life in his Banquet and, in On the Resurrection, argued against Origen that the body is no chain on the soul and that the same body will rise.
Findings: 0
Also noticed: "asked how Methodius dared to write against Origen now" leaves "now" without a reference point, so the question reads oddly.

## tree/suetonius
Heart: A Roman biographer of the emperors whose two short lines, Claudius driving out Jews rioting "at the urging of Chrestus" and Nero punishing the Christians, are among the earliest outside mentions of Christ's followers, later read and reused by Christians.
Findings: 0
Also noticed: none

## tree/hippolytus-of-rome
Heart: A Greek-writing teacher at Rome in the early 200s who held that the Son is another beside the Father yet one God with him, refused to yield to bishops Zephyrinus and Callistus on that and on forgiveness, and was exiled to Sardinia in 235 and kept by Rome as a martyr.
Findings: 1
- "In 235 he was sent to Sardinia with Rome's bishop" | missing: why | weight: 2 | asks: Why were they sent there, and by whom?
Also noticed: none

## tree/tacitus
Heart: A Roman historian who despised the Christians yet gave the fullest outside account of Nero blaming them for the fire of 64 and killing them cruelly, naming Christ as put to death under Pontius Pilate.
Findings: 1
- "First those who confessed were seized." | missing: what | weight: 1 | asks: Confessed to what, being Christians or setting the fire?
Also noticed: none

## tree/josephus
Heart: A Jewish priest and general who surrendered to Rome in the war and at Rome wrote his people's history, whose few lines on John the Baptist, James and Jesus Christians copied for centuries and argued over.
Findings: 0
Also noticed: "That year he gave himself up to Vespasian" places the surrender in 66, but the opening puts it at Jotapata in 67.

## tree/cyprian-of-carthage
Heart: A new Christian made bishop of Carthage who led Africa's church through persecution, plague and division, taught that the church is one mother outside which no one has God as Father, resisted Stephen of Rome on baptism, and was put to the sword in 258.
Findings: 1
- "At Rome a second bishop, Novatian, was set up against the one already chosen" | missing: why | weight: 2 | asks: Why was a rival bishop set up, over what?
