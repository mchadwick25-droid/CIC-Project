## tree/hippolytus-of-rome
Heart: Hippolytus, a Greek-writing teacher at Rome, held that the Son was another beside the Father yet one God with him, fought Zephyrinus and Callistus over doctrine and forgiveness, and died in exile to be remembered as a martyr.

## tree/tacitus
Heart: Tacitus, a Roman historian who despised the Christians, gave the fullest outside account of Nero's cruel punishment of them after the fire of Rome, and Christians later answered and retold him.
Findings: 0

## tree/josephus
Heart: Josephus, a Jewish priest and general who went over to Rome, wrote histories at Rome whose few lines on John the Baptist, James and Jesus Christians copied and argued over for centuries.

## tree/cyprian-of-carthage
Heart: Cyprian, bishop of Carthage, led his church through persecution, plague and division, taught that the church is one mother outside which no one has God as Father, resisted Rome on baptism, and died a martyr claimed by later Africans.
