# Who scan, era 7 (2026-11-12)

Approved to proceed (coordinator, under Mark's full-auto instruction of 2026-11-12). Read-only scan of era 7 (1517–1650 CE, "The Reformation Era") of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Counts, 255 rows (a paired row counts once): **WHO 113, STORY 48, LISTED 86, WANTED 8**. Judgment-call rows: about 110. Councils are not people and carry no verdict and no table here (a separate scan holds them); gatherings are named in the rows of their participants. All five live person entries (Luther, Calvin, Tyndale, Ignatius of Loyola, Grotius) come out WHO, so none is flagged. The Library holds no Ottoman, Jewish or secular-humanist chronicler of 1517–1650, so no row carries the OUTSIDE WITNESS label.

## How the list was built

- Era 7 has 41 census entries: 36 movements and 5 live person entries.
- Named voices and `people` lists of the movements (old rules), merged by alias.
- Carried forward from the era 6 audit (Not scored, "Era 7 by date"): Barbosa, Alvares, Castanhoso, Telles, Gouvea, Menezes, Ludolf, Houghton with the Carthusian martyrs of 1535, and the Stoglav (a gathering, so a lead and not a row).
- Found in text and found in corpus-map: shapers named in era 7 census texts and authors placed by the corpus-map rows of an era 7 entry.
- Six drafting workers (Sonnet) sorted the candidates by region and opened the cited lines. One Opus subagent then opened the cited lines for every WHO, STORY and WANTED row (182) and 39 LISTED rows (`Opus_Check_Notes.md`).

## Rulings applied

- The standard of eras 1 to 6 (Decision-Log 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12): WHO is church-shaping with a body of work or a following and enough life to tell; recorders are WHO with a thin own life flagged; women are WHO if they founded, taught or led; rulers and popes are STORY unless they have their own voice; founders known through enemies need two witnesses for the teaching; hostile inside writers are WHO as recorders only where a life can be told, with polemic marked as polemic, else LISTED; OUTSIDE WITNESS is for objective historians outside the church, never partisans or polemicists.
- Pairing: "if two people are tied together in their sources with similar identity its ok to make that a single who". Paired rows are judgment calls.
- A later retelling alone gives LISTED until an early source is held. A WANTED source must be able to carry a life, not only writing.
- Era is by date of work: work after 1650 goes to era 8.
- "Major?" column (proposal only): the map shows up to 4 names per tradition and 5 per river per era, chosen from the major Whos.
- Editions tagged "[suggested; to confirm]" were named from the workers' memory, not from the census or the Library.
