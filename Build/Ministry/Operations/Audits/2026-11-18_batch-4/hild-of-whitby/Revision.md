- **Ending on the person.** The old brief ended on Bede's five-bishops quote. The outline asked for that close with Opening 1, but under the new Standard a story ends on the person, and on a later writer only when that is the point. Bede's count is the measure of her house, not of her, so the quote moved one paragraph up. The story now ends on her death and "All who knew her called her Mother." - **EXTRA.** "She took the faith with King Edwin, not with her father": done through finding 1.
