- **Opening.** Unchanged: Gregory's pointer from his wonders to the Rule, two sentences, ending on Q3. It leads straight into the Rule.

So the paragraph on what came of the Rule (Lombard sack, Paul the Deacon, 742, 802) now comes straight after the Rule, and the life follows, ending on his death. All the sentences moved are kept word for word, except as the findings changed them.
- The work-and-reading sentence moved from paragraph 2 to the start of paragraph 3, so the essence is complete inside the mark.
