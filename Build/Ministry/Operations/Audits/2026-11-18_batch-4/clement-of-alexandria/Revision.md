(b) "By his own account" became "In Clement's own words". (c) "Of the last" became "Of the last teacher he found".
4. **MINOR, martyrs and Alexander stand alone.** Martyrs: "In his own time, Clement wrote, ..." with the outline's gloss of martyr, and "Yet he taught ..." as its own sentence. No motive and no flight are added (D3). Alexander is framed as "The last we hear of Clement alive ..." (beat 7), and the paragraph now ends on Alexander's later letter and Clement's death (M9).
5. **MINOR, "Paul's word for that guide".** Now "The word Clement borrowed from Paul meant the household servant who walked a child to his teacher."
