# Outline check: Antony the Great

## 1. The heart of his life, written before reading Outline.md

From the census entry (`knownFor`: "The Egyptian hermit whose withdrawal to the desert, and the Life written of him, drew monks after him and spread monasticism east and west"), the source sheet and what he is known for:

- **The turn.** A young Christian of a well-off Egyptian village family heard the Gospel "go and sell that thou hast" read in church, took it as spoken to him, gave his land to the villagers and his money to the poor, and gave himself to "discipline" (ascesis): work with his hands, prayer, fasting (E2, E3, S7). Documented as the *Life*'s account.
- **The life chosen instead, ever further out.** He learned from older ascetics near the village, then went to the tombs, then a deserted fort across the river for nearly twenty years, then the inner mountain, growing his own food so as to be "troublesome to no one" (E4, E6–E8, E11). Documented as the *Life*'s account. Not "the first monk": the *Life* itself shows hermits before him; it says no monk yet knew "the distant desert" (S5, D3).
- **Others drawn after him.** When he came out, he persuaded many to the solitary life; cells rose in the mountains, "the desert was colonised by monks" (rendered "the desert became a city of monks"), and "he directed them all as a father" (E8, E9). Documented as the *Life*'s account. Disciples later in Egypt, Libya, Palestine, Syria, Arabia: Widely Accepted as Sozomen's report about 90 years on (I3b).
- **What he defended.** He came to Alexandria to serve the confessors in the persecution, and later to denounce the Arians and teach that the Son of God is not a created being; he stood with his bishop Athanasius (E10, E14, I4). Documented as the *Life*'s account, with Athanasius's own *History of the Arians* for his letters.
- **The pattern that travelled.** The *Life*, written soon after his death and long credited to Athanasius (Widely Accepted; writer's service to him Contested), was sent to monks abroad as "a sufficient pattern of discipline", soon put into Latin by Evagrius, called by Gregory of Nazianzus the laws of the monastic life told as a story, and at Trier and Milan it turned court officials and Augustine (I1, I2, A1). Documented.

In short: the hermit who took the Gospel at its word, went into the desert, drew a city of monks after him and guided them, and whose *Life* carried that pattern east and west. The weight of his impact is the *Life* as the pattern for monks (the sheet ranks it first, I1).

## 7. Findings

1. **MINOR. Beat 3, the "point ahead" goes too far.** In Augustine's account the *Life* reached Trier. Augustine himself "had heard" of Antony (AUG 13117), from Pontitianus. Correction: replace "Point ahead: it would reach Augustine in Milan (I2)." with "Point ahead: its story would reach Augustine in Milan, told by a man who knew the book (I2)."
2. **MINOR. The Augustine link says more than its source.** "read of him" is not in I2, and the outline's own Do not say rules out his reading the *Life*. Correction: "Augustine of Hippo — heard of him from Pontitianus; wrote of him in the *Confessions*, quoting the Gospel reading that turned him (L15, I2)."
3. **MINOR. Who tells it states the authorship as settled.** "long credited to Athanasius, bishop of Alexandria, who says he saw him many times" makes Athanasius the speaker, which D1 holds open. Correction: "long credited to Athanasius, bishop of Alexandria; its writer says he saw him many times (D1)."
4. **MINOR. Gregory's date is an editor's.** The heart's "about twenty-five years on" rests on Robertson's dating of 380 (A1). The sheet says its time gaps "are not story material" (sheet, Time gaps). Correction: in the heart, change "Gregory of Nazianzus, about twenty-five years on, called it" to "Gregory of Nazianzus, preaching later in the same century, called it". Add to Do not say: "Do not give a year or a count of years for Gregory's sermon; the date 380 is an editor's (A1). Say 'later in the same century'."
5. **MINOR. Healings in the narrator's voice.** Beat 6's "Crowds and healings drove him" leaves room to tell the healings as fact. The *Life* has "the signs which the Lord wrought by him" (NP4 32431–32432). Add to Do not say: "Do not tell the healings as fact in the narrator's voice. Say that people crowded to him for healing, or 'the *Life* says...'; the voice that sent him to the mountain stays out or in the *Life*'s voice (E11, D8)."
6. **MINOR. A qualifier is dropped in Opening 2.** "twenty years walled in a ruined fort" drops the source's "nearly" (VA §14, NP4 31600–31601). Correction: in Opening 2, change "twenty years" to "nearly twenty years". Add to Do not say: "Do not say 'twenty years'; say 'nearly twenty years' (E8)."
7. **MINOR. Q2's job claims more than the record shows.** "the image his tradition is remembered by" has no source. Correction: change the job to "why he would not stay among people, in an image a later historian retold (Sozomen, E17)."
