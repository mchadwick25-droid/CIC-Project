## What to do
1. **Write `Outline.md` to the current template.** Set **the heart of their life** first, as substance: what they did and made, what it held, and what it changed. Keep every line the old outline had right, and keep its checked renderings and line references. Rearrange the beats so the heart is told in the first minute: about 150 words for a person, about 130 for a council. Choose an opening that leads straight into the heart. Pull "What came of them" forward where the heart needs it. Add to **Do not say** every point in Heart_Test.md's side notes that applies.
2. **Source sheet.** Use the existing sheet. If the heart needs a claim the sheet does not hold, find it in the Library (`cic/texts/`), check it in its file, and add it to `Source_Sheet.md` under a heading "Added for batch 4", with file, lines, witness kind, confidence and the verbatim text. Never invent. If the Library will not hold it, say so.
3. **Self-check for the four faults** before you finish: one witness's claim told as fact; an editor's view given to the source; a date or detail moved onto the wrong event; and the record's force, coercion or conflict softened by leaving it out.
