## The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Marcion, a shipmaster from Pontus who taught at Rome in the middle of the second century. He taught that the God of Jesus was another, higher God than the Creator who gave the law. Everything we know of him comes from his opponents.
- **What happened:** He set the law against the gospel in a lost book, the *Antitheses*. He kept his own Scripture, one Gospel and Paul's letters. His opponents say he cut them down, and he said he was restoring them. Rome expelled him, but his churches spread and lasted for two centuries, as far as Persia.
