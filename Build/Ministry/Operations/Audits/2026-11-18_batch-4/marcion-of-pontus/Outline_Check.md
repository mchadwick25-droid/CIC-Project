# Outline check: Marcion of Pontus (batch 4)

## The heart of their life (checker's own, written before reading `Outline.md`)

Marcion, a shipmaster from Pontus, taught at Rome in the middle of the second century that the God of the law and the prophets, who made the world, is not the Father of Jesus: Christ revealed another, good God above the Creator (Justin, in his lifetime, ANF 14944–14945, 16131–16135: Documented; Irenaeus agrees, ANF 33574–33582). His chief work, by Tertullian's report, was to set the law against the gospel, in a book of his own, the *Antitheses*, now lost (T 23734–23741, 29246–29248: Documented as report). He kept a Scripture of his own: one Gospel, a form of Luke, and a set of Paul's letters. His opponents say he cut them down; he and his followers said he was restoring what defenders of Judaism had added (ANF 33581–33590; T 23767–23770, 29249–29255: Documented as report of each side). Put out of the church at Rome, he built churches of his own; Justin saw "many of every nation" follow him, and two centuries later Epiphanius found them from Rome to Persia (ANF 14945–14946; EPI 7464–7473: Documented). The church's writers answered him book after book, Justin, Irenaeus, Tertullian's five books and others, that one God made the world and is the Father of Jesus (I3, I4: Documented). Everything known of him comes from opponents. Whether he caused the church's list of books is Contested and stays out.

## Findings

**1. BLOCKING. Connection field: Justin does not speak of churches.** The line reads "his churches were many in his own lifetime, Justin says". Justin speaks of many *people*: "has caused many of every nation to speak blasphemies" (ANF 14945–14946) and "this man many have believed" (ANF 16135–16136). In the Greek he "has made many ... deny God, the maker of this universe" (JG 732–734). He never mentions churches. The churches come from Tertullian (T 29323–29330) and Epiphanius (EPI 7464–7473). A writer could repeat this line and put a claim in Justin's mouth that he never made.
Correction: replace "his churches were many in his own lifetime, Justin says, and two centuries later Epiphanius still finds them from Rome to Persia (I1)" with "in his own lifetime Justin says he had led many "in every race of people"; Tertullian says his followers made churches; and two centuries later Epiphanius still finds them from Rome to Persia (I1, B3)".

**2. MINOR. Justin's book "in his lifetime".** What came 2 says "Justin wrote a book against him in his lifetime". Irenaeus quotes Justin's "book against Marcion" (ANF 44907–44917) but gives it no date. The held lines date only the *Apology*, which says Marcion "is even at this day alive" (ANF 14943–14944).
Correction: "Justin wrote against him while Marcion was still alive, in his *Apology* to the emperor, and also wrote a book against him, which Irenaeus quotes: ..." The Connection field's "from Justin in Marcion's lifetime" is supported by the *Apology* and can stay.

**3. MINOR. Q1 rendering drops the clause that names him.** The quoted Greek begins "Μαρκίωνα δέ τινα Ποντικόν", but the rendering starts at "Even now". My own rendering of JG 729–731: "And a certain Marcion, from Pontus, who even now is still teaching those he has persuaded to believe in some other god, greater than the Creator." The outline's two sentences match mine for the clauses they keep. The old outline kept the first clause.
Correction: either restore "There is a certain Marcion, from Pontus." at the start of the rendering, or cut the Greek and printed English to begin at "ὃς καὶ νῦν" / "who is even at this day alive" and mark the rendering as an excerpt.

**4. MINOR. The first minute is crowded.** Beats 1–3, the opening of What came 1 and a clause on the church's answer must fit in about 150 words. Beat 1 also carries Irenaeus's full report, and beat 3 carries the followers' defence and the Galatians appeal. If a writer tells all of these in order, the churches will fall past word 150, which is the fault the heart test found.
Correction: in beat 1, mark Irenaeus's "author of evils" detail "after the minute if room is short". In beat 3, mark the followers' defence and the Galatians appeal "after the minute". Keep his own claim ("the defenders of Judaism" had added to the Gospel) in the minute.
