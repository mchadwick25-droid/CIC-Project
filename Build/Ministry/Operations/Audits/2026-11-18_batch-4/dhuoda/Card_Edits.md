# Card edits: Dhuoda (batch 4)

Base: the census card for `dhuoda` in `cic-website/data/world-census.json`. Everything not listed here is word for word.

## New

1. **quotationOriginals, the mirror (new span).** Modern: "mind and body, as in a picture in a mirror." Printed: "ut quasi in picturam speculi, me mente et corpore legendo et Deum deprecando intueri possis" (DH 3165–3167, re-read; sheet A1). Work: end of chapter VII. Uncertain: "Paris copy only." **Awaiting second reading.**
2. **quotationOriginals, no teacher like a mother (changed span).** Old modern: "My son, you will have teachers who will teach you more lessons, and greater ones, for your good. But they will not teach on equal terms, with a heart burning in the breast, as I do, your mother." New modern, as the brief quotes it: "but they will not teach on equal terms, with a heart burning in the breast, as I do, your mother." Old printed: "Fili, habebis doctores ... sicut ego genitrix tua." (lines 3168–3171). New printed: "sed non jfiquali conditione, animo ardentis in pectore, sicut ego genitrix tua." (DH 3169–3171, re-read). The "jfiquali = aequali. Paris copy only." note is kept. **Awaiting second reading.**
3. **quotationOriginals, Q1 ("I see many people ...").** Unchanged in modern wording and source (DH 1893–1894, re-read); its `secondReading` "same" is copied from the census card.
4. **briefReferences, added** after "Liber manualis, book I and following":
   - "Liber manualis, end of chapter VII (Paris copy only): the book as a mirror in which William sees her, mind and body; he is to show it to his little brother when the boy can read." Evidence: DH 3163–3168 (A1), DH 3172–3178 (T8); brief paragraph 2.
   - "Liber manualis, table of chapters: seventy-three headings, among them prayer for every rank of the Church, for kings and for all the faithful dead." Evidence: DH 2316, 2473–2474, 2478, 2503, 2527 (A2); brief paragraph 3.
   - "Liber manualis, ch. 69: be a just judge and take no gifts." Evidence: DH 11368–11370 (T7); brief: "judge without taking gifts".
   - "Liber manualis, ch. 73: her epitaph, which she asks William to have written on her tomb so that passers-by pray for her." Evidence: DH 11966–11968, 11999–12002 (E17); brief, last paragraph.
5. **briefReferences, Annals of Saint-Bertin.** Old: "...put to death on Charles's order; 850: William killed at Barcelona." New: "...put to death on Charles's order; 848: William takes Ampurias and Barcelona by trickery; 850: William killed at Barcelona." Evidence: AB 2416–2417 (E20); brief: "In 848 William seized Ampurias and Barcelona by trickery".
