# Card edits: Columba

Base: the census card for `columba` in `cic-website/data/world-census.json`. Everything not listed here is word for word. No `briefLength` (the census validator does not allow it).

## Clearly wrong

None. No card statement is contradicted by a held source or by the finished brief.

## Quotation originals

Kept, with their second readings copied (modern wording and source unchanged):

- "But whatever kind of man he was, ... for life under a rule." Bede III.4, BEDE 5866-5869; Latin BEDL 18882-18885.
- "our most reverend Father Columba and his successors". Bede III.25, BEDE 8122-8123.
- "Here I must stop, at the bottom of this page. Let Baithene write what comes next." Adomnán III, AD 10787-10789.

Removed (the brief no longer quotes them):

- "those who seek the Lord will lack no good thing" (now told without quotation marks).
- "of their own accord".
- "My sons, I entrust these last words to you. Love one another without pretense, and live in peace."

New, awaiting second reading:

- "to make a pilgrimage for Christ". Printed: "wishing to make a pilgrimage for Christ from Ireland to Britain, he sailed forth" (`cic/texts/adomnan_life-of-saint-columba_huyshe1905.txt` 2224-2225).
- "according to the example of their first teacher". Printed: "according to the example of\" their first teacher" (`cic/texts/bede_ecclesiastical-history-english-people_giles1903.txt` 5862; the quotation mark is OCR). Latin: "iuxta exemplum primi doctoris illius" (`cic/texts/bede_opera-historica-v1-historia-abbatum-lat_plummer1896.txt` 18879).
- "their error". Printed: "came among them, corrected their error" (Bede file 5884-5885).
- "with all his household". Printed: "a certain countryman, with all his household, hearing the word of life through an interpreter when the holy man preached, believed, and, believing, was baptized" (Adomnán file 7610-7613).

## Brief references

1. Old: "his last day, the Psalter, \"they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing\", and Baithene to write what follows; his last words, heard by Diormit alone; the midnight bell, the altar, and the hand raised to bless."
   New: "his last day, a Saturday, the old man weary with age; the Psalter, the verse on those who seek the Lord, and Baithene to write what follows; Baithene his successor; the altar, and the hand raised to bless."
   Evidence: the brief's last paragraph; the quoted words are not in the brief, and the last words and midnight bell are gone. AD 10622-10627 (old, weary), 10685-10686 (Saturday night), 10785-10789, 10801-10803 (Baithene succeeded), 10866-10877.
2. Old: "born of noble parentage; the voyage in his forty-second year, two years after the battle, \"to make a pilgrimage for Christ\"; never an hour without prayer, reading, writing or work."
   New: drops "; never an hour without prayer, reading, writing or work" (not in the brief).
3. Old: "Adomnán, Book II: King Brude's gates opening \"of their own accord\", and the king's lasting honor."
   New: "Adomnán, Book II: King Brude's gates, shut against him, and the king's lasting honor; preaching through an interpreter, and a countryman baptized \"with all his household\"."
   Evidence: brief, "Columba preached to them through an interpreter ... honored him for life." AD 7609-7613, 7894-7897.
4. Old: "... the abbot over bishops; \"whatsoever he was himself\"."
   New: "... the abbot over bishops, \"according to the example of their first teacher\"; his successors' self-control, love of God and life under a rule; Egbert, who corrected \"their error\"."
   Evidence: brief paragraphs 1, 4 and 5; BEDE 5856-5869, 5880-5886.
5. Old: "Bede III.3: Aidan, a monk of Iona, sent to Oswald in 635."
   New: adds "at the king's request for a bishop; his see on Lindisfarne; the English taught the monks' rule by Irish monks; Aidan's Easter by the custom of his country, from the fourteenth to the twentieth moon."
   Evidence: brief paragraphs 3 and 5; BEDE 5726-5764, 5782-5795, 5749-5752.
6. Old: "Bede III.25: ... the king's ruling."
   New: adds "; Colman's return to the Irish with those who would not change." Evidence: brief paragraph 5; BEDE 8227-8233.
7. Old: "Bede III.4 and V.22: Iona kept its custom until 715 or 716."
   New: adds ", when Egbert brought it to the Roman Easter." Evidence: BEDE 5876-5886, 14983-14989.
8. Old: "World census ...: his setting; a church led by abbots rather than city bishops."
   New: adds "; pilgrimage as leaving home for good." Evidence: brief, "leaving home for good"; census gloss of peregrinatio (Source_Sheet S6).

Unchanged: "Adomnán, Book III: twelve disciples sailed with him." and "Annals of Ulster and Annales Cambriae: 562 for the voyage; Huyshe: about 563, and 597 for the death."
