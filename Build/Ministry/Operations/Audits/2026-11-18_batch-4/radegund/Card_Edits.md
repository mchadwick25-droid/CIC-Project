## briefReferences

1. Fortunatus reference.
   - Old: "the kings' quarrel over the captive; her care for poor women at Athies; her plea"
   - New: "the royal captive taken as spoil; her plea"
   - Evidence: the brief tells neither the quarrel nor Athies; it says "she was taken as spoil". Fortunatus: "cuius esset in praeda regalis puella, fit contentio de captiva" (Krusch 1888, 28562–28564).
2. Letter reference, the tyrant.
   - Old: "her request that the bishops stop anyone"
   - New: "her request that the bishops guard the sisters from any tyrant, and stop anyone"
   - Evidence: brief, "Her letter asked the bishops to guard her sisters from any tyrant." Dalton 19881–19882, "to protect us from any tyrant".
3. Letter reference, quoted words.
   - Old: "\"I make prostration vicariously through this letter\""
   - New: "\"I bow down through this letter in my place\""
   - Evidence: quotation marks only for words verbatim in the brief; Dalton's English is not in it. The new words stand in the brief's first paragraph.
4. Bishops' letter (IX.39).
   - Old: "a nun who enters may never leave."
   - New: "a nun who enters may never leave; virgins flock from their dioceses to take up her Rule."
   - Evidence: brief, "Women came from the bishops' own dioceses to live by it." Dalton 19528–19530, "many virgins have eagerly flocked out of our dioceses to accept thy Rule" (chapter 39 runs 19406–19592).
5. The Cross (IX.40).
   - Old: "with King Sigibert's letters. Baudonivia, Life II: the Cross set in gold and gems. Gregory, Glory of the Martyrs 5, and Baudonivia: the comparison with Helena."
   - New: "with King Sigibert's letters; the bishop of Poitiers will not receive it, and King Sigibert sends the bishop of Tours, who brings it in with psalms, candles and incense. Baudonivia, Life II: the request to the emperor. Krusch: the name Holy Cross."
   - Evidence: brief, paragraph four (the emperor, "came to be called Holy Cross", the refusal and the bishop of Tours). Dalton 19601–19610, 19620–19625; Baudonivia, "lignum crucis Domni ab imperatore expetere" (Krusch 1888, 30246–30249); Krusch, "nomen S. Crucis accepit" (28153). The brief gives neither the gold and gems nor Helena.
6. Death reference.
   - Old: "about two hundred nuns at the bier; the bishop of Poitiers away."
   - New: "about two hundred nuns at the bier."
   - Evidence: the brief does not say the bishop was away.
7. Revolt reference.
   - Old: "neighboring bishops. History X.16: \"the custom of the lady Radegund\"."
   - New: "neighboring bishops, and Gregory copies the whole letter. History X.16: the abbess's answer that she brought in no new custom, only what was done under the lady Radegund."
   - Evidence: brief, "Gregory later copied the whole letter into his History" (Dalton 19821–19827, IX.42) and its last sentence. The quoted words are Dalton's, not in the brief (Source_Sheet A1; Dalton 21416–21418; GRL 33579–33581).
8. Lamp reference removed.
   - Old: "Gregory, Glory of the Martyrs 5: the lamp before the Cross that overflowed, and \"from then on I preached the power of the holy Cross\"."
   - New: (removed)
   - Evidence: the brief does not tell the lamp.
