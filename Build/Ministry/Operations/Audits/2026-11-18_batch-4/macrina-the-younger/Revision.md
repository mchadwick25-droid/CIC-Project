- **Essence complete at word 136** ("Teacher").
- **Also told:** Ferrara 1438 follows the teaching (words 137 to 196). Then the refusal of marriage. Then the women's and men's sides with Peter, the famine babies and the young widow (words 260 to 320), told as the house grows after her father's choice and her refusal.
