# Outline check: Macrina the Younger

Checker, batch 4, 2026-11-18.

## My account of the heart (written before reading Outline.md)

Macrina, eldest child of the family of Basil and Gregory of Nyssa, turned her household in Pontus into a house of prayer and shared work. After her betrothed died she refused marriage, stayed with her mother, and gradually led her mother to live as an equal with the slave women, sharing their food, bed and work, with prayer and hymns night and day. Out of that household grew a community with a women's side, which she led, and a men's side led by Peter, whom she had raised; in famine it took in crowds and abandoned babies. She shaped her brothers: Peter, Gregory (whom she rebuked and taught), and, by Gregory's account (contested), Basil. Her deathbed teaching on the soul and the resurrection, met with a prayer that God had freed us from the fear of death, was written down by Gregory, in the *Life* and in the dialogue with her as "the Teacher"; that dialogue was cited in her voice and argued over at Ferrara in 1438. Almost all of it comes through one loving brother.
