## The test, from one hearing

- **Who or what:** a council of bishops that the emperor Constantine called to Nicaea, in today's Turkey, in 325.
- **What happened:** Arius taught that the Son had a beginning. The council answered with a creed calling the Son "begotten, not made" and of one being with the Father. It condemned Arius and two bishops, and the emperor exiled Arius and enforced the sentence. It also settled Melitius, one Easter and twenty canons.
- **Why the connection matters:** later councils (Constantinople, Chalcedon) set its creed first, though it divided the church for decades. Its canons travelled as written law to Carthage and Persia, where copies were read aloud and checked.

All three lines come easily from one hearing.

## Findings

- **M1 (MINOR), the heart's last part is at the edge.** "yet for decades it divided the church" starts at word 152, 22 past the 130 mark. That is within "about 20" but only just. If the writer touches the opening, trim about ten words before word 130. The obvious place is the attribution clause in sentence 2 ("wrote this in a letter before the council, after his bishop had driven him out"), which could lose "after his bishop had driven him out" since the quote already says he was persecuted. Do not cut the council's charge: the outline requires the two voices kept apart. This is optional. The heart already lands.
- **M2 (MINOR), two phrases hard on one hearing.**
  - "lent them the public post" may stop a second-language listener. Suggested: "let them travel by the imperial post, with horses" (S4).
  - "those who had given way under the emperor Licinius without being forced" is vague, because "given way" does not say to what. Suggested: "those who had fallen away from the faith under the emperor Licinius without being forced" (canon 11, "fell", NC 4124–4128).
- **M3 (MINOR), one sentence past about 30 words.** The canons sentence is 32 words. Suggested split: "And twenty rules, called canons, set the order of the church. They ran from how a bishop is made to mercy for those who had fallen away ..."
- **M4 (MINOR), Carthage wording.**
  - "the bishops of Carthage" suggests many bishops of one city. The record is an African council meeting at Carthage (NC 32157, 32523).
  - "their bishop Caecilian" was by then their late bishop. The source calls him the "predecessor of your holiness (who was present at it)".
  - Suggested: "the bishops of Africa, meeting at Carthage, read aloud ... from the copy Caecilian, once their bishop, had carried home from Nicaea." This also says outright that he sat at Nicaea, where the outline's connection wants it.
