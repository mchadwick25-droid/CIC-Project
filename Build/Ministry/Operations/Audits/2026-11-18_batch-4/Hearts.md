# The hearts, as the researchers set them (for Mark)

## Clement of Alexandria
Around 200, Clement was a presbyter who taught in Alexandria (office and city Documented; that he taught, Widely Accepted). He answered Christians who feared Greek philosophy: God gave it to the Greeks as he gave the Law to the Hebrews, as a guide to Christ, and he kept "whatever has been well said" (Documented, his own words, held only in English translation). Eusebius and Jerome say Origen was his pupil and followed his example (Widely Accepted; Origen's own words name Pantaenus, not Clement). Jerome later used Clement's books against a critic who said quoting pagan books defiled the church (Documented).
Length about 2:20. Opening: Christians "frightened" of philosophy "as children are at masks".

## Columba
An Irish monk who left Ireland for good as a pilgrim for Christ and founded a monastery on Iona (Documented; the year Contested). Bede, about 130 years later, says many houses in Britain and Ireland grew from Iona and Durrow, with Iona at their head, and its abbot, a priest, over even bishops "according to the example of their first teacher" (Documented as Bede's account of his own day). Bede says he turned the northern Picts to the faith (Contested in scope). In 635 his monastery sent Aidan to the English at Lindisfarne (Documented).
Length about 2:30. Opening: Bede's doubt about the man set against his certainty about the heirs.

## Martin of Tours
A Roman soldier who asked to leave the army, saying he was Christ's soldier and might not fight. He became a monk, then bishop of Tours, and never stopped living as a monk: a wooden cell, eighty disciples, many of his monks later made bishops. At Trier he pleaded that heretics should not be killed and that no worldly judge should try a church's case. His friend Sulpicius wrote his life while he lived (all Documented as Sulpicius's account, resting on him alone; "a model for later saints' lives" Dominant Modern Reconstruction; the honor of his tomb Documented through Gregory of Tours).
Length about 3:00. Opening: his words at Worms, "Until now I have served you as a soldier".
