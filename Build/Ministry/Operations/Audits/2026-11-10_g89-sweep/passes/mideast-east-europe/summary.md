## Notes

1. The Catholic Encyclopedia articles (Labourt, Fortescue) are dated studies inside the era, but partisan. Each row needs a second, non-Catholic study.
2. Mark's test: Propaganda and the Capuchins brokered most unions here (1672, 1698, 1724, 1778, 1781). That was a policy, not a movement, and each union made a separate living church. The policy belongs in VII.19 and VIII.29.
3. The Jesuits brokered the 1698 union (in the synod's own words, V) and survived in Russia after 1773 (Pius VII's 1814 bull, V). Both are named lines for a Jesuit era 8 home, if Mark rules one for China.
4. The Library already holds sources these rows do not use (Charon for VII.23, Paisii for VII.18).
5. ME-8, ME-10 and ME-3 give parents to IX.73, IX.70 and IX.40.
6. Section A is open only for ME-6 and ME-20 (the Tolstoyans).
7. Not swept: the Czech lands after 1627, Kosmas the Aitolian, the Balkan Franciscans. The Mennonites in Russia go to the rivers pass.
