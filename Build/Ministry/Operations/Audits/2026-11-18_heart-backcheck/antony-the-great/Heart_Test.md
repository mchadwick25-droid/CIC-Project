## 1. The heart, before hearing the story

Antony gave away his inheritance after one Gospel reading and went out into the Egyptian desert to live a life of prayer, work and fasting. Many came after him, so that "the desert became a city of monks", and he guided them as a father. The *Life* written of him became the pattern for monks, and it carried his way of life east and west, as far as Rome, Gaul and Augustine's Milan. That spread is why he is on the tree.

What the sources hold for each part:
- **The withdrawal and the disciplined life** (Gospel reading, gifts to the villagers and the poor, the tomb, the fort, twenty years alone): Documented as the *Life*'s account (E1–E8, S7). The *Life* is a book of praise, and Robertson grants "idealisation". Its authorship by Athanasius is Widely Accepted; that its writer served Antony is Contested (D1).
- **Others followed him and he directed them:** Documented as the *Life*'s account (E8, E9: "he directed them all as a father"). His disciples in Egypt, Libya, Palestine, Syria and Arabia: Widely Accepted as Sozomen's report, about 90 years later (I3b). Hilarion of Palestine learning from him: Jerome's later and "highly idealised" account (I3c). That he was the first monk is Contested and not part of the heart (D3).
- **The *Life* as the pattern, and its spread:** The *Life*'s own purpose and its claim of reach are Documented as the *Life*'s words (I1a, I1b). The Latin translation by Evagrius within about twenty years is Documented (I1c). The Trier officials and Augustine's garden are Documented as Augustine's own account (I2). Robertson's "kindled the flame ... all over the West" is an editor's judgment (I1e).

Nothing central is missing from the source sheet. The outline carries all three parts, though his role as guide of the monks (E9) sits only in the sheet; the outline gives just "Many followed" (beat 4).

## 2. Hearing the story

The story is 481 words. The first 150 words carry:
- **1–61:** the church scene and the Gospel reading. "That choice took him into the desert" (words 56–61).
- **62–75:** the emperor Constantine and his sons wrote him letters (a late-life marker).
- **76–124:** parents' death, the fields given away, learning from nearby hermits, bread and salt.
- **125–150:** how we know: one book, a *Life* credited to Athanasius.

Where each part of the heart first arrives:
- **Withdrawal to the desert:** word 56, in one clause. The ascetic life follows at 109–124. In the first minute, in part.
- **Many followed him; the desert filled:** word 212 ("many wanted to live as he did"), landing at 238–247 ("the desert became a city of monks"). Not in the first minute. Given one sentence of weight later. His guiding of them is never told.
- **The *Life* as the pattern, spreading west:** word 331 ("Written for monks abroad as a pattern"). Not in the first minute. Given real weight later: words 331–481, Latin, Spain to Africa, Trier, Augustine.
- **Spread east** (Palestine, Syria, Arabia, Hilarion): never told, though the outline offers it (What came of him, item 3).

The heart lands in the first minute in part: the withdrawal only. The *Life* does appear at word 128, but as a source ("His story is told in one book"), not as the book that spread monasticism. Where the time goes: about 60 words of scene, 49 of early biography, 26 of how-we-know. After the first minute, about 100 words (151–247) go to the tomb, the demons and the fort.

## 3. Verdict

**FIX.** The material is in the outline and sources, but the first minute gives only the withdrawal.

Missing from the first minute, and what in the outline can carry it (no new claims):
- **That many followed him into the desert:** outline beat 4, "Many followed, and, in the *Life*'s Greek, 'the desert became a city of monks'" (E8).
- **That the *Life* became the pattern and spread west:** "What came of him", item 1: written for monks abroad as "a sufficient pattern", soon in Latin, heard of in Spain, Gaul, Rome and Africa (I1a–c). The how-we-know line at words 125–150 can be turned into this: the book that tells his story is also the book that carried it.
- **That it spread east too:** "What came of him", item 3: disciples in Egypt, Libya, Palestine, Syria and Arabia (Sozomen, much later), and Hilarion (Jerome).

Room can come from the early biography (76–124) and the Constantine marker (62–75), both of which can move later.
