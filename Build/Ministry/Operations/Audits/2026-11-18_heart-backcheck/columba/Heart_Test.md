## 1. The heart

Columba was an Irish monk who left Ireland for good as a pilgrim for Christ and founded the monastery on Iona. Iona became the head of a family of monasteries in Britain and Ireland, ruled by its abbot even over bishops. From it the faith went to the northern Picts and, through Aidan, to the English. He is on the tree as the founder of that house, whose heirs carried his way for more than a century.

What the sources hold:
- Pilgrimage for Christ, sailing to Britain with twelve disciples: Documented, Adomnán's words (E4, S6). Year Contested (D1).
- Founded a monastery on Iona, and Durrow before it: Documented (E5, E2). Who gave the island: Contested (D4).
- Iona as head of many houses, its abbot over bishops "after the example of their first teacher": Documented as Bede's account of his own day (I1, S7).
- The Picts turned to the faith: Bede's claim, Contested in scope; Adomnán shows only households and a chief baptized through an interpreter, and a king who honoured him (E8, E9, D6).
- Aidan sent from Iona to the English in 635: Documented (I2). It is his monastery's act, not his own.
- His way defended at Whitby, 664, and kept on Iona until about 716: Documented as Bede's account (I3, I4).

Nothing central is missing from the sheet or the outline. The outline's thread names the heart plainly.

## 3. Verdict

**FIX.**

Missing from the first minute: what Iona became. A head house of many monasteries in Britain and Ireland, from which the faith went to the Picts and to the English.

What in the outline can carry it, with no new claims:
- What came of them 1: Bede, many houses grew from his, Iona "the principal of them all", its abbot a priest even over bishops "according to the example of their first teacher" (I1, S7).
- What came of them 2: Aidan, "a monk of the island called Hii", sent to the English in 635 (I2).
- Beat 5: Bede says he turned the northern Picts to the faith; tell it as Bede's claim, Contested in scope (D6).
- Room can come from the opening scene and the Brude gates wonder, which together fill about 75 of the 150 words. The Psalter scene can stay as the opening if it is shortened, or move to the death beat where it also belongs.
