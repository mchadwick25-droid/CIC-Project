## The (c) items

**C-1. "Dominic Sells His Books" (the-dominican-order, story 0): ‘so severe that many were dying of hunger’ and ‘I will not study on dead skins while men are dying of hunger.’** Neither is held in English. Both are held in Latin, but in the 1233 canonization testimony of Brother Stephen of Spain, not in Jordan. See `acta-sanctorum_augusti-tomus-1-lat_1750.txt` 66808–66818 (commentary citing the Bologna relation) and 108396–108404 (the testimony itself): "incepit invalescere fames pessima in regione illa, ita quod multi pauperes ibi fame moriebantur … vendidit libros suos, manu sua glossatos … dicens: Nolo studere super pelles mortuas, et homines moriantur fame: cujus exemplo quidam magnae auctoritatis similia fecerunt." The same file holds Jordan's Life (prologue from about line 92245; the Palencia famine at 95976–95988). It has the famine and the sale of the books, but no saying, no "annotated in his own hand", and no others following his example. **BLOCKING (card content, for the lead):** going by the held texts, the card gives Stephen's words and details to Jordan. It says "in the words Jordan preserves", "Jordan notes that they were annotated in his own hand" and "The gesture, Jordan says, shamed…", and the caveat says "the words are as Jordan gave them". Leaving the item for the lead is right, since fixing this goes beyond the quotation. But a plain telling of the saying alone would not lose the card's point. The real issue is the attribution, and the escalation should say so.

**C-2. "John of Ephesus Baptises the Pagans of Asia — By Order" (syriac-orthodox-west-syriac-christianity, story 1): ‘compelling’.** It is not held. In `john-ephesus_ecclesiastical-history-part3_paynesmith1860.txt` the mission passages (III.36–37, lines 10120–10275; II.44, lines 7506–7520) have no compulsion language. Every "compel" or "by force" in that file is about Chalcedonian persecution of John's own party (e.g. 1888, 2242). In `john-ephesus_lives-eastern-saints-po18_brooks1924.txt` (mission passages 37985–38035 and 39040–39047) there is none either. **BLOCKING (card content, for the lead):** the held texts support neither the word nor the sentence around it ("he does not pretend it was voluntary either: he writes of ‘compelling’ and of the fear of imperial law"). A plain telling cannot fix this, so leaving it for the lead is right. But the escalation should state that the claim is unsupported in what we hold, not just that a plain telling would lose the point. The coercive reading belongs to the reference (Whitby), and the caveat already calls it "inferred". It may warrant a `formation_confidence` tag. Separately, and as NON-BLOCKING, the card's figures differ from the held texts: "ninety-six churches" against 99 in EH II.44 (line 7512) and 98 in the Lives (39044), and "seventy thousand" by "his count" against "eighty thousand" in the Lives (39043). Seventy thousand appears only in Payne Smith's editorial preface (161). These may come from sources we do not hold, but the lead should see them.

**C-3 (on the Dominic and John items together):** neither card's "verification" field changed ("from reference summaries; primary text not yet read"). That is correct under "change nothing else".
