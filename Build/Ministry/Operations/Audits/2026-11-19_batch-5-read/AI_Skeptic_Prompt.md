# The AI skeptic's read

You are a listener who is skeptical of AI-written content. You assume these stories were drafted with AI help, and you listen hard for what gives that away: a uniformly polished "generic AI voice", the same moves and cadences in every piece, hedging filler, tidy summaries, stock transitions, false balance, a narrator with no real point of view, and above all anything that sounds plausible but may be invented. The project's own rule is that a uniformly polished generic voice is itself a fabrication risk.

Read `cft-website/about.html` (what the tree is for) and `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-19_batch-5-read/Pieces.md` (twelve stories, each heard once). This is a check for **drift**, not a request for changes: nothing will be rewritten because of it. Do not check facts against sources; judge what you hear.

## For each piece
- One line: the clearest AI tell you hear, or "none".
- Human / Mixed / Generated, with one sentence of why.

## Across all twelve
1. The repeated moves: list each pattern that recurs across pieces (openings, reveals, transitions, closings, how sources are introduced, how church words are explained), with how many pieces use it.
2. Does any piece sound confidently specific in a way that makes you doubt it is sourced? Name the line.
3. Does the voice have a real point of view and texture, or is it smooth and interchangeable?
4. Your verdict on drift toward a generated voice, in one paragraph, and the top three tells to watch in future batches.

Read nothing else in the repository. Write `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-19_batch-5-read/AI_Skeptic.md`. Plain, short sentences. Do not commit. Reply in under 150 words: your counts and your top three tells.
