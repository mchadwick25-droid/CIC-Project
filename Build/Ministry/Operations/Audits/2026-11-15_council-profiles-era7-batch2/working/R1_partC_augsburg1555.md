## Blocking

**C-B1. Blocking (the scene witness given the wrong kind).** Brief, paragraph 5: `The rest comes from Jesuits in the city, from Pallavicino, and from Paolo Sarpi, the Venetian friar he wrote to answer.`
- The brief's room witness is Polanco: the arrival, the ride of "almost a league", the news of Julius's death and Morone's departure (POL5 l.15369-15376). The brief uses no letter written by a Jesuit in Augsburg; Nadal's and Salmerón's letters appear only in the entry. So the listener can only take "Jesuits in the city" to mean Polanco.
- Polanco was not in the city. He writes of the Jesuits there in the third person ("praeparabant se nostri", POL5 l.15377). The editors cite his own letter to Nadal at Augsburg, sent on Ignatius's commission ("Ita Polancus, ex com., Patri Nadal, 1.* Aprilis 1555", POL5 l.15394-15395). The entry's own `record` puts him down as "later narrative", "the secretary of the Society of Jesus, writing its history". The sentence turns a later chronicle into an eyewitness account. That breaks pattern rule 1 and C2 in the one sentence that tells the listener whose account stands in.
- Fix, brief (19 words): `The rest comes from Polanco's chronicle, from Pallavicino, and from Paolo Sarpi, the Venetian friar Pallavicino wrote to answer.`
- Fix, entry `uncertain[0]`, second sentence: "The account of the room comes from the Catholic side: Polanco's chronicle, the letters of Nadal and Salmerón from the city, and Pallavicino, and from Sarpi."

**C-B2. Blocking (a link no held line makes, across a sentence join).** Brief, paragraph 11: `Sarpi says the bargain was over cities and bishops: "The Catholicks were content that the Cities should do as they pleased." When subjects of Ferdinand and of Bavaria asked for the same freedom, Pallavicino says, they were told to follow their lords' religion or leave.`
- "The same freedom" points back to Sarpi's cities. Pallavicino says nothing of the cities here. His subjects were "inuogliati dalla dissoluzione de' circonuicini". They asked "di non sottostare à più dura condizione che gli altri, ma di viuere anch'essi sciolti dall'osseruanza de' mandamenti ecclesiastici" (PALL2 l.27523-27526). Their example was their neighbors' looseness, and they asked to be freed from the church's commands. Sarpi's cities are the Interim cities and their return to the Augsburg Confession (SARPI l.38433-38442). The join makes Pallavicino's subjects ask for Sarpi's city bargain. This is the era 5 fault of a claim moving to the wrong witness when sentences are joined.
- The entry has it right: "asked for the freedom of their neighbors" (`outcome[5]`).
- Fix, brief (13 + 10 words, same count as now): `Subjects of Ferdinand and of Bavaria asked for their neighbors' freedom, Pallavicino says. They were told to follow their lords' religion or leave.`

## Verified

Sept. 1555)" (l.16247-16248). §15 protects an estate "von wegen der Augspurgischen confession" from force "wider sein conscienz, gewissen und willen" (l.16272-16276), in "fürstentumben, lan-|den und herschaften" (l.16278-16279). Agreement comes only "durch christliche, freuntlihe [OCR], friedliche mittel und wege" (l.16284-16285). §16 "gleichergestalt" for the estates "der alten religion anhengig, geistlich oder weltlich" (l.16288-16290), so the brief's "The same held" is faithful. §17 "alle andere ... genzlich ausgeschlossen" (l.16290-16292), naming no group. §18 "(Reservatum ecclesiasticum)" covers "erzbischof, bischof, prelat" leaving "unser alten religion", with honor spared and the chapter to choose "eine person der alten religion verwant" (l.16292-16301). §19 is Mirbt's summary (l.16301-16302). §20 suspends church jurisdiction "bis zu entlicher vergleichung" (l.16302-16316). §24 lets subjects of either religion go "mit iren weib und kindern", "unverhindert menniklichs", against "abtrag der leibeigenschaft und nachsteuer" (l.16316-16323). §25 makes it "ewig werender friet" if no agreement comes by council, national assembly, colloquies or diets (l.16323-16332). §106 covers the Kammergericht (l.16332-16336).
