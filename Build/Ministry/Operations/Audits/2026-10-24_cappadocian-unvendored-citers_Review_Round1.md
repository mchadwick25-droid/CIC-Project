## Boundary calls

- **(a) Sasima, "a dusty village where the post changed horses".** Sound. It is verbatim at LM:40 ("Soon after Basil became bishop … he forced his friend to accept the see of Sasima, a dusty village where the post changed horses."). It is a modern translator's description, and it fits the Greek the assessment reports. Only the readability point in finding 10 remains.
- **(b) Eunomian "split from the church and changed baptism. Our sources disagree".** Not invented, but an overcorrection. See finding 8.
- **(c) family-archive under `gregory-nazianzus-de-vita-sua`.** Yes, this is a misattribution: De vita sua does not contain Ep. 52. The honest fix is not a hedge, because Ep. 52 is vendored in English. See findings 1 and 1a.
- **term.philoptochia rated verified-via-authority / Widely Accepted.** This fits. The lead load-bearing source, Oration 43, is itself verified-via-authority. Its claims hold verbatim: "opened the stores of those who possessed them" (N207:42005-42006), "the new city … a hospital for the sick" (:42736-42738), "cleansing leprosy, not by a word, but in deed" (:42783-42784). Widely Accepted is conservative but defensible. Only the field inconsistency in finding 5 must change.
