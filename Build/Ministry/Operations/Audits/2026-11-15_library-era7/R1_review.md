# Review, round 1: Library era 7 fill (2026-11-15)

One fresh Opus reviewer, read and grep only. Scope: the rights row and imprint of all 56 new files; the header
and record claims of 27 files against their text; the wants table, cautions, shelf rows and search log. The
reviewer could not view page images, so the sixteen imprints read on an image, and the stamps and binding of the
Schirrmacher scan, are unverified by the review.

**Verdict: REVISE.** No file breaks the rights rule and no edition is wrong. Four record texts made claims the
text contradicts, and several open points could be settled from the text.

## Blocking

1. **Schirrmacher, the colloquy's line range.** Header, registry, shelf rows and search log gave the report of the
   sessions as running to OCR 2211. It runs from OCR 1210 to "atque sic cessarunt" at 1836. OCR 1842-2203 is a
   register of Luther's letters of 1529, most listed by addressee only. A verse exhortation stands before the
   report. The Marburg part does not print the fifteen articles or the signatures, so "whether ... not
   established" can be answered: they are not printed.
2. **Las Casas caution.** The wants list and the header of volume IV said he tells his own part "only in volumes
   IV and V". The third-person "el clérigo Casas" is only there, but he speaks as an eyewitness in the first person
   in every volume (for example volume III, file lines 437, 522, 760).
3. **Corpus Reformatorum 27.** The header said the German Confutatio was not located and the Latin's end was not
   pinned. The Latin ends at OCR 12804; "B. Confutatio Germanica" opens at OCR 12871.
4. **Kidd shelf notes.** "In their own languages" and "in their own words" overstate: no. 43, the emperor's
   declaration, is in English only, and no. 45, the Edict, is Latin extracts.

## Should fix

1. Reichstagsakten: the Edict (no. 92) is printed in full in German, OCR 41730-42894; the French declaration is
   no. 82; the second title page names Adolf Wrede as editor.
2. Kidd: the Edict is extracts with the middle sections summarized; the fifteenth Marburg article is a Latin
   version, and its signature line has nine names.
3. Förstemann prints the recesses of 22 September (no. 205) and 13 October (no. 249); in no. 305 the editor says he
   leaves out the text of 19 November.
4. Balan: the chronological index dates Aleander's letters nos. 61, 64, 67 and 70 to 5, 16, 18 and 19 April; no. 68
   is the "Acta comparitionis Lutheri".
5. Sée's Vallée des pleurs carries the notes and appendix of an unnamed "Correcteur"; a caution is needed.
6. Weimar 30/3: the fifteenth article is at about OCR 12354-12532; the Marburg part ends at about 12729; the
   header left out the "Rhapsodiae" and the Zurich summary report.

## Minor

Gracián's year is at OCR 26. Laemmer's Campeggio letters of 1530 begin on 13 May (OCR 2582). The Chinese
Repository heading is at OCR 1299-1302. Revilliod signs the Jussie preface as editor. The first volume of the
Chronicles does not name its translator on the title page. Currie's year is at OCR 58 and Evliya I/1's at 758.
Reu: nothing later than 1930 except one citation that reads "Luther Jahrbuch 1932", probably a slip; no copyright
notice; the preface says Jacobs's translations were used. Ibn Verga is named in Gentius's preface.

## Rights, all 56 files

Every Publication Date agrees with its Rights line, registry entry and queue row. Every OCR title-page year that
can be read matches. No reprint leaf, Nachdruck or reprinter's name was found in any file; the hits were library
stamps, host notices or misreads. Schirrmacher: the OCR has the Perthes title page of 1876, a preface dated
"Rostock, im Mai 1876", errata and a printer's line at the end, and none of the words Amsterdam, Grüner, Nachdruck
or 1968. Weimar: forewords dated July 1910 and a Weimar printer's line; no "unveränderter Abdruck". Leo Africanus
and Montaigne: original title pages with their printers' lines. Machiavelli volume 4: a copyright notice of 1882.

## Records

from about 27873). The count of sixteen imprints read on images matches the search log.
