## BLOCKING

**B1. The essence leaves out Polycarp, and its replacement goes past the source.** The approved heart and the outline's essence both say that Polycarp gathered the letters and sent them on. The story says instead: "The churches he wrote to carried them on". The only held witness to the letters being carried on is Polycarp, who sent to Philippi "the Epistles of Ignatius written by him to us, and all the rest … which we have by us" (ANF 3608–3618). No other church he wrote to is shown carrying them. Polycarp's part is not heard until about word 440.

- **Change:** in paragraph 2, replace "The churches he wrote to carried them on, and later writers quoted them." with "Polycarp, the bishop of Smyrna, sent on the ones he held, and later writers quoted them."
- **Paragraph 4:** "he met its bishop, Polycarp, face to face" may then become "he met Polycarp face to face".
- **Paragraph 8:** keep it as it stands. The kit lets the essence reach ahead, and paragraph 8 adds the occasion, that Philippi had asked.
- **Room in the outline:** the outline's Connection line ("seven letters, carried on by the churches he wrote to") left room for this. Reword it to "seven letters, which Polycarp, who met him at Smyrna, sent on to Philippi when that church asked (I1)".
- **Do not say line to add:**
  > - Not "the churches he wrote to carried them on". The only held witness to the letters being passed on is Polycarp (I1). Say "Polycarp, the bishop of Smyrna, sent on the ones he held".
