## The outline

- **Do not say:** the review's line was added word for word, after the line on "fifteen letters" and before the line on "the wheat of Christ":
  > - Not "the churches he wrote to carried them on". The only held witness to the letters being passed on is Polycarp (I1). Say "Polycarp, the bishop of Smyrna, sent on the ones he held".
- **Connection:** its first sentence now reads "what passed from Ignatius was seven letters, which Polycarp, who met him at Smyrna, sent on to Philippi when that church asked (I1)." This is the review's wording (B1). The Connection's next sentence ("Polycarp, who met him at Smyrna, gathered the ones he held…") now says the same thing again. It was left as it stands, because the review gave no wording for it.
- **The essence** still says "seven letters to the churches that came out to meet him". MINOR 3 notes this, but gives new wording for the brief only, so the outline's essence was not changed.
