## Quoted spans: all awaiting second reading

The brief has six quoted spans; each has one entry, and no `secondReading` is set.

1. "It is as if a skilled artist had carefully made a fine portrait of a king from choice small stones. Then someone broke up the man's likeness. He moved those stones, fitted them together again, and made the shape of a dog or a fox, and a poor one at that." Printed: the Greek, HOLL 31907-31911, ending where the brief stops ("χατεσχευασμένην"); line-end hyphen joined (ἐπι-/σήμων). English printed: ANF 30994-30998. Awaiting second reading.
2. "The church has received this preaching and this faith, as I said. Though it is scattered through the whole world, it guards them with care, as if it lived in one house. It believes them alike, as if it had one soul and one heart. It preaches them, teaches them and hands them on in harmony, as if it had one mouth." Printed: the Greek, HOLL 32715-32719; the line-end hyphen joined, and the editor's margin numbers ("81,1", "Ὁ182") and the page mark "|" left out. The file prints "προ-/ἔφαμεν", so the card keeps the OCR's "προἔφαμεν" (the outline had silently corrected it to "προέφαμεν"). English printed: ANF 31419-31425. Awaiting second reading.
3. "the twelfth place from the apostles". Printed: ANF 39849-39851 (from the Latin; neither Latin nor Greek is held). Awaiting second reading.
4. "I remember that time more clearly than recent years. ... And I can tell how he spoke of his time with John and the others who had seen the Lord." (the brief's full span). Printed: EUS 30807-30815, the whole sentence, since the brief leaves its middle out unmarked; the `<pb>` tag inside "manner" (EUS 30812) aside. Awaiting second reading.
5. "the blessed elder". Printed: HIPP 10028, "the blessed presbyter Irenæus". Awaiting second reading.
6. "a very exact inquirer". Printed: TERT 43167, "Irenæus, that very exact inquirer into all doctrines". Awaiting second reading.

Every printed and English printed wording was checked against its lines with tags removed. Spans 3 and 6 also stand verbatim in their files as the brief gives them. Spans 1, 2, 4 and 5 are renderings and do not.

## Conflicts with the entry's existing fields

None. Notes:
- `dates` "c. 177-190": the card gives no year 190. It says his last trace is the Easter quarrel toward the close of the second century (EUS-ed 24497-24499). That does not contradict the entry.
- `sourcing` says *Against Heresies* survives whole in Latin and English. The card says the same, adding that the Latin is an old translation and most of Book I survives in Greek. The Latin itself is not in the Library (sheet line 18).
- `legacy` names Peter Lampe. The card says only that historians still argue, since Lampe is not held (I5).
