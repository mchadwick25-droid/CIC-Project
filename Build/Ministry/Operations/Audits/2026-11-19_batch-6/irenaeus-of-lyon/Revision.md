## Findings and what was done

- **B1. Essence not complete in the first minute.** Made all three of the review's changes, word for word: the turn sentence now names Irenaeus as bishop of Lyon; "The bishop was Irenaeus of Lyon, and his five books against them are the fullest answer we still have." is replaced by "As a boy he had heard the old bishop Polycarp of Smyrna, and he became bishop himself after a persecution in 177. His five books against these teachers are the fullest answer we still have."; the old paragraph 4 (the Asia boyhood) is deleted. "Asia" is no longer heard, so it needs no gloss. The story still never says he was born or grew up in Smyrna. The later "Irenaeus became bishop after Pothinus, Eusebius says, …" stays, as the review directs.
- **M1. Held-back name reveal.** Fixed by B1's first replacement.
- **M2. "Gaul" without its plain sense.** Added "today's France" as the review gives it. The sentence now reads "That, wrote Irenaeus, bishop of Lyon in Roman Gaul, today's France, about the year 180, is what the followers of Valentinus did with Scripture."
- **M3. Force softened.** Replaced "Others died for their faith, and their ashes were swept into the Rhône," with "Others were put to death, and their burned remains were swept into the Rhône," word for word.
- **M4. Year credited to the wrong witness.** Replaced with the review's wording: "In the year 177 a persecution struck the Christians of Lyon and Vienne, on the river Rhône. Their bishop, Pothinus, more than ninety years old, the survivors wrote, was beaten and died in prison."
- **M5 with C1. Dangling "carried by Irenaeus", and the quarrel clause.** Replaced with the review's wording: "From prison they asked Irenaeus, one of the elders who led the church with its bishop, to carry their letters to Rome." The elder gloss is kept; nothing says he wrote the letter or which side of the quarrel it took.
- **C2.** Cut "and lost that office" after "had become an elder at Rome".

No other sentence was touched.
