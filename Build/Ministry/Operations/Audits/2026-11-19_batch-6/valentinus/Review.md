## The three lines, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Valentinus, a Christian teacher at Rome in the middle of the second century, who founded a school with its own teaching about God and the world.
2. **What happened:** He taught at Rome for some twenty years; Tertullian says he hoped to become a bishop, broke away in anger when another man was chosen, and was expelled more than once. His school taught a hidden Father, powers coming forth in pairs and a world made by a lesser maker, and it outlived him and split in two.
3. **Why the connection matters:** The church's writers answered his school at length and, in doing so, set out that one God made the world. Those same hostile books are almost all we have of him, even his own words, until the 1945 find.

### MINOR

**M1. A gloss puts the narrator on Tertullian's side.** The passage reads: "That was the church that kept the true teaching, and Tertullian puts the break down to ambition and a wish for revenge." Said in the narrator's voice, the first clause states as fact that the church kept the true teaching. The kit says the narrator never carries any side's view forward. The sentence also joins two ideas with "and".
Change: "That was the church that kept the true teaching, and Tertullian puts" → "By that he meant the church that kept the true teaching. He puts". This gives "By that he meant the church that kept the true teaching. He puts the break down to ambition and a wish for revenge." The plain sense stays.

**M2. "an unnamed pair, Unspeakable and Silence" sounds like a contradiction when heard once.** A pair is called unnamed and then named in the same breath. Irenaeus's point is that the pair is "inexpressible by any name" (IR 31523).
Change: "an unnamed pair" → "a pair beyond any name".

**M3. The Hippolytus quote carries three "he"s.** In "he did not love being alone. For, he says, he was all love", the listener cannot tell the Father from the unnamed speaker. Quote only the span that needs its own words. It stays word for word inside the outline's rendering.
Change: 'the Father was alone, and "he did not love being alone. For, he says, he was all love, and love is not love unless there is something to love."' → 'the Father was alone, and he was all love, and "love is not love unless there is something to love."'

**M4. "and more pairs after them" says more than the source.** Irenaeus says ten powers came from Word and Life and twelve from Man and Church (IR 31527–31530), with no pairing stated. The outline says "more powers".
Change: "more pairs after them" → "more powers after them".

**M5. "They had wished" turns a standing wish into a past event.** The source reads "ye would have death distributed to you" (CL 39091–39092), and the outline has "wished to have death given out to them".
Change: "They had wished for death to be given to them" → "They wished for death to be given to them".

**M6. "not by Christ" is broader than Clement's charge.** Clement says nature would have shone forth "apart from the Saviour's appearance" (CL 40959–40960). The outline has "not by Christ's coming".
Change: "not by Christ." → "not by Christ's coming."

**M7. "kept the only account of the school" drops the qualifier "almost".** Clement and, later, Epiphanius also kept accounts. The outline's own I3 and the tradition entry say "known almost only through them". The outline's What came 3 also has "kept the only account", which gave the writer room for this.
Change: "Those same books kept the only account of the school for eighteen centuries, until" → "For eighteen centuries the school was known almost only through those same books, until".
Suggested Do not say line for the outline (MINOR, so optional): Not "the only account of the school" or "known only through them". Say "known almost only through them" (I3).
