## 3. Not applied: M13

**The fixer is right.** `barhadbeshabba_cause-de-la-fondation-des-ecoles-po4-syr-fra_scher1908.txt` holds the whole of PO 4. Lines 15906–15907 sit in chapter XXVI, "Histoire de saint Éphrem le docteur" (page head [85], 295). The running heads "HISTOIRE NESTORIENNE" are at 15850 and 15930, and the volume's contents give the Chronicle of Seert (line 652). Barhadbeshabba's *Cause* begins at 16773. The file has no other hit for "Diatessaron" or "Tatien". So the passage is not a witness from about 600, and the check's ground for putting it before Isho'dad fails.

**Does the Chronicle belong in the sheet? Yes, as a late corroborating witness, not as primary support. It changes no beat and no confidence level.**
- Its own editor dates it late. The author "est postérieur au patriarche Išo'barnoun (+ 828)" and "il est du moins certain que notre auteur vivait après le IXe siècle", perhaps in the first half of the 1200s (PO 4 file 12095–12096, 12140–12167). So it is no earlier than Isho'dad (c. 852). The same editor warns that it repeats itself, Ephrem included, and gathers uncritical, sometimes legendary material (12169–12175).
- It is worth listing for one reason: it is the only held witness to Ephrem's commentary that the sheet reads directly. Isho'dad and Bar Salibi reach the sheet only through Hogg. It also has to be kept from a writer, because it calls Tatian "le grec Tatien", which conflicts with his own "born in the land of the Assyrians" (Do not say, line 104).
