## Not applied

- **M13** (Barhadbeshabba as primary support for Ephrem's commentary). The passage the check cites, `barhadbeshabba_cause-de-la-fondation-des-ecoles-po4-syr-fra_scher1908.txt` 15906–15910, is not Barhadbeshabba's *Cause*. That file holds the whole of PO 4. Its header says the *Cause* starts at about raw line 16752, and the pages around 15906 carry the running head "HISTOIRE NESTORIENNE" (lines 15850, 15930), the Chronicle of Seert (title at line 652). The *Cause* section has no hit for "Diatessaron" or "Tatien" (the only hits in the file are 15906–15907). So the passage is not a witness from about 600, and the check's grounds for putting it before Isho'dad fail. The 'Abdisho lines in Mai, vol. 10 (235–236, 4134) say only that he followed Tatian's example in gathering into one, and that a "Diatessaron" exists as an example. They are not the "did not add a single saying" line that I3 credits to Hogg. What the search does show: the Chronicle of Seert says twice that Ephrem explained the Diatessaron (the PO 4 file, 15903–15910, and `chronicle-of-seert_histoire-nestorienne-premiere-partie-fasc2-patrologia-orientalis-5-ara-fra_scher-dib1910.txt` 15751). Whether to add the Chronicle to I1, and with what date, is left to the recheck.
