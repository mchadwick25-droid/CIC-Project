# Second Readings (batch 6, B6-01 to B6-71)

## Method

For each id I set our wording (`Second_Reading_Key.md`, as it stands in the brief) beside the blind rendering (`Second_Readings_Blind_A.md`, `_B.md`, `_C.md`). Where they differed, I opened the cited lines in `cic/texts/`, read the story's card `quotationOriginals` entry (`printed`, `original`, `uncertain`), and read the sentence of the brief that holds the quote. Where the blind reader noted a scan misread, a split word or a word-count quirk, I took it as the same passage unless the lines said otherwise. Where our wording is a fragment or is cut, I judged only the words we use, and whether the cut changes the sense in the brief's sentence. Following Mark's guidance (Decision-Log, "Batch 6: where sources disagree", 2026-11-19), where two held sources differ, a wording held by either is credited.

- **SAME**: same meaning.
- **MINOR**: a small difference a listener would not take differently.
- **MEANING**: a listener would understand something different.

Several requests sent the blind reader to the original language (B6-08, 09, 37-41, 46, 50, 55, 57, 58, 62, 64, 66, 67, 69). Where the card holds both the original and a printed English, both sets of lines are the same passage.

## MEANING items: replacement wording

### B6-20 (ignatius-of-antioch, `quotationOriginals[2]`)

The held source, ANF vol. 1, *Letter to the Romans* 4 (`cic/texts/anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-irenaeus.xml` 7040-7042), reads: "I am the wheat of God, and let me be ground by the teeth of the wild beasts, that I may be found the pure bread of Christ." It is a plea, and the brief's own lead-in ("He begged them … to let him be food for the beasts") frames it that way. Our "and I am ground" turns the plea into a statement.

Smallest replacement, in the brief's quoted span and the card's `modern`:

- Replace "and I am ground" with "and let me be ground".

Full span: "I am God's wheat, and let me be ground by the teeth of wild beasts, so that I may be found the pure bread of Christ."

Note: Lightfoot's Greek reads the indicative (ἀλήθομαι, "I am ground"), which would hold our present wording, but no Greek of the letter is vendored in `cic/texts/`. If one is vendored and cited on the card, our present wording would be held and this item would fall to MINOR under the "where sources disagree" guidance.
