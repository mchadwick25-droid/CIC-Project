# Second Readings, Blind A (batch 6, B6-01 to B6-24)

## B6-01
So we humans turn out to be ruled in three ways: by Nature, all of us the same; by Fate, each differently; and by our own freedom, each as he chooses.

## B6-02
For look: wherever we are, we are all called by the one name of Christ, Christians.

## B6-03
And when that new world is set up, all evil turmoil will stop and all rebellion will end. The foolish will be convinced, and everything lacking will be made up.

## B6-04
When you were rich, you were useless. But now you are useful and fit for life.

## B6-05
Sir, I have heard some teachers claim that there is no other repentance than the one that happened when we went down into the water and received forgiveness of our past sins.

## B6-06
For he will hardly live.

(Footnote on the same line gives the reading of two manuscripts: "he will hardly live to God.")

## B6-07
So the Scripture spoke truly when it says,

## B6-08
And so it should indeed be read, but it cannot be read publicly to the people in church, either among the prophets, whose number is complete, or among the apostles, to the end of time.

Read past: the printed text has "se publicare" and "completum"; I followed the editor's bracketed emendations "sed publicari" (but ... be made public) and "completos" (complete, of the prophets). The editor's own note is misprinted "publieari" for "publicari".

## B6-09
than that apocryphal Shepherd of adulterers

(Latin ablative of comparison, completing "more widely received among the churches than ...".)

Read past: the scan reads "moecbomm^" for "moechorum" (of adulterers); "Bamabae" just before is "Barnabae".

## B6-10
He also claimed that Noah's ark was made as a symbol of the Church. In it were dogs, wolves, ravens, and every creature, clean and unclean. So he argues that the same must hold for the Church.

## B6-11
He did not say, "I and the Father am one," but "are one." For the word "are" is not used of one person. It refers to two persons, and one power.

## B6-16
It is said that Hippolytus the presbyter, at the time when the tyrant's orders were pressing hard, always remained in the schism of Novatus.

Read past: the scan reads "scismk" for "scisma" (schism) and "XOvali" for "Novati" (of Novatus). The capitals in the line are the editor's marking of letters, not words, and do not change the reading.

## B6-17
Damasus tells these things as he heard them; Christ tests all things.

(Note: "probat" can mean "tests" or "approves"; "Christ is the judge of all of it" carries both.)

## B6-18
From Syria all the way to Rome I am fighting with beasts, on land and at sea, by night and by day.

## B6-19
But suppose, as some godless people (that is, unbelievers) say, that he only seemed to suffer. It is they who only seem to exist. Then why am I in chains? Why do I long to fight the wild beasts? Am I dying for nothing, then?

## B6-20
I am God's wheat. Let me be ground by the teeth of the wild beasts, so that I may be found to be Christ's pure bread.

## B6-23
It is as if a skilled artist had made a beautiful image of a king out of precious jewels. Then someone takes this likeness of the man completely apart and rearranges the gems. He fits them together into the shape of a dog or a fox, and does even that badly.

## B6-24
As I said before, the Church received this preaching and this faith. Though she is scattered across the whole world, she guards it carefully, as if she lived in one house. She also believes these teachings as if she had one soul and one heart. She proclaims them, teaches them and hands them on in full agreement, as if she had only one mouth.
