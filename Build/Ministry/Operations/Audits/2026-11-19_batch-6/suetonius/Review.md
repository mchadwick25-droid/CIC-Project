## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Suetonius, a Roman scholar outside the church, who wrote lives of twelve of Rome's rulers around 120, and was a friend of Pliny and for a time Hadrian's secretary.
2. **What happened:** in two lines, each lost in a list of an emperor's acts, he says Claudius drove from Rome the Jews rioting "at the urging of Chrestus", and that under Nero the Christians, a "new and harmful superstition", were punished, which he counts as a blameless act.
3. **Why the connection matters:** these are among the very few outside notices of the first Christians at Rome; historians read the first beside Acts and still argue whether "Chrestus" means Christ, and Christians like Tertullian knew Romans got their name wrong.

All three lines come clear from one hearing.

## Findings

### BLOCKING

**B1. The Nero half of the essence is not told in the first minute.**
- **Where:** paragraph 2: "and the Christians get this line and one other, under Nero."
- **Why:** the approved heart and the outline's essence both have "under Nero the Christians were punished". In the first minute the listener learns only that a second line exists. Its content waits until word 270.
- **Change to:** "and the Christians get this line and one other: under Nero, they were punished." Beat 4 then tells the line in full, and the essence may reach ahead (Kit Part 3, Story beats), so nothing is told twice.
- **Do not say line to add to the outline** (beat 1 left room for it): "Not the Nero line named without its content in the essence. Say in the first minute that under Nero the Christians were punished (E3)."

### MINOR

**M1. "He seated German envoys with the senators" says more than the source.** Claudius "allowed the envoys of the Germans to sit in the orchestra". They had moved there "of their own accord" on seeing the Parthian and Armenian envoys with the senate (ROL 25907–25916; E2; the outline's opening has "let German envoys sit"). Change "and he seated German envoys with the senators at the theater" to "and he let German envoys sit with the senators at the theater".

**M2. "He" after "Chrestus" can be heard as Chrestus.** Paragraph 2 ends "…who Chrestus was." Paragraph 3 opens "A close friend of Pliny the Younger, he served…". Heard once, the nearest man is Chrestus. Change "A close friend of Pliny the Younger, he served" to "Suetonius was a close friend of Pliny the Younger, and served".

**M3. "Meanwhile" misstates the time.** "Christian writers, meanwhile, complained…" follows the 1909 editors, but Tertullian wrote around 200. The order is the outline's weight ranking, which is sound, but "meanwhile" joins the two in time. Change "Christian writers, meanwhile, complained" to "Long before, Christian writers had complained".
