## The entry's existing fields

No field of the card contradicts `sourcing`, `voices`, `legacy`, `why`, `floorNote`, `relationsSummary`, `dates` or `placeLabel`. Three points are not supported by the research, and are left as they are:
- `dates` "c. 110-155" (and `start` 110, `end` 155): no research line gives 110. The only 110 found is in the editor's note heading Jerome's chapter 17, "Bishop certainly (Salmon) 110" (JER 39838–39839), a date for his time as bishop, not his birth. The card says no ancient source gives a year, and names McGiffert's 155 or 156 and the Ante-Nicene note's about 167.
- `sourcing`, "Jerome and the Chronicle of Eusebius point to 167": Jerome's Lives 17 gives only the reign (JER 39850–39851), and the Chronicle is not among the research files.
- `sourcing`, "some scholars argue that its thirteenth chapter was added later": not in the research. The card says only which parts survive in Latin alone.

One more point, outside the card: once the brief becomes the entry's `longDescription`, its eight quotations are renderings, not printed words. The validator's verbatim check will need them listed in `tools/quote_renderings.json`.
