## 6. Originals

The seven Martyrdom entries carry Gebhardt's Greek, as they should; the other two (Irenaeus to Victor, and Philippians 7) have no held Greek (Eusebius V is not held in Greek; the letter has no readable Greek). But five `original` wordings copy six of the OCR's faults into the card ("8&0", "Alps", "xci", "γενόέϑλιον", "εἴς τὸ τὴν", "μὲ ἠδίχησεν"). That is not what Gebhardt prints. The source sheet's F3 names "8&0" for ἕξ, "Αἶρε" and the χ for κ faults, and its section 3 gives the readings (SS Q8, Q18, and line 128 for Αἶρε). In "εἴς τὸ τὴν" the article τὸ cannot stand before τὴν; the τε ... καὶ that follows needs τε, as SS Q18 reads it. These are only scan faults. FIX 1 gives the printed Greek.
