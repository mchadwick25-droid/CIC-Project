## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Tacitus, a Roman historian who despised the Christians, writing about fifty years after the great fire of Rome in 64.
2. **What happened:** he wrote the one chapter that tells most fully how Nero, to kill the rumour that the fire was set on an order, put the blame on the Christians and had a huge number killed as a show, and in it he says Christus was put to death under Pilate.
3. **Why the connection matters:** that hostile chapter became what later readers used: Tertullian called him a liar on another matter, Sulpicius Severus retold the deaths and called the dead innocent, and later historians made it their main outside record and argued over it.

No line is blank or wrong.

## Findings

### BLOCKING

- **B1. The essence completes at word 175.** The Christus clause of the essence arrives only with Q1. In paragraph 2, after "of what Nero did to the Christians", add the outline's beat 1 clause: **"…of what Nero did to the Christians, and in it names their founder, Christus, put to death under Pilate."** The essence is then complete within paragraph 2 (about word 105). Q1 in paragraph 4 stays as it is, as the essence reaching ahead and the chapter then giving his own words. No Do not say line: the outline's beat 1 already holds the clause.
- **B2. "Hatred of the human race" without its plain sense.** The outline's Words to explain gives it a plain sense: held historians read the verdict to mean the inquiry proved they were Christians, not that they set the fire (D3, Dominant Modern Reconstruction). The Connection also names "what they were convicted of" among the three things the listener must be told. A listener hearing the charge bare may take it as a finding of fact about the Christians. After "…than of hatred of the human race." add: **"Historians a century ago read that to mean the inquiry proved them Christians, not that they set the fire."** Do not say line to add to the outline: *Not "hatred of the human race" left unexplained, or its plain sense held back to the later readers. Where it is first heard, give the held historians' reading, credited to them: the inquiry proved they were Christians, not that they set the fire (D3).*
- **B3. "Main outside record … of Christus" overclaims.** Paragraph 9: "Later historians made the chapter their main outside record of these deaths, and of Christus put to death under Pilate." The outline holds the chapter as the main evidence for Nero's punishment and "an outside notice of Christus under Pilate" (I1). As written, it is the main outside record of Christus, which nothing held says, and Josephus is on the tree. Replace with: **"Later historians made the chapter their main outside record of these deaths, and a Roman's witness that Christus was put to death under Pilate."** Do not say line to add to the outline: *Not "the main" or "the chief" outside record of Christus or of Jesus. The chapter is the main outside record of these deaths and an outside notice of Christus under Pilate (I1).*
