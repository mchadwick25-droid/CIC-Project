## The three lines, from one hearing

- **Who or what:** the council that Marcian and Pulcheria called at Chalcedon, across from Constantinople, in autumn 451. Leo of Rome sent legates and his Tome.
- **What happened:** it deposed Dioscorus of Alexandria. It changed a draft's "from two natures" to "in two natures" after the legates threatened to leave and the bishops cried "We believe as Leo". It banned every other creed and ranked Constantinople after Rome, which Leo voided.
- **Why the connection matters:** Egypt, Syria and Armenia refused it, though they also condemned Eutyches. They confessed "one nature from two" and kept Dioscorus as a martyr. Both sides claimed Cyril. Alexandria broke communion and Palestine's monks chose their own bishop.
