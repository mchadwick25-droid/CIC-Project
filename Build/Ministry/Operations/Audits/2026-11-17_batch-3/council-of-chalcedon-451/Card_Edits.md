# Card edits: Council of Chalcedon (451)

No existing words were changed. Everything in the original card is kept word for word.

## New
- `briefLength`: "about 3 minutes 45 seconds". The brief is 561 words by `wc -w` (the profiler counts 558); at the outline's rate of 150 words a minute that is about 3 minutes 45 seconds. Mark ruled up to 560 words (Decision-Log, 2026-11-18).
- `briefReferences`: 15 entries, one for each source the brief draws on, with file lines.
- `quotationOriginals`: 13 entries, one for each quoted phrase in the brief, each with the modern wording exactly as the brief has it, the printed wording from its file and lines, and the work. `secondReading` is left out. The quoted phrases are: Dioscorus's "from two" (npnf214 19254-19260), "Peter has spoken thus through Leo" (20000), "Leo and Cyril taught the same thing" (20002-20003), Anatolius's "not for the faith" (Mansi 7 9802-9819, Latin OCR as printed), "the most holy Leo or Dioscorus?" (20170-20171), "We believe as Leo" (20172-20173), the Definition (Evagrius Greek 5244-5246, with Percival's English at 20286-20292), "the wives and daughters of the Alexandrians" (Evagrius Greek 5396-5398, Walford 3394-3395), "for the orthodox faith" (History of the Patriarchs 34869-34870), "the crown of martyrdom" (35109-35110), "an opposite infection" (Severus, Select Letters VI, 720-722), the robe (Zachariah 2181-2183, with Michael's French at 2859-2860 in `uncertain`), and "foreign from our body" (Zachariah 2375-2376). Every printed string was checked against its lines by script.
- `sources.writtenLater`: four files added because the quotations cite them: Evagrius's Greek (Bidez and Parmentier), Severus's Select Letters Book VI part 1, the History of the Patriarchs (Evetts), Michael the Syrian tome 2 (Chabot). As at Nicaea release 50.

## Left alone (not wrong, or no source found against them)
- `traditions[0]` (Imperial and Juridical Christianity): "Leo's legates presided over the votes". The brief says the officials ran the council, and Session IV has the imperial judges calling the votes (npnf214 20123-20132). But Percival's note at 20033-20037 says the Latin reads that Leo "commanded our littleness to preside in his place", and the Greek differs. No held source contradicts "presided"; the point is disputed. Not found, left in place.
- `uncertain[3]`: "The Greek text of the definition now reads 'of two natures'". Evagrius's Greek (5244-5245) reads ἐν δύο, "in two". Hefele (npnf214 20361-20370) speaks of the Greek text of the acts, and Mansi's garbled Greek column reads ἐκ δύο, so the sentence is not contradicted. Left in place.
- `issue[4]`, Zachariah on the Nestorian party at court asking for Theodoret to preside: the chronicle says so (2272-2276); not in the brief; left.
- Nothing on the card contradicts the brief. Armenia's condemnation of Eutyches (brief: "Ormanian says Armenia did") is in Ormanian 2502-2512 but is not on the card's own text; the new reference carries it.
