# Claim Check: "The Coptic, Syriac and Armenian churches condemned Eutyches too." (2026-11-18)

Brief line 15. Outline cites E4, E5, B20 for it.

## Coptic: HELD, but only through its leader and only in the Syriac chronicle
- The Coptic History of the Patriarchs (`cic/texts/severus-al-muqaffa_history-patriarchs-coptic-church-v1_evetts1904.txt`) names Eutyches once (34513), in the Nestorius story. It says nothing against him. The Coptic Synaxarium (Basset 1907) never names him. No Coptic-held text condemns him.
- The support comes from the Syriac chronicle, about Timothy Aelurus, Dioscorus's successor at Alexandria. `cic/texts/zachariah-rhetor_chronicle_hamiltonbrooks1899.txt` 4093–4097:
  > "But Timothy, when in banishment, wrote not alone against the Nestorians, but also against the Eutychianists. And this appears from his letters to Alexandria and Palestine, against those who hold the opinions of Eutyches, and do not confess Christ to be of the same nature with us in the flesh as well as of the same Nature with the Father in the Godhead."
- Dioscorus's letter from exile (Zachariah 2372–2376, E4) rejects the idea that Christ's flesh was "foreign from our body". It does not name Eutyches, so it does not support "condemned Eutyches".
- Neither the Outline nor the Source Sheet cites this passage. E4 as cited does not carry the Coptic part.

## Syriac: HELD
- `cic/texts/severus-of-antioch_select-letters-book6-part1_brooks1903.txt` 718–722 (E4):
  > "they are men that deserve anathema, because they unnecessarily extended the heresy of Eutyches."
- `cic/texts/severus-of-antioch_select-letters-book6-part2_brooks1904.txt` 4222–4223:
  > "they also term those who are infected with the phantasmal tenet of Eutyches Manichees"

## Armenian: HELD
- `cic/texts/ormanian_church-of-armenia_gregory1912.txt` 2508–2511 (E5):
  > "Far, indeed, from adopting the doctrine of Eutyches, his name, together with those of Arius, of Macedon, and of Nestorius, was officially condemned."
- Also 5307–5309: "The name of the latter is officially and solemnly anathematised by the Church".

## Replacement
The claim is held for all three churches, so no replacement is required. The Coptic part rests on Zachariah 4093–4097, which the Source Sheet does not yet cite. Either add it under E4, or use this sentence, which says only what the cited sources hold:

> "Their leaders condemned Eutyches too: Timothy of Alexandria, Severus of Antioch and Armenia's bishops."
