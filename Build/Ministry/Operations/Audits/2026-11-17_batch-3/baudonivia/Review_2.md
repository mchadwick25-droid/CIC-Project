## Findings

**MINOR 1 — "Franks came at her with swords and clubs."** The source says the Franks "tried to defend" the shrine with swords and clubs (29669, "conabantur defendere"), not that they attacked the queen. "Came at her" turns a defence of the shrine into an assault on her person. Change to: "Franks came with swords and clubs to defend it, but she sat her horse unmoved until it burned."

**MINOR 2 — the gift left to the house is not said.** The outline's connection names "the gift she left them": Radegund left the Cross in her monastery "for the honour of the place and the saving of the people" (30321). The brief has it "brought in", with no sense that it stayed with the nuns. If a few words allow, add after "bring it in": "and she left it in the house, for the honour of the place and the saving of the people."

**MINOR 3 — the manuscripts sentence breaks the Cross thread.** Moving "In most manuscripts her book follows Fortunatus's..." to after the Helena quote (or to just before the death) keeps the Cross and Helena together and lets the legacy land as one move.

**MINOR 4 — longest sentence 32 words**, past the Standard's "about 30" ("When the Frankish kingdoms moved against each other..."). Could split before "and set her nuns to pray for them": "She set her nuns to pray for them."
