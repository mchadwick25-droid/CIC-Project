## quotationOriginals

The new brief quotes three spans. "What she did in the land of the East," ... "blessed Radegund did in Gaul." is broken around "Baudonivia says of the empress Helena...". It counts as one quotation, so it keeps one entry.

1. **Added**: "I chose you, daughters. You are my light, my life, my rest and all my happiness. You are the new planting."
   - printed: "Vos elegi filias, vos, raea lumina, vos, mea vita, vos, mea requies totaque felici- > 'tas, vos, novella plantatio." This is verbatim OCR from SRM2 lines 29901-29903. Line 29902 is blank, and "> '" is scan noise before "tas".
