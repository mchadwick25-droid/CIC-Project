## 1. The heart, and the first minute

**The heart of his life:** Justin was a philosopher who became a Christian and kept the name "philosopher". At Rome he taught anyone who came to him, and he wrote to the emperor to defend Christians, who were punished for the name alone, claiming Christ as the Word that every race has shared. About 165 he was beheaded at Rome, and the church remembers him as "philosopher and martyr".

**Does the first minute carry it?** Yes. Word 150 falls inside "he was beheaded at Rome for this faith" (words 146–154). By then the listener has the philosopher, the teaching at Rome, the petition, the name-and-deeds claim, the Word with Quote 2, Socrates, a clause naming the Sunday account, and the death.

**Listener read (grade 7.2, under the floor of 8, Neo's voice):** not flat and not talking down. The substance is all there and said plainly: *logos* as both "word" and "reason", the Word shared by every race, the hard edge with Trypho set beside the friendship, and the trial exchange. The low score comes from short, clean sentences, not from thinned thought. The joins in the first movement are a little blunt. "He counted Socrates among them, and he set out openly what Christians did on Sunday" ties two separate ideas with a bare "and". Read aloud, though, it works as a documentary narrator's summary. No change needed for the score.

## Findings

**B1. BLOCKING. "On that day" now points to the day of his death.** The death sentence was moved forward, and the Sunday paragraph now follows it. So "On that day, he wrote, Christians heard …" comes straight after "About 165, he was beheaded at Rome for this faith." Heard once, "that day" is the day of the beheading, and "he wrote" seems to come after his death. That misleads a listener on one hearing.
Correction: replace "On that day, he wrote, Christians heard" with "On Sunday, he wrote, Christians heard".

**M1. MINOR. "About 165" is unclear heard once.** The outline's spoken form is "about the year 165" (Setting, Time). A bare number after a sentence about Sunday can be heard as a count, not a date.
Correction: replace "About 165, he was beheaded at Rome for this faith." with "About the year 165, he was beheaded at Rome for this faith."

**M2. MINOR. "His road there" has nothing clear to point back to.** It follows the Sunday paragraph, so "there" could be Rome, Sunday or the faith. Review_2 praised the earlier signpost, "His road to that faith".
Correction: replace "His road there ran through the philosophers' schools." with "His road to that faith ran through the philosophers' schools."

These three changes add 3 words, which keeps the brief at about 461 words, still inside the three-minute ceiling. The death still falls inside word 150 (the sentence runs from word 146 to word 156).
