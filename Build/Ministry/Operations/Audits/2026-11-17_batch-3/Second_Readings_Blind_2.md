## third-council-of-toledo-589

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q1
- Lines read: 36661-36662 (context 36655-36668), `collectio-canonum-ecclesiae-hispanae-toledo-iii-lat_1808.txt`
- Passage taken: "Spiritus aeque Sanctus confitendus a nobis et praedicandus est a Patre et Filio procedere et cum Patre et Filio unius esse substantiae". Line 36662 runs on into "tertiam vero in Trinitate Spiritus Sancti esse personam". I stopped at the colon after "substantiae".
- Rendering: We must likewise confess and preach that the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son. He is of one substance with the Father and the Son.
- Notes: "aeque" means "likewise, equally", not "fairly". "substantia" is the creedal term (one being), not matter or material; "substance" can mislead a modern reader. The accents ("aequé") are the print's own, not a scan fault.

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q2
- Lines read: 20314 (context 20308-20318), `chronica-minora-2_...mommsen1894.txt`
- Passage taken: "ratione potius quam imperio converti ad catholicam fidem facit", finishing the word "con-verti" on line 20316. The clause it belongs to opens on 20313-20314: "sacerdotes sectae Arrianae sapienti colloquio aggressus".
- Rendering: He approached the priests of the Arian sect with wise discussion. He brought them to convert to the catholic faith by reason rather than by command.
- Notes: OCR read through: "impcrio" = imperio, "sectac" = sectae, "saccrdotes" = sacerdotes. "imperio" means "by command, by authority", not "by empire". "sacerdotes" covers bishops as well as priests. "catholic" here names the church of the creeds, not a later denomination.

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q3
- Lines read: 12068-12070 (context 12063-12074), `paulus-emeritanus-braulio-sisebut-pl80-lat_migne1863.txt`
- Passage taken: "aut pro religione in qua nunc ab ineunte aetate mea permansi libentissime moriar." Line 12068 opens with the half before it: "sed eo ritu quo vixi vivam".
- Rendering: Or I will most gladly die for the religion I have held from my early youth until now.
- Notes: OCR read through: "permarsi" = permansi, ":tate" = aetate. "religio" is close to modern "religion" here, but it means the speaker's own faith and rite, not religion in general.

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q4
- Lines read: 36900-36902 (context 36895-36906)
- Passage taken: "Omnis ergo, qui fidem et communionem ab Ario venientem, et hucusque a nobis retentam adhuc tenere desiderat et de tota cordis intentione non damnat, anathema sit." This runs to line 36904; the given lines stop at "de to-".
- Rendering: The faith and communion that came from Arius, we kept until now. Whoever still wants to hold it, and does not condemn it with his whole heart, let him be anathema.
- Notes: OCR read through: "ta.cordis" = tota cordis. "anathema" means cut off from the Church under its curse, not merely "disliked". "communion" means church fellowship, not only the sacrament.

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q5
- Lines read: 36981-36986 (context 36976-36990)
- Passage taken: "nulli dubium est nos nostrosque decessores errasse in haerese Ariana, et fidem evangelicam atque apostolicam nunc intra ecclesiam catholicam didicisse."
- Rendering: No one doubts that we and those before us went astray in the Arian heresy. Now, within the catholic church, we have learned the faith of the gospel and the apostles.
- Notes: OCR read through: "fi: dem" = fidem. "decessores" are predecessors (forebears in office and faith), not "the deceased".

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q6
- Lines read: 20408-20409 (context 20403-20412)
- Passage taken: from "post longas catholicorum neces" within the sentence "in praesenti vero sancta Toletana synodo Arrii perfidia ... ita radicitus amputata ... ut ulterius non pullulet". I rendered the whole sentence to line 20410, since the given words cannot stand alone.
- Rendering: In this present holy synod of Toledo, the false faith of Arius was cut off at the root. This came after long killings of catholics and slaughter of innocent people. The named prince, King Reccared, pressed it on, so that it should never sprout again.
- Notes: OCR read through: "catholicoruni neccs" = catholicorum neces, "insistentc" = insistente. "perfidia" means faithlessness or false belief, not personal "treachery" in the modern sense.

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q7
- Lines read: 37680-37681 (context 37675-37685)
- Passage taken: "[Quoniam] pene per omnem Hispaniam sive Galliam idolatriae sacrilegium inolevit".
- Rendering: For the sacrilege of idolatry has taken root almost all over Spain and Gaul.
- Notes: "sive" here joins the two regions ("and"), not a choice ("or"). "Gallia" is the Gothic kingdom's province north of the Pyrenees, not modern France. "pené" accent is the print's.

### third-council-of-toledo-589 Q8
- Lines read: 37255-37258 (context 37250-37268)
- Passage taken: "ut propter roborandam gentis nostrae novellam conversionem omnes Hispaniarum et Galliae ecclesiae hanc regulam servent". The rule it names follows on 37261-37265 (the creed recited before communion), which I did not render.
- Rendering: To strengthen the recent conversion of our people, all the churches of the Spanish provinces and of Gaul are to keep this rule.
- Notes: "gens" means a people or nation (here the Goths), not "race". "Hispaniarum" is plural: the provinces of Spain. Stray hyphen at the start of 37258 is a scan fault.

## council-of-chalcedon-451

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q1
- Lines read: 65953-65956, with the margin gap filled from 65905 (context 65900-65908, 65948-65960), `mansi_...tomus-6...txt`
- Passage taken: "Ex duabus suscipio, duas non suscipio. Cogor [impudenter agere: de anima] est mihi sermo." The margin at 65905 also gives "mihi res est" as a variant for "est mihi sermo".
- Rendering: "From two" I accept. "Two" I do not accept. I am forced to speak boldly. What I am speaking about concerns my soul.
- Greek, `npnf214_seven-ecumenical-councils.xml` 19256-19259: "τὸ ἐκ δύο δέχομαι· τὸ δύο οὐ δέχομαι". Rendering: I accept "from two". "Two" I do not accept.
- Notes: OCR read through: long s ("fuícipio", "fufcipio", "eft", "fermo"). The noun left out is "natures": "from two natures", not "two natures". "impudenter" means without shame or restraint; "boldly" is gentler than the Latin. The Greek column in Mansi is garbled and was not used.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q2
- Lines read: 92414-92415 (context 92410-92425)
- Passage taken: "Petrus per Leonem ita locutus est."
- Rendering: Peter has spoken this way through Leo.
- Notes: OCR "e(t" = est. Peter here is the apostle, speaking through his successor Leo, bishop of Rome.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q3
- Lines read: 92421-92422 (context 92410-92425)
- Passage taken: "Leo et Cyrillus similiter docuerunt, anathema ei qui sic non credit." The anathema clause finishes on 92423.
- Rendering: Leo and Cyril taught alike. Anathema to anyone who does not believe this way.
- Notes: OCR "fimiliter" = similiter, "fic" = sic. "similiter" means "in the same way", not "in a similar but different way".

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q4

- Notes: OCR read through: "eff" = est, "fed" = sed, "noh" = non. "fides" here means doctrine, not personal trust. The Greek column beside it is garbled and was not used.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q7
- Lines read: 10060-10063 (context 10050-10070)
- Passage taken: "Ergo addite definitioni, secundum judicium sanctissimi (patris) nostri Leonis, duas esse naturas unitas in Christo inconvertibiliter, et insepa-". The text breaks at "insepa-": lines 10064-10065 are blank and 10066 begins a new scene. The rest of the sentence is missing from the scan.
- Rendering: Then add to the definition, following the judgment of our most holy father Leo, that there are two natures united in Christ without change, and insepa[rably]...
- Notes: Scan fault: the sentence is cut off after "insepa-". "definitio" is the council's formal statement of faith, not a dictionary definition. "(patris)" is the editor's bracket.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q8
- Lines read: 5244-5246 (context 5240-5249), `evagrius-scholasticus_historia-ecclesiastica-grc_bidez-parmentier1898.txt`
- Passage taken: "ἕνα καὶ τὸν αὐτὸν Ἰησοῦν Χριστὸν υἱὸν κύριον μονογενῆ ἐν δύο φύσεσιν ἀσυγχύτως, ἀτρέπτως, ἀδιαιρέτως, ἀχωρίστως γνωριζόμενον". The sentence runs on with "οὐδαμοῦ τῆς..."; I stopped at "γνωριζόμενον".
- Rendering: One and the same Jesus Christ, Son, Lord, only-begotten, is made known in two natures. They are without confusion, without change, without division, without separation.
- Notes: The text here reads "ἐν δύο φύσεσιν" ("in two natures"). "γνωριζόμενον" means "recognized, acknowledged", not "known about". I split the sentence and supplied "is" and "They are" to keep one thought each; the Greek is one participial phrase.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q9
- Lines read: 5395-5398 (context 5390-5400)
- Passage taken: "Κἀντεῦθεν τῶν στρατιωτῶν παροινούντων ἔς τε τὰς γαμετὰς καὶ θυγατέρας τῶν Ἀλεξανδρέων, τῶν προτέρων πολλῷ δεινότερα προελθεῖν."
- Rendering: After that, the soldiers committed drunken outrages against the wives and daughters of the Alexandrians. Things far worse than before followed.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q10
- Lines read: 34870-34871 (context 34866-34874), `severus-al-muqaffa_history-patriarchs-coptic-church-v1_evetts1904.txt`

- Rendering: He suffered harsh persecution for the orthodox faith from the emperor Marcian and his wife. They drove him from his see through the biased action of the council of Chalcedon.
- Notes: OCR "us wife" = his wife, "he council" = the council. "partial" means biased, not "incomplete". "prince" means the emperor. "orthodox" is the writer's own name for his church's faith, not Eastern Orthodoxy.

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q11

### council-of-chalcedon-451 Q13
- Lines read: 2860-2861 (context 2856-2864), `michael-the-syrian_chronique-tome2-livres-viii-xi-fra_chabot1901.txt`
- Passage taken: "et déchira en dix mille morceaux la tunique intègre du Christ tissée d'une seule pièce". The subject is the assembly at Chalcedon (2857).
- Rendering: And it tore into ten thousand pieces the whole tunic of Christ, woven in a single piece.
- Also, English, `zachariah-rhetor_chronicle_hamiltonbrooks1899.txt` 2182-2183: "and tore into ten thousand rents the perfect Robe of Christ, woven from the top throughout". Rendering: And it tore the whole robe of Christ into ten thousand pieces, the robe woven in one piece from the top down.
- Notes: "intègre" means whole, intact, not "honest". In the English, "perfect" means whole, unbroken, and "rents" means tears. The trailing asterisk and "*" footnote marks in the French are the edition's.

### pachomius Q4
- Lines read: 10339 (context 10335-10342), `cassian_institutes-and-contra-nestorium_petschenig1888.txt`
