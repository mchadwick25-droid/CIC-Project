- **What happened:** Oxford banned his teaching on the bread; a London council condemned him in 1382 and he was banished from Oxford; an English Bible came from his circle; summoned to Rome, he excused himself, and by his editor's account died of a stroke at Lutterworth in 1384, with no friend's record left; in 1415 Constance declared him a heretic and ordered his bones dug up, and Martin V later ordered them burned.
