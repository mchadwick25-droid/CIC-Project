- **What happened:** The king's confession was read. The Gothic leaders cursed their old faith and judged their predecessors. ## Findings

**BLOCKING 1. The idolatry canon and its force are missing.** The outline's Length line names idolatry as business that "must not be lost behind" the main issue. Beat 6 gives canon XVI's force: idolatry had spread "through almost all Spain and Gaul". Each bishop, with the local judge, was to hunt it out, destroy it and punish its followers short of death. Masters who allowed it among their slaves were cut off from communion (C6, ACTS 37680-37694, re-checked). The outline check made this omission blocking at the outline stage. Leaving it out lets the Gothic question stand for the whole council, and it softens the record's force.
*Change:* add one sentence to the canons paragraph, for example: "Idolatry, the council said, had spread through almost all Spain and Gaul, so each bishop, with the local judge, was to hunt it out and punish its followers short of death." Do not tie it to the child-killing canon (Do not say).

**BLOCKING 2. The impact is missing: whether the decisions held.** The story ends on the day. The outline keeps "What came of them" items 1-3 and the Saragossa acts in the Connection. The Council shape puts the impact in "whether its decisions held, and who they divided". The listener never hears any of this:
- Three years later, Saragossa allowed chaste former Gothic priests to serve again after a new blessing. It had relics from the Gothic church tested by fire, cut off from the church anyone who hid them, and consecrated again the churches converted bishops had consecrated before their Catholic blessing (I2).
- A year after the council, Seville complained that some bishops were ignoring Toledo's decree on clergy living with women (I5).
- John of Biclaro, the winning side, said the heresy was now cut out "by the roots" (I1).
As told, "every congregation ... now said one creed" stands as the last word, with nothing to show what the council's orders met on the ground.
*Change:* add a short paragraph before the closing one. Tell Saragossa through its acts (re-blessing, relics tested by fire, hidden relics, churches consecrated again), and give the Seville complaint in a clause. Credit John's "by the roots" to John if it is used. To keep the length, cut elsewhere (see minor 1).

Neither blocking error came from room the outline left: the outline requires both. No Do not say line is needed.

**MINOR**

1. **Length.** At 481 words the brief is over the outline's "about 430", and the blocking fixes add words. Make room by tightening the anathema paragraph ("Every word of this comes from the winning side. No Gothic witness survives" can be one sentence), the Sunna dating clause (minor 2), and the closing creed line, which repeats the canon already told in the canons paragraph.
2. **The Sunna dating clause.** "before the council, if John's Sunna is Paul's, as it seems" is lost on one hearing. Say it plainly, for example: "John of Biclaro records a bishop Sunna exiled two years before the council, and he seems to be the same man."
3. **"a notary stood and read".** "Stood" is not in the acts (ACTS 36631-36632: "pronuntiante notario clara voce"). Write "a notary read the king's book of faith aloud, in a clear voice."
4. **"on rebaptism".** The listener has not been told that the Gothic church rebaptized Catholics who joined it (beat 1). Either work in a clause ("on baptizing again those who came over to them"), or say the Gothic church had once rebaptized converts.
5. **"the old king".** Leovigild is never named or tied to Reccared. Name him once as Reccared's father, for example in "Reccared became Catholic in his first year as king, after his father Leovigild died". This also splits the 35-word sentence, which is past the Standard's limit of about 30.
6. **The edict.** "with exile and the loss of property for those who defied it" flattens the graded penalties. Clergy were excommunicated, a noble lost half his property, and a lesser man lost all he had and was exiled (C9). One clause can carry it.
7. **What called the council.** The Council shape asks for it. One clause would do: the king summoned every bishop of his realm, to give thanks for the conversion and restore church order (E1).
