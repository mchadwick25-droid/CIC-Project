## Findings

**BLOCKING 1. The first minute tells only half the heart.** The opening tells what the Goths gave up but not what they took up, or that it was their whole church, king and nobles included. The word "Catholic" and Reccared do not arrive until around ninety seconds in. *Change:* within the first 130 words, say that the Goths' own church, led by its king, gave up that faith for the Catholic faith of their Roman subjects. This can be one clause after the second prayer, for example that the king, his bishops and his nobles were joining the Catholic church of their Roman subjects. Then trim to make room. The Gregory of Tours framing and the "Those few words held the whole quarrel" line can each lose words without loss. The outline left room for this: its Length line asks for both halves of the heart, but its recommended opening carries only the first. *Do not say line to add to the outline:* "Do not let the first 130 words tell only the old prayer and the new: say there that the Goths' church, with its king, gave up its faith for the Catholic faith of its Roman subjects."

**BLOCKING 2. The council's force and its other business are left out.** The story keeps only the transfer of property. It drops the king's edict and its penalties (C9), married clergy parted from their wives (C4), Sunna's refusal and exile by boat (B9, Q3), and the canons beyond the Goths on Jews and on idolatry (C5, C6). Then it quotes John's "cut out by the roots" without the force around it. A listener hears a willing, peaceful, purely doctrinal turn, which is a misleading implication. One dispute also stands for the whole council, against the Council shape. *Change:* restore beat 6 in two or three sentences, and Sunna in one or two, as the outline asks. Use the outline's wording: "punished short of death" for idolatry, "the children of such marriages were to be baptized" for the Jews, and the edict's three penalties as recorded. Sunna is told as Paul of Mérida's hostile account and placed before the council. If space is short, keep the edict and the canons on Jews and idolatry before Sunna. To hold the length near three minutes, cut elsewhere. "Spain's councils said the creed with them at Mass in 653 and 681" and the four city names can each lose words. *Do not say line to add to the outline:* "Do not leave out the force: the edict's penalties (C9) and the canons on Jews and idolatry (C5, C6) must be told, at least one sentence each.
