# Outline check: the Third Council of Toledo (589)

**Verdict: FIX.** Three blocking findings, then minor ones. Everything not named below was opened at its cited lines and holds: setting, the session and tome (E1-E6), the converts' answer and anathemas (E7-E11), the paired bishops (E12), canons II, V, IX and the edict, Saragossa, Narbonne, Seville I, Gregory the Great's letters and Dialogues, Isidore, and the Sunna passage in Paul.

## Quotes: my renderings first, then the comparison

1. ACTS 36683-36685: "So, most holy fathers, these most noble peoples, who have been joined through us to the Lord's gains, I offer to the eternal God through your hands as a holy and pleasing sacrifice." The outline's rendering holds. The text is intact in the OCR.
2. PAUL 12065-12070: "I do not know what penance is. Let it be known to you, then, that I know nothing of penance, and I will never be a Catholic. But I will live by the rite I have lived by, or I will die most gladly for the religion I have kept from my early youth until now." The outline's rendering holds. It is framed as Paul's report, and Paul's hostile frame ("not giving up his old tyranny", 12064-12065) is kept out of the quote. **Timing:** Paul gives no year. The placement before the council holds, but only through John. John's Sunna is exiled in Reccared's second year (JB-M 20328-20330), and John and the acts both put the council in the fourth year (JB-M 20388; ACTS 36600). In Paul, Sunna refuses before he is put on the boat (12063-12073). Matching Paul's Sunna to John's is a sound inference (the same king, the plot, the exile), but it is still an inference.
3. ACTS 36981-36986: "No one doubts that we and our predecessors went astray in the Arian heresy, and that we have now learned the faith of the Gospel and the apostles within the Catholic church." The outline's rendering holds.

## BLOCKING

**B1. The coercion in canons XIV and XVI is cut out of beat 6.** ACTS 37646-37656: the children of a Jew and a Christian wife are to be "taken for baptism". Jews may hold no public office through which they could punish Christians. Christians whom Jews have circumcised or brought into Jewish rites go free, with no price paid. ACTS 37680-37697: every bishop, together with the district's judge, is to search out idolatry and destroy it. They are to punish its followers with whatever penalty they can, short of the death penalty ("salvo discrimine animae"), on pain of excommunication. Masters who do not stamp it out among their slaves are put out of communion. The outline gives only "Jews might not have Christian wives, concubines or slaves" and "idolatry had spread". The template forbids leaving out the record's force.
*Correction to beat 6:* "Jews might not have Christian wives, concubines or slaves. Children of such marriages were to be baptized, and Jews were barred from public office (C5, full canon ACTS 37646-37656). Idolatry had spread 'through almost all Spain and Gaul'. Each bishop, with the local judge, was to hunt it out, destroy it and punish its followers short of death; masters who allowed it among their slaves were excommunicated (C6, ACTS 37680-37697)." Extend C5 and C6 on the sheet to these lines. Add to **Do not say:** "the council forced the Jews to convert" → "the council ordered the children of Jewish-Christian marriages baptized"; it ordered no general conversion.

**B2. The later Spanish use of "and from the Son" rests on an editor, but the held primary text carries it.** In the ACTS file itself, Toledo VIII (era 691 = 653, ACTS 43163) gives the creed in full, "as we profess and say with one voice at the solemnities of the Mass" (ACTS 43515-43517), with "ex Patre et Filio procedentem" (43537). Toledo XII (681) does the same: "in missarum solemniis" at 48222, and the words at 48245-48246. The sheet's Library sweep never opened the later councils in its own main file.
*Correction:* rewrite I4 and impact item 2 as follows. "Canon II put the creed in the people's mouths at every Eucharist (C1, C2). Sixty-four years later, the bishops at Toledo VIII wrote out the creed they said 'with one voice' at Mass, and it has 'from the Father and the Son' (ACTS 43515-43537; again at Toledo XII in 681, ACTS 48222-48246). Documented. Whether the creed of 589 itself carried the words cannot be seen in the held text, which gives only the opening words. A modern editor, Henry Percival, says it did (NPNF14 13697-13714). Dominant Modern Reconstruction (editor)." Percival's own claim is "the earliest date at which we find the words actually introduced into the Creed", not "the first creed to carry" them. Change the Do-not-say line to: "a modern editor says the creed of 589 already carried the words, and the councils of Toledo after it say it at Mass with them."

**B3. "Our catholic faith" is given as the Homoian synod's own name for its faith, but that depends on the reading.** The manuscripts read "ad nostram catholicam fidem" (JB-PL 64853). Mommsen emended this against them to "a nostra catholica fide" ("scripsi", JB-M 20210 apparatus). In his text, the phrase is John's own name for the Nicene faith. The sheet calls Mommsen "the clean checking copy" and treats the PL text as a variant, but never says the reading it relies on is the manuscripts' against the critical editor. As the Goths' self-description, it is Contested, not Documented.
*Correction to beat 1:* drop "called their faith 'our catholic faith' and the other". Write instead: "John of Biclaro, a Catholic, reports that they ruled converts 'from the Roman religion' need not be baptized again." Add to **Do not say:** "the Goths called their own church catholic" → leave it out; the manuscripts and the critical edition disagree on who says "our catholic faith".

## MINOR

- **Beats 1 and 5, "580":** Mommsen's margin reads "580?" (JB-M 20152). Write "in Leovigild's twelfth year, about 580".
- **Beat 1, Agilan:** he said the old bishops were wrong to assert the Son equal to the Father (GT-E 10202-10204). Use that, not "the Son is less than the Father".
- **Beat 3, "the noble Segga":** John gives him no rank, and Paul does not name him. Write "Segga". Sunna's "of Mérida" comes from Paul, not John.
- **Beat 3:** Sunna's exile (John) and the small boat (Paul) are one event. Write it once, so it does not read as two.
- **Impact 1, Saragossa:** canon III re-consecrates only the churches a converted bishop consecrated "before he had received the blessing from a Catholic bishop" (ACTS 34319-34324). Keep that qualifier. Canon I also removed from office the priests and deacons who had not kept a chaste life (34294-34297). Canon II cut off from the church anyone who hid relics (34311-34313). Add to **Do not say:** "the Arian relics were burned" → "tested by fire" ("igne probentur").
- **Impact 1 heading:** make it "The Gothic church in Spain ended, as its opponents recorded it". Saragossa, three years on, was still dealing with hidden relics.
- **Impact 4, Argimund:** John calls it a palace plot in a time of "quiet peace" (JB-M 20427-20429) and ties it to no faith. Under "What came of them" it implies a link to the council. Move it to **Leave out**. If it is kept, add that his fellow plotters were put to death.
- **Connection, "a Gothic and a Catholic bishop":** Tuy, Lugo and Porto lie in Gallaecia, the former Suevic kingdom. The acts call these men converts "from the Arian heresy" (ACTS 37172), not Goths. Write "a bishop converted from the Arian church and a Catholic bishop".
- **Impact 3:** the Seville decree concerns clergy living with women (ACTS 59273-59291). Say so.
- **Impact 5:** Gregory says three immersions or one are both lawful, and advises one in Spain only because of the heretics (GG1 32604-32612). Keep that qualifier, or drop the item.
- **Quote 3's job:** "decessores" means predecessors in office, not ancestors. Write "their predecessors", not "forebears".
- **Quote 1 citation:** the quote runs from ACTS 36683, not 36684.
- **Do not say, add:** "Reccared converted at the council" → he became Catholic in the tenth month of his first year (JB-M 20313), and the council made it public. Also: "pagan child sacrifice" → canon XVII is parents killing their children so as not to have more (ACTS 37703-37712). Do not tie it to the idolatry canon.
- **Length:** 3 minutes is justified for this council, but seven beats and six impact items will not fit in about 430 words. Mark impact items 5 and 6 (Rome, Leander) as one clause each or optional, so the canons' coercion and the Connection keep their room.
- **Spoken name:** "the Goths' own church" and "the Catholic church of Spain" do not mislead. Keep them.
- **Library sweep:** the corpus-map buckets match the sheet, and the count of 59 files naming Reccared reproduces. The one miss is B2.
