## 1. Setting

**S1. Date and place in the acts.** ACTS 36600-36602. Own words. Documented.
> "anno regnante quarto gloriosissimo atque piissimo et Deo fidelissimo domino Recaredo rege, die viri. [VIII.] iduum majarum era pcxxvir. [DCXXVII.] haec sancta synodus habita est in civitate regia Toletana ab episcopis totius Hispaniae vel Galliae"

BRUNS 14612-14614 reads the date cleanly: "die VIH. Iduum Majarum era DCXXVII". The eighth day before the Ides of May is 8 May by the Roman count. Era 627 is the Spanish era; NPNF and Barmby give 589 (GG1 32579-32583 note: "formally renounced Arianism and adopted the Catholic Creed at the Council of Toledo, a.d. 589"). The year 589 is the editors' reckoning: Documented as an editors' conversion, not a year any ancient witness writes.

**S2. "Gaul" means the Visigothic part of Gaul.** BRUNS 14650-14652, editor's note (Mansi). Editor. Widely Accepted.
> "Legendum Galliae, non Galliciae ut in quibusdam, quia Gallia Narbonensis in regno Gothorum continebatur, nen integra quidem sed Septimania duntaxat."

Gallaecia (the north-west, the former Suevic kingdom) is named separately in canon II: ACTS 37356-37357 "per omnes ecclesias Hispaniae, Galliae-ye] [vel] Gallaeciae".

**S3. Mommsen's marginal year is 590.** JB-M 20388: "-.90? ANNO VIII MAVRICII IMPERATORIS, QVI EST RECCAREDI REGIS IIII ANNVS." Editor's margin with a question mark. Note only for the card: the editors disagree by a year on John's dating. Contested (editors).

**S4. The heading: sixty-two bishops; the Arian heresy condemned.** ACTS 36590-36595. Own words. Documented.
> "CONCILIUM TOLETANUM TERTIUM / SEXAGINTA DUORUM EPISCOPORUM, / IN QUO ARIANA HAERESIS IN HISPANIA. CONDEMNATUR."

**S5. Tradition names.** Census (`cic-website/data/world-census.json`, field `informalName`): "The Church of Visigothic Spain" (nicene-visigothic-iberia); "The Gothic, Vandal and Lombard Churches" (gothic-and-vandal-homoian-christianity); "The Homoian Churches" (homoian-arian-christianity); the council itself: short name "Toledo 589", informal "Toledo III". Every held ancient witness calls the Gothic church "Arian". No held source in this sweep explains the word "Homoian".

## 2. Before the council: the Homoian kingdom (what called it)

**B1. Leovigild's synod of Homoian bishops at Toledo, his twelfth year.** JB-M 20154-20160 (JB-PL 64850-64857 agrees). Contemporary (John was alive and in exile, see W1), hostile. Documented that John reports it.
> "Leovegildus rex in urbera [urbem] Toletanam synodum episcoporum sectae Arrianac [Arrianae] congregat et antiquam haeresem novello errore emendat, diccns de Romana religione a nostra catholica fidc vcnientes non debere baptizari, scd tantumraodo per nianus inipositionem ct comnumionis praeceptione poUui et gloriam patri pcr filium in spiritu sancto dare. per hanc crgo seductionem plurimi nostrorum cupiditate potius quam impulsione in Arrianum dogma dcclinant."

PL reads "ad nostram catholicam fidem venientes". The point for a witness reading: the Homoian synod called its own faith "our catholic faith" and the Nicene faith "the Roman religion"; it dropped rebaptism for converts; its prayer of praise was "glory to the Father through the Son in the Holy Spirit". The last sentence is John's hostile verdict on the converts' motives.

**B2. The council condemns that same booklet.** ACTS 36965-36971 (anathema XVI). Own words (Gothic bishops). Documented.
> "Quicumque libellum detestabilem duodecimo anno Leovigildi regis a. nobis editum, in quo continetur Romanorum ad haeresem Arianam transductio, et in quo gloria Patri per Filium in Spiritu Sancto malé a nobis instituta continetur; hunc libellum si quis pro vero habuerit, anathema sit in aeternum."

Note "a nobis editum": the Gothic bishops own the booklet as theirs.

## 3. The council: events

**E1. The king's summons and purpose.** ACTS 36603-36610. Own words. Documented.
> "Quum pro fidei suae sinceritate idem gloriosissimus princeps omnes regiminis? sui pontifices in unum convenire mandasset, ut tam de ejus conversione quàm de gentis Gothorum innovatione in Domino exultarent ... Non incognitum reor esse vobis ... quód propter instaurandam disciplinae ecclesiasticae formam ad nostrae vos serenitatis praesentiam devocaverim: et quia decursis retro temporibus haeresis imminens in tota ecclesia catholica agere synodica negotia denegabat"

The king says heresy had kept the Catholic church from holding synods. John: "praecepto principis Reccarodi congregatur" (JB-M 20392).

**E2. Three-day fast; the session.** ACTS 36616-36620. Own words. Documented.
> "triduanum est exinde praedicatum jejunium, sed quum die octavo iduum majarum in unum coetum Dei sacerdotes adessent et oratione praemissa unusquisque sacerdotum competenti loco resedisset, ecce in medio eorum adfuit serenissimus princeps"

## 4. The council: decisions (canons and edict)

**C1. The creed before the Lord's Prayer: the king's reason.** ACTS 37255-37269 and 37284-37288. Own words (king). Documented.
> "ut propter roborandam gentis nostrae novellam conversionem omnes Hispaniarum et Galliae ecclesiae hanc regulam servent: ut omnes sacrificii tempore ante communionem corporis Christi vel sanguinis juxta orientalium partium morem unanimiter clara voce sacratissimum fidei recenseant symbolum, ut primüm populi quid credulitate teneant fateantur et sic corda fide purificata ad Christi corpus et sanguinem percipiendum exhibeant ... nec se quisquam jam de ignorantia fidei excusabit a culpa, quando universorum ore cognoscit quid catholica teneat et credat ecclesia."

**C2. Canon II.** ACTS 37353-37364. Own words. Documented.
> "consultu piissimi et gloriosissimi domini Recaredi regis sancta constituit synodus: ut per omnes ecclesias Hispaniae, Galliae-ye] Gallaeciae secundüm formam orientalium ecclesiarum, concilii Constantinopolitani hoc est centum quinquaginta episcoporum symbolum fidei recitetur, ut priusquam dominica dicatur oratio voce clara a populo praedicetur"

The held text gives the creeds only by their first words ("Credimus in unum Deum Patrem omnipotentem: et cetera", ACTS 36745-36760, 37073-37086). Whether the recited creed carried "and from the Son" cannot be checked in the held Latin. Percival says it did (NPNF14 13713-13714, "The creed of Constantinople as recited contained the words 'and from the Son'") and that 589 is "the earliest date at which we find the words actually introduced into the Creed" (13697-13699). Editor. Dominant Modern Reconstruction (Percival), not visible in the held text. In the held text the words stand in the tome (E4) and anathema III (E9).

**C3. Canon IX: the Arian churches.** ACTS 37526-37534. Own words. Documented.
> "ut ecclesiae quae fuerunt in haeresi Ariana nunc autem sunt catholicae, ad eos episcopos cum suis rebus pertineant, ad quos parochiae ipsae in quibus ecclesiae fundatae sunt pertinere videntur."

**C4. Canon V: the converts' married clergy.** ACTS 37443-37455. Own words. Documented.
> "Compertum est a sancto concilio episcopos, presbyteres et diacones venientes ex haerese, carnali adhuc desiderio uxoribus copulari: ne ergo de cetero fiat ... non sub uno conclavi maneant"

The Homoian clergy had wives. The canon orders them to live apart from them. Its last clause: women kept by clergy "ab episcopis venundatae pretium ipsum pauperibus erogetur" (37462-37464): sold by the bishops, the price given to the poor (a variant in note 36, 37470: placed in a convent instead).

**C5. Canon XIV: Jews.** ACTS 37646-37656. Own words. Documented.
> "ut judaeis non liceat christianas habere uxores vel concubinas neque mancipium christianum in usus proprios comparare; sed et si qui filii ex tali conjugio nati sunt assumendos esse ad baptisma; nulla officia publica eos opus est agere"

**C6. Canon XVI: idolatry.** ACTS 37680-37684. Own words. Documented.
> "Quoniam pené per omnem Hispaniam sive Galliam idolatriae sacrilegium inolevit"

**C7. Canon XVII: parents killing children.** ACTS 37703-37708. Own words. Documented.
> "ut in quibusdam Hispaniae partibus filios suos parentes interimant fornicationis avidi"

**C8. Other canons, in brief.** Own words. Documented. I (old canons and Roman bishops' letters in force), ACTS 37319-37336; III-IV (church property; a parish church may become a monastery), 37411-37435; VII (Scripture read at bishops' tables), 37502-37508; X (widows and virgins not forced to marry), 37543-37555; XI-XII (penance by the old canons; penitents shorn or change dress), 37576-37616; XVIII (one synod a year, with judges and royal agents present, 1 November), 37741-37770; XX (bishops not to burden their clergy), 37806-37825; XXII (the religious buried with psalms only, not the usual funeral song), 37851-37875; XXIII (no dancing and shameful songs at saints' feasts), 37890-37897.

**C9. The king's edict and its penalties.** ACTS 37905-37927 and 38016-38027. Own words (king). Documented.
> "ut si qua definita sunt in hoc sancto concilio habito in urbe Toletana anno regni nostri feliciter quarto, nulli contemnere liceat, nullus praeterire praesumat"
> "si episcopus, presbyter, diaconus aut clericus fuerit, ab omni concilio excommunicationi subjaceat: si vero laicus fuerit et honestioris loci persona est, medietatem facultatum suarum amittat fisci viribus profuturam; si vero inferioris loci persona est, amissione rerum suarum mulctatus in exilium deputetur."

## 5. After the council and impact (heaviest first)

**I1. The end of the Gothic Homoian church in Spain, as the winners recorded it.**
- John, JB-M 20408-20410. Contemporary. Documented that John says so.
> "in praesenti vero sancta Toletana synodo Arrii perfidia post longas catholicoruni necss atque innocentium strages ita radicitus amputata insistentc principe memorato Reccaredo rege, ut ulterius non pullulet catholica ubique pace data ecclesiis."
- John's comparison, JB-M 20401-20407: "renovans temporibus nostris antiquum principem Constantinum Magnum sanctam synodum Nicaenam sua illustrasse praesentia, nec non et Marciamnn [Marcianum] ... siquidem in Nicacna urbc haeresis Arriana et initium sumpsit et damnationem meruit radicibus non amputatis".
- Isidore, ISID 119262-119270 (History §53). Later. Documented: "abdicans cum omnibus suis perfidiam, quam hucusque £ 5thorum [Gothorum] populus, Ario docente, didicerat". And ISID 119327-119329: "In'ipsis enim regni sui exerdiis catholicam fidem adeptus totius Gothiesx gentis populos, inoliti erroris labe deserta, ad cu'!um rectae fidei revocat."
- No text by the Visigothic Homoian church itself is held. Its own words reach us only through hostile witnesses (B1, B2, B5, B9, E8). Fredegar's book-burning (B8) is the only held report of what happened to its books: late and alone. Inferential-Thin.

**I2. What became of the Homoian clergy, churches and relics: the Council of Saragossa, Reccared's seventh year.** ACTS 34271-34325. Own words (a later council, era 630). Documented.
> "ut presbyteri qui ex haeresi Ariana ad sanctam catholicam ecclesiam conversi sunt, qui sanctam et puram fidem atque castissimam tenuerint vitam, accepta denuo benedictione presbyteratus sancté et puré ministrare debeant: ceteri veró ... ab officio depositi sint in clero." (34288-34297)
> "ut reliquiae in quibuscumque locis de Ariana haeresi inventae fuerint prolatae a sacerdotibus, in quorum ecclesiis reperiuntur, pontificibus praesentatae igne probentur" (34307-34311)
> "ut episcopi de Ariana haeresi venientes, si quas ecclesias sub nomine catholicae fidei consecraverint, necdum benedictione percepta a catholico sacerdote consecrentur denuo." (34319-34324)

**I3. Plots after the council: Argimund.** JB-M 20428-20466. Contemporary. Documented that John says so.
> "Argimundus adversus Reccaredum regem tyrannidem assumere cupiens ... socii eius impiam machinationem confessi condigna sunt ultione interfecti. ipsc autom Argimundus ... primum verheribus interrogatus. deinde turpiter decalvatus, post haec dextra amputata exemplum omnibus in Toletana urbe asino sedens pompizando dedit et docuit famulos dominis non esse superbos."

John does not say Argimund was Arian. Do not make him so.

**I4. The creed at the Eucharist, and "and from the Son".** C1-C2. Percival, NPNF14 13623-13628 (editor, citing Hefele): the Goths "were required to sign the creed with the addition. From this time it became for Spain the accepted form, and was so recited at the Eighth Council of Toledo in 653, and again in 681 at the Twelfth Council of Toledo." Editor. Widely Accepted for later Spanish use; the 589 creed text itself is as in C2.

**I5. Did the decrees hold? Councils that followed.**
- Narbonne, the same year (Reccared's fourth), ACTS 60864-60868. Own words. Documented: "in urbe Narbona, secundüm quod sancta synodus per ordinationem gloriosissimi domini nostri Reccaredi regis in urbe Toletana finivit, die calendarum novembrium Deo auspice in unum convenimus". The bishops of Gaul meet on 1 November, as canon XVIII required.
- Seville I, Reccared's fifth year, era 628, ACTS 59276-59281 and 59299-59305. Own words, Leander signing. Documented: "quia quidam episcoporum non observantes nuper editum concilii Toletani decretum minüs solliciti in subjectis existunt". Some bishops were not keeping the Toledo decree on clergy and women.
- Saragossa (I2).

**I6. Rome's welcome.**
- Gregory to Leander, GG1 32576-32579 (Barmby: I.43, dated 591 in his note, 32583-32584). Contemporary. Documented: "I cannot, however, at all fully express in words my joy on having learnt that our common son, the most glorious King Rechared, has been converted with most entire devotion to the Catholic faith". The letter speaks of the conversion, not the council.
- Gregory to Reccared, GG2 3872-3875 (IX.122; Barmby dates it 598-9, 3857). Contemporary. Documented: "on hearing of the power of a new miracle in our days, to wit that the whole nation of the Goths has through thy Excellency been brought over from the error of Arian heresy to the firmness of a right faith".
- The letter in Reccared's name, GG2 2254-2266 (IX.61). Barmby: "The genuineness of this letter is considered doubtful." Contested. Its "three years passed" (2271) dates nothing firmly.
- Gregory on a law of Reccared against the Jews, GG2 3929-3934: "your Excellency having issued a certain ordinance against the perfidy of the Jews, those to whom it related attempted to bend the rectitude of your mind by offering a sum of money; which your Excellency scorned". Documented that Gregory says so; that this is canon XIV is not stated.
- Later royal law, VCODE 21925-21931: "The authority of the law promulgated by our lord and predecessor, Recaredus, declaring that Christian slaves shall under no circumstances remain in the power of Jews". Later; Documented that a later law cites Reccared's law. Its link to canon XIV is not stated.

**I7. Leander, as his brother remembered him.** ISID 122769-122776 (On Famous Men). Later, a generation; Leander's younger brother (ISID 20310 heading "Sanctus Leander, Isidori frater" is an editor's index). Documented that Isidore says so.
> "Leander ... ut, et fide ejus atque industria populi gentis Gothorum ab Ariana insania ad fidem catholicam reverlerentur"

**I8. Leander's homily for the conversion (attributed).** ACTS 38309-38311 (heading), 38343 (note), 38326-38339 (text). Attributed: the heading names him; the editors' note says two manuscripts lack it. Own words if genuine. Contested as to authorship.
> "Homilia? Sancti Leandri in laudem ecclesiae ob comversionem gentis post concilium et confirmationem canonum edita."
> "61 Deest haec homilia in A. et E. 3. desumpta est ex codice BR."
> "et quorum asperitatem quondam gemebamus, de eorum nunc gaudemus credulitate. Ergo materia gaudii nostri tribulationis praeteritae occasio fuit. Gemebamus dum gravaremur, dum exprobraremur"
Also ACTS 38484-38487: "ut quia superbia linguarum diversitate ab unione gentes separaverat, eas rursum gremio germanitatis colligeret caritas". The homily also calls heresy a concubine, not a wife (38559-38560): hostile imagery; not for the story.

## 7. Where sources disagree or come late

- **Number of bishops.** 62: ACTS 36593; JB-PL 65057 + 65083 ("episcoporum nu-" ... "mero lxii"). 72: JB-M 20392 ("numcro LXXII"). The signatures count 62 in person (E13).
- **How Reccared turned.** By "reason rather than command" (John, B8); by a debate of bishops and a test of miracles (Gregory of Tours, B8); by Leander, on his father's deathbed charge (Gregory the Great, B8); by secret baptism, then the gathering and burning of Arian books and a general baptism (Fredegar, B8). The acts' own description (tome, B8) speaks of anointing or the laying on of hands, not baptism: Fredegar conflicts with the acts and with canon XV against rebaptism.
- **Whose words the tome is.** The king's voice in the acts; Leander's, per Dalton citing Labbe (E3). Editor only.
- **Leander's homily.** Attributed; two manuscripts lack it (I8).
- **"And from the Son" in the creed.** Held text abbreviates the creed (C2).
- **The year.** 589 by the editors' conversion of era 627; Mommsen's margin puts John's entry at 590? (S3).
- **All witnesses are Catholic.** The Homoian church is heard only through its opponents.
