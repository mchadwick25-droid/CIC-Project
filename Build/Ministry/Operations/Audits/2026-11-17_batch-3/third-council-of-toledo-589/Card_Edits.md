# Card edits: the Third Council of Toledo (589)

No existing statement was changed. Everything from the original card is word for word.

## Added
- `briefLength`: "about 3 minutes 20 seconds" (the brief has 501 words; the Nicaea card gives 549 words as 3 minutes 40 seconds, about 150 words a minute).
- `quotationOriginals`: eight quotations, each modern wording exactly as the brief has it, the printed wording as the file has it (OCR and line-end hyphens kept), the true readings in `uncertain`. No `secondReading`. Lines re-read in the files:
  1. "from the Father and the Son": ACTS 36661-36662.
  2. "by reason rather than command": John of Biclaro, Mommsen, 20314-20316.
  3. "I will most gladly die for the religion I have held from my youth until now": Paul of Mérida, 12068-12070.
  4. "that came from Arius": ACTS 36900-36901.
  5. "No one doubts that we and those before us went astray ...": ACTS 36981-36986.
  6. "after long killings of Catholics and slaughter of the innocent": Mommsen 20408-20409.
  7. "through almost all Spain and Gaul": ACTS 37680-37681.
  8. "our people's new conversion": ACTS 37255-37258.
- `briefReferences`: nine entries (the acts in three parts, John of Biclaro, Isidore, Dalton's note on 586, Paul of Mérida, Saragossa, Seville I).
- `sources.writtenLater`: Paul of Mérida, Lives of the Fathers of Mérida (Latin, Migne PL 80), the file quotation 3 cites. Every other cited file was already listed.

## Left alone
- issue[1]: "what it called 'nostram catholicam fidem'". The manuscripts read "ad nostram catholicam fidem"; Mommsen emends to "a nostra catholica fide" (JB-M apparatus, 20210). The card follows the manuscripts and the brief does not say it. Contested, not wrong.
- attended[1]: "Two places in the printed text are damaged." The source sheet found one damaged see (Frosclus, ACTS 38210); the second place was not found. Not found, left in place.
- Brief against card: sixty-two bishops, the anathema numbers, canons II, IX, XIV and XVI, the fifteenth on rebaptism, and the 586 and 587 years agree; nothing in the card contradicts the brief.
