## One hearing

- **Who or what:** A council of sixty-two bishops at Toledo, the Visigothic capital, in May 589, with King Reccared among them.
- **What happened:** The Goths' own church cursed the faith "that came from Arius" and passed into the Catholic church: its churches handed over, its clergy parted from their wives, its priests re-blessed and relics tested at Saragossa. Sunna refused and was exiled; other canons, backed by the king's edict, ruled on Jews and idolatry.
- **Why the connection matters:** Two churches became one, and every congregation, Gothic and Roman, was to say one creed before communion.
