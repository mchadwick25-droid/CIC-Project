## One hearing

- **Who:** Arius, a parish priest of Alexandria in the early 300s, who taught that the Son of God had a beginning.
- **What happened:** He clashed with his bishop around 318 and was removed, set his teaching in verse, was condemned at Nicaea in 325 and exiled, and his books were ordered burned. Taken back by the emperor, he was refused communion in Constantinople and died before the day he was to be received.
- **Why the connection matters:** Nicaea's creed curses his circle's own phrases as what the church denies, and "Arian" became his opponents' name for bishops who refused it, saying they had received him, not followed him.
