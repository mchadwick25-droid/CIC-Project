No Do not say line is needed, because this is not a content error.

**MINOR 2. The flash-forward order.** "The widows of the fallen" and "the coming battle" arrive before the battle is told, so a listener briefly loses the sequence. *Change:* either open the Vahan paragraph with a plain time marker ("Thirty years after Vardan's death..."), or move the Vahan paragraph after Avarayr. If it moves, the story would end on the settlement, which the Standard allows.

**MINOR 3. Who tried to break the door.** "When Persian troops tried to break open a church door, the people stoned them." In Elishe it is "the Mogbed himself, with the Magi, and a strong force", and the stones hit "the heads of the Magi, and of the Mogbed" (N 2648-2659). *Change:* "when the chief Magus and his troops tried to break open a church door, the people stoned them."

**MINOR 4. The yielding ends on the defenders' framing alone.** The brief closes the pretence with Elishe's Vardan: "we deceived ... He knows we held firm". It leaves out the counter-voices the outline carries: Christians in the Persian camp scorned it as denial (L12 80605-80613), and Lazar's Vardan says he "denied" Christ in appearance and meant to repent (L12 91184-91194). Vardan's tears in Lazar keep this from misleading, so it is not blocking. If length allows after finding 1, one clause would balance it.

**MINOR 5. The church's later memory is thin.** The martyr shape puts the impact in how the church remembered him. The brief carries that only through Lazar's "title of martyr" and Vahan's rising. It leaves out the outline's second impact item: "saint Vartan", the later hymns for the Vardanans, and the feast. The outline's named connection, Vahan, is told in full, so this is optional.

**MINOR 6. The 38-word sentence.** The opening sentence runs the Lazar lead-in straight into the quote. *Change:* end the narrator's sentence at "saw another meaning." and let the quote stand as its own sentence.
