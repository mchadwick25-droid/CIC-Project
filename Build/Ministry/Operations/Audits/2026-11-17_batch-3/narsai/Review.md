## The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Narsai, a Persian-born teacher of the Church of the East. He headed the school at Edessa, in the Roman Empire, where he taught Scripture by Theodore of Mopsuestia's commentaries.
- **What happened:** Accused of heresy and of siding with Persia, and warned he would be burned, he fled by night to Nisibis, just inside Persia. There Bishop Barsauma asked him to found a school, and students followed him from Edessa.
- **Why the connection matters:** The teaching condemned at Edessa carried on across the border. The school at Nisibis outlasted him under his kinsman and his rules, and became where the Persian church's schools grew.

**One-hearing test alone: PASS.** All three lines came from one hearing, and nothing reads as a children's book or talks down.

Where I got lost:
- Paragraph 1, last sentence ("Nisibis, just inside Persia, where Christians under the shahs formed the Church of the East"): the left-out verb and three ideas in one sentence are hard to take in at speed.
- Paragraph 4, "In 489 the Persian school at Edessa was rooted out": a listener cannot tell this is the school Narsai had led, or who did it.
- The brief never says plainly that Nisibis became the Church of the East's school. The listener has to work it out from the quote.

## Findings

**B1. BLOCKING: a conflict the outline keeps is missing.** The outline's last line ("Kept, not left out: the charges against him at Edessa and at the Persian court, and the threat of fire… told plainly") and beat 6 keep the denunciation before the Persian king. Leaving it out removes half the record's conflict. It also leaves a misleading impression: the only charge the listener hears is that he sided with Persia, and Persia then appears simply as a welcoming refuge. The record has him suspected on both sides. What to change: add one sentence in the History's voice, after the school is founded. For example: "Even in Persia, Barhadbeshabba tells, two of his own students later denounced him to the king as an enemy of the Persian empire." Make room for it by cutting elsewhere (see M1). Do not add the king's change of mind as fact. It is the History's alone.
- *Do not say line to add to the outline* (the "kept" line sits at the end of **Leave out**, where a writer can miss it): "Leaving out the charge at the Persian court, or the threat of fire. Say: the History tells that students later denounced him to the Persian king as an enemy of Persia."

**M1. MINOR: over length.** 420 words against about 375. Trim to fit B1 and still land near 375. The places to cut are the clauses that repeat: "Their church called Theodore the Interpreter" is not used again, and paragraph 5 lists three things in one sentence.

**M2. MINOR: the 489 closure is unclear and has no agent.** "In 489 the Persian school at Edessa was rooted out" does not tell the listener that this was the school Narsai had led, or who closed it. The outline gives the agent through Simeon (Bishop Cyrus and the emperor Zeno; Bar Hebraeus editors 3743-3747). Say, for example, "In 489 the school he had left at Edessa was closed for good; his enemy Simeon says by the bishop and the emperor's order." Keep it beside the students' arrival, not as their cause (Do not say).

**M3. MINOR: a run of one-act sentences.** Paragraph 4, from "In 489" to "written rules", is three short sentences that each tell one act. Join them with time or contrast, as the Standard asks.

**M4. MINOR: the geography sentence is hard to hear.** Paragraph 1's last sentence leaves out its verb and carries three ideas. Split it. For example: "Edessa lay in the Roman Empire. Nisibis lay just inside Persia, where Christians under the shahs formed the Church of the East."

**M5. MINOR: the connection depends on the quote.** Apart from Barhadbeshabba's quote, the brief never says that the Nisibis school served the Church of the East. One narrator clause, drawn from the outline's Thread and What came of them 1 ("the school that trained the Church of the East's teachers"; "institutions that came from it now exist in several places", Cause 17464-17465), would let the connection land before the quote.
