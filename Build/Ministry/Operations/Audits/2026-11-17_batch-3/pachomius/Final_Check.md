## One hearing

- **Who or what:** Pachomius, an Egyptian conscript turned monk on the upper Nile in the fourth century.
- **What happened:** Moved by Christians' kindness in prison, he built at Tabennisi a fellowship of monks under one rule, shared clothes, food and work, with a house for his sister's women; he died in a plague about 346-348, and his communities grew to thousands.
- **Why the connection matters:** Jerome's Latin carried his rule west, where Benedict of Aniane set it among the rules beneath Benedict's Rule.
