**Phrase:** "the Arian heresy" (the adjective, as a one-word label)

**Rendering:** **Arian**

**Note:** "Arian" means "of Arius", and it is Athanasius's hostile label for his opponents' teaching. The rendering keeps "heresy" because it is his verdict, not a neutral description. In these lines, "his death" refers back to a death that these lines do not name.

**Phrase:** "the two" (τὸ δύο), the formula Dioscorus refuses, as a one-word label

**Rendering:** **"Two"** (set against the formula he accepts: "From two")

**Note:** The whole meaning is the contrast between "from two" (ἐκ δύο), which he accepts, and plain "two", which he rejects. The rendering has to keep both formulas side by side. The Greek leaves out the noun. These lines do not say "natures", so anything that adds it is the reader's addition from context, not from the text.
