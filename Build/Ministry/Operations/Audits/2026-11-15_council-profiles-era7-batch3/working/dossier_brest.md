# Dossier: the Union of Brest, October 1596 (the two synods of Brest-Litovsk)

Research dossier for the era 7 council profile `union-of-brest-1596`. Rivers (copied from the brief): Greek East, Latin West.

The subject is two meetings held in the same town on the same days, 6 to 10 October 1596 (old style): the union synod called by Metropolitan Mykhail Rahoza, which accepted communion with Rome; and the Orthodox synod in a private house, which deposed him and his bishops. Each side's own documents are held. Mark wants it told fairly to both. All paths are under `cic/texts/`. Line numbers are file lines. "(my tr.)" marks my English. OCR spellings are kept inside quotes. Old-style (Julian) dates are the sources' own; I have converted nothing.

## 2. Candidate opening scenes

**A. Saturday morning, St Nicholas church (union side; Skarga's account, RZEP l.1043-1092).**
"Nazaiutrz w Sobotę ... przez dzwony y głosy, z wielką liczbą panow świeckich y ludu pospolitego" (l.1043-1058): on Saturday the bishops in vestments went into St Nicholas, announced by bells, with many lords and common people. They sat "po iedney stronie posłowie Papiescy y Krolewscy, po drugiey Metropolit z Włádykami" (l.1070-1072: on one side the papal and royal envoys, on the other the metropolitan with the bishops). "wystąpił wielebny Hermogen Archiepiscop Połocki, maiąc list na pargaminie podpisany y pieczętowany, wstąpiwszy na wyższe mieysce czytał głośną y znaczną mową" (l.1086-1091; my tr.: the reverend Hermogen, archbishop of Polotsk, came forward with a letter on parchment, signed and sealed, went up to a higher place and read it in a loud, clear voice). After it, "rzućili się do pocałowaniń Biskupi kośćioła Zachodnego, do Biskupow kośćioła Wschodnego" (l.1252-1254: the bishops of the Western church threw themselves to kiss the bishops of the Eastern church), and at the Latin church of St Mary they sang Te Deum (l.1262-1266); "wszytek lud wierny, hoynie łzy wylewal" (l.1274-1275: all the faithful people shed tears freely). Strongest single scene; it is the winners' telling.

**B. The same days, a stone house in Brest (Orthodox side; the Orthodox synod's own account in RIB7 and the newsletter in AZR4).**
AZR4 106 I: on 6 October the clergy, knights and town envoys gathered "до господы княжат и его милости воеводы Кіевского ... до каменицы пана Райского" (l.14284-14286: to the lodging of the prince, the voivode of Kyiv, the stone house of Pan Raisky) and sent to the metropolitan to ask where to meet; he put them off (l.14286-14293). The Orthodox scriptum: "Трывало тое ожиданье наше ажь ку нешпору" (RIB7 l.59744-59745: our waiting lasted until vespers). Their envoys looked for the metropolitan "и въ господѣ его, и въ церкви, и на иншихъ мѣстцахъ ... знайти не могли" (l.59825-59827: in his lodging, in the church and elsewhere, and could not find him). The newsletter's version: the metropolitan and bishops, warned, "крылися у господѣ ксендза бискупа Луцкого" (AZR4 l.14304-14305: were hiding at the lodging of the Latin bishop of Lutsk). Good opening for fairness: a room of people waiting for a shepherd who does not come.

**C. Thursday, the metropolitan's lodging (both sides tell it).**
Skarga: within half an hour the king's envoys learned that envoys from "koło iakieś z swym Marszałkiem" (a circle with its own marshal) were already in Potii's house, and found them reading from a paper that blamed the metropolitan "iż z Rzymiany przestaie" (that he consorts with the Romans) and threatened him "wyklinanim y złożenim z stolice Metropolickiey" (RZEP l.402-422). AZR4 106 I tells the same visit from the other side: the envoys read their message "веселое поселство" (an unwelcome-sounding "cheerful embassy", ironic), asked for the paper back, "одно ей имъ не дано" (it was not given them), and came back without an answer (l.14365-14370).

**D. 10 October, Rahoza's letter (the signer's own regret).**
"около соединенія нашого съ костеломъ Римскимъ, которое есмо заварли въ суботу, девятого дня Октебра, ачъ, въ правдѣ, съ великимъ жалемъ нашимъ и обливаньемъ слезъ, же-сьмы порозорваны съ братьею нашою милою" (AZR4 l.14934-14938; my tr.: about our union with the Roman church, which we concluded on Saturday, the ninth of October, though in truth with great grief and shedding of tears, that we are torn from our dear brethren). A strong close or a turn, not an opening.

## 3. Issue (each side with its witness)

- **The union side's reasons, in its own act**: the Church stands "на единомъ Петрѣ, яко на камени" (AZR4 l.13824-13825); the patriarchs of Constantinople left the union of Florence and "въ моцъ поганьскую Турецкую впали", whence simony and the spread of heresies "ижъ ся ереси розсѣяли и всю правѣ Русь опановали" (l.13867-13876); so they sent Potii and Terletsky to Clement VIII asking to be received "заховуючи намъ обряды и церемоніѣ церквей Восточныхъ" (l.13893-13907). Same in THEIN3 l.23348-23410 and RZEP l.1093-1240.
- **Rahoza's own grievance against the patriarchs** (letter of 10 Oct): the Greek elders "заколотили святокупствомъ, выклинаньемъ, обнаженьемъ нашое владзы, а инымъ простымъ холопомъ подавали достоенства" (AZR4 l.14868-14872; my tr.: stirred things up with simony, excommunications, stripping our authority, and giving dignities to simple peasants). LIK reads "chłopom ... w bractwach" (the brotherhoods) into this (l.7883-7885); that gloss is Likowski's.
- **The 1595 conditions**: the bishops' articles ask that "Matrimonia Sacerdotalia ut integra constent" (THEIN3 l.21225) and accept the new calendar only "si secundum morem antiquum fieri non possit", keeping Easter and the feasts (l.21210-21214).
- **The Orthodox lay leader's charge before the synod**: Ostrozky's circular of 24 June 1595: "мнимые пастыры наши, митрополитъ зъ епископы, въ волкы претворишася" (AZR4 l.9959-9960; my tr.: our so-called shepherds, the metropolitan with the bishops, have turned into wolves), having secretly agreed "яко христопродавца Іюда зъ жидовами" (l.9969-9970).
- **The Orthodox synod's three articles** (its own scriptum, RIB7 l.60082-60098 and 60183-60189): (1) to punish the clergy "што приватне отъ зверхности патріаршей отступили ... черезъ скиненье съ тыхъ урядовъ" (deposition); (2) that this "мало оголошеномъ помѣстномъ синодѣ" (poorly announced local synod) decide nothing on union "безъ позволенья патріарховъ и всей Церкви Восточной"; (3) not to accept the new calendar, "канономъ церковнымъ явне противного". HAR summarises the same four points in Latin (l.12041-12053).

## 4. Called by

- **Royal summons through the metropolitan**: Rahoza's depositions say "за волею и росказаньемъ его королевской милости, зложили есмо соборъ на день шостый мѣсяца Октябре по старому, яко и зъ универсаловъ его королевской милости" (AZR4 l.14576-14579, no. 107; same in no. 108, l.14648-14651).
- **Papal mandate**: Skarga's *Obrona* says the synod "roskazany iest od nawyższego Pasterza ... Papieża Clemensa osmego" (RZEP l.1372-1375) and was held with the knowledge of the primate Stanisław Karnkowski and with the king's leave (l.1380-1392). Clement VIII calls it "a ... Michaele eorum Metropolitano ad praescriptum mandati nostri indictam" (THEIN3 l.23567-23568).
- **Who convened, as argued**: Skarga's envoys: "Metropolit Synod złożył, nie Nikifor, ani Balaban, ani Kopistyński" (RZEP l.688-690). The Orthodox scriptum says they came to a synod "за позволеньемъ его королевской милости, такъ духовнымъ яко и свѣцкымъ зложеный ... хочъ за барзо глухимь обвѣщеньемъ" (RIB7 about l.59625-59629: summoned with the king's leave for clergy and laity, though with a very muffled announcement).
- **Short notice**: Rahoza says the king first wanted it at Christmas, then it was suddenly renewed and he had "только девять дней" (AZR4 l.14903-14918: only nine days).

## 6. Dates (old style, each with its witness)

- **Opening, 6 October**: RZEP l.324-326 ("6. dnia Oktobra, wedle starego Kalendarza"); AZR4 no. 105 l.14120-14121; Rahoza nos. 107-108. AZR4 106 I gives "шостого Октебря по старому, а 17 по новому" (l.14281-14282): the newsletter's own new-style figure, not corrected here. Skarga: the bishops waited one day for the royal envoys (l.338-340), who came "dnia drugiego w dźień Czwartkowy" (l.345-347).
- **Friday 8 October**: the envoys' address to the Orthodox (RZEP l.505-1012; "W Piątek", l.511). The Orthodox evening reply (RZEP l.1019-1027; RIB7 l.60209-60447).
- **The union act: 8 or 9 October, contested.** 8th: AZR4 no. 103 text (l.13817, "Октобрія осмого дня"); THEIN3 Latin version (l.23352, "octava die"); the census edge (§14). 9th: Skarga's printed act (RZEP l.1104); Skarga's narrative puts the reading on Saturday (l.1043); Rahoza's own letter, "въ суботу, девятого дня Октебра" (AZR4 l.14935-14936); LIK prints "dnia 9" (l.7664). Rahoza's depositions of Balaban and Tur are dated 9 Oct (l.14612-14613, 14701).

## 7. Decided (item by item)

**Union synod**
1. Union with Rome under Clement VIII: "То есмо днесь на семъ сѵнодѣ учинили" (AZR4 l.13931-13936: this we have done today at this synod), professing the faith and giving obedience "столицы Римской Петра святого Клименту осмому и его наслѣдникомъ".
2. Rites kept: "заховуючи намъ обряды и церемоніѣ церквей Восточныхъ Греческихъ и Рускихъ, а ни якоѣ отмѣны въ церквахъ нашихъ не чинячи" (AZR4 l.13904-13907, as the papal grant they had asked for).
3. Depositions: Balaban (no. 107; "складаемъ тебе зъ столицы епископства твоего", l.14592-14594), Nikifor Tur (no. 108), Kopystensky, Balaban and the refusing clergy (no. 109). Skarga: the synod's sentence deposed the two bishops and excommunicated Nikifor and the members of the rival gathering (as LIK tells it, l.7761-7766, citing AZR IV nos. 107-108).
4. Calendar (not in the act; Rahoza's report): "А календаръ и пасхалію по старому держати всимъ заховали, по новому хто всхочетъ" (AZR4 l.14979-14981; my tr.: we have kept the calendar and paschal tables in the old way for all; the new for whoever wishes).

**Orthodox synod**
1. Reply to the royal envoys: union welcome, but not now and not this way: "доброго фундаменту тоей згоды на тотъ часъ не видимо" (RIB7 l.60323-60327, Cyrillic column; LIK prints it in Polish, "dobrego fundamentu tej zgody na ten czas nie widzimy", l.7567-7568). They would come "когды вся Церковь Восточная, а особливе патріархове" agree (LIK l.7570-7578; RIB7 l.60336-60447).
2. Deposition of Rahoza, Potii, Terletsky, Herman, Zbiruisky, Hohol: AZR4 no. 104, "абы ... митрополитъ Михаилъ былъ изверженъ, также и помѣненые епископове ... изо всей шаты архіерейства были наги" (l.14076-14082). HAR's Polish: "Metropolit Michal i pomienieni wladykowie obnazeni byli wszelakiego Archierejskiego urzedu" (l.12251-12253).
3. Petition to the king to give the sees and church goods to others "которыи власне релѣи Греческой были" (RIB7 l.60559-60572; LIK l.7959-7962).
4. Letters to the dietines to back them at the coming diet (RIB7 l.60574-60582).
5. A sworn protestation never to obey the deposed (RIB7 l.60584-60605) and the protest filed at the Brest court (l.60685-60712).
6. Clearing of Zizanii and the Vilnius brotherhood priests (AZR4 no. 105, l.14205-14221).
7. Nikephoros's circular: priests who refuse the union may serve freely, commemorating "патріарха Гавріила" until new bishops are put in place (AZR4 l.15052-15065).

## 8. Outcome (through events)

- Two hierarchies: MOUR: "thus the Church of Little Russia was divided into the Orthodox and the Uniate, both preserving ... the same form ... of external rite" (l.6611-6615). MOUR Notes: "From those times, down to our own, two Churches have existed in Lithuania" (l.19957-19958).
- Royal backing: Sigismund III's universal of 15 December 1596 (HAR l.12641-12645; LIK l.8020-8029, which says it told the Ruthenians to have no dealings with Balaban and Kopystensky).
- Papal backing: Clement VIII's congratulation, with the line "aliqui ex Ruthenis Episcopis magis tenebras dilexerint, quam lucem" (THEIN3 l.23582-23583), and the annulment of the patriarch's excommunication (THEIN3 l.23607-23640).
- Nikephoros arrested and tried at the Warsaw diet, 11 March 1597; the held account is Orthodox and calls his accusers "апостатовъ Рускихъ" (AZR4 l.15802-15807). LIK: sentenced to prison at Malbork, where he died (l.8001-8002), and Ostrozky walked out saying "Niech go (Nikifora), sobie zje (król)!" (l.8059-8060); LIK cites AZR IV pp. 218-219 and Golubev for the speech. I did not find the "zje" line in AZR4's doc 117 text (searched l.15794-16950); treat it as Likowski's.
- Reach beyond one tradition: Greek clergy of the patriarchates of Constantinople and Alexandria signed the Orthodox decree (HAR l.12271-12276); Cyril Lucaris was there (HAR l.12016-12018; MOUR l.6600). Later Orthodox memory in Kyiv (Kopystensky, Filippovich, Mohyla) held the deposition as a true council's act (RIB4 l.71810-71826; l.9040-9055; AIUZR7 l.5284-5289). The census already records the 1946 Lviv assembly that "voted to annul the Union of Brest of 1596" (census, the-ukrainian-greek-catholic-underground).

## 10. Short quotable lines (acts first)

1. "абы на единомъ Петрѣ, яко на камени церковъ Христова моцнѣ стоячи" (AZR4 l.13824-13826, union act). My tr.: that Christ's church should stand firm on one Peter, as on a rock.
2. "Nos igitur hodie in hac Synodo id fecimus" (THEIN3 l.23406-23407, Latin version). My tr.: We have therefore done this today in this synod.
3. "яко гнилые члонки" (AZR4 l.14129, Orthodox synod's charter no. 105, of the metropolitan and bishops cut off from the body of Christ). My tr.: like rotten limbs.
4. "доброго фундаменту тоей згоды на тотъ часъ не видимо" (RIB7 l.60323-60327, Orthodox reply). My tr.: for the present we see no good foundation for this agreement.
5. "Na namowę braterską nie na woynę tuśmy się ziechali: na Synod, nie na nieprzyiaćielskie granice: na zgodę nie na zwady" (RZEP l.627-630, the royal envoys to the Orthodox, in Skarga). My tr.: We came here for brotherly talk, not for war; for a synod, not an enemy frontier; for agreement, not quarrels.
6. "wielkie są rzeczy y głębokie, a v nich głowy są miałkie" (RZEP l.1023-1025: the Orthodox answer as Skarga reports it). My tr.: these are great and deep matters, and their heads are shallow. Hostile report; set it beside no. 4.
7. "съ великимъ жалемъ нашимъ и обливаньемъ слезъ, же-сьмы порозорваны съ братьею нашою милою" (AZR4 l.14937-14938, Rahoza). My tr.: with great grief and tears, that we are torn from our dear brethren.
8. "бо папѣрови мало можно давати вѣры" (AZR4 l.14912-14913, Rahoza). My tr.: for one can put little trust in paper.
9. "Ино ни одноѣ овечки словесной за собою потягнути и взяти не могли" (AZR4 l.14497-14498, Orthodox newsletter). My tr.: and they could not draw a single sheep of the flock after them. Partisan.
10. "lud wierny, hoynie łzy wylewal" (RZEP l.1275). My tr.: the faithful people shed tears freely.
11. "Bluźnienie tam było, nie klątwa" (RZEP l.3640-3641, Obrona, of the Orthodox excommunication). My tr.: it was blasphemy there, not an anathema.
12. "якъ вторый Моусей знашолъ ся и свѣтлымъ и многополчнымъ и силнымъ въ Берестью станулъ" (RIB4 l.77447-77451, Kopystensky on Ostrozky, 1621-22; the prince is named at l.77327). My tr.: like a second Moses he appeared, and stood at Brest radiant, with many troops, and strong.

## 11. Contested points

1. **Date of the union act** (8 vs 9 Oct): see §6. AZR4 l.13817 and THEIN3 l.23352 vs RZEP l.1104 and Rahoza AZR4 l.14935-14936.
2. **Date of the Orthodox deposition** (8, 9 or 10 Oct): AZR4 l.14008 (editors); HAR l.12260; LIK l.7849-7851; RIB7 l.60698-60700.
3. **Nikephoros's authority.** For: he styles himself "отъ всихъ четырехъ святѣйшихъ патріарховъ посланый" (AZR4 l.15038); the newsletter: sent "отъ Гавріилія патріарха Константинопольского" (l.14297-14298); Kopystensky: sent "отъ Іереміи Константинопольского" (RIB4 l.71813-71815); HAR (Greek Catholic): the see was vacant and Meletios of Alexandria, its administrator, "misit ... Nicephorum" as his vicar (l.12011-12015). Against: Skarga's *Obrona*: "Był: ale nie od Patryarchy, iedno od koronnych nieprzyiaćioł" (RZEP l.3608-3611); PEL: his commission from Jeremias II (decree of 2 Nov 1593, printed in the Apokrisis) lapsed at Jeremias's death and Meletios gave him none (l.1395-1421, 1447-1449); LIK: "rzekomy wysłaniec" (l.7287) and a spy (l.7940). The two Greek Catholic historians disagree with each other here.
4. **Which meeting was the synod.** Skarga: "ta schadzka wasza nie tylo Synodem się zwać nie może" (RZEP l.680-682); LIK calls it "antysynod" (l.7367) and "zborzysko", a label he says he will use throughout "dla krótkości" (l.7402-7403); PEL "Aftersynode" (l.1385). The Orthodox say the metropolitan's was the "помѣстномъ синодѣ" and theirs the gathering of the whole church; Mohyla calls the bishops "отступници"; MOUR calls the union gathering "the conventicle of the Uniates" (l.6608).
5. **Did the Orthodox reject union outright?** Skarga reports a flat refusal (RZEP l.1019-1027). Their own reply is conditional: welcome, but only with the patriarchs and the whole East (RIB7 l.60233-60447; LIK l.7541-7578, who calls the conditions such as to remove all hope, l.7580-7584).
6. **The armed escort.** Skarga: armed "iako до woyny" (RZEP l.349-350); LIK "200" (l.7302). Kopystensky praises Ostrozky "многополчнымъ" at Brest (RIB4 l.77448).
7. **Whether Balaban and Kopystensky had signed for union earlier.** The royal envoys: news of union came three years before from all the bishops, "od W. M. oycze Władyka Lwowski, у od W. M. oycze Władyka Przemysłki, iako podpisy ręku waszych własnych pokazuią" (RZEP l.465-471); MOUR: they "learned the wicked use made of their signatures" (l.6569-6572). Caution: AZR4 no. 58 (editors' date 28 Jan 1595, l.8403-8406) is a pro-Roman synodal act headed by Balaban whose signatures (l.8476-8516) overlap with the 1596 Orthodox signers (Nikifor Tur, Masalski, Nestor Kozmich as marshal). I have not worked out what it is; do not use it without further reading.
8. **Who wrote the Apokrisis.** LIK "Broński ... sócyanin" / "Aryanin" (l.8384, 8340); PEL "Bronski, ein Calvinist" (l.27193). RIB7's editors leave the pseudonym.
9. **Who was marshal of the clergy's circle.** LIK: Nikifor (l.7454-7455); AZR4 no. 105: Nestor Kozmich (l.14235, 14247; unsure, OCR break).
10. **The wine and water at the union liturgy.** Mohyla's notes: at the first liturgy at Brest God turned the wine to water (AIUZR7 l.5284-5297); Skarga, he says, called it a mistake of pouring water twice (l.5300-5305). The editors note that what stands in brackets is "не рукою Могилы" (l.5330); a bracket opens at l.5289 and the note closes at l.5337, so part or all of the Brest passage after l.5289 may be in another hand. Skarga's own words on it are not held (searched RZEP for "kielich", "wino", "wod"; LIK l.7200-8400 the same).

## 17. Length

The full 2:30 ceiling (about 400 words) is warranted, but not more. Both meetings have their own acts and dated signed letters; there is an eyewitness narrative from each side (Skarga's account; the Orthodox scriptum and the newsletters), and the two can be set against each other day by day (Thursday's embassy, Friday's talks, Saturday's ceremony and the rival deposition). That allows a fair telling that gives each side its own room and its own words. The limits are honest ones to say once: the two main narratives are partisan, the Ekthesis is lost to the Library, and the dates of the two decisive acts differ by a day between witnesses. The drafter should not try to settle the dates or Nikephoros's standing; telling them as contested uses words. Quotes from RIB7 and AZR4 must be rechecked against page images (both headers say so).
