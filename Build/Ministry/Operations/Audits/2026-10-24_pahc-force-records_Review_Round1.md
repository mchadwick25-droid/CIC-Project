## Specific checks the brief asked for

- **boundary-drawing, Smyrnaeans 7.** The submitted text does not say the docetists "held no rival" eucharist. It says: "He does not say they held a rival eucharist." That matches the source, which is silent on a rival rite: A1:8220-8224 "They abstain from the Eucharist and from prayer, because they confess not the Eucharist to be the flesh of our Saviour Jesus Christ"; A1:8230-8231 "keep aloof from such persons". Sound. The paraphrase "they stay away from the eucharist and prayer" is faithful.
- **systematic, Hegesippus.** The text says "Hegesippus writes of staying in Rome until Anicetus, though scholars dispute that line." The NPNF note bears this out: E:24646-24647 "And when I had come to Rome I remained there until Anicetus"; note at E:24647-24648 "The interpretation of this sentence is greatly disputed"; the manuscripts read διαδοχὴν ἐποιησάμην, which many render "I composed a catalogue of bishops down to Anicetus", while the editor prefers the emendation διατριβήν, "now very widely adopted" (E:24696-24697). Sound. "'In every succession, and in every city'" is verbatim, E:24716.
