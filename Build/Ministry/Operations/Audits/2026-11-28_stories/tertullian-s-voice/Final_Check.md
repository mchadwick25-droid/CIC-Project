## What changed (Revision.md)

- **B1** "Yet later in life he joined a strict movement, the New Prophecy, and wrote against the bishops." HELD: Jerome, "presbyter of the church until middle life, afterwards ... lapsed to the doctrine of Montanus" (npnf203 40506-40508); "directly against the church" (40511); On Modesty, "not the Church which consists of a number of bishops" (ANF4 9542-9545).
- **B2** "Jerome, who wrote his life, says the envy and insults of the clergy of Rome drove him to it." HELD: "driven by the envy and abuse of the clergy of the Roman church" (npnf203 40507-40508; JERL *invidia ... et contumeliis clericorum Romanae Ecclesiae*). The Augustine sentence after it holds (AUG 2868-2871, *transiens ad Cataphrygas, quos ante destruxerat, coepit etiam secundas nuptias ... tanquam stupra damnare*).
- **B3** "For that teaching, he wrote, Tertullian became a heretic; later he broke from the prophets too and gathered meetings of his own." SUMMARY HOLDS: AUG 2867-2873, *Non ergo ideo est Tertullianus factus haereticus: sed quia ... coepit ... secundas nuptias ... damnare, et postmodum etiam ab ipsis divisus, sua conventicula propagavit*. Read twice: true, and nothing is told twice. MINOR only: "that teaching" points back three paragraphs, and beside the sentence before it a listener may take it as the Tertullianists' teaching. Optional exact change in `tertullian-s-voice.brief.txt`: "For that teaching, he wrote," to "For that teaching on marriage, he wrote,".
- **M1** "...and Cyprian, the secretary said, would often ask, "Give me the master," meaning Tertullian." HELD: JERL 55623-55626, *referreque sibi solitum ... ac sibi crebro dicere, Da magistrum*. The sentence before ("an old man in Italy who had met Cyprian's secretary") matches *Romae vidisse diceret*.
- **M2** "In the same book he put it in two words." SUMMARY HOLDS: *personae, non substantiae nomine* (KRO 12559), *unam substantiam in tribus cohaerentibus* (KRO 12561), *personas singulas* (KRO 14060).
- **M3** "Marcion, as Tertullian tells it in another book, cut the Scriptures down to suit himself, and Valentinus kept them whole but bent their meaning. There he answered them with a plea from the law courts, one that throws a case out before it is argued." HELD: Prescription 38, "Marcion expressly and openly used the knife, not the pen ... Valentinus, however, abstained from such excision ... by removing the proper meaning of every particular word" (ANF3 21808-21818), credited to Tertullian.
- **M4** "And he claimed: "I am the heir of the apostles."" HELD (ANF3 21751; Oehler t2 2289).
- **M5** "He defended the Christians to Rome's governors," HELD (Apology 1, ANF3 1448-1452, *Romani imperii antistites*).
