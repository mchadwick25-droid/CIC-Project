- Event told twice: none. The joining is in the essence (line 3) as the essence, and in Jerome's reason (line 9). Augustine's "went over" (line 17) is told only as his ground for the heresy, with new matter: he had once argued against them, and he condemned second marriage as sexual sin. The Apology's date is given twice (lines 1 and 3), but that is not an event.

## Card (`tertullian-s-voice.card.json`, `briefReferences`)

- Entry 1: accurate. The brief does set the book around 200, and 197 is Widely Accepted (EUS-ed 10294–10303). No modern work is named.
- Entry 3: MINOR (item 3). "the faith handed from Christ to the apostles and their churches" names something the brief does not tell. The brief says only that the Scriptures "belonged to the churches the apostles founded, such as Rome, which still kept what the apostles taught" (line 7); Christ's handing-on is not in it. The rest of entry 3 matches the brief: ch. 34's other god and the Aeons, ch. 38's knife and bent meaning, ch. 37's property lines, and Against Marcion I.5's swarm. Exact change below.
- Entry 4: accurate. "carnally-minded" is Holmes's English for *psychici*, which the brief renders "the unspiritual". On Monogamy 1 supports the brief's "no second marriage", and Jerome 53 supports its reason.
- Entry 8: accurate and in the brief's terms: the works still read, the Tertullianists, the going-over and second marriage as fornication (*stupra*), the later break, and the basilica handed over. "Phrygians" stays inside a reference line, which is allowed.
