# Who scan, era 6 (2026-11-12)

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-12). Read-only scan of era 6 (1300–1517 CE, "The Late Medieval Era") of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Counts, 162 rows (a paired row counts once): **WHO 69, STORY 22, LISTED 57, WANTED 14**. Judgment-call rows: 43. Councils are not people and carry no verdict (see Councils below). Both live person entries, Thomas à Kempis and John Wyclif, are WHO, so none is flagged. The Library holds two outside witnesses of the window in their own text (al-Umari and al-Maqrizi, both WHO) and no Jewish chronicler.

## How the list was built

- Era 6 has 26 census entries: 24 movements and 2 live person entries (Thomas à Kempis, John Wyclif).
- Named voices and `people` lists of the movements (old rules), merged by alias.
- Moved from era 5 (Open-Gaps 219): Bernard Gui, Eckhart, Tauler, Suso, Belibaste, Molay, John of Brunn and Guiard of Cressonessart.
- Found in text: shapers named in era 6 census texts, and people placed by the corpus-map rows of an era 6 entry, marked "found in text" or "found in corpus-map" in the Source tag column.
- Four drafting workers (Sonnet) sorted the candidates by region and opened the cited lines. One Opus subagent then opened the cited lines for every WHO, STORY and WANTED row and 36 LISTED rows (`Opus_Check_Notes.md`).

## Rulings applied

- The standard of eras 1 to 5 (Decision-Log 2026-11-11): WHO is church-shaping with a body of work or a following and enough life to tell; recorders are WHO with a thin own life flagged; women are WHO if they founded, taught or led; rulers are STORY unless they have their own voice; founders known through enemies need two witnesses for the teaching and may rest the life on one; OUTSIDE WITNESS is a WHO label for objective historians outside the church (Muslim, Jewish and secular chroniclers, never polemicists).
- Pairing: "if two people are tied together in their sources with similar identity its ok to make that a single who". Paired rows are judgment calls.
- A later retelling alone gives LISTED until an early source is held. A WANTED source must be able to carry a life, not only writing.
- Era is by date of work: work after 1300 and before 1517 is era 6; work before 1300 goes to an era 5 supplement unless an era 5 row exists.
- "Major?" column (proposal only): the map shows up to 4 names per tradition and 5 per river per era, chosen from the major Whos. Mark confirms it in each batch.
- Editions tagged "[suggested; to confirm]" or "[recollection; to confirm]" were named from the drafting workers' or checker's memory, not from the census or the Library. The Library thread confirms them.
