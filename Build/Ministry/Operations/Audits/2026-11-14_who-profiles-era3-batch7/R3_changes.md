## A correction to R3-A-M1

The fix worker flagged, and the drafter confirmed against the file, that the reviewer's sentence on Charrhae misreads John of Ephesus. In his list of consecrations (`john-ephesus_lives-eastern-saints-po19-mixed-issue_brooks1926.txt` l.10639-10651) each man is named with his monastery and then his see: Dimet "from the monastery of Aphthonia in the eity [OCR] of Laodicea", made a metropolitan, and "Sergius from the monastery of Aphthonia in Charrhae", made a bishop in Syria, beside John "in Kenneshrin" and John "in Sura Romanorum". Charrhae is Sergius's see, and the line names one monastery of Aphthonia, which Brooks's note ties to Kenneshre. The sentence was removed from the brief. The card's `uncertain` item "Beth Aphthonia and Kenneshre" and the records claim and contested claim that carried the same reading were rewritten to read Charrhae as Sergius's see, and to say that the heading of the Life gives no place for Beith-Aphthonia. This changed participant text after round 3; it goes to the coordinator for a cap ruling.
