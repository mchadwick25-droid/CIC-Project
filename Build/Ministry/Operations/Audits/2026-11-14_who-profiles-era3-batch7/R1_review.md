# R1 review: eight Who profiles, era 3 batch 7

Three independent Opus reviewers, each at first sight of its drafts and at full effort, reviewed the batch in three parts: part A (Boethius, Philoxenus of Mabbug, John of Beth Aphthonia), part B (Fulgentius of Ruspe, Primasius of Hadrumetum, Avitus of Vienne) and part C (Eugippius, Ciaran of Clonmacnois, and the README). Totals: 7 blocking and 49 minor (part A 4 and 15, part B 1 and 17, part C 2 and 17).

## Part A


Reviewer: A, independent, first sight of these drafts. Bar: `Who_Profile_Pattern.md` review checklist 1 to 14 and the standards it points to (Style Guide 1.1 to 1.12); `CLAUDE.md` "Trust and source fidelity" and "Accessible and rigorous"; the Who profile rulings in `Decision-Log.md`, 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-14 (majors capped at 2:30, about 405 words, a cap and not a target; non-majors and recorders at most about 325 words). Known for this part, by the coordinator's calls: Boethius and Philoxenus of Mabbug are major; John of Beth Aphthonia is a Who as a recorder, as the author of the second Life of Severus, with the identity question (is he the archimandrite John bar Aphthonia?) told as contested and bar Aphthonia's facts kept apart and given as his. I hunted hardest for false absences, editors' views or years told as settled, wrong citations, witness swaps, non-verbatim quotes and contested points told one way. I did not edit any repo file. I made no paid or model-API calls and used no web.

Profiles: `boethius` (major), `philoxenus-of-mabbug` (major), `john-of-beth-aphthonia` (recorder), with their cards, audio scripts and `records.json` entries. Files are in `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Who-Profiles/era3/batch7/`. Brief line numbers refer to the `.brief.txt` files, where each paragraph is one line and blank lines count. Card fields are named by key and list position. Other line numbers point to files under `cic/texts/`.

Method. I ran `vq.py` on every quotation in the three briefs and cards, and read each OCR miss by line number. I read each source passage in context to see who speaks: the ancient author, a document he copies, an editor's note or heading, or a catalogue entry. I checked each factual sentence of each brief against its passage and compared each `records.json` entry with its brief and card. Before accepting absence and quantifier claims I searched the Library for the person under the spellings the sources use (Boethius, Boetius, Boetii, Severinus; Philoxenus, Philoxène, Xenaias, Xenaja, Akhs'noyo, Akhsenaya; Aphthonia, Aphtonia, Beith-Aphthonia, Kenneshre, Qennesrin, Kenserin; Dometios; Polycarp with chorepiscopus; Ennodius, Paraenesis; "Life of Severus" in all forms). That search turned up held files the drafts do not use: the Ethiopic Life of Severus by Athanasius of Antioch (Goodspeed and Crum, PO 4, in `barhadbeshabba_cause-de-la-fondation-des-ecoles-po4-syr-fra_scher1908.txt` l.663-665, 32888-32915), Joshua the Stylite on Xenaias at Edessa (`joshua-the-stylite_chronicle_wright1882.txt` l.1482-1486), Philoxenus's own "Sur les hérésies" in Nau's PO 13 (`chronicle-of-seert_histoire-nestorienne-seconde-partie-po13-ara-fra_scher1919.txt` l.10503-10506, 14714) and Severus's letter 48 "To Philoxenus bp. of Hierapolis" (`severus-of-antioch_select-letters-book6-part1_brooks1903.txt` l.493). - **A-B1 BLOCKING (items 1, 3; a catalogue of saints not in the Roman Martyrology told as the Roman Martyrology; records misstate the witness).** Brief l.13: "The Roman Martyrology, as the Bollandists print it, honors him as a martyr at Pavia." Severino Boctio Martyrc Ticini … &in altera Catalagaearamqainanfantia Alartpralogia Ramana, Ticini S. Severini Uoet II Manyris, sub Thcodorico Gnthorum Regej". Read through the swaps, this is "Ferrarius, in Catalogo Sanctorum Italiae agit de S. Severino Boetio Martyre Ticini … & in altero Catalogo eorum qui non sunt in Martyrologio Romano, Ticini S. Severini Boetii Martyris, sub Theodorico Gothorum Rege". The words "qai nan fant ia" are "qui non sunt in". The entry the drafts give as the Roman Martyrology's is from a catalogue of saints who are *not* in the Roman Martyrology, which the Bollandists quote. The brief, two card fields and the records all tell the reverse of the source. What stands is the Bollandists' own day list, l.8153-8155, "S. Severinus Boetius, Exconsul Romanus, Martyr Ticini apud Insubres", after Pope John I. Fix, brief l.13, second sentence: "The Bollandists, in their Acta Sanctorum of saints' days, list him as a martyr at Pavia." Fix, card `legacy.remembered` 3: "The Bollandists, in the Acta Sanctorum of 1739, list him under 27 May, after Pope John I, as a martyr at Pavia. They also quote an Italian catalogue of saints who are not in the Roman Martyrology, which names him a martyr at Pavia under Theodoric." Fix, card `uncertain` 5, last sentence: "No ancient source used here calls him a martyr; the title comes from the Bollandists and the later catalogue they quote." Records: rewrite the Acta Sanctorum claim and contested-claim side to the Bollandists' list (l.8153-8155) and the catalogue "eorum qui non sunt in Martyrologio Romano" (l.16489-16493, reading reconstructed from heavy OCR), keep it Inferential-Thin, and drop "the Roman Martyrology entry they print" everywhere.
- **A-M1 MINOR (item 5).** Brief l.11: "in the year his editor dates to 524". A role stands in for the named witness; it is Mommsen's margin (records claim on Marius). Mommsen is already named in l.1. Fix: "in the year Mommsen dates to 524".

A letter Cassiodorus wrote in Theodoric's name praises him for putting Pythagoras, Ptolemy the astronomer, Euclid, Plato and Aristotle into Latin."
- **A-M3 MINOR (items 1, 7).** Card `uncertain` 1, last sentence: "The Bollandists report that the account of the Anonymus Valesianus is doubted, since others say the sword." In the passage cited (l.16337-16342; heavy OCR) the doubt belongs to a named writer who saw the text in manuscript ("Suspectam hanc habet narrationem C… sibi ex MS. exhibitam prius quam vulgaretur"), and the Bollandists go on to answer it ("Sed hoc non legitur apud Procopium, vel apud Auctorem Catalogi Pontificum…"). The card gives the doubt and leaves out the answer. Fix: "The Bollandists report one writer's doubt of the account of the Anonymus Valesianus, since others say the sword, and answer that the sword is not in Procopius." Or drop the sentence. Records contested claim 1: add the answer and mark the reading as reconstructed from heavy OCR.
- **A-M4 MINOR (items 1, 4).** Card `legacy.traces` 3: "Thomas Aquinas opens his article on the definition of a person in the Summa of Theology with the definition "Boetius assignat"". The Latin is a verb phrase ("which Boethius gives"), quoted as if it were the definition's name.
