# Hand-fetch list for Mark: Library era 10, batch 2 (2026-11-15)

Hosts that refuse automated clients are not worked around. This list gives, for each wanted item, the record to open
by hand and the edition to look for. It was compiled by a Sonnet searcher from web-search results and plain fetches of
open sites, and read by the Library thread. No request was sent to HathiTrust, Papers Past, prlib.ru, dlib.rsl.ru or
pentecostalarchives.org.

Marks: [S] the URL was seen in search results and not opened. [F] the URL was fetched plainly and read. [P] a URL
pattern only, not confirmed. Gallica and rusneb.ru refused a plain fetch (403) and were not tried again.

What to check on every item: the title page or masthead must print a year of 1930 or earlier (a reprint counts by its
own date). Send the file with the page it came from and the host's own rights line.

**Taken by the Library in this batch, so no longer for hand-fetching:** item 20 (Bell, Documents on Christian Unity,
Oxford 1924, with the encyclical of January 1920 in English) and item 9 in its third edition (Loisy, L'Évangile et
l'Église, Bellevue 1904). The first edition of 1902 is still wanted from Gallica.

**Looked at again and still without a printed year:** item 6 (Blair) and item 5 (Penn-Lewis, fourth edition). A cover
or title page with a year would settle each.
