So the sound link is the man named in that dedication, not the stone's content.
- Writing (folded into the fix): "his journey" has no antecedent in the line. Abercius is never named.

- Option B (keeps a real Montanist link, but adds new content to the card, so it is a change order and Mark's call): add the Eusebius dedication to the card's `caveat` and `sources` from the two vendored files above, then: "Phrygia was the home of the New Prophecy. The census account of 622-843 begins with Heraclius, who reigned through 622.
