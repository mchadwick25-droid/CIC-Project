## How the list was built

- Leads: every "supplement-scan lead" and every "moves by date of work, to the era N supplement" line in the ten era audits and in Open-Gaps 219 to 227, and the "Not scored" and "Other eras by date" sections of the audits.
- Corpus-map: a script listed the 1,219 authors of the 4,007 corpus-map rows, then removed every author whose name matched a person cell of any audit table (632 by a strict match). 587 were left (300 with no match, 287 with a partial match). Five drafting workers (Sonnet) triaged them by hand: councils, synods, anonymous and corporate works, modern scholars and editors, and namesake traps are skipped.
- Already scored in an era scan: left out and listed under "Already scored". Era is by date of main work; tradition is the one the corpus-map gives.
- Five drafting workers drafted 248 rows; eight repeats between workers were merged (Eucherius, Junillus, Leonard of Chios, Conti, Maba Seyon, Smyth, Heckewelder). A sixth worker did the follow-up pass named in answer 1 (15 rows).
- One Opus subagent opened the cited lines of every WHO, STORY and WANTED row, re-searched every WANTED and "not held" claim with whitespace-tolerant patterns (the OCR double-spaces names), checked all rows for duplicates against the ten era audits, and read 32 LISTED rows (`Opus_Check_Notes.md`). The same agent then checked the ten hagiographic founders and the 15 follow-up rows.
