## After one hearing

- **Who this was:** Cyril, bishop of Alexandria in Egypt from 412 to 444, who taught that Christ is one: the Word, God's own Son, was himself born of Mary.
- **What happened:** He took his post amid a riot. Socrates, a hostile witness, blames him for shutting the Novatian churches, driving out the Jews and the shame of Hypatia's murder. He wrote to Nestorius that Mary is Mother of God, and Nestorius was removed in 431. Ephesus approved his letter, and his archdeacon listed costly gifts to the court.
- **Why the connection matters:** Chalcedon in 451 took his letters as a guide yet said "two natures". The churches of Egypt, Syria and Armenia held to his "one nature" instead and made his words their test of faith. Severus called every word of his "a law of the church".
