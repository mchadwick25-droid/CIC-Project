# Card edits: Cyril of Alexandria

Card writer, batch 5, 2026-11-18. Base: the census card for `cyril-of-alexandria` (identical to `Card.existing.json`, checked by object comparison). Every existing key and value is kept word for word and in order, except the one clearly wrong sentence below and the sources added. Result: `cyril-of-alexandria.card.json`.

## New

1. **`quotationOriginals`** (old: none). Seven entries cover all eight quoted spans in the brief ("one nature" is quoted twice and has one entry). Every span is new, so none has a `secondReading`. All are **awaiting second reading**.
   - Bush. Modern: "He came like fire upon the bush in the desert. The fire kept playing over the bush, and yet the bush was not burned up." Printed: "for He came in likeness of fire on the bush in the wilderness, and the fire kept playing on the shrub yet was it not consumed." Work: Cyril, *That Christ is One*, tr. Pusey. Evidence: `cic/texts/cyril-alexandria_five-tomes-against-nestorius-scholia-christ-is-one_pusey1881.txt` lines 19878-19881. **Scan fault, needs a listing:** line 19880 reads "play- / ing" and line 19881 reads `4  ivriarp-  ing  "^  ou  the  shrub` (margin note and a misread "on"). The census check cannot read past this by rule, so `tools/quote_scan_readings.json` needs an entry for lines 19880-19881 before the card validates. I did not edit that shared file.
   - Q1. Modern: "An ordinary man was not born first of the holy Virgin, ..." Printed (Pusey's English): "For not mere man was first born of the holy Virgin, and then the Word of God came down upon Him, but united from the very womb, He is said to have undergone birth in the Flesh," Evidence: `cic/texts/cyril-alexandria_three-epistles-grc-eng_pusey1872.txt` lines 4334-4336. Greek beside it, lines 954-957 of the same file (a footnote "2" after "πρῶτον" is left out).
   - Gold. Modern and printed: "the gold of iniquity". `cic/texts/nestorius_bazaar-of-heracleides_driver-hodgson1925.txt` line 15817.
   - "one nature" (twice in the brief). Printed: "the one nature of the Word", Cyril's second letter to Succensus as John of Damascus quotes it. `cic/texts/npnf209_hilary-poitiers-john-damascus.xml` lines 33086-33087.
   - "one incarnate nature of God the Word". Printed as John of Damascus gives it "according to the blessed Cyril", same file, lines 32751-32752. The entry's `uncertain` says that Pusey and Hefele print slightly different wording and that the Greek is not held. No held text puts exactly these words in Cyril's own mouth.
   - "no commingling, no confusion". Same words in Pusey's note quoting the second letter to Succensus, line 19847 of the Pusey 1881 file.
   - Severus. Modern: "the letter of the holy Cyril to Nestorius. Every word he spoke, one could say, is a law of the church." Printed: "the epistle of the holy Cyril (every utterance of whom one would say is a law of the church) to Nestorius". `cic/texts/severus-of-antioch_select-letters-book6-part1_brooks1903.txt` lines 2106-2108 (line 2105, which the source sheet starts at, has a scan fault "teachino^" and is left out).
   - All printed wordings except the bush were checked by the census fold (case, accents, punctuation folded; line-end hyphens joined) against their cited lines: they stand. The Greek is not testable by the fold (it folds to nothing), so lines 954-957 were checked by eye.

## Clearly wrong

1. **`uncertain[2]`** (Nestorius's bribery charge).
   - Old: "...Epiphanius, Cyril's own archdeacon. Those letters are not among the sources used here."
   - New: "...Epiphanius, Cyril's own archdeacon. Those letters are held in Latin in Mansi V."
   - Evidence: `cic/texts/mansi_sacrorum-conciliorum-collectio-tomus-5-431-441-lat_welter-facsimile1901.txt` lines 97081-97311 (the letter of Epiphanius, "Archidiaconi & Syncelli Cyrilli", to Maximian, with the gifts and the 1,500 pounds of gold, "libras mille quingentas"), and lines 81294-81385 (the letter of Acacius of Beroea, which also speaks of the money Cyril gave). Driver and Hodgson's note, Nestorius file line 15955-15957, sends the reader to these letters in Mansi. The brief's sentence on the archdeacon's list of gifts and the debt rests on the first. Only those words changed.
