## The three lines (heard once, before reading anything else)

1. **Who this was:** Martin Luther, a friar who taught at Wittenberg, whose teaching that forgiveness is God's free gift, received by trusting God's promise, gave the Lutheran churches their name.
2. **What happened:** In 1517 he protested against the sale of pardons. In 1520 the pope condemned his books, he burned the popes' law book, and he was cut off from the church. At Worms in 1521 he would not take back his books, was outlawed and hidden, and put the New Testament into German; he wrote hymns for the people to sing. In 1525 he first warned both rulers and peasants, then urged the rulers to strike the rebels down, and later condemned the victors' bloodlust. The whole Bible in German followed by 1534.
3. **Why the connection matters:** He put Scripture and song into the people's own language, so ordinary people read and sang, and his churches still printed his hymns two centuries later.
