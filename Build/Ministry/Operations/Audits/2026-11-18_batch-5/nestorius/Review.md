## The test, from one hearing

2. **What happened:** he was condemned as splitting Christ in two, which he denied. His writings were ordered burned, he was dragged about in exile in southern Egypt, and near the end he wrote his defense under a borrowed name.
3. **Why the connection matters:** the Church of the East kept that book in Syriac and honoured him in its rites. It also refused to be called his church: "Nestorius, then, followed them, and not they him."
