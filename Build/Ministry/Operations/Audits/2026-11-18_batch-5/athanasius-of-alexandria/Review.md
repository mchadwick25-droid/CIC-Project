# Review: Athanasius of Alexandria

## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who.** Athanasius, bishop of Alexandria in Egypt from 328 to 373, who held to Nicaea's creed that God's Son is "of one being" with the Father, fully God, against the party of Arius.
2. **What happened.** He had to leave his city five times: removed by a council at Tyre and sent to Trier in 335, fled to Rome in 339, hid six years after soldiers came for him in 356, was driven out by Julian in 362, and hid four months in 365. In those years he wrote the *Life of Antony*, and in his Easter letter of 367 he listed the books of Scripture.
3. **Why the connection matters.** His book on the hermit was put into Latin, and his list of books went into Greek church law in 692 and into a 1921 Church of England proposal for sharing communion with the Orthodox churches.

All three lines come clear from one hearing.
