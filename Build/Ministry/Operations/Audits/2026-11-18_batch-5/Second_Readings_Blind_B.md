# Second Readings, Blind B (B5-21 to B5-40)

## B5-21
**Source (French):** "des murmures, des blâmes, des chefs d'accusation et des témoignages" (10 words)

**Rendering:** complaints, reproaches, charges and testimonies

## B5-22
**Source (Latin):** "mulieres concubinas habendi licentiam faciebant; imo ipse Barsumas hujusmodi apud se in cella mulierem servabat, quam sine pudore uxorem legitimam vocabat" (21 words)

**Rendering:** they gave licence to keep women as concubines; indeed Barsauma himself kept such a woman with him in his cell, whom he shamelessly called his lawful wife.

## B5-23
**Source (French):** "Maintenant, Nisibe est agitée, troublée et ballottée comme une mer tumultueuse, et si une lettre d'excommunication n'est pas envoyée promptement par Votre Paternité et ne pacifie pas la ville, Je ne resterai point dans l'épiscopat de Nisibe; Nisibe elle-même ne restera pas soumise au grand empire des Perses." (48 words)

**Rendering:** Now Nisibis is stirred up, troubled and tossed like a stormy sea. If a letter of excommunication is not sent quickly by Your Paternity to calm the city, I will not stay on as bishop of Nisibis. And Nisibis itself will not stay subject to the great empire of the Persians.

## B5-24
**Source (French):** "comme le médecin qui ampute un malade" (7 words; "mé- decin" is split across the line break in the scan)

**Rendering:** like the doctor who amputates a sick man

## B5-25
**Source (French):** "mais seulement sur ce point" (5 words)

**Rendering:** but only on this point

## B5-26
**Source (French):** "la divine assemblée" (3 words)

**Rendering:** the divine assembly

## B5-27
**Source (French):** "Mar" (1 word)

**Rendering:** Mar (the Syriac title "my lord", used of bishops and saints)

## B5-28
**Source (French):** "le bienheureux Mar Bar Çauma" (5 words). The scan reads "Bar CGauma"; I take "CGauma" as a misread of "Çauma".

**Rendering:** the blessed Mar Bar Sauma

## B5-29
**Source (French):** "Ici aussi, de même, nous disons que le Christ a été crucifié, que le Christ est mort et est ressuscité, mais nous ne disons pas que Dieu a été crucifié et est mort." (33 words)

**Rendering:** Here too, in the same way, we say that Christ was crucified, that Christ died and rose again. But we do not say that God was crucified and died.

## B5-30
**Source (English):** "If I had known that thou wast with me, I would, with all care, have accepted this great work; but now depart in peace, and pray for me;" (28 words)

**Rendering:** If I had known that you were with me, I would have taken on this great work with all care. But now go in peace, and pray for me.

## B5-31
**Source (Latin):** "sacerdotium nequaquam pecuniis venditur" (4 words)

**Rendering:** the priesthood is by no means sold for money

## B5-32
**Source (French):** "si le Dieu tout puissant a permis, et si le roi — que Dieu conserve sa vie! — à consenti à la reconstruction des églises, nous avons 1 néanmoins aller six fois en sa présence pour cette affaire" (38 words, counting the dashes as the request appears to). The scan has two misreads: "à consenti" for "a consenti", and "1" for a lost word; I read "nous avons dû néanmoins aller".

**Rendering:** though Almighty God has allowed it, and the king (may God preserve his life!) has agreed to the rebuilding of the churches, we still had to go before him six times about this matter

## B5-33
**Source (English):** "Appoint as Bishops whomsoever ye and the pious Kardagh shall choose, and in the place of the third [person who should be present], let the Book of the Gospels be laid on the [episcopal] throne on the right hand; thus by the hand of God perform the ordination of the first Bishop" (52 words). The scan reads "w^homsoever"; I take it as "whomsoever". The bracketed words are the translator's.

**Rendering:** Appoint as bishops whoever you and the devout Kardagh choose. In place of the third person who should be present, let the Book of the Gospels be laid on the bishop's throne on the right. In this way, by the hand of God, carry out the ordination of the first bishop.

## B5-34
**Source (Latin):** "Quilibet verus Christianus, sive vivus sive mortuus, habet participationem omnium bonorum Christi et ecclesiae, etiam sine Uteris veniarum, a Deo sibi datam." (22 words). The scan reads "Uteris"; I take it as a misread of "literis" (letters).

**Rendering:** Every true Christian, living or dead, has a share in all the benefits of Christ and the Church, given him by God, even without letters of pardon.

## B5-35
**Source (Latin):** "Nisi convictus fuero testimoniis Scripturarum aut ratione évidente (nam neque Papae neque Conciliis solis credo, cum constet eos errasse saepius et sibi ipsis contradixisse), victus sum Scripturis a me adductis captaque est conscientia in verbis Dei: revocare neque possum neque volo quidquam" (43 words, with the line-split "pos- sum" counted as the scan divides it). The accent in "évidente" is a scan artifact for "evidente".

**Rendering:** Unless I am convinced by the testimony of the Scriptures or by clear reason (for I do not trust the Pope or councils alone, since it is plain that they have often erred and contradicted themselves), I am bound by the Scriptures I have cited, and my conscience is captive to the words of God. I cannot and will not take anything back.

## B5-36
**Source (German):** "Gott helff mir. Amen." (4 words)

**Rendering:** God help me. Amen.

## B5-37
**Source (German):** "Ich kann nicht änderst, hie stehe ich" (7 words). I take "änderst" as a misread of "anderst", the period form of "anders".

**Rendering:** I cannot do otherwise; here I stand

## B5-38
**Source (English):** "Therefore let everyone who can, smite, slay, and stab, secretly or openly, remembering that nothing can be more poisonous, hurtful, or devilish than a rebel." (25 words)

**Rendering:** So let everyone who can strike, kill and stab, in secret or in the open. Remember that nothing can be more poisonous, harmful or devilish than a rebel.

## B5-39
**Source (English):** "the furious, raving, senseless tyrants" (5 words)

**Rendering:** the furious, raging, mindless tyrants

## B5-40
**Source (English):** "every one may drink for himself from the fresh spring" (10 words)

**Rendering:** everyone can drink for themselves from the fresh spring
