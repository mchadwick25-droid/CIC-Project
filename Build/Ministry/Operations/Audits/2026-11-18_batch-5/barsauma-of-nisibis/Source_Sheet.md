# Source sheet: Barsauma of Nisibis

Researcher's sheet for batch 5 (2026-11-18). The job: the frontier bishop and the synods that set the Church of the East on its own path in Persia. The writer uses the outline, the shapes and the standard only; this sheet is the evidence behind the outline.

**Witness kinds and gaps.**
- OWN = Barsauma's six letters to the catholicos Acacius and to bishops (SO 35200–35757). Chabot dates them between August 485 and 491, most of them September 485 to February 486 (SO-ed 35643–35656). They are the nearest witness.
- CONTEMP (institutional, his own church) = the acts of the synod of Acacius, February 486 (SO 19440–20060) and of the synod of Babai, November 497 (SO 20286–20560).
- CONTEMP-NEAR, hostile = Babken's Armenian letter from Dvin, about 506 (TM); Simeon of Beth Arsham's letter (SIM), a Miaphysite bishop of the next generation (census dates Simeon c. 497–524; LAB 8723–8725 calls him "presque contemporain").
- LATER, friendly (~110 years) = Barhadbeshabba, end of the 500s (CFS-ed 20826–20828; CEN entry for Barhadbeshabba).
- LATER (~120 years) = the synod of Gregory, 605 (SO 31393), which cites Beth Lapat.
- LATER, hostile (~150 years, oral) = Maruta of Tagrit's letter, copied by Michael the Syrian (1100s) (MS 33136–33142, 33430–33437).
- LATER (~300 years) = the compiler of the *Synodicon*, about 770–790 by Chabot (SO-ed 20275–20277).
- LATE = Elias of Nisibis (1000s), Mari (1100s), Bar Hebraeus (1200s, hostile), 'Amr (1300s).
- MODERN = Chabot, Labourt, Scher, Connolly, Guidi, Abbeloos and Lamy.

## 1. Setting

S1. **Nisibis, a frontier city.** Chabot: "Nisibe était sur la frontière des deux empires, elle appartenait aux Perses depuis la honteuse cession qu'en avait faite Jovinien en 363" (SO-ed 35296, 35299, 35292; split by the OCR). Editor's note; Widely Accepted. Barsauma's own letter places Nisibis between Roman and Persian armies and calls the city liable to slip from Persia (E7, E8). Spoken: "Nisibis, a city on the border between the Roman and Persian empires".

S2. **Century and dates.** The synod of Beth Lapat is April 484 (SO-ed 19607–19609; SO 31683 "au mois de nisan de la 27° année de Pérôz"); his reconciliation August 485 (SO-ed 19623–19634); the synod of Acacius February 486 (SO-ed 19718). "The late 400s." Documented.

S3. **Tradition, spoken name.** Census: "The Church of the East under Persia", shortName "Church of the East" (CEN, tradition entry `the-church-of-the-east-under-persia`). Spoken: **"the Church of the East, the church of the Persian empire"**. Its head is the catholicos at Seleucia-Ctesiphon, the Persian capital on the Tigris (SO 35473 "la sainte église de Séleucie et Ctésiphon").

S4. **Already self-governing before him.** The census tradition entry: "In 424 the bishops of the Persian Empire ruled that their catholicos at Seleucia-Ctesiphon answered to no bishop beyond their own frontier" (CEN). So independence of jurisdiction is not Barsauma's work. Widely Accepted (census sourced to SO).

S5. **The king.** Peroz, King of kings, who died in 484 (BH-ed 3874–3878, citing Elias: Peroz killed about the eclipse of 14 January, year 795 Greek, 484; SO-ed 19623 "Les années de Balas se comptent à partir du 23 juillet 484"). Documented.

S6. **The wider quarrel.** Barsauma's own letter speaks of "la confusion que Satan a excitée dans le pays des Romains" (SO 35372–35374). Chabot guesses this means Zeno's Henoticon (SO-ed 35412–35416): editor's guess only.

## 2. Events

E1. **Edessa and a Persian school.** Simeon of Beth Arsham lists the Persian students who followed Ibas at the "school of the Persians" in Edessa: "Barfaumas 4 / Mare Carduenfis fervus" (SIM 74345–74346; "4" is a note mark; also in BH 3681–3683, quoting Simeon: "Barsau-/mas, Marce [Marae] Carduensis servus, qui « natans in ni-/disn [nidis] dieebatur [dicebatur]"). Hostile, near-contemporary; Documented as Simeon's statement. Barhadbeshabba, friendly, in his life of Narsai: "Mar Barsauma, eveque de Nisihe [Nisibe], etait alle a Edesse ... Comme Mar Baisaiuna [Barsauma] etait un homme (a Tesprit [l'esprit]) penetrant, il iui sutlit [lui suffit] de peu de temps pour apprendre et expliquer les (saints) Livres avec leur sens, mieux que tous les autres" (HE 36538–36542; OCR accents lost; the OCR name form is my reading as Barsauma). Documented as his praise; not used in the outline.

E2. **Expelled, then bishop.** Simeon: when Ibas died (457) the Persians were driven from Edessa, and those expelled became bishops in Persia, "nefarius Barsaumus Nisibis" (BH 3776–3783, quoting Simeon). Barhadbeshabba, more simply: "Barsauma vint à Nisibe et fut élu évêque" (CFS 20607). When he became bishop is **Contested**: D1.

E3. **Peroz's man for Roman affairs.** Mari: "Produnt Peroz a Babuaeo hominem sagacem petiisse qui ipsum de rebus romanorum edoceret, et a Babuaeo Barsaumam fuisse electum" (MARI 14356–14357, as printed). LATE (1100s), friendly. Contested. Chabot hedges further: he became bishop "grace à la faveur du roi Pérôz ... qui le fit même, semble-t-il, marzban ou gouverneur de la ville" (SO-ed 20232–20234). Editor's hedged reading; Inferential-Thin.

E4. **He welcomes Narsai (clause only; sibling's job).** "Barsauma pria Narsaï de vouloir bien rester chez lui et de fonder une école dans la ville et lui promit son secours" (CFS 20615–20617); the school counts "l'admirable Narsaï et Mar Bar Sauma qui l'établirent et qui l'affermirent" (CFS 21698–21699). LATER, friendly; Documented as the school's account.

E5. **The synod of Beth Lapat, April 484.** Barsauma's own first letter: he and his bishops "nous avons entièrement et complètement détruit et annulé le volume que nous fîmes dans le Beit Houzayé, et dans lequel se trouvent des murmures, des blâmes, des chefs d'accusation et des témoignages contre le bienheureux Baboui, catholicos" (SO 35255–35258). OWN. Documented. His third letter: "Dans le temps que Satan a suscité une querelle entre moi et Mar Baboui, je suis monté et j'ai cédé à l'instigation de tous les évêques délinquants contre lesquels il voulait porter un jugement ... nous nous réunîmes dans la ville de Beit Laphat, que nous y tînmes un synode et statuâmes des canons par la passion humaine" (SO 35397–35406). OWN. Documented. The Armenian letter of about 506 names the leaders as "Akak und Barsauma und Mani und Johanan und Paule und Mika" holding synods "einmal in Guntsapuh" (TM 36716; Beth Lapat per TM-ed 36721) from "das 27. Jahr der Herrschaft des Peroz" (TM 36702–36715). CONTEMP-NEAR, hostile. Documented as that letter's statement. The synod of Gregory (605) names its heads as "le bienheureux Mar Bar Çauma, évêque métropolitain de Nisibe, et Nânai, évêque métropolitain de Pherat" (SO 31684–31686). LATER.

E6. **What Beth Lapat decided, as far as held.** Its acts are lost; the compiler left them out (I1).
  - On Theodore of Mopsuestia, as quoted in 605: "À cause des bruits malveillants répandus contre lui par les hérétiques en divers lieux, personne de nous ne doit concevoir de doute au sujet de ce saint homme. Car, pendant sa vie, il fut considéré comme le plus illustre et le premier parmi les docteurs de la religion" (SO 31687–31690). LATER witness to the text; Documented as the 605 synod's quotation.
  - On marriage: the synod of 497 accepts Beth Lapat "mais seulement sur ce point", the point being clergy marriage (SO 20484–20486; see I2). CONTEMP. Documented that Beth Lapat legislated on marriage; its wording is not held.
  - Fragments quoted by Elias of Nisibis (1000s): canon XII against buying ordination, and a canon "Qu'il ne convient pas que l'évêque métropolitain, c'est-à-dire le chef des évêques, soit établi par les écrits du roi" (SO 41825–41838). LATE. Chabot thinks these "les seuls restes du fameux concile" (SO-ed 41784–41786) and that "extérieurs" means recourse to the Byzantine emperor (SO-ed 41876–41878): editor's guess.

E7. **The catholicos Babowai's death, 484. Contested (D2).** Babowai (Chabot's "Baboui") was hanged by a finger on Peroz's order. Mari: Babowai wrote to the Roman emperor Zeno lamenting "tradidit nos Deus in manum regni impii"; "Barsaumas metropolita Nisibis vero astu ea potitus illam ad P^^-roz [Peroz] fransmisif [transmisit]" (MARI 14445–14447); Mari elsewhere: "Ferunt Barsaumam mox faclum [factum] doluisse", and that a translator, Isaias, twisted its sense (MARI 14362–14363). Barsauma's own letters say only "Satan a suscité une querelle entre moi et Mar Baboui" (SO 35397–35398).

E8. **Reconciliation with Acacius, August 485.** The compiler: Barsauma came to Beth Edrai to the catholicos Acacius, "fit une rétractation de tout ce qu'il avait fait ... dans la ville de Beit Laphat" and promised to annul it before all the bishops at Seleucia-Ctesiphon; Acacius "reçut Bar Çauma après sa rétractation : ils firent la paix" (SO 20186–20197). Acacius was his fellow student at Edessa (MARI 14389–14390 "una cum Barsauma in schola edessena literis vacavit"; BH 4264–4266). Documented.

E9. **The frontier letter (letter 2).** "voici deux années successives que nous sommes affligés d'une disette de pluie" (SO 35277–35279); southern tribes raided "même dans le territoire des Romains" (SO 35279–35282); "Nous ... persuadâmes au général de venir à Nisibe, près du marzban; et il fut reçu par lui en grand honneur. Tandis qu'ils étaient ensemble à boire, manger et se réjouir, les Tou'ayé ont eu l'audace d'aller, au nombre de quatre cents cavaliers, fondre sur les villages inférieurs des Romains" (SO 35323–35328); "le général et les officiers ... s'emportèrent contre nous, croyant que nous avions agi avec astuce" (SO 35330–35332). He cannot come to the synod "à cause des difficultés au sujet des frontières" (SO 35376–35377). OWN. Documented. Letter 4: the marzban told the king "l'évêque de Nisibe connaît cette affaire des frontières: sa présence est très nécessaire" (SO 35590–35591). OWN reporting the marzban. Documented. "Marzban" = the Persian governor (SO-ed footnote and LAB 8167).

E10. **The plea for force (letter 3).** "Maintenant, Nisibe est agitée, troublée et ballottée comme une mer tumultueuse ... je ne resterai point dans l'épiscopat de Nisibe; Nisibe elle-même ne restera pas soumise au grand empire des Perses" (SO 35479–35483). "écris impérieusement une lettre d'excommunication et frappe-les, comme le médecin qui ampute un malade ... Menace-les dans tes lettres de les dénoncer au roi et à ses officiers, s'ils ne rentrent pas en eux-mêmes" (SO 35508–35512). And the obedience: "J'ai appris en effet, par les choses mêmes qui me sont arrivées que, tant que Ni-[sibe] ne sera pas sous l'obéissance et la direction de celui qui [siège] sur le trône de la sainte église de Séleucie et Ctésiphon, la région orientale éprouvera des dommages et de graves calamités" (SO 35467–35479, split by OCR; Labourt reads "celui qui siège sur le trône", LAB 8849). OWN. Documented. Chabot thinks letter 3 may belong to another time (SO-ed 35625–35628).

E11. **The synod of Acacius, February 486, Seleucia.** Barsauma was not there: "ni Bar Çauma ni les évêques de sa province ne figurent au synode de 486" (SO-ed 35650–35651); he wrote in advance: "nous acceptons et recevons ... toutes les choses que vous ferez dans votre synode ... et ne nous attendez pas" (SO 35596–35602). OWN. Its canons (CONTEMP, Documented):
  - Canon I: "la confession des deux natures de la divinité et de l'humanité. Personne de nous ne doit oser introduire le mélange, la commixtion ou la confusion ... nous réunissons en une seule majesté et en une seule adoration les diversités des natures ... la confession d'un Dieu parfait et d'un homme parfait" (SO 19739–19746).
  - Canon II: wandering ascetics in black must leave the towns for the monasteries and obey the bishops and visitors (SO 19752–19795).
  - Canon III: "qu'aucun évêque n'établisse des obstacles ou des difficultés pour empêcher le mariage" (SO 19812–19813); deacons may marry (SO 19871–19872), and new deacons are to be examined on "leur union légale" (SO 19878); a priest who wishes to marry, or to remarry after his wife dies, "son évêque n'a pas le droit de s'opposer à son désir" (SO 19947–19951). The reason given: adultery and fornication had brought "le mépris et la dérision" from outsiders (SO 19814–19817). Labourt reads canon III as "renouvelé d'un de ceux de l'anti-concile de Beit Lapat" (LAB 8677–8678): modern reconstruction, consistent with I2.

E12. **Quarrel again, 491.** The synod of 497 annuls the anathemas thrown "depuis la 4° année de Qawâd ... soit par le bienheureux Mar Acacius ... contre l'excellent Mar Bar Çauma, évêque de Nisibe ... soit par Mar Bar Çauma lui-même ... contre Mar Acacius" (SO 20491–20498). The fourth year of Kawad began July 491 (SO-ed 20513–20520). CONTEMP. Documented. So his promise in letter 3 to be "pour toujours" a disciple of that see (SO 35378–35384; 35463–35467) did not hold.

E13. **His marriage. Contested (D4).** Mari: "Barsaumas ilaque [itaque] Mauunaeam [Mammaeam] monialera [monialem] uxorem duxil [duxit], nuptiasque episcopis ac presbyteris hcitas [licitas] fecit" (MARI 14386–14387). Bar Hebraeus, hostile: "ipse Barsumas hujusmodi apud se in cella mulierem servabat, quam sine pudore uxorem legitimam vocabat" (BH 3760–3763).

E14. **Death.** "La date de sa mort est inconnue. Elle est postérieure à [4]91" (SO-ed 20210–20212; OCR "41"). By 496 his successor Hosea held the see (CFS-ed 20820–20829; LAB 8902; GUI 9884–9888). A later hand in Elias's chronicle: "Eo obiit Barsauma metropolita Nisibis et institutus est post eum Hosea" (ELI 5291–5292; margin note, year not certain in this edition). Bar Hebraeus gives two reports: nuns in Tur Abdin killed him "clavibus cellarum suarum"; "alii vero Nisibi in ecclesia Mar Jacobi ejusdem sepulcrum ostendunt" (BH 4547–4552). LATE, hostile. Mari: he died while Acacius, back from the Romans, meant to depose him (MARI 14234–14238). LATE.

## 3. Impact, heaviest first

I1. **The book that left him out.** The *Synodicon*'s compiler: "La raison pour laquelle nous n'avons pas inséré ce synode dans ce livre avec les synodes des Pères de l'Orient est celle-ci" (SO 20183) ... "puisque Bar Çauma lui-même, de son vivant, attesta qu'il avait été tenu par mauvaise volonté et par passion humaine, que sa constitution fut contraire aux canons de l'Église" (SO 20257–20259); "les originaux avec leurs signatures sont conservés dans les archives du patriarcat" (SO 20253). LATER (~300 years). Documented. He kept the letters of retraction instead (SO-ed 34786–34806).

I2. **Married clergy, from the patriarch down (497).** The synod of Babai, by the king's order, made "une réforme relativement au mariage légitime" (SO 20468–20471): "nous avons permis que : depuis le patriarche jusqu'au dernier de la hiérarchie, chacun puisse ouvertement contracter un mariage chaste, avec une seule femme, pour engendrer des enfants" (SO 20480–20482). "Nous admettons aussi, mais seulement sur ce point, l'assemblée qui a eu lieu dans le pays du Beit Houzayé, dans la ville de Beit Laphat, en l'an 27 de Pérôz" (SO 20484–20486). CONTEMP. Documented. Mari, later: married clergy "ut Acacii ac Barsaumao [Barsaumae] mens fuerat" (MARI 14249). LATE. **What he defended:** married clergy.

I3. **Theodore made the church's sure teacher (605).** The synod of Gregory recalls "la divine assemblée qui eut lieu dans le Beit Houzayé, dans la ville de Beit Laphat ... dont les chefs ... étaient le bienheureux Mar Bar Çauma" (SO 31682–31685, OCR "Bar CGauma"), quotes its ruling on Theodore (E6), and adds: "Et nous aussi, d'accord avec eux, nous avons décrété et défini que ... tous ceux qui remplissent l'office de docteur dans les églises ... doivent méditer les écrits de cet homme" (SO 31723–31726). LATER (~120 years). Documented. **What he defended:** Theodore's reading of Scripture.

I4. **Neighbours saw a separate church.** Babken's Armenian letter (about 506): the true faith held "bis in das 27. Jahr der Herrschaft des Peroz. Aber zu jener Zeit erschien der Teig der Bosheit" (TM 36702–36705), naming "Akak und Barsauma" (TM 36711) holding synods (TM 36716); the Nestorians claim Greeks and Armenians share their faith and "der Kanones, welche Akak und Barsauma und Mani und Paule und ihre Genossen aufgestellt haben" (TM 36823–36832), which the Armenians deny (TM 36838–36842). Simeon of Beth Arsham: "His in Synodis varios novz [novae] fidei .Canones excogitantes , à vera San-/Gtorum [Sanctorum]: Patrum Fide fefe fegrega-/runt [sese segregarunt] : ab eajinquam,Fide3quam [ab ea, inquam, Fide, quam] ipfi [ipsi] à Sanctis Apoftolis [Apostolis] traditam accepe-/rant" (SIM 75090–75099). CONTEMP-NEAR, hostile. Documented as their statements.

I5. **The hostile memory: the man who set Persia's faith apart.** Bar Hebraeus (1200s), copying Michael's source: "Tunc aif [ait] fiarsumfs [Barsumas] Nislbenus [Nisibenus] ad Pherozem regem : « nisi diyersa [diversa] fiat fides christianorum in regionibus tuis commordktium [commorantium] a fide christianorum regionum Graecarum, nunqpiam [nunquam] illi cor suum et' affectum erga te sincerum habebunt. «" (BH 3852–3858); Peroz consented, "praecepit ut quod vellet ageret" (BH 3858–3859). Maruta's letter, in Michael: Barsauma told the king Nestorius was exiled for telling the Romans to submit to Persia, and "Si tu me confies les chrétiens qui sont dans ton empire, je leur enseignerai sa doctrine, et ils ne seront plus les espions des Romains" (MS 33545–33553). Both say he then went with soldiers through the north, killing those who refused (BH 3956–4073; MS 33567–33590); "Ferunt numerum a blasphemo Bar-/sula 3 Nisibeno interfectorum ad sep-/tem millia et septingentos ascendisse" (BH 4073–4075; "3" is a note mark; Bar Hebraeus's mocking "Barsula", "son of the sandal", BH-ed 4089–4091); Michael "sept mille huit cents" (MS 33650–33651). LATER/LATE, hostile. Maruta admits: "nous écrivons rapidement ce que nous avons appris de vive voix de vieillards véridiques" (MS 33433–33437). Chabot: the account "perd une grande partie de son autorité ... par l'aveu sincère de l'auteur ... plus d'un siècle et demi après les événements" (MS-ed 33473–33479). Labourt rejects the numbers: "il ne faudrait pas admettre les chiffres fantaisistes de Barhébraeus" (LAB 8213–8214). Documented as hostile claims; the killings are Contested.

I6. **Modern views of his part.** Connolly: "With the foundation of this School began, undoubtedly, the definite formation of what is now known as the Nestorian Church, severed from the communion of the Churches of the Roman Empire" (CON 4484–4487). Labourt: his work was "double : une œuvre personnelle, satisfaire son ambition et se rendre indépendant des patriarches; une œuvre proprement ecclésiastique, lutter contre le monophysisme et légiférer sur le mariage des clercs" (LAB 8050–8054). Dominant Modern Reconstruction; editors' views, not the sources'.

## 4. Links

- **Narsai of Nisibis** (census name; on the tree): welcomed and kept at Nisibis by Barsauma; the school counts both as founders (CFS 20613–20617, 21698–21699). Sibling: a clause here.
- **Simeon of Beth Arsham** (on the tree): wrote a letter against him and his synods (SIM 73308, 75048–75099).
- **Philoxenus of Mabbug** (on the tree): Bar Hebraeus and Michael say he wrote two books against Barsauma's canons (BH 4126–4131; MS 33641–33643). LATE, hostile; Contested.
- **Elias of Nisibis** (on the tree): quoted Beth Lapat's canons (SO-ed 41781–41784).
- **Barhadbeshabba 'Arbaia of Halwan** (on the tree): wrote of him (CFS, HE).
- **Mar Aba I**, **Babai the Great**: no direct tie held. Babai the Great is a different man from both catholicoi named Babai/Babowai.
- Acacius, catholicos: classmate, rival, then reconciled; received his letters. Not on the tree.
- Babowai (Chabot "Baboui"), catholicos 457–484: the target of Beth Lapat. Not on the tree.

## 5. Where sources disagree or come late

D1. **When he became bishop.** Chabot: after the expulsion at Ibas's death in 457 (SO-ed 20231–20232). Abbeloos and Lamy cite Simeon for the same and a Vatican manuscript for 435 (BH-ed 3651–3660). Scher cites Elias and Ebedjesu for 435 (CFS-ed 20624–20626, 20820). The Elias edition held has a margin note "Institutus est Bar (l. Barsauma) episc. Nisibis" at the year 767 Greek, about 456 (ELI 5230). Contested.

D2. **His part in Babowai's death.** Own letters: only a quarrel stirred by Satan (SO 35397). Mari (1100s): he passed on the letter, then regretted it (MARI 14356–14364, 14446–14447). 'Amr (1300s): the physician Gabriel twisted the letter; Barsauma, back from an embassy to Zeno, exposed him (AMR 13949–13958). Maruta via Michael and Bar Hebraeus: he had Babowai killed himself (MS 33556–33562; BH 3836–3850). Chabot's note gives 'Amr for "sur la délation de Bar Çauma" (SO-ed 20206–20207). Labourt prefers an anonymous account in which "Il y en a qui disent" he sent the letter to Peroz (LAB 8387–8405). Contested.

D3. **Force.** Chabot (editor): royal troops "à l'aide desquelles il propagea par la violence ses doctrines" (SO-ed 20234–20236), built on the later writers. The killings and numbers come only from the hostile tradition (I5). Labourt: "Il est possible qu'il y ait eu quelque sang versé" (LAB 8212). Simeon, near-contemporary and hostile, does not charge him with the killing or the marriage (LAB 8716–8722, Labourt's observation). Contested.

D4. **Marriage.** Mari: married the nun Mamoe (Mammaea) lawfully, perhaps chastely, at the king's wish (MARI 14386–14387, 14210–14211); Bar Hebraeus: a concubine (BH 3760–3763). Barhadbeshabba tells a quarrel with Narsai over a woman (sibling material). Contested.

D5. **Chronology of Mari, 'Amr, Bar Hebraeus.** Chabot: Bar Hebraeus "est si inexact dans son récit de l'histoire de Bar Çauma" (SO-ed 41058); Mari's chronology "généralement fort peu exacte" (SO-ed 35552–35556).

D6. **Not to confuse.** The archimandrite Barsauma at Ephesus 449 (MS 2134–2143); Barsauma of Susa (LAB 13790); the catholicos Babai of 497 and Babai the Great (CEN).
