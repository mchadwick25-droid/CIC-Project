# Second Readings (batch 5, B5-01 to B5-61)

## Method

For each id I set our wording (`Second_Reading_Key.md`, as it stands in the brief) beside the blind rendering (`Second_Readings_Blind_A.md`, `_B.md`, `_C.md`). Where they differed, I opened the cited lines in `cic/texts/`, and the original-language lines where the request pointed there. I also read the story's card `quotationOriginals` entry (`printed`, `original`, `uncertain`), and the sentence of the brief that holds the quote. Where the blind reader noted a scan misread, a stopped sentence or a word-count quirk, I checked the scan at those lines to confirm it is the same passage. Where our wording is a fragment or is cut, I judged only the words we use, and whether the cut changes the sense in the brief's sentence.

- **SAME**: same meaning.
- **MINOR**: a small difference a listener would not take differently.
- **MEANING**: a listener would understand something different.

Requests B5-13, 55, 56, 57, 58 and 61 sent the blind reader to the original language, while the key cites the printed English lines. The card holds both for each (`original` / `originalLines`), and both sets of lines are the same passage.
