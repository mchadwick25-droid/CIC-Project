# Second Readings, Blind A (batch 5, B5-01 to B5-20)

## B5-01
**Source (English):** "to entitle the book with the title of this man’s name, lest on account of his own name, in that many abhorred it, they should not /be willing to read [it] and be converted to the truth" (37 words)
**Rendering:** to give the book this man's name as its title, so that people would not refuse to read it and turn to the truth because of the author's own name, since many hated that name. (The "/" before "be" is the edition's page or folio marker, not part of the text.)

## B5-02
**Source (English):** "Mother of God" (3 words)
**Rendering:** Mother of God. (The scan prints a stray space after the opening quotation mark: "‘ Mother of God’".)

## B5-03
**Source (English):** "Give me, my prince, the earth purged of heretics, and I will give you heaven as a recompense." (18 words)
**Rendering:** My prince, give me the earth cleared of heretics, and I will give you heaven in return.

## B5-04
**Source (English):** "Mother of man" (3 words)
**Rendering:** Mother of man (that is, mother of a human being).

## B5-05
**Source (English):** "Mother of Christ" (3 words)
**Rendering:** Mother of Christ.

## B5-06
**Source (Greek):** "ἀχώριστος τοῦ φαινομένου ϑεός, διὰ τοῦτο τοῦ μὴ χωριζομένου τὴν τιμὴν οὐ χωρίζω. χωρίζω τὰς φύσεις, ἀλλ᾽ ἑνῶ τὴν τεροσχύνησιν." (20 words)
**Rendering:** God is not separated from the one who is seen. For that reason I do not separate the honour of the one who is not separated. I separate the natures, but I unite the worship. (I read the scan's "τεροσχύνησιν" as "προσκύνησιν", worship or adoration; the scan has misread the opening letters. The line break "χω-ρίζω" is joined.)

## B5-07
**Source (English):** "as though it were some terrible phantom" (7 words)
**Rendering:** as if it were some frightening ghost.

## B5-08
**Source (English):** "a mangled hand and side" (5 words)
**Rendering:** a hand and side badly injured.

## B5-09
**Source (English):** "But may Nestorius be anathematized; but may they say what I pray them to say concerning God." (17 words)
**Rendering:** Let Nestorius be cursed and cast out, then; only let them say about God what I beg them to say. (The scan's "/" before "But" is the edition's page marker, left out of the count.)

## B5-10
**Source (English):** "Mar Nestorius the persecuted martyr" (5 words)
**Rendering:** Saint Nestorius, the persecuted martyr. ("Mar" is the Syriac title for a saint or bishop, "my lord". The scan's line break "perse-cuted" is joined.)

## B5-11
**Source (English):** "Nestorius, then, followed them, and not they him," (8 words)
**Rendering:** So Nestorius followed them; they did not follow him.

## B5-12
**Source (English):** "for He came in likeness of fire on the bush in the wilderness, and the fire kept playing on the shrub yet was it not consumed." (26 words)
**Rendering:** for He came in the form of fire on the bush in the desert. The fire kept flickering over the shrub, yet the shrub did not burn up. (The scan prints "play-ing" across a line break with a stray "^" note mark, and "ou the shrub"; I read "ou" as "on".)

## B5-13
**Source (Greek):** "οὐ γὰρ πρῶτον ἄνθρωπος ἐγεννήθη κοινὸς ἐκ τῆς ἁγίας παρθένου, εἶθ᾽ οὕτω καταπεφοίτηκεν ἐπ᾿ αὐτὸν ὁ Λόγος" ἀλλ᾽ ἐξ αὐτῆς μήτρας ἑνωθεὶς, ὑπομεῖναι λέγεται γέννησιν σαρκικὴν," (26 words)
**Rendering:** For an ordinary man was not first born of the holy Virgin, with the Word then coming down upon him afterwards. Rather, he was united from the womb itself, and so he is said to have undergone birth in the flesh. (The scan's '"' after "Λόγος" stands for a Greek raised dot (·), a pause mark, not a quotation mark. A margin numeral "2" after "πρῶτον" is left out.)

## B5-14
**Source (English):** "the gold of iniquity" (4 words)
**Rendering:** the gold of wrongdoing (that is, the bribe money).

## B5-15
**Source (English):** "the one nature of the Word" (6 words)
**Rendering:** the one nature of the Word.

## B5-16
**Source (English):** "One Incarnate Nature of the Word" (6 words)
**Rendering:** one nature of the Word, made flesh.

## B5-17
**Source (English):** "no commingling, no confusion" (4 words)
**Rendering:** no mixing together, no blending.

## B5-18
**Source (English):** "the epistle of the holy Cyril (every utterance of whom one would say is a law of the church) to Nestorius," (21 words)
**Rendering:** the letter of the holy Cyril to Nestorius. (One could say that every word Cyril spoke is a law of the church.)

## B5-19
**Source (French):** "par mauvaise volonté et par passion humaine" (7 words)
**Rendering:** out of ill will and human passion.

## B5-20
**Source (French):** "Satan 4 suscité une querelle entre moi et Mar Baboui" (10 words)
**Rendering:** Satan stirred up a quarrel between me and Mar Babowai. (The scan prints "4" for the French verb "a"; I read "Satan a suscité". "Baboui" is the edition's spelling of the name.)
