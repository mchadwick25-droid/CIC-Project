## The three lines (one hearing of `john-calvin.brief.txt`, before reading the rest)

- **Who:** John Calvin, a French scholar who wrote the Institutes to defend believers burned in France. In 1536 Geneva's preacher, Farel, held him in the city with a curse.
- **What happened:** he gave Geneva a written plan for its church: pastors, teachers, deacons, and lay elders who met with the pastors as a church court. The city's councils kept the power to punish. They beheaded a man in 1547, sent Bolsec away for calling Calvin's God a tyrant, and burned Servetus, whose death Calvin wanted. He was voted out in 1538 and was back in 1541, but heard once, nothing says why he went back or that anyone asked him to. He died in 1564.
- **Why the connection matters:** churches in France, meeting in secret in 1559, and in Scotland in 1562 built the same offices and worship. His name became the label for the Reformed family, which began at Zurich before him.
