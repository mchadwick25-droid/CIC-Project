## Worded by me

- **B1 (beat 6).** The check gives the order and most of the words. My text, line 51: "April 1531, the field outside Antwerp, "My name is Tyndale" (M11, paraphrase or a few words); he spoke of exile, hunger and cold, and would not come home on any promise (M11). The king, through his minister, called Tyndale's newest book, his answer to the king's chancellor, Thomas More, full of "seditious, slanderous lies", and said he was glad to have him out of the realm; but the minister added a line saying the king would show mercy if Tyndale submitted (M10a(f), M11; DT 1950–1990, 2072–2084; paraphrase; the book is named by the editor). In May the envoy showed him that line, and Tyndale answered with Q2 (DT 2059–2101). No reply from the king to the offer is held.
