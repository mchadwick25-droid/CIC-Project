- **Who this was:** Augustine, a teacher of public speaking who turned to Christ in a garden in Milan and became bishop of Hippo, a port town in Roman North Africa, where he wrote the Confessions and the City of God.
- **What happened:** A child's voice sent him to Paul's letters and Ambrose baptized him. As bishop he changed his mind and came to hold that God's grace comes first. He backed the emperors' laws to force the Donatists back, and opposed Pelagius, whose followers were condemned and punished by law. He died while the Vandals besieged Hippo, telling them to keep the library.
- **Why the connection matters:** His teaching that grace comes before anything a person does passed through his books to the bishops at Orange in 529, and a thousand years later to Calvin, against Rome, and to Jansen, inside it. It shaped the Western churches and divided them.

## For Mark: does the tightening limit the story, or is it as good?

**What the shorter version loses.** The ordination, Augustine seized and made a priest in tears, is vivid but a side scene; the essence still makes him bishop. Losing the Goths and the "two loves" costs more colour: the sack of Rome is the big event most listeners know, and the two loves were the one moment that showed what the City of God says. Now the book is only named. Losing Luther costs the best-known name on the tree, and a witness your approved heart names.

**Does any loss weaken the heart or the connection?** Not the heart. Grace comes first, and the garden, "But God's grace won" and the Donatists all stand. The connection is a little thinner without Luther. Calvin still carries the reformers against Rome, but the line you approved named both.

**Which holds attention better, heard once?** The shorter one. In v2 the middle stacks Rome, the two cities and Pelagius into one dense paragraph. The tightened version moves cleanly from one dispute to the next.

**My recommendation:** the tightened version, with Luther's two v2 sentences put back (about 4:56). It keeps all your heart named and still moves faster than v2.
