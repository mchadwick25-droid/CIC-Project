# Outline check: Babai the Great

Checker, batch 5, 2026-11-18. Written before reading `Outline.md`.

## My own heart (from census entry, source sheet and what he is known for)

**Essence.** Babai, abbot of the great monastery on Mount Izla near Nisibis and never a bishop, held the Church of the East together through the long years (about seventeen to twenty, contested) when the Persian king Khosrau would allow it no catholicos: at the senior bishops' request he went from monastery to monastery driving out teachers the church condemned (Documented as Thomas of Marga's account). His writings, above all the Book of the Union on how Christ is one, fully God and fully human, were kept and honoured by the church (Documented), and modern historians judge they did most to settle its teaching on Christ (Dominant Modern Reconstruction). That is why he is on the tree.

**Also told.** His harsh reform at Izla: married monks stripped of the habit, expelled with their families, their cells burned; the monk Jacob driven out; the monks' rebuke and the dispersal that, by Thomas, filled the East with monasteries (Beth Abe among them). His refusal of communion to Henana's followers until they cursed Henana; his book against Henana. The 612 hearing before the king, which by his own Life of George was argued by George and Henanisho with the bishops (Seert: Babai helped by his pen); the king's "no head while they proclaim Nestorius". George's crucifixion (615) and Babai's Life of him. In 628 he refused to become catholicos and went back to his cell; Thomas's angel story stays in Thomas's voice. Death about 628 (contested).

## Findings

### BLOCKING

1. **Words to explain, "person"** (Outline line 34). The gloss "one single Christ, one who acts and is worshipped" says more than its source. LAB 15845–15862 says nothing about acting or worship, and "worshipped" is a theological claim a writer could put in the audio. **Correction:** replace the line with: `**person** (in "one person"): ordinary English; "one single Christ", the one Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God (SO-LG 42206–42207 "dans une seule union indivisible de la personne unique, de l'unique Seigneur Jésus-Christ, Fils de Dieu"). Add nothing about how he acts or is worshipped.`

### MINOR

2. **Connection, misattribution** (line 88). "By Babai's own account, ... his book against Henana was accepted by the bishops (E7)": the acceptance is the Chronicle of Seert's account (CS 29560–29562), not Babai's. **Correction:** "By Babai's own account, the bishops put the same teaching before the king in 612 (E6; Chabot: in sense, not word for word); a later chronicle says the bishops accepted his book against Henana (E7, CS 29560–29562); ..."
3. **Connection, count and support** (line 88). It says "three things" but lists four, and "set his church apart from its neighbours" cites no source. **Correction:** change "three things" to "four things", and after "neighbours" add "(CEN tradition `longDescription`: its answer on Christ 'was not Chalcedon's, nor the Miaphysites'')".
4. **Also told, chronology** (line 7). "His reform began at home" implies the Izla reform came before the visitation. No source dates it, and Labourt ties the abuses to his "fréquentes absences" (LAB 17793–17794). **Correction:** "At home, at Izla, his rule was harsh, and its force was real."
5. **"across the north"** (lines 7, 65). Thomas says "the country of the East" (TM 3019–3020); "the north" is Labourt's word (LAB 17828). **Correction:** "founded new monasteries across the East, Thomas says, Jacob's Beth Abe among them".
6. **Jacob's ban, sequence** (lines 7, 55). The ban was laid after Jacob had gone (TM 2722–2725, "when the blessed Jacob had thus gone forth ... had laid a ban"). **Correction (beat 2):** "Then Babai drove out Jacob ... and afterwards laid on him a long written ban"; in Also told: "Then he drove out a gentle monk, Jacob, and afterwards laid a long written condemnation on him".
7. **Do not say, Jacob citation** (line 119). "unjustly" is at TM 2766–2767, in Thomas's account of what the fathers saw. **Correction:** "Thomas says he had 'without fault been driven forth unjustly' (TM 2766–2767)".
8. **Who tells it / beat 2, missing cite** for Thomas writing at Jacob's monastery. **Correction:** add "(TM 609, 4369)".
9. **Words to explain, Magian** (line 39). "with its fire temples and priests called magi" has no cited source. **Correction:** "Magian / Zoroastrian: 'the Persian state religion' (KH 2016 'religionem Magocum' [Magorum]; CEN tradition 'Zoroastrian shah')"; drop the temples and priests.
10. **Words to explain, missing passing words**:
    - **Seleucia** (beat 5): add "Seleucia: one of the twin royal cities on the Tigris, the Persian capital (KH 2019 'Behardasir (Seleucia)'); or say 'the capital'".
    - **the fathers** (Do not say, from TM 2570): add "the fathers: the monastery's senior monks".
11. **hypostasis gloss** (line 33). "fully real in its own right" leans toward two separate beings, which is the point opponents pressed. **Correction:** "say 'each nature real and complete, keeping what is its own' (SO-LG 42410 'gardant leurs propriétés'; WIG 13851–13853)".
