## The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Babai, a monk and abbot of the Great Monastery on Mount Izla near Nisibis, in the Church of the East under the Persian king, in the early 600s. He was never a bishop.
- **What happened:** for close to twenty years the king allowed the church no head. The senior bishops had Babai visit the monasteries and drive out those they judged heretics. He ran a harsh reform at Izla, at a real cost. The bishops set their faith before the king in 612, and in 628 Babai refused to become head of the church.
- **Why the connection matters:** his *Book of the Union* set out how Christ is one, God and man: Christ died, God did not. That teaching set his church apart, and historians judge his books helped make it the church's settled teaching.

All three lines can be filled from one hearing. The third line only comes together at the 612 paragraph, about two and three-quarter minutes in (see BLOCKING 1).

## Findings

### BLOCKING

1. **The essence is not complete until word 423.** The approved heart and the outline's essence both include that his church kept and honoured his books, with the historians' judgment that they settled its teaching. In the brief this comes only at the end of the 612 paragraph. The first 150 words tell what he did, but not what it changed or why he is on the tree. **Change:** after "He also wrote the Book of the Union, on how God and man are joined in the one Christ." add: "His church kept and honored his books, and historians judge that they helped settle its teaching on Christ." Then, so that the judgment is not told twice, end the 612 paragraph with: "Two centuries on, Thomas counted eighty-four of his works still honored in the church." To hold the essence near 150 words, open with "Thomas of Marga tells how the Persian king Khosrau swore by the Sun, his god, ..." in place of the first two sentences. That keeps the witness and the oath and saves three words. **Do not say line to add to the outline** (the outline left room for this: its essence holds the books' keeping, but What came 1 says "Tell it inside beat 4 or straight after it", and beat 1 is where the essence must be said): `Not the books' keeping, or historians' judgment that they settled the church's teaching, held back to beat 4. Say both in the essence, within the first 150 words; beat 4 then gives Thomas's count (TM 4189–4190), not the judgment again.`

### MINOR

1. **"They were let back only after cutting Henana off from the church with a solemn curse."** Babai's words (SO 42217–42219, "tant qu'ils n'avaient pas anathématisé Henana ... on ne leur donnait pas la communion") give a condition: no communion until they cursed him. They do not say that any of them did curse him and were readmitted. **Change:** "They could come back only by cutting Henana off from the church with a solemn curse."
2. **"many left to found monasteries across the East"** gives a purpose the source does not. Thomas says they were scattered, and then "filled the country of the East with monasteries" (TM 3016–3023); the outline says "Many left. They spread out and founded new monasteries". **Change:** "and many left, and founded monasteries across the East, among them Jacob's, where Thomas would later write." No words saved or added.
3. **"That answer set his church apart from its neighbors."** It follows "the king answered" by three sentences, so a listener can hear it as the king's answer. **Change:** "That teaching set his church apart from its neighbors."
