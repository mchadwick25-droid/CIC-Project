# Who scan, era 9 (2026-11-12)

Approved to proceed (coordinator, under Mark's full-auto instruction of 2026-11-12). Read-only scan of era 9 (1815–1906 CE, "The Missionary Era") of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Counts, 249 rows (a paired row counts once): **WHO 34, STORY 11, LISTED 50, WANTED 154**. The Spiritualism / New Thought rows (6 rows, 10 people) were removed under Mark's ruling of 2026-11-12 (see "The coordinator's answers", item 4).

Judgment-call rows: 124. Era 9 has no live person entries in the census (John Wesley, the nearest, is era 8), so nothing is flagged on that count. Councils carry no table here; gatherings are named in the rows of their participants and new ones are listed as leads. The Library holds almost nothing of 1815–1906: 154 of the 249 rows are WANTED. No row carries the OUTSIDE WITNESS label, because no secular, Jewish, Muslim, Asian or African historian of these churches is held for the period.

## How the list was built

- Era 9 has 62 census entries (all movements, no person entries).
- Named voices and `people` lists of the entries (old rules), merged by alias.
- Carried forward from earlier eras: nothing was carried to era 9 by name; the era 7 audit sent its post-1650 works to era 8.
- Found in text and corpus-map: shapers named in the era 9 census texts and the corpus-map rows of these entries.
- Six drafting workers (Sonnet) sorted the candidates by region and opened the cited lines. One Opus subagent then opened the cited lines for every WHO and STORY row (33), checked every WANTED row for absence (168), and read 24 LISTED rows (`Opus_Check_Notes.md`).

## Rulings applied

- The standard of eras 1 to 7 (Decision-Log 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12), including the Melanchthon rule of era 7: where a person's own held work, plus a following or held witnesses, carries a tellable life, the row is WHO with a thin life flagged, not WANTED.
- Recorders are WHO with a thin own life flagged (era 2). Hostile inside writers are WHO as recorders only where a life can be told, else LISTED (Gui, Walsingham, Knighton). They are never an outside witness.
- Popes and rulers are STORY unless they have their own voice. A later retelling alone gives LISTED. Pairs are one judgment-call row.
- Era is by date of main work: works mainly before 1815 go to era 8, after 1906 to era 10, listed under "Other eras by date" with no verdict.
- "Major?" column (proposal only). The map shows up to 4 names per tradition and 5 per river, chosen from Whos. Of the 51 "yes" marks on WANTED rows, none can reach the map until a source is held; 14 "yes" marks sit on WHO rows.
