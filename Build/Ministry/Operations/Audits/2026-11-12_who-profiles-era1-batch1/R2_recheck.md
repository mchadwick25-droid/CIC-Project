## 3. New faults in changed text

No new blocking finding. New minor points:

- **N1 MINOR (Josephus).** "William Whiston, who translated Josephus in the eighteenth century" (brief and card) takes its date from the vendored file's metadata header (l.5, "a translation first made by Whiston in the eighteenth century"). The date is not stated in the text itself. It is acceptable as an identifier.
- **N2 MINOR (Marcion).** "Tertullian, his fiercest opponent, even speaks of 'the continence of Marcion.'" "Fiercest" is an unearned superlative that no named witness supplies. "Even" keeps a small rhetorical beat in the closing line. Holl cites Tertullian's *sanctissimus* (Holl l.7504), and this English line from Praescr. 30 is a fair stand-in for it. Write "Tertullian, who wrote five books against him, speaks of ...".
- **N3 MINOR (Valentinus).** "with a pair called Truth near the top". Truth is one member of a pair. In Irenaeus the pair is Father and Truth; in Hippolytus it is Nous and Aletheia (anf05 l.9209). Write "with Truth in one of the highest pairs".
- **N4 MINOR (Valentinus).** The brief says "Two opponents, Irenaeus and Hippolytus of Rome, describe the same teaching" with no caveat. The card says Hippolytus "may lean on Irenaeus in part", but no witness is named for that. Either the translator note supports it, or it is reviewer advice that the drafter has stated as fact. Name the basis or drop the clause. The identifier "Hippolytus of Rome" occurs in anf05, which also calls him Bishop of Portus near Rome.
- **N5 MINOR (Hermas).** "Then come the commands." is a short transitional beat line. "would not allow adulterers even that second chance" follows "repent but once", so the listener hears "one chance" and then "that second chance" for the same thing. That is term drift (item 9) unless the brief explains that Tertullian counts repentance after baptism as the second.
- **N6 MINOR (Clement).** "The followers of Valentinus ... divided people differently." This floats briefly, but the next sentence gives it content, so it passes. Noted only.
