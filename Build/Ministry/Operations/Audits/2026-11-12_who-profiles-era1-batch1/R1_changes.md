# Changes made after R1 (drafter's list)

Previous versions are kept in scratchpad/r1/. Every brief, card and records.json changed. Audio scripts were rebuilt from the briefs.

## Blocking findings
- Josephus B1: card's "known only from Josephus" line replaced with Josephus plus Suetonius, Vespasian 5; Suetonius added to sources; record re-tagged Widely Accepted.
- Josephus B2: closing changed. Brief now ends on Whiston's own admission ("of late been greatly questioned by many"), and the card's "still argued" now reads "argued in McGiffert's day, in 1890".
- Josephus B3: brief now gives McGiffert's four views, including the wholly-genuine view he called untenable and Ewald's view; Whiston is in a separate paragraph on his own.
- Josephus B4: card now says the critics doubted the whole phrase naming James as the brother of Jesus. The brief quotes Whiston's wording and names it as Whiston's translation.
- Ignatius B1: brief and card now end the letters dispute with McGiffert in 1890 saying Zahn and Lightfoot settled it for the shorter Greek; card adds that some still denied them; record re-tagged.
- Ignatius B2: Romans quote restored in full ("both by land and sea, both by night and day").
- Marcion B1: bishop's-son story now credited to Against All Heresies (printed with Tertullian) and Epiphanius; Sinope alone left as Epiphanius-only; records split.
- Marcion B2: closing now names Holl's argument (Tertullian's silence) and ends on Tertullian's "the continence of Marcion" (anf03 Prescription 30).
- Clement B1: brief now says Eusebius writes that all were driven away, and that McGiffert reads Clement into that line and dates it 202; card matches; record re-tagged Dominant Modern Reconstruction.
- Clement B2: card count changed to "a few chapters of Eusebius"; sources list Book 6 chapters 3, 6, 11, 13, 14.
- Eusebius B1: brief now places Epiphanius once as writing decades later on Athanasius's side; card matches.
- Hermas B1: brief rewritten to 311 words.
- Valentinus B1: opening now holds only Tertullian's story, with no place or time.
- Valentinus B2: teaching paragraph rebuilt: paired powers with Truth from Irenaeus and Hippolytus of Rome (Refutation VI); division by nature from Irenaeus (I.6) and Clement; the fallen power marked as Irenaeus's account.
- Valentinus B3: "the first" dropped from the card.
- Valentinus B4: card now says his words survive in opponents' quotations, chiefly Clement's; Hippolytus added.
