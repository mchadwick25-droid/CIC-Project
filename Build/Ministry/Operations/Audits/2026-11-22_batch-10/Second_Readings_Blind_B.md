## B10-16
Source: `cic/texts/npnf203_theodoret-jerome-gennadius-rufinus.xml`, lines 45775-45780. English translation.

Text read: "Wherever therefore I have found in his books anything contrary to the truth concerning the Trinity which he has in other places spoken of in a strictly orthodox sense, I have either omitted it as a foreign and not genuine expression or set it down in terms agreeing with the rule of faith which we find him constantly assenting to."

## B10-17
Source: `cic/texts/jerome_opera-pl23-part1-with-regula-pachomii-lat_migne1883.txt`, lines 31112-31115. Latin.

Text read: "[in libris περὶ ᾿Αρχῶν] simpliciter quod in Greco habebatur expressi; non ut crederet lector his qui interpretabar; sed ne crederet illis, que tu ante transtuleras."

Rendering: "I simply set down what stood in the Greek, not so that the reader would believe the things I was translating, but so that he would not believe the things you had translated before."

Scan: OCR damage. "Greco" stands for "Graeco"; "que" for "quae"; the Greek title just before reads "᾿Αρλῶν", which must be "Ἀρχῶν" (On First Principles). "his qui interpretabar" is ungrammatical as printed; I read "qui" as an OCR error for "quae" ("the things which I was translating"), which matches the parallel "illis, que [quae] tu ante transtuleras". That reading is an inference from the grammar, not visible in the scan.

## B10-18
Source: `cic/texts/eusebius_werke-bd2-2-historia-ecclesiastica-vi-x-rufinus-x-xi-grc-lat_schwartz-mommsen1908.txt`, lines 27383-27385. Latin.

Text read: "quae nos ita gesta non opinione vulgi, sed ipso Edesio Tyri presbytero postmodum facto, qui Frumentii comes prius fuerat, referente cognovimus."

Rendering: "We learned that these things happened this way not from popular rumour, but from the account of Edesius himself, who had earlier been Frumentius's companion and was later made a presbyter at Tyre."

Scan: clean. "Tyri presbytero postmodum facto" could also be read as "a presbyter of Tyre, made so afterward"; the sense is the same.

## B10-19
Source: same file, lines 27376-27377. Latin.

Text read: "et quem alium invenimus virum talem, in quo sit spiritus dei in ipso sieut in te, qui haec ita possit implere?"

Rendering: "And what other man like this shall we find, one in whom the Spirit of God is, as it is in you, who could carry these things out as you have?"

Scan: "sieut" is OCR for "sicut". The words are Athanasius's, spoken to Frumentius before the council of priests (line 27376). "in quo ... in ipso" repeats the "in whom" in the Latin; I render it once. "ita" ("in this way, so") I render as "as you have"; it could also be "in this way".
