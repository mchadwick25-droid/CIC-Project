**Stay barred:**

- For Eutyches and Theodoret: Leo's "not a trial but a robbery" (leo-of-rome) and "not of judges but of robbers" (dioscorus-of-alexandria): the same judgment on the same council of 449. Chalcedon's definition and the Syriac chronicle's "robe of Christ" (council-of-chalcedon-451): the council's text and its story's ending.

His "is said to have written" (pamphilus-of-caesarea) stays for the same reason. Mark kept this bar (2026-11-23).
