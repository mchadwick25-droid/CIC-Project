# Outline check: Council of Ephesus (431)

## My account of the heart, written before reading Outline.md

**Essence (mine).** In 431 the emperor Theodosius II called the bishops to Ephesus because Nestorius, bishop of Constantinople, refused to call Mary Theotokos, "Mother of God" (the question being how God and a man are one in Christ). Cyril of Alexandria opened the council before John of Antioch and the bishops of Syria arrived, over the protest of the emperor's officer, Count Candidian, and deposed Nestorius in a single day; John's own assembly, days later, deposed Cyril. The council split the church of the East in two, and its verdict on Nestorius held in the empire and was built on at Chalcedon; the Church of the East in Persia never took it as its own. (Documented for the events; the Church of the East's stance Contested.)

**Also told (mine).** What called it: Nestorius's preaching against "Mother of God", Cyril's answer, Celestine of Rome's prior condemnation, the emperor's call for Pentecost with everything held over (Documented; Rome's details EDITOR). How it ran: the sixteen-day wait, John's message "do not put off the Synod" and his reason (road, dead horses) against the council's charge (Contested motive); Candidian's protest of 22 June; three summonses and soldiers at Nestorius's house against his side's mob, shut churches and threats (each side's claim); the sentence "with many tears"; Cyril's torches (his partisan account). Its other business: no other creed beside Nicaea's (Canon VII); Pelagius's condemnation upheld (Documented). Rome's legates confirmed it. The emperor's Count John arrested Nestorius, Cyril and Memnon; the council was dissolved (EDITOR from Count John's report and the edict; Widely Accepted for the month). The peace of 433: John's side's confession, "a union of two natures ... one Christ", Mary Mother of God, at the price of accepting Nestorius's deposition; Theodoret and others did not follow on that (Documented; date Widely Accepted). Impact: verdict held and Chalcedon kept Ephesus's order; Church of the East's distance (Wigram vs Badger, Contested) and memory (Barhadbeshabba, hostile); Nestorius's own "Cyril was everything".
