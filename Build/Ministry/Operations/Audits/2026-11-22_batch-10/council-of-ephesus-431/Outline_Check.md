# Outline check: Council of Ephesus (431)

## My account of the heart, written before reading Outline.md

**Essence (mine).** In 431 the emperor Theodosius II called the bishops to Ephesus because Nestorius, bishop of Constantinople, refused to call Mary Theotokos, "Mother of God" (the question being how God and a man are one in Christ). Cyril of Alexandria opened the council before John of Antioch and the bishops of Syria arrived, over the protest of the emperor's officer, Count Candidian, and deposed Nestorius in a single day; John's own assembly, days later, deposed Cyril. The council split the church of the East in two, and its verdict on Nestorius held in the empire and was built on at Chalcedon; the Church of the East in Persia never took it as its own. (Documented for the events; the Church of the East's stance Contested.)

**Also told (mine).** What called it: Nestorius's preaching against "Mother of God", Cyril's answer, Celestine of Rome's prior condemnation, the emperor's call for Pentecost with everything held over (Documented; Rome's details EDITOR). How it ran: the sixteen-day wait, John's message "do not put off the Synod" and his reason (road, dead horses) against the council's charge (Contested motive); Candidian's protest of 22 June; three summonses and soldiers at Nestorius's house against his side's mob, shut churches and threats (each side's claim); the sentence "with many tears"; Cyril's torches (his partisan account). Its other business: no other creed beside Nicaea's (Canon VII); Pelagius's condemnation upheld (Documented). Rome's legates confirmed it. The emperor's Count John arrested Nestorius, Cyril and Memnon; the council was dissolved (EDITOR from Count John's report and the edict; Widely Accepted for the month). The peace of 433: John's side's confession, "a union of two natures ... one Christ", Mary Mother of God, at the price of accepting Nestorius's deposition; Theodoret and others did not follow on that (Documented; date Widely Accepted). Impact: verdict held and Chalcedon kept Ephesus's order; Church of the East's distance (Wigram vs Badger, Contested) and memory (Barhadbeshabba, hostile); Nestorius's own "Cyril was everything".

## Findings

**1. BLOCKING: misleading implication (Cut first item 1).** Once beat 2 is cut whole, the story tells only that Cyril opened before John arrived and that Candidian begged for "just four days". The listener is never told that the council had already waited sixteen days past the appointed day, or that John's own metropolitans brought word not to put the council off. That tilts the opening against Cyril's side, which the record does not do (ACTS 18579–18597, Cyril's side's own letter; Documented for the wait and the message). **Correction:** replace Cut first item 1 with: "1. Beat 2: Cyril's arrival with fifty bishops and the credit to Socrates and the editors. To the card. Saves 28 (2 facts, 1 witness). Keep, moved to the head of beat 3: they had already waited sixteen days past Pentecost, the appointed day (E4; ACTS 18579–18581; Documented). John's men's message not to put off the council goes to the card." Change the closing line to: "Count before the list 828; the list saves 243; count after 585, at the approved 585." Add to Do not say: "Not that the council 'would not wait' or 'opened at once'. It had waited sixteen days past the appointed day (E4); Candidian asked for four more."

**2. MINOR: Library sweep misses a held primary text from John's side.** `cic/texts/npnf203_theodoret-jerome-gennadius-rufinus.xml` (its Theodoret letters row is in the Antiochene bucket the sweep names) holds Letter CLII, "Report of the (Bishops) of the East to the Emperor, giving information of their proceedings, and explaining the cause of the delay in the arrival of the Bishop of Antioch" (lines 36836–36880), in clean English. It says they deposed Cyril and Memnon and excommunicated the rest "until they shall reject and anathematize the Chapters ... issued by Cyril, which are full of the Eunomian and Arian heresies", and charges that Cyril and Memnon "shut the sacred churches and martyrs' shrines". Beat 4 and Who tells it rest John's voice on Hefele's English, from a historian hostile to John, checked against damaged Latin. **Correction:** add to the sheet's E10: "John's assembly's own report to the emperor, NPNF 3 Letter CLII (npnf203…xml 36847–36880; OWN of John's side, English; the editor's notes at 36839–36846 and 36862–36864 are EDITOR): deposition of Cyril and Memnon; Cyril's Chapters 'full of the Eunomian and Arian heresies'." Cite it in beat 4 beside E10. In Who tells it, change John's line to "in its sentence on Cyril (Mansi's Latin, Hefele's English) and its own report to the emperor (NPNF 3, Letter CLII)". In the Library sweep, under Used, add "NPNF 3 Letter CLII (John's assembly's report)".
