## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who or what:** a council of bishops that the emperor Theodosius II called to Ephesus in 431, to settle whether Mary could be called Mother of God, and so how God and a man are one in Christ.
2. **What happened:** Cyril of Alexandria opened it before John of Antioch's bishops came, over the emperor's officer's protest, and deposed Nestorius. John's assembly deposed Cyril. The emperor arrested Nestorius, Cyril and Memnon and dissolved the council. In 433 John's side confessed "two natures" and "Mother of God" and accepted Nestorius's deposition, though Theodoret would not.
3. **Why the connection matters:** the empire's churches kept the verdict, and Chalcedon in 451 took Cyril's letters as its guide. The peace had a price, and the council's reach stopped at the border: the Church of the East never made it its own.
