## The four lines

- **Who or what:** a council of bishops called to Ephesus by the emperor Theodosius II in 431.
- **What happened:** Cyril of Alexandria opened it before John of Antioch's bishops came, over the emperor's officer's protest, and it deposed Nestorius. John's assembly deposed Cyril. The emperor's commissioner arrested all three and the emperor dissolved the council. Later John's side and Cyril made peace, and the price was Nestorius's deposition.
- **What it was about:** whether Mary could rightly be called Mother of God, and so how God and a man are one in Christ.
- **Why the connection matters:** the empire's churches kept the verdict, and Chalcedon in 451 took Cyril's letters as its guide. Beyond the border, the Church of the East's own history honored Nestorius as a saint and called Cyril impious.

None is blank or wrong.
