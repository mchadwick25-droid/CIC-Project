# Second Readings (B10, what it was about pass)

## Method

For each id in `Second_Reading_Key.md`, our wording was first checked word for word against its brief (`ephrem-the-syrian.brief.txt`) and against the card entry's `modern` (`ephrem-the-syrian.card.json`, `quotationOriginals[0]`). Both match exactly. The card's `printed` was checked against `cic/texts/palladius_lausiac-history_clarke1918.txt` line 471, and it matches Clarke's sentence exactly. Our wording was then set beside Reader A's blind rendering (`Second_Readings_Blind_A.md`). Where they differ, the cited line, its surrounding passage (Lausiac History 40.2-3) and the card's `uncertain` note were read.

Verdicts: SAME (same meaning), MINOR (a difference a listener would not take differently), MEANING (a listener would understand something different).
