# Second Readings, Blind (Reader A)

## B10A-01

Source: `cic/texts/palladius_lausiac-history_clarke1918.txt`, line 471 (printed English, Clarke 1918).

Passage read: "See, on your behalf I appoint myself hospitaller."

Rendering: "Look, I am making myself the keeper of the guest-house, acting for you."

Notes: "Hospitaller" names the person put in charge of a house that takes in and cares for strangers, the sick and the poor. "On your behalf" means acting for the rich men he is speaking to, as their agent with their money. The scan of this sentence is clean; nothing is unclear.
