## What carries the weight (each story is OVER)

- **council-of-ephesus-431:** 53 narration facts (583 words); beat 3 alone has 10 points (Candidian, summons, soldiers, sentence, signatures, Nestorius's letter, Memnon), beat 4 five, beat 6 five, beat 7 five, and What came five.- **eutyches:** 50 facts; beat 4 (Ephesus 449) has 9 points and beat 2 and 5 six each; What came 1 carries 10 (Eutychians, Timothy's curse, Severus, the body charge, the Tome).
- **theodoret-of-cyrrhus:** 56 facts, the most of the person stories after Rufinus; beat 6 (Chalcedon) has 9, beat 5 seven, beat 1 five, beat 4 six, What came nine. Two full strands (controversy and histories) plus the hostile chronicle, 553, Maron and Leo's praise.
- **pelagius:** 50 facts; beats 3 (Diospolis) seven and 6 (418) seven, beat 1 and 2 eight between them, What came five, ending five. The glosses and the opponents' credits (Augustine four times) add 136 words of carry.
- **john-cassian:** 48 facts; What came carries eight points (Rule, Gennadius, Cassiodorus, African bishop, Eucherius, Gregory); beats 6 and 7 five each; beat 2 (Constantinople and the receipt) four.
- **rufinus-of-aquileia:** 58 facts, the most of any; beat 7 (Church History) six, beat 3 six, beat 4 six, ending five (Sicily), What came seven. Three full quotes plus four short quoted phrases add 137.
- **frumentius:** 50 facts; What came carries 11 points (calendars, 95 bishops, historians, John of Nikiu, Ethiopian details) and beat 7 (the letter) six; 13 witnesses, since the whole life rests on one chain and a hostile letter.
- **mesrop-mashtots:** 52 facts; beat 5 (across the border, force) seven and beat 6 (Georgians, Albanians, pupils, the expelled teacher, hermit years) eight, What came nine. The vision quote alone is 60 words.
- **ulfila:** 50 facts but the heaviest carry: quotes and short phrases 166 (Q3 is 46, Q1 62) and 20 credited witnesses (120); beat 4 (the creed and four answers on its date) seven points, What came nine, beat 1 five.
- **priscillian:** 47 facts; What came has 11 points (martyr cult, Toledo, quarrel, Ithacius, chronicler, Leo, law of 407, Braga, 1885); beat 1 seven and beats 4 and 5 five each. Nearest to LONG (38 words over 675).
