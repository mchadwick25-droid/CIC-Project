## The three lines, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Frumentius, a boy taken captive with Edesius when everyone else on their ship from Tyre was killed, who rose at a foreign king's court and became the first bishop there, in Aksum.
2. **What happened:** he served the king, helped the widowed queen rule, gathered the Roman Christian traders to pray and gave them building sites; when the prince came of age he went to Alexandria to ask Athanasius for a bishop, and Athanasius made him that bishop and sent him back.
3. **Why the connection matters:** Aksum's church took its bishop, and kept taking its bishops, from Alexandria; that tie is why, when Athanasius fell, the emperor Constantius ordered Frumentius sent to Egypt to be judged by the bishop put in Athanasius's place.
