## The four lines

- **Who:** Frumentius, a boy from a family of Tyre who was spared when everyone else on his ship was killed, grew up at a foreign king's court, and became the first bishop of the land Rufinus calls India, which the emperor's letter names Auxumis, Aksum.
- **What happened:** He rose to help rule the kingdom and gathered the Roman Christian traders to pray. Then he went to Alexandria to ask Athanasius for a bishop, and was made that bishop himself. Later the emperor Constantius ordered the rulers of Auxumis to send him to Egypt to be judged.
- **What it was about:** A church begun among traders got its bishop from Alexandria. That tie pulled it into Athanasius's quarrel with the emperor over the teaching about the Son: Arius taught the Son had a beginning, and Athanasius held that he shares the Father's own being.
- **Why the connection matters:** Aksum's church began as a daughter of Alexandria's. So its bishop was answerable there, was hunted when Athanasius fell, and centuries later Alexandria still made Ethiopia's bishop.
