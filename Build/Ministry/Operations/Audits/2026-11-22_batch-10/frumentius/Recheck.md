# Recheck: Frumentius (`frumentius`)

## Verdict: FIX (two wording replacements; no source problem)

- **Worded by me / Not applied:** none in `Fixes.md`, so there is nothing to judge there.
- **BLOCKING 1 (Robertson):** fixed in Also told item 4, beat 5 and Do not say. All three now say "reads most naturally", "no earlier than the end of 346", and that 328 is the editor's reckoning. This matches N204 1519, 5679-5684 and 10405-10408.
- **MINOR 1-9:** each was applied as worded, and the sentences on either side still read correctly, apart from the two items below.
- **Beats:** they still follow the life once, in order (voyage, court, traders, home, Alexandria, bishop, letter), and the approved heart is kept. Moving "especial authority" into beat 7 leaves What came 1 pointing back to it, not retelling it.

## Replacements

1. Beat 3: "meeting places in each place" reads wrongly. Rufinus's "per loca singula" (RUF 27329-27330) means place by place. Replace "urged them to make meeting places in each place, to gather and pray in the Roman way," with "urged them to make meeting places, place by place, where they could gather to pray in the Roman way,".
2. Beat 7: my own clause for the stone is unclear. Replace "use it only if one more Cut first item is not needed elsewhere; otherwise to the card." with "it would take the count to about 683, past 675, so use it only if the writer drops another item of about 35 words; otherwise to the card."
