# Source sheet: Mesrop Mashtots

Files cited:
- **T2** = `cic/texts/langlois_collection-historiens-armenie-tome2-fra_1869.txt` (Langlois, Collection des historiens de l'Arménie, tome II, 1869; raw OCR, damaged in places).
- **T12** = `cic/texts/langlois_collection-historiens-armenie-tomes1-2-fra_1867-1869.txt` (a second, cleaner OCR of the same tome II print, bound with tome I). Every Koriun line quoted below was re-read in both; T12 line numbers are given where a quote may be used.
- **SYN16** = `cic/texts/patrologia-orientalis-tomus16_synaxaire-arabe-jacobite-barmahat-bachons-synaxaire-armenien-tre-fra_graffin-nau1922.txt` (Armenian Synaxary of Ter Israel, month Tré, tr. Bayan, 1922).
- **SYN5** = `cic/texts/chronicle-of-seert_histoire-nestorienne-premiere-partie-fasc2-patrologia-orientalis-5-ara-fra_scher-dib1910.txt` (PO 5; holds Bayan's Armenian Synaxary, month Navasard, in the same volume).
- **MICH** = `cic/texts/michael-the-syrian_chronique-tome3-livres-xii-xxi-fra_chabot1905.txt`.
- **KART** = `cic/texts/kartlis-tskhovreba_histoire-de-la-georgie-pt1-histoire-ancienne-fra_brosset1849.txt`.
- **LIT** = `cic/texts/armenian-church_divine-liturgy_1908.txt`.
- **CONY** = `cic/texts/rituale-armenorum-lectionary_conybeare1905.txt`.

Witness kinds: **own words** (none held); **contemporary** (Koriun, his pupil); **later** (Lazar of Pharp, about two generations on, by Langlois's dating; Moses of Khoren, date Contested: fifth or eighth century, per the Library's own note in `cic/corpus-map/armenian-christianity-after-avarayr.yaml`); **late** (Michael the Syrian, 12th century; the Synaxary of Ter Israel, 13th century by its file header); **editor** (Langlois, Emine, Saint-Martin, Brosset).

What stands behind Koriun: the Armenian original is **not held**. He is held only in J.-Raphaël Emine's French translation (1869) of the 1854 Venice text (T2 2856-2865). Every Koriun quote is a later translation, quoted word for word from the French and rendered by us from it. Two OCR copies of the one print agree on each quoted line.
