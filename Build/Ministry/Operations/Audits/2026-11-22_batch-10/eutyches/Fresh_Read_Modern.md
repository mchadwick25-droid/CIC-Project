### BLOCKING 1. Evag-E 6794-6797 does not support the "followers" sentence

The brief: "A later writer on their side called the churches that refused the council followers of Dioscorus and Eutyches."

The lines (Evag-E 6780-6797) are not Evagrius's own words but a letter of the monks of Palestine to Alcison, which he quotes (chapter heading 6777, "LETTER TO ALCISON FROM THE MONKS OF PALESTINE"). After Peter's death "Alexandria, Egypt, and Africa remained at unity among themselves" while the West demanded "the anathematising of Nestorius, Eutyches, and Dioscorus"; then: "the genuine followers / of Dioscorus and Eutyches were reduced to a very / small number" (6795-6797). The letter does not call the churches that refused the council followers of Dioscorus and Eutyches. It sets a dwindling few "genuine followers" apart from those churches. So the sentence misattributes the words (a letter-writer, not "a later writer") and gives them a meaning they do not hold: one witness's claim told beyond what it says.

The label is already carried, with its witness, by the sentence before, from the council itself (Councils 20172-20173, "Those who contradict are Eutychians"; Evag-E 5189-5192).

**Change** (cut the sentence; name the churches in the next one):
- "The bishops at Chalcedon cried that Leo's opponents were Eutychians. A later writer on their side called the churches that refused the council followers of Dioscorus and Eutyches. Yet those churches did not count themselves his followers."
