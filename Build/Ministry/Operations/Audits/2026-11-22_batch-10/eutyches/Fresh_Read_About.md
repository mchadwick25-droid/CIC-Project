## 1. The test, from one hearing

**The four lines.**
- **Who:** Eutyches, an old abbot in Constantinople in the 440s.
- **What happened:** He told a meeting of bishops that Christ had one nature after God became man. He was condemned in 448, restored at Ephesus in 449 amid shouting, and condemned again at Chalcedon in 451.
- **What it was about:** What Christ is, as God and as man: one nature after the union (his view), or two natures each keeping its own character (Leo's Tome). His judges also recorded him denying that Christ's body shared our nature.
- **Why the connection matters:** His name became a label. Chalcedon's side called the churches that refused the council "Eutychians". Yet those churches kept his fight against Nestorius and cursed him by name, as Timothy did at Alexandria, because they rejected his account of Christ's body.
