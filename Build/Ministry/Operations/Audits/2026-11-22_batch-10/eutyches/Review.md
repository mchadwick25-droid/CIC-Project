## Findings

### BLOCKING 1. A date given to the chronicle, which gives none.
Paragraph 9: "About 475, that chronicle says, Timothy Aelurus returned from exile as bishop of Alexandria." The chronicle tells the recall (Zach 5248) and the return (Zach 5555-5560) but gives no year; the outline marks the date "the chronicle gives no year, *Widely Accepted*". As heard, the date is credited to the source. This is one of the kit's four faults (a modern view given to the source).
**Change:** "About 475, Timothy Aelurus returned from exile as bishop of Alexandria. The prefect, that chronicle says, was thought to be a Eutychian, ..." (date in the narrator's voice; the chronicle credited for the scene). Keep the rest of the paragraph.
**Do not say, add to the outline:** "About 475, the chronicle says" → the chronicle gives no year; give "about 475" in the narrator's voice and credit the chronicle only for the scene in church (Zach 5248, 5638-5643).

### MINOR 1. The crowd line is moved into the letter to Leo.
Paragraph 5 opens "Eutyches wrote to Leo ..." and goes on "He had been handed, he said, to a crowd ready to drag him through the city as a heretic." That line is from his petition to the council of 449 (Perry 23513-23516); the letter to Leo speaks instead of "the assault of military force" (Leo 4575-4577). The outline keeps the two apart.
**Change:** "He later told the council that he had been handed to a crowd ready to drag him through the city as a heretic."

### MINOR 2. "A faction had condemned him before the hearing."
The letter says they published "the sentence of my degradation, which they were getting ready against me before the inquiry" (Leo 4573-4574), and that "a faction had been formed" (Leo 4531-4532).
**Change:** "that a faction had readied his sentence before the hearing".

### MINOR 3. Whose words the bishop denied.
Paragraph 7: "A bishop who had judged him said the words written as his were not his." Heard once, "his" can mean Eutyches. The sentence Basil denied was his own (Perry 23860-23866).
**Change:** "A bishop who had judged him said the record gave him words he had never spoken, then said he did not remember."

### MINOR 4. "But" implies a cause the source does not give.
Paragraph 3: "... he had wanted only a quiet life. But Nestorius ... had been deposed, and his teaching was said to be reviving." The "But" makes Nestorius's revival the thing that ended his quiet; he himself blames Eusebius's charge (Perry 23349-23351; Leo 4519-4521).
**Change:** "By then Nestorius, once the city's bishop, had been deposed, and his teaching was said to be reviving."
