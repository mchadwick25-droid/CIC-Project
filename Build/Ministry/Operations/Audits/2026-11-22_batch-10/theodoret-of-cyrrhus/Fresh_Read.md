## The three lines

- **Who:** Theodoret, a fifth-century bishop of Cyrrhus, a small town in northern Syria: a leading voice of Antioch's teaching on Christ, and a historian.
- **What happened:** he answered Cyril's twelve curses, stood with John of Antioch's side at Ephesus in 431, accepted the peace of 433 but would not accept Nestorius's removal, and wrote the Church History and the Religious History. He was deposed in his absence in 449. At Chalcedon in 451 he was given back his see only after he said "Anathema to Nestorius". In 553 his writings against Cyril were condemned.
- **Why the connection matters:** his History became a link in the church's one continuous story, and historians on both sides of Chalcedon carried it on, even while one side remembered him as an enemy. He defended Christ as God and man unmixed, and his own place was bought by cursing his friend.

## Findings

**BLOCKING 1. His own claim told as fact (para 7).** "An order from the emperor then kept him in Cyrrhus, with no charge and no accuser." That there was no charge and no accuser is Theodoret's own complaint (Letter 81, NP3 31456-31458), and the acts of 449 show an accuser, the presbyter Pelagius, with a written charge (P449 13316-13323). The kit's first fault.
Change to: "An order from the emperor then kept him in Cyrrhus, with no charge and no accuser, he wrote."
Do not say, to add to the outline: Not "with no charge and no accuser" as the narrator's fact. Say "he wrote": it is his own complaint in Letter 81, and the acts of 449 name an accuser.

**BLOCKING 2. His teaching on Mother of God misstated (para 4).** "So Mary is Mother of God, the one who gave birth to God, he wrote, because of the union." Set inside "he wrote", the gloss makes him say she gave birth to God, which is what he denied: "not because she gave birth in natural manner to God, but to man united to the God that had fashioned Him" (NP3 4733-4734). The outline's beat 2 carries the qualifier; the brief dropped it. This is the centre of what he defended.
Change to: "So Mary is rightly Mother of God, he wrote, not because she bore God by nature, but because of the union."
Do not say, to add to the outline: Not "Mother of God, the one who gave birth to God" inside his own words. Keep his qualifier: "not because she bore God by nature, but because of the union" (NP3 4733-4734).

**MINOR 1. "Curses" before its gloss (para 2).** "Twelve curses" is heard in the essence; its church sense ("solemn acts that cut a person off from the church") comes two paragraphs later. Plain enough heard once; no change needed unless the reviser has the words.

**MINOR 2. Nestorius unplaced.** The listener is never told who Nestorius was, so "rightly removed" (para 5) asks the listener to infer the council of 431's verdict. If words allow after the two fixes (the brief is then at 675), "the deposed Nestorius" could become "the deposed bishop Nestorius"; otherwise leave it.
