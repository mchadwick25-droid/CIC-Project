## Findings

1. **BLOCKING. Item 13 and Room for it, cut 5: the new Chalcedon wording would mislead a listener.** "He asked to explain his faith first, but they shouted for a plain curse" makes it sound as if he would not condemn Nestorius at all until he was forced to. The acts (NP3 3316-3329) say twice that he did condemn him: "not only Nestorius and Eutyches, but any man who thinks not rightly, I avoid", and "I anathematize Nestorius and Eutyches, and every one who says that there are two Sons". The bishops wanted a plain curse, with no explanation and no company. That is the force of the scene. Leaving Eutyches out is still sound. Leaving out that he had already condemned Nestorius is not.
   Replace cut 5 with:
   > 5. Paragraph 8, the unexplained name (item 13): "He offered to condemn Nestorius and Eutyches together, with his faith explained, but they shouted" becomes "He said he condemned him, but asked to explain his faith first; they shouted". To the card (Eutyches). −1.

   In item 13, replace "The offer is told as "asked to explain his faith first" (NP3 3305-3331)." with:
   > The offer is told as "He said he condemned him, but asked to explain his faith first" (NP3 3316-3331): he did condemn Nestorius, together with others, and the bishops demanded the plain curse alone.

2. **MINOR. Items 1 and 6, the Ephesus clause:** "without waiting for John's; Theodoret stood with John's" is hard to follow when heard once. Each "John's" makes the listener supply a noun. Replace the new text in the third bullet of "Words the explanations add" with:
   > "At Ephesus in 431, his English translator says, Cyril's side condemned Nestorius without waiting for John's bishops; Theodoret stood with them." +12.

3. **MINOR. Items 3 and 4, paragraph 4:** "Theodoret charged that they mixed God and man into one." The words "into one" go beyond the source. Counter-statement II (NP3 4767-4771) and XI (NP3 5143-5148) charge a "mixture" that destroys what each nature is. They do not say "into one". Cutting the two words also makes room for finding 1. In the second bullet of "Words the explanations add", replace "Theodoret charged that they mixed God and man into one." with "Theodoret charged that they mixed God and man." Change "+23" to "+21".
   A note, with no change needed: the first curse (NP3 4616-4619) falls first on anyone who will not confess that Christ "is in truth God", and only "therefore" on anyone who will not call Mary Mother of God. The brief's shorter line is not false, and its next sentences carry the union. The card should give the full wording.

5. **MINOR. Item 7, the witness wording:** Letter 82 (NP3 31540-31556) says the heresy charge went to "the imperial ears", but it "did not meet with the success that they expected". The synods charge went in a despatch "to the right honourable and glorious commander and consul", not to the emperor. Replace "and told the emperor he kept calling synods at Antioch" with:
   > and then wrote to the commander and consul that he kept calling synods at Antioch

   The brief's own wording ("who said he split Christ into two sons") is sound as it stands.

6. **MINOR. Item 10, "No held line gives a fuller reason":** this is too strong. The Eastern bishops' petition (NP3 36858-36870, written by John) gives a fuller reason: the council met before the bishops it was told to wait for had arrived, against the emperor's envoy. Item 1 already carries that reason into the brief. Replace "No held line gives a fuller reason; add none." with:
   > His own letters give no fuller reason; the Eastern party's (an irregular council that did not wait for John's bishops, NP3 36858-36870; Jackson NP3 2826-2835) is carried by item 1. Add none.
