## The four lines

2. **What happened:** at Marseilles he founded two houses and wrote the Institutes and the Conferences, which carried Egypt's monastic rules and teaching into Latin; Benedict's Rule later named the Conferences for the evening reading, and his thirteenth Conference, on grace and free will, drew Prosper's attack.
