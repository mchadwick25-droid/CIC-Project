**Essence:** Ulfila, bishop of the Goths, led persecuted Gothic Christians across the Danube. He gave them letters and the Scriptures in Gothic, and taught to his death the creed that the Son is "like" the Father, according to the Scriptures. The Nicene historians trace the Goths' faith to him, and Socrates gives Valens a share. Most of his life rests on his pupil Auxentius.

**Also told:** the Cappadocian forebears (Philostorgius alone); "Eusebius" as consecrator (the full name is a modern historian's reading); the persecution and martyrs; Kings left out of his Bible; four accounts of when he took the creed, as one scene; the Goths' later faith; the surviving Gothic Bible.

**Connection:** a Christianity in the Goths' own tongue, in one creed, which they kept after him.
