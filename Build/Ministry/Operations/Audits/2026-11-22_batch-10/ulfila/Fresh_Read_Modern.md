## Step 1. The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Ulfila, a bishop of the Goths who led persecuted Gothic Christians across the Danube onto Roman soil.
- **What happened:** He gave the Goths letters and the Scriptures in Gothic, and taught them the creed that called the Son "like" the Father. He confirmed that creed at Constantinople under Constantius. He fell ill in Constantinople when called to a debate, and died there, leaving his creed in writing.
- **What it was about:** The Son's relation to the Father. Ulfila's creed held that the Father alone is unbegotten and the Son is like him according to the Scriptures, and subject to him. It refused Nicaea's "of one being". His opponents called this faith Arian.
- **Why the connection matters:** The Goths took their Christianity from him, in their own language and in his creed, and still held it in the historians' day. That is why Socrates, Sozomen and Theodoret argue over when he took it.

**Named things, each explainable after one hearing:** the persecution and its martyrs; the crossing as a new Exodus (Q1, glossed); the letters and the Scriptures, with Kings left out and why; the creed "like the Father"; "Arian" (glossed through Arius); "unbegotten" (glossed); Nicaea's "of one being" (glossed) and "a devilish invention"; the council at Constantinople and its ban on "substance" (with its reason); the quarrel over when he took the creed, and why it matters; Socrates's Valens and the chief; Sozomen's embassy and the promise; Theodoret's eloquence and bribes; Athanaric's punishment and why; the debate and its purpose; the testament. None fails.

**Essence:** what he did was lead the persecuted Goths across, and give them letters, Scripture and one creed. What it changed: the Goths' faith for generations. Why he is on the tree: he is the root of Gothic, Homoian Christianity. The hook (the crossing, three sentences and Q1) is itself the first part of the essence, told in the first 60 words. The letters and Scripture come at about word 155, and the creed and its name by word 186. That is just past "about 150", inside the first 75 seconds. The only words before it are Q1 and its required gloss, so I make no finding. **Also told:** where he came from (made bishop at thirty, with Philostorgius's account); the persecution; the letters and the Bible, with Kings and the surviving fragments; the teaching in his pupil's words; the four-witness doubt; Athanaric; the death and testament; what came of him (the Goths' faith). All are present, each with its weight. Cappadocia, Selinus, Jordanes and Isidore are cut by the outline's Cut first list.

**Order:** after the essence, the life runs once and in order. Consecration, the seven years, letters, teaching, Constantinople under Constantius, the doubt scene, Athanaric under Valens, the summons, the death, the testament. The doubt scene reaches into Valens's reign and back, but the Kit asks for exactly that when the doubt is itself a scene.
