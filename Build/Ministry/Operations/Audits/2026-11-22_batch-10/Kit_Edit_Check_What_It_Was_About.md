## Findings

**1. Kit Part 3, Story beats: the semicolons detach "its source, and its confidence level" from the beat.** As edited, the last list item reads as if only a puzzling word has a source and a confidence level. The sentence runs about 50 words. Replace the last sentence of the field with:

> Each beat gives the reason for the act, the meaning of any word or custom that would puzzle a newcomer, its source, and its confidence level. For each charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision it names, it also gives what that was about, in one plain line with its source.

**2. Kit Part 1, the hostile-view rule: one sentence of about 50 words.** Replace from "A hostile view is told openly" to the end of the bullet with:

> A hostile view is told openly and credited to its source. An enemy's charge is given in its own content ("his accusers charged that ..."), never dropped because only an enemy says it. The narrator never carries any side's view forward, and never softens the record by leaving out its force or conflict.

**3. roles/checker.md, item 3: the added clause makes an overlong sentence longer (about 80 words).** "With what to move to the card" now also attaches to the new clause, where it makes no sense, and "and the tradition's spoken name doesn't mislead" trails at the end. Replace item 3 with:

> 3. The impact ranking and what they defended are right, the shape fits, and the tradition's spoken name doesn't mislead. The length fits: recount the words the beats, quotes, glosses and credited witnesses must carry. An outline that, less its cut-first list, still runs past its approved length is BLOCKING, with what to move to the card. So is a cut-first list that drops what a named charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision was about.

**4. roles/researcher.md, item 1: a parenthesis nested inside a long list (about 55 words).** Replace the sentence beginning "Cover the setting" with:

> Cover the setting, the events, the impact ranked heaviest first (what later witnesses carried on and used), links to other people, and where sources disagree or come late. For every charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision, give what it was about in its own terms, from each side the held texts give, an enemy's included.

**5. roles/reviewer.md, step 1: "whose content ... could not say" drifts from the ruling's "could not explain".** Replace:

> List each charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision the story names: one whose content a listener could not say after one hearing is BLOCKING.

with:

> List each charge, teaching, book, quarrel or decision the story names: one a listener could not explain after one hearing is BLOCKING.
