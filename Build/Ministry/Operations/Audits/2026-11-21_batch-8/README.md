## Quotes

Mark's ruling (2026-11-21): a quote may be used again where its author or subject influenced more than one tradition or person. The slate reads it so: a quote lifts when it does a new job in the second story; a bar stays when the second use would tell the same event the same way, or when the line is a council's or an editor's text that belongs to the other story. Any other quote already on the tree, not listed here, stays barred; a researcher who wants one asks.

**Lifted (may be used, for a new job):**
- Francis: his Testament's lines on the lepers (`the-franciscan-movement`, documented stories), to open his story (hearts ruling, 2026-11-21).
- Hus: "Yet in hope I would wish my soul to be where John Wyclif's soul is." (`john-wyclif`).
- Leo's own words: "not of judges but of robbers" (`dioscorus-of-alexandria`); "very unwary and exceedingly ignorant" (`eutyches`); "the victory you and we together had won" (`theodoret-of-cyrrhus`); Leo's lines in `hilary-of-arles` ("such as no layman could make and no priest listen to"; "Hilary of holy memory").
- Jerome's own words about his teachers, friends and opponents: "my teacher in the Scriptures" (`gregory-of-nazianzus`); the Didymus line (`didymus-the-blind`); the Apollinaris line (`apollinaris-of-laodicea`); "inseparably bound to me in brotherly love" and "the scorpion lies beneath the ground" (`rufinus-of-aquileia`); "becomes either a stone or a God" (`evagrius-ponticus`); "no better than a villainous slave" (`palladius-of-helenopolis`); "You urge me to revise the old Latin version" (`damasus-of-rome`); his lines in `clement-of-alexandria`, `lactantius`, `pliny-the-younger`, `tertullian-s-voice`, `pamphilus-of-caesarea`, `lucifer-of-cagliari`, `priscillian`, `damasus-of-rome`, `epiphanius-of-salamis`, `marcella-of-rome`, `fabiola-of-rome` and `melania-the-elder`. When those stories are rebuilt, each line is checked again so it does a different job in each.

**Stay barred:**
- "The whole world groaned, and was astonished to find itself Arian" (`arius`, `councils-of-ariminum-and-seleucia-359`): two stories already use it for the same event.
- Chalcedon's definition, and "for the removal of the false doctrines of Eutyches" (`council-of-chalcedon-451`, `eutyches`): the council's text.
- Theodoret's own letters to Leo ("I have done pastoral duty in eight hundred churches"; "I await the sentence of your apostolic see"; "with a pen"): Theodoret's words.
- Editors' verdicts: Feltoe's "not very creditable encounter with Hilary" (`hilary-of-arles`); Fremantle's note on the Life of Hilarion (`hilarion-of-gaza`).
- Every quote in Constantine's live text stays with it. Inside the batch, no quote is shared.
