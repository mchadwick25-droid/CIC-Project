# Leo of Rome: researcher's reply

**Essence.** Bishop of Rome 440 to 461. In 449 he wrote the letter called the Tome, holding Christ one person, fully God and fully human; in 451 the Council of Chalcedon took it as its rule of faith (Documented). He held that Peter ruled through him, and rejected the canon that set Constantinople next to Rome (Documented, his own words).

**Also told.** Ephesus 449, which he called a robbery (Documented, his and his editor's accounts). Some bishops at Chalcedon doubted the Tome; it was adopted only at the fourth session (Documented). He declared the canon void by Peter's authority, but it stood (Documented). Attila, 452: the cause of the retreat is Contested. Each side later read him against the other (Documented as their views).

**Shape and length.** Founder or leader. About 4:05, about 615 words; about 255 must-carry words counted first; a cut-first list brings it to about 570. Opens at Chalcedon, his letter read aloud to Greek-speaking bishops.

**Connection.** The Tome passed into Chalcedon's rule of faith; his claim to speak for Peter lasted, against Constantinople's rank, for centuries.

**Quotes** (each rendered from a damaged Latin scan; Feltoe's English confirms each).
- "To be hungry, to be thirsty, to grow tired and to sleep is plainly human."
- "Nor need a letter set down everything that could be done at Ephesus, in what was not a trial but a robbery." Lifted from `dioscorus-of-alexandria`; here his own verdict.
- "Yet worldly things stand on one footing, and the things of God on another. No building will stand firm except on the rock the Lord laid as its foundation."

**Sources differ.** Attila: Prosper, close to Leo, credits God and Leo's presence; Jordanes, citing Priscus, the Huns' fear of Alaric's fate; Hydatius, in Spain, famine, disease and the eastern emperor's army, and no Leo. The Tome: Gennadius says Prosper dictated it; a Syriac chronicle says Theodoret won Leo over with a gift.

**Gaps.** Ephesus 449 is told only from Leo's side. Priscus's own text is not held.

**Four-fault check.** Run. Caught an editor's reading given to Leo and a misplaced 1215 date; both fixed.
