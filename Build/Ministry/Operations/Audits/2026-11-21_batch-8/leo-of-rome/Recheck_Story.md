## The fix

**New wording says more than its source.** The review's M11 recast says Leo was made bishop while he was away in Gaul. Prosper (E1, PROS 54626–54634) says Rome went without a bishop for more than forty days waiting for the deacon Leo, who was then in Gaul. He was summoned home by a public embassy, and only then ordained ("accitus et gaudenti patriae praesentatus … episcopus ordinatur"). So he was chosen while he was away, but made bishop after he came back. The outline's E1 says "elected and ordained".

Replace:

> Leo was a deacon, away in Gaul, when he was made bishop of Rome in 440.

with:

> Leo was a deacon, away in Gaul, when he was chosen bishop of Rome in 440.

The fix adds no words.
