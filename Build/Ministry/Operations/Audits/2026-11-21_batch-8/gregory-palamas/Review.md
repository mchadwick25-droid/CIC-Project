## Findings

### BLOCKING

**B1. The Philokalia, part of the approved heart, was left out to fit.**

- "Also told" (What came of him) holds "the Philokalia … gathered his writings". The Connection's second point holds "his writings on prayer went on in the Philokalia". The Shape names "the readers of the Philokalia" as who learned it.
- The cut-first list allowed only the Paisius/Optina clause to go, and the writer used all eight numbered cuts as well. Under Mark's rulings of 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21, leaving out a part of the heart to fit is blocking.
- **Change:** after "…acclaims his name every year", split the 32-word sentence and add the Philokalia: "…acclaims his name every year. The next Sunday, he adds, keeps his memory. In the eighteenth century, the Philokalia, a Greek book of the old teachers of prayer, gathered his writings."
- The added sentence uses the outline's own gloss. "In the eighteenth century" keeps the Do not say line about when the Philokalia was compiled. The split also fixes M5.
- **Do not say (add to the outline):** "Do not drop the Philokalia to fit. It is in Also told and in the Connection. Only the Paisius and Optina clause is optional."

**B2. "The emperor John Kantakouzenos" followed by "with the emperor presiding" in 1341 implies that Kantakouzenos was emperor and presided in 1341.**

- The 1341 emperor was another man (E12, KAN1 Latin 32619-32622). Kantakouzenos became emperor only after the war (E17; the story's own "now emperor").
- On one hearing, the listener takes the 1341 emperor to be Kantakouzenos. Then the empress "fought Kantakouzenos" and he is "now emperor" in 1351, and neither makes sense.
- **Change:** "The emperor John Kantakouzenos, a friend and ally of Gregory Palamas," → "John Kantakouzenos, later emperor and an ally of Gregory Palamas,". Also cut "with the emperor presiding" (see the cuts below).
- This also removes "friend", which the outline does not hold. The outline calls him "his ally and patron".
- **Do not say (add to the outline):** "Not 'the emperor John Kantakouzenos' for any time before 1347, and not 'the emperor presiding' in 1341 without naming him. The emperor of 1341 was another man. Say 'John Kantakouzenos, later emperor'."
