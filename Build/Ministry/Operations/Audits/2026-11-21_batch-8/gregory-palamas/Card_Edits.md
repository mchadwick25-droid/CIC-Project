## Quoted spans (Greek checked by hand)

1. "navel-souls." Greek Ὀμφαλοψύχους, KAN1 32673 (clean); Latin Omphalopsychos, KAN1 32701, as the facing print. 1 word: checked at card.
2. Palamas, "Those who call them navel-souls ... soul is in the navel?" Greek printed from PHK2 42167-42170, the only Greek print. One fault, "χα-τηγοροῦσι" for κατηγοροῦσι, read as the only word that fits and backed in sense by Migne's Latin "quos accusant" (PG150 64680; the Latin is itself damaged and interleaved with Greek, so it is not printed). The rest matches letter for letter. Awaiting second reading. **Register entry needed**: PHK2 lines 42168-42169, scan "χα-\nτηγοροῦσι" reads "κα-\nτηγοροῦσι", kind OCR misread (chi for kappa). Under the Irenaeus ruling a chi-for-kappa original was left out; here it is one word, with the sense confirmed, and I kept it. The checker may rule it out.
3. Palamas, "So when you hear the Fathers ... agree with the Fathers." Greek printed from PG150 67527-67533. The second print, PHK2 45852-45860, agrees word for word; each scan's faults are resolved by the other (PG150 προποῦσαν, ἔχφανσιδ, ὁμόÀoyo; and Latin letters xat, xal read as in PHK2; PHK2's ἐνέρ-упау and Cyrillic ха!, ко!, and the digit 9 for δ᾽ read as in PG150). Both print chi for kappa throughout; read as kappa. Awaiting second reading. **Register entries needed**, for PG150 lines 67527-67533: ἀχούσῃς, ἀμέθεχτον, ᾿ἀχείνην (ἐκείνην), ἀνεχφοίτητόν, χαὶ, ἀνεχφαν-τον, μεθεχτὴν, φυσιχῶς, προποῦσαν (προσοῦσαν), xat, ἔχφανσιδ (ἔκφανσιν), xal, ὁμόÀoyo; (ὁμόλογος). The same question about chi for kappa as in item 2; here two prints agree, which is stronger.
4. Gregoras, "in labor with the teachings of many gods ..." 16 words: awaiting second reading. **The Greek is left out.** GRE3 530-531 prints ὀδίνοντα for ὠδίνοντα (an omicron where the verb has an omega) and ϑ for θ; no held print confirms the omega, and supplying it would be from knowledge. `printed` is the facing Latin, GRE3 552-553, "dogmata polytheismi simulque atheismi parturiret", which is clean.
5. Philotheos, "The heavenly to the heavenly." 5 words: checked at card. **Problem for the checker.** The only print is PHIL 8235-8236, whose first half is broken ("éns-ράνια"; ἐπουράνια or οὐράνια). Under the ruling the Greek is not printed whole, and no English or Latin print is held. `printed` is the clean second half only, "εἰς τὰ ἐπουράνια" (8236), with the note. If the checker wants the whole phrase, the register would need "éns-\nράνια" read "ἐπου-\nράνια", which rests on the repetition and not on a second print (Outline_Check M1 found that line 8243 is Philotheos's own narration, not an echo of the dying words, so it does not confirm).
