## Conflicts with existing fields

The person has no entry yet, so there are no fields of its own to contradict. The tradition entry `the-franciscan-movement` (its `longDescription`, `why`, `legacy`) differs from the card in four places; none is changed here.
- It says a cloth merchant's son and a merchant's inheritance. The card says trader's son: Celano calls him a shrewd trader (CEL 983-984) and says his father brought him to give up all his property (CEL 1398-1402); inheritance is Bonaventure's word (BON 966-970). No held source names the father's trade in one word.
- It says by 1209 Innocent III gave verbal approval. The card gives 1209 by Robinson and 1210 by Howell's table, Sabatier and Wadding.
- Its Testament lines are another English (Early Documents), not Robinson's.
- It names the song the Canticle of the Creatures; the card uses Robinson's Praises of the Creatures and the brief's Canticle of Brother Sun.
