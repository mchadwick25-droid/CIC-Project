## Conflicts with the census entry's own fields, and claims not found

- `voices` calls him "the first churchman to make an emperor do public penance". No held source says first, so the card does not say it. Not contradicted; not supported. Not found.
- `floorNote` (defender of the Nicene faith) and `legacy` (the rite; Augustine brought to God) agree with the card. `legacy`'s "within the church, not above it" is left out of the card (barred in the Outline).
- `longDescription` (not in scope) puts the basilica sermon in 386 and the birth at Trier about 340. The card gives the editor's 385 against Augustine's 386, and the editor's "probably" for Trier. Noted only.
- The Sheet says On the Duties of the Clergy was "written about 391" (AMB 741-743). The chronology says 391 to 392 and "about this time", so the card says 391 to 392.
- Not found: Paulinus's wonder stories of the bees and the fire (the Sheet names them but cites no line) are left off the card, as is the Council of Aquileia attack by Maximinus (no line cited); only the editor's sentence on Aquileia (AMB 1096-1100) is on it.
