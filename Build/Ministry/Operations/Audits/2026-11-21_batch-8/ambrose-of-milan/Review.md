## The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Ambrose, the governor of the province around Milan, whom a shouting crowd made the city's bishop in 374 before he was even baptized; he was bishop until he died in 397.
- **What happened:** he refused to hand a church over to the empress's party, through fines, prisons and soldiers, while his people kept vigil and sang hymns; he baptized Augustine; he shielded the Christians who burned a synagogue; and after the massacre at Thessalonica he would not lead communion with the emperor Theodosius present until the emperor did public penance.
- **Why the connection matters:** the singing that began in those nights spread across the West, and later historians in Latin and Greek kept retelling the penance, an emperor bowing to the church's discipline.

All three lines come clear from one hearing.
