# Outline check: Catherine of Siena

Checker, batch 8, 2026-11-21.

## My own heart (written before reading `Outline.md`)

**The essence.** Catherine, a dyer's daughter of Siena, took the habit of the Dominican Sisters of Penance (the Mantellate) and lived it at home rather than in a convent. Though she could hardly write, she dictated letters to popes, cities and rulers, and in 1376 went to Avignon for Florence and pressed Gregory XI, face to face and in letters, to make peace and bring the papacy back to Rome. He went that September. How much she moved him is disputed: her confessor Raymond says she led him to it; her own letters say the resolve was already his; later writers credit Bridget's revelations or a bishop's retort. She is on the tree as the tradition's voice for the return from Avignon. (Documented for the acts; Contested for her share in the return.)

**Also told.** A circle (her "famiglia") gathered round her and called her mother; her secretaries took down her letters (Documented). Her letters press reform of the clergy, the return and a crusade (Documented). The Avignon peace mission for Florence failed when Florence's envoys would not deal with her (Documented). Sent by Gregory to Florence in 1378, she was nearly killed there and stayed until peace was made under Urban VI (Documented; the stopping of the attacker in Raymond's voice). In 1378 she dictated the Dialogue, a book of four petitions and God's answers (the Son as bridge), to her secretaries, who say she was in ecstasy (Documented; the trance in the witnesses' voice). After the schism she went to Rome and worked for Urban VI; she died there in 1380, aged about 33 (Documented / Widely Accepted). What came: the return and the argument over it (Contested); the Dialogue put into Latin, printed, carried to England (Documented); her circle and Raymond's Life carried her on; canonized by Pius II (Widely Accepted).

Shape: Founder or leader (she founded nothing formal; the "built" thing is her circle and the voice of her letters).
