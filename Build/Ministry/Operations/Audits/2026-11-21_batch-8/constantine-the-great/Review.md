## The three lines, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Constantine, the Roman emperor under whom the persecuted church became the church of the emperors.
2. **What happened:** he won Rome in 312 under a sign of Christ (told by two witnesses who differ), decreed freedom of religion with Licinius at Milan, called the bishops to Nicaea over Arius and urged the word homoousios, later pressed Athanasius to take Arius back and exiled him to Gaul, and sought baptism only when dying.
3. **Why the connection matters:** with him the church passes from persecution to imperial favour and imperial power: an emperor now calls councils, shapes a creed's wording, and exiles bishops and priests.
