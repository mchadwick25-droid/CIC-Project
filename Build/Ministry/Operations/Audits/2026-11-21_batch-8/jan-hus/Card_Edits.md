## Conflicts and claims not found

- No Jan Hus entry exists, so no field of it can conflict.
- The tradition entry's documented story "The Sixth of July at Constance" calls Sigismund "the emperor". The card gives king of Hungary and of the Romans, crowned emperor only in 1433 (DOC 13107; LHW 13098-13100). The tradition entry is not Hus's own; it needs its own fix.
- The tradition entry's story says Hus answered that he committed his soul to Christ. REL 17293-17294 holds it. Not used on the card, because the brief does not tell it.
- The brief's "dead since 1384" (Wyclif) has no cited line in the sheet; it comes from the Wyclif story (Outline.md line 37). The card does not repeat it.
- The bull's date, 15 May 1408, is the sheet's reading of "Idib. Maji, pontificatus nostri anno sacundo" (DOC 18233) for Gregory XII; the year is reckoned, not printed.
- Sheet S3 gives 1402 for his first rectorship from LUT; the safe-conduct itself calls him bachelor of divinity and master of arts (DOC 13090-13091). Consistent.
