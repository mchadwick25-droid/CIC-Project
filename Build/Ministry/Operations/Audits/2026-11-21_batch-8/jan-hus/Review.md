# Review: Jan Hus

Story: `jan-hus.brief.txt`. Read once, straight through, before the outline.

## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who.** Jan Hus, a priest and teacher at Prague's university who preached to the city in Czech at the Bethlehem Chapel and would not condemn the English scholar John Wyclif.
2. **What happened.** He went to the council at Constance under King Sigismund's letter of protection. He was arrested, refused to swear that he had taught what he said he never taught, and was burned on 6 July 1415.
3. **Why the connection matters.** His death turned a university quarrel into a nation's movement. The lords of Bohemia and Moravia sealed a letter calling him a good man and vowed to defend his preachers. The cup he defended became the emblem of the Hussite and Bohemian Brethren movement, and Luther later judged him burned against a sworn safe-conduct.

None of the three lines is blank or wrong.
