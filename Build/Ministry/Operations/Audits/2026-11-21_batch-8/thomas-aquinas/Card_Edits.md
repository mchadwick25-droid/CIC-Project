## Differences from the research files, found on re-reading

- The sheet's BUL lines 62599-62602 do not hold the phrase on the most certain rule of doctrine; it stands at BUL 62580-62586, and the bull's rubric uses Angelici Doctoris at about 62591. The sheet says no source gives a date for the title; the bull's year is 1567.
- ASM 174654-174656 reads the year of Pius V's order as MDLXxvil in the Library's scan; the card takes 1567 from the bull (BUL 62520-62535).
- ASM 175865-175870 (his sister) belongs to Tocco's account of the castle, as the sheet says; the card credits Tocco.
- The 1735 print (ASM35) reads the straw line at 117761-117762, not 117763-117764 as the sheet has it.
- The outline says others finished the Summa and names no one; the fourteenth-century note the editors print (ST12 75705-75716) names William Sudre. The card names him and says the editors dispute who compiled the Supplement.

## Conflicts with existing fields

The person has no entry yet. The tradition entry `the-dominican-order` (its `why`, `legacy`, `sourcing`) was read for its lines on him; the card does not contradict it. One point sits outside it: the live Bonaventure story on the tree, as the outline reports it, has Gregory X name Bonaventure a cardinal in 1273, before the road to Lyon, which does not fit the remark Bartholomew gives Reginald on that road. The card states the oddity in `uncertain` and holds both.
