## The three lines, from one hearing

1. **Who.** Thomas Aquinas, a Dominican friar of the thirteenth century who taught theology at Paris and wrote the Summa of Theology.
2. **What happened.** He was born to the counts of Aquino around 1225 and joined the Dominicans at Naples. His family seized him and held him, and the witnesses differ on who held him and for how long. He studied under Albert, became a master at Paris and wrote the Summa. In December 1273 he stopped writing and said that everything he had written seemed to him straw. He died at Fossanova in 1274 on the way to a council, and was declared a saint in 1323.
3. **Why the connection matters.** He taught a way of setting out the case against the faith and then answering it, on the rule that no truth can be proved false. His order bound its teachers to that way of teaching. A pope praised it, his feast was ranked with the great Doctors, and by Tyndale's day the preachers were split into his followers and Duns's.
