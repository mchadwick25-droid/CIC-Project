**B1. "She saw none of that." is false.** It follows the 1580 province, and Teresa lived two years past it: the brief of 22 June 1580 (FOUND-ed 2378–2384), her death in October 1582 (E29). **Change:** delete the sentence "She saw none of that." *Do not say (add to the outline):* Not "she saw none of that", nor any line that puts the province of 1580 after her death. She lived two years past it (E28, E29).

The outline's Connection makes this its second point: "how her teaching passed… printed only after her death, at Salamanca in 1588" (Outline line 90). The writer dropped it, although it is not on the cut list, contrary to Hearts ruling 2. **Change:** after "…and later an order." add "After her death, once the Inquisition agreed, her books were printed at Salamanca in 1588." (+15 words; I2, LIFE-L 2415–2428.)
*Do not say (add to the outline):* Not "she never saw the book again" as the last word on her books. In What came, tell that they were printed at Salamanca in 1588, after the Inquisition agreed (I2).
