Batch 8, 2026-11-21, under `Brief-Kit/roles/reviewer.md` and `Reviewer_Prompt.filled.md`. The story was read once, straight through, before the outline. The "For Constantine the Great only" section of the prompt does not apply here: this folder has an `Outline.md` and an approved heart.

## 1. The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** Huldrych Zwingli, people's priest at Zurich's main church, to whom the Reformed churches look as a founder.
- **What happened:** at a public debate in 1523 he made Scripture the judge. The council ordered that all preaching rest on Scripture, and within two years it removed the images and ended the Mass. The same council drowned the rebaptizers. At Marburg he and Luther signed articles together but split on whether Christ's body is in the bread. He urged war on the Catholic cantons and died at Kappel in 1531.
- **Why the connection matters:** his method was to test by Scripture in public and let the council decide and enforce. Zurich kept that method, and his reading of communion parted him from Luther. Geneva, under Calvin, later signed with Zurich on that very point.

All three lines can be written. I got lost at the ending. I did not know that "Master Ulrich" was Zwingli, and "his widow" could mean the new teacher's widow. "That May" after Marburg also left the order unclear. Nothing reads as a children's book.

**My heart, written before reading `Hearts.md`.** From 1519 he preached through the Bible at Zurich's Great Minster. In 1523 he defended that preaching before the city council, with Scripture as the judge. The council ruled that preachers must preach only what Scripture could defend, and by 1525 Zurich had removed its images and ended the Mass. The Reformed churches look to him as a founder. Also told: the council's force (its threats, the drowning law, Manz drowned) and his approval of the law, given with grief; the break with Luther at Marburg; the war letter and his death at Kappel; Bullinger in his place; and Geneva joining Zurich in 1549. This matches the approved heart (`Researcher_Reply.md`; `Outline.md` lines 5-10). No census entry exists yet for `huldrych-zwingli`, which is a new person entry, so I took the job from the approved slate line.

## 3. Findings

### BLOCKING

**B1. The 1519 preaching comes after the essence, as a jump back, at word 183.** The approved essence opens "From 1519 ... preached the Bible book by book". The story tells the 1523 to 1525 essence and then goes back with "Years before, in 1519, ...". The kit counts a jump back after the essence as a failure on one hearing. Put the 1519 preaching inside the essence and pay for it there:
- Replace "The bishop's spokesman wants the universities to judge the new preaching, and a priest rises to answer him." with "When the bishop's spokesman wants the universities to judge, the preacher, Huldrych Zwingli, answers him." This also fixes M1.
- Replace "So said Huldrych Zwingli, people's priest of Zurich, who preached at the Great Minster, the city's main church." with "Since 1519 Zwingli had preached the Bible book by book as people's priest at the Great Minster, the city's main church."
- Replace "He had asked to defend his preaching in public." with "He had asked to defend that preaching in public."
- Replace "Within two years Zurich took the images out of its churches and ended the Mass" with "Within two years Zurich removed its images and ended the Mass".
- Delete the paragraph "Years before, in 1519, instead of the set Sunday readings, he began preaching straight through the Gospel of Matthew."

With these changes the essence is complete at word 160, about 1:04 (tested with the scorer). "Book by book" is the outline's own essence wording (O 5). Do not keep "since 1519 ... straight through the Gospel of Matthew": by 1523 he had gone on to Acts and the letters (S3, E1).

Do not say, to add: "Tell the 1519 preaching inside the essence, before the council's ruling. Never tell it after the essence as 'Years before'. With Quote 1 in the opening, the essence fits the first minute only if the hook is two short sentences."

**B2. The images and the end of the Mass are told twice.** The essence says "Within two years Zurich took the images out of its churches and ended the Mass". Beat 2 then says again "In 1524 images and organs were taken out of the churches, and on April 12, 1525, the council ended the Mass." Delete that second sentence (21 words). The paragraph keeps the threats, the "restrained" ruling and "A city council, not a bishop, now decided what its church would do."

Do not say, to add: "Once the essence has told the images and the Mass, beat 2 does not tell them again. It adds only what is new: the threats and the 'restrained' ruling."

**B3. The drowning letter is cut so that it reads as regret, not approval.** The story stops at "wished the new faith had not begun with such examples". In the letter, the next words turn that wish into approval (KD 25407-25412; sheet D7). The Do not say list (O 113: "approved it, with grief") and the four-fault check (O 155: "Zwingli's own approval of the law" kept) both require it. As told, a listener takes the grief and misses the approval, so the record's force is softened. After "had not begun with such examples." add: "But, he went on, it pleases God to guard in this way against evils to come." This adds 16 words and is the outline's own paraphrase (O 56). Ananias is not needed. If he is named, he gets the outline's gloss.

Do not say, to add: "Never end the 7 March 1526 letter at 'such examples'. Its next clause, that it pleases God to guard in this way against evils to come, carries his approval and stays."

**B4. The ending gives Zwingli's house to the wrong man and loses the listener.** EG 48403-48413 names "M. Heinrich Bullinger, an M. Uorichs (Zwingli) statt" over the minster's teaching. The Bible lesson and "M. Uorichs seligen hof" were given to a different man, "M. Theodorum Buochmann von Bischofzell, theologum". The story's "to the new teacher of the Bible lesson, Heinrich Bullinger" is a factual error. It also leaves out the approved "Bullinger took his place" (O 10), and "Master Ulrich" is never tied to Zwingli. Replace the last paragraph with:

> In March 1532 the records of the Great Minster name Heinrich Bullinger over its teaching in Zwingli's place; he led Zurich's church for four decades. Its overseers gave the house of "Master Ulrich, of blessed memory", as the record calls Zwingli, to the new teacher of the Bible lesson. He agreed to share it with Zwingli's children until the widow found another home.

"Four decades" is from I1 (CEN `entry.tile`) and O 62. The quoted span is unchanged.

Do not say, to add: "'He gave the house to Bullinger' or 'the new teacher of the Bible lesson, Heinrich Bullinger'. The record names Bullinger over the teaching in Zwingli's place. The Bible lesson and the house went to another man, a theologian from Bischofszell, given by role (EG 48403-48413). 'Master Ulrich' is always tied to Zwingli where it is heard."

**Length with all four fixes:** 655 words by the scorer (tested), longest sentence 27 words. Without the optional cuts M4 and M5 it is about 666 words. It stays under 675.
