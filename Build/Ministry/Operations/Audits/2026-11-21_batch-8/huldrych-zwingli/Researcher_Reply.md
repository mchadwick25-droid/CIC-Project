# Huldrych Zwingli: researcher's reply

**Essence.** From 1519 people's priest at Zurich's main church, preaching the Bible book by book; in 1523 he defended his preaching in public debate before the city council, which ruled that every preacher must preach only what Scripture could defend; by 1525 Zurich had removed its images and ended the Mass (Documented: the decrees and his own words). The Reformed churches look to him as a founder (Widely Accepted: editors and the tree).

**Also told.** The council's force: it drowned Anabaptists from 1526, and Zwingli approved the law with grief (Documented). Marburg 1529: he and Luther signed fifteen articles but split on Christ's bodily presence in the bread (Documented). He urged war and died at Kappel in 1531 (the letter Documented; the death Widely Accepted). Bullinger succeeded him, and in 1549 Calvin's Geneva signed one agreement on the Supper with Zurich (Documented).

**Shape and length.** Founder or leader. About 4:10, about 625 words, never past 675; about 260 of them quotes, glosses and credits. Opening: the council hall, 29 January 1523.

**Connection.** Test each practice against Scripture in public and let the council decide; Zurich kept the method, and Geneva joined it on the Supper.

**Quotes** (none lifted, none on the tree).
1. "I say we have judges here who cannot err and take no side: the Holy Scriptures, which can neither lie nor deceive…"
2. Marburg Article 15: "We have not agreed at this time whether Christ's true body and blood are bodily in the bread and wine. Yet each side shall show the other Christian love…"
3. "This peace that some press for so hard is war, not peace…"

**Sources differ.** How Marburg ended (Collin, Luther, Aurifaber); why he went to Kappel (Foxe, from earlier writers; Jackson); who blockaded; the first debate's date (23 or 29 January).

**Gaps.** Bullinger's chronicle, Myconius's Life and the German record of the first debate are not held. Luther's report of Zwingli in tears is held only in a damaged scan: paraphrase only. No sibling.

**Four-fault check.** Run. Caught an inferred "followers", two misattributed records, and "river" for "water". All fixed.
