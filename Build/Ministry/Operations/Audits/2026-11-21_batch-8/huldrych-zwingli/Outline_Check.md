# Outline check: Huldrych Zwingli

Checker, batch 8, 2026-11-21. Written before `Outline.md` was opened, from the census tradition entry (`the-reformed-cities-zurich-and-geneva`), `Source_Sheet.md` and what Zwingli is known for.

## My own heart (written first)

**The essence.** From 1519 Zwingli, Zurich's people's priest, preached straight through whole books of the Bible, and in January 1523 he had his teaching tested in a public debate before the city's council with Scripture as the only judge. The council ruled for him and ordered every preacher to preach only what Scripture could defend; over the next two years the city removed its images and ended the Mass. That made Zurich the first of the Reformed churches, and his break with Luther at Marburg in 1529 over how Christ is present in the Lord's Supper split the Protestant reform into two families, Lutheran and Reformed.

**Also told.**
- The method: the council, not a bishop, decided; dissent was to be punished (decree of 29 January 1523; mandate of October 1523).
- The force of it: the same principle, pushed further by Grebel and Manz, became believers' baptism; the council made rebaptism a drowning offence (7 March 1526) and drowned Manz (5 January 1527); Zwingli reported the decree with approval and with regret.
- Marburg: brought by Philip of Hesse; agreement on fourteen articles and most of the fifteenth, disagreement on the bodily presence; each side's account of how it ended (Luther refused to call them brothers; Zwingli's tears are Luther's report).
- War: he urged war on the Catholic Five Cantons (1529, "this peace is war"), the First Peace of Kappel; the blockade of 1531; his death on the field at Kappel, 11 October 1531, as the city's chief pastor with the army.
- What came of it: Bullinger took his place at Zurich; Luther read his death as God's judgment, Foxe as a martyr's; Geneva's agreement with Zurich in 1549 (Consensus Tigurinus) bridged the Reformed cities.
