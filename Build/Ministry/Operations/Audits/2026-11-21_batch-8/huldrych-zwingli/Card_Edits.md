## Conflicts and claims to check

1. **Quotation 1 cannot pass the line check as it stands.** The print breaks the sentence after "These we have": a footnote (JK01 2189-2210) and a page heading (2213) stand in lines 2188-2214. The `printed` text omits them, as the speech does. The validator needs a `drop` reading in `tools/quote_scan_readings.json` for those lines (the footnote, and the running head with its page number), or the entry must be split. I did not edit that file.
2. **"Uorichs" in the minster record (quotation 4).** The scan prints "Uorichs" at EG 48403, 48407, 48409 and 48410, where the same book prints "Uolrichs" (EG 7790, 23023, 23343, 31442, 31883) and Kidd prints "Uolrich" for Zwingli (KD 21966, 21980). It is a scan fault in the name, but a held print confirms the reading only by parallel text in the same edition and in Kidd, not by a second print of this record. The entry keeps the page as printed and says so. If the card checker holds that a parallel is not a confirming print, the entry loses its original and the span has no printed wording, since no English of this record is held.
3. **The letter of 7 March 1526 (KD 25408-25412).** The brief says that Zwingli went on to say it pleases God to guard in this way against evils to come. The print reads "Deus non sumus cui sic placet futuris malis prospicere", and its last lines are cut and run together (25411-25412). No second held print of the letter exists (searched `cic/texts/` for "crepundia" and "Ananiam"). The words "non sumus" may be a fault, or the clause may say something else. The card records this under `uncertain` and does not settle it. The brief's sentence therefore rests on one print and one reading of it.
4. **The tree's entry for the Reformed cities** (the starting list for a new person) conflicts with the sources in three places, all in `uncertain`: its tile has him begin preaching on 1 January 1519, where Kidd has his admission that day and Myconius has the preaching begin on Christmas day 1519; its `why` says he tested every inherited practice in public debate, where the records show the debates took up his teaching and then images and the Mass; its "first of the Reformed churches" in the slate line is not in any held source, which says only that the Reformed churches look to him as one of their founders (JK01 264-266). Its `voices` and `people` lines (people's priest from 1519), "Second War of Kappel" and "four decades" agree with the sources.
5. **Foxe's age for him (forty-four, four years younger than Luther, FX 26366-26367)** disagrees with the birth that Kidd and Jackson give; kept under `uncertain`, not used.
6. **Claims not found.** "The records of the chapter" as Kidd's source for the 1518 election: Kidd cites Zwingli's Opera (KD 21197-21198), not the chapter's records, so the card says only "by Kidd". The brief's "five Catholic cantons ... of central Switzerland" has no wording in a held source for "central" (Kidd names the "Five Forest Cantons", KD 26088); the card leaves the brief's gloss to the brief. Sheet items I8 and the Great Minster are the tree's own entry, not a file in `cic/texts/`.
7. **Credited, not told as fact:** that Calvin never met Zwingli is the tree's own `why` line; the heart in the ashes and the burning of the body are Myconius's and Foxe's reports; "act of aggression" is Kidd's verdict.
