# Outline check: Bernard of Clairvaux

## The checker's own heart (written before reading `Outline.md`)

From the slate job, the tradition entry `cistercian-monasticism`, the source sheet and what Bernard is known for.

**Essence.** Around 1113 a young man of a Burgundian knightly family entered the struggling house of Cîteaux with more than thirty companions, his brothers among them (William of Saint-Thierry). Made abbot of the new house of Clairvaux, he became the voice the Cistercians spread through: by his death, by Geoffrey's count, some seven hundred monks at Clairvaux and a hundred and sixty houses under his care. Through his sermons on the Song of Songs and his letters, the tree calls him the most listened-to churchman in Europe. He is on the tree as the Cistercians' most famous voice.

**Also told.**
