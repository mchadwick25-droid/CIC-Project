## The fix

The reviser moved "Nivard followed a little later." to the head of the next paragraph. That placement causes two errors:
- It now follows the reach-ahead "In time, popes and kings would come to ask his word." So "a little later" is heard as later than the popes and kings.
- It comes just before "Two years later, the abbot of Cîteaux sent him ... and made him their abbot". So "him" is heard as Nivard, and the two years count from Nivard's arrival.

Both errors say more than the source. William has Nivard follow soon after the entry of 1113 (VP 16252–16256). Bernard, not Nivard, was made abbot two years after he entered (essence; S4).

Replace paragraph 3:

> Bernard, with more than thirty companions, his friend William, who heard him preach, says, entered Cîteaux, a poor new monastery, where monks kept the old Rule of Saint Benedict strictly. He says the house was so small it had almost lost hope of lasting. In time, popes and kings would come to ask his word.

with:

> Bernard, with more than thirty companions, his friend William, who heard him preach, says, entered Cîteaux, a poor new monastery, where monks kept the old Rule of Saint Benedict strictly. Nivard followed a little later. The house was so small it had almost lost hope of lasting. In time, popes and kings would come to ask Bernard's word.

Replace the start of paragraph 5:

> Nivard followed a little later. Two years later, the abbot of Cîteaux sent him

with:

> Two years later, the abbot of Cîteaux sent him

This replacement makes three changes:
- It places Nivard where M2 asked.
- It keeps William credited once (M3). He is credited for the whole paragraph, as "They lived on barley bread" already is.
- It names Bernard where "his" would otherwise follow Nivard.

The fix is −2 words, which brings the story to about 676. The essence then ends at about word 152.
