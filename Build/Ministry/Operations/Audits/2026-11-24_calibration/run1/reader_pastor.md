- **P11 top:** a man at prayer and study, a real quarrel (gourd or ivy) told as a scene, every side given its due, and an ending that returns to the bitter seed and sweet fruit.
- **P04 second:** "Whom, then, will you wash?" is devotion in his own words. Doctrine comes out of worship. The "Gregory says" tags are the main cost.
- **P08 third:** a hard, combative bishop shown from the inside (the Euphrates water, the curtain), neither excused nor mocked, ending with the church as the cure.
- **P02 third from bottom:** the doctrine paragraph is fair and serious, but the rest is "by his account, at his table" over and over. Unexplained terms (indulgences, Diet, absolution). The 1525 and 1543 writings are named and then dropped, which reads as dodging.
- **P01 second from bottom:** a summary paragraph, not a story. It names danger and leadership without one person or one belief to hold onto.
- **P09 bottom:** an institution, not a faith. It is crowded with unexplained labels (Jansenism, Unigenitus, concordat, Ex illa die, vicars apostolic), and it reads "(in a plain English rendering)" aloud. No believer appears anywhere.
