## Principles for items 17 to 108 (Mark: "a", 2026-11-09)

 P1 Evidence sets an entry's span, not the era lines.
 P2 A distinct body with its own period and its own voice in the Library gets its own entry.
 P3 A living tradition past its entry's end gets a successor entry, not a stretched one; with no Library voice for the later period, hold it as a named gap (dashed thread), queue the wanted texts with the source-research thread, build when they arrive.
 P4 A person gets a mark if they pass ruling 14, or have 10+ own works on a shelf, or are named repeatedly in later traditions' own texts; told within the tradition the Library shelves them in, carrying its door; no fitting tradition, no door.
 P5 A branch, order, wing or colonial arm already told by an existing entry stays inside it, unless it passes P2 on its own evidence.
 P6 A thin lead (a category, a stale note, no Library evidence) is left out.
