# Whitespace-tolerant re-run of the WANTED rows (2026-11-12)

Coordinator's ruling: step one of the combined list. Names are double-spaced in many OCR files, so a single-space search can wrongly report a person as not held (John of Parma, Pascal of Vittoria, Joan of Arc and Andrew of Perugia were missed this way in the supplement scan).

## Method

- All 464 WANTED rows of the ten era audits and the supplement (after counting a person carried between eras once). For each row the name was split into persons (pairs separately) and searched in `cic/texts/` (`.txt`, `.xml`, `.md`, excluding the index files) with ripgrep, case-insensitive, with `\s+` between the words of the name (the first three words of the name, titles dropped), and the surname alone for one-word names.
- A row counts as "found held" only when the hits show the person's own work or a life of them in a held file, not a mention or a namesake.
- 123 of 464 rows had a hit anywhere; 50 had a hit in a file whose name carries the surname or 15 or more hits in one file; 71 had two or more hits for a multi-word name. Each of those was read by file name, and the contexts were opened for the ones below.

## Result

- **Found held: 1.** Charles Simeon (era 9). The audit says "grepped Charles Simeon: none". Wesley's Journal vol. 7 (Curnock, 1909) holds Wesley's own entry of 20 December 1784 on meeting Simeon at Hinxworth ("two kindred souls", wesley-j_journal-v7_curnock1909.txt, about line 2766), a footnote of the editor that tells Simeon's birth at Reading in 1759, his conversion at King's College and his ordination in 1782 (about lines 2795-2830), and Marsden's biography names him (marsden_life-and-work-of-samuel-marsden_jb-marsden1913.txt 600). It is a witness and a short life by another hand, not his own work. The row is dropped from this list and logged as a change-order item for the coordinator (rescore Simeon, probably WHO flagged "no own text held" by the two-witness rule for heads, or LISTED as a later retelling). The Carus memoir is not held.
- **Already recorded in the audit row (the hit confirms the audit, the row stands):** Margery Kempe (the short print "taken out of the boke of Margerie kempe" in cell-of-self-knowledge, 11 hits: the audit row says so), Theophylact Simocatta (Photius, Bibliotheca, an epitome: the audit row says so), James Davenport (Chauncy, Seasonable Thoughts: a hostile source, the audit row says so), Adolphe Monod (about twenty lines in Bersier's introduction to the 1872 synod: the audit row says so), Maron (Assemani, Bibliotheca Orientalis: logged in the supplement), Samson Occom, Ann Griffiths, Samuel Davies, Anne Dutton, Isaac Watts, Philip Doddridge, Robert Hindmarsh, Judith Sargent Murray, William Wilberforce, Muratori, John Henry Newman, John Keble, Abraham Kuyper (a held work or edition exists but not the source that carries the life; each audit row says so and names the life wanted).
- **Mentions or namesakes only, rows stand:** the other rows with hits, including Oswald of Worcester (wills in the York registry), Benedict Biscop and Ceolfrid, Antony and Theodosius of the Caves, Boris and Gleb, Thomas Aquinas, Thomas Becket, Innocent III, Marco Polo, Dominic de Guzman, William and Catherine Booth, Joseph Smith, Walter Scott, the Maronite and Coptic patriarchs of the 19th and 20th centuries (the hits are the Charon and Abu Salih volumes naming other patriarchs of the same names), Ilia II, Janani Luwum (a Gothic Bible: other words), Ben and Lois Roden, Tikhon (Bellavin) (Ligarides, Mouravieff: other men), Peter Abelard (the Martyrs Mirror names him in a line on Arnald of Brescia: the Historia calamitatum is not held), and the single-hit rows (Gregory the Illuminator, Ibn Ishaq, Paulus Alvarus, Adam of Bremen, John of Capistrano, Sarah Crosby, Edward Irving, Jabez Bunting, Henry Bleby and the rest of the one-hit list).
- Limits. Two-word names searched as written; a person known under another spelling (Dallet's Korean forms, Mar and Abba forms, Latin forms) may still be missed. A person whose life sits in a file under a different name (an editor's note) would show only as a hit. The Library thread should repeat the search with the spelling it uses before acquiring any source.
