## Source verification

- Augustine, Contra Epistulam Parmeniani I.1 (`cic/texts/augustini_scripta-contra-donatistas-pars-i-iii_petschenig1908-1910.txt`, lines 2163-2177): "et Parmenianus quidem primo eum per epistulam uelut corrigendum putauit; postea uero etiam concilio eorum perhibent esse damnatum. epistulae itaque Parmeniani quam scripsit ad Tychonium reprehendens eum, quod ecclesiam praedicaret toto orbe diffusam, et ammonens ne facere auderet ..." The rebuke and the warning are stated without a hedge. The condemnation carries "perhibent", "they report". The rebuke is about preaching the church spread through the whole world, not about any book.

## Findings

### 1. SUBSTANTIAL: the rewrite says the bishop forbade the book
Old (`don.dw.what-we-argued-among-ourselves` `text`): "We produced no school, no system, no line of doctors - one book, by one man, whom we silenced."
New: "No school, no system, no line of doctors is attested among us - only one book of interpretation, by a man our bishop told to stop preaching it."
`positions[2]` new: "One work of interpretation is attested among us, and its author was told to stop preaching it."
In both, "it" can only be the book. The source says Parmenian rebuked Tyconius for preaching that the church is spread through the whole world (I.1, "quod ecclesiam praedicaret toto orbe diffusam"). `don.source.tyconius-liber-regularum` gives the cause as "his universalist ecclesiology", and `don.figure.tyconius` puts the book "in the same period", not as the thing forbidden. The old wording did not make this link. The new wording adds a claim. "is attested among us" is also scholar's phrasing in the spoken `text`; this voice names its uncertainty by what it can show (as the branch's own edit to `how-we-read-and-heard` does).
Fix (`text`): "We can show you no school, no system, no line of doctors - only one book of interpretation, by that same man." The paragraph just above already tells the rebuke in full.
Fix (`positions[2]`): "'we were not a teaching movement. One work of interpretation is attested among us, by Tyconius, whom our bishop told to stop preaching that the church spans the earth. Our councils ruled on standing, not on doctrine'"

### 2. SUBSTANTIAL: "his following did not exist" is left in the paragraph this branch rewrote
`don.dw.how-we-read-and-heard` `text`, the sentence just before the edited one: "His book survives; his following did not exist."
This states an absence as a fact. The sources say no following is attested, and Monceaux says he was "perhaps" the only member left. `don.term.liber-regularum` says he "gathered few followers" and that "no following is attested". OG-31's own basis is that "no following of his is attested". The edited sentence that follows ("So our method of reading comes down to this") rests on it.
Fix: "His book survives; we can show you no one who followed him."

### 3. NOT SUBSTANTIAL: OG-31 does not register the unhedged "cut off" in the records it edited, or keep its flagged items open
The branch hedges the condemnation in `what-we-never-settled` because the story keeps "perhibent". The same fact stays unhedged in two records this branch edited: `don.dw.how-we-read-and-heard` `text` ("and cut him off") and `don.dw.what-we-argued-among-ourselves` `text` ("and was cut off, and was never taken back"), plus `divergence_note` ("the silencing of Tyconius ... are Documented"). It is also unhedged in `don.dw.room-for-dissent` (which OG-30 item (9) already names), `don.dw.hypocrites-and-those-who-left`, `don.dw.becoming-one-of-us`, `don.dw.the-word-catholic-and-no-door-today` and `don.limit.theology-beyond-tyconius` ("His own council condemned him for it"). That is a contested point presented as settled, and it is outside Mark's ruled item, so it need not be fixed here. But it is known, and OG-31 is silent on it. OG-31's status line also closes the entry without saying that the three flagged items stay open. The world front prints the ruled overstatement on the public site.
Fix (OG-31, not yet merged, so it can be edited in place): add under "Not changed": "The condemnation is stated without Augustine's hedge in `don.dw.how-we-read-and-heard` and `don.dw.what-we-argued-among-ourselves` `text`, and in `don.dw.room-for-dissent`, `don.dw.hypocrites-and-those-who-left`, `don.dw.becoming-one-of-us`, `don.dw.the-word-catholic-and-no-door-today` and `don.limit.theology-beyond-tyconius`; flagged for the build thread."
