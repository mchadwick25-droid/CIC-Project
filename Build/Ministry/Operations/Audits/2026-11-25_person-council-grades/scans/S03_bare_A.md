## tree/theodoret-of-cyrrhus
Heart: Theodoret was Antioch's leading fifth-century voice for Christ as one, God and man unmixed. He answered Cyril's twelve curses and stood by his deposed friend Nestorius. He was deposed in 449 and let back at Chalcedon only after cursing Nestorius. He was also a historian of the church and of the Syrian monks.
