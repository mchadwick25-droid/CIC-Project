line of every file (title page, imprint year, reprint signs at the front and the back), and 41 files in depth
(header against title page, two content-note claims against the text, a mid-file OCR sample, the shelf row),
among them every file not in English. This is the reviewer's report, shortened; line numbers are file lines
unless marked OCR.

## Blocking

- **B1. `eddy_retrospection-and-introspection_1892.txt`.** The OCR of the title page shows no year; the only
  years in the OCR are the copyright notices of 1891 and 1892, and the header said so.
- **B2. `offord_jerry-mcauley-his-life-and-work_2nd-ed1885.txt`.** The title page prints "Second edition, New
  York, published by the New York Observer" and no year; the only year is "Copyright, 1885".
- **B3. `cock_twee-der-eerste-geschriften-nld_1882.txt`.** The header gives de Cock as author. The son's
  foreword (about lines 384-388) says the letters were issued by his father, not written by him. There are
  forewords by the widow and the son.
- **B4. `kuyper_tractaat-van-de-reformatie-der-kerken-nld_1883.txt`.** The header's publisher, "Höveker (&
  Wormser)", is not on the title page, whose OCR reads as "Höveker & Zoon".

## Not blocking

Content-note or row details the text does not support:

1. Allgemeine Missions-Zeitschrift 1918: the obituary is about three pages (pp. 174-176), not ten.
2. Presbyterian Review: the article ends at its signatures, OCR 2417-2418, not "around line 1500".
3. Bavinck, Magnalia Dei: the locus covers only the Voorrede; the book is in numbered sections, not "articles
   of the confession"; the contents are at line 28769.
4. Phillips: "a minister who travelled with Roberts" has no supporting line; "standard life" is the press
   notices' phrase.
5. Pusey's sermon: "a late sermon" is unsupported. The year is at OCR line 40.
6. Kuyper: "the run-up to the 1886 Doleantie" comes from outside the file.
7. Booth: the dedication names no one.
8. Spurgeon: the "contents for January 1870" is an advertisement's, not the sermons'.
9. Veniaminov, part 2: the word count given for part 1 is wrong.
10. Der Lutheraner: the filename says 1845; the header says 1844.

Shelf rows:

11. Butcher: one row on two entries, one of whose windows ends in 1854; split it or mark it `needs-ruling`.
12. One of China's Scholars: the printing of 1909 is outside the entry's window; `needs-ruling`.
13. Fifteen loci have words such as "throughout", "not located" or "search" where line numbers should be.
14. The Offord and Eddy rows cite a copyright notice as the title page.

Imprint citations: for thirteen files the year is visible but the OCR line cited is wrong by 4 to 50 lines.

Rights notes that are not failures: four years are printed but not on a title-page imprint (a printing
history, a periodical's number heading, a volume heading, a colophon imprint); Millennial Dawn's title page
reads 1886 on a later printing; the Newberry rights text on the Doctrine and Covenants is not an NC license;
eleven Google scans carry Google's request about non-commercial use, which is a request and not a license.

Commentary: thirty-two content notes mention items "not taken" or "located", and twelve speak of the search
itself ("the next pick", "see report", "so avoid them"). These belong in the search log.

Checked claims that held include Stone's chapter starts, Finney's lectures, Newman's parts and appendix, the
Medhurst pamphlet starts, Booth's contents and appendix, Hale's sources, Dollinger's preface and contents, and
the three Audo loci in the Vatican council volume. The OCR-quality statements were honest wherever sampled.
