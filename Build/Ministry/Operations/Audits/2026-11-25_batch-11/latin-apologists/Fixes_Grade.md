## What the sources hold

1. **When (opening).** No old source dates the Octavius or the walk at Ostia (outline, When and where: "Rome and Ostia, undated (the Octavius; Contested)"; `Fixes_Review.md`, fix 7). A clause naming the unknown date is barred (Do not say) and was cut once as a gap-only tell (`Review.md` 7). The one when the old sources give is Minucius's own: he writes after Octavius died, of a day from their friendship, now past. ANF4 17198-17200: "as if I were returning to past times, and not merely recalling in my recollection things which have long since happened and gone by"; ANF4 17203-17204: "when he departed <i>this life</i>"; ANF4 17217-17219: "when my thoughts were traversing the entire period of our intimacy and friendship, the direction of my mind fixed itself chiefly on that discourse of his". Latin, MinLat 114-116: "in praeterita redire, non ea quae iam transacta et decursa sunt, recordatione reuocare". Own words; Documented. Kit Part 1 allows a when only "as roughly as the old sources allow". The tradition's span ("from around 200 to the reign of the emperor Diocletian") stays where it is, in the essence sentence. Added to sheet, Setting (new row after "The Octavius is a memory of a dead friend"), and to outline Openings 1.
2. **Add-on line.** "Minucius wrote it after Octavius died." (ANF4 17203-17205; Documented) is folded into the opening's first sentence, where it now carries the when. Outline beat 2 notes the move.
3. **Why the church at Nicomedia was torn down.** Lactantius gives a reason. ANF7 10152-10154 (On the Deaths of the Persecutors 12): "A fit and auspicious day was sought out for the accomplishment of this undertaking; and the festival of the god Terminus ... was chosen, in preference to all others, to terminate, as it were, the Christian religion." Own words, contemporary, at Nicomedia, hostile to the persecuting emperors; English 1886; Latin not held. Documented that he says it. The aim covers the next day's torture order too (ANF7 10174). He also blames Galerius, urged by his mother, for pressing Diocletian (ANF7 10139); that cause is the lactantius story's and goes to Leave out. Added to sheet, Setting (new row after "Lactantius's own word"), and to outline Words to explain and beat 5.

## Cuts, and where each goes

- **1a.** "On the way to the sea": Leave out. The shore walk stays; nothing in the brief now puts the image anywhere else.
- **1.** "early one morning": Leave out; the first sentence says "a day". "Three friends" becomes "the two of them and Caecilius" (+1).
- **1b.** "their friend ... Felix": Leave out; Minucius is named in full in the first sentence.
- **1c.** "a pleader, or court lawyer" and "in his dialogue the Octavius" move up, not lost; "tells it" goes to Leave out.
- **2.** The add-on line goes into change 1, not lost.
- **2a.** "in time": Leave out.
- **3.** "was torn down" repeated "leveled it": the repeat goes to Leave out; the burning, the leveling and the torture order stay. Galerius's part: Leave out (the lactantius story).
