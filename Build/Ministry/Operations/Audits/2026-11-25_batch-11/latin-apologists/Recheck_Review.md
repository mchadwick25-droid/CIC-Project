# Recheck of the review fixes: The Latin Apologists (`latin-apologists`)

I checked only what `Fixes_Review.md` changed in `Outline.md`.

## Verdict: FIX

### What holds

**The new lines say no more than their sources.**
- **Serapis gloss:** holds at MinLat 220-223 and ANF4 17252-17256.
- **Beat 2's close:** "Minucius wrote it after Octavius had died" holds at ANF4 17201-17205. With the date clause gone, the gap-only tell is gone too.
- **Jerome line:** holds at NPNF2-6 16477-16480 and 16704-16721. Tertullian's books hold "all the wisdom of the world", Minucius "ransacked all heathen literature", and Arnobius and Lactantius give "an epitome of Cicero's dialogues". It is later, about 90 years on, and Documented that Jerome says it.
- **Diocletian line:** holds at ANF7 10079, 10161 and 10174. The prefect and officers burn the Scriptures. The guards level the church, by Diocletian's choice. Next day comes the edict: "without any distinction of rank or degree, they should be subjected to tortures". It also holds at NPNF2-1 43484-43491. The tie to Arnobius is rightly marked Inferential-Thin.

**The cuts.** Cuts 7-13 take neither the heart nor a required explanation. After cut 8, the New Prophecy gloss still says what the movement was, and after cut 7 the Do not say line on baptism still holds.

**The length.** The plan fits 675. The draft was 676. The review's changes add about 18 words, and cuts 7-8 take 19. Fixes 9 and 10 add about 49, and cuts 9-13 take about 56. That leaves about 668.

**The order.** The beats still run once, in order. The Jerome line falls after beat 5 and before the Ending, which closes on Lactantius's own words about Minucius. Part 1 allows that close.

### What needs fixing

**Placing the Diocletian line.** The gloss says to give the line "where the story first names it". That is one of two places, and both fail:
- **The essence** ("to the persecution under Diocletian"). The essence would then tell the levelling of the church, and beat 5 would tell it again (ANF7 4487). One event would be told twice.
- **Beat 4** ("In Diocletian's reign"). The line would then sit beside Arnobius's burned writings, which implies the Inferential-Thin cause the gloss forbids.

DMP 12-13 and DI V.2 tell the same event, the church torn down where Lactantius taught. So the line belongs once, in beat 5, merged with ANF7 4487.

## Exact replacements in `Outline.md`

**1. The heart, essence.** This changes the wording only; the heart's substance is unchanged. It is the option the review itself offers in fix 10.
- Replace: `who from around 200 to the persecution under Diocletian argued their faith`
- With: `who from around 200 to the reign of the emperor Diocletian argued their faith`

**2. Words to explain, the Diocletian's persecution entry.**
- Replace: `say it where the story first names it: "Diocletian was the emperor. Lactantius says his officers burned the Scriptures and levelled the church at Nicomedia, and next day an order made every Christian open to torture."`
- With: `say it once, in beat 5, as the account of the church torn down where Lactantius taught (DI V.2, ANF7 4487, and On the Deaths of the Persecutors 12-13 tell the same event; Widely Accepted): "Lactantius says the emperor's officers burned its Scriptures and levelled it, and next day an order laid every Christian, whatever his rank, open to torture."`
- Replace: `Keep it apart from the ANF7 4487 sentence, which stays without a cause. Arnobius's burned writings`
- With: `Never put it in the essence or in beat 4, nor beside Arnobius's burned writings`
- Replace: `Inferential-Thin as a cause, so never say the edicts caused them.`
- With: `Inferential-Thin as a cause, so never say or imply the edicts caused them.`

**3. Beat 5.**
- Replace: `He was teaching there when, he says, the church was thrown down.`
- With: `He was teaching there when the Christians' church was torn down. Lactantius says the emperor's officers burned its Scriptures and levelled it, and next day an order laid every Christian, whatever his rank, open to torture (ANF7 4487, 10161, 10174; Documented).`

**4. Names.**
- Replace: `Diocletian — the emperor; one line (Words to explain, Diocletian's persecution)`
- With: `Diocletian — the emperor (essence; beat 4 by reign; beat 5 line under Words to explain)`

After these changes, the story reaches its end at about the same length as before, because the merged sentence in beat 5 replaces both the old clause and the separate line.
