## The brief changes

- **Count.** All seven changes applied to a scratch copy outside the repository: each old string occurs once, and the result is **674** by `wc -w` (limit 675). The new first sentence is 24 words, and so is the Nicomedia sentence.
- **Change 1, the when.** ANF4 17198-17204 (MinLat 114-116) has Minucius looking back on "past times ... things which have long since happened and gone by". The same passage has Octavius already dead ("when he departed *this life*"), and ANF4 17217-17219 fixes his mind "chiefly on that discourse". So "After his friend Octavius died, Minucius Felix ... wrote down a day from their friendship" says no more than the source. It names no date and has no gap-only clause, so the Do not say line holds. Documented, own words. "A pleader, or court lawyer" (ANF7 4470) and the title move up with nothing lost.
- **Change 3, Lactantius's reason.** ANF7 10152-10154 says the festival of Terminus was chosen "to terminate, as it were, the Christian religion". The brief says "on a day picked to end Christianity". "As it were" marks his play on Terminus, not a weaker aim, so dropping it adds nothing. The clause sits under "Lactantius says", so it is told as his, as the sheet requires ("Documented that he says it"). The burning and leveling match ANF7 10161-10164 and the next day's order matches ANF7 10174. Galerius (ANF7 10139) stays out, as Leave out says. The Latin is not held: a search for *Terminalia* and *De mortibus persecutorum* in `cic/texts/` finds only modern notes.
- **The cuts.** Nothing that matters is lost. "On the way to the sea", "early one morning", "their friend ... Felix", "tells it", "in time" and the repeated "was torn down" are all detail or repeats. The heart and the charges stay. So do the torture, the burning and leveling, the torture order, Q1-Q3 and every credited witness. Every gloss Part 1 requires also stays: apology, Serapis, pleader, rhetoric, New Prophecy, Nicomedia, and what the persecution did. "In time" adds nothing that Jerome's "afterwards" needs here.
- **One hearing.** The opening reads clearly. "The two of them" points back to Minucius and Octavius. Caecilius now comes in without "friends", but what he does shows who he is, and the scolding ("for letting Caecilius bow") ties him to Minucius. That is not a finding.
