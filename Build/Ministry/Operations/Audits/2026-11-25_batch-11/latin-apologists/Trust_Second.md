## BLOCKING findings

1. **"By the road to the sea, Caecilius saw an image of the god Serapis and raised a hand to his mouth, kissing it toward the god."** SOFTENED OR CHANGED. No road is in the source. MinLat 220-224: "cum diluculo ad mare inambulando litore pergeremus ... Caecilius simulacro Serapidis denotato ... manum ori admouens osculum labiis inpressit". ANF4 17252-17258: "when in the early morning we were going towards the sea along the shore". The outline's gloss ("whose image stood by the road to the sea") goes past the source too. **Change:** "As they walked along the shore toward the sea, Caecilius saw an image of the god Serapis and raised a hand to his mouth, kissing it toward the god." Outline Do not say: "the image stood by the road" → they were walking along the shore toward the sea.

2. **"His turning rests on Jerome alone, who wrote about eighty years later that dreams drove him toward belief, and the bishop would not take him in."** SOFTENED OR CHANGED. Only the story of how he came over (the dreams, the bishop, the books) rests on Jerome. Arnobius gives the turning itself in his own words, and the brief quotes them two sentences later. ArnLat 1129-1130: "uenerabar, o caecitas, nuper simulacra"; ANF6 40657: "But lately, O blindness, I worshipped images". The outline and source sheet both say "the story" or "conversion story" rests on Jerome (Source_Sheet §6). As written, the listener hears a claim that the quote then contradicts. **Change:** "The story of how he came over rests on Jerome alone, who wrote about eighty years later that dreams drove him toward belief, and the bishop would not take him in."

3. **"Of those he knew, he wrote, Minucius Felix had stood well among the pleaders, ..."** SOFTENED OR CHANGED. LactLat 22701: "ex iis qui mihi noti sunt"; ANF7 4470: "Of those who are known to me". Lactantius means writers known to him. Heard once, "those he knew" suggests he knew Minucius in person, a link no source gives. **Change:** "Of the writers known to him, he wrote, Minucius Felix had stood well among the pleaders, ..." (the rest unchanged).
