## The heart

**It is the right heart.** The sources show a church of town congregations, each around one bishop. Cyprian's letters say he decides nothing "sine consilio uestro et sine consensu plebis" (CyL 2294-2296). Ep. 19 says the bishops meet with the clergy, "praesente etiam stantium plebe", to settle the fallen (CyL 2925-2927). Ep. 228 tells the clergy not to leave their people (Pos 4556-4561). Persecution, plague, a rival church and the Vandals are what the record shows pressing on it.

**It is on time.** The opening runs to word 111. The essence begins at word 112, and its first sentence (who, when, where and what held them) is done by about word 145, inside the 150. The full essence ends at word 191.
