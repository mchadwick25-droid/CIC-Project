## FIX

**1. Optatus's second sentence can be heard as calling the Donatists false teachers.** In "So he did not call them heretics, who taught falsely about God", the clause "who taught falsely about God" can attach to "them" on one hearing. That brings back the contradiction B5 was raised to remove. Replace, in beat 5, in Words to explain (Optatus's line), and in Connection:

- "So he did not call them heretics, who taught falsely about God." → "So he did not call them heretics: to him, heretics taught falsely about God." (+2)
- Pay for it with: "In the 390s, at the town of Hippo, in today's Algeria," → "In the 390s, at Hippo, in today's Algeria," (-3). This matches Opening 1 as the outline already holds it ("In the 390s, at Hippo"). "In today's Algeria" still places the town.
- Net -1: 761.
