## B11-09
Source: Cyprian, *Letters* (Hartel), lines 6825-6828, Latin.

Rendering: A great crowd of the brothers and sisters came together. All of them spoke with one voice, giving thanks to God. They showed the joy in their hearts with tears, and embraced them as though they had been set free from prison that very day.

Note: "fraternitas" is the whole Christian community ("the brotherhood"); "poena carceris" is literally "the punishment of prison". "Them" are the people being received back, named in the sentence before.

## B11-10
Source: Eusebius, *Church History* VI (Schwartz), lines 2515-2516, Greek.

Read as: εἰ τοῦτό ἐστιν μόνον τὸ δοκοῦν ὑμῖν παρέχειν μικροψυχίαν, ἀναγινωσκέσθω.

Rendering: If this is the only thing that seems to be causing petty quarrels among you, let it be read.

Scan: "do- >z00»" across the line break is read as δοκοῦν, and "μιχροφυχίαν" as μικροψυχίαν. The word means small-mindedness, fussy scruple or petty friction; "causing you petty-mindedness" is the literal sense. "It" is the Gospel of Peter, named just before.

## B11-11
Source: Eusebius, *Church History* VI (Schwartz), lines 2510-2513, Greek.

Read as: ἀδελφοί, καὶ Πέτρον καὶ τοὺς ἄλλους ἀποστόλους ἀποδεχόμεθα ὡς Χριστόν, τὰ δὲ ὀνόματι αὐτῶν ψευδεπίγραφα ὡς ἔμπειροι παραιτούμεθα, γινώσκοντες ὅτι τὰ τοιαῦτα οὐ παρελάβομεν.

Rendering: Brothers, we receive Peter and the other apostles as we receive Christ. But the writings falsely put out under their names we reject, as people who know better, because we know that such things were not handed down to us.

Scan: "xai" is καὶ; "παρελά- >Bousv" is παρελάβομεν. The "we" (ἡμεῖς γάρ, "For we") stands just before the passage start and is carried by the verbs. "ὡς ἔμπειροι" is "as experienced people".
