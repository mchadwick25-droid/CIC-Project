# Card edits: Apocryphal and Pseudepigraphal Literature (`apocryphal-and-pseudepigraphal-literature`, tradition)

Card written: `apocryphal-and-pseudepigraphal-literature.card.json`, from `Card.existing.json`. A tradition has no `card` field, so the card file holds the entry's card fields in the same order as `Card.existing.json`. `why`, `relationsSummary`, `teaser` and `documentedStories` are left word for word in the card file; their changes are listed under **Entry fields** for release, as with the Antioch card in this batch. Every other field not listed below is word for word. Four new keys come at the end: `uncertain`, `sources` (in the `cyril-and-methodius` form, `ownWords` and `about`), `briefReferences`, `quotationOriginals`.

File short names: Schwartz = `eusebius_werke-bd2-2-historia-ecclesiastica-vi-x-rufinus-x-xi-grc-lat_schwartz-mommsen1908.txt`; NPNF 2.1 = `npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`; CSEL 20 = `tertullian_opera-pars1-lat_reifferscheid-wissowa-csel20.txt`; ANF 3, ANF 8, ANF 9; Gebhardt = `acta-martyrum-selecta_gebhardt1902.txt`; McClure = `egeria_pilgrimage-of-etheria_mcclure-feltoe1919.txt`; NPNF 2.3, 2.4, 2.6. All under `cic/texts/`.

**Check run.** With the card fields merged into a scratch copy of the census outside the repository (the brief as `longDescription`), `node tools/validate-census.mjs <copy> --skip-quotes` gives 0 errors. The validator does not check a tradition's quotations, so a scratch script of my own, using the validator's fold and line rules, checked each `modern` against the brief's three quoted spans, and each `printed`, `original` and `englishPrinted` at its file and lines: all pass. Each quoted span in `legacy` and `uncertain` stands verbatim in a file in `sources`.

## Clearly wrong (card fields)

1. `voices[0]`.
   - Old: "The Protevangelium of James - anonymous, mid-second century, and"
   - New: "The Protevangelium of James - under James's name, and"
   - Evidence: the book names James as its writer, ANF 8 37167 ("And I James that wrote this history"); the brief: "One under James's name". "Mid-second century" is a date only modern works give: outline, Do not say ("the mid-second-century Protevangelium", any date for the books: no composition date); Part 1, Rigor.
2. `voices[3]`.
   - Old: "condemned for its use at Rhossus by Serapion of Antioch, and recovered from an Egyptian grave in 1886"
   - New: "condemned for its use at Rhossus by Serapion of Antioch; a gospel fragment in Peter's voice was found in an Egyptian grave in 1886"
   - Evidence: that the Akhmim fragment is Serapion's book is a modern identification (Source sheet C5, Dominant Modern Reconstruction; outline What came of it 5 and Leave out). The find: ANF 9 435-441 ("discovered ... in a grave ... at Akhmîm ... in 1886"); Peter's voice, ANF 9 784 ("But I Simon Peter").
3. `legacy`, first sentence.
   - Old: "Marian devotion in both East and West rests in large part on the Protevangelium of James."
   - New: "A book under James's name, the Protevangelium of James, tells of Mary's parents and her childhood in the Temple."
   - Evidence: a claim the brief no longer makes (the approved brief: "Almost everything later Christians believed about her early life comes from it"); Part 1 bars it from `legacy`. The new sentence keeps the antecedent for "came from it" and holds only what the brief and ANF 8 hold: 36612, 36648 (Joachim, Anna), 36784-36799 (the temple from three), 37167 (James).
4. `legacy`, last two sentences.
   - Old: " And the Jewish books in this collection, written under the names of ancient figures, survive only because Christians copied them. Ironically, they were kept by the tradition that had parted from the one that produced them."
   - New (What came of it, which the outline sends to the card): " The verdicts went on as well. In 367 Athanasius of Alexandria called such books "an invention of heretics". Jerome still knew the Acts of Paul and Thecla, and counted them "among the apocryphal writings". Later, writing to a mother about her daughter's reading, he said: "Let her avoid all apocryphal writings". If she read them, she should understand "that it requires infinite discretion to look for gold in the midst of dirt.""
   - Evidence, removal: a claim the brief no longer makes (the approved brief made it); outline Leave out: "Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs and Jewish pseudepigrapha (no held old witness in the window; card's claim modern)"; Source sheet, Set aside.
   - Evidence, addition: outline What came of it 1 ("card only"), 2 and 4 ("Card"). NPNF 2.4 68714 ("(For 367.)"), 68825 ("an invention of heretics"); NPNF 2.3 39502-39505; NPNF 2.6 20705-20710.
