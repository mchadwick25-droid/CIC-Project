## B3: what Serapion's heresy was

**Search.** HE 6.12 in Schwartz–Mommsen Bd 2,2 (Greek and Rufinus) and npnf201; Clement, Stromata 7.17 (anf02 53018–53021); Hippolytus, Refutation 8 (anf05 12442–12444). No old source of the period says what the Rhossus backers' teaching was. Clement and Hippolytus name Docetae, but neither mentions Rhossus, Serapion or a gospel of Peter. The tie is McGiffert's (npnf201 note 1838, modern).

**What the source keeps.** Serapion's own Marcianus sentence, the one line between "their mind … in some heresy" and the borrowed gospel.

**Source_Sheet.md**
