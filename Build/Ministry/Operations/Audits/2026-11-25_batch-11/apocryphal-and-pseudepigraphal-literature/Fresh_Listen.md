## 1. The test

- **Who these people were:** filled. Christians in the second to fourth centuries who wrote, read aloud and argued over gospels and apostle stories that never entered the New Testament, and the bishops and teachers who judged them.
- **What happened to them:** filled. Serapion first allows the Gospel of Peter, then refuses it. The writer of Paul's travels is found out and leaves his office. Eusebius ranks the books low. Even so, the Acts of Thecla is still read at her church in Egeria's day.
- **What their faith was about:** filled, but thin. I can say they loved the apostles and wanted the stories the Gospels leave out, such as Mary's childhood and Thecla. Some took Thecla as permission for women to teach and baptize. But the teaching that turned Serapion against the book is never said, so one side of the quarrel stays empty for me (see 4).
- **Why the connection matters:** filled. The books were refused by bishops but kept, copied "in many later copies", and read in worship at Thecla's shrine.

## 2. The opening

After the first three sentences I have who (Bishop Serapion and the church at Rhossus), where (Rhossus, on the Syrian coast near Antioch), when (about 200) and why there (a quarrel over a gospel with Peter's name). Nothing missing. For a listener with no church background, "gospel" is not said plainly: see 7.

## 3. Sentences that fail on one hearing

1. "He wrote that he saw what one Marcianus held: a man who contradicted himself, not understanding what he said." On one hearing I cannot tell whether "a man" is Marcianus or what he held, and I never learn what he held. The meaning lost is why Serapion changed his mind. Linked to the BLOCKING item in 4. MINOR on its own; Marcianus is named once and never acts (minor by the bar).
2. "In one form of Thecla's book, this was where she taught many and fell asleep." I took "fell asleep" literally. The meaning lost is that she died there. MINOR. Change: "this was where she taught many and died."
3. "Tertullian ... answered them that Paul did not even let a woman learn too boldly." I could not follow "learn too boldly". I lost what Paul's rule had to do with teaching and baptizing. MINOR; noted, since the sense is the source's.
