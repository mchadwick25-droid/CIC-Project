## BLOCKING findings

### B1. "and the spirit behind them hostile to the whole church" (paragraph 5)

Both trust reads: ASSUMPTION ADDED. HE 29716-29738: the spirit reviled the Church "because the spirit of false prophecy received neither honor from it nor entrance into it"; the faithful's finding was that the utterances were "novel" and "profane". The brief turns a result into the churches' reason.

- **Change:** "Finding them novel and profane, they drove the prophets' followers out, barred from communion: shut out of the church's fellowship and its shared meal."
- **Outline:** holds it (beat 3). The outline left room: beat 3's *Reason* reads "the churches judged the sayings novel and the spirit hostile to the whole church". Change that line.
- **Do not say** (add): "the churches found the spirit hostile to the whole church" as their reason → the anonymous says the spirit reviled the church because the church refused it (HE 29716-29720); the finding was "novel" and "profane" (HE 29736).

### B2. "The prophets' side called the bishops killers of the prophets the Lord had promised." (paragraph 5)

Both trust reads: SOFTENED. HE 29746-29754: "they called us slayers of the prophets because we did not receive their loquacious prophets, who, they say, are those that the Lord promised to send". "Us" is the presbyter's side, not the bishops, and the reason is dropped, so it sounds as if someone was killed. Both listeners (MINOR) could not tell what "the Lord had promised" belongs to. One change covers all three.

- **Change:** "The prophets' side called their opponents killers of the prophets, for refusing the prophets the Lord had promised to send."
- **Outline:** holds it (beat 3: "for refusing those the Lord had promised to send"). The outline left room: beat 3 says "the prophets' side called the bishops". Change to "their opponents".
- **Do not say** (add): "called the bishops 'slayers of the prophets'" → "they called us" (HE 29746), the anonymous presbyter's own side.

### B3. "In Phrygia, where the prophets had first spoken, ..." (paragraph 9)

Both trust reads: SOFTENED. Ardabau lay "in that part of Mysia, which borders upon Phrygia" (HE 29577-29579); Procopius says only "The Montanists, who were settled in Phrygia" (Proc 2195-2196).

- **Change:** "In Phrygia, the Montanists shut themselves inside their churches, set them alight and died in the flames."
- **Outline:** holds it (Proc 2195-2197). The outline left room: Ending 1 carries the same clause. Cut it there.
- **Do not say** (add): "Phrygia, where the prophets had first spoken" → Ardabau was in Mysia, on the Phrygian border (HE 29577-29579).

### B4. The opening and the heart: where and when missing, heart late (paragraphs 1-3)
