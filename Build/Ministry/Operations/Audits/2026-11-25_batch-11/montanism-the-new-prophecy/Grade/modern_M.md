## montanism-the-new-prophecy
Heart: In second-century Phrygia, Montanus, Priscilla and Maximilla claimed the Paraclete spoke through them in ecstasy, calling the church to a stricter life; the churches of Asia cast them out, Tertullian defended them, and their opponents' record traces them to the burning of their Phrygian churches under Justinian.
