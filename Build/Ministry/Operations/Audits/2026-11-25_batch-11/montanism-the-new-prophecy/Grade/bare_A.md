## montanism-the-new-prophecy
Heart: The New Prophecy was a second-century Christian movement from Phrygia whose prophets, Montanus, Priscilla and Maximilla, said the Paraclete spoke through them in ecstasy and called the church to a stricter life. The main church rejected them, Tertullian defended their discipline, and they held out until Justinian's empire forced them to give up their beliefs.
