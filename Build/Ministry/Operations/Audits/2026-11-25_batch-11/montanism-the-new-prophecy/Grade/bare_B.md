## montanism-the-new-prophecy
Heart: The New Prophecy was a second-century Phrygian movement whose prophets, Montanus, Priscilla and Maximilla, claimed the Paraclete spoke through them in ecstasy and called the church to a stricter life. The main church examined and expelled them, Tertullian later defended their rules, and they survived in Phrygia until Justinian's empire forced them out.
