## The heart

Right for the subject. The sources show a movement held together by the prophets' voice taken for the Paraclete (ANF03 47250-47251; HE 29745-29748) and the stricter life it asked (ANF04 9770-9787; CSEL20 15495-15499), tested and expelled by the churches of Asia (HE 29721-29738), and still in Phrygia centuries on (PanH 18466-18511; NPNF202 30574-30577; Proc 2195-2197). The essence (who: the New Prophecy, its three prophets; when and where: second-century Phrygia; what held them: the Paraclete speaking now, a stricter life) is told by the end of sentence 6, about 160 words in, within the first minute. The testing that defines what they faced follows at sentences 10-15.
