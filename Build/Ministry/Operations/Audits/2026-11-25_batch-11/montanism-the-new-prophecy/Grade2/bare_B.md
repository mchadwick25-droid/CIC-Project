## montanism-the-new-prophecy
Heart: The New Prophecy was a second-century Phrygian movement whose prophets, Montanus, Priscilla and Maximilla, held that the Paraclete spoke through them in ecstasy and called the church to a stricter life. The wider church, whose writers left most of the record, drove them out over the manner of their prophecy and their stricter rules, yet the movement lasted into the sixth century.
