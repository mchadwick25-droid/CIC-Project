## montanism-the-new-prophecy
Heart: A second-century movement in Phrygia, the New Prophecy, held that the Paraclete spoke now through its ecstatic prophets, Montanus, Priscilla and Maximilla, and called the church to a stricter life of fasting, single marriage and facing martyrdom. The wider church rejected the prophets and shut their followers out, yet the movement lasted, with Tertullian as its defender and Pepuza as its holy place, until Justinian's empire crushed it.
