## Heart

Right heart for the record: prophets in Phrygia who said the Paraclete spoke through them now, tested and cast out by the churches of Asia, defended by Tertullian, and still at Pepuza and in Phrygia centuries later. Who, when, where and what held them (the Paraclete speaking now, in ecstasy, calling to a stricter life) are told by word 145, inside the first minute.

## MINOR findings (apply word for word or note; none blocks)

1. **P3, "whose name is lost".** Eusebius gives no name. But the held text of HE V.16.17 carries the words "in the same work of Asterius Urbanus" (HE 29847-29848), and ANF07 prints the fragments under that name, so "lost" settles a point the record leaves a little open. Exact change: "a church elder whose name is lost" → "a church elder whom Eusebius does not name".
2. **P4, "writing forty years later".** The source counts from Montanus's start (HE 30456-30458). Coming straight after "The elder says...", a listener may count from the elder. Exact change: "writing forty years later" → "writing forty years after Montanus began".
3. **P5, "for refusing the prophets the Lord had promised to send".** It sits inside the prophets' side's charge, so it holds. One hearing could take it as the narrator's word. Optional exact change: "for refusing the prophets the Lord had promised to send" → "for refusing prophets who, they said, were the ones the Lord had promised to send".
4. **P8, "came down there".** Greek κατιέναι is present ("comes down"), as Q3 has it. In English reported speech, "came down" can sound like a past event. Exact change: "the heavenly city, came down there" → "the heavenly city, comes down there".
