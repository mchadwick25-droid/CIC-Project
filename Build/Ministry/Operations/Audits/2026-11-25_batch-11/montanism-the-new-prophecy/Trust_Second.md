### BLOCKING

**S11. ASSUMPTION ADDED.** "Finding them novel and profane, and the spirit behind them hostile to the whole church, they drove the prophets' followers out, barred from communion: shut out of the church's fellowship and its shared meal."
- Source, HE 29716-29738 (npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml): "the arrogant spirit taught them to revile the entire universal Church under heaven, because the spirit of false prophecy received neither honor from it nor entrance into it. For the faithful in Asia met often ... and examined the novel utterances and pronounced them profane, and rejected the heresy, and thus these persons were expelled from the Church and debarred from communion."
- The anonymous writer gives the reviling of the Church as a result of the Church's refusal, not as a reason the faithful found. The reason he gives for the expulsion is the examination: novel and profane. The brief turns the result into a cause.
- **Change:** "Finding them novel and profane, they drove the prophets' followers out, barred from communion: shut out of the church's fellowship and its shared meal."

**S12. SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** "The prophets' side called the bishops killers of the prophets the Lord had promised."
- Source, HE 29745-29754: "they called us slayers of the prophets because we did not receive their loquacious prophets, who, they say, are those that the Lord promised to send to the people".
- The writer is a presbyter ("our fellow-presbyter Zoticus", HE 29544) and says "us", the church side, not "the bishops". The brief also drops the stated reason (refusing the prophets), so it now reads as if the bishops had killed prophets.
- **Change:** "The prophets' side called the church's leaders killers of the prophets, for refusing the prophets the Lord had promised to send."

**S28. SOFTENED OR CHANGED (detail moved).** "In Phrygia, where the prophets had first spoken, the Montanists shut themselves inside their churches, set them alight and died in the flames."
- Source, Proc 2195-2197 (procopius_secret-history_athenian-society1896.txt): "The Montanists, who were settled in Phrygia, shut themselves up in their churches, set them on fire, and perished in the flames".
- Where they first spoke, HE 29577-29579: "a certain village called Ardabau in that part of Mysia, which borders upon Phrygia" (Greek in the note: ἐν τῇ κατὰ τὴν Φρυγίαν Μυσίᾳ). Procopius says nothing of where the prophets first spoke, and the anonymous puts the first prophesying in Mysia, by the border. The brief's own opening says "where Mysia meets Phrygia".
- **Change:** "In Phrygia, where they had lived since the prophets' day, ..." is not held either; cut the clause: "In Phrygia, the Montanists shut themselves inside their churches, set them alight and died in the flames."
