## Findings

### F1. BLOCKING: Brief cuts 3 and 4 do not exist, and the plan runs over 675

The brief carries neither Justinian's property threat nor the anonymous's foretold wars and thirteen-year count. Its paragraph 9 has only the order, the agents and the fire, and paragraph 6 has only the oracle of the end. So cuts 3 and 4 save nothing.

The count: I applied every Review change (B1-B13, MINOR 1-5), the beat 6 line and cuts 1-2 to the brief, in the Review's own wording where it gives one. `profile_readability` gives **710 words**, and grade 10.2, which fails the ceiling of 10. Without the beat 6 line it is still 686. The Review's estimate of about 676 was low: its B8 and B9 additions run longer than it counted. The fixer's estimate of about 665 rested on cut 3.

**Replacement** for "Brief cuts, to stay at 675", items 3 and 4 and the sentence after them:

> 3. The women bishops, presbyters and prophesying virgins (beat 6): card. They are Epiphanius's report of one branch in his own day, and the heart does not need them. (About -24.)
> 4. Apollonius's money charge: "He also says Montanus set up collectors of money and paid his preachers." Card, with Cut first 5's other charges; Pepuza named Jerusalem stays. (-12.)
> 5. In B5, drop the repeated region: "The faithful of the Roman province of Asia met often, in many places, to examine the new sayings." "Roman province" already answers the listener; the opening places western Turkey. (-6.)
> 6. Split the B8/B9 sentence, so that no sentence runs past 25 words: "He defended their rule of one marriage only: no second marriage, even after a husband or wife had died. When Christians were hunted for their faith, he urged them to face death rather than flee. He took such rules for the Paraclete's teaching."
>
> With cuts 1-6 the projected brief counts 662 words, grade 9.8, longest sentence 37 words: a pass.

Beat 6 then loses its women-clergy sentence, which moves to the card. "How they practiced" keeps its line, marked "card".

### F2. BLOCKING: a false claim about the heart

Fixes item 10 and Brief cuts item 4 say the prophesying virgins "stay, because the heart names them" and "they are the women prophets the heart names". The heart (Outline line 7) names three prophets, Montanus, Priscilla and Maximilla, and no women clergy or virgins of the fourth century. The Review raised the line as a note for the outline check, from "How they practiced", not from the heart. F1's replacement removes this sentence. Nothing else needs changing.

### F3. MINOR: who reviled the church (B1)

HE 29716-29719 says the spirit "taught them to revile the entire universal Church". It is the followers who revile, taught by the spirit. Beat 3 and the Do not say line make the spirit the one who reviles.

- Beat 3, replace "the anonymous adds that the spirit reviled the church because the church refused it (HE 29716-29720)" with "the anonymous adds that the spirit taught them to revile the whole church, because the church refused it honor and entrance (HE 29716-29719)".
- Do not say, replace "the anonymous says the spirit reviled the church because the church refused it (HE 29716-29720)" with "the anonymous says the spirit taught them to revile the church because the church refused it (HE 29716-29719)".

### F4. MINOR: "after baptism"

Words to explain, "one marriage only": the sources say "after believing" (ANF04 7266) and "post fidem" (CSEL20). Replace "no second marriage after baptism" with "no second marriage after becoming a Christian". The source sheet (line 150) needs the same change.
