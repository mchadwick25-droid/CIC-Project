**B1. S5, "one large, mixed church."** ASSUMPTION ADDED (second trust read). Hippolytus has Callistus's "school ... in antagonism to the Church" (H 13872), people "rejected by numerous sects" (H 13884-13886), and Natalius's rival group with its own bishop (E 32244-32246).
- Change: "In the first half of the 200s, the Christians of Rome were one large, mixed church." → "In the first half of the 200s, the Christians of Rome were a large, mixed church." (0 words.)
- **Outline holds it**, but the fix **changes the approved heart's wording**: the essence's "one large, mixed church" becomes "a large, mixed church". Setting's "the one church of the city" changes the same way.
- Do not say: | "one church" or "one large, mixed church" before 251 | "a large, mixed church" (H 13872, 13884-13886; E 32244-32246) |

**B2. S8, "a learned teacher."** NOT FOUND (second trust read). No old source calls Hippolytus a teacher or learned. "One of the most learned men" is only the NPNF editor's note (E 35651-35652). Eusebius: he "presided over another church, has left writings" (E 35447-35449).
- Change: "…comes from Hippolytus, a learned teacher who withstood two of Rome's bishops and lost." → "…comes from Hippolytus, a writer of that time who withstood two of Rome's bishops and lost." (+2 words.)
- **Outline holds it** (E 35447-35449). The fix **changes the approved heart's wording** ("a teacher at Rome who opposed two bishops and lost" → "a writer of that time who opposed two bishops and lost"). The outline's own Do not say row ("Hippolytus was a rival bishop" → "a learned teacher who withstood the bishops") and its Who tells it row ("A learned teacher at Rome") carry the error and change too.
- Do not say: | "learned teacher" or "teacher" for Hippolytus | "a writer of that time" (E 35447-35449) |

**B3. S22, "Many gave way."** SOFTENED OR CHANGED (both trust reads). "licet quidam terrore ipso conpulsi ... ruerunt", inside "ecclesia stat fortiter in fide" (CyL 1127-1129).

- Do not say: | "Many gave way" (Rome, 250) | "Some gave way" (D3, "quidam ... ruerunt"; "ecclesia stat fortiter in fide") |

**B4. S23, "Rome's elders and deacons wrote."** ASSUMPTION ADDED (second trust read). Ep. 8 names no writers (CyL 1053). Its close sends greetings from "presbyteri" as a third party (CyL 1177-1178). - Change: "Rome's elders and deacons wrote of them to the clergy of Carthage:" → "Rome's clergy wrote of them to the clergy of Carthage:" (−2 words.)
- **Outline holds it** (CyL 1235). The outline's Who tells it row ("Rome's elders and deacons, 250"), Source_Sheet D3 ("the presbyters and deacons of Rome") and Cut first 15 ("keeping that Rome's elders and deacons wrote") carry the error and change.
- Do not say: | "Rome's elders and deacons wrote" (Ep. 8) | "Rome's clergy wrote" (CyL 1235, "epistulae clericae"; the letter names no writers) |
