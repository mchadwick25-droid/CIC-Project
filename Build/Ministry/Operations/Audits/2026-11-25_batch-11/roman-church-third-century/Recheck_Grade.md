# Recheck of the grade fixes: roman-church-third-century

Checked: the six exact old/new changes in `Fixes_Grade.md` (the when plus the six trims), against `Outline.md`, `Source_Sheet.md` and the vendored sources at their cited lines. All six old strings were found once each in the live brief and applied to a scratch copy outside the repository.

## Verdict: FIX (outline only)

The brief changes all hold and stand as written. `Outline.md` no longer matches the brief in three places. Apply the three replacements below (R1-R3). R4 is optional.

## The when: "early in the 200s"

- Eusebius, `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml` 32210-32214: Zephyrinus succeeds "about the ninth year of the reign of Severus". The Little Labyrinth's author "narrates another event which occurred in the time of Zephyrinus". The writer himself says "not long ago, but in our own day" (32223-32224). At 35480-35482, Zephyrinus dies "having held his office for eighteen years".
- The Liberian list (`liber-pontificalis_..._duchesne1886.txt` 66792-66796) dates him from the consuls of 198 to those of 217.
- So the sources place the whole episode, the bishop-making along with the penance, only inside Zephyrinus's time, about 198/199 to 217. "Early in the 200s" claims nothing narrower than that span. Rating it Widely Accepted is right. The fix rightly avoids "around 200", because no source dates the fall itself.
- On one hearing: paragraph 2's "first half of the 200s" uses the same sense of "the 200s", so a listener hears the century, not the decade 200-209. The participle "a group teaching that Christ was only a man make him" has the same shape as the old "that taught" clause and is no harder to follow. **Holds.**

## The trims

1. "a group teaching that": a joining word only. **Holds.**
2. "saying he forgave them all": this is the outline's own wording (beat 3, C7), and the credit "Hippolytus says" stays. **Holds.**
3. "such women took drugs": "then" carried Hippolytus's "Whence" (H 13938, C13). The tie to the rule survives in "such women", in "not wanting a child by a slave", and in "he called the rule a teaching of adultery and murder". The record's force and the Do-not-say pairing (rule with charge, line 160) are kept. **Holds.**
4. "Fabian died a martyr, leaving Rome without a bishop.": this is true to D1, D2 and D7, since his death did leave the see empty. **Holds.**
5. "Cornelius, made bishop in 251, counted his church:": the count stays exactly as Cut first 31 leaves it. **Holds.**
6. "Cornelius wrote how the people met them:": this is plain idiomatic English, and the credit stays. **Holds.**

None of the trims touches the heart, a quote, a credit, Hippolytus's reasons (C4), Decius's motive (D1), or any other explanation Part 1 requires.
