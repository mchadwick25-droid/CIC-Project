## 3. Sentences that fail on one hearing (MINOR)

1. **"Scarcely was he taken back into communion, the church's shared bread and cup."** The inverted, old-fashioned "Scarcely was he" can be heard as "he was hardly taken back", meaning *not* taken back. Lost: the scene's outcome (he was let back, but only just). Change: "He was let back into communion, the church's shared bread and cup, but only just."
2. **"They argued in public over whether the church could take back grave sinners, whom a Christian woman of rank might marry, and how to speak of Father and Son, until a stricter party broke away in 251."** (37 words.) On one hearing "grave sinners, whom a Christian woman of rank might marry" sounds like one phrase: the woman marries the sinners. Lost: that these are three separate quarrels. This is the essence. Paragraph 5 later sets it right, so the thread holds. Change: "They argued in public over three questions: whether the church could take back grave sinners, whom a Christian woman of rank might marry, and how to speak of Father and Son. In 251 a stricter party broke away."
3. **"Callistus pointed, his enemy says, to the weeds in Christ's parable, left to grow with the wheat."** With no church background I do not know the parable, so I do not hear what Callistus meant: that the church keeps sinners alongside the faithful. Change: "Callistus pointed, his enemy says, to Christ's story of the weeds left to grow with the wheat until harvest: the church, too, held sinners alongside the faithful." (The trust read should confirm that this sense stands in the source.)
