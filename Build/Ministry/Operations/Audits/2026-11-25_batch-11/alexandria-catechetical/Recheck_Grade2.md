## Findings

**BLOCKING 1. New sentence 2 brings an Inferential-Thin judgment into the story and announces a gap.** The sentence is "The words suggest she was burned to death, but Eusebius never says so outright." The sheet rates the burning Inferential-Thin (C5), and the fix pass keeps that rating. Kit Part 1, Rigor: a judgment "that the outline rates Dominant Modern Reconstruction or Inferential-Thin, stays out of the story and the card." Saying it as an inference does not change that. Its second half is also a gap-only tell. Part 1, Craft, rules it out twice: "No sentence that only says what is missing", and "do not announce it unless the gap is itself the story". Here the gap is not the story. On the missing detail, Explain it says: "tell it as they give it, with no reason added and no word on the missing one". The quote already carries the record's own word, "fire". Drop the sentence, and keep the Do not say line as it was.

**MINOR 2. Cuts 1b, 1c and 1d are not needed once sentence 2 goes, and 1c costs fidelity.** Adding only sentence 1 (+7) needs just 7 words cut, and 1a (-4) with 1e (-3) brings the brief back to 628. The source's own reason for the division is time for "the deeper study of divine things, and for the investigation and interpretation of the Sacred Scriptures" (E-en 34407-34409). "Scarcely gave him time to breathe" is only its aside. So "or to study Scripture himself" holds the reason, and should stay. 1b ("one after another", E-en 34411) and 1d ("for his faith and his work on Scripture", E-en 41313-41314) are the source's own words, and nothing pays for removing them now.

**MINOR 3. The sheet's new line cites Serenus's "through fire" at E-gr 1607 alone.** The διὰ stands at E-gr 1490 and the πυρὸς at 1607, across a page break. Potamiaena is in the next chapter (6.5), not "the same passage".

**Noted, not a finding.** `Held_Findings.json` holds one finding for this story, "When Origen left for Caesarea, about 231" (asks why; weight 1, both readers). Mark's ruling scopes the Alexandria fix to the baptism by fire, so it is outside this pass. `Fixes_Grade2.md` cites the file but does not say why the finding is left.
