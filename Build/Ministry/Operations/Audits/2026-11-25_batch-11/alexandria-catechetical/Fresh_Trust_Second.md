## The heart

Right for its subject. The record shows a community taught by a line of teachers who were each other's pupils (Clement, Origen, Heraclas, Dionysius), reading Scripture in layers, and then a quarrel over that very way of reading settled by three days of examination from Scripture. That is the tradition kit's who, where, what held them, and what they faced, and it stays on learners, not one founder.
