## The heart

**Right heart.** The heart is a city's faith learned as a school, Scripture read in layers, and a pupil turned bishop who answers dissent by three days of open argument. The record carries all of it: Eusebius's school (E-en 28537-28539), Origen's classroom and its division (E-en 34407-34418), Origen's three senses (Ph 76), and Dionysius's own account (E-en 41343-41381). It keeps to the learners, not one teacher's life. It ends on the community itself.
