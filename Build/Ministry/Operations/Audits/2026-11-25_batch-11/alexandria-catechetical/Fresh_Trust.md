## The heart

**The right heart.** The sources show a community taught in the faith by a chain of teachers, in what Eusebius calls a school (E-en 28537-28539). Pagans and catechumens were among the learners, and some died for it (E-en 32670-32925). Scripture was read in layers (Ph ch. I §11). The same pupils led the instruction and the bishop's chair down to Dionysius, who answered a book "against allegorists" with three days of examination from Scripture (E-gr 10720-10735). The heart names that, and the story carries it through real people, many of them ordinary learners.
