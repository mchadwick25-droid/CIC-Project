# Recheck of the review fix pass: The Post-Apostolic House Churches

Targeted recheck of what `Fixes_Review.md` changed in `Outline.md` and `Source_Sheet.md`, against `Review.md` and the vendored sources. Live brief read: `post-apostolic-house-church.brief.txt` (675 words, `wc -w`). No `.v1` file opened.

## Verdict: FIX

Two replacements, both in Ending 1 (and its echo in Cut first 25). Everything else holds.

## The four additions

1. **"An informer had named them."** (Opening 1). HOLDS. mirbt 2786: "Alii ab indice nominati esse se Christianos dixerunt et mox negaverunt; fuisse quidem, sed desisse". These are the same people the opening already describes: they said they had been Christians and had given it up. npnf201 19487–19489 agrees ("Others, accused by an informer"). Contemporary, hostile; Documented. It stays clear of the anonymous list (Leave out) and of pliny-the-younger's wording. Cite right.

2. **"for the name of Christ"** (Beat 3). HOLDS. anf01 4793–4794: "bound for the name [of Christ]". The bracket is the 1885 translator's. The longer recension's "His name" (anf01 4805) and Pliny's "nomen ipsum" (mirbt 2774–2775) support the reading. Widely Accepted, which the story may use. Words to explain and sheet I10 match. Cite right.

3. **What each side kept at Easter** (Ending). HOLDS in substance, with one gap (Fix 1). npnf201 31136–31140: Asia kept "the fourteenth day of the moon, on which day the Jews were commanded to sacrifice the lamb". npnf201 31217–31222 and 31262–31265: the others ended the fast only "on that of the resurrection", "the Lord's day". npnf201 31415–31419 supports "tried to cut them off from fellowship" ("attempted to cut off from the common unity"). No outcome is told, which is right (V5; Do not say). The gap: "the moon's fourteenth day" goes unexplained, so a listener cannot tell why it clashed with the Lord's day. Also, "others kept only the Lord's day" can be heard as "others kept nothing but Sunday".

4. **"who handed the apostles' teaching down to his time"** (Ending). NOT AS TOLD (Fix 2). anf01 39851–39853: "In this order, and by this succession, the ecclesiastical tradition from the apostles, and the preaching of the truth, have come down to us." The fixer rightly holds this only as Irenaeus's own claim (V6: "Documented that he says so"). But the outline's sentence puts the handing-down in a relative clause that a listener hears as the narrator's fact. It also makes the five men the ones who did the handing, where Irenaeus names the whole succession. The story must tell it as what he says.

## Fixes

**Fix 1 (Ending 1, and Cut first 25's quoted Ending):**
Replace
"the churches of Asia kept Easter on the moon's fourteenth day, where others kept only the Lord's day."
with
"the churches of Asia kept Easter on the Passover date, the moon's fourteenth day; others kept it only on the Lord's day."
(+3. npnf201 31136–31140, 31217–31222, 31262–31265. In Words to explain, add to "the moon's fourteenth day": "the day the Jews sacrificed the Passover lamb (npnf201 31137–31138)".)

**Fix 2 (Ending 1, and Cut first 25's quoted Ending):**
Replace
"Elsewhere he counts these men among its bishops, who handed the apostles' teaching down to his time."
with
"Elsewhere he counts these men among its bishops, and says the apostles' teaching came down to his day through their line."
(+4. anf01 39823–39853. Add to Do not say: "'who handed the apostles' teaching down' as the narrator's fact → 'he says the apostles' teaching came down … through their line'; Irenaeus's own claim (anf01 39851–39853).")
