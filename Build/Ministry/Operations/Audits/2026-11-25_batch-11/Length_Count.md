## What carries the weight (LONG and OVER stories)

- **syriac-edessa-nisibis (LONG):** Beat 5 (seven points: the move to Edessa, the rivals, three teachings, the warning, the death) and Beat 2 (five: the letters, the covenant rule and its reason); Beat 4's Persian persecution adds five.
- **antiochene-church-third-century (LONG):** Beat 4 (five points: Malchion, the last meeting, the letters of communion) and Beat 5 (five: the house, the petition, the ruling and its plain sense), with Beat 2's four visits of Firmilian.
- **roman-church-third-century (LONG):** Beat 7 (five points: election, the count, the second bishop, the sixty bishops), Beat 3 (four) and the Ending (four); twelve glosses, 96 words, are the heaviest in the batch bar Latin Pastoral.
- **apocryphal-and-pseudepigraphal-literature (LONG, just over):** Beat 2 (six points: Thecla's trial, Tertullian's answer, the writer) and Beat 1 (five: Serapion's read-through).
- **ebionite-nazoraean-current (LONG):** Beat 4 (six points: Epiphanius's names, places, Pella, the curse), Beats 1, 2 and 5 (five each), and 72 words of witness credit (twelve credits).
- **greek-apologists-second-century (LONG, just over):** Beat 2 (six points on Justin) and Beat 4 (five on Athenagoras); the opening carries three more.
- **latin-apologists (LONG):** Beats 1, 2 and 3 (five points each) and the Octavius scene; Beat 4 adds four and two quotes.
- **latin-pastoral-congregational-christianity (LONG):** Beat 2 (six points: the fallen, the second bishop, the envoys), the opening (four), Beats 5 and 6 and the Ending (four each); 13 glosses (104 words) and 129 words of quote.
- **manichaeism (OVER, by 12 words):** Beat 3 (seven points on Elect, Hearers and the alms), Beat 6 (five on China), the opening (three); quotes are 151 words, the heaviest in the batch bar the House Church.
- **marcion-marcionism (LONG, just over):** Beats 2, 3 and 5 (four, four and three points); all nine Cut first items sit outside this Total.
- **montanism-the-new-prophecy (LONG, just over):** Beats 3 and 5 (four points each); Beats 1 and 4 add three and two.
- **post-apostolic-house-church (OVER, by 9 words):** Beat 1 (six points on the manual), Beats 2 and 3 (five each) and the quotes (149 words, with Justin's Sunday at 70 and the Didache at 55); 37 facts is the most in the batch.
