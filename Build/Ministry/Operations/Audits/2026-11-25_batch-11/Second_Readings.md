# Second Readings (B11)

Batch 11, ids B11-01 to B11-38, across thirteen pieces. Blind readings: `Second_Readings_Blind_A.md` (01-13), `Second_Readings_Blind_B.md` (14-26), `Second_Readings_Blind_C.md` (27-38). Key: `Second_Reading_Key.md`.

## Method

1. **Brief and card.** For each id, our wording in the key was checked word for word against `<slug>/<slug>.brief.txt` and against the named entry's `modern` in `<slug>/<slug>.card.json`, by script and then by eye. 36 of 38 match exactly in both. B11-02 and B11-03 are quotations broken by the brief's own words. The key marks the break with "...". The brief has `"Faith is taught without letters," that is, to those who cannot read. "Its handbook, ..."` and `"If the argument won," he wrote, "we were not ashamed ..."`. The card's `modern` joins the two parts as one sentence. The quoted words are the same in all three, so both stand.
2. **Blind rendering.** Each id was set beside its blind rendering. Where they differ, the cited lines in `cic/texts/` and the card's `quotationOriginals` entry (`printed`, `original`, `englishPrinted`, `uncertain`) were opened and read.
3. **Card checks.** Every `Card_Check.md` and `Card_Check2.md` in the batch was searched for a change to a quotation's `original`, `printed` or `lines`. None proposes one. The changes they list touch other fields: alexandria FIX 5 rewrites `quotationOriginals[0].uncertain`, and greek-apologists FIX 2, "honour" to "honor", is already in the brief and card. So every id is judged against the card as it stands. Ebionite and post-apostolic have no card check.
4. **Span boundaries.** The blind readers flagged boundary choices on B11-32, -33 and -38, and word counts on B11-05, -06 and -12. Each was checked at its lines:
   - **B11-32.** The quoted clause depends on Pliny's "Adfirmabant autem hanc fuisse summam vel culpae suae vel erroris" (Mirbt 2789). The brief supplies that frame outside the quotation marks: "Part of their guilt, or their error, they told him, was this:". "Part" is right, because the quote gives only the first item of the *summa*, and the oath follows at 2791. The span ends at "secum invicem", as the card's `printed` does.
   - **B11-33.** The span ends at "thy neighbour as thyself;" (ANF 7 12704-12706), before "and all things whatsoever ...", as the card's `printed` does. The blind reader's "you shall love your neighbour" fills in the verb that the Greek leaves understood. Ours, "Second, your neighbour as yourself", keeps the ellipsis, as the print does. Same meaning.
   - **B11-38.** Our quotation stops at "alliance" (Chabot 16166). The brief gives the verb outside the marks: "go about freely and without fear", which is "circulassent en toute liberté et sans crainte". The blind reader rendered the whole clause. Chabot's note on ܩܝܡܐ (16179-16180), "tout le clergé y compris les moines", supports the card's `uncertain`.
   - **B11-05.** The request's count of 37 includes the scan's free-standing "?" after "profiteris". Both readings end at the same "Christianus sum", before "hoe uno ae" (15440).
   - **B11-06.** Our span ends at Συλλουκιανιστὰ. Its 11 words include the note mark "5". The blind reader went on to the next two words, ἀληθῶς Εὐσέβιε, "truly Eusebius", at 45746. Those words are the letter's closing address and lie outside our span. The brief's frame, "ended that letter with the name Lucian's pupils gave themselves", stays fair, because the name is the last thing the letter says before that address.
   - **B11-12.** The span is 24 words, "sciant" to "decessisse", by our count. The blind reader counted 25 but ended at the same word.
5. **Scan repairs.** Each scan repair the blind readers made agrees with the card's `uncertain` note: xci, Hoais, χατηχουμένη (01); cíooi, αἰδού-σμεροι (03); uir, veo-, 77, xai (04); iv, the note mark 5 and the apparatus line (06); do- >z00», μιχροφυχίαν (10); xai, παρελά- >Bousv (11); ἃ for a (15); ah, religionein (21); the stray mark before plenissima (23); X5 (25); Evan-gelium, bi- A. bere (27); da>- (28); statira (30); sohti (32); the note mark after alliance (38). Two scan points have no `uncertain` note, and neither needs one: "Hiero-/solymam" (14) is joined in the card's `original`, and "poterint" (08) is the edition's own spelling.
6. **Spelling.** Every key wording was searched for British forms (-our, -ise, -tre, doubled -ll-, -ence). The results are listed under Spelling below.
