## BLOCKING

**S32.** "A Chinese chronicle written four centuries later says that some seventy of their women of their faith at the capital all died."
- Source: "A la capitale, soixante-dix religieuses manichéennes périrent toutes." (`manichaean-texts_traite-manicheen-retrouve-en-chine-pt2-in-journal-asiatique-11-1-fra_chavannes-pelliot1913.txt` 14089-14095, from the *Fo tsou t'ong ki*, 13th century. Chapter 54 gives 72: 14114.)
- **SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** *Religieuses* means women in religious life. "Women of their faith" turns them into ordinary female believers, which changes who died. The sentence also says "their" twice ("of their women of their faith"). The outline's Do not say row has the right wording.
- **Exact change:** "A Chinese chronicle written four centuries later says that some seventy of their women religious at the capital all died."

- **S29** "the lightest of penalties, given because those same Christians pleaded for him". Latin 16832-16836: "eis ipsis christianis a quibus perducti sunt intercedentibus, levissima poena ... in insulam relegatus est". This is an ablative absolute, read causally as NPNF also reads it ("on the intercession of the Christians"). The source says they pleaded for "them" (Faustus "cum aliis nonnullis"). Not a fault.
- **S33** "a scroll written in Mani's script, in a Turkish tongue". KHU 15395-15409: "written in the clear unequivocal letters of the Manichaean alphabet ... the ancient Turkish speech"; "a book-roll".
