## BLOCKING

**S32. SOFTENED OR CHANGED.** "A Chinese chronicle written four centuries later says that some seventy of their women of their faith at the capital all died."
- Source: "A la capitale, soixante-dix religieuses manichéennes périrent toutes." (`manichaean-texts_traite-manicheen-retrouve-en-chine-pt2-in-journal-asiatique-11-1-fra_chavannes-pelliot1913.txt` 14093-14095; the compiler of the *Fo tsou t'ong ki*, 13th century, 14089-14090.)
- *Religieuses* are women in religious life, not women believers in general. "Women of their faith" widens the dead from vowed religious to any Manichaean women, and the doubled "their" garbles the line on one hearing.
- Exact change, the outline's own wording (Story beat 6; Do not say, the 843 row): "A Chinese chronicle written four centuries later says that some seventy of their women religious at the capital all died."
