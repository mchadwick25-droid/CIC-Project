## Worded by me

- **Finding 1, heart, "What they believed":** the check names this field but gives no text. New: "the Light bound up in the food they ate (KHU 17364-17371)." KHU 17368-17371: "(that part of) the light of the Five-God, that, every day, we have absorbed in our food".
- **Finding 1, line range in beat 3 and in the alms gloss:** the check cites "KHU 17364-17371, 17436-17440". Lines 17436-17440 hold only the garbled Turkish transcription. The English "go to the Bad Place." is at KHU 17443, as the source sheet's alms row gives it. Both places now read "(KHU 17364-17371, 17443)". The words of the check's text are unchanged.
- **Finding 1, `Source_Sheet.md` D, alms row:** after "the Hearers' alms carry it to God to be purified", new: "(this reading rests on Le Coq's note, modern, KHU 18038-18050; the prayer itself does not name food or the Elect)". KHU 18038-18039: "is, perhaps, the light coiitaiued in these oflei'ings of food"; KHU 16990-17011 (article 11) names alms, angels and the light of the Five-God, but not food or the Elect.
- **Finding 6, the recount and the further cuts:** the check's items 8-14 leave the count at 713 by the `Length_Count.md` method (687, plus 84 for six facts and three credits the fixes add, less 58 for items 8-14). The check's new header says the list reaches 675 or under, so I added the smallest cuts that get it there and drop no explanation and no heart field:
  - "15. The 843 chronicle's \"some seventy of their women religious at the capital all died\", with its late-chronicle credit (B26, beat 6), about 20 words: **card**. Keep the edict that closed their temples across the empire." (CHI 14089-14095; the heart names only the closing of the temples.)
  - "16. The ending's \"nearly fifteen feet long\" (B30), about 4 words: **card**. Keep the scroll, its script and its tongue." (KHU 15416-15417.)
  - "17. The opening's \"In the Baths of Sossius, before the people\" (B17), about 7 words: **card**. Keep Hippo, 392, and the debate."
  - The recount paragraph after the list, ending "for **674 words**. By the check's own word estimates the total is lower still." Items 16 and 17 are each counted as one fact; if the counter takes either as part of another fact, the total is 685 and one more cut is needed.
- **M4:** the check says add NPNF 4313. Line 4313 of `npnf104` is blank; Faustus's preface ("brief and pointed replies to the captious objections of our adversaries") is at 4314-4315. Beat 5 now reads "(C7; CFa English 4305-4312, 4314-4315)".
