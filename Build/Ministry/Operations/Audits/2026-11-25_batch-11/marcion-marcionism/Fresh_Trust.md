## BLOCKING

**30. "There John Chrysostom had once urged an earlier bishop to drive out Marcion's followers, offering the aid of the imperial edicts."** SOFTENED OR CHANGED.
- Source, Thdt 17542-17545 (Theodoret, Church History V.31, tr. Jackson): "On hearing that in our district some men were infected with the plague of Marcion he wrote to the then bishop charging him to drive out the plague, and proffering him the aid of the imperial edicts."
- What changed: in the source, what is driven out is "the plague", the teaching the men were "infected with", not the men themselves. "Drive out Marcion's followers" says the people were to be expelled, which the source does not say. "Urged" is also softer than "charging".
- Exact change: "There John Chrysostom had once charged an earlier bishop to drive Marcion's teaching out of the district, offering the aid of the imperial edicts."
- Do not say line for the outline: "Chrysostom urged the bishop to drive out Marcion's followers" → he charged the bishop to "drive out the plague" that some men there were "infected with" (Thdt 17542-17545). The object is the teaching, not the people. (Outline beat 5 words it as "drive out Marcion's followers", which is where the error came in.)

## SUMMARY HOLDS (with evidence)

3. "Beside him they raised Metrodorus, a presbyter of the rival Marcionites." Pion 5600-5602: ἀνώρθωσαν οὖν αὐτὸν ἐπὶ τοῦ ξύλου, καὶ λοιπὸν μετὰ ταῦτα καὶ πρεσβύτερόν τινα Μητρόδωρον τῆς αἱρέσεως τῶν Μαρκιωνιστῶν. "Raised" carries over to Metrodorus by the "καὶ"; "rival" fairly gives *hairesis*.

5. "The Marcionites were the churches that followed Marcion after Rome's church cast him out, in the middle of the second century." ANF3 21334-21339 (Prescription 30): "in the reign of Antoninus for the most part ... in the church of Rome ... they were more than once expelled." "Churches" is their own word for their meetings, as Cyril reports it (Cyr 20356-20363).

6. "For about three hundred years, from Rome to Persia and down to the villages of Syria, they gathered with their own presbyters, teachers and martyrs." Epi 7469-7471: "Ῥώμῃ καὶ ἐν τῇ Ἰταλίᾳ ... οὐ μὴν ἀλλὰ καὶ ἐν τῇ Περσίδι"; Thdt 31511: "eight villages of Marcionists". The span runs from Antoninus's reign to Theodoret's twenty-five years as bishop (Thdt 31501). Presbyter: Pion 5601; teacher: Apelles (E-en 29002); martyrs: Pion 5601-5602, E-gr 24595-24598.

8. "They held one belief above all: Jesus came from a good Father, above the God who made the world, and Jesus abolished that God's law and prophets." ANF1 33577-33582: "Jesus being derived from that father who is above the God that made the world ... abolishing the prophets and the law." "Good" is from ANF1 33570 ("benevolent") and Tertullian's "good" God throughout I.27. "Above all" is the outline's "what held them together", a fair judgment from the sources, which argue with them on this point first.

9. "Tertullian, a writer at Carthage, says Marcion was cast out for his restless curiosity, which spread to others." ANF3 21338-21339: "on account of their ever restless curiosity, with which they even infected the brethren, they were more than once expelled." Tertullian says it of Valentinus and Marcion together; the compression to Marcion keeps its meaning.

12. "Late in the second century, a writer from Asia named Rhodon found Marcion's followers divided over how many first sources, or beginnings of all things, there are." E-en 28896-28928: "At this time Rhodo, a native of Asia ... He says that this heresy was divided in his time into various opinions"; the opinions are one, two or three principles (E-en 29002, 29073-29085).

13. "Apelles, one of their teachers, held there was only one, one God, where Marcion held two." E-en 29002: "acknowledges one principle"; E-en 29151-29152: "his opinion concerning God ... he spoke of one principle"; E-en 29196: "one unbegotten God"; E-en 29073-29085: "two principles, as does the mariner Marcion himself."

17. "Their good God, he said, punished no one, and only cast the sinner away out of sight." ANF3 24459-24464: "the god whom you do not fear ... prohibiting a thing without annexing the sanction of punishment ... reply, that he is to be cast away out of sight."

18. "So he pressed them on persecution." Kr 16281-16286: "qui deum non times quasi bonum ... quid non et in persecutionibus ..." The "so" is Tertullian's own chain of argument.

19. "An accused Christian was told to sprinkle incense from a box to the gods, and whoever did it denied Christ and went free." Kr 16286: "statim oblata acerra animam negatione lucraris"; Pion 5561-5563 ("Θύεις; ... Ἄλλοι πολλοὶ ἔθυσαν καὶ ζῶσι"). "Sprinkle" explains the custom of the *acerra*.

22. "He was supposed to be, Eusebius says, a bishop of the Marcionites, with zeal for religion, as he thought, but not with true knowledge." E-gr 24593-24595: "τῆς κατὰ Μαρκίωνα πλάνης ἐπίσκοπός τις εἶναι δοκῶν Ἀσκληπιὸς ζήλῳ μέν, ὡς ᾤετο, εὐσεβείας, ἀλλ᾽ οὔτι γε τῆς κατ᾽ ἐπίγνωσιν". Both hedges are kept. Note only, not a finding: "of the Marcionites" drops Eusebius's πλάνη ("error"), but his blame still stands in "not with true knowledge".

23. "When the persecutions ended, the emperor Constantine issued an edict, a law addressed by name to the heretics, those who, their enemies said, chose error, the Marcionites among them." E-en 66068-66071: "Constantine's Edict against the Heretics ... to the heretics ... ye Novatians, Valentinians, Marcionites"; the gloss: ANF3 20274-20279 (heresy from the Greek for "choice").

26. "Yet their meetings went on." Shown by Cyril's present-tense warning about "the meetings of the heretics, the Marcionists" (Cyr 20356-20358), after the edict.

27. "At Jerusalem in the fourth century, explaining the creed's 'one Holy Catholic Church' to those about to be baptized, Cyril named the Marcionites first among the heretics whose meetings were also called churches." Cyr 20353-20359: "the word Ecclesia is applied to different things ... a Church of evil doers, I mean the meetings of the heretics, the Marcionists and Manichees ... the Article, 'And in one Holy Catholic Church'". Those about to be baptized: Cyr 20476 ("Ye shall be enlightened by the Laver of regeneration").

31. "Theodoret worked to bring them to the one Son, who was, he wrote, not only good but just." Thdt 35004-35010: "against the victims of the corruption of Marcion, I have never ceased to struggle; trying to convince ... Marcion's mad adherents that He is not only good but just."
