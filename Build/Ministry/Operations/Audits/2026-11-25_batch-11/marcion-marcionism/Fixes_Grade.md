## Fix 1. Add-on line that sounds machine-made

**Finding:** "Irenaeus, like every witness this story draws on, was their enemy." | add-on statement.

**Decision: fold, not cut.** The point is on the outline's Never cut list ("the one-witness sentence", `Outline.md` 214) and is placed there to be said once, early (`Outline.md` 144). So it moves to where the witnesses first appear, just before Tertullian, the first writer the story names, and leads into them rather than trailing after Irenaeus.

**Old brief text** (paragraph 2, last three sentences):

> Tertullian, a writer at Carthage, in North Africa, says Marcion was cast out for his restless curiosity, which spread to others. Irenaeus, a bishop writing within a generation, says Marcion cut from both books every passage that called the maker of the world the Father of Jesus. Irenaeus, like every witness this story draws on, was their enemy.

**New brief text:**

> Everything this story tells of them comes from their enemies. Tertullian, a writer at Carthage, in North Africa, says Marcion was cast out for his restless curiosity, which spread to others. Irenaeus, a bishop writing within a generation, says Marcion cut from both books every passage that called the maker of the world the Father of Jesus.

**Support (no new content):** `Outline.md` 144, "Every witness this story draws on was their enemy"; `Outline.md` 133-143, each witness the brief uses marked an opponent (Constantine's edict and Theodoret as the actors' own words, both hostile); `Source_Sheet.md` 25, "Every witness to the Marcionites below is an opponent unless marked ...
