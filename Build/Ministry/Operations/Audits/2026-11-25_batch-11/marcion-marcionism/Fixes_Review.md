### B10, "heretics" (the outline lacked it)

- **Words to explain**, new entry "heretics". Tertullian, Prescription 6: false teachings "called in Greek *heresies*, a word used in the sense of that *choice* which a man makes when he either teaches them (to others) or takes up with them (for himself)" (ANF3 20274-20279; Oe 461). - **Do not say**: a bare "heretics", or "people who disagreed with the church", is not to be used. Neither is the listeners' "judged to teach falsely", which no held line says.
- **Never cut**: the heretics' gloss is added.
