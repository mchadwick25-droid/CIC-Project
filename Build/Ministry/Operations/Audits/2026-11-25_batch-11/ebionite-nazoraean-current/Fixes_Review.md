# Fixes from the review: The Ebionites and Nazoraeans

Source: `Review.md` (verdict REVISE, 2026-11-25). This pass fixes in `Outline.md` and `Source_Sheet.md` the findings the review sends back to the researcher: B1, B2, B4, B5 and B7. B3, B6, M4, M5, M6 and M8 are the reviser's, and the outline already holds what they need, so they are not touched here. No `.v1` file was opened. The brief was not edited.

**Changes to the approved heart's wording (for Mark): B1 and B2.** Both rewrite the essence. "When and where" changes with B1. "What came of them" keeps its wording (B7).

Old essence: "From the middle of the second century to about 400, in Palestine and Syria, there were Jews who believed that Jesus was the Christ and went on keeping the Law of Moses: circumcision, the Sabbath, the food laws. Their own books are lost. We meet them only through church writers who argued against them, and those writers do not agree on what they believed about Jesus."

New essence: "From the middle of the second century to about 400, church writers wrote of Jews who believed that Jesus was the Christ and went on keeping the Law of Moses: circumcision, the Sabbath, the food laws. The later writers place them in Syria and beyond the Jordan. Their own books are lost, so we meet them only through other church writers, nearly all of whom argued against them, and those writers do not agree on what they believed about Jesus."

## B1. Place and end (heart)

Source lines checked:
- Pan 25026–25028 (Epiphanius, about 375; later, hostile; Documented as his report): Beroea in Coele Syria, the Decapolis around Pella, Basanitis. The first place-list held.
- PL23 48161–48162 (Jerome, 392; contemporary; Documented as his report): "in Berea urbe Syrie".
- CSEL34 23235 (Jerome, 404; contemporary; Documented as his report): "usque hodie". No witness gives an end.
- Justin (JusG 2152–2173), Irenaeus (ANF1 33535–33543) and Origen give no place. This is an absence.

Outline:
- Essence: "in Palestine and Syria, there were Jews who" becomes "church writers wrote of Jews who", and "The later writers place them in Syria and beyond the Jordan." is added. The cites now name A9, A11, A13, A14, Pan 25026–25028, PL23 48161–48162 and CSEL34 23235. The census `region` is marked as not spoken.
- When and where: it now says the witnesses write from Justin's day to 404, and that none gives an end. Places come only from Epiphanius on. "Palestine and Syria" is gone.
- Setting, spoken places: "Palestine and Syria" becomes "Syria and beyond the Jordan", as the later writers place them.
- Do not say: two rows added, as the review gives them.

Source sheet:
- New row A13 records the absence: no place given before Epiphanius.
- New row A14, "Beyond the Jordan": Pan 25027–25028 and 25033 ("τὴν Περαίαν"), and HE 15294 ("Perea called Pella"). Confidence: Widely Accepted for the geography. One caveat: no old held line spells out "the Jordan". The words gloss Peraea, and only McGiffert's modern note (HE 15294–15295) says "beyond the Jordan". The outline already used "Pella, a town beyond the Jordan" (H1) before this pass.
- The "Spoken words" line no longer offers "Palestine and Syria".

- Do not say: one row added.
