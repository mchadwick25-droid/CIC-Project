## Line by line

**B1, the place and the end (essence, When and where, Setting, Do not say, A13, A14).** These hold.
- Pan 25026–25028 gives Beroea "περὶ τὴν Κοίλην Συρίαν", the Decapolis "περὶ τὰ τῆς Πέλλης μέρη" and Basanitis.
- PL23 48162 gives "in Berea urbe Syrie".
- CSEL34 23235 gives "usque hodie". No witness gives an end, so "wrote of ... to about 400" holds.
- A13's absence holds. Justin (JusG 2162–2173) gives no place.

On "beyond the Jordan": no held old line names the river, so the fixer's flag is right. The phrase may still stand as the narrator's plain gloss, for three reasons:
- The old texts give the places: Epiphanius's Decapolis around Pella and Bashan (Pan 25026–25028), and Eusebius's "a certain town of Perea called Pella" (HE 15294).
- "Peraea" (Pan 25033) means "the land beyond".
- McGiffert's note (HE 15294) only clarifies where these places lie. The kit allows that use, and A14 marks it as modern.

One caution, a note only. Pan 25033 is the disciples' settlement in the Pella beginning (Cut first 8), not Epiphanius's present-tense list. So "Syria and beyond the Jordan" stands on 25026–25028 with HE 15294. It must never be told as the descent. The Do not say and Leave out entries already guard this.

**B2, "nearly all of whom argued against them" (essence, hook beat, Who tells it, head note, Do not say).** This holds. Justin would take them in "ὡς ὁμοσπλάγχνοις καὶ ἀδελφοῖς", on terms (JusG 2166–2173). The other witnesses in the brief, Irenaeus, Origen, Epiphanius and Jerome, are hostile.

**B4, "two heresies, side by side" (Beat 4, E11, Do not say).** This holds. Pan 12578 reads "ἐνάτη Ναζωραῖοι, δεκάτη Ἐβιωναῖοι". The beat matches the Do not say entry on one group or two ("Epiphanius two").

**B5, "Elsewhere he calls it the Gospel according to the Hebrews" (Beat 5, F8, Do not say).** This holds.
- F8 (PL23 44506–44509, "quo utuntur usque hodie Nazareni") is the only held line by Jerome that ties the name to the Nazoraeans. PL23 48097 says the same, but it is Migne's footnote quoting F8, not a second witness.
- F5 (PL23 48081–48083) gives the name and the translation, without the Nazoraeans.
- Matthew stays out, as Cut first 5 rules.

The cite for the copying is one line short. "describendi facultas fuit" stands at PL23 48163, not inside 48155–48162 (MINOR 1).

**B7, the Juliana cut (Cut first 4, Beat 2, Names, Connection, G2b, Do not say).** This holds. The cut sentence is 13 words in the live brief. G2b's absence is real: HE 34744–34752 says only what the writings were, and McGiffert's modern note says they "have vanished".
