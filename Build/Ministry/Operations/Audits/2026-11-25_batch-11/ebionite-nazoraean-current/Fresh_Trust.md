## The heart

Right for its subject. What the sources show is exactly the story's heart: Jews who believed Jesus was the Christ and kept the Law, known only through writers who mostly opposed them and who part on what they believed about Jesus (sentences 12, 16, 20, 29). It is told on time: who, when, where and what held them together are in by word 147; the one-witness clause and the doubt close the essence at word 178, about a minute and ten seconds at the approved pace. The essence proper is within the first minute, so the heart is not late. No modern view, no Pella descent, no settled verdict on the doubt; the story ends on Jerome's own words about them.
