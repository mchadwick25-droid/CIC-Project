## Why these and no others

- Items 11 and 12 take nothing from the record: Quote 3 opens on his taking bread, and Augustine is now only the one Jerome wrote to.
- Item 13: Quote 1 already gives Justin's refusal to shut them out, and his condition stays in his answer to Trypho.
- Item 14: the essence gives their Law-keeping; Irenaeus's circumcision and Jerusalem "as if it were the house of God" stay.
- Item 15: Irenaeus's "an apostate from the Law", with its gloss, carries the break with Paul in Irenaeus's own time. The charge in Epiphanius's report reached back from about 375 into the Irenaeus beat. Item 9 (Symmachus) is not applied: it carries the connection.
- Item 16: the rejection from both sides is still told, in the synagogue curse, in Quote 1 and in Jerome's verdict (Quote 2). Their being "Jews in everything", and Epiphanius's "cannot tell" on Christ, stay.
