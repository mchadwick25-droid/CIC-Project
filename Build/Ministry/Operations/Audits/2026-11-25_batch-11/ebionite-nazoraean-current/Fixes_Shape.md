# Fixes: story shape (Ebionites and Nazoraeans)

Ruling: Mark, 2026-11-25, Decision-Log "Batch 11: the eight briefs, Manichaeism's length and the grade fixes", "yes to all". Grade: `../Grades/listener.md`, `ebionite-nazoraean-current` (Form: summary, "a chain of outsiders' descriptions; the community itself never acts in a scene"; Opening: missing where; bare statement "the poverty of their Law"). The brief is not edited. No `.v*` file was opened.

## What the sources allow (said plainly)

The held texts give **one dated scene in which the community itself acts**: the Nazoraeans of Beroea, in Syria, let Jerome copy the gospel they used (F7, PL23 48161–48162, "Mihi quoque a Nazarwis, qui in Berea urbe Syrie hoc volumine utuntur, describendi facultas fuit"; written 392). They also give one named member at work (Symmachus putting the Hebrew Scriptures into Greek: G1; G2, HE 34737–34738; G3, VI 40556–40561) and the Nazoraeans naming themselves after Nazareth (C6, Pan 24398–24399, "οὗτοι γὰρ ἑαυτοῖς ὀνομα ἐπέϑεντο ... Ναζωραίων"). All else is custom reported by outsiders, or what others did to them. No speech of theirs is held beyond their gospel as Jerome quotes it (F6) and their charge against Paul as Epiphanius reports it (B5b, card). No scene, speech, host, year or setting is added to fill this. The strongest shape the sources allow: the people followed in time order through what they kept and what was done to them, each beat opening on them, the witness credited inside, and Beroea as their one scene of their own.

## For the writer and Mark

- The Pella flight (H1, H2) is the earliest event the sources hold about them and would give the story a stronger first place. It stays out under the length ruling (Cut first 8). Restoring it would be a change order for Mark, not this pass.
- The opening's where is the talk's place, in a later witness's voice. If Mark prefers the people's place in the first three sentences instead, the essence's "Syria and beyond the Jordan" line can move up into the hook at about the same cost.
- Grade 8.0 on the last brief sits at the floor; the rebuilt beats should not shorten sentences further.
