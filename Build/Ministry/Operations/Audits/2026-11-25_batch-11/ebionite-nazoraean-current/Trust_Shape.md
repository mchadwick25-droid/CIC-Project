## The heart

The heart is right for this subject. The sources show Law-keeping Jewish believers in Jesus. The witnesses who report them disagree on what they believed about Jesus, and every witness but Justin opposed them. The brief carries that doubt to Jerome's 404 letter, which ends it in his words. That matches the slate's job and the outline's heart.
