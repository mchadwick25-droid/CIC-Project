## alexandria-catechetical
Heart: In Alexandria, from about 180 to the mid-200s, Christian teachers led pagans and learners step by step through Scripture toward its deeper sense, with Origen's classroom at the center. The same habit of open teaching let Dionysius settle the Nepos dispute by public examination, not by decree.

## antiochene-church-third-century
Heart: Antioch's third-century church, one body under its bishop and bound to churches everywhere by letters, judged and finally put out its own bishop, Paul of Samosata, for teaching that Christ was from below; its line was later traced through Lucian to Arius.

## ebionite-nazoraean-current
Heart: Jews who believed Jesus was the Christ and still kept the Law of Moses, known only through writers who mostly argued against them, were named heretics by the church and cursed in the synagogues, held to be neither Jews nor Christians.
