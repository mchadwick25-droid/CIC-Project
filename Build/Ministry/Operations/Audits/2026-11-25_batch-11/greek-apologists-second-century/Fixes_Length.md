## The writer's three flags

- **Valentinians:** the sheet holds no gloss for them (only Justin's list, anf01 19254–19255), so they are left unnamed (Cut first 8). Connection and What came of it 2 updated.
- **"Could kill but not harm":** checked against the live justin-martyr page (`cft-website/tree/justin-martyr.html`). Its quotes are the First Apology 46 passage on the Word, "word", "reason", "the only philosophy that is sure and useful", Rusticus's question and Justin's answer. The line is not among them, so it is not barred and no Do not say line is needed. It goes to the card anyway (Cut first 17).
- **"First of all" for taxes:** the Greek is πανταχοῦ πρὸ πάντων πειρώμεθα φέρειν (1apol-grc 506–507); ANF "more readily than all men" (anf01 14580–14581). Beat 2 now reads "Everywhere they tried to pay their taxes more readily than anyone else". One line added to Do not say; no line removed.
