Change: "He was one of a number of Christians who wrote in Greek in the second century, from Athens and Rome to Sardis and Antioch." (0 words)

Do not say, to add: "a circle", "a group", "a movement" for the apologists → "a number of Christians who wrote" (no old source links them).

### B2. "at secret meals" (Trust second: NOT FOUND)

Athenagoras has Θυέστεια δεῖπνα, "Thyestean dinners" (athen-grc 179–180). Theophilus has "that we eat human flesh" (anf02 10239–10244). Neither says secret. The first trust read held "secret" on Trypho's "having extinguished the lights" (anf01 17948–17949). The second read's finding stays BLOCKING until it is fixed or a third reader shows it holds. The fix is recommended.
Change: "…of eating human flesh at their feasts and of incest…" (0 words)
Outline: **carries the error** (Words to explain: "eating human flesh at secret meals", citing anf02 10239–10244, which does not say it). Back to the researcher.
Do not say, to add: "secret meals" → "at their feasts" (athen-grc 179–180, δεῖπνα).
