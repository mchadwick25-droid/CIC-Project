## New and changed lines

- **Marcion (B7), beat 2 and sheet rows 60–61.** Story words: "He also named Marcion, still teaching another god above the Creator, whose followers, also called Christians, were not persecuted for their teaching." My rendering of 1apol-grc 729–731, 739–743: "Marcion of Pontus, who even now is still teaching those who obey him to hold some other god greater than the Creator … All who start from these men are called Christians … we know that they are neither persecuted nor killed by you, at least for their teachings." The line says no more. "Still teaching" is right (ὃς καὶ νῦν ἔτι ἐστὶ διδάσκων); "alive" is ANF's and is rightly dropped. "Whose followers" narrows Justin's "these men" (Simon, Menander, Marcion) to Marcion's, a true subset. "For their teaching" keeps κἂν διὰ τὰ δόγματα, and the Do not say line guards it. Documented, own: right. Cites right.

The fixer is right that no held text says newness caused the verdict (anf02 10248–10251 and 11092–11095 only pair "recent" with "foolishness" and "false"). Placing the two views side by side is the honest answer to B9. Documented, own: right.

## MINOR

- **M-R1. Beat 2's note on Justin's reason over-cites 735–743.** "Others bore the name and went free, while Christians were hunted for it": 735–743 holds "called Christians … not persecuted". The other half of Justin's reason is ch. 26's opening (1apol-grc 706–709, οὐ μόνον οὐκ ἐδιώχθησαν ὑφ᾿ ὑμῶν, ἀλλὰ καὶ τιμῶν κατηξιώθησαν). "Hunted for the name" is the opening's line (athen-grc 104–111), not ch. 26's. Replace "That is Justin's own reason for naming him to the emperor: others bore the name and went free, while Christians were hunted for it." with: "That is Justin's own reason for naming him to the emperor: men the demons put forward were not persecuted but honoured (1apol-grc 706–709), and their followers bore the name and went free. The contrast with Christians hunted for the name is the opening's; do not tell it again." This changes no story words and no count.
