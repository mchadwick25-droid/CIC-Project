## FIX

**R1. The harmony told inside the Addai legend says more than the legend (B2).** Addai 65 gives only the word: the readings "of the Old and New Testament, of the Diatessaron". It names neither Tatian nor the four Gospels. Those come from Eusebius (26424-26433: Tatian "formed a certain combination and collection of the Gospels ... to which he gave the title Diatessaron"). Phillips's note (219) makes the same link, but that is the editor's. As now worded, "woven by Tatian" sits inside what the legend pictures.

- In `Outline.md`, The heart, essence, replace: "Edessa's later founding story pictures its people hearing the four Gospels read as one story, woven by Tatian" with: "Edessa's later founding story pictures its people hearing the Diatessaron read: the one story Tatian wove from the four Gospels". This adds 2 words. The legend's own word is kept, and the narrator glosses it on Eusebius's witness.
- Make the same change in beat 1 ("Edessa's later founding story pictures its people hearing the four Gospels read as one story, woven by Tatian, a teacher born in the east, a pupil of Justin" → "Edessa's later founding story pictures its people hearing the Diatessaron read: the one story Tatian wove from the four Gospels") and in the Do not say entry's right-hand wording.
- Add to Do not say: "the founding story says Tatian's harmony was read" → "the founding story names the Diatessaron; Eusebius says Tatian made it".

**R2. "Among them" now points to the wrong bishops (B9).** In beat 6 the line follows "agreed that a city would no longer have two or three bishops, only one". So "them" reads as those two or three bishops. In beat 6 and in Connection, replace "Among them was the bishop of Nisibis, Ephrem's old city, now inside Persia" with "Among the forty was the bishop of Nisibis, Ephrem's old city, now inside Persia". This adds 1 word.
