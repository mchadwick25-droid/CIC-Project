## Worded by me

- **B5, what Arius taught and what the quarrel was** (beat 6, Words to explain, Who tells it): "later, in Alexandria, a presbyter named Arius said the Son of God had a beginning, and God had none; Arius wrote that his bishop preached that the Son always was". Held lines: Thdt 6025-6031 and 6045-6046, Greek Epiph-Gk 45607-45615 and 45736-45738 (Arius's own words); Thdt 5278-5283 and 5291-5295 (Theodoret, later, hostile: presbyter of Alexandria, and the other side). New sheet rows Ar1, Ar3. The story narrates it, because the `arius` story quotes the "We are persecuted" line (a Do not say line now bars it).
- **B6, why Arius was driven out** (beat 6, ending): "drove him out of the city because he would not agree", credited to Arius. Held lines: Thdt 6025-6027; Epiph-Gk 45607-45609. Theodoret's other account, that Alexander deposed him, is on the sheet (Thdt 5301-5305). New sheet row Ar2, own words, Documented that Arius gives this reason.
- **B7, Alexander's "successor"** (beat 6, Words to explain, Do not say): no held text gives it a plain sense. The Greek word, διαδεξάμενος (Migne Theodoret 117750, an editor's lemma; new row L4-Gk), means only "having taken over from". So the story drops the word and tells Alexander's own setting: "that bishop, Alexander, set Lucian in a line from Paul to Arius; Lucian, he wrote, kept out of communion with the bishops for many years" (Thdt 5752-5775). The sentence is split, with Lucian as the plain subject. "Same stock" is glossed "shoots of the same root" (Thdt 5774, "offsets"). A new Do not say line bars "successor". Ending: "One of them, Arius, now driven out of Alexandria (beat 6 gives the reason, Ar2)".
- **Length, Ruled** (Cut first): the fixes add about 45 words, and about 51 move to the card: the angel line (Ruled 1), "Everyone grieved in private" (Ruled 2), Eusebius's "judged most fittingly" (Ruled 4, beat 5) and Jerome's copies (Ruled 5, beat 6). Each is marked `[cut: card, Ruled n]` in place. These are estimates; the writer recounts.
