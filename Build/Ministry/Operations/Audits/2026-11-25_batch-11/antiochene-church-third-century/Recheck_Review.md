# Recheck of the review fixes: The Church of Antioch in the Third Century

## Verdict: FIX

The new lines hold at their sources:

- **Essence (B1):** "refusing to confess" matches E-Gk 11903-11904 (οὐ βούλεται συνομολογεῖν). "Over him, until at last they put him out" matches G5 and M1.
- **Ar1 and Ar2:** they match Thdt 6025-6031 and 6045-6046. Arius's account of his own expulsion is credited to him, and the "We are persecuted" line is barred.
- **Ar3:** Theodoret is marked later and hostile, and his "jealousy" stays out.
- **The "shoots of the same root" gloss:** fair to "offsets of the same stock".
- **The B4 ruling line:** stands on A1.
- **The one item not applied:** rightly kept. "A bishop must be an example" sits in the heart, which is never cut.

The heart is kept, and the plan fits the 675 ruling. Three changes are needed.

### 1. Beat 6 runs out of order

Arius's quarrel and expulsion (in Alexander's time as bishop, Thdt 5276-5283, after 312) and Alexander's charge are told before Lucian's death under Maximinus. That is a jump back. Move the death, the sermon and the Explain it ahead of Arius.

In beat 6, replace everything from "Where Arius is first heard" to the end of the beat with:

"In the persecution under the emperor Maximinus he was taken to the emperor at Nicomedia, defended his faith, and was imprisoned and killed (L2, L3; Eusebius). John Chrysostom, in a sermon on Lucian two generations later, told how he answered under torture (Q3; credited). Explain it: why he died: for his faith, defended before the ruler (L3). Confidence: Documented; the answer is Chrysostom's account. Then, after his death, where Arius is first heard, say who he was and what the quarrel was, in narration (no quote: the `arius` story quotes Arius's letter, and every quote in it is barred): later, in Alexandria, a presbyter named Arius (Ar3) said the Son of God had a beginning, and God had none (Ar1); Arius wrote that his bishop preached that the Son always was, and drove him out of the city because he would not agree (Ar1, Ar2; Arius's own account, credited to him). Then the bishop's charge, split in two with Lucian the plain subject: that bishop, Alexander, set Lucian in a line from Paul to Arius; Lucian, he wrote, kept out of communion with the bishops for many years (L4, L4-Gk). And Arius and his party, he said, were shoots of the same root (Thdt 5772-5775, \"offsets of the same stock\"; an enemy's charge, credited, in his image). Do not say \"successor\" (see Do not say). Confidence: Documented that Arius and Alexander say so; Alexander's line from Paul is his charge (D5)."

### 2. "Successor" is still prescribed in three places

The new Do not say line bars the word "successor", but three earlier lines still tell the writer to use it.

- **Do not say, row "Lucian taught Arius's doctrine", Right column.** Replace "Alexander charged that Lucian was Paul's successor (L4), as a charge" with "Alexander set Lucian in a line from Paul to Arius (L4, Thdt 5752-5775), as a charge".
- **Do not say, row "Lucian succeeded Paul as bishop".** Replace the Right column with "Alexander set Lucian in a line from Paul to Arius (L4), kept in his mouth; no office or teaching tie added; never \"successor\"".
- **Connection.** Replace "An enemy of Arius called Lucian Paul's successor, who stood out of communion for years (L4)" with "An enemy of Arius set Lucian in a line from Paul, and said he stood out of communion for years (L4)".

### 3. Beat 5 now tells the expulsion twice

The B4 line repeats Paul being driven out. Delete the earlier sentence "Paul was driven out by the worldly power (A1, Eusebius, in paraphrase)." and keep the one in Explain it.
