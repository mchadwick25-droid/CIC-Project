## BLOCKING

**F1. Paragraph 5, sentence 2. SOFTENED OR CHANGED.**
- Story: "He scolded those who would not cheer and wave handkerchiefs as in the theater, when the people should listen with reverence, as in the house of God."
- Source, E-Gk 11887-11891: "τοῖς μὴ ἐπαινοῦσιν μηδὲ ὥσπερ ἐν τοῖς θεάτροις κατασείουσιν ταῖς ὀθόναις ... τοῖς δ᾽ οὖν ὡς ἐν οἴκῳ θεοῦ σεμνοπρεπῶς καὶ εὐτάκτως ἀκούουσιν ἐπιτιμῶν καὶ ἐνυβρίζων". E-Eng 42156-42160 gives it as: "he rebukes and insults those who do not applaud ... but who listen reverently and orderly as in the house of God".
- The fault: in the bishops' charge, the people he scolded were the ones who *did* listen with reverence. The story turns that fact into a rule ("should listen"). The charge loses its point, which is that he insulted reverent listeners.
- Exact change: "He scolded those who would not cheer and wave handkerchiefs as in the theater, though they listened with reverence, as in the house of God."
