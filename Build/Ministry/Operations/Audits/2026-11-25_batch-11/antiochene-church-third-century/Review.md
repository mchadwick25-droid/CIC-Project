## BLOCKING findings

### B1. "met again and again to put him out" (paragraph 2)

Both trust reads. Trust.md 1: "'To put him out' gives a purpose the source does not give ... Only the last meeting put him out (E-Eng 41936-41954)." Trust_Second.md 2: the same, and "it clashes with paragraph 4". Trust.md 1 also marks "denying" as stronger than the charge: "οὐ βούλεται συνομολογεῖν" (E-Gk 11903-11904), he would not confess it. Trust_Second.md let "denying" stand on E-Eng 42058 ("who denied even his own God and Lord"), but that is not a third reader's clearance, so it stays BLOCKING until fixed.

- **Change:** "When their own bishop was charged with refusing to confess that the Son of God came down from heaven, the bishops of the East met again and again over him, until at last they put him out."
- **Outline:** holds what is needed (T2 "would not confess"; G5 "at different times and frequently"; M1 the last meeting). The outline left room: its essence carries both faults, "denying" and "to put him out".
- **Do not say** (add to the outline): Wrong: "the bishops met again and again to put him out" / "Paul denied that the Son came down". Right: "they met often over him (G5); only the last meeting cut him off (M1)"; "the bishops charge that he would not confess it (T2)".
- **Note for the lead:** the essence wording is the heart Mark approved on 2026-11-25. Correcting it in the outline is a change to an approved text, so it is named here as a change order, not made quietly.

### B2. "Paul called Jesus Christ a man from below" (paragraph 3)

Both trust reads. The bishops quote "Ἰησοῦν Χριστὸν κάτωθεν" (E-Gk 11907; E-Eng 42177-42178): no "man". "Man" is Eusebius's word (T1, E-Gk 11433-11435).

- **Change:** "They wrote that Paul said Jesus Christ was from below, and Eusebius says he held that Christ was by nature an ordinary man."
- **Outline:** holds it (T1, T2). The outline left room: beat 1's Explain it reads "a Christ who did not come down from heaven but was a man from below". Change that line to match.
- **Do not say:** Wrong: "Paul called Christ a man from below". Right: the bishops quote him, "Jesus Christ is from below" (T2); "ordinary man" stays with Eusebius (T1).

### B3. "Everyone grieved in private, and no one dared accuse him." (paragraph 5)

Trust_Second.md let it stand; no third reader has cleared it, so it stays BLOCKING. Listen_FirstTime.md 3.3 (MINOR) also hears it as contradicting the bishops' meetings, and asks for "In his own church, they wrote". One change covers both.

- **Change:** "In his own church, they wrote, everyone grieved in private, but feared his tyranny and power too much to accuse him."
- **Outline:** holds it (Source_Sheet C9 has the full line). The outline left room: beat 3 gives C9 without the fear.
- **Do not say:** Wrong: "no one dared accuse him" without its reason. Right: "they so feared his tyranny and power" (C9).

### B4. "He left the teaching alone and let those bishops' letters decide" (paragraph 7)
