## (e) Significance and stances

He defended Christians before the emperor on the ground that a name proves nothing (own Apology; the trial account shows the name still decided his case; no reply survives). He taught Christ as the Word shared by every people (own Apology; his hearer Tatian's Address shows the opposite view of Greek learning; no held source shows who took the teaching up). He defended one God, the Creator, against Marcion, and Irenaeus quotes him for it. The record stops at who took up the Word teaching.

## (f) Craft self-check

Line of the story: a philosopher who asked the emperor to judge Christians by deeds and not a name, taught that the Word belongs to every people, and died on the name he had defended, leaving a book Irenaeus used against Marcion.

- Good storytelling. Weakest: "Justin had also written against Marcion, who preached a god greater than the Creator." It comes after the death. Bar: Gregory of Tours, "Two centuries later the historian Paul the Deacon sent readers to..." The bar also closes on later use after the life, so the order holds, but the "also" is a seam.
- Flow. Weakest link: "No answer to him survives in the sources we have. He expected to be plotted against, he wrote, perhaps by Crescens". Bar: Origen, "In his eighteenth year he took charge of teaching the faith in Alexandria, because everyone else had fled." The step from the missing answer to the plot is by order only; no source joins them.
- No AI tells. Weakest: "since a name alone proves nothing good or bad". Bar: John Chrysostom's opening. It is Justin's own argument (First Apology 4), not a saying of ours.
- No hanging sentences. Weakest: "Tatian says Crescens tried to have Justin killed." Short, but it is the middle of three witnesses. Bar: Athanasius, "Sozomen adds a rumor that Athanasius had hidden the man himself."
- Sentence test / everything relevant. Weakest: "His hearer Tatian did not follow him here, for Tatian's Address to the Greeks bids their learning farewell." It is the only consequence the held sources give for the Word teaching. Bar: Gregory of Nyssa, "The sources we have do not show what came of this teaching in his own day."

Sentences cut on the sentence test: 9 (the four schools, the "flame" line, the bath, the Sunday service, "questioned and found ignorant", "fully persuaded", Eusebius on the ancients, the scourging, Heraclitus).
