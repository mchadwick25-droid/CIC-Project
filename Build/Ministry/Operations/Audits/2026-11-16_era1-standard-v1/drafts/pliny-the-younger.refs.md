## (e) Significance and stances

An outside witness, so no stance of his own. What he reports: how he tried Christians, and what former Christians told him of their worship and oath. What came of it: Trajan's ruling (E 19522-19531); Tertullian's use of the report as proof that inquiry found no crime, and his attack on the ruling (T 1565-1586); Eusebius's statement, from Tertullian, that the persecution was checked to a degree (E 19541-19552). The record stops there in the held files: no other ancient writer held in the Library was found using the letter, and Eusebius knew it only through Tertullian.

## (f) Craft self-check

Line of the story: a governor who did not know how to try Christians tried them, reported what he learned of their worship to Trajan, and got a ruling that Christian writers then argued over.

1. Good storytelling. Weakest: "They also swore an oath to keep from theft, robbery and adultery." A report line. Bar: Benedict, "His monks promise 'stability, a changed way of life, and obedience'". Mine is flatter but short.
2. Flow. Weakest link: "...yet his own inquiry turned up no crime." to "Trajan answered that Christians ...". The reply follows the letter by order only. Bar: Gregory of Tours, "Gregory refused. Only the Son was made man ... he answered". A reviewer may want "Trajan answered" tied back to the request for advice.
3. No AI tells. Weakest: "Pliny writes as the judge who had already sent Christians to punishment, and he calls their faith insanity, yet his own inquiry turned up no crime." It is the trust sentence the outside-witness form asks for. Bar: Methodius, "Both men admired Origen, so neither is a neutral witness."
4. No hanging sentences. Weakest: "He found 'nothing except a depraved and excessive superstition.'" It ends a paragraph on a quotation, but it is the result of the torture in the sentence before. Close: Eusebius's judgment with its limit, which is where the held record ends.
5. Everything relevant. Weakest: "Those who denied it he released, once they had prayed to the gods and cursed Christ." Kept because it shows what the test was. Bar: Constantine, "Maxentius was driven into the Tiber at the broken bridge."

Sentences cut on the sentence test: 8 (McGiffert's description and the uncle, the dating sentence, the image with incense and wine, "one even twenty years before", the harmless meal, the spread to villages and countryside, the pardon clause of Trajan's reply, Tertullian's argument as a separate sentence).
