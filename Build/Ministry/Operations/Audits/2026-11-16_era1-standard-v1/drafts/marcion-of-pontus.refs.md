## (e) Significance and stances

- Stance 1, another God above the Creator, with the law set against the gospel (Justin, Irenaeus, Tertullian agree). Consequence: churches of his own that spread in his lifetime (Justin) and were still found two and three centuries later (Epiphanius, Theodoret).
- Stance 2, a cut-down Luke and Paul. Consequence: the church's answer on its Scriptures; Irenaeus's own text names Marcion in his case for four Gospels (III.11.7-9), and Tertullian's fourth book answers from Marcion's Gospel.
- The record stops with Theodoret's villages; no held source read here tells how the Marcionite churches ended, and the brief does not say.

## (f) Craft self-check

Line of the story: a teacher at Rome set another God above the Creator and cut the Scriptures to fit, and his churches spread, drew the church's answer on its Gospels, and lasted three centuries.

- Good storytelling. Weakest: "The cut-down Scriptures drew an answer." Beside Gregory of Tours, "and a king put that faith to the test." Mine is a plainer hinge, but it carries the cause.
- Flow. Weakest link: from "the first-born of Satan." to "We have none of Marcion's own books, so nearly all we know of him comes from men who thought as Polycarp did". Beside Benedict, "Gregory wrote to show what God did through a saint, so the book is full of wonders."
- No AI tells. Weakest: "His own churches spread all the same" ("all the same" is a light connective). Beside John Chrysostom's opening paragraph.
- No hanging sentences. The close: "Theodoret of Cyrrhus, a bishop in Syria, wrote that he had led eight villages of Marcionites over to his own faith." Beside Ephrem's close, "a month later, Palladius says, he died there." It ends where the record ends.
- Everything relevant. Weakest: "Tertullian, but neither earlier witness, tells that Marcion first belonged to the church at Rome, which put him out". It is the one life fact kept, and it sets up the separate churches. Beside Methodius, "Little else about Methodius is certain."
- Sentences cut on the sentence test: 9 (shipmaster, the money, the offer to repent, Cerdo, the two dates, "author of evils", the wasps line, the whole seduction paragraph counted as one, "The churches outlasted both writers").
