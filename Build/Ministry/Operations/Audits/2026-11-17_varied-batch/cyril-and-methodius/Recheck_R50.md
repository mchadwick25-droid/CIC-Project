## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who:** two brothers from Thessalonica, the scholar Constantine (later Cyril) and Methodius, sent from Constantinople in the 860s to teach the Slavs of Moravia in their own language.
2. **What happened:** Constantine made the first Slavic letters and they put the church's services into Slavic; Latin clergy said only Hebrew, Greek and Latin were fit for God, and Constantine answered them; after his death in Rome, Methodius was held by Frankish bishops, banned and then allowed by the pope to sing Mass in Slavic, with the Gospel read first in Latin.
3. **Why the connection matters:** after Methodius died his disciples were driven out and carried the letters and Slavic services to Bulgaria, and from there they reached the Rus: the start of the Slavic churches' own written language.

All three lines come clear on one hearing.
