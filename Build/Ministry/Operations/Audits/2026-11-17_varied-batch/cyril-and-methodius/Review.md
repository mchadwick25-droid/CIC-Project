# Review: Cyril and Methodius

Reviewer: independent of the writer. Brief: `cyril-and-methodius.brief.txt` (431 words by the scorer).

## 1. Listener read (once, straight through, before anything else)

- The opening question lands. "Who can write words on water" is strange in a good way, and the reason for it comes two paragraphs later.
- Paragraph 2 tripped me. "With his brother Methodius he began the mission of Cyril and Methodius" names Cyril inside a sentence about Cyril, and "the Slavic church that grew from it" hangs loose. I recovered, but I heard the seam.
- The Venice answer about rain and sun is the moment I would remember. "Partners ploughing a single furrow" sticks too.
- At paragraph 6 I drifted: Frankish bishops, two and a half years, a Salzburg writer, all in two sentences, and no reason why they held him. "Went back" to where?
- "The first bishop of the Bulgarian tongue" puzzled me: a bishop of a language?
- The ending is clear but quiet. "Rus" means nothing to a newcomer.
- What I came away with: he made letters for the Slavs, argued that God's rain falls on everyone, his brother carried on and was jailed for it, a pope backed them, and their students took the letters to Bulgaria and beyond.

## 4. Verdict: PASS

No BLOCKING findings. Nothing is wrong, unsupported, misquoted or past a limit, and the listener was never lost for good. The MINOR findings below should be applied before it is approved to proceed. None of them needs another full review round, only a targeted recheck.

## 5. Findings

1. **MINOR. Paragraph 2: the tradition sentence stumbles and says slightly more than the outline.** "With his brother Methodius he began the mission of Cyril and Methodius, and the Slavic church that grew from it" names Cyril inside his own sentence, and makes him the founder of a church, where the outline names a tradition. Change: recast it, e.g. "He and his brother Methodius began what is remembered as the mission of Cyril and Methodius. The Slavic church grew from it." Keep the outline's spoken words.
2. **MINOR. Paragraph 4: the Q1 frame drops the rebuke.** The rendering is "Then how are you not ashamed to fix on three languages only?" The brief's frame, "How then, he asked, could they 'fix on three languages only'?", loses "not ashamed", and that rebuke is the point of the line. Change: quote the full sentence as rendered. It is no longer, and it removes a second "he asked".
3. **MINOR. Paragraph 5: "begged" adds feeling.** Q2 gives a plain command ("Do not give up your teaching for the mountain's sake"). Change "begged him" to "told him". Also, "go back to life as a monk" assumes the listener knows Methodius had been a monk, and the brief never says so (see 4).
4. **MINOR. Paragraph 6 and balance: Methodius is under-told, and the reason for his arrest is missing.** The standard asks for the reason for any act in the same sentence. Outline beat 6 gives it: Frankish bishops said he was teaching in their land, and he said it belonged to St Peter. "Went back as a bishop" also needs its place, "for the Slavs of Pannonia". If room allows, one clause from beat 1, that Methodius had governed a Slavic district and then become a monk, sets up the furrow handover and gives the second brother a life. Use the words freed by findings 1 and 6, so the total moves toward 380, not past 431.
5. **MINOR. Paragraph 7: "the first bishop of the Bulgarian tongue" puzzles a newcomer.** In the outline it is a quoted phrase. Change: give it as the Life's phrase and say in the same sentence what it means from the outline's own content, e.g. that he was the first bishop to serve the Bulgarians in their own language.
6. **MINOR. Paragraph 7 goes flat (grade 7.8).** It has five short separate statements. Join the cause and the act ("Because Bulgarian priests could barely follow Greek, Clement…"), and fold "The Life says he handed down…" into the sentence that already names the Life. That shortens the paragraph and lifts the grade.
7. **MINOR. Paragraph 8: "Rus" is unexplained.** Tie it to what the sentence already has, e.g. "and in Rus, the chroniclers' own land". This adds nothing beyond the outline, since the chroniclers wrote in Kiev.
