# Card edits: Clement of Alexandria

## Edit 1: place
- Old: "Alexandria; later with Alexander of Jerusalem, and at Antioch"
- New: "Alexandria; later with the imprisoned bishop Alexander, and at Antioch"
- Evidence: the prison letter names no place ("here", EUS 33615; Source_Sheet N3: do not name Jerusalem or Cappadocia as Clement's location). Eusebius has Alexander first bishop in Cappadocia and only afterwards at Jerusalem (EUS 33520–33533). Brief: "Alexander, a bishop held in prison".

## Left alone
- knownFor "and taught Origen": Eusebius (EUS 33015–33080) and Jerome (NP3 40277–40278) both say it; no held source contradicts it (Origen's own letter is silent, D4). The card's legacy trace already credits Jerome. Not clearly wrong.
- dates "last heard of about 212": this is McGiffert's date for the last notice (EUS 28673), given with "about"; nothing held contradicts it. Not clearly wrong.

The new brief's fields, made to pass the census check. Nothing else on the card moved.
