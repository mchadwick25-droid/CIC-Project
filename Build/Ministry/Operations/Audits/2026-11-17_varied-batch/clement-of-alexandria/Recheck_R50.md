2. **What happened:** after a search for a teacher he found one in Egypt; he argued that Greek philosophy was God's guide leading the Greeks to Christ, wrote his notes as the Miscellanies, lived among daily martyrdoms, and carried an imprisoned bishop's letter to Antioch.
