# Second Readings: clement-of-alexandria

## 1. I tracked him down where he
Source: cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml 27489-27492
Second rendering: I tracked him down, hidden away in Egypt, and there I found rest.
Ours: I tracked him down where he was hidden in Egypt, and I found rest.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning: the search, the hiding place in Egypt and the rest he found are all carried.

## 2. For it was a guide to
Source: cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml 27891-27893
Second rendering: Philosophy was a guardian to lead the Greek mind to Christ, as the Law led the Hebrews.
Ours: For it was a guide to bring the Greek mind to Christ, as the Law brought the Hebrews.
Verdict: same
Reason: "Guide" fairly carries Paul's schoolmaster, the servant who led a child to school; ours keeps "it" where the context makes philosophy the subject.

## 5. Clement wrote that every day he saw martyrs (narrated paraphrase)
Source: cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml 34507-34509
Second rendering: Every day we have martyrs before our eyes in plenty, people burnt, impaled and beheaded.
Ours: Clement wrote that every day he saw martyrs, people who died rather than deny Christ, burnt, impaled and beheaded.
Verdict: minor
Reason: Clement writes "we", Christians who watch it together, not "I"; "he saw" is fair since he counts himself among them. The gloss "people who died rather than deny Christ" is the source sheet's (S4), not Clement's, and is narrated, not quoted. Nothing added to the force of the scene.
