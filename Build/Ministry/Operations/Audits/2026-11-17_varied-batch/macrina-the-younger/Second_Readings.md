# Second Readings: macrina-the-younger

## 1. You, Lord, have set us free
Source: cic/texts/gregory-nyssa_opera-omnia-pg46-grc-lat_migne1863.txt 71687, 71690
Second rendering: "You, Lord," she said, "have freed us from the fear of death."
Ours: You, Lord, have set us free from the fear of death.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning; ours drops the "she said" frame, which the brief supplies around the quote.

## 2. Here is the cloak, here is
Source: cic/texts/gregory-nyssa_opera-omnia-pg46-grc-lat_migne1863.txt 72083-72085
Second rendering: Look, here is the cloak, here is the veil for her head, and the worn-out shoes for her feet. This is her wealth.
Ours: Here is the cloak, here is the head-covering, and the worn-out shoes on her feet. This is her wealth.
Verdict: minor
Reason: Ours says the shoes are "on her feet", which could suggest she is wearing them; the Greek says only "shoes of the feet", and Lampadia is pointing to them as all that Macrina owned. Smallest fix if wanted: "the worn-out shoes for her feet".
