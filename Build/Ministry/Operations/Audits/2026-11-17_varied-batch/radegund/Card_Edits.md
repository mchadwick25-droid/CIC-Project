# Card edits: Radegund

## Edit 1: `legacy.remembered`, Fortunatus

- Old: "Venantius Fortunatus stayed at Poitiers because of her, he writes,"
- New: "Venantius Fortunatus stayed near her by her wish, he writes,"
- Evidence: the line he writes, "Martinum cupiens voto Radegundis adhaesi" (`cic/texts/venantius-fortunatus_opera-mgh-aa-4-lat_leo-krusch1881.txt` 16035, OCR damaged), says he stayed close by Radegund's wish. It does not name Poitiers (Source_Sheet.md I6 and the Fortunatus entry: "the line does not name Poitiers").

## Left alone

- The Cross "set in gold" credited to Gregory. The card does not say it. Its trace reads only "Gregory of Tours tells how she sent to the East for wood of the True Cross", which Gregory does say (Dalton 1927, 19601–19607). The gold is credited to Baudonivia in the brief references.
