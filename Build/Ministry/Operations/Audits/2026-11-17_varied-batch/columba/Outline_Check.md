# Outline check: Columba (Colum Cille)

**Verdict: FIX**

I re-opened the cited lines in `cic/texts/` (AD, BEDE, BEDL, AU, AC). All three quotes are verbatim. The shape, the length and its reason, the setting, the spoken name and "Who tells it" hold. The impact ranking holds: the family of monasteries under Iona is what every later witness uses, and Whitby is marked DEFENDED. Three beat lines say more than the text does.

## Findings

1. **BLOCKING. Beat 6, "His old white horse".** Adomnán never calls the horse old. He calls it "the white horse, a faithful servant ... the one which used to carry the milk pails to and fro between the byre and the monastery" (AD 10746–10749). Correction: "The white horse that carried the milk pails lays its head on his chest."

2. **BLOCKING. Beat 7, "he lifted his hand to bless the monks".** In the text Diormit lifts the hand: "Diormit then lifts up the holy right hand of the Saint that he may bless the choir of monks. But the venerable Father himself at the same time moved his hand as much as he was able" (AD 10868–10872). Correction: "Diormit raised his right hand, and he moved it as much as he could to bless the monks, then died."

3. **BLOCKING. Beat 1, "the Welsh annals say 521".** The annal says only "Lxxvii. Sanctus Columcille nascitur" (AC 2399). The figure 521 is the 1860 editor's, printed in the margin (Source_Sheet S4, D5). Correction: "Adomnán gives no year; a Welsh annal, as its modern editor dates it, puts his birth in 521", or drop the year and say "early in the sixth century".

4. **MINOR. Beat 7, "fell at the altar".** "Fell" can be heard as a collapse. The text says "on bended knees falls down in prayer at the altar" (AD 10842–10843). Correction: "knelt in prayer at the altar".

5. **MINOR. Beat 7, last words "to his monks".** Adomnán says they were addressed to the brethren but heard by "his attendant alone" (AD 10814). Add "heard only by Diormit", so the writer does not set the monks in the room.

6. **MINOR. Beat 5, "families were baptized".** The held text gives one countryman "with all his household" (AD 7609–7613) and one old chief (AD 4295–4297). Correction: "a household, and an old chief, were baptized".

7. **MINOR. Beat 2, "about 563; Bede says 565".** The Irish annals (AU 4809–4810) and the Welsh annals (AC 2417) both say 562. The 563 is Huyshe's reckoning (D1). Correction: "The annals say 562 and Bede 565; about 563 is the usual reckoning."

8. **MINOR. Impact 1, "Its abbot was a priest, and even bishops were under him".** Bede describes Iona in his own day ("has for its ruler", BEDE 5856–5862). Correction: "In Bede's day its abbot was a priest, and even bishops were under him..."

9. **MINOR. Impact 3, "until 716".** Bede gives both "till ... 715" (BEDE 5877–5878) and a 716 chapter heading (BEDE 14983). Correction: "until about 716".

## Checked and sound

- **Quote 1** (AD 10787–10789), **Quote 2** (AD 10815–10817) and **Quote 3** (BEDL 18882–18885, with the OCR mark removed) match the file. I made my own renderings first. Theirs drop nothing that matters and add nothing; "charity" is rightly rendered as "love". In Quote 2, "live in peace" for "with peace" is acceptable.
- **Bede's request** to Iona for a bishop: "after they had sent Aidan" with "they" meaning the monks of Hii (BEDE 15028–15030), and Aidan "a monk of the island called Hii" (BEDE 5794–5795).
- **Colman at Whitby** (BEDE 8121–8128), Oswy's ruling (BEDE 8205–8209) and the year 664 (BEDE 8236–8237).
- **Durrow** comes before the voyage (BEDE 5847–5851). The givers of Iona are tagged Contested (BEDE 5800–5803; AU 5173–5175). Brude and the gates (AD 7874–7897). Twelve disciples (AD 9409–9410).
- **The granary** and "enough for the year" (AD 10689–10697). The Psalter stop (AD 10783–10789). "June" is attributed to the annals (AU 5980–5981).
- **"Who tells it"** says plainly that the record is late and that no witness from his own lifetime is held. The Leave-out list is sound, including the AU battle-prayer annal (AU 4539–4540).
