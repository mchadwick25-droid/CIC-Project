## The removed Stokes/Reeves birth-year claim

The writer removed it as "not found in the held files". That is mistaken. It is in the held file `cic/texts/stokes_lives-of-saints-from-the-book-of-lismore-iri-eng_1890.txt`, lines 24009–24012, in Stokes's notes: "The statement that Columba's birthday was the seventh of December and a Thursday, points to the years 517 and 523, Reeves, Columba, lxix. But Bishop Reeves thinks a.d. 521 'most likely to be the true period.'" (OCR "Ixix".) The old card reported it correctly.

The card is not wrong without it, so this does not block. But the removal rested on a false premise, and the claim is true and useful: without it the card's "His years and age" entry shows 521 only from Huyshe and the Annales Cambriae, and hides that 521 is Reeves's choice among readings. Recommended: restore it at the end of `uncertain[3]`, for example: "Whitley Stokes reports that the birthday the Book of Lismore Life gives, a Thursday, 7 December, points to 517 or 523, but Bishop William Reeves thinks 521 most likely."
