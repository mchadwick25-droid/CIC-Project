# Second Readings: columba

## 1. they that seek the Lord shall
Source: cic/texts/adomnan_life-of-saint-columba_huyshe1905.txt 10785-10787
Second rendering: Those who seek the Lord will lack no good thing.
Ours: they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing
Verdict: changes meaning
Reason: Ours keeps Huyshe's archaic wording verbatim. To a modern listener "shall not want any good thing" can sound like "will not desire any good thing", the opposite of "will not lack". Smallest fix: "Those who seek the Lord will lack no good thing."

## 2. Here I must stop, at the
Source: cic/texts/adomnan_life-of-saint-columba_huyshe1905.txt 10787-10789
Second rendering: "Here," he says, "I must stop at the foot of this page. Let Baithene write what follows."
Ours: Here I must stop, at the bottom of this page. Let Baithene write what comes next.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning; ours drops the "he says" frame, which the brief supplies.

## 3. of their own accord
Source: cic/texts/adomnan_life-of-saint-columba_huyshe1905.txt 7887
Second rendering: The doors fly open in great haste, by themselves.
Ours: of their own accord
Verdict: same
Reason: Verbatim fragment of Huyshe's printed English ("of their own accord"), which is still current English.

## 4. My sons, I entrust these last
Source: cic/texts/adomnan_life-of-saint-columba_huyshe1905.txt 10815-10817
Second rendering: My sons, I entrust these last words to you: have sincere love for one another, and keep peace among you.
Ours: My sons, I entrust these last words to you. Love one another without pretence, and live in peace.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning; "love one another without pretence" carries "mutual and unfeigned charity", and "charity" is rightly rendered "love".

## 5. our most reverend Father Columba and
Source: cic/texts/bede_ecclesiastical-history-english-people_giles1903.txt 8122-8123
Second rendering: Our most honoured father Columba and those who came after him.
Ours: our most reverend Father Columba and his successors
Verdict: minor
Reason: Verbatim from Giles. "Most reverend" now sounds like an archbishop's formal title; Colman means a father held in deep respect. Optional fix: "our most honoured Father Columba and his successors".

## 6. But whatever kind of man he
Source: cic/texts/bede_ecclesiastical-history-english-people_giles1903.txt 5866-5869; original cic/texts/bede_opera-historica-v1-historia-abbatum-lat_plummer1896.txt 18882-18885
Second rendering: But whatever kind of man he was, we hold this as certain about him. He left successors known for great self-discipline, for love of God, and for keeping a monastic rule.
Ours: But whatever kind of man he was, this much about him we hold as certain. He left successors known for great self-control, for the love of God, and for life under a rule.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning; "life under a rule" carries regularique institutione.
