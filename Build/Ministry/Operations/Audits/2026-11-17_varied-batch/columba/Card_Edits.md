# Card edits: Columba

## Edit 1: `uncertain[0]`, date of the Life

- Old: "whose Life Wentworth Huyshe places between 679 and 704;"
- New: "whose Life Wentworth Huyshe places between 692 and 697;"
- Evidence: 679–704 is Adomnán's abbacy (title page, `cic/texts/adomnan_life-of-saint-columba_huyshe1905.txt` 87; "Adamnan's abbacy was from 679 to 704", 10426). Huyshe dates the Life: "Adamnan was in Ireland in 692, and again in 697 ... It is thought that he wrote the Life of St. Columba between the two visits" (1995–2000).
