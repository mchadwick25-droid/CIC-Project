# Second Readings: antony-the-great

## 1. the desert became a city of
Source: cic/texts/athanasius_opera-omnia-pg26-grc-lat_migne1857.txt 70548-70549
Second rendering: And the desert was made a city by monks.
Ours: the desert became a city of monks.
Verdict: same
Reason: Same meaning: the desert turned into a city peopled by monks; ours avoids the misleading "colonised".

## 2. For I had heard this of
Source: cic/texts/augustine_confessiones-lat_knoll-csel33.txt 13470-13473, 13501
Second rendering: For I had heard this about Antony. He came in by chance while the Gospel was being read. He took the reading as a warning meant for him, as if the words were spoken to him: "Go, sell everything you have, give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me." By that word from God he turned to you at once.
Ours: For I had heard this of Antony. He had come in by chance during the Gospel reading, and was urged on by it. It was as though the words read were spoken to him. 'Go, sell all that you have, give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven. And come, follow me.' By such a divine word he was at once turned to you.
Verdict: minor
Reason: Ours renders admonitus as "urged on", which suggests being spurred forward; the Latin means warned or prompted. A listener still takes away that the reading moved him at once to turn to God.
