## The test, from one hearing

1. **Who:** Antony, a young Egyptian Christian farmer who became the most famous of the early desert monks.
2. **What happened:** hearing the Gospel's "sell what you have and give to the poor", he gave away his farm, went into the desert, and lived walled up in an old fort for twenty years; when he came out, so many followed him that, in the Life's words, "the desert became a city of monks".
3. **Why the connection matters:** the Life written about him travelled west in Latin; at Trier it turned two court officials to the monastic life, and the story of Antony's Gospel reading moved Augustine to his own conversion in a Milan garden.

All three lines come clear on one hearing.
