Pamphilus wrote the Apology for Origen with Eusebius, who calls it “written by us and Pamphilus”; Jerome, Rufinus's opponent, denies that Pamphilus wrote it. Tauler's sermons were taken down from his preaching by his hearers.

How the count was made

The count used the same 1,209 shelf files and alias lists as the P2-G3 count, with four changes. Indexes, contents pages and XML footnotes were masked. A mention counted only inside the work the tradition's shelf holds. Each mention was read in context by Sonnet checkers, in two passes, and aliases that only ever found other people were dropped. Opus reviewers then reopened every counted passage. Theodoret, Eunomius and Mar Aba I fell out at review: the first two because the traditions that named them began while they were alive, Mar Aba because his passages lay outside the shelved lines. No model was called outside this conversation. The scripts, both passes and both review rounds are in the audit folder Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-10_later-worlds-people/.
