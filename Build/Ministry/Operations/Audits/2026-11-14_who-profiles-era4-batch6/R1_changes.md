# R1 changes: era 4 batch 6

Every round 1 finding is listed with what was done. The drafter reopened each cited line before applying the fix. Briefs were rescored with `engine.m1.gates.grade_text`, cards and records were rebuilt by script, and the audio was rebuilt from each brief with `[calmly] ` at the start and `[warmly] ` at the start of the last paragraph. Every quotation in the briefs and cards was checked again by script against its file, whitespace- and hyphen-tolerant. The only mismatches left are OCR forms, which the records note: "I he sinner Agatbon" across a page break after "And as for me," (SEV l.45965, 46143), "tliings tliat", "wliat I can- not pcrform", "Ghrist" (PO5 l.928, 1013-1017), "l am" (l.8463), "becomc" (l.10419), "1 have" and "ihe Coptic" (SEV l.2136, 2375).
