# Second Readings (batch 9, B9-01 to B9-30)

## Method

The comparer did not do the blind readings. For each id in `Second_Reading_Key.md`:

1. Our wording was checked against the brief (`<slug>/<slug>.brief.txt`, whitespace joined) and against the card entry's `modern` (`<slug>/<slug>.card.json`). Every wording stands once, word for word, in its brief. All but B9-19 equal the `modern` of the entry the key names.
2. Our wording was set beside the blind rendering (`Second_Readings_Blind_A.md`, `_B.md`, `_C.md`).
3. Where they differ, the cited lines in `cic/texts/` and the card's `printed` or `original` were opened. For B9-20 and B9-28, the Greek and Latin behind the English were also opened (below).
4. Verdicts: SAME (same meaning), MINOR (a difference a listener would not take differently), MEANING (a listener would understand something different).

### Spans checked

Each request's word count was compared with the whitespace tokens of the card's `printed` (or `original`). All 29 match, so the counts are tokens of the card's text.

- **B9-06.** The request pointed at the right span. The 15 tokens are 12 Greek words plus three free-standing question marks (" ;", as PG 31 prints them at 72547-72549). The span runs from Τίνα to διάγων ; and equals the card's `printed`. Blind reader A guessed the 15 took in Τὸ δὲ καλὸν. That guess is wrong, and A's rendering covers exactly the span.
- **B9-19.** The request pointed at the wrong span. The key names `quotationOriginals[0]`, the one-word "Unbegotten", which is already "checked at card". So the request asked for "Unbegotten" (1 word), and reader B rendered only that. Our wording is the `modern` of `quotationOriginals[3]` (Whiston, line 38, "if it be demonstrated ... the Unbegotten Substance", 41 words). No blind reading of it exists, so no verdict is given. It needs a fresh blind request for entry [3]. The point a reader would test: our wording gives Whiston's "if it be demonstrated" as a statement, "It has been shown". The card says this follows Basil's Greek (a scan held as a second witness only).
