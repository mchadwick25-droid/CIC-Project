## What carries the weight

- **basil-of-caesarea (LONG):** The 44 facts sit mostly in six beats: Beat 4 (bishop and the official, 6 points), Beat 2 (Pontus and the rules, 6), Beat 6 (the Spirit, 6), Beat 1 (Athens and the turn, three credited accounts, 5); the impact (5 points: Rufinus, Benedict, 787, 381, Sozomen's house).
- **eunomius-of-cyzicus (OVER):** Beat 6 (Theodosius, the 383 creeds and the banishment, 8 points) and Beat 3 (the Apology, 6) carry the most; then Beats 4 and 5 (Basil's answer, his own church and exile, 5 each), Beat 2 (Cyzicus told three ways, 5), and the impact (laws, books, survival, 5).
- **apollinaris-of-laodicea (LONG):** The impact (8 points: the false names, the Jovian confession, Cyril, Justinian) is heaviest; then Beat 6 (condemnation and laws, 4), Beat 1 (expulsion with two reasons, 4), Beat 4 (the teaching, 4), and the hook (3). Eleven witnesses are named, the most of any outline.
- **evagrius-ponticus (OVER):** The impact (10 points: Gennadius, Cassian, Thomas of Marga, Jerome, the councils, Hefele) and Beat 4 (the desert, 8 points) carry it; Beats 3 and 5 (Melania, the book, 5 and 4) and the essence (5) follow.
- **eusebius-of-nicomedia (LONG):** Beat 4 (recall, Eustathius, Athanasius, Tyre, the grain ships, 6) and Beat 1 (5) are heaviest; the impact (5) and Beat 2 (three accounts of one signature plus Q2, 3) follow. Nine credited witnesses.
- **hilary-of-poitiers (OVER):** Quotes alone are 117 words, the heaviest of the batch. Beat 6 (return to Gaul, Saturninus, Lucifer, Martin, 7 points), the impact (6) and the essence (6) carry the facts; Beat 7 (Milan, 3 points) is the clearest cut.
- **cyril-of-jerusalem (OVER):** The impact (8 points: the note, Jerome, Theodoret, John's custom, Egeria) and Beat 4 (the quarrel and first removal, 6) lead; Beats 1, 2, 5 and 6 carry 5 each. Nine glosses (72 words) are the heaviest per story after Epiphanius.
- **epiphanius-of-salamis (OVER):** The impact (11 points: Augustine, Photius, Theophilus, John, 787, the Caroline reply, the tomb, Jerome) and Beat 7 (Constantinople and the voyage, 7) carry it; then Beats 4 and 6 (5 each). It has eleven glosses (88 words) and three quotes (99 words), and runs about 133 words past the next story.
- **damasus-of-rome (OVER):** Beat 1 (the election in three accounts, 9 points) and the impact (9, including the Luciferian petition) are heaviest; then Beat 5 (court and law, 6), Beats 3, 4 and 6 (5 each), and quote 2 (the law, 58 words, the longest single quote in the batch).
