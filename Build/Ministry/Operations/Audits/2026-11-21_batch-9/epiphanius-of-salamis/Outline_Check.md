# Outline check: Epiphanius of Salamis (`epiphanius-of-salamis`)

## My own heart, written before reading `Outline.md`

From the census entry, the slate's job line, the source sheet and what he is known for.

**Essence.** A monk trained among the ascetics of Palestine and Egypt, chosen bishop of Salamis in Cyprus, who wrote the Panarion, a "medicine chest" setting a cure beside each of eighty heresies; the book became the list later writers on heresy followed (Augustine says he followed its order). In old age he took the fight against Origen's teaching to Jerusalem and then to Constantinople, where he refused to pray with John Chrysostom. He is on the tree as the church's great cataloguer of heresy and as a leading accuser of Origen in the quarrel that ended in John's fall.

**Also told.**
- What the book holds and how he made it: eighty heresies as wild beasts with antidotes; what he learned by study, hearsay and his own eyes; copying opponents' own texts whole (Ptolemy to Flora); his youthful brush with a sect and the eighty names he gave the bishops.
- His charges against Origen, in his own letter (souls once angels, the devil restored, the resurrection of the flesh, Eden allegorised; the rivers he drank from), and what he defended (the flesh's resurrection, the literal paradise, the "ancient faith").
- His force, told plainly: the seized and gagged ordination of Paulinian; the torn curtain at Anablatha; the decree in Cyprus against Origen's books at Theophilus's request; ordaining outside his see at Constantinople and refusing John.
- The record's conflict, each side credited: Jerome's account of the mocking in Jerusalem; Socrates' "simple mind" and Theophilus using him vs Sozomen's long aversion; the Long Brothers' "have you read our works?"; the contested denunciation in the Church of the Apostles.
- What came of him: the Panarion as the template (Augustine, Photius); the Origen campaign and John's fall (followed, not caused by him in any source); both sides of the image quarrel claiming him at 787 and in the Caroline Books; remembered as holy (Sozomen, Jerome).
- Ending: death at sea on the voyage home, with the hearsay parting words.

Confidence: book and its preface Documented (own words); Jerusalem Documented as Jerome's partisan account; Cyprus/Constantinople Documented as the historians' reports, with the denunciation Contested; parting words hearsay.
