### B1. BLOCKING: the second book is dropped, and Gennadius's translation is put on the Praktikos.
Also told (Outline line 14) holds a second book that set verses of Scripture against each of the eight, as Christ answered his tempter. Dropping it moves a detail onto the wrong work: the story says Gennadius "put that book into Latin himself", and "that book" can only be the Praktikos. Gennadius translated the *Suggestions against the eight principal sins*, the Scripture book: "This work I have, under instructions, translated into Latin" (npnf203 : 42330-42337).
**Change:** after "since the mind is made for prayer." add: "A second book set verses of Scripture against each of the eight, as Christ answered his tempter." In the Gennadius sentence, "put that book into Latin himself" becomes "put that second book into Latin himself". **Do not say (add to the outline):** "Gennadius translated the Praktikos" or "that book" pointing at the Praktikos. Say: Gennadius put the second book, the one against the eight from Scripture, into Latin.

### B2. BLOCKING: the bread is put on a three-month ration.
"Palladius says he lived on a pound of bread and a pint of oil in three months." Palladius: "he used to eat a pound of bread, and in three months a pint of oil" (clarke1918 : 459 §10). The three months belong to the oil only; the sentence as heard gives him a pound of bread a season.
**Change:** "Palladius says he ate a pound of bread, and a pint of oil lasted him three months." (same length)
**Do not say (add to the outline):** "a pound of bread and a pint of oil in three months". Say: "a pound of bread, and a pint of oil in three months" (the period is the oil's only).

### B3. BLOCKING: "That oath made Evagrius Ponticus a monk."
The oath sent him out of the city (clarke1918 : 457 §6-7). In Jerusalem he slipped back into his old ways, and it was his promise to Melania "that you will keep to the mark of the monastic life" that made him a monk (457 §8-9). The slate's job names this: "brought to the monk's life by Melania". The essence sentence gives the oath a cause the record gives to Melania, and the story then contradicts itself two paragraphs later.
**Change:** "That oath made Evagrius Ponticus a monk of the Egyptian desert, where he named eight tempting thoughts that trouble a monk and taught that prayer is the mind's own work." becomes "That oath set Evagrius Ponticus on the road to the Egyptian desert. There he became a monk, named eight tempting thoughts that trouble a monk, and taught that prayer is the mind's own work." (+4 words)
**Do not say (add to the outline):** "the oath made him a monk". Say: the oath sent him from the city; his promise to Melania bound him to the monk's life.
