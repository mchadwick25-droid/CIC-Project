- **What happened:** he wrote rules for monks living together, built a great lodging for the poor, the sick and lepers, stood up to the emperor's official, and defended the Holy Spirit's place beside the Father and the Son. He died just before a council confessed it in 381.
- **Why it matters:** his pattern of monks living together to serve one another reached the West. Rufinus put the Rule into Latin, Benedict pointed his monks to it, and a council of 787 told double monasteries to keep it.
