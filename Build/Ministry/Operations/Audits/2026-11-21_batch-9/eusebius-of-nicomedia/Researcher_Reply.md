**Essence.** When Alexander put Arius out, Eusebius, bishop of Nicomedia, the emperor's city, took up his cause. After a short exile following Nicaea, he led the bishops who drove Arius's opponents from their churches, and he died bishop of Constantinople. Nearly all we know of him comes from his enemies. Documented.

**Also told.** His letter: the Son was not made from the Father's being (Documented). Nicaea, told three ways, his own plea last (Contested). Constantine's fury, the exile, the return and the campaign against Athanasius. Jerome naming him as Constantine's baptizer (Widely Accepted). The party "those around Eusebius" outliving him. Ulfila (Dominant Modern Reconstruction).

**Shape.** Founder or leader. It opens on Arius's letter, then has seven beats in order and ends on his death.

**Length.** About 3:50, about 575 words. It must carry 187 words: quotes 67, glosses about 45, credited witnesses about 75. Narration takes about 390.

**Connection.** A party his enemies named after him. Socrates says his allies took up "all his authority".
