**Essence:** Cyril taught the people preparing for baptism through Lent, in the great church beside Golgotha. He went through the creed line by line, then after Easter explained the rites. The lectures survive because hearers took them down, an old manuscript note says. As bishop he was driven out three times in the quarrels over Arius, and Jerusalem's way became a pattern a Western pilgrim wrote home about.

**Also told:** what the lectures held; the Easter-week rites, whose authorship is disputed (some copies name John); his election told by three voices; Acacius, the famine sale, the appeal; 381 and the 382 letter; the lectures cited by Jerome and Theodoret; John's "our custom"; Egeria.
