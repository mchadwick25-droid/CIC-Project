## Findings

**1. BLOCKING. The Egeria quote now reads as Lent teaching.**

Once the order-of-teaching sentence went to the card, nothing places the applause. The quote follows straight after "forty days of public teaching was their custom. Egeria, that pilgrim, described it". A listener hears the applause as Lent teaching, but Egeria's line is from Easter week, in the tomb chapel (EG 5908–5926). The outline's Do not say list names this exact error.

Change:
- From: "Egeria, that pilgrim, described it for the women at home. "The bishop talks"
- To: "Egeria, that pilgrim, described it for the women at home. Of Easter week in the tomb chapel she wrote: "The bishop talks"

This adds 8 words. To pay for them, cut "by modern reckoning," from "Around 348, by modern reckoning," (3 words; the Do not say list allows "around 348" alone) and "that pilgrim," (2 words), for a net of +3, about 735 words. This restores only a setting clause for the quote, not the order of teaching that Mark moved to the card. Because it touches his length ruling, it should go to him with this note.

**Do not say line to add to the outline:** "Egeria's applause quote with no setting after the forty days → introduce it as Easter week, in the tomb chapel (EG 5908–5926)".

**2. MINOR. The hook repeats itself.**

"and they answer with a shout. He answers it:" is heard almost word for word in the quote that follows. Optional change: "Cyril of Jerusalem asks his hearers. They shout, and he answers:" This is word-neutral or shorter.

**3. MINOR. Jerome's charge can be heard the wrong way.**

"to win the see from the followers of Arius" can be heard as winning it against them. Jerome says the see was promised to him by Acacius and the Arians (JC 45505–45512). Change to: "to be given the see by the followers of Arius". This costs no extra words.
