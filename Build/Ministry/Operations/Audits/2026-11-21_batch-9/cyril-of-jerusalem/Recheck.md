## Worded by me
- **Beat 4, "first churchman".** Held at SS 9440–9443 ("the first and indeed only clergyman who ventured to break through ecclesiastical usage, by becoming an appellant, in the way commonly done in the secular courts"). Credited to Socrates, in his voice. It is still cut-first item 5 and on the card list. Holds.
- **Do not say, the two MINOR 2 lines.** SS 9434–9437 ("owing to fear, he had not made his appearance during two whole years, after having been repeatedly summoned") and SS 9437–9440 ("appeal to a higher jurisdiction: and to this appeal the emperor Constantius gave his sanction") hold both.
- **Ending 2, the conditional.** TJ 41502–41505 ("often expelled ... and at last received, held the episcopate for eight consecutive years, in the reign of Theodosius") and SS 15642–15646 (died about this time; editor's note 386) hold it. Keeping the death in beat 6 for Ending 1, and taking it out for Ending 2, means the death is told once either way.
- **Metropolitan gloss.** SS 34706–34709 says only that Cyril claimed metropolitan rights. G 726–727 is the editor's "the Metropolitan Acacius". Marked as the editor's. Holds.
- **Length line, beat count.** Six beats; the line now carries the independent count. Holds.
