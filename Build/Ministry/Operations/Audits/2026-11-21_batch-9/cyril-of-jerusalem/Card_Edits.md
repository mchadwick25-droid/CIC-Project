## The two lines Mark ruled onto the card

1. **Theodoret quoting the fourth lecture.** Already on the card: `legacy.traces[1]`, "Theodoret of Cyrus, in his Dialogues, cites the fourth lecture as a testimony of Cyril, bishop of Jerusalem." Held at TJ 24428-24429 ("Testimony of Cyril ... From his fourth catechetical oration"). No edit.
2. **Egeria's order of teaching.** New `legacy.traces[2]`. Old: none. New: the chair in the great church with the candidates sitting around and their fathers and mothers standing by, the Scriptures from Genesis through the forty days, the creed after five weeks, the Easter-week teaching in the cave of the Anastasis with the doors shut, and that she never names the bishop. Evidence: EG 5816-5858 (chair, fathers and mothers, Genesis, five weeks), EG 5908-5926 (Easter week, the cave of the Anastasis, "the doors are shut").
