You count, for each of nine outlines, the words its story must carry. You did not write them and you change nothing in them. Read Part 3 ("Length") of `Build/Ministry/Features/Church-Family-Tree/Brief-Kit/Kit.md` first.

The outlines: `Build/Ministry/Operations/Audits/2026-11-21_batch-9/<slug>/Outline.md` for basil-of-caesarea, eunomius-of-cyzicus, apollinaris-of-laodicea, evagrius-ponticus, eusebius-of-nicomedia, hilary-of-poitiers, cyril-of-jerusalem, epiphanius-of-salamis, damasus-of-rome. Read nothing else.

For each, count from the outline itself, not from its own stated count:
- **Quotes:** the words of each modern rendering it plans to use.
- **Glosses:** each entry under Words to explain, at about 8 words each where it is worked into a sentence.
- **Witnesses:** each witness the story must credit by name or role (Who tells it, and every "credit" or "say so" in the beats and Do not say), at about 6 words each.
- **Narration:** every distinct fact the hook, essence, beats, what came of them, connection and ending must tell, at about 11 words each.

Total at 150 words a minute. The kit aims at about 450 words (3:00) and allows up to about 675 (4:30) where the sources and the content call for it. Verdict: AIM (480 or under), LONG (481 to 675), OVER (above 675).
