# Card edits: Eunomius of Cyzicus (`eunomius-of-cyzicus`)

Existing card `Card.existing.json` (equal to the census card) kept word for word apart from the two corrections below. Written to `eunomius-of-cyzicus.card.json`. Checked with `tools/validate-census.mjs` on a scratch copy of the census with the card and brief set in. The only errors are the ones the release step clears: no `secondReading` on the three quotations of six words or more (left out as instructed), and the brief's two long modern quotations not yet listed in `tools/quote_renderings.json`. Every cited wording stands in its lines. Files are under `cic/texts/`; `npnf202` = `npnf202_socrates-sozomen-ecclesiastical-histories.xml`, `npnf203` = `npnf203_theodoret-jerome-gennadius-rufinus.xml`.

## Clearly wrong (two)

1. `legacy.traces[2]`: "much of the church would have gone over" now reads "the greater part of the church would have gone over". Evidence: Sozomen 6.26, `npnf202` 39172-39177 ("the greater part of the Catholic Church would have been subverted"); the brief ("the greater part of the church"); the outline's Do not say.
2. `uncertain[8]`: "No source in the Library gives the year of his birth or of his death." now reads "No ancient source in the Library gives the year of his birth or of his death. Freese, the translator of Photius, gives 393 for his death." Evidence: `photius_library-v1_freese1920.txt` 1988 ("Eunomius (d. 393)"). The outline's Do not say bars any year in the story; the card now says where the one year comes from.
