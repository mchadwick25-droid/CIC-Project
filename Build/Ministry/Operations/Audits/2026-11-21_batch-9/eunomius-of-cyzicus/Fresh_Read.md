## 1. The test, from one hearing

- **Who:** a fourth-century teacher from Cappadocia, Aetius's pupil, briefly bishop of Cyzicus, whose followers took his name.
- **What happened:** he taught that "unbegotten" is what God is, so the begotten Son cannot share the Father's being. Basil and then Gregory of Nyssa answered him. He lost Cyzicus, was exiled twice under Valens, and was banished under Theodosius after the creeds of 383. After his death his body was moved and his books ordered destroyed.
- **Why it matters:** his argument forced Basil to say exactly what the word does and does not name: God's being is unbegotten, but "unbegotten" is not his being. A later historian thought most of the church would have followed him but for Basil and Gregory.
