# Outline check: Eunomius of Cyzicus (`eunomius-of-cyzicus`)

## My own account, written before reading the outline

From the census entry (`eunomius-of-cyzicus`, `anomoean-eunomian-christianity`), the slate job line and the source sheet.

**The essence.** Eunomius, Aetius's pupil and secretary, argued in his Apology that "unbegotten" is not just a word about God but God's very being, so the Son, begotten by the Father's will, cannot share that being and is unlike him in substance (own words, Documented). The claim drew the answers of Basil and then Gregory of Nyssa, who worked out against him that "unbegotten" names how the Father is, not what God is; that is why he is on the tree: the sharpest form of the non-Nicene case, and the foil the Nicene answer was sharpened against (I1-I3; the "what it changed" part is Dominant Modern Reconstruction, Sozomen's "would have been subverted" his own judgment).

**Also told.**
- Pupil and secretary of Aetius; deacon under Eudoxius; with Aetius, leader of the "unlike" party in 360 (Documented, Philostorgius and Socrates).
- Briefly bishop of Cyzicus, the date contested (Constantius or Valens), and his departure told in more than one account (Contested).
- What the Apology says: God simple, without parts, the unbegotten being; generation would mean division; the Son made by will, "when he was not before", like the Father in act, not being; "consubstantial" refused; what he defended, the "Monarchy of God" (own words, Documented).
- The break with Eudoxius over Aetius, a church of his own named for him, exiles under Valens (Documented / Widely Accepted, one witness for the exiles).
- 383: his creed read and rejected by Theodosius; banished for house meetings (Socrates's reason); exile, death soon after, burial at Dacora, later moved to Tyana and his books ordered destroyed (Philostorgius, one witness).
- What came of him: the answers he drew (Basil, Gregory of Nyssa, Nazianzen, five counted by Jerome); his followers condemned by name in 381 and his books ordered burned by law in 398; the sect left no church; the Apology survives, the later reply only in Gregory's quotations.
- Who tells it: his own Apology, his admirer Philostorgius (only in Photius's hostile summary), and his opponents, each credited.

## Quotes, my own renderings first

- **Quote 1** (PG 29 99678-99681, read with Whiston 38). Mine: "So if it has been shown that neither he himself nor anything else existed before him, but that he is before all things, then 'unbegotten' follows. Or rather, he himself is unbegotten being." The outline's rendering matches, except "goes with him" for *akolouthei* (finding 10). Its "he himself" for the neuter *auto* follows Whiston and Basil's own paraphrase at 101273-101274, so it stands.
- **Quote 2** (PG 29 101276-101277; the second OCR at 102840-102841 reads "Ἐγὼ"). Mine: "I myself too would say that God's being is unbegotten, but not that the unbegotten is his being." The outline's rendering matches. It supplies "I would not say", which it marks.
- **Quote 3** (Walford 328). The words are verbatim. Its use is finding 3.

## BLOCKING

1. **Beat 2 softens the force at Cyzicus.** "In place of a bishop who had been put out" hides who put him out. Socrates: "an imperial edict was published in which it was ordered that Eleusius should be ejected, and Eunomius installed in his place" (`npnf202...xml` 12456-12458). Sozomen: the citizens "did not choose to have another bishop", and Eunomius "expelled Eleusius, for he was furnished with an imperial edict to that effect, and took possession of the churches himself" (37854-37861). This is the fourth fault, the record's coercion left out.
   **Correction.** Replace Beat 2's first sentence with: "The bishop of Constantinople made him bishop of Cyzicus. He came with an imperial edict, which put out the city's bishop, whom its people wanted to keep, and he took over the churches (C1; Socrates 12456-12458, Sozomen 37854-37861). That bishop's people built a church outside the walls (C1)." Add this to Do not say: "'He replaced a bishop who had left' or 'had been put out' with no agent → 'an imperial edict put out the bishop and installed him' (C1)." Cut-first item 2 still drops only the church outside the walls; the edict stays.

2. **"At a time when he was not" adds a claim the source does not make.** Whiston 52 has "produc'd when he was not before his own proper Production, but that he was begotten before all things, by the Will of God and the Father". The Greek at PG 29 108494-108495 is "μὴ ὄντα ... τὸν Υἱὸν γεγεννῆσθαι" ("the Son was begotten, not being"). Neither says "a time". A listener would hear Arius's "there was a time when he was not", which the Apology does not say.
   **Correction.** In Also told (line 7) and Beat 3, replace "at a time when he was not" with: "he did not exist before he was begotten, and he was begotten before all things, by the Father's will (A7; Whiston 52, 58)." Add this to Do not say: "'There was a time when the Son was not' or 'at a time when he was not' → 'he did not exist before he was begotten; he was begotten before all things' (A7)."

## MINOR

3. **Quote 3 retells the live `philostorgius` sentence the same way.** That story says: "He rated Aetius higher in argument, and Eunomius higher for 'a style well suited to learners.'" Beat 1 gives the same comparison with the same words. The slate lifts the quote for the friendly witness's view, and its reuse ruling bars the same event told the same way.
   **Correction.** In Beat 1, replace "who rated Aetius higher in argument but Eunomius higher for clear teaching and Quote 3" with: "who praised him as a teacher with a clear way of explaining, Quote 3 (T6)." Leave the comparison with Aetius out, and add it to Leave out: "Philostorgius's ranking of Aetius above Eunomius in argument (T6): told on `philostorgius`."

4. **Beat 1 is out of order.** Gregory's farmer father, the plough, the shorthand and the orator (E1) come before Aetius in Gregory's account (3364-3387). Beat 1 tells them after Alexandria.
   **Correction.** Reorder Beat 1 to E1, then S4 and E2 with T6, then E3, then E4 and E5. No wording changes.

5. **The year 360 has no cited line.** Philostorgius 230 (E5) names the council at Constantinople but gives no year.
   **Correction.** In Setting and Beat 1, cite the census `anomoean-eunomian-christianity` `relationsSummary` ("The break began at Constantinople in 360"), marked Widely Accepted (the tree's tradition entry).

6. **The Library sweep misses the creed of 383.** It is listed as not held, but it is held in quotation. The NPNF editor says his "ektheois tes tisteos, presented to the emperor Theodosius in the year 383, is also extant" (`npnf205...txt` 3076-3080). Gregory's Book II in the NPNF numbering (8727-) quotes "the wording of their doctrine": "We believe in the one and only true God ... not honouring Him with a lying title" (9025-9029). No held line names Book II as the reply to the creed of 383.
   **Correction.** In Also told (line 10), replace "The creed of 383 is not held." with: "The creed of 383 is held only in quotation: Gregory of Nyssa quotes and answers a Eunomian creed in Against Eunomius Book II, NPNF numbering (npnf205 8727-; its opening at 9025-9029), and the NPNF editor says the creed given to Theodosius in 383 is extant (3076-3080). No held line ties Book II to 383 by name. The story does not quote it; it goes to the card." Make the same change on the source sheet: move "the creed of 383" from Not held to Used/Set aside with these lines, and keep its quotes with Gregory (the slate bars the Gregories' quotes).

7. **Impact item 3 leaves out the force of the law of 398.** The law also makes hiding such a book a capital crime: "capite esse plectendum" (`codex-theodosianus_latinlibrary.txt` 7130). "Montanists" is unglossed.
   **Correction.** Replace the sentence with: "A law of 398 ordered the books of Eunomian clergy sought out and burned before the judges, and made hiding one a crime punished by death (I5)." Drop "and of the Montanists", which leaves nothing to gloss, or else add "Montanists" to Words to explain with "another banned church party".

8. **A dropped qualifier.** Sozomen says "the greater part of the Catholic Church" (39174-39175), not "much".
   **Correction.** In impact item 1, replace "much of the church" with "the greater part of the church". Add this to Do not say: "'much' or 'some' of the church → 'the greater part', as Sozomen judged it (I2)."

9. **Photius's "for years" is hearsay.** Photius says "It is said that while he was in labour with this work he spent several Olympiads" (Photius 11996-12001).
   **Correction.** In Beat 6, replace "a reply to Basil he had worked on for years" with "a reply to Basil that, Photius had heard, took him years". Or drop "for years".

10. **Two smaller fixes.**
    - **Quote 1's verb.** "Then 'unbegotten' goes with him" weakens *akolouthei* ("follows"). Basil's objection turns on that verb (B1; PG 29 99701, "ἑπόμενον αὐτῷ τὸ ἀγέννητον"). Replace it with "Then 'unbegotten' follows."
    - **The Do not say line on "a creature".** Its reason is incomplete. Eunomius himself says the Son was "made" and "created by the Power of the Unbegotten God", "the most perfect of the subordinate Beings" (Whiston 58, 102). Replace the line with: "'A creature like any other' or 'a mere man' → he called the Son made and created, but above everything else that was made, and its maker (Whiston 58, 102); 'a mere man' is an editor's gloss (Zenos, npnf202 13038-13040)."
