## Verdict: REVISE

## Findings

**1. BLOCKING. The law's penalties are left out.**

The outline's connection says the listener must understand that the law "attached the state's penalties to that faith". The brief says only "Everyone else, it said, would bear the shame of heretical teaching." The law goes on to threaten God's punishment first and then the emperors' (CTH 83606–83607). A listener hears a label, not a threat, and that softens the record's force.

Change:
- From: "Everyone else, it said, would bear the shame of heretical teaching."
- To: "Everyone else, it said, would bear the shame of heretical teaching, and the punishment of God and then of the emperors."

This adds 10 words. To pay for them, cut "The office had been won with blood." from the hook (7 words), which also mends the repeat in finding 2, and "The empire's officers ended it, not any council." (8 words; this is the editor's reading, and the next sentence already shows it). That is a net of −5, about 669 words.

**Do not say line to add to the outline:** "'everyone else would bear the shame' alone → give the law's threat too: God's punishment, then the emperors' (CTH 83603–83607)".

**2. MINOR. The hook and the essence repeat each other.**

"The office had been won with blood." is followed at once by "He won the office in a fight that left many dead." This is fixed by the cut in finding 1.

**3. MINOR. The court sentence is long and nested.**

"An emperor ordered that any bishop who held on to his church after Damasus and a council of five or seven bishops condemned him be brought to Rome by the governors." This is 31 words and nested. Change it to: "An emperor ruled on bishops whom Damasus and a council of five or seven had condemned. Any who held on to his church was to be brought to Rome by the governors." This costs no extra words. If room allows, joining the one-sentence paragraphs from "In the 370s" to the court into one paragraph, linked by time, would lift the flat middle.

**4. MINOR. "At its sharpest" is too abstract to follow on one hearing.**

"his years show the church at its sharpest, when emperors took sides in its quarrels" is unclear. The outline's own reason is plainer: the state's officers settled his election, and then a law used his name to sort Christians from heretics. Optional change: "He is on the tree because in his years emperors took sides in the church's quarrels, and used his name to do it." This is about word-neutral.

**5. MINOR. Two outline items went without being on the Cut first list.**

The Antioch challenge (THD 15486–15506) and the Eastern bishops' "the thrice blessed Damasus deposed" (THD 37893–37895) were dropped, although the list says to keep the second. With no room left, they are better carried by the card than restored. The card checker should confirm that both are on it.
