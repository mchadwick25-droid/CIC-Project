# Outline check: Apollinaris of Laodicea (`apollinaris-of-laodicea`)

Checker, batch 9, 2026-11-21.

## My account of the heart (written before reading Outline.md)

**Essence.** Apollinaris, a scholar and bishop of Laodicea in Syria and a firm ally of Athanasius and of Nicaea, tried to answer how Christ could be one, and taught that in Christ the divine Word took the place of a human mind. The church condemned the teaching (at Rome under Damasus, and at Constantinople in 381), his followers formed churches of their own, and his writings, kept alive under orthodox names, carried the formula "one nature of God the Word made flesh" to Cyril of Alexandria and into the later quarrels over Christ. He is on the tree as the great Nicene teacher whose answer about Christ was rejected, and whose words shaped the next two centuries anyway.

**Also told.** Twice put out of the church at Laodicea (the hymn to Dionysus; then by George, for Athanasius's friendship or the sophist's, the historians differ). With his father, recast Scripture in classical forms when Julian barred Christians from the classics (who wrote what is contested). Taught Jerome at Antioch. His songs sung at work and at the loom, which drew followers; Vitalius at Antioch; separate churches under their own bishops. Epiphanius, his old admirer, at first would not believe it. Gregory of Nazianzus's reply ("what is not assumed is not healed": the sibling's text, a clause credited to Gregory). Condemned at Rome and at Constantinople 381; imperial laws against his followers. Died under Theodosius, year unknown. Afterward: followers drifting back into the church carrying the one-nature teaching; the forgeries exposed in the sixth century; Vincent's warning of the great teacher who fell.
