2026-11-16 | Round 1 review, era 10 batch 2b, the periodicals

## Scope

All 45 files in `files.txt` (31 numbers of The Christian East, 14 volumes of The International Review of Missions), their 93 shelf rows in `cic/corpus-map/_staging/`, their REGISTRY entries and seed rows. For each file: the 12-line header, the masthead or title leaf, every cited line, the stated end of the text, both ends of every row, and a scan for church leaders' documents and for host marks, copyright and permission lines. Line numbers below are OCR lines (file line = OCR line + 12). The byte count of the text after the header equals the count in the Source line for all 45 files, and one file (Christian East X.3) was fetched again and is identical by checksum. No file carries a host mark, copyright line or rights field; the archive.org records of two items were read and have no rights field. Page images were read for 19 numbers of The Christian East and three volumes of the Review (table below). After the file review, `Search_Log.md`, the CFT-Wants section "Era 10, batch 2b, the periodicals" and Open-Gaps 300 were checked against the files. Their counts agree with the files: 45 files, 31 + 14, 4,325,430 words (761,254 + 3,564,176), 1,757,789 words for 1914-1920, 37 single-document rows on The Christian East. The two holds were tested and both stand. The Proceedings of the Church Missionary Society are held under Mark's ruling R15 on the film's own notice ("© MM 1967 / Reproduction of this film prohibited"). The Christian East XI.4 prints "Winter, 1930-31" and cites a letter of March 1931, so it was printed in 1931. As a foreign work of 1931 it is not free until January 2027. No file that was taken should have been held. The latest date in any file taken is 1930 (Christian East X.4 has appeal leaves of 1930, and the Review for 1930 has Quarterly Notes of October 1930), and each print's own date is 1930 or earlier.
