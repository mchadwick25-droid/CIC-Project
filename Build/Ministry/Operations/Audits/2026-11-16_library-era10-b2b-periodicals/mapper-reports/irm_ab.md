# irm_ab: International Review of Missions, vols 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19 (1921, 1924-27, 1929, 1930)

Method note: the files have no contents table, so articles were mapped by the "By ..." by-lines and the heading above each. A heading line is given as the first line of the title. Items 5 to 9 are mostly the same for all seven files and are grouped in the common notes at the end. Where a per-file detail differs it is stated in the section. I also wrote one stray scratch file (work/by27.txt) in violation of the brief; it contains only grep output.

Common to all seven files:
- Every file opens with a volume title leaf, then the January masthead. No advertisement leaf, library slip or microfilm target found.
- Publisher lines: "THE INTERNATIONAL REVIEW OF MISSIONS / EDINBURGH HOUSE, EATON GATE, SLOANE SQUARE, LONDON, S.W.1"; "HUMPHREY MILFORD, OXFORD UNIVERSITY PRESS / LONDON, TORONTO, MELBOURNE AND BOMBAY"; "COMMITTEE OF REFERENCE AND COUNSEL / 25 MADISON AVENUE, NEW YORK CITY" (419 FOURTH AVENUE in 1929 and 1930); printer "Morrison & Gibb Limited" (OCR garbles it as Gisp, Grips, Gras, Giss, Gibb).
- Each number ends with a "PRINTED BY MORRISON AND GIBB" line. From 1924 a "Quarterly Notes, being the Bulletin of the International Missionary Council" is bound at the end of each number. Each number also has Reviews of Books, Shorter Notices, Notes on Contributors and Missionary Bibliography, in that order.
- Item 5, voices: the journal speaks in surveys, the Quarterly Notes and unsigned notices. Named writers speak in the articles. Schlunk's 1921 article was "written in German and the translation has been revised by the author" (1921 line 8891 block).
- Item 6, OCR: body text is clean. Samples from the middle and the three-quarter point read cleanly in all seven files. Garbled: running heads, German, Dutch and French bibliography entries, margin headings, and the digit 3 read as 8 (see item 8).
- Item 9, host marks: no "digitized", "google", "archive.org" or "microfilm" hit in any file. "Permission" and "reproduce" occur only in ordinary prose (counts: 1921 16, 1924 14, 1925 20, 1926 10, 1927 14, 1929 7, 1930 17). 1929 line 7223 "no copyright restrictions" and line 30504 "deprived of all rights in the land" are ordinary prose too.
