# Mapper report ce_ab: The Christian East, vols. V-VI (8 numbers, Feb 1924 - Jan 1926)

All lines were checked by `grep -n` or `sed -n`. Where I did not pin an end line, I say "ends before the next heading" or "end not mapped". No file shows a publisher or printer line. The only masthead lines are title, volume, number and month/year. Hits for "Ltd" and "Press" are text mentions only. Quotes keep the OCR as it stands. Item 9 (host marks and rights lines) found nothing in any file unless stated. Item 8 (later years) found nothing unless stated.

Short names below: DL files are `per_christian-east_the-christian-east_<date>_<vol>_<no>__..._djvu.txt`.
