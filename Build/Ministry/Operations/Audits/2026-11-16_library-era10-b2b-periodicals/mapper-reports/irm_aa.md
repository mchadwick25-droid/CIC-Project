# irm_aa: International Review of Missions, vols 3-9 (1914-1920)

General (all seven): masthead lines are the same in form; no month or number is printed on the title leaf. Each file opens with the volume title leaf (lines ~1-50), then the January number. Each quarterly number opens with a bare "THE / INTERNATIONAL / REVIEW OF MISSIONS" leaf followed at once by the first article (no contents table in the body; the volume index sits at the end). Each number ends with a printer line. Editor J. H. Oldham in all. Articles in 1914-15 carry no "By" line at the head; authors come from "Notes on Contributors" (order-matched where I say "per notes order"; this is my matching, not printed). Where the print gives "By X", that is quoted. Mixed voices: unless noted, the journal's contributors speak in their own names; translations are noted in the Notes ("written in French/German/Dutch ... translation revised"). OCR: columns clean, running heads and page numbers intrude, bibliography blocks have OCR noise, non-Latin scripts garbled; years "1913" often read as "1918" (same for 1913 read 1918 in vols 1914-17; the "later year" hits below are mostly this).
Rights/host grep: no hit for digitized/google/archive.org/microfilm/copyright/all rights in any of the seven files. "reproduc" hits are ordinary text (counts: 1914 9 hits, first lines 16037, 22647, 23289; 1915 9; 1916 19; 1917 5; 1918 13; 1919 5; 1920 5).
