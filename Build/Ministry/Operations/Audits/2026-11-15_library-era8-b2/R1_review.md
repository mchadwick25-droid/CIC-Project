## Verdict

The batch does not pass round 1. There is one blocking finding (F1): the Campbell reprint's publication date and filename were taken from an OCR misreading ("1906"), and the header argues the correct catalog date of 1908 away. Seven findings are substantial (F2-F8). Each one misstates what the file says: the Todd copying direction, Gibbons's signed dedication, Dutton's tract count, the Gleaner play ranges and dates, McGready's narrative title, and Vieira parte 12 "printed in his lifetime". Seventeen findings are minor (F9-F25). No finding concerns rights: every file has a printed date of 1930 or earlier that its header reads correctly, apart from the misread year in F1, which still falls before 1930. The 25 findings touch 20 files; 17 files have no findings. Round 2 should recheck only F1-F25.
