## Trims

Four briefs ran over after the fixes and were trimmed to length. Removed from briefs and kept on cards: Bardaisan's admission that he once followed the astrologers, his dialogues against the Marcionites, and the disciple's authorship of the dialogue; Tatian's travels and Ephrem's commentary; Dionysius's exile under Valerian. Removed: two short beats from Dionysius's rescue scene, and a repeated Zephyrinus line from Hippolytus.
