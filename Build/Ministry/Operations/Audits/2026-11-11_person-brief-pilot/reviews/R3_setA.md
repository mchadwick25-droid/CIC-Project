## Specific watch items
- **John's own Eutropius words (N9 17699-17724).** This is a fair paraphrase.
  - "Spoke to soften the people's hearts toward him" matches "a desire to soften your minds towards him, and to induce you to compassion" (17699-17701).
  - "The church, like a mother, had hidden her enemy under her cloak" matches "having received her enemy as a captive, she spares him ... like an affectionate mother has concealed him under her cloak" (17721-17724).
  - Joining the two with "for" compresses §3 and §4 of the sermon, but it is faithful to the sermon's argument.
  - The scene now gives both Socrates's reading (N2 17360-17362) and John's own. C1 is resolved.
- **Cyril, "followed, especially".** The source reads "we ever follow the opinions of the holy Fathers, specially of our blessed and all-celebrated Father Athanasius" (Cy 5392-5394).
  - "Especially" sits outside the quotation marks and is the modern form of "specially", so it is acceptable.
  - "Especially" keeps the sense that Athanasius stands among other Fathers, and the card's "among the Fathers" makes this explicit.
  - The peace context is supported at Cy 5100-5104.
- **"Sixty years after his death" (brief) and "Honored sixty years after his death" (card).** These rest on May 373 (accepted in R2) and on 433 at npnf203 4410-4413 ("In 433 formal peace was made ... John of Antioch and Cyril"). 433 minus 373 is 60. Supported.
- **"A family tomb" for Socrates's "an ancestral tomb" (N2 13061-13062).** This is fair. "Ancestral" means belonging to one's forebears, which is what "family" means here. It also avoids Robertson's narrower "his Father's tomb" (N4 7096-7097). A2 is resolved.
