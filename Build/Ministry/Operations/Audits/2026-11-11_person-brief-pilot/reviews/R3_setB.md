## Gregory of Nyssa: REVISE (card, one line)

Brief: all changes check (NZ 46254-46258; NY 880-883; SC 26747 and 43991 for the canons; NY 942). 300 words, in band.

Card: legacy_traces (NJ 20803-20806; SC 23134-23137 lists "Gregory of Nyssa"; SC 26747, 43991) and legacy_remembered (NJ 15091-15096, 36815-36818; NJ 41712-41714) check.

1. **BLOCKING (minor): the Theosebeia line misstates the evidence.** The card says the tradition "seems to rest on a letter of Gregory of Nazianzus". The file says it "rests mainly on two passages", the second being Gregory's own On Virginity, where he complains he is "cut off by a kind of gulf from this glory of virginity", and that "the two passages above, when combined, may have led to the tradition" (NY 609-645). Leaving out Gregory's own passage makes a contested question look one-sided. Fix: replace "It seems to rest on a letter of Gregory of Nazianzus that calls a Theosebeia a priest's yokefellow, and none of Gregory's own writings speaks of her as his wife." with "It seems to rest on two passages: a letter of Gregory of Nazianzus that calls a Theosebeia a priest's yokefellow, and Gregory's own complaint in On Virginity that he is cut off from virginity. None of his writings speaks of her as his wife."
