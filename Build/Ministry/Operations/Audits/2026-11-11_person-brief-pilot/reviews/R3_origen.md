## Changed claims, audio

1. Levels of Scripture (paragraph 3). Supported by A4 32386-32403. Body = "that common and historical sense" for "the more simple individuals"; soul for those who "have commenced to make considerable progress"; "those ... who are perfect ... edified by the spiritual law itself"; "as man is said to consist of body, and soul, and spirit, so also does sacred Scripture".
