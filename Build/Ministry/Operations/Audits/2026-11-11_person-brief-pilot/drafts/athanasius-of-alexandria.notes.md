## Contested points and how marked
- Arsenius story: told as Socrates tells it, with Sozomen's mention that "it is said" Athanasius had concealed him; the council still deposed him (Sozomen). Neither side is presented as proven.
- Chalice and Arsenius charges are given as what "his enemies charged".
- Trier exile reason is "Socrates says"; Socrates also reports (N2 5368-5371) "some affirm that the emperor ... [wanted] unity ... since Athanasius was inexorable in his refusal to hold any communion with Arius". That was cut for length.
- Age at election: "about thirty" is Robertson's inference; his opponents said he was under the canonical age (N4 1514-1517).

## Dropped / not used
- Nicene "of one being": the file prints "One in essence with the Father"; the brief follows the file.
- Life of Antony "desert" theme: used only Athanasius's own statement that he attended Antony. No claim that Antony shaped his theology.
- Rufinus's story of the boy playing bishop (N2 4002-4017; N4 1583-1600): told by Rufinus and Socrates; Robertson marks it "with at least a strong note of interrogation". Left out.
- Dates of the second to fifth exiles and causes (Constantius's raid in 356, Julian, Valens): sourced in Robertson (N4 5900-7110) but not told, for length.
