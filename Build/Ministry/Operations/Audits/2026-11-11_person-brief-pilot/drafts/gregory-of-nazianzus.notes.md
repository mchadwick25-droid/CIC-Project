## The quote
File NZ, Letter CI (Ep. CI), To Cledonius the Priest Against Apollinarius, lines 44690-44693 (line breaks joined with spaces).
Printed: "For that which He has not assumed He has not healed; but that which is united to His Godhead is also saved."
The file has a non-breaking space after "salvation." before "For"; the brief uses ordinary spacing. No trimming.

## Contested or uncertain points and how marked
- Whether Gregory ever governed Sasima: told as "Browne and Swallow think he probably never governed the place" (their own "it is probable"). The Letter to Basil (NZ:45786-45806) shows Gregory himself writing "your Sasima", and I did not resolve it.
- Why the Egyptian bishops objected (a canon against translations of bishops): omitted for length; the brief says only that they objected.
- The date of Sasima: not stated in the brief (Clarke's Life of Macrina in another file says 372; the Prolegomena gives no year in the passage I read).

## Dropped (coordinator checklist)
- The Letter to Cledonius quote is used; nothing dropped from the checklist except: "presiding ... and resigning" is kept; "Anastasia" kept. Not told: the Maximus episode, Jerome hearing him (NZ:23143), the Arian assassin, farewell oration lines, and the Arianzus retirement and death date (NZ:23187 and NZ:43353 give the retreat to Arianzus; the death year is not given in the same form in those passages, so I left it out).
- "Unwilling ordinations" is told through the priesthood and Sasima only.
