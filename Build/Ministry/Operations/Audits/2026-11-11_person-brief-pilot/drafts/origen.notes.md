## Contested points and how marked
- Scope of 553 condemnation: marked "how far it went is still argued" (the brief names no witness there to save words; sources above).
- Doctrines drawn from On First Principles: marked by saying we read it mostly in Rufinus's Latin and Jerome accused Rufinus of altering it. The devil's repentance is given as Epiphanius's charge, not as Origen's settled teaching.
- Eusebius is the source for the boyhood and Decius stories; the brief says "Eusebius says" at each.
