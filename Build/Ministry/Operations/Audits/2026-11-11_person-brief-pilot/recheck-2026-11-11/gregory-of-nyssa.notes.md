## The quote
File: NY (npnf205), Great Catechism, chapter XXI, lines 44059-44061 (line breaks joined with spaces).
Printed: "alteration is a kind of movement ever advancing from the present state to another; and there are two forms of this movement; the one being ever towards what is good, and in this the advance has no check, because no goal of the course to be traversed [1995] can be reached"
Used in the brief: "the advance has no check" — a contiguous substring ending at a comma (clause boundary); no ellipsis added, and I did not use the "no goal ... can be reached" half because a footnote marker [1995] sits inside it.

## Contested point and how marked
Restoration of all: the volume's editors read him as teaching it; they themselves cite sermons that speak of eternal fire. Told as "Moore and Wilson read him as ... though they cite sermons ... that hint at no such ending." Not settled in the brief. Also: Moore and Wilson are the only witnesses for the reading, and the Great Catechism line is mine, not theirs.

## Dropped (coordinator checklist)

- "Endless reaching toward God" as a formula: used only through the quote; no separate claim about the doctrine's name.
- Peter/Basil forgery story, Theosebeia marriage (the editors reject it), pilgrimage letter: omitted for space.
- Gregory's own text of Against Eunomius: not searched for a quotable line; the Eunomius claims rest on the editors' summary.
- The Trinity: only through the Eunomius point; no separate claim.
- Death date: dropped (Clarke says about 395, the volume's last record is a synod in 394).
