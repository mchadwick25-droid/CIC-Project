## Contested / partisan points and how marked
- Palladius is named as John's defender at each use (accounts reform, journey, death). Socrates is named as the cooler witness, and I used his sharper judgments of John (stern, irritable, speech offensive).
- Socrates and Palladius differ slightly on the place of death (Socrates: "in Comana"; Palladius: at a shrine five or six miles outside the town). The brief says "near Comana".
- The Oak: told as Socrates tells it (deposed for refusing the summons). Not presented as a verdict on whether the charges were true.
- The "cooler witness" wording is my characterization from Socrates's balanced tone (e.g. N2 17243-17248, 18612-18625 where he faults John's teaching on repentance); flag if you want it neutral.

## Dropped from coordinator's checklist
- "Bishop's preaching on wealth": covered by the Homily 50 quote and Palladius's finance reform. No separate claim that he preached against wealth in general beyond what the quote shows. (Palladius's note at M 3737ff, quoting Puech that he is "the panegyrist par excellence of almsgiving", is a modern editor's remark and was not used.)
- Cucusus: not told. Palladius's contents list (M ~704-706) shows arrival at Cucusus in autumn 404 and removal to Pityus in June 407; I did not read the body to confirm, so skipped.
- Sozomen is mentioned by the coordinator as a witness; I used him in the Athanasius brief, not here (I did not read Sozomen Book VIII on John).
- Socrates's irony that the bishops used a canon framed against Athanasius to depose John (N2 18476-18487) is sourced and tempting; cut for length.
- "Calvin and the Lutherans quoted him": not in any file checked; not used.
