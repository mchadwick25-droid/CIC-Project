# Who scan, era 4 (2026-11-11)

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-11). Read-only scan of era 4 (622–1054 CE) of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Counts, 157 rows (a paired row counts once): **WHO 46, STORY 9, LISTED 59, WANTED 43**. Judgment-call rows: 86. Two further rows (Adam of Bremen, Nestor the chronicler) are carried to the era 5 scan by the date of their work. Councils are not people and carry no verdict (see Councils below). Of the two live person entries, Bede is WHO and Heraclius is STORY, so Heraclius is flagged (Mark: "Story, keep live"). The Library is thin for this era: of the 43 WANTED rows, most are people whose named source is not held at all.

## How the list was built

- Era 4 has 27 census entries: 25 movements and 2 person entries (Bede of Jarrow, Heraclius). Four of the movements (Medieval Irish, Medieval Welsh, Church of the East under the Abbasids, Post-Aksumite Ethiopian) name nobody in their voices; their shapers come from their sources and the corpus-map rows.
- Named voices and `people` lists of the movements, merged by alias.
- Carried from era 3 (13 rows, re-scored): Muirchu, Adomnán, Jonas of Bobbio, Aidan, Colmán, Cogitosus, Braulio, Ildefonsus, John of Nikiu, Maximus the Confessor, Sophronius, Antony of Choziba, al-Baladhuri. Moved here by Mark's era 3 addendum (era 4 by date): Mennas (Benjamin I's brother), al-Tabari, Ibn Ishaq. Also Tirechan.
- Found in text (census longDescription, legacy and sources): Theodore of Tarsus, Willibrord, Nennius, Beuno, Winefride, Prudentius of Troyes, Nithard, Sebeos, Thomas of Marga, five Coptic patriarchs, and others marked "found in text".
- Not scored, not named in any census text but held in the Library: Wilfrid, Cuthbert of Lindisfarne, Cedd, Chad, Fursey, Elipandus of Toledo, Felix of Urgell, the Lismore saints (a supplement scan after all eras; see "Mark's answers and the coordinator's calls").

## Rulings applied (Mark, 2026-11-11)

- The standard of eras 1 to 3: WHO is church-shaping with a body of work or a following and enough life to tell; recorders are WHO with a thin own-life flagged; women are WHO if they founded, taught or led; rulers are STORY unless they have their own voice; founders known through enemies need two witnesses for the teaching and may rest the life on one; OUTSIDE WITNESS is a WHO label for objective historians outside the church.
- Pairing (Mark, 2026-11-11): "if two people are tied together in their sources with similar identity its ok to make that a single who". Paired rows are flagged as judgment calls.
- The checker's rule on WANTED (coordinator's routine call): the named source must be able to carry a life, not only writing. Five WANTED rows fell to LISTED on it.
- "Major?" column (proposal only; coordinator, 2026-11-11): the map will show up to 4 names per tradition and 5 per river per era, chosen from the major Whos. The column proposes yes, no or judgment call for each WHO, with one reason. Mark confirms it in each batch.
