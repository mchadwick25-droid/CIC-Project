# Who scan, era 1 (2026-11-11)

Approved to proceed (Mark, 2026-11-11). Read-only scan of era 1 of the Church Family Tree under Mark's Who standard. The census, the site and the records were not changed by this scan.

Final counts, 64 people: **WHO 19, STORY 8, LISTED 33, WANTED 4**. Judgment calls: 14.

## The four categories

- **WHO:** shaped the church; left a body of work (their own, recorded by hearers or disciples, or produced with their circle) or a following; the record stands on its own and carries enough life to tell a story, not only theological argument.
- **STORY:** a compelling testimony, a life or witness worth telling in its own right.
- **LISTED:** named in their tradition's sources conversation, with no profile and no story.
- **WANTED:** would be a WHO if the Library held one named source; the source is named.

## Rulings applied (Mark, 2026-11-11)

- Two independent witnesses for a founder known only through enemies; the brief marks polemic as polemic and names whose it is. Epiphanius drawing on Irenaeus is one witness. Valentinus: WHO. Marcion: WHO. Mani: WANTED. Callistus: LISTED.
- Perpetua: STORY (a first-person testimony). Methodius of Olympus: WHO, a named exception (his live brief tells a story and he shaped the debate over Origen).
- Paul of Samosata: WANTED. Alexander of Jerusalem, Cornelius of Rome, Lucian of Antioch (details Contested) and Gregory Thaumaturgus: STORY.

## Addendum (2026-11-11, added with the era 2 scan): outside witnesses and a recorder
