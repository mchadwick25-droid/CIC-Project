## Systematic findings

1. Five "not held" claims were wrong: the Persian martyr acts, the Muratorian Canon, Priscilla's oracles, Cornelius's own letters, and Gregory of Nyssa's Life of Gregory Thaumaturgus.
2. Credit sometimes went to the wrong source: Socrates, not Eusebius (Acesius); Palladius (Potamiaena's master); Epiphanius and Michael the Syrian (Bardaisan's life).
3. Namesake traps for the briefs: Dionysius of Rome (anf07) vs Dionysius of Alexandria; the Loofs 1905 file is Nestoriana, not the 1924 Paul of Samosata fragments; the anf08 Decretals are forgeries; the Ptolemy in Justin's Second Apology is not the Valentinian; the anf08 Theodotus is the Valentinian, not the cobbler.
