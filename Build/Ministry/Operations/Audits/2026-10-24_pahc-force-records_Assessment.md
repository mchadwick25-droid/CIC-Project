# House Churches force records: assessment of the "world's own experience" prose, 2026-10-24

Independent Opus assessment (read only, no model calls) of nine `records/pahc/force/` records. They carry the same unsourced "WORLD'S OWN EXPERIENCE" pattern that the 19 October claim check found in `pahc.force.neronian-persecution`, which was fixed on 2026-10-23. The `description` field is an evidence-head field (`engine/m1/spoken_fields.py:131-133`): it reaches the voice as grounding and is the citation card's fallback label.

Path keys: A1 = `cic/texts/anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-irenaeus.xml`; A2 = `cic/texts/anf02_hermas-tatian-athenagoras-theophilus-clement-alexandria.xml`; A7 = `cic/texts/anf07_lactantius-apostolic-constitutions-didache-liturgies.xml`; E = `cic/texts/npnf201_eusebius-church-history-life-of-constantine.xml`. Line numbers are raw lines in those files. The fixing session spot-checked A1:7085-7087, A1:4222-4225, E:24716-24717 and A2:1013 verbatim.

The common fix: name the voice (Ignatius, 1 Clement, the Martyrdom of Polycarp) in place of "the world", add the missing loci, and cut felt-state sentences no source supports or mark them as the record's inference.

## transmission-network (Documented; its divergence_note rates the selection mechanisms Inferential-Thin)
- Copied and carried onward at a sister church's request: supported. Polycarp, Phil 13, A1:3616 "we have sent to you, as you requested"; Mart. Pol. 20, A1:4263-4267 (ch. 20 is likely a later addition). Keep.
- Kept because it answered a dispute: supported elsewhere. Dionysius of Corinth in Eusebius HE 4.23, E:25115-25117 "have read your epistle. From it, whenever we read it, we shall always be able to draw advice, as also from the former epistle, which was written to us through Clement". Re-cite to `pahc.source.eusebius-historia-ecclesiastica`.
- Copying decided "community by community", not by a central institution: unsourced, and partly contradicted. Hermas, Vis. 2.4.3, A2:1013 "Clemens will send his to foreign countries, for permission has been granted to him". Reframe as the record's inference (Inferential-Thin) and note Hermas.

## alexandria-emergence (Documented)
- The absence statement is consistent with the corpus. Keep. Optional: note that `pahc.source.barnabas` is "often argued Alexandrian".

## authority-consolidation (Contested)
- Obedience to one bishop "like strings tuned together": supported as Ignatius's program, A1:4828-4831 "fitted as exactly to the bishop as the strings are to the harp". Attribute it to Ignatius.
- A presbyter council as the ordinary shape elsewhere: supported. A2:1026 "the presbyters who preside over the Church"; A1:1935-1936 (1 Clem 44); A1:3392 (Polycarp, Phil 5) "being subject to the presbyters". Add Phil 5.
- "with no felt lack from the absence of any single presiding figure": unsourced felt state. Reframe as "its letters name no single presiding figure and register no want of one".
- Polycarp addressed as bishop but writing as one with the presbyters: supported. A1:8535; A1:3232-3233. Add loci.

## roman-mediterranean-world (Widely Accepted)
- A letter read across cities without translation: inference from the shared Koine; the vendored texts are English. Reframe as the record's inference.
- Roads and harbours there for travellers: supported for Ignatius's own journey. A1:7085-7087 "both by land and sea… being bound to ten leopards"; A1:8855; A1:3610. Narrow it and cite these.
- A sister's letter "felt like proof of belonging to something larger": unsourced felt state. Cut it, or re-cite to Phld 10, A1:7859-7861 (churches sent a deacon "that he may rejoice along with them"), and say what they did.

## monepiscopacy-consolidation (Contested)
- The honest absence statement stays.
- "this world's own texts… record… only the gradual, regionally uneven disappearance of the plural-college alternative": misattributed. The gradual disappearance is a modern reconstruction (Lampe). The nearest in-window witness is Irenaeus to Victor in Eusebius HE 5.24.14, E:31528-31529 "the presbyters before Soter, who presided over the church which thou now rulest". Reframe, and consider citing it.

## systematic-theological-mode-elsewhere (Dominant Modern Reconstruction)
- "no awareness of a fully secured, systematised succession account": contestable. Hegesippus in Eusebius HE 4.22, E:24646-24647 and E:24716-24717 "In every succession, and in every city"; 1 Clem 44, A1:1928-1929. Limit the claim to Ignatius, 1 Clement, Hermas, Polycarp and the Didache, and name Hegesippus as an in-window precursor.
- "felt experience… still-urgent question": that the question was argued is supported (A1:1937-1938); "felt" and "urgent" are not. Reframe as "the core texts argue the question; they do not assume it settled".

## martyrdom-meaning (Contested)
- "for the one awaiting his own arrest": factual error. Ignatius writes already under guard, A1:7087 "being bound to ten leopards, I mean a band of soldiers". Correct it.
- Longing to be consumed rather than spared: supported for Ignatius (A1:7037-7039, A1:7042). Attribute it to him by name.
- Truest imitation of Christ: supported (A1:7044; A1:7175-7176). Keep; add Rom 6.
- The community "gathered annually" at the burial site: overstated. Mart. Pol. 18 states an intention, A1:4220-4225 "as opportunity is allowed us… the Lord shall grant us to celebrate the anniversary". Reframe: Polycarp's own church at Smyrna gathered his bones and planned to keep the anniversary there as they could.
- Carrying his example forward: supported (A1:4228-4229). Keep.

## two-ways-catechetical-inheritance (Dominant Modern Reconstruction)
- Taught two ways before baptism: supported (A7:12700-12702; A7:12914). Keep.
- A live, continuing choice: supported. Add Didache 6:1 and 16:2 (A7:12889; A7:13143).
- "not a nature settled once and for all": the record's own contrast. Reframe as the record's reading.
- "inherited as a tool": the independent witness Barnabas 18 is supported; the inheritance (a pre-Christian origin) is a modern reconstruction. Attribute it.

## boundary-drawing (Inferential-Thin)
- A rival teaching denying Christ's real suffering, argued against letter after letter: supported for Ignatius (A1:6612-6619; A1:8098; A1:4971). Attribute to Ignatius; add Eph 7 and Smyrn 4.
- Refusing a rival eucharist and keeping the one eucharist were the same act: in Smyrn 7 the docetists abstain from the eucharist (A1:8220-8221). They are not said to hold a rival one. Reframe: Ignatius treats keeping the one eucharist under the bishop as the boundary itself (A1:8278; A1:7497-7498).
- "not experienced as two different decisions" "for a member": unsourced member experience. Cut it, or mark it as the record's inference.
