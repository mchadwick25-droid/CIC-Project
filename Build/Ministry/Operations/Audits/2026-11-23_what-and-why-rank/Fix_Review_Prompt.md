# Fix review (Opus)

You review a fix writer's revisions of Church Family Tree pieces against Mark's rules in `Fix_Prompt.md` (same folder): read it first. You see the original text, the findings, and the writer's reply.

For each piece, check:

1. **Every finding is resolved**: the what or why is now said, or the mention is gone, or the modern writer is out of the text.
2. **Nothing is invented.** Open every source the writer cites at the lines given and confirm it says what the new text says. A claim with no source, or a source that does not hold it, is BLOCKING.
3. **No new fault**: no new bare statement, no modern scholar or "historians" in the text, no guess told as story, no quote that is not word for word in its file, no fact lost that was right before.
4. **It still reads well aloud**: same voice, short sentences, nothing past about 25 words, American spelling, no AI tells, length within about 15 percent.
5. **Modern works** listed are works the piece or its sources actually named.

A finding that is only "could be fuller" is not a problem.

The "Heart" line in the packet is a reader's working note, not text on the site: never review it and never block on it. A documented story's or map story's title and teaser are on the site: if a cut leaves one repeating the removed claim, that is BLOCKING, and the writer may give a revised title or teaser.

Write one file at the path you are given:

```
## <piece key>
Verdict: PASS / REVISE
- <BLOCKING or MINOR>: <what is wrong, with the file:lines you checked>
```

PASS means the revised text can go live as written. REVISE lists only what must change.
