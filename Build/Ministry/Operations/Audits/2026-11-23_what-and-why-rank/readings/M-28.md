## tree/john-wesley
Heart: Wesley, an Anglican priest, felt his heart warmed at Aldersgate in 1738, took to preaching outdoors from 1739, and gathered converts into societies and weekly classes that became the Methodist churches.

## tree/polycarp-of-smyrna
Heart: Polycarp, the old bishop of Smyrna, refused to curse Christ and was put to death, a man whom Irenaeus linked back to those who had seen Jesus and who kept communion with Rome despite the Easter quarrel.

## tree/methodius-of-olympus

## tree/constantine-the-great
Heart: Constantine, after the vision before the battle of 312, ended the persecution and made himself the church's patron, calling the council at Nicaea, and was baptized only when dying.

## tree/basil-of-caesarea
Heart: Basil gave up teaching rhetoric for a life of prayer and shared work, wrote rules for monks, built a lodging for the poor and sick, and as bishop defended the Holy Spirit's place beside the Father and the Son.

## tree/gregory-of-nazianzus
