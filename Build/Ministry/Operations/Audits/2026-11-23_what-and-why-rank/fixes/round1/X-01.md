## tree/the-romanian-greek-catholic-church-suppression-and-return
### Revised text
Decree 358 of 1 December 1948 dissolved the Romanian Greek Catholic Church. Its property passed to the state, and most of its church buildings came into Orthodox use. For forty-one years it had no legal existence. On 31 December 1989 Decree-Law 9 restored it. Our sources do not say why the state acted either time. On 14 March 1990 John Paul II named Alexandru Todea metropolitan of Făgăraș and Alba Iulia.

## worlds-tree/syriac-edessa-nisibis/legacy
### Revised text
The woven Gospel of these churches did not last forever. In the 400s, bishops moved against it. Rabbula of Edessa ordered every church to read the Distinct Gospel, meaning the separate Gospels. His order, as quoted, gives no reason. Theodoret of Cyrrhus, writing against heresies, said the harmony cut out the passages showing that Jesus was born of David's line. He took more than two hundred copies from his own churches and put the four Gospels in their place. Its Syriac text did not survive. It is known today only through quotations and translations into other languages.
