alternatives presented were adopt-without-the-ethical-lens and
no-standardization).

1. **Smart's seven dimensions as the fixed spine that is always
   *asked***, with per-world yield stated as a finding — a thin
   dimension is a result, not a coverage failure (the same discipline
   Doc_04 applies to candidates not advanced).

   additions, named as additions.

   contradiction without either side losing; the lens synthesis reaches
   the Representative through world-side records (`field_relations`,
   `contested_claim`, `voice_profile` inputs) per Pass 1's Article-3
   firewall reading.

Smart's names or take CiC's own — decided per document in S6.1's
