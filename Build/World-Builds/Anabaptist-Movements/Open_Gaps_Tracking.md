## OG-4: Schleitheim: the missing pages, the German witnesses and the header note (2026-10-23)

**Source:** Registry rows 22, 40, 24, 23.
**Status:** Open. Binding on Doc_03 (use of the Confession). Owner: Library thread.

McGlothlin's file lacks printed pages 6 and 7 (file lines 628 to 646). It holds the last paragraph of the Sixth Article (lines 647 to 654) but not the Sixth's heading or its statement on the sword. Jackson's Zwingli (row 40) has the same closing argument. The Sixth is in full in Jackson's Zwingli and in Beck's shortened German. Beck's text omits the Fourth Article's clause on weapons; the English and Böhmer's garbled German have it. The McGlothlin file header (lines 18 to 20) says the original German articles "were never printed and survive only in archival manuscript". The corpus-map note says only that the German "survives only in archival manuscript". McGlothlin's own text (lines 490 to 491) says "they were never printed". Böhmer's introduction contradicts all three. **Request:** correct the header and the corpus-map note; a page-image read of the Fraktur articles in Böhmer's section IV (from line 1805) would give the first full German text.
