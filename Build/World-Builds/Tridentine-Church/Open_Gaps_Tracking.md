## OG-20: The Catechism edition and its rights basis (2026-10-23)

**Source:** `cic/texts/council-of-trent_roman-catechism_mchugh-callan1923.txt` (title page lines 72 and 73, imprimatur line 107, imprint line 110, header lines 5 to 8); rows 10 and 11.
