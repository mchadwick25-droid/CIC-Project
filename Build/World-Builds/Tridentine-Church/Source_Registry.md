# Source Registry — The Tridentine Church

Per `Source_Registry_Template.md` V1.0. Co-equal Step 2 output with `Doc_02_Source_Ecology.md`. **Living document: append-only.** A row is never renumbered or deleted. Doc_02 may not name a source in support of a specific claim unless that source has a row here. Every work in `cic/corpus-map/the-tridentine-church.yaml`, every dossier §1 work, §2 cross-link and §3 lead, every work the census names, and every work the field-bibliography sweep surfaced has a row. A work assigned only to another world has no row.

**Status:** DRAFT, Revision 2, awaiting Round 2 targeted recheck. The saturation statement at the end is not closed.
**Boundary checked against:** `Doc_01_World_Identification_Boundaries_Orientation.md` (Revision 3, Approved to proceed): the window 1517–1650; Trent, Rome and Milan; a whole-church world; strand-singular for the corporate-magisterial material, with Bellarmine's devotional voice a live second candidate for Doc_04. Boundary Status is judged by what a source speaks for, never by the publication date of a modern edition.

**Type:** P (Primary) / S (Secondary) / M (Material/External) / L (Period Lexicon).
**Confidence (citation reliability):** A = the Licensed-For passages were read and matched in the vendored file by a structure marker (heading, running head, session or chapter, or the edition's own title). For an Excluded row, A means the passage that makes it a Named Comparandum or an Out-of-Boundary source was read and matched. B = a specific work or locus is named and exists in the vendored file, but the Licensed-For content was not itself re-read. C = a real author and work, no locus checked, not vendored. D = a body or genre of material, no single text named, or a source that was sought and not found. No row is E.
**Structure markers:** line numbers are lines of the vendored file in `cic/texts/`. The Waterworth and Catechism files, and most others, are double-spaced inside the OCR, so a quotation of one sentence can span three or four file lines.
**Scan-quality note:** every vendored file is OCR text. Quotations in this Registry and in Doc_02 were matched against the files after whitespace and line-break-hyphen normalisation, and after reading long s (ſ) as s. No other letter of any quotation was corrected. Where the OCR itself prints f for s (the Bullarium, the Collectanea, Pius V, the Acta and others), the quotation keeps the scan's f. The Latin headers say wording is to be checked against a page image before it is quoted; no page image was available, so every Latin quotation is verified against the scan text only. An inner double quotation mark inside a quotation is shown as a single mark.
**Rights:** each vendored row states the rights line of the file's own header. A work that is not vendored states the basis as far as a source in the build gave it, or says that rights were not checked. The Library must verify rights before any acquisition.
**Added columns:** Quotability (verbatim-ready, second witness only, unusable), Voice (own voice, opponent voice, narrator, editor, or a mix), Holdings disposition (used, used limited, deferred with a reason, out of scope with a reason, for acquisition), and Language and edition (primary and cross-check). A row with no Licensed-For content does not carry an A.
