# DEPLOYMENT LEXICON CHUNK TEMPLATE
## Church in Conversation — V7
### Version 1.0

**File naming convention:** [world-code]lex[number]_[term-slug].md  
**Example:** alexlex001_logos.md  
**What this is:** One chunk in the Deployment Lexicon — the construction lexicon
restructured as self-contained retrievable files, one per term, for runtime retrieval.  
**Governed by:** Interpretive Lexicon Development Framework v2.0 Part VI and
Constitution Article 30.

## World Meaning

[HOW THIS TERM FUNCTIONS WITHIN THE WORLD'S OWN ECOLOGY — Level 2 depth in accessible
language. This is what a participant receives when they ask about this term mid-encounter.
It should make the term's function intelligible without requiring the full scholarly
apparatus.

Write this section in accessible language that does not require scholarly background,
but grounded in the world's own ecology. Show how the term works — what it does, what
it connects to, what it makes possible for someone formed in this tradition. Do not
define the term from outside. Show it from inside.

For Tier 1 terms: several paragraphs tracing the term's ecological function in depth.
For Tier 2 terms: one to two paragraphs at reduced depth.
For Tier 3 terms: the Quick Meaning above is sufficient; this section may be brief or
reference the Quick Meaning.

Check before saving: no analytical-distance markers in this section. Not "scholars
believe," not "this term reflects," not "the sources indicate." The World Meaning
should read as if it comes from someone who knows this term from the inside, rendered
accessible for someone who does not.]

## Final Assembly Instruction

When complete:

1. Replace all [BRACKETS] with world-specific content.

2. Remove all builder notes in {curly braces}.

3. Check World Meaning for analytical-distance markers. Remove any of the following
   if they appear: "scholars believe," "this reflects," "we can see," "evidence
   suggests," "the sources indicate," "historically," "reconstruction." Replace with
   language grounded in the world's own ecology.

4. Where the CT tag is applied, confirm that CT Contest Type is completed — not left
   as template. State specifically which type of contest applies and what the contest
   is about.

5. Where Reported-Experience Status applies, confirm the verbatim marker text is
   present exactly as stated above.

6. Save as [world-code]lex[number]_[term-slug].md
