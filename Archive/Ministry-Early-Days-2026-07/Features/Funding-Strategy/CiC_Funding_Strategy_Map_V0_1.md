## The foundation — the core system everything else sits on

The 100+-world database (in progress), the Atlas, per-world Representatives (fidelity-locked —
each world stays only itself), the four presentation modes (seeker, re-assessing, pastor/teacher,
academic — registers, never access levels), and Living Table multi-Representative conversation.
Nothing above this line is ever gated by payment; every rung of the tier ladder below is built on
top of full access to this foundation, differing only in *time* and in *additional, separately-
built* services.

---

## The access ladder (organization tiers)

Staggered rollout — each rung added only once the one before it shows real signal, not on a fixed
calendar. **Working** direction on the shape; **not yet decided** what "capped" means in an actual
number.

| Rung | What it includes | Rollout phase | Funded by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free / Base** | Full conversational model + Atlas, same rigor, no asterisk — bounded only by time | **Launch** | The keep-it-open gesture (below) |
| **Subscription** | More time/usage headroom + genuinely covers operational and development cost, not just API + a community layer (shared reflection space) | Phase 2 — build once usage data exists, or design early and hold | Subscription revenue |
| **Deeper / Tailored** | More time again + role/function-specific paid features (pastor, academic, homeschool) | Phase 3+, signal-gated | Tailored-tier revenue |
| **Unlimited** | Everything, no caps | Phase 3+, signal-gated | Top-tier revenue |
| **Institutional** *(parallel track, not a rung above Unlimited)* | Multi-seat access for an institution's people, time and features scaled to the group | Runs on its own relationship clock the whole time | Institutional licensing |

## Revenue streams

### Self-serve product revenue (anonymous website visitors)
- **The keep-it-open gesture** — after the experience, not during or before. **Working:** direct
  Stripe on-domain (Patreon and Ko-fi evaluated and set aside — real numbers in Decision-Log).
  **Not yet decided:** final terminology (candidates: "keep the Table open," "hold a seat,"
  "become a Steward" — deliberately not tipping-register language).
- **Subscription / Deeper-tier / Unlimited / Institutional revenue** — the paid rungs of the
  ladder above. **Affirmed shape, not yet built or priced.**

### Directed giving / sponsorship (churches, major donors)
- **Sponsor a world's academic review**, **sponsor making the next world available** (echoes the
  existing $3,500 World Sponsorship figure), **sponsor a world's ongoing academic standing**
  (relational, ongoing — introductions and endorsement-seeking, not just a transaction).
  **Affirmed family**, three distinct nameable asks rather than one vague line.

### Relational / warm capital (Mark's own network and introductions)
- **The Letter to Friends** — a real, already-drafted asset (`Ministry/Communication/
  Church_in_Conversation_Letter_to_Friends.docx`), warm and personal, not a grant pitch. **Source
  material only** — stale on facts (one world, pre-testing, blank cost figures), needs a real
  content refresh and Mark's confirmation before use. Timing not yet decided.
- **Founding-cohort ask** — one-time, non-recurring "help bring this into the world" identity,
  distinct from both the gesture and any subscription. **Parked.**
- **Impact investing / mission-related investment** — a capital category distinct from both
  nonprofit grants (mostly 501(c)(3)-gated) and a friends-and-family round; possible specifically
  because of the PBC/share structure. **Parked**, unexplored beyond naming it.

### Non-monetary capacity (time and expertise, not money)
- **Academic Advisory Circle** — cross-tradition by design. Does three jobs at once: builds
  strategy/connections toward the best scholarly reviews, is a structural safeguard against any
  single institutional relationship looking like undue influence or a singular point of view, and
  is real organizational health (counsel and accountability without full board weight).
  **Affirmed — "I really want to build this."** Not yet designed: who, how convened, compensation.
- **Volunteer/contributor layer** — theology students helping with source research under
  supervision, a structured reader/feedback program. **Affirmed.**

## Features cutting across the ladder

**Tier-linked paid features** (Deeper/Tailored rung): pastor discussion guides and session-hosting
tools; academic citation export and the **comparative-brief tool** (parallel per-tradition
presentation, explicitly not synthesis — full detail in `Business-Plan-Idea-Box.md` §1, **Parked**);
homeschool lesson plans and a simple progress view.

**Access-broadening, cutting across every rung, not paid features:** translation/multi-language
access; sliding-scale/regional pricing on paid rungs; a low-bandwidth/text-first mode; public and
community access points (libraries, community centers).

**Organizational-health support, not revenue:** an **L3B donor-facing extraction** — pulling a
credibility piece from the already-existing, already-standardized `L3B Formation World
Construction Framework` and `Blueprint` docs, not documenting the method fresh (**Affirmed**);
the Colorado PBC's required annual benefit report, repurposed as a public "state of the Table"
letter rather than treated as pure compliance (**Parked**).

## Governing principles (the frame around all of it)

- Rigor and sourcing are never for sale — only time and genuinely new, separately-built services.
- "And," not "vs./or" — false oppositions get dissolved, not chosen between, wherever a real both/and exists.
- Sponsorship confers honor, never influence — and no single institutional relationship gets to
  carry outsized weight or the appearance of one.
- Every pre-existing draft is source material only until reworked and confirmed — a factual patch
  doesn't promote a document to "finished."
- Terminology avoids the tipping register entirely, not just the word "tip."
- Prove each rung with real signal before adding the next.
