## The protected language, for direct use in funding materials

- **Name:** Church in Conversation — no "The."
- **Measurement line (use verbatim, never paraphrase):** *"We measure whether each Christian movement is represented in an engaging and accurate way — never its impact. That's bigger than us."*
- **CTA:** "Come and join us at the Table."
- **Flagship phrase:** "documented Christian movements" (never "real Christian communities").
- **Never-say, top of list:** any promise of "finding the right answers" or transformation — testify, never promise.

## Donor-specific rules already decided (Kit Part 2, register-flexing section)

- **Donor as steward, never hero.** "You fund a door that will never try to sell anyone anything" — not donor-as-hero framing (StoryBrand-style); the Positioning Brief/Kit explicitly rejected that as misaligned with a formation project.
- **The seeker's voice always outranks the donor's** — a written rule (the Alpha precedent). Donor materials should never be louder or more prominent than participant-facing material in tone or claim.
- **Ask tied to the next concrete artifact** ("help us build the next world"), never to organizational need in the abstract — the BibleProject flywheel pattern, already endorsed as the most directly transferable funding mechanic found in research.
- **Low-frequency, high-substance updates** — never industrialized ask-frequency (the Olive Cooke research finding).
- **ECFA/AFP/BBB truthfulness standards are the floor CiC already exceeds** — usable as positioning ("our constitution goes further than the sector's own accreditation standards require"), never as a defensive crutch.
- **Never overhead-ratio or efficiency-percentage marketing** — repudiated by the sector's own watchdogs (GuideStar/Charity Navigator/BBB, 2013); show what the money built instead (the evidence-loop pattern, not the "100% model" framing).
