## Decisions you build on (all Mark-confirmed 2026-07-17; do not re-litigate)

- **Naming:** "Church in Conversation," no "The" — organization and masterbrand on
  everything public. Era series titles as public packaging under it (*Conversations
  with the Early Church* now; Reformation etc. later), always with the masterbrand.
  Short forms: CiC (internal), "the Table" (experiential). → Wordmark work is
  unblocked; encode the masterbrand/series tether in the name-treatment rules.
- **The big idea / test:** an open invitation into conversation with the whole
  church — offer (invitation, conversation) + guard (trust, safety,
  witness-not-recruitment). Invitation leads; the trust answer stands one breath
  behind.
- **Three words:** Transparent · Inviting · Experiential.
- **Voice pairs (Mark's own):** witness but not recruiting · rigorous but not
  distracting · accessible but not shallow · technology but not seen · relational
  but not human.
- **Register:** the BibleProject balance — everyday-English surface, scholarly
  floor (10th-grade accessible floor is already a logged product standard);
  conversational; one notch playful (relational, never academic); modern carrier of
  ancient things; strongly welcoming; personal in warmth with the founder
  backstage. North-star voices: N.T. Wright, Tim Mackie, Justo González —
  scholar-hosts; warmth through shared discovery, not comfort.
- **Primary CTA:** "Come and join us at the Table" (the ask names the value:
  participation). The chair ("a chair pulled out for you") stays as the welcome
  gesture. "Come sit at the Table" retires from CTA use → your gap list.
- **Never-say, top line:** "finding the right answers." Then: promised
  transformation (testified only — "the brand prays for what it refuses to
  promise"), manufactured urgency, public "the only," hype superlatives, "we'll
  reconstruct your faith," engagement-metric boasting, founder spotlight.
- **Audience rules:** address wide, prioritize deep (public address is positive and
  open — the curious, the searching, the rebuilding; internal priority remains the
  unraveling and the stuck). **The seeker voice is never outranked by the donor
  voice** — write this into the message architecture as a standing rule.
- **Founder:** backstage; visible only through his journey (the approved
  deconstruction-to-reconstruction testimony — draft paragraph in the decision log,
  2026-07-17) and God's provision. Project voice is canonical; the Letter stays a
  letter.
- **Mission posture:** stated plainly, never hidden, never pushed. Steer between
  the three failure modes: recruitment tool / entertainment / neutral museum.
