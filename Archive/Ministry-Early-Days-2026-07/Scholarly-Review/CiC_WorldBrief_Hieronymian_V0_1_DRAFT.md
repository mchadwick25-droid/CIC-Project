# CHURCH IN CONVERSATION

## World Brief — Hieronymian Ascetic-Literary Christianity (World #9)

Reviewer's ten-minute orientation · distilled from the world's own build documents (World Profile, approved 2026-07-12) · every claim traces to a build document

# The world in one paragraph

A reconstruction of the ascetic-scholarly household network around Jerome and the aristocratic Roman women — Marcella, Paula, Eustochium, Fabiola — spanning Rome and Bethlehem, c. 382–420 CE, from Jerome's arrival in Rome to the generational cluster of deaths that ended the community's own formation ecology. Its defining character, as its own documents present it: renunciation and scholarship as a single discipline, not two — the correction of a word and the correction of the appetite as the same kind of act — funded by voluntarily divested senatorial wealth and held together by earned reputation and patronage, never by office. The world died with its founders; no successor to its central textual-defense practice is named anywhere in its own record.

# The evidentiary base (Doc_02, Source Ecology) — and the one-sided-preservation problem

This world's evidence is, overwhelmingly, one man's corpus. Jerome's letters, prefaces, commentaries, and polemics are nearly the entire primary record — and Doc_02's central finding is stated without softening: **every account of the four named women's own agency, without exception, survives only in Jerome's hand.** No text composed by Paula, Eustochium, Marcella, or Fabiola exists. Their reported words are Jerome's literary construction, carried at Inferential/Thin confidence as evidence of what they actually said. The scholarship the build leans on (Cain, Cooper, Clark) treats Jerome's portraits of these women as partly propagandistic, self-vindicating constructions — and the build carries that reading at full strength rather than muting it. A further layer: Cain's critical-edition work establishes that the letter collections were curated, in some cases by Jerome himself shaping his own legacy — even the *selection* of what survives is his editorial choice, not a neutral sample.

Two rare exceptions where "the other side" survives: the Augustine correspondence (both authors' own letters) and the Origenist rupture (Rufinus's *Apologia* against Jerome's — equally polemical, in opposite directions). One secondary voice (Palladius) is flagged as citation-unverified and barred from supporting any specific claim until confirmed. No papyri, no administrative records, no securely attributed archaeological remains of the monastery itself — named as a significant gap, not smoothed over. The vivid 384–385 Rome crisis (Blaesilla's death after severe asceticism, the loss of Damasus's protection, Jerome's departure) is Documented as a sequence across multiple letters — but all of them Jerome's own, narrated by the interested party; the claim that a formal synod judged him is held at Inferential/Thin and barred from embellishment.

# What organizes the world (Doc_04, Gravity Discovery — six-test discipline)

Three Primary gravities: ***Hebraica veritas*** (Hebrew textual authority — with an explicitly flagged confidence split: the principle's attestation is Widely Accepted-to-Documented, but its content, including Jerome's actual Hebrew fluency, is Contested per Williams); **ascetic self-impoverishment** (*renuntiatio* — with the corrected admission that Epp. 108, 22, and 77 are three texts by one author in one idealizing genre, not independent attestation; the real corroboration is social-historical scholarship on senatorial renunciation); and ***patrocinium*** (patronage as the operative authority structure — the strongest overall result).

Supporting: letter-writing (*epistula*) — deliberately held below Primary on an evidentiary-circularity flag (the source base is almost entirely letters, so "letters organize the world" partly measures what survived) and on its character as enabling medium rather than independent force; and controversy/doctrinal dispute (Origenist, Pelagian, the Augustine fight) as pressure absorbed by the ecology, not a force generating its own formation practice.

Tensional: **Marcella's independent exegetical authority** — real, ecologically required, and single-source (Ep. 127, written by Jerome after her death, partly for his own vindication): Contested, failing the Primary threshold under the Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check. The review record here is method-discipline evidence in itself: Round 1 caught the draft resolving the strand question with a non-sequitur (a bipolar-geography result manufactured by the document's own decomposition choice), and the fix withdrew that argument at every site it appeared. A named proportionality check confirms the outcome — the Jerome-centric gravity at Primary, the one gravity centering a woman's independent intellect at Tensional — is evidence-generated, not impression-generated, and carries the asymmetry forward rather than declaring it resolved.

Three candidates were considered and not advanced (hospitality practice, Hebrew study as a discrete practice, the monastic daily schedule) — each with a stated reason, each an example of the method declining a claim rather than inflating one.

# One strand — and that is a finding

This world is strand-singular: one authority logic, not two rival ones. What it has instead of strands is one currency — demonstrated scriptural learning, personally verified — held in two structurally different positions: Jerome held it in material dependency, unable to exercise it without a patron's continuous funding; Marcella held the identical currency independently, requiring no patron. The build tested whether Marcella's authority constituted a genuinely distinct basis (which would have fractured the strand-singular finding) and concluded, on Contested single-source evidence read closely, that it does not — while refusing to let that conclusion erase her from the ecology. The world's own Integrative Observation names this entanglement-versus-independence pattern as the single structural fact refracted through every analytical lens.

# What the participant actually sees

Ten always-present Tier 1 terms (*Hebraica veritas*, *Vulgata*, *renuntiatio*, *virginitas*, *vidua*, *patrocinium*, *epistula*, Origenism, exegesis-as-practiced-authority, *praefatio*) from a fifteen-term lexicon, each chunk carrying its own distortion-risk note (e.g., moderns hear "translate from the original" as obvious method; this world heard it as a destabilizing claim against the church's received text). Twelve tier-classified stories — six Tier 1 (the journey to Bethlehem, the Rome crisis, the Oea "ivy/gourd" incident, the Rufinus rupture, the 416 mob attack, Marcella's death in the 410 sack), four Tier 3 formation-ideal accounts explicitly not usable as biographical fact (Paula's epitaph-narrative, Marcella's exegetical standing, the Ciceronian dream, the desert romances), two Tier 4 marked reconstructions, zero invented narrative (audited).

The Representative is **Albina** — a deliberate construction decision, escalated to and decided by the project lead: not Jerome (the option carrying the greatest risk of re-collapsing the world into a Jerome biography), not Marcella or Paula (the ecology's most evidentially fragile and most temporally truncated figures), but a period-typical, non-documented *vidua* — an ascetic widow of the household, the status category Paula, Marcella, and Fabiola shared — with no invented biography beyond the role. She speaks a composite "we" spanning the world's whole bounded life, keeping documented disagreement visible ("some among us held one view, some another") rather than smoothing it. One disclosed residual: Albina is also, in the sources, the name of Marcella's mother; the construction explicitly disclaims any identification, and the project lead selected the name with that risk disclosed, not overlooked.

# The honest limits the world itself declares (Profile Section 8; Doc_09a Absent Stories)

The women's own unmediated voice — structurally absent, and the Representative says so: "we will not pretend to give you their voice unmediated, because we cannot."

The unnamed monastic multitudes, household dependents, and enslaved persons whose lives their patrons' renunciation reorganized: no surviving source individuates any of them, and no story is manufactured for them — six absent stories are named explicitly, including family resistance never told in the resisters' own words and Jerome's Jewish teachers never given independent voice.

Jerome's Hebrew fluency: the labor is real; its completeness is Contested, and the Representative is instructed not to claim more than the record bears.

The daily liturgical schedule at Bethlehem: the fact of a discipline is attested, its timetable is not, and it is not supplied.

No living tradition: two counter-candidates (the Hieronymite orders, Jerome's Doctor-of-the-Church veneration) were considered and rejected; the community's own institutional life does not survive its founders.

# Where a reviewer might poke first

Is the build's handling of the co-constitution problem — women recoverable only through Jerome's pen — actually carried into every layer, or does the vivid material (the epitaphs, the deathbed scenes) still function as biography in practice?

Is the Marcella call right? Her authority classified Tensional on single-source, Contested evidence — is that honest calibration, or does the Cross-Check under-weight the strongest attested case of independent female scriptural authority in the period?

Does the choice of Albina — a typical, non-documented widow — solve the Jerome-centering problem or merely relocate it, given that everything the composite voice knows still comes through Jerome's corpus?

Are the deliberate withholdings defensible — the demotion of letter-writing on the circularity flag, the three declined gravity candidates, the refusal of a Tier 2 layer this world's evidence doesn't have?

And the live test: talk to the Representative. Ask it what it cannot know. See whether it answers like a witness or like a chatbot.
