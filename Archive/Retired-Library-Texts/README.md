# Retired Library texts

Texts that were vendored into `cic/texts/` and are retired from it. Nothing here is read by the program.

## augustine_epistulae-praefatio-indices-lat_goldbacher-csel58.txt

Alois Goldbacher, ed., Augustine's *Epistulae*, Pars V (praefatio and Indices I-VI), CSEL 58, Vienna and Leipzig, 1923. It holds no letter text.

The scan behind the file (Internet Archive item `corpusscriptorum58auguuoft`) is a facsimile printed by the Johnson Reprint Corporation in 1961. Its own title page says "Reprinted with the permission of the original publishers" and "First reprinting, 1961". The file's header records the rights basis as the 1923 publication date, which does not cover the 1961 reprint. The Library excludes the 1962 Johnson Reprint facsimile of CSEL 33 on the same ground. No other public scan of CSEL 58 exists on the Internet Archive, so the volume is not vendored.

`augustine_epistulae-praefatio-indices-lat_goldbacher-csel58.yaml` is the corpus-map staging file that assigned the text to the Latin pastoral and congregational shelf.

## van-braght_martyrs-mirror_sohm1886.txt

Thieleman J. van Braght, *The Bloody Theater, or Martyrs Mirror*, in Joseph F. Sohm's English. Retired on 2026-11-16 (Open-Gaps 307, "Library, the Martyrs' Mirror swap").

The scan behind the file (Internet Archive item `MartyrsMirror`) is a Herald Press printing. The verso of its title leaf prints "Second English edition, thirty-third printing, 2012" and "Copyright © 1938 by Mennonite Publishing House" (the file's lines 119 to 124), and the volume opens with a "Publisher's Preface to the Second English Edition". The file's header records the rights basis as the 1886 publication date, which does not cover this printing. Its text is reset and differs from the 1886 print in spelling and capitals.

The Library now holds the Elkhart printing of 1886 as `cic/texts/van-braght_martyrs-mirror_sohm-elkhart1886.txt` (Internet Archive item `bloodytheatreorm00brag`, the Library of Congress copy). The nine census sources and the shelf rows that cited the retired file cite the same passages there. Line numbers in the two files differ.

`van-braght_martyrs-mirror_sohm1886.yaml` is the corpus-map staging file that assigned the retired text to its shelves.
