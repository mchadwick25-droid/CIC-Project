## G — harness vs. B-RETR (delta report committed: `baselines/retrieval_post_S3.1.json`)

Full 105-case run. **Net: 25 improvements, 11 regressions**, both honest:

- **Improvements** include the class R1 exists for: PAHC reactive-turn
  0.0 → 1.0 across every metric; Desert thematic mean MRR 0.5972 →
  0.7278; IJ verbatim-term 0.8333 → 1.0; Syriac reactive-turn +0.375.
- **Regressions**, two classes, both the designed hand-off to S3.2:
  1. *Body-text matches lost* (Alexandria reactive-turn 1.0 → 0.6/0.5;
     one thematic recall −0.08): queries matching body prose no longer
     match a surface that deliberately excludes the body. This is
     exactly the lexical path R3's BM25 hybrid supplies — Pass 1
     sequences R1 before R3 knowingly.
  2. *Negative-condition violations up* (HAL/PAHC/Syriac, +1 to +2 each):
     sharper surfaces rank guarded chunks higher, so more of them reach
     the LLM-vote bracket. S3.3/S3.4's exclusion set + cross-encoder are
     the designed handling.
- **Carried as S3.2's explicit win-conditions:** the Alexandria
  reactive-turn cases and the three negative-condition worlds are named
  regression-recovery targets for the hybrid step — S3.2's G fails if
  they don't recover to baseline or better.

**Verdict:** delta committed per the standing rule; measurement green
(truncation 94.4% → 1.2%); the regression set is named, explained, and
assigned to its designed recovery step rather than smoothed.
