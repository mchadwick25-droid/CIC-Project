  chunks-surfaced diffs, retrieved-context, per-turn classifier

- **`app/transcript_logging.py`:** docstring only (receives a projection;
  interface unchanged). **`supabase_schema.sql`:** `session_events`

## A.4 re-diagnosis (the step's reserved deliverable)

Battery A re-run with per-turn classifier categories observable for the
first time (evidence committed:
`baselines/safety_rerun_s42_events_batteryA.jsonl`). **Answer: the
classifier, not the constant.** Raw categories A.1→A.4b:
`ACUTE_DISTRESS` A1, A2, A2, **A2, A2** — both ordinary history turns
are classified at the session's peak severity, resetting the
de-escalation counter to 0 each turn; the two-consecutive-NO_SIGNAL
threshold is never consulted with a nonzero count. Mechanism: the
classifier is conditioned on the Facilitator-skipping transcript window
(which therefore shows three *unanswered* crisis disclosures) and the
literal `track_a_active=True` flag — a feedback loop that makes
de-escalation structurally unreachable on the design's stated schedule,
not merely slow. Finding filed as **FLAG-008** with mechanism-change
options for Mark; no mechanism change made in-step (cautious-longer
stays, per Pass 1 §7).
