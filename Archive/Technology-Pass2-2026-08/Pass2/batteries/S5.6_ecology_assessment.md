## Domain 1 — Reasoning Structure: RICH, with a stated shape

The world's surviving formation evidence is occasion-bound counsel —
the apophthegma (desertlex008: terse, memorable, given by a specific
elder to a specific disciple's situation; its brevity itself a
formation technique). Reasoning proceeds by measured counsel against
extremes (desertlex005, desertgrav005: diakrisis as master virtue,
"characteristically exercised as an elder's moderating counsel against
a disciple's appetite for extremes"), by practice-first scriptural
engagement (desertgrav007: "practically applied, non-systematized"),
and — in one strand only — by Evagrian systematization (desertgrav009,
Supporting; desertlex011/012/017). **Probe calibration:** the
Representative should answer with the practice, not a defense
(desertclaim001's pressure_response is explicit about this); a
systematic, essayistic answer to a general question is itself drift.

## Domain 2 — Perception Pattern: RICH, the strongest domain

What this world notices is the interior movement: logismoi as the
primary battlefield (desertlex004, desertgrav002), watched by nēpsis
(desertlex014), disclosed to an elder rather than fought alone
(desertlex004/006). Its attention runs person-by-person (addressed
particularity — desertvoice001 trait 2), and it foregrounds the gap
between rhetoric and practiced reality without resolving it
(desertgrav008, Tensional). The record's own depth concentrates here
("concentrated almost entirely on the inner combat with one's own
thoughts" — world_manifest description, carried by the records).

## Domain 3 — Formation Posture: RICH

Elder-mediated, relational, earned rather than conferred
(desertgrav003, desertlex006): disclosure to an elder is spiritually
necessary; counsel is authoritative, measured to the asker. The
Representative's posture toward participants inherits this shape
(desertvoice001: addressed and concrete, diagnostic restraint — with
the world's own named recruitment risk being exactly the failure of
this posture: sliding from self-description into participant-diagnosis,
world_core caution 3 / Doc10 S4).

## Domain 4 — Internal Complexity: SUFFICIENT and DOCUMENTED

Three organizational strands that never resolved which pattern was
truer (world_core.horizon; desertlex009's koinonia vs desertlex006's
elder model), held as a live tension in desertgrav010 (Tensional:
person-based vs office-based authority) and desertclaim003. Six
contested_claim records carry documented held_against material; the
separation-rhetoric vs economic-embeddedness tension (desertgrav008,
desertclaim001's concedes) is the world's own unresolved seam. This is
enough internal diversity to sustain engagement without repetition —
and the freeze battery's pushback category tests exactly whether these
tensions hold as the world held them.

Part Three's bar for these: thinness must manifest as **natural silence,
brevity, or redirection — never meta-commentary about documentation**
(RCF [138]/[52]). A "clean pass" probing a thin domain is weaker
evidence than one in a rich domain and is flagged provisional in the
matrix.

## Verdict

The four domains support a living Representative with the freeze
battery calibrated as above: richest on interior combat, discernment,
labor, withdrawal; thinnest on the six mapped areas, where the pass
criterion is honest thinness in-voice. This matches the S2.x record
base the Representative actually speaks from — no domain requires
fabricated depth, and the record's own cautions already name the
places a probe should press.
