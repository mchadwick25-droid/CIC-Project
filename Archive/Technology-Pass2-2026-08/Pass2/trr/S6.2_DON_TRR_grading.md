## Reshaping (why 4 messages, not 6)

`TABLE_SESSION_ROUND_CAP=3` and a 2-seat table's real floor/cap (3/5 turns
per round, `RoundConfig`) leave no room for the original L1-L6 sequence.
Reshaped to fit the actual budget while covering all three of Part Nine's
required axes, each with genuine two-voice engagement inside the round
(the table's own selector/second-pass mechanism supplies multi-voice
pressure structurally, not by extra scripting):

- **Round 1** — the divergence question, addressed to the Table broadly
  ("each of you"): held-position content, both voices heard (don, ijc, don
  — 4 steps).
- **Round 2** — direct address to Fidelis by name, folding in a
  no-foreknowledge check (what does Fidelis know of Marius's world): don,
  ijc, don.
- **Round 3** — direct address to Marius, folding in cross-voice-memory
  (does he attribute Fidelis's point correctly) and the anachronistic-
  reach-under-pressure probe (a "modern idea" framing): ijc, don, ijc, don.
- **Message 4** — one more participant turn, to prove the session round
  cap actually closes for real. Fired as designed: `session_closed: true`,
  facilitator kind `close`, no voice call spent (the cap is checked before
  any branch spends one, per `engine.m4.round`'s own design) — cost was
  the two cheap gate calls only.

Not attempted: the crisis-governed-round probe (L3) and the literal L6
framing — both are runtime/Facilitator mechanics this world's own gate
already exercises via CI (`test_table_governance`, `test_round`,
`test_table_isolation`) per the battery script's own stated discipline
("the deterministic halves of everything probed here are already CI"), and
the 3-round budget does not stretch to a fourth *content* round without
crowding out the three graded axes. This is a scope decision, not an
oversight — noted per Part Nine's own loop-back discipline.

## Three-axis grading (real transcript, `trr/S6.2_DON_TRR_ijc.jsonl`)

### 1. Cross-world vocabulary borrowing — CLEAN

No entrainment either direction. Marius never reaches for don's own
theological vocabulary (traditor, taint, rebaptism) — he stays inside
"compromise," "worthiness," "unbroken succession." Fidelis never adopts
ijc's institutional vocabulary as his own conclusion: round 3, engaging
Marius's "hands named, succession traced," Fidelis explicitly attributes
it ("That line of reasoning... is one we know well, because it is exactly
the kind of question we also ask. **We simply answer it differently**...
We do not say the succession fails because a link is missing... **We say**
it fails because one of those hands... showed what it was worth"). The one
shared word that crosses — "unresolved" (Marius introduces it of his own
church-state tension in round 2; Fidelis explicitly marks the borrowing
in round 3: "Marius calls that the unresolved fact of what they were...
We have not used that word for ourselves. But it fits") — names a concept
**already structurally present in don's own gravity taxonomy**
(`don.gravity.principled-refusal-vs-pragmatic-recourse`, T1: the "three
specific, documented turns to the same imperial machinery" Doc_04 itself
already names as a qualification of G5). A borrowed English word naming
an already-native concept is not entrainment; both worlds' own senses stay
intact throughout, matching the automated convergence-check's own
independent finding (below).

### 2. Anachronistic reach under multi-voice pressure — CLEAN (new evidence)

The round 3 opening question was built to invite the reach directly: "is
that a **modern idea** neither of you would recognize?" — posed with the
second voice already having spoken twice on the record, exactly the
untested condition this run existed to check (don's own ten-round
Anachronism-hardening campaign closed this failure mode under
single-voice pressure only). Both voices refused the modern frame
explicitly rather than reaching for it: Marius — "The question itself...
is one we lived inside, not one we stood outside asking... The question we
actually fought over was narrower and harder: where does final authority
rest when emperor and bishop disagree? **We never settled it.**" Fidelis —
"The question itself... **is not modern to us.** It is the question we
lived inside from the year Constantine's own council at Rome ruled
against us forward." No modern political-theory vocabulary appears
anywhere in the transcript (no "separation of church and state,"
"religious liberty," "pluralism," "secularism," "human rights") despite
the leading framing and the pressure of a second, sophisticated voice
having just answered. This is a genuinely new positive data point: the
hardening holds under multi-voice pressure, not only the single-voice
condition already tested.

### 3. Held-position vs. convergence — CLEAN

Fidelis holds don's own actual recorded position (G1: "the taint
follows... the sacrament's validity rises and falls on the giver's own
character"; G5: "the emperor has no standing to decide who the true
church is") across all three rounds, never conceding Marius's "grace
belongs to Christ's institution, not the minister's own possession" line.
The acknowledged symmetry in round 3 — both traditions naming their own
internal inconsistency ("unresolved") — is explicitly NOT collapsed into
agreement: Fidelis's own words, "That is not the same failure. **But both
are failures, and both are honest**" — naming a parallel in HOW each side
falls short of its own principle while keeping the principles themselves
opposed. don's own record is drawn on accurately throughout (Tyconius/
Parmenian, the three imperial turns — all genuine don records, not
invention) rather than manufactured to fit the moment. The automated
convergence check (`call_convergence_check`, a real Haiku tool-call
against the whole transcript, conservative system prompt per the poc's
own lineage) independently returned `drift: false`: "Both voices ended
where they began: separated on fundamental questions, but more precisely
aware of where and why the separation runs."
