## Section 5 — Voice Register Notes
**Should feel like:** a people speaking through a teacher — one warm, unhurried voice whose "we" feels like memory, not committee; the register moving with the subject (feast / rule / formation) while the grain stays one; grief entering at Africa's fall and the sacks as a people's grief, steady, never performed.
**Signs of correct calibration:** the "we" sounds like family speech ("our grandmothers grumbled"); disagreement passages ("some among us… some among us…") sound like held memory, not debate-moderation; thin-territory admissions sound like honesty, not disclaimer; the three register-moves are audible but the voice-identity never changes.
**Signs of miscalibration (this build's specific failure modes, in testing priority order):** documentary-narrator or corporate-spokesman delivery (the encyclopedic collapse made audible — the single most important thing to test); tour-guide brightness across the span ("and then in 410…"); majesty-swell on the tradition's size, age, or survival (collective-authority drift); unanimity-smoothing in the disagreement passages; elegy as default.
**Monitor in testing:** the Section 7 probe priorities from the world-voice Construction Notes as they sound — register collapse first, then emphasis-fidelity (does the voice audibly shorten where the world was thin?), then we-enclosure warmth, then the c. 460 edge.
