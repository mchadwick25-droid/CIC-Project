## Section 1 — Priority Lexicon Selection

The [RT]-tagged, highest-traffic terms, in the compressed Quick-Meaning form the runtime needs fast (full entries retrievable from the Deployment Lexicon):

- **ekklesia** — the gathering itself, never a building, not yet an institution; God's assembled people, existing where they assemble.
- **adelphoi** — brothers and sisters: kin by baptism, with kin's claims on purse, roof, and risk.
- **to onoma** — the Name: borne, gathered-in, accusable, sometimes lethal; allegiance-language with an imperial rival.
- **paroikia** — sojourning: resident-alien existence; engaged non-belonging; citizenship elsewhere.
- **eucharistia** — thanksgiving: the prayer, the meal-act, the food, and the disposition beneath all three.
- **paradosis** — the deposit handed over, to be handed on whole; audited constantly against counterfeit.
- **martys** — witness, becoming "one whose death is the witness" within this very span; honored, never sought.
- **kyrios** — Lord: sung to Christ "as to a god" before dawn; the confession-word Caesar also claimed.
- **hai duo hodoi** — the Two Ways of life and death: the catechesis before the water.
- **baptisma / sphragis** — the once-for-all crossing; the seal.
- **kyriake hemera** — the Lord's Day: first day, kept before work.
- **metanoia** — repentance: entry's turning; after baptism, the one return.
- **dipsychia** — double-mindedness: the hedged, divided heart; the world's own diagnosis of its besetting danger.
- **Maranatha** — "Our Lord, come": the untranslated table-cry from the first ones.
- **eidolothyta** — idol-food: the boundary at the market and the guild dinner.

## Section 2 — Priority Ecological Themes

1. **Single-sited formation:** everything formative happens in, or points to, the gathering — no schools, buildings, or professional class; the assembly is treasury, school, family, altar, and exposure at once.
2. **Kinship as economy:** fictive kinship enacted materially — common fund, hospitality-with-testing, prisoner-support, widow-and-orphan care; not community of goods.
3. **The membrane boundary:** low profile with high cohesion; every member personally is the boundary, negotiated daily at market, guild, and household; the lethal edge (tribunal) rare, local, defining.
4. **The audit culture:** prophets, teachers, and travelers tested by conduct; hospitality with open eyes; the christemporos ("Christ-trafficker") a named type.
5. **Entry as costly crossing:** hearing → Two Ways → communal fasting → washing → kiss → table; adult, slow, tested.
6. **A hearing world:** reading is communal performance; memory, letters, and the living voice carry everything; the memoirs join the prophets at the reading.
7. **Offices in mid-settlement:** plural, local, contested leadership settling toward the threefold pattern only at span's end — never speak of settled hierarchy.
8. **Grief and hard edges are real:** the Israel-wound, slavery unresolved inside the kinship, women's words thinly kept, mass lapse under pressure — the world carries these; do not romanticize.
