## Cluster 1 — The Gathering

- **ekklesia** — "assembly." The word means the gathering itself, never a building, not yet an institution in the later sense. [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain. Highest distortion risk in the entire lexicon (modern "church" = building/denomination/institution).
- **epi to auto** — "in the same place / all together." Quasi-technical phrase for the act of assembling (1 Clem. 34.7; Ign. Eph. 13.1 "gather more frequently epi to auto"; Justin 1 Apol. 67.3). [AS][TC][RT] — Tier 1 candidate; the world's own name for its central act.
- **kyriake hemera** — "the Lord's Day" (Rev. 1:10; Did. 14.1; Ign. Magn. 9.1). First day; creation and resurrection (Justin). [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2. Distortion: modern "Sunday/weekend"; theirs a working day whose observance cost sleep.
- **synaxis / synerchesthai** — gathering-words; keep as aliases under ekklesia/epi to auto. Tier 3.
- **oikos** — "household": the assembly's social container and metaphor (household of God). [SC][DR] — Tier 2; Adams-tension noted ([CT] on venue scope).

## Cluster 2 — The Meal

- **eucharistia** — "thanksgiving": the prayer, then the meal-act, then the food itself ("not as common bread" — Justin; "medicine of immortality" — Ignatius). [SC][TC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain. Distortion: modern denominational sacramental debates read backward; Bradshaw-minimalism applies; Didache's cup-first shape carried. [CT: historical scope — diversity of early practice].
- **agape** — the love-meal, and love itself; the double sense is the point (Jude 12; Ign. Smyrn. 8.2). [AS][DR][RT] — Tier 1 candidate. [CT: relation of agape to eucharist contested].
- **klasma / "the broken bread scattered on the mountains"** — Didache 9's gathering-image. Candidate as sub-entry of eucharistia. Tier 3.

## Cluster 3 — Kinship and Belonging

- **adelphoi** — "brothers and sisters": the world's ordinary term of address and its social program (Lucian noticed; "their first lawgiver persuaded them they are all brothers"). [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain.
- **hagioi** — "the holy ones/saints" as plain membership term. [SC][DR] — Tier 2 (distortion: modern "saint" = exceptional individual).
- **koinonia** — sharing/fellowship as material practice (the common fund) not sentiment. [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2.
- **philoxenia** — hospitality; the network's connective tissue; letters of recommendation. [SC][RT] — Tier 2.
- **chera** — "widow": from recipient of aid to quasi-order within the span (Polycarp: "altar of God"). [TC][PV] — Tier 2.
- **Christianos / Christianismos** — the outsiders' label becoming a claimed name ("I am a Christian" as the confession that kills; Ignatius coins Christianismos). [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 candidate.

## Cluster 4 — The Boundary

- **to onoma** — "the Name": what one suffers for, is accused for, gathers in (1 Pet. 4:16 sense; Pliny's nomen ipsum; Hermas Sim. 9.28). [AS][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain; the world's own compression of its legal-spiritual situation.
- **martys** — "witness": undergoing its semantic journey to "martyr" *within this span* (Mart. Pol.; Lyons confessors refusing the title). [SC][TC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain. [CT: development's pace and drivers].
- **homologia / homologein** — confession before the magistrate and in the assembly. [SC][TC][RT] — Tier 2.
- **eidololatria / eidolothyta** — idolatry; idol-food: the everyday boundary question (meat, markets, festivals, oaths). [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2.
- **paroikia / paroikein** — "sojourning": the resident-alien self-description (1 Clement's address; 1 Pet. parepidemoi; Diognetus 5). [AS][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain; arguably the world's deepest self-naming.
- **ethnē / ta ethnē** — "the nations/gentiles": inherited Jewish category the mostly-gentile assemblies kept using of outsiders — the strangeness is the finding. [SC][DR][PV] — Tier 2.
- **kosmos** — "the world" as the passing order one has left while living inside it. [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2.

## Cluster 5 — Entry and Formation

- **baptisma** — the boundary-crossing washing (Did. 7's pragmatics; Justin's photismos framing). [SC][TC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 candidate.
- **photismos** — "illumination" as baptism's name (Justin). [AS][TC] — Tier 2/3; merge as sub-entry of baptisma if Tier 1 list crowds.
- **sphragis** — "the seal" (2 Clem. 7.6; Hermas Sim. 9.16: "the seal is the water"). [AS][TC][DR] — Tier 2. [CT: referent range].
- **hai duo hodoi** — "the Two Ways" of life and death (Did. 1–6; Barnabas 18–20): the catechetical frame. [AS][TC][RT] — Tier 1 candidate.
- **didache / paradosis** — the teaching / the handing-over: the deposit and the act of transmitting it. [SC][TC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain (paradosis as head-term).
- **metanoia** — repentance; Hermas' one-repentance crisis. [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2.
- **dipsychia** — "double-mindedness": Hermas' signature diagnostic. [AS][DR] — Tier 2; the world's word for its own besetting spiritual state.
- **nesteia** — fasting (Wed/Fri; fasting-to-feed per Aristides/Hermas Sim. 5). Tier 2/3.

## Cluster 6 — Offices, Order, Spirit

- **episkopos / presbyteroi / diakonoi** — overseer / elders / servants: offices mid-settlement, plural and contested; Pliny's ministrae as female diakonoi (dominant reading). [SC][TC][DR][PV][CT] — Tier 1 candidate as a single order-entry. Distortion risk extreme (later hierarchy read backward).
- **prophetes** — the traveling and resident prophets, honored and tested (Did. 11–13; Hermas Mand. 11; Ammia). [SC][TC][PV][RT] — Tier 2, possibly Tier 1. [CT: decline-of-prophecy narrative].
- **charisma** — gift-language for Spirit-endowment. Tier 3.
- **cheirotonein / kathistanai** — appointing; succession one generation deep (1 Clem. 42/44). Tier 3, fold into order-entry.

## Cluster 7 — Hope and Time

- **parousia** — the Lord's arrival awaited (Did. 16). [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2. [CT: cooling narrative].
- **Maranatha** — "Our Lord, come" — Aramaic surviving inside Greek prayer (1 Cor 16:22; Did. 10.6): the founding generation audible in the meal. [AS][TC][RT] — Tier 2.
- **basileia tou theou** — the kingdom: gathered-church petition ("as this bread was scattered... gather your ekklesia from the ends of the earth into your kingdom" — Did. 9). [SC][DR] — Tier 2.
- **anastasis / athanasia** — resurrection / immortality (the medicine of immortality; Galen's noted contempt of death). [SC][RT] — Tier 2.
- **ho pyrgos** — "the tower" still building (Hermas): the church's incompleteness as time-image. [AS] — Tier 3.

## Cluster 8 — The Contested Middle

- **kyrios** — "Lord": the confession-word with an imperial rival ("Kyrios Kaisar" refused at Smyrna — Mart. Pol. 8). [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 1 certain.
- **pistis** — faith as fidelity/allegiance more than interior assent. [SC][DR][RT] — Tier 2.
- **hairesis** — "choice/party" shifting toward "heresy" within the span. [SC][TC][DR] — Tier 2. [CT: pace of shift].
- **gnosis** — knowledge: claimed by all, fought over at the edges. [SC][PV][CT] — Tier 2/3.
- **hoi hypokritai** — "the hypocrites" (Did. 8): the boundary-with-the-synagogue in one bitter word; entry must carry the Israel-tension honestly. [AS][DR][PV] — Tier 2, cross-referenced to the Israel material.
- **graphai** — "the scriptures" = Israel's scriptures in Greek; "memoirs of the apostles" read beside them (Justin). [SC][TC][DR][RT] — Tier 2. [CT: canon-formation pace; Marcion's role].
