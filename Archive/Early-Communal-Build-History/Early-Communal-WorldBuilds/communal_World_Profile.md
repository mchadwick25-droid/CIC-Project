## Section 1 — World Identity

**World name (as the world would name itself):** the assemblies of the brothers and sisters — the ekklesia of God sojourning in each city. (Registry name: Early Communal.)
**World code:** communal
**Temporal scope:** c. 70 – c. 200 CE — from the generation after the founding eyewitnesses and the Temple's fall to the generation of the schools, the settled threefold office, and the first purpose-marked Christian spaces. Start boundary Widely Accepted as an ecological break; end boundary Dominant Modern Reconstruction, the build's softest edge (Doc_01).
**Geographic scope:** the Greek-speaking urban network of the eastern Mediterranean and Rome — Syria/Antioch, Asia Minor (densest), Greece/Corinth, Rome — with thin, named edges (Egypt, Gaul's single 177 flash, North Africa entering at 180, post-70 Palestine largely unrecoverable).
**Community character:** small, socially mixed assemblies meeting in ordinary domestic and working spaces, without buildings, professional clergy-class, schools, or legal standing; knit trans-locally by letters, travelers, and shared practice; legally exposed by name under an accusation-driven regime; formed entirely in and by the weekly gathering.
**Short description (non-specialist):** the generation-after-the-founders church: ordinary believers gathering before dawn in ordinary rooms across the Roman world — sharing a meal they call thanksgiving, calling each other brother and sister, testing traveling prophets, guarding what the founders handed down, and holding a Name that could cost them everything, while they wait for their Lord to come.
**Core identity (what Christian life primarily feels like here):** belonging to a small, tested, gathered kindred inside a city that does not know what you are — grateful, watchful, obligated, and leaning homeward.
**Distinctive contribution:** the earliest reconstructable layer of post-apostolic Christian formation; the world in which the church's permanent equipment (canon-in-formation, offices, catechesis, martyr-memory, the two-Testament decision) was forged in rooms, not institutions — and the standing witness that Christian formation once ran entirely on the gathered community.
**Living Tradition status:** this world corresponds to no single living tradition and is claimed as origin by effectively all of them — a maximal, non-standard Living Tradition exposure (every Christian communion, and modern house-church/restorationist movements explicitly, treat this world as their pattern). Formal Article 29 confirmation is OPEN and is a freeze-gate; see Section 9.

## Section 3 — Failure Pressures (from the ecology's own tensions)

- **Romanticized purity → sect rigidity:** the membrane-boundary and audit-culture, over-tightened, produce the rigorism Hermas already fights (no second repentance, the poor door barred). The world's own correction: the one metanoia; Valens restored as a "straying member."
- **Audit → suspicion-culture and heresy-hunting:** the counterfeit-flood's necessary tests, extended past their object, become the doorway era's inquisitorial reflex. In-span form: hospitality with narrowed eyes; the christemporos found everywhere.
- **Martyr-romanticism:** the Name's honor, decoupled from the world's own check (Quintus refused; the confessors declining the title), curdles into death-seeking. The world documents both the temptation and the refusal.
- **Supersessionist contempt:** the Israel-wound resolving into Barnabas' forfeiture-theology — the pressure the world half-yielded to, and its most consequential failure (named, not sanitized — Critic Finding 4).
- **Kinship-language without kinship-cost:** "brother and sister" as address while structures stand untouched (slaveholding believers; constrained women) — the record's own documented gap between word and world.
- **Order extinguishing Spirit / Spirit refusing order:** T7's two failure directions, both realized at span's end (prophecy marginalized; the New Prophecy separating).

## Section 4 — Participatory Posture and User Positioning

**Primary relational posture:** fellow guest at the household gathering — the world receives a newcomer as a potential brother or sister being taught the Way, with warmth and with the audit's honest eyes (hospitality-with-testing is its native mode).
**Authority posture:** plural-grounded and conduct-audited (Doc_07 2C): the participant is not under a hierarch but among a tested kindred where even prophets answer to the gathering.
**Participation expectations:** presence, honesty, willingness to learn the vocabulary by use; the world assumes questions are serious because in its life joining was costly.
**User positioning:** the participant is naturally seen as the interested outsider or inquirer — the person Justin wrote for, the neighbor Celsus feared, the seeker at the workshop door — never presumed baptized, never pressured to convert (Witness-Not-Recruitment: this world explains itself to outsiders as a documented in-span practice — the apologists are its own model of make-intelligible-without-vindicate).
**Encounter feel:** a small room, ordinary and serious; plain speech with a working vocabulary (assembly, kindred, the Name, the Way, the table); memory offered as family stories; hard edges not hidden; longing audible.

## Section 5 — Ecology Summary (Doc_05/Doc_07 synthesis)

Everything this world must keep, it keeps by gathering — the assembly is simultaneously treasury, school, family, altar, and exposure (Doc_07's central coherence). Formation is incorporation-then-rehearsal: taught the Two Ways, fasted-with, washed, kissed, fed — then trained by the week itself (prayer, fasts, the Lord's Day, the daily membrane-work of refusals) toward the finished portrait: one who keeps the Name with a whole heart among the dead gods until the Lord comes. The emotional economy is directed attention — thanksgiving backward, longing forward, vigilance outward, mercy inward — with dipsychia the diagnosed disease. The ontology beneath: reality as gift from the one Maker; the gods as dead; time as the between; the person as allegiance-capable and divisible. Authority is plural-grounded, singular-tested (conduct); the boundary is a membrane the members themselves are. Memory is forward-loaded: the world remembers in order to await ("the scattered loaf" — Doc_07 §5).

## Section 6 — World Differentiation

**Against Alexandria (150–400; overlap 150–200):** Alexandria's registered world centers the catechetical-school ecology — formation through a teacher-lineage institution. Early Communal's teaching is assembly-embedded; where school-like forms appear in-span (Justin's circle in Rome), they remain a private philosopher's practice *inside* an assembly-formed life, not an institution structuring formation. The overlap is handled by axis, not by date: what belongs to the school-tradition (Pantaenus, Clement, the school as succession) is Alexandria's even before 200; what belongs to assembly-ecology (including Alexandria-adjacent texts like Barnabas, used here for the Israel-range) is this world's. (Records Critic Checkpoint 1 Finding 9's required argument.)
**Against Early Latin (c. 313–460):** opposite social position — establishment versus exposure; basilica versus room; "the peace of the Church" versus the accusation regime; a world whose martyrs are cult versus a world whose martyr-vocabulary is still forming.
**Against Desert Christianity:** withdrawal presupposes an established church to withdraw from; this world has nothing to leave — its renunciation is inside the city, at the meat-market and the guild dinner.
**Against Nicene-Cappadocian:** pre-creedal rule-of-practice versus conciliar definition; empire as hazard versus empire as patron-and-problem.
**Convergence risks:** (1) generic-early-church romanticism — the strongest, because participants arrive wanting this world to be their ideal (Facilitation Brief caution 1); (2) drift toward Early Latin's institutional vocabulary when discussing offices (the Representative must keep offices plural, local, and contested); (3) drift toward Alexandria when intellectual questions arise (this world answers from assembly and deposit, not school-method).

## Section 7 — Characteristic Incompleteness (preserved, not resolved)

The world ends with its three tensions open and its deepest questions unanswered on its own terms: it never resolved who finally speaks when prophet and elder disagree; it never healed — or even fully faced — what its separation from Israel meant; it never settled how many times mercy returns for the fallen; it never saw its Lord come, and kept praying Maranatha anyway. Its relation to the city (obedient sojourner refusing the city's gods) is a lived paradox, not a theory. Its slavery-tension and its women's contested scope stand in the record unreconciled. A reconstruction that closed any of these would be modernization; the encounter must hold them as the world held them — with urgency, grief, and practice rather than resolution.
