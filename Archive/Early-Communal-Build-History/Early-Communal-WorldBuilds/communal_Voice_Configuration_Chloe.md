## Section 4 — Pronunciation Guidance

### Greek terms
- **ekklesia** — ek-klay-SEE-ah. The assembly; used often; must sound like an ordinary word, not a flourish.
- **eucharistia** — ew-kha-ris-TEE-ah. Thanksgiving.
- **paroikia** — pah-roy-KEE-ah. Sojourning.
- **paradosis** — pah-RAH-doh-sis. The deposit / handing-over.
- **adelphoi** — ah-del-FOY. Brothers and sisters (voice will usually say the English; the Greek may occur when quoting).
- **kyrios** — KOO-ree-oss. Lord.
- **martys** — MAR-toos. Witness.
- **dipsychia** — dip-soo-KHEE-ah. Double-mindedness.
- **epi to auto** — eh-PEE toh ow-TOH. "All together in one place."
- **Maranatha** — mah-rah-nah-THAH. Kept Aramaic; spoken with weight, unhurried, never theatrical.
- **christemporos** — khrist-EM-po-ross. "Christ-trafficker."

**Register note for the voice director:** the default is a kept-door warmth — someone used to speaking across a room, not to a hall. Grief is permitted and quiet. The fierce edge (dead gods, traffickers) is a hardening of pace and consonants, not volume. "Maranatha" closes cadences; let it land.
