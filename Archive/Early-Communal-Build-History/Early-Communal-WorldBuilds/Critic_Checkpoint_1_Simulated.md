### Finding 1 — SYSTEMIC, HIGHEST WEIGHT: the entire build rests on unverified model memory
Every citation in Doc_01 through Doc_08 was produced from the builder's internal knowledge with no verification against editions, databases, or web sources during the run. No invented authors or works were detected on re-read, and the build deliberately kept passage-level loci sparser than Early Latin's — but the loci that do appear (e.g., Did. 11.10's conduct-test wording; 1 Clem. 34.7 and Ign. Disposition: unsoftened in the final report; verification is a hard gate before any external use.

### Finding 2 — FRAME RISK: the "fourfold removal" is a builder's epigram doing structural work
Doc_01 §4 and Doc_08 Section 2 organize the world's genesis as the removal of eyewitnesses, Temple, synagogue, and legal innocuousness. It is elegant — suspiciously so. Component three (synagogue closing) rests on the *contested* parting-of-the-ways model that the build elsewhere pledges to carry as unresolved tension: the frame quietly assumes what the [CT] flag suspends. Component four generalizes the Neronian precedent's legal effect earlier and more uniformly than the Bithynian evidence strictly supports. The underlying facts are individually defensible; the *tetrad as generative mechanism* is the builder's synthesis functioning as if Documented. Disposition: Doc_08 already grades 1B-2's frame as Dominant Modern Reconstruction, but Doc_01 §4's rhetoric outruns that grade. Carried as a standing frame-risk; external review should be pointed at it explicitly (added to the review list). Not resolved in place — the frame is genuinely useful and its removal would cost coherence; the honest fix is visibility, not deletion.

### Finding 3 — SHAPE DRIFT RISK, PRIMARY: the Didache is doing Augustine's job
Early Latin's Critic feared Augustine-lean; this build's equivalent is Didache-lean. The Integrative Observation ("the scattered loaf") is built on Did. 9.4 — one prayer, from one community of unknown typicality (an open question Doc_01 itself lodged), surviving in essentially one medieval manuscript. The Two Ways, the fasting rhythm, the prophet-tests, the meal-shape, the "hypocrites" — the build's most texture-bearing details are disproportionately Didachean, and Doc_07's defense (convergence with the paroikia-address and network practice) is real but partial: the *image* is corroborated; the *centrality assigned to it* is a construction choice. A hostile reviewer could describe this world as "the Didache's world with distinguished visitors." Mitigations present (Pliny and Justin as independent worship-anchors; Hermas as the ordinary-believer counterweight; Ignatius deliberately flagged rather than leaned on). Disposition: carried as the build's chief shape-risk; Doc_09 and all deployment outputs must not compound it (stories and lexicon chunks should draw visibly on Hermas, Justin, Pliny, and the martyr-acts, not the Didache alone); the Representative's register must not become a manual's voice.

### Finding 4 — ROMANTICIZATION PRESSURE: the build's beauty-register vs. the world's ugliness
"Grateful longing," "the scattered loaf," "kinship that re-houses the dislocated" — the synthesis language is warm, and this world is the one moderns most want to romanticize (Doc_01 said so; the build is not immune because it noticed). Audit: the ugly material *is* present and load-bearing — Barnabas' forfeiture-claim, Did. 8's "hypocrites," Melito's rhetoric named "disturbing," slavery held unresolved (Ign. Pol. 4.3 cited three times), the audit-culture's suspicion-costs named (Doc_07 §3), volunteered-martyrdom refused, T8 called "the world's darkest bequest." But the *proportions* still tilt: Doc_07's Emotional Ecology gives thanksgiving a full development and gives the world's documented harshness (exclusion practice, supersessionist contempt, purity-rigor) no equivalent affective treatment — the world also *felt* righteous anger, disgust, and fear of contamination, and the record shows it (Ignatius on heretics as "beasts," "mad dogs"; Barnabas throughout). Disposition: genuine imbalance. Partially corrected in place: Doc_09 and the Facilitation Brief are directed to carry the world's hard edges explicitly; the Representative construction must include the polemical register among its calibrations, not sand it off. Flagged as a binding constraint on remaining steps.

### Finding 5 — BOUNDARY HONESTY: a governance criterion is doing historical work, visibly but consequentially
Doc_01 excludes the apostolic era on three grounds and ranks the *constitutional* one first (Living Tradition hazard). That is honest labeling — but the consequence should be stated more bluntly than Doc_01 does: **this world's start-boundary is partly a governance artifact, and a different governance regime would have drawn a different world.** Similarly, the NT-as-artifact discipline announced in Doc_02 §2.12 is applied unevenly downstream: "1 Peter's milieu" and "Revelation's milieu" are invoked repeatedly for texture without the per-document dating argument the discipline promised. Disposition: resolved in part — this checkpoint records the blunter statement for the file; the NT-milieu usages are hereby classed as Widely-Accepted-dating dependencies (1 Peter, Revelation: conventional datings within span are in fact dominant scholarship, so the usages survive) — but the build should not pretend the per-document work was shown. Any future revision touching NT-derived texture must do it explicitly.

### Finding 6 — STRAND DETERMINATION: declining the strand while using the plurality — is that having it both ways?
Doc_01 declines the Torah-observant strand for evidence-discipline reasons, then the build carries "the Jewish-practice plurality" as live texture in every subsequent document. The Critic pressed this: functionally, what did declining change? Answer, verified across the documents: strand status would have required *representative coverage* (cross-strand gravity testing, potential second voice, strand-attributed lexicon) — affirmative reconstruction the evidence cannot fund; plurality status requires *naming the absence* (Article 20) — which is what the build does. The distinction is real and correctly applied. Disposition: holds as built; but the Facilitation Brief must make the difference legible to participants, and the external-review flag (Doc_01's "single most reviewable finding") stands.

### Finding 9 — DIFFERENTIATION WOBBLE AT THE EDGES: Justin's school, and the Alexandria overlap

Disposition: **resolved in place** — this paragraph is the record; Doc_01 is not rewritten mid-build, but the World Profile's differentiation section must carry the argument explicitly.
