## 1. The "lenses" mechanism: did it work?

### Structurally, the lens set was never actually standardized

Two separate lens systems exist — Doc_05's ecological-reconstruction lenses and Doc_07's integration lenses — and neither converges across worlds. Doc_05 ranges from 5 umbrella lenses with nested dimensions (W1, Syriac) to 11 flat sections (IJC). Doc_07 ranges from 5 (IJC, two of them merged pairs) to 9 (W1, Syriac, Alexandria, Desert).

That's because **the two governing documents contradict each other**, and the builds noticed:

- `L4-Templates/Integrated_Ecology_Analysis_Template.md` specifies a **fixed** five required lenses (2A–2E). Framework V7.3 Step 7 says the opposite: "the lens set is world-derived, not prescribed." W1's Doc_07 discloses this in its front matter and follows the template; Alexandria, HAL, IJC, and Desert follow the Framework and derive their own sets. Same step, two incompatible instruments, four worlds one way and two the other.
- Syriac's Doc_05 §0 flags a contradiction *inside* the Framework: Step 5's Activities list names eleven finer dimensions, while the same section's closing line names five umbrella lenses — "present verbatim in both V7.3 and V7.4_DRAFT, not a version-specific error this document can silently correct." Syriac resolved it by building a nesting table mapping the eleven into the five.
- Syriac's Doc_07 §0 also notes Step 7 is **the only step in the Framework's Part VII Reference Workflow with no Part-number citation** — the lens step is the least-governed step in the pipeline.

### Where a lens genuinely surfaced distinct, evidence-grounded insight

**Desert's Doc_07 is the best-differentiated in the corpus**, because its lenses draw on structurally different *kinds* of evidence, not just different passages:

- §1 Affective/Emotional on the *logismoi* taxonomy (Doc_03 §1.5), producing a real finding — "emotional life and intellectual analysis are not separate domains here, they are the same activity viewed from two angles."
- §2 Intellectual on Evagrius's anomalous Greek philosophical education (Doc_01 §4) — arguing the systematization is "native to this world's *content*... but not to this world's *characteristic mode of producing knowledge*."
- §6 Memory on the post-430 compilation gap (Doc_02 §2.3) — "this world's memory... was not designed by its own participants to be a memory at all."
- §9 Material Culture on Kellia's 1,500 excavated structures and the Nepheros papyri (Doc_02 §5.1–5.2) — literary, archaeological, and papyrological streams doing separate work.

**Syriac's Doc_07 enforces differentiation procedurally**: every lens carries a mandatory "What this lens reveals that Doc_05 alone did not show" subsection. 2D is the strongest — Doc_01 and Doc_05 had recorded the Roman/Persian difference as one of *intensity*; the Boundary lens establishes it is **categorical**: "These are not the same force at different strengths — they are two different kinds of pressure, and the two boundary logics they produce differ in kind, not merely in degree."

**Syriac's 2F pre-emptively defends itself** against exactly this charge: memory structures are "a further, independent line of evidence for the same underlying finding rather than a redundant restatement of it." The defensive framing is the tell — 2D, 2F, 2G, and 2H all deliver the same Roman/Persian bifurcation through four different materials.

## 2. Representative voice quality: what source condition is named as the cause

I asked the readers to classify each named root cause as (a) source existed but wasn't reachable, (b) source didn't exist, (c) source existed in the wrong form, or (d) something else.

**The distribution is the headline finding, and Syriac's own record states the pattern explicitly:** failures caught in *construction-time illustrative* testing skew heavily to **(b)** — genuine evidence gaps, correctly identified and correctly declined. Failures caught in *live* testing against the actual assembled prompt skew heavily to **(a)** and **(c)** — material that existed but was unreachable, or reachable only in a form that broke voice. `Open_Gaps_Tracking.md`: "This is exactly the outcome live testing exists to produce — real defects illustrative-only testing structurally could not reach."

### The cleanest instance of your exact question — Desert, the leaking jug

Papnoute attributed Abba Moses's leaking-jug story to "Macarius." The correct attribution existed in Doc_09a Story 2.1 **and was already present, correctly, in the always-loaded World Capsule Core**. I verified the capsule text directly (`Desert-Monasticism\desert_World_Capsule_Core.md` line 88): it carries the story as an *unnamed compressed allusion* — "one who carried a leaking jug to a judgment so that his own sins would be seen running out behind him." The name is not attached to the image.

The fix was to promote it into the Permanent Prompt as an explicit ownership rule: "The jug that leaked was Moses's own, carried to a judgment against himself, not against another — and when we tell it, we tell it as his." **Same information, different placement and form.** Nothing was added to the record; the record was reorganized.

## 3. The "Ecological Function" section

### Part one: quality is better than expected

41 chunks read in full across five worlds, plus a 90-file structural sweep. **26 GENUINE / 10 PARTIAL / 2 RESTATES / 3 absent** (all three Tier 3, all legitimate under the template, two stating the omission in-file). There are no undocumented gaps.

**32 of the 38 chunks that have the section name a directional dependency (84%)**, in real vocabulary: "presupposed by," "the precondition for," "downstream of it," "the material source of," "is the mechanism behind," and one clean counterfactual ("remove the Logos and the ecology fragments into four disconnected domains").

**Quality varies by world, and it tracks house style rather than subject matter or tier:** Desert 9/9 genuine, Alexandria 6/8, IJC 4/5, Syriac 4/7, HAL 3/9. The mechanism is grammatical — Desert's entries are *verb-shaped* (sustains, generates, governs, moderates), which forces a direction; HAL's are *noun-shaped* ("anchors G3," "the organizational bedrock"), which can label importance without ever naming what leans on what.

Three best, in full:

- `Imperial-Juridical-Christianity\Lexicon-Chunks\ijclex008_haeresis.md`: "*Haeresis* is what *homoios* was named as, once our own settlement changed — a participant who understands this term understands that the same juridical machinery that names and excludes was, at another point in this world's own record, the machinery enforcing the very teaching later so named."
- `Desert-Monasticism\Lexicon-Chunks\desertlex007_cheironaxia.md`: "Sustains withdrawal materially; directly generates this world's own documented tension between withdrawal's rhetoric of total separation and its actual, ongoing economic entanglement with surrounding villages." (Twenty-eight words naming both a dependency *and* the contradiction it produces.)
- `Alexandria-Catechetical-School\Lexicon-Chunks\alexlex016_allegory.md`: carries a reading method through to why teachers outrank offices in this world, then to the post-Nicene pressure point.

Clearest collapse: `Hieronymian-Ascetic-Literary\Lexicon-Chunks\hal_lex06_patrocinium.md` — the Quick Meaning ("this world's actual authority structure, standing in place of church office") and the Ecological Function ("the organizational bedrock of this community's authority structure... differentiation from episcopal-authority formation worlds") are the same sentence twice. **Nothing is named as depending on patronage** — even though `hal_lex03_renuntiatio.md` separately identifies renunciation as patronage's material source. The reciprocal link exists in the corpus; this entry does not make it.

A distinct fourth failure mode worth naming, invisible unless you check what the section is *for*: four chunks use the slot for **builder-facing content** rather than ecology — lexicon bookkeeping (`syrlex007_ihidaya`: "should be treated as its own distinct entry rather than folded into it"), evidentiary calibration duplicating the Distortion Risk section (`hal_lex12_grammaticus`, where the real dependency claim is sitting one section earlier in World Meaning — "the ecology is stated in the wrong box"), and build-process audit log (`hal_lex11`: "this candidate was tested directly and found insufficient").

### Part two: the cultural-relational insight is genuinely stranded

This is the sharp result. **The string "Ecological Function" appears in exactly two Representative-side documents across all six worlds** — and one is a superseded archived draft, while the other is a *negative* reference. `CiC_W1_Representative_Voice_Construction.md` cites it only to explain an absence: Tier 3 terms "carry no developed World Meaning or **Ecological Function** content at this tier."

Zero Permanent Prompts. Zero World Capsule Cores. Zero Representative Construction Notes. Zero Phase 1–7 documents.

The downstream apparatus never asks for it:

- **`L3C-Representative-Methodology\CiC_L3C_Representative_Construction_Framework_V3.2.docx`** — the governing methodology for building the Representative — mentions the lexicon exactly twice, both as input-document titles in a list ("Doc_03 — Lexicon Candidate List", "Doc_06 — Full Interpretive Lexicon"). It never names Ecological Function, World Meaning, Quick Meaning, or Distortion Risk as content to draw on.
- **`L4-Templates\Representative_Construction_Notes_Template.md`** references the Deployment Lexicon once: "Native terms: [Which terms from the Deployment Lexicon (Doc_06) are native to..." — a list of *which* terms, not their content.
- **`Representative_Permanent_Prompt_Template.txt`** allots the lexicon one slot: "The vocabulary through which you understand everything: [**3–5 KEY TERMS** in the world's own language, used naturally as you would use them — woven into sentences, not listed and defined]." Three to five terms, out of 9 to 45 built chunks per world.
- **The Lexicon Deployment Index does not track Ecological Function at all** — not even presence/absence. I checked IJC's directly: it indexes Tier, seven tag types, Aliases, Related-Terms, Source Registry cross-reference, and Author-Gravity risk. Ecological Function is not a queryable dimension.

**The contrast with Related-Terms is the instructive part.** Related-Terms *is* structured, *is* indexed, and *did* travel: IJC's Doc_07 §2 built its entire flagship integration finding on the Doc_06 Related-Terms graph. It took three drafting passes and three review rounds to get right — but it was used, because it was in a form something downstream could query. Ecological Function holds the same class of information in prose, and nothing downstream reads it.

**Review never tests the quality bar either.** Reviews check the section for presence/absence only. Desert's Doc_06 Round 1 S2: "Six of seven Tier 2 entries omit Ecological Function." HAL's Round 1 Finding 3: Ecological Function is "reworded (mostly benign)" between Doc_06 and the chunks. No review in any world asks whether an Ecological Function delivers dependency insight or restates the Quick Meaning.

### One honest qualification

Where an insight *also* exists in Doc_07 or Doc_08, it reaches the Representative by that route instead. I traced two cases directly:

- **Syriac Iḥidaya.** The Capsule Core's "The Language You Think In" does carry the christological weight ("It shares its root with the very word that names Christ as the Only-Begotten, so that your own single-heartedness is never merely your own achievement standing apart from his") — but that content is already in the chunk's **World Meaning**. The Ecological Function's *distinct* contribution (the qyama dependency) did not travel.
- **Desert cheironaxia.** The capsule carries the village-entanglement tension at line 54 — but traceably from Doc_08 Force 2A-i and Doc_07, not from the chunk. Line 70 carries cheironaxia itself with only the World Meaning content.

So the section is producing real work at reasonable quality, into a slot nothing downstream reads — and what survives does so by redundancy with the ecology documents, not by the lexicon route.

## 4. Forces as bidirectional, or purely causal

### The template makes bidirectionality structurally inexpressible

`L4-Templates\[world-code]_Forces_Document.md` mandates three layers for every force in every cell:

- **Layer 1 — Historical Event:** what happened.
- **Layer 2 — World's Own Experience:** how the world *received* it.
- **Layer 3 — Formation Impact:** "**How this... force shaped** the formation ecology."

Every layer runs force → world. There is no layer, anywhere, for the world's response changing the force. The single forward-time concession is Cell 2A's instruction to note "if impacts shifted as the force shifted" — force changes, therefore impact changes; still one-directional.

The connective apparatus is the same. Section 4 defines its notation explicitly: "'Force 1A-1 → Force 2B-2' means an initiating external force **produced** an ongoing internal response." Section 5 asks only "how this force **shaped, produced, or sustained** this gravity." I enumerated every arrow in all six Doc_08s: **every one runs force→force or force→gravity.** None runs gravity→force.

And "bidirectional," where the corpus uses the word, means **index bookkeeping only** — whether a Force Index row displays both its incoming and outgoing arrows. Syriac's Doc_08 revision log: "The Force_Index.xlsx 'Force Index' sheet's Cross-Cell-Connections column was not applied bidirectionally in every row." Never world-force reciprocity.

### Four entries that break the mold — and what happened to each

**1. Desert — the strongest, and it works.** Force 2B-ii's Layer 3: "the very intellectual achievement that made this force so formative for Strand C **also made it structurally narrow in exactly the way that would later prove consequential**." Then Force 3A-i's Layer 3 closes the loop: "this external force's severity is directly explained by Cell 2B-ii's own internal finding: gravity 9's structural narrowness... **made it a legible, isolable target for external condemnation** in a way that the broader, more diffusely-attested gravities were not." The synthesis at 3B-i is explicitly **conjunctural**: "an internal structural vulnerability... combined with an external trigger... that **specifically exploited** that vulnerability. Neither force alone explains the outcome; their interaction does." The gravity table calls it "the clearest cross-cell case in this document."

**2. Syriac Connection 8 — the most telling, because of the apology attached.** Force 1B-2 (Early Qyama Institutional Commitment) → Force 2B-1 (Authority-Structure Ambiguity). The entry itself concedes the schema cannot hold it:

> "**This is a gravity-to-gravity relationship (Confirmed Gravity C2 reshaping Confirmed Gravity C4) rather than a force directly acting on another force**, but it is carried here as a named connection between the two forces that respectively initiate and sustain those gravities."

The only world-thing-reshapes-thing relationship in the entire Doc_08 corpus had to be smuggled in as a force-to-force connection — **and it was missed entirely in the first draft.** A Round 1 reviewer caught it as a gap: the relationship "was never surfaced in this document."

**3. HAL 3A-2 — where the classification rule actively demoted it.** The 416 Pelagian mob attack: "This world's own prior doctrinal position-taking is part of why this particular external force found this particular target — **a genuine, secondary observation retained here even though it does not override the actor-origin classification above.**" Round 2 review had moved this force from Cell 3B (Internal) to 3A (External) on an "actor-origin criterion," which forced the world's own causal contribution into a subordinate clause.

**4. IJC Connection 3 — the only backward-in-time arrow in the corpus.** Force 2A-2 (Western fragmentation) → Force 1A-1 (the alliance), "**reshaping the earlier force's own later meaning** for Strand A specifically." The Force Index row for 1A-1 records "← 2A-2 (**reshaped by, later**)." Still force→force rather than world→force, but it is the only case of a later force reaching back to alter an earlier one.

(Syriac's Connection 4 is the mirror image, one direction over: "An initiating internal commitment is **measurably changed in degree** (though not in kind) by a sustained external force acting on it later.")

### The vocabulary you're describing already exists — one document upstream

This is the most directly actionable finding for your hypothesis. From `CiC_L3B_Formation_World_Construction_Framework_V7.3.docx`, the Interaction Test, verbatim:

> "Can this gravity's relationship to each other identified candidate gravity be specifically described as **reinforcing, competing, or reshaping** — or does it merely coexist alongside them without demonstrated interaction?"

**"Reshaping" is the Framework's own word for what you're describing.** It lives in Doc_04's Interaction Matrix and nowhere else in the pipeline.

- **Syriac's matrix carries a genuinely reciprocal cell**: C4 row → C6 "Reshaping (persecution reshaped the succession's own continuity)"; C6 row → C4 "Reshaping." Symmetric in both directions.
- **Alexandria has the most sophisticated notation in the corpus**: "T4 ↔ C2 (**C→X**)" — a relationship that *changes type over time* (competing, then reshaping toward memory after Nicaea). Plus "C5 ↔ T2: X" and "C2 ↔ T3: X."
- HAL carries 5 reshaping cells, including a symmetric G3↔G5(M) pair.
- Desert carries zero — it uses different vocabulary entirely.

Three further facts about it:

1. **It self-organized.** `Doc_04_Gravity_Discovery_Template_V1.0.md` — written *after* four worlds were built, because Doc_04 was "the least structurally supported step in the pipeline" — records: "**this is the one structural element all four prior worlds converged on independently even without a template**; this section simply codifies existing good practice." The interdependency instinct arose in every world without being mandated.
2. **The template requires coverage but never defines the vocabulary.** It mandates "full pairwise coverage" and says nothing about reinforcing/competing/reshaping — which is why Desert doesn't use the terms at all.
3. **It does not travel.** "Reshaping" reaches a Representative-side document in only 2 of 6 worlds (Alexandria Phase 2, Syriac Phases 1/2/5). It appears in **zero Permanent Prompts and zero World Capsule Cores.**

And it is expensive to maintain in its current form: W1's Doc_04 Interaction Matrix took **seven review rounds**, with rounds 4, 5, and 6 each finding the prior round's declared-complete re-derivation still incomplete. W1's own standing note warns: "Doc_08 (which compiles a comparable matrix) should not treat this document's discipline as a solved template."

## Summary of what the evidence shows

1. **Lenses partly worked, unevenly, and were never standardized** — the two governing instruments contradict each other on whether the lens set is fixed or world-derived, Step 7 is the only uncited step in the Framework, and reviewers caught lens-redundancy findings in four of six worlds. HAL's Doc_07 diagnoses its own five lenses as one fact refracted five ways, and that self-diagnosis was promoted into its World Profile. The lens synthesis's only stated downstream purpose survives in 2 of 6 worlds; Alexandria's newer build deliberately deleted it.

2. **Voice failures are overwhelmingly organization failures, not evidence failures.** Genuine evidence gaps appear as *correctly handled* almost everywhere; only IJC's Leo sparsity is named as a gap that caused a failure. The dominant named cause is material that was built, reviewed, indexed, and cleared — and then not reachable at speech time. The Desert leaking jug (right story, right file, right capsule, name detached from image) and Syriac's Simeon bar Sabbae (one narratable martyrdom sitting in an adjacent folder while the prompt pointed grief at six unnarratable names) are the two cleanest instances of exactly the pattern you named.

3. **Ecological Function delivers real insight about 68% of the time and is read by nothing downstream.** The string appears in zero Permanent Prompts, zero Capsule Cores, and zero Representative-construction documents; the Representative Construction Framework names the lexicon only as an input title; the Deployment Index doesn't even track the section's presence; and review checks it for existence but never for quality. Related-Terms — the same class of dependency information, but structured and indexed — travelled and got used.

4. **Forces are written one-directionally because the template has no slot for anything else,** and the four entries that broke through did so by apologizing for it (Syriac), being demoted by a classification rule (HAL), or being caught only by a reviewer (Syriac again). But the interdependency vocabulary you're describing is already in the project under the Framework's own word — **"reshaping"** — living in Doc_04's Interaction Matrix, self-organized across all six worlds before any template required it, and carried into neither Doc_08 nor any runtime artifact.
