About This Tradition

Audio Brief 1:00

Read along

Three separately founded bodies share this entry, but not a confession. Each is described from its own published texts. The Way International began in 1942 as Victor Paul Wierwille's Vesper Chimes radio ministry. It took its later name in 1955. His Power for Abundant Living appeared in 1971, and Jesus Christ Is Not God in 1975, a title that states its own position. Sun Myung Moon founded the Unification Church in Seoul on 1 May 1954. It was later known as the Family Federation for World Peace and Unification. Moon's Divine Principle appeared in Korean in 1966. Eusebio Joaquín González founded La Luz del Mundo in Guadalajara on 6 April 1926. From 1964 a second apostle led it, in succession from him.

Documented Stories

Two Thousand Couples and a Tax Trial

On 1 July 1982, in Madison Square Garden, Sun Myung Moon and his wife Hak Ja Han blessed 2,075 couples in a single ceremony, most of whom had been matched by Moon himself, many of them meeting their partners only days before. Identical white gowns and dark suits filled the arena floor; the couples answered his questions in unison and were sprinkled with holy water as they passed the platform. The Blessing is the Unification Church's central sacrament, and this was its largest performance yet outside Korea. Six weeks earlier, on 18 May, a federal jury in Manhattan had convicted Moon of conspiracy and of filing false tax returns for the years 1973 to 1975, over some $150,000 of interest on bank accounts held in his name which the defense argued belonged to the church. He was sentenced to eighteen months, and after appeals served thirteen, at the federal prison in Danbury, Connecticut, from July 1984 to August 1985. What surprised observers was who came to his side. The National Council of Churches, the Southern Baptist Convention's agencies, the Presbyterian Church, Jerry Falwell and the American Civil Liberties Union, among some forty organizations, filed amicus briefs on his appeal, arguing that the government had intruded on how a religious body holds its funds. They said plainly that they rejected his theology.

Legacy and Today

Why It Is on the Tree

Three separately founded movements share this entry. The Way International grew out of Victor Paul Wierwille's radio ministry in the 1940s. Sun Myung Moon founded the Unification Church in Seoul in 1954. La Luz del Mundo was founded in Guadalajara in 1926, and from 1964 a second apostle led it in succession from its founder. Each has its own scripture, founder claim and history, told here on its own terms.

Legacy

All three were still active in 2010. La Luz del Mundo had one change of leader, in 1964. The Way had two after its founder. The Unification Church was still led by its founder. Court records were part of these bodies' public record too. The Way's leadership changes ran alongside litigation, and in 1982 a federal jury convicted Sun Myung Moon of filing false tax returns.

Sources

On the Nicene Creed

Each of these three movements is assessed on its own published confession and claims of authority, not as one group. Each body's own teaching made its own claim for its founder's authority. On their own texts up to 2010, none of the three affirmed the Trinity or Christ's divinity as the historic creeds meant them.
