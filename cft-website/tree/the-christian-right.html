The Christian Right

A movement across churches

About This Movement

Synthesized voice — not a recording.

Jerry Falwell, Tim LaHaye, Charles Stanley, D. James Kennedy and Greg Dixon founded the Moral Majority on 6 June 1979. It named four principles of its own: 'pro-life, pro-family, pro-morals, and pro-American'. From its base in Washington, it worked in politics for those aims. In August 1980 Falwell called it 'totally political', not a religious group. Its backers, he said, were not all evangelicals. That month Ronald Reagan spoke in Dallas, at a briefing run by another group, the Religious Roundtable. He told the crowd, 'I endorse you and what you're doing.' The Moral Majority closed in 1989. That same year Pat Robertson founded the Christian Coalition, with Ralph Reed as its executive director. Reed went on to found the Faith and Freedom Coalition in 2009.

Grew Out Of

  • Fundamentalism Dominant Modern Reconstruction Major — Jerry Falwell and other ministers founded the Moral Majority in 1979. Historians such as Daniel K. Williams trace its roots back to the fundamentalists. This rests on their books.
