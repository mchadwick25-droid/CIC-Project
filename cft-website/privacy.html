Privacy

No cookies, no accounts

The tree sets no cookies. It has no accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.

Counting visits

We count visits without cookies. We use Cloudflare's own traffic numbers and its cookie-free Web Analytics. These counts do not identify anyone.

Cloudflare also hosts the site.

What your browser keeps

The map remembers which switches you turned on or off. That choice is saved in your own browser only. It is never sent to us.

Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Typefaces

The page typefaces load from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google a request when a page opens.

The feedback bar

Pressing "This helped" sends only the name of the page.

Sending a report sends the page, your reason and your text. It sends an email address only if you choose to give one.

"Tell us what you think" sends the page and your text. It sends a first name or email only if you choose to give them.

Feedback goes to Church in Conversation's server, run by the same team. It carries no visitor ID and no cookie. We use it to fix and improve the tree.

Links and gifts

A link to Church in Conversation takes you to that site. Your browser tells it only that you came from the Church Family Tree site. It does not say which page.

Gifts are paid on Stripe's own pages. Stripe holds your name, email and payment details. We do not receive your card details.

Questions

Write to info@churchinconversation.com with questions. You can also ask us there to delete a report you sent.
