plain_meaning: A Christian widow who refuses to remarry and takes up a strict, devout life. This
  was the path of Paula, Marcella, and Fabiola.
world_word: vidua
false_friend:
- a merely negative state - the absence of remarriage (it was an actively chosen vocation with its
  own discipline and, for at least one of these women, real recognized standing)
senses:
  informational: 'A widow of this rank faced real pressure to remarry - family expectation, social
    convention, sometimes a specific insistent suitor. To refuse, and take up fasting, plain dress,
    and scriptural study instead, was its own distinct renunciation: a life already lived once, deliberately
    not repeated.'
  evidential: All three widows' accounts are Jerome's own epitaph genre; the category itself and
    its social pressure are corroborated by the wider evidence for aristocratic Christian widowhood
    in this period.
  personal: The self-mastery this asked - continentia - was not practiced in the abstract. It was
    lived out one refusal at a time, against a family's plans and a suitor's persistence.
  translational: 'Not a nun in the later sense: no vows to an order, no rule, no habit - a household
    discipline, chosen and socially costly.'
quick_meaning: A widow who refuses to remarry and lives a plain, studious, praying life.
