THE MOST CONSEQUENTIAL ATTRIBUTION CAUTION IN THIS CORPUS: this is the one
text transmitted under the women's own names, and it is precisely the text
scholarship holds Jerome composed for them. It must never be cited as the
women's authentic voice. Its evidentiary value is real but different: it
shows how the community presented the women's voice publicly, in Jerome's
prose. See hal.contested.ep46-authorship and hal.limit.f5-women-own-words.
