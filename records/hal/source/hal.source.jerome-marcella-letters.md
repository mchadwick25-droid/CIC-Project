XXIII, XXIV, XXV, XXVI, XXVII, XXVIII, XXIX, XXXII, XXXIV, XXXVII, XXXVIII,

apparatus on authenticity or dating, which remains consult-only future work.

Evidentiary weight: these letters are the volume evidence behind Marcella's
scriptural seriousness (a dozen-plus letters answering her questions on
Hebrew words, textual variants, and disputed passages within roughly two
Rome years) - corroborating for her learning; her independent post-385
standing still rests on Ep. 127 alone.
