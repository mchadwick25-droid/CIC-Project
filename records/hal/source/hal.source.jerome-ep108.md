Work presence verified directly: div2 'To Eustochium.' n=CVIII at file line
20791. Formally titled Epitaphium Sanctae Paulae in the manuscript tradition
(confirmed by the prior build's addendum review for THIS letter; the same
formal title was NOT confirmed for Ep. 127, which is epitaph-genre in
function only).

Standing cautions, carried at full strength: single-source for nearly
everything it reports; literary epitaph genre (idealization, didactic
purpose); words placed in Paula's mouth are Jerome's construction
(Inferential-Thin as her actual speech); the Nitria passage attests a
specific encounter with Bishop Isidore and an unnamed monastic multitude,
while its famous desert-father names are a rhetorical flourish, not a
reception roster (Arsenius did not reach the desert until c. 394, after
Paula's visit); daily-life and schedule detail drawn from this letter is
Dominant Modern Reconstruction at best when generalized. The scale of
Paula's fortune and the completeness of her self-impoverishment are
Contested (genre amplification vs. fact) - see
hal.contested.paula-renunciation-totality.
