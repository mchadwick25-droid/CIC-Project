THIS IS THE DOCUMENT THE QUARREL IS ABOUT, and this world held every
later round of it without holding this. Its registry already carries
Rufinus' Apology to Apronianus, his Apologia ad Anastasium, Jerome's
Apology Against the Books of Rufinus, and Anastasius' letter to John of
Jerusalem - four texts arguing about a preface that was not in the
corpus. It is now.

WHAT RUFINUS ACTUALLY DID IN IT, in the file's own words. He opens by
praising Jerome's preface to the Homilies on the Song of Songs, quotes
its most extravagant compliment to Origen, and then observes that Jerome
"promises, indeed, in that very preface" to give Roman readers the Song
of Songs commentary and much else of Origen, and did not - "finding
greater pleasure in compositions of his own." Naming a friend's
unfulfilled promise while praising him is the provocation, and the ANF's
own inline gloss identifies the unnamed "brother and colleague" as
Jerome.

THE SECOND HALF IS THE SUBSTANTIVE CHARGE. Rufinus states that he has
smoothed passages he believed heretics had corrupted - which is what
Jerome went after, and what makes the Latin De Principiis a text about
its translator as much as its author. This world is built around a
literary quarrel between two men formed together at Aquileia; it can now
read the sentence that started it rather than only the shouting
afterwards.
