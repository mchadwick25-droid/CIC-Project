Ep. 112 verified directly: div2 'To Augustine.' n=CXII at file line 22428.

The rare two-sided exchange in this world's record: both correspondents'
own letters survive independently (see hal.source.augustine-letters for
Augustine's side in his own collection). Documented throughout; the dispute
is substantive on both sides - Augustine's resistance to abandoning the
Septuagint's received authority was a serious churchman's objection, not
obscurantism, and records drawing on this source must not flatten him into
a foil.
