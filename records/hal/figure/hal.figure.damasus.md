Figure of the world's initiating and crisis forces (hal.force.jerome-
arrival, hal.force.clerical-precarity). The correspondence between Jerome
and Damasus on scriptural questions (Epp. 15-36 range; the pre-382 items
are pre-horizon) shows the commission relationship in action. His patronage
was of a different kind than Paula's - clerical protection, not material
funding - and the record keeps the two distinct: when Damasus died the
protection failed; Paula's funding did not.
