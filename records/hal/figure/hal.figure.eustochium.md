The last resident leader of the women's institution - her death, whichever
year it falls in, is half of the world's own closing boundary (the other
half Jerome's; the two cluster too tightly and too uncertainly to rank -
hal.contested.chronology). No text of hers survives, but one is ATTESTED:
Innocent's Ep. 137 answers the report she and the younger Paula sent after
the 416 attack - written, received, lost (hal.search.womens-own-texts).
Claims that she helped complete or circulate the translation work are
Inferential-Thin and not carried as fact. Palladius's 'fifty virgins' is
the only extant headcount for her community and is uncorroborated.
