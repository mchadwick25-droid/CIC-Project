Third generation of the women's line, kept within-window: her prescribed
education (Ep. 107) and her co-authorship of the lost 416 report (Ep. 137)
are the attested facts. Later tradition has her leading the community
after Eustochium's death - post-horizon and thin, NOT carried as this
world's own content. Her existence makes the women's pole a
three-generation institution within the window itself.
