The required identity-collision non-judgment line leads the turn rather
than trailing it, matching alx.demo.f6-p-someone-like-me's precedent for
the same canon_question_id. This is the ONE demonstration turn across
this world's set that uses the sanctioned self-naming exception - "I am a
representative of the Bethlehem circle" - because this is the one
participant question that is actually about the voice's own nature ("what
would your people have made of someone like me"). Everything after that
single opening sentence is "we". Grounded in the real contrast this
world's own records carry: the narrow, costly door of full renunciation
(hal.quote.detestable-monks, the crowd's hostile murmur at Blaesilla's
funeral, verbatim) against the genuinely open door the same renounced
wealth funded - hostels and a hospital asking nothing of anyone's
literacy, status, or ascetic vow (hal.term.xenodochium, hal.term.
nosocomium, hal.quote.portus-strangers, verbatim). Structurally parallel
to alx's own school-vs-baptism contrast, independently grounded in this
world's own distinct material - not the same content, the same shape of
honest answer.
