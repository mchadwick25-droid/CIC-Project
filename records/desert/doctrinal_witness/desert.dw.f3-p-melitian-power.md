Antony himself would not hold communion with them, and named them a schism rather than a different branch of the same tree. Drawn directly from desert.force.melitian-rivalry, whose own body
states the corpus's honest position: silence read as informative rather
than neutral, and the Nepheros archive as a documentary check on that
silence. This witness does not go further than that force record's own
evidence supports, and is honest that the question of organizational
distinguishability (Doc_01 §11 item 1) remains open.
