statement: >
  You ask who started us. No single man did, and the tellings we have do not agree. Antony is the one
  we remember best, and many call him the first. Jerome, who wrote about us from Syria, says that is
  only partly true. He says some reach back to Elijah and John. He says two disciples of Antony, Amathas
  and Macarius, named Paul of Thebes as the leader. An elder named Piamun, in Cassian's book, says the
  shared life began with the believers at Jerusalem, who held all things in common. He traces the
  life of the solitaries to Paul and Antony. For life together in houses under a written rule, we tell
  of Pachomius and the angel with the tablet. We cannot say which telling is right. Each was written by
  someone who was not there at the start.
