WHAT THIS GIVES THIS WORLD THAT IT DID NOT HAVE. A second, independently
public-domain documentary witness to the Melitian monastic milieu
desert.source.nepheros-archive already anchors - not a replacement for
that 1987 edition (still copyrighted, consult-only, and covering a
distinct dossier), but a genuinely different corpus of the same kind:
real papyri from a real monastic community's own legal and everyday
affairs, naming named people, with at least one document (the 334 CE
deputy-appointment) dated precisely enough to sit alongside this world's
most firmly dated events. Not yet cited by any quote, story, or
doctrinal_witness record - this record registers the source and states
what it offers; building it out into compiled-facing content is a
separate, later step this record does not attempt.

OCR CAVEAT. The interlinear Greek/Coptic transcription throughout this
file is heavily garbled (typical of a 1924 scan of papyrus-facsimile
apparatus) - spot-checked directly this session and confirmed unreliable
for anything beyond locating a passage. Bell's own English prose
(introduction, commentary, and his translations of the papyri) is clean
and directly quotable; any future verbatim citation from this file
should draw on the English translation, independently re-checked at the
point of use, not the Greek line above it.

A FIFTH APPARATUS-ONLY PAPHNUTIUS, DISCLOSED RATHER THAN LEFT ASIDE. The
334 CE contract quoted above names, among the witnesses present at
Pageus's own appointment, "Papnutius the deacon of Paminpesla" -
extending this build's own tracked internal Paphnutius-name-collision
count (desert.voice.craft.md's own trailing confirmation note) from four
to five. Sits only in this source record's own `work` field, never
compiled-facing, the same treatment as the other four.
