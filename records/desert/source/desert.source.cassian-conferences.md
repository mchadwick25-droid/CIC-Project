Named-elder discourses here (Abba Moses on the goal of the monk, Abba
Isaac on prayer, etc.) are Cassian's literary reconstruction of teaching
he heard c. 385-400, published c. 426-429 - NOT transcripts. Attribution
discipline for quote records: a Conferences quote is voiced as Cassian's
record of the elder, never as the elder's own verbatim words. Edition
check 2026-08-21: all twenty-four Conferences are present as divisions in
the vendored file (a drafting-pass claim that the NPNF edition omits
Conference XII was checked against the file and found wrong at the
division level); whether Gibson left individual passages untranslated
inside the chastity/nocturnal-illusions material is NOT yet verified -
check at first citation of those conferences rather than assuming either
way.
