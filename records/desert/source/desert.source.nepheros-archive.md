Why a Melitian archive sits in a Nicene-majority world's corpus: it is
the period's best documentary control on what the literary sources
idealize - real letters about money, goods, and legal standing from
real monks. It anchors the economic-embeddedness force (with Goehring
and Kellia) and keeps the Melitian presence visible rather than letting
Athanasius's opponents vanish from a record his tradition dominates
(Doc_02 SS6, axis 3).
