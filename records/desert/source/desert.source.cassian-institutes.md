Rights verified 2026-08-21 from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public
Domain). Divisions located: Works of John Cassian line 14912, Institutes
line 16537.

Cassian is a genuine participant-observer (roughly fifteen years among the
Egyptian ascetics, mostly Scetis) AND the clearest instance of the
transition Doc_01 SS2.4 names as exit from this world: teaching gathered
into stable written form addressed to outside/later audiences. Weight
corroborating, not load-bearing: what Cassian systematizes (especially the
Evagrian eight-faults scheme, transmitted without Evagrius's name) is
evidence for the teaching he received, filtered through two decades,
another language, and a Gallic monastic program. Fleet note: this volume
was opened and worked during the earlier Syriac-era passes (repo history
"Cassian pass: a vendored corpus nobody had opened").
