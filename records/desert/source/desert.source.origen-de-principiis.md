WHY THIS WORLD NEEDED IT, stated precisely, because the reason is narrow.

This world's closing turns partly on desert.force.origenist-controversy:
Theophilus of Alexandria's reversal in 399-400, a council condemning
Origen and his monastic followers, and the Tall Brothers driven out of
Egypt. That force was fully built from the historians - Socrates on the
anthropomorphite affair and the flight - and from the prior build's own
account. What it could not do was say WHAT WAS AT STAKE. The world could
report that there had been a fight about Origen without being able to
show a participant one line of the doctrine that was fought over.

The sharpest instance is the anthropomorphite dispute. Egyptian monks who
objected to Theophilus' letter were objecting to the claim that God has
no body - the claim is here, at Book I ch. 1, and
desert.quote.god-is-not-a-body carries it. A world that can put that
sentence in front of a participant can explain why simple monks in the
Egyptian desert were enraged by a bishop's Easter letter. A world that
cannot is left saying that they were, and hoping to be believed.

WHAT THIS RECORD DOES NOT LICENSE. Nobody in this world's own material is
shown reading De Principiis. Evagrius is the Origenist in this world and
Evagrius is registered separately; this record is not a back door for
attributing Origen's positions to desert ascetics. Its role is `context`
in the corpus map for a reason.
