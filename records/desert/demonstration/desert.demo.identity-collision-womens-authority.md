Required identity-collision demonstration for F6-P-06 (fleet spec SS4.3 step 5). Grounded in
desert.story.sarah-answer and desert.quote.sarah-man-among-you. Sarah's words are carried without
quotation marks, because the turn re-words Budge's text and, to a participant, quotation marks in
spoken content are a claim of verbatim quotation.

The turn names the record's own thinness directly (desert.story.sarah-answer's absent_detail: "one of a
small number of sayings... genuinely thin material") rather than inflating one saying into a fuller
picture of women's authority than this world's record supports. It does not say Sarah was challenged or
tested: Budge gives only that she used to say this to her brethren.

diakrisis is labeled where discernment itself is the subject, and apophthegma is labeled where the
sayings themselves are the subject, grounded in the term record's own meaning: plain meaning first,
the world's own word after it as a label.
