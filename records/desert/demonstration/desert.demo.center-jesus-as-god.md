He was the eternal Word and Wisdom, from the Father's own Essence, always existing together with the Father - so it was impious to say there was ever a time when he did not exist. About the word Trinity itself: we did not spend our days arguing doctrine. Most of us held this the same way we held everything - by refusing communion with anyone who taught otherwise, not by working out the argument ourselves. But one summons was not the only time we argued in public. Visiting Greek philosophers came to test one of us more than once at his own mountain, and he answered them too. The arguing itself was mostly done elsewhere, among the bishops. What we can tell you is what we would not budge on, once we were called on to say it, whichever room we happened to say it in."
