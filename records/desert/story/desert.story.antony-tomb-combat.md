text: >-
  The tradition shows Antony shutting himself inside an abandoned tomb, to face what total
  combat against the interior enemy could mean at its furthest edge. Demons came to him there in
  the night, in a crowd, and beat him until he lay on the ground unable to speak. A friend found
  him and carried him to the village church, where people sat around him as around a corpse. But
  at midnight Antony revived, and asked to be carried back to the same tomb. The demons came
  again, now shaped like wild beasts filling the space around him. He answered them: if there
  were any real power in you, one of you would have been enough. The Lord has made you weak, so
  you come at me in numbers. Attack if you can. And if you cannot, why trouble me for nothing?
  At the worst of it, a light broke into the tomb. The demons vanished, and the pain left him at
  once. He asked the vision where it had been at the start of his struggle. The answer: I was
  here, but I waited to see your fight. Now that you have endured and were not overcome, I will
  always be your help.
telling: "Athanasius writes that Antony went to the tombs, which lay some distance from the village. He asked an acquaintance to bring him bread at long intervals. Then he entered a tomb, and the man shut the door on him.\n\nOne night the enemy came with a crowd of demons and beat him until he lay speechless. Next day the acquaintance found him lying as though dead and carried him to the village church. Kinsfolk and villagers sat around him as around a corpse.\n\nAbout midnight Antony came to himself. Everyone was asleep except the acquaintance, so he motioned with his head and asked to be carried back to the tombs without waking anybody.\n\nAlone again, he lay unable to stand. That night the demons seemed to break through the walls in the likeness of lions, bears, leopards, serpents and wolves. Antony mocked them: \"If you had any power, one of you would be enough. But the Lord has made you weak, so you try to frighten me with numbers.\"\n\nThen the roof seemed to open, and a ray of light came down. The demons vanished and the pain stopped. Antony asked, \"Where were you? Why did you not appear at the start, to end my pain?\" A voice answered: \"Antony, I was here, but I waited to see your fight. You have endured and have not given way. So I will always help you, and I will make your name known everywhere.\""
