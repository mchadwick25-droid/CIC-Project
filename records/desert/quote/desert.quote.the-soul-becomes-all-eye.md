The most famous image in the Macarian corpus and the clearest single sentence of the affective
tradition: the goal is not a cleared mind but an indwelt and illuminated one. Set beside
desert.quote.the-nous-beholds-its-own-radiance, which is Evagrius on the same territory, the contrast
is the whole point of vendoring both - Evagrius has the nous behold its OWN proper radiance, this has
the soul irradiated by Another and become his seat. Neither record harmonises them.
