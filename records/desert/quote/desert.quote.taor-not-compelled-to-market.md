  The square brackets are Budge's, supplying the objects: beautiful apparel, a veil, and sandals,
  which she refuses. A modern reader is likely to hear ascetic self-denial - giving up nice things.
  She is saying something more specific and more practical. Clothes and shoes are what you need in
  order to be seen in public; she does not go out, so she does not need them. Thirty years in one
  house, in rags, at her work at all hours, is the reason she gives, and it makes the refusal a
  consequence of enclosure rather than a mortification chosen for its own sake. Note too that the
  other women of the house DID go down to the church each week and she did not: she is the
  exception within her own community, not its rule.
