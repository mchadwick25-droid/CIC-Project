Verified verbatim 2026-08-22 against the vendored file, the same
division (SS68) desert.force.melitian-rivalry already cites for the
adjacent Melitian material. This record is the corpus's own emic
instance of doctrinal boundary-drawing by refusal (Antony's rejection
of Melitian communion) and, in the same section, his driving away of
Arians who came to test him.

SS69, immediately following in the same file, has Antony summoned by
the bishops to Alexandria to publicly teach against the Arians, stating
the Nicene position in positive terms; that material is carried by the
companion record desert.quote.antony-nicene-formula, not this one.

speaker_or_author names the narrator (Athanasius), since the compiled
text is his third-person narration of Antony's action ("he drove
them..."), not Antony's own reported speech - matching the convention
desert.quote.pachomius-angel-tablet and desert.quote.arsenius-flee-
tace-quiesce use for a non-standard speaker; the associated-with
relation to desert.figure.antony is kept, since the action narrated is
still his.
