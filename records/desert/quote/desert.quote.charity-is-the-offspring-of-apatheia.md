The chain run in reverse - charity from apatheia, apatheia from the ascetic life, that life from the
commandments, the commandments watched by the fear of God, that fear born of right belief - and then
the turn: belief itself is "an indwelling good which exists naturally even in those who have not yet
believed in God."

That last clause bears directly on desert.dw.grace-and-effort. Cassian's Conference XIII argues the
grace-and-effort question from cases; this says something structurally stronger and stranger, that
the starting point is already given to everyone. The two are not the same position and this record
does not harmonise them.
