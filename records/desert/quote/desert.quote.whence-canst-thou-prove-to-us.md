modern_lens_note: >-
  'This is so' points back two sayings: the brethren have just been told the monastic life has three orders - beginners, half-perfect, perfect - and here they ask him to PROVE it. Two things in the answer. Where the proof comes from: 'the words of our Lord in the Gospel', with no appeal to a rule, an office, or a council. And how it is read: the parable of the sower is not about farming and not about four kinds of listener - the three failed soils are people in the world, and the three yields are the three grades of monk. A reader who wants the plain sense will not find it here. The text is being made to answer the question the community actually had.
