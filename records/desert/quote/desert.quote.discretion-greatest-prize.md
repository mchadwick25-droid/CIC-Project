THIS RE-GROUNDS A GRAVITY FROM UNREADABLE TO VERBATIM.
desert.gravity.diakrisis rested on desert.source.apophthegmata-patrum,
which has no vendored edition, so nothing under it could be quoted at
all. Abbot Moses on discretion is the same teaching, from a man Cassian
sat with at Scetis, and it is in the corpus and always was.

AND THE CHAPTER THAT FOLLOWS IS AN APOPHTHEGM IN EVERYTHING BUT NAME.
Cassian goes straight on: "while I was still a boy, in the region of
Thebaid, where the blessed Antony lived, the elders came to him to
inquire about perfection" - a gathering of elders putting a question to
Antony and arguing it out. That is the form of the Sayings, recorded by
a Latin writer whose text this world can read. The Apophthegmata remains
the richer and more various corpus and the fetch list still wants it;
what changes is that this world is no longer mute until it arrives.
