WHY THIS IS FLAGGED, NOT JUST ADDED. The vendored file's own endnote at
this passage cites Tertullian's De Corona Militis and a fragment of
Irenaeus for the same no-kneeling custom in general early-church usage,
outside any monastic context. This world's own sources do not show the
Nitria/Kellia/Scetis communities observing this as their own distinctive
practice - Cassian reports it as Egyptian-monastic, but the custom's own
history is wider than this world. Named here so this quote is not
mistaken for evidence of a practice unique to this world's own
communities.
