WHAT THIS MOVES. desert.term.logismoi rested on the Vita Antonii and on
two unreadable sources - Evagrius, who has no vendored edition, and the
Apophthegmata, which has none either. The eight-thought scheme is the
core of this world's psychology and the world could not quote a list of
it. Now it can, from an abbot of Scetis, named, in a text it has held
all along.

AND THE DIVERGENCE NOTE IS THE INTERESTING PART. Cassian is transmitting
Evagrius' scheme and does not say so anywhere. He was writing in the
years after Origenism had been condemned and Evagrius' name had become
dangerous, and he took the material west without the attribution. So the
one readable witness this world has to its own psychology is also a
witness to how a movement handles a teacher it can no longer name - which
is a fact about the desert worth being able to show, not a defect in the
source.
