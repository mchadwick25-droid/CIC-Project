The desert answer to the effort question, stated as a refusal of the dichotomy rather
than a choice between its horns. Chaeremon's own worked examples in this chapter are the
point: Paul the persecutor and Matthew the publican were drawn while wanting the opposite
(grace first), while Zacchaeus and the thief on the cross "by their own desires brought
violence to bear on the kingdom of heaven" (will first). Neither pattern is made to
govern the other.
