The whole architecture in one sentence: faith, fear of God, continence, patience and hope, apatheia,
love, knowledge of nature, theology. desert.term.apatheia and desert.term.theoria both carried this
scheme with a "(consult-only)" locus and could not show it. This is the showing.

Two things are worth noticing against the modern expectation. Love is not the summit - it is the door
to what comes after. And apatheia is not the goal either; it is what love is born from. A reading
that stops at apatheia, which is how the word usually reaches a participant, has stopped one step
before the thing the step was for.
