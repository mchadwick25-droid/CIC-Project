This world's own account of what the Cross did, argued rather than asserted: the old
oracles fell silent, the knowledge of God spread, celibacy became thinkable, and death
stopped being the thing that could make a person do anything. The last of those is the
hinge between this world's Christology and its practice, and nothing in the corpus
carried it until now.

The ellipsis marks omitted argument about the relative fortunes of the two religions,
which is polemic rather than Christology; the sentences kept are consecutive with each
other in SS79.
