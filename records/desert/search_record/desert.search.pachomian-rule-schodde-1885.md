CLEARED 2026-08-27. Every condition this record set on itself has been
met: the file is vendored (cic/texts/pachomius_rules-ethiopic_schodde1885.txt),
its rights are read from its own text rather than asserted, and its
relationship to the Praecepta is stated in desert.source.pachomian-corpus
- it has none directly, being Englished from Dillmann's Ethiopic, with
Schodde saying in his own introduction that he was not able to consult
the Latin. Records may now quote it, under the recension discipline the
source record sets out.

WHAT THE FILE ACTUALLY TURNED OUT TO BE, against what this record
expected. It expected a secondary version at some remove - "a real
limitation" that "must ride in every locus". That is still true and
still rides. But two things in the file were not anticipated:

  - The Rule text is 4,420 words of legislation in three parts, not an
    excerpt or a summary. Part I is the angel's ordinance; Part II the
    practical horarium and the women's house across the river; Part III
    a late Ethiopic homily on ten symbolic associations that survives in
    no other language at all. Schodde ranks their authority differently
    and so must this world.
  - The angel-and-tablet frame is INSIDE the rule document. This world
    had been describing that frame as Palladius' and Sozomen's
    hagiographic packaging around a rule reported from outside. It is
    not; it is how the received legislation opens. The correction is
    carried in desert.source.pachomian-corpus.

WHAT THE EARLIER SEARCH GOT WRONG, kept here because the shape of the
error is worth remembering. desert.search.pachomian-rule-english-pd
returned not_found, and it was not a shortage of public-domain material
that produced that verdict - it was the question. It asked for a
pre-1929 printed monograph translation of Jerome's Latin or the Coptic.
The translation that existed was of the Ethiopic, and it was in a
periodical. A search that names its base text and its format in advance
can only find what it already imagined.
