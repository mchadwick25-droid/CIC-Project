Three searches from different directions returned only in-copyright moderns: Benedicta Ward, The Sayings of the Desert Fathers: The Alphabetical Collection (1975, Cistercian) - the standard, already carried here as consult-only; John Wortley, Give Me a Word (SVS Press, 2014); Wortley, The Anonymous Sayings (Cambridge, 2013); Wortley, More Sayings (Cambridge). WHAT IS PUBLIC DOMAIN, AND WHAT IT IS NOT: Budge, The Paradise of the Holy Fathers (1907), complete but the SYRIAC recension of 'Anan-Isho'; and J. O. Hannay, The Wisdom of the Desert (London, 1904), on archive.org, public domain by date but explicitly a SELECTION and also from the Syriac. Neither is the Greek alphabetical arrangement. FIGURE WORTH RECORDING: the Apophthegmata attributes forty-seven sayings in total to the three ammas - Theodora, Sarah and Syncletica. The ammas' whole surviving corpus is that small, which is why its accessibility decides this world's gender axis outright rather than marginally. DO NOT VENDOR THE WARD PDFs that circulate on parish and institutional websites. They are the 1975 Cistercian translation, in copyright, posted without a licence; using one would be infringement and would also put unverifiable text into a corpus whose whole point is that wording can be re-checked. Ward stays what she already is here: consulted, never quoted."

THIS CLOSES A ROUTE RATHER THAN OPENING ONE, and that is worth having on
file. Two of this world's three "no edition exists" verdicts turned out
on 2026-08-27 to be badly-phrased queries (see
desert.search.pachomian-rule-schodde-1885 and
desert.search.evagrius-dysinger-pd). This one was asked from three
angles - by collection name, by manuscript tradition, and by the ammas'
own names - and it holds.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ACQUISITION CASE. Budge vol. 2 remains the only
complete public-domain sayings corpus in English and is still worth
fetching. But it is the Syriac recension, and whether it carries the
ammas is UNVERIFIED - the first thing to check when a file lands is
whether Theodora, Sarah and Syncletica are in it. If they are not, this
world's gender axis cannot be closed from any public-domain source that
presently exists, and the honest limit at
desert.limit.f5-women-own-words becomes permanent rather than pending.
