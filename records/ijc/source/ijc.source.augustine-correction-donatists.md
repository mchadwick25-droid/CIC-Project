Rights verified from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public
Domain). The work's div2 opens at line 19266 inside the div1 "Writings
in Connection with the Donatist Controversy" (line 9984). Passages
verified directly at line 19617 (the compulsion argument and Augustine's
account of his own earlier view) and line 19704 ("we constrain them to
come in to our communion by the violent authority of the laws").

THIS FILLS A NAMED HOLE IN A PRIMARY GRAVITY.
ijc.gravity.orthodoxy-enforcement carries citation_specificity B and a
divergence note saying that the mechanism is Documented while the
content "rests more heavily on modern reconstruction (Hanson) than on
surviving self-testimony." Its three sources are two church historians
and a collection of creeds. It had no first-person defence of the
mechanism by anyone who actually used it. This is that document: the
most influential theologian of the Latin church arguing, in his own
voice, that the church may use the state's coercive power against
schismatics, and grounding it on Luke 14:23.

WHAT MAKES IT UNUSUALLY HONEST EVIDENCE. Augustine does not pretend the
compulsion is anything else. At 19704 he states the Donatist complaint
in their own terms and concedes its factual content - that Catholics
constrain them "by the violent authority of the laws" - and disputes
only the motive imputed. And at 19617 he records that before the laws
came into Africa it seemed "to certain of the brethren, of whom I was
one," that no one should be compelled. A man describing his own change
of mind on the use of force is a better witness to what the alliance did
to the church than any narrative of it from outside.

HOW IT MUST BE CARRIED. Not as this world's endorsement. The argument
here is the intellectual foundation of centuries of religious coercion,
and it is written by a figure the tradition venerates; a record set that
quoted it without saying so would be doing the thing this project exists
not to do. It is assigned as this world's own voice because it IS this
world's own voice - that is the point, and the discomfort belongs to the
participant, not to the source record.
