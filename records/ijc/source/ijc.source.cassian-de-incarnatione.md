WHAT MAKES IT THIS WORLD'S RATHER THAN A THEOLOGY SOURCE. The doctrine
is not why it is here. The commissioning is. Cassian addresses the
dedicatee by name in the preface - "my dear Leo, my esteemed and highly
regarded friend, ornament that you are of the Roman Church and sacred
ministry" - and the ANF's own note quotes Gennadius (De Vir. Illust.
lxi) that "at the request of Leo, then archdeacon of Rome and afterwards
Bishop, he wrote seven books against Nestorius." Both are at the locus
and were read there.

THE PATTERN IS THE POINT. Before any council had ruled, the Roman
archdeaconate reached into Gaul to have a Latin refutation of the
bishop of Constantinople written to order, and the man who commissioned
it became the pope whose Tome the Council of Chalcedon would receive
twenty-one years later. This world already carries ijc.figure.leo and
ijc.story.tome-that-would-not-bend for the second half of that; this is
the first half, and it shows the see of Rome acting as a commissioning
authority in an Eastern dispute two decades before its most famous
intervention.

CARRIED AS `corroborating`, not load-bearing: the primacy-claiming
gravity does not depend on this document, and the dating of the request
rests on Gennadius rather than on Cassian's own words. What Cassian
supplies directly is the dedication.
