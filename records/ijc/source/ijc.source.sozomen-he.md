Rights verified 2026-08-21 from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public
Domain). Translator verified from the volume's own front matter
("Chester d. Hartranft", line 21654).

Read alongside, not instead of, Socrates. Because no public-domain
English translation of the Theodosian Code itself is available to this
build (ijc.search.theodosian-code-english, not_found), Sozomen VII.4 is
this world's registered narrative witness for the Edict of Thessalonica
(380) - the law's existence and content are Documented in the
scholarship; this build's own citation path for its text runs through
Sozomen's report, and records citing the edict carry that distinction.
Same standing post-Nicene retrospective bias as Socrates (legacy Doc_02
SS2); more openly panegyrical toward the reigning house (his work is
dedicated to Theodosius II).
