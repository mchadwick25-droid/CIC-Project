WHY THIS APPEND: the binding Homoian-recentering obligation (Step 0
SS4.1) requires treating Homoian theology as a considered position, not
a caricature - but the one piece of Homoian self-testimony in the
legacy Registry (Auxentius on Ulfila, row 23) has no public-domain
English translation (ijc.search.auxentius-ulfila-english, not_found).
De Synodis is the closest vendorable witness: a contemporary quoting
the actual creed formulae of the Homoian-era councils at length, in
public-domain English - hostile in allegiance (pro-Nicene) but
conciliatory in method (written to persuade Gallic bishops that the
Eastern formulae were not all simple blasphemy). Used for the
formulae's content and for the era's texture; never as Homoian
self-testimony, which it is not. Hilary belongs natively to no other
world in the current portfolio; boundary checked against the legacy
Registry's exclusion rows before adding.
