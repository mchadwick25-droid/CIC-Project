  There my mother, Thy handmaid, bearing a chief part of those cares and watchings, lived in prayer. We, still unmelted by the heat of Thy Spirit, were yet moved by the astonished and disturbed city. At this time it was instituted that, after the manner of the Eastern Church, hymns and psalms should be sung, lest the people should pine away in the tediousness of sorrow; which custom, retained from then till now, is imitated by many, yea, by almost all of Thy congregations throughout the rest of the world.

A world asked whether its practices came from the apostles has, in this one case, an honest and different answer: this one started in Milan, in 386, and everyone copied it.

this same passage - ijc.dw.ancient-custom for "a practice's beginning honestly dated",
ijc.limit.f5-ordinary-day for "the single congregational interior".

Monica is kept in the excerpt deliberately. The one interior scene this world can offer of ordinary
people at worship has a named woman keeping vigil in it, and cutting to the institution of hymnody
would lose the only person in the room.
