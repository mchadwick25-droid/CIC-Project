WHY THIS QUOTE AND NOT A WARMER ONE. This world's registry is otherwise
almost entirely composed of people who approved of the alliance, writing
from inside it - Eusebius above all, whose figure record this world
carries. Augustine is inside it too and takes it apart: every measure of
imperial success the Christian empire congratulated itself on is
conceded to the pagans in one sentence, and what is left is a list of
restraints. It is the period's own answer to its own triumphalism, and
without it this world only argues with the alliance from the outside
(Ambrose) or not at all.
