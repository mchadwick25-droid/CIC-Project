Verified verbatim against the vendored file at npnf104 line
19617. The two sentences are consecutive in the file with one
clause removed between them - after "find fault with being compelled"
the file continues "but consider whether they be so compelled," followed
by three sentences on the supper as the unity of the body of Christ and
on the Donatists' own compulsions, and then the chapter break at
"Chapter 7.—25." The gap between the two sentences is marked with an
ellipsis in the `text` field; the first sentence's dash spacing matches
the source's unspaced em dash ("hedges—that is... schisms—are").

The second sentence truncates at the verbatim point - "...the madness of
the Donatists was..." - with a real ellipsis marking the cut. The clause
that would continue it ("that although the madness of the Donatists was
to be checked, it should be by other means") is a paraphrase, not
Augustine's wording, and lives in confidence.divergence_note instead of
the quoted text. Verified character-for-character against the vendored
file.

WHY THIS BELONGS TO THIS WORLD AND NOWHERE ELSE.
ijc.gravity.orthodoxy-enforcement is a PRIMARY gravity whose own
divergence note concedes that its content rests more on modern
reconstruction than on surviving self-testimony, and whose three sources
were two church historians and a collection of creeds. This is the
missing thing: the mechanism defended in the first person by the man
using it.

AND THE SECOND SENTENCE IS WHY IT IS NOT PROPAGANDA. Augustine records
that he had held the opposite view, and names himself among those who
held it - "of whom I was one." The record of a change of mind about the
use of force, written by the man who changed it, is better evidence for
what the alliance did to the church than any account of it from outside.
At line 19704 he goes further and concedes the Donatists' own
description of the facts, that Catholics constrain them "by the violent
authority of the laws," disputing only the motive.

CARRIED WITHOUT ENDORSEMENT, and the world's own framing must keep it
that way: this argument is the intellectual foundation of centuries of
religious coercion in the West. It is here because it is this world's
own voice, which is exactly the point, and the discomfort is the
participant's to have rather than the record's to manage away.
