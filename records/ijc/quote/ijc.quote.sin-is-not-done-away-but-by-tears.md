modern_lens_note: >-
  Asked how to forgive someone who is not sorry, this world's answer is that it did not, and would not claim God does either. The man addressed had ordered thousands killed in a stadium and was not yet repentant, and Ambrose - who owed him his position and says in the same letter that he loves him - closed the altar and waited. The hard clause is the last one: not even the Lord 'forgives any but those who repent.' That is a real answer and a limited one. It says what the sinner owes; it does not say what the wronged person is supposed to do while waiting, and this world's record does not contain that page.
