Text verified verbatim against the vendored file 2026-08-21 - the
Creed of 325 proper (not the fuller 381 form), with the edition's
inline Greek glosses and bracketed alternates omitted per its own
bracket conventions ("[from heaven]", "[we believe]" are the editor's
marks; the parenthetical Greek after "begotten" and "one substance"
likewise). The world's central confessed answer to who Jesus is - the
words the century's whole enforcement contest was about.
