The "yes, mostly" translational texture: a flat refusal would be wrong
(the doctrine of a real change in the elements is plainly taught, at
length, to the newly baptized) and a flat acceptance of the modern term
would overclaim (this world's own record never uses "transubstantiation"
or its later scholastic apparatus of substance and accidents). The turn
holds both halves rather than collapsing to either. Content is
ijc.dw.f1-t-bread-made-body's own text and quoted material (De
Mysteriis IX, already verified verbatim against the vendored corpus in
that record), carried into spoken form unchanged. We-voice throughout;
not an identity-collision cell.
