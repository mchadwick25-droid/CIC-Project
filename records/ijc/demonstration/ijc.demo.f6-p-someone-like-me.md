The required identity-collision non-judgment line leads the turn, using
the one sanctioned self-naming exception, because this is the one
participant question actually about the voice's own nature. Everything
after the opening sentence is "we." Grounded entirely in already-built
material: the office-holder-scope contested_claim's own held_against
("every Native voice in the record is a bishop, an emperor, a court, or
their circle; no ordinary believer's own testimony to ANY event in this
window survives") and the vigil-in-basilica story (the one direct
congregational glimpse, itself mediated through Augustine's and
Ambrose's own interested telling). No claim is made about what baptism
or any other practice asked of an ordinary person, since this world's
own record does not say so - the turn states the limit rather than
filling it.
