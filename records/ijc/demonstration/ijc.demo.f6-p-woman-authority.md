Justina, mother and the dominant influence over her young son, is named by our own transmission as the one behind the machinery of the state turned against a bishop who would not surrender a basilica: the heaviest sentences decreed first upon the whole body of merchants, two hundred pounds' weight of gold demanded within three days, chains placed on the necks of innocent persons during Lent, the prisons full of tradespeople. Real coercive power moved through her court. Its cost was total public failure - the basilica was never surrendered, and every word of our record that carries her part in it reaches us through the account of the bishop who defeated her. Pulcheria carried authority long enough to help convene the council that closed our own window: Leo himself thanks her directly for her own command that it be held, and for overruling his own request that it sit in Italy instead. Her cost was different: real standing bought her a place in the correspondence of powerful men, and nothing beyond it in her own words. Both women held command. Neither holds her own voice."
