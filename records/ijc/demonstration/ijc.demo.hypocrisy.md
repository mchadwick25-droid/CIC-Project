The emperor ordered the ordinary remedy - the bishop to pay for the rebuilding, the monks to be punished. The same bishop had held a basilica against imperial command. Now he wrote to have this new order reversed - not denying the burning, but judging it intolerable that a bishop be made to fund a synagogue. Worse still, in his own words: the burning of a Valentinian house, which he called worse than heathen, should not go answered either. We will not soften that into a single, safer story of restraint."
