NARROWED 2026-08-27. This record claimed its writers read scripture "as men
arguing for the church's faith, not as men telling us what the reading cost
them," and cited exactly one source - ijc.source.augustine-confessions -
which is the source that falsifies the second half of that sentence.
Confessions III.5 is a man saying precisely what the reading cost him, and
VIII.12 is scripture reached for in a crisis and opened at random. Both are
now opened as quotes and named in nearest_material.

The same defect this build found twice in desert on the same day: a limit
scoped its search to less than the world holds, and here the file it had not
read was the one file it cited. What survives is narrower and more useful -
the ORDINARY reader is still missing, and Augustine is the least ordinary
reader in the corpus.
