One partial route was checked and is REJECTED ON REASONING, recorded so
it is not re-proposed as an easy win. Aquinas' Catena Aurea quotes the
Opus Imperfectum extensively, and Newman's 1841 English translation of
the Catena is public domain. That is technically a public-domain English
channel to some of this text. It should not be used as non-Nicene
self-testimony: those are excerpts selected by a thirteenth-century
Dominican, for scholastic purposes, under the false Chrysostom
attribution - the losing side's voice arriving pre-filtered and
re-labelled by the winning tradition. That is the exact transmission
pathology Doc_02 sec 7 exists to name, and using it here would deepen
the problem while appearing to close it.

Bearing on this world's own gravities: none. The work is a Matthew
commentary, not a witness to imperial doctrinal enforcement, and even a
vendorable copy would not touch the Doc_04 Candidate 3 Cross-Check
divergence, which concerns what the Homoian establishment confessed
rather than what a later Arian exegete wrote about Matthew.
