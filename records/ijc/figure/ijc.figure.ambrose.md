Strand C's whole documented substance. Author Gravity per the reviewed
Doc_02 SS2, carried on every use: self-authored accounts of his own
confrontations, with independent corroboration (Augustine) for the 386
standoff only; McLynn (1994) is the referenced skeptical corrective to
his self-presentation. The competency-transfer point is carried with
Doc_05 SS1's own correction: his civil-to-episcopal path was
exceptional, not a world-level rule - what generalizes is that the
skills this world's authority structures reward are administrative and
rhetorical. The bee-swarm infancy legend is Paulinus's (Tier 3, told
only as attributed tradition - ijc.story.bees-of-milan). His
predecessor at Milan, Auxentius, held the see as a Homoian appointee
for roughly two decades - the Homoian establishment was local history,
not rumor, for his congregation. (Distinguish that elder Auxentius of
Milan from the Auxentius of Durostorum whose letter on Ulfila is the
Homoian self-testimony fragment, and from the Auxentius of the 386
sermon's title - a Homoian bishop present at court in 386; the name
recurs on the Homoian side and conflation is a real hazard.)
