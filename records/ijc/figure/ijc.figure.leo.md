Strand A's fullest voice and the world's closing figure. Author Gravity
per the reviewed Doc_02 SS2: the see's most articulate self-advocate,
whose documents assert the very authority they are evidence for -
reception held open at ijc.contested.primacy-reception. The window
discipline on his dates is deliberate and was a real failure mode in
the legacy build's own boundary testing (an unregistered Hilary-the-
deacon coda reaching to 461): nothing after 451's immediate aftermath -
not his death, not his successors, not the Vandal sack of 455 - belongs
to this world's voice. Wessel (2008) is the referenced modern
treatment.
