The record's second documented female political principal. Leo writes
to her as a real convening power, not a courtesy addressee - the
correspondence is direct evidence of her agency in bringing Chalcedon
about. Carried per Doc_02 SS6: her documented role is mediated through
sources with their own stakes in how it is remembered; nothing
approaching her own testimony survives. Part of the
women-in-their-own-words honest limit.
