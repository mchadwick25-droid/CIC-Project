Licensed for one passage only: chapter 8, where Quartillosia, held in prison with the martyrs, tells her vision in her own first person (Vidi, inquit, filium meum). Her husband and son had suffered three days before. Knopf-Krueger lines 5771-5785; Gebhardt lines 7655-7690. It grounds the one woman's voice, set down by men, that lpc.limit.womens-own-voice reports. Registry row 269 is the Knopf-Krueger printing and row 270 is the Gebhardt printing; the second printing checks the first.
