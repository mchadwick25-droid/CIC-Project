Grounded directly in Cyprian, Epistle LXXII to Jubaianus, section 21 (ANF numbering; Oxford and Hartel 73), and On the Unity of the Church, section 6 -- verified against the vendored files at the lines above. The quoted phrases are the ANF English word for word. An ANF editor's bracketed note after "out of the Church," in Epistle LXXII, which says the maxim "has been terribly misunderstood and cruelly abused," is not Cyprian's and is left out. On the Unity of the Church section 6 lies outside sections 4 and 5, the passage whose two recensions are the subject of lpc.contested.de-unitate-recensions. The sources give no date for either passage in this record. The Latin of Epistle LXXII is OCR text with misreads.
