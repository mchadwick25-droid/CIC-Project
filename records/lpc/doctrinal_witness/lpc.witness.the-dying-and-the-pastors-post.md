positions:
- Honoratus, a bishop in Mauritania, asked whether bishops should flee in the dangers of those years.
  Augustine answered that a bishop sought out by persecutors may flee, if others stay behind. But when
  the danger falls on all alike, he held, those who depend on their ministers must not be left. Ministers
  who flee to save themselves, not their people, leave the people without the ministry their life
  depends on.
- One reason he gave was the dying. When danger reaches its extreme, he wrote, a crowd of both sexes and of all
  ages gathers in the church, "some urgently asking baptism, others reconciliation, others even the doing
  of penance." If the ministers are absent, people leave this life "either not regenerated or not loosed
  from their sins." If they are at their posts, "all are aided,—some are baptized, others reconciled to
  the Church. None are defrauded of the communion of the Lord's body."
