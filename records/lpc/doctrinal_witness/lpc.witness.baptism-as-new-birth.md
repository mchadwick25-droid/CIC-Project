positions:
- Cyprian wrote to Jubaianus about people who come to the church from heresy. They ought to be baptized, he
  said, so that, prepared "in the lawful, and true, and only baptism of the holy Church, by divine
  regeneration, for the kingdom of God," they "may be born of both sacraments." He gave his reason from
  Scripture, because it is written, "Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the
  kingdom of God."
- So the word for what baptism did, in our bishop's own letter, was regeneration, a birth. He wrote it
  where he argued that baptism outside the church could not give it.
tensions:
- The letter is about whether baptism outside the church counts. It does not describe how the birth was
  felt, what a candidate understood by it, or what it did for a child. It does not say what the two
  sacraments are.

- The record gives no account of how the birth was felt or understood; the speaker must not supply one.
