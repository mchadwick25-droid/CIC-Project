Grounded directly in Cyprian, Epistle LVIII to Fidus, sections 2 and 5 (ANF numbering; Oxford and Hartel 64), and Augustine, Enchiridion, chapters 43 and 93 -- verified against the vendored files at the lines above. The quoted phrases are the ANF and NPNF English word for word. An ANF editor's bracketed note with verses by George Canning sits inside section 5 after "according to Adam,"; it is not Cyprian's and is left out. The letter's first section gives the council's number, "sixty-six"; the sources give no date or place for it. The Latin is OCR text with misreads. No year for the Enchiridion is stated here, and none is stated by the vendored sources for this world.
