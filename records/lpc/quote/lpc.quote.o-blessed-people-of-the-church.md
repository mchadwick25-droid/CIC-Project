text: O blessed people of the Church, who as well in sight as in feeling, and, what is more, in outspoken
  words, suffered with such a bishop as theirs; and, as they had ever heard him in his own discourses,
  were crowned by God the Judge! For although that which the general wish desired could not occur, viz.,
  that the entire congregation should suffer at once in the fellowship of a like glory, yet whoever under
  the eyes of Christ beholding, and in the hearing of the priest, eagerly desired to suffer, by the sufficient
  testimony of that desire did in some sort send a missive to God, as his ambassador.

modern_rendering: O blessed people of the Church! In sight and in feeling, and what is more in words spoken
  aloud, they suffered with such a bishop as theirs. As they had always heard him say in his own sermons,
  they were crowned by God the Judge. The common wish was that the whole congregation should suffer at
  once, sharing a like glory. That could not happen. Yet some, under the watching eyes of Christ and in
  the hearing of the bishop, truly longed to suffer. Through that longing, a fit witness, each of them
  sent a kind of letter to God, as his envoy.
