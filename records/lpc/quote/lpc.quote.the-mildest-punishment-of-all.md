Augustine, Enchiridion, chapter 93 (chapter XCIII in the Latin text). English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf103_augustine-holy-trinity-doctrinal-moral-treatises.xml, lines 22998-23007. Latin (Bruder, 1838) at cic/texts/augustine_doctrina-christiana-enchiridion-lat_bruder1838.txt, lines 11867-11875 (OCR text, with misreads; the Latin prints Mitissinia for Mitissima). The passage is the whole of chapter 93. In the Latin, Mitissima sane omnium poena is 'the mildest punishment of all' and praeter peccatum, quod originale traxerunt is 'apart from the original sin they drew with them'. For infants, see chapter 43 (English at lines 21502-21507; Latin at lines 10419-10425), which says that in baptism 'infants die only to original sin; those who are older die also to all the sins which their evil lives have added to the sin which they brought with them'. The passage carries the witness lpc.witness.infants-and-the-sin-of-another.
