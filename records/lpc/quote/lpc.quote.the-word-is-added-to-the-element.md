  The word is added to the element, and the sacrament comes to be. It is as if it too were a kind of visible

Latin at cic/texts/augustine_in-ioannis-evangelium-tractatus-lat_migne-garnier1902-t3-pt2.txt, lines 37171-37176 (OCR text). Section 3 opens with John 15:3 itself, 'Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you', which is left out, and everything after 'a kind of visible word' is left out. In the Latin, Detrahe verbum, et quid est aqua nisi aqua? is 'take away the word, and what is water but water?'. The passage carries the gravity lpc.gravity.sacramental-ordination-validity.
