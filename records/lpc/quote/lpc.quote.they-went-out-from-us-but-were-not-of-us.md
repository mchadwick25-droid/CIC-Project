Augustine, Homily 3 on the First Epistle of John (NPNF Homily III), section 5. English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf107_augustine-homilies-john-soliloquies.xml, lines 14469-14472. Latin at cic/texts/augustine_in-ioannis-evangelium-tractatus-lat_migne-garnier1902-t3-pt2.txt, lines 48396-48407 (OCR text). The sentences before the passage in section 5 are left out, and so is everything after 'because they were not grain', which says the final sorting comes at the fanning of the threshing-floor in the day of judgment. The editor's note at line 14471 reads 'Two mss. Benedictionem Eucharistiae' and is not part of the passage. In the Latin, grana is 'grain' (the OCR runs several words together in this stretch), and the sacraments named are Baptismum, Benedictionem and Eucharistiam.
