modern_lens_note: A modern listener may not know 'professest'. The Latin, confiteris, is to declare openly;

Augustine, Sermon 8 in the NPNF numbering (58 in the Maurist numbering), section 13. English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf106_augustine-sermon-mount-harmony-gospels-homilies.xml, line 11171. Latin at cic/texts/augustine_sermones-1-340-lat_migne-pl38.txt, lines 27719-27724 (OCR text; the sermon starts at line 27271). The sentence before the passage, at line 11170, tells the candidate to say the Creed on rising and before sleep, and gives the reason that repetition keeps forgetfulness out; it is left out. The sentence after it, on the Apostle's 'unclothed' and 'naked', is cut off by notes at lines 11172-11177 and is also left out. In the Latin, Commemora fidem tuam is 'call your faith to mind', which in context (commemorate vos ipsos, PL 38 line 27716) means reciting the Creed to oneself; tanquam speculum is 'like a mirror'; confiteris is an OCR reading ('condieris'), and the sense and NPNF both require it. The sermon carries the gravity lpc.gravity.preaching-and-catechesis in its catechetical form.
