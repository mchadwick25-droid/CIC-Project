text: Let it be the office of love to feed the Lord’s flock, if it was the signal of fear to deny the

Augustine, Tractate 123 on the Gospel of John (NPNF Tractate CXXIII), section 5. English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf107_augustine-homilies-john-soliloquies.xml, line 13429. Latin at cic/texts/augustine_in-ioannis-evangelium-tractatus-lat_migne-garnier1902-t3-pt2.txt, lines 46148-46154 (OCR text). The first sentence of section 5, on the Lord asking three times and the threefold confession answering the threefold denial, is left out; so are the sentences after 'pleasing God', which explain the words 'Lovest thou me? Feed my sheep' and list the 'lovers of their own selves'. In the Latin, Sit amoris officium, pascere dominicum gregem is 'let it be the duty of love to feed the Lord's flock'.
