Augustine, Our Lord's Sermon on the Mount, Book II, chapter XVIII, section 61. English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf106_augustine-sermon-mount-harmony-gospels-homilies.xml, lines 2888-2889. The first sentences of section 61 are left out: the statement that there are two things in which we must beware of rash judgment, and the two examples, the man excused from fasting by his stomach and the manifest glutton and drunkard. The passage is the closing sentence of the section. No Latin of De sermone Domini in monte is vendored in the Library, so no Latin lines are given and the English was not checked against the Latin. The passage carries the gravity lpc.gravity.penitential-discipline in its form of reproof that keeps hope of a return.
