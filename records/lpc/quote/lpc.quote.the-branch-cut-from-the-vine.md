  given outside the Church's unity is not repeated when a person comes in; what comes only inside is the

  not stay with a person's limb when it is cut off from the body.

Augustine, Sermon 21 in the NPNF numbering (71 in the Maurist numbering), section 32. English at cic/texts/npnf106_augustine-sermon-mount-harmony-gospels-homilies.xml, lines 12936-12937. Latin at cic/texts/augustine_sermones-1-340-lat_migne-pl38.txt, lines 32254-32262 (OCR text; the sermon starts at line 30996). 'Wherefore' points back to the sentence at lines 12926-12932 about baptisms among schismatics and heretics. That sentence and the next read: 'the washing of the flesh is not repeated in their case. For "this form of godliness" was not wanting to them even when they were without; but there is added to them "the Unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace," which cannot be given but within.' Translators' notes break the first of them, and another sentence and a note stand between them and the passage, so they are not part of it. In the Latin, forma pietatis and virtus pietatis are the form and the power of godliness (2 Timothy 3:5), and corporalia Sacramenta are the bodily sacraments. 'In them' (in eis) could mean the separated people or the sacraments, and both the English and the rendering keep that open. The sermon carries the gravity lpc.gravity.sacramental-ordination-validity.
