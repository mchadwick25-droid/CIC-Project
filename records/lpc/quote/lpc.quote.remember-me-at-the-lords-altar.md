Augustine, Confessions, Book IX, chapter XI, section 27. English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf101_augustine-confessions-letters.xml, lines 14192-14195. Latin (Knoell, CSEL 33) at cic/texts/augustine_confessiones-lat_knoll-csel33.txt, lines 14839 and 14889-14890; the page's apparatus and a page break lie between those lines and are not part of the passage. The words are Monica's as Augustine remembers and reports them. Monica's death is dated 387 by the chronological table in the vendored NPNF volume (cic/texts/npnf101_augustine-confessions-letters.xml, lines 3258-3260); Confessions IX.xi.28 (line 14225) says she died in "the fifty-sixth year of her age, and the thirty-third of mine." The sentences before them in section 27, on her fever and on her words 'shall you bury your mother', are left out, as is the sentence after. In the Latin, ponite hoc corpus ubicumque is 'lay this body anywhere' and ut ad domini altare memineritis mei is 'that you remember me at the Lord's altar'. The passage carries the witness lpc.witness.remembered-at-the-altar.
