Cyprian, Epistle LII in the ANF numbering (Epistle LVI in the Oxford edition and in Hartel, CSEL 3), to Fortunatus and his other colleagues, section 2, the clause 'when it was decided in the council that penitents in peril of sickness should be assisted, and have peace granted to them'. English in the ANF translation at cic/texts/anf05_hippolytus-cyprian-caius-novatian.xml, lines 34210-34211. The words before it, 'For', and the words after it, on those who ought to receive peace first, are left out. Latin (Hartel, CSEL 3) at cic/texts/cyprian_epistulae-lat_hartel1871-csel3-tei.txt, lines 8585-8586. In the Latin, cum in concilio placuerit paenitentiam agentibus in infirmitatis periculo subueniri et pacem dari is 'since it pleased the council that penitents in peril of sickness be helped and peace be given'. The passage is read with lpc.quote.before-even-a-deacon. The passage carries the witness lpc.witness.peace-for-the-dying-penitent.
