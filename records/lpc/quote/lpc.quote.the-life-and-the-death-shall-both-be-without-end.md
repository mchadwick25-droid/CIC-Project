text: After the resurrection, however, when the final, universal judgment has been completed, there shall
  be two kingdoms, each with its own distinct boundaries, the one Christ’s, the other the devil’s; the
  one consisting of the good, the other of the bad,—both, however, consisting of angels and men. The former
  shall have no will, the latter no power, to sin, and neither shall have any power to choose death; but
  the former shall live truly and happily in eternal life, the latter shall drag a miserable existence
  in eternal death without the power of dying; for the life and the death shall both be without end. But
  among the former there shall be degrees of happiness, one being more pre-eminently happy than another;
  and among the latter there shall be degrees of misery, one being more endurably miserable than another.

modern_rendering: After the resurrection, when the final judgment of all has been completed, there will
  be two kingdoms. Each will have its own clear boundaries. One is Christ’s, the other the devil’s. One
  is made up of the good, the other of the bad. Both are made up of angels and human beings. The first
  will have no will to sin, and the second no power to sin. Neither will have any power to choose death.
  The first will live truly and happily in eternal life. The second will drag out a wretched existence
  in eternal death, without the power of dying. For both the life and the death will be without end. Among
  the first there will be degrees of happiness, one happier than another. Among the second there will
  be degrees of misery, one more bearable than another.
