text: Know, nevertheless, that I am placed in the midst of a great tribulation; and, as if you were present
  with me, I remember your former love day and night, God only knows. And therefore I ask that you will
  grant my desire, and that you will grieve with me at the (spiritual) death of my sister, who in this
  time of devastation has fallen from Christ; for she has sacrificed and provoked our Lord, as seems manifest
  to us.

modern_lens_note: 'A modern listener may hear ''the death of my sister'' and assume she had died. She had not. The translator

  that could still be grieved and pleaded over.'
modern_rendering: All the same, know that I am in the middle of great distress. Day and night I remember the love you
  showed me before, as if you were here with me. Only God knows it. So I ask you to grant my wish, and
  to grieve with me over the spiritual death of my sister. In this time of ruin she has fallen away from
  Christ. She has offered sacrifice and provoked our Lord, as it seems plain to us.
