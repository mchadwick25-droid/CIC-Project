  who shall not inherit the kingdom of God, may be daily perpetrated, and daily atoned for by almsgiving.

Augustine, Enchiridion, chapter 70 (chapter LXX in the Latin text). English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf103_augustine-holy-trinity-doctrinal-moral-treatises.xml, lines 22327-22335. Latin (Bruder, 1838) at cic/texts/augustine_doctrina-christiana-enchiridion-lat_bruder1838.txt, lines 11218-11227 (OCR text, with misreads; the footnotes after the chapter are not part of it). The passage is the whole of chapter 70. A scripture-reference note after 'license to sin,' sits inside the range and is left out. In the Latin, non ad hoc emendus quodam modo, ut ea semper liceat impune committere is 'not to be bought, as it were, so that those sins may always be done with impunity'.
