  not to claim it, since belief is given by the Father.

  My Father.”

The sermon was delivered at the Table of the martyr Cyprian (sermon heading, lines 19865-19867). A page-break tag (NPNF p. 502) falls inside line 19899 and is stripped. Section 3 goes on at lines 19900-19915 with Philippians ii. 12-13; that is cut by notes and is left out. The section's next clause in the Latin, Gaudeat, quia datum est ('let him rejoice that it has been given'), would complete the thought but is outside the passage. The Latin passage follows an editor's chapter heading at PL 38 lines 51151-51152. In the Latin, Mirum est is 'it is strange', arroget sibi is 'claim it for himself' and datum fuerit is 'has been given'. The sermon carries the gravity lpc.gravity.grace-and-human-incapacity.
