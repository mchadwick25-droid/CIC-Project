Cyprian, To Demetrianus (An Address to Demetrianus, ANF Treatise V), section 24, from 'What will then be the glory of faith?' to 'end to their torments.'. English in the ANF translation at cic/texts/anf05_hippolytus-cyprian-caius-novatian.xml, lines 46246-46253. Latin (Hartel, CSEL 3) at cic/texts/cyprian_ad-demetrianum-lat_hartel1868-csel3-tei.txt, lines 914-919. The section number '24.' at the start of the English is not part of the passage. Section 23 before it, in which Christ 'will call down on them that believe not, eternal punishments in the fires of Gehenna', and the sentences after it, on souls and bodies and the persecutor seen for ever, are left out. In the Latin, cremabit addictos ardens semper gehenna is 'an ever-burning Gehenna will burn up the condemned' and nec erit unde habere tormenta uel requiem possint aliquando uel finem is 'nor will there be any source whence they may at any time have respite or end to their torments'. The passage is read with lpc.quote.no-repentance-is-too-late, which speaks of the time still left. The passage carries the witnesses lpc.witness.no-salvation-outside-the-church and lpc.witness.punishment-without-end.
