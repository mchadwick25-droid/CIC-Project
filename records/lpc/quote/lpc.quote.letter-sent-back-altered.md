modern_rendering: I have also read another letter. It did not clearly state who wrote it or who it was written to. In

  removed or changed from the original. So I have sent the letter back to you just as it reached me. That
  way you can check whether it is the very same one you gave to Crementius the subdeacon to carry.

Epistle III in the Ante-Nicene Fathers numbering. Cyprian sends back a letter that seems altered so the Roman clergy can compare it with the one given to the sub-deacon Crementius.
