text: Afterwards there broke out a dreadful plague, and excessive destruction of a hateful disease invaded
  every house in succession of the trembling populace, carrying off day by day with abrupt attack numberless
  people, every one from his own house. All were shuddering, fleeing, shunning the contagion, impiously
  exposing their own friends, as if with the exclusion of the person who was sure to die of the plague,
  one could exclude death itself also. There lay about the meanwhile, over the whole city, no longer bodies,
  but the carcases of many, and, by the contemplation of a lot which in their turn would be theirs, demanded
  the pity of the passers-by for themselves.
