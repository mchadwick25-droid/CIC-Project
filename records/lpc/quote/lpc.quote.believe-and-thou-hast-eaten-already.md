Augustine, Tractate 25 on the Gospel of John (NPNF Tractate XXV), section 12. English in the NPNF translation at cic/texts/npnf107_augustine-homilies-john-soliloquies.xml, lines 4496-4497. Latin at cic/texts/augustine_in-ioannis-evangelium-tractatus-lat_migne-garnier1902-t3-pt2.txt, lines 20107-20111 (OCR text). The passage is the middle of section 12. The sentences before it, which quote John 6:27-28, and everything after 'Believe, and thou hast eaten already', are left out. In the Latin, Ut quid paras dentes et ventrem? is 'why do you make ready teeth and stomach?'. The passage carries the gravity lpc.gravity.grace-and-human-incapacity through its reading of believing as 'the work of God'; the sentences just after it state the point more plainly and are not in the passage.
