modern_rendering: The councils held in each district and province must themselves give way, beyond all doubt, to the
  authority of full councils. Those full councils are formed for the whole Christian world. And even among
  full councils, the earlier ones are often corrected by those that follow them. This happens when some
  real experience brings to light things that were hidden before, and what once lay hidden becomes known.
  And it happens without any storm of sacrilegious pride, without any arrogant puffing up of the neck,
  and without any strife of envious hatred. It happens simply with holy humility, catholic peace and Christian
  love.
