A joint letter sent by Fortunatus, Alypius and Augustine in 400. The text quotes Matthew 16:18. The letter then names the bishops of Rome in order, thirty-seven names from Linus to Siricius, and ends the list with Anastasius as the present bishop. The ellipsis stands for the thirty-three names the source gives between Anacletus and Siricius, from Evaristus to Damasus.
