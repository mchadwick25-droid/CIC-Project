  afterwards his daughter, with the anxious consideration of affection, sought for the corpse of her father,—was

  care of love, looked for her father's body. He was found half dead, and he was pulled out and revived.
  He was left behind against his will, parted from the companions he himself had sent on ahead. But as
  we see, the reason he was left behind was so that the Lord could add him to our clergy. It was also
  so that the Lord could grace our presbyters with glorious priests. Their ranks had been left bare because
  some had fallen away.
