text: God continually makes His sun to rise, and from time to time gives showers to nourish the seed,
  exhibiting all these kindnesses not only to His people, but to aliens also. And if a man professes to
  be a son of God, why does not he imitate the example of his Father? “It becomes us,” said he, “to answer
  to our birth; and it is not fitting that those who are evidently born of God should be degenerate, but
  rather that the propagation of a good Father should be proved in His offspring by the emulation of His
  goodness.”

modern_lens_note: 'A modern listener may hear ''aliens'' as beings from elsewhere and ''degenerate'' as
  depraved. Here ''aliens'' are strangers, people outside the church, and to be ''degenerate'' is to fall
  short of one''s birth. Pontius is reporting the sermon Cyprian preached in the plague: God is kind to
  outsiders, so His children must be too.'
modern_rendering: God makes His sun rise without ceasing. From time to time He gives rain to feed the
  seed. He shows all these kindnesses not only to His own people, but to strangers too. If a man claims
  to be a son of God, why does he not follow his Father's example? “It is right for us,” he said, “to
  live up to our birth. Those who are clearly born of God should not fall short of their birth. Instead,
  the good Father's line should be proved in His children, as they strive to match His goodness.”
