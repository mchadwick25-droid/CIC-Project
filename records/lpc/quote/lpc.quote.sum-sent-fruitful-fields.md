text: Finally, we give you the warmest thanks that you have wished us to be sharers in your anxiety, and
  in so great and necessary a work—that you have offered us fruitful fields in which we might cast the

  from this heavenly and saving operation. We have then sent you a sum of one hundred thousand sesterces,
  which have been collected here in the Church over which by the Lord's mercy we preside, by the contributions
