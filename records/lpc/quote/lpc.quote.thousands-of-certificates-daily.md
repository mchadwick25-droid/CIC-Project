text: Moreover, when I found that those who had polluted their hands and mouths with sacrilegious contact,
  or had no less infected their consciences with wicked certificates, were everywhere soliciting the martyrs,
  and were also corrupting the confessors with importunate and excessive entreaties, so that, without
  any discrimination or examination of the individuals themselves, thousands of certificates were daily
  given, contrary to the law of the Gospel, I wrote letters in which I recalled by my advice, as much
  as possible, the martyrs and confessors to the Lord’s commands.

modern_rendering: Some had stained their hands and mouths by touching what was sacrilege. Others had stained

  everywhere. They were also corrupting the confessors with pushy and excessive pleas. As a result, thousands
  of certificates were given every day, against the law of the Gospel. No one sorted or examined the people
  themselves. So I wrote letters to call the martyrs and confessors back to the Lord's commands, as far
  as my advice could.
