BOUNDARY FIGURE - the Tours node's own author, and a named actor inside his own narrative frame at
points. Narratable: true, but bounded, and the bound is the whole point of the record. He is not the
protagonist of any story, and Doc_09 §6 item 4 deliberately declined the one episode in which he is
(his own visit to Martin, Vita XXV - Martin washing the visitor's feet), because its content is already
the substance of gallic.term.example-imitation and every Tours story's tier justification. What makes
him narratable rather than a mere source is that he is present in the telling as a person: the
mother-in-law's correspondent in Letter III, with "mock outrage at how his private writings keep
reaching her," giving "the following particulars which are comprised within my own knowledge" on
condition she read them to no one; and the listener at the Dialogues ("that wooden seat of his (which
you all know)"), whom Gallus addresses. Those are frame appearances - the "I" of the Tours stories is
his even where the narrative is Gallus's - and the Representative may name him as such ("Sulpitius,
writing to his mother-in-law within months of the death, tells - on the report of those who were
present - that ..."), which is how every Tier 1 Tours story record's usage guidance already has him
appear. The seven story relations are therefore declared as the author-figure of each Tours story, not
as a character in each.

Why he is load-bearing beyond his stories: Doc_04 §5's Author Gravity flag - G6 (virtus) is
single-voice, Sulpitius's - is the reason G6 is Tensional and not Primary; the whole northern half of
this world is one man's corpus, and Doc_02 §1.1's restatement stands - "one ancient biographical
source on Sulpitius (Gennadius) and two modern editorial treatments," not two ancient authorities.
Gennadius's Pelagian charge is carried here as Gennadius's own flat statement, a biographical datum of
Contested standing, and is not connected to G3: Tours "has no doctrine of grace, by chronology" (Doc_07
§2D), and this record must not become a back door for one.
