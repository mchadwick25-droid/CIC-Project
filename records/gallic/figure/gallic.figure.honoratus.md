The Lerins node's founder, protagonist of its one story, and the addressee - twice, under two titles -
of the part of Cassian's Conferences that opens with Archebius. Narratable: true, argued with the limit
stated: the one narrative told of him is a disciple's funeral sermon (Tier 3), read only in bounded
windows of rough OCR Latin whose sense this build cannot vouch for; what can be told is how he came to
the island and how he left it, and nothing in between (Doc_09 §8 item 1 - the most consequential
absence in the repository, carried in gallic.story.honoratus-and-the-island's own absent_detail and
forward to the honest_limit step). He is related to the Archebius story because Cassian's narrative
names him as its dedicatee and the chunk's own Formation Ecology Connection makes him that story's
Gallic reader - "the story's afterlife is gallicstory014" - while the record states that the
Lerins/abbot identification of Cassian's "holy brothers" is the editorial apparatus's, not Cassian's own
word.

Why he is load-bearing beyond his one story: Doc_04 G1 calls Hilary's sentence about him "the southern
node's own founding narrative stating the conjunction in its own words, independent of Cassian and of
the editorial apparatus" - the strongest cross-node convergence in the build (Doc_04 §6 point 1) rests
in the south on him; and the documented, dated link between Marseilles and Lerins (Doc_01 §2.3) is
Cassian's dedication to him. The relation to gallic.figure.vincent is the Lerins house itself, not any
attested personal contact - Vincent's entry to the island is undated (Inferential-Thin, Doc_01 §2.3)
and no Native text puts the two men in one scene. Hilary of Arles, his disciple, successor, and
biographer, is not given a figure record: he appears only as the narrator of the one Lerins story, and
his own sermon is the least-read text in this build.
