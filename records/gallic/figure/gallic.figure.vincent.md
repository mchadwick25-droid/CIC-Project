NO-STORY BOUNDARY FIGURE, declared rather than omitted, and the one figure in this set carried as
narratable: false - argued, not defaulted. Vincent is this world's only Lerins voice read in full and
the author of its explicit interpretive rule (G4's formal statement, 434), so he is unmistakably
load-bearing; but nothing narratable survives of him, and Doc_09 examined the candidates and declined
each with reasons (Doc_09 §6 items 9-12): his own occasion for writing (Comm. ch. 1) is "a preface, a
paragraph of self-location, not a narrative with an action" - first-person, datable, and already
deployed whole in Doc_05 §8 and gallic.term.commonitory-peregrinus; the theft of the second book
(Gennadius ch. LXV) is "one sentence ... no actor, no scene, no telling," carried in the transmission
record; his catalogue of heresiarchs is a narrative form, not a story; and his handling of Celestine's
letter (ch. 32) is "an argument about a letter's meaning, not a narrative of its arrival." Doc_09 §8
item 5 states the larger absence he belongs to: no Native voice narrates this world's own controversy,
and Vincent, who came nearest, chose to write a memory-aid on doctrine, not a life. The Representative
can quote him at length and cannot tell a single thing he did. That is a different condition from
Cappadocian's Eustathius (whose side of a real story is untellable because his enemies wrote it) and
the reason the field is false here rather than true-with-limits: there is no event of Vincent's to tell
from any side. Narratable would become true if a future pass builds Doc_09 §6 item 9 as a story after
all; this record would then be revised, and says so now.

Relations: no story-level entries, by the discipline above. The one figure relation, to

them in a scene together, and Vincent's arrival on the island is undated.
