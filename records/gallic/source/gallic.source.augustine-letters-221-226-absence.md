The actual Massilian reports, in the reporters' own words, are the single most consequential unvendored source for this world's central controversy -- and their absence is a declared, general editorial-selection omission, not a content-motivated one: npnf101's own Prefatory Note states 225-226 are omitted as 'letters written by others to Augustin' (not his own) and 221-224 as 'miscellaneous smaller letters,' with Pelagian-controversy material cross-referenced, not suppressed, to NPNF's three dedicated anti-Pelagian volumes. Confirmed directly at this Registry's own construction: the vendored letter sequence runs CCXX to CCXXVII; the Prefatory Note states the Benedictine edition holds 272 letters, of which 160 are translated in this volume.
