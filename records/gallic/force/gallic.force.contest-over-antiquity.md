description: >-
  Doc_04 G4's forces notation recorded a lead - that the same rule which guarded the Massilian
  position was, per Heurtley's Appendix III (unread there), 'later applied against it.' Appendix III
  was read directly at Doc_08, and the lead resolves differently from how it was carried. It is
  Heurtley's note on Comm. ch. 32 [85], where Vincent himself quotes Celestine's letter to the
  Gallican bishops - 'Rebuke these people. Restrain their liberty of preaching,' and 'if the case be
  so, let novelty cease to assail antiquity' - and reads it as a verdict for his own side: 'not that
  antiquity should cease to subvert novelty, but that novelty should cease to assail antiquity.'
  Heurtley's note states that the letter was obtained by Prosper and Hilary at Rome and directed at
  the bishops of southern Gaul for their 'connivance ... with the unsound teaching of their clergy,'
  and that Vincent's handling 'has been very commonly thought, and with reason, to indicate a
  Semipelagian leaning' - his repeated 'if the case be so' showing 'an evident wish to shift the
  charge of novelty from those against whom it had been brought, and fix it upon the opposite
  party.' Appendix II adds Prosper's own words: the Massilians 'obstinationem suam vetustate
  defendunt' - defend their obstinacy by antiquity. What this establishes, at editorial-reading
  strength: the antiquity/novelty instrument was applied AGAINST the Massilian clergy inside the
  window - by Celestine's letter as the editors read it, and by Prosper's report - and re-applied by
  Vincent against Augustine's party in 434. 'It is not a post-window fracture; it is an in-window
  contest over who holds the consent of the fathers' (Doc_08 Cell 3B, Force 3B-3). WORLD'S OWN
  EXPERIENCE (Layer 2): the keeper at Lérins heard the Apostolic See say that novelty must cease to
  assail antiquity, and knew which side was antiquity: his own. The men who complained to Rome were,
  to him, the devisers of novelty; the letter forbade THEIR liberty of preaching. From within there
  was no contest over the instrument - there was only its correct application. That his opponents
  were saying the same of him, in the same words, is what the world's own text does not record.
  FORMATION IMPACT (Layer 3): 'This is G4's fracture - not in the world's own experience, where the
  rule held firm to the end, but in the ecology's outward relation, where a boundary drawn by
  consent invited a contest over who held the consent.' It shaped what was passed on: the rule of
  antiquity, detached from the position it had been used to defend, became quotable by every later
  party; the position itself went into the record under the opponents' name for it, 'the remnants of
  the Pelagians,' and reached the fifteenth century as something to be cut out of Cassian for
  orthodoxy's sake. The world's boundary logic 'produced its own most acute external pressure' - and
  at the ending, the terms in which the world would be remembered by others.
