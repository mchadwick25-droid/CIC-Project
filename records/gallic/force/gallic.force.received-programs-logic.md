description: >-
  Inside the southern formation program, before and apart from any outside report, the teaching
  later reported as 'the Massilian position' sits as a remedy for pride at the summit of the manual.
  Institutes XII, on pride, turns at ch. 9 into eleven chapters on grace: the monk who feels
  progress is to say 'Not I, but the grace of God with me' (XII.9); 'not giving my own opinion, but
  that of the elders, that perfection cannot possibly be gained without these, but that by these
  only without the grace of God nobody can ever attain it' (XII.14); 'this is that genuine faith of
  the ancient fathers which still remains intact among their successors' (XII.19); and the work's
  actual closing sentence, 'we cannot possibly perform anything connected with the attainment of
  perfect virtue without His assistance and grace' (XII.33). Conf. XIII itself arises as a formation
  problem at morning service, when 'Abbot Germanus was troubled by great scruples' over whether
  effort accomplishes anything; its terminus is a declared limit (XIII.18) (Doc_08 Cell 2B, Force
  2B-1). WORLD'S OWN EXPERIENCE (Layer 2): the brother fasted, watched, laboured, kept the cell, and
  laid every thought bare to the senior - and then, at the moment he felt the fast had done
  something, he was to say 'Not I, but the grace of God with me.' The efforts were commanded and
  could not be dispensed with; they could not of themselves gain anything. This was not a doctrine
  argued but a humility taught, and the fathers' own. When the brothers were troubled at morning
  service, the answer was that the will is always free and the grace of God always with it, and that
  how both are true no mind can fully grasp. The psalter gave the words for it before reason did.
  FORMATION IMPACT (Layer 3): 'This is G3 from within - Primary-grade within Marseilles' - and the
  formation logic's last word, the 'training in not I.' It made G7 non-optional and non-sufficient
  at once, tied G3 to G4 in the same breath ('the genuine faith of the ancient fathers'), and gave
  G8 its idiom for the argument (Job the athlete). It trained the 'Not I' at the moment of felt
  success and the compunction that rises with purity. 'It is the internal force on which the
  external force 2A-1 acted: the report carried to Africa and Rome was a report of this teaching,
  which is why the pressure bore on the manual's summit.' Node-bound southern; Tours has no doctrine
  of grace.
