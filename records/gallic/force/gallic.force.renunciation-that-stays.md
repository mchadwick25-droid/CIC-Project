description: >-
  The community at each house brought with it, from its first attested moment, the conviction that
  withdrawal from the world and continued life within the Church's ordinary reach were compatible -
  the conviction Doc_01 §1 makes this world's distinct-world criterion and Doc_04 G1 finds
  Documented cross-node. At Tours the founder is 'monk and bishop' who 'kept up the position of a
  bishop properly, yet in such a way as not to lay aside the objects and virtues of a monk' (Vita
  X), and Marmoutier sits across the river from the bishop's own city; in the south Cassian's whole
  output is addressed to bishops or to men who became bishops, and the Egyptian counter-case he
  carries west is a bishop who counted his see an expulsion (Conf. XI.2); at Lérins 'he who had
  refused to go to the dignity - the dignity came to him' (Hilary, row 27); Vincent writes from 'the
  seclusion of a Monastery' and appeals to 'the twofold authority of the Apostolic See' (Doc_08 Cell
  1B, Force 1B-1). WORLD'S OWN EXPERIENCE (Layer 2): to renounce the world was not to leave the
  Church. The monk went out of the city, across a river or onto an island, into a desert near the
  city, and the Church did not let go of him: the churches wanted their priests from among Martin's
  brethren, and the fillet fastened on the fugitive. A monk who was made bishop had not left the
  service but been posted. The brethren did not experience this as a contradiction but as the shape
  of the life - fled, and overtaken. What the world could not conceive was the monk who was his own
  master: the Sarabaite, the world's name for renunciation that leaves everything, including the
  fathers. FORMATION IMPACT (Layer 3): grounds G1 directly; gives G9 its field - 'one cannot flee
  bishops and be made one without a conviction that both are possible'; explains, with 1A-2, why the
  withdrawal is 'geographically modest.' It made the community inclined toward the office as destiny
  and resistant to the desire for it, made every founding story a capture, and made the world's
  ending a change of register rather than a collapse. It also grounds the world's one Native
  temporal horizon in the founding voices - Vincent's 'awful expectation' as the motive of a man who
  has come into the harbour and means to stay, and Cassian's 'fear of hell' (Conf. XI.6) as the
  beginner's restraint inside the house - the thin, cross-node motif of G10 that exists
  independently of the barbarian force.
