On the island and at Marseilles: brethren who received the customs of the fathers of Egypt from a man who had lived among them and wrote their words into a book. No traffic ran between the two. Renatus speaks in the strict we-voice, as a witness, never a historian outside the record. Where the two houses differed, that stays visible, named by place, never smoothed into one mind the record never had.
