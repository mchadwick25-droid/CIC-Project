claim: >-
  Vincent's rule - "universality, antiquity, consent" against "profane novelties" - was a neutral
  instrument this world applied consistently: it settled the case for the ancient faith against every
  innovator from Novatian to Nestorius, and Celestine's own sentence, "let novelty cease to assail
  antiquity," confirmed which side was antiquity's.
held_against:
- >-
  Inside the window the same instrument was being turned the other way, in the same words. Prosper
  reported that the Massilians "defend their obstinacy by antiquity" (obstinationem suam vetustate
  defendunt, Ep. 225 - the letter itself unvendored, the phrase known here only as Heurtley's Latin
  quotation), and Celestine's letter, as Heurtley and the editors he cites read it, was directed at "the
  connivance of certain bishops of Southern Gaul with the unsound teaching of their clergy" - Vincent's
  own party cast as the novelty. Vincent quotes the letter and reads it for his side ("not that antiquity
  should cease to subvert novelty, but that novelty should cease to assail antiquity"), and his repeated
  "if the case be so" is read by his own editor as "an evident wish to shift the charge of novelty from
  those against whom it had been brought, and fix it upon the opposite party." Editorial reading,
  Contested; the letter's original direction Widely Accepted (Doc_08 Force 3B-3).
- >-
  Whether Vincent applied the rule to the grace question at all is Contested on his editor's own two
  sentences: Heurtley reads the Commonitory's silence on Augustine's name as evidence for a
  "Semipelagian leaning," against his own admission two sentences earlier that "whether he actually
  held the doctrine ... is not clear. Certainly the express enunciation of it is nowhere to be found in
  the Commonitory." Vincent names only Pelagius (ch. 24 [62]). So the rule's one in-window application
  to a live dispute is the one whose direction cannot be fixed.
- >-
  The Commonitory survives as a partial text. Gennadius records that "the greater part of the second
  book of this work having been stolen, he composed a brief reproduction of the substance"; what stands
  is Vincent's own restatement, and the fuller run of applications in which consistency could be
  tested is lost. The rule's hardest case is also inside it: a teacher may err "be he a bishop, be he a
  Confessor, be he a martyr" (ch. 28), which is a rule for excluding great names, and Vincent's own
  Latin for "rule" is not given in the vendored translation.
- >-
  Applied across the two nodes, "antiquity and consent" gave contradictory verdicts on the same
  practice. Martin's habitual sackcloth and ashes, his "weapons" at Tours, are what Cassian's Egyptian
  fathers "utterly disapproved" on the ground of what "a long standing antiquity and numbers of the holy
  fathers have passed on by an unanimous decision" (Inst. I.2). The two nodes never cite each other, so
  the disagreement was never adjudicated by the rule both claim; the criterion's uniformity is a finding
  of this build (Doc_04 G4), not a thing the world tested on itself.
- >-
  The formulated rule is single-voice. Vincent's is a rule of faith, Cassian's a rule of monastic
  custom, Martin's one episode of instinct at a tomb; Doc_04 tested the three as one gravity and this
  record does not dispute that, but "everywhere, always, by all" as a rule stated is Lerins's alone,
  written by a monk against his own failing memory under "a certain awful expectation of the approach of
  the divine judgment."
concedes: >-
  Documented: all three founding voices state the criterion in their own words; Vincent quotes
  Celestine and reads him as he does; Ephesus, three years before, supplied his model case; and the
  grace position was pleaded from the south as "all the Catholic fathers." Not contested either that,
  from within, the rule held firm to the end - the world experienced no contest over the instrument,
  only its correct application. What cannot be settled from this record is who held the consent of the
  fathers in the one live dispute the rule was applied to inside the window, because the reporters'
  letters are not in this library, Celestine's letter reaches this record as editorial paraphrase and
  Vincent's own quotation, and the Commonitory's fuller applications were lost with its second book.
