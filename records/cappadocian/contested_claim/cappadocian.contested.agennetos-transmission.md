claim: >-
  Eunomius' agennētos ("unbegotten") argument - that this single word names God's own essence
  exhaustively, delivering knowledge of God as God knows himself - is fairly represented by this
  world's own working summary and refutation of it.
held_against:
- Beyond Eunomius' one intact surviving work, the First Apology, his position reaches this world's own
  record almost entirely through his refuters - the Apology for the Apology survives only as quoted
  inside Gregory of Nyssa's own refutation, with no independent text to check it against
- Basil's own three-book Against Eunomius, the fight's opening full-scale refutation, does not survive
  in this build's manifest as an open, checkable edition of the treatise itself - its content is known
  here through the Prolegomena's scholarly summary and a separately vendored short letter (Epistle
  XVI), not a verified primary text of the treatise, a correction made during this build's own B-2 pass
  after an earlier record wrongly claimed the treatise text was verified-via-authority
- This world's own working summary of Eunomius - "he claims to know God as God knows himself" - is
  itself hostile paraphrase, carried as such by the lexicon entry it is drawn from (Doc_06 entry 16),
  not a statement Eunomius' own surviving Apology makes in those words
- Every refutation that carries Eunomius' position forward into this world's record - Basil's, both
  Gregorys' - is adversarial by genre and purpose, written to defeat the position rather than to state
  it fairly for its own sake
concedes: >-
  Eunomius' First Apology survives intact and in this world's own vendored, independently verified
  text - an unusually direct control most condemned fourth-century figures never get, and this build
  can and does check the refutations against his own words for at least this one round of the fight.
  What cannot be settled from this record is how fairly the refutations, and this world's own working
  summary drawn from them, represent the whole of a position whose second work and whose entire
  refutation-context survive only through the opponents who were trying to defeat it.

Index D as "adversarial transmission: beyond Eunomius' one surviving Apology, his position reaches us
through his refuters; the world's summary of him is hostile paraphrase, carried as such." Held for
cappadocian.gravity.triune-confession per this step's Primary-gravity minimum (claim B on that gravity,
alongside cappadocian.contested.settlement-historiography as claim A); associated-with recorded
reciprocally on the gravity record in the same pass.

Named honestly, per this build's own standing discipline (the same discipline as prior-step FLAG-029):
"adversarial transmission" does not sit cleanly inside the lexicon template's four canonical Contest
Type categories (Meaning / Historical scope / Application to this world / Relationship to present-day
traditions) the way ousia/hypostasis's contest does. It is a transmission-integrity problem, not a
meaning-dispute or a scope-dispute - closer in kind to an Author Gravity flag than to a contest over
what a term means or how far its reach extends. This record names that mismatch rather than forcing
agennētos's own contest type into one of the four labels it does not actually fit.

during this build (2026-08-31) after wrongly claiming the treatise text itself was verified-via-
