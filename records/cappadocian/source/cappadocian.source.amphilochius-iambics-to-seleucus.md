The one piece of Amphilochius of Iconium's own voice this build actually holds
in an open English rendering, against Doc_02 §1.6's and Source Registry row 65's
prior statement that none had been located.

WHAT IT IS. Roughly 240 words: a warning that not every book called Scripture is
a safe guide ("some are tolerably sound and others are more than doubtful"), an
enumeration of the Old Testament proto-canonical books with Esther omitted and
then noted as added by some, the New Testament books with the Revelation
similarly held back and then reported as added by some "but by far the majority
say that it is spurious," and a closing claim to state "the most true canon of
the divinely given Scriptures."

WHAT IT IS NOT, and why row 65 is corrected rather than deleted. Row 65's
underlying judgment -- that this world knows Amphilochius overwhelmingly as
addressee and first receiver rather than through his own extended voice -- is
correct and survives this finding intact. One canon-list extract does not give
this build a corpus, a theology, or a pastoral portrait in his own words. What
row 65 got wrong was the flat factual claim that no open English edition of any
of his own work had been located, which was not true of the shared library at the
time it was written; the corrected row states the narrower truth instead.
