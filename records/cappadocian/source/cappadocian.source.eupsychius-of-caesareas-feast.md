Eupsychius' martyrdom and its annual feast, Doc_02 SS8's newly-added Tier 1 story candidate -- and this world's own corrected self-description (row 83). Correction carried forward at full strength: an earlier Registry draft named this row 'homilies on Eupsychius' feast' by Basil and Gregory of Nyssa -- Doc_02 SS3 is explicit that no In Eupsychium homily is attested by anyone (unlike the Forty, Gordius, Julitta, and Mamas homilies at row 20); only the feast itself is Documented. The martyrdom's narrative content, absent a homily, would most likely come from Sozomen HE 5.11 (row 62), an external source, never from an in-world preached source.
