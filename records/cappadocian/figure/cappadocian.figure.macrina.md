"Macrina unmediated does not exist" - Doc_09's own Absent Stories line,
carried here rather than softened. Every story naming her is a Gregory-
text; this figure record and its two story records (macrina-deathbed,
macrina-refusal) carry the mediation IN the telling itself ("her brother
wrote it, and we hear her through him"), per Critic Finding 6, rather
than treating the mediation as a reason to omit her - this is deliberately
NOT a boundary-figure ("no-story") case; see cappadocian.story.macrina-
deathbed's own body note for why. Distinct from Macrina the Elder
(Basil's grandmother, Doc_02 §1.4) - a different, non-Gregory-mediated
female formation-agent this world's record also names; not given her own
figure record in this batch since no Doc_09 story entry narrates her, a
decision named here rather than silently made.
