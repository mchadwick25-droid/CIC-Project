NO-STORY BOUNDARY FIGURE, declared rather than omitted. Eunomius has
vendored writings and multiple existing term records depending on him
(cappadocian.term.agennetos, cappadocian.term.homoousios,
cappadocian.term.epinoia-energeia), but no Doc_09 entry names him
directly. Entry #15 ("the money-changers of the capital arguing the
Unbegotten") is clearly Eunomian-adjacent content - agennētos, "the
Unbegotten," is his own signature term - but Doc_09's own text does not
name him anywhere in that entry, and no other numbered entry does either;
see cappadocian.story.money-changers-unbegotten's own body note for the
same point stated from that story's side. relations[] is left empty
rather than manufacturing a story-level connection Doc_09 itself does not
make. His own First Apology and 383 Confession, both verified-direct,
ground this figure record's real, careful bridge_line even without a
Doc_09 story - he is one of the rare defeated parties in this world's own
evidentiary base who is "partially self-voiced" (Doc_02 §6 item 5).
