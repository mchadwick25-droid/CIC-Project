The world's institutional center: metropolitan of Caesarea, famine
patron before his own episcopate, defender of the Spirit's honor,
legislator of the ascetic brotherhoods, and founder of the poorhouse-
hospital complex Gregory of Nazianzus called "the new city." Also the
world's single largest surviving voice, which is itself a named risk
(Doc_02 §1.1's own "Builder rule": no world-level claim may rest on
Basil alone without an explicit Author Gravity flag). His own accounts of
Eustathius (after their rupture) and of Eunomius are adversarial evidence,
not neutral report - see cappadocian.figure.eustathius and
cappadocian.figure.eunomius. cappadocian.source.basil-moral-famine-
homilies and cappadocian.source.basil-martyr-homilies-forty-gordius-
julitta-mamas, both cited across this figure's own story record, are
unverified in this world's own Source Registry; that honesty is carried
at each citing story record rather than repeated here.
