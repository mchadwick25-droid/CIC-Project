NO-STORY BOUNDARY FIGURE, declared rather than omitted. Load-bearing for
Doc_04's Tensional Gravity 10 (precision against reserve) and Tensional
Gravity 11 (renunciation against order), and for Primary Gravity 1's own
sharpening (the ascetic mentor become the pneumatological adversary,
Doc_04 §6's own 1↔11 interaction note) - with two dedicated source
records already registered (cappadocian.source.basil-eustathius-rupture-
letters; cappadocian.source.epiphanius-on-eustathius-pneumatomachians).
But Doc_09's own Absent Stories section states plainly: "Eustathius' side
of the rupture — the world's ascetic father as he saw himself — is not
tellable; only the wound as Basil recorded it." No numbered Doc_09 entry
narrates the rupture from Basil's own side either - it is referenced
across several term and story records' own body notes (e.g.
cappadocian.term.koinonia) but never itself narrated as a story. This
figure record is therefore real, narratable: true, with a genuine bridge_
line - but deliberately carries no story record, and relations[] is left
empty rather than manufacturing a story-level connection Doc_09 itself
declines to assert. Adversarial transmission throughout: "no works
survive" under his own name (Doc_02 §1.5); everything here is the
account of a wounded former disciple, or of his censors.
