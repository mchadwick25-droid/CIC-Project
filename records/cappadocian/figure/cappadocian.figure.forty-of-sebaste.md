Collective figure, following the same convention used elsewhere in this
project for a group speaking or acting as one attested body (compare
pahc.figure.church-of-smyrna): the tradition this world actually preserves
names the company as a whole, not forty individual soldiers by name, and
this record follows that attested shape rather than inventing forty
separate figure records the evidence does not support. This world's own
freshest martyr memory (Doc_04 §4) - grandparents'-generation, not
great-great-grandparents'. The family into which Basil, Macrina, and
Gregory of Nyssa were born held a direct relic-connection to this cult
through their mother, Emmelia (see cappadocian.figure.emmelia and
cappadocian.story.forty-sebaste's own text).
