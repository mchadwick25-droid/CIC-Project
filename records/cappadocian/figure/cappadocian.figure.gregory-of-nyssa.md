The least publicly effective of the three brothers in his own day - Basil's
own letters complain of his political naivety, at work level (Doc_02
§1.3) - and the most speculative; his mystical-theological syntheses
(perpetual progress, the darkness of knowing) are his own developments,
not this world's common property, a named risk this build flags every
time it is tempted to make his own vocabulary the whole world's word
(Doc_04 §2, candidates-not-advanced). CITATION CAUTION carried from Doc_02
§1.3: the acquired NPNF2 v5 edition of Against Eunomius uses a
pre-Jaeger book division that will not match modern scholarly citation.
