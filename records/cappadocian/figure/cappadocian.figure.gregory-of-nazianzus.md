The world's uniquely interior voice - no other source tells us so much
about how it felt to live this world's own contests. Also the sharpest
self-fashioning problem in the world's record (Doc_02 §1.2): his self-
curated letter collection means later readers inherit the archive he
wanted kept, a transmission force operating inside the primary evidence
itself. His will, which survives and manumits slaves he owned, is one of
this world's few directly against-the-grain data points on slavery
(Doc_02 §6 item 3), cited at work level only.
