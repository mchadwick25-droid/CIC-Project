Cousin of Gregory of Nazianzus; the circle's junior member and first
receiver of its most consequential treatise. One short piece of his own
argument is held and readable - a warning to Seleucus that not every book
called Scripture is a safe guide, framing a list of the trustworthy books
that this build's only rendering of it does not actually contain - which is
worth knowing about him and is nowhere near enough to portray him from. Per Doc_01 §1, Iconium and
its rural discipline are named in this world's own geographic scope, not
merely a source it happens to cite (Doc_02 §1.6).
