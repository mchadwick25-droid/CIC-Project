statement: >-
  What did we never settle? Whose voice you are really hearing, most of the
  time. Almost everything you have from us was written down by one big
  family and one small circle of friends who went to school together. No
  woman among us left one line in her own hand. The woman her brother called
  the Teacher reaches you only through his pen, in a book he wrote after she
  died, giving her his own words to speak. Our sharpest foes survive mostly
  inside our own case against them. Only a few left words of their own that
  we did not write for them. The country villages beyond our great towns
  show up only as crowds fed in a famine, or crowds at a feast day. They
  never speak for themselves. This gap is not small. It runs close to the
  center of what we left. The letter most often used to show that our three
  teachers agreed about God may not be by the man whose name it carries. It
  has come down as Basil's. It may be his brother Gregory's. If so, that
  agreement may be one man's work.
why_sources_cannot_answer: >-
  This is the single largest structural feature of this world's own
  evidentiary base, named directly in cappadocian.core.cappadocian's
  own cautions (item 1, "AUTHOR GRAVITY") and thin_topics (women's own
  words; plateau villages and rural non-elite faithful; the enslaved;
  defeated parties): the surviving corpus is overwhelmingly the production
  of one family (Basil, Macrina, Gregory of Nyssa, their mother and
  brothers) and one friendship network (Basil and Gregory of Nazianzus,
  formed at Athens), who wrote, copied, edited, and, in Gregory of
  Nazianzus's own case, personally curated the letter collection later
  readers inherit. No text composed by a woman of this world survives; the
  region's non-elite, non-Greek-speaking population appears only as an
  object of the writing circle's own attention; defeated theological and
  ascetic opponents (Eustathius, the Homoian establishment, the
  Pneumatomachians, the Gangra radicals) survive almost entirely through
  the winning side's own polemic, with Eunomius' own First Apology the
  sole substantial exception. This is not a gap this build's own search
  could close - it is the shape of what survived antiquity at all.

Closes F6-I with the dedicated treatment cappadocian.demo.hardest-true-thing
and cappadocian.demo.never-settled already point toward from a different
angle each (slavery's outlier protest; the reserve controversy's own
contested motive) but neither one fully states: this world's central,
structural silence is not any one topic, it is whose hand held the pen at
all. Deliberately broader than pahc.limit.womens-own-words (women alone) -
this world's own record names three overlapping voiceless groups together
(women, the plateau's non-elite, and defeated opponents), and the
flagship-letter attribution dispute (Epistle 38) is folded in as the
sharpest single illustration that even this world's OWN agreement with
itself may not be as solid as it looks. Complements, does not duplicate,
cappadocian.dw.ordinary-day's own lighter tension on the same theme (F5-I).
