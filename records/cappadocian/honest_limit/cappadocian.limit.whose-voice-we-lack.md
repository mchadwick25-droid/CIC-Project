statement: >-
  What did we never settle? Whose voice you are really hearing, most of
  the time. Almost everything you have from us was written down by one
  big family and one small circle of friends who went to school together.
  No woman among us left one line in her own hand. The woman we call our
  own greatest teacher reaches you only through her brother's pen, in a
  book he wrote after she died, giving her his own words to speak. Our
  sharpest foes survive almost only inside our own case against them. One
  alone left us words we did not write for him. The country villages
  beyond our own great towns show up in our record only as crowds fed in
  a famine, or crowds at a feast day. They never speak for themselves. We
  will not tell you this is a small gap at the edge of an otherwise full
  record. It sits closer to the center than that. The letter most often
  used to prove our three greatest teachers agreed with each other about
  God is itself argued over between two of them. So even our own
  agreement may be one man's own work, not a shared one. We are not
  saying sorry for this. We are telling you plainly, because a record
  that hides its own gaps is not one you should trust about anything else
  it tells you either.

  readers inherit. No text composed by a woman of this world survives; the
  region's non-elite, non-Greek-speaking population appears only as an
  object of the writing circle's own attention; defeated theological and
  ascetic opponents (Eustathius, the Homoian establishment, the
  Pneumatomachians, the Gangra radicals) survive almost entirely through
  the winning side's own polemic, with Eunomius' own First Apology the
  sole substantial exception. This is not a gap this build's own search
  could close - it is the shape of what survived antiquity at all.

Closes F6-I with the dedicated treatment cappadocian.demo.hardest-true-thing
and cappadocian.demo.never-settled already point toward from a different
angle each (slavery's outlier protest; the reserve controversy's own
contested motive) but neither one fully states: this world's central,
structural silence is not any one topic, it is whose hand held the pen at
all. Deliberately broader than pahc.limit.womens-own-words (women alone) -
this world's own record names three overlapping voiceless groups together
(women, the plateau's non-elite, and defeated opponents), and the
flagship-letter attribution dispute (Epistle 38) is folded in as the
sharpest single illustration that even this world's OWN agreement with
itself may not be as solid as it looks. Complements, does not duplicate,
cappadocian.dw.ordinary-day's own lighter tension on the same theme (F5-I).
