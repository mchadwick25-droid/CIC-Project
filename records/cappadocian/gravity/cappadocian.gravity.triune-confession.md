description: >-
  The world's irreducible doctrinal and doxological core: the incomprehensible (akatalēptos) God rightly
  glorified in worship, defended with exactness against definition (Eunomius) and against demotion (the
  Spirit-fighters), so that precision of confession is itself an act of reverence (eusebeia), and the
  baptismal formula is the community's own charter.

  This confession formed people; it was not merely a conclusion they reached. The fight was fought over the
  very words ordinary worship uses - a doxology's preposition, the baptismal formula - and argued from
  worship itself: baptism, belief, and the giving of glory, Basil argues, stand or fall together as one
  connected sequence, so that rejecting his doxology as unauthorized means, by his own logic, also being
  willing to reject baptism or the confession of faith. The argument reached down to the marketplace too -
  the money-changers complaint is hostile witness to how far popular doctrinal talk had spread. Catechesis,
  hymn-practice, festal preaching, and canonical discipline all carried the confession forward. Whoever was
  baptized, sang, or kept a feast in this world was formed by the contested confession, not merely alongside
  it.

  The evidence for this runs across genuinely independent streams - dogmatic, liturgical, legislative,
  homiletic, institutional - and is well documented. It sharpens under every hostile force and relaxes under
  none: Homoian court pressure produces the doxology stand and the treatise defending it; Eunomian confidence
  produces the drive toward precision about what cannot be defined; the ascetic rupture with Eustathius
  produces the pneumatological line drawn in the sand. Under the Theodosian settlement it wins - and the
  world's own distinct militancy dissolves with that victory; the confession's own success ends the world
  that fought for it.

  It runs across every register: the household (the creed taught at home), the brotherhood (the ascetic
  critics' own arguments with Basil), the congregation (doxology, baptism, festal preaching), and the
  bishop's own see (councils, canons). Across the pneumatological fracture, this confession is what is
  actually being contested.

- Nicaea's defense, the 381 creed, and the communion law - the conciliar-credal stream
- the Catechetical Oration and the baptismal orations forming new Christians directly in the confession

This gravity has a demonstrated relationship with every other confirmed gravity in this world - the only
one with no declared absence. It reinforces the ascetic-reordering gravity (the brotherhoods carry and
argue the confession, and the confession in turn frames the ordered life's own charter - the world's two
substantial Primary gravities are interlocked), the martyrs-land gravity, the household-lineage gravity, and
the bishop-patron gravity. It reshapes, and is reshaped by, the contested-church gravity (the contest gives
the confession its embattled, portable form, and the confession defines the contest's own sides), the
paideia-converted gravity, and the athens-fishermen gravity, along with the hesychia-summons gravity, the
precision-reserve gravity, and the renunciation-order gravity - these fractures overlap in the same person:
Eustathius, the ascetic mentor who became the pneumatological adversary, gives this world's pneumatology its
own texture of personal grief.

It is produced under the contested inheritance this world begins from - carried here as enabled-by, its
founding force. Eunomius's own confidence and the pneumatological rupture force it toward precision;
the empire's own policy oscillation makes it portable; the Theodosian settlement both consummates it and
dissolves it as a live contest; canonization then fixes it in place. Eustathius (cappadocian.figure.eustathius)
is carried as associated-with, load-bearing for this gravity's own sharpening - the same overlap named
above. cappadocian.quote.baptize-believe-glorify-in-sequence carries Basil's own words for the
baptism-belief-glory sequence this gravity's own formation evidence turns on. Canon_cells left empty,
