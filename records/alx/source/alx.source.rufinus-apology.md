Work presence verified: div2 "The Apology of Rufinus" at file line 46229.
Didymus is called "the seer" in Book III's chapter on Jerome's boast of his
teachers (file lines 49429-49432, where Rufinus says Jerome boasts of being
"the pupil of Didymus the seer" and that he himself stayed at Alexandria six
years and then two more), and "the seeing Didymus ... prophet" in Book I
(lines 48652-48654). The NPNF editor's note there says Jerome delights in
calling him the Seer, in contrast to his blindness.
