Domain). Loci verified by direct read: Socrates HE IV.20 (file lines
13310-13322): Athanasius 'departed this life in the second consulate of
Gratian and Probus, having governed that church amidst the greatest
perils forty-six years'. DATING DISCREPANCY carried honestly: this
edition's own editor note reads that consulate as 371 A.D. and cites
Jerome's Chronicle as discordant; modern consensus reads Athanasius's
death as 373. The figure record states both rather than silently picking.
IV.25 ('Of Didymus the Blind Man', div at line 13797) is the third
independent Didymus testimonium beside Palladius and Jerome.
