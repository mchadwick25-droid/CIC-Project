Date attestations verified by direct read of the vendored loci, replacing
the prior build's UNVERIFIED reference-work note. PRECISION CAUTION on
the Stromateis I.1 tribute ('the true, the Sicilian bee... engendered in
the souls of his hearers a deathless element of knowledge'): Clement does
NOT name his final teacher - the identification with Pantaenus runs
through Eusebius (HE V.11), so any use of the tribute must say 'the
teacher scholarship identifies as Pantaenus'. His own teaching survives
only as fragments in later transmission (anf08's Pantaenus section; the
second fragment via 7th-c. Maximus - late carrier, flag it). The
India/Bartholomew mission story (HE V.10; De viris 36) is tellable as
reported tradition, not verified event.
