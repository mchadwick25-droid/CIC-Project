operative through every later step): Origen's surviving output is a
large fraction of ALL surviving Alexandrian Christian literature; the
screening question - is this the ecology's pattern or Origen's? - stays
active wherever he is the main witness. His father Leonides's martyrdom
(HE VI.1-2) is prime story material. Transmission caution: his most
contested positions survive through interested intermediaries (Rufinus
softening, Jerome quoting adversarially) - see
alx.contested.origen-positions. Within this world's horizon his standing
is UNRESOLVED AMBIVALENCE, not condemnation: the first Origenist
controversy erupts only c. 399-400, the conciliar condemnation in 553 -
both at or past the horizon's edge.
