narratable: false is deliberate and carried from the prior build's
accepted refusal: almost nothing narratable survives about Clement's
life beyond succession notices and his departure under persecution -
stories about him would be invention. His WORDS are the world's richest
early material (three major works plus Quis dives, all vendored). The
one narratable story inside his kept words - John and the young robber
(Quis dives 42) - belongs to a story record citing
alx.source.clement-quis-dives, not to his biography.
