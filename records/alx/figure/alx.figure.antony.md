the Desert world (the planned second build) as to Alexandria, and the
attribution is held open. In THIS world he appears as Athanasius's
portrait - the formation ideal the Alexandrian bishop held up - not as
the desert's own self-understanding. Rubenson's literate-Antony thesis
(copyrighted, consult-only) contests even the 'unlettered' premise.
