text: For he who holds possessions, and gold, and silver, and houses, as the gifts of God; and ministers
  from them to the God who gives them for the salvation of men; and knows that he possesses them more
  for the sake of the brethren than his own; and is superior to the possession of them, not the slave
  of the things he possesses; and does not carry them about in his soul, nor bind and circumscribe his
  life within them, but is ever labouring at some good and divine work, even should he be necessarily
  some time or other deprived of them, is able with cheerful mind to bear their removal equally with their
  abundance. This is he who is blessed by the Lord, and called poor in spirit, a meet heir of the kingdom
  of heaven, not one who could not live rich.

master. Serves F5-T (how did you look at money and poverty) and F4-T

percentage). Verified verbatim.
