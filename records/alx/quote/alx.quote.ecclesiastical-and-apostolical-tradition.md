modern_lens_note: >-
  The answer to 'was the Bible your only authority' is no, and this is the sentence that says why. Origen is about to speculate further than almost anyone in his century, and he opens by fixing a boundary he will not cross: what the churches have gone on teaching since the apostles. Scripture is read INSIDE that rule, not over it. Note also what the rule is not - it is not a book, not a council, and not an office; it is a continuity of teaching, and Origen's test for it is public and comparative: does this differ from what the churches hold?
