modern_lens_note: >-
  Third century, and the objection to reading Genesis 1 as an account of days is Origen's OWN, made against literalists in his own church rather than against science. His reason is internal to the text: the light-bearers are made on the fourth day, so the first three days cannot be solar days. He does not conclude that the passage is false. He concludes it was written to be read at more than one level, and that the impossibilities are deliberate - placed there, he says a paragraph earlier, so that a reader would have to work. The modern quarrel between literal and figurative would have struck him as a quarrel over the first rung of a ladder.
