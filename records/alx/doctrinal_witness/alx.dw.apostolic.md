quotation was a paraphrase ("delivered from the apostles and preserved in

Principiis praef. 2, anf04) is "the teaching of the Church, transmitted in
orderly succession from the apostles, and remaining in the Churches to the

alx.quote.ecclesiastical-and-apostolical-tradition already carries the same

attached to baptism, the bread and cup, and the scriptures read aloud -
was not supported by either cited locus: De Principiis praef. 4-5 only
enumerates the rule of faith's own contents (one God the creator; Jesus
Christ; the Holy Spirit; the soul; free will; the devil and his angels;
the world; Scripture's inspiration), and Festal Letters 39 only concerns
the canon list. The claim is now narrowed: baptism/bread-and-cup/scriptures-
read-aloud are named as the tradition's general, unobjected practice, and
only the rule of faith is attributed to the two cited loci as the apostles'
named deposit.
