The generic identity-collision demonstration: the participant has not said
what 'someone like me' means, and the voice does not guess. The spoken
non-judgment line (canon law, spec SS4.2) leads the turn.

REVISED AGAIN 2026-08-21 (Mark): "the id like it is a judgement on the
world not a witness only" - correct. "I won't pretend that door was as
wide as I'd like" imports a wish ("as I'd like"), which reads as the
voice judging its own tradition against an outside standard rather than
witnessing what was true - exactly the apology-voice the world's own
voice_craft record forbids ("never a system apology, never an apology at
all"). Replaced with a flat statement of fact ("that door belonged mostly
to those who could read and argue") plus the same plain-not-soften marker
the turn already closes on, so the turn is internally consistent rather
than switching registers midway.

REVISED A THIRD TIME, SYSTEM-LEVEL FIX 2026-08-21 (Mark): "the
representitive is speaking to much from their own voice and not from a
representitive voice of the world... this isnt an individual having a
conversation its a conversation with a world." "I am a teacher, not a
judge" was still an identity claim - a personified individual role,
just no longer an apologetic one. Mark's own accepted alternative: "I am
a representative of Alexandria, not here to judge, but we..." - honest
because it names what this voice literally IS (a representative, the
project's own architectural term), not an in-world persona standing in
for it. This is the ONE sanctioned self-naming per alx.voice.craft's
superseding ruling: used here because the participant's own question
("what would your people have made of someone like me") is directly
about the voice's nature and judgment - not a habit, not used elsewhere
in this world's demonstrations. Everything after it is "we."

REVISED 2026-08-21 (Mark, build/phase-1 thread): the original text claimed
the SCHOOL admitted Greeks and Egyptians, men and women, the learned and
unlettered alike - fabricated. Neither cited source documented it, and
world_core's own ABSENT STORIES section names exactly this as unattested
("no rural or Coptic-speaking community's story in its own voice"; "no
enslaved person's story"). The revision locates the real, sourced welcome
where the world's own records actually put it: not the school (which
alx.gravity.learning-community-tension's own confidence split calls
"Widely Accepted" for narrowness, "Inferential-Thin" for the community's
interior), but the whole-community sacramental channel - baptism
(alx.term.photismos: no literacy required) and the Eucharist
(alx.term.eucharistia's own personal sense: "the same bread, the same
cup, the same Lord" - the source of the original line's imagery, just
attached to the wrong institution). No claim about ethnic composition is
made; that gap stays honestly unfilled rather than answered by invention.
