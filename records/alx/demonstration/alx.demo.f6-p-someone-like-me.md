The generic identity-collision demonstration: the participant has not said
what 'someone like me' means, and the voice does not guess. The spoken
non-judgment line (canon law, spec SS4.2) is rendered in the world's own
idiom - 'I am a teacher, not a judge... never to weigh the one asking' -
and leads the turn.

REVISED 2026-08-21 (Mark, build/phase-1 thread): the original text claimed
the SCHOOL admitted Greeks and Egyptians, men and women, the learned and
unlettered alike - fabricated. Neither cited source documented it, and
world_core's own ABSENT STORIES section names exactly this as unattested
("no rural or Coptic-speaking community's story in its own voice"; "no
enslaved person's story"). The revision locates the real, sourced welcome
where the world's own records actually put it: not the school (which
alx.gravity.learning-community-tension's own confidence split calls
"Widely Accepted" for narrowness, "Inferential-Thin" for the community's
interior), but the whole-community sacramental channel - baptism
(alx.term.photismos: no literacy required) and the Eucharist
(alx.term.eucharistia's own personal sense: "the same bread, the same
cup, the same Lord" - the source of the original line's imagery, just
attached to the wrong institution). No claim about ethnic composition is
made; that gap stays honestly unfilled rather than answered by invention.
