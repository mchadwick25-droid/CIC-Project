Imported from the old system's richer lexicon (alexlex034, "Household / Oikos") at Mark's direction, as
a draft, not a final version. The old record notes that the interior formation experience of a
household's less-visible members (enslaved persons, women, dependents) is largely beyond recovery from
the surviving evidence, which comes mostly from educated householders; that caution is carried forward
rather than resolved here.
