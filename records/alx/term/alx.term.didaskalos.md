Imported from the old system's richer lexicon (alexlex029, "Teacher / Didaskalos") at Mark's direction,
as a draft, not a final version. The old record notes that the Alexandrian catechetical school's exact
institutional form (a chartered school with continuous succession, versus a looser teaching tradition
later formalized by Eusebius) is itself disputed among historians - that institutional question is left
for its own governed entry, not yet imported, rather than asserted here.
