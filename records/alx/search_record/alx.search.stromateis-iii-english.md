This gap matters more than it looks: Stromateis III is
one of the corpus's most direct witnesses for marriage, household, and the
body (canon F5-T marriage cell, F6 identity-collision cells on marriage and
divorce). Without it in English, those cells lean on the Paedagogus and on
secondary scholarship. Options for Mark are laid out in
SOURCE-REQUEST-MANIFEST.md (gap G1): acquire a copyrighted modern translation
for consultation-only (not vendorable, spec principle 14), or accept the
thinness and record it as an honest limit.
