claim: >-
  We are a complete church and not a faction: our own bishops, basilicas, councils and an unbroken
  traditor-free ordination line, contesting the same sees town for town across four provinces - two hundred
  and seventy-nine of our bishops seated at Carthage in 411 against two hundred and eighty-six of theirs.
  The name catholicus is not settled by the state granting it to our rival, and before the case is decided
  the name they claim is an empty one; whoever is found to be a Christian will hold it at the end.
held_against:
- Optatus turned this communion's own legal self-designation into the charge against it. Donatist bishops
  signing a formal petition to Constantine as "of the party of Donatus" is preserved by him precisely so
  that he can argue they acknowledged belonging not to the Church of Christ but to a man's faction, and
  the rival's popular song names them the same way (don.term.pars-donati;
  don.source.augustine-psalmus-contra-partem-donati)
- The catholicity argument was pressed on the record at 411 and this communion's answer to it was procedural
  rather than substantive. The other side asked that the accusations made against a church spread through
  the whole earth be heard that day and found true or false; Petilian answered that the name they claim
  is empty until the case is decided - which contests the timing of the title, not the geographical argument
  behind it (don.story.conference-of-carthage-411)
- The state had already answered the naming question before the hearing opened. Marcellinus conceded the
  principle and then said he was bound to follow the imperial rescript, which had been pleased to call the
  other side catholic; this communion's own counter to the word survives not as an argument but as a pun
  - catholicus as the place where wrongdoing goes unpunished - reported by a seventeenth-century editor
  annotating a sermon whose full Latin this build has not read (don.term.catholicus)
- Individual bishops' conduct and motives - as against the bare institutional fact - reach this record hostile-mediated
  throughout, Optatus Gildonianus's characterisation being this world's own named example
  (don.gravity.parallel-hierarchy's own divergence_note)
- No vendored source gives this communion's own everyday self-naming in its own words - what Augustine
  actually calls them, and in which contexts, remains an open question. The name this movement is
  remembered by was given to it by the people who defeated it (don.term.pars-donati)
concedes: >-
  The bare institutional fact is Documented to an unusually high degree even by this world's hostile-source
  standards - Optatus, Augustine, and the imperial and conciliar record all agree that two complete rival
  hierarchies existed and contested the same sees - so nothing here is contested about the church's
  existence. Three things are conceded about the claim built on it. The precise numbers do not agree even
  inside this communion's own vendored transcript: the volume's editorial summary gives 286 against 279,
  while the acts' own roll-call reports the Catholic subscriptions at two hundred and sixty-six with twenty
  further bishops present who had not yet subscribed, and neither figure corrects the other here. The
  claim's confidence about institutional continuity ends precisely where the vendored record ends, not
  where the movement itself ended. And the catholicity argument was never answered on its own terms in
  any surviving text of this communion's own.
divergence_partners:
- ijc.contested.primacy-reception (world imperial-juridical) - a genuine and unusually exact divergence,
  because each claim is the other's counter-evidence. That claim holds that Rome's primacy claim was
  generally received as binding within its window, and concedes that what the record does not show is
  uncontested, universal reception. This communion is an in-window instance of exactly that
  non-reception - two hundred and seventy-nine bishops of one province-cluster who denied that any wider
  verdict, conciliar or imperial, settled who the church was. What that world concedes as a limit, this
  world lived as its whole institutional life.
- ijc.contested.canon-28-meaning (world imperial-juridical) - the same question about who gets to name a
  see's standing, in the Greek East rather than Latin Africa - a rank claimed, contested at the council
  that granted it, and argued over afterwards. The divergence is in what the contest was about - there,
  precedence between sees inside one communion; here, whether there is one communion at all.
- pahc.contested.rivals-undefeated (world post-apostolic) - a divergence in period rather than in position,
  and worth naming because that claim's own methodological instruction cuts directly at this one. It
  insists that rival Christianities be read as live, undefeated neighbours rather than as later,
  external, already-settled heresies. Applied here, the same instruction bars reading two rival African
  hierarchies through the outcome of 411 - which is what this claim needs, and also what makes the claim
  contested rather than simply true.

Built for the Table Readiness Round from the cleared Doc_04 SS3.4 (candidate G4, six of six PASS with
Dependency revealing Supporting rather than Primary status, classified Supporting - the integrating
and institutional centre - at SS4) together with don.story.conference-of-carthage-411, which is the
one place in this world's whole corpus where this communion's bishops argue their own institutional
standing in their own recorded words rather than through an opponent's quotation.

WHY A SUPPORTING GRAVITY WARRANTS A CONTESTED CLAIM AT ALL. Doc_04 classifies this Supporting because
it supplies the container the Primaries operate within rather than a distinct formative
practice-cluster of its own. That is a finding about formation, not about contestation: the naming
contest, the catholicity argument, and the bishop counts are among the most sharply argued things in
the surviving record, argued on the record at 411 by both sides, and a Table Readiness Round that
carried the four Primaries and left this out would leave the Representative without the one claim its
opponents pressed hardest in the room.

THE CLAIM'S CLOSING SENTENCE IS PETILIAN'S OWN, CARRIED FROM THE TRANSCRIPT, not composed here -
don.story.conference-of-carthage-411 renders it from the vendored Migne printing, and that record's
own OCR caution governs it: a careful reading of a difficult scan, not a settled critical-edition
text. The 279/286 figure in the claim is the one this world's own documents already carry; the
roll-call tally that does not match it is carried in concedes rather than quietly dropped, exactly as
that story record carries it.

WHAT IS NOT ASSERTED. This record does not claim that the catholicity argument is answerable, or that
this communion had an answer that failed to survive. don.term.catholicus is explicit that the one
counter preserved is a pun reaching this record through modern editorial mediation, from a sermon
whose full Latin has not been read, and that the distinction between that kind of mediation and
ancient Author Gravity is a risk this build carries rather than resolves. canon_cells left empty,
matching this world's records generally.
