claim: >-
  The agonistici are our own - contenders, called by a name they gave themselves, taking the martyrs' side
  of a fight that was being fought against us - and not the roaming, lawless brigands the other side's
  literature makes of them.
held_against:
- The only genuinely independent attestation of the group is legislation against it. Codex Theodosianus
  16.5.52 (412) fines circumcelliones ten pounds of silver - the only rank in that law fined in silver
  rather than gold - which establishes that the group existed and was legally distinguishable, and nothing
  at all about what it was (don.gravity.circumcellion-agonistici)
- The self-designation itself is not independently attested. Agonistici reaches this record only through
  Augustine's own report of it, is confirmed ABSENT from the Theodosian Code, and its source passage in
  Augustine remains unidentified - so even the claim that they called themselves this is single-channel
  reportage from an opponent (don.term.agonistici; don.core.donatism caution 4)
- Character, scale and typical conduct survive only in hostile polemic. Doc_04 SS5 names this the one candidate
  in this world's entire gravity discovery that clearly FAILS a strict cross-voice standard for anything
  beyond bare existence and self-designation - no Donatist-voiced or non-mediated text in the vendored corpus
  corroborates any of it
- The scholarly readings do not agree and this build adjudicates none of them. Frend's older reading treats
  more of the hostile portrait as evidentiary and ties it to a native-social-protest thesis about rural
  Numidia's Punic- and Libyan-speaking substrate, itself contested; Shaw's Sacred Violence is the standard
  corrective, reading the same portrait as considerably overstated; Boyd (1905) restates the hostile
  characterization uncritically and is explicitly not licensed for it. Frend's thesis is flagged in this
  world's own cautions for priority second-opinion review before it supports any specific claim
  (don.core.donatism caution 3)
- The claim cannot be made across this world at all. The phenomenon is regionally concentrated in Numidia
  on this world's own boundary-document terms, with no attested reach to Carthage, Cirta or Hippo - which
  is why the gravity itself is classified Supporting and scope-qualified rather than ecology-wide
concedes: >-
  Everything above the bare fact of existence. This communion's own record cannot supply what these people
  did, how many they were, how they understood themselves, or whether the name they are said to have used
  was one they used - and the one thing that can be checked outside hostile literature, an imperial fine,
  says only that they were there and were worth naming in law. The evidence divides across three tiers
  and not two, which is what makes this the sharpest divergence in this world's whole gravity discovery:
  existence Documented on independent legislation, the self-designation reliable reportage but
  single-channel, character and conduct Dominant Modern Reconstruction at best. A claim about their
  character therefore rests on the weakest of the three, and this record does not borrow the strength of
  the first tier to support the third.
divergence_partners:
- cappadocian.contested.eustathian-radicals (world cappadocian-trinitarian) - the closest comparable claim
  in the fleet and a genuine divergence in what the record allows. There, a censured movement's own voice
  is likewise gone, and everything known of it comes through the canons that censured it and the letters
  of the man who broke with its leader - but Gangra's twenty canons legislate against named practices,
  which lets that record be read against the grain and find a movement making serious structural claims
  about gender and about slavery. The law that attests the agonistici names a fine and a rank and nothing
  else. This world has strictly less to read against the grain than that one does, and the claim above is
  correspondingly weaker, not merely differently sourced.
- hal.contested.marcella-agency (world hieronymian-ascetic-literary) - named because this fleet already
  treats it as the reference case for the shape of problem this claim has - the single most consequential
  source for the strongest claim is entirely the framing or opposing author's own words. The divergence
  is that in this case the opposing author is not the only source - an imperial law is - but the law
  cannot carry any part of the claim actually in dispute.

Built for the Table Readiness Round from the cleared Doc_04 SS3.5 (candidate D-A, classified Supporting
and scope-qualified to the Numidian regional sub-ecology at SS3.5's own Disposition and at SS4) and
from Doc_06 SS2, which is the direct source this step's own instruction points at: this world's
lexicon roster carries exactly ONE [CT]-tagged term, donlex018 Agonistici, and its specified Contest
Type is "Meaning + Historical scope" - whether the group's hostile literary characterization
substantially reflects its actual character and scale, or is substantially a polemical construction of
its Catholic and imperial opponents. That is the contest carried here, in the record type built for
it.

DOC_06 IS OTHERWISE A TIER-CONFIRMATION INDEX, and this step found the same thing the B-2/B-3 pass
found: its substantive contested-claim content is the single CT table at SS2, one row wide. A sweep
for "CT-" tags across all nine Doc_0X files and the Review-Artifacts returns that one table, its
companion-index check sheet, and review notes about it - no per-gravity CT-tag apparatus exists in
this world's construction record. The other seven records in this set are therefore built from Doc_04's
own per-gravity sections and Doc_07's synthesis, as this step's own instruction anticipated, rather
than from tags that are not there. This record is the one built from the CT tag itself.

THE THREE-TIER SPLIT IS THE POINT AND IS NOT FLATTENED. don.gravity.circumcellion-agonistici carries
it at full strength and this record repeats the discipline: bare existence, self-designation, and
character are three different evidentiary tiers, and the formation_confidence recorded on the gravity
is the third tier's because that is the tier a formation claim would actually rest on. The same logic
governs concedes above.

FREND IS NAMED, NOT USED. don.core.donatism caution 3 flags the native-social-protest thesis as the
most contested single argument this world's Registry draws on, with Shaw, Brown and Tengstrom as named
counterpoints, and flags it for priority second-opinion review before it supports any specific claim.
It supports no claim here; it appears in held_against as one of the readings between which this build
does not choose. canon_cells left empty, matching this world's records generally.
