claim: >-
  The three turns to imperial and proconsular machinery - the 313 petition to Constantine, the 361 petition
  to Julian for the restoration of confiscated basilicas, and the 390s use of existing anti-heretical
  legislation against our own Maximianist dissidents - are named, undenied exceptions held inside a
  principled refusal, not evidence that the refusal was opportunism all along. The gap was held openly,
  inside the same conviction, and naming it was never thought to require closing it.
held_against:
- The characterisation is the build's, not this communion's. Doc_04 SS3.6 states it plainly - calling this
  "principled refusal against pragmatic exception" rather than simple incoherence is that document's own
  synthesis of Doc_01 SS5's language, a defensible reading but not attested anywhere as this communion's
  own self-description of the tension. The two poles are Documented; the name given to the space between
  them is not (don.gravity.refusal-against-recourse's own divergence_note)
- The instances track advantage exactly, which is what an unsympathetic reading needs. Doc_07 SS4's own
  forces analysis finds that each of the three falls at precisely the moment the external power briefly
  offered something to gain - a hearing before Constantine, Julian's toleration, legal leverage against
  an internal rival. A pattern that fits opportunism without remainder is not evidence against opportunism
- The earliest instance is the founding moment itself. The 313 petition arises alongside the rigorist rupture
  rather than decades after it, so this is not a principle later compromised under pressure but a principle
  and its exception entering the record together (Doc_08 Cell 1B; don.gravity.refusal-against-recourse)
- The 390s instance turns the machinery on this communion's own dissidents - the identical use it condemns
  when the machinery is turned on itself. Whatever else the three instances are, they are not symmetrical
  with the refusal in what they ask the state to do
- No account of the tension from this side survives at all. There is no Donatist chronicle, administrative
  record, or documentary self-defence of any of these acts, so nothing in the record shows this communion
  reasoning about the gap, defending it, or noticing it (don.core.donatism thin_topics)
concedes: >-
  Every one of the three instances is Documented on its own historical terms and none of them is disputed,
  minimised or explained away here: Anulinus's relatio sits inside Optatus's own vendored appendix, and
  the Maximianist-era legal invocation is corroborated in Augustine's own primary text. What this
  communion's record cannot supply is the thing that would settle the contest - any evidence that the
  people who did these things experienced them as exceptions rather than simply not connecting them to
  the doctrine at all. The claim above states how the tension was held; the record supports that the
  tension existed and was never hidden, and stops there.

WHY THIS IS A SEPARATE RECORD FROM don.contested.refusal-of-imperial-legitimacy. The two are not the
same contest and Doc_04 already keeps them apart, testing T1 before G5 so that G5's own Persistence
result can be qualified by a named tension rather than smoothed into an unqualified PASS. On the
Primary's own record the three recourse episodes are material held AGAINST the refusal, as an
opponent would hold them. Here the contested question is the further one: whether the build's own
name for the gap - principled exception rather than incoherence - is a reading the record supports.
Doc_04 SS3.6 answers that question honestly in the negative on the evidence, and this record carries
that answer rather than defending the more comfortable framing.

THE CLAIM IS STATED IN THIS WORLD'S OWN VOICE AND IS THEN NOT PROTECTED. That is the discipline a
contested_claim record exists for: the claim field says what this world holds, held_against says what
the record itself will not underwrite, and concedes does not retreat to a softer version of the claim.
The softer version - that the exceptions were minor, or late, or forced - is available and is not
taken, because the record does not support it: the earliest exception is founding, and the pattern
fits advantage exactly.

A NO-RELATIONSHIP FINDING CARRIED FROM THE GRAVITY RECORDS. Doc_04 SS6 states explicitly that T1 and
T2 have no demonstrated relationship - they operate on different axes, external state relations
against internal disciplinary consistency - and both gravity records carry that as a finding rather
than an oversight. No relation to don.contested.rigor-against-reception is declared here either, for
the same stated reason. canon_cells left empty, matching this world's records generally.
