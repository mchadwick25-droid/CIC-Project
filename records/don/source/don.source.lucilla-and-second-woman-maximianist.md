Gender/Affirmative Duty discharge (Doc_02 SS6) and a Doc_09 Story Inventory candidate -- Optatus I.16 describes Lucilla, a wealthy Carthaginian laywoman, as the schism's own proximate cause ('at her instigation, and through her bribes'); Augustine's Letter XLIII SS26 draws Lucilla's own name into a direct parallel with a second, unnamed woman behind the later Maximianist council against Primian. Both passages were directly read and confirmed this session (13 occurrences in the Optatus file). The vendored edition's own translator's apparatus cites an Augustine letter numbered 'clxii' that does not match NPNF's own Letter CLXII -- very likely an older, pre-standard numbering scheme; the content is identified and vendored, but that specific numbering equivalence is not asserted.
