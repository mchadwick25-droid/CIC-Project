Monceaux names the three martyr texts as a deliberate trio: 'Un sermon: la Passio Donati. Un recit: la Passio Marculi. Une lettre: la Passio Maximiani et Isaac' -- confirmed verbatim -- and cites Migne columns that corroborate this Registry's own row 19/20 column citation. French-language; no public-domain English translation exists.
