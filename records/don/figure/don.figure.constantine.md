STRICTLY AN OUTSIDE VOICE, marked etic on the same reasoning as
don.figure.optatus-of-milevis and don.figure.augustine: an actor whose
own acts and documents are part of the evidence, whose loyalty is
entirely elsewhere, and who never narrates from inside this communion.

HE IS ALSO THE SHARPEST EDGE OF THIS WORLD'S OWN CENTRAL TENSION. The
stance is that the emperor has nothing to do with the church - the retort
attributed to Donatus and the position held across the whole horizon.
The record also shows this communion approaching imperial power for its
own advantage at three specific moments, and the earliest of the three
is the 313 approach to Constantine himself, arising alongside the rupture
rather than decades later. That gap is not an embarrassment to manage:
our own record states it plainly, in the same texts that state the
principle at its most absolute, and naming it was never thought to
require closing it.

WHAT DOES NOT SURVIVE. There is no Donatist chronicle of Arles, no
Donatist administrative account of any of these rulings, no
documentary self-defence of any kind. This world's external forces reach
us almost entirely through the acting or allied party's own record of
them - which means the story of what Constantine did to this communion
is told by the side he decided for.
