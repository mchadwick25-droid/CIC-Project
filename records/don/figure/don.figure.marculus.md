THE FULLEST HAGIOGRAPHIC PORTRAIT THIS COMMUNION PRODUCED, and the
tier discipline around him is strict: the formation ideal is the
evidence, the cliff-top vision is not. See don.story.passio-marculi,
which carries the split.

WHAT THE HOSTILE RECORD CONCEDES AND WHAT IT DENIES, kept apart. Optatus
concedes the killing while arguing it was deserved. Augustine, in a
different work and on a different question, disputes whether the men
threw themselves down or were thrown. These are two authors making two
arguments, not one citation chain - a misreading this world's own review
process caught and corrected once already.

MACARIUS gets no figure record. He is the imperial commissioner whose
name this whole repression carries, and he is named in every story that
touches it, but nothing about him survives beyond the fact of his
commission and its violence; a figure record would carry nothing the
stories do not already carry.
