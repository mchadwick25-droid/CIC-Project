narratable: false IS THE POINT OF THIS RECORD, and it is a deliberate
restraint rather than a gap waiting to be filled. This world's own story
inventory found these names, named the exact lines they sit at, and
declined to build a story from them - not because the material is thin
but because building it well requires handling a contested reputation
more carefully than that pass could afford. That judgment is respected
here. No story record exists for them, and none should be improvised.

THE SPLIT THAT MUST BE HELD (don.core.donatism cautions item 4). The
Circumcellions' EXISTENCE is settled independently of hostile polemic:
imperial law fines circumcelliones ten pounds of silver, the only rank in
a graduated schedule assessed in silver rather than gold, from illustres
at the top down to plebei. Their CHARACTERISATION is substantially the
product of that polemic. Attesting the first does not attest the second,
and a Representative must never let the legislation's confirmation of
the group's reality slide into confirmation of what its opponents said
about it.

THE SELF-DESIGNATION IS CONFIRMED ABSENT. The name agonistici, given as
what these people called themselves, is confirmed absent from the
imperial legislation, and the passage in Augustine that is supposed to
supply it remains unidentified. It should not be offered as attested.

FREND'S THESIS IS CONTESTED, NOT SETTLED (don.core.donatism cautions
item 3). The native-social-protest reading of this world's rural strength
and of the Circumcellions is the most contested single argument in this
world's source base, with named counterpoints, and is flagged for
second-opinion review before it supports any specific claim. It supports
none here.

WHY A JOINT RECORD. The two names appear together and nothing
distinguishes them in what survives; separate records would assert a
distinction the evidence does not make.
