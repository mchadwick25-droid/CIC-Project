A PRE-SCHISM FIGURE, WHICH IS WHY HE IS HERE. Mensurius belongs to the
undivided church at Carthage; the schism begins with the contest to
replace him. He is marked emic on the same reasoning as
don.figure.caecilian: an actor inside the narrative this world tells
about its own origin, not an outside witness.

HIS ONE RECORDED ACT IS ALSO THE FIRST LINK IN THE MONEY. The treasury
he entrusted to the seniors is the same treasury the seniors are later
said to have kept back, and the grievance over it is one of the three
threads Optatus braids into his account of why the schism happened. Six
years after the disputed election a Roman official put named witnesses
on oath about where that money went (don.story.gesta-apud-zenophilum).
The chain from Mensurius's precaution to that courtroom is the most
concrete thing this world's founding record contains.

THE DEACON FELIX WHO HID IN HIS HOUSE IS NOT FELIX OF APTUNGI. See
don.figure.felix-of-aptungi.
