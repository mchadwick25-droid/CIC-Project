AN OUTSIDE VOICE, marked etic: the state's own officer, whose court this
was, whose two edicts set the terms, and whose notaries produced the one
transcript in this world's record that no adversary curated. He is not a
member of either communion.

HIS ONE RECORDED MOMENT OF CANDOUR IS THE WHOLE PROCEEDING IN MINIATURE.
Asked which side owns the name Catholic, he answered that it is certain
the name belongs, after the contest, to those with whom the truth is
found - and then said he was nevertheless obliged to follow the form of
the imperial rescript, which had already been pleased to call the other
side by it. A judge who conceded the principle and was not free to act on
it. See don.story.conference-of-carthage-411.

HE ALSO OFFERED OUR BISHOPS CHAIRS and said it weighed on him to sit
while so many venerable men stood. Petilian thanked him and refused. That
exchange is on the record and is the single most humane moment in it;
it should not be dropped in favour of the outcome.

HOMONYM, FOUND AT THIS COMPILATION STEP AND NOT FLAGGED IN ANY OF THIS
WORLD'S CONSTRUCTION DOCUMENTS. The Passio Donati sermon names a tribune
Marcellinus among the agents who seized the basilica at Carthage,
alongside the count Leontius and the duke Ursatius - a different man, at
a different persecution, roughly a century earlier. Two tribunes named
Marcellinus standing beside imperial coercion of this communion is
exactly the shape of confusion this world's own cautions already warn
about for the two Maximians and the two Optatuses. See
don.story.passio-donati-sermon, whose body note carries the same
warning from the other end.
