Paired figure, following this project's own precedent for two people the vendored source itself always names and treats together (compare cappadocian.figure.forty-of-sebaste): Macrobius's letter narrates Isaac and Maximianus as one martyrdom account, not two independently developed lives, and this record follows that attested shape rather than inventing two separate figure records the evidence does not differentiate beyond their two distinct deaths (Isaac killed by the flogging itself; Maximianus surviving to be thrown into the sea).
