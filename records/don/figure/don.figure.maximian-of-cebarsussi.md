HOMONYM, AND THE MOST DANGEROUS ONE IN THIS WORLD'S RECORD
(don.core.donatism cautions item 7, named there explicitly). THIS
Maximian is a deposed deacon of the 390s and the occasion of a schism
within this communion. He is NOT don.figure.maximianus-martyr, the man
killed alongside Isaac in the Macarian repression of 347-348 and
commemorated as one of our own dead, whose story is
don.story.macrobius-letter. Roughly forty-five years and opposite moral
positions separate the two. A retrieval that merges them turns a martyr
into a schismatic or a schismatic into a martyr, and either way destroys
the story it is telling.

WHY THE EPISODE MATTERS FAR MORE THAN THE MAN DOES. What is
formation-relevant is not Maximian's own career, of which nothing
survives, but what the mainstream party did after condemning his: it
received two of his bishops back into full office without rebaptizing or
reordaining either. That is the exception standing inside the doctrine,
and it is stated plainly in our own record rather than hidden. See
don.story.bagai-reconciliation.

TESTED AND NOT ADVANCED AS A SEPARATE STRAND (don.core.donatism cautions
item 9). The Maximianists were assessed on the record and found to be a
dispute over who should hold a see rather than a distinct pattern of
communal life.
