STRICTLY AN OUTSIDE VOICE, and marked etic for that reason. Optatus
belongs to the other communion; he is not a member of this one and never
narrates from inside it. He is here because half this world's founding
narrative is unreachable without him, which is the exact shape of the
central evidentiary problem: the richest account of our own beginnings
is the one written to discredit them.

THE APPENDIX IS THE SUBTLER PART. Its items are genuine - a real
proconsular inquiry, a real court transcript, real conciliar acts - and
their genuineness is not in question. What is his own act is the
choosing. Four separate stories in this inventory rest on that dossier,
and every one of them lands against us: Felix cleared, a forger
confessed, Silvanus found a betrayer, the Cirta bishops declining to
finish their own question. A record that cites the appendix must name
the selection, not only the document.

DO NOT FLATTEN HIM INTO A CARICATURE EITHER. He is a careful compiler
who preserved documents that would otherwise be lost, and his hostility
does not make his transcripts forgeries. The discipline is to separate
what the documents say from what he says about them, and this
inventory's stories do that case by case.

HOMONYM (don.core.donatism cautions item 7). He is NOT Optatus
Gildonianus, the Donatist bishop of Thamugadi who enforced the
Maximianist reconciliation with troops - see
don.story.bagai-reconciliation. Nor is he the Optatus who appears among
the Catholic bishops in the 411 transcript.
