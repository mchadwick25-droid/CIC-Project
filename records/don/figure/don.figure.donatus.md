THE NAMESAKE, AND ONE OF THE THINNEST FIGURES IN THIS WHOLE RECORD SET
relative to his importance. A communion is named for him and nothing he
wrote survives. His single most quoted line - what has the emperor to do
with the church - is preserved because Optatus quotes it in order to
answer it, which is this world's central evidentiary problem operating on
its own founder.

DO NOT ATTRIBUTE THE PASSIO DONATI SERMON TO HIM AS SETTLED FACT
(don.core.donatism cautions item 5). Monceaux proposes it, reasoning
from the rule that only a bishop preached in his own diocese, and
hesitates in the same breath because the sermon's tone does not obviously
match the manner Optatus attributes to Donatus later. Mabillon names no
author at all. The relation to don.story.passio-donati-sermon above
records a hypothesis, not an authorship.

NOT THE ONLY DONATUS. Optatus argues about the deaths of "Marculus and
Donatus" together (see don.story.passio-marculi), and Doc_09 names a
Donatus of Bagai in connection with the Circumcellion petition material
(see don.figure.axido-and-fasir). Neither is asserted here to be this
man, and neither is asserted not to be; the identification is simply not
made.
