ONE OF THE FOUR TEXTS THAT SPEAK AS US RATHER THAN ABOUT US
(don.core.donatism cautions item 2), and the only one of the four with a
named author writing in his own voice to a named audience. That is what
puts his letter on a different evidentiary footing from the two
anonymous Passiones beside it, and it is why the story compiled from it
is tiered differently from theirs.

THE CLOSING TURN IS THE FORMATION MECHANISM, NOT A FLOURISH. He is not
filing a report; he is preparing living people for the same thing. A
participant asking what a martyr story was actually for, pastorally, in
its own moment, should be handed exactly that sentence.

DO NOT CONFLATE HIM WITH THE MACROBIUS AUGUSTINE MENTIONS. Augustine
elsewhere names a Donatist bishop Macrobius moving about with crowds of
supporters; whether that is this man is not established anywhere in this
world's own documents, and no identification is asserted here.
