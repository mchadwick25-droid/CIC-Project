THE PRIMATE THROUGH THIS COMMUNION'S HARDEST INTERNAL PASSAGE AND ITS
FINAL PUBLIC ONE. The Maximianist schism is a rupture against him
personally: a council at Cebarsussi deposed him in 393 and set up a rival
primate, and a council at Bagai of three hundred and ten bishops
condemned that party in 394 and kept him. What followed - the reception
of two condemned Maximianist bishops without rebaptism or reordination -
is the sharpest internal tension in this world's whole record. See
don.story.bagai-reconciliation.

HIS SILENCE AT 411 IS A READING, NOT A FACT. His name heads the mandate
read into the transcript, so his authority is on the record; the
argument was carried by Emeritus and Petilian. Monceaux reads that as a
considered strategy of silence, in deliberate contrast to their advocacy.
That is a secondary judgment about a negative - a man not speaking - and
it is recorded here as Monceaux's reading rather than adopted as this
world's own account of his conduct.

THE WOMAN BEHIND THE SCHISM AGAINST HIM IS NEVER NAMED. Augustine
parallels her to Lucilla, by name, in the same paragraph, and leaves her
nameless - see don.figure.lucilla's own body note.
