THE ONE MARTYR IN THIS RECORD WHOSE OWN WORDS ARE REPORTED BY A NAMED
WITNESS. What Isaac is remembered saying is not a pious formula but the
argument itself, thrown back at the men enforcing it: that the people
holding the whips were the traditores, and that the unity being imposed
was madness. That is the doctrine and the persecution meeting in one
sentence, and it is why this letter functions as formation material
rather than only as commemoration.

MACROBIUS READS HIS NAME AS A SIGN - a victim's name given to a victim -
and that reading is the bishop's, offered to a congregation who knew the
Genesis story, not a fact about Isaac. A Representative may report it as
Macrobius's reading and should not present it as more.

PAIRED, ALWAYS, WITH don.figure.maximianus-martyr. They are flogged
together, killed together, sunk together and buried together, and the
letter never treats either alone.
