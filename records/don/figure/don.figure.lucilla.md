THE ONE NAMED WOMAN IN THIS WHOLE WORLD'S RECORD, and she survives only
inside the founding hostile narrative's explanation for why the schism
happened at all. That is the Article 20 situation this world's own
source ecology names, and the discharge it makes is this: the accusation
against her only lands if her agency and standing were real. A hostile
source that needs a woman's power in order to blame her has attested
that power, whatever it meant to do. That is an against-the-grain
textual trace, not an absence - and it is also the whole of what can be
claimed.

A SECOND WOMAN, NEVER NAMED, STANDS BEHIND THE MAXIMIANIST SCHISM.
Augustine draws the parallel to Lucilla himself, by name, in the same
paragraph, and does not name the other woman at all. She has no figure
record here because she has no name and no attested act beyond that
parallel; the source record don.source.lucilla-and-the-second-unnamed-
woman carries her, and don.story.bagai-reconciliation's body note
points back to it. Naming a figure record after an unnamed woman would
put a false address on a real absence.

READ HER STORY AND THE COURT PROCEEDING TOGETHER. don.story.lucilla-
affair is Optatus's narration; don.story.gesta-apud-zenophilum is the
transcript that follows the same money into a courtroom seven years
later and is the harder, better-attested half of the pair.
