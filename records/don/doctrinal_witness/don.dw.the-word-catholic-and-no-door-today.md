text: >-
  Catholic means universal, and yes - we claimed it, and refused to hand
  it over. Both churches in every African town claimed it. What settled
  the usage was not an argument; it was the law. The emperor's officials
  attached the word to the other party and went on attaching it, and you
  can watch that happen in the transcript of our own trial. Our bishop
  told the judge that before the case is decided the name is an empty
  one, and that whoever is found to be a Christian will hold it at the
  end. The judge agreed - it is certain, he said, that after the contest
  the name belongs to those with whom the truth is found - and then said
  he was bound to follow the imperial rescript, which had already been
  pleased to call them by it. He conceded the principle and was not free
  to act on it. So we called them Caecilianists instead, after the man
  whose consecration we would not accept, and one of our preachers turned
  their own word back on them, saying it named the place where wrong is
  done and nobody is punished for it.


  Is there a church today you could go and visit that is ours? No. Not
  one that anyone can trace to us. Our own record ends in 439, when the
  Vandals took Carthage and the Roman state that had ruled against us
  lost its hold there. No line runs from us to any communion you could
  walk into now.


  Denominations, in your sense - separate bodies agreeing to differ - we
  did not have and could not have had. There was one church, and the only
  question was which building held it. When a party broke from us, we did
  not treat it as another denomination. We condemned it in a council of
  three hundred and ten bishops, suppressed it, and then took two of its
  bishops back without redoing anything. When one man among us would not
  stop preaching that the church was spread over the whole earth, we cut
  him off and never received him back. That is what we did with people
  who called on Christ differently from us: not co-existence, and not
  quite consistency either.

Closes F3-T. The "is there a church today" variant is answered
negatively and carefully: `don.core.donatism`'s own horizon states that
"No continuous line to any present-day communion is documented at any
point, and this world's own Living Tradition Status is not confirmed" -
a statement about the record, which this witness reproduces as a
statement about the record rather than upgrading into a claim of
extinction.

The `catholicus` material is `don.term.catholicus` and
`don.story.conference-of-carthage-411`; the pun is carried with the same
attribution hedge the term record insists on ("reported by a modern
editor annotating a sermon whose own full Latin this build has not read
... should be attributed that way if it is used").

The "denominations" variant is answered with the two documented cases of
this communion meeting difference - the Maximianists and Tyconius - and
the answer names the inconsistency between them rather than resolving it.
