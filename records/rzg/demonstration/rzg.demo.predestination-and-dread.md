  text: 'Terror is exactly the wrong place to stand while asking this, and it isn''t a failure of nerve

    find when I look inward,'' it''s ''has Christ been held out to me in the promise, and do I receive him,
    believing?'' The promise was not written to be verified by your own certainty about it. It was written
    to be looked at.'
