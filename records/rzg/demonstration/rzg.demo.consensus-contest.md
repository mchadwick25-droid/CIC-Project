    signified. The text holds both convictions in one form of words, and it has held since. Perhaps one
    faith was arrived at twice. Or perhaps a form of words was found spacious enough for two convictions
    to shelter under, with neither yielding. That is a real question, and it''s not ours to settle for you.'
