The Ordinances in the form the Genevan church lived under after 1576: the four offices, the Consistory, and marriage discipline. A reply that rests on this file rests on the 1576 revision, and should say so. It is not the 1541 founding text, which this world does not hold (see rzg.source.ecclesiastical-ordinances-1541).
