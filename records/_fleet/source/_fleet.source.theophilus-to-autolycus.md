Rights verified from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public Domain).

Dating: the edition's own introductory notice fixes Theophilus's episcopate
at A.D. 168-188 (Eusebius); Book III.28's editorial footnote fixes the
author's own chronological reckoning to the death of Verus, A.D. 169.
Together these support a composition window of c. 169-188 for the
treatise, without pinning a single year. Book II.15's own footnote on
Τριάδος: "The earliest use of this word 'Trinity.' ... He does not lug
it in as something novel: 'types of the Trinity,' he says, illustrating
an accepted word, not introducing a new one." A separate footnote on the
same passage glosses the referent as God, His Word (assigned elsewhere by
Theophilus to the Son), and His Wisdom (here assigned to the Holy Spirit)
- see _fleet.contested.theophilus-triad-referent for whether this triad is
the same referent as the doctrine later formalized at Nicaea.
