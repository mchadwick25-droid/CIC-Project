  The household catechism program the founder and the confessional voice prescribed from 1529 onward
  substantially failed at the parish level: the Saxon visitation records show a program that was preached,

Not unparked from a Doc_06 [CT] tag - this world carries only two, on justification and the two governments
(Doc_06 SS2), and Doc_06 SS2.3 explicitly considered and declined to tag this exact reception debate [CT],
reasoning that it "concerns whether the program was received, not what the term 'catechism' or 'household'
meant," and so belongs to Distortion Risk and to Doc_04's own confidence divergence rather than to the
lexicon's [CT] mechanism. That reasoning is sound on its own terms - Doc_06's [CT] tag is scoped to term
meaning - but it also means the reception debate was never routed toward this record type at all, and would
have gone unbuilt on the two-tags-only reading of this task's own opening framing. Found instead by the
check the process document's own B-6 row requires beyond Doc_06's tags: whether Doc_04 or Doc_08 name a
further genuinely contested item on one of this world's five Primary gravities. G4 (the household catechism)
does, and by the build's own account, sharply: Doc_04 SS3 G4 calls its Confidence/Gravity Cross-Check
divergence "flagged and the build's highest-stakes," and witt.gravity.household-catechism carries the same
language forward.

This is, on the evidence, the best-evidenced of this batch's four claims by one real measure and the
weakest by another, and both are stated rather than smoothed over. It is the only one of the four resting on
scholarly sources that are actually rowed in the Source Registry with names, dates, and publication facts,
rather than on in-text tension (the two [CT] tags) or an existence-only search hit (the
door-posting's Iserloh row) - a materially sturdier footing for calling it a live scholarly contest at all.
It is also the one furthest from anything this library can check directly: all four secondary works remain
unread, and the primary evidence they argue about - the visitation protocols - is not vendored in any form,
only named as absent. This record states both facts plainly rather than letting the stronger provenance of
the sources imply a false confidence about what they actually show.
