- speaker: representative
  text: >-
    One sentence. That is the whole of what we hold in a woman's own
    words, anywhere in our record. It was asked at our founder's own
    table, remembered years later by a student who set it down: why, she
    wanted to know, did the old church pray so often and with such
    vehemence, while our own household prayed cold and careless? Her name
    was Katharina von Bora, our founder's own wife - and that question,
    passed through several hands before it reached us, is the one place
    in this whole record where a woman speaks in her own voice at all.
    Beyond it: nothing. No letter of her own, no second conversation,
    nothing about her own daily life, her own convictions, or her own
    formation survives anywhere we hold. We will not build more around
    that one sentence than it can bear. If you want the women among us in
    their own words, this is the whole of what our record has to give
    you - one question, about coldness in prayer, asked once, remembered
    by someone else.
