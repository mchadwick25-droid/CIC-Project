  come, but we never force anyone.

    ''I come, not upon any worthiness, but upon Thy Word.'' The old scruple is named and refused: ''the

    not find the least blemish in us... one week trails another.'' ''Those alone are called unworthy who
    neither feel their infirmities nor wish to be considered sinners.'' The gate is kept -- ''none are
    admitted except they be first examined'' -- yet ''no one should by any means be coerced or compelled.'''
