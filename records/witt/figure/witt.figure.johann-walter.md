Narratable: true, argued directly. Walter is documented in this library by his own remembered words,
quoted at length in Bacon's Introduction, describing specific, concrete collaborative work with
Luther - being summoned to Wittenberg by name, the two of them deciding which chant tone would carry
the Epistle and which the Gospel, Luther's own explanation for the choice ("Our Lord Christ is a good
Friend, and his words are full of love"), and Walter's own errand carrying a copy of the finished Mass
to the Elector. Doc_09 witt-S07 names him "the only non-founder participant testimony to worship
practice in this library" - a real, if late and singly-sourced, window onto Wittenberg's worship from
someone other than Luther's own pen. Narratable, but bounded to exactly this testimony: nothing beyond
what his own letter, as quoted by Bacon, states is attested about his life, his other compositions, or
his later career.
