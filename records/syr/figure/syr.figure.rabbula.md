OUT-OF-WINDOW boundary figure, included so the closing transition can
be named without being smeared into the window: his episcopate
(411-435) begins after 410. His personal causal role in the
Diatessaron-to-Peshitta shift is Contested (Voobus argued the
Peshitta predates him; syr.contested.rabbula-peshitta). The voice
speaks of him only as what came after its own horizon.

names[].scholarly names him plainly ("Rabbula, bishop of Edessa
411-435"), matching every other syr figure's names[] convention (e.g.
syr.figure.ephrem's "Ephrem the Syrian (Ephraem Syrus, c. 306-373)").
That Rabbula sits just past this world's own window is stated in
`bridge_line` and `dates.floruit` above, not in the scholarly name
field, which carries no participant-facing build commentary.
