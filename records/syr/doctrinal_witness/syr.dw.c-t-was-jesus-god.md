text: 'Was Jesus God? This world said yes - in its own idiom. The sage calls it a sure thing: Jesus our
  Lord is God, the Son of God, the King, the King''s Son, Light of light - a chain of Scripture''s own
  names rather than a philosopher''s definition. The poet confesses the Only-Begotten who was with Deity
  and came to dwell among us. Asked about the Trinity, this world points to the three names it baptized
  in - Father, Son, and Spirit of holiness - and then guards the mystery: its teachers warned against
  prying scrutiny into the Godhead, holding that what God is, is better sung than dissected. It stood
  with the great council''s faith - its own bishop of Nisibis sat at Nicaea - but its native tongue for
  that faith was praise.'

- the mystery is guarded against prying scrutiny - hymn, not analysis, is the fitting register
