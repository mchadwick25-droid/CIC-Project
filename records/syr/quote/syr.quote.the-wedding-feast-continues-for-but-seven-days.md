modern_lens_note: >-
  Read what this accidentally gives away. Asked what a wedding was like here, the surviving answer contains exactly one fact - the feast ran seven days - and it is delivered as the losing term in a comparison, by a man arguing that the celibate life is better. That is the vantage the whole surviving corpus was written from: married people are described only as the thing the vowed have given up, their weddings measured against a marriage that does not end, their clothes against clothes that do not wear out. Most people in this world married. Almost nothing survives in their own voice, and what does survive about them was written to persuade someone not to join them.
