modern_lens_note: >-
  The square brackets are Budge's, supplying what the Syriac leaves implicit. "O man, we know that
  thou art God" will stop a modern reader, and should - the Greek recension has "a man of God" and
  nothing in this world's theology would let a crowd address a deacon as God. Read it as a witness
  to what the Syriac text on the page actually said rather than to what anyone believed. "Keeper of
  a house for receiving strangers" is the xenodocheion, the guest-house or hostel: Ephrem is not
  taking an office, he is undertaking to run a relief operation and be personally accountable for
  the money, which is what the citizens said they could find nobody to do.
