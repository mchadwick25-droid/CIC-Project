Imported from the old system's lexicon (syrlex010, "Aphrahat's Anti-Jewish Demonstrations") at Mark's
direction, drafted after deeper research into what the corpus actually supports directly.

Corrected from the old record in two ways. First, sourcing: the old record cited only modern scholarship
(Koltun-Fromm, Lehto) with no primary-text anchor. This record anchors its evidential claim to a
directly verified primary-source quote already in the corpus (syr.quote.aphrahat-anti-jewish-frame,
Dem XVII.1, verbatim and voiceable, presented honestly in historical context), and cites
Koltun-Fromm only at corroborating confidence
for the broader scope claim - the same confidence-tiering discipline already used throughout this
project, not a new precedent. Second, scope: world_core caution 4 names this as "roughly four of
Aphrahat's Demonstrations, with strands in Ephrem" - wider than the old record's Aphrahat-only framing -
and this record follows caution 4's own fuller scope.

This material is already substantially carried elsewhere in the new system, independent of this record:
world_core caution 4 states the one-sidedness plainly; the voice_craft guard states it in Mar Yausep's
own first-person voice; and two doctrinal_witness records (syr.dw.failures, syr.dw.unsettled) already
speak this content, tied to real canon cells, as the world's own answer-ground for its hardest true
things. This term record adds the one thing those don't provide: citation-anchored glossary retrieval
(Track B) for when a participant's own question or wording calls for it specifically.
