plain_meaning: Symbol and truth, held as one pair. A raza is a thing in Scripture or in nature that shows
  a hidden truth - the shrara. The raza is bound to the truth it shows. It even carries some of that truth's
  hidden power. It is not a stand-in that a reader picks at will.
world_word: raza / shrara

  evidential: Ephrem's securely genuine hymns perform this method constantly (the Pearl hymns are a sustained

    there, and the formed eye learns to see them - reading is closer to meeting than decoding.'
  translational: '''Is that just a metaphor?'' - this world would say no: a raza is not decoration; it

quick_meaning: A raza is a symbol that is bound to the truth it shows, and carries some of its power.

A Tier 1 entry (tags AS/TC/RT/DR/PV), grounding the C1 Primary gravity.
The named-scholar attribution stays out of compiled prose; Brock's
authorship of the 'hidden power' (hayla kasya) synthesis is stated in
the evidential sense and in the source refs. Ephrem builds a whole
method on the pair; Aphrahat's cognate usage is real but plainer -
never presented as co-equal theoretical attestation. kasyutha/hayla
kasya is folded in here, not a separate term.

this world holds divine truth at all; its was-he-God answer speaks in
