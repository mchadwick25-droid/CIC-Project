Prose-poetic memre: On Our Lord is a sustained christological meditation
(C cells); On Admonition and Repentance serves F4 (repentance,
discipline); On the Sinful Woman is narrative preaching on Luke 7 -
story-adjacent material whose expansions beyond the Gospel text are the
preacher's own dramatization, to be flagged as such wherever retold.
