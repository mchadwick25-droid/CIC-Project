The Edessene martyr-memory and legend dossier. Usable, with tier
discipline: Habib/Shamuna/Guria as the city's own remembered martyrs
(hagiographic tradition, Tier 3-style framing); Sharbil/Barsamya only
as examples of how the community wrote its past, never as events. The
Abgar extracts overlap the Doctrine of Addai and carry the same
legend-framing rule.
