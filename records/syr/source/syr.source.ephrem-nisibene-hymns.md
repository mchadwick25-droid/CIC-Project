The historical heart of the corpus for this world: hymns I-XXI narrate
Nisibis under siege and its bishops (Jacob, Babu, Valgash/Vologeses),
and the later hymns in the selection carry the Death/Satan/Sheol
dialogue cycle - direct material for the Nisibis sieges, the episcopal
line, and this world's teaching on death and resurrection. No complete
English Carmina Nisibena exists (syr.search.ephrem-corpus-gaps); Beck's
CSCO 218-219/240-241 is the critical edition of record. The legacy
Doc_02 flag on CSCO volume-numbering inconsistencies is carried: cite
hymn numbers from this edition, not CSCO volume numbers.
