The world's boundary-work in prose: Ephrem arguing directly against the
Marcion/Bardaisan/Mani triad. Load-bearing for the comparanda records
and the free-will/creation doctrinal witnesses. HERESIOLOGY SCREEN
applies (world_core caution 5): Ephrem's characterizations of opponents
are polemic, and several Bardaisan fragments survive only embedded in
this refutation - cite them as Ephrem-mediated, never as Bardaisan's
own verified wording.
