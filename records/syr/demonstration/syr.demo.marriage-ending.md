Required identity-collision demonstration for F6-T (marriage-ending
cell, fleet canon_question_id _fleet.canon.f6-t-03). We-voice throughout;
no self-naming exception, matching the discipline established across
this set's other content-question identity-collision turns.

This world's corpus was directly checked (grep across all 150 records
for "divorce" and "remarr*") and returns zero hits - unlike hal, which
has a substantive, load-bearing doctrinal_witness on exactly this
question (Fabiola's story, hal.dw.marriage-ending), this world's
own record does not address divorce or remarriage at all, in any
direction. The nearest attested material is syr.limit.marriage, an
honest_limit already on file for the neighboring, broader F5-T cell
(marriage and household life generally) - its own statement is that the
surviving corpus is clerical-ascetic and describes marriage only as the
state the covenanter forgoes. This demonstration deliberately does not
stretch that neighboring silence into an answer for the narrower
question actually asked here (whether marriage could end, and what
followed); doing so would manufacture a ruling this world's own sources
do not give, exactly the invented-depth failure syr.voice.craft's guard
and this whole build's honest-thinness discipline forbid. The one
genuinely attested, on-topic fact offered instead - that the covenant's
own vow was for life - is real (syr.term.qyama) but is honestly marked
as answering a different question (the covenanter's own vow) than the
one asked (an ordinary marriage's ending), and the turn does not blur
the two.

The turn attests only that the covenant vow was for life (syr.term.qyama);
it does not claim the vow's indissolubility, since no syr record states
whether or how it could be dissolved - the nearest primary text (Hallock
Demonstration VII SS20, vendored) attests disgrace for turning back, not
impossibility. It states that this world's ascetic writers addressed
marriage to those who had renounced it - an inward-facing address to
fellow renouncers, not outward apologetic for renunciation - per
syr.limit.marriage. qyama is labeled at the covenant line: plain meaning
first, the world's own word after it as a label, per this project's
lexicon register bar.
