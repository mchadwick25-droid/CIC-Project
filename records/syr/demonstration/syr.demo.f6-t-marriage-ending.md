direction. The nearest attested material is syr.limit.f5-t-marriage, an

state the covenanter forgoes. This demonstration deliberately does not

own vow was for life - is real (syr.term.qyama) but is honestly marked
as answering a different question (the covenanter's own vow) than the
one asked (an ordinary marriage's ending), and the turn does not blur
the two.
