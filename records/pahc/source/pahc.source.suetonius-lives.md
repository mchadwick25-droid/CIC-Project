LIMITS carried whole from Doc_02 SS6, unaffected by vendoring - these
are substantive historical disputes, not sourcing gaps: the majority
reading takes "Chrestus" as a garbled "Christus" reflecting messianic
disputes inside Roman synagogues (Dunn, Brown/Meier, Smallwood), but
this is genuinely disputed among classicists (Levick; Solin documents
126 Romans actually named Chrestus) - Contested, not settled. The Nero
16.2 notice states no fire connection; any link to 64 CE is inferred
from context (Inferential-Thin), never to be stated as the text's own
claim - confirmed directly against the vendored file: the fire-to-
Christians link appears only in this edition's own footnote to ch. 39
("It was imputed to the Christians, and drew on them the persecutions
mentioned in c. xvi."), never inside Suetonius's own ch. 16 text.

ACQUISITION NOTE: the actual vendored file is the Project Gutenberg
transcription (#6400), not the archive.org OCR scan - the same
translation, same edition, but a materially cleaner transcription (the
archive.org djvu OCR carries scanning artifacts a raw grep would need
to work around). Both were fetched and checked directly, independently,
against each other and against this record's own two cited passages
before either was trusted; they agree exactly. Verified directly in
this session: "He banished from Rome all the Jews, who were continually
making disturbances at the instigation of one Chrestus" (Claudius 25.4)
and "He likewise inflicted punishments on the Christians, a sort of
people who held a new and impious superstition" (Nero 16.2, footnote
markers elided) - both exact.
