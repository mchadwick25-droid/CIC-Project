Rights verified from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public Domain).
Transmission (Doc_02 SS1.5): no Greek manuscript preserves the whole
text - the modern text is an explicitly eclectic reconstruction (Codex
Sinaiticus to Mandate 4.3.6; Codex Athous to Similitude 9.30.3, with a
documented forgery-adjacent complication in its transmission, the
Simonides episode of 1855-56; P.Mich. 129 for Similitudes 2-9; Bodmer 38
for Visions 1-3; the ending of Similitudes 9-10 rests on Latin alone).
Cited as scripture by Irenaeus, Clement of Alexandria, and Origen;
included in Codex Sinaiticus; later excluded by Athanasius - its
canonical status was genuinely fluid.

WEIGHT: Strand B's second witness - Vision 2.4.3's plural "presbyters
who preside over the Church" reinforces 1 Clement's collegial picture;
also this world's best window into ordinary Roman penitential and
ethical concern (the second-repentance question) rather than doctrinal
argument. Genre discipline for downstream records: what is attested is
that Hermas presents his formation through reported visions - not a
claim that the visionary content is historical event.
