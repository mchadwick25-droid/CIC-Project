account; only the incidentally-preserved practice pattern beneath the

triangulation with Tertullian's Apology 39 on the communal fund is the
prior build's OWN synthesis, disclosed as such - both texts
independently attest mutual aid; no named scholar is on record making
that exact cross-text link.
