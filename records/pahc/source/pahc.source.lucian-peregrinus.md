A FILE NOW EXISTS, and it is the Fowler translation this record named as
the safe candidate. rights_status refers to it as well as to the ancient
text.

WHAT ELSE ARRIVED WITH IT. The acquisition is the whole of Volume IV, not
the Peregrine alone: Slander a Warning, The Hall, Patriotism, Dipsas, A
Word with Hesiod, The Ship, Dialogues of the Hetaerae, The Runaways,
Saturnalia, Cronosolon, four Saturnalian Letters, A Feast of Lapithae,
Demosthenes, The Gods in Council, The Cynic, The Purist Purized, and the
Fowlers' Notes Explanatory of Allusions. Several bear obliquely on this
world - The Runaways on the same false-Cynic theme, the Saturnalian
material on obligation between rich and poor, A Feast of Lapithae on a
shared meal going wrong - and are registered `provisional` in the corpus
map so that nobody concludes the volume holds only the Peregrine. THE
NOTES ARE 1905 APPARATUS and are never quotable as Lucian.

USE DISCIPLINE carried whole from Doc_02 SS6 and Doc_09 (Story 013):
the portrait of Peregrinus himself is satire and unusable as a positive
account; only the incidentally-preserved practice pattern beneath the
satire is evidence (Jones and Bremmer read it this way). The
triangulation with Tertullian's Apology 39 on the communal fund is the
prior build's OWN synthesis, disclosed as such - both texts
independently attest mutual aid; no named scholar is on record making
that exact cross-text link.
