Melito 70117, Hegesippus 71396, Dionysius of Corinth 71715, Rhodon

Apollonius 72748.

THIS WAS FOUND INSIDE A PILE LABELLED AS SOMETHING ELSE. The assignment
run put twenty-three anf08 works on this world and they read, at a
glance, as an apocryphal-Acts import. They are not one thing. The volume
splits by its own div1 sections: "Apocrypha of the New Testament" (line
35806) holds the Acts and infancy gospels, and "Remains of the Second
and Third Centuries" (line 69910) holds these - which are not apocrypha
at all. They are the surviving voices of this world's own generations,
and it had never opened them.

WHAT IT GIVES THIS WORLD THAT IT SAYS IT LACKS. The registry entry calls
this world "thinner on women's own words, enslaved members, the
non-literate majority, and any material remains." Hegesippus' notice on
the Lord's own grandnephews is direct evidence about the non-literate
majority: two smallholders with thirty-nine plethra between them,
working the land with their own hands, brought before an emperor and
released as too poor to matter. Nothing else in this world's corpus
looks at an ordinary Christian family's means and says a number.

AND IT GIVES A PRIMARY GRAVITY ITS SHARPEST CASE.
pahc.gravity.translocal-network is about the correspondence that held
scattered congregations together. Polycrates' letter is that network
under strain: the bishop of Ephesus writing to the bishop of Rome, in
the name of the bishops he has convened, to say that Asia will not move
its Passover and is not frightened.

NOT DRAWN ON FROM THIS SECTION, and each for its own reason: Maximus of
Jerusalem (10.9), whose attribution is itself disputed and is one of the
two works still flagged `needs-ruling` in the corpus map; Pantaenus
(10.15), who is alexandria-catechetical's; and Pseud-Irenaeus (10.16),
the Letter of the Churches of Lyons and Vienne, which the corpus map
carries as `provisional` on the attribution and which deserves its own
record rather than a mention here.

ALSO NOT YET DRAWN ON (flagged 2026-09-09, supplemental review, not
acted on): Apollonius's own fragments against Montanism (anf08 lines
72746-72790, c. 211 CE) - a further independent primary-voice witness
to the New Prophecy controversy, distinct from the Anonymous/"Asterius
Urbanus" material already drawn on at pahc.quote.asia-rejected-new-
prophecy. Available at zero further acquisition cost if a future pass
wants a second anti-Montanist voice; not built out here to keep this
integration pass narrow. Claudius Apollinaris, the other Montanism-
adjacent name in this section's author list, is a false lead on
inspection - his own preserved fragments here concern the Thundering
Legion and the Quartodeciman dispute, not Montanism.
