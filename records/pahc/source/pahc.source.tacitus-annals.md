A FILE NOW EXISTS. rights_status refers to the vendored file as well as
to the ancient text.

SCOPE DISCIPLINE, WHICH IS THE MAIN THING THIS RECORD NOW GOVERNS: what
was acquired is chapter 44 of Book 15, on its own, 375 words. It is the
chapter this world needs and it is the only Tacitus in this corpus. A
record that wants Annals 15.38-43 on the fire itself, or the Histories on
Judaea, does not have them and must say so rather than reach.

THE MEDICEAN READS "chrestianos", corrected by a later hand to
"christianos". This translation prints "Christians" and gives no note.
Nothing about what Romans called this group may rest on the English.

LIMIT carried whole from Doc_02 SS6: the passage's authenticity is not
seriously disputed among specialists, but whether a discrete,
fire-linked persecution of Christians AS A NAMED GROUP actually occurred
is a live, non-fringe debate (Brent Shaw vs. Christopher Jones) -
distinct from the passage's textual authenticity, and never to be
collapsed into it downstream. This event (with the deaths of Peter and
Paul under Nero, c. 64-68) is what Doc_01/Doc_08 treat as this world's
generative trigger - the closest thing it has to an origin event.
