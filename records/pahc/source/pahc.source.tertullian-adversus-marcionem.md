USE DISCIPLINE: hostile, out-of-window testimony. Everything known of
Marcion's own teaching here comes through an opponent's polemic -
Marcion's own Antitheses and his edited scriptures do not survive.
Usable for the disclosure obligation (Marcionite churches as an
established rival institution by c. 180-208, per Tertullian's and
Irenaeus's shared treatment) and for the conventional break date with
its Moll caveat; never as a fair statement of what Marcionites
themselves believed in their own words. Lieu's framing (Marcion as a
genuine rival whose challenge helped construct the category of "heresy")
is the modern calibration this build follows.
