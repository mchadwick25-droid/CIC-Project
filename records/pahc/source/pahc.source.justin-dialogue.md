Rights verified from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public Domain).
Same thin manuscript base as the First Apology (Parisinus graecus 450).
WEIGHT: this world's fullest display of how its scriptures were actually
read - proof-from-prophecy, the memoirs of the apostles read alongside
the prophets - and of the porous, argued Jewish-Christian boundary
(Doc_01 SS2.1's Ways-That-Never-Parted caution applies: the Dialogue is
an argument in progress, not a report of a settled separation). Also a
witness to inner-Christian plurality: Dialogue 80's acknowledgment that
some "who are called Christians" hold views Justin rejects, while
others he counts right-minded disagree with him about the millennium -
inside evidence that the boundary of the movement was still being drawn.
