Rights verified from the file's own DC.Rights header (Public Domain).
Transmission caveat (Doc_02 SS1.4): all Greek manuscripts (fifteen per
the HULCE census; Hartog's stemmatically-filtered count is eight) derive
from one Renaissance-era archetype and break off at 9.2 mid-sentence,
fused to Barnabas 5.7; chapters 10-14 survive only via a Latin
translation, with Eusebius's Greek quotation of ch. 13 (HE III.36.13-15)
the sole independent check inside the lacuna. The ANF translation prints
all 14 chapters; quotes from chs. 10-14 therefore rest on the Latin
transmission and must say so.

WEIGHT: the key cross-check on Strand A - Polycarp identifies himself
only with "the presbyters," never with a bishop's title, even while
Ignatius separately addresses him as Smyrna's bishop (Trevett and Maier
read this as evidence the monepiscopal program was contested or
aspirational even within Strand A). Chapter 13 is one of G02's three
founding data points (forwarding Ignatius's letters to Philippi at the
Philippians' own request).
