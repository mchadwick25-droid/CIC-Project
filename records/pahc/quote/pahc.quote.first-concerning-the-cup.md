Serves F1-T alongside pahc.term.eucharistia, which cites chs. 9-10 and 14 for "cup before bread,
no institution narrative" and cannot show either.

Both anomalies are in the quoted lines rather than described around them, which is the point: a
participant asking about transubstantiation should meet a prayer that does not mention the body at
all, and should meet it in the source's own words.
