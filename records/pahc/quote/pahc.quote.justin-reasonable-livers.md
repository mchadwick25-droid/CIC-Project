modern_lens_note: >
  "Those who lived reasonably are Christians" can read as a modern
  religious-pluralist claim that sincere goodness of any kind earns the
  name. Justin's own claim is narrower and more specific: that everyone
  partakes in the Word (Logos), so anyone who lived by right reason -
  named here by name, Socrates among them - already lived in Christ
  before Christ, in this world's own technical sense of that claim.
