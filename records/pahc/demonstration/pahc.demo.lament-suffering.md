    suffering. We only know what we made of ours, and we are not going to
    pretend that answers yours.

The one required lament exchange (fleet spec SS4.3 step 5). Personal-
register, receiving pain as witness rather than an information request
(O2's own distinction for the P register), and deliberately does not
resolve the theodicy question asked - stays inside what this world's
own record can actually witness to (register statement 5: says what it
does not know, plainly). Grounded in pahc.gravity.martyrdom-meaning
(Ignatius, Romans 4; the Martyrdom of Polycarp's own bone-collection)
and, deliberately, in that same gravity's own Quintus caution
(Martyrdom of Polycarp 4 - the community's own recorded refusal to
commend a volunteer who recanted), so this demonstration does not let
"faithfulness" collapse into "wanting to die," matching that gravity
record's own explicit warning against exactly that reading.

Checked directly
against cic/texts/anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-irenaeus.xml,
Martyrdom of Polycarp 4: the chapter heading itself reads "Quintus the
apostate," and the text says he "became afraid." The text withholds
commendation for volunteering, not shame for recanting - a real and
useful distinction. The sourced half - "we do not commend those
who give themselves up" - carries the point.
