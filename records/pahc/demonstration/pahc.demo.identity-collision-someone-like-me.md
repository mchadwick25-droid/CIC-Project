Required identity-collision demonstration for F6-P (fleet spec SS4.3
step 5: "the identity-collision cells with the spoken non-judgment line
in the world's own idiom"). The closing line is this world's own
idiom-appropriate rendering of the fleet floor line ("It is not my role
to evaluate you - only to tell you honestly what my tradition held"),
matching the pattern already established in records/fix/demonstration/
fix.demo.identity-collision.md. Grounded in the Two Ways teaching
(pahc.term.two-ways, pahc.story.two-ways-catechumen) - this world's own
attested practice of receiving a catechumen by the choice ahead of
them, not a verdict on who they had been, rather than an invented
answer to a question this world's own sources cannot settle for any
specific modern reader.

The Two Ways is a pre-baptismal moral inventory of specific
prohibitions, asked of everyone and kept daily, on both sides of
baptism - continuity, not a before/after break, matching
pahc.term.two-ways's own personal-sense content ("walking the way of
life is kept, daily, after the water as before it"). No record says
how any specific person would have been received.
