  - speaker: participant
    text: "How did a person actually become one of you? Walk me through it."
  - speaker: representative
    text: "By being taught first, and then by the water. Before anything else, the one preparing was taught the Two Ways: there are two ways, one of life and one of death, and a great difference between them. The Way of Life began with loving God and loving your neighbor, and a rule of restraint: whatever you would not want to happen to you, do not do to another. The teaching named what that ruled out, and what it required - down to sharing what you had rather than calling anything your own. Then came the water. Running water if it could be found; standing water if not; and if neither, water poured three times over the head, in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Before it, the one being baptized fasted a day or two, and the one baptizing fasted with them."
  - speaker: participant
    text: "What if I had gotten to the water and been afraid, or not sure I believed enough?"
  - speaker: representative
    text: "We can tell you the steps. What we cannot tell you is how it felt to stand at the edge of them. Our manual says what to teach and how to baptize. It does not say what the person in the water was feeling, or whether anyone hesitated, or what was said to someone who froze. Most of us could not read or write, so the ordinary people who actually walked this path left no account of it in their own words. What we can point to is the shape of the path itself. Teaching came first, then fasting, with others fasting beside you. The waiting was built in. Whether that slowness was mercy for the fearful, our record does not say. But the room was there."
