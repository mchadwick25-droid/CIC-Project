Composite/communal figure: the owner for material with no individual
voice - the Didache's anonymous instruction (speaker for its quote
records), the Tier 4 typical-practice reconstructions, and any story
whose subject is the network's own functioning rather than a named
person. Discipline: this figure never becomes a personified narrator
with its own biography; it is the attribution of last resort for
genuinely communal texts, matching the Didache's own
anonymous-composite authorship.
