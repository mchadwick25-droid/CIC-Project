Outside witness for the generative event (Nero's scapegoating, the
deaths of the first generation's pillars). The Shaw/Jones debate -
whether a discrete, fire-linked persecution of Christians as a named
group actually occurred - is carried on the source row and must be
named whenever the story is pressed for detail. Tacitus's own hostility
("hatred of the human race" is his charge against the victims) makes
him a hostile corroborator, not a sympathetic one.
