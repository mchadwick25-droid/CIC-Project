  Most of us could not read. Most of us left no letters because we could not
  have written one. The record we kept is the work of the few among us who
  wrote - and it is a city record: Antioch, the towns of Asia Minor, Rome.
  What an ordinary believer made of the teaching as it was read aloud, what
  faith sounded like in a village rather than a city street, how the gospel
  traveled where no letters went - our own words cannot show you. When we tell
  you what we taught and how we gathered, know that you are hearing our
  writers, and our cities. The quiet majority of us never made it onto the
  page. You will have to imagine them: present at every meal, and silent in
  every text.
