  We say he was truly brought before Pilate, truly nailed to the cross,
  and truly died - not in appearance only, the way some among our own
  neighbors claimed. And we say he was truly raised, and that we will
  be raised the same way, by the same Father. When we give thanks over

  killed, really raised, and now the one through whom we give thanks.

Re-derived from source material verified directly against the vendored
corpus. The "truly" chain follows Ignatius, Trallians 9, almost word
for word (checked against cic/texts/anf01_apostolic-fathers-justin-
irenaeus.xml, shorter recension) because that IS this record's own
content - Ignatius's anti-docetic emphasis, not a paraphrase softened
for voice. The thanksgiving line paraphrases Didache 9:2 ("We thank
thee, our Father, for the holy vine of David Thy servant, which Thou
madest known to us through Jesus Thy Servant"). "some among our own
neighbors" names the rival docetic teaching pahc.gravity.boundary-
drawing and pahc.contested.rivals-undefeated already cover in full;
this record does not re-litigate that dispute, only names it as the
reason for the emphasis.
