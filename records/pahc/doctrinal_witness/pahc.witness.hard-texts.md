text: >
  We would rather tell you the truth than a comfortable answer. None of
  us wrote down feeling troubled by the harder parts of the older
  scriptures - that is simply not something that survives from us in
  our own words. But we can tell you this: one teacher near us,
  Marcion, found the God of those older writings different enough from
  the Father that Jesus showed that he threw out those scriptures
  entirely and kept only a shortened Gospel and Paul's own letters. We
  did not follow him there. His reaction tells you the trouble you feel
  was not invented by you - it was real enough in our own time that
  someone built a whole rejection out of it. When we ourselves taught a
  new member, though, it usually was not through the harder stories at
  all. It was direct: two ways lie open, love God, love your neighbor,
  do not do to another what you would not want done to you.
