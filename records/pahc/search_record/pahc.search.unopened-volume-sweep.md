THE WINDOW DID MOST OF THE WORK, and this world's list was the cleanest of
the five because of it. Seven of nine volumes are fourth-century or
Montanist-period material for a world that ends in 200. For desert or ijc
a late volume is a judgement call; here it is a boundary, and the
boundary is this world's own.

THE HARD CASE WAS anf04, and it is worth recording that it was hard.
Tertullian's fourth part carries On the Veiling of Virgins, To His Wife,
On Monogamy, On Modesty and De Fuga in Persecutione - which is to say
household order, marriage, congregational penance and whether to run,
every one of them a subject this world is built around. The temptation to
take them was real. They are c. 206-220, written after Tertullian had
moved toward the New Prophecy, and this world has anf03's Apologeticus of
197 for exactly the reason that 197 is inside the window and 208 is not.
Declined, and named here so that the next person to notice how apt those
treatises look finds the reasoning rather than an unexplained gap.

THE ONE ADMISSION HAD TO ARGUE FOR ITSELF and then corrected this world.

pahc.force.selective-canonization is a transmission force - it is about
what happened to this world's books afterward, not about what this world
believed - and it said Hermas was "excluded by Athanasius". That claim
needed the Festal Letter of 367, and this world had never opened it. Read
directly, Athanasius does not exclude the Shepherd. He names a second
class of books, "not indeed included in the Canon, but appointed by the
Fathers to be read by those who newly join us", and puts the Shepherd and
the Teaching of the Apostles - the Didache - in it, beside Wisdom,
Sirach, Esther, Judith and Tobit.

BOTH of this world's own nearly-lost texts, moved together, in one
sentence, from the church's reading to the catechumens'. That is a better
account of how they thinned than a ban would have been: a book handed to
beginners is a book fewer people have reason to copy. The force record's
description and manifestation are corrected rather than quietly amended,
and pahc.quote.appointed-to-be-read carries the sentence.

The same letter also gave pahc.source.second-century-apocrypha the
ancient statement of its own problem - apocrypha as "an invention of
heretics, who write them when they choose... and assigning to them a
date, that so, using them as ancient writings, they may find occasion to
lead astray the simple". A fourth-century bishop describing
pseudepigraphy as a technique, back-dating included.
